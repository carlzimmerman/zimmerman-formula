## The four items the orchestrator asked for
1. **Fitted (DC14 run) log M_disk vs photometric log M*, per galaxy:** columns `DC14_logMdisk` and `logMstar_phot` in the CSV (124 galaxies with both). **Median difference −0.14 dex; 16–84% −0.67 to +0.42; |diff| > 0.3 dex for 74 of 124, > 0.5 dex for 46; range −1.67 to +0.96.** The quoted 1σ error of log_Mdisk has median 0.32 dex (max 0.63). So the dynamically fitted stellar mass differs from the SED mass by typically 0.3–0.5 dex, in both directions. The disc mass in the DC14 run is approximately X times the halo virial mass (`log_X + log_Mvir` versus `log(M_disk + M_bulge)`: median difference +0.035 dex, 90th percentile of |difference| 0.16, max 0.33; medians of separate posteriors, so not exact; my reading). 15 galaxies have a bulge component (`log_Mbulge`, `BT_ratio`, `rotation_curve = DC14MGEsgasdarkHernquist`); the other 111 are `DC14MGEsgasnone`.
2. **The sgas gas component:** `gas_density` is a **fit parameter, a surface density in M☉ pc⁻²**, with the prior box 0 to 15 M☉ pc⁻² (the `min_boundaries`/`max_boundaries` lines of ID0003's `run_parameters.txt`: `s_gas=0.00` to `15.00`; assumed the same for all galaxies, not checked for the others). **No gas extent, radius or scale height is stated in any of the files** (the only thickness parameters in `model.txt` are `aspect = 0.15` and `thickness_profile = gaussian`, which I read as the disc's, not the gas's). **The gas density is prior-dominated: for 117 of 126 galaxies the upper 95% limit is ≥ 14 M☉ pc⁻² (prior max 15) and for 119 the lower limit is below 1**; the median fitted value is 7.6 M☉ pc⁻², about half the prior range.
3. **Coverage of `true_Vrot.dat` (25 rows per galaxy, median):** maximum |R|/R_e on the positive side has median 6.81 (min 1.78, max 15.58), negative side median 6.72; 115 of 126 reach beyond 3 R_e. These rows are the model's slit profile, so the coverage is the model's extent, not the data's: the files do not say how far the MUSE data reach. (The pilot's `rad_Re` = `dx_arcsec`/R_e with R_e = `rad_kpc` (derived parameters) / `kpc` (model.txt): verified for all 126 within 2%.)
4. **Is the stored v "true", deconvolved, pressure-corrected?** The files do not say. All 126 models have `adrift = DalcantonFull` and `dispersion_profile = thick`. An indirect hint (mine, with my assumptions, UNVERIFIED): comparing the table's v at R = 2.2 R_d (taking R_d = R_e/1.678, exponential disc; and reading `v22` as the velocity at 2.2 R_d) with the derived `v22` gives a median ratio 0.69 (16–84%: 0.51 to 0.83), and dividing by sin i gives a median 0.87 (0.72–0.96); the ratio correlates with sin i (r = 0.57). That is consistent with the table's v being the inclination-PROJECTED (line-of-sight) rotation velocity, further reduced by ~13% relative to v22 (asymmetric drift and/or my R_d assumption). It is NOT established. Whichever it is, a deprojection or an asymmetric-drift term has to be checked before the table's v is used as a circular velocity.

## What this does and does not give the per-galaxy RAR idea
It gives per-galaxy v(R) and σ(R) along the slit (model), R_e, inclination, redshift, the SED stellar mass and a gas surface density that the data barely constrain. It does not give a measured gas mass, the gas extent, or the model's baryon rotation curves; and the stellar mass the DC14 run used differs from the SED value by more than 0.3 dex for 60% of the galaxies.

## Addendum: what the two papers' text says about the radial extent and the velocity convention (read-only check, later 2026-09-29)
Sources: the arXiv HTML text of MUSE-DARK-III (arXiv:2604.22613) and MUSE-DARK-I (arXiv:2506.19721), read through a page summariser (section numbers as it reported them; UNVERIFIED against the PDFs). No file was fetched.
- **Radial extent of the data.** MUSE-DARK-I Section 2: "rotation curves extended up to 2−3 times the effective radii"; Section 2.1: velocities probed "up to 10−15 kpc, corresponding to 2−3 times the effective radii"; its Figure 9 marks 2 × R_e and, per Section 5.2.1, an "S/N < 1" limit beyond which measurements become uncertain. **Neither paper states a per-galaxy R_last/R_e or the fraction reaching 2 R_e.** MUSE-DARK-III states no radial extent (it only excludes measurements at r < 2 kpc, Appendix B). So the `true_Vrot.dat` coverage (median 6.8 R_e) is the model slit; the data's own reach is stated only as 2–3 R_e.
- **Velocity convention.** MUSE-DARK-III Appendix B: circular velocity "corrected for pressure support (asymmetric drift) following Dalcanton & Stilp (2010), such that v_c²(r) = v_⊥²(r) + v_AD²(r)", with v_⊥ the observed rotation velocity and v_AD the pressure correction, and "all velocity terms are intrinsic, i.e. corrected for any instrumental effects, including seeing". MUSE-DARK-I (Figure 9): v_⊥(r) is "corrected for inclination and instrumental effects". The pressure term: v_AD = α σ²(r/r_d) with α = 0.92 (Dalcanton & Stilp), a thick disc with σ_thick(r)/v_c(r) = h_z/r and a dispersion floor σ₀, h_z = 0.15 R_e (MUSE-DARK-I Section 2.1–2.2; the same 0.15 as `aspect` in `model.txt`).
- **Consequence for my earlier hint.** The earlier hint that the table's v looked inclination-projected is probably wrong. The text says v_⊥ is already deprojected and instrument-corrected, so the more likely reading is: `true_Vrot.dat`'s `v_kms` is v_⊥, the rotation velocity BEFORE the drift term is added, and the derived `v22` is v_c = sqrt(v_⊥² + v_AD²). Then the median ratio v_table/v22 = 0.69 (16–84%: 0.51 to 0.83) means v_AD is a large part of v_c² for many of these low-mass galaxies (median v_AD²/v_c² ≈ 0.52 at 2.2 R_d under my assumptions R_d = R_e/1.678 and v22 at 2.2 R_d). This is my inference from the papers' formula, not stated for the released file; whether the file stores v_⊥ or something else is still not stated.
- **Stellar mass and gas (MUSE-DARK-I Sections 2.2–2.3, 5.2.3).** Priors on M*: "log(M⋆/M☉) = log(M⋆/M☉)_SED ± 0.15" in some fits; **"for DC14, Dekel–Zhao, baryon-only: we used no priors on M⋆"**, and for DC14 "stellar mass was a free parameter (contained in X)" with X = log(M⋆/M_vir). So the DC14 run (the one III uses) has no SED prior on M*, which agrees with the 0.3–0.5 dex differences in the files. Gas: "constant Σ_HI surface mass density leading to v(r) ∝ √(Σ_HI r)", the unknown HI content is marginalised (Section 2.2); three HI parameterisations (constant, Martinsson et al. 2016, Wang et al. 2025) are tested in an appendix. **Not stated in the text read: the prior range on Σ_HI (the files show 0–15 M☉ pc⁻²), the HI extent relative to the galaxy, or the fraction of galaxies with unconstrained HI.**
