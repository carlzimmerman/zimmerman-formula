## What the files hold
- **`DC14_3_true_Vrot.dat`** (25 rows): columns `dx_arcsec`, `rad_Re`, `flux_slit`, `v_kms`, `sig_kms`, i.e. a signed profile along the slit of the model's "true" (deconvolved) velocity and dispersion, from −2.25 to +3.06 R_e (−2.02 to +2.75 arcsec). v runs −145 to +154 km/s; σ falls outward from 49.3 km/s at the centre to 37.7 km/s at 3.06 R_e. (The file has no header describing "true" and no units line; whether v is the intrinsic circular velocity, the rotation velocity after the Dalcanton asymmetric-drift term, or something else is not stated in the file.)
- **`DC14_3_galaxy_parameters.txt/.dat`**: the fitted galaxy parameters with 95% intervals: x, y, z, flux, radius (4.50 ± 0.03 pixel), Sérsic n (0.90), inclination 56.9°, PA 238.6°, concentration, virial velocity 160.6 ± 14.5 km/s, velocity dispersion 36.6 ± 1.0 km/s, **`gas_density` 10.56 ± 3.94 M☉ pc⁻² (95% CI 0.88 to 14.83)**, log X = −1.97, line ratio.
- **`DC14_3_derived_parameters.txt/.dat`**: `v22` 149.4 ± 1.6 km/s, `log_Mdyn` 10.41, `log_Mvir` 12.07 ± 0.11, `Rvir`, **`log_Mdisk` 10.11 ± 0.10**, `fDM_at_Re` 0.76 ± 0.06, halo density slopes, `rs_kpc`, `rad_kpc`, `v2kpc`.
- **`DC14_3_model.txt`** and `run_parameters.txt`: the model is `ModelDecomp` with `rotation_curve = DC14MGEsgasnone` (DC14 halo, **MGE stellar disc**, **`sgas` gas**, no bulge), `adrift = DalcantonFull` (the Dalcanton–Stilp pressure correction), `dispersion_profile = thick`, `thickness_profile = gaussian`, `aspect = 0.15`, redshift 0.6217; the fit uses MULTINEST, 14 free parameters.
- **`stats.dat`**: best χ² 1.03, BIC 16231.5, N_dof 15610, log Z 16211.7.

## What the pilot answers
1. **Fitted vs photometric stellar mass, for this galaxy:** the DC14 run's `log_Mdisk` = 10.11 ± 0.10 against the photometric `logMstar_phot` = 10.08 (`musedark_joined.csv`, muse_id 3): they agree to 0.03 dex, whereas the baryons-only fit for the same galaxy gives 10.80. One galaxy only: nothing here says the other 125 behave the same, and the paper says M* is not fixed by photometry.
2. **Gas:** the file gives a gas surface density (10.56 ± 3.94 M☉ pc⁻², a broad interval) and the `sgas` label, not a gas mass or radius; a total M_gas would need the gas profile's extent, which the files do not state.
3. **Per-radius baryon components:** the numeric files do NOT contain v_bar(R) or g_bar(R). `true_Vrot.dat` has velocity and dispersion only. The disc component is an MGE built from the mass map (`mass_3.fits`, 331 KB, listed but not fetched), the gas component is `sgas`, and the halo is DC14 with the parameters above; the components' rotation curves would have to be reconstructed with GalPaK3D's own model code or read from the plot (`RC_decomp_3.png` 138 KB, `DC14_3_rotcurve.pdf` 49 KB; neither fetched).
4. **Dispersion:** the dispersion profile (`dispersion_profile = thick`) is part of the model, so the pressure-support term uses a σ that declines outward here, not a constant.
