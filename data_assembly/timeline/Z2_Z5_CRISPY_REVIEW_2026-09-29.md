# z ~ 2-5 review and ways to use what we hold (data front, 2026-09-29)

Basis: `sample_ledger.csv` rows with z 2-5, the calc thread's standing text (`campaign_fresh_gravity/STANDING_2026-09-29.md`, CFG196/197/162/164/170) and the NOEMA3D tables. Ideas, not results: nothing here is computed beyond counts, and nothing decides flat a0 against a0 ~ H(z).

## What z 2-5 holds (counts from the local tables)
ALPAKA I 16 of 28 at z >= 2 (6 at z >= 3); Amvrosiadis+2025 26 of 30 parent at z >= 2 (12 modelled); CRISTAL 32 ([CII], model-based baryons); Roman-Oliveira 5 (z 4.3); Danhaive 41 (no gas); plus Lelli+2023 (2), Big Wheel, DLA0817g1, PKS 0529-549 (one each). The calc thread's verdicts at z > 2: CFG196 CANNOT DISCRIMINATE (0.91 sigma vs 2; reach only ~1.3-1.4 R_e), CFG197 no separation (median M_dyn/M* 5.1 far above both floors).

## Ideas, ranked by what they would change
1. **NOEMA3D supplies the ingredient CFG196 said was missing.** CFG196's best variant (1.54 sigma) needed curves to 3 R_e with measured CO gas; NOEMA3D has CO curves to a mean 2.74 R_e for 10 discs, CO/dust/[CI] gas and SED M*, and one disc is also in PHIBSS 2013. Blocked only by the curves being figure-only (paper 1 Fig. 6). Needs the source PDF (14.8 MB, arXiv:2604.18503v2; the companion is 5.7 MB) and the owner's go; then a frozen-criteria pre-flight in the calc thread before any curve is read.
2. **Replace the gas prior with a measured spread.** CFG164/170 turn on the assumed molecular-gas fraction. NOEMA3D paper 2 tabulates S_CO, S_CI and S_dust for six discs (CO + [CI] + dust), so three independent gas masses per disc can be rebuilt with the stated factors. That gives an empirical width for the gas ceiling instead of a scan. Data reconstruction only; the calc thread would decide use.
3. **Measure the pressure axis instead of scanning it.** CFG162/175 scan s = 1-3 because it is unknown which sigma supports the disc. Girard+2021 reports ionised sigma ~2.45x molecular; KURVS/KROSS/RC41 use H-alpha, CRISTAL [CII], NOEMA3D and ALPAKA CO. A same-galaxy two-tracer table (Ubler+2018 z = 1.4, Lelli+2023's two discs, Girard+2021, any RC100 disc with both H-alpha and CO) would give a measured range. Read-only literature hunt; none of it is in the repo yet.
4. **The regime gap is the real obstacle, and extended cold gas is the only bridge.** No sample holds measured gas AND g_bar < a0 at z > 1. Molecular reservoirs reach beyond the stars (Rybak+2024, 19 lensed sources at z 2-4.5, abstract only), and [CII] halos exist at z 4-6. A hunt for radial CO/[CII] gas profiles plus rotation in gas-dominated z > 2 discs would target g_bar < a0 with measured baryons. Candidates in hand: ALPAKA I CO profiles, Amvrosiadis, CRISTAL.
5. **Relabel the ledger.** Rows tagged "Newtonian/high acceleration only" (Lelli+2023 at 3-4 a0, ALPAKA 2-32 a0) use the flat-a0 reading. H(z)/H0 is about 3.0 at z = 2, so under the rival those discs sit near 1 a0(z), the transition regime where the laws differ most. Housekeeping: Price RC41, RC100/Genzel+2020 and NOEMA3D-II are also missing or thin in the ledger.

## Plainly
None of this is a breakthrough by itself. The data in hand cannot decide the front; the two things that could are (a) a measured-gas, low-g_bar, z > 1 sample, which does not yet exist (ideas 1, 4), and (b) Gaia DR4 on 2 December 2026, the one pre-registered sharp test (CFG200: ownership vs merged 2.3-3.7 sigma).
