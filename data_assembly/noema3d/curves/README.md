# NOEMA3D paper-1 kinematic profiles, digitised from the raster figure (PDF Fig. 5; HTML Fig. 6)

Source: `~/new_physics/_external_data/noema3d/arxiv_2604.18503v2.pdf` (page 15, one raster image 3862 x 2376 px, extracted by `render_fig.py`). Digitised on the owner's go in the data chat; the calc thread's CFG214 criteria (8340f3750) were frozen before any value was sent. Nothing here is an acceleration, a0 or a velocity ratio, except the declared validation gate V2.

## Files
`noema3d_fig5_data_markers.csv` (948 rows: galaxy, panel = velocity / dispersion / flux, series, colour, R_arcsec, R_kpc, value, err_up, err_down, visible_fraction, recovered_at_shared_bin, px_x, px_y), `noema3d_fig5_model_curves.csv` (4795 rows: the drawn model lines sampled every 4 px, with `interpolated` and `gap_px_to_visible_line`), `noema3d_fig5_axis_calibration.csv` (per panel and axis), `noema3d_fig5_legends.csv` (series labels and colours, read from the printed legends), `noema3d_fig5_outermost_data_radius.csv`, `validation_report.json`, `SHA256SUMS.txt`, `overlays_A/` (each panel with the detected marker centres crossed; magenta = recovered at a shared bin), and the `*_variantB.csv` files (the independent second read).
Code: `fig_geometry.py` (frames, ticks, OCR calibration), `extract.py`, `run_all.py`, `validate.py`.

## What the figure shows (as printed)
- **V-axis label:** "Velocity [km/s]". The x axes read "Offset along the major axis [arcsec]" (bottom) and "[kpc]" (top). Both are signed offsets: the curve is plotted on both sides of the centre, NOT folded.
- **Observed, not deprojected:** the printed curve is the line-of-sight velocity along the slit (G4_38065 reaches about 100 km/s at inclination 18 deg, i.e. V_c sin i).
- **Model line = the best-fit 3D model cube read out like the data** (paper Sect. 3.4: the model velocity map is extracted "in a similar fashion as done for the actual data"), so it is the beam-smeared observed-projection model, NOT V_c or V_rot of the intrinsic disc. One data series and one model line per weighting (natural, robust5, robust1, robust0.1, taper, tapered); the label -> colour map is in `legends.csv` (OCR reads "robust1" as "robustl" and "robust0.1" as "robusto.1"; normalised in the CSVs).
- **R_e,disk is not drawn** in any panel. The figure has no marker for the outermost radius; it is the largest |R| marker per series (V4 file).
- Panels per galaxy: velocity, dispersion and normalised CO flux, data and model.

## Validation (`validation_report.json`)
- **V1 axes:** all 90 axes (30 panels x bottom, top, left) calibrated by OCR of the tick labels snapped to the detected tick marks, with a repair for dropped decimal points. Max residual over span: arcsec 0.005 (0.16%), kpc 0.052 (0.26%), velocity 0.72 km/s (0.16%); median 0.002 arcsec, 0.021 kpc, 0.20 km/s. Pixel-to-kpc check: kpc per arcsec from the two axes agrees with the Planck18 angular scale (comparison only) to better than 0.4% for 9 of 10 galaxies; **G4_38232 differs by +3.2%** (8.69 vs 8.42; the cause was not established).
- **V2 gate (declared before the comparison): model V at +-R_e,disk / sin i (Table 1 inclination) vs Table 3 V_c, within 5%, series = the model line reaching the largest |R|.** 1 PASS (G4_38232, taper, +0.9%), 9 FAIL and excluded (deviations +7.1% to -45.6%, mostly negative). The comparison is against a beam-smeared line-of-sight model, so a post-hoc diagnostic (V2b, not part of the gate) lists every series with R_e,disk over the largest beam major axis: the deviation is largest where that ratio is small (G4_24078 0.39: -38% to -44%; G4_17555 0.47: -46% natural, -23% robust5) and near zero or positive where it is 1.7-1.9 (G4_38065, G4_38232), consistent with beam smearing but not proven (inclinations are the paper's Table 1 values; several traces are partly hidden). Do not read the gate failures as errors in the extraction alone or as errors in the paper.
- **V3 / C4 duplicate read (G4_38065 velocity, the first panel of the figure by the declared rule):** two independent variants (different colour tolerance, template thresholds and opening radius) match all markers (13/13 natural, 20/20 robust5): rms dV 0.15 and 0.18 km/s (max 0.48 and 0.57), rms dR 0.023 and 0.021 kpc; model curves rms 0.15 (natural) and 0.07 km/s (robust5). Tick residuals of that panel: 0.098 km/s, 0.026 kpc, 0.0023 arcsec (both variants share the calibration).

## Known limits (read before use)
- Markers are found by template scan: **occluded markers can be missed and dense same-colour clusters can be double- or under-counted.** The three panels of a galaxy share radial bins, so a bin is kept if it is found in at least two panels (or is fully visible in one) and re-fitted at that x in the third (`recovered_at_shared_bin` = 1). Even so the counts per series differ by up to 3 between panels for a few galaxies (G4_23011, G4_38232, GN4_18574 blue series has only 4-6 markers vs 14 red and 14 green); check `visible_fraction` and the overlays.
- **Error bars:** vertical strokes walked in the marker column; bars hidden under other markers are truncated; horizontal error bars are not read.
- **Model lines** are hidden under same-colour markers where those are dense, so the line is interpolated (`interpolated` = 1, `gap_px_to_visible_line`); where no visible line lies within 15 px of R_e,disk the gate reports "no visible line". G4_38232 (three overlapping series) traces are the least reliable.
- The G4_38232 top-axis kpc calibration is suspect (+3.2% in scale); use R_arcsec there.
- Data and model pixel centres are good to about 1 px (about 0.3-0.7 km/s or 0.01 arcsec depending on the panel).
