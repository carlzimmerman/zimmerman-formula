# ADDENDUM 1 to FROZEN_CRITERIA_2026-09-30.md (written after the frozen-rule run failed C1; the failed run stays committed)

## What happened
The frozen rule was run as written on all 35 galaxies (`sins_ao_*_FROZENRULE.csv`, commit after f9290e8d4). Result: C1 0 of 35 within 25% (median ratio mine/published 10.2), C3 median rms 598 km/s. The frozen method is declared NOT VALIDATED on the real cubes. C2 (independent-noise injection) passed at 6.7% rms, so the fitter works when the noise model is right.

## Diagnosis, from input-side quantities only (no velocity was compared to a published value to choose anything below)
Measured in line-free channels (first 150 and last 150 channels of the cut cubes, before any fit): the standard deviation of the data is 1.2x to 4.0x the value in the noise cube (three galaxies checked: Deep3a-15504 1.2, ZC407302 1.85, Q1623-BX502 4.0), because of sky-line residuals and spatially correlated noise. Step 1 of the frozen method also shrinks the error by the independent-pixel factor 1/(2 sqrt(pi) s) (about 0.22 for 0.15 arcsec), which is wrong for correlated pixels. The S/N > 5 acceptance then passes noise peaks (1000+ accepted spaxels per galaxy, velocities up to +-1500 km/s).

## Amendment (applies to a NEW run, labelled `_AMEND1`; the frozen-rule outputs are kept)
1. Error per spaxel: replace the propagated cube error by the empirical robust rms (1.4826 x MAD) of the SMOOTHED spectrum of that spaxel in channels more than 2500 km/s from Halpha (channels of the cut cube cover roughly +-17000 km/s). Same for variants A and B.
2. Velocity bound in the fit: |v| < 700 km/s relative to z_Halpha of Table 1 (was 1500, an implementation bound, not a criterion); fit window unchanged (+-1800 km/s).
3. Everything else (S/N > 5 or 4, sigma 20-400, velocity error < 40, strip, bins, controls and pass lines) is unchanged, including the C1 and C3 pass lines.
4. C2 is re-run with correlated noise: white noise smoothed spatially with the same 0.2 arcsec kernel, rescaled to the real noise level, to show the amended method does not break the injection result.
No number in this amendment was tuned on C1 or on any published velocity; the only real-cube numbers used are the line-free-channel scatter above. If the amended run fails C1 the method stays NOT VALIDATED and that is what will be reported.
