## Anything in the PDF that makes (a), (b) or (c) unworkable
1. **(a) is broader than Amendment 15(b)'s text.** 15(b) (PREREGISTRATION_DR4.md line ~1561) counts "any DR4 non-single-star table carrying an astrometric orbit, an acceleration solution, or a spectroscopic orbit". Revision 1 (a) counts `nss_vim_fl` and `nss_non_linear_spectro`. The PDF describes `nss_vim_fl` as "non-single-star models for sources compatible with an [sic] Variability Induced Mover (VIM) solution" (p. 843), which is not an orbit, an acceleration or a spectroscopic orbit, and `nss_non_linear_spectro` as "non-single-star orbital models for spectroscopic binaries compatible with a trend" (p. 777). Whether these fall under the three named kinds is a reading, so revision 1 (a) should say it is extending 15(b) to them (it already says it governs where they conflict) or count them only under an explicit reading.
2. **(b): the `source_id_secondary` semantics need an unordered test.** `source_id_secondary` exists in only three tables: `nss_resolved_pair`, `nss_epoch_flags` and `optical_pair`. In `nss_resolved_pair` the columns are "Source Identifier for the primary source" and "for the secondary source" with no statement of which is the brighter; in `optical_pair` the bit coding says bit 1 is the brightest source (`source_id`) and bit 2 the faintest (`source_id_secondary`, p. 852). So "exactly this pipeline pair's two components" must be matched as an unordered pair, and a component can appear in either column, so the third-source test must search both. The (b) implementation note should say so.
3. **(b): `solution_type` strings.** The PDF lists the type names in the description of `solution_type` (pp. 788–791) but does not print the stored strings; case or format in the archive may differ. The loader should take the strings from the release-day data model and record them.
4. **(c) diagnostic:** `optical_pair.acceleration_flag` bits identify the brighter or fainter member by brightness, so assigning a bit to a specific pipeline component needs the same unordered/brightness logic. `nss_multiplicity.related_sources` is an array ("maximum 20 in a radius of 4 arcsec", crowded fields; p. 776).
5. **Not unworkable, but unresolved:** whether pairs at 8″ or wider are in `nss_resolved_pair` at all (no range stated; the only radius in the draft is the 4″ on p. 776). If none are, (b) and V17a/V17b are moot for this sample but harmless.
