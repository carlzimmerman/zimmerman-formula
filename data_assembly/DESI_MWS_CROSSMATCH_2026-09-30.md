## Result (coadded spectra; 1,050,901 DESI rows read)
- Match: Gaia id = `REF_ID` (315 rows) or position within 1.5 arcsec to the Gaia position moved to 2015.5 (16 rows, all under 1 arcsec). **331 DESI rows match 317 distinct components** (287 pass the recommended cuts `RR_SPECTYPE = STAR`, `RVS_WARN = 0`, `SUCCESS`, `VSINI < 30`). Programs: main-backup 224, main-bright 70, main-dark 6, sv3 10, sv1 12, special 5, sv2 3.
- **The estimate of 1,523 components in the target windows (Phase A) was an upper limit on DESI's completeness: DESI actually observed 317, about 21% of that.**
- Matched stars: Gaia G 11.8 to 16.9 (median 14.7; 231 brighter than 16, 56 between 16 and 19.2). DESI random radial-velocity error (best spectrum per star): 16th/50th/84th percentile 0.08 / 0.16 / 0.29 km/s; the documented systematic floor (1 km/s bright, ~2 km/s backup) comes on top.
- **Comparison with Gaia DR3 RVS for the 183 components that have both:** DESI minus Gaia median +0.27 km/s, robust scatter 3.4 km/s (16th-84th: -3.3 to +3.7), median |difference| over the combined catalogue error 1.08: consistent, and dominated by Gaia's larger errors at G ~ 14-15.
- **Pairs** (`wide_binaries_dr3.csv`, 6,210 pairs): both components with a good DESI RV: **18**; one component: 244; at least one: 262. Pairs with both components having an RV from Gaia or DESI: **3,838 against 3,746 from Gaia alone (a gain of 92 pairs, not the ~680 of the Phase A upper limit)**. The El-Badry-variant file (6,230 pairs): 19 pairs with both DESI RVs, 3,876 vs 3,779 (gain 97).
- **Relative radial velocity of the 18 pairs with both DESI RVs**: median |delta RV| 1.62 km/s (16th-84th: 0.45-3.33), i.e. of the size of the systematic floors; it cannot measure the orbital velocity difference (median projected relative velocity of these pairs, 0.3 km/s).

## Single epochs
303 matched targets have a good single-epoch spectrum and **131 have two or more** (584 epoch rows). Peak-to-peak RV between epochs: median 1.33 km/s (16th/84th/95th percentile 0.51 / 4.19 / 12.3); 18 targets vary by more than 5 km/s and 8 by more than 10 km/s. Using the catalogue random errors alone 98 of the 131 have chi2/dof > 3, which only reflects the missing systematic floor; **with a 1 km/s floor added in quadrature, 22 of 131 have chi2/dof > 3**. These are RV-variable candidates (unresolved binaries or triples) whose pairs deserve a flag; nothing here is a gravity inference.

## What this gives and does not give
Gives: 317 DESI velocities for our components (287 clean), 18 pairs with both, 92 pairs newly having an RV on both components, 131 stars with repeat epochs and 22 candidate variables. Does not give: a DESI velocity-difference test of the pairs (the error floor is larger than the signal), nor a full El-Badry+21 match (that catalogue is not on disk). Gaia DR4 (2 December 2026) will supply the RVs and astrometry for the bulk of the sample.
