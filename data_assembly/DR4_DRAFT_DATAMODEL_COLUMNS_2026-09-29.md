(https://anonftp.cosmos.esa.int/pub/GAIA_PUBLIC_DATA/Gaia_DR4/dr4-prerelease/, 2,840,832 bytes, **sha256 d807eae98acbeec0a0af2ce6a5d7d352298df6f270a1f13207cc8d1becf42c66**), downloaded 2026-09-29 with the owner's go, kept OUTSIDE the repo in `~/new_physics/_external_data/gaia_dr4_draft_datamodel/`.

## CORRECTION to `DR4_DATAMODEL_PREVIEW_2026-09-29.md` (d9ac3a13f)
That note said the ESA web page does not name DR3's `nss_non_linear_spectro` and `nss_vim_fl` for DR4. The draft data model DOES list both (10.6 and 10.9), so they carry over; the web page list was not exhaustive. The draft also lists a new `nss_epoch_flags` (10.2) and `optical_pair` (10.10).

## What the draft says, per item

| item | DR3 name the frozen pipeline used | DR4 draft name (table.column) | notes from the draft |
|---|---|---|---|
| main catalogue | `gaia_source` | `gaia_source` (123 parsed columns) | "only contains those sources from the tables all_source_astrometry, all_source_photometry and all_source_rvs that meet [quality] criteria"; its parameters are "a consolidation of the best results obtained across DPAC", with origin flags `astrometry_origin`, `g_photometry_origin`, `colour_origin`, `radial_velocity_origin` (and for `vbroad`); "do not correspond to those of a homogeneous module" |
| astrometry | `parallax`, `parallax_error`, `parallax_over_error`, `ra`, `dec`, `pmra`, `pmdec`, `l`, `b` | all present in `gaia_source`; `pm` (total proper motion) is new; `parallax`, `parallax_error`, `ra`, `dec`, `pmra`, `pmdec` also in `all_source_astrometry`, `crowded_field_source`, `gaia_source_environment` (per `dr4_draft_where_is.csv`) | |
| photometry | `phot_g_mean_mag`, `phot_bp_mean_mag` | `gaia_source.phot_g_mean_mag`, `.phot_bp_mean_mag`; also `all_source_photometry`, `crowded_field_source`, `gaia_source_environment.phot_g_mean_mag` | |
| **WP3** `ruwe` | `gaia_source.ruwe` | **`gaia_source.ruwe` stays**; also in `all_source_astrometry`, `bright_source_astrometry`, `nss_acceleration_astro` | the draft says: "Starting with DR4, astrometric_gof_al is probably more useful than ruwe as a general goodness-of-fit measure" (`gaia_source.astrometric_gof_al`) |
| **WP3** `ipd_frac_multi_peak` | `gaia_source.ipd_frac_multi_peak` | **stays in `gaia_source`**; also in `all_source_astrometry` | |
| **WP3** `radial_velocity` (+ error) | `gaia_source.radial_velocity`, `radial_velocity_error` | **stay in `gaia_source`** (with `radial_velocity_origin`, `rv_nb_transits`, `rv_expected_sig_to_noise`, `rv_renormalised_gof`, `rv_invalid_reason`, `rv_single_outlier_probability`); `all_source_rvs` (101 parsed columns) is the per-module table | so the move the plan anticipated does not appear in the draft; the values in `gaia_source` may differ from module-specific ones in `all_source_rvs` |
| `astrometric_params_solved` (DR3) | `gaia_source.astrometric_params_solved` | **no column of that name in the draft**; `gaia_source.astrometric_params` exists (my reading: the renamed equivalent; not verified) | rename to confirm |
| **cut 12** NSS flag | `gaia_source.non_single_star` | **`non_single_star` is only in `all_source_flags`, not in `gaia_source`** | `all_source_flags` also has `in_gaia_source`, `sea_env_flag`, `has_epoch_astrometry`, `has_epoch_photometry` and 25 more `has_*`/`in_*` flags |
| **cut 12** NSS tables | `nss_two_body_orbit`, `nss_acceleration_astro`, `nss_non_linear_spectro`, `nss_vim_fl` | `nss_acceleration_astro`, `nss_epoch_flags` (new; epoch-level flags per transit: `source_id`, `source_id_secondary`, `solution_type`, `transit_id`, `al_ccd_used`, `al_used`, `g_used`, `bp_used`, `rp_used`, `rv_used`), `nss_masses`, `nss_multiple_orbits`, `nss_multiplicity` (`source_id`, `number_of_pairs`, `related_sources`), `nss_non_linear_spectro`, `nss_resolved_pair` (91 parsed columns), `nss_two_body_orbit`, `nss_vim_fl`, plus `optical_pair` | `nss_resolved_pair` is "non-single-star astrometric models for resolved pairs of sources being physically bound (in contrast with chance alignment pairs)"; `optical_pair` holds pairs the resolved-binary pipeline judged chance alignments (`source_id`, `source_id_secondary`, `separation`, `acceleration_flag`; the astrometry to use is in `gaia_source`); `nss_multiplicity` lists all pairs a source is in ("a maximum 20 in a radius of 4 arcsec"). (My inference: wide binaries in the pipeline's pair list may also appear as `nss_resolved_pair` rows; that would affect how cut 12 is applied and is an owner decision.) |
| **cut 13** all-source astrometry | full `gaia_source` neighbours | `all_source_astrometry` (96 parsed columns: includes `source_id`, `ra`, `dec`, `parallax`, `parallax_error`, `parallax_over_error`, `pmra`, `pmdec`, `ruwe`, `astrometric_gof_al`, `ipd_frac_multi_peak`, `duplicated_source`, `visibility_periods_used`, `astrometric_excess_noise`, `l`, `b`); G magnitude is in `all_source_photometry.phot_g_mean_mag` | the draft says `all_source_astrometry` is "complemented by" `all_source_photometry` and `all_source_rvs` |
| **cut 13** crowded fields | `crowded_field_source` (FPR) | `crowded_field_source` (83 parsed columns, incl. `parallax`, `parallax_error`, `pmra`, `pmdec`, `phot_g_mean_mag`, `ruwe`, `astrometric_gof_al`, `l`, `b`), `crowded_field_source_environment_match` (`sea_source_id`, `cf_source_id`, `winner`, `rule`, `astrometric_error_ratio`, `separation`, `both_in_gaia_source`; matches within 120 mas between crowded-field and SEAPipe sources, with `both_in_gaia_source` flagging possible duplicates), `all_source_match` (`source_id`, `sea_source_id`, `cf_source_id`, `winner`, `rule`, `astrometric_error_ratio`, `separation`) | |
| **cut 13** source environment | none in DR3 | `gaia_source_environment` (33 parsed columns: `source_id`, `parent_source_id`, `astrometry_origin`, `matched`, `ra`, `dec`, `parallax`, `parallax_error`, `parallax_over_error`, `pmra`, `pmdec`, `phot_g_mean_mag`, `astrometric_duplicate_source_id`, `distance_to_parent_source`, `parent_env_flag`, `matched_to_parent`, `l`, `b`) | "astrometry, photometry, and related information for sources detected in the surrounding of sources in the main Gaia source catalogue by the SEAPipe processing"; `all_source_flags.sea_env_flag` marks sources having such neighbours |
| **cut 9** extinction (per source) | `gaia_source.ag_gspphot`, `ebpminrp_gspphot` (DR3) | `gaia_source.ag`, `gaia_source.ebpminrp`, `gaia_source.extinction_origin` (consolidated, with an origin flag); the GSP-Phot-named columns `ag_gspphot`, `ebpminrp_gspphot` are in `ap_xp` (132 parsed columns), `distance_gspphot` and `teff`, `logg`, `mh`, `ap_origin` also in `gaia_source`; `interstellar_medium_params` (45 parsed columns) and `mcmc_samples_gsp_phot` exist | the `ap_*` variant of cut 9 reads `ap_xp`; the consolidated `gaia_source.ag` has an `extinction_origin` flag that must be read on release day |
| **cut 9** extinction (map) | SFD98 (external) | `total_galactic_extinction_map` (`healpix_id`, `healpix_level`, `a0_median`, `a0_uncertainty`, `a0_min`, `a0_max`, `num_tracers_used`, `optimum_hpx_flag`, `status`, `a0_mean`, `a0_mean_absolute_deviation`, `a0_mode`) and `total_galactic_extinction_map_opt` (10 parsed columns, level 9, "appropriate for extragalactic sources") | per HEALPix cell, monochromatic A0; not per source |
| DR3 link | | `dr3_neighbourhood` (6 parsed columns) | a DR3→DR4 cross-match table; relevant to the DR3 dry-run stand-in |

## What to confirm on release day
Every name above; in particular (i) whether `gaia_source` keeps `ruwe`, `ipd_frac_multi_peak`, `radial_velocity` and `radial_velocity_error` (the draft says yes); (ii) whether `non_single_star` is still only in `all_source_flags`; (iii) the table list and columns of the `nss_*` group including `nss_epoch_flags` and whether `nss_resolved_pair` contains the pipeline's pairs; (iv) whether `astrometric_params` replaces `astrometric_params_solved`; (v) the extinction columns `ag`, `ebpminrp`, `extinction_origin` versus the `ap_xp` GSP-Phot columns; (vi) the cut-13 tables `all_source_astrometry` (with G from `all_source_photometry`), `crowded_field_source`, `gaia_source_environment` and the match tables.
