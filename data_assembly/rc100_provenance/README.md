## 2. Definitions (quotes from the PDF text layer)
- **V_c(R_e) (A1).** Eq. 7 (PDF p. 25, Appendix A; eq. 1 on p. 7): V_circ^2(r) = V_disk^2 + V_bulge^2 + V_DM^2. Eq. 8: "V_rot^2(r) = V_circ^2(r) - 3.36 sigma_0^2 (r/R_e)" for "an exponential disk with a constant velocity dispersion sigma_0" (the Burkert et al. 2010 self-gravitating-disc correction, "always negative and proportional to (r/R_e)"). So V_circ^2 = V_rot^2 + 3.36 sigma_0^2 (r/R_e) is the paper's own relation, matching A1 in form. Whether the Table 3 column (11) is V_circ or V_rot is not written next to the table; by the paper's definitions (f_DM is defined with V_circ) it is V_circ from the mass model. **R_e in eq. 8 is the disk R_e.**
- **f_DM(R_e) (A2).** Eq. 1: "f_DM(R_e) = V_DM^2(R_e) / V_circ^2(R_e)" ("the ratio of the DM and circular velocities at R_e"). Halo = spherical NFW, "We account for adiabatic contraction of the halos only when stated so"; in Method A "the dark matter fractions were calculated as an average of a regular NFW halo and a contracted NFW halo". R_e is the disk R_e: in Method A "in most cases, the inclination and effective radius are fixed from photometric-determined values" (HST) and fitted in some; in Methods B and C R_e is a free parameter with a Gaussian prior centred on Method A's value. The Table 3 R_e is thus the average of the three methods' values, not a fixed catalogue value.
- **M_baryon prior.** "Gaussian priors for log M_baryon centered on M_baryon,init derived from ancillary data", with "M_baryon,init = M_star + M_gas" (p. 8); M_star from SED fitting (Wuyts+2011 procedure); "The scaling relations of Tacconi et al. (2020) provide the cold gas mass estimate, M_gas, given the redshift and stellar mass" (so scaling-relation gas, not measured CO, not dust; the text never mentions HI or an alpha_CO value). **The prior width is not stated in RC100.** The width for the same Bayesian method in Price+2021 (arXiv:2109.02659, Table 2, the method RC100 calls B) is "Gaus(log10(M*,SED + M_gas), 0.2 dex)" with bounds [9, 13] dex; RC100 refers to Price+2021 for the flat priors. Method C (PDF p. 9) additionally has "wide Gaussian priors ... for log M_vir centered on abundance-matching relations (Moster et al. 2018)". Method A (least squares) has no prior.
- **Selection** (PDF p. 5). "We start from the same subset of rotation-dominated (v_rot/sigma_0 > 2.3) SFGs at log(M_star/M_sun) > 9.5 and with -0.6 < delta_MS < 1, representing 65% of the full Halpha+CO parent surveys", then "removed galaxies for which residuals from night sky line emission ... affects part of their Halpha line emission profile" and "excluded galaxies that are insufficiently spatially resolved ... and for which the radial coverage of detected line emission is too limited"; "we were less strict than G20" (Genzel+2020); no fixed S/N, size or size/beam cut. Differences across redshift, as the paper states them: at lower masses "the IFU and mm interferometry samples contain increasingly fewer galaxies with increasing redshift mainly because of (i) the additional K-band magnitude cut applied for the Halpha surveys, and (ii) the lower galactic metallicities suppressing CO emission"; "a small excess of above-MS galaxies among the PHIBSS z > 1 CO samples"; a reduced Halpha detection rate at the reddest colours and at delta_MS <~ -0.85. Redshift bins: z 0.6-1.2 (33 galaxies) and 1.2-2.5 (67). The paper gives no separate mass or size cut per redshift end.

## 3. Overlap with RC41 (Price+2021 / Genzel+2020)
- RC100 is described as an expansion: "We expand on Genzel et al. (2017, 2020) and Price et al. (2021)"; "Building on RC41, we followed a similar strategy as detailed by G20"; RC41 galaxies are "included in RC41 (G20)" and drawn as open circles in Fig. 1. **Every RC100 galaxy, RC41 members included, is refitted with all three methods and the Table 3 value is the three-method average**; the paper does not print a per-galaxy statement that the 41 use identical inputs. Method A is the G20 least-squares (DysmalPy) approach; Method B is the Price+2021 MCMC (free log M_baryon, R_e, sigma_0, f_DM(R_e), flat f_DM and sigma_0 priors); Method C is a new MCMC with free log M_vir (f_DM derived). Some targets combine "seeing-limited + AO data sets" and "deeper observations of CO targets" than in RC41. RC100's mean curve extent (2.2 R_e, range 1.3-4 R_e) is "slightly lower on average compared to RC41", a consequence of the less strict selection. So RC100 is a re-analysis of the RC41 galaxies with three methods averaged, with some RC41 data sets extended, not a new dataset for those 41, and it differs in method from RC41's single-method tables.
- Data-front notes from other lanes: RC100's row 32 (EGS 13035123) is NOEMA3D's G4_38065 (paper Table 1 lists 9 NOEMA CO galaxies "NOEMA3D team, in prep."); its RC100 values (log M_baryon 11.08, R_e 10.19, f_DM 0.19, V_c 196, sigma_0 16) can be set against NOEMA3D's own Table 3 (11.52, 11.0, 0.18, 299, 18.3); both are from different fits and are only listed, not compared.
