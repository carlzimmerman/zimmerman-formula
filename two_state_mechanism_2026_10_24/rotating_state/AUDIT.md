# Audit disposition

Finite implementation and invariants verified within reported tolerances.
Physical interpretation remains conditional; no independent reviewer used.

Hamiltonian functional derivative independently checks kinetic normalization,
self-field half factors, external potential and source mass/charge factors.
Roundtrip split steps check reversible implementation. Exact symmetric subspace
provides an independent nonlinear analytic benchmark, not just an energy check.
Number norms include cell volume; total initial mass1 and net charge0.
Both species have the same bulk velocity prescription via mass-weighted phases.
Initial normalization is part of preparing a state, not a time-step correction.

Main result interpretation: outward support is the projection of electric force
against gravity for positive carriers, not radial force balance including quantum
stress, centrifugal support or time dependence. The window thresholds are
illustrative measurement choices; no dynamics depend on this classifier. Large
support fraction in epsilon.1 cannot be called charged tethered matter. No
criterion demonstrates bound free matter, and no actual RAR residual is evaluated.

Core/exterior energies sum exactly by construction, independently of the
measured time integration error. Thus the partition identity alone is not an
energy conservation test. Long-range energy localization is conventional.
Edge mass and box sensitivity expose limitations of periodic-wave escape.
No thermodynamic entropy, thermal temperature or equilibration rate is derived.

Convergence: temporal refinement and energy checks pass; sharp-core mass is
not spatially converged. Angular momentum drift can arise from the periodic
kernel and grid, not just temporal error. Do not claim isolated exact angular
momentum conservation. Asymmetric h1 boundary contamination is substantial.
No broader scan is warranted as a population prediction before addressing these
issues and resolving the same physical mass across environments.

Radiative estimate is deliberately separate from simulated energy: no radiation
is subtracted. Assumed coherent acceleration is not derived and not an upper
bound. Source charges, gravity, coherence and trajectories would be needed for
full retarded emission. Matter-wave energy transport is the implemented channel.
All quoted dimensional numbers use the explicit mass-size/inventory recipe.

Source literature describes analogous gravitational cooling and dipole radiation;
it does not establish this model's state, capture or astrophysical efficacy.
