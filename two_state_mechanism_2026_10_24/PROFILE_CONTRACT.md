# TS2b continuation contract

After the retention experiments, evaluate instantaneous acceleration at the
original test radii of the corrected dwarf and cluster profiles. Use the
same 90% baryon loss, full T-to-F conversion, cold start, softening and
8 dynamical-time duration. Both N=128/dt=0.0001 and N=256/dt=0.00005 are
run. Retain the original energy and bound-fraction resolution thresholds.
Report all 16 temporal samples and min/max over t>=4. No acceleration
pass/fail threshold is selected and no fit is attempted.

This is a post-hoc diagnostic: the question is whether retained mass actually
produces excess acceleration inside the specified radius. It is not a
prediction of an equilibrium rotation curve or stellar dispersion. Physical
durations are reported; times longer than an object's age cannot count as
an explanation of that object. The initial baryon profile is idealized and
the final baryon mass is only 10% of that initial mass.
