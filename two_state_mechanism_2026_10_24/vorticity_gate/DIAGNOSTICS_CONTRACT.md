# Additional discriminating checks

The bounded-angle potential also has a positive auxiliary-strain representation.
Let B=sigma_b^2/L_rho^2, r=omega^2/B, S(x)=sin(pi*x)/pi and e=u W_full'(u).
The proposed local coupling beta*e*[z^2+r(z-S)^2] has its unique minimum at
z=r*S/(1+r) when e>0. Eliminating this fast strain gives beta*e*C*S^2, exactly
the tested barrier. This is a constructive effective interaction, not a
microscopic derivation of its coefficients or of baryon coupling. At B=0 use
the continuous cold rotating limit; at omega=B=0 a vacuum prescription remains
necessary. A positive kinetic term for z could be supplied but is not tested.

Check this elimination on 65 r values in [1e-8,1e8] and 41 x values in [0,2].
Compute the uniform-phase escape path over x in [0,1] on 501 nodes, N=128.
Its maximum energy above the T state is only an upper bound on the true
spatial escape barrier. No Arrhenius lifetime is inferred without the
receiver temperature, noise spectrum and nucleation dynamics.

Try finite perturbations x=.9 and .5 for linearly stable f=.95 endpoints,
keeping beta=20 and ell=30 kpc. Scan beta={16,20,24} and ell={24,30,36} kpc
for stability signs, with f=.3 and .95, all four mass/size profiles. These are
post-hoc robustness checks, not new predeclared success criteria.

Counterexample: equal counterrotating streams with zero mean flow have C=0
even if each stream is individually ordered. Record that prediction explicitly.
The proposed statistic therefore does not encode every notion of a cold disk.
