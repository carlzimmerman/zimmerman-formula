# Follow-up: can a thermal collective phase supply the correct nonanalyticity?

The required saturating polarization has P=chi E-b E|E| with b>0, hence matter
free energy F=-chi E²/2+(b/3)|E|³. The sign of the cubic term is essential.
A correct power with the opposite sign does not source positive baryonic gravity
under the predecessor's weak-field cancellation.

Route1: a three-dimensional Gaussian thermal collective mode with field-induced
mass M=g*|E| has fluctuation free energy
Delta F=(kBT/2)*integral_{|k|<Lambda} d³k/(2pi)³ log[(k²+M²)/k²].
After subtracting its analytic quadratic term kBT*Lambda*M²/(4pi²),
F_nonanalytic=-kBT*M³/(12pi)+O(M⁴/Lambda).
Direct differentiation before expansion gives
partial F/partial M=kBT*M/(2pi²)*[Lambda-M*atan(Lambda/M)], confirming the sign.
Consequently the nonanalytic polarization is +kBT*g³ E|E|/(4pi): it ENHANCES
rather than reduces polarization. Adjusting the analytic background susceptibility
does not change this sign. E²-induced mode masses are assumptions about coupling;
the calculation is not a fully interacting critical exponents calculation.

Route2: a stable mean-field tricritical order parameter can generate the same
cubic power by minimizing F(phi,E)=-(lambda/2)E²*phi²+(v/6)phi⁶ at r=u=0,
with lambda,v>0. The minimum obeys phi²=sqrt(lambda/v)*|E| and
F_min= -lambda^(3/2)*|E|³/(3*sqrt(v)). Again the sign is the opposite of that
required. If the electric term is +lambda E² phi²/2 instead, phi=0 remains the
minimum and no cubic term appears. Reaching r=u=0 itself requires two conditions;
this calculation does not derive that tuning or a self-organized phase.

Route3: if E couples directly and linearly to a critical polar order parameter
with quartic free energy, minimizing u*phi⁴/4-E*phi gives P~E^(1/3), i.e. a
singular susceptibility, rather than a finite linear susceptibility with the
required negative quadratic correction. Other universality classes, constraints,
nonlocal couplings or nonequilibrium states can differ. These three elementary
mechanisms are excluded only in the explicitly stated forms.

Thus the nodal fermion ground state gives the desired sign but has a thermal
cutoff; these two simple thermal mechanisms give a persistent cubic term with
the wrong sign. This does not prove all finite-temperature interacting phases
fail. No current route derives formation, countercharge inventory or galaxy
selection, and these remain open rather than silently assigned.
