sub-result: the displayed cutoff half-filled nodal-ring Hamiltonian gives the
specified T0 response, including actual dipole matrix elements. This is an
effective band construction, not a demonstrated microscopic many-body phase.

DOS derivation: p_perp dp_perp=m_star dxi; integrate azimuth and transverse polar
angle to get g_deg*m_star/(2pi*hbar³*v_z)*u du. Requires cutoff below the ring
energy p0²/(2m_star), so the integration circle never crosses the radial boundary.

nonzero interband response are algebraic facts. This does not establish spatial
rotation/inversion representations, allowed microscopic dipole operators,
lattice formation, interactions or a gauge-complete relativistic Hamiltonian.
Calling this an interacting quantum critical phase would overstate the result.
The fermion response is exact for the quadratic Hamiltonian in an imposed field.

The Legendre field energy and homogeneous Hamiltonian tests avoid that confusion.
The fixed-D mode tests use a single field orientation and its repeated two-level
blocks, not all spatial band/field modes. They omit actual countercharge motion.
No double-counted field self-interaction or microscopic source charge is claimed.

velocity operators, with the full physical current identified by the dipole
commutator. It cannot be transferred to an arbitrary collective Bloch transition

have the stated negative cubic coefficient. They are not exact interacting
