# Joint inventory, bandwidth, and Fermi-state test

**Claim scope:** a conditional inconsistency in identifying the effective nodal
fermions with independent, nonrelativistic, local dipolar matter units of the
same charge and mass used in the repulsive tether construction. This is NOT a
no-go theorem for emergent fermionic quasiparticles of a different medium.

## Hypotheses, with the constituent mass denoted m_+

1. Each occupied band unit contains opposite charges +/-q, has positive rest mass
   M=(1+epsilon)*m_+, and dipole transition element d=q*ell. Its number density is
   n=rho_M/M. Binding is nonrelativistic; no large negative binding contribution
   changes the mass identity. rho_M is rest-mass density, not added free mass.
2. The bare cutoff band has n=C*Lambda^2/2 and chi_bare=C*d^2*Lambda=2*n*d^2/Lambda.
   This identifies the response state count with the SAME gravitating units.
3. Weak-field tether cancellation uses beta=q/m_+ with
   beta^2=4*pi*G*(1+chi_eff); f=chi_eff/(1+chi_eff).
4. For the contact-response diagnostic, delta=1-lambda>0,
   chi_bare=delta*chi_eff and the low-field scale is E_c=Lambda*delta^2/d.
   Matching the deep acceleration coefficient requires beta*E_c=f*a0.
   lambda=0 is the preceding noninteracting critical band.
5. The same local pair has two subluminal velocity operators and a dipole-current
   commutator bound 2*Lambda*d <= 2*hbar*q*c, hence Lambda*ell<=hbar*c.
   This requires the simple two-current dipole identity, not merely a causal
   effective susceptibility. Collective/nonlocal currents are outside its scope.
6. For the Fermi comparison only, these units are fundamental fermionic particles
   with at most nu=8 spin/flavor states, in a nonrelativistic translational state.
   Use the generous momentum envelope |p|<=M*c. This is NOT inferred from v<c
   for arbitrary relativistic particles: relativistic momentum is unbounded.
   It is an explicit optimistic envelope for nonrelativistic halo matter.
   Bound pairs need not be fermions; if they are bosons or if the nodal fermions
   are emergent excitations, this identification fails and this test does not apply.

## Eliminate the independently chosen bandwidth and dipole length

From hypotheses 1--4,

    Lambda = f*a0*m_+*ell/delta^2,
    rho_M*ell = (1+epsilon)*f^2*a0/(8*pi*G*delta) = K/delta.

Thus m_+, Lambda, ell, chi and density cannot all be independently assigned.
Holding chi_eff, rho_M and epsilon fixed, ell=ell_0/delta with
ell_0=(1+epsilon)*f^2*a0/(8*pi*G*rho_M).
The current bound then implies

    m_+ <= hbar*c*delta^2/(f*a0*ell^2)
         = [hbar*c/(f*a0*ell_0^2)]*delta^4.

The near-critical interaction cannot arbitrarily increase the bandwidth at fixed
inventory. It makes this mass ceiling *smaller*. This fourth-power reduction is
within the displayed mean-field mapping and local-current assumptions, not a
universal statement about critical matter.

For lambda=0 only, also record the predecessor's optional canonical
oscillator-strength ceiling

    Omega_max^2 <= 12*pi*G*rho_M/(epsilon*f), Omega_max=2*Lambda/hbar,
    m_+ <= hbar/(2*f*a0*ell)*sqrt(12*pi*G*rho_M/(epsilon*f)).

This additional ceiling requires canonical local constituents and the earlier
sum-rule inventory. The main inconsistency below already uses the more generous
current bound. No sum-rule extension to the interacting fit is assumed here.

## Fermi state counting is a different, simultaneous requirement

For nu internal states and occupied physical momenta inside |p|<=M*c,

    n <= nu/(2*pi*hbar)^3 * (4*pi/3)*(M*c)^3
      = nu*M^3*c^3/(6*pi^2*hbar^3).

Combining n=rho_M/M yields

    m_+ >= [6*pi^2*hbar^3*rho_M/(nu*c^3)]^(1/4)/(1+epsilon).

This is a necessary packing condition, even allowing every state in this large
momentum ball. A narrow nodal band cannot increase this capacity. A realistic
nonrelativistic velocity envelope below c strengthens the lower mass bound.
The physical momentum of microscopic particles is essential; a quasiparticle
band momentum and effective inertial mass cannot be substituted without deriving
their relation to the total gravitating material.

At fixed microscopic dimensionless parameters, m_current,max scales as rho_M^2,
whereas m_Fermi,min scales as rho_M^(1/4). Their ratio scales as rho_M^(7/4).
Consequently, if the intervals do not intersect at the largest allowed rest
inventory, lowering rho_M cannot restore overlap. Additional positive kinetic,
field or countercharge energy uses more of the gravitating budget and is not
available as additional free rest-mass capacity in this screen.

We evaluate rho_M=rho_available=3*eta*M_b/(4*pi*b^3) for each earlier profile,
eta=5.364, a0=9.3603e-11 SI. This grants the entire available dark reservoir to
the medium, leaving none for the desired free/tether populations, so it is an
optimistic necessary test. Cases: previous chi_eff=11.220184543019636, lambda=0;
and the new finite-grid static-fit candidate chi_eff=138.62249546824629,
lambda=.3875. epsilon=.1 and 1e-8; nu=8.

## What the test can and cannot settle

An empty mass interval under these hypotheses rejects the identification of
these band units with the same local fundamental nonrelativistic fermions.
It does not reject the algebraic nodal spectrum, the controlled preparation
calculation, Bose constituents, a bosonic composite, emergent fractionalized
fermions, strongly bound relativistic units, or a different source coupling.
Those alternatives require a fresh microscopic construction; one cannot keep
the present mass inventory, dipole matrix elements and particle interpretation
unchanged while invoking them.

This closes a specific local-particle route by simultaneous necessary
conditions. It does not derive an alternative medium or establish population
separation. The live-gravity and three-dimensional countercharge problems are
still obligations. No system label appears in any equation or acceptance rule.
