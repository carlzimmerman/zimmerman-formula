**C. Zimmerman workflow, 2026-06-14.** *The rival-fill for the LCDM (GR + cold dark matter + Λ) column,
one cell per framework prediction. Honesty rule held BOTH ways: N/A only where LCDM genuinely says nothing;
credit LCDM's clean WINS (clusters, lensing morphology split) at full weight; do not manufacture a rival
prediction or a framework win. Every load-bearing external claim web-verified (author+year) on 2026-06-14;

> **The one-line frame for the column:** LCDM is **NOT N/A** on the galaxy/cluster/lensing observables — it
> competes on 8 of the 9, fitting them with a per-object dark halo (a fit, not a forced prediction). It is
> genuinely **N/A on exactly two cells**: the *fundamental* a₀ value (#1) and a₀(z) evolution (#9), because
> LCDM has **no acceleration scale to predict or to evolve**. On those two the framework is distinctive by
> default. LCDM **cleanly WINS** two cells outright — clusters (#7) and the lensing morphology split (#8) —
> and on the two SEP-forbidden cells (#5 EFE, #6 wide binaries) LCDM makes a clean ZERO-effect prediction
> that becomes a *kill of LCDM* only **if** a robust boost is confirmed (not yet).

## The honest tally for the LCDM column

- **Genuine N/A (LCDM says nothing): 2 cells** — #1 (the fundamental a₀ value) and #9 (a₀(z) evolution).
  These are the framework's *only* structurally distinctive cells, and they are distinctive precisely because
  LCDM has no acceleration scale. **Do not manufacture an LCDM prediction here.**
- **LCDM cleanly WINS: 2 cells** — #7 clusters (η≈2× residual, ~1.9–3.7σ, robust) and #8 the lensing
  morphology split (8.6–9.2σ, a₀-independent). **Credit both at full weight; they are durable framework
  losses.**
- **LCDM-IMPOSSIBLE doors (clean LCDM zero, kill-if-confirmed): 2 cells** — #5 EFE and #6 wide binaries.
  LCDM's zero is currently **unfalsified** (EFE dormant/wounded; wide binaries DR4-gated). These are the only
  cells where the framework can *kill* LCDM, and neither is won today.
- **LCDM competes & reproduces (shared, non-diagnostic): 3 cells** — #2 RAR (with a real but absorbable
  tightness/diversity *tension*), #3 BTFR (a clean LCDM success, scatter even smaller than observed), #4 dwarf
  σ (works with feedback-defusable cusp-core/TBTF tensions).

**Net:** the LCDM column is N/A on only 2 of 9 (both because LCDM lacks an acceleration scale), wins 2 outright,
holds a clean unfalsified zero on 2 more, and reproduces the remaining 3. The framework does **not** break LCDM
in this column today; its distinctive footing is confined to the two N/A cells (#1, #9), and its only *kill*
leverage (the two doors) is unconfirmed.

## The both-ways guards I held (do not let the column drift)

1. **#1 is NOT a framework win.** a₀ is *defined* as c²√(Λ/32π) — the LCDM "coincidence" it matches is
   structural, not a postdiction LCDM lost. N/A means N/A, both ways.
2. **#2 RAR tension is real but absorbable, NOT a kill.** Sims reach 0.06–0.09 dex; Stone–Courteau measure
   intrinsic 0.11 dex "consistent with ΛCDM." Report the tightness as a *fine-tuning cost*, never a break.
3. **#3 BTFR credits LCDM fully** — EAGLE/APOSTLE reproduce slope+scatter (scatter *smaller* than observed).
   Do not borrow the RAR's tension language for the BTFR; they differ.
4. **#7 and #8 are full-weight LCDM wins** — concede them loudly; the framework's *lower* a₀ makes clusters
   ~12% worse, and the lensing split is a₀-independent (no escape).
5. **#5/#6 LCDM zeros are clean but kill-if-confirmed** — do not write them as "LCDM wins"; write them as
   "LCDM unfalsified, framework door live-but-unconfirmed."
6. **#9 N/A guard** — the apparent-a₀ LCDM-degeneracy is asymmetric (LCDM post-dicts free; framework hides
   defensively); do not inflate the degeneracy into a framework win.

*Quarantine held: the coefficient Z / 32π is never asserted as derived anywhere in this column.*
