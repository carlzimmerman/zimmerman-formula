### The honest reads on Part A
- **string / SUSY / QFT-SM are N/A on all 9** — not a dodge, a fact: superpartners, strings, and SM fields are
  particle-scale; galaxies are classical-gravity. SUSY *motivates* a WIMP, but that feeds the **LCDM column**, not a
  distinct galaxy-dynamics prediction. Do NOT manufacture a rival cell here.
- **GR-alone = "GR alone fails (Keplerian); with CDM = the LCDM column"** on every dynamical row (#2,3,4,6,7,8);
  genuinely **N/A** only on #5 (it forbids an EFE, same as LCDM) and #9 (no scale to evolve).
- **Verlinde is the one real non-MOND competitor in this route** and it makes predictions on **7 of 9** (#1,2,3,4,6,7,8)
  and a #9 (rising a_M). It is NOT N/A. Credit it: it has a value for the scale, a RAR, a lensing apparent-DM, a dSph
  and WB prediction. But verified status: **RAR in tension (lowered M/L + unseen radius-correlated residual), dwarfs
  failing on the larger sample, Solar-System tension, and it makes the DISFAVORED rising a0(z)** — so on the framework's
  distinctive front #9, Verlinde lands on the wrong side of the sign.
- **Numeric flag for the quarantine (worth Carl seeing):** Verlinde's a_M = cH0/6 ≈ 1.1e-10 is numerically near the
  framework's *footing-bug* ρ_total/cH0 → 1.13e-10 value, and it RISES ∝cH(z) — i.e. Verlinde IS the "rising a0∝cH(z)
  rival" the a0(z) memos quarantine as NOT the framework's prediction. The framework's declining √ρ_DE is what
  distinguishes it from Verlinde on #9.
