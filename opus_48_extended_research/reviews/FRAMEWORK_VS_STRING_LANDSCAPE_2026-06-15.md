   particles is the category-opposite of a framework that says there are none. Deepest incompatibility — not a tuning,

3. **No string vacuum produces a0~√Λ.** In a compactification, Λ is a flux-set vacuum property with NO connection to an
   inertia/acceleration scale. The a0=√Λ tie is not a landscape output.

## 3. String theory's GENUINE open problems (defensible, mainstream — credit at full weight)

These are real and citable — they make string theory HARD TO TEST and INTERNALLY STRESSED, but they do NOT make it
"wrong":

  WHICH vacuum we inhabit — so it does not predict Λ, the SM couplings, or the spectrum; it post-dicts via anthropics.
  A theory that accommodates ~everything predicts ~nothing at the vacuum level.
- **No controlled de Sitter vacuum:** 25+ years and string theory has not produced an agreed, fully-controlled
  construction of a stable dS vacuum (our universe). The swampland program is the in-house admission this is hard.
- **No unique test at accessible energy:** no superpartners at the LHC; the string scale is generically near M_Pl; the
  landscape's flexibility blunts falsifiability. (This is the standard Woit/Smolin critique AND a mainstream concern.)
- **Swampland-vs-landscape is unresolved:** string theory is in tension WITH ITSELF on whether dS vacua exist at all.
