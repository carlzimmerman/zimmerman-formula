(4) THE BARYON BUDGET: how much of the ~2x can honest baryon accounting supply?
============================================================================================
  In DEEP MOND g_pred ~ sqrt(g_bar), so a baryon boost B shrinks eta by sqrt(B):
  starting deficit (dS-Unruh): eta(a0_FRAME)=2.33, eta(a0_MOND)=2.07

                               baryon source  boost B  eta_FRAME  eta_MOND
           baseline (gas + stars, fstar=0.2)     1.00       2.33      2.07
      + ICL/BCG undercount (~25% of stellar)     1.05       2.28      2.02
  + missing baryons to R500 (~18% below cosmic)     1.18       2.15      1.91
  + IGIMF stellar+remnant doubling (Kroupa 2026)     2.00       1.65      1.47
         ALL stacked (ICL + missing + IGIMF)     2.48       1.48      1.32

  KEY SUBTLETY (honest): the IGIMF 'doubling' is a doubling of the STELLAR+remnant mass, NOT the
  total baryon mass. In clusters HOT GAS dominates (gas ~ 5-7x stellar within R500). So doubling
  stars raises TOTAL baryons by only ~(1 + f_star_extra), not 2x. Let f_star/f_gas ~ 0.15-0.20:
    f_star=0.10 of baryons: doubling stars -> total baryon boost B=1.10 -> eta_FRAME 2.33->2.23, eta_MOND 2.07->1.98
    f_star=0.15 of baryons: doubling stars -> total baryon boost B=1.15 -> eta_FRAME 2.33->2.18, eta_MOND 2.07->1.93
    f_star=0.20 of baryons: doubling stars -> total baryon boost B=1.20 -> eta_FRAME 2.33->2.13, eta_MOND 2.07->1.89
    f_star=0.30 of baryons: doubling stars -> total baryon boost B=1.30 -> eta_FRAME 2.33->2.05, eta_MOND 2.07->1.82

  => The Kroupa/IGIMF '2x heavier => matches MOND' claim works because THEIR baryon accounting
  finds clusters are 2x heavier in TOTAL (they include large ICL + remnant + revised gas), AND
  they use a0=1.2e-10 with a different nu. With the FRAMEWORK's LOWER a0 and the WEAKER dS-Unruh
  nu, the SAME honest +18-25% baryon gain only takes eta from ~2.33 to ~2.1 -- it does NOT close.

============================================================================================
(5) HONEST RECONCILIATION with the IGIMF '88% of dynamical mass' claim
============================================================================================
  IGIMF result: M_bar = 88% of M_dyn at a0=1.2e-10 => eta_IGIMF = 1/0.88 = 1.14.
  To go from our baseline eta_MOND=2.07 to 1.14 needs B = (2.07/1.14)^2 = 3.31x baryons.
  That is a 3.3x TOTAL baryon increase -- consistent with their 'clusters 2x heavier' headline.
  Apply that SAME 3.3x boost at the FRAMEWORK a0 with dS-Unruh nu:
     eta_FRAME = 2.33/sqrt(3.31) = 1.28  (residual 28% over parity)
  => EVEN WITH the full contested IGIMF 2x-baryon boost, the FRAMEWORK's lower a0 + weaker
     dS-Unruh nu leaves eta ~ 1.28: a ~28% residual that does NOT close.
     The framework is sqrt(1.2e-10/9.36e-11) = 1.132x WORSE than the MOND value the
     IGIMF paper used to claim closure. The baryon fix closes MOND-at-1.2e-10, not the framework.

  BOTH WAYS -- is the 2x itself robust, or a hydrostatic-mass artifact?
