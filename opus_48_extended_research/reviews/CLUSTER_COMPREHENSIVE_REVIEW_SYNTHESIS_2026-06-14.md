## THE HEADLINE (a genuine, under-credited, distinctive hit — and an honest wall)

**The density-law a₀ = (c/2)√(G·ρ) predicts the measured cluster acceleration scale of Tian et al. 2024 with ZERO
free parameters.** Tian+2024 (A&A 684 A180, arXiv:2402.12016) measured the cluster radial-acceleration relation on
50 MaNGA + 20 CLASH BCGs + 64 clusters and found a cluster scale **g† = (2.0±0.1)×10⁻⁹ = 17× the galactic a₀**,
with intrinsic scatter **0.02 dex (4.9%)** — a tight, elevated scale that *universal-constant* MOND has no reason
to produce (eMOND has to *postulate* A₀(Φ)). The framework **derives** it: at the cluster mean overdensity (R200 ≈
200·ρ_crit), the density-a₀ boost is √(ρ/ρ_DE) = **17.1×** — landing ON Tian's 17× with no tuning. And because
R200/R500 are *fixed-overdensity* radii, every cluster has ~the same mean density → a **nearly-universal**
a₀_cluster → which reproduces Tian's 0.02-dex *tightness* for free. This is real, distinctive, order-correct, and
genuinely under-credited.

**But it is NOT a cure** (three independent walls, all credited at full weight):
1. **No DERIVED smoothing scale threads the ~300-450 kpc core window.** Read at the local BCG-core density (~10⁵
   ρ_DE) the boost is ~320× and *erases* the tight galaxy SPARC RAR; read at the mean-R500 density (~28×) it
   *over-closes* (η→0.42). The framework's only derived length is the Gpc dS-Unruh horizon; r_DE self-normalizes to
   ~2 ρ_DE. The cluster-MEAN ~Mpc scale that works is **tuned, not derived** — the banked trap.
2. **The per-galaxy form is already FALSIFIED at 10.5σ — by the framework's OWN data.** Density-a₀ predicts
   a₀_local>a₀_field for *any* galaxy in a dense region. The framework's SPARC environment test
   (`A0_COSMICWEB_ENVIRONMENT`) gives d log a₀ / d log(1+δ) = **+0.052±0.043** — 1.2σ from zero, **10.5σ from the
   predicted +0.5**. Externally echoed by Bilek/Renaud/Samurović 2026 (arXiv:2603.23591, 17 ETGs): isolated AND
   non-central galaxies follow the **standard** RAR; only centrals deviate, and they read it as collisionless
   invisible mass, explicitly NOT an a₀ shift.
3. **The data is degenerate** — the elevated cluster scale reads equally as residual collisionless mass
   (Bilek 2026; Famaey 2026 Bullet) as it does as a density-a₀ shift.

**Honest grade: a real zero-parameter hit at the cluster-MEAN scale, but the same law's per-galaxy prediction is
already excluded and the cluster hit rides a non-derived scale. LIVE-as-falsifiable-signature, NULL-as-cure.**

## WHAT IS ACTUALLY GOING ON (the latest data resolves the "2×" into a heterogeneous, mostly-central, mostly-soft residual)

The cluster "missing mass" is **NOT one coherent universal 2× deficit.** The 2024-2026 data decomposes it:

1. **CENTRAL + gas-shaped, not a flat outskirts floor.** Three independent lines + the framework's own calc agree
   the residual lives in the core and dies to ~1 outward: Famaey/Pizzuti/Saltas 2025 (CLASH lensing, PRD) — inner
   constant-density core + ~450 kpc exponential cutoff, missing-to-gas ratio ~10 *remarkably uniform*; Kelleher &
   Lelli 2024 (A&A 688 A78, X-COP) — inner core + outer r⁻⁴ (finite total mass), missing/baryon ~1-5 at 200-300 kpc
   → 0.4-1.1 at 2-3 Mpc; the framework's own β+NFW deep-MOND η(r) is **monotone-decreasing outward in 9/9** (c500 ×
   β) combos (generic deep-MOND scaling η = √(G/a₀)·M_tot/(r·√M_bar), not a profile artifact).
2. **PART non-equilibrium.** Kelleher & Lelli: mergers push the ratio up to ~5, "may indicate out-of-equilibrium
   dynamics rather than actual missing mass." Famaey 2026 Bullet (arXiv:2605.10022, on Rihtaršič+2026 JWST lens
   model): residual is **collisionless, centered on galaxies, NOT neutrinos**. Relaxed fraction definition-dependent
   (30-75%).
3. **PART mass-systematic.** Li+2024 (22 eRASS1 clusters): weak-lensing mass runs **~110% higher** than hydrostatic
   + kinematic (which agree with each other). That single unresolved systematic **brackets the R500 η across
   [~1.0, ~2.33]** — the magnitude is NOT pinned tighter than that by current data.
4. **PART baryon-budget.** Cosmic baryons are now FOUND (eROSITA outskirts ~80% at R200m) but in the WRONG PLACE
   (outskirts, collisional) to fix g_bar *at* R500. Zhang/Zonoozi/Kroupa 2026 (PRD 113 043027, arXiv:2602.06082):
   top-heavy-IGIMF stellar remnants close the *core* to ~88% in 46 nearby clusters (a MOND-SHARED rescue, costs the
   framework nothing; NS/BH remnants ARE collisionless baryons tracking galaxies, defeating the old "residual is
   collisionless so can't be baryons" point).

**Net physical picture:** an irreducible, uniform, **central, gas-tracking** residual survives in relaxed
gas-complete clusters, wrapped in a disequilibrium + WL-mass + baryon-completeness skirt that makes the raw "2×"
overstate the equilibrium truth. Real, MOND-SHARED — neither a coherent universal missing-mass nor pure systematics.
