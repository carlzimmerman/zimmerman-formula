## The prediction

∇δΨ = 2(g_obs − g_N), so ∇Ψ = 2g_obs − g_N. The **gravitational slip** is therefore

  **γ ≡ Ψ/Φ (gradient ratio) = 2·√(1 + a₀/g_N) − 1.**
