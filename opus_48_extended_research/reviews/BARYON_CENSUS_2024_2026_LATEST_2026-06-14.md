## 1. The latest census — how much baryon is really in a cluster?

The 2024–2026 census is now precise and consistent across three independent probes (X-ray stacking, kSZ+lensing,
outskirts X-ray). The picture is **strongly radius- and mass-dependent**:

| probe | within R500 | at R200m / virial | beyond |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Popesso+2024** (eROSITA stacking, A&A 2026) hot gas fgas500 = 2.23e-7·(M500/M⊙)^0.39 | groups 20–40% cosmic; massive 65–80%; reaches cosmic only at ~1e15 | gas **doubles** R500→R200; cosmic within R200 only for massive clusters | — |
| **kSZ census** (2507.14136, McCarthy/Schaan-type) | ~30% cosmic at group center | — | baryons return to cosmic only at **2–3 virial radii** |
| **Siegel+2025** (joint X-ray+kSZ+WL, ApJ) | groups 5–7%, clusters 10–12% (2× below FLAMINGO) | — | efficient expulsion **beyond several R500** |
| **eROSITA outskirts stacking** (2509.25317 / A&A 2026) | ~65% cosmic by R500c | fgas rises continuously; **~80% cosmic (clumping-corr.), ≳100% uncorrected at R200m** | detected to 2×R200m (~4.5 Mpc); baryon overdensity 30 at R200m |

**Bottom line on the budget:** Carl's premise is CONFIRMED and sharpened by the latest data. The eRASS1 X-ray gas

not invented: they sit **between R500 and R200m**, recovered as the gas profile climbs from ~65% (R500) to ~80–100%
(R200m). The census 2024–2026 has essentially *solved the cluster missing-baryon problem* (the baryons are found, in
the expelled/infalling outskirts gas). **This does not touch the MOND deficit** — see §2–3.

## 2. How much does η drop if the within-R500 baryon is completed? (the new numbers, real eRASS1)

η ≡ g_obs/(ν·g_bar) at R500, framework a0 = 9.36e-11, dS-Unruh/simple ν, real eRASS1 (Bulbul+2024, N=9830):

| within-R500 baryon budget | f_b(R500) source | η_median | η_geomean |

**Completing the budget with the latest (Popesso) census is a real ~⅓ reducer (2.18 → 1.93), and the absolute

you must invent baryons that the census says are NOT there (they are at >R500). The reducer is genuine; the closure
is not.

## 3. The two crux tests, re-run on the latest data — BOTH hold

**(a) The missing gas sits BEYOND R500 → cannot lower g_bar AT R500.** The 2026 eROSITA outskirts stacking is now
*direct*: the gas fraction "continuously increases beyond R500c and reaches ≳100% of cosmic at R200m," with only
~65% recovered *by* R500c, and the >R500 gas is explicitly *additional* to within-R500 measurements. g_bar(R500)
depends only on mass enclosed *within* R500; expelled gas at R500<r<R200m contributes zero to it. **Confirmed,
now with a direct radial profile rather than an inference.**

**(b) The deficit is UNDIMINISHED where the gas budget is complete.** Using the Popesso R500 power-law per cluster:

| M500 bin | Popesso gas / cosmic at R500 | η_median |
|---|---|---|
| 1e13–1e14 | 35% | 1.88 |
| 1e14–3e14 | 52% | 1.96 |
| 3e14–5e14 | 68% | 1.94 |
| 5e14–1e15 | 83% | 1.87 |
| **>1e15 (gas = 100% cosmic at R500)** | **100%** | **1.87** |
| clusters where Popesso gas ≥ 99% cosmic (N=67) | 100% | **1.86** |

**η is FLAT at ~1.9 across the entire mass range, and stays ~1.86–1.87 exactly where the within-R500 gas reaches
the full cosmic value.** This is the deep-MOND signature (η ∝ 1/√M_bar, but g_bar/a0 falls with mass so the
amplification grows): completing the budget helps the depleted groups and the gas-complete clusters *equally and
uniformly*, never closing. The banked "deficit undiminished in gas-complete clusters" is **robust on the newest
data** — and it now has a clean physical reading: the deficit is NOT a budget effect, because where the budget IS
complete (η would have to fall to 1) it sits at 1.87.

## 4. The decisive 2024–2026 blows to the missing-baryon hypothesis specifically

- **Pizzuti & Famaey 2025 (PRD, 2410.02612, CLASH lensing):** the MOND residual missing mass is **CENTRALLY
  concentrated** — a near-constant missing/gas density ratio ~10 inside a ~450 kpc core, then a sharp r^(−4…−6)
  cutoff. The missing mass *follows the gas in the center* and **dies faster than the gas outward** (ratio rises
  2.7→7.3 with decreasing baryonic acceleration). The expelled census baryons sit at LARGE radii (>R500); the MOND
  residual sits at SMALL radii (<450 kpc). They are **spatially disjoint** — the found baryons are in the wrong
  place to be the missing mass.
- **Bullet Cluster, JWST lensing 2026 (2605.10022):** the residual MOND missing mass is **centered on the GALAXIES,
  not the gas → it must be largely COLLISIONLESS.** Expelled/infalling hot gas (the census baryons) is collisional
  and lags in mergers. So the missing mass cannot be the missing hot baryons even in principle — it has the wrong
  collisional behavior.
- **McGaugh (lead MOND phenomenologist) himself rejects the missing-baryon out:** "I do not like the missing-baryon
  hypothesis for clusters in MOND. I struggle to imagine how we could hide the required amount." He frames clusters
  as MOND's standing **unsolved** problem.

## 5. The framework's distinctive lens — does a0↔Λ / density-a0 / modified inertia change this?

- **Modified inertia (dS-Unruh) does NOT help and is +13% WORSE.** At R500 the sample is 96% deep-MOND
  (median g_bar/a0 = 0.037), where MI and MG share the same virial law M_dyn = (9/4)σ⁴/(Ga0); the framework's
  *lower* a0 (9.36 vs 1.2e-10) inflates η by √(1.2/0.936)=1.13. The framework inherits the deficit and pays a 13%
  surcharge. **No framework-distinctive reducer here.**
- **The density-a0 superset a0=(c/2)√(Gρ_total) is the ONLY framework-distinctive out** — but it is orthogonal to
  the baryon census: it would raise *cluster a0* (Mpc-overdensity boost ~15×, pulling η→1.2–1.5), not add baryons.
  It rides on an underived smoothing scale and the local/clumpy reading breaks the galaxy RAR. The baryon census
  neither helps nor hurts this channel — it is a separate (unforced) angle.
- **Framework-honest reading of the census:** the census is *good news for ΛCDM's baryon accounting* (baryons
  found) and *irrelevant to closing the framework's cluster deficit* (found in the wrong place, collisional not
  collisionless, central residual disjoint from outskirts gas).
