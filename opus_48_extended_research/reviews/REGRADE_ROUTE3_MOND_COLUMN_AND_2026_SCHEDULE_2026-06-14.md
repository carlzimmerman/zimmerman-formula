1. **a0-only, at FIXED interpolation shape:** framework (9.36e-11) vs MOND (1.2e-10) gap is only **~0.056 in γ** —
   *MOND-DEGENERATE*, below the contamination floor (~0.10) and the interp systematic. So the wide-binary channel is
   **NON-diagnostic of a0** (`ROUTE3_GEXT_PIN_AND_DR4_FORECAST`: gap 0.092 simple-μ, 0.056 same-shape). The route's

   higher a0" is **standard-MOND-as-AQUAL (simple-μ modified gravity)**; the framework is modified INERTIA

   MOND being AQUAL, not a which-a0 test.

simple-μ band — Carl's catch). The honest correction is that the route mis-labels the framework-vs-MOND wide-binary

This does **not** flip the row — wide binaries remain a clean framework-vs-NEWTON detection (~3σ at DR4) and
