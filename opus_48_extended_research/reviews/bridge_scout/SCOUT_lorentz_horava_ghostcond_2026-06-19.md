## FINDINGS (people, exact papers, verdicts)

### (ii) POSITIVITY / CAUSALITY — the real consistency test of GAP-1. VERDICT: PERMITS (constrains, does not kill).
- **Grall & Melville, "Positivity Bounds without Boosts," arXiv:2102.05683 (PRD 105, L121301, 2022).** The first
  positivity bounds for EFTs that break Lorentz BOOSTS (spontaneously or explicitly). CRUCIAL for us: they confirm
  in-text that **a Lorentz-invariant UV completion is NOT required** ("a UV Lorentz symmetry was not required to
  translate the properties into bounds") — only unitarity, causality/analyticity, locality, rotations/translations.
  => These bounds DO apply to a ghost condensate (which has no LI UV completion). They do not exhibit the ghost
  condensate as an example (their examples are superfluid + inflaton), so no direct pass/fail is printed.
- **Serra & Trombetta, "IR side of bounds on Theories with Spontaneously Broken Lorentz Symmetry," arXiv:2412.19745
  (Dec 2024, v3 Apr 2026).** The decisive both-ways result: this is a **PERMISSIVE** finding, NOT a no-go. It
  re-expresses the analyticity/causality bounds as a low-energy kinematic condition — **gapped excitations must
  propagate SLOWER than the gapless mode below the mass gap** (an acoustic-metric statement). Theories with
  spontaneously broken Lorentz symmetry CAN respect unitarity/causality. => The positivity literature PERMITS, with
  a concrete consistency CONDITION the framework must check (gapped-slower-than-gapless), the framework's
  wrong-sign-then-stabilized P(X). A positivity KILL would have been as important as a bridge; there is NO kill.
- **Creminelli, Janssen, Senatore, "Positivity bounds on EFTs with spontaneously broken Lorentz invariance,"
  arXiv:2207.14224 (JHEP).** Companion bounds, but these REQUIRE a CONFORMAL UV completion — which the ghost
  condensate explicitly does NOT have (it is an IR object with no LI/conformal UV). So this stronger family is
  INAPPLICABLE to the framework: it neither bridges nor kills. (Important not to mis-cite it as a kill.)
- **Creminelli, Delladio, Janssen, Longo, Senatore, "Non-analyticity of the S-matrix with spontaneously broken
  Lorentz invariance," arXiv:2312.08441 (JHEP 06 (2024) 201).** Shows the boost-broken S-matrix is NON-analytic
  (energy-dependent radial/Goldstone mixing in LSZ). Tangential warning, not a kill: it means naive analytic
  dispersion-relation positivity must be reformulated for the framework — which is exactly what 2102.05683 /
  2412.19745 do. Constrains the METHOD, not the model.

NET on (ii): the strongest, most-applicable bound (boost-free positivity, 2102.05683 + 2412.19745) PERMITS the
framework's P(X) and hands it ONE falsifiable consistency check (gapped mode slower than gapless below the gap).
This is a genuine "real consistency test" that the framework PASSES in principle — report at full weight, both ways.

### (iii) MUKOHYAMA ghost-condensate-as-DM — touches GAP-1/GAP-4. VERDICT: structural analogue, CONFIRMS amount is FREE.
- **Lim, Sawicki, Vikman, "Ghost Dark Matter," arXiv:1001.4634 (JCAP 2010).** A ghost-condensate scalar whose
  energy density redshifts as a^-3 cold DUST — this IS the framework's Q-mode mechanism, independently constructed.
  Real GAP-1 support (the dust is a known consequence of the ghost-condensate kinetic structure, not unique to the
  framework). BUT both-ways: the FRW background is indistinguishable from LCDM only for the Lorentz-breaking scale
  M >~ 1 eV (LSS pushes M >~ 10 eV), and the DM ABUNDANCE is set by M + initial conditions, **NOT pinned by the
  theory** => CONFIRMS GAP-4 (Omega_dm = free shift-charge/IC), does not close it. Honest concession at full weight.
- **Mirzagholi & Vikman, "Imperfect Dark Matter," arXiv:1412.7136 (JCAP 2015) + Capela/Sibiryakov-line "Gravitational
  focusing of Imperfect Dark Matter," arXiv:1609.08580.** Mimetic/higher-derivative scalar => irrotational dust on
  large scales, imperfect-fluid (sound speed/viscosity) deviations on small scales. Same family. The amount can
  "become the main source of DM" when shift symmetry breaks in radiation domination — a MECHANISM that gives an
  abundance, but still IC/scale-dependent, not a unique prediction. Partial GAP-1/GAP-4 lead.

### (i) HORAVA Ricci-flow / shift-symmetry — GAP-2 (SO(4,1) gate / frame origin). VERDICT: tangential, NOT a derivation.
- **Frenkel, Horava, Randall, "Topological Quantum Gravity of the Ricci Flow" line: arXiv:2010.15369, 2011.06230,
  2011.11914 (2020-21).** The "shift" / shifted-spacetime-diffeo symmetry here is a BRST/topological gauge artifact
  of N=2 topological nonrelativistic gravity (Perelman dilaton = lapse, tau = Goldstone of time-translation +
  anisotropic rescaling). It is NOT a physical ghost-condensate shift-symmetric kinetic term, and the theory is
  TOPOLOGICAL (no propagating MOND/dust sector). So Horava's recent program does NOT supply a UV ORIGIN for the
  framework's P(X) or close the SO(4,1) gate. Tangential keyword-match; report as NONE/tangential, not a bridge.
- Standard (non-topological) Horava/khronometric gravity DOES supply a propagating preferred-frame khronon and IS
  the natural covariant home for a preferred frame (Blas-Pujolas-Sibiryakov healthy non-projectable extension;
  "Status of Horava Gravity," arXiv:2307.13039). This is BACKGROUND/embedding (already known, AeST-adjacent), not a
  NEW derivation of the kinetic term — counts as "constrains/embedding," not a fresh bridge.
