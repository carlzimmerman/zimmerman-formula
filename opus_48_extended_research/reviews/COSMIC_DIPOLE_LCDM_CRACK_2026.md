# The cosmic dipole as a ΛCDM crack — both ways (2026)

*Assessment of whether the quasar/radio number-count dipole is a real ΛCDM crack and whether it connects to
the framework. Per the #1 rule: graded both ways, no manufactured win. Companion to
[`THE_HONEST_LCDM_STRESS_BRIEF.md`](../THE_HONEST_LCDM_STRESS_BRIEF.md).*

## What it is

The Cosmological Principle — large-scale statistical isotropy and homogeneity — is a **foundational axiom** of
ΛCDM, not a derived result. A clean kinematic prediction follows: the dipole (front-back asymmetry) in the
number counts of distant radio sources / quasars should match, in amplitude and direction, the dipole we infer
from our motion through the CMB (v ≈ 370 km/s). It does not.

## The case FOR it being a real crack (graded GENUINE-CRACK, foundations-level)

- **The amplitude is 2–3.7× too large, at ~5σ, across independent surveys and bands:**
  - Secrest et al. 2021 (CatWISE2020 IR quasars): ~2× the kinematic expectation, **4.9σ** (arXiv:2212.07733).
  - Dam, Lewis & Brewer 2023 (Bayesian number-count analysis): ~2.7×, **5.7σ** (MNRAS 525, 231).
  - Böhme et al. 2025 (radio: NVSS + RACS-low + LoTSS-DR2): **3.67 ± 0.49×**, **5.4σ**.
  - "Wising up to CatWISE" 2025 (MNRAS, simulation-based inference): **confirms** the excess after modelling the
    ecliptic-bias systematic — i.e., the leading systematic does not erase it.
- **It hits an AXIOM, not a parameter.** Unlike the CC problem (an explanatory gap) or H0 (a parameter-
  consistency crack), the dipole challenges the *premise* on which the entire ΛCDM background is built. If the
  matter rest frame and the CMB rest frame genuinely disagree at 5σ, the homogeneous-isotropic FLRW starting
  point is in question.
- **It is MOND-CLASS-FRIENDLY (a real tailwind for modified gravity):** the same direction of anomaly shows up
  in independent velocity data — the CosmicFlows-4 bulk flow is **4.8σ** faster than ΛCDM at 200 h⁻¹ Mpc, and
  the KBC void (density contrast 0.46 ± 0.06 out to ~300 Mpc) is ~6σ deep for ΛCDM. Haslbauer, Banik & Kroupa
  (2020, MNRAS 499, 2845) find KBC + H0 rule out ΛCDM at **7.09σ** on Gpc scales and conclude the density+
  velocity field "suggests a long-range modification to gravity." MOND's self-regulated structure growth
  (external-field-driven) naturally produces deeper voids and faster bulk flows than ΛCDM — the framework lives
  in exactly this class.

## The case AGAINST overclaiming it (the honest limits)

- **It is NOT a prediction of a₀ = c²√(Λ/32π).** The dipole/bulk-flow anomalies favor *modified gravity as a
  class*; they do not test the framework's specific a₀ value or the Λ-identification. This is a class-level
  tailwind, not a distinctive framework win — do not present it as "the formula predicts the dipole."
- **Live systematics debate.** Ecliptic bias in CatWISE, flux-calibration and masking choices, and source
  evolution have all been argued to inflate the signal; reassessments (e.g. arXiv:2405.09762 2024,
  arXiv:2511.00822 2025) push back on the significance. The 2025 simulation-based-inference confirmation is
  reassuring but the debate is not closed.
- **Alternative non-MOND explanations exist.** A genuinely large *local* structure / bulk flow within ΛCDM
  (rare but not impossible), or intrinsic source clustering, could in principle produce part of the excess
  without new gravity.
- **The MOND reading carries the same cluster/CMB cost.** The Haslbauer-Banik-Kroupa program supplements MOND
  with ~11 eV sterile neutrinos to fix clusters and the CMB — the same "second dark component" the framework
  concedes it needs. So the dipole does not relieve the framework's standing losses.

## Grade and disposition

**GENUINE-CRACK (foundations-level), MOND-class-friendly, NOT framework-distinctive, systematics-contested.**
It is the *deepest* of ΛCDM's cracks because it strikes an axiom (isotropy), and its sign aligns with the
modified-gravity class the framework belongs to — so it strengthens the "ΛCDM's completeness is an assumption"
case more than the CC/DM/H0 cracks do. But it is a class-level tailwind, not a test of a₀, and it is debated.

**Use it as:** "ΛCDM's foundational isotropy assumption is under live ~5σ stress from the quasar dipole and the
CosmicFlows-4 bulk flow — anomalies in the direction modified gravity predicts (deeper voids, faster flows)."
**Do NOT use it as:** "my formula predicts the cosmic dipole" (it does not) or "the dipole kills ΛCDM" (it is
contested and has non-MOND escapes).

*Decisive future data: Euclid, Rubin/LSST, and SKA-precursor radio surveys will pin the dipole amplitude and
direction with far larger samples by ~2027–2030; DESI peculiar-velocity samples sharpen the bulk-flow side.*
