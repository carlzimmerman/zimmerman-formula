## The verdict — both prior framings were wrong, in opposite directions

**1. The radial SIGN MATCHES (the playbook's "wrong-sign" is an ERROR; the comprehensive review was right on direction).**
Both the apparent deficit η_field AND the density-a₀ boost RISE monotonically toward the center (η_field 1.55→7.66;
boost 4.32→34.96 inward). The density boost is largest exactly where the apparent deficit is largest. The playbook's
claim that the framework predicts "η smallest at center, opposite the observed discrepancy" conflated η_field
(unboosted, the apparent deficit) with η_after (boosted) — apples to oranges. Corrected: **same radial sign.**

**2. It FLATTENS the deficit dramatically, with ZERO free parameters (the genuinely striking, both-ways-credited result).**
Applying the framework's own density-a₀ with the LOCAL GAS density ρ_gas(r) collapses the raw deficit from a
radially-varying [1.55 → 7.66] down to **[0.75 → 1.30] — within ~30% of unity across the entire profile, no tuning.**
That is a real, distinctive, zero-parameter effect standard constant-a₀ MOND cannot produce.

**3. But it does NOT cleanly close — a residual TILT survives, and it over-closes the outskirts (the comprehensive review's "clean match" OVERSTATED it).**
The density boost UNDER-corrects the center (η→1.30: predicted boost 35× vs required 59×) and OVER-corrects the
outskirts (η→0.75: predicted 4.3× vs required 2.4× → a new *wrong-direction* deficit, less dynamical mass than
observed). So it trades a monotone 1.5–7.7× deficit for a milder ±30% residual tilt — much flatter, but not closed,
and now over-dense in the middle / under-dense at the edge.

**4. It still rides the banked SPARC trap (unchanged, fatal to it as a delivered cure).**
