# The CLASH + XRISM Core-Shape Measurement — a decisive, falsifiable test of the cluster-core residual

## The question (and why it is decisive, not a fishing trip)

The framework's *only* open soft-spot is the cluster **core** residual: inside ~420 kpc a rich, relaxed cluster
(M500 ≈ 1e15 M☉) needs a missing collisionless mass **M_res(<420 kpc) ≈ 2.30e14 M☉** (lensing core = X-ray
core, ratio 1.03), while the framework's modified-inertia phantom supplies only **~4.0e13 M☉** — a **~5.8×
bare-core undershoot**, a ~1.9e14 M☉ shortfall. This is a *shared* relativistic-MOND deficit (the framework's MI
is identical to AeST modified-gravity to machine precision in the core), not a framework-specific kill.

Whether ~half of that core can be closed **with zero new particles** hinges on a single, currently-**contested**
empirical fact: **the radial SHAPE of the residual mass.**

- **Gas-tracking** (Famaey–Pizzuti–Saltas 2025, arXiv:2410.02612 — CLASH lensing, 16 clusters): the residual is
  *cored*, follows the hot gas, missing-to-gas ratio ~10, exponential cutoff ~430 kpc, remarkably uniform.
  Stellar remnants track the **stars** (BCG-peaked), not the gas → **wrong shape → IGIMF remnants killed**, core
  stays an irreducible shared gap.
- **Galaxy-tracking** (Famaey 2026, arXiv:2605.10022 — new JWST Bullet lens model): the residual "should be
  mostly collisionless, **since it is centred on the galaxies**," fit by two cored Plummer spheres
  (r = 430 kpc and 150 kpc). If the residual follows the **galaxies/stars**, then **IGIMF stellar remnants**
  (neutron stars + stellar black holes — *real collapsed baryons*, no new species; Zhang–Zonoozi–Kroupa 2026,
  arXiv:2602.06082) **plus** the framework's own nonlinear AeST Y–Q field boost (a genuine **+17–20%** central
  mass, galaxy-safe, reproduced vs Durakovic–Skordis 2312.00889 Figs 5–7) together cover **roughly half the
  core with no new particles.**

These two readings make **opposite, measurable predictions** for one number — the slope of M_res(r)/M_baryon(r)
across 50–420 kpc. **That is the falsifiable test.** It is not a calculation we can settle; it is an observation.

## (1) The target

**Primary: RX J1347.5–1145** (z = 0.451). The single object that maximally satisfies all four data legs:
- **Lensing (deep):** CLASH HST 16-band strong lensing (Einstein radius 35″) + wide-field ground/Subaru weak
  lensing; WL mass (3.5±0.5)e15 M☉. One of the best-constrained cluster cores in the sky; Euclid/Rubin will
  sharpen the 0.5–1 Mpc WL shear this decade.
- **X-ray:** one of the most X-ray-luminous clusters known → a prime XRISM/Resolve velocity-dispersion and
  deprojected-HSE target; rich Chandra/XMM gas profile already in hand.
- **Stellar light:** HST CLASH photometry resolves BCG+ICL + member galaxies → a deprojected stellar-mass map.
- Massive (T ~ 10–15 keV) → deep-MOND core, the regime where the residual is largest and the shape cleanest.

**Systematics anchor / in-hand control: Abell 2029** (z = 0.078). XRISM/Resolve **already measured**
non-thermal pressure **2.6 ± 0.3%** in the core (≤2% out to R2500=670 kpc), σ_v = 169 ± 10 km/s — an
exceptionally relaxed, cool-core cluster with HSE under exquisite control. A2029 pins the non-thermal
budget (the dominant HSE systematic) *now*; RX J1347 carries the deep strong+weak lensing that A2029's
low redshift lacks. Use A2029 as the HSE-systematics calibrator, RX J1347 as the shape-measurement target.

*Alternatives, same logic:* any CLASH cool-core with an XRISM pointing — MACS J1206, A2390 (Euclid ERO
strong+weak lensing already published), or A2667 (XRISM cool-core turbulence in hand).

## (2) The measurement

Build, on one rich relaxed cluster, the **deprojected residual mass profile**:

  **M_res(r) = M_tot(r) − M_baryon(r) = [ lensing + XRISM-HSE total ] − [ X-ray gas + deprojected stellar light ]**

and ask the single discriminating question: **is M_res(r)/M_gas(r) FLAT/cored (gas-tracking) or does it RISE
toward the center / track the stellar light (galaxy-tracking)?**

- **M_tot(r):** joint strong+weak lensing (model-independent in the core) **cross-checked** against XRISM
  deprojected HSE with the non-thermal pressure pinned ≤2% (the A2029-calibrated value). Lensing–HSE agreement
  in the core is already 1.03 at matched mass — a built-in consistency check.
- **M_baryon(r):** X-ray gas (deprojected β-model / onion-peel) + stellar mass from resolved BCG+ICL+member
  photometry. Compute **both** ratios: residual/gas (the FPS observable) **and** residual/stellar-light.
- **The discriminant:** gas-tracking predicts residual/gas ≈ flat ~10 (FPS); galaxy-tracking predicts
  residual/gas **rising** inward (13→3 from 100→420 kpc) while residual/**stellar-light** stays flat.

## (3) Precision needed

- **Radial S/N:** ≥5 independent deprojected bins across 50–420 kpc, each with M_res/M_gas to ≤25% — enough to
  separate a *flat* ratio from one rising by ~4× inward at >3σ. Existing CLASH strong-lensing cores already
  resolve M(<r) to ~10% at <100 kpc; the limiting leg is the deprojected gas+stellar baryon map.
- **Deprojection systematics:** triaxiality/projection is the chief lensing bias — mitigated by selecting a
  *relaxed, round, cool-core* cluster (RX J1347 is symmetric; A2029 maximally relaxed) and by the
  lensing-vs-HSE cross-check.
- **XRISM non-thermal control:** the make-or-break HSE systematic. A2029 fixes it at **≤2%** (XRISM, in hand);
  applied to RX J1347 this caps the HSE total-mass error from turbulence at the few-percent level — the
  ingredient that *was not available* before XRISM and is what makes this test newly decisive.
