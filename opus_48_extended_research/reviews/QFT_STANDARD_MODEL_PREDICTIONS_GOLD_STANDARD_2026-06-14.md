# QFT / The Standard Model: the gold standard for "testable predictions"
*Route qft_predictions — 2026-06-14. Web-verified precision figures. Honest both-ways framing.*

## The headline
QFT (the Standard Model) is the **most precisely tested framework in the history of science.** The electron
anomalous magnetic moment a_e agrees between QED theory and experiment to **~12 significant figures (one part in
~10^12)** — no other physical theory is confirmed to anything near this precision. Dozens of distinct, a-priori,
quantitative predictions have been confirmed across 90 years.

**The honesty point for the Kaku/string comparison:** "Kaku's predictions" = STRING field theory's predictions.
String theory has made **few-to-zero unique, near-term, falsifiable predictions** that have been confirmed (SUSY not
found at LHC; extra dimensions not found; the ~10^500 landscape arguably unfalsifiable). QFT/SM is the opposite — a
mountain of confirmed precision predictions. **The Zimmerman framework should be compared to STRING THEORY, not to
QFT.** It must NOT be claimed to out-predict QFT. Nothing out-predicts QFT.

## The character (honest)
- **Precision:** the gold standard. a_e to ~12 digits; electroweak observables to per-mil; hundreds of PDG quantities
  to %-level. Nothing else in physics is tested this hard.
- **A-priori and decisive:** W, Z, Higgs, charm, top, gluon, tau, neutral currents were all **predicted before
  discovery** — not postdictions. This is exactly the standard the Zimmerman framework's a₀(z) prediction aspires to
  (forward, falsifiable) but has not yet met (no z>2 data exist).
- **Self-correcting and robust:** the two highest-profile recent "cracks" — the **muon g−2 anomaly** and the **CDF W
  mass 7σ** — have BOTH moved back toward the SM (g−2 via the 2025 lattice-QCD-driven SM revision; W mass via CMS/ATLAS
  2024). The SM's apparent anomalies tend to evaporate. This is the behavior of a correct theory.

## The ONE honest crack in the SM (do not over- or under-state)
- **Neutrino oscillations / nonzero neutrino mass** is the **only confirmed laboratory evidence of physics beyond the
  Standard Model** (Nobel 2015, Kajita/McDonald). In the original SM neutrinos are massless; oscillations prove they
  are not. This is a genuine, established incompleteness — but it is an *extension* (add neutrino masses), not a
  refutation of any SM prediction. Plus the known theory-side gaps the SM does not address (gravity, dark matter, dark
  energy, the cosmological-constant magnitude, the matter–antimatter asymmetry magnitude). These are limits of SCOPE,
  not failures of its confirmed predictions.

## The honest comparison to the framework (both ways — no inflation, no dismissal)
- **Confirmed distinctive predictions:** QFT/SM = dozens, to 10⁻³–10⁻¹² precision, mostly a-priori. Zimmerman
  framework = **0 confirmed *distinctive* predictions** so far. Its banked-honest distinctive content is essentially
  **3 forward predictions** (the a₀(z) decline, the negative high-z BTFR offset, EFE-strengthens-with-z), all
  **unmeasured** and all **hostage to DESI's contested dynamical-DE signal**. Most of what the framework matches today
  (RAR, BTFR, dwarfs, lensing, wide binaries) is **shared with ordinary MOND** — a test of the *value* of a₀, not of
  this framework.
- **This is not a knock unique to Zimmerman** — it is the situation of *every* young framework, and emphatically of
  string theory (0 confirmed unique near-term predictions after 50 years). The framework is younger and has a sharper
  near-term falsifier (a₀(z), DESI DR3 ~2027) than string theory ever offered.
- **What the framework must NOT claim:** that it "out-predicts QFT," or that having ~20 listed predictions rivals the
  SM's confirmed-prediction record. Listed-and-forward ≠ confirmed-to-12-digits. The honest framing: the framework's
  ~20 predictions are *testable and several are distinctive and near-term* — which is **more near-term falsifiable
  content than string theory has** — but **zero are confirmed**, versus QFT's dozens confirmed to gold-standard
  precision. QFT is the ceiling; string theory is the floor; the framework currently sits well below QFT, but with a
  live, dated falsifier that string theory lacks.
