## PART III — WHERE ΛCDM CLEANLY WINS (the honest losses — concede them loudly)

These are durable; they will **not** collapse under review. Conceding them is what buys the brief a hearing.

1. **Galaxy clusters** (LCDM-WINS) — ~2× missing mass at R500 on **9830 real eRASS1 clusters**
   (η_median=2.149, 96% deep-MOND). Robust to every escape: full cosmic f_b=0.156 only drops η to 1.53; the
   a₀(z) lever supplies ≤21% of the +0.66 dex needed (and would break the galaxy a₀ pin). The framework's
   *lower* a₀ makes it ~12% *worse* than textbook MOND (η∝1/√a₀). Honest significance ~1.9–3.7σ against a
   0.10–0.20 dex systematic floor — but the sign + robustness are certain. AeST has an *unproven* intrinsic
   escape (Durakovic-Skordis 2024 oscillatory mass term); do not oversell it.
2. **The CMB 3rd acoustic peak** (LCDM-WINS) — baryonic MOND predicts a 3rd peak too low; Planck falsifies it
   (~200:1 against no-CDM). AeST fits it only with a tuned dust-mimic Ω≈0.265 — and since **a₀ is provably
   absent from linear cosmology** (a force modification adds exactly 0 to Ω), that Ω is an integration constant
   *put in by hand*: the headline "two dark sectors are one number" **provably does not hold at the CMB**.
   **⚠️ Both-ways guard:** this does *not* falsify a₀=√Λ (a₀ is absent from linear physics) — inflating it to
   "the CMB kills a₀=Λ" is a manufactured loss in the other direction and is itself refutable.
3. **The weak-lensing RAR morphology split** (LCDM-WINS) — at fixed g_bar, early-type galaxies sit **+0.26 dex
   above** late-types (**8.8–9.2σ**, 15/15 bins, Brouwer+2021). A property-independent, type-blind force law
   (which the framework structurally is) predicts **one** RAR regardless of morphology — it **cannot** make
   this split. **a₀-INDEPENDENT** (no footing/M/L/a₀(z) wiggle room). The framework's own reviewers call it
   *"the strongest standing anti-framework result"* (the per-class concentration escape collapses:
   measured ΔlogC≈+0.001, opposite-signed; `reviews/lensing_rar/agentH_perclass_C.md`).
4. **The Cassini Solar-System quadrupole** (LCDM-WINS, near-term) — the RAR needs a ~30% MOND boost at the Sun
   (g_ext~1.8 a₀); Cassini bounds the extra radial acceleration to ≤2% (Q₂=(1.6±1.8)×10⁻²⁷ s⁻²). AQUAL/QUMOND
   cannot fit RAR + quadrupole together (8.7σ fiducial, 3–15σ across MW mass models). The framework inherits
   this via AeST (modified *gravity*); the modified-inertia escape has no covariant/CMB-safe completion. The
   lower a₀ only shaves 36%→28%. **A z=0 loss that does not wait for z~3** — a referee raises it first.
5. **The Bullet Cluster offset** (LCDM-WINS) — a *qualitatively distinct* second cluster failure: the lensing
   mass sits on the collisionless galaxies, not the X-ray gas. Relativistic MOND fits it only by importing the
   *same* second tuned dark component conceded at the CMB. Clusters (equilibrium) + Bullet (offset) + CMB (3rd
   peak) are **three faces of one structural cost** — the framework needs a clustering dark sector that
   a₀=√Λ does not predict.
6. **a₀(z) declining** (FRAMEWORK-EXPOSURE, leaning unfavorable) — the framework's *one* distinctive
   beyond-MOND prediction (−0.033 dex by z=3). The most direct measurement (MUSE-DARK III) shows a₀ *rising*;
   de-systematized to ~1.5σ against (never reaching zero), rescued only by the Mayer/Magneticum degeneracy —
   which is *asymmetric in cost* (ΛCDM post-dicts the rise parameter-free; the framework can only hide behind
   it defensively) and buries the −0.033 dex signal below the 0.026 dex BTFR scatter floor. Currently both
   unfalsified **and** unconfirmable. Filed straight — it survives review *because* it does not overclaim.
