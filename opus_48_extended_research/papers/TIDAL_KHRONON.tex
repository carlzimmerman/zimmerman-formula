\begin{abstract}
We analyse, from first principles, a preferred-foliation (khronometric) extension of MOND
in which the interpolating function is generated by one constitutive function
$F(X,Y)$ of the acceleration invariant $X=c^4 a_\mu a^\mu/a_0^2$ and the spatial-curvature
invariant $Y=c^8\bar{\mathcal R}_{\mu\nu}\bar{\mathcal R}^{\mu\nu}/a_0^4$, with
$F=-2\sqrt X+2\ln(1+\sqrt X)+\varepsilon\,X^2(1+X)^{-4}\,Y$.
The baseline architecture (a khronon with a nonlinear acceleration function) is that of
Blanchet--Marsat; the tidal $Y$-sector and the specific closed $F$ are new.
We derive the exact static weak-field system and find it is irreducibly two-potential:
the lapse equation carries the MOND kernel $\mu=1+F_X=x/(1+x)$ and the matter source,
while the spatial equation carries a fourth-order tidal operator acting on the trace-free
Hessian of the curvature potential, sourcing gravitational slip at $O(\varepsilon)$.
The familiar single-potential equation is recovered exactly at $O(\varepsilon)$ on shell
if and only if the aether coupling $\eta_K=0$; remarkably, $\eta_K=0$ is independently
forced by gradient stability of the khronon at every finite $X$.
The theory has $2+1$ propagating modes, $c_T=c$ exactly, $\gamma_{\rm PPN}=1$ exactly at
$\varepsilon=0$ for every acceleration, and $F$ is $C^1$ at $X=0$ because the
$\sqrt X$ pieces cancel, so a quadratic action around Minkowski exists.
Solving the exact nonlinear AQUAL background (validated against Blanchet--Novak to
$1.9\%$) and its linear tidal response, we find the Solar-System quadrupole
$q_{zz}(\chi)=q_{zz}(0)+\chi\,dq/d\chi$ with $dq/d\chi=+0.30$--$0.41$ against
$q_{zz}(0)=-0.23$ to $-0.26$: the tidal sector \emph{suppresses} the quadrupole, with a
Cassini-compatible window $\chi\in[0.5,0.8]$, $\chi=\varepsilon c^4/(GMa_0)$, inside the
elliptic domain. However, the same $Y$-sector feeds the tensor dispersion
$\omega^2=c^2k^2[1+2\varepsilon A(X_0)(kc^2/a_0)^2]$, anti-suppressed by
$(kc^2/a_0)^2\sim4\times10^{42}$ at LIGO frequencies; GW170817 forces
$\varepsilon\lesssim5\times10^{-54}$, twenty-nine orders of magnitude below the
$\varepsilon\sim10^{-24}$ the window requires. The spatial-curvature form of the tidal
sector is therefore excluded, and the frozen theory collapses to $\eta_K=0$ khronometric
MOND, inheriting the full radial-acceleration-relation/Cassini tension of Desmond, Hees
\& Famaey. We identify the minimal repair --- building the tidal invariant from
$D_{\langle\mu}a_{\nu\rangle}$, which is lapse-built and tensor-silent, preserves
$\gamma_{\rm PPN}=1$, and carries the identical static response --- as the natural next
candidate, requiring its own consistency program. The acceleration scale $a_0$ remains an
input throughout: on FLRW, $X=Y=0$ and $F(0,0)=0$ exactly, so the action supplies neither
dark energy nor a derivation of $a_0$.
\end{abstract}

\section{The action}
Fields $(g_{\mu\nu},T,\psi_m)$;
$u_\mu=-\nabla_\mu T/\sqrt{-\nabla_\alpha T\nabla^\alpha T}$,
$h_{\mu\nu}=g_{\mu\nu}+u_\mu u_\nu$, $a_\mu=u^\alpha\nabla_\alpha u_\mu$,
$\bar{\mathcal R}_{\mu\nu}={}^{(3)}\!R_{\mu\nu}-\tfrac13h_{\mu\nu}{}^{(3)}\!R$,
\begin{equation}
S=\frac{M_{\rm Pl}^2c^3}{2}\!\int\! d^4x\,N\sqrt h\Big[{}^{(3)}\!R+K_{ij}K^{ij}
-\lambda_K K^2+\eta_K a_ia^i-\frac{2a_0^2}{c^4}F(X,Y)\Big]+S_m[g,\psi_m],
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
F(X,Y)=-2\sqrt X+2\ln(1+\sqrt X)+\varepsilon\,\frac{X^2}{(1+X)^4}\,Y,
\qquad a_0=9.3619\times10^{-11}\,{\rm m\,s^{-2}}.
\end{equation}
Parameters: $\lambda_K,\eta_K,\varepsilon,a_0$. $F(0,0)=0$ exactly; and
$F=-X+\tfrac23X^{3/2}-\tfrac12X^2+\dots$, so $F$ is $C^1$ at the origin: the MOND
non-analyticity is deferred to cubic order (strong-coupling length
$\sqrt{\ell_{\rm Pl}c^2/a_0}\simeq0.13$ mm).

\section{Exact static weak-field system}
With $N=1+\Phi/c^2$, $h_{ij}=(1-2\Psi/c^2)\delta_{ij}$, the invariants split by
potential: $X=|\nabla\Phi|^2/a_0^2$ (lapse only) and
$Y=(c^4/a_0^4)S_{ij}[\Psi]S_{ij}[\Psi]$ (curvature potential only), with
$S_{ij}[f]=\partial_i\partial_jf-\tfrac13\delta_{ij}\nabla^2f$.  The exact variation gives
\begin{align}
\text{(I)}\quad &\nabla^2\Psi+\nabla\!\cdot\!\Big[\big(F_X-\tfrac{\eta_K}{2}
+\varepsilon A'(X)Y\big)\nabla\Phi\Big]=4\pi G\rho,\\
\text{(II)}\quad &\nabla^2(\Psi-\Phi)=-\frac{\varepsilon c^4}{a_0^2}\,
\partial_i\partial_j\big[A(X)\,S_{ij}[\Psi]\big],\qquad A(X)=\frac{X^2}{(1+X)^4}.
\end{align}
Matter sources only (I).  Eliminating $\Psi$ reproduces the single-potential MOND
equation exactly at $O(\varepsilon)$ iff $\eta_K=0$; any $\eta_K\neq0$ drives the
static kernel to $-\eta_K/2$ as $x\to0$ and destroys deep MOND.  Independently, the
perturbative analysis at finite $X_0$ shows $\eta_K=0$ is the unique gradient-stable
choice ($\eta_K>0$ reproduces the Blanchet--Skordis-type low-$k$ instability;
$\eta_K<0$ is Newtonian-unstable).  Deep MOND and $v_\infty^4=GMa_0$ (coefficient 1)
follow; the $Y$-sector alters neither asymptote.

\section{Consistency results}
Degrees of freedom: $\pi^{ij}$ is unmodified by $F$; the lapse equation is elliptic
(second-class pair), leaving $2$ tensor $+$ $1$ khronon mode; the $\bar{\mathcal R}^2$
term adds spatial derivatives only.  No-ghost requires $\lambda_K>1$; FLRW gives
$G_{\rm cosmo}=2G/(3\lambda_K-1)$, pushing $\lambda_K\to1$.  $c_T=c$ exactly around
$X=0$, and the $\mu$-sector cannot shift $c_T$ on any background ($X$ carries $h_{ij}$
undifferentiated).  $\gamma_{\rm PPN}=1$ exactly at $\varepsilon=0$ for every $X$;
the $O(\varepsilon)$ slip is $\gamma-1\sim-\tfrac{16}{5}\chi x^{-3}$, negligible at
Saturn.  $\alpha_{1,2}$ reproduce the khronometric values.  On FLRW, $X=Y=0$: $a_0$ is
an input, and the action supplies no dark energy.

\section{The Solar-System response and the gravitational-wave no-go}
In units $GM=a_0=1$ the exact AQUAL background ($\varepsilon=0$, $\mu=x/(1+x)$)
reproduces the Blanchet--Novak quadrupole to $1.9\%$.  The linear tidal response gives
\begin{equation}
\frac{dq_{zz}}{d\chi}\Big|_0=+0.304\ (\eta_e=1.583),\qquad +0.413\ (\eta_e=1.852),
\qquad \chi\equiv\frac{\varepsilon c^4}{GMa_0},
\end{equation}
against $q_{zz}(0)=-0.226,\,-0.259$: the quadrupole is \emph{suppressed}, crossing the
Cassini band for $\chi\in[0.48,0.91]$ --- inside the elliptic domain ($\chi\lesssim1.7$)
--- i.e.\ $\varepsilon\sim1.1\times10^{-24}$, an emergent scale
$M_*=\varepsilon c^4/(Ga_0)\sim0.6\,M_\odot$.  But the tensor dispersion
$\omega^2=c^2k^2[1+2\varepsilon A(X_0)(kc^2/a_0)^2]$ with
$(kc^2/a_0)^2\simeq4\times10^{42}$ at $100$ Hz and $A\simeq0.03$ along the Galactic path
segment gives, from GW170817, $\varepsilon\lesssim5\times10^{-54}$.
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\ \varepsilon_{\rm needed}\sim10^{-24}\ \gg\ \varepsilon_{\rm allowed}
\lesssim5\times10^{-54}:\ \text{the }\bar{\mathcal R}^2\text{ tidal sector is excluded.}\ }
\end{equation}
The excluded object is specifically the \emph{spatial-curvature} form of the tidal
invariant.  The lapse-built alternative
$Y_a=(c^8/a_0^4)D_{\langle\mu}a_{\nu\rangle}D^{\langle\mu}a^{\nu\rangle}$ is
tensor-silent, preserves $\gamma_{\rm PPN}=1$, and has the identical static response at
$O(\varepsilon)$; it defines the next candidate and requires its own full program.

\section{Conclusions}
The frozen action is a mathematically healthy realisation of khronometric MOND with an
elegant $C^1$ constitutive function, but its genuinely new sector is forced inert by
GW170817, and with it the first-order Cassini reconciliation.  What survives is sharp:
the two-potential weak-field structure, the double derivation of $\eta_K=0$, the exact
$\gamma_{\rm PPN}=1$, the validated suppression mechanism as a \emph{static} statement,
and a precisely identified repair.  $a_0$ remains underived, and $\varepsilon$
unprotected; neither claim is made.
