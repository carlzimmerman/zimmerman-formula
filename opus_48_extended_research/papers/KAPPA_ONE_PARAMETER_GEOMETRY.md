## Abstract

The de Sitter–MOND framework writes the galactic acceleration scale as $a_0 = c^2\sqrt{\Lambda/32\pi} = \kappa\,c\sqrt{G\rho_{\rm DE}}$,
and the *form* $a_0\propto c^2\sqrt{\Lambda}$ together with the gravitational kernel $\sqrt{8\pi/3}$ (including the
half-integer power of $\pi$) are forced. We ask whether the single residual coefficient $\kappa=\tfrac12$ is forced or
free, and prove a sharp result: **$\kappa$ is the framework's lone free number, and it is provably unforceable** by the
three standard consistency conditions — ghost-freedom, unitarity, and a holographic / degree-of-freedom bound — each
failing for a structurally distinct, machine-verified reason. We then identify what $\kappa$ *is*: the framework
coefficient $\sqrt{2/Z}=(3/8\pi)^{1/4}=0.58779$ (with $Z=\sqrt{32\pi/3}$) is **pure geometry**, carrying no degree-of-freedom,
thermal, or entropy content, and it equals exactly the **single-degree-of-freedom ($g_*=1$) limit of the
Cohen–Kaplan–Nelson (CKN) UV–IR bound** — the geometric identity $3/8\pi = (\text{Schwarzschild }\tfrac12)/(\text{ball }\tfrac{4\pi}{3})$.
An honest count of the Standard Model's degrees of freedom ($g_*=106.75$) gives a CKN coefficient of \ensuremath{\sim}0.18–0.41,
never the framework value, so $\kappa$ is **geometric, not microscopic**. The framework is therefore a *provably*
one-parameter theory of gravity and the dark sector — one knob, irreducible and geometric — versus $\Lambda$CDM's six
parameters plus an undetected particle. We state plainly what this does and does not establish: it fixes the parameter
economy and the irreducibility of the lone number; it does **not** derive $a_0$'s value, make the sector zero-parameter,
or confirm the framework against $\Lambda$CDM. This is a structural theorem about an effective theory at a frontier — not
a theory of everything yet, as frustrating as it may be.

## 1. The question

A modified-dynamics account of galaxy rotation organizes everything around one acceleration, $a_0 \approx 1.2\times10^{-10}\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$.
The de Sitter–MOND framework proposes that this scale is set by the cosmological constant,
$$a_0 = c^2\sqrt{\frac{\Lambda}{32\pi}} = \frac{c}{2}\sqrt{G\rho_{\rm DE}} = \frac{cH_\Lambda}{Z}, \qquad Z=\sqrt{\tfrac{32\pi}{3}}=5.789,$$
evaluated on the dark-energy density alone, giving $a_0 = 9.36\times10^{-11}\ \mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$. Prior work establishes
two facts we take as given. First, the *form* $a_0\propto c^2\sqrt{\Lambda}$ is over-determined — multiple independent
mechanisms force the de Sitter–Unruh interpolation and the $\sqrt\Lambda$ scaling. Second, the **gravitational kernel**
$\sqrt{8\pi/3}$, including the $\sqrt\pi$ that no curvature-free thermal route produces, is forced by the Einstein density
normalization. What remains is a single dimensionless coefficient. Writing
$$a_0 = \kappa\,c\sqrt{G\rho_{\rm DE}}, \qquad \rho_{\rm DE}=\frac{\Lambda c^2}{8\pi G},$$
the framework's value corresponds to $\kappa=\tfrac12$: solving $a_0=\kappa c\sqrt{G\rho_{\rm DE}}=c^2\sqrt{\Lambda/32\pi}$
gives $\kappa=\tfrac12$ exactly. The question of this paper is sharp: **is $\kappa$ forced, or is it the theory's one free
number?**

## 2. The scale–fraction split

$\kappa$ is the multiplicative coefficient *outside* the root, while the $\sqrt{8\pi}$ that fixes its value lives *inside*
the Einstein density $\rho_{\rm DE}=\Lambda c^2/8\pi G$. Any condition that lives on the de Sitter–Unruh temperature
spectrum $W(a)=\sqrt{a^2+(cH_\Lambda)^2}$ depends only on the dimensionless ratio $a/cH_\Lambda$ — the *scale*, the
inside-root structure $Z$ — and can never reach the *outside fraction* $\kappa$, because the spectrum never sees the $8\pi$
density normalization. Symbolically, $a_0=\kappa\,c^2\sqrt{\Lambda}/\sqrt{8\pi}$ and $Z=2\sqrt6\,\sqrt\pi/(3\kappa)$, so at
$\kappa=\tfrac12$ one has $4/Z^2=3/8\pi$ and $Z=\sqrt{32\pi/3}$. This **scale–fraction split** is the structural reason a
forcing of $\kappa$ must come, if at all, from a condition on the *action normalization*, not the spectrum. We test the
three candidate conditions.

degree-1-homogeneous ($\phi_-$-linear) action; the equation of motion $N\,m(\ddot q_+ + \omega^2 q_+)=0$ divides cleanly by

interior ghost boundary singles out a value). Ghost-freedom cannot fix even the scale, let alone $\kappa$.

**(ii) Unitarity reaches only signs, causality, and ratios — never an absolute normalization.** Schwinger–Keldysh /

**(iii) Holography reaches only the scale $cH$ — never the fraction.** From $\{G,\hbar,c,H\}$ the *unique* $\hbar$-free

$\mathcal O(1)$ to the target is *choosing* $\kappa$, which is circular.

The needed number $1/Z=\sqrt{3/32\pi}=0.1727$ is built from the Friedmann $3$, the Einstein $8\pi$, and the free-fall

- **Credit.** The framework is a *provably* one-parameter theory of gravity and the dark sector: $\kappa=\tfrac12$ the lone

  **structural / consistency theorem about the framework's own parameter economy; it does not by itself confirm the
