\author{Carl P.\ Zimmerman}

\begin{abstract}
We construct a bimetric completion of MOND phenomenology carrying the acceleration-scale
identity $a_0^2=\kappa^2G(-K(\QQ))$ --- the MOND scale as the dark sector's pressure --- and
push it through every consistency gate our previously published filter
[10.5281/zenodo.22004372, 10.5281/zenodo.22015358] can pose. The construction: two
Einstein--Hilbert sectors; a shift-symmetric khronon $\phi$ whose $\beta=1$ DBI kernel supplies
dark energy ($w=-1$ exactly), a cold dust component, and a derived $a_0(z)$; and an interaction
built from the khronon-projected connection difference
$C_M{}^i{}_{jk}=h^i{}_\mu h_j{}^\nu h_k{}^\rho\,C^\mu{}_{\nu\rho}$, entering as
$V=\sqrt{h}\,[N F(X)+\hat N B(X)]$ with $X$ the projected quadratic scalar normalised by
$a_0^2(\QQ)$. Results, each with a committed self-checking script: \textbf{(1)} after the
Hassan--Rosen-type shift redefinition $u^i=(N^i-\hat N^i)/\hat N$, $C_M$ is exactly lapse-free,
and the interaction's lapse Hessian vanishes identically --- all four entries. \textbf{(2)} The
quasi-static reduction of a bimetric theory is a two-potential system whose force law is a
M\"obius map in $F'$, not AQUAL; inverting it for the $a_0$-line
$g_{\rm obs}^2=g_{\rm bar}^2+a_0g_{\rm bar}$ yields the required interaction slope in closed
form, $\hat\alpha F'=(1-R)/(R(1+r)-1)$ with $R=(\sqrt{1+4x^2}+1)/2x$, and it is single-valued
and monotone: the legality condition that excludes the same phenomenology in
aether--scalar--tensor theory is satisfied here. The construction's free content is one function
fixed by the $a_0$-line plus one number, $r=M_f^2/M_g^2$. \textbf{(3)} The sign that makes
gravity stronger rather than weaker is derived, not chosen: calibrating the Einstein--Hilbert
quadratic coefficient against the Newtonian limit ($M^2=1/8\pi G$ recovered exactly) forces the
\emph{mixed} contraction $C^i{}_{jk}C^j{}_{ik}=-|\nabla\psi|^2$. \textbf{(4)} Both lapses appear
linearly in the Hamiltonian; $u^i$ is determined algebraically, absorbing one momentum
constraint; the degree-of-freedom count is $7=2+5$, the ghost-free bimetric number, conditional
on one bracket. \textbf{(5)} That bracket is tested: $\{F(X),B(X)\}=0$ identically (two
functions of the same phase-space scalar), collapsing $\{C,\hat C\}$ to two cross terms; and on
an explicit lattice representative, with every constraint imposed at every site and both
controls passing exactly, the on-shell bracket is \emph{nonzero} at a generic real point.
First-classness is universal, so the ghost-propagating alternative is refuted on the
representative: the pair is second class and the Boulware--Deser mode is removed there.
\textbf{Not claimed:} closure. The exact kinetic reduction, the continuum statement, and full
Dirac consistency remain, and are listed. Every prior error made in this programme --- six,
roughly half manufactured deficits and half manufactured wins --- is recorded, dated, with
directions stated, in the repository's retraction log.
\end{abstract}

\section{The construction}

The action:
\begin{align}
S&=\frac{M_g^2}{2}\!\int\!\sqrt{-g}\,R[g]+\frac{M_f^2}{2}\!\int\!\sqrt{-\hat g}\,\hat R[\hat g]
+S_\phi[\phi,g]\nonumber\\
&\quad-m^2M_{\rm eff}^2\!\int\! N\sqrt{h}\Big[F(X)+\tfrac{\hat N}{N}B(X)\Big]+S_{\rm m}[g,\Psi],
\label{eq:action}
\end{align}
with $1/M_{\rm eff}^2=1/M_g^2+1/M_f^2$ and matter coupled to $g_{\mu\nu}$ alone. The khronon
sector $S_\phi$ carries $\QQ=\sqrt{-g^{\mu\nu}\partial_\mu\phi\partial_\nu\phi}$ and the
$\beta=1$ DBI kernel $K(\QQ)=-\rho_\Lambda c^2\sqrt{1-(\QQ-\QQ_0)^2/\Lambda_D^2}$, giving
$w=-1$ exactly at the minimum, dust excitations carrying the dark abundance as a conserved
shift charge, and --- via the promotion
\begin{equation}
a_0^2(\QQ)=\kappa^2G\,(-K(\QQ)),
\label{eq:promotion}
\end{equation}
the identity that is this programme's contribution --- the derived evolution
$a_0(a)/a_0(0)=(1+\sigma^2)^{-1/4}$, $\sigma\propto\nu_0/a^3$. At the minimum
$a_0=\kappa c\sqrt{G\rho_\Lambda}=9.3619\times10^{-11}\,{\rm m\,s^{-2}}$ (alternative footing
$1.1279\times10^{-10}$; both carried throughout). $\kappa=\tfrac12$ is \emph{fitted},
$0.529\pm0.034$, never derived. The $a_0$--$\Lambda$ \emph{coincidence} is prior art
\cite{M99,BLT,Pik,KK}; the identity \eqref{eq:promotion} and its consequences are not.

The interaction's scalar is built from the relative connection
$C^\mu{}_{\nu\rho}=\Gamma^\mu{}_{\nu\rho}[g]-\hat\Gamma^\mu{}_{\nu\rho}[\hat g]$ (a tensor,
as in bimetric MOND \cite{BIMOND}), projected fully spatially with the khronon's foliation
$n_\mu=\partial_\mu\phi/\sqrt{-(\partial\phi)^2}$, $h_{\mu\nu}=g_{\mu\nu}+n_\mu n_\nu$:
$X$ is the \emph{mixed} quadratic contraction of $C_M$, normalised by $a_0^2(\QQ)$. The
foliation is a dynamical field, not a gauge choice; full diffeomorphism invariance is kept.

\section{Five results}

\emph{1. Lapse-freeness.} In unitary gauge, the physical lapse $N$ cancels from $C_M$
identically at the projection stage, and the residual $\hat K_{jk}(\hat N^i-N^i)/\hat N$
is absorbed by the invertible redefinition $u^i=(N^i-\hat N^i)/\hat N$:
\begin{equation}
C_M{}^i{}_{jk}=\big({}^{3}\Gamma^i{}_{jk}-{}^{3}\hat\Gamma^i{}_{jk}\big)-\hat K_{jk}u^i,
\end{equation}
free of both lapses. The interaction's lapse Hessian then vanishes \emph{identically} --- all
four entries, not a diagonal zero (twice in this programme a diagonal zero was mistaken for
degeneracy; $\det H=-(\text{mixed})^2$ punishes that mistake).

\emph{2. The force law, and the function it fixes.} Matter couples to $g$ alone, so the hatted
potential equation is sourceless and the quasi-static reduction is a M\"obius map,
$g_{\rm obs}/g_{\rm bar}=[1+\hat\alpha F']/[1+(1+r)\hat\alpha F']$, with
$\alpha/\hat\alpha=M_f^2/M_g^2\equiv r$ exactly (each sector divides the same interaction flux
by its own Planck mass). Inverting for the $a_0$-line gives, in closed form,
\begin{equation}
\hat\alpha F'(x)=\frac{1-R}{R(1+r)-1},\qquad R(x)=\frac{\sqrt{1+4x^2}+1}{2x},
\label{eq:Ax}
\end{equation}
$x=g_{\rm obs}/a_0$. It is negative-definite, monotone, and single-valued in $X$; it vanishes
in the Newtonian limit (the interaction switches itself off --- solar-system screening from the
required force law, not from a kernel choice) and tends to $-1/(1+r)$ in deep MOND. Only the
product $\hat\alpha F'$ is observable, so the interaction strength is degenerate with $F$'s
normalisation: the construction's free content is Eq.~\eqref{eq:Ax} plus the single number $r$.

\emph{3. The sign, calibrated.} Computing $\sqrt{-g}R$ at quadratic order and matching the
Newtonian limit recovers $M^2=1/8\pi G$ exactly; carrying that calibration through the
variation gives ${\rm sign}(\alpha)={\rm sign}(c)$ for contraction coefficient $c$, and MOND
(enhancement, not weakening) requires $\alpha<0$: the mixed contraction
$C^i{}_{jk}C^j{}_{ik}=-|\nabla\psi|^2$ is forced. No negative coupling is inserted by hand.
(The sign was asserted wrongly once and reversed once before being calibrated; both are in the
retraction log. The lesson --- a coefficient asserted is a coefficient wrong --- is now a
standing rule.)

\emph{4. Constraint structure and the count.} Substituting the redefinition, both lapses appear
linearly: $H=\int N\,C+\hat N\,\hat C+\hat N^i(\mathcal H_i+\hat{\mathcal H}_i)$ with
$C=\mathcal H_{\rm EH}+\sqrt hF(X)$ and $\hat C=\hat{\mathcal H}_{\rm EH}+\sqrt hB(X)+u^i\mathcal
H_i$. The relative shift $u^i$ is determined algebraically (its equation is linear), absorbing
one momentum constraint --- the Hassan--Rosen shape \cite{HR}. Counting: $24-6-2-2=14$ phase-space
dimensions, i.e.\ $7=2+5$ degrees of freedom, the ghost-free bimetric number --- \emph{provided}
the orthogonal combination of $(C,\hat C)$ is second class. If instead both were first class the
count is $8$, and the eighth is the Boulware--Deser ghost.

\emph{5. The ghost test.} Two of the four terms of $\{C,\hat C\}$ vanish identically: the two
Einstein--Hilbert constraints commute (disjoint pairs), and $\{F(X),B(X)\}=0$ for \emph{any}
$X$ --- two functions of the same phase-space scalar Poisson-commute, a one-line theorem that
survived the withdrawal of its own earlier, wrong proof. The two survivors are cross terms,
each one sector's Einstein--Hilbert constraint against the other sector's interaction piece. On
an explicit representative --- anisotropic minisuperspace on a three-site lattice, exact
rational arithmetic --- with \emph{every} constraint imposed at \emph{every} site and the
surface reached through the free function's own values (which enter linearly; no branch
ambiguity; all nine remaining momenta generic reals), the on-shell bracket evaluates to
$-7.35\neq0$. Both controls pass exactly: interaction off $\Rightarrow$ bracket $\equiv0$;
$F,B$ constant $\Rightarrow$ bracket $\equiv0$. Since first-classness must hold at every
phase-space point, one generic point of failure refutes it: \emph{on the representative the
pair is second class, the Boulware--Deser mode is removed, and the count of five results
stands at $7$.}

\section{What remains, and what is claimed}

\emph{Discharged since the ghost test:} the exact ADM kinetic reduction. Deriving
$H_{\rm kin}=-p_ap_b/4b+ap_a^2/8b^2$ by exact Legendre transform (no $p_b^2$ term --- the
declared form had one) and the exact $\sqrt hR^{(3)}$ potential, the on-shell bracket remains
nonzero: the verdict does not rest on the declared coefficients. \emph{Also discharged, by its own control:} the representative's full-rank multiplier
matrix. Running the identical test on pure GR --- known first class in the continuum --- gives
nonzero on-shell brackets of rank 2 at magnitudes within a factor of four of the interacting
theory's, and both the control's and the theory's obstructions change under a boundary-stencil
swap (open to periodic) alone. A test that freezes Einstein gravity's own lapse measures the
discretisation; the frozen-lapse finding is therefore \emph{uninformative on the
representative}, not adverse. The asymmetry with the ghost verdict is principled: that verdict
rests on the bracket being \emph{nonzero}, whose control (interaction off $\Rightarrow$ bracket
exactly zero) passed on the same lattice, artifact-free. \emph{Remaining, one theorem-grade
item:} the continuum Dirac analysis --- general second-classness of the orthogonal combination
and termination of the algorithm, in the continuum where the derivative-of-delta closure the
lattice breaks is restored. \emph{Remaining on the physics fronts, unchanged:} a Boltzmann
computation for the coupled system; lensing $\Phi+\Psi$ in the combined weak-field limit;
clusters; and whether the dark sector's dust remains bound in galaxies.

\emph{Observational shape carried over:} the derived $a_0(z)$ predicts an essentially static
baryonic Tully--Fisher normalisation across $0<z\lesssim20$ (velocity shifts of
$10^{-9}$--$10^{-6}$ at $z=1$--$5$) followed by a cliff (1\% at $z\approx26$, 10\% at
$z\approx48$) --- a shape no constant-$a_0$ or smoothly-evolving-$a_0$ model has, and a
two-sided one: a robust per-cent-level detection of $a_0$ evolution below $z\sim5$ would
require $\nu_0$ three to four orders of magnitude above its independent ceilings and falsifies
the law outright.

\emph{Claimed:} the construction; the five results; and that this is the first candidate in
this programme --- after a published three-condition filter and the recorded deaths of every
predecessor --- to pass every gate it has faced, including the ghost test that killed the rest.
\emph{Not claimed:} a closed theory; a derived $\kappa$; or the absence of dark matter --- the
dark abundance is present in full as a field's conserved charge, and both the $w=-1$ result and
the CMB depend on it.
