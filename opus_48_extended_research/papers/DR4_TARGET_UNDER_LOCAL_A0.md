## Abstract

The framework built on a₀ = κc√(Gρ_Λ) = 9.3619×10⁻¹¹ m/s² carries a hash-frozen,
pre-registered Gaia DR4 wide-binary target (Amendment 10: velocity-boost band
γ_v = 1.1614–1.1814 canonical / 1.1917–1.2267 alternate footing). This note reports two
refereed results *about* that registration — the registration itself is untouched.

**1. A conditional charge meter.** In this framework a₀ is a local quantity — 𝒜(𝒬) = a₀² ∝
−𝒦(𝒬) depends on the local shift-charge density — and the operative (steady-state-continuity)
reading of the charge a Milky-Way-class flow carries at the solar circle suppresses the local
a₀ by an amount governed by the framework's one free dimensionless charge ν₀ ∈
[2.14×10⁻⁵, 1.77×10⁻⁴]. Propagated to the registered asymptote: at the ν₀ **floor** the target
moves ≤0.006 at every corner of the solar-overdensity bracket and both footings — inside the
frozen bands' own widths. At the **ceiling** the move is corner-split: −0.004 (sparse corner)
to −0.093 canonical / −0.109 alternate (dense corner, ~5× the band width). **DR4 therefore
functions as a ν₀-meter only conditionally** — decisive if the solar-circle charge overdensity
sits toward the dense (abundance-matched, fast-assembly) corner, silent at the sparse corner.
Settling that overdensity is the named prerequisite.

**2. The EFE-present exact two-body solve.** The registered band's top was built by applying an
*EFE-free* exact two-body benchmark (finite-y factors 1.41–1.74) to the *EFE per-star*
response. We solve the full nonlinear AQUAL pair in a uniform external field (colinear,
axisymmetric; machinery gates: a μ ≡ 1 run returns B = 1.002, and the small-mass limit
returns 1.450 vs the closed-form linear 1.4732). Result: the exact pair boost is
**0.71–0.99×** the linear per-star value at 5–30 kAU, rising monotonically toward it — the
EFE-free benchmark correction **overcorrects with the external field on**. Both directions
stated: the registered band top is *conservative* (a >1.23 no-verdict becomes less likely),
**and** the same suppression puts the colinear per-pair signal √B = 1.03–1.21 at or below the
frozen floor 1.1614 over most anchor separations — a DR4 reading *below* the registered band
becomes more likely as well.

## 1. The frozen registration

force: γ_v = 1.1614–1.1814 (canonical footing) / 1.1917–1.2267 (alternate), no-verdict edge

## 2. Why the target could move at all: a₀ is local, and the drain reading is now settled

The framework's a₀ is set by the dark sector's pressure at the local charge density. A halo
that *kept* its captured charge would suppress the local a₀ fatally (that branch is dead
independently); a draining halo carries only the in-transit flow. Two readings of that flow's
density coexisted in the corpus — a residence-time estimate and steady-state continuity,
disagreeing by 16–119×. That fork is now settled: **for the committed picture (smooth capture,
cold radial infall, no halt), mass conservation makes ρ = Ṁ/(4πr²v) exact; the residence
estimate was the same argument with an inconsistent virialised shape.** Continuity is
operative. Its solar-circle overdensity, re-derived from committed anchors both ways, brackets
1.5×10³–1.6×10⁴ (mean-charge units) — the dense corner being abundance-matched halo mass with
standard fast assembly, the sparse corner baryon-scaled mass accreted over a full Hubble time.

## 4. The EFE-present exact two-body solve

The linearized AQUAL operator about the external background, μ₀[∇² + L₀(ê·∇)²]φ = 4πGρ
(μ₀ = 1/ν₀, L₀ = d ln μ/d ln x = 0.3674 canonical), has a closed-form anisotropic Green's
function; the pair force is exact at O(ε): B(θ) = ν₀/√(1+L₀sin²θ), spanning 1.2598 (⊥) to
1.4732 (∥) — a 17% orientation spread. The full nonlinear colinear solve (cylindrical grid,
Route A kernel, observed-x boundary condition with the linear pair far field) is gated by two
machinery checks — μ ≡ 1 returns B = 1.002; the small-mass limit returns 1.450 vs 1.4732 —
and gives, for equal solar masses:

| s [kAU] | y_int | B_exact | B_exact/B_∥ | √B_exact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | 2.53 | 1.051 | 0.713 | 1.025 |
| 10 | 0.63 | 1.183 | 0.803 | 1.088 |
| 20 | 0.16 | 1.345 | 0.913 | 1.160 |
| 30 | 0.07 | 1.454 | 0.987 | 1.206 |

The exact pair sits *below* the linear per-star value everywhere and approaches it
monotonically from below — the external field suppresses the mutual deep-MOND enhancement
that the EFE-free benchmark (finite-y factors 1.41–1.74) was built from. Consequences, both
directions: the registered band **top** (which applied that benchmark multiplicatively) is
conservative, so a >1.23 no-verdict is *less* likely; and the per-pair √B sits at or below
the registered **floor** 1.1614 for s ≤ 20 kAU, so a DR4 reading below the band is *more*
likely. Box-size, grid, and boundary sensitivities are 0.2–1.6% (checked); the perpendicular
nonlinear case needs 3D and is carried as the stage's stated approximation (its *linear*
value is exact above); the alternate-footing tensor (ν₀ = 1.5840, L₀ = 0.4112) is on record,
the nonlinear table being canonical-footing.

## 5. What this does not establish

- These are **asymptote-level and per-pair numbers**, not the composed band observable: the
  registration's composition grades (population synthesis, orientation statistics, mass
  spectrum) shift both results by the same benchmark factors as the amendment itself. The
  *directions* are the findings.
- The ν₀-meter is **conditional on the solar-overdensity corner**, and its favourable reading
  (a new instrument) rests on the dense corner; the sparse corner shows nothing. Both are in
  the table.
- The two-body result is **colinear-exact only**; the perpendicular nonlinear correction is
  plausibly also ≤1 (the suppression mechanism is orientation-blind) but unproven here.
- Nothing here modifies the frozen registration, its hash chain, or the in-force band.
