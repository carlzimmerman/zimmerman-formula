# Finite wavelength discriminator

Continue the exact ADM calculation on an explicit expanding canonical
clock + canonical matter scalar + constant positive V background. This is
stiff scalar matter (sound speed1), not dust or realistic late cosmology.
At observation a=H=Mpl=1, epsilon_phi=.05,.5,2 and epsilon_m=.01,.3,.9;
V=3-epsilon_phi-epsilon_m>0. Both scalar velocities obey vdot=-3Hv.
Lstar=.001,1,100 and k_phys/H=10,100,10000.81 cases.

Compute full K, symmetric-integrated stiffness G and antisymmetric mixing A,
retaining all background derivatives. Frozen frequencies solve
det(G-omega² K)-omega² A12²=0. This local frozen-frequency calculation
is trustworthy for propagation only when |omega| >> H and coefficients vary
slowly; negative mass-scale omega² alone is not a UV gradient instability.
Record both roots and their H ratios, including every negative value.
Use40/80 digits, normalized coefficient and root agreement <1e-25.

omega/H values .1,1,10,100. No modified threshold to obtain a stability pass.
