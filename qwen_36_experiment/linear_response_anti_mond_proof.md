$$ \delta\langle\hat{O}(t)\rangle = \int_{-\infty}^{t} K(t-t') J(t') dt' $$

$$ K(t) = \mathcal{F}^{-1}[\chi_R(\omega)] = \frac{1}{2\pi} \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} d\omega\, e^{-i\omega t} \chi_R(\omega) $$

$$ \chi_R(t) = i \theta(t) \langle [\hat{O}(t), \hat{O}(0)] \rangle_{\text{dS}} $$

$$ \delta m = \frac{2}{\pi} \mathcal{P} \int_0^\infty \frac{\rho(\omega)}{\omega^2} d\omega $$

To produce MOND (a lowering of inertia, $\delta m < 0$), the spectral density must possess a region of **negative weight** (an inverted population, $\rho(\omega) < 0$).
