## The door
`mu_eff = 1 + rho/2 > 0` requires `rho > -2`, and tn18 already sits at **80% of that bound**. But deeper
negative bands are exactly what a stronger MOND regime needs. So positivity and deep MOND are in direct
competition, and the trade can be computed. Either it yields a quotable falsifiable bound, or it is a no-go:
ghost-freedom and deep MOND mutually exclusive.

## Why it works with the framework
It converts an abstract consistency condition into a number about galaxies — the smallest `y` the mechanism can
represent — which is directly comparable to data the framework already fits.

## Concrete first calculation
1. Maximise `|delta_m/m_0|` subject to `rho > -2` (and to A5's complete-positivity bound if that door is done).
2. Convert to the smallest representable `y = g_bar/a_0`.
3. Compare to SPARC's range (the deep anchor is `y ~ 0.06`, `nu ~ 3.97`) and to dwarf spheroidals, which probe
   deeper still.

## Settles if / refuted if

incompatible in this mechanism. Clean, publishable, and it costs one script.

mechanism must break down at even lower acceleration.
