## The door
Couple matter not to `g_mu_nu` but to an effective metric `g_eff = g + F(phi, grad phi)`, so the MI correction
comes from the coupling rather than from modifying Einstein gravity. This sits on the corpus's **own open-escape
list** from 2026-08-01, after three no-goes closed the direct-action route for the generic form class. It is
explicitly listed as not-yet-closed ("disformal rho_m" is an open item).

## Why it works with the framework
Einstein-Hilbert stays untouched, so solar-system tests, binary pulsars and GW propagation are safe until the
new sector does something. And the framework is modified **inertia**, which is naturally a statement about how
matter couples — this is arguably its most natural covariant home.

## Concrete first calculation
1. Write the most general disformal `g_eff = A(phi, X) g_mu_nu + B(phi, X) d_mu phi d_nu phi` with
   `X = (grad phi)^2`.
2. Derive the matter equation of motion on this metric for a test particle.
3. Ask: in the weak-field, slow-motion limit, can it reproduce `g_obs^2 = g_bar^2 + a_0 g_bar` — and if so,
   what does it force A and B to be?
4. Check for superluminality: the effective light cone of `g_eff` versus `g`. This is where disformal models
   usually die, so test it early.

## Settles if / refuted if
CONFIRMS: an admissible (A, B) reproducing the a0-line with subluminal propagation ⇒ a covariant home for the
framework's exact law.
KILLS: superluminality or a ghost for every (A, B) that reproduces the a0-line ⇒ closes the last listed escape
from the 2026-08-01 no-goes, which is a significant negative result.
