coincidence is real: `Sigma_a0 = 107 Msun/pc^2` crossing `log M_* = 9.6-10.2` puts the step at 10. But the
decisive tests are **underpowered, not null**: only **18% power** at the observed 0.06 mag (needs D = 0.14 mag
or N ~ 2505 against 449).

a_0-footing-blind — which is unusual and useful.
