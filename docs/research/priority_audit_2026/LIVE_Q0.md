# Q₀ pin: project-specific conditional inference, not a confirmed physical bound

2026-09-09. Scope: `opus_48_extended_research/papers/PINNING_Q0_IN_AEST.md` (August 13, v4 August 14), its Stage 58 script, and later `RETRACTIONS.md` entries. Literature priority, not a full rerun/referee audit.

## Exact claim worth separating

The repository combines

\[
X=\sqrt y\,c/v,\quad
y=[(g_{\rm tot}-g_N)/a_0]^2,\quad
Q_0=Xa_0/c^2
\]

to obtain, for positive excess acceleration and speed,

\[
Q_0=\frac{g_{\rm tot}-g_N}{cv}.
\]

The cancellation of explicit \(a_0\) is valid algebra. The substantive added assumption is that the scalar gradient producing the MOND force also fixes the adopted radial accretion/drain speed through the paper's scalar-flow identification. That assumption is not a measured velocity constraint or a general consequence of AeST. The paper's \(2.4\times10^{-3}\)–\(1.5\times10^{-2}\,\mathrm{Mpc}^{-1}\) band uses its particular flow and kernels; it acknowledges radial drift by factors 2.1–2.7. Thus this is a **candidate conditional parameter inference**, not a new universal field equation.

## Original AeST versus this addition

[Skordis–Złośnik's primary paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2007.00082v3), first public [June 30, 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.00082), gives the quasi-static decomposition \(\Phi=\widehat\Phi+\varphi\), mass combination \(\mu_H^2=2K_2Q_0^2/(2-K_B)\), and free cosmological initial charge. It does not contain this drain-speed equality or numerical Q₀ band. Its parameter sets have \(Q_0=0.1,10^{-4},1\,\mathrm{Mpc}^{-1}\), not the proposed interval. These statements were checked in the action discussion and Figure 1.

Consequently “both MOND-compatible fits lie inside the band” applies only to the note's **one-dimensional \(\sqrt{K''}\) comparison**, not their actual Q₀ values or complete parameter sets. Also, the note's abstract says \(K''\sim10^2\)–\(10^3\) is equivalent to its band; its own \(\sqrt{K''}=33\)–1295 instead gives \(K''=1089\)–1,677,025. Its quoted fit values 122 and 195 correspond to roughly 15,000 and 38,000. This inconsistency is reported, not silently corrected in the manuscript.

## Current survival status

The motivating abundance relation is conditional on full dark-matter charge, \(\beta=1\), and the selected dark-energy normalization. `RETRACTIONS.md:1035` subsequently excludes that single-field cosmology: the same commitments fix \(\Lambda_D/Q_0=\nu_0\Omega_\Lambda/\Omega_{\rm dm}\), conflicting with the repository's growth constraints. Earlier constant/evolving-dark-energy claims also receive explicit restrictions at `RETRACTIONS.md:1010`.

**Distinction:** an excluded host does not invalidate the displayed cancellation or erase priority in proposing the conditional estimate. It does prevent counting its physical band as an established surviving prediction without replacing and validating the failed premises. Preserving it as a conditional calculation is appropriate; describing it as a measured constraint on general AeST is not.
