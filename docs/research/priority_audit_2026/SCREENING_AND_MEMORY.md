## 1. Sanitha Elit: close screened-action overlap

Primary record: [SSRN 7246800](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7246800), DOI [10.2139/ssrn.7246800](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7246800), *The Solar-Galactic External-Field Boundary Problem in Hybrid 5D Gravity*. SSRN's indexed author abstract gives date written 7 August 2026 and posting 12 August. [PhilPapers version history](https://philpapers.org/versions/ELITSE) lists uploads on 7 and 8 August. Historical PDFs were not retrievable. The [author manuscript mirrored on ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/411853743_The_Solar-Galactic_External-Field_Boundary_Problem_in_Hybrid_5D_Gravity) was readable.

The current manuscript, §§8.2–8.5, introduces a spatial Helmholtz filter \(W=(1-L_s^2\Delta_u)^{-1}\), \(\Phi_f=W\Phi\). Its action gives

\[
\Delta\Phi-W\nabla\cdot[(1-\mu(|\nabla\Phi_f|/a_0))\nabla\Phi_f]=4\pi G_4\rho.
\]

It uses \(\mu(x)=x/(1+x)\), a \(cH_0/(2\pi)\) benchmark and two elliptic auxiliaries. The available text contains both an open Hamiltonian-count sentence and later closure claims; those claims were not independently certified here. Crucially, current mirrored content cannot all be backdated to the first upload. No “Zimmerman” match was found in the extracted manuscript.

**Comparison with Carl's files (our analysis).** This is close architectural prior work, not an exact duplicate. `PAPER4_filtered_action_2026.tex` instead filters a separately Poisson-sourced potential \(u\):

\[
\Delta u=4\pi G\rho,\qquad
\Delta\Phi=4\pi G\rho+S\nabla\cdot[(\nu(|\nabla Su|/a_0)-1)\nabla Su].
\]

The location of the filtered variable matters: filtered AQUAL and filtered QUMOND are not the same nonlinear boundary-value problem. Carl's exact inverse-exponential kernel, Gaussian option, central tidal ratio and comparable-mass computations require their own comparisons. Carl also had a different, unsuccessful spatial-filter/slip prototype locally on 13 June, before Elit's record; its public-disclosure date is not authenticated here. The broad shared idea must not be advertised as uniquely originating in September, but neither can all of Carl's spatial-filter work be declared later than Elit's. Local chronology is in [PROVENANCE.md](PROVENANCE.md).

**Classification:** close adjacent architecture; earliest exact-version priority partly unresolved; no evidence of copying established.

## 2. Geza Kovacs: curvature-memory relaxation

Primary [record](https://ai.vixra.org/abs/2604.0006) and [v1 manuscript](https://ai.vixra.org/pdf/2604.0006v1.pdf), *Gravity Imprints, Time Fades: A Curvature-Memory Relaxation Model with Exact MOND Steady State*. The record's citation metadata says 1 April 2026, while submission history says 2 April 2026 20:35:34; the manuscript itself says March. These are different date claims, not interchangeable priority timestamps.

The proposed relaxation equation is \(\dot s=H_0[y-s^2/(1-s)]\), with \(g_{\rm eff}=g_N+a_0s\). It approaches the simple MOND interpolation, not Carl's exponential law, and uses \(a_0=cH_0/(2\pi)\). It discusses formation history and lingering effective halo response. No “Zimmerman” match was found in the extracted PDF. This establishes public thematic overlap, not a complete relativistic theory or copying.

Carl's paddle/wake intuition can remain credited as his contribution to this project's direction. That is different from asserting first invention of gravitational memory. Earlier modified-inertia work is documented in [RELATIVISTIC_LITERATURE.md](RELATIVISTIC_LITERATURE.md).
