# Exact source checks, 2026-10-28

No full external source text is retained in this directory. These are bounded
application checks; no worldwide novelty claim or comprehensive review is made.

## Interacting-vacuum pressure and transfer

David Wands, Josue De-Santiago and Yuting Wang, *Inhomogeneous vacuum energy*,
[arXiv:1203.6776v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1203.6776v2), dated 16 April 2012.
The primary PDF was read directly. Section II equations (1), (5)-(8), and
Section III.B equations (17)-(21) define the vacuum stress, its gradient
transfer, and the corresponding matter momentum force. This verifies the
general obligation to include vacuum momentum transfer. The paper's Q has a
different sign convention from our scalar Q, which is energy into random
kinetic motion. Our use depends on the stress divergence itself, not a copied
scalar sign. It supplies neither our kinetic EOS nor its coefficient.

Overlap classification: known after translation of notation for the vacuum
pressure/force requirement; adjacent result only for our specialized closure.
Authentication, equation extraction and this application were checked.

## Recent logotropic diffusion correspondence

Miguel Cruz and Samuel Lepe, *The logotropic dark fluid as an energy diffusion
in unimodular gravity*, [arXiv:2609.21006v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.21006v1),
17 September 2026. The primary HTML was read directly, particularly Sections
II-III equations (2)-(13), IV.1 and V. The source fixes 8piG=c=1 and discusses
a homogeneous FLRW background.

It rewrites an assumed logotropic total fluid as pressureless matter plus a
geometric vacuum term, with Q_source=3A log(a_scale)+constant and
dot(Q_source)=3AH. Its pressureless matter density is the total enthalpy,
rho_source=rho_rest-A in its units, rather than our separately conserved
stream rest-mass density. The source's Q is an energy density; our Q is a
rate per volume. Its total logotropic pressure is not our positive random
kinetic pressure with K=3p/2. The source assumes the logarithmic diffusion
law to establish the dictionary and does not calculate A from a kinetic
parent or fix A/vacuum independently.

Application verdict: verified as a homogeneous dictionary; inapplicable as
a derivation of the current kinetic closure or amplitude. Overlap: adjacent
result only. A perturbative or halo transfer needs a specified covariant
current and constitutive map. Background conservation alone does not fix
entropy perturbations or the physical sound speed for a more general system.

The searches used the exact phrases 'interacting vacuum energy barotropic
adiabatic sound speed instability Wands De Santiago Wang 2012' and
'logotropic dark matter kinetic pressure vacuum energy exchange instability
sound speed'. They locate these sources; their scope is not sufficient for
an 'apparently new' classification of the specialized equations.
