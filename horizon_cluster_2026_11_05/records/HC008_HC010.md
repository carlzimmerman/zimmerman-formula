## Main correction: 16 catalog BH entries are not secure mass detections

Tracing the source chain reveals an important problem with treating all 85 entries as measured masses:

Gaspari 2019 Table 2 → van den Bosch 2016 Table 1, reference 14 → Beifiori et al. 2012 Table 1, upper-limit section.

The affected entries are N0315, N0383, N0541, N0741, N1052, N1667, N1961, N2110, N3801, N3862, N4036, N4203, N4278, N7331, N7626 and N4636. For every one, the catalog log mass matches the log geometric mean of two inclination-dependent upper limits to <0.011 dex, and the quoted error matches half their log range to <0.011 dex. An inclination range between upper limits is not a mass detection with a Gaussian measurement error. This is a numerical/source-chain finding, not a claim about the authors' intent or all later measurements of these galaxies.

Example NGC7331: the primary table gives 1.6e8 and 6.9e7 solar masses as upper limits for nuclear gas disc inclinations 33 and 81 degrees. Their geometric mean is about 1.05e8, reproducing the downstream log mass 8.02. The primary source explicitly separates upper limits (Sample A) from definite values (Sample B).

Sources authenticated from versioned public HTML:
- [van den Bosch 2016, v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1606.01246v2), Table 1 and reference 14.
- [Beifiori et al. 2012, v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1109.6265v2), Section 2 and Table 1.
- [Gaspari et al. 2019, v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1904.10972v2), Tables 2–3 and their notes.

Local raw HTML is retained but git-ignored. audit_bh_sources.py extracts the chain and verifies all 16 numerical matches; data/bh_upper_limit_audit.json contains exact primary rows and source SHA256 hashes. This is not yet a complete primary-source audit of every remaining object.

Earlier 85-object fits remain reproducible exploratory catalog calculations, but **must not be described as fits to 85 secure measured BH masses**. Their Gaussian-error interpretation is compromised by these entries. The new exclusion tests below are the preferred association sensitivity evidence. No old artifact is silently overwritten.

## Radius-matched NGC7331 test: central gravity fails in amplitude and shape

Local SPARC NGC7331_rotmod.dat has 36 radii at D=14.7 Mpc; the fixed outer window r >= rmax/2 contains 17 radii, 19.24–36.31 kpc. Use

    Vbar² = Vgas |Vgas| + Udisk Vdisk² + Ubul Vbul²
    delta_g = (Vobs² - Vbar²)/r
    M_equivalent_central(r) = r (Vobs² - Vbar²)/G.

The signed gas term is required: inner gas contributions can be outward. Nine stellar mass-to-light choices span Udisk=0.3,0.5,0.7 and Ubul=0.5,0.7,0.9. All 17 outer points have positive discrepancies for every choice. Nominal Udisk=0.5,Ubul=0.7 requires equivalent central mass rising from about 9.51e10 to 3.23e11 solar masses.

Rescaling the primary source's 33-degree upper limit to 14.7 Mpc gives 1.923e8 solar masses. Across all nine choices and all outer radii, the required central equivalent is 210–1957 times this particular inclination-dependent upper limit. It is **not** an orientation-independent bound. This nevertheless shows the scale mismatch under that published assumption. If the true BH mass is smaller, the required multiplier is larger.

The raw deeper002 radial results use the downstream 1.262e8 rescaled catalog value as a fixed reference only, because that computation began before the primary-source classification was resolved. Those dimensionless multipliers must not be interpreted as measured BH ratios. The plotted mass deficit itself is independent of that reference; it uses the primary 33-degree upper limit for comparison.

Allowing any constant multiplier of a central inverse-square force also gives poor radial shape: for the nine fixed stellar choices, the weighted squared-speed residual statistic is 535–551 for 17 points and one fitted multiplier. This is a diagnostic, not a calibrated rejection probability: distance/inclination, correlated velocity systematics, stellar M/L gradients and omitted molecular-gas corrections are not fitted. The required equivalent central mass grows with radius, whereas a fixed point mass does not.

This constrains ordinary central gravity or a constant amplification of it, in the stated model. It does not rule out all nonlocal or radius-dependent horizon couplings. A viable new coupling must specify that radial behavior and independent predictions. There is no usable population BH–discrepancy fit in this exact local SPARC overlap: NGC5055 is source-excluded and NGC7331 is an upper-limit-derived entry. Treating 17 radial bins as 17 galaxies would be invalid.

## Cluster audit: correct integral, no observational fit

The older kimik3_push/scripts/K016_cluster_xcop_profile.py states

    rho(r) = rho0 (1+x²)^(-3 beta/2), x=r/rc,

but implements Mgas proportional to atan(x)-x/(1+x²). Exact differentiation gives 2x²/(1+x²)², implying density 2rho0/(1+x²)², not the stated beta=2/3 profile rho0/(1+x²). It also never uses the varied beta in its robustness loop. For beta=2/3, the correct dimensionless mass integral is x-atan(x). Its infinite-radius mass diverges, so normalization must use a finite aperture.

Recomputed 18 cases with gas mass normalized inside R500: beta=.6,2/3,.7, rc/R500=.08,.1,.12, two original acceleration scales. Analytic kernel/density derivatives agree with independent finite differences; quadrature agrees with the beta=2/3 analytic mass integral. The original source is preserved unchanged; its formula/claimed-beta interpretation is flagged here.

Corrected phantom-density fitted slopes in 40 kpc–R500 span -1.372 to -1.341. Original reconstructed nominal slopes are -1.368 and -1.403. Thus this correction does not destroy the approximate slope resemblance, but the old robustness claim was not a valid beta variation. At R500 the corrected phantom/baryon mass ratios are 2.550 and 2.843; they are identical across density shapes at fixed total enclosed mass, radius and kernel by construction. They are not nine independent validations.

These are illustrative models, **not fits to measured X-COP profiles**. The original 2e13-solar-mass central point component is retained to isolate the integral correction, not endorsed as a realistic BCG mass. No agreement with actual cluster residual amplitudes or lensing is established. The old script's stated observed slope has not been independently validated here. The retained piecewise kernel has a small join jump (~-2.38e-7); derivative checks avoid that join.
