# HC-007 — rotation measurement-error sensitivity

2026-11-05. Continuation of HC005_HC006.md; the physical mechanism remains unresolved.

## Computation

For the 27 eligible rotation galaxies, fit a latent bivariate Gaussian population in log stellar mass and log BH mass, with log velocity conditionally linear in both. Integrate the latent variables analytically to obtain a full three-dimensional Gaussian likelihood with each object's measurement covariance. Use the modern BH masses and uncertainties from galaxies.json, including modern stellar masses and their quoted uncertainties. This avoids relying on the incompletely parsed symmetric BH errors in davis.json. Velocity errors come from the rotation source. This is an exploratory error-model sensitivity calculation, not a selection-corrected causal analysis.

| Error assumption | BH coefficient in log v | Intrinsic velocity scatter, dex | Twice log-likelihood improvement over zero BH coefficient |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Quoted errors, diagonal covariance | 0.04742 | 0.03239 | 3.916 |
| Quoted errors, shared-distance covariance | 0.04712 | 0.03173 | 4.288 |
| Half quoted errors | 0.05581 | 0.03814 | 9.341 |
| No measurement errors | 0.05497 | 0.04161 | 10.195 |

Shared-distance covariance assumes stellar mass scales as D squared and BH mass as D, caps the shared component at the marginal quoted errors, and leaves those marginals fixed. It is a sensitivity assumption, not a measured covariance matrix. Half/no-error cases deliberately test the role of assumed uncertainty; they are not preferred descriptions of the data.

The positive fitted coefficient survives these error assumptions. The likelihood preference for including it weakens substantially with quoted errors. We have not computed profile confidence intervals or calibrated a sampling distribution; the likelihood improvements are not reported as sigma significance or p-values. The result does not establish a black-hole force. Gaussian population assumptions, sample selection, omitted gas and structure, and lack of matched radii remain material limitations. Structural controls in HC005/006 also weaken out-of-sample performance, on a 26-object subset.
