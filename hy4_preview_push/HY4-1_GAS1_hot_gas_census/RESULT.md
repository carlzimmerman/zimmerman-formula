## 1. Answer first

**No.** Form B's amount problem is not a missing-baryon problem, and the flank the fable52 README named is now closed
on both of its exits.

The gas that would bring the law's demand inside the cosmic budget is a **fixed surface density**,

    Sigma_gas = a0 / (pi G x_b^2),      x_b^2 = R_b (1 + R_b) = 5.41 x 6.41 = 34.6781

    canonical: 9.3603e-11 / (pi x 6.674e-11 x 34.6781) = 1.287355e-02 kg/m^2 = 6.1627 Msun/pc^2
    alt:       1.1312e-10 / (pi x 6.674e-11 x 34.6781) = 1.555779e-02 kg/m^2 = 7.4477 Msun/pc^2

constant in radius, extending from the budget radius `R_budget = x_b r_M` outward. It does not depend on the galaxy:
it is the column whose enclosed mass `pi R^2 Sigma` makes `r_M(M_bar) >= R/x_b`, i.e. makes the law's share exactly
5.41 M_b at that radius. Evaluated on the record's own KiDS host-free bins it demands

| | bin 3 (1.29 Mpc) | bin 2 (1.72 Mpc) | bin 1 (2.27 Mpc) | bin 0 (2.62 Mpc) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M_gas_req, canonical | 3.234e13 | 5.716e13 | 1.000e14 | 1.326e14 Msun |
| M_gas_req, alt | 3.910e13 | 6.909e13 | 1.209e14 | 1.603e14 Msun |
| in units of M_b (canonical) | 384 | 678 | 1197 | **1913** |
| in units of M_b (alt) | 464 | 820 | 1447 | **2312** |

Every L* galaxy would need to carry the gas halo of a **cluster** (X-COP measures 0.8-3.3e14 Msun at 1.6-2.6 Mpc) out
to 2.6 Mpc. Three independent lines exclude that, and one of them — the amplitude — is the one that matters, because it
says the reading does not merely need implausible gas: **it destroys the very fit it was invoked to save.**

| line | canonical | alt | fires |
|---|---|---|---|
| **G1** gas needed / all baryons in a sphere of that radius | 284, 327, 433, 574 | 344, 396, 523, 695 | yes |
| **G2** gas needed / everything measured there | 21.0, 5.43, 2.57, 1.96 | 25.4, 6.56, 3.10, 2.37 | yes |
| **G3a** gas needed / B21's own hot gas (from their two files) | 3750, 524, 497, 328 | 4532, 633, 601, 397 | yes |
| **G3b** gas needed / GO1's measured group gas, at x >= 2.83 R500 | +8.0 to +13.3 sigma | +10.0 to +16.3 sigma | yes |

| **G5** required gas density / rho_b (lower bound on n) | 1.38 | 1.67 | no |

**The amplitude line.** The measured outer amplitude of the host-free set is 1.4002 +- 0.2211 (canonical) /
1.2740 +- 0.2012 (alt) of the law. Put the amount-fixing gas into `g_bar` and the same data sit at
**0.039 +- 0.007 / 0.032 +- 0.006** of the law — 36 / 41 times below the law, and more than 100 sigma below the
record's 0.891 gate line. B21's own much smaller hot-gas estimate already moves their published outer points from
1.50 to 0.47 (KO1 R6, reproduced here to 1e-4: 1.4999 +- 0.0706 and 0.4704 +- 0.0336). The amount-fixing gas is
328-3750 times larger than B21's, so it moves the points far past 0.47 and off the bottom.

**So the missing-baryon reading is self-defeating on the galaxy leg:** the gas it needs to make the cold budget
balance is the gas that makes the law over-predict the outer signal by a factor of ~25. It cannot fix the amount and
keep the fit.

## 3. The gas the reading needs (Step 2), with the arithmetic

**(L1) law-driven, per footing.** The demand is inside the budget iff `X(x) <= 5.41` iff `x <= x_b` iff
`M_bar(<R) >= (a0/G) R^2 / x_b^2`, so

    M_gas_req(R) = (a0/G) R^2 / 34.6781  -  M_b

Arithmetic at the outermost bin, canonical, R = 2.6178 Mpc = 8.0757e22 m:

    (a0/G)         = 9.3603e-11 / 6.674e-11            = 1.40262 kg m^-2
    (a0/G) R^2     = 1.40262 x (8.0757e22)^2           = 1.40262 x 6.52169e45 = 9.14713e45 kg
    / x_b^2        = 9.14713e45 / 34.6781              = 2.63827e44 kg
    - M_b          = 2.63827e44 - 6.931e10 x 1.989e30  = 2.63827e44 - 1.37857e41 = 2.63689e44 kg
                   = 2.63689e44 / 1.989e30             = 1.3257e14 Msun  = 1913 M_b          (printed 1.326e14)

Column: `M/(pi R^2) = 2.63689e44 / (pi x 6.52169e45) = 1.28736e-2 kg/m^2 = 6.1627 Msun/pc^2`, which is exactly
`a0/(pi G x_b^2)` (KA3, 2e-15). The profile is a **uniform sheet**: `M ∝ R^2`, `rho_3D ∝ 1/R`.

The implied gas density: 1.765e12 to 3.562e12 Msun/Mpc^3 (canonical, bins 0 to 3) against the cosmic
**rho_b = 6.204e9 Msun/Mpc^3** and **rho_m = 3.975e10 Msun/Mpc^3** — i.e. **284 to 574 times the cosmic mean baryon
density and 44 to 90 times the cosmic mean matter density**, at 1.3 to 2.6 Mpc.

**(L3) data-driven, footing-free.** With `M_3D,obs = R^2 D/G` (isothermal s=1 conversion of the measured
`D = 4 G ESD`; point-mass bracket 0.785x) and requiring `M_cold <= 5.41 M_bar`:

    M_gas_req = M_3D,obs / 6.41  -  M_b

which gives 2.49e12 to 3.39e12 Msun (29.6 to 40.2 M_b, 14.5-15.2 % of the enclosed mass). This is the *smallest* gas
column that makes the cold remainder cosmic — and it is still 2.3 to 3.5 times the gas fraction actually measured at
these radii by GO1 (0.045-0.068 of the lensing mass, against 0.156 required). It is 30 to 140 times smaller than the
law-driven requirement, because the law-driven requirement also has to make the *law's* prediction reach that far out.

pass isolation), the required gas density is 8.58e9 / 1.04e10 Msun/Mpc^3 against rho_b 6.20e9: **Q5 = 1.38 / 1.67**.
Not decisive with the lower bound; the true space density of M* > 1e9.5 galaxies is ~1e-2 Mpc^-3, which would give
~30, but that number is not on disk, so it is not graded.

## 6. Post-freeze changes, all disclosed

- **KA8 revised at run time.** The mass-bin inversion through `Fig-9` that KA8 was written against is noise-limited:
  Fig-9's ESD is *not* monotone in g_bar at fixed mass (mass bin 1 runs 0.970, 2.113, 1.789, 1.518 ...), so the
  inversion is not well defined. KA8 was moved onto the measured `Fig-3` profiles: all four mass bins give a positive
  enclosed mass rising from 0.30 to 2.60 Mpc, with M_tot(2.60 Mpc) = 6.8e12, 7.0e12, 1.6e13, 2.1e13 Msun.
- **G3a built differently than pre-registered.** For the same reason, B21's gas is carried as the *unbinned*
  gas-to-stellar ratio (extracted from the two monotone RAR files) times the P1 stack's own baryon mass, rather than
  inverted per mass bin. The pre-registered mass-bin route is reported as unusable.
- **M3 amended.** The criteria said `M_gas_req` rises by exactly 10 under a0 x 10. That is exact only for the
  `(a0/G) R^2 / x_b^2` term (which is graded, and gives 10.000000000000), because `M_gas_req` subtracts `M_b`, which
  does not scale with a0. `M_gas_req` itself rises by 10.0047-10.0234; both numbers are printed.
- **KA6b added at run time (reported, not a gate).** The P1 amplitudes at all three veto radii reproduce KO1 E7 to
  2e-4. It is not in the frozen KA list, so it cannot produce NO VERDICT.

## 8. What this closes, and what it does not

- It closes the fable52 README's item 1 ("a measured gas mass at Mpc radii around isolated galaxies") **as a route to
  rescuing form B on the galaxy leg**, without waiting for the measurement: whatever that measurement finds, if it
  finds enough gas to balance the cold budget it will at the same time push the outer points to ~0.03-0.04 of the law,
  which fails by 36-41x. The two are not independent exits; they are the same gas counted twice.
- It does **not** close the question of what the gas does at cluster and group scale. There the required column is
  within a factor of ~1 of what X-COP measures (0.37-1.26), so on clusters a baryon reading of the amount is still
  open — and, notably, form B fails clusters for other reasons (PD1: X-COP and groups FAIL at 5.41 M_b,sys).
- It does **not** touch the amount problem itself (AMT1: the share is 11x the cosmic budget and 56x what the record's
  dynamics deliver). It removes one proposed escape from it.
