## Limits

- **The premise.** Every corrected number rests on the catalogue mass being the mass of the Sérsic model inside the
  modelling circle (the catalogue paper's text and EUC17's one-lens check). If the masses are infinite-profile totals,
  this lane's corrected numbers are void and the committed words stand.
- **The extrapolation.** `M★ / f` normalises the fitted Sérsic body to the catalogued in-mask mass and extrapolates
  its wings to infinity; it is not a measurement of outer light. The corrections are a tenth of a dex at the median
  (secondary 0.108, W_B 0.132, complement 0.036), with a tail to about 0.23 dex at the 84th percentile.
- **G3's common scale.** LNJ1's primary adds EUC12's constant (+0.036 dex), measured between the uncorrected ECLIPSE
  masses and CIGALE / SLACS. Under the correction that offset is no longer the one EUC12 measured; it was not
  re-derived here (the criteria allow one substitution only). LNJ1's catalogue-scale bracket (shift 0) reads NO SWITCH
  canonical and NO POWER alt and is already inside its FRAGILE tag.
- **G3 alt** stands on 22 lenses at s ≥ 1, none above the band; three lenses fewer would turn it to NO POWER.
- **G2** reads 25 noisy spectra on a dispersion scale that EUC5 set post hoc; EUC8 says every Part B word inherits it.
- **G1** is EUC4's secondary bin, labelled secondary; no word of the wide bin (EUC17's row) is changed here.
- The EUC6 "large part" line (one third of +3.38) is this lane's reading; the slope's interval includes zero.
- Process: the f = 1 harness was run first; then primary, F1a, F1b and invert were each run once; no code changed
  between the two steps. The exact-shift script computes all five modes in one process.
- No measurement of a₀ or κ. No outcome here is a pass of the lensing gate.
