# Source and acquisition record

Accessed 2026-11-04. Local calculations are described separately in CONTRACT.md and REPORT.md.

1. [CONDOR survey](https://sites.google.com/rug.nl/condorsurvey): ten massive galaxies, resolved CO(3–2) and band-7 continuum. [ALMA large programmes](https://almascience.eso.org/alma-data/lp): programme 2024.1.00100.L, PI Francesca Rizzo.
2. Live [ALMA ESO TAP service](https://almascience.eso.org/tap): saved response archive_metadata.csv. POST /sync, REQUEST=doQuery, LANG=ADQL, FORMAT=csv. Query: `SELECT target_name,obs_id,s_ra,s_dec,s_resolution,t_exptime,sensitivity_10kms,data_rights,obs_release_date,frequency_support,band_list FROM ivoa.obscore WHERE proposal_id='2024.1.00100.L'`. Spectral-window rows collapsed by observing unit, J-prefixed calibrator names excluded. Dates may change; inventory.json records exact UTC/Eastern values and unit IDs. Metadata access only, no proprietary science data.
3. [Tadaki et al. 2023, v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.02703), Table 1: U4-16442 and U4-28473 redshifts, integrated CO(3–2) fluxes, CO and 4.4-micron sizes. U4-16442 F32=.70+/-.08 Jy km/s, ReCO=1.33+/-.30 kpc; U4-28473 F32=.79+/-.08, ReCO=1.56+/-.30 kpc.
4. Local KMOS3D catalogue `data_assembly/kmos3d_phibss/kmos3d_catalog.csv`: U4_16442 logMstar11.22, z2.21777, angular Rhalf.508 arcsec; GS4_30534 coordinate match to GMASS1084 at approximately .477 arcsec, logMstar11.31, z1.55204, angular Rhalf.889 arcsec. Catalogue masses are alternative published inferences, not exact truths.
5. [Decarli et al. 2016, molecular gas reservoirs](https://www.eso.org/public/archives/releases/sciencepapers/eso1633/eso1633c.pdf), Tables 1 and 3, section 4.2: GMASS1084=ID2=ASPECS3mm.2=C2. Lprime10=18+/-2 x10^9; gas mass65+/-8 x10^9 solar masses for alpha3.6. Optical effective radius8.3 kpc and CO extent>15 kpc motivate, but do not measure, the exponential gas-size scenarios. Published inclination/mass discrepancies are a limitation.
6. [Liu et al. 2021](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.06646), Table 1: same-name ID2993 candidate at z1.19, logMstar11.0. Position/size/gas association was not authenticated, so not used for an absolute curve. Its archive CO window157.31–158.24GHz gives the conservative z interval used in the analytic bound.
7. [Valentino et al. 2020](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.12521), appendix D catalogue reference: attempted retrieval of CDS J/A+A/641/A155 via cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr, vizier.cds.unistra.fr, vizier.cfa.harvard.edu and cdsarc.u-strasbg.fr failed with timeouts; A&A HTML returned403. No missing catalogue values were imputed.
8. [Navarro, Frenk & White](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/9611107): explicit NFW profile family, not a unique predicted halo for each target.
9. [Dutton & Maccio 2014, v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1402.7073), equations10–11: log10 c200=a+b log10[M200/(10^12 h^-1 Msun)], b=-.101+.026z, a=.520+(.905-.520)exp(-.617 z^1.21). Original h=.671 used in mass units. The approximate cosmology mismatch with forecast distances is stated in halo_contract.json. Fixed mean relation is used for the reported robustness result; an additional +/- .22 dex sensitivity variant is not used because not all fits converged.

The prediction law is inherited from local PAPER66 and its revision report, not inferred from any of the forthcoming CONDOR cubes. This calculation does not establish that other researchers have published target-specific competing preregistered predictions.
