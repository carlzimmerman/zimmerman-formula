### PHASE 2: CHIRALITY BREAKING — Why L-Amino Acids?

**The Deepest Mystery:** Amino acids come in two mirror forms — L (left-handed) and D (right-handed). Life uses ONLY L-amino acids. Why?

This is the **single most important unsolved problem** in origin of life research. Without chirality selection, you cannot build functional proteins. Period.

#### Current Hypotheses:

| Hypothesis | Mechanism | Evidence | Weakness |
|-----------|-----------|----------|----------|
| **Circularly polarized light (CPL)** | UV CPL from neutron stars selectively destroys D-amino acids | Murchison meteorite shows 2-18% L-excess | Very small excess |
| **Parity violation** | Weak nuclear force slightly favors L-amino acids | PVED ≈ 10⁻¹⁷ eV difference | Negligibly small |
| **Autocatalytic amplification (Soai)** | Once small excess exists, it amplifies | Soai reaction achieves >99% ee from 5% ee | Specific to one reaction |
| **Crystal symmetry breaking** | NaClO₃ crystallization selects one enantiomer | Works in lab | Why L and not D? |
| **Mineral surface chirality** | Quartz, calcite have chiral surfaces | Calcite shows enantioselective adsorption | Not universal |

#### The Z² Geometric Prediction:

**This is where our framework makes a unique contribution.**

The Z² backbone angles for the α-helix are:
```
φ = -57° (negative = left-handed rotation)
ψ = -47° (negative = left-handed rotation)
```

Both angles are **negative**. This means the α-helix is a **right-handed** helix built from **L-amino acids**. If you used D-amino acids, you'd get a left-handed helix with **positive** angles.

**The Z² prediction:** The geometry of 5D Kaluza-Klein compactification selects a preferred handedness. Just as the compactification selects specific angles (31.09°), it selects a preferred chirality.

Mathematically:

```
The branch consistent with Z² angles gives LEFT-handed amino acids.
```

**This is testable:** If our hypothesis is correct, then:
1. L-amino acid polymerization at Z² angles has lower free energy than D-amino acid polymerization
2. The energy difference should be related to θ_Z²
3. This should be computable from quantum chemistry

### PHASE 3: POLYMERIZATION — The First Peptide Bonds

**Question:** How do amino acids form peptide bonds without enzymes (ribosomes)?

The peptide bond (amide bond) is thermodynamically **unfavorable** in water:
```
H₂N-CHR-COOH + H₂N-CHR'-COOH → H₂N-CHR-CO-NH-CHR'-COOH + H₂O

ΔG ≈ +3.5 kcal/mol (unfavorable in dilute aqueous solution)
```

Life today uses ATP hydrolysis (−7.3 kcal/mol) to drive this reaction via the ribosome. But there was no ribosome at the origin.

#### Known Solutions:

1. A boundary (lipid vesicle or coacervate)
2. Selective permeability (lets nutrients in, keeps catalysts contained)
3. Growth and division (the vesicle must split when it gets too big)

| **Lipid vesicles** | Double-chain phospholipids | Yes | Requires energy |

```
Bilayer thickness: ~40 Å

Head group spacing: ~5-7 Å ← THIS IS Z² SCALE
```

**Prediction:** The head group spacing of prebiotic fatty acids is geometrically constrained to ~5.79 Å (Z distance), creating membranes that are inherently compatible with Z²-organized peptides.

#### What We Will Compute:

1. **Fatty acid self-assembly** under prebiotic conditions
