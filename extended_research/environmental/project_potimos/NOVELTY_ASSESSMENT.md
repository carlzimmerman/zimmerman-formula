## 6. Z/2 Pore Size (2.89 Å)

### Our Claim
Pore diameter of Z/2 = 2.89 Å optimizes lithium vs. sodium selectivity.

### Prior Art Search Results

**Pore size research exists:**
- Li⁺ ionic diameter: 1.18-1.52 Å (crystallographic)
- Li⁺ Stokes diameter: 4.8 Å
- Li⁺ hydrated diameter: 7.64 Å
- Na⁺ ionic diameter: 1.98-2.04 Å

**Selectivity pore sizes studied:**
- 3.5-8.5 Å range for monovalent/divalent separation
- Subnanometer pores in NASICON materials
- MXene interlayer: 2.9-6.4 Å

**2.89 Å specifically:** NOT FOUND as design target.

### Assessment
The **derivation** of 2.89 Å from Z/2 is novel. The **general concept** of subnanometer pores for ion selectivity has prior art.

**Recommendation:** Claim novelty on derivation method, not general concept.
