FOR J0332: aperture-corrected image fit and stellar-mass assessment
2026-11-13 — supersedes the earlier stellar-mass tension interpretation

RESULT

The smooth, unscreened galaxy branch of the adopted theory is compatible
with FOR J0332 under the stated lens/source assumptions. The corrected
comparison requires 8.80e11 Msun of stars inside the modelling aperture,
versus the catalogue Salpeter estimate 6.78e11 Msun with a 16–84% interval
5.33–8.27e11 Msun. The offset is 0.113425 dex, a factor1.298, or1.313 of
the quoted upper-side mass errors. This is mild conditional tension, not
a significant exclusion. It is also not a calibrated goodness-of-fit test
or a confirmation of the complete theory or its dark-energy connection.

I previously compared a finite-aperture catalogue mass with an infinite
Sersic mass normalization. That comparison was incorrect. The earlier
approximately2.4sigma/1.6times stellar-mass tension claim is withdrawn.
The fitted field/image solution remains usable; its mass prior and physical
interpretation are corrected here. No new physical effect was discovered.

MODEL AND DATA

The fit uses the actual Euclid VIS pixels, released PSF/RMS/masks, known
lens/source redshifts0.986/3.773, and the catalogue's asymmetric multi-band
stellar-mass constraint. It fixes a0=9.3603e-11m/s² from the adopted
relation a0=(c/2)sqrt(G rho_DE), with rho_DE a mass-equivalent density.
The axisymmetric AQUAL equation with mu(x)=x/(1+x) is solved and its field
projected along the line of sight. This is not a scalar spherical shortcut.

The point light shape is fixed (circularized Re1.13918arcsec,n5.86872,
q.813379), edge-on, with constant stellar mass-to-light ratio. Stellar mass,
two external-shear components, source regularization and a pixelated source
are fitted/marginalized as described in the original image analysis.
The released four-image association is imposed with source-plane diameter
<=.3arcsec; line-of-sight convergence is fixed to zero. The SED prior uses
Salpeter living stars plus remnants, unchanged. This is a VIS likelihood
plus the released SED summary, not a fresh joint eight-band SED fit.

APERTURE CORRECTION

ECLIPSE-D Sections3.1/3.2 define the physical-property SED input as the
unconvolved lens model summed within the modelling circle. Section6.1
explicitly acknowledges missing outer light and underestimated total masses.
The defined mass is therefore not automatically an infinite-profile mass.
See finite_mask_evidence.md for exact primary-source locations/provenance.

Integrating the fixed unit-total Sersic profile over the actual selected
pixel union/cutout gives f_ap=.8038930178. Under the SAME constant-M/L,
infinite-profile assumption already used by the field library,

  M_aperture = f_ap M_infinite.

Thus the extrapolated SED total is8.43e11 Msun (logM11.925889), while the
image-required infinite mass is1.09475e12 Msun (logM12.039314). Comparing
aperture masses directly avoids mixing these definitions. The unobserved
outer profile is an assumption, not a new measurement of extra stars.

A separate released-map/analytic-infinite normalization gives f=.798448;
this changes the required aperture mass to8.74e11 Msun and the discrepancy
to1.279 upper-side error units. The small difference reflects finite image
rendering/normalization. The actual pixel-integrated geometry is primary.
The full geometric circle extends beyond the cutout and is not substituted
for the actual pixel mask. Non-VIS map fluxes agree closely with catalogue
maximum-likelihood fluxes; VIS differs by1.11%, so exact universal table/map
identity is not claimed.

POSTERIOR AND VERIFICATION

The corrected prior is p_SED(logM_infinite+log10f_ap). Existing posterior
samples were reweighted by the exact new/old prior ratio, preserving the
original image likelihood and half-normal asymmetric mass surrogate.
Four original chains passed their registered R-hat/autocorrelation checks.
Importance ESS is9993.39 of10000 stored samples; this is a weight-overlap
measure, not the number of independent MCMC samples. Per-chain weighted
log-mass medians span0.000167dex. Five target densities recomputed from
pixels and field maps match the recorded original values exactly.
The total-mass posterior median changes by only0.0000427dex.

These narrow intervals freeze physical nuisance assumptions and must NOT
be presented as complete galaxy-mass uncertainties.

A representative corrected-posterior sample has chi²3094.19 on6711pixels,
and source-association diameter.299931arcsec. The original constrained
local optimum had chi²3091.62. A matched reconstruction using fixed published
SIE parameters has chi²3124.04, but its parameters were not reoptimized.
These are image consistency checks, not a gravity-model Bayes factor.
The released diagonal RMS is not a calibrated full covariance. The source
prior and association gate also materially affect mass inference.

The original finite field/projection/grid/refinement and positive-source
checks remain applicable. New main and pipeline-sensitivity bounded runs
both completed; their v2 provenance manifests validate. The aperture audit
also completed and validates. Reproduction commands are in those manifests.

WHAT REMAINS UNRESOLVED

The full screened covariant action is not fitted: screening scale, boundary
conditions and granularity are not uniquely supplied by this lens. The
current branch is a smooth-galaxy approximation with explicit scope.
Inclination, shape covariance, IMF/SPS systematics, M/L gradients and the
line-of-sight environment have not been jointly marginalized. Raw NIR
masked fits were attempted but do not establish an independent precise
light shape: conservative PSF-contamination masks remove the useful centre,
and results depend on masks. Those limitations are retained, not replaced
with optimistic tiny fit errors.

ECLIPSE-D uses spectroscopic redshifts when available and photometric ones
otherwise. A separate +.00653358dex luminosity-distance-only prior shift is
reported as OPTIONAL sensitivity; presence of a catalogue photo-z does not
by itself prove the redshift actually used by this object's SED fit. It is
not treated as a mandatory correction or a full stellar-population refit.

FOR has no match in the searched DESI strong-lens VAC and no independent
stellar velocity-dispersion measurement was established. A single lens at
one adopted a0 does not establish how a0 depends on dark-energy density.
Existing Lean identities certify scoped algebraic relations only. They do
not certify the data, numerical solution, empirical success or novelty.
