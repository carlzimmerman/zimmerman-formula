The b convention is sigma_R^2/sigma_z^2. The source explicitly warns that
Jeans solutions need not admit a nonnegative phase-space distribution.
We use the general equations, not its later MGE-specific closed form.

Assumptions: steady axisymmetry, cylindrical velocity-ellipsoid alignment,
zero meridional streaming/cross moment, constant b. Define P=nu*sigma_z^2.
Vertical equilibrium gives P(R,z)=integral_z^infinity nu(R,u)*Phi_z(R,u)du.
Radial equilibrium then gives nu*<v_phi^2>=b*(R*P_R+P)+R*nu*Phi_R.
The force is the same isolated, oblate, smooth unscreened fixed-a0 AQUAL
field used for host lensing. The tracer is its unit-normalized oblate Abel
Sersic density, at edge-on inclination. No new spherical substitution.

With sky axes(u,v) along the projected major/minor axes, intrinsic axes are
x=u, y=los*sin(i)-v*cos(i), z=v*sin(i)+los*cos(i). The LOS velocity has
coefficients sin(i)*y/R, sin(i)*x/R, cos(i) for(v_R,v_phi,v_z).
Thus <v_los^2>=sin(i)^2*(sigma_R^2*y^2/R^2+<v_phi^2>*x^2/R^2)
+cos(i)^2*sigma_z^2. Integrate nu times this quantity along the LOS and
through a circular aperture after Gaussian seeing convolution. The exact
Gaussian-to-offset-circle throughput is a noncentral chi-square CDF with
2degrees of freedom. Divide by the identical light-weight integral.

For the Satoh k=1 sensitivity only, mean_v_phi=sqrt(<v_phi^2>-sigma_R^2).
Report where its radicand is negative; never claim a physical k=1 case if
material weighted support fails. The aperture central second moment is
< v_los^2 >_ap - < v_los >_ap^2, not merely the raw RMS for an offset fiber.
The k=0 nonrotating moment case has zero aperture mean by construction.
