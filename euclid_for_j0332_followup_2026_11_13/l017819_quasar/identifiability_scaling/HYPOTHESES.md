# Exact conditional claim card

The coefficient field is the real numbers. The field and source-plane values lie in a real normed vector space; positions may range over an arbitrary type. Let a>0 and λ>0. At fixed coordinate geometry and fixed unit-mass density profile, replace M,a,g by λM,λa,λg. For any fixed scalar function μ, the pointwise argument ‖g‖/a is unchanged and the flux μ(‖g‖/a)g scales by λ, including g=0. No division at a=0 is used in that theorem.

The supplied `divergence`, `boundary` and `project` objects are **abstract linear maps**. Their linearity is encoded in their Lean types, not established for an analytic domain here. Conditional on an original source equation D(F_a(g))=Mρ_unit, the scaled source equation follows. Boundary data scale with the field; fixed nonzero external/boundary data generally do not qualify. A fixed linear deflection projection includes the chosen distance and weak-field prefactors. No PDE existence, uniqueness, regularity, numerical convergence, isolated asymptotic solution or relativistic completion is proved.

The lens map is β(θ)=(1−κ)θ−α(θ)−Γθ. Here Γ is a linear map (the trace-free shear used in the application is a special case), and α is an arbitrary reduced deflection function. Replacing α by λα, Γ by λΓ, and κ by λκ+1−λ gives β'=λβ. Define S'(b)=S(b/λ). Then S'(β'(θ))=S(β(θ)) at every θ. This equality holds for arbitrary brightness functions, and the rescaling preserves continuity when the source is continuous. Any fixed deterministic observation operator, including PSF convolution/pixel integration, preserves equal image functions. The formalization does not claim that an arbitrary fixed source grid, positivity-plus-grid restriction, regularization functional or source-size/flux prior is closed under this transformation.

For physical identifiability, the scaled parameter values must remain in the allowed model family. Fixed κ=0, constrained environment/shear, known mass or M/L, calibrated intrinsic source size/flux, time delays or independent dynamics may restrict or break the family. Fixed distance factors, tracer profile and geometry are essential. A transformed external κ here is a **phenomenological lens-plane term**; because AQUAL is nonlinear, this proof does not realize it as a self-consistent extra matter sheet whose field can simply be superposed on the galaxy field. It does not say every λ is allowed by a physical nonnegative density, environment prior, or full covariant theory.

The user's additional postulate is a²=(c²G/4)ρ_DE. With c,G fixed, a→λa and ρ_DE→λ²ρ_DE preserve that equation algebraically. Positive ρ_DE changes for positive λ≠1. **Distances remain held fixed**: no Friedmann solution or self-consistent cosmological family with changed dark-energy density is constructed. This is why fixed-geometry image algebra alone cannot establish a dark-energy measurement.

The optional second-moment statement is only linear-functional algebra: if a fixed-tracer/fixed-orbit Jeans-plus-aperture construction really is a linear functional Q of g with compatible boundary data, then Q(λg)=λQ(g), so the modeled squared dispersion scales accordingly. Neither that representation nor a real galaxy's anisotropy/rotation is established in Lean.

Dependency chain: positive norm scaling and field arithmetic → pointwise flux homogeneity → supplied linear divergence/source and boundary identities → supplied linear deflection projection → explicit κ/Γ mass-sheet lens map → source redefinition → image-operator equality. The density-squared postulate is a separate algebraic leaf. The abstract linear maps and observational model are assumptions, not consequences of the image equality.

This is an application of known AQUAL homogeneity and mass-sheet algebra, not a novelty claim. No Euclid data, DESI dispersion, a0 value, mass posterior, source association, or gravity model pass/fail is certified by these identities.
