### 5. Olbers' Paradox = Why the Night Is Dark

Here is the deep physics in the title. If the universe were **infinite, eternal and static**, every line of sight would eventually hit a star, and the night sky would blaze as bright as the Sun's surface. It doesn't. The night is dark.

```
Infinite + eternal + static → bright night sky
Observed → dark night sky
Therefore → the universe is not all three
```

The resolution: the universe has a **finite age**, so light from far enough away hasn't reached us yet — and **expansion** stretches and dims the light that has. The darkness of every night is direct evidence that the cosmos **began**.

**To wait for tonight is to wait for a cosmological measurement.** Every dark sky is a data point.
