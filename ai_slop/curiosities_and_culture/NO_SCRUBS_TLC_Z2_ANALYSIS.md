## The Song

"No Scrubs" (1999) from TLC's *FanMail* is a **selection criterion set to a beat**. The song lays out, in plain terms, exactly what kind of candidate gets rejected — and it lays it out *before* any particular candidate shows up. From a Z² perspective, this is the most important habit in experimental science: **freeze your criteria first, then look at the data.** Decide what counts as a fail before you know who's knocking. A scrub is not someone you reject because you didn't like the result; a scrub is someone who failed a rule that was written down in advance.

## The Deeper Point

"No Scrubs" is a lesson in **honest selection**:

1. **Write the rule first**: the criteria come before the candidate
2. **Make it binary**: CUBE letters, pass or fail
3. **Check it independently**: three voices, one verdict
4. **Don't move the goalposts**: a fail is a fail, no matter who it is

The song is a field guide to clean science:
- A frozen criterion (the verses)
- A binary test (the title, CUBE letters)
- Three independent checks (T-Boz, Chilli, Left Eye)
- A published result (four weeks at #1, BEKENSTEIN)

The hardest discipline in research is verifying a fail as hard as a win — never manufacturing either. TLC never manufactured a win. If the candidate failed the rule, the answer was no. That's not cold; that's **honest**.
