From a Z² perspective, this is the **paradox of apparent motion in a fixed point**: the system appears to evolve but returns to the same attractor. The changes are superficial; the deep structure is invariant. 2Pac catalogs what should change and laments that it doesn't.

2Pac recorded this in 1992. He died in 1996. It was released in 1998. The issues he described in 1992 were present in 1986 (the Hornsby original). They're present now. The song samples the past, speaks to the present, echoes into the future — because nothing changed.

The title is a prayer. The observation is a lament. The paradox is the truth: "Things will never be the same" AND "that's just the way it is." Both are true. That's the trap.
