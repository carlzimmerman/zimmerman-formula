## The Song

"Chester" (1770) is America's first war anthem. William Billings, a Boston tanner and self-taught composer, created a hymn tune that became the unofficial anthem of the American Revolution. Sung by colonial militias marching to battle, it fused sacred music with political defiance: "Let tyrants shake their iron rod, and slav'ry clank her galling chains, we fear them not, we trust in God, New England's God forever reigns."

No recording exists from 1770. But the composition - four-part harmony, simple melody, powerful text - has been reconstructed and performed for 250+ years. More remarkably: it encodes the topology of symmetry breaking in its very structure.
