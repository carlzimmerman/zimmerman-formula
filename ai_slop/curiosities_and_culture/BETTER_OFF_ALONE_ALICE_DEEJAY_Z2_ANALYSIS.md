## The Song

"Better Off Alone" (1999) is a Dutch trance anthem built on one of the most recognisable synth riffs of the era. The title is a claim about **isolation**: some things work better when nothing else is pulling on them. From a Z² perspective, that claim is literally true in low-acceleration gravity. In MOND-type laws, a galaxy far from any neighbour feels the **full** low-acceleration boost of its own mass. Put the same galaxy inside a bigger object's field and the boost gets washed out — the **external field effect**. An isolated dwarf shows its own physics most clearly. It really is **better off alone**.
