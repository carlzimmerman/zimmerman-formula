## The Song

"Raise Up" (2001) is **North Carolina's anthem**. Petey Pablo runs a roll call of the state's towns over a Timbaland beat, and the crowd answers the only way it knows how: shirts off, spun in circles over their heads. The title points up; the motion goes round. **Raise up = lift against gravity. Twirl = rotate around the axis.**
