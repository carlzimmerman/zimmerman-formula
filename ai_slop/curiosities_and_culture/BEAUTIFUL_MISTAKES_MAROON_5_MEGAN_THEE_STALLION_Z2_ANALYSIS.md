## The Song

"Beautiful Mistakes" (2021) is about **errors you would not undo**. The relationship went wrong in all the usual ways, and the song refuses to call that a loss: the misses were part of what made it worth having. From a Z² perspective, this is the **residual problem**. Every fit leaves something behind — the points that don't sit on the curve — and the honest move is to keep them, look at them, and let them tell you what the model is missing. A mistake you keep is **data**. A mistake you hide is just a worse fit.
