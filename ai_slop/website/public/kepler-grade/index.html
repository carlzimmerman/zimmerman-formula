Kepler Grade Prediction Chart

Every testable prediction found on the record, dated where the data has a date, with the framework's number, what the rivals say, and what would count against it. The confronted record and the retracted claims are on the same page so the chart does not read as a list of wins.

Standing terms. κ = ½ is fitted, not derived. Two footings, canonical a₀ = 9.36e-11 and alt 1.13e-10 m/s², are never pooled and are written can / alt. The cold energy's mass is still required. This is not a closed theory. Built 2026-10-11 from nine sweeps of the repo (predictions ledgers, prep_2026 pre-registrations, lanes CFG1 to CFG700, the atomos kill sheet, real_research, hunt_2026, the multi-model push folders, git history since April, memory notes). Some lane folders were only keyword-screened, so this is thorough but not a guarantee of completeness. Release dates for Euclid, Rubin, JUNO, LISA and 4MOST come from the record's own inventories and are not independently verified. Rules applied. Verdicts use raw data against framework-native predictions. Rows tied to the retired modified-inertia arm are tagged MI-ARM and are not live. Rows from AI exploration branches (deepseek, kimik3, hy4, the OpenAI math lane) are tagged BRANCH: non-authoritative, and several conflict with the standing record. Rows from the old clock-host candidate are tagged CANDIDATE-CONDITIONAL.
K1

Kepler grade. A number fixed before the data exist (frozen criteria or a sealed hash), a date, and a stated result that counts against it.

K2

Capped. The number is sharp, but the test cannot reach 3σ with today's systematics, the rivals predict nearly the same, or it is conditional on an assumption.

K3

Open. A real prediction with no usable data, no date, or only a forecast.

K4

Kepler slop. Speculative or numerology-era predictions. Dated and testable, so they could still land, but no derivation backs them and the record has retracted or downgraded several. Never cite as framework results.

Neutrino predictions in detail

Everything the record says about neutrinos in one place. Each number is read from the file named in the source line.

What is forced and what is not. The framework forces one number: the vacuum-energy scale EL = ρDE1/4 = 2.2395 meV. It does not force mlightest = EL; setting the O(1) coefficient to exactly 1 is a fit by inversion, with no geometric factor behind it. Any cosmic density1/4 (critical, matter, 10× dark energy) lands at 1.3–3.6 meV, so the match to neutrino masses is generic, not specific to ρDE. Seven literature mechanisms were computed and none sets mν (the MaVaN acceleron is the one real candidate, but its coupling scale is fitted and it carries a cs² < 0 instability). Falsifying this hypothesis leaves a₀ = √Λ untouched.

Standing cautions and closed doors

Σ is hot, not cold energy.

A 0.0613 eV neutrino sum is hot dark matter and cannot be the cold energy (CFG507).

A coherent chain is not evidence.

The lepton chain (TM1 + Dirac + m₁ = ρΛ1/4) is Lean-certified for its implications only (LEPTON_CHAIN 1–3, MODULAR_S4_FIXED; 10 theorems, zero sorry). Lean does not certify TM1, Dirac neutrinos, the coincidence, or κ.

Closed doors.

Residual symmetries (FS01–03), textures (FS06/13c; the one survivor A2 gives Σ ≈ 0.065 eV, now above DESI and forbidden by the Dirac argument), modular A4 (FS07), modular S4 fixed points (FS17: TM1 is a 24/48 coin flip), swampland × evolving dark energy (FS18, not crispy), Koide routes. Do not re-run.

Scale gap, not mechanism.

The neutrino is the one SM sector where the scale gap closes (about 1 order, against 108 for the electron), but the closing is generic and mechanism-free. Radiation drag as the source of a₀ is about 1e-19 a₀: dead.

Older neutrino numbers.

The Z²-era values (m₁ about 1.5 meV, Σ 58–60 meV, δCP 195°) conflict with this chain; see the K4 rows. They are not pooled.

The a₀(z) coupling.

The tower mν(z) and growth-step rows ride the same dark-energy gate (G5) as the a₀(z) test; the June deposit behind them (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20977421) used the retired modified-inertia framing, though the evolving-ρDE logic does not need it.
