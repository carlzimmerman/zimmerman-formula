# Office Hours with Claude: Z² Framework Foundations

A step-by-step curriculum to understand the mathematical and physical foundations of the Z² Unified Framework (v8.8.8).

## Learning Philosophy

Each topic builds on the previous. Don't skip ahead until you're comfortable with the prerequisites. The goal is **deep understanding**, not memorization.
