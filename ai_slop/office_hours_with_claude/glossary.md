# Glossary: Z² Framework Terms

Quick reference for key terms used in the Z² Unified Framework.

---

## Mathematical Terms

### A

**Abelian group** - A group where ab = ba for all elements. Example: U(1).

**ADM formalism** - Arnowitt-Deser-Misner decomposition of spacetime into space + time.

**Algebraic topology** - Using algebra (groups, rings) to study topological spaces.

**APS theorem** - Atiyah-Patodi-Singer index theorem for manifolds with boundary.

### B

**Betti number** bₙ - Dimension of n-th homology group. Counts n-dimensional "holes."
- b₁(T³) = 3 (three 1-cycles = three generations)

**Blow-up** - Replacing a singular point with a smooth space (usually a sphere).

### C

**Cartan subalgebra** - Maximal abelian subalgebra of a Lie algebra. Its dimension = rank.

**Chirality** - Handedness of a spinor (left or right). Z₂ projection eliminates right-handed zero modes.

**Cohomology** - Dual to homology. For smooth manifolds, computed using differential forms.

**Compactification** - Making extra dimensions small and finite.

**Connection** - Mathematical object describing parallel transport. Gauge fields are connections.

### D

**D-brane** - Extended object in string theory where open strings end.

**De Rham cohomology** - Cohomology computed using differential forms. H^n = closed forms / exact forms.

**Differential form** - Antisymmetric tensor field that can be integrated over submanifolds.

### E

**Eigenvalue** - Scalar λ such that Av = λv for some vector v.

**Euler characteristic** χ - Alternating sum of Betti numbers: χ = b₀ - b₁ + b₂ - ...

**Exceptional divisor** - The smooth space (usually ℂP¹ ≅ S²) that replaces a blown-up singularity.

### F

**Fiber bundle** - Space E projecting to base B with fiber F over each point.

**Fixed point** - Point p where group action satisfies g·p = p. T³/Z₂ has 8 fixed points.

**Fundamental group** π₁ - Group of loops up to homotopy. Measures 1-dimensional holes.

### G

**Gauge group** - Symmetry group of a gauge theory. Standard Model: SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1).

**Geodesic** - Shortest path on a curved space. Free particles follow geodesics.

**Group** - Set with associative operation, identity, and inverses.

### H

**Hermitian matrix** - Matrix equal to its conjugate transpose: A† = A.

**Hilbert space** - Complete inner product space. Setting for quantum mechanics.

**Hodge numbers** h^{p,q} - Count harmonic (p,q)-forms on a complex manifold.

**Holonomy** - How vectors rotate under parallel transport around a loop.

**Homology** - Cycles modulo boundaries. Measures holes in a space.

**Homotopy** - Continuous deformation. Two maps are homotopic if one deforms to the other.

### I

**Index** - Integer topological invariant, often computed via index theorems.

**Intersection number** I_ab - Topological count of brane intersections. I_ab = 3 in Z² framework.

### K

**Kaluza-Klein** - Theory unifying gravity and electromagnetism via extra compact dimensions.

**Künneth formula** - Computes homology of product spaces from factors.

### L

**Lie algebra** 𝔤 - Tangent space of Lie group at identity, with bracket [·,·].

**Lie group** - Group that's also a smooth manifold. Examples: U(1), SU(2), SU(3).

### M

**Manifold** - Space that locally looks like ℝⁿ.

**Metric tensor** g_ij - Defines distances, angles, and volumes on a manifold.

### N

**Non-abelian** - Group where ab ≠ ba in general. Examples: SU(2), SU(3).

### O

**Orbifold** - Space locally like ℝⁿ/G for finite group G. Has singularities at fixed points.

### P

**Poincaré duality** - For closed n-manifold: H_k ≅ H_{n-k}. Symmetry of Betti numbers.

**Principal bundle** - Fiber bundle where fiber is a Lie group.

### Q

**Quotient space** X/G - Space with points identified by group action.

### R

**Rank** - Dimension of Cartan subalgebra. rank(G_SM) = 4.

**Representation** - Homomorphism from group to matrices. How group acts on vector space.

**Ricci tensor** R_ij - Contraction of Riemann tensor. Appears in Einstein equations.

**Riemann tensor** R^i_jkl - Measures curvature of a manifold.

### S

**Simplex** - Building block: point, edge, triangle, tetrahedron, etc.

**Singularity** - Point where space is not smooth. Fixed points of orbifolds.

**Spinor** - Object that transforms under spin representation of rotation group.

**Structure constants** f_abc - Define Lie bracket: [T_a, T_b] = if_abc T_c.

### T

**Tangent space** T_pM - Space of directions at point p on manifold M.

**Tensor** - Multi-indexed object transforming covariantly under coordinate changes.

**Topology** - Study of properties preserved under continuous deformation.

**Torus** T^n - Product of n circles: T^n = S¹ × ... × S¹.

**Trace** Tr(A) - Sum of diagonal elements. Tr(A) = Σ eigenvalues.

**Twisted sector** - States localized at orbifold fixed points.

### U

**Unitary matrix** - Matrix with U†U = I. Preserves inner products.

**Untwisted sector** - States from bulk of orbifold (not localized at fixed points).

### Z

**Z₂** - Simplest non-trivial group: {1, -1} under multiplication.

## Physical Terms

### α

**α (fine structure constant)** - Electromagnetic coupling strength. α⁻¹ ≈ 137.

**α_s (strong coupling)** - QCD coupling strength. α_s ≈ 0.118 at M_Z.

### Ω

**Ω_Λ (dark energy density)** - Fraction of universe in dark energy. Ω_Λ ≈ 0.685.

**Ω_M (matter density)** - Fraction in matter. Ω_M ≈ 0.315.

### Other

**Chiral fermion** - Fermion with definite handedness (left or right).

**Cosmological constant** Λ - Energy density of vacuum.

**Dirac mass** - Mass term coupling left and right chiralities.

**Electroweak** - Unified SU(2)×U(1) theory of weak and EM forces.

**Gauge boson** - Force carrier (photon, W, Z, gluon).

**Generation** - Family of quarks and leptons (three total).

**Gluon** - Gauge boson of SU(3) strong force. 8 gluons.

**Higgs field** - Scalar field giving mass to particles via spontaneous symmetry breaking.

**Hierarchy problem** - Why is v << M_P? Why is electroweak scale so small?

**Holographic principle** - Bulk physics encoded on boundary.

**Majorana mass** - Mass term using only one chirality. ν = ν^c.

**Planck scale** M_P - Scale where quantum gravity dominates. M_P ≈ 10^19 GeV.

**QCD** - Quantum Chromodynamics, theory of strong force.

**QED** - Quantum Electrodynamics, theory of EM force.

**Renormalization group** (RG) - How couplings change with energy scale.

**Standard Model** - SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1) gauge theory of particle physics.

**VEV** - Vacuum expectation value. v = 246 GeV for Higgs.

**Weinberg angle** θ_W - Electroweak mixing angle. sin²θ_W ≈ 0.231.
