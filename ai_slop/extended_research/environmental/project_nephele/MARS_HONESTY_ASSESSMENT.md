## Critical Analysis of Each Claim

### 1. Jarosite Lattice Spacing

**What we claimed:** Jarosite has 5.78 Å spacing, giving 0.18% Z-offset (best in solar system).

**Honest assessment:**

| Question | Reality |
|----------|---------|
| Is jarosite on Mars? | YES - Confirmed by Opportunity (2004) |
| Does jarosite have ~5.78 Å spacing? | **COMPLICATED** |

**The complication:**

Jarosite has hexagonal crystal structure with:
- a = 7.30 Å
- c = 17.21 Å

Where does 5.78 Å come from?
- It's approximately c/3 = 5.74 Å
- Or it could be the Fe-Fe distance in the structure

**We should have specified WHICH spacing we mean.**

The 0.18% offset may be correct for c/3, but this needs verification against actual crystallographic data.

**Honest confidence: 60-70%** that our lattice spacing choice is physically meaningful.

### 6. Magnetic Oases

**What we claimed:** 5 regions on Mars have magnetic fields >245 Gauss capable of CISS.

**Honest assessment:**

The magnetic anomalies ARE real:
- Measured by Mars Global Surveyor magnetometer
- Terra Sirenum does have ~1500 nT surface field
- Approximate conversion: 1500 nT ≈ 0.015 Gauss (NOT 1500 Gauss!)

**WAIT - UNIT ERROR?**

Let me check: Mars magnetic anomalies are measured in nanotesla (nT).
- 1 Tesla = 10,000 Gauss
- 1500 nT = 1500 × 10⁻⁹ T = 1.5 × 10⁻⁶ T = 0.015 Gauss

**We may have confused nT with Gauss!**

| What we claimed | Reality |
|-----------------|---------|
| Terra Sirenum: 1500 Gauss | More like 0.015-0.15 Gauss |
| CISS threshold: 245 Gauss | From molecular physics |
| Mars fields exceed threshold? | **PROBABLY NOT** |

**This is a potentially serious error that undermines the magnetic oasis argument.**

**Honest confidence: 20-30%** that Mars crustal fields can support CISS.

**Note:** This needs verification. If the unit conversion is wrong, the entire magnetic oasis section is invalid.

### 1. Optimism Bias
We wanted Mars to score high (it's exciting!) so we:

- Used the most favorable interpretation of data

We reported Ω_Z = 0.946 (3 decimal places) when the uncertainty is probably ±0.2.

### 4. N=1 Extrapolation
The entire framework is based on Earth. We don't know if it applies elsewhere.

## Appendix: Magnetic Field Unit Check - VERIFIED ERROR

**CONFIRMED: We made a 100,000× unit error.**

Mars Global Surveyor measured crustal magnetic field anomalies in **nanotesla (nT)**, not Gauss.

From peer-reviewed literature:
- Terra Sirenum strongest: ~1500-2000 nT at surface
- InSight measured ~2012 nT (Langlais et al. 2019)
- 1 Gauss = 100,000 nT

**Conversion:**
- 2000 nT = 2000 / 100,000 Gauss = **0.02 Gauss**
- CISS threshold: 245 Gauss
- Mars fields are **~12,000× BELOW the CISS threshold**

**This COMPLETELY INVALIDATES the "magnetic oases" argument.**

Mars crustal fields, while strong for a planet without an active dynamo, are nowhere near sufficient for CISS activation in our framework.

**Status:** CONFIRMED ERROR - Magnetic oasis section should be marked INVALID
