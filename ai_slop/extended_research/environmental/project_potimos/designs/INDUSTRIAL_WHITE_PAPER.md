## 1. Executive Summary

Traditional wastewater treatment relies on **phase-transfer** (moving contaminants from water to a solid substrate like activated carbon), which creates a secondary waste stream requiring landfill disposal.

**Project Potimos** utilizes **Topological Bond Regularization (TBR)** to achieve **terminal mineralization** of perfluorinated compounds and microplastics. By aligning mechanical transducer frequencies with the geometric Z-constant of the vacuum (5.789 Å), we induce resonant scission of C-F (485 kJ/mol) and C-C (346 kJ/mol) bonds at a fraction of the energy cost required for standard electrochemical or thermal oxidation.

### Core Innovation: The 10¹² Frequency-Energy Scaling Bridge

| Domain | Value | Physical Regime |
|--------|-------|-----------------|
| f_Z = c/Z | 518 PHz | Photonic (UV) |
| f_Z/10¹² | 518 kHz | Acoustic (Sonochemistry) |
| Cavitation concentration | ~10¹² | Energy scaling |

The **same 10¹² factor** appears in both frequency scaling and energy concentration during cavitation. This is the "gear ratio" connecting quantum-scale bond vibrations to industrial-scale acoustic treatment.

### 3.1 Stage 1: Berry-Phase Momentum Filter

**Principle:** Topological Hall Effect for momentum-space separation

| Parameter | Specification |

**Mechanism:** Berry curvature creates anomalous velocity for polar molecules. PFAS (high electronegativity) trapped in edge states; water passes through bulk.

### 3.2 Stage 2: M-CISS Spintronic Isomer Sieve

**Principle:** Chiral-Induced Spin Selectivity for isomer separation

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| Substrate | CoFeB ferromagnetic nanopores |

| Rejection | 99% (remaining contaminants) |
| Energy | 0 (passive) |
| Isomer Selectivity | > 95% (L vs R) |

**Mechanism:** Helical PFAS chains become spin-polarized. Spin-torque repulsion rejects molecules whose spin conflicts with membrane magnetization.
