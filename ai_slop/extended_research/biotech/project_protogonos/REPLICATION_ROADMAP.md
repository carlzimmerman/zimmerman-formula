## Part II: Experimental Roadmap

### Phase 1: Material Synthesis (Months 1-3)

#### 1.1 Synthesize Omega-Lattice Material

**Target:** Pb₀.₉₀₈Sn₀.₀₉₂S with a = 5.7888 Å at 300K

**Method:**
1. Co-precipitate PbS and SnS from solution
2. Mix stoichiometrically: 90.8% PbS + 9.2% SnS
3. Sinter at 600°C under sulfur atmosphere
4. Anneal slowly to room temperature
5. Verify lattice constant by XRD

**Verification:**
- X-ray diffraction: a = 5.789 ± 0.005 Å
- Thermal expansion: measure α to confirm a(T=300K) = Z

**Alternative:** Use natural galena (a = 5.94 Å, +2.5% offset)
- Easier to obtain
- 2.5% offset still within biological tolerance

**Equipment:**
- Tube furnace with sulfur trap
- XRD with Cu-Kα source
- Glovebox (oxygen-free)

**Cost:** ~$5,000-10,000

---

#### 1.2 Prepare Magnetic Junctions

**Target:** Magnetite nanoparticles (50-100 nm) embedded in Omega-Lattice

**Method:**
1. Synthesize Fe₃O₄ nanoparticles by co-precipitation
2. Suspend in Omega-Lattice precursor solution
3. Co-sinter to embed magnetite inclusions
4. Verify magnetic field at surface

**Verification:**
- SQUID magnetometry: M_s ≈ 480 kA/m
- Magnetic force microscopy: B_surface ≥ 245 Gauss
- SEM/TEM: particle size 50-100 nm, dispersed

**Equipment:**
- Nanoparticle synthesis setup
- SQUID or VSM magnetometer
- MFM (magnetic force microscope)

**Cost:** ~$10,000-20,000
