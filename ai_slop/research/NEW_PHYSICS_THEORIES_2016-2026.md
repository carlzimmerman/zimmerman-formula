## Key Themes Across the Decade

### 1. Quantum Information → Gravity
The "It from Qubit" paradigm dominated: ER=EPR, island formula, quantum extremal surfaces, emergent spacetime from entanglement.

### 2. Dark Matter Paradigm Shift
WIMPs declined; axions, primordial black holes, fuzzy DM, and modified gravity alternatives rose.

### 3. Cosmological Tensions
DESI's evolving dark energy + Hubble tension pushed cosmology toward new physics beyond ΛCDM.

### 4. Experimental Accessibility
Graviton detection, time crystals, topological qubits moved from impossible → experimentally addressable.

### 5. Swampland Constraints
String theory shifted from "anything goes" landscape to falsifiable swampland conjectures.

## Notable Patterns

### Theories That Gained Traction
- Primordial black holes as dark matter
- Evolving dark energy (post-DESI)
- Island formula for black hole information
- Asymptotic safety quantum gravity
- Fuzzy/ultralight dark matter

### Theories That Lost Favor
- Simple WIMP dark matter
- Low-energy supersymmetry
- Static cosmological constant (challenged)
- Firewall hypothesis (superseded by soft hair/islands)

### Emerging Directions (2025-2026)
- Postquantum classical gravity (Oppenheim)
- Graviton detection experiments
- Time crystal applications
- Mirror matter / hidden sectors
- Hot dark matter revival
