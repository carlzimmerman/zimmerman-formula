**Compiled: May 2026**

**Purpose:** Systematic evaluation of which theories from the last decade actually make falsifiable, testable predictions versus those that are mathematical frameworks, interpretations, or untestable in principle.

## Scoring Criteria

- **3+ predictions**: Genuinely testable with specific, quantitative predictions
- **1-2 predictions**: Some testable aspects but limited
- **0 predictions**: Mathematical framework only, or untestable in principle
- **VERIFIED**: Already confirmed experimentally

## Critical Observations

### 1. Quantum Gravity is the weakest category
- 48% have ZERO testable predictions
- Most require Planck-scale physics or observing black hole evaporation
- Exception: LQC, Swampland conjectures, Oppenheim's theory

### 2. Dark Matter and Cosmology are strongest
- 70-75% have 3+ predictions
- Many being actively tested (ADMX, DESI, LIGO)
- These are "real physics" with experimental programs

### 3. Quantum Information theories are most verified
- 42% already experimentally confirmed
- These are laboratory-scale physics, not cosmology

### 4. "Foundational" theories are untestable
- All interpretations of QM have zero new predictions
- They reframe existing physics, don't predict new phenomena

### 5. Many "new theories" are really incremental
- Extensions of existing frameworks (LQC variants, SUSY variants)
- Mathematical reformulations (Mimetic gravity, Amplituhedron)
- Conceptual frameworks without dynamics

## What Would Constitute a TRULY New Testable Theory?

### Criteria:
1. Makes SPECIFIC quantitative predictions
2. Different from all existing theories
3. Testable with current or near-future technology
4. Not just a parameter fit or reformulation
5. Falsifiable (can be proven wrong)

### Best examples from the list:
- Oppenheim's Postquantum Classical Gravity (bet with 5000:1 odds)
- DESI Evolving Dark Energy (being tested NOW)
- Axion searches (ADMX running)
- Primordial Black Hole searches (LIGO constraining)
- X17 boson (multiple experiments attempting)

### Worst examples (labeled as "theories" but aren't):
- ER=EPR (untestable conceptual claim)
- Amplituhedron (wrong theory - N=4 SYM isn't our universe)
- Many-Worlds variants (interpretations, not theories)
- Island formula (requires impossible observations)

## Conclusion

Of 100 "new physics theories" from the last decade:

- **~40 are genuinely testable** with specific predictions
- **~25 are incremental extensions** of existing frameworks
- **~15 have no testable predictions** whatsoever
- **~7 have already been verified** (and thus aren't "new" anymore)
- **~15 are being actively tested** right now

The most productive areas for new physics are:
1. **Dark matter alternatives** (axions, PBHs, SIDM)
2. **Dark energy evolution** (DESI results)
3. **Gravitational wave cosmology** (LIGO/Virgo)
4. **Quantum information/condensed matter** (time crystals, topological phases)

The least productive area is:
1. **Quantum gravity** (most proposals are untestable)
