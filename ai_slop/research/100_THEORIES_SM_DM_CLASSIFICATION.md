## Classification Criteria

**Standard Model Based:**
- YES = Extends/modifies SM, assumes SM as foundation
- NO = Replaces SM or operates outside SM framework
- PARTIAL = Uses some SM elements but not fundamentally SM-based

**Dark Matter Framework:**
- YES = Explicitly includes or predicts dark matter particles
- NO = No dark matter (may explain via modified gravity or other means)
- ALTERNATIVE = Proposes alternative to particle DM

## Key Findings

### 1. Standard Model Dependency
- **65% of theories are SM-based** (extend or modify SM)
- **29% are not SM-based** (quantum gravity, alternative cosmologies)
- **6% are partially SM-based** (use some SM elements)

### 2. Dark Matter Distribution
- **38% explicitly include particle dark matter**
- **44% have no dark matter** (mostly QG and quantum info)
- **6% propose DM alternatives** (modified gravity)
- **12% vary** (could include DM depending on parameters)

### 3. Category Patterns

**Quantum Gravity:** Almost entirely non-SM, no dark matter
- Focus on spacetime structure, not particle content

**Dark Matter Theories:** Mostly SM-based, all include DM (by definition)
- Split between particle DM (80%) and modified gravity (20%)

**Cosmology:** Mixed SM basis, mostly includes DM
- ΛCDM framework dominates

**Particle Physics BSM:** Almost all SM-based, ~70% include DM candidates
- Natural DM candidates from SUSY, extra dimensions, hidden sectors

**Quantum Info:** All SM-based, no DM
- Laboratory-scale physics
