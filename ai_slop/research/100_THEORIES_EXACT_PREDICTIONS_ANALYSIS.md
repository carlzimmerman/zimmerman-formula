## Definition

**EXACT empirical prediction** = Specific numerical value that can be compared to measurement
- Example: "m_axion = 10 μeV" or "r = 0.003" or "τ_proton > 10³⁴ years"

**QUALITATIVE prediction** = Direction/existence without specific number
- Example: "Dark energy should evolve" or "Signal should be detectable"
