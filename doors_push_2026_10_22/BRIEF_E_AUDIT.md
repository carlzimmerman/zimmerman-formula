# Doors push, wave E (2026-10-22): audit of the screened theory's four open items

Everything in `BRIEF.md` applies. This wave adds the owner's data rule (2026-10-10) and a target.

## The target

The best-surviving candidate is the **screened theory**: PAPER49's one-field action with stiffness length
L★ ≥ 24 pc (CFG691/740/820). Galaxies follow g_N = g²/(g + a₀); systems small next to L★ (Sun, outer
globulars, wide binaries) are screened toward Newton; ultra-faint dwarfs need bound cold mass. Its four
open items: (1) the screened Cassini solve (D7d, running — do not duplicate); (2) dwarf satellites
~2 dex above Newton; (3) κ, L★ and u fitted, not derived; (4) no covariant parent.

## The owner's data rule (binding)

Every verdict compares **raw measurements** (rotation curves, velocities, dispersions, shear, counts,
ephemeris residuals) with the **framework's own predictions**, against a comparison model built the same
way. Never in a verdict: ΛCDM-calibrated machinery (halo mass functions, concentrations, r200c/NFW
priors, HMcode, hydro-sim feedback fits, ΛCDM priors, "within X% of ΛCDM" yardsticks), **and never
standard-MOND machinery as a yardstick or hidden ingredient** (QUMOND phantom-as-gravity where the
action gives something else, EFE recipes, deep-MOND shortcuts, "MOND predicts X"). If an external
ingredient is unavoidable, report it separately, labelled "externally calibrated", with the verdict's
sensitivity to it. Measurement nuisances (photo-z, shear bias, distances, SPS M/L) are survey
calibrations: list them and propagate them; do not hide them.
