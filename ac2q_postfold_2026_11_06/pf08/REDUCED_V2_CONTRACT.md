# Explicit compatibility amendment

Before further evolution, require reconstructed momentum mean minus independent
mean <=1e-10 absolute; differentiated momentum mean error <=1e-10;
unaugmented differentiated momentum equation residual <=1e-8. Keep reconstructed
momentum <=1e-8, represented canonical rate error <=1e-6 and all spectral
tails <=1e-3. These tests expose periodic solvability rather than absorbing it
in an augmented constant. No criterion is a physical singularity detector.
