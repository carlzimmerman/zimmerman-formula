# Candidate no-stress lemma allowing unbounded clock rapidity

This is a separate restricted class, not an extension of every hypothesis in
the large-r lemma. Retain bounded r,A,P, positive r lower bound, cap margin,
regular limiting metric, continuous scalar with uniform convergence and strong
same-clock endpoint Pi matching, native bulk identification and finite stress
measures. Allow the unit timelike clock n to have unbounded coordinate components.
Assume the actual limiting stress conservation law has no normal dipole on a
timelike interface. No upper bound on rapidity is used below.

In a regular conformal chart, a clock slice is a graph t=tau_label(x) with
|dt/dx|<1. Across a strip of coordinate width epsilon its proper length is at
most C epsilon, regardless of rapidity. The exact relations
 ePi=exp(2beta)D, eA=Wp/r
hold along these slices. Bounded r,A imply D_+ bounded, hence positive Pi
variation is O(epsilon). Strong matching endpoint Pi makes negative variation
o(1). Thus integral mu d ell=o(1), uniformly in the clock labels considered.
Uniform phi convergence and bounded exterior clock traces keep those labels
in a bounded interval; dt/dx bounded controls crossing times. Coarea gives
integral mu dVol=integral dphi/r integral mu d ell=o(1).

The full-switch on-shell longitudinal stress is
 T_ab=H h_ab+(rH_r-mu Q)n_a n_b, Q=1 on mu support.
Its base trace is exactly
 tr_base T=2H-rH_r+mu Q=2H-rH_r+mu.
This identity removes the unbounded boost. The first two terms are bounded by
the invariant/cap hypotheses and the last has vanishing layer L1 mass.
Therefore the base trace has no surface measure. Tperp=H+P Wp is bounded too,
so there is no transverse surface stress.

No-dipole stress conservation implies N^a S_ab=0. In the longitudinal 1+1
plane, a symmetric surface tensor orthogonal to the spacelike normal in both
slots must be S_ab=s T_a T_b for the unit timelike tangent. Its base trace is
-s. Since that trace vanishes, s=0. All surface stress is zero.

Thus arbitrarily large boosts alone cannot carry a finite timelike surface
stress while the invariant state, cap, Pi matching, coarea and finite-measure
hypotheses hold. This proof does not bound individual products mu n_a n_b;
it allows them to concentrate and cancel, but the total trace and normal
conditions force the total surface tensor to vanish.

The proof needs finite total stress measures and a regular metric so trace
and normal contractions commute with the limit. It does not exclude combined
unbounded invariant r and boost defects, cap/source concentration, unmatched
Pi traces, singular geometry, nonmeasure stresses, or unidentified bulk defects.
It also proves no outgoing-field uniqueness or existence. No P-jump route is
included because the same-clock endpoint matching remains a hypothesis here.
