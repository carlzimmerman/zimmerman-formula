# Combined concentration: a counterexample to an algebra-only exclusion

Target tested: whether native pointwise stress algebra plus a finite surface
measure and the normal no-dipole condition force every timelike surface stress
to vanish, after allowing simultaneous invariant-clock and boost escape.
That algebra-only assertion is false. This is NOT a native solution or a
nonuniqueness example for the same incoming Cauchy data.

Use an orthonormal (time,normal,y,z) frame. Let A=Astar>0,P=0. Native on-branch
states with r->infinity have H~gamma r^4/2, rH_r~2gamma r^4. A concentration
weight proportional to r^-4 gives the finite stress tensor
 S_high=diag(3c,c,c,c), c>0.
There is no multiplier in this high-r state. Q=Astar r>1; D is fixed by the
native algebraic auxiliary relation. The stress uses actual native constitutive
values, but no differentiated constraint is asserted for this sequence.

At off-branch states take rL=1/Astar, Q=1 and
 mu=2gamma rL^4+d, d>0. Then rH_r=2gamma rL^4 and the coefficient of n_a n_b
is rH_r-mu=-d. Let the clock boosts tend to opposite null directions and scale
the volume weights as c/(2d lambda^2), with
 n^a=(sqrt(1+lambda^2),+/-lambda,0,0).
Their H h_ab terms vanish in this concentration scaling, while the pair gives
 S_boost=diag(-c,-c,0,0).
The multiplier's unweighted mass tends to zero. The boosts contribute opposite
mixed components, which cancel. The total constitutive concentration is
 S_total=diag(2c,0,c,c).

It is nonzero, finite, tangent to a timelike interface (S_normal,b=0), and hence
passes the normal no-dipole algebra. Its base trace is -2c, carried by the
large-r part; the boost-only trace argument does not forbid it. Its transverse
pressure is c. This demonstrates concretely why the large-r/bounded-boost and
bounded-r/unbounded-boost lemmas cannot be combined by taking their union.

The construction uses the same finite Astar in both constituent state families
and needs no cap concentration. Nonetheless it has NOT been integrated to
fields phi,A,P and a metric satisfying their spatial/time equations, compatible
clock gradients, conservation including tangential evolution, or prescribed
incoming data. A naive smooth approximation of these algebraic states can fail
those requirements. It is not evidence that this defect is dynamically realized,
selected, or nonunique; it is an obstruction to a proposed purely algebraic
universal no-stress proof.

Next decisive route: use the exact differential constraints and clock
compatibility to exclude or construct such a combined defect from native
incoming evolution. Outgoing uniqueness requires that additional dynamical
work. Assigning the displayed surface tensor by hand would be an extension,
not a demonstrated native continuation.
