# Independent audit: regular rolling potential

**Verdict: proved under the declared local C2/positive-range and cap
conditions.** The extension introduces controlled sources, not new
principal terms. It therefore extends the audited local theory and
localized-packet breakdown argument to a selected native rolling potential
where these local conditions hold.

Let W=W(P,phi), with gamma,Khold,M constant, and set
Zpot=V_phi+W_phi. Variation of phi gives div(J_phi)=-E Zpot, so the
sign in

    n(r)=-(hK+r^2 D_r u+W_P v+2BJ+Zpot)/R

is negative. h,R,c and the fixed-A genuine-nonlinearity coefficient are
independent of V and W, since these are additive and have no r dependence.

Differentiating e(A)=W_P/r gives
n(W_P)=r W_PP v+r W_Pphi. Consequently the rescaled inverse gains
**+W_Pphi/r** in e(u), with the e(v) formula unchanged apart from the
new scalar rate. Its matrix is unchanged; only inhomogeneous terms change.

For rho=rh-H, rho_phi=+Zpot. The new contribution -r Zpot from rR n(r)
cancels +r Zpot from explicit clock dependence. The exact identity
n(rho)=-(rho+p)K-2(rho+p_perp)B survives. Since e(phi)=0, the spatial
pressure identity survives as well. The extra term in n(CA) is cancelled
by the new inverse source. All six residual equations therefore retain
their forms with the new Vrate and its partial derivatives.

No phi derivative appears in an actual spatial derivative along a clock
slice. In particular differentiating Zpot contributes only
Zpot_P e(P)=W_Pphi e(P). The exact Riemann, curvature, metric-wave and
weighted gradient formulas retain their forms. New differentiated source
terms remain bounded or linear in the controlled gradients.

## Constitutive regularity, not a hidden smoothness assumption

Assume a0(phi)>0 and V(phi)>0 on a compact clock interval, with positive
lower bounds, and V,a0 of C2 regularity with bounded derivatives there.
For the native hold law and a uniform cap margin, W_PP(P,phi) is locally
Lipschitz jointly in P,phi, although it has its original P=0 cusp.
W_Pphi is locally Lipschitz as well. Its phi derivative uses a0'' and
(a0')^2, not a third derivative of a0; its P derivative is W_PPphi,
which is bounded on this compact domain. Zpot has the required locally
bounded/Lipschitz derivatives using V'',a0'', and cap separation.

Thus the local strong-solution proof still uses uniform first Lipschitz
source bounds, not nonexistent W_PPP regularity. In clock coordinates
the coefficients are explicitly time dependent, but this adds bounded
terms to the same approximation, difference and continuation estimates.
The source's time dependence is handled on the fixed compact clock interval.

If a0^2=xi G V with fixed positive xi G and V positive C2, these a0
conditions follow locally. A potential zero, pole, nonsmooth point,
loss of cap separation or varying gamma is not covered by this argument.

The initial auxiliary/ADM construction is evaluated at phi0 and is
unchanged. The compact bootstrap time can be chosen within the chosen
potential interval, independently of packet width. Therefore sufficiently
narrow packets give the same restricted native finite-gradient-breakdown
result for such a regular rolling potential. This is not a prediction
of the observational fixture's event time or any postbreak selection.
