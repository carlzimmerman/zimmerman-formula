# Native polarization-jump stress: a bounded-layer exclusion route

Candidate derivation for audit. This removes continuous P only in the restricted
class explicitly stated here; it is not a universal singular-selection result.

## Pure off-branch defect

Suppose metric coefficients, r,A,P, unit-clock rapidity and the hold-law source
are bounded, r stays positive, and phi has a common positive tangential clock
trace across a timelike interface. Allow a downward P jump and a finite positive
KKT multiplier measure mu. All algebraic H,rH_r stresses remain bounded; the
only surface stress is
 S_ab=-weaklimit(mu Q n_a n_b), Q=1 on support(mu).
Normal stress has a definite sign: S_NN=-integral(n.N)^2 dmu. If the limiting
stress conservation law has no dipole, S_NN=0 forces n.N=0 mu-almost-everywhere.
Thus any such surface defect is negative-energy dust comoving with the clock;
it cannot be assigned an arbitrary pressure or orientation.

The clock-slice equation eA=Wp/r makes A have one common layer value A_star
under the bounded geometry/source and crossing-length assumptions. Add the
following explicit compactness condition: the tangential derivative of phi
converges to its common trace s>0 also in the multiplier-weighted layer, not
just at exterior endpoints. Then n.N=0 on multiplier mass gives r=s there;
Q=Ar=1 implies A_star=1/s if nonzero multiplier mass exists. At either outer
state timelike-clock kinematics gives r_out<=s and native Q_out>=1, hence
 1<=Q_out=A_star r_out=r_out/s<=1.
Both outer states must therefore have Q=1 and clock normal tangent to the
interface. In particular such a defect cannot join strictly on-branch exterior
states Q>1. This is independent of stationary versus time-dependent profiles.

The weighted tangential derivative condition is essential. Uniform phi
convergence alone does not identify phi_t against a concentrating mu; rapid
correlated oscillations are not excluded by this argument. Also no dipole and
native bulk-stress identification require a regular limiting geometry/current
framework. These are substantive restrictions.

## Off-branch endpoint secant obstruction

For a further restricted interface whose one-sided states lie in open off-branch
regions, Q=1 implies A=1/r and eA=Wp/r gives a=e(log r)=-Wp. The dust surface
mass comes from D=eP+2PE: in a regular transverse coordinate mu_surface=-[P]
for a downward jump, since the bounded r² and PE terms contribute no delta.

The normal Einstein jump equations for negative dust with no transverse
surface stress give
 [E]=mu_surface/(2M), [a]=mu_surface/(2M).
For the second relation use nK+ea=a²-K²+R_base/2: the interface is comoving,
so nK has no normal delta, while R_base/2 has delta mu_surface/(2M).
Combining with a=-Wp gives
 [Wp]=Khold[P], Khold=1/(2M).
But Wpp(P)=Khold/(1-Khold|P|/a0)^2>=Khold, with strict inequality except
at the single point P=0. Integrating Wpp-Khold between any distinct finite-cap
endpoint values gives a strictly positive secant excess. The equality is
impossible unless [P]=0. Thus this pure off/off finite dust jump is excluded.

This secant argument assumes the one-sided derivative of Q is zero; merely
Q=1 at an isolated switching trace is insufficient. Additional concentration
in H, rH_r, boost, cap source or metric can invalidate the pure-dust reduction.
No pair of valid native singular continuations is constructed here.
