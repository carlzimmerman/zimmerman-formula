# Exact-source scope check, 2026-11-06

Ian Tice, *Quasilinear symmetric hyperbolic systems*, author-hosted version
updated April4,2017:
https://www.math.cmu.edu/~iantice/notes/quasilinear_hyperbolic_systems.pdf

Inspected Assumption2.3.1 and Theorems2.4.1–2.4.2 (PDF pp16,19–21).
The setup requires integer k>1+n/2, sufficiently smooth local coefficients
and source, positive symmetric time matrix, and Hk data. Its frozen-system
iteration uses an L2 contraction and high-Sobolev bounds.

Application label: inapplicable as a direct theorem for the exact AC2q cusp
and nonlocal source. The smooth-approximation construction borrows the energy
method; periodic-domain adaptation, spatial-inverse mapping bounds and uniform
native Lipschitz estimates are supplied internally, not attributed to Tice.
No novelty claim is made. An Oxford/Bressan chapter was discovered but exact
access failed and it supplies no proof leaf. No full source text was retained.
