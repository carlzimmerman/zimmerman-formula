# PF11 — positively fed combined layer excluded in the regular matched class

**The positive-feed escape does not work in the stated regular matched
Einstein-interface class when continued from zero initial slice charge.**
This closes the specific remaining mechanism from PF10 without assuming finite
interior scalar-current variation. It does not prove that all singular native
continuations are impossible, or select an outgoing solution.

## Decisive coupled result

[Proof](FED_LAYER.md), [independent actual-file audit](review/FED_LAYER_AUDIT.md).
Let sigma be surface energy density, p transverse pressure, a transverse area,
v the positive clock speed along the timelike interface, and q its clock-slice
charge. The native stress and scalar contraction force

    p=sigma/2,    q=2a sigma/v.

The outer current/stress relation and the gravitational surface-energy equation
then force

    (sigma sqrt(a)/v²)'=0.

Thus sigma=C v²/sqrt(a). Zero initial slice charge fixes C=0. Inward scalar
flux cannot create the proposed sheet: the same flux must also satisfy energy
conservation, and the two rates are incompatible with growth from zero.
Matching outer flux is now a consequence, not a prior assumption.

No upper bound on invariant clock r or boost, no finite interior-current measure,
and no hyperbolicity-barrier threshold enter this theorem. The nonactivation
argument requires only positive continuous bounded clock speed, not a global
bound on its derivative. A bounded common smooth incoming slice supplies zero
charge when the controlled approximation and side-flux limits connect it to the
interface interval.

## Exact scope

The proof retains smooth native approximants, finite total stress, a regular
strictly timelike planar interface, nondegenerate metric with the ordinary
Einstein junction limit, identified regular outer equations/traces, regular
convergent side fluxes, uniform clock convergence, r bounded away from zero,
cap/source bounds, positive anchored A, and strong same-clock Pi matching with
common regular tangential traces. The initial clock-slice charge must converge
to zero. Israel plus outer Codazzi supplies energy conservation; the proof does
not assume convergence of arbitrary interior connection-times-stress products.
[Source/normalization check](GR_SOURCE_CHECK.md).

This is a necessary-equation contradiction, so solving the remaining native
equations cannot rescue a positive fed layer inside the class. Conversely,
passing these balances with C>0 would not establish a native solution.

## What this means for AC2q

The PF09 combined stress mixture is algebraically feasible but cannot arise as
an initially empty, positively fed native sheet in this class. Together with
PF09 strong-solution breakdown, this rules out that proposed repair of the
breakdown. It does not prove that every breaking datum must form precisely
this interface, and it does not establish a global continuation theorem.

If such a sheet is required while these matching/regularity conditions are
retained, a completion must change at least one balance, surface constitutive
relation or matching datum. Adding surface stress by hand is additional input;
it is not a prediction of AC2q as written. This conditional need for completion
is not a universal proof that new physical degrees of freedom are unavoidable.

Open alternatives: a native continuation losing polarization/clock matching or
cap bounds; null/degenerate interfaces; singular metric or unidentified bulk
defects; failure of regular side-flux convergence; or an uncontrolled initial
charge defect at birth. None has been constructed or shown to be uniquely
selected. A pre-existing sheet with C>0 is not a same-incoming-data example.
