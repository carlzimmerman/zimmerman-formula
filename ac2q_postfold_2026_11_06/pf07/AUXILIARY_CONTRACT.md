# PF07 exact auxiliary identities

Verify the full-switch constrained auxiliary action on and off branch, its
KKT multiplier convention, topological integration by parts, clock-slice
polarization equation, scalar current contraction, and normal canonical/Hilbert
stress identity. SymPy exact real algebra, positive gamma and nondegenerate
lapse/spatial scale. Optional reversed topological sign must fail the matching
of the polarization equation. These algebra checks do not prove the separate
compactness, trace, crossing, or boundedness hypotheses of the layer theorem.
