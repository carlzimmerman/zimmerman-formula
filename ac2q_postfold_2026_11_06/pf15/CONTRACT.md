Research base a3aa3abdeb (PF14). Target remains a full native continuation and
uniqueness/nonexistence result, not merely another necessary condition.

R1: derive the Cantor-part identities from the original bounded weak equations.
Test whether Einstein stress conservation adds a condition beyond scalar and
clock compatibility on diffuse singular derivatives.

source; determine what would and would not follow even if Cantor parts vanish.

or whether an absolutely continuous but unbounded derivative is still allowed.
