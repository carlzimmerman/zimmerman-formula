# Checked SBV theorem and the missing application

Primary source: Fabio Ancona, Laura Caravenna, Andrea Marson,
*SBV regularity of Entropy Solutions for Hyperbolic Systems of Balance Laws
with General Flux function*, arXiv:2409.06087v4 (header dated 1 September 2025; internal date
3 September 2025), retrieved PDF checked 2026-11-06.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2409.06087
The fetched 36-page rendering contains Theorem 7 (SBV-regularity) on PDF p3.
The first-page footer identifies the exact v4 revision. No full text is retained
locally and no local source hash is claimed.

Theorem 7 assumes a strictly hyperbolic C1 matrix, genuine nonlinearity (or its
stated nondegeneracy extension), small total variation, and the vanishing-viscosity
solution constructed in the cited theory. Source assumption (G) requires time
continuity, uniform Lipschitz dependence on x and state, and an integrable
spatial-derivative envelope. Its conclusion removes spatial Cantor parts outside
at most countably many times; the paper also states spacetime SBV regularity.

Application to AC2q is unverified. Its auxiliary Jacobian is bounded in the weak
class, but neither a closed source satisfying (G) nor identification of a native
full-Einstein solution with the selected viscosity process has been proved.
The theorem therefore cannot currently eliminate AC2q Cantor parts or select
its continuation. Even a successful SBV application would still require an
argument controlling unbounded absolutely continuous derivatives.

A second search hit, the older *The Chain Rule for the Divergence of BV-like
Vector Fields*, labels its Theorem 7.2 consequences CONDITIONAL on a positive
answer to its Question 6.2. It is not used here as an unconditional SBV theorem.
No claim about absence of other applicable literature or global novelty is made.
