Base 191cfbc063 (PF17). Original target: native weak existence and unique
continuation, or a proved obstruction requiring extension. Do not substitute
a conditional transport theorem for this target.

R1: remove opposite-family compression using the native integrating factor;
identify exactly which divergence remains.

non-Lipschitz transport criterion, with the native flux and source explicitly
identified. Determine whether the criterion follows from native bounds.

linear wave transport from full coupled existence/uniqueness.
