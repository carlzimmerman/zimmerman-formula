# Exact source checks, 2026-10-28

These are bounded source-to-target checks, not a literature-wide novelty
audit. Primary text was opened through web tools. No full copyrighted text
is copied into the repository. Equations in DERIVATION.md were reconstructed
locally; the following external implications retain their stated conditions.

## Vacuum thermodynamics

F. R. Klinkhamer and G. E. Volovik, *Self-tuning vacuum variable and
cosmological constant*, Physical Review D 77, 085015 (2008),
[arXiv:0711.3170v6, 17 April 2008](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0711.3170v6).
The exact PDF title/version and sections II.A-II.B were checked.
Equations (10)-(13) identify equilibrium vacuum energy as epsilon-q epsilon_q
and inverse compressibility as q^2 epsilon_qq. Zero external pressure fixes
zero equilibrium gravitating energy; stiffness remains microscopic input.
Their V denotes volume; our V denotes energy density. Our s-to-q mapping
is an additional hypothesis, not a conclusion of this source. Verdict:
thermodynamic identities verified; application to PN28 conditional.

## Dilaton relation

D. K. Hong, J.-W. Lee, B. Lucini, M. Piai and D. Vadacchino,
*Casimir scaling and Yang–Mills glueballs*, Physics Letters B 775 (2017),
89-93, [published PDF](https://scoap3-prod-backend.s3.cern.ch/media/files/22067/10.1016/j.physletb.2017.10.050_a.pdf),
DOI 10.1016/j.physletb.2017.10.050. Section 3.2, equations (13)-(17),
was checked. The PCDC equation f_D^2 m_D^2=-16 E_vac assumes light-state
saturation; the condensate-independent contribution is subtracted.
Consequently it concerns a sector energy with negative sign for positive
mass squared, not a predicted positive cosmological vacuum. Identifying
this dilaton and its decay constant with the PN28 scalar is unproved.
Verdict: source identity and its qualifications verified; direct positive-V
normalization inapplicable, proposed extended transfer conditional.

## Known logarithmic limit

K. Boshkayev, R. D'Agostino and O. Luongo, *Extended logotropic fluids as
unified dark energy models*, European Physical Journal C 79, 332 (2019),
[published article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1140/epjc/s10052-019-6854-9),
12 April 2019, DOI 10.1140/epjc/s10052-019-6854-9. Equations (34)-(37)
derive a shifted negative-power pressure's logarithmic limit with the
exponent tending to zero and amplitude diverging at fixed product.
The source density is its fluid density, whereas VR28 uses an internal y
and derives a separate particle density rho(y). Overlap label: known after
translation of the pressure variable for the elementary limit; only an
adjacent result for the complete stream/scalar construction. The source
does not fix our exponent, vacuum offset or state-selection mechanism.

Searches used vacuum-compressibility, dilaton-anomaly and
Chaplygin/logotropic-limit queries. No global priority claim, exhaustive
classification of UV models, or transfer of their cosmological fits was made.

## Additional quantum-stability discriminator

S. P. Martin, *Two-loop effective potential for a general renormalizable
theory and softly broken supersymmetry*,
[arXiv:hep-ph/0111209v2, 29 March 2002](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-ph/0111209v2).
Equations (1.1),(1.3),(3.2) were checked: the real-scalar one-loop term has
coefficient 1/(64pi^2), field-dependent mass to the fourth power, and the
modified-minimal-subtraction constant 3/2. We use only that scalar Gaussian
term. The exponential EFT application is justified separately by its
quadratic fluctuation operator, not by claiming the paper proves that an
exponential theory is renormalizable. No two-loop or supersymmetry result
is imported. Verdict: normalization verified; relevance to PN28 conditional
on the explicitly posited relativistic vacuum-sector extension.
