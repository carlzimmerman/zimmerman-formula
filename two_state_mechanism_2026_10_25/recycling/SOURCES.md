# Source check

- Ryan, Gurian, Shandera & Jeong, *Molecular Chemistry for Dark Matter*,
  arXiv:2106.13245v2 (5 Aug2022),
  https://arxiv.org/html/2106.13245 . Sections IV–V scale ordinary-hydrogen
  reaction rates and discuss their limitations. The work explicitly distinguishes
  that method from fresh multiparticle calculations. It does not supply exact
  state-resolved collision amplitudes for our mass ratio100 spectrum. No fitted
  rate or neutral-molecule binding energy from that work was imported here.
- Hassaine et al., *Electron impact ro-vibrational transitions and dissociative
  recombination of H2+ and HD+*, arXiv:2507.14602 (2025),
  https://arxiv.org/html/2507.14602 . Its MQDT construction uses neutral-system
  channels, channel couplings and nuclear states. It reports state dependence
  and differences from older rates. These ingredients are absent from our
  one-electron g/u calculation; dimensional rescaling cannot be labeled an exact
  replacement. No rate from that work was imported.
- Galli & Palla, *The Chemistry of the Early Universe*,
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/astro-ph/9803315 . Consulted for reaction inventory;
  no numerical coefficient imported.

These are context and scope checks, not numerical certification of our code.
No novelty claim is made. Reaction and photon equations are derived in the
local files and tested independently against their stated invariants.
