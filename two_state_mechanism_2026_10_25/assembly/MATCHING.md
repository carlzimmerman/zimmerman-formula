# Connecting the molecular parent to local effective parameters

The potential determines a local spring curvature K=U_g''(R_eq) in E_H/a_D²,
not an independent fitted stiffness. Nuclear vibration has Omega_vib=sqrt(K/mu_N)
in E_H/hbar units. The same electronic surfaces determine Delta_gu=U_u-U_g.
At M100, the finite-difference check gives K≈.0628331, Omega_vib≈.0354494,
Delta_gu≈.275599. These scales are not resonant (ratio≈7.77).

This does NOT identify the homonuclear radial vibration with the polar oscillator
of the previous Rabi benchmark. Radial ground-surface vibration is inversion-even
and cannot supply the missing g-u electric-dipole perturbation by itself. Neighbor
asymmetry or another actual polar mode must be derived. The result illustrates
why setting a local mode to resonance is not a material derivation. No nodal band
or critical isotropic spectrum follows just from the molecular bound state.

The audit also independently reconstructs ideal-species Helmholtz free energy
from masses, the atomic partition and the computed molecular partition. It
checks reaction chemical potentials and U=-T² d(F/T)/dT at fixed composition,
confirming the internal binding/rotational energy used in the isolated endpoint.
These checks validate the restricted thermodynamics, not omitted chemistry.
