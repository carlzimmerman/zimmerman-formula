# Independent pre-comparison background derivation

This calculation was written by the coordinator from the raw round-one ADM action before reading the round-two relativity derivation. It checks only the spatially flat, vacuum, homogeneous solution and transverse polarization sector; it does not audit the scalar reduction.

With T=t, P=0, N=N(t), h_ij=a(t)^2 delta_ij, the action per comoving volume (after the Einstein boundary term) is

L = -3(2+3 beta) a adot^2/(4 K N) - Lambda N a^3/(2 K).

The lapse equation gives H^2 = 2 Lambda/[3(2+3 beta)], where H=adot/(a N). The scale-factor equation at N=1 gives (2+3 beta)(2 a addot+adot^2)=2 Lambda a^2; constant positive H solves both. Thus a fixed bare Lambda does not give the GR Hubble rate when beta is nonzero.

If S1 means a0=kappa sqrt(Lambda/(8 pi)) in c=1 units with rho_DE=Lambda/(8 pi G), then a0/H=kappa sqrt[3(2+3 beta)/(16 pi)]. This is conditional on that dictionary for vacuum energy; interpreting an inferred cosmological density with a different cosmological Newton constant is a separate mapping, not an algebraic replacement.

At P=0 the transverse comoving covector perturbation has quadratic action a eta dot(P_T)^2/2 - a K P_T^2/2 - gamma k^2 P_T^2/(2a). The longitudinal lapse coupling drops out by transversality. Its equation is ddot(P_T)+H dot(P_T)+(K/eta+gamma k^2/(eta a^2))P_T=0. In physical p_T=P_T/a on exact de Sitter it becomes ddot(p_T)+3H dot(p_T)+(2H^2+K/eta+gamma k^2/(eta a^2))p_T=0. Positivity of these coefficients is not a claim about the constrained scalar sector or nonlinear health.

External leaves: none; the starting action and S1 dictionary are assumptions. Independent derivation scope: homogeneous lapse/scale-factor variation and transverse quadratic action only. The scalar sector requires a separate derivation and review.
