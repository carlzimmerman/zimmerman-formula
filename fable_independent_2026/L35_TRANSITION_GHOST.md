## The short answer

reduction, the entire scalar UV kinetic coefficient is generated by the switch's derivatives and is an exact
total derivative in disguise:

```
a_UV  =  -(1/b) d/dr [ b(r)^2 eta'(r) ],        b(r) = E (G r^2 + B E/3)/12,
G = 1/J - 1 = e^{2w + 1/6} - 1,                 B = e^S f(S).
```

## 2. Why it happens: both plateaus are exactly marginal

`a_UV = E A/6 + h,rhorho/4` with `A = 1 + eta G`. For **any locally constant** `eta`, the `E A/6` term cancels
the `-2EA/3` inside `h,rhorho/4` identically — checked at eta = 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1 to 4e-62. Both plateaus
therefore return `a_UV = 0`, not a positive number. The healthy plateau result is a *different* reduction
(IC10/IC11's algebraic pressure elimination: `PX = 1.5547 > 0`, `Q_clock = 6.0332 > 0`, `cs^2 = 0.2577`), which
this lane also reproduces.

The consequence is the whole story: **every bit of the scalar UV kinetic coefficient comes from `eta'` and
`eta''`.** Derived symbolically for an arbitrary `C^2` switch and an arbitrary `rho`-independent pressure:

```
a_UV = -(E G r/3) eta'  -  (E G r^2/12 + B E^2/36) eta''
     = -(1/b) d/dr[ b^2 eta' ],   b = E(G r^2 + B E/3)/12.
```

In IC12's own 501 samples the switch factor `4 r eta' + r^2 eta''` is negative at **all 419** transition
samples — it never changes sign — while `G = 1/J - 1` crosses zero at n = 207, which is the same step at which
`a_UV` crosses zero. The pressure's own contribution never exceeds **4.5 %** of `a_UV`. So in the lead's run,
what flipped was the *background*, not the switch and not the pressure.

## 3. The theorem, hypotheses stated exactly

**Setting.** `h = h_base + eta(r)[delta_10 - B]`, `h_base = -E rho^2/3 + (rho-independent)`,
`delta_10 = -E rho^2 G/3`, `r = -rho E/3`, trace kinetic factor `A = 1 + eta G`. `E > 0`, and `G` and `B`
depend only on `(xi, u)` — **not** on `rho` — for any pressure of the form `f(Xtilde)`, because `Xtilde` is
fixed by `(xi, u)`. `a_UV = E A/6 + h,rhorho/4`.

**Lemma** (S1, S2 — derived, not assumed). `a_UV = -(1/b) d/dr[b(r)^2 eta'(r)]`, `b = E(G r^2 + B E/3)/12`.

**THEOREM.** Fix `(xi, u)` with `G != 0`. Let `eta` be `C^2` and non-constant on a transition interval
`[r1, r2]` with `eta'(r1) = eta'(r2) = 0`. Then `a_UV < 0` on a subset of positive measure of `(r1, r2)`, for
**every** `B` in R.

*Proof.* `b` is a quadratic in `r` with at most one positive root `r0`. Put `beta = r0` if `r0` lies in
`(r1, r2)`, else `beta = r2`. On `(r1, beta)`, `b` has one strict sign, and integrating the Lemma against the
weight `b`, `int_{r1}^{beta} b a_UV dr = -[b^2 eta']_{r1}^{beta} = 0`, because `eta'(r1) = 0` and either
`b(beta) = 0` or `eta'(r2) = 0`. If `a_UV >= 0` there, then `b a_UV` has one sign and integrates to zero, so
`a_UV = 0` on `(r1, beta)`, hence `b^2 eta'` is constant = its value 0 at `r1`, hence `eta` is constant there;
repeating on `(beta, r2)` (where `eta'(beta) = 0` by continuity) makes `eta` constant on all of `[r1, r2]`.
Contradiction. ∎

**COROLLARY (pressure-blind).** At any interior `r*` with `eta''(r*) = 0` — one exists, since `eta'` vanishes
at both ends and not in between — `a_UV(r*) = -(E G r*/3) eta'(r*)`, containing no `B` at all. The IC5 switch
has a **rising** branch (`r^2` in (1/2, 3/4)) and a **falling** branch (`r^2` in (5/4, 3/2)) with opposite
`sign(eta')`, while `sign(G)` is a single number at fixed `(xi, u)`; so one of the two branches is ghostly
whatever `f` is chosen. Verified numerically: raising `B` by `1e6` leaves `a_UV(r*)` unchanged to 12 digits,
and for **each** sign of `G`, all three switches tested are negative on one branch.

**Along a trajectory**, where `E`, `G`, `B` vary with `r`, the same conclusion follows with the weight
`mu = exp(int a/b)/b`, since `mu b = exp(int a/b) > 0` kills the boundary term and `sign(mu) = sign(b)`. The
extra hypothesis is that `b` does not vanish on an open subinterval. Section 5 tests this case on the model's
own solved trajectories.

**Numerical corroboration.** 7 switches (IC5 mollifier, smoothstep, smootherstep, raised cosine, a `t^3`-warped
skew, a wiggly one, a two-stage one) × 9 pressures (0, IC12's, 1e-4, 1e-2, the value tuned so `b` changes sign
inside the interval, 1, 1e3, 1e6, and a **negative** one) = **126/126 combinations have `a_UV < 0` somewhere in
the transition**; the least negative minimum over all 126 is −0.0168. The b-weighted integral vanishes to a
relative 1e-64.

**What is NOT claimed.** Nothing about Hamiltonian redesigns that break the Lemma's setting — in particular a
correction whose second `rho`-derivative does **not** cancel the `E A/6` term, so that the plateaus stop being
marginal. That is the door this lane leaves open, and it is where IC13/IC14 should push.

radius. `eta = 1` for `r^2` in [3/4, 5/4]; `eta = 0` for `r^2 <= 1/2` and `r^2 >= 3/2`.

* **The Solar System and the galactic disc are exactly ghost-free.** A static system has `p = 0` identically,

## 6. Verdict

**The transition ghost is generic.** It is not a property of IC12's pressure function: it follows from the
exact marginality of both plateaus, which makes the whole scalar UV kinetic coefficient a total derivative in
`b^2 eta'` that must change sign across any smooth interpolation, and from a pressure-blind inflection point
where `B` multiplies zero. The pressure nevertheless has real, verified power on the sliver the lead's run
occupies, where the whole condition is `G r^2 + e^S f(S) E/3 > 0` — met most cheaply by the geometric
condition `(1 - u) xi < 1/12`, whose crossing is *exactly* where IC12's ghost begins — and an explicit
admissible `f = 0.05 (2X)^4` takes that route and keeps `a_UV > 0` at every transition sample it solves; but
every pressure whose solution goes deep into the transition turns ghostly later, so this is a deferral and
should be reported to IC13/IC14 as one. Priced honestly, the ghost is absent from the Solar System and the
disc and lives in a 0.2 Mpc-thick turnaround shell at 1.5–1.7 Mpc around a Milky Way (13–15 Mpc around a
cluster), where an O(1) wrong-sign kinetic coefficient grows at a rate unbounded in `k`, the slowest fitting
mode e-folding in 0.115 Myr.

**The one door left open, stated as the next constructive task:** the theorem's setting requires that the
`eta`-multiplied correction's second `rho`-derivative cancel the `E A/6` term, which is what makes both
plateaus marginal. A redesign that breaks that cancellation — leaving a *positive* `a_UV` on the plateaus
rather than zero — is outside everything proved here. That, not another pressure function, is where IC13/IC14
should push.
