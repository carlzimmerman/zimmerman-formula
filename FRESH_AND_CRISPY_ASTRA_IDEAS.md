## F01 — Prove what a density-only carrier model forgets

**Calculate:** Compare phase-conjugate homogeneous carrier states with identical species densities, total charge, full stress and auxiliary source. Derive whether their charge-transfer rates have opposite signs, then identify the smallest extra coherence variables that close the first two derivatives.

**Prediction / decisive test:** An admissible equal-input/different-rate pair gives a lower bound on every density-only closure's local error; otherwise show which retained observable distinguishes it.

**Why this is a different calculation:** CONVERSION already derives current exchange; C37 evolves conversion. This asks for an exact information-loss theorem.

**Start from:** `conversion`, `perspective`.

## F02 — Find the large-scale noise caused by missing kick recoil

**Calculate:** Expand a localized event's later density change into mass, displacement-dipole and quadrupole moments. Compare single-tracer kicks, deterministic velocity shells and locally balanced recoil events, retaining tracer-mass dependence.

**Prediction / decisive test:** Determine the leading small-k stochastic power and whether it survives the continuum limit. Require a modeled recoil reservoir; removing net box momentum is insufficient.

**Why this is a different calculation:** L377 kicks tracers and measures power; neither it nor C20 isolates the event-multipole conservation defect.

**Start from:** `pm`, `twomode`.

## F03 — Test whether distant laboratories remain coupled by the global mean

**Calculate:** Join two fixed-volume compact regions by a shrinking neck. Derive the constrained susceptibility of mean t in A to a source change in B, keeping the single whole-leaf mean-one constraint.

**Prediction / decisive test:** Prove factorization or obtain a nonzero limiting susceptibility with volume-ratio scaling. Componentwise normalization is a different action; instantaneous response alone does not violate criterion B.

**Why this is a different calculation:** C11 sends volume to infinity; this keeps volume finite and removes geometric communication. Generic cluster-decomposition prose is not this calculation.

**Start from:** `host`, `barrier`.

## F04 — Glue the heat-field action across a transparent interface

**Prediction / decisive test:** Recover the uncut semigroup, adjoint and force without a spurious surface source; bound the error from independent periodic or Neumann subdomain filters.

**Start from:** `host`.

## F05 — Derive the finite-filter virial defect

**Prediction / decisive test:** Obtain an exact dilation identity and a controlled finite-xi correction, or an explicit structure-dependent virial normalization. Recover the unfiltered limit.

**Why this is a different calculation:** g02c explicitly restricts its scale-invariant virial benchmark to xi=0; a finite-filter Ward identity was not located.

**Start from:** `twobody`.

## F06 — Account for cosmological work done by the filter

**Calculate:** For S_a=exp[xi² Delta_com/(2a²)], differentiate the on-shell canonical energy on a periodic expanding background, keeping constant proper xi and a0 and retaining dS_a/dtime.

**Prediction / decisive test:** Derive a Layzer-Irvine-type balance and quantify the secular error from dropping filter work. Distinguish a prescribed-background target calculation from a common-action cosmology.

**Why this is a different calculation:** Homogeneous FRW has no spatial heat contribution; static energy derivatives do not calculate expansion work.

**Start from:** `twobody`, `frw`. **Needs:** F05.

## F08 — Predict the phantom-force noise from actual discrete stars

**Prediction / decisive test:** Produce a physical covariance floor and a criterion for smooth-baryon validity. Separate real stellar discreteness from numerical macroparticle noise.

**Start from:** `collective`, `zero`.

## F10 — Resolve the light-neutral-mass memory map
