# The Kepler Stories

Short fiction about Johannes Kepler, read in order.

1. [The Oracle of Prague](01_the_oracle_of_prague.md). In a Prague where computing engines came before telescopes, a crystal Oracle trained on Tycho's data absorbs every idea it is given. Kepler beats it with a sealed prediction the sky can check.
2. [The Outermost Solid](02_the_outermost_solid.md). A cold, glowing sphere and a brass cube carry Kepler past the planets and out to other stars, then down onto a zoysia lawn in 2026, where a man at the door has been expecting him.
3. [Two Cups](03_two_cups.md). Kepler wakes in a Southern ranch house. Over coffee, his host Everett explains the year, the new world, the trouble with modern gold, and what he needs Kepler for.
