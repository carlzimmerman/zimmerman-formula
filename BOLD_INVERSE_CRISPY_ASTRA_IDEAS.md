## I01 — Invert the force map subject to positive baryonic mass

**Calculate:** Given a synthetic resolved potential, invert the full double-filter source-to-force map with rho_b>=0, fixed total mass and bounded source size. Build a conservative feasible-source envelope rather than naive inverse heat amplification.

**Prediction / decisive test:** Predict a minimum baryonic concentration or prove that no source in the stated class can produce the target force. A finite optimizer failing is not an exclusion.

**Start from:** `zero`, `xc5`.

## I07 — Reconstruct missing stress and test whether five fields can realize it

## I08 — Infer nonlinear saturation from linear conversion gain

**Prediction / decisive test:** Predict a second amplitude-dependent observable with no independent quartic fit. Keep mass/coupling degeneracies and finite-time gain ambiguity explicit.

**Start from:** `conversion`, `pump`.

## I09 — Optimize a physical source that separates the two exponential laws

**Calculate:** Within a fixed positive two-component source family, solve exact-RAR and exact-AQUAL operators separately and optimize a nuisance-projected tidal/lensing contrast subject to fixed mass, size and resolution.

**Prediction / decisive test:** Predict a realizable source geometry and a discriminating ratio, or bound the maximum separation below the stated precision. Do not identify the two spherical interpolation laws.

**Start from:** `xc5`, `weighted`.

## I10 — Build a three-wavenumber null for the heat filter

**Calculate:** Derive the linear phantom susceptibility around a nonzero uniform external field at three physical wavenumbers. Eliminate its common amplitude and xi from the logarithmic response ratios, retaining tensor orientation and both filter placements.

**Prediction / decisive test:** Predict a parameter-eliminated consistency relation plus leading finite-amplitude corrections. Track response signs and compare each kernel separately; failure would test the filter architecture.

**Start from:** `zero`, `xc3`, `recipe`.
