## 4. Disclosures and limitations

- **Kernel.** The estimator's law uses ν_e (CFG933's sealed kernel). The S2 action's law is g_N = g²/(g + a0). At the Coma
  UDGs' g_bar/a0 the two differ by at most 4.2e-4 in ln ν. The engine's E is a ratio within S2's own law.
- **HUD stellar masses (R6).** They use a declared round Υ_g ∈ {0.5, 1, 2} and M_g,⊙ = 5.11, not a colour–M/L relation. The
  colour errors in the table are large. Gas mass is 1.33 M_HI. Global f_gas is used; the HI is more extended than the stars,
  so the enclosed gas fraction inside r½ is lower. That can keep more of the inner medium bound, but bound medium is still at
  most on the law (§1).
- **H-EX (R4).** "Cold share" here means the law-medium difference between the pre- and post-loss baryons, frozen at r12.
  It is an upper bound for a free component; its dynamics after the loss is not modelled.
- **D-T hover (R5).** Only the scale ratio is given. The record has no solved satellite-hover dynamics (CFG945 is open).
- **β-model.** The host field comes from Freundlich+2022's X-ray hydrostatic model as coded in L23. It is a total
  (observed) field. Its law inversion gives the phantom part.
- **Engine scope.** The baryon loss is uniform in radius and γ = 0 (CFG939's N4 convention; CFG939's N5 has Mach > 1 at r½
  for these systems). The pre-loss core stays frozen (CFG939's convention, a +0.001 bias at most). The medium is truncated at
  R0 = 50 r_M, inside the catchment.
- **Q2 rule.** The frozen rule rewards any E_ceil > 0, however small. This was not anticipated, and the verdict is reported
  as computed, with the flag above.
- No new data were downloaded. Everything is the record's: the Coma table (Freundlich+2022, as used by L23/CFG31/CFG933),
  CFG655's DF2/DF4 table, the Lelli+15 TDG table (via CFG939), and the Leisman+17 HUD table.
