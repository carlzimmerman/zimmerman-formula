## 7. Controls and MUTATE (each flips a load-bearing cell; exit 1 when the control bites)

- **C3 (reported):** vector versus table at R_e (attack d1).

- **M1 (inject a known offset):** D_obs x 10^{+0.20} for every disc: every median delta must rise by exactly 0.2000 (1e-9), the rival's class must leave DISFAVOURED-under, the flat law's must become DISFAVOURED-over. Bites (exit 1) iff both class changes happen.
