2. **CFG240 reading (7b73ef6a0).** T4 (`T4_sigma_floor`): σ(log a₀) ≥ 3σ/√N with the baryon calibration free is **0.60 dex for N = 1** at σ = 0.2 dex per galaxy; the break-even design needs y ≳ 8 with deep points (y < 0.3) and the single galaxy has neither, only intermediate y; T3 (`T3_newton_limit`) does not bite at y ≤ 1.2. **So, as in lanes A and D, the regime is conditioned and what limits the lane is the baryon side (a lower limit: no gas on disk, no stellar size) and the beam, not CFG240's conditioning theorem.**
