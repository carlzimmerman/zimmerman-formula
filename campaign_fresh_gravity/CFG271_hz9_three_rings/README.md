## Bottom line
1. **At the outer ring (R = 2.68 kpc = 0.99 r_D) the pressure-corrected g_obs = (V² + 2σ₀² R/r_D)/R is 4.40e-10 m/s² (4.7 a₀; 3.77e-10 with rotation only, the pressure term adds 0.067 dex), against a stars-only g_bar of 1.06e-10 for log M\* = 10.3 (M103) and 3.86e-11 for 9.86 (M986):** D = g_obs/g_bar,\* = **4.14 (M103) and 11.4 (M986)**, δ_FLAT = **+0.43 and +0.73 dex**. **Both rows have a root, which is an UPPER bound: s\* ≤ 14.8 (M103; 68 % 5.9–35, 95 % 1.8–72) and ≤ 48.9 (M986; 68 % 21–111, 95 % 8.5–239).** No floor, no ceiling. Both sit above the H(z) value 9.43 and the PROXY 6.28 and far above FLAT's 1: **with stars only the bound is vacuous for the three laws.**
2. **What FLAT at the canonical scale would need is a total baryon mass of 7.1 × 10¹⁰ M☉ whatever the branch:** extra gas of **2.56 × M\*** for M103 (total 3.56 × 2.0e10) and **8.81 × M\*** for M986 (total 9.81 × 7.2e9), the same disc mass because it is set by the dynamics. The extrapolated scaling gas of the class-D sensitivity (B2; μ_mol 1.36 and 2.06; **z = 5.54 is outside the range the relation was calibrated on**) supplies a total of 4.7e10 (M103) and 2.2e10 (M986): it leaves roots at **s\*(B2) = 3.8 (M103) and 12.9 (M986)**, i.e. the scaling gas is short of the FLAT requirement in both branches; M103's B2 root lies between FLAT and the PROXY, M986's above both the PROXY and H(z).

4. **The stellar scale is the dominant systematic.** No stellar size is on disk, so the [CII] disc scale (r_D = 2.7 kpc, Parlanti+23) is borrowed for the stars. **A compact stellar disc (scale ÷ 3, 0.9 kpc) raises g_bar at 2.68 kpc by 0.59 dex and lowers the bound by 1.59 dex for M103 (14.8 → 0.38, below the canonical scale) and by 0.73 dex for M986 (48.9 → 9.1, the H(z) value);** scale × 1.5 raises the bounds by +0.41 / +0.36 dex. The other knobs are smaller: rotation only −0.16 / −0.14, σ₀ = 73 +0.14 / +0.13, the SINS convention +0.09, inclination 51° / 65° +0.15 / −0.11 (M103) and +0.14 / −0.10 (M986), spherical baryons +0.03 / +0.02, kernel P2 +0.09 / +0.04. The recipe half-widths are **1.61 dex (M103) and 0.76 dex (M986)** — the first dominated by the compact-stars knob — against 68 % statistical half-widths of 0.39 and 0.36 dex. **The ±0.15 dex baryon bands span 9.2–22.8 (M103) and 33–71 (M986); the ±0.30 bands 5.2–34 and 22–102.**
5. **The stellar-mass conflict does not close.** The two branches are two baryon assumptions 0.44 dex apart (Parlanti+23 Table 2 via Capak+2015, 9.86 ± 0.23, IMF unstated; the corpus row's 10.3 with no source or error, declared ± 0.20), **never pooled and neither preferred**; they differ by a factor 3.3 in the bound (14.8 against 48.9) and agree on the baryon total FLAT would need. **Neither branch's bound discriminates FLAT, H(z) or PROXY once the stellar scale and the gas are uncertain: class D, no law statement.**
6. **Regime and beam.** The stage-A pre-flight found the baryon side conditioned (y = g_bar/a₀ = 1.13 for M103 and 0.41 for M986 at the outer ring; lever −2.6 and −1.8; no ILL-CONDITIONED row), unlike lanes C and E, and **the CFG240 conditioning theorem (7b73ef6a0) is not what limits the lane: the lower-limit baryon side, the unresolved stellar scale and the beam are** (T4's floor for one galaxy is 0.6 dex at 0.2 dex per galaxy). **BEAM-LIMITED: the beam is 1.0″ = 6.09 kpc FWHM (the corpus quotes 6.5 kpc), HWHM 3.05 kpc; R/HWHM = 0.18 / 0.53 / 0.88, so all three rings lie inside the HWHM; the rotation interpretation passes 2 of 5 W15 criteria (PA mismatch 66°); the third ring's σ = 4.8 ± 8 km/s is suspect.**

## Disclosures
- **The lane is not blind** (criteria §0). The pre-flight conditioning verdict (conditioned, drawable) differs from the request's expectation of NOT POSSIBLE: the bound is drawable but vacuous and beam-limited; the labels carry that.
- **σ₀ = 51 ± 9.5 km/s is Parlanti+23's Method-II value** (a 1-D fit to the flux map and integrated spectrum, the paper divides it by 1.6; it agrees with the corpus's galaxy-mean ring σ = 50.3, which includes the suspect third ring); **r_D = 2.7 kpc is their [CII] disc scale, not an effective radius**, and is borrowed for the stellar disc. **The inclination draw 58 ± 7° is added to the Monte Carlo** because the corpus states that it is not in the V errors (the Mrot rows take none: their 0.4 dex is the paper's). The z of every row is 5.5413 (Parlanti's 5.529 is noted).
- The stat columns of the points file hold percentiles of the Monte Carlo draws that have a root; the Mrot rows have 4–6 % of draws without a root. The downward baryon-band corners violate the stars-only lower limit and are labelled so. The mechanical flags are FLAT `nnn` (and `YYY` only for the widest row, M103 Mrot, whose 95 % interval 0.56–462 contains 1), H(z) `YYY` for five rows (`nnn` for M986 three rings), PROXY `YYY` for four; for upper bounds on a lower-limit baryon side they are not a law statement. One measurement run; no re-run to change a result.
- **Nothing was fetched.** A gas mass ([CII] or dust luminosity) and a stellar size for HZ9 are the two missing pieces; a cube-based fit would need a download and the owner's go in this chat.

## What this lane cannot say
It does not measure a₀ at z = 5.54: one galaxy, a model-determined curve inside one beam, a weak rotation interpretation, an M\* conflict of 0.44 dex, no gas (a lower limit), no stellar size (a 1.6 dex systematic), and an extrapolated scaling relation for the gas sensitivity. A floor NO ROOT with stars only would be robust against any added gas; a root with stars only is an upper bound on s\*; class D carries no law statement; the two stellar-mass branches are never pooled.
