## Bottom line

**None of the three readings gives candidate B a mechanism.**
- **Reading (i): a cosmic front.**
  - It can meet the CMB epoch only if the passage is initial data or at least 99 times faster than light. Even then its imprint must be a clustering density, so the reading reduces to B's cold component.
  - A front at light speed crosses only 1.0% of the last-scattering sphere by recombination.
  - The act of compacting is door 11B′'s class, which is already a no-go.
- **Reading (ii): a passage at formation.** Depending on what "formation" means, it is (iii-a) or (iii-b).
- **Reading (iii-a): set at each system's own first turnaround, then frozen.**
  - It reproduces FG001's ownership classes. It passes the committed tidal-dwarf test (Newtonian, χ² 1.09 for 6) and inherits Cassini through ownership.
  - That is bookkeeping, not a mechanism. As a latch inside an action it is CFG48 G3's FAIL; as a label it is CFG48 G3's PARTIAL.
  - Its amount either fails (a literal freeze is 15.6× short at the edge) or is B's law restated.
- **Reading (iii-b): a pass at any collapse.**
  - It fails the tidal dwarfs (the law is worse than Newton by Δχ² +5.2 to +12.0) and Cassini (1258–1545× the planetary bound).
  - It loses DR4 Arm C.
- **The sweeping idea (T5).**
  - A front from one direction clusters nothing, because its divergence is zero.
  - Sweeping needs a sink, which is door 11A (16–205× the orbital energy).
  - In standard cosmology Λ suppresses late growth by 21%.

**What 11D adds beyond B:** at most, (iii-a) names the ownership label's assignment rule as the system's first decoupling from the Hubble flow. That is B's own turnaround switch plus a freeze. The rule fills the "one unmodelled assignment rule" that CFG48 listed for the prescribed label. It does not supply the dynamics, the amount, or the cold mass, and **the mass is still required**.

## Reading (iii) in detail (H1–H4)

- **H1: PARTIAL.** "Compacted at the system's own first turnaround, frozen after" assigns every FG001 class correctly:
  - top-level systems own;
  - accreted systems keep what they owned at infall;
  - tidal dwarfs, globular clusters formed in a galaxy, DF2/DF4, wide binaries and the Solar System never decoupled from the Hubble flow on their own, so they are Newtonian.
  - Nested systems need T5's max rule, which is an extra postulate: a group turning around after its members would otherwise add a second compaction.
  - DR4 Arm C (γ̂ = 1.000) follows from the rule without a separate wide-binary postulate. But the rule was written knowing FG001, so this is not an independent prediction.
  - 1.000 is also what Newton and ΛCDM predict (CFG63). DR4 can test (iii-a) against the unscreened law only, and at P2 that separation is 2.3–3.7σ (CFG200).
  - The two rules could differ for a globular cluster that turned around in its own minihalo before its host galaxy. The committed outer-halo GC gate does not discriminate there (the law needs M/L_V ≈ 1.0, Newton gives 1.70).
- **H2: PASS for (iii-a), FAIL for (iii-b).** (A2) also fails, because tidal debris would inherit an advected compaction. The six TDGs are a small sample whose discs have turned less than one orbit (the authors' caveat).
- **H3: no dynamical reason for the amount.** The one-time event can only fix who owns. How much is owned is either wrong (A1, A2) or B's law restated (A3).
- **H4: NON-DIAGNOSTIC.** Under the flat tie nothing changes.
