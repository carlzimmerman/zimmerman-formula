## 10. What this lane cannot say
It does not measure a₀ at z = 3.09. The gas is a CO(1–0) luminosity measured with a 3.3″ × 2.6″ beam (the disc scale is an assumption: the stars' 2.24 kpc, with the dust size as a knob) under a conversion that the paper itself leaves between 0.8 and 3.6 (its own dust calibration 2.1 ± 0.3 depends on the gas-to-dust ratio); the JVLA calibration (about 10 %) is not in the Monte Carlo; the stellar mass is one CIGALE fit for an X-ray AGN host with a two-peak NIRCam image and an unconstrained ALPAKA fit; the kinematic inclination and the thin-disc ones disagree by 3σ; near the floor boundary a few per cent decide root against floor. **A floor with a lower-limit baryon set (G1 would have been one) is robust against any added baryon; a floor with the 3.6 rows is only as robust as the conversion 3.6.**
