## What the numbers say
- **What the measured L′(1–0) changed.** CFG283 used the ALPAKA L′(3–2) (3.0 × 10¹⁰, r_J1 = 1 as a lower limit) for the gas floor; the measured CO(1–0) luminosity is 1.52× larger (r₃₁ = 0.65), so the gas-only lower-limit bound tightened from **s\* ≤ 27.6 (vacuous) to ≤ 10.0 (resolved, conditioned)**, and the stars + lowest-gas row moved from a nominal root (≤ 1.40, no-root 0.363, CFG284 B1) to a nominal floor (D = 0.94, no-root 0.538): the same knife edge from the other side.
- **The conversion ladder (assumed conversions applied to the measured L′; not data).** *Gas only:* α_CO 0.8 / 1.0 / 1.36 → ROOT, s\* ≤ 10.0 / 6.3 / 2.5 (RESOLVED-ROOT); 2.1 / 3.6 / 4.36 → FLOOR (no-root 0.945 / 1.000 / 1.000). *Stars + gas:* FLOOR from 0.8 up (UNRESOLVED at 0.8 and 1.0, no-root 0.53 / 0.66; RESOLVED-FLOOR from 1.36, 0.88). So **the gas-only row has a root only below α_CO ≈ 1.7, and the stars + gas row only below 0.70, which is below the lowest conversion in use.**
- **The equivalents (α_CO in units of the measured L′(1–0)):** floor boundary 1.660 (gas only) / 0.698 (with the stars); FLAT 1.536 / 0.573; PROXY 1.333 / 0.370; a₀ ∝ H(z) 1.126 / 0.164. The gas-only values lie inside the range the paper considers (0.8–3.6, dust-calibrated 2.1); the with-stars values lie below it.
- **The budget.** M\*/M_dyn(< R_ext) = 0.58; (M\* + M_gas(0.8))/M_dyn = 1.06; (M\* + M_gas(3.6))/M_dyn = 2.75; M_gas(3.6)/M_dyn = 2.17 (a scale; M_dyn = V²R/G = 7.6 × 10¹⁰ M☉).
- **The knobs on G1 (Δ log₁₀ s\*):** thin-disc inclinations from the JWST and ALMA axis ratios (57.3° / 54.5° against the kinematic 75° ± 6°) +0.32 / +0.39; R_e × 1.5 +0.28; helium × 1.36 −0.29; the gas at R_ext/1.2 and the dust R_e and the spherical variant ±0.1–0.2; kernel P2 +0.16; pressure +0.04; recipe half-width 0.58 dex, all 20 variants keep a root. On S0 the stellar-mass group now dominates (the new AGN-contamination variant M\* × 10^−0.50 gives +0.76 dex; half-width 0.95 dex, 0.75 in CFG284 without it).
- **The SELFTEST.** 100 worlds exactly on the law at s = 2: on the G1 baryons 78 rooted and 22 floors, the 95 % interval contains s = 2 in 68 of the 78, rooted s\* median 3.6 (16–84 % 0.71–8.1); on the S1 baryons 65 rooted and 35 floors, coverage 58 of 65, median 6.2. As in CFG284, the rooted-only median sits above the truth, so a rooted lower-limit row is an upper bound, not an estimate.
- **MUTATE=1** (V_ext and σ_ext × 2): all six rows have a root; s\*: S0 212, S1 100, S2 18.2, G1 262, G2 140, G3 31.8; all roots equal the independent inversion to 4e-16.

## What this lane cannot say
It does not measure a₀ at z = 3.09. The gas is one CO(1–0) luminosity from a 3.3″ × 2.6″ beam (the gas disc scale is an assumption: the stars' 2.24 kpc; the paper gives no CO size for A7), the JVLA absolute calibration (about 10 %) is not in the Monte Carlo, and the conversion is the paper's open question (0.8 to 3.6; its dust calibration 2.1 ± 0.3 depends on the gas-to-dust ratio); **A7 is an X-ray AGN host, its NIRCam image has two peaks off the dust position and ALPAKA's own SED fit could not constrain its stellar mass, so the S rows rest on one CIGALE fit that may be biased high**; the kinematic and thin-disc inclinations disagree by 3σ (the thin-disc values raise G1's bound by +0.3–0.4 dex); near the floor boundary a few per cent decide root against floor (S1). **A floor with a lower-limit baryon set would be robust against any added baryon; the 3.6 floors are only as robust as the conversion 3.6, which the paper itself argues for on morphological grounds and brackets by its own 2.1 ± 0.3.**
