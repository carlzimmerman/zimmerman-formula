  - Nothing is re-run.
- **S0 (comparison):** the matched S0 run of each framework run. It has the same ICs, engine family and pipeline, and no source, so its gravitating field is its particle field. The matching is CFG555's `RUNS` map; for CFG530 it is `cfg526_S0_L<L>.npz` at the same N.
- **Redshift.** Only the z = 0 states were saved. P_grav exists at z = 0 only; particle P exists at z = 1, 0.5 and 0 in each run's JSON `snap`.
  - Framework P_F(k, z) = P_part,F(k, z) · B_F(k, z), with B_F(k, 0) = P_grav,F / P_part,F at z = 0.
  - **PRIMARY:** B_F(k, z) = B_F(k, 0) at every z (held).
  - **Declared bracket "fade":** B_F = 1 + (1 − z)(B_F(k, 0) − 1) for z ≤ 1, and B_F = 1 for z > 1.
  - S0: P_S0(k, z) = P_part,S0(k, z).
  - Where a run's JSON lacks z = 1 / 0.5 snapshots (the CFG530 / CFG526 caches), P(k, z > 0) = P(k, 0) · [D(z)/D(0)]² from the PM's own growth. This is disclosed per run.
  - Interpolation: log P linear in z between the nodes 0, 0.5 and 1. For z > 1, P(k, z) = P(k, 1) · [D(z)/D(1)]², with D the PM's own linear growth (`Dgrow`, its background).
- **k range:** resolved range [k_lo, k_hi], with k_lo = the first PM bin centre and k_hi = k_Nyq/4 = π N/(4L).
  - Below k_lo: the PM's own IC spectrum (EH no-wiggle, σ8 = 0.811, the engines' only ΛCDM input) × D(z)², matched to the PM value at k_lo.
  - Above k_hi: a power law with each model's own slope fitted in log-log over [k_hi/2, k_hi] at each z node.
  - The same rule applies to both models. Only data points that are ≥ 90% supported inside [k_lo, k_hi] are kept (B3), so these extensions carry ≤ 10% of each kept point.
- **Runs.** Each is a framework run against its matched S0; foot is canonical (can) or alt; "DE" = a0 tracks dark energy.

### B3. Data, nuisances, scale cuts

- **Data:** the KiDS-1000 and DES Y3 measured ξ± with their published covariance (sub-block of the kept points) and n(z), and the published scale cuts of §1 of the criteria. A further resolution cut is applied per run.
- **Resolution cut.** A data point is kept only if at least 90% of its absolute Limber–Hankel integrand lies at k within [k_lo, k_hi].
  - The integrand is ℓ |J̄_{0/4}(ℓθ)| q_i q_j P_S0(k, z)/χ², over ℓ and χ, with k = (ℓ + ½)/χ, evaluated with that run's S0 spectrum and nominal nuisances.
  - The kept θ ranges per bin pair, N_kept and the fraction of the published data vector lost are reported per run.
  - A run with N_kept < 10 in a survey is NOT DIAGNOSTIC there.
- **Nuisances (measurement only, the surveys' own calibration priors as in criteria §2):**
  - photo-z shifts: KiDS correlated SOM prior; DES N(0, σ_i);
  - DES multiplicative shear bias m_i (KiDS m is in its covariance);
  - NLA IA: KiDS A_IA flat [−6, 6]; DES A_IA, η flat [−5, 5].
  - KiDS δc is NOT used (set to 0; not in the owner's list).
  - **Two settings, both reported:** "IA marginalised" (PRIMARY) and "no IA" (A_IA = 0).
  - Nothing cosmological is free. The amplitude is whatever the PM produced from its σ8 = 0.811 ICs.
- **Feedback:** the PRIMARY has NO feedback (our PM lacks it). Context only, flagged EXTERNALLY CALIBRATED: both models are multiplied by the HMcode-2020 BAHAMAS ratio S_fb(k, z; T) = P_feedback / P_DMO (CAMB) at fixed T = 7.6, 7.8 and 8.0, not fitted. These enter no verdict.

### B4. Statistics and classes (per survey × run; framework vs its own S0)

- χ²_min(framework | data), χ²_min(S0 | data), each over its own nuisances (data χ² + Gaussian priors), and N_kept and p-values.
- **Δχ² = χ²_min(framework) − χ²_min(S0).** Positive means the framework is worse.
- **Robustness set:** (a) priors × 2; (b) no IA; (c) the "fade" boost bracket; (d) DESI distances.
- **Classes:**
  - EXCLUDED: Δχ² ≥ 9 and min over the robustness set ≥ 9.
  - TENSION: Δχ² ≥ 4, not EXCLUDED.
  - CONSISTENT: Δχ² < 4 and max over the robustness set < 9.
  - NOT DIAGNOSTIC: otherwise, or N_kept < 10, or the run's MUTATE tooth NZ2 fails for that survey.
- **Footing summary** (PRIMARY set only, per survey):
  - EXCLUDED if every PRIMARY run of that footing is EXCLUDED;
  - CONSISTENT if every one is CONSISTENT;
  - otherwise MIXED, with the list.
  - A negative Δχ² earns CONSISTENT at most.
- **Absolute fit (reported):** χ²_min / N_kept and the p-value of S0 and of the framework against the data, with no feedback. A 200 (100) Mpc/h box carries its own sample variance at k_lo; this is not in the covariance (disclosed).
