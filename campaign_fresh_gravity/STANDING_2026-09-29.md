# Candidate B: standing, 2026-09-29

**Bottom line.**
- The law's galaxy-scale form holds, and κ = ½ is fitted, not derived. No committed action produces candidate B.
- We ran standard ΛCDM halos through B's own pipelines. Relative to them, B has one specific failure: the KiDS early/late lensing split. It holds for the early-minus-late difference only. It is shared by any model whose predicted difference at fixed g_bar is negligible, but not by every colour-blind model: a colour-blind Moster halo fits at 6.9/7. It is robust to the jackknife covariance (4.4σ) and to stricter isolation (4.1σ). B's own stellar-mass calibration reduces it but does not remove it: 2.8σ on the released data, 3.6σ with the jackknife. It is fragile only to systematics a jackknife cannot see.
- ΛCDM's own clean comparisons here are also weak: several are compatibility checks. So "one specific failure" describes this machinery. It does not say the data favour either model.
- Only one open test separates B from anything: Gaia DR4 (2 Dec 2026), and only from the bare MOND-type law. Nothing on hand separates B from ΛCDM at 3σ.

## 4. The two decisive tests (CFG63 7b8640ded) [RE-RUN-CLEAN]
- **Gaia DR4 wide binaries, 2 Dec 2026.** B predicts γ̂ = 1.000; the bare law's floor is 1.161 (alt 1.192). With the frozen σ_sys = 0.02, the cap is 8.1σ, and 3σ needs about 4,300 pairs (alt 2,900) out of ~30,000 expected.
  - Kill lines: γ̂ ≥ 1.084 kills B (frozen text; CFG63 computes 1.083). γ̂ ≤ 1.077 kills the bare law (frozen text; CFG63 computes 1.079).
  - It cannot separate B from ΛCDM or Newton, which both predict 1.000. For B it is a survival test, never a confirmation.
- **a₀ at z ≈ 2.5.** B predicts a flat a₀. The 0.25-dex mass-scale floor caps the test at 2.3σ against a₀ ∝ H(z) and 1.3σ against ΛCDM-native, whatever the sample size. No usable disc is on disk (CFG54 8b6473b7a). PHIBSS's N = 0 is a knife-edge on an assumed velocity radius, and CFG52's pooled z ≥ 1.5 result depends on excluding one +1.14-dex object (CFG90 0137d584d).
  - CFG52 (9ffb95d57) forecasts that 2–4 discs with 5–10% velocity errors and independent mass errors of 0.1–0.2 dex would give 3σ. But a correlated mass-scale systematic caps the test near 2.3σ whatever the sample size, so the calibration must reach about 0.1 dex.
- **Every other test** is capped below 3σ by a systematic floor (ultra-faints 2.5σ, groups 1.8σ) or cannot be forecast from committed budgets (KiDS).
