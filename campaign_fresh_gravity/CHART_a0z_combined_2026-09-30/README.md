## What is on it
- **y-axis:** the implied a₀ scale s\* = a₀,implied / 9.3603e-11 m/s² (the canonical footing; CFG223's estimator: the a₀ scale that zeroes the set-median of δ = log₁₀[D/ν_mono(g_bar/a₀)]), with a₀ in 10⁻¹⁰ m/s² on the right-hand axis. **z ≈ 0:** the grey box is the two footings (9.3603e-11 and 1.1312e-10 m/s², ratio 1 to 1.2085); the two diamonds are SPARC's best free fits at Υ = 0.50, 1.37e-10 (ν_mono) and 1.78e-10 (P2), quoted from CFG4_README H2b, not re-derived here.
- **Curves (CFG223, from `cfg223_results.json`):** flat a₀ (black); a₀ ∝ H(z) (red); the ΛCDM effective-a₀ PROXY (purple, dotted above z = 5); a₀ ∝ √ρ_DE with the DESI DR2 CPL (teal, dotted above z = 2.5, band = DESY5 and Union3).
- **Marker shape = the record's gas class:** circle = class D (baryon masses fitted with the dynamics or a scaling prior: RC100 corrected table, CRISTAL fit route); square = class S/L (one or two UN-optimised tracers: CRISTAL independent route, which is class L = SED M\* + dust gas at T_d = 50 K; the six ALPINE [CII] rotators and SPT0418-47, classes S and S/L); star = class M (Dunne+22 per-galaxy optimised multi-tracer gas). Hollow square = the ALPINE6 pooled set (median rule over the six rotators). **Colour = the lane:** blue RC100, orange CRISTAL, brown class-M sample, green ALMA sample.
- **Statistical bars** (thick 68%, thin 95%; over the Monte Carlo or bootstrap draws that HAVE a root) and **systematic bars** (shaded, inner dark and outer light) are separate styles. **The systematic bars are not on a common footing across lanes:** RC100 and CRISTAL carry CFG223's ±0.15 / ±0.30 dex baryon-mass shifts; the class-M and ALMA points carry their own joint class bands on gas and stars (class M: gas ±0.03 / ±0.093 dex with stars ±0.20 / ±0.30; class S/L: gas ±0.213 / ±0.671 with stars ±0.20 / ±0.30). A downward triangle at the floor is "no root" (g_obs ≤ g_bar: the Newtonian floor that every law has, ν ≥ 1, so no a₀ can fit), for a whole point or for one corner of a band; an upward triangle marks a bar clipped at the top.
- **Points are shifted in z by up to 0.2 for legibility** (true z in the CSV).

## How to read it (and how not to)
The blind pre-flights of CFG228 and CFG229 said, before any velocity was used, that these two samples CANNOT separate the a₀(z) laws at their declared bands (NOT POSSIBLE under every rule); the bars on the chart are that statement drawn out: the class-M points are six floor triangles and one starred galaxy, and every ALMA bar spans a decade or more, with the systematic bars reaching the floor (the joint outer band removes the root). For the ALPINE points the pressure-term choice alone moves s\* from no root (α = 0) through 0.71 (α = 3.36) to 4.33 (α = 6.71, the primary; ALPINE6): the vertical position of an ALMA point is a statement about calibration, not about evolution, and its nearness to any curve carries no information. The CFG223 points are the only ones with bars narrow enough to be compared with the curves, and they carry author-fitted baryon masses whose priors are not independent of the dynamics (CFG223, CFG227 limits). Nothing here says the data favour a law.
