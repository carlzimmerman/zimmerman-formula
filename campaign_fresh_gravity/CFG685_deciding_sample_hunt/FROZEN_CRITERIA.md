# CFG685 — the sample that decides tracking vs rival a0(z) (FROZEN, before any survey result)

Block CFG680–689. Owner "both". CFG683: the fork needs log10[a0(z)/a0(0)] at z ≈ 1–2.5 to e ≤ 0.15 dex (with the CMB's Ω_m;
post-hoc) or 0.02–0.05 dex (CMB-free). Record (CFG213–229, CFG224): at z > 1 a0 is limited by the baryon calibration; in the
deep-MOND regime (g_bar ≲ a0) the lever is ~1 (a0 = g_obs²/g_bar), at g_bar/a0 = 1–4 it is 2.5–4.8.

## Scope
Survey (record first, then literature; no bulk downloads, no fetch without the owner's yes) of every known or proposable
z = 1–2.5 rotating-disc sample, scored on: N discs; outer radius reached in g_bar/a0 (deep-MOND fraction); gas mass route
(number of independent tracers: CO lines, [CI], dust, [CII]) and stated calibration error; stellar mass error; inclination and
pressure-support systematics; public availability.

## Score
e_est = lever(g_bar/a0) × √(σ²_gas,shared + σ²_star + σ²_pressure) / (shared part does NOT average down with N), with lever from the
record (1 deep, 2.5–4.8 at 1–4). Rank by e_est. Report e_est against 0.15 dex (CMB-Ω_m target) and CFG684's CMB-free target.

## Decision rules
- CANDIDATE DECIDER: e_est ≤ 0.15 dex with public data → propose a lane (needs the owner's go for any fetch).
- PROPOSAL TARGET: e_est ≤ 0.15 dex only with new observations → write the observation spec (instrument, lines, depth), not a proposal text.
- NONE: no sample reaches 0.15 dex even in principle with current instruments → say so plainly.
Literature numbers are PROVISIONAL until checked against the paper's own table (standing rule).
