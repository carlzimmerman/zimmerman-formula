# Early-type target for the settling / capture lanes (2026-10-13; from this chat's CFG712–714, read with the owner chat's CFG721)

κ = ½ FITTED; cold energy mass required; no particle species; not "theory closed". A target, not a mechanism.

## What the raw lensing now says about massive early types
1. **The law alone fails them on an imaging-only observable** (CFG712, 43 SLACS lenses, predictions sealed before scoring): slopes
   predicted from light + the law are too steep by +0.24 ± 0.05 (canonical, INCONSISTENT) / +0.19 ± 0.05 (alt, TENSION); no per-lens
   predictive power. Same shortfall as h53/B7 and CFG580.
2. **A heavier IMF does not fix it** (CFG713): more stars steepen the profile; H-IMF passes for only 14–16 % of lenses.
3. **What is required** (CFG713): an extra, extended (≈ isothermal) cold component holding ≈ 0.35 of the projected mass inside θE
   (≈ R_e/2) — ≈ 3× the law's own cold mass there — with ordinary (Chabrier-like) stars. Scatter lens to lens; no trend with size or
   stellar mass in SLACS.
4. **The same component explains the weak-lensing excess** (CFG714): extended isothermally it predicts the KiDS red − blue excess at
   ~100 kpc (+0.255 ± 0.052 vs +0.220 ± 0.030 observed, owner chat CFG721) with nothing re-fitted.
5. Blue/disc galaxies sit near the law (CFG663); colour and structure carry the excess about equally (CFG721).

## The target (any capture/settling mechanism must produce it)
- **Early types:** extra isothermal cold mass with **V_x² ≈ 0.7–0.8 V_f²** (V_f⁴ = G M_b a0), present from ~5 kpc to ~100 kpc.
- **Discs:** ≈ none (on the law).
- **Selector:** something that tracks galaxy type (colour/structure), not size or stellar mass alone; the age signal is not robust on
  near-IR masses (owner chat CFG669), so a pure-age selector is disfavoured.
- Must not break: SPARC rotation curves (discs), satellites' EFE-free boost (PAPER44), the Solar System, GW170817 (CFG670).

## Caveats
Isothermal shape assumed; de Vaucouleurs light for all SLACS lenses; Etherington's γ is a global power-law fit; SLACS is more massive
than the KiDS early-type sample; red − blue read as red − law. ΛCDM halos also fit both scales: this is a target for the framework,
not a discriminator between the framework and ΛCDM.
