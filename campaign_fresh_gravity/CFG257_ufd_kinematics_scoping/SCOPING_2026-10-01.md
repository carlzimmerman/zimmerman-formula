# CFG257: UFD and dwarf-satellite kinematics newer than, or not in, the CFG28/29 inputs (data-front scoping, 2026-10-01)

Request: the orchestrator's CFG257 task (CFG244 lean toward bound cold cores, driven by the ultra-faints; B's UFD failure 3.5-3.8 sigma in CFG28/29). **Nothing was downloaded into the repository, no data table was opened, and no CFG28/29 number was re-run.** Only paper and release pages were read. The fetch tool cached some PDFs outside the repo (Arroyo-Polonio+26 4.6 MB, Geha Paper II 0.68 MB) that I read as text. "How read": **text** = full text; **abstract** = abstract page only; **page** = a web page through a summariser; **snippet** = search snippet only (not citable).

## What the CFG28/29 inputs already are (read from the repository)
- `real_research/data/dsph/lvd_dwarf_mw.csv`: the Local Volume Database (Pace 2024, arXiv:2411.07424; OJAp/ApJS record in `PROVENANCE.md`), downloaded 2026-09-03 from the `main` branch. LVD's GitHub release page lists v1.1.1 (12 Aug) as the latest release I could see; the repo copy is a `main` download, so it can be newer than v1.1.1 (release notes do not mention velocity dispersions).
- 40 ultra-faint-like rows (M_V > -7.7) carry a dispersion or limit: **31 resolved + 9 upper limits**. By `ref_vlos`: **15 systems from Geha+2026 (ApJ 999, 140)**, and one each or two each from Li18, Heiger24, Simon20, Fritz19, Bruce23, Tan25, Hansen24, Simon17, Chiti22, Chiti23, Buttry22, Walker15, Pace25, Koposov15/18, Cerny25/26, Li17, Sandford26 (Boo I), Li26 (Boo III, arXiv:2607.07803), Chiu26 (Willman 1).
- **So the inputs already contain most 2024-2026 results**; what follows is what they do not contain, or what would change them.
- The repo also holds `lvd_dwarf_m31.csv`: 43 M31 satellites, 36 with a dispersion or limit, 9 of them with M_V > -7.7 (7 resolved, 2 limits).
- CFG29's cited bounds checked: McConnachie & Cote 2010 (arXiv:1009.4205, ApJ 722, L209): "unidentified binaries" with an intrinsic ~0.2 km/s can give apparent dispersions up to ~4.5 km/s (**abstract-level, matches**); Ou+26 (arXiv:2609.19407, submitted 16 Sep 2026; Binary Observation Simulator, true sigma 0.75-3.5 km/s, fb 0.1-0.9, baselines to 10 yr): "even a 10-yr baseline can leave ~10-120% relative bias for systems with sigma_true <~ 1 km/s" (**page: matches CFG29's quote**); Geha+26 UMa I 7.16 (+1.04/-1.21) in the Geha Paper II table (matches CFG29's 7.2 +- 1.1); Eri II 6.9 +1.2/-0.9 (Li+17, from the LVD column).

## Reading for CFG257's question
1. **The sample CFG28/29 tested is already post-2024 for most systems.** Replacing LVD values by the uniform DEIMOS values (Geha II) matters for 4 systems (Boo I -0.098, Eri IV -0.127, Willman 1 -0.073, Boo III +0.49 dex); the Boo III case is a tidally disrupting system.
2. **The only published binary correction for many CFG28/29 systems is Arroyo-Polonio+26**, with a median effect of -0.07 dex (free f) to -0.10 dex (f = 0.7) on 8 systems, against an offset of 0.30-0.33 dex: the same sign as the binary escape, about a fifth to a third of the size.
3. **The multi-epoch evidence is thin**: one long-baseline dispersion (Boo I, 4.0 km/s, nine variable stars flagged) that does not shrink toward the single-epoch-corrected value; MUSE-Faint V finds no inflation in Leo T.
4. **No Gaia DR3 internal-PM route exists for UFDs**; **M31 has 9 UFD-class entries with 2-4 times larger errors**.
5. **What would help most (needs the owner's go)**: the Geha Paper I/II tables (Tables A1-A5; sizes not read) and the Boo I S5 data; neither adds a new UFD, but they give per-star velocities with variable flags for a re-estimate under one pipeline.
