**Power analysis (the main finding): both nulls have low power against a systematic carrying half the split.**

Cross null: 80% power at alpha = 0.01 needs eps about 0.74 (omnibus) or 0.57 (1-dof shape-matched); 'disfavoured at 50% or more' overstates it (32% power at eps = 0.5); it excludes E = B additive-type systematics at the full split (lambda = 36). Shape-matched cross fit A = -0.045 +/- 0.167 (95% upper limit 0.23 of the tangential split); per-class leak into the cross -0.02 +/- 0.04 (early) and -0.05 +/- 0.09 (late), assuming the leak has the tangential shape. **Near/far null is essentially blind to near-only systematics:** the 1-dof fit gives A_nf = +0.03 +/- 0.29; the amplitude difference near - far is +0.039 +/- 0.077 dex (95% upper limit 0.165); if the whole far split were a separation-dependent systematic the test excludes it only when the far/near strength ratio q is below 0.5. **Permutation calibration:** chi2_7 is anti-conservative: the permuted 99th percentile is about 20.4-20.8, not 18.5, and power at eps = 0.5 drops to 0.23 (cross) and 0.04 (near/far).

**Is the far-only 23.2/7 a smaller-N fluctuation? No.** Permutation (2000 within-patch class-label permutations): empirical p 0.0055 (chi2_7 says 0.0016, optimistic by about 3x); near-only 0.018; the all-source split at most 0.0005 (the permutation floor). Random 51% lens subsamples of the all-source split give a median chi2 of 21.3 (16-84%: 14.7 to 29.6), P(>= 23.2) = 0.39. The far, near and all-source splits are highly dependent, not independent confirmations.

**What 'passes both nulls' does and does not exclude (attacks).** **E-mode-only class-dependent errors (invisible to both nulls) are not excluded:** the split is a factor 1.51; a class-dependent multiplicative shear bias would need 51% (KiDS-1000 calibration about 1-2%, from the literature, not derived here); a class-dependent baryonic-mass or g_bar-assignment offset would need about 0.5 dex (local K1 slope 0.29-0.44; typical M/L or IMF uncertainty 0.1-0.2 dex): the largest untested avenue. **Covariance x1.8:** the all-source split reaches p = 0.05 at f = 1.58 and p = 0.15 at f = 1.81; the far-only split reaches p = 0.05 at f = 1.28; inflating the covariance destroys the nulls' power too (lambda falls by f^2; power at eps = 0.5 is 0.06 at f = 1.81); the cross-shear noise calibration bounds a jackknife under-estimate at s <= 0.99 (joint 14-vector chi2 6.46/14, p 0.954) to 1.29 (15 bins), ruling out shape-noise error of that size, but it does not see tangential-only terms (cosmic variance, IA, photo-z); the patches are spatially contiguous (98.7% of nearest sky neighbours share a patch). **Lens photo-z:** far-source amplitudes in lens-z terciles 0.10 +/- 0.09, 0.17 +/- 0.06, 0.20 +/- 0.09 (constant, p 0.72); a lens z offset of about 0.24 would be needed to fake the whole split through Sigma_crit (own flat LCDM), realistic offsets give <= 2% for far and <= 7% for near sources; not tested: class-dependent outlier fractions or an offset of the g_bar assignment; mild flag: the cross difference in the highest lens-z tercile is 13.5/7 (p 0.06). **Satellites and 2-halo:** far-source inner minus outer amplitude -0.01 +/- 0.09 (near +0.28 +/- 0.19, all +0.11 +/- 0.08): no outward-growing term, but the isolation flags were not read, so per-lens satellite fractions are untested; the near-source dilution is similar in both classes. **Dilution direction:** near amplitude >= far amplitude; class-dependent dilution in early types would shrink the early-late split, not create it.

**What the agent would attack.** (1) Wording: CFG116's 'a systematic at about 50% of the split or more is disfavoured' should read 'excluded only for eps of about 0.6-0.75 (omnibus), eps below about 0.23 shape-matched'; 'source-side dilution and alignment excluded' is too strong given the near/far power. (2) Report permutation-calibrated p-values, not chi2 tails: far-only 0.0055, not 0.0016. (3) The near/far assignment and WX content rest on a staging pass that cannot be independently re-checked from the staged files, and K8c did not pass as frozen. (4) The residual risk is E-mode-only: class-dependent baryonic-mass calibration, shear calibration and lens photo-z, none testable from the staged sums.

Nothing here says the data favour the framework; kappa = 1/2 is FITTED.
