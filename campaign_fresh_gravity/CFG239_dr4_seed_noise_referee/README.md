## Correction to the "roughly 5%" recommendation (arithmetic note, `CFG239_arith.py` / `CFG239_arith.out`, written after Amendment 18 draft rev 4, b58f7e692)

The "5.4% to 9.2%" in the claim-(3) row and in the (f) row of the Amendment 18 table was sqrt(1 + r^2) - 1 with r = SD(gamma-hat)/sigma_fit and **no sigma_sys**, i.e. the fractional increase of sigma_fit itself when sigma_build is added in quadrature. It was NOT r x 100 (33 to 44%), NOT r^2/(1 + r^2) (10 to 16%), and NOT the draft's sigma_tot convention; it should not be quoted as an understatement of sigma_tot. In the draft's convention (sigma_tot = sqrt(sigma_fit^2 + 0.02^2), understatement = sqrt(1 + (sigma_build/sigma_tot)^2) - 1, sigma_build = r sigma_fit): DR3's N (sigma_fit 0.0514 to 0.0568 from my runs) gives 4.7% (r = 0.333, alt, K = 100) to 8.1% (r = 0.437, canonical, SEED+0..50 set); the pre-registered DR4 sigma_fit = 0.019 gives 2.6% to 4.4%. The rev 4 figures (2 to 5% at DR4, 4 to 8% at DR3's N for r = 0.3 to 0.44) are consistent with this arithmetic. The DR4 sigma_fit itself is a pre-registration number (line 631), not something these runs can check.
