**What must be said with it.**
- **It is a confirmation run, not a discovery.** The r^−3 value was first found by a scan (CFG813-diag). Here the input is
  the measured eROSITA slope, but the outcome was anticipated.
- **A residual mass trend remains, within the threshold.** The light half is short (1.48, Z +2.0) and the heavy half is
  over (0.78, Z −1.6), so the pass is helped by the two halves pulling in opposite directions.
- **K1 failed as frozen.** It expected 0.761, the scan value with steepening at 1.5 R500. The criteria's own steepening
  point (2.2 R500) gives 0.686. K2 was not evaluable.
- **Inputs are provisional:** λ → M*, and the eROSITA slope (taken from the abstract) and the r200m conversion.
- **Scope:** 76 of 318 eRASS1 clusters. ACCEPT and the full sample are not yet tested this way. eFEDS passed under H-EX
  (CFG809).

**Reading.** With retention set by a *measured* outskirt slope, and no free parameter, the expelled-gas rule (cold energy of
gas the system no longer holds goes free) fits the eRASS1 cluster lensing cores and outskirts. It also explains why clusters
act as if ~30% of their catchment baryons were expelled.
