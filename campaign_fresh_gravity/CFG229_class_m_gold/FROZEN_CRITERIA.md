## 4. Gas mass and the helium factor (checked, not assumed)
**M_gas = 1.36 × 10^`logMH2`** of the chosen table (the gravitating molecular mass including He; HI is neglected, the catalogue's `HI/H2` column being empty for all seven; stated as a limit). Statistical error `e_logMH2` (0.09 to 0.11 dex). **Class-M band on the gas: inner ±0.03 dex (CFG224b K_direct 0.021 to 0.032) and outer ±0.093 dex (K_local of α_CO, B3)**, read from `cfg224b_conversion_drift_results.json`. Controls C2a to C2c verify the He convention numerically (§13); the gas-table sensitivity uses the other Dunne tables of BX610.

## 5. Baryon model and g_bar (declared; ours)
**One thin exponential disc (Freeman) carrying stars plus gas, R_d = R_e/1.678:** V²(R) = 2GM/R_d · y²[I₀K₀ − I₁K₁](y), y = R/(2R_d); g_bar = V²/R at the stated R (the function of CFG216 / CFG227 `disc_v2`). **Sensitivities, reported beside and never substituted:** (a) stars at R_e,* = 2 R_e with the gas at R_e (CFG227's post hoc variant); (b) the spherical enclosed-mass form g = G M[1 − (1+x)e^{−x}]/R², x = R/R_d; (c) **R_e × 1.5 and ÷ 1.5** (declared geometry band); (d) the other Dunne tables (BX610).

## 6. Kinematics and g_obs
**g_obs = V²/R** with the primary definitions of §3 (each source's own convention: ALPAKA V_ext not pressure-corrected, SINS V_c with 3.36 built in, ALESS V_circ as modelled). **Variants, beside and never substituted:** (a) **pressure**: V_c² = V² + α σ² with α ∈ {0, 1.68, 3.36} for ALPAKA (V_ext, σ_ext), and for SINS V_rot (α = 0), α = 1.68 and the published V_c (α = 3.36); ALESS V_circ as published and V_c² = V_circ² + 3.36 σ² (CFG227's S1 variant); (b) **ALPAKA ring variants**: the outermost DIGITISED ring value at R_ext (`alpaka1_vrot_digitised`, ring with the largest R, panel V), and V_ext at the MEAN radius of the last two rings; (c) **ALPAKA inclination**: V × sin i_used / sin i_alt with i_used the inclination the authors deprojected with (i_HST for 15, 18, 19, 20; **i_ALMA = 26° for 22**, the paper's stated exception) and i_alt the other one of (i_HST, i_ALMA) from `alpaka1_geometry`; ±1σ of i_used. These are the dominant systematics of the ALPAKA points and are reported per galaxy.

## 7. Laws, kernels, footings, δ (as CFG227)
FLAT (F = 1); PROXY (Z1 `lcdm_native`, read-only); a₀ ∝ H(z) (E(z), Ω_m = 0.315); M-DEC (√ρ_DE with (w₀, w_a) = (−0.838, −0.62) from the record). **δ_L = log₁₀[g_obs / (g_bar ν_mono(g_bar/(a₀ F_L(z))))]**, ν_mono, canonical footing 9.3603e-11; the alt footing 1.1312e-10 and the P2 kernel reported beside.

## 8. Bands and error terms (declared where stated)
Gas: inner ±0.03, outer ±0.093 (class M, above). **Stars: inner = the source's 1σ in dex (ALPAKA: e_mstar/(M ln 10); SINS: 0.2, the paper's adopted value; ALESS 122.1: 0.21); outer ±0.30 dex, DECLARED (covers SED-method, star-formation-history, IMF and AGN-contamination systematics, UNVERIFIED for this sample).** Geometry: R_e × 1.5 and ÷ 1.5 (declared). ALPAKA inclination: the truncated-normal distribution of i_used ± the tabulated error (truncated to 5° to 85°), which enters g_obs as (sin i_used/sin i)²; **σ_int = 0.15 dex (declared, as CFG227)**; velocity statistics from the tabulated errors (σ_V in dex = 2 × 0.4343 × mean(err)/V).

## 9. The PRE-FLIGHT (Q1): `cfg229_preflight.py`, committed BEFORE `cfg229_score.py` exists
**Blind to the velocities by construction:** the inputs are split by the input script into `cfg229_inputs_static.csv` (z, R, R_e, M*, errors, gas, inclinations, velocity ERRORS) and `cfg229_inputs_kin.csv` (the velocities and dispersions). The pre-flight reads only the static file (control C8: its output is bit-identical when the kinematics file is removed).
- **Mocks on the ACTUAL galaxies:** truth T ∈ {FLAT, H(z), PROXY}; g_obs,true = g_bar ν(g_bar/(a₀ F_T(z))) with g_bar from the declared baryons; log g_obs = log g_obs,true + ε_V + ε_inc + ε_int (ε_V ~ N(0, σ_V,i); ε_inc from the ALPAKA inclination distribution; ε_int ~ N(0, 0.15)); the observed g_bar is computed from M* 10^{ε*} and M_gas 10^{ε_g} with ε* ~ N(0, inner stellar 1σ), ε_g ~ N(0, `e_logMH2`). 1,000 mocks per (set, truth), seed 229 (20,000 for the marginalised run).
- **Reported per set (all seven; each galaxy alone; the five ALPAKA; BX610 + ALESS 122.1):** (i) **signal = |median over mocks of the set-median δ_FLAT when T = H(z)|** (and T = PROXY) — the frozen definition, with the noise-free value reported beside to show the difference (CFG237 found CFG227 had used the noise-free one while the text said median over mocks); (ii) **SD = the standard deviation of the set-median δ_FLAT over the mocks when T = FLAT**; (iii) **S = the calibration systematic = the larger of the two noise-free set-median shifts of δ_FLAT (truth FLAT) at the joint outer corners τ_gas = τ_* = +B and −B (B_gas = 0.093, B_* = 0.30), S_max**; S_gas alone (gas ±0.093), S_* alone (stars ±0.30), S_half (half the range) and the same set with the INNER bands (gas ±0.03, stars = the source's 1σ) are reported beside. **Rule: a separation of FLAT from H(z) (from the PROXY) in a set is called "POSSIBLE" only if signal > S_max + 2 SD with S_max the JOINT outer-band systematic (gas and stars together); the same rule with S_gas alone is printed as "POSSIBLE at the class-M gas band alone" and the inner-band version as the best case; otherwise "NOT POSSIBLE". Only the joint outer-band verdict gates the wording.**
- **A second statistic, robust to a common baryon offset:** the within-set OLS slope of δ_FLAT against log₁₀ y (y = g_bar/a₀): signal, SD and the same rule with S the slope shift under the same band corners.
- **Per galaxy:** y = g_bar/a₀, the noise-free Δδ between FLAT and H(z), between FLAT and the PROXY, the total error budget σ_tot,i and the ratio |Δδ|/σ_tot,i.
- **Post hoc, labelled (Q3):** the baryon precision (common dex on stars and gas) at which the pooled separation becomes possible; the radius R* at which y = 3 for each galaxy and the signal there.
- **Changes after a first run:** if any script is edited after its first run, the first run's outputs are kept as `*_firstrun.*` and the change is listed in the README as post hoc.
- **Gate:** the scoring script reads the pre-flight JSON. **No sentence of the README may say that any law is separated, preferred or disfavoured unless the pre-flight rule said POSSIBLE for the set being discussed** (floor-passing subset included, recomputed by the same function); otherwise the table is descriptive and the README says which error term dominates.

## 10. The Newtonian floor (law-independent admissibility), in `cfg229_score.py`
Every law here has ν ≥ 1, so **D < 1 means the baryon model exceeds the dynamics at that radius for ANY of these laws** (a mass-level or geometry inconsistency, not a law effect). Status per galaxy: **PASS** if D ≥ 1 at the baseline; **BAND-DEPENDENT** if D < 1 at the baseline but D ≥ 1 at the most favourable corner of the declared outer bands (gas −0.093, stars −0.30, R_e chosen among ×1.5, ÷1.5 to minimise g_bar, α = 3.36, the ALPAKA ring and inclination variant that maximise g_obs); **ROBUST FAIL** otherwise. Pooled law statements use the PASS set only (the pre-flight rule recomputed on it, N ≥ 3); the all-seven pooled median and its bootstrap interval are still printed, labelled descriptive.

## A1. The quantity (descriptive, not a verdict)
For each galaxy (a set of one) and for each pooled set (ALL7; the floor-PASS subset; ALPAKA5; BX610 + ALESS 122.1) the **implied a₀ scale s\*** of CFG223: **the root of median_i log₁₀[D_i / ν_mono(g_bar,i/(a₀ s))] = 0** with ν_mono and the canonical a₀ = 9.3603e-11 m/s², by the committed vectorised bisection `implied` of `CFG223_a0_over_cosmic_time/cfg223_a0_over_time.py` (log₁₀ s on [−3, +3], 64 iterations; the same function is used, not re-derived). **The implied a₀ in m/s² is s\* × 9.3603e-11 and does not depend on the footing; against the alt footing (1.1312e-10) the ratio is s\*/1.2086.** Both footings and the two kernels (ν_mono primary, P2 beside) are printed. A galaxy with D ≤ 1 (the floor failure) has NO ROOT (no a₀ in [10⁻³, 10³] times the footing zeroes δ: ν ≥ 1 cannot produce D < 1); a root at the edge of the interval is flagged as a LIMIT. The expected s\* under each law is the same estimator applied to the seven placed on the law (noise-free, nominal baryons, g_obs = g_bar ν(g_bar/(a₀ F_L))); F_L(z_i) itself is printed per galaxy (FLAT 1; PROXY; H(z); M-DEC).

## A2. The error budget (every line separate; none merged into a verdict)
