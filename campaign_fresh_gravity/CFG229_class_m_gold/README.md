## Bottom line
1. **The class rule is implemented in code and returns the referee's seven** (ALPAKA 15, 18, 19, 20, 22; SINS Q2343-BX610; ALESS 122.1), with two negative controls (+30 arcsec on every declination and z × 1.05 both remove every positional match). **Dunne's `logMH2` is M_H2 WITHOUT helium** (paper text; `logMH2 = log aCO + logLCO` on 504 of 504 CO-table rows and `= logLCI + aCI − log 1.36` on 140 of 140 `xd` rows, to 0.001 dex; median `aCO` of `opt_ad` 3.262 against Table 10's 3.2 for M_H2 and 4.3 for M_mol), so the gravitating gas is 1.36 × 10^`logMH2`: +0.134 dex, which the referee's extension (without it) lacked.
2. **This sample cannot separate the a₀(z) laws, and the pre-flight said so before any velocity was used.** The seven sit at g_bar/a₀ = 4.8 (ALESS 122.1), 12 (BX610), 26 to 32 (ALPAKA 19, 20), 54 (ALPAKA 18) and 151 to 157 (ALPAKA 22, 15): the Newtonian regime, where FLAT and a₀ ∝ H(z) differ by 0.005 to 0.09 dex per galaxy. Pooled: signal 0.028, statistical SD 0.108, joint outer-band systematic 0.170 (gas alone 0.063, stars alone 0.122): **NOT POSSIBLE in every set under every rule** (outer, gas-only, inner, marginalised, within-set slope), for H(z) and for the proxy. **Even with perfect baryons the pooled signal (0.03) is below 2 SD (0.16)**, because the declared intrinsic scatter (0.15 dex), the velocity errors and the ALPAKA inclinations set the floor (ALPAKA 18, i = 26° ± 18°: SD 0.48 dex alone).
3. **What the data show (descriptive): six of the seven have g_obs BELOW g_bar** (D = g_obs/g_bar = 0.20 to 0.46, median 0.29; only ALESS 122.1 has D = 1.91). Every law here has ν ≥ 1, so **D < 1 is a baryon-model inconsistency for ANY of them**, not a law effect. The floor is met only at corners that stack several declared extremes (status: **PASS 1, BAND-DEPENDENT 6, ROBUST FAIL 0**; e.g. ALPAKA 19 reaches D = 1.16 only with V at the smaller mean-ring radius, i − 1σ, α = 3.36, R_e × 1.5 and the baryons at −0.093 / −0.30 dex together). **Unlike CFG227's S1 (stellar masses), here the class-M GAS is a main driver**: gas alone has D = 0.33 to 0.44 for ALPAKA 18, 19, 22 and 0.74 for BX610 (stars alone D = 0.26 and 0.34 for ALPAKA 15 and 20, where M*/M_dyn ≈ 5). The post hoc diagnostic M_bar/M_dyn(< R) (total baryons over the spherical dynamical mass; crude) is 3.1 to 6.3 for the six.
4. **Pooled class-M statistic (N_M = 7 ≥ 5, this lane's own rule; descriptive):** median δ_FLAT **−0.551** [−0.572, −0.364], PROXY −0.563 [−0.575, −0.384], H(z) −0.576 [−0.579, −0.413], M-DEC −0.549 [−0.571, −0.361]: **the four laws differ by at most 0.03 dex in the median; the non-zero level is the baryon-model offset above.** Because the pre-flight said NOT POSSIBLE, no sentence here says any law is separated, preferred or disfavoured.
5. **Implied a₀ (descriptive, not a verdict; CFG223's estimator s\*): six of the seven have NO ROOT (D ≤ 1: no a₀ can make ν < 1).** Only **ALESS 122.1 has one: s\* = 8.82, a₀ = 8.25e-10 m/s²** (8.8 × the canonical 9.36e-11; 7.3 × the alt footing 1.131e-10; 6.0 × the 1.37e-10 that the record quotes as SPARC's best free ν_mono fit at Υ = 0.50; FLAT 1.0, PROXY 1.79, H(z) 3.07, M-DEC 0.87 expected at z = 2.02). **Error budget (ALESS 122.1):** statistical 68% ×4.2 to ×14.4 and 95% ×1.5 to ×22.6 (Monte Carlo over the velocity, the random baryon errors; the declared intrinsic scatter is NOT in it); gas class band ±0.03 / ±0.093 dex → s\* 7.9 to 9.7 / 6.2 to 11.8; M* band ±1σ / ±0.30 dex → 6.6 to 10.6 / 5.5 to 11.2; joint corners inner 5.9 to 11.7, outer **3.72 to 15.1**; pressure α = 0 / 1.68 / 3.36 → 8.8 / 13 / 17.9; geometry (R_e × 1.5, ÷ 1.5, stars at 2 R_e, spherical) 14.2 / 7.9 / 10.9 / 14.7; no He 13.2; P2 kernel 12.8; ALPAKA inclination and ring variants do not apply. Envelope over every band corner and variant: **3.7 to 17.9**. **Kernel lever d log₁₀ s\*/d(baryon dex) = −2.0** at its own D (local kernel slope 0.116): INFORMATIVE by the A3 rule. **Read this plainly:** it is ONE galaxy, a dispersion-supported system (σ = 157 km/s, V/σ = 3.4) whose V_circ(2 r_e) = 533 km/s is the authors' model output; its δ_FLAT = +0.226 lies within 1.3σ of every law's noise-free expectation (FLAT +1.22σ, PROXY +1.02σ, H(z) +0.74σ, with the pre-flight SD 0.184 that includes the intrinsic scatter); the s\* scale is large because the kernel is nearly flat at y = 4.8, so a +0.23 dex excess in g_obs becomes a factor 8.8 in a₀; and the pre-flight shows that adding the declared intrinsic scatter widens a single galaxy's 16 to 84% range of log₁₀ s\* to about 1.1 dex under truth FLAT. **a₀ cannot be calculated from these seven**: six give no root, one gives a number with ±0.3 dex statistical, a factor 4.8 systematic envelope and the intrinsic scatter on top. ## What would make a z ≥ 2 class-M test informative (post hoc, arithmetic)
Outer radii (y ≈ 3 at 11 to 20 kpc, 1.3 to 8 × the stated ones), rotation curves that resolve the disc (ALPAKA R_ext is about one beam for IDs 15, 19, 22), inclinations known to better than about 5° (the ALPAKA 18 and 22 values are 26° ± 9 to 18°), stellar masses and geometry that do not leave the baryon inventory above the dynamics (M_bar/M_dyn 3 to 6 here), and baryons known to a few hundredths of a dex AND the intrinsic scatter below about 0.05 dex (Q3: at the stated radii nothing helps). A galaxy is informative about a₀ only near y of order 1 to 5: ALESS 122.1 is the one here.

## Limits
One radius per galaxy and one thin exponential disc for stars and gas with one R_e (sizes differ; no bulge; the ALPAKA R_e for 15, 19, 22 is R_ext/1.2); ALPAKA V_ext averages the last two rings and is not pressure-corrected; the ALPAKA inclinations are fixed in the authors' fits; AGN hosts (15, 18, BX610) carry SED-mass systematics; ALESS 122.1's M* (MAGPHYS) and its dynamical model are the authors'; the class-M gas inherits Dunne+22's normalisation and its per-galaxy statistical error, HI is neglected, the He factor is a fixed 1.36; the stellar band (outer ±0.30) is declared; the floor (D ≥ 1) presumes ν ≥ 1 and a thin-disc g_bar; the M_bar/M_dyn diagnostic compares total baryons with the spherical dynamical mass (crude); the implied a₀ shows no root wherever D ≤ 1, is lever-amplified where it exists, and omits the intrinsic scatter in its statistical interval; a class-M label says a per-galaxy optimised multi-tracer gas mass exists, not that its absolute scale is right.
