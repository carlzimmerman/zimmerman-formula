**Is it a conflict or only a boundary at 2 sigma? A boundary.** The gap between the nine-set upper edge and the SLUGGS lower edge is 0.06 in phi on the grid (0.0525 continuous) canonical and 0.11 (0.1065) alt. On a 0.0005 grid with linear interpolation, the smallest acceptance level at which all ten populations overlap is **2.07 sigma canonical (at phi = 0.73) and 2.09 sigma alt (phi = 0.71); at gamma = 3 it is 2.62 / 2.63 sigma; the nine alone overlap at 1.48 / 1.46 sigma.** Only SLUGGS and the LVD are in tension (the pair alone gives 2.07 / 2.09 sigma, the same as the full ten). So 'empty' here means 2.07 sigma against a 2 sigma line; it says nothing beyond that.

**Sensitivity to the slopes.** A uniform shift of every gamma_i by -0.1 opens the ten-set ([0.55, 0.71] canonical, [0.50, 0.66] alt); +0.1 already empties the SLUGGS 2 sigma window itself (2.01 sigma at phi = 1): a knife edge in a slope uncertain by more than 0.1. Monte Carlo with independent Gaussian scatter on each slope (200 draws, seed 20260929): sigma_gamma = 0.2: the ten-set is non-empty in 88/200 (canonical) and 70/200 (alt) draws; sigma_gamma = 0.4: 132/200 and 121/200 (MUTATE at gamma = 3: 0/200 at 0.2, 7 and 9 of 200 at 0.4). Leave-one-galaxy-out (canonical): dropping any one of 10 of the 16 galaxies opens the ten-set (window about [0.6, 0.71]); dropping NGC 4494 (offset -0.112) or NGC 2768 empties the SLUGGS window instead.

**What the agent would attack.** (1) **The slopes are not per-galaxy published slopes:** they are one relation, gamma = clip(-0.63 log M* + 9.81, 2, 4), applied to the catalogue stellar mass (the clip never binds; gamma runs 2.49 to 3.43); the agent could not check whether the source quotes 3-D or projected slopes; if projected, the 3-D gamma is about 1 higher and SLUGGS sits at +5.6 sigma at phi = 1 (R4 shift +1.0): **please confirm from the source.** (2) No slope uncertainty enters the error (SLUGGS's error is the sample SEM of the offsets); a slope error of 0.1 to 0.2, plus the isotropy assumption (the rule's SLUGGS offset at phi = 1 falls to 0.56 sigma with radial orbits, CFG113), swamps the 0.06 gap. (3) The edges are 0.01 grid points; the continuous canonical SLUGGS lower edge is 0.76. (4) Nine of the ten populations are not independent (taken from CFG71's scans, several share inputs: ultra-faints, Bootes I, Tucana II). (5) The per-population 2 sigma intersection is not a joint test. (6) Wording that says 'not with dynamical SLUGGS masses' overstates a 2.07 sigma boundary.

Nothing here says the data favour the framework; kappa = 1/2 is FITTED.
