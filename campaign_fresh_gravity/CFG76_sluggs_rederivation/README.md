# CFG76 -- independent re-derivation of CFG55's SLUGGS / JAM headline

**Question (frozen before the first run: `CFG76_SPEC_FROZEN.txt`).** Does CFG55's headline reproduce from independently written code: with JAM-calibrated dynamical stellar masses the law's mean offset over 16 SLUGGS early types is +0.097 dex (3.99 sigma) and the derived rule's +0.046 (2.58 sigma), canonical footing (alt +0.088 / +0.047)?

**Result: it reproduces exactly, under a different reading of the lane than its README gives.**
- With the plain RAR kernel nu = 1/(1 - exp(-sqrt(y))) everywhere and the dynamical mass rescaled M_JAM ~ D to the SLUGGS distance, the offsets are +0.096984 +/- 0.02431 (3.989 sigma) and +0.045640 +/- 0.01771 (2.577 sigma), matching CFG55 to 2e-6 per galaxy (canonical) and 8e-5 (alt); Hernquist-fraction (+0.087 / +0.033), without-NGC 7457 (+0.096 / +0.047) and JAM x 1/2 (law +0.216, 9.1 sigma) rows also match.
- **CFG55's README is wrong on two points.** (i) The lane's numbers come from the RAR kernel, not nu_mono (with nu_mono the offsets are about +0.001 higher; conclusions unchanged). (ii) 'L_r and r_1/2 moved to the SLUGGS distance' (L ~ D^2 at fixed M/L) is not what the lane does: it rescales M_JAM ~ D. The agent's frozen primary took the literal reading (nu_mono, L ~ D^2) and got +0.098 (3.98 sigma) / +0.047 (2.66 sigma), inside the 0.0015 gate tolerance but not at strict rounding; at ddof = 0 the rule error would read 0.017, not 0.018 (the lane uses ddof = 1). The distance choice changes log M* by up to 0.05 dex in the 6 galaxies whose SLUGGS and ATLAS3D distances differ, the means by about 1e-3.
- **Not reproduced: the Salpeter population row.** The agent gets +0.076 / +0.021 against the lane's +0.049 / -0.033 (it took ATLAS3D logML_Salp x L; the README does not define 'Salpeter population masses').

**Post-hoc attack diagnostics (declared as such; `cfg76_posthoc_attack.py`, `posthoc_attack.log`):**
- **The GC density slope gamma = 3 is fixed for every galaxy, and it decides the size of the residual.** Law offset +0.017 (0.7 sigma) at gamma = 2.0, +0.053 at 2.4, +0.097 at 3.0, +0.134 at 3.6; zero at gamma = 1.83. Rule offset -0.046 at 2.0, -0.005 at 2.4, +0.046 at 3.0, +0.087 at 3.6; zero at gamma = 2.45. So the 4.0 / 2.6 sigma is a statistical error at a fixed slope, not a per-galaxy measured one, and the rule's residual vanishes for gamma about 2.4-2.5. (Whether real SLUGGS GC slopes are near 3 is not tested here.)
- Leave-one-out law range 0.085-0.110. Without M87, NGC 4365, 4374, 5846 (N = 12): law +0.055 (2.7 sigma), rule +0.026 (1.3 sigma). By stellar mass: log M* < 11.2 (N = 8) law +0.055, rule +0.043; >= 11.2 (N = 8) law +0.139, rule +0.048. The result leans on the group and cluster centrals, where omitted hot gas and folded-in intracluster GCs and rotation can inflate the observed dispersion (h50's caveat).
- **A name-key artefact in h50's sample:** it looks up 'NGC720' in a dict keyed 'NGC0720', silently dropping NGC 720 and NGC 821 (133 GCs). With the key corrected the SLUGGS-mass law over 21 galaxies is +0.080 +/- 0.022, and the JAM sample with NGC 821 (N = 17) gives law +0.100 +/- 0.023, rule +0.053 +/- 0.018 (3.0 sigma). The story does not change, but the '19 of SLUGGS' and '16 with JAM' populations are defined by an accident of parsing.
- Sizes and masses come from different surveys (Hernquist a_h from SLUGGS R_e, calibration radius from ATLAS3D r_1/2; ratio median 1.23, range 0.61-1.70), so 'half the stars inside r_1/2' is inconsistent with the Hernquist profile (0.28-0.57); using the Hernquist fraction lowers the offsets by about 0.01. The calibration takes the JAM mass as the total mass in a 3D sphere at y of order 1-3, where nu_mono and nu_RAR differ. The rule's collapse mass is evaluated from the dynamically calibrated M*, off Mandelbaum's Chabrier scale, and NGC 7457 falls below the table and is clamped. The errors are the galaxy-to-galaxy sample SEM (scatter about 0.1 dex) and ignore the bin dispersion errors.

**Controls.** Passed: closed-form limits (point mass GM/(4r) to 2e-4; deep-MOND sqrt(GMa0)/3 and sigma_los = sigma_r to 1e-4; the EdS turnaround (3 pi/4)^2 to 1e-8 with the agent's own LCDM solution 1+delta_ta = 11.76; M200m -> M200c to 6e-15; nu_mono = nu_RAR for y < 2), the sample counts (27 galaxies, 3440 GC velocities, 19 in the dispersion sample), the 16-galaxy ATLAS3D cut, JAM x 1/2 (+0.216, 9.0 sigma by the agent's ddof = 1 error). **Failed as declared:** the ddof = 0 reading of G1 (kernel-independent). MUTATE (JAM x 1/2) raises both offsets by 0.118-0.153 dex, fails the reproduction gate G1 and exits 1; the agent's spec wrongly said G3 (SLUGGS-mass rows) must also fail (they do not depend on JAM). The main run exits 1 by design (the ddof = 0 failure).

**Scope.** The CFG55 arithmetic is right; the residual's size rests on the fixed gamma = 3 and the group/cluster centrals. Not evidence for or against the framework; kappa = 1/2 is FITTED. The agent read CFG35, CFG36, h50 and hunt_lib code (not CFG38/55/71) for the collapse-mass function, edge phantom, GC estimator and nu_mono table; it compared to CFG55's committed JSON only after the pre-declared runs.
