**Reading.**
- **Inside 0.31 Mpc the outer-halo problem closes with no free parameter.** The census supply (N = 6–10) exceeds what KiDS
  demands there (N ≥ 3–4, CFG803). CFG801's failure is fully repaired from the record's own pieces: the law, the CFG748
  infall, the census and the cosmic ratio.
- **Beyond about 0.4 Mpc the galaxy's own catchment runs out.** The edge sits at 320–450 kpc, but the stacked lensing
  signal continues to 2.6 Mpc.
- This is where B21 warn that their isolated sample is contaminated by neighbours (the two-halo term). In the framework,
  neighbours' cold energy also lenses, so a two-halo term is expected, but **it is not computed here**.
- Until it is, the outer signal is an open problem, not a pass.

**Caveat.** The census f_ret = 0.10 is an observational input carried in the record. It was not fitted here, and it is not
derived.

**Controls.** K1 (N = 10 at log M_b 10.5) and K2 (B = 83.9) PASS. MUTATE (f_ret = 1) reproduces the CFG801 edge failure
(Δχ² +197), so the test bites.
