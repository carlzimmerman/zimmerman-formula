## 1. Data and the frozen cut (columns only)
Catalogue columns (`data_assembly/mightee_hi_catalogue_2026-10-02/catalogue_columns_from_paper.csv`): `z_HI`, `D_L_Mpc` (H₀ 70, Ω_M 0.3, no cosmic flows), `log_M_HI` (± err), `SNR_3D`, `W_50_km_s` (± err; busy-function fit at 50 % of the peak; **not inclination corrected**), `incl_deg`, `axis_ratio`, `log_M_stel` (± err; BAGPIPES, Chabrier, delayed SFH), the five flags. **Frozen cut:** (a) the **golden sample**: low_confidence = blended = confused = bad_ellipse = contaminated = 0; (b) 0.02 ≤ z_HI ≤ 0.093 (peculiar velocities of 300 km s⁻¹ are ≤ 5 % in distance above z = 0.02); (c) **45° ≤ incl ≤ 80°**; (d) log M_HI ≥ 9.0 (the high-z end is mass-limited; the low-z end is mass-matched below); (e) W50 ≥ 80 km s⁻¹ and σ_W50/W50 ≤ 0.15; (f) SNR_3D ≥ 8. The cut is applied by code to the columns; the survivors are counted at stage A, never looked at.
**Redshift windows by rule:** terciles of z_HI of the survivors (equal counts), W1 (nearest), W2, W3 (farthest).

## 2. The chain (CFG260/281's, for comparability; every choice frozen here)
W_c = (W50 − δ)/(1 + z) with δ = 0 (primary; knob δ = 11 km s⁻¹); **V = W_c / (2 sin i) with the catalogue inclination** (primary; sensitivity: the isotropic sin 60° of CFG260/281); M_gas = 1.33 M_HI; **M_b = M_gas + M\*** (the catalogue's BAGPIPES mass; knob: ± 0.25 dex on M\*); **R = D_HI/2 with D_HI = 10^(0.506 log M_HI − 3.293) kpc** (CFG260's relation; knob ± 0.15 dex); g_bar = G M_b/R², g_obs = V²/R. **Estimator:** CFG223's median-residual s\* (`hzq_core`), ν_mono, canonical a₀ = 9.3603 × 10⁻¹¹ m s⁻² (the SPARC-empirical scale s = 1.282 for the controls; alt footing for M1), bootstrap over galaxies (B = 4,000). Reported per window and for the pooled sample: s\*, a₀, the bootstrap 68/95 % intervals, the y quartiles (regime), the baryon bands ± 0.15/± 0.30 dex, the recipe half-width (quadrature of the knobs above plus the HI-size relation variant D_HI ± 0.15 dex and the H₀ = 67.4 distance variant).

## 3. Calibration controls (frozen; evaluated before any a₀ number is read; each can fail)
- **CC1 (local level):** the nearest window W1, mass-matched where needed, must return s\* within ±0.15 dex of the SPARC-empirical scale 1.282 AND within ±0.15 dex of CFG281's ALFALFA matched value 1.449; the recipe half-width must be ≤ 0.20 dex; otherwise the chain is reported **NOT CALIBRATED** (CFG281's rule).
- **CC2 (SPARC closure of the baryon side):** the recipe's baryon side (M_b = 1.33 M_HI + 0.5 L[3.6], R = D_HI/2 from the HI-size relation, g_bar at R) applied to the SPARC galaxies on disk (Lelli+2016c table, quality ≤ 2, i ≥ 30°, V_flat > 0) with g_obs = V_flat²/R must return s\* within ±0.15 dex of 1.282; this isolates the size-relation and point-mass geometry bias from the width and inclination side.
- **CC3 (distance drift):** the difference of s\* between W1 and W3 (mass-matched) must be ≤ 0.10 dex (CFG281's clause); a larger drift is the finding (a distance-dependent bias of the chain) and the chain is NOT CALIBRATED across distance.
- **CC4 (SELFTEST):** widths fabricated from the law at s_true = 2 on the survivors' own M_HI, M\*, inclinations (0.15 dex scatter, 10 % errors), 100 worlds: the chain's estimator returns s_true within its stated bias (CFG281: −0.023 dex) and the 95 % interval covers it in at least 90 of 100 worlds; the real widths are not loaded.
- **MUTATE=1:** widths × 1.1892 (s\* must rise by 0.30 ± 0.05 dex); **MUTATE=2:** sin i_eff = 0.70 on the isotropic variant (CFG281: +0.37 ± 0.06). M5: the chain core reproduces CFG281's committed s\* when fed CFG281's arrays.

## 4. Decision map (no verdict words)
**D1** calibration status (CC1–CC3). **D2** the level: s\*(W1), s\*(W2), s\*(W3) and the pooled value with their bootstrap and recipe widths. **D3** the distance trend: the slope of log s\* against log D_L (three windows, bootstrap) and ρ_i = s\*(W_i)/s\*(W1) with the pull against ρ = 1 on statistics alone and with the recipe widths; reported against CFG281's ALFALFA windows (s\* = 1.61 at 20–50 Mpc and 1.26 at 50–85 Mpc: a drop of 0.107 dex between the two windows, whose mean distances differ by a factor of about two; CFG281 recorded the drift 0.105 and did not fit a slope) and against the BUDHIES ρ = 0.18 at z = 0.2. **D4** what the trend would mean for BUDHIES if it continued: Δ log s\* = slope × log₁₀(D_BUDHIES/D_W3); stated as an extrapolation, not a result. **D5** if the chain is NOT CALIBRATED, no a₀ statement is made and the row stays off the chart.

## 5. Stage structure and hand-estimate slots (for the orchestrator to fix before the first run; no hand estimate of any lever)
Stage A (counts only: survivors after each cut, the window edges, the y and M_HI distributions; no V, g_obs or s\*); the SELFTEST; the measurement once; MUTATE=1 and 2; README; explicit-path commits. **Slots:** survivors N (plausible 60–200); s\*(W1) in [1.1, 2.0]; |log s\*(W3)/s\*(W1)| ≤ 0.3 dex; the CC2 closure within ±0.2 dex.

## 6. What this lane cannot say
It does not test FLAT against the rival (the lever is 2–4.5 %). The inputs are catalogue values (no resolved kinematics): widths from integrated profiles, optical inclinations with an assumed q₀, BAGPIPES masses, a HI-size relation, distances without cosmic flows, and a point-mass geometry at D_HI/2; the sample is HI-selected and mass-limited at high z. A passed calibration control would show the chain's level is right locally, not that it is right at z = 0.2.
