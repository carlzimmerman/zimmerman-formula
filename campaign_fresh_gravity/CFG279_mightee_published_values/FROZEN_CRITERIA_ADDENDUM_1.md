## 2. Three corrections (all pre-run)
**(a) The ν_mono ≡ exponential-RAR clause of C2 is false and is replaced.** The criteria (§4 C2) froze "ν_mono equals 1/(1 − e^{−√y}) to 1e-9 on a grid (so s = a₀/0.93603 is an identity of kernels)". `CFG4_common` keeps `nu_rar` (the exponential RAR, "a reference description of the data's shape, not a base") and `nu_mono` (FP1's committed monotone kernel) as different functions. Measured in scratch: the two agree to ≈ 3e-9 relative for y ≤ 1 (table-interpolation noise, so even there the 1e-9 clause fails), to 2.1e-4 relative at y = 2.54 (where the RAR's phantom h = y(ν − 1) peaks), and **differ by up to 2.4 % (Δν = 0.025) above it** (at y ≈ 14; 2.3 % at y = 10; 1.1 % at y = 5; 0.74 % at y = 100). Replacement: the script reports max |Δν|/ν for y ≤ Y_PEAK and for y > Y_PEAK; the clause "≤ 1e-3 for y ≤ Y_PEAK" is the control (can fail); the s column of the points file is labelled a **convention**: s = a₀(published, exponential RAR) / a0_canonical is NOT an a₀ implied through ν_mono; the equivalence holds only for the low-acceleration part of a RAR (y ≲ 2.5), and the conversion for a whole-sample level cannot be given without the per-galaxy data. My earlier working statement "ν_mono ≡ exponential RAR numerically" (calc-chat summary, memory of the plan) was an unverified assumption and is withdrawn.

**(b) CFG258's canonical constant.** b₃ is computed as 5.23e-10 / `K4.A0['canonical']` (= 5.587412935), CFG258's own definition (`B3 = A1 / A0[foot]`), not 5.23/0.93603 (5.587428); the §4 C2 tolerance of 1e-9 then applies to CFG258's committed f_iii and f_rival. The same constant is used for s (the s column divides by 0.9360325).

**(c) The second clause of both frozen reactivity controls cannot pass.** §4 froze: MUTATE=1 (world on the anchored slope) must be "CONSISTENT with ANCH and DISFAVOURED against FLAT at the formal width"; SELFTEST (world on FLAT) must be "CONSISTENT with FLAT and DISFAVOURED against ANCH at the formal width". With the paper's own MIGHTEE-only errors the formal σ_b is 1.51–1.55 per unit z and the FLAT–ANCH gap is 4.548, i.e. **2.94σ (MUTATE) and 3.01σ (SELFTEST), below the threshold 3.29 at the formal width**. The real run's ANCH pull (−3.69σ) reaches it only because the observed slope is negative. The slip is mine (I froze a clause without computing it). The frozen numbers below replace the second clauses; the as-frozen clauses are evaluated, printed "AS-FROZEN … expected FAIL by arithmetic" and are not load-bearing.
