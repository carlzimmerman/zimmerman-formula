# CFG276 — GN20 (z = 4.055): near-Newtonian; the authors' best baryon set accounts for the circular velocity within 10 %; no constraint on a₀

**Bottom line.** Übler+24's circular velocity at R_e = 3.6 kpc is 496 km/s (g_obs = 2.2 × 10⁻⁹ m s⁻², 24 a₀; the model's pressure term is in it once). The independent baryons give y = g_bar/a₀ = 8–26 at R_e. **With the Boogaard+25 resolved radiative-transfer gas (2.87 × 10¹¹ M☉) plus the Tan+14 stars (1.1 × 10¹¹), the baryons alone supply the Newtonian pull to within 10 % (median D = g_obs/g_bar = 0.90 at R_e, 1.05 at 2 R_e): the R_e row is at the FLOOR by 0.04 dex only (64 % of Monte Carlo draws have no root), the 2 R_e row has s\* ≤ 1.04, and both are ILL-CONDITIONED (recipe widths 1.1–1.3 dex).** With the older Hodge+12 gas (1.3 × 10¹¹) or stars only the roots are vacuous upper bounds (s\* ≤ 11.7 and ≤ 44.3). At y ≳ 8 a 10 % change in g_bar moves s\* by a factor of 5–10 (CFG240 T3), so GN20 cannot test a₀(z). κ = ½ is FITTED. No law statement is made.

**Chart row (fixed by the frozen rule): `GN20 [stars+RT; Re]` = FLOOR, median D = 0.904, z = 4.055** — **not an informative floor**: it is 0.04 dex below 1, the baryons would have to fall by only 0.06 dex for s\* = 1, a 0.15 dex baryon shift (the inner band) already gives a root (s\* = 7.9), and in a world on the law at s = 2 the R_e rows sit at the floor in 29–41 % of 100 worlds (SELFTEST). **What moves the roots.** The inclination is the largest kinematic lever: the Hodge/Bik 30° reading (v_circ(R_e) = 574 km/s, g_obs × 1.34) raises s\* by +0.31 / +0.42 dex (stars; stars + H12) at R_e and by +1.06 dex for the RT row at 2 R_e; the inflow model (v_circ = 472) lowers it by 0.11 / 0.18 dex (0.43 / 0.77 dex at 2 R_e, where the model's enclosed mass falls from 10^11.68 to 10^11.51); the compact gas (R_e = 2.5 kpc) lowers the H12 root by 0.73 dex. The baryon bands move the roots over an order of magnitude (e.g. [stars + H12; R_e]: 25.5 / 0.67 at −0.15 / +0.15 dex).

## What this lane cannot say
It does not measure a₀ at z = 4.055: one galaxy at y = 4–26 with a model-dependent circular velocity (a halo and a baryon prior sit inside the model that produced it), a 16 % difference in V between the 38° and 30° inclination readings (0.13 dex in g_obs), a 2.2× disagreement between the two gas masses (α_CO 0.8 vs 2.76), one literature stellar mass, and a near-Newtonian lever. A floor with the lowest independent set would be robust against any higher set; here the floor is only 0.04 dex deep. The total independent baryons with the RT gas (4.0 × 10¹¹ M☉) are 1.5× Übler's fitted M_bar (2.6 × 10¹¹), so the model's dark-matter fraction of 0.30 rests on a smaller baryon budget than the independent one; with the independent stars + RT baryons the Newtonian pull alone is already about the circular velocity (D 0.90–1.05). Class S/L carries no law statement.
