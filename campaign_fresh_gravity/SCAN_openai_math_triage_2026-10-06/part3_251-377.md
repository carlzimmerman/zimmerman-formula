## Deep-read conclusions

**377 (infinity-harmonic C^{1,alpha}, d >= 3).** Operator treated: Delta_infty u = u_i u_j u_ij = 0, the p -> infinity limit. The deep-MOND equation is the p = 3 Laplacian with a source: div(|grad Phi| grad Phi) = 4 pi G a0 rho. Differences that matter: (1) p = 3 is a finite exponent, for which C^{1,alpha} regularity of p-harmonic functions is classical (Uraltseva, Uhlenbeck, Evans, Lewis, DiBenedetto, Tolksdorf) and extends to bounded right-hand sides; infinity-harmonic is the genuinely degenerate case, and this paper settles a d >= 3 gap that does not affect p = 3. (2) No source term; the paper treats homogeneous solutions only. (3) The Holder exponent alpha_d is not explicit (in (0, 1/3]), so no sharp constant. (4) Full normalized-in-oscillation interior estimate gives no uniqueness, no external-field (EFE) statement and no superposition statement. Conclusion: no application to P5; score 1 (relation noted, as requested). One side remark: the infinity-Laplacian appears physically only as a limit in a "p large" family, which is not the MOND regime.

**362-364 (kinetic theory) for P7.** Not deep-read. From the abstracts: 362 is global smoothness for ONE-species relativistic Vlasov-Maxwell with compactly supported density (charged particles, repulsive-type field, relativistic velocity bound), not Vlasov-Poisson with attractive gravity; the relevant classical results (Pfaffelmoser, Lions-Perthame) already give global Vlasov-Poisson existence and say nothing about excess small-scale power. 363 shows non-uniqueness of weak entropy solutions for Boltzmann (a warning about weak-solution uniqueness, not a growth law). 364 derives Boltzmann from particle systems (kinetic limit). None gives nonlinear aggregation, a growth rate, or a MOND-modified Poisson statement. Score 1 each, no P7 input.

## Bottom line

Nothing in families 251-377 forces or closes any of P1-P8. About 113 of 127 are pure-math outside our needs (operator algebras, group theory, homotopy, Banach spaces, symplectic geometry, quantum information). The only concrete tool is 374 (OT stability of Brenier maps), already adopted by others as the settling framework, and its uniform-source/convex-domain hypotheses do not match our non-uniform target, so use it only as a heuristic until checked. 377 does NOT bear on the 3-Laplacian deep-MOND equation (it is p = infinity, homogeneous, non-explicit exponent). 362-364 give no P7 handle. P1 (rational 4/32 pi) and P3 (5.36) have no relevant family here; P4 and P6 only analogies. Recommended: no further reading from this range.
