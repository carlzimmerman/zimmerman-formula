# What would decide between the readings -- 2026-09-29

Documentary: it collects committed forecasts and lane results and adds no new physics or number. Every row cites its lane. "Cap" is the significance a test cannot exceed whatever the sample size, because of a systematic floor. Nothing here says the data favour the framework; kappa = 1/2 is FITTED; nothing says the theory is closed.

The three readings: **B** (candidate B: the Lambda-tied law a0 = kappa c sqrt(G rho_Lambda) with a cold conserved fluid, with or without the derived cold-mass rule), the **bare MOND-type law** (the same law with no cold fluid; Arm A in CFG63), and **LCDM** (standard halos; the comparator of CFG42/CFG67/CFG69).

## 1. Gaia DR4 wide binaries -- the one test that can separate B from anything (CFG63)

- **Data and date:** released 2 December 2026; the pipeline is built and the pre-registration is frozen (Amendments 13, 14, 15). About 30,000 pairs expected; the DR3 dry run had 10,624.
- **Separates:** B (gamma-hat = 1.000) from the bare law (1.1614 at Arm A's floor; alt 1.1917). Cap 8.1 sigma (6.1 with Arm A's own +-0.0175); 3 sigma needs about 4,342 pairs (alt 2,940); 5.8 sigma at N = 30,000 (4.0 at DR3-like precision).
- **Does not separate:** B from LCDM or Newton (all predict gamma-hat = 1.000). A Newtonian outcome is the survival of a test B could have failed, not a confirmation.
- **Kills:** gamma-hat at or above 1.083 (frozen text 1.084) kills B, Newton and LCDM (the chain ceiling survives to 1.155); gamma-hat at or below 1.079 kills the bare law. Any Newtonian value kills the bare law cleanly (hidden triples push gamma-hat up, never down). A boost result needs the pre-registered stability checks before it kills B (contamination is a one-sided bias).
- **Floor and unforecastable items:** sigma_sys = 0.02; hidden-triple contamination is outside it; whether N = 30,000 survives the frozen cuts; the alt Arm A top (1.2267) lands in the 1.23 no-verdict zone in 43-49% of true outcomes; DR4's error scaling is flagged approximate.

## 2. a0 at z about 2.5 -- discriminating only against H(z)-tracking, and no data yet (CFG52, CFG54, CFG63)

- **Prediction:** the flat law shifts the galaxy zero point by 0 (branch A); LCDM about +0.334 dex; an a0 proportional to H(z) about +0.58 dex against flat. A flat a0 is also what a bare MOND law with a constant a0 predicts, so this test cannot separate B from it.
- **Data needed:** 2 to 4 (canonical) JWST integral-field plus ALMA discs at z about 2.5 with g_bar < a0 and mass calibration below about 0.1 dex; the registered test needs 3 rotators at +-0.10 dex or 4 at +-0.20.
- **Now:** nothing usable is on disk. CFG52: no clean object at z >= 1.5 with g_bar < 0.3 a0 (canonical; the alt footing has one further RC100 object, zC 410041, z = 2.45, about 0.295 a0); of 242 galaxies with g_bar < a0 only 16 have a flat-versus-a0E(z) gap above 1 sigma and none above 2. CFG90 reproduces these counts exactly only with CFG52's undocumented conventions (the 242 includes a ledger object and a KROSS gas recipe; the pooled N = 14 is 15 objects minus one +1.14 dex object, weighted, and keeping it flips the rival's sign). CFG54: PHIBSS gives N = 0 of 51 (canonical; about 2 on the alt footing by scaling the printed ratios), so it is non-diagnostic either way.
- **Cap (correlated 0.2-dex mass-scale systematic):** 2.3 sigma against a0 proportional to H(z); about 1.3 sigma against LCDM-native (1.7 on a different floor reading; the floor bridge is derived in CFG63, not committed). To reach 3 sigma the floor must fall to about 0.19 dex (against H(z)) or 0.11 dex (against LCDM-native). LCDM-native cannot be killed at 2 sigma with the present floor.
- **Kills:** a mean offset above the flat value below 0.07 dex kills H(z) tracking (2 sigma, N of 10 or more); above 0.5 kills flat B.

## 3. Ultra-faint dwarfs -- capped, and the readings are not separable (CFG42, CFG46, CFG51, CFG66, CFG63, CFG69, CFG73, CFG74, CFG78)

- **What separates:** the bare law from B's derived cold-mass rule (Delta = 0.38 dex). The rule is itself not a passing reading (CFG45).
- **Cap 2.5 sigma:** floors are the Upsilon_V range (0.077) and the rule's collapse-mass floor (0.133), 0.154 together; the binary-treatment shift (0.104, CFG46) lowers the cap to 2.1. About 5 systems reach 2 sigma at today's scatter; 3 sigma is never reached. Perfect dispersions save at most a factor 1.46 in N, since the scatter between systems (0.20 dex or more) sets N.
- **Now:** 40 single-epoch systems (bare-law median +0.325 dex, 3.8 sigma; the rule -0.06, closed but a weak test); 8 statistically binary-corrected (CFG46: law +0.295 / +0.206 / +0.171 at f = 0 / free / 0.7, 1.8 to 1.0 sigma; rule -0.151; reproduced independently, CFG78, with an inherited error recipe and a rule value that is a clamp artefact); 2 multi-epoch (Bootes I +0.22, 2.3 sigma, its cold component alone -0.07 +- 0.20; Tucana II +0.47, 3.6 sigma, tidally disturbed). The data sit midway between the two readings (+0.21 against midpoint 0.19), so neither is excluded. A luminosity ordering of the offsets is not detected on the 40 systems (CFG83: tau_c +0.18, p = 0.09) and only flagged, below the declared threshold, on the 8.
- **Kills (8 systems):** a median offset above the law larger than 0.35 kills the isolated law; below 0.03 kills the rule; between is neither (today is here).
- **LCDM:** the ultra-faint gate is weakly diagnostic for a standard NFW halo (it passes over about four decades of halo mass, CFG69/CFG73; scatter and contraction change nothing, CFG74; only an assumed equal-mass cored halo makes it discriminate). B and LCDM are not separable here.
- **Needs:** more multi-epoch systems (only 2 today; Hydrus I, Reticulum II, Ursa Major II have no repeat epochs in the catalogue used) and a collapse-mass floor that data cannot shrink.

## 4. KiDS-1000 lensing (CFG61, CFG67, CFG77, CFG63)

- **Not forecastable from committed budgets:** the cost (29.4-49.3 in delta-chi2) and the standard-halo spread (50.2) both scale with area, so their ratio (0.59-0.98) is fixed; the B-versus-NFW difference is about a tenth of the spread.
- **The colour split (early minus late at fixed g_bar):** the law's chi2 is 28.1 / 7 (p = 2.1e-4), reproduced independently (CFG77); the law's chi2 equals the zero-model chi2 (28.085 against 28.074), so any model whose predicted early-minus-late difference at fixed g_bar is negligible (B, the bare MOND law) fails equally at face value, while halo models in which lensing mass grows with M* (colour-split or colour-blind Moster) fit (6.5/7 and 6.9/7, CFG67); it is fragile to unmodelled scatter (errors x1.5 give p = 0.086). It was reported after the first run (the frozen amplitude statistic is degenerate and H1/H2 failed as coded). A LCDM control with Mandelbaum masses fits the difference (chi2 6.5 / 7; its own H2 failed), and swapping early and late breaks it (124). The hot-baryon escape (extra baryons in early lenses) needs at least about 0.25 to 0.5 of the stellar plus cold mass (best fit about 1 to 1.5), with the ladder not reproduced to its digits.
- **Needs:** the released covariance carries no systematic terms (intrinsic alignments, satellites, photo-z, colour contamination) and the isolation cut cannot be re-derived from the file; a better standard-halo model or class-dependent systematics would change the reading.

## 5. What no committed test can do

- **Kill LCDM.** Every committed forecast that could separate LCDM from B is capped (a0(z): 1.3 sigma; ultra-faints: not separable; X-ray groups: LCDM's halo mass is free, so it fits by construction) or has an identical prediction (Gaia). LCDM also fails to fit some rows in the lanes' machinery (CFG69: 15 of 32 (row x {law, sum}) cells specific to B, but the comparator is generous and its declared MUTATE control failed).
- **Separate B from the bare law in X-ray groups at R500:** Delta = 0.012 dex (1.41 against 1.45), a 1.8 sigma cap on B against a free-mass halo, 2.6 at R2500; the hydrostatic allowance (20%, 0.079 dex) is the floor.
- **Confirm B over Newton or LCDM with Gaia** (identical prediction).

## 6. Standing failures of B that the tests above do not decide (for orientation)

SLUGGS with dynamical masses (law +0.097, 4.0 sigma; rule +0.046, 2.6 sigma; reproduced independently, CFG76; the sigmas are statistical errors at a fixed globular-cluster slope gamma = 3, and vanish for gamma of about 1.8 / 2.5); the sum rule's over-prediction of the classical satellites (M31 LVD -2.67 sigma) and the Local Volume field dwarfs; no universal debris fraction at 1 sigma per population, though a common phi = 0.5 to 0.7 exists at 2 sigma for CFG59's ten populations (CFG75); the missing objects (Gap 1, a legal bound-only owned switch; Gap 2 as an identification with Gap 1). ## Not forecastable or not in hand

The KiDS budget; ultra-faints against bare MOND with the external field; groups against LCDM as a contrast (a trend test with no per-group sigma committed); Gaia contamination and the size of the surviving sample; whether the DR4 pipeline's error scaling holds; the a0(z) sample (none exists yet).
