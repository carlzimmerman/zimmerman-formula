# Closure map — 2026-09-28

Three maps for the question "does one action satisfy every gate?":
- [`GATES.md`](GATES.md): the frozen empirical gate list, with candidate B's status on each and the committed script that scores it.
- [`ACTIONS_AND_NOGOS.md`](ACTIONS_AND_NOGOS.md): every action-level construction tried, each no-go's exact hypotheses, and the three open gaps between B (an effective law) and one action.
- [`LEAN_CATALOGUE.md`](LEAN_CATALOGUE.md): what the Lean corpus certifies. The composable core is `fable_independent_2026/lean_2026/ChainCert`.

**Bottom line of the maps.** No committed action produces candidate B. Its pieces exist in three separate places, and each of the three gaps (a legal bound-only switch; a fluid that IS the law's dark density; one action with both the a₀–Λ tie and the fluid) has committed evidence that it is missing. Candidate B itself also has open or marginal gates that a complete action would still have to pass.
The gate list was frozen before any assembly attempt, so the assembly cannot move the goalposts. **Read the updates below and `GATES_STATUS_2026-09-29.md` with the frozen lists: `GATES.md` is the 09-28 freeze, superseded row by row (SLUGGS, Local Volume and M31 rows in particular), and `ACTIONS_AND_NOGOS.md` is superseded by CFG43, CFG44, CFG48, CFG49, CFG50, CFG60 and CFG70. Gap 3 is partly filled (CFG43); Gaps 1 and 2 reduce, as an identification and not a theorem, to one object (`GAPS_1_2_JOINT_STATUS.md`).**

**Update 2026-09-28 (CFG43).** Gap 3 is partly filled: the a₀–Λ tie can be written into a conserved fluid's stress cap in one action with no new local degree of freedom (`../CFG43_fluid_tie/`). The cap's entry form is postulated, and a barotropic saturating cap is excluded as a mechanism (it needs a new constant and covers about one decade in mass). Gap 2 is untouched.

**Update 2026-09-28 (CFG44).** Gap 2 has an exact target and a scoped no-go: no barotropic EOS, local closure, second force, constraint action, phantom-potential coupling or density slaving produces it. Only a temperature-slaved fluid (postulating the BTFR) and a locally virialised collisionless fluid with a postulated anisotropy survive, as restatements. The missing object, C(r) = ρ_c r³ g_tot = (a₀/4π) M_b(<r), is a non-adiabatic, nonlocal enclosed-mass energy exchange — which is Gap 1's ownership object. The three gaps are therefore two: a legal bound-only, owned switch, and the tie (partly filled, CFG43). (`../CFG44_fluid_target/`)

**Update 2026-09-28 (CFG50).** The one CFG44 exclusion gap, a fluid whose stress is the tidal tensor of a baryon-sourced auxiliary potential, was tested: it fails on reciprocity (the reaction on the baryons is O(g_law)) and on a 0.5 ceiling for the ghost-free force. Gap 2 still reduces to Gap 1. (`../CFG50_tidal_closure/`)
