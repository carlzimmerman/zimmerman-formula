# Door 11: a flowing Λ-vacuum — frozen question, variants and gates (written 2026-09-29, before any door-11 script or number)

**Origin.** The owner's picture: the dark-energy vacuum is not static; it flows, and it is boosted each time it crosses matter. Read physically: gravity is the acceleration of a flowing medium (the Painlevé–Gullstrand "river" picture of GR, where free fall is being carried by inflowing space), the medium is the Λ-vacuum so its natural acceleration scale is c√(Gρ_Λ) ~ a₀, and the boost on crossing matter makes the total push depend on the matter crossed (the enclosed mass). This file freezes how the idea is tested. It is a pre-registration of a test, not a claim that the idea works. The menu of variants was written knowing the target and knowing the ten-door result (a missing object must carry an acceleration scale; cold-matter dynamics never give r_M ∝ M^½), and says so.

**What must NOT count as a pass.** A prescribed flow is a restatement: any g(r) can be written as a steady flow with v² = 2∫g dr. A variant passes only if a *dynamical law* for the flow (an equation of motion for the vacuum medium, or a field equation), with constants tied to Λ and κ = ½ (FITTED) and nothing else, produces the required flow as its solution around baryons.

**Target.** The law's total acceleration, g_tot = ν(g_N/a₀) g_N with a₀ = κ c √(Gρ_Λ), P2 kernel primary (ν = ½ + √(¼ + a₀/g_N)), ν_mono reported; equivalently, for P2 and a point mass, CFG44's C(r). The cold dark component stays plain CDM (the flow replaces the law's phantom, not the cold mass).

## Variants (scored separately, never pooled)

- **11A — Inflow ("sink").** A radial inflow of the Λ-medium into each bound system, the rest frame being the system's own. Starting facts to verify, not assume: in GR (Painlevé–Gullstrand–de Sitter) v² = 2GM/r + Λc²r²/3 and a = −d(v²/2)/dr, the two terms add in v² with no cross term; a *linear* velocity superposition v = v_N + v_Λ produces a cross term whose acceleration scales as r^(−½) (rotation speed ∝ r^¼), not the deep-MOND r^(−1). The variant must find a flow law whose steady solution gives g ∝ √(G M a₀)/r at large r and Newton at small r, with the boost-on-crossing (a source term in the flow equation localised on the baryons) as the mechanism.
- **11B — Streaming ("past").** A uniform flow of the Λ-medium in one cosmic direction (its rest frame the CMB or the Hubble-flow frame), galaxies as obstacles that boost the flow as it passes through them. The force is then anisotropic and frame-dependent.
- **11C — Covariant completion.** The flow promoted to a field: a unit timelike vector (Einstein-aether) or a khronon whose gradient defines the flow, with the Λ-tie in its action (the generalized-Einstein-aether MOND route of Zlosnik, Ferreira & Starkman is the nearest published relative, from memory; verify). The record's exclusions bear directly on this variant and must be addressed first: the exponential khronometric MOND instability (FC-KH), KM1 (a₀ tracks the CMB-frame speed unless c₂ ≫ 1e-4), KM3 (C-H/K 1PN = GR), and the V0 region-gate obstruction; say which one applies and why it does or does not.

## Erratum 1 (2026-09-29; found by the CFG181 referee; the gates are otherwise unchanged)

The Target line writes the P2 kernel as ν = ½ + √(¼ + a₀/g_N). That is the "simple" interpolating function, not P2. **P2 is ν = √(1 + a₀/g_N)**, i.e. g_tot² = g_N² + a₀ g_N, as used by CFG44's code and CFG174. Any door-11 variant that took the kernel from this file must state which one it used; the simple kernel is a legitimate alternative to report, not the primary.
