# CFG727 pre-run addendum (2026-10-14, before any CFG727 script was run)

1. **The KiDS early-type target (4) is predetermined.**
   - H-HT's tethered/free bookkeeping equals CFG721's condensed source.
   - CFG721's T-K stage already shows a free fraction of exactly 0 for every galaxy-mass system (log M_b 10–11.5) at
     M_hot/M_b = 1. Its prediction is −0.034 (Z +3.78), on both kernels and footings.
   - In the CFG652 catchment rule, hot gas changes the supply only when (M_c + M_h)/0.9 > M_c/0.1, i.e. M_h > 8 M_c. So the
     bracket h ∈ {0, 0.5, 1, 2} cannot open a free component either.
   - Target 4 is therefore a known FAIL. CFG727 can reach at best PARTIAL. This is stated before running; the frozen rules
     are unchanged.
2. **Implementation choices (the criteria leave them open), fixed now:**
   - BCG/member split 0.5/0.5 (CFG652's F_BCG) where no catalogue BCG mass exists. eFEDS uses the catalogue Mbcg where > 0.
   - Members follow ρ ∝ r^−2.5 between 0.1 and 1 R_X (brackets −2 / −3), with R_X = R500 (eRASS1, eFEDS). ACCEPT uses the
     CFG781 fixed point R_X = rx_of(M_gas(<R_X)).
   - BCG R_e = 0.015 R_X (brackets ×0.5 / ×2).
   - Brackets are run at ν_s canonical only.
3. **Relation to CFG781 (Argon's rule)** is reported side by side.
   - CFG727: free = supply − tethered capacity (zero below ~10¹³ M☉).
   - CFG781: free = 5.364 M_hot (non-zero wherever hot gas exists).
