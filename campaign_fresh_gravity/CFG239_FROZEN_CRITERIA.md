## 2. CLAIM (1): the mechanism. Code-level statement and test

**What the code says (read, not yet run; line numbers of the frozen file):**

- Call order per trial t (Python evaluates arguments left to right, line 496: `trials[t] = vt_of(o1, o2, draw_x(len(a)))`): `rng.standard_normal((n,3))` for o1 (line 494), the same for o2 (line 495), `rng.random(n)` inside `draw_x` (line 490), then inside `vt_of`: `rng.choice([-1.0, 1.0], n)` (line 475), `rng.standard_normal(n)` for M1 (line 477), `rng.standard_normal(n)` for M2 (line 478). - The output is `np.std(trials, axis=0)` over 212 trials (ddof = 0). The cut (line 618) is `sig_vt <= 0.1 * max(1, vt0/2)` with vt0 deterministic from the observables.

**Statement to be checked (1a, sensitivity):** removing ONE pair at position j, or permuting the array, changes n and/or the position of every remaining pair; therefore the stream offset of (almost) every draw of every remaining pair changes, and each remaining pair's sigma_vt is an independent re-draw, not a perturbation. Predicted by the code alone: the fraction of remaining pairs whose sigma_vt is bit-identical after a removal is about 0 (at most the pairs before position j in trial 0's first block); the median |relative change| is that of two independent 212-trial standard-deviation estimates: SE_rel = 1/sqrt(2 x 211) = 0.0487 (Gaussian), difference SD = sqrt(2) x 0.0487 = 0.0689, median |diff| = 0.6745 x 0.0689 = 0.0465, 90th percentile 1.645 x 0.0689 = 0.113. (The calc chat's 4.8-4.9% and 11.8% are compatible with a FULL independent re-draw; I will test that identification directly, not assume it.) Caveats stated now: numpy's ziggurat `standard_normal` consumes a variable number of raw words, and `choice` goes through buffered 32-bit draws; both depend only on the stream's own values, not on pair content, so they do not create alignment; they make "offset" approximate, which is why I trace by stamps rather than by arithmetic.

**Statement to be checked (1b, the noise magnitude):** the flip RATE is a property of (212-trial Monte Carlo noise) x (density of pairs near the threshold), and exists under any global seed change even with a perfectly per-pair stream; the stream alignment explains only why ANY change of the array re-rolls. These are two different statements; the draft's "Mechanism" bullet and its item (f) can be read as one. I will test them separately.

## 3. CLAIM (2): about 2.5% of final pairs flip; sum p(1-p) = 133.5 against 142.3

**Definitions fixed here.**
- **Population.** U = the array handed to `vt_error_mc` (about 10,231 pairs, identical across G-only builds because stages A-F are fixed). A build's FINAL set F_k = {i in U : A_V ok and sigma_vt,k,i <= 0.1 max(1, vt0_i/2)}. Pairs are identified by the tuple (source_id1, source_id2), never by position. Pairs that fail A_V cannot flip the final set and are excluded from U' = U with A_V ok; I report Sigma p(1-p) for both U and U' (if the calc chat's 133.5 used U, it over-counts by the A_V-failing marginal pairs).
- **Flip (predicate).** For builds k and 0: one-way flips N(0 -> k) = |F_0 \ F_k| (in the reference, absent from k); I also report N(k -> 0) = |F_k \ F_0| and the symmetric difference (about twice). "One-way" means the reference-minus-build count; the quoted 142.3 is its mean over k = 1..9. "About 2.5% of final pairs" = one-way flips / final count: ALL 157.8/6,201 = 2.5%, G-only 142.3/6,200 = 2.3%, EF-only 151.8 = 2.4% (note the draft says 2.6% in "What was found", which is the all-ordered-pairs figure 162.3/6,201, and "about 2.5%" in the mechanism bullet; the draft never defines "flip").
- **p.** p_i = P(sigma_hat_i <= thr_i) over the Monte Carlo seed at FIXED array. Three estimators: **E1** ten seeds SEED + k, k = 0..9 (the calc chat's design; k = 0 is itself one of the ten and is also the flip reference: a bias source). **E2** E1 x n/(n-1) = 10/9 (the unbiased correction: E[p_hat(1-p_hat)] = p(1-p)(1 - 1/n)); prediction from the claim's own numbers: 133.5 x 10/9 = 148.3. **E3** a disjoint estimation set of K = 200 seeds (SEED + 1000 .. SEED + 1199), times K/(K-1). Flip counts are then taken on seeds DISJOINT from E3's: the ten seeds SEED + 0..9 (reproduces the calc chat's 142.3-type number, reference k = 0) and a validation set SEED + 5000 .. 5009 (45 unordered pairs, 90 ordered one-way counts).
- **Expectation.** For two independent draws E[N(0 -> k)] = Sigma_i p_i(1 - p_i) = Sigma q_i.
- **Null and test (pre-declared).** H0: the observed one-way count is a Poisson-binomial with per-pair probability q_i = p_hat_i(1 - p_hat_i) (E3); for a MEAN over builds sharing a reference the null is simulated (10^5 replicates of 10 independent Bernoulli builds from p_hat, statistic = mean over k = 1..9 of N(0 -> k)). Analytic cross-check: Var(N01) = Sigma q_i(1 - q_i) (about 120-130), so SD about 11 per count; the mean over nine builds sharing a reference has SD about 6-7 because the reference adds a positive covariance Sigma p(1-p)^3. Two-sided p-value from the null. **The 133.5 versus 142.3 difference (8.8) is judged "within noise" iff it is < 2 null SD of the mean; I expect z about 1.3, i.e. this comparison cannot discriminate a 6% error, and I will say so.**
- **Pair-level calibration (because Sigma p(1-p) is invariant under shuffling p across pairs).** Observed flips must concentrate where p_hat is intermediate: >= 95% of validation-set flips occur in pairs with p_hat in (0.02, 0.98) (E3), and the observed flip frequency in pairs with p_hat(1-p_hat) > 0.1 is within 15% of its prediction. This is the test that MU5 (section 6) breaks.
- **Threshold-distance analysis (the attack "is it the stream or the near-threshold pairs?").** r_i = (mean of sigma_hat over the 200 seeds)/thr_i. Pre-declared: >= 90% of validation flips have |ln r_i| <= 0.25 (about 5 x 4.9%); the flip rate in |ln r| < 0.05 is >= 20 x the rate in |ln r| > 0.3; Gaussian expectation SE_rel = 0.049 is a LOWER bound if the per-pair trial distribution is heavy-tailed, so I also report the median of the per-pair relative scatter across the 200 seeds and its 90th percentile (the calc chat's 0.050 is a median over all pairs).

**Pass lines (claim 2):** CONFIRMED if (i) my E3 prediction and my observed mean one-way count on SEED + 0..9 agree within 2 null SDs and within [0.90, 1.10] in ratio, (ii) the observed G-only mean (my re-run, k = 1..9) is within max(10, 2 SD) of 142.3, (iii) the calibration and threshold-distance lines above hold. ## 4. CLAIM (3): SD(gamma-hat)/sigma_fit about 0.3, no N trend over a factor of 4

**Estimator.** gamma-hat and sigma_fit per footing are the pipeline's own `--catalog` run printed values (registered seed 20261216, the same in every build, so the spread is build-only). SD = ddof-1 standard deviation over builds; sigma_fit = the mean printed sigma over builds; ratio = SD / mean sigma_fit. Precision (declared before the run): relative SE of an SD over K builds about 1/sqrt(2(K-1)): K = 10: 24%, K = 51: 10%, K = 100: 7%. Fit-only control: the k = 0 CSV refit with seeds 20261216 + j, j = 1..50 (carries the forward model AND bootstrap: declared as in the calc chat's design).

**N-trend test (pre-declared ladder, seeds pinned).** On K = 30 builds (seeds SEED + 3000 .. SEED + 3029, G-only), each final table is thinned by a hash bucket of my own: bucket = ((source_id1 * 11400714819323198485) mod 2^64) >> 62 (Fibonacci hashing, 4 buckets; the calc chat used a sha256 byte). Rungs: N about 1,550 (four disjoint buckets), 3,100 ({0,1}, {2,3}), 6,200 (all). SD/sigma_fit per rung with the subsets pooled as sqrt(mean variance); slope = least-squares slope of ln(ratio) on ln N over the three rungs with a 2,000-resample bootstrap over BUILDS (the rungs share builds; the four quarter-subsets of one build share the seed but are disjoint pairs). **Power (computed now):** per-rung relative errors about 7% (N = 1,550, pooled over four subsets), 10% (3,100), 13% (6,200); the slope SE about 0.11 per unit ln N (ln 4 = 1.39 span), so |slope| >= 0.22 is detectable at 2 sigma; the alternatives "build noise independent of N as a systematic" (ratio grows as sqrt(N), slope +0.5) and "noise scaling as 1/N" (slope -0.5) are each excluded at about 5 sigma. **What the test cannot do:** it is a thinning of ONE DR3 sample, not an N change of the pair population, and DR4 (N about 30,000) is 4.8 times beyond the top rung; any DR4 statement is an extrapolation and I will label it so.

**Seed-averaging (the "irreducible part").** From the K = 100 builds: SD of the mean over non-overlapping groups of m builds, m = 1, 2, 5, 10, 20; if the build noise is independent across seeds, SD_m sqrt(m)/SD_1 = 1 +- 0.25. **Interpretive cross-check (random-thinning calibration).** If flipped pairs carried only the average per-pair information, replacing about 140 of 6,200 pairs at random would give SD/sigma_fit about sqrt(2 x 133/6,200)/sqrt(2) = 0.15 (my rough hand estimate, not a measurement). ## 7. Attacks and alternatives; what each implies for the Amendment 18 text
