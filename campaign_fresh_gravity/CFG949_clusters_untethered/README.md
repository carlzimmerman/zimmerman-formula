## The hypothesis
Theorem D-T (CFG941): cold energy that is tethered to the law (its excess is the phantom) has no self-gravity. H-UT: in clusters
(hot, merging, never in tethered equilibrium) none of the cold energy is tethered. It is then ordinary self-gravitating cold matter
at the cosmic ratio, distributed by its own native collapse, and the law plays no role on cluster scales.
- **Model (reading B0):** M_lens = baryons (CFG687 pipeline: β-model gas cut at 3 Mpc, stars f* 0.20) + M_c,NFW, all Newtonian.
- **Supply:** M_c = (1 − f_b) M_ta = 5.364 × catchment baryons, with the record's census f_ret (S-cen).
- **Profile:** native collapse debris, truncated NFW with ρ_s = C ρ̄_m(z_c) (CFG930 rule, C = 3000), z_c from the native EPS
  half-mass condition, boundary r_ta(z_i), all at each cluster's own redshift.
- **Footings:** B0 contains no a0, so its numbers are identical on both footings.

## Galaxy side (Task 3)
- **Untethered density.** Median cluster (c 23.8, r_s 234 kpc): ρ_c = 2.1e5 / 4.6e4 / 8.0e3 M☉ kpc⁻³ at R = 0.25 / 0.5 / 1 Mpc.
- **Untethered mass around a 5e10 M☉ member,** as a fraction of the member's baryons (Hernquist a = 3 kpc):

| member at R | within 10 kpc | within 30 kpc | within 100 kpc |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.25 Mpc | 0.030 | 0.57 | 18.6 |
| 0.5 Mpc | 0.006 | 0.12 | 4.0 |
| 1 Mpc | 0.001 | 0.02 | 0.7 |

- **The internal law of cluster members.**
  - Inside ~10 kpc it is unchanged to ≤ 3%.
  - Beyond ~30 kpc in a cluster core, the cluster's untethered matter dominates the member's outskirts. CFG941 R2 says free mass
    enters a tethered medium's equilibrium as R(u) = g_b + g_f, so it would act on the member like extra baryons.
  - So H-UT predicts that cluster members' outer kinematics and lensing are not the isolated law.
- **The CFG945 link (qualitative, from CFG941's force law; not computed; CFG945's directory not read or touched).**
  - Untethered host matter enters Φ but not the member medium's own potential χ. So the member's tethered medium and its stars
    feel the same uniform cluster pull. The hover differential Δa = g_host of CFG941's lemma arises only when the host's field is
    tethered medium.
  - So H-UT gives cluster members universal free fall and no EFE (tides apart), unlike satellites of tethered (galaxy) hosts.
    That is a sharp contrast to test: cluster members against galaxy satellites.

## What H-UT needs and does not have
- **A selector,** saying which systems untether: clusters do, galaxies do not. CFG939 found S4 not derivable from the
  single-medium action. H-UT adds this rule, not a constant.
- **The groups between galaxies and clusters** (eFEDS 131, the KiDS early-type excess) are not tested here.
- **NFW's form and C = 3000** are declared generic collisionless-collapse conventions (NFW97, as in CFG930), not derived in the
  framework. The FRAGILE label reflects how much C, the boundary and the supply convention matter.
- In clusters, H-UT tests Ω_c/Ω_b = 5.364 plus collapse. It does not test a0 or κ.
