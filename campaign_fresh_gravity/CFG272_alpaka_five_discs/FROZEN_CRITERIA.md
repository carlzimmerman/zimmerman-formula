## 4. Estimator and statistics (the programme's, as CFG274)
- **s\*** = CFG223's median-residual root (CFG229's a0implied, ν_mono, canonical 9.3603e-11); root only if the row's D > 1, otherwise NO ROOT; absolute a₀ footing-independent (control M1).
- **Per-galaxy Monte Carlo (CFG229's, B = 10,000, seed = crc32(row label) mod 100000):** V_ext by a split-normal with its published errors; the inclination by a normal around the adopted value with the published error truncated to [5°, 85°] (g_obs × (sin i_adopted / sin i_draw)²); log M\* by a normal with σ = e_M\*/(M\* ln 10); R_e and R_ext fixed; percentiles over the draws that have a root with the fraction without a root reported; a row whose central estimate has no root is NO ROOT whatever its draws do. Pooled P4: galaxy bootstrap, B = 10,000.
- **δ_FLAT** and the **distance to the floor** Δ_floor = log₁₀ D and **the baryon shift for s\* = 1**, as CFG274.
- **ILL-CONDITIONED** iff the noiseless-world baryon lever |d log₁₀ s\*/d(baryon dex)| ≥ 10 or cannot be computed because ±0.03 dex of baryon mass destroys the root (CFG274 Addendum 1's reading).
