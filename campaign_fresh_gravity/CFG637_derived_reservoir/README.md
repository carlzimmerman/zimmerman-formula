## Verdict (frozen rule)

- **Candidate B: DERIVED RESERVOIR FAILS T3 X-COP and T4b Bullet.**
  - The derived reservoir is empty at every scale, so B collapses to CFG634's R0.
  - Groups pass and clusters fail by the same mechanism: the settled phantom takes all the supply but parks it at 1–3 Mpc.
  - The record's "B passes clusters" needed the cosmic-share identity reading (RI). The derivation does not give that reading.
- **H-CB: DERIVED RESERVOIR FAILS T3 X-COP; T4b is TENSION.**
  - The derived reservoir fixes the X-COP inner band (0.86 / 0.89, PASS) and the Bullet main aperture (z +0.3) and core (0.79 of P16).

- **No single derived rule passes groups, X-COP and the Bullet together.** This holds for either source, under either method, and for every bracket.
  - The derivation removes the per-scale convention problem in one direction only. It gives groups their R0 (empty reservoir) without any choice, but it does not give clusters enough central-to-R200 mass.
  - The shortfall at the X-COP headline radius is 33–40% of M_HSE (H-CB) and 40–45% (B).

**How hard the fail was checked.**

- The fail depends on two inherited inputs:
  - CFG634's X-COP supply convention (catchment M_b(<R200)/0.90; the cluster's turnaround catchment may hold more);
  - the record's rule that the phantom profile extends to the edge or r_ta.
- If either is wrong, the conclusion moves. That is now the precise open question: does the settled phantom really extend past R200 to r_ta in clusters?
