**The gate weakness, quantified (two things beyond the README's wording).** On a fine ladder of 68 halo-mass multiples, |z| <= 2 at every point from x0.01 to x33, and z = -2.46 at x100 (LCDM-WORSE). The low-side crossing (z = +2) is at x0.007 (x0.005 gives z +2.02), so the x0.01 pass is a hair inside the line; on the high side z crosses -2 at x60.8, so the gate is inside 2 sigma up to about x60, not only x33. The pass window spans roughly 3.9 dex of M_h, about 1.1 sigma per dex against a statistical scale of 0.12 dex.

**The circularity caveat (CFG32's g_obs is Humphrey's fitted NFW+stars model), quantified post hoc.** A model-independent g_obs is not available in the table, so the built-in LCDM form cannot be removed. Humphrey's own fitted halo with Kroupa stars scores -0.031 +/- 0.066 (z -0.48); the Moster/Duffy halo sits 0.09 dex above that form-matched limit. The pass is therefore mostly the form match plus a compensation: Moster halos are 0.4-1.5 dex heavier than Humphrey's M_vir in 6 of 7 and Duffy c is 0.2-0.4 times Humphrey's in 6 of 7; Moster M_h with Humphrey's own c gives -0.320 (z -2.58). Permuting the halos among galaxies leaves the sample mean essentially unchanged (0.035 to 0.059, |z| <= 2 in 100%) because the mean of per-galaxy offsets is permutation-invariant to first order; the identity assignment sits at the 74th percentile of 5040 permuted galaxy-galaxy errors (median 0.078 against 0.091), so correct galaxy-halo matching does not visibly help.

**Controls.** Passed: kernel limits (C0); Hernquist enclosed mass against the integral of its density; NFW M(<R200) = M_h to 2e-16 over 147 halos and the closed form against quad to 2.5e-14; the Moster round trip, the hand value M*/Mh(1e12) = 0.03428, all M_h inside 10^9-10^15.5; M_h -> 0 gives log10(g_obs/g_bar) exactly; the ladder is strictly monotone; P1 (headline) and P3 (gate weakness). MUTATE=1 (x100 halos) scores z -2.457 and fails {P2, H1b, M1} where the main run fails {P2}, exit 1. **P2 failed as declared and is kept:** the target list rounded CFG79's 3-dp per-galaxy values again to 2 dp (NGC 4472 alt +0.32 against CFG32's +0.31; the unrounded value is 0.31465); a post-hoc row P2' shows the unrounded values match CFG32's printed strings on both footings: a harness artefact, not a physics discrepancy. The first run crashed on a bug of the agent's (a factory-versus-function call in the M_h -> 0 control), fixed with no criterion changed (`cfg84_main_firstrun.out` kept).

**What the agent would attack.** (1) The central-galaxy Moster halos for the group and cluster centrals (NGC 1407, 4472, 4649, 6482) are 0.4-1.5 dex heavier than Humphrey's fits; the pass depends on a cancellation between too-heavy halos and too-low c and reverses when either is fixed, so it is not evidence about the halo model. (2) The gate has almost no discriminating power (about +-2 dex of M_h): a compatibility check, not a test of LCDM or of B. (3) B's shortfall is only marginal (z 1.70) and the 2-sigma line depends on the IMF and radial floors, the Salpeter-versus-Kroupa mapping (R4a moves z from +0.49 to +0.31) and seven galaxies. (4) The decisive next step is unchanged: the deprojected gas density and temperature profiles.

Nothing here says the data favour B or LCDM; kappa = 1/2 is FITTED. Run in place: `python3 cfg84.py` (rc 1 by design, P2), `MUTATE=1 python3 cfg84.py` (rc 1); output identical to the agent's.
