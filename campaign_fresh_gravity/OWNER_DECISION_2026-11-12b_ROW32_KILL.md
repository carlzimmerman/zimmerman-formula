# Owner decision, 2026-11-12b (dated note; no frozen file edited)

**Decision:** FALSIFIER_MATRIX row 32's first-registered mass kill is RETIRED. Row 32 stands with the
re-scoped slope kill only.

The owner was asked, after the external-field decision of the same day
(`OWNER_DECISION_2026-11-12_EXTERNAL_FIELD_READING.md`), "Retire row 32's first-registered mass kill
and keep only the re-scoped slope kill?" and answered: "Yes, retire it".

## What is retired

- The kill "a measured M(<200 kpc) >= 1.0 x 10^12 Msun" (registered with DW1, 2026-11-11).
- The plateau prediction it guarded: M(<150 kpc) = M(<200 kpc) = 6.3 x 10^11 (later 5.26 x 10^11 from
  G239, and 5.07 x 10^11 beyond 71 kpc in hunt item 38).

Reason: under the adopted reading of the external field the framework's own line gives
1.37 x 10^12 (canonical) and 1.50 x 10^12 (alt) inside 200 kpc with census baryons, so the kill would
fire on the framework for being itself. The plateau was a product of the earlier reading.

## Row 32 as it stands

- **Prediction:** over 100 to 200 kpc the Milky Way's enclosed mass rises almost in proportion to
  radius. With census baryons d ln M / d ln r = 0.963 (canonical) / 0.967 (alt), a mean enclosed
  density slope of -2.037 / -2.033 (the record's "-2.03"). With Miller and Bregman hot gas added it is
  1.13 (-1.87). The paper's own NFW (1.19 x 10^12, c = 12) gives 0.618 (-2.38).
- **Kill, unchanged from the G240 re-scope:** a measured slope of -2.25 or steeper over 100 to
  200 kpc, that is d ln M / d ln r <= 0.75.
- **Instrument:** an enclosed-mass profile from 100 to 200 kpc with its radius-to-radius covariance.
  None exists; the DESI DR2 release stops at 80 kpc and publishes no covariance.
- **Status:** ARMED.

## Clarifications recorded with the decision (not changes)

- "gamma" in the re-scoped kill is the slope of the MEAN ENCLOSED density, d ln M / d ln r - 3, as the
  2026-11-12 correction of G238 established; it is not a local density slope. The threshold is the
  same number either way it is written.
- The prediction no longer rests on G240's AQUAL plateau, which the 2026-11-12 correction lane
  withdrew. It is the no-field line, which the adopted field (e_N 0.00016 / 0.00011) leaves unchanged
  inside 300 kpc.
- The slope numbers above are arithmetic on the committed line (DW1's kernel and census, G239's angle
  average), not a graded lane.
- Hot gas makes the predicted slope shallower, so it moves the prediction away from the kill; the kill
  line itself does not depend on it.
