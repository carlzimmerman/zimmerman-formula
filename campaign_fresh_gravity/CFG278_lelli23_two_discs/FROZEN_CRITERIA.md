## 3. g_obs, g_bar, Monte Carlo
- **g_obs:** V_rot²/R at every digitised point of a galaxy (all lines pooled, as the authors do in their fits; the points are not independent: one fit per line, one beam, the same baryons), R = R_arcsec × kpc/″ (the programme's cosmology: 8.45 and 8.69 kpc/″). **No pressure correction (the paper: V/σ ≳ 17–22; the corrections would be ≤ 0.02 dex: knob (b)).** V errors: the digitised bar half-widths, normal. **Inclination:** V × sin i₀/sin i with i ~ N(54.5°, 5.0°) and N(71.6°, 4.1°) clipped to [20°, 85°], one common draw per Monte Carlo sample for all points of a galaxy (the paper transforms V and its errors the same way and states that the inclination is "a nuisance parameter").
- **g_bar = g_stars + g_gas:** stars = a deprojected de Vaucouleurs bulge (R_e,bul = 0.12″ = 1.0 kpc and 0.35″ = 3.0 kpc, Table `tab:photo`) carrying the fraction **f_bul = M_bul/M\* = 0.33 (zC-400569) and 0.63 (zC-488879) of the stellar mass — a dynamics-informed shape derived from the authors' baryon-only fits (M_bul = 0.2 and 0.5 of M_bar = M\* + M_gas: 2.5 × 10¹⁰ / 7.8 × 10¹⁰ and 1.34 × 10¹¹ / 2.11 × 10¹¹) because the photometry cannot give it (their bulge parameters are "very uncertain" and the F160W bulge flux from the tabulated μ_e, R_e is not meaningful); the total stellar mass is independent (SED)** — plus an exponential (Freeman) disc of R_e,disc = 0.76″ (6.4 kpc) and 0.88″ (7.6 kpc) (the zC-488879 lens is folded into the disc: a declared simplification); **gas** = an exponential disc of the same R_e as the stellar disc (the CO profile is not tabulated).
- **Monte Carlo (B = 10,000, seed = crc32("278|" + row label) mod 100000):** V by a normal with its bar half-width, the inclination draw, log M\* normal (0.30 dex), log M_gas normal (0.213 dex); geometry and radii fixed.
- **Estimator:** CFG223's median-residual s\* over the points of a galaxy (`H.s_status`: ROOT / FLOOR (median D ≤ 1) / CEILING (median D > 1, s\* > 1000)), canonical 9.3603e-11, ν_mono. **ILL-CONDITIONED** iff the noiseless lever |d log₁₀ s\*/d(baryon dex)| ≥ 10 or cannot be computed. δ_FLAT, Δ_floor and the baryon shift to s\* = 1 as in CFG270–276.
