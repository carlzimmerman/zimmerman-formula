# CFG278 — FROZEN CRITERIA, ADDENDUM 1 (written and committed BEFORE the script's first execution; `FROZEN_CRITERIA.md` is untouched)

> **κ = ½ FITTED. No verdict words.** Two corrections and one disclosure found while writing the script.

1. **Disclosure (a stage-A blindness slip).** To define the "digitiser-flagged points" knob (criteria §1 and §4 (g)) I read the `notes` column of `lelli2023_rotcur_digitised.csv` together with the galaxy, line and radius columns, and printed it. **The notes carry, for every one of the 25 points, the pixel rows of the error-bar caps ("caps rows 309/427" and so on).** With the calibration printed in the digitiser's README those pixel rows encode V ± error, so the stage-A blindness of this lane is weaker than the criteria §0.2 states: I have seen the cap pixel rows of all 25 points. I did not convert them to velocities (no velocity was computed from them, and I do not intend to), and no velocity value was seen; but the lane is **NOT blind** on the error-bar geometry of the digitised points, and its hand estimates (§7) were frozen before this read from the table means alone.
2. **Knob (g) is redefined:** the flagged points are the **three occluded markers**, identified by the digitiser's notes beginning "PARTLY HIDDEN" or "overlapped" (zC-488879 CO(2-1) #1 and #2; zC-400569 Hα #1), not the five "points to treat with care" of the digitiser's README (the other two are tightest-margin points, not occluded, and the notes alone do not identify them by a rule that I can state without reading the velocities). The script derives the flags from the notes at **stage B only**; **stage A does not load the notes column** (the loader reads galaxy, line and radius only), so the stage-A pre-flight stays as blind as the data allow.
3. **TeX pattern slips fixed before the first execution:** three of my loader patterns (the table fluxes, the SED masses, a bracket) did not match the paper's exact spelling (a `$` before versus after the `^{…}`); they are corrected in the script. The per-line labels of the CSV are `Halpha`, `CO(3-2)`, `CO(4-3)` (zC-400569) and `CO(2-1)`, `CO(3-2)` (zC-488879), counts 6/5/4 and 5/5 as expected.

The script is committed with this addendum and **not yet run**. Order unchanged: stage A → SELFTEST → stage B once → MUTATE=1 → README.
