# CFG278 — the two ALMA CO discs of Lelli et al. 2023: zC-400569 sits at the baryon floor, zC-488879 has a conditioned root, and both are set by the SED stellar masses

**Bottom line.** The multi-line rotation curves of zC-400569 (z = 2.240; 15 points from Hα, CO(3-2), CO(4-3)) and zC-488879 (z = 1.470; 10 points from CO(2-1), CO(3-2)) are nearly flat (mean 254.1 and 336.0 km/s, SD 21.6 and 11.0) and cold (σ_CO ≲ 15 km/s), so they need no pressure correction (the knob moves g_obs by 0.004 and 0.002 dex). With independent baryons (SED stellar mass of Liu+19 with a declared 0.30 dex, CO-based gas with the paper's two bracketing conversion factors) **the two galaxies fall on opposite sides: zC-400569 is at the FLOOR in all three branches (median D = g_obs/g_bar 0.46–0.59), and zC-488879 has a ROOT in all three, s\* ≤ 37.5 / 33.4 / 14.1, and these are the first rows of the lane series that are conditioned (lever −3.0 to −5.4; only 1–2 % of Monte Carlo draws have no root).** The decisive input is the stellar mass, in opposite directions: the SED mass is 2.8× the authors' dynamical value for zC-400569 (the baryons would have to fall by 0.29–0.39 dex for s\* = 1) and 4× below it for zC-488879 (the baryons would have to rise by 0.38–0.69 dex, a factor 2.4–4.9, for s\* = 1: the size of the conflict the paper itself flags, "uncertain up to a factor of 4"). These rows therefore state the baryon-budget conflict of the SED masses, not a measurement of a₀(z). κ = ½ is FITTED. No law statement is made.

**Chart rows (fixed by the frozen rule, `[stars+CO a=0.4]`, the lowest-baryon independent set with gas):** **`zC-400569 [stars+CO a=0.4]` = FLOOR, median D = 0.569, z = 2.240** (79 % of Monte Carlo draws have no root); **`zC-488879 [stars+CO a=0.4]` = root s\* ≤ 33.4 (MC 68 % [14.6, 72.1], 95 % [4.3, 133]; recipe half-width 0.24 dex), z = 1.470, conditioned.** I recommend plotting the zC-488879 point as a baryon-limited upper-bound marker labelled "SED stellar mass 4× below the dynamical value; FLAT needs baryons × 4.5", not as a measurement at s\* ≈ 33. The file `cfg278_points_stageB.csv` carries all six rows.

**What moves zC-488879.** The statistical-plus-declared Monte Carlo (SED 0.30 dex, gas 0.213 dex, V errors, inclination) puts 1.9 / 4.3 / 5.1 as the 95 % lower edges, so FLAT (s\* = 1) is outside them; but the baryon bands span the conflict: all baryons × 10^+0.15 / +0.30 give s\* = 24 / 15 (stars), 21 / 12.7 (α = 0.4), 7.7 / 3.2 (α = 4.3), and the stellar mass would have to be 0.4–0.7 dex higher (the paper's own dynamical value is +0.6 dex) for s\* = 1. Other knobs are small: no bulge +0.12 to +0.22 dex, f_bul × 2 −0.05, gas R_e × 0.6 −0.06 (−0.38 for α = 4.3), the P2 kernel +0.07 to +0.13, the inclination ±1σ +0.05 / −0.04, dropping the innermost point of each line or the three occluded markers −0.06 to −0.11. The authors' own MOND fit (a₀ = 1.2 × 10⁻¹⁰ fixed) lowers their fitted masses by 10–30 % and is "compatible with the z ≈ 0 value", but those masses are free parameters (class D), so it carries no information on s\* here.

## Provenance and design (see `FROZEN_CRITERIA.md`, `FROZEN_CRITERIA_ADDENDUM_1.md`)
- **Data.** The curves are read from the paper's raster figure `Rotcur.jpg` (e-print on disk, `_external_data/arxiv_src/2302.00030`) by a delegated offline digitiser (`data_assembly/lelli2023_rotcur_digitised/`: 25 points on the integer-pixel tick lattice, accuracy 0.13 / 0.15 km/s in V and 0.0004″ in R, byte-identical on re-run; its README lists the points to treat with care). **M5:** the digitised means equal the paper's table values to 0.1 and 0.0 km/s (254.10 and 335.98 against 254 ± 41 and 336 ± 29; the paper's ± is an uncertainty of the mean, not the scatter of the points) and the per-line counts are 4/5/6 and 5/5. No download was needed beyond the e-print already on disk.
- **Baryons.** Stars: the SED masses (21.9 ± 6.5) × 10¹⁰ and (5.2 ± 1.2) × 10¹⁰ M☉ with a declared σ = 0.30 dex; shape from the HST fits (de Vaucouleurs bulge R_e = 1.0 and 3.0 kpc, exponential disc R_e = 6.4 and 7.6 kpc) with the bulge fraction f_bul = 0.33 and 0.63 taken from the authors' baryon-only fit (the photometry cannot give it: the bulge parameters are "very uncertain"). Gas: M_gas = α_CO × L′_CO(1-0), L′ from CO(3-2) (R₃₁ = 0.60) and CO(2-1) (R₂₁ = 0.85) fluxes, α_CO = 0.4 and 4.3 (the paper's bracket): 9.4 × 10⁹ / 1.0 × 10¹¹ (zC-400569) and 7.8 × 10⁹ / 8.4 × 10¹⁰ (zC-488879); the authors' dynamical α_CO (2.1, 2.9) are fit outputs and are not branches (they give 4.9 × 10¹⁰ and 5.7 × 10¹⁰ M☉).
- **M6 validates my geometry against the authors' own baryon-only fits:** with their fitted masses (zC-400569: M\* = 7.8 × 10¹⁰, M_gas = 4.9 × 10¹⁰, M_bul = 2.5 × 10¹⁰; zC-488879: M\* = 21.1 × 10¹⁰, M_gas = 5.7 × 10¹⁰, M_bul = 1.3 × 10¹¹) my geometry reproduces the digitised V_rot with median |ratio − 1| = 0.099 (median ratio 0.90) and 0.057 (0.94).
- **Process (everything kept).** Criteria 9a45bbdae (with the digitiser's files); addendum 1 + script 4d8e526d5 (before the first run); stage A b617f991b (5/5 controls; zC-400569 rows ILL-CONDITIONED, zC-488879 rows conditioned); SELFTEST 1f1aada8a (the three conditioned rooted rows return s = 2 inside their 95 % interval, 3 of 3, and 99–100 of 99–100 in the 100-world repeat; the 95 % intervals of even the conditioned rows are 1.5–2 dex wide); measurement, MUTATE=1 and this README: see the commit that adds this file.

## What this lane cannot say
It does not measure a₀ at z = 1.47 or 2.24. The SED stellar masses disagree with the authors' dynamical masses by 2.8× (zC-400569, SED higher) and 4× (zC-488879, SED lower), which is the whole difference between a floor and a root here; the CO conversion factor spans ×11 (its effect on the zC-488879 root is only a factor 2.4: 33 → 14); the bulge fraction and sizes are not measured; the atomic gas is neglected (the paper: it "may dominate the total gas budget at high z"), which would add baryons and lower both roots; the points are correlated (N_eff ≈ 6 and 4) and partly beam-smeared. A FLOOR with the lowest independent set is robust against any higher set; zC-488879's roots are upper bounds only in the stars-only row. Class S carries no law statement.
