**Reading.**
- CFG748's collisionless cold infall fills onto the law out to 20–50 r_M. That fits KiDS-1000 lensing exactly as well as
  the law with no edge, so CFG801's failure is repaired with dynamics already in the record, with no new term.
- **The cost is supply.** A galaxy must draw cold energy from about **3–4 times its own baryons** to fit the inner
  0.31 Mpc, and about 16–24 times to fit all radii. That means applying the 5.364 ratio to its whole surroundings, not
  just its stars and gas.
- **Plausibility** (stated, not graded): galaxies hold only a fraction of the baryons around them. A factor of 3–4 is
  ordinary. A factor of 16–24 for the outer radii is large, but the outer bins are where two-halo contamination (from
  neighbours) is largest.
- C itself presumes about 200 M_b (N ≈ 37) within 200 r_M. It is generous, not derived.

**Disclosed.**
- In the primary range a catchment edge at N = 4–6 (21–33 r_M) fits better than no edge (Δχ² −8 to −9). N was scanned,
  so this is an observation, not a result.
- Fill is held at 0.948 beyond 50 r_M; this is declared and affects only the secondary range.
- A few line-of-sight integrals raised scipy accuracy warnings at large x.
- Bin masses are provisional.

**Controls.** K1 (B = 83.9) and K2 (D(1) = 280.9, the CFG801 edge) PASS. MUTATE (fill cut at 5.84 r_M) reproduces the edge
failure (Δχ² +197), so the test bites.

**Open problem, now sharp.** Derive the catchment supply, i.e. how much surrounding baryon mass feeds each galaxy's cold
energy, from the framework itself (turnaround catchment, CFG654/656 machinery). If it gives N ≳ 3–4 at galaxy masses,
the outer-halo problem is closed without knobs.
