# Where the program stands — 2026-09-28

The theory work has gone as far as calculation can take it. The questions still open are decided by data, and the data have dates. This page is the bottom line; each claim points to a committed script. Nothing here says the theory is closed.

**The failures, ranked by significance and sorted by what they hit, are in [`FAILURES_EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY_2026-09-28.md`](../FAILURES_EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY_2026-09-28.md).**

## The answer in one paragraph

The framework ties the galaxy acceleration scale to the cosmological constant: a₀ = κ c √(G ρ_Λ), with κ = ½. The form (G, c, ρ) is forced; κ is fitted and has not been derived. With that a₀, a MOND-type law fits galaxy rotation curves well. The framework still needs dark matter's *mass*: a cold fluid with the same cosmic amount as CDM, which it treats as the law's dark density inside bound systems. No data set yet prefers it over ΛCDM, and no data set yet excludes its best version. Two measurements decide what calculation cannot: Gaia DR4 wide binaries (2 December 2026) and a₀ at redshift ≈ 2.5.

## What was tested and failed

- **Relativistic completions.** The single-metric, AeST-type, khronometric and tidal-khronon routes were excluded, by Cassini, GW170817 or instability. The one surviving covariant action, V0, is not yet complete: its region gate is obstructed when varied.
- **Dark-matter-free readings.** Pure MI and every carrier without a cold mass were excluded. The mass is required.
- **Inside candidate B:**
  - The Milky Way's ultra-faint satellites fail at **3.5–3.8σ** once refereed (CFG28, CFG29). They were first quoted at 7–8σ (CFG18). Tides, noise and unresolved binaries do not explain it.
  - **The derived rule trades the ultra-faint failure for a smaller one (CFG42).**
    - With leftover collapse debris the ultra-faints move from +0.325 dex (3.8σ) to −0.06 dex (−0.4σ).
    - It over-predicts the classical satellites by 0.11–0.12 dex (M31 LVD −2.7σ, MW classical −1.8 to −1.9σ), the cusp problem.
    - The debris must switch off where the phantom already supplies the mass. That is a design constraint on T5's max rule (dark = the larger of phantom and cold budget, not their sum), not a fix.
  - **Binary galaxies do not discriminate (CFG30).**
    - With circular orbits, the pairs move 1.4–1.5× faster than B predicts, even with generous baryons.
    - Under B's own timing orbits (no halo friction), the mean speed is right (A = 1.05–1.12). But the fall with separation (4.2σ) and the rise with mass (5.3σ) are not, and the same timing makes MW–M31 1.6–1.75× too fast.
    - ΛCDM shows the mirror image: it fits the mean with circular orbits and over-predicts with timing orbits.
    - Which orbits apply turns on whether B's dark component exerts dynamical friction. B has not yet specified that.
  - **The Coma ultra-diffuse galaxies pass under B's own rule (CFG31).**
    - They sit at 1.3 / 1.1σ (2.3σ with stars alone), not the recorded 4.9σ. That number came from the external-field reading, which B drops.
    - They agree with the M31 satellites under the same rule.
    - The pass is only as strong as the ownership rule, which Gaia DR4 tests.
  - **The X-ray ellipticals are a large shortfall, not an established failure (CFG32).**
    - B's law falls short by a factor of about 1.9, at 1.7 / 1.6σ. Seven galaxies and the IMF limit the significance.
    - The shortfall grows outward, and three galaxies carry it.
    - The deprojected gas profiles decide it.
  - **The SLACS lenses pass a lensing-vs-dynamics test only within its declared floor (CFG33).**
    - Under B's law the lenses need 1.2–1.3× Salpeter stellar mass. B's own ATLAS3D dynamics give 0.85× at the same dispersion.
    - That gap is 1.5–1.7σ with a 0.10-dex cross-survey floor and 7–8σ on statistics alone.
  - **The derived cold-mass rule (CFG35–CFG38).**
    - B's cold fluid is conserved, so it keeps its collapse mass.
    - With measured colour-split collapse masses (Mandelbaum+2016 weak lensing) the rule leaves every star-forming spiral and every passive disk below log M_* 11.2 on the law.
    - It fits the SLUGGS massive early types (0.4σ, against the law's 3.3σ deficit) and half-closes the X-ray ellipticals.
    - Four more massive S0/S0a with HI curves (Di Teodoro+2023; CFG41) are consistent with both the law and the rule, after a selection-bias correction (rule −0.105 ± 0.131, 0.8σ). The test does not decide between them.
    - Open: one massive S0 (UGC 2487, +0.14 dex); groups and clusters cannot be scored non-circularly.
  - **The most massive star-forming disks are faster than the law, marginally (CFG40).**
    - Ogle+2019's 23 super spirals (log M_* 11.1–11.7), scored with the law at each speed's own radius, sit +0.11 dex fast (1.4σ), with the nine fastest at +0.17 dex (2.06σ) and a mass trend at 1.95σ.
    - The declared test fails at the margin. The derived cold-mass rule cannot cure it: the measured blue collapse masses are consumed by the law's phantom.
    - The 0.2-dex stellar-mass systematic sets the floor. Di Teodoro+2023's HI curves (CFG41), corrected for their speed-selection bias, sit −0.03 ± 0.07 dex from the law: consistent with this result at 1.3σ, not a contradiction.
  - **The a₀ ladder under B (CFG34).**
    - The cluster rung's 6.3σ belongs to the phantom-only reading. Under B the clusters close.
    - X-ray groups are short: 1.4× at R500 (1.5–1.8σ, with a generous hydrostatic allowance) and 1.9× inside R2500 (2.6σ).
    - The shortfall tracks the baryons the groups have lost (ρ = −0.96). B's cosmic share should probably follow the baryons a system collapsed with, not today's. That is a T5 design constraint, not yet derived.
  - Chae's external-field signal costs 1.7–2.2σ on the canonical footing and 2.7–3.0σ on the alt (CFG8); the declared H1 failed in 3 of 4 cells.
  - A universal *sharp* phantom edge cannot serve KiDS and the Local Group at once (CFG21), but that is **not** framework-specific (next section).

## What was closed in this round (CFG23–CFG27)

- **KiDS against the Local Group (CFG23).** Standard ΛCDM halos run through the same machinery hit the same Local Group wall, harder: T = 24–72 against the framework's 26–28. The Local Group's spherical zero-velocity radius does not discriminate.
- **KiDS alone (CFG23).** A 30%-lower-concentration NFW fits KiDS at χ² 99.3, against the framework's 104.7. KiDS does not prefer either model.
- **The cold budget against KiDS (CFG24, CFG27).**
  - Owning the phantom at the turned-around level, as the framework's own switch says, cuts the cost to 29.4 / 32.9 (canonical).
  - The same machinery moves ordinary halos by at least 50.2.
  - **Not decidable with this machinery; not an exclusion.** The alt footing sits at the margin (45–49).
- **The inflow-derived edge (CFG25).** FG016's KiDS kill, the computed answer to "where does the cold fluid go?", is not framework-specific. The same spherical-infall engine misses KiDS for ordinary halos too.
- **An erratum (CFG24).** The budget's galaxy sample contained the Milky Way's own group with a bogus 4.4 × 10¹² M☉. Fixed; every budget edge moves up by about 0.006.

   - **The pipeline is built and validated end to end on DR3.** The runbook is `prep_2026/gaia_dr4_prep/catalog_builder/README.md`. Before running, check the DR4 column names, the non-single-star tables and the dust cut.
2. **a₀ at redshift ≈ 2.5.**
   - Branch A predicts no shift in the galaxy zero point; ΛCDM expects about +0.33 dex. At 0.13 dex per object the flat law is 2.57σ from ΛCDM-native (1.5–2.4σ if an evolving dark-energy branch holds), so this is the one test that can go decisively either way only for branch A, and it needs several well-measured discs.
   - MUSE-DARK III's rising a₀ to z ≈ 1.4 is not diagnostic: ΛCDM simulations produce the same apparent rise (`real_research/A0Z_MUSE_DARK_III_CONFRONTATION.md`).
   - Two to four well-measured discs at z ≈ 2.5 settle branch A against ΛCDM; the registered test cannot run on the data on disk (0 of 2 deep-MOND rotators; CFG1 O4).
3. **The dark sector's edge (optional theory work).** It needs an N-body-grade model of an isolated galaxy's surroundings for both models. That is a bigger project, and it does not decide the theory.

## Never claim

- That dark matter is gone. The mass is required.
- That κ = ½ is derived.
- That any data set favours the framework over ΛCDM.
- That the theory is closed or complete.
- Any Standard Model or theory-of-everything result. That sector is walled, and those claims were publicly retracted.
- That the Local Group or the budget tension excludes the framework (CFG23, CFG27).

**One dependency to keep in view.** The a₀-from-Λ link assumes Λ is a real cosmological constant (a de Sitter horizon). If dark energy turned out otherwise, the value would survive only as a postulate.
