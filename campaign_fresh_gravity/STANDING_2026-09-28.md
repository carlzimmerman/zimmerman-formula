# Where the program stands — 2026-09-28

The theory work has gone as far as calculation can take it. The questions still open are decided by data, and the data have dates. This page is the bottom line; each claim points to a committed script. Nothing here says the theory is closed.

## The answer in one paragraph

The framework ties the galaxy acceleration scale to the cosmological constant: a₀ = κ c √(G ρ_Λ), with κ = ½. The form (G, c, ρ) is forced; κ is fitted and has not been derived. With that a₀, a MOND-type law fits galaxy rotation curves well. The framework still needs dark matter's *mass*: a cold fluid with the same cosmic amount as CDM, which it treats as the law's dark density inside bound systems. No data set yet prefers it over ΛCDM, and no data set yet excludes its best version. Two measurements decide what calculation cannot: Gaia DR4 wide binaries (2 December 2026) and a₀ at redshift ≈ 2.5.

## What was tested and failed

- **Relativistic completions.** The single-metric, AeST-type, khronometric and tidal-khronon routes were excluded, by Cassini, GW170817 or instability. The one surviving covariant action, V0, is not yet complete: its region gate is obstructed when varied.
- **Dark-matter-free readings.** Pure MI and every carrier without a cold mass were excluded. The mass is required.
- **Inside candidate B:**
  - The Milky Way's ultra-faint satellites fail at 7–8σ (CFG18).
  - Chae's external-field signal costs 1.7–2.2σ (CFG8).
  - A universal *sharp* phantom edge cannot serve KiDS and the Local Group at once (CFG21), but that is **not** framework-specific (next section).

## What was closed in this round (CFG23–CFG27)

- **KiDS against the Local Group (CFG23).** Standard ΛCDM halos run through the same machinery hit the same Local Group wall, harder: T = 24–72 against the framework's 26–28. The Local Group's spherical zero-velocity radius does not discriminate.
- **KiDS alone (CFG23).** A 30%-lower-concentration NFW fits KiDS at χ² 99.3, against the framework's 104.7. KiDS does not prefer either model.
- **The cold budget against KiDS (CFG24, CFG27).**
  - Owning the phantom at the turned-around level, as the framework's own switch says, cuts the cost to 29.4 / 32.9 (canonical).
  - The same machinery moves ordinary halos by at least 50.2.
  - **Not decidable with this machinery; not an exclusion.** The alt footing sits at the margin (45–49).
- **The inflow-derived edge (CFG25).** FG016's KiDS kill, the computed answer to "where does the cold fluid go?", is not framework-specific. The same spherical-infall engine misses KiDS for ordinary halos too.
- **An erratum (CFG24).** The budget's galaxy sample contained the Milky Way's own group with a bogus 4.4 × 10¹² M☉. Fixed; every budget edge moves up by about 0.006.

## What decides the rest

1. **Gaia DR4 wide binaries, released 2 December 2026.**
   - **The pre-registration** is frozen, with Amendment 14 filed (commit ef0556b4e).
   - **The chain's law gives a ceiling** on the frozen statistic: consistent at γ̂ ≤ 1.0725 (canonical) or 1.0900 (alt); **falsified at γ̂ ≥ 1.157 / 1.174**, if the frozen stability checks pass. It can kill that version; it cannot confirm it.
   - **The pipeline is built and validated end to end on DR3.** The runbook is `prep_2026/gaia_dr4_prep/catalog_builder/README.md`. Before running, check the DR4 column names, the non-single-star tables and the dust cut.
2. **a₀ at redshift ≈ 2.5.**
   - The framework predicts no shift in the galaxy zero point; ΛCDM expects about +0.33 dex. This is the one test that can go decisively either way.
   - MUSE-DARK III's rising a₀ to z ≈ 1.4 is not diagnostic: ΛCDM simulations produce the same apparent rise (`real_research/A0Z_MUSE_DARK_III_CONFRONTATION.md`).
   - Two to four well-measured discs at z ≈ 2.5 settle it.
3. **The dark sector's edge (optional theory work).** It needs an N-body-grade model of an isolated galaxy's surroundings for both models. That is a bigger project, and it does not decide the theory.

## Never claim

- That dark matter is gone. The mass is required.
- That κ = ½ is derived.
- That any data set favours the framework over ΛCDM.
- That the theory is closed or complete.
- Any Standard Model or theory-of-everything result. That sector is walled, and those claims were publicly retracted.
- That the Local Group or the budget tension excludes the framework (CFG23, CFG27).

**One dependency to keep in view.** The a₀-from-Λ link assumes Λ is a real cosmological constant (a de Sitter horizon). If dark energy turned out otherwise, the value would survive only as a postulate.
