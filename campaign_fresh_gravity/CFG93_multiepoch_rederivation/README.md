The agent read the four README files named, the CFG51 `reduce_walker.py` docstring, the Walker+2023 ReadMe and data (49,367 rows, 38 systems); it opened no CFG51/CFG66 script, .out or JSON until its frozen main and MUTATE runs were done, and afterwards read only the sigma_law lines of CFG51, the |g| and bootstrap code of CFG66 and the CFG51 results JSON to explain differences. **Differences and causes.** The frozen main run failed 7 of 37 pass lines, all at the third decimal or in a definition, except C3b (below). The Bootes I offsets are 0.0009 dex high (2.47 against 2.46 sigma): CFG51 uses the LVD `rhalf_sph_physical` (circularised) radius and computes its floor as half the offset range over Upsilon_V = 1, 2, 4 and the deep-MOND estimator, not a fixed 0.076; with both conventions the agent gets +0.21939 +/- 0.08913 (2.4613 sigma) for Bootes I and 3.6255 sigma for Tucana II (CFG51's unrounded 0.21939 / 0.08913 / 2.4613 and 3.6251); CFG51's README states neither convention. The |g| error line failed as frozen (the mean of the component errors, 24; CFG66's 15.9 is the error along the gradient direction, post hoc 15.87). Two bugs in the agent's code were fixed after the first run and disclosed (a deep-MOND control with a0 = 1e30 overflowing; a single-Gaussian start added to the mixture fit, which changed nothing: the 'negative T*' draws were rounding-level and P stayed 0.132; pre-fix run kept as `FIRSTRUN_*`).

**Simulations (real epoch cadence, resampled from the actual members).** No binaries (12 cells, N = 30-100, sigma = 2 and 4): the cleaned estimator is slightly low (median sigma-hat/sigma 0.96 at N = 30, 0.98 at N = 50, 0.99-1.00 at N = 100), the 1 sigma interval covers 0.65-0.69 (C2 passes). Binaries at fraction 0.5 (Duquennoy-Mayor periods, giant-primary periastron cut): the flagging catches only 9% of injected binaries at the Bootes I cadence and 13% at Tucana II's; the recovered binary fraction is 0.48 / 0.49 (C3a passes); **the cleaned sigma is biased high by +3-5% at sigma = 4 and by +13-18% at sigma = 2, with coverage 0.30 at N = 100, sigma = 2 (C3b fails, as declared)**; at f = 0.7, sigma = 2, N = 100 the bias is +27% with coverage 0.16. Dropping the periastron cut raises the bias to +12% (Bootes I, sigma = 4, N = 50); a short-period-heavy or flat-in-log-period population gives +7%.

**Sensitivity of the cleaned offset (canonical).** Membership cuts barely matter (velocity window 3-5 sigma, logg cut 3.5 / 4.5 / none, proper-motion 2-4 sigma, no parallax cut: Bootes I +0.217 to +0.228, Tucana II +0.449 to +0.470); cleaning threshold p < 0.05 or 0.001 changes nothing for Tucana II. Cleaning with only the first 2 epochs is powerless for Tucana II (sigma 6.74, +0.685); the first 3 give +0.474. A per-epoch error floor of 0.5 or 1 km/s: no flip; 2 km/s drops Bootes I to 2.04 sigma (1.82 alt) and Tucana II stays 2.9 sigma. Error scaling x0.8 to x1.5: no flip. Star bootstrap with cleaning re-run: Bootes I offset +0.16 to +0.26, Tucana II +0.30 to +0.52. Epoch-error calibration (all matched systems): the bulk is calibrated, but the tail is heavy (7% of two-epoch and 12-15% of >= 3-epoch members have p < 0.01); binaries and underestimated errors cannot be told apart.

**What the agent would attack.** (1) **Tucana II's 3.6 sigma rests on untestable stars:** of the 12 cleaned stars only 7 are testable multi-epoch stars, and these alone give sigma = 2.31 (+0.80 -0.52), offset +0.22 +/- 0.14 (1.55 sigma); the other 5 are single-epoch, two of them (-115.2 and -118.6 km/s, e about 1.2-1.3) lie 8-12 km/s from the multi-epoch mean and come from the same 2022 run, and the pooled fit treats them as members; leave-one-out sigma 3.06-4.30; the cleaning ladder is monotone (sigma 7.48 -> 5.30 -> 4.06 (p < 0.01) -> 3.45 with the paper's flag, keeping 8 of 14 stars, offset +0.394, 2.46 sigma), the signature of residual binary contamination. (2) **Bootes I cleaning is almost powerless:** only 2 of 33 multi-epoch stars are flagged, 22 of 55 members are single-epoch and only 18 have >= 3 epochs; the offset is essentially the all-epoch-mean sigma of about 3.9 km/s (>= 2 epochs 4.13, 2.55 sigma; >= 3 epochs 3.81, 1.97 sigma); a post-hoc exceedance test with sigma_true = the bare-law 2.36 km/s and exact cadence gives P(sigma_clean >= 3.91) = 0.033 at f = 0.5 (giant-cut population), 0.09 at f = 0.9, and 0.08-0.45 for the no-cut, short-period-heavy and flat populations; for Tucana II 0.014 (f = 0.5) to 0.11 (f = 0.9, no cut). So the Bootes I excess is compatible with law plus binaries under weaker assumptions; Tucana II is harder to explain that way but the stricter test above weakens it. (3) The floor dominates the error (Bootes I statistical 0.046 dex, Upsilon_V floor 0.076; a 2 km/s epoch floor flips Bootes I). (4) The hot-component question is unresolved (mixture not detected, bootstrap P about 0.16; total sigma 3.94 is a mixture-agnostic restatement; cold alone -0.07 +/- 0.20). (5) Two objects, not a population; membership is re-derived, not the paper's; no epoch or instrument zero-point offsets are modelled; the Tucana II gradient is unconstrained at 12 stars (about +-3.4 km/s across one half-light radius).
