2. **Why it does not move.** The KM median (+0.3245 at DV0) is rank 16 of 40, inside a dense stretch (+0.313, +0.315, +0.322, +0.325, +0.329, +0.330). Five of the eight corrected systems (Hyd I, Boo I, Leo IV, Car II, Leo V) already lie below it and only get lower; Ret II (+0.475) is the one that crosses downward under DV2 and DV3, and in DV1 Boo III (−0.124 → +0.370) crosses upward: each crossing moves the median one rank, about 0.003 to 0.005 dex. Under DV4 the two crossings cancel. A planted c on the eight alone (post-run, MUTATE=2's curve) confirms how weakly the median responds: c = −0.10 gives 0.0000, −0.20 gives −0.0029, **−0.33 gives −0.0081, −0.50 gives −0.0710** (a step: three systems cross and the median drops to a sparser stretch). Arroyo's corrections are −0.07 / −0.10 dex on the eight, a fifth to a third of the −0.33 dex that would have to be removed from every one of the 40 (MUTATE=1: with a uniform −0.33 the offset is −0.006 / −0.026 dex at DV0 and −0.001 to −0.029 across the variants, |z| < 0.4).

## Limits

The statistics are order statistics of 40 numbers: they are insensitive by construction to systems far from the median, and this lane's answer ("B's offset does not move") is a statement about the KM median and its bootstrap error, not about each system (a mean-based statistic would respond more: by hand arithmetic the eight corrections have a mean of −0.081 (free f) / −0.117 (f = 0.7) dex, which spread over 40 systems is −0.016 / −0.023 dex on a mean; not computed). The correction is Arroyo-Polonio's median ratio; their asymmetric posteriors and DEIMOS's errors are not propagated; the Moe & Di Stefano population model (primary 0.8 M☉, single-epoch data) is applied to LVD values that are not its input datasets, and DV4 applies a model correction on top of DEIMOS values from which variables were already flagged and removed (a double count to some degree). Leo IV is among the systems Arroyo-Polonio finds unresolved after correction (the ratio is applied regardless); Ret II's footnote (its f = 0 value 4.53 against 3.2 in its literature input) is in an appendix I have not read; Eri III (a 90 % upper limit) is not among the 40. P2 (14) and P3 (34) are unchanged. T2, T3 and T4 of CFG28 are not re-scored (they need per-system errors, which the ratio does not define). A re-measurement from per-star velocities (Geha Papers I and II, the Boo I S5 data) would need the owner's go for the download in the calculation chat.
