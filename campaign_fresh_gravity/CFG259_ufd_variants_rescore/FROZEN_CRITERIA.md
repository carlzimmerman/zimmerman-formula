## 7. What this does NOT cover

The corrected values' own uncertainties (Arroyo's asymmetric posteriors, DEIMOS's errors) are not propagated; the correction is a median ratio. The Arroyo model (Moe & Di Stefano population, primary 0.8 M☉, single-epoch data) is applied to LVD values that are not its input datasets; DV4 applies a model correction on top of DEIMOS values from which variables were already flagged and removed, which double-counts to some degree. The systems are not re-measured; no per-star velocity is used. The statistics are order statistics of 40 numbers; they are insensitive by construction to systems far from the median, and this is a statement about those statistics, not about the systems. Reading (b)'s collapse-mass and concentration rules, the P2 / P3 populations and the kernel are the committed ones. A re-measurement from per-star velocities (Geha Papers I and II, the Boo I S5 data) would need the owner's go for the download in the calculation chat.
