## 2. Classification scheme (frozen)

Symbols: a₀, c, G, ρ (ρ_DE, or ρ_crit on the second footing), ħ, k_B, π tracked as a positive symbol, the law's κ, and each
record constant (α, L*, y_t, Z/β², D, λ, K_B, ...) as a free symbol. Substitutions:
- the law: a₀ = κ c √(Gρ);
- the footing's own Friedmann relation: H² = (8π/3)Gρ (H_Λ on ρ_DE; H₀ on ρ_crit, where ρ_crit = 3H₀²/8πG exactly);
- Λ = 8πGρ/c², R_H = c/H.

So no Ω_Λ enters. A relation that needs H₀ together with ρ_DE is flagged "uses Ω_Λ".

Each row has a provenance flag taken from its source: **held** (the record holds or derived it), **posit** (an
identification the record tested as a choice and closed), **target** (κ = ½, the 32π or a §2 forbidden premise is written in:
Gρ_Λ = 4a₀², Λ = 32πa₀²/c⁴, J = Gρr²/c² = 1, premise A), **rejected** (the record rejected it on stability or data), **pending**
(registered, unrun).

Classes, assigned by the script in this order:
1. **NA:** no a₀, or no c and no carrier. Listed to show coverage, not counted.
2. **INEQ:** an inequality or bound.
3. **DEF:** the law or an algebraic rewrite of it.
4. **ID:** an equality satisfied for every κ after the substitutions (sympy simplify gives 0). This includes zero modes, a₀(z)
   shape laws, c as a propagation speed or a unit, and ratios that are fixed functions of q. **WI** (written in) is ID or
   determinate with provenance target.
5. Otherwise solve for κ² (positive root):
   - **HBAR:** ħ survives (ħ-weight ≠ 0).
   - **TRADE:** κ² depends on any free record constant.
   - **MASS:** κ² depends on a mass or radius.
   - Otherwise determinate, κ² = r·π^k:
     - **H-ODD** or **H-EVEN** if the relation's carrier is H or a horizon radius;
     - **RHO-PI** if the carrier is ρ or Λ and k ≠ 0;
     - **PF** if k = 0, split into **PF-RAT** (κ² rational) and **PF-ALG** (κ² irrational algebraic);
     - **CONT** if κ² contains a transcendental kernel constant.
6. Rows with no symbolic form (closures with no number, ill-defined energies, pending ħ audits, menus) carry a declared class
   (ILL, PEND, MENU, ID) with the source's reason quoted.

**Natural values.** For an ID ratio of H-type (CFG775's table, the a₀(z) branches) the script also reports the κ that the posit
"ratio = 1" would give. These posits are not in the record, so they are report-only and never counted.

**Counts (frozen definitions):**
- **N_c:** rows where c meets a₀ (everything except NA).
- **N_pfdet:** determinate PF rows (PF-RAT or PF-ALG) whose provenance is held, posit or rejected (not target, not pending).
- **N_half:** N_pfdet rows with κ² = 1/4 exactly.
- **N_live** = N_half: a π-free, ħ-free, determinate, non-identity equality giving exactly ½ without the target written in.
  Each such row would become its own frozen door, not a result of this lane.
- **N_wi_half:** PF rows with κ² = 1/4 whose provenance is target (reported; the history of written-in halves).
