# The canonical statement of the theory: v3 (2026-10-18). Supersedes v2 (2026-10-16) only where stated

**What this is.** A dated revision. The 2026-10-14 and 2026-10-16 files are untouched; their sealed predictions stand with
their release dates and hashes. This revision records what the 2026-10-17/18 lanes (CFG820–829, CFG838–839) changed in the
dwarf part of the open list. Nothing here adds a fitted constant.

## What changed
**The dwarf failure is re-located: it is a property of faint pressure-supported dwarfs, not a failure of the law's g(g_bar)
relation at low acceleration. Whether isolated dwarfs carry less of it than satellites is unresolved (CFG860, CFG873).**
*(Wording corrected 2026-10-19 by CFG873; v3 as first written said "it is a satellite property". Isolated field dwarfs carry
part of the excess, so "satellite" was not supported.)*
- **Isolated galaxies obey the law where the dwarfs do not.** KiDS-1000 lensing of isolated late-type (blue) galaxies at the
  dwarfs' own baryonic accelerations (10^−13.3 ≤ g_bar < 10^−11.9 m/s²) gives an amplitude over the law of
  A = 1.016 ± 0.086. That excludes the satellite dwarfs' ×2.75 at 20σ (CFG829). The law, with κ fitted on discs, holds for
  isolated late types down to g_bar ≈ 7 × 10⁻¹⁴ m/s² with no extra parameter.
- **Where dwarfs and discs overlap they agree.** At g_bar ≥ 10⁻¹² m/s² pressure-supported dwarfs sit with SPARC discs
  (difference +0.05 ± 0.07 dex, CFG828). The excess appears only below SPARC's floor (21 dwarfs, mean +0.44 dex).
- **With correct stellar masses the dwarf excess is uniform and age-independent.** SFH-based M/L (E-MILES × Weisz+14) gives
  25 of 28 independent dwarfs above the law, mean ln(σ_obs/σ_law) = +0.38, with no age trend (CFG827). The apparent age trend
  (CFG824/826) was stellar M/L; "fossil binding" as an age effect is not supported.
- **What the excess is not:** stiffening gravity (globulars need L* ≥ 24 pc, dSphs ≤ 22 pc: CFG820/821); free cold energy of
  expelled gas (lands too far out: CFG822); a placement problem alone (the whole catchment supply barely suffices at Υ = 2,
  fails at Υ = 1: CFG823); present-day tides (ρ = 0.00, CFG838); pericentric tides in the framework's own MW field
  (ρ = +0.08, CFG839).
- **Still open for satellites:** kinematic systematics in faint systems (binaries handled for UFDs only), non-equilibrium
  beyond the tidal estimates, or real bound cold mass in satellites (which would need a satellite-specific supply rule).

**Clarification (2026-10-18, same night, owner chat): scope of "satellite property".** KiDS lensing probes 30–300 kpc around
isolated galaxies; the dwarf dispersions probe r_1/2 ≈ 0.1–1 kpc. CFG829 therefore shows that the law's g(g_bar) relation holds
for isolated systems at these g_bar, i.e. the dwarf excess is not a failure of the law's deep regime as a function of g_bar.
It does NOT exclude extra bound mass concentrated inside small systems: the only systems reaching g_bar < 10⁻¹² m/s² at
sub-kpc radii are the satellite / pressure-supported dwarfs themselves (the two gas-free field dSphs, Cetus +0.13 and Tucana
+0.17 dex, are the only same-scale isolated comparison, N = 2). "Satellite property" should be read as "property of the
faint dwarfs (satellite vs. small-scale origin not separated)".

**Robustness amendment (2026-10-18, CFG840):** FRAGILE by its frozen rule — with the window shifted 0.3 dex lower or B21 lens
masses lowered 0.2 dex, blue lenses sit 1.23–1.31× above the law (> 2σ). Read "on the law" as "within ~0.1 dex of it"
(A 0.90–1.31 across variants). The exclusion of the dwarfs' ×2.75 holds in every variant (≥ 12σ).

**Addendum (2026-10-19 night, CFG876–877): the component is a finite clump, not an atmosphere.** CFG870's extended form
(mass ∝ r, read as a universal cold-energy temperature floor) is BROKEN by 18 large out-of-sample dwarfs (CFG876: Crater II,
Antlia II, Cassiopeia III). A finite clump fixed by earlier lanes, σ = 7.53 km/s and M_cl = 81σ⁴/(4Ga0) = 5.3e6 M_⊙ (capping
at ~240 pc), fits MW classical, M31 + field, UFDs, both out-of-sample groups and SPARC within ~2σ in group means on both footings (CFG877,
post-hoc model choice; PARTIAL only through M31 + field at 2.2σ; Crater II fails individually, as the law alone does).
Working description: one bound, self-gravitating cold-energy clump per faint dwarf. Underived: σ ≈ 7.5 km/s (≡ 5e6 M_⊙). Individually, at central values, Draco and Sextans need 2.4× and 3.7× the clump mass (Codex audit, 2026-10-20); one-clump universality is under test (CFG880).
For the cold-energy equations: CFG638's bath σ_J² is not a universal floor.

**Addendum (2026-10-19 night, CFG878): the fragment mass.** Under the full law a self-gravitating isothermal cold clump has
M = (0.75–0.95)·81σ⁴/(4Ga0), so the clump mass follows from its σ alone. The record's own bath (the settled cold energy's Jeans
dispersion) makes fragments ≈ 3–4 × the host's baryons, a one-to-one scaling EXCLUDED by CFG872 (4.9σ). The cold-energy
equations therefore need a universal velocity scale from outside the cold sector. Candidate (post-hoc, not in the record):
the thermal speed of neutral primordial gas at the hydrogen-cooling floor (8000–10⁴ K → 7.4–8.2 km/s → 3.6–7.1e6 M_⊙), set
once in the first systems that can cool. Not derived until that mechanism is written into the equations and tested.
