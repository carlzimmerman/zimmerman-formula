## 3. g_obs, g_bar, D (CFG227's class-S rule, CFG229's functions)
- r = 2 r_e, r_e = `re_arcsec` × (kpc per arcsec at the galaxy's z, flat ΛCDM, H₀ = 67.4, Ω_m = 0.315); **g_obs = V_circ(2 r_e)² / r** with V_circ as published (pressure-corrected once).
- **g_bar:** stars M\* = 10^logMstar and gas M_gas = 10^logMgas (as published, α_CO = 0.92) in **one thin exponential disc with R_e = r_e and R_d = R_e/1.678**; g_bar = the Freeman disc acceleration at r (`CFG229_class_m_gold/cfg229_score.py`'s `disc_v2`/`gdisc`, reproduced to 1e-6 against CFG227's committed S1 g_bar as control C3). D = g_obs/g_bar.

## 4. The estimator and the statistics (the programme's)
- **s\*** = the root of median_i log₁₀[D_i / ν_mono(g_bar,i / (a₀ s))] = 0 (CFG223's estimator, `CFG229_class_m_gold/a0implied.py` imported read-only), canonical a₀ = 9.3603e-11; **a root exists only if the row's median D exceeds 1**; otherwise **NO ROOT** (the chart's floor convention s = 0.001). Absolute a₀ is footing-independent (control M1).
- **Per-galaxy interval (CFG229's Monte Carlo):** B = 10,000 draws (seed = crc32(row label) mod 100000); V_circ by a split-normal with its published errors; log M\* and log M_gas by normals with their published errors (r_e held fixed; the published, collapsed M\* errors are used as published and the baryon bands cover the rest); 16–84 and 2.5–97.5 percentiles of log₁₀ s\* over the draws **that have a root**, with the **fraction of draws without a root** reported; a galaxy whose central estimate has no root is **NO ROOT** whatever its draws do. **Pooled rows:** galaxy bootstrap, B = 10,000, central values.
- **δ_FLAT** = log₁₀[g_obs / (g_bar ν_mono(g_bar/a₀))] (CFG227's well-conditioned residual) per galaxy with its Monte Carlo 68 % interval, reported beside s\* (a descriptive column).
- **Distance to the Newtonian floor and to the canonical level (dex, per galaxy, at the central values):** Δ_floor = log₁₀ D (positive: baryon mass could rise by that much before the root disappears; negative: the baryons would have to FALL by |Δ_floor| for a root to exist); Δ_1 = the baryon mass shift (dex, all baryons × 10^Δ at fixed g_obs) that gives s\* = 1 (reported where it exists in [−3, +3]).
- **Ill-conditioning (decided from the baryon side only):** a row is **ILL-CONDITIONED** iff the noiseless-world baryon lever |d log₁₀ s\*/d(baryon dex)| ≥ 10 (about y ≳ 8; CFG229's −56 to −70 at y > 10): its s\*, even with a root, is a statement about a tiny residual D − 1 (ν_mono(y = 12) = 1.03) and is drawn only as a marker with its interval, never as an a₀ measurement.

## 5. Bands, named systematics and knobs (declared, not measured)
- **Baryon bands (the programme's standard, on every baryon mass):** × 10^(±0.15) inner and × 10^(±0.30) outer at fixed g_obs (D × 10^∓); s\* re-solved on the central values; a band solution without a root sets the noroot-corner flag. **Class-S extras (CFG227 / CFG224b):** gas mass only ±0.213 (inner) / ±0.671 dex (outer); stars only ±0.15 / ±0.30 dex (no stellar band existed in CFG227); reported in extra columns.
- **Named systematics:** the SED stellar mass (MAGPHYS; collapsed errors; poor-SED flags; CFG237 puts the offset there), the CO size as the stellar disc scale, the pressure convention, helium, the beam (7 of 8 discs have 2 r_e below the beam major axis), α_CO tuned on the same dynamics (circular).
- **Knobs (the recipe half-width = the quadrature over knobs of the largest |Δ log₁₀ s\*| inside each knob, only over knobs that leave a root; reported with the number of knob-variants that have no root):** (a) stellar disc at **2 R_e,CO** (CFG227's post hoc sensitivity; the gas stays at R_e); (b) R_e × 1.5 and × (1/1.5) for both components; (c) **spherical** baryons (enclosed disc mass as a point mass); (d) **pressure** P− (V_rot only: V_circ² − 3.36 σ²) and P+ (V_circ² + 3.36 σ², CFG227's sensitivity, a double count); (e) **helium:** the published gas multiplied by 1.36 (if the published α_CO excludes He; unstated) ; (f) kernel P2 for ν_mono.
- **Expected laws for the flags:** FLAT s = 1; H(z) and PROXY from CFG223's `curves` at the galaxy's z; three letters per law (in the 95 % interval / in its hull with the inner band / with the outer band), written `na` when the row has no root.

## 6. Stage A — the blind pre-flight (committed with its outputs before stage B is run)
**No g_obs, D, δ or s\* of any of the eight discs is computed:** the velocity columns are not loaded. It uses the baryon side (g_bar at 2 r_e from M\*, M_gas, r_e), the published errors and a noiseless world.

**Controls (each can fail):** **C1** the eight rows exist with the stated ids, z, finite baryon and geometry inputs and the sha256 of both CSVs (printed); **C2** the disc function: Freeman's peak V² = 0.3872 G M/R_d to 0.003 and g → GM/r² far from a compact mass to 1e-3, and equal to CFG229's `gdisc` on 200 random inputs to 1e-12; **C3** g_bar of the eight discs equals CFG227's committed S1 `g_bar` column to 1e-6 relative (only the deviation is printed; no other column of that file is used); **C4** the imported estimator equals CFG223's original bit for bit on 200 random sets; **C5** noiseless identity: D = ν(g_bar/(a₀ s_true)) returns s_true to 1e-6 dex for s_true = 0.5, 1, 2.5 on each galaxy's baryon side; **C6** coverage: noiseless world (D = ν(y)) with the published V, M\*, gas errors injected on 300 mock datasets per galaxy: the 68 % Monte Carlo interval (B = 1,000) contains the truth in ≥ 60 % and the 95 % in ≥ 88 % of the mocks that have a root (reported per galaxy; the near-Newtonian rows are expected to fail by construction, and the failure is the finding); **C7** the baryon-side table: y = g_bar/a₀, ν_mono(y), the lever, the ill-conditioning flag, 2 r_e/beam.

**Reported (pre-flight tables):** A1 y and the lever per galaxy and the number of ILL-CONDITIONED rows; A2 the noiseless-world s\* spread (the 68 % half-width in dex of s\* when the data are exactly on the law and the published V errors are injected: the precision forecast); A3 the effect of each knob on y and on g_bar (baryon side only: the stellar disc at 2 R_e, R_e × 1.5 / 1/1.5, spherical, helium, M\* ± the baryon bands); A4 the pooled-row forecast (the median y of P8 / P6).

**Decisions (the frozen map):** **PF-D1 ESTIMATOR VALIDATED** iff C1–C5 pass (C6 is reported). **PF-D2 ILL-CONDITIONED rows** as §4. **PF-D3 DRAWABLE as an a₀ point** per row iff PF-D1 passes and the row is not ILL-CONDITIONED; every other row is drawn as a marker (floor triangle if it has no root, labelled open symbol with its interval otherwise) and never as a measurement.

## 10. What this lane cannot say
- It does not measure a₀ at z = 2.3–4.4: the baryon model is ours, the stellar masses are SED fits whose offset the record already places in the SED mass, the gas conversion is tuned on the same dynamics, and the discs are near-Newtonian at the measured radius.
- A no-root row is a statement about baryons against dynamics (g_bar above g_obs at 2 r_e), not about the law; a root slightly above the floor is an ill-conditioned residual.
- It does not separate FLAT from H(z) or PROXY and says nothing about the pooled mix of redshifts.
