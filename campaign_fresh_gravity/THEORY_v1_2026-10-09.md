# The Theory, v1 (2026-10-09)

A single statement of the framework as the committed record now defines it: its axioms, the rules that follow from them (each with the lanes that tested it), what is still put in by hand, what it predicts with dates, and an honest scoreboard. This document makes no new claims. Every number cites a committed lane in `campaign_fresh_gravity/`.

κ = ½ is fitted. The cold energy's mass is still required. No dark-matter particle is proposed. The theory is not closed.

---

## 1. Statement

> Gravity has one acceleration scale, set by dark energy: **a₀ = κ c √(G ρ_DE)**. Inside bound systems the extra pull the law requires is not new gravity. It is **cold energy**, a second dark component with a fixed cosmic amount, settled into exactly the profile the law dictates. A system can settle only the cold energy it holds. That cold energy is drawn from the system's own surroundings and is never taken back. Outside bound systems, cold energy behaves like ordinary cold matter.

## 2. Axioms (inputs)

| | axiom | status |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | **The law.** g = ν(g_N/a₀) g_N with kernel ν(y) = 1/(1 − e^(−√y)) (ν_mono, the McGaugh+16 RAR form). | The kernel is an experimental variable. Alternatives tried (ν_simple, ν_standard) fail SPARC (CFG468, CFG531). |
| A2 | **The scale.** a₀ = κ c √(G ρ_DE), κ = ½. | κ is **fitted**. Twelve derivation attempts failed. Measured κ is 0.42 ± 0.10 (CFG493). Two footings, 9.36 × 10⁻¹¹ and 1.13 × 10⁻¹⁰ m s⁻², reported separately and never pooled. |
| A3 | **a₀ tracks dark energy.** a₀(z) = κ c √(G ρ_DE(z)); flat only if w = −1. | DESI DR2 pins a₀(z)/a₀(0) to about ±0.04 dex (CFG512). |
| A4 | **Cold energy exists** with Ω_c/Ω_b = 5.364. | The amount is an input (CFG507: origin not derived; CFG360: no baryon tie). It is a second component distinct from dark energy (CFG508). |
| A5 | **The switch.** The law acts inside bound systems; outside, cold energy is cold matter. | Candidate B. Its full action does not exist yet. |
| A6 | **Supply.** Each system settles the cosmic share of its original baryons. | A **postulate**. Six attempts to derive it under G9 failed (CFG461, 462, 488, 490, 494, 497). |

## 3. Rules that follow (tested, no knobs)

| rule | statement | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| R1 **Phantom = settled cold energy** | The law's extra mass is cold energy that has settled, not extra gravity. | Underlies every rule below. |
| R2 **Supply edge** | The phantom grows only until the system's own cold energy runs out: r_edge = r_M / ln(1 + f_ret f_b/(1 − f_b)), which is 5.85 r_M at full retention. | CFG424 onward; PAPER45 v2.1 (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23240853). |
| R3 **Mass conservation per catchment** | Cold energy settled into a halo is removed from that halo's turnaround sphere. | Switching it off restores a 15% clumping excess (CFG424, CFG518 MUTATE). |
| R4 **Census placement** | f_ret is each system's real retained baryon fraction (about 0.1 for galaxies, 0.86–0.90 for clusters). | Fixes early-type lensing levels, SPARC, MW satellites and clusters (CFG515). Growth stays OK at 256³ and 512³ (CFG518). |
| R5 **Draw from the outer reservoir** | Cold energy is drawn only from the shell between the edge and the turnaround radius; settled cold energy is never drawn back. The law reads unsmoothed baryons. | Halos then match the law's own profile at every resolution, core R 0.94–0.98 (CFG527, CFG530). Earlier draws robbed cores (CFG521, CFG524, CFG526). |
| R6 **Round** | Settled cold energy is spherical, set by enclosed mass, not a flattened phantom disc. | Beats QUMOND's phantom disc on the MW vertical force in 32/32 cells (CFG514, CFG516). |
| R7 **No external-field effect** | A system's internal dynamics do not depend on the field it sits in. | Force-with-EFE is excluded by cluster satellites (CFG478) and by DR3 (CFG447). |
| R8 **Shared supply** | Systems whose catchments overlap share one supply. | Reduces the MW+M31 timing mass by 28% and removes most of the Local Group timing miss (CFG522). |

## 5. Scoreboard (2026-10-09)

**Passes:**
- rotation curves (SPARC, 0.10 dex);
- MeerKAT local a₀;
- clusters, including the Bullet Cluster (needs the cold mass);
- Andromeda and Local Volume dwarfs;
- solar-system and binary-pulsar tests;
- large-scale structure growth, zero-knob (16/16, two 512³ realisations, CFG518 census placement);
- MW vertical force (R6);
- external outer rotation-curve slopes with no bias (CFG533).

**Conditional:**
- MW ultra-faint dwarfs;
- Local Group timing (residual 4σ on the strict radial statistic);
- small-scale growth convergence (CFG530 running);
- the relativistic chassis (option A, the lenient black-hole reading).

**Tensions:**
- central ellipticals (SLUGGS, 2.8–3.5σ statistical, CFG528b);
- isolated-lens inner mass (KiDS, footing-dependent: canonical fails, alt closed by +0.1 dex M*, CFG529/531);
- MW outer slope (3–5σ steeper; a fitted NFW fails too; distance-scale-dependent; external galaxies show no bias, CFG532b/533).

**Common pattern:** every tension sits in massive, old, settled systems. Hypothesis H (history-dependent settling) is under test as CFG534.

**Dead variants:**
- the ungated chassis (growth ×7);
- GR plus a relaxing cold fluid (CFG489);
- the f_ret = 1 edge for galaxies (CFG513, CFG515);
- the 5.85 r_M edge on KiDS.

## 6. Open problems
1. Why κ = ½.
2. What sets the cold energy's amount (Ω_c/Ω_b = 5.364).
3. Deriving the supply postulate (A6) and the settling rules (R2–R5) from an action.
4. Candidate B's full action, including the switch.
5. The massive-system tension: history-dependent settling (H), stellar populations, or a real failure.

## 7. What would kill it
- A robust rise of a₀ with redshift, matching a₀ ∝ H(z) at z ≈ 2.5.
- Gaia DR4 wide binaries deviating from Newton in the pre-registered way.
- Gaia DR4 vertical force preferring a flattened phantom/dark disc over round cold energy.
- Gaia DR4 confirming a 15–27 kpc slope of −0.25 or steeper with σ ≤ 0.04 at census baryons. That would be a shape exclusion, though it would also hit smooth dark-matter halos.
- A sealed ALMA prediction (CFG571) failing when its data are released.
