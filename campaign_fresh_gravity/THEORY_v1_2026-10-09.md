# The Theory, v1 (2026-10-09)

A single statement of the framework as the committed record now defines it: its axioms, the rules that follow from them (each with the lanes that tested it), what is still put in by hand, what it predicts with dates, and an honest scoreboard. This document makes no new claims. Every number cites a committed lane in `campaign_fresh_gravity/`.

κ = ½ is fitted. The cold energy's mass is still required. No dark-matter particle is proposed. The theory is not closed.

---

## 1. Statement

> Gravity has one acceleration scale, set by dark energy: **a₀ = κ c √(G ρ_DE)**. Inside bound systems the extra pull the law requires is not new gravity. It is **cold energy**, a second dark component with a fixed cosmic amount, settled into exactly the profile the law dictates. A system can settle only the cold energy it holds. That cold energy is drawn from the system's own surroundings and is never taken back. Outside bound systems, cold energy behaves like ordinary cold matter.

## 5a. The cold-energy dynamics (CFG539, 541, 542)
- **Equation of motion:** class-A settling drift v_s = −α 1_B 1_C τ_ff ∇ψ, ∇²ψ = 4πG d, mass conserved. The supply edge EMERGES (inside-out fill matches the census formula to <1%, CFG541).
- **One principle (CFG542, Pb):** a metriplectic Onsager principle with a dark-energy scalar partner φ. It gives the drift and the energy sink from a single statement, conserves total energy exactly, and makes entropy non-decreasing. The sink is DERIVED: Q = ρ_c α τ_ff ∇ψ·∇Φ ≥ 0, driving φ̈ + 3Hφ̇ − c²∇²φ + V′ = Q/φ̇. Its effect on a₀ is ≤ 4e-6 dex.
- **Still open:** α is FREE (bounded by causality, α ≲ 1). The switch ('bound') and the catchment are INPUTS; a gate reading the cold energy leaks (MS1), one reading baryons does not. A canonical scalar partner is singular where w crosses −1, which DESI's chains do. Thin-reservoir drift speeds are open. **Kinetic consistency fails as is (CFG544):** position-only drift leaves settled cold energy with σ = 0, so the halo collapses radially within 1–2 Gyr and overfills the core. The derived two-sided fix fails (it pumps energy in). A velocity relaxation toward the local Jeans dispersion passes every test but its target temperature is POSITED (the CFG461 question). Kinetic support also softens the sharp edge outward by about 25–55%. The settling law therefore needs a derived velocity-space part. CFG550: a phase-space principle (KL relaxation toward the law's phase-space phantom) DERIVES a target T = V_f²/2 but FAILS: the untruncated target heats the edge, spills the halo, and blows up clusters. Lynden-Bell leaves T free; the bathtub fill fails at the edge. Key reading: FIX-2 works because its target is the Jeans dispersion of the CURRENT density (self-consistent), not the law's temperature. Deriving that self-consistent target is the open step. CFG554: maximum entropy at fixed density with tensor Jeans balance DERIVES a self-consistent (anisotropic) target but FAILS in clusters (the dilute tail heats, σ_r² up to 7 V_f²). FIX-2 as a metriplectic relaxation is valid and conserves angular momentum, but its isotropic target needs an isotropy constraint added (POSITED). It is also not α-robust (fails at α×0.5). Overfill is still not handled. CFG558: isotropy is CHOSEN, not forced. The bracket couples only to the velocity trace, but the temperature must come from the tensor Vlasov stationarity, so the scalar constraint is one added principle (and the bench prefers it). The knob-free overfill rule fails (unbounded where ρ_ph → 0; cluster blow-up). Not α-robust: at α×0.5 the MW settles by 13.8 Gyr but its edge spills. FIX-2 remains the working velocity part, with its target POSITED. Angular momentum can be conserved with a radial slide plus a velocity-space term.

## 5b. Dark energy's history (CFG549)
The framework does not predict ρ_DE(z): it uses ρ_DE as an input. Its own ingredients give Λ plus an unobservable settling trickle (about 1e-7). It offers an independent MEASUREMENT channel: galaxies' a₀(z)/a₀(0) = √(ρ_DE(z)/ρ_DE(0)), with κ cancelling.

## 7. What would kill it

- Gaia DR4 wide binaries deviating from Newton in the pre-registered way.
- Gaia DR4 vertical force preferring a flattened phantom/dark disc over round cold energy.

- A sealed ALMA prediction (CFG571) failing when its data are released.
