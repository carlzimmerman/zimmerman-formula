# CFG82 -- can per-galaxy globular-cluster density slopes be estimated from on-disk data? (feasibility; NOT FEASIBLE)

**Question.** CFG76 (post hoc) found that the SLUGGS estimator's fixed tracer slope gamma = 3 decides the size of the residual (law offset 0 at gamma about 1.83, rule 0 at about 2.45). Can per-galaxy slopes be estimated from data already on disk (`real_research/data/`; no downloads), and the offsets re-scored?

**Verdict: no. The lane stopped after the feasibility step; no gamma_i was estimated, nothing was re-scored, and there is no MUTATE control.**

**On disk.** `sluggs_forbes2017_gcvel.tsv` is Forbes+2017 Table 5 (radial velocities): star, RA, Dec, HRV, e_HRV, Rgal in arcmin and nothing else (3573 rows; 3440 GC velocities used by CFG76). `sluggs_forbes2017_galaxies.tsv` is Table 1 (D, logM*, R_eff, type, environment, Vsys, sigma, position, N). `atlas3d_fj_table.tsv` has stellar r_1/2, R_e, L and JAM M/L for the 16 JAM galaxies. **Absent:** GC magnitudes and colours (Forbes 2017 Tables 2-4), any photometric candidate catalogue (so no parent sample and no spectroscopic completeness), GC number-density profiles, published GC slopes or Sersic fits, and the mask geometry and slit-collision information.

**Selection biases acting on a slope from Table 5.** (1) Spectroscopic incompleteness: the observed count is n_GC(R) times a completeness in (R, magnitude, colour) that is unknown. (2) Coverage: only a few DEIMOS masks per galaxy; occupied 30-degree sectors in the outer bins range from 0.25 to 1.0; NGC 4374 and NGC 4697 have no data beyond 4 R_e and NGC 7457 reaches only about 2.9 arcmin, so a naive slope measures the mask footprint as much as the GCs. (3) Radius range: Rgal_max is only 5-14 arcmin for most galaxies, the same 1-5 R_e range the CFG76 estimator uses. (4) Poisson noise: in a full-azimuth toy with gamma = 3, sigma(gamma) is 0.25, 0.16, 0.09, 0.06 for N = 40, 100, 250, 600; ten of the 21 galaxies with more than 30 velocities have fewer than 110 GCs. (5) The blue/red GC mix cannot be separated (no colours), though the two populations have different slopes.

**Toy (`cfg79_toy_selection.py`, seed 79, `toy.log`; footprint sizes are the agent's guesses, so it shows the size of the effect, not the real bias).** A true power law with an assumed DEIMOS-like footprint, fitted by a naive truncated power-law MLE that ignores the footprint, gives: single 16' x 4' strip, true gamma 3 -> 3.81, true 2 -> 2.96; three narrow masks, 3 -> 3.45, 2 -> 2.53; wide well-spread masks, 3 -> 3.04, 2 -> 2.08; full azimuth recovers the truth. The bias runs from about 0 to +0.8 depending on geometry, which is larger than the gap between the law's zero point (about 1.83) and the rule's (about 2.45). A naive MLE on the real Table 5 (`cfg79_feasibility.py`, `feas.log`, diagnostic only, not an estimate of gamma) gives about 2.65 to 3.7, mostly near 3.

**What would be needed (not fetched; the references are the agent's recollection, unchecked).** The SLUGGS wide-field GC photometry with the spectroscopic-target flags and the GC number-density profiles: Forbes+2017 (AJ 153, 114) Tables 2-4 (VizieR J/AJ/153/114); Usher+2012 (MNRAS 426, 1475) photometry and colour distributions; Alabi+2016 (MNRAS 460, 3838) SLUGGS mass profiles with tracer power-law slopes (the closest published gamma_i to what the estimator needs); Harris, Harris and Alessi 2013 (ApJ 772, 82) composite profiles. **Any fetch needs the user's explicit go.**

**Reading.** gamma = 3 is untested against real SLUGGS slopes; real GC 3D slopes may well lie in 2 to 3.5, but nothing on disk can say where this sample sits. This is not evidence for or against the framework; kappa = 1/2 is FITTED.
