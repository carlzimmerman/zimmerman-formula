# CFG821: the classical dSphs want L* ≤ 22 pc; the globulars want ≥ 24 pc. INCONSISTENT (conditional: the dSphs are short even unscreened)

**Data.** 11 MW classical dSphs (LVD). Υ_V = 2 (0.15 dex width). Isolated law (record rule for satellites), with an EFE
bracket. CFG691 screening.

| model | χ² unscreened (10 dof) | χ² Newton | best L* | 95% range |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| isolated law, canonical | 58.4 (p ~ 1e-8) | 896 | 5.75 pc (grid floor) | **5.75–22 pc** |
| isolated law, alt | 51.8 | 896 | floor | 5.75–22 pc |
| law + EFE, canonical | 158.9 | 896 | floor | 5.75–19 pc |

**Frozen class: INCONSISTENT** (L*_95,up = 22 pc, below CFG820's 24 pc), **CONDITIONAL**: the unscreened law already fits
the dSphs badly.

**What is driving it.**
- Several dSphs sit well **above** even the unscreened isolated law at Υ = 2:
  - Draco 9.6 vs 4.6;
  - Ursa Minor 8.9 vs 4.6;
  - Canes Venatici I 7.7 vs 4.4;
  - Leo II 7.5 vs 5.4;
  - Sextans 7.9 vs 4.4.
- Fornax, Leo I and Antlia II fit; Crater II sits below.
- Any screening lowers the prediction further, so the fit pushes L* to its floor.
- The "upper bound" is therefore the **dwarf mass shortfall** showing up, not a clean measurement of stiffness.

**Reading.**
- The globulars must be screened (L* ≥ 24 pc). The dSphs must not be (L* ≤ 22 pc), *and* they need more than the law.
- With gravity alone, no single L* serves both. This is the long-standing globular-vs-dwarf dichotomy in a sharp,
  quantitative form.
- The consistent resolution in this framework is the two-population picture:
  - dwarfs carry **real bound cold mass** beyond the law (as the UFDs do; CFG820's consequence);
  - globulars do not.
- With that, L* can sit ≥ 24 pc. The screening then makes the dwarfs' required cold mass even larger.
- This is a strong, quantitative constraint on any dwarf-galaxy solution: it must supply roughly the observed σ² at
  Newtonian or near-Newtonian gravity.

**Controls.** K1 and K2 PASS. MUTATE (S = 0, fully screened) leaves only Newton (χ² 896), so there is no bound and the test
bites.

κ fitted; not theory closed.
