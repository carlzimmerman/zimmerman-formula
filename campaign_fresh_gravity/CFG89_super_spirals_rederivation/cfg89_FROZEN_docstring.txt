   then a slowly rising phantom h' = 0.05 h_p / (y + y_p) (h continuous at y_p); nu_RAR = 1/(1 - exp(-sqrt y)) everywhere; P2 = sqrt(1 + 1/y) (CFG64's P2).
   Expectation declared now: for these galaxies y = g_N/a0 is mostly < 2.54, so nu_mono == nu_RAR there (identical numbers) and the CFG64 reading 'P2' is a DIFFERENT kernel from RAR
   (the CFG56 README's 'RAR kernel (CFG64 P2)' conflates them); CFG56's own numbers use nu_mono, which equals RAR where y <= 2.54.

   Freeman / point / spherical; CFG56: half the range of the mean over B/T_r, B/T_r+0.10, max(B/T_r-0.10, 0)) + M_* term (half the shift of the mean between M_* x10^{+0.2} and x10^{-0.2}, all

   Per-galaxy inclination error dlog v = |cot i| (5 deg) / ln10 and dlog v = dv/(v ln10) are computed and reported (R-rows), not used in the headline sigma.
