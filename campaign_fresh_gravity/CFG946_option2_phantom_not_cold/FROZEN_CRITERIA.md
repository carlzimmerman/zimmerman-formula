## 3. Theorems to be checked (sympy + numerics; DERIVATION states assumptions)

- **L-AR (action–reaction).** For any coupling −σ(ρ_i)φ in an action, the matter that feels φ's force sources φ. Hence a
  sector "sourced by baryons only and felt by dust" (O2a-1, O2b) is non-variational.
- **L-G (Galilean lemma + dilation).** Exact uniform-gradient superposition for a field whose gradient is a universal force
  ⇔ its local second-order static equation depends on the fields only through second derivatives. Then φ_μ(x) = μ²φ(x/μ),
  ρ_μ(x) = ρ(x/μ) is again a solution (mass μ³M), so the flat-curve amplitude A = lim r|∇φ| scales as μ², while the deep law
  needs A = √(GMa₀) ∝ μ^{3/2} (and the deep regime is preserved for μ < 1). Hence no such sector supplies the deep law; any
  sector that does carries an EFE. The X share of the deep amplitude scales as μ^{1/2} → 0.
- **D-T′ (generalized D-T).** A cold static dust held in the law by any always-on local force F(u), whose own field acts on
  baryons with multiplicity m and on itself with multiplicity m′: the law forces F′(0) = m′ for every m and for every law with
  the deep limit g_N → g²/a₀, i.e. the holding force cancels whatever linear self-gravity the dust feels.
- **N-L (no linear regime).** For a sector whose deep-regime force is √(a₀ g_N) of its source field, dg_X/dg_N → ∞ as
  g_N → 0: the response about FRW is not Lipschitz; the plane-wave equation ξ̈ = C√ξ has two solutions from ξ = ξ̇ = 0.
- **T-O2 (main).** Assumptions: (A1) option 2: cold energy is pressureless, universally coupled dust in free fall with
  Poisson self-gravity; (A2) every other sector is local, second order, translation/rotation invariant, with universal forces
  (passive = inertial); (A3) isolated rotators obey the law with the profile-independent deep amplitude A = √(GM_b a₀) for any
  assembly history, the law being selected by static equations; (A4) no EFE: uniform external fields leave a satellite's
  internal dynamics unchanged (CL-AR at uniform order; CFG925 R3). Claim: no construction satisfies A1–A4. Route: L-G ⇒ the
  deep extra force is dust mass ⇒ cold energy IS the phantom in the deep regime (contradicting option 2's premise) ⇒ static
  selection needs supported dust (CL-AR Prop. 2) ⇒ D-T′ cancels its self-gravity (contradicting A1 where the support acts),
  while free-falling dust has no static selection (Jeans families). Open by assumption: non-static, non-local supply
  (CL-AR's open branch; option 3, CFG947). The data corollary drops A4 and quantifies the EFE route's cost.
