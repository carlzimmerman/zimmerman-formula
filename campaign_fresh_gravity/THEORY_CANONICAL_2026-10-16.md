## Changes to the five statements
**S1 (scale), S2 (field), S5 (black holes): unchanged.** One addition to S2: its rest state is the law exactly (AG4, Lean
12/12).

**S3. Cold energy: revised supply rule.**
- **Removed:** "each halo settles only its own supply". KiDS-1000 isolated-galaxy lensing excludes a halo truncated at
  the galaxy's own 5.364 M_b share (CFG801: Δχ² +197 within 0.31 Mpc, +753 all radii).
- **Replaced by:** each system draws cold energy from its **catchment**, the baryons within its turnaround radius. The
  supply is 5.364 × (catchment baryons) = 5.364 N M_b,gal, with N = 1/f_ret.
  - Collisionless infall fills onto the law out to 20–50 r_M (CFG748, CFG803).
  - With f_ret from the observational census, this fits KiDS within 0.31 Mpc with no fitted parameter (CFG804).
  - The framework's two-halo term narrows the outer deficit from +149 to +11 (CFG805).
- **Input, not derived:** f_ret. A framework-native derivation failed (CFG806).

**S4. Untethering: revised.**
- **Removed:** the photo-evaporation selector for ultra-faint dwarfs (CFG782: DEAD; it flags about half of M31's
  satellites).
- **Added, as a working rule:** the cold share of baryons a system has **expelled** is free (H-EX; CFG809). Retained
  baryons tether only their own share.
  - It improves every eRASS1 gas tercile over the plain law (core A_MID 1.85 → ~1.1–1.2).
  - It gives the best eFEDS group fit (p 0.04–0.09).
  - With retention from the measured eROSITA outskirt slope it passes on the 76 λ-matched clusters (CFG814; a
    confirmation run).
  - **It fails on the full 318** (CFG815): the middle-mass tercile is short ×~1.6 (Z ≈ +4). That shortfall is real:
    selection bias is only 0–10% (CFG817). Unbinding, placement and splashback do not fix it (CFG812/818/819).
- **Still open:** ultra-faint dwarfs (now without a selector), and the galaxy-scale early-type excess (H-EX predicts
  +0.013 vs +0.280; CFG811).
