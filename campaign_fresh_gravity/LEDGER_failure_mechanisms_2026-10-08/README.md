# Failure-mechanism ledger, 2026-10-08

**The owner's principle (2026-10-08):** "physical reality must have a mechanism and explanation for anything that fails in the framework."

This ledger takes every item on the status board that is red (FAIL), amber (CONDITIONAL) or blue (NOT DECIDABLE), and every KILL from 10-07/08 (CFG428, CFG429, CFG431, CFG432, CFG358, CFG461). The board is `closure_map/status_picture_2026_10_03.py` and `explainers/crispy_fried_chicken_status_board.md`. For each item it records:
- what failed, and what the failure applies to;
- the explanations the record already contains, with their status;
- the missing ingredient, where nothing on the record explains the failure;
- whether the failure might be a data or assumption artefact.

**Rules kept.**
- This is a reading of committed lanes. No new computation was run, and no verdict is changed here.
- Every explanation is either cited by lane ID or labelled **NEW HYPOTHESIS**, with its test.
- Artefact risk is **flagged, not adjudicated**. A parallel audit lane covers the data.
- κ = ½ is fitted. No dark-matter particle is added: the cold fluid's mass is still required, and its amount is underived. This is not "theory closed".

Sources read: `STANDING_2026-09-29.md` (including the appendices through 10-08), `WORKING_MODEL_SETTLED_PHANTOM_2026-10-06.md`, `closure_map/RECIPE_GATE_AUDIT_2026-10-03.md`, and the lane READMEs cited.

Status words used:
- **tested-pass** / **tested-fail**: a committed lane ran it;
- **untested-testable**: the test is stated;
- **ruled out**: a committed lane excludes it;
- **owner call**: the record leaves the reading to the owner.

Applies-to labels:
- **B** = candidate B (the law with the bound-only switch, plus the conserved cold fluid);
- **chassis** = the ungated filtered C-H/K khronon (the law on everywhere);
- **reading** = a specific sub-model, rule or assumption.
