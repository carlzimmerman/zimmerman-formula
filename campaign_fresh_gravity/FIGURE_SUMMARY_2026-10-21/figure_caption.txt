Working-note summary of a modified-gravity framework, not peer reviewed. Galaxy law g_N = g²/(g + a0) with a0 = ½c√(Gρ_DE), κ = ½ fitted; the two a0 footings (filled 9.3603e-11, open 1.1312e-10 m s⁻²) are never pooled. (a) SPARC radial acceleration relation: 3389 points from 175 discs, binned medians and the law; weighted rms 0.101 / 0.099 dex with one global mass-to-light ratio. (b) Offset from the law by class: SPARC discs and eRASS1 cluster outskirts (KiDS-1000 lensing) sit on it; cluster cores, Coma ultra-diffuse galaxies and Local Group dwarf spheroidals sit 0.24–0.37 dex above; tidal dwarfs and NGC 1052-DF2/DF4 sit below, at the Newtonian expectation. This excess family is unexplained. (c) Raw KiDS-1000 cosmic shear: four framework runs minus matched controls, all CONSISTENT; the lead is amplitude-driven and absolute fits are mediocre; DES Y3 is diagnostic only for NC2x. (d) Implied a0(z) from RC100, ALESS 122.1, ALPINE, CRISTAL and MIGHTEE-HI rotation data against flat a0, a0 ∝ H(z) and the framework's DESI BAO + Pantheon+ band: not yet decidable. The structure-formation engine has no established physical parent.
