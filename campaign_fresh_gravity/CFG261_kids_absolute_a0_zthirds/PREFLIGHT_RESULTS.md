- The data entered the pre-flight through (i) the per-patch sums and jackknife covariances, (ii) the C7 blind calibration, whose per-pair levels were formed internally and only the two aggregates (mean and SD of Δ/σ_Δ) printed, and (iii) the jackknife σ of the thirds' difference (a spread, no value). No absolute level, amplitude or difference was printed.
