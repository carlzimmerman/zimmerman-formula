## 3. g_obs, g_bar and the gas route
- **Radius** r = 2.2 r_d = 1.31 RHALF (the radius of the fit's V_22), RHALF from arcsec to kpc at the galaxy's z (flat ΛCDM, H₀ = 67.4, Ω_m = 0.315).
- **g_obs (headline, pressure-corrected):** V_c² = V_22² + 2 σ₀² (r/r_d) = V_22² + 4.4 σ₀² (Burkert+10's exponential-disc asymmetric-drift term at r = 2.2 r_d, σ₀ = the fit's `sig0`), g_obs = V_c²/r. **The rotation-only reading V_22² (no pressure, a lower bound on the circular speed) is a knob and a reported column.** V_22 is deprojected with the fixed inclination.
- **Headline baryons B0 = STARS ONLY (a lower limit):** M\* = 10^LMSTAR in one thin exponential disc (Freeman) with R_e,\* = RHALF (the HST H-band stellar half-light radius, semi-major axis) and R_d = R_e/1.678, g_bar at r (CFG229's `gdisc`). **A root with B0 is an UPPER bound on s\*; a floor NO ROOT with B0 is robust against any added gas.**

## 4. Estimator and statistics (the programme's, as CFG272–274 with the floor / ceiling split)
