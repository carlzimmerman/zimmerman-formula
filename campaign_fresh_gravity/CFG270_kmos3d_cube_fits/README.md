## Bottom line
1. **With stars only and the pressure-corrected V_c² = V_22² + 4.4 σ₀² at r = 2.2 r_d, D = g_obs/g_bar,\* has a median 1.65 in the 72 headline fits (T1; 16–84 %: 0.88–2.78; range 0.10–12.6).** δ_FLAT(stars only) is positive for 45 of the 72 (median **+0.084** dex; 16–84 %: −0.12 to +0.30): the stars fall short of the canonical-law dynamics by a median 0.08 dex, **a small gap compared with lane D's 0.52 dex**. Nothing here excludes FLAT, H(z) or PROXY.

4. **The bound exists only with the pressure term.** The headline V_c² = V_22² + 4.4 σ₀² (the Burkert+10 exponential-disc term at r = 2.2 r_d) raises g_obs by a median **+0.20 dex per fit** (+0.30 dex in the median g_obs). **With rotation only (V_22², no pressure) the pooled median D is 0.88 and PT1 has no root** (the rotation-only reading is at the floor); with the SINS convention (+3.36 σ₀²) s\* falls by 0.28 dex. ## Disclosures
- **The lane is not blind** (criteria §0) and the **bound-hit flags used in the T1 selection are fit outputs**; **35 of the 72 T1 fits carry the `edge` flag** (all 35 are r_t at its lower bound, the one bound hit the frozen tier allows); their median D is 1.54 against 1.70 for the 37 non-edge fits (reported, not a selection). The RC100 flag is the frozen name recipe's 18 fits (14 in T1), not the data card's 22 (three more through SINS positions and one doubtful row that the name recipe does not reproduce).
- V_22 is the arctan model's intrinsic circular speed at 2.2 r_d with the inclination fixed from the H-band axis ratio (the fit's errors do not include that uncertainty; ±5° moves PT1 by ±0.21 dex). M\* has no catalogue error (declared 0.20 dex); the stellar scale is the HST H-band half-light radius; the missing gas is the dominant unknown. These discs are not independent of the RC100 class-D points or the SINS lane (the overlap flags are in the points file).
- The stat columns of the points file hold percentiles of the Monte Carlo draws that have a root (per fit) or of the full bootstrap including floor draws (pooled); for a floor row they are placeholders at s = 0.001 and the chart should draw a floor triangle; no fit is at the ceiling in the main run. The downward baryon-band corners violate the stars-only lower limit and are labelled so. One measurement run; no re-run to change a result.

## What this lane cannot say
It does not measure a₀ at z ≈ 2.0–2.7: the baryons are a lower limit, the dynamics are a model's circular law with a hand-added pressure term (without which PT1 has no root), 35 of the 72 headline fits sit at a parameter bound, and the systematics (0.61 dex) exceed the statistical interval. A floor NO ROOT with stars only is robust against any added gas; a root with stars only is an upper bound on s\*; class D carries no law statement.
