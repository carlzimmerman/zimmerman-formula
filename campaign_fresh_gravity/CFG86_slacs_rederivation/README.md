**The attack on 'B-specific statistically' (definitions frozen before running).** S_a: |Delta_LCDM| <= 2 err. S_b: (B - LCDM) > 2 sqrt(errB^2 + errL^2), unpaired, using B's smallest gap (+0.152). The wording is supported in a cell iff S_a and S_b. 54 declared cells: halo multiplier {1/3, 1, 3} x tie {solved Salpeter, solved Chabrier-equivalent (s = 0.562), fixed Chabrier-equivalent} x {z = 0, z-consistent} x {Duffy, relaxed, Dutton-Maccio}. **S_a holds in 52% of cells, S_b in 78%, both in 52%: verdict CONDITIONAL** (ROBUST would need at least 90%); with the floor, LCDM passes in 100% of cells.
- **What flips S_a:** lighter halos (the LCDM statistical gap crosses +2 sigma at a multiplier of about 0.72; at x1/3 and z = 0 LCDM sits at +0.062 to +0.075 dex, 2.6 to 3.4 sigma stat; S_a holds in 17% of cells at x1/3, 61% at x1, 78% at x3), a Chabrier-equivalent tie (+0.066 to +0.080 solved, +0.075 to +0.090 fixed, at z = 0), and z = 0 halos (S_a holds in 41% of z = 0 cells against 63% of z-consistent cells); the concentration relation matters little (50 to 56%).
- **What flips S_b:** 12 of 54 cells (9 at x1/3 with a Chabrier tie, 3 at x1 with the fixed Chabrier tie at z = 0), where B minus LCDM shrinks to +0.023 to +0.03. Over all 54 cells B minus LCDM has median +0.118 and range +0.023 to +0.266, and never goes negative; with CFG80's paired error scale (0.016), 51 of 54 cells pass.
- **Zero-point axis (the 0.10-dex floor):** a Salpeter zero-point offset d between Auger and ATLAS3D shifts LCDM's gap by exactly -d (checked by full re-solve at +-0.10). S_a holds only for d in [-0.012, +0.086]; at d = -0.10 the LCDM gap is +0.137 (a 5.5 sigma statistical gap that LCDM shares with B), at +0.10 it is -0.063. B's own statistical gap stays above 2 sigma only for d < +0.108.

B's +0.15 to +0.17 lies between the halo-free gap and the LCDM base: the stars-only gap that any model with a small projected dark contribution at R_E (B's phantom, about 14 to 20% in projection) inherits.

**What the agent would attack.** (1) The label 'SPECIFIC-TO-B' for the statistical gap rests on one base cell (1.5 sigma) whose bootstrap error (0.025) is a quarter of the floor; it becomes a shared 3 to 5 sigma gap with halos about 30% lighter, a Chabrier-equivalent tie at z = 0, or a zero-point d <= -0.02. **The robust statement is the zero-point-invariant one: B is about 0.12 dex above LCDM, at 7 sigma or more if the paired errors hold. That is a difference between the models, not a failure of either.** (2) The Moster tie gives group-to-cluster halos for single ellipticals; the two lighter-halo variants that S_a is most sensitive to are the ones CFG80 flagged. (4) The z-consistent halo should be a frozen declared variant, not a caveat. (5) B's side was not tested here.
