The mu_uni values do not change with the boundary condition; the 10% edge counts differ at small m2 (6/24 against 8/24 within 10% at m2 = 1e-16). The costs change a lot: at m2 = 1e-12 the screening length ell = 3.9 kpc is comparable to the Sun's 8 kpc and to the 1 kpc inner edge, so the Sun potential is set by the truncation (-9e5, +1.4e6, -1.4e6 for Dirichlet at r0 = 1, 2, 4 kpc against +7.6e7, +8.2e7, +1.0e8 for Neumann); the signs flip, and so does the flagship force at m2 = 1e-16. **Deviations and disclosures.** The first scan used a damped-Newton solver that stalled at small m2 and gave noisy Sun potentials; it was rewritten (unknown delta = chi0 - t, energy-descent modified Newton with a Cholesky-regularised Hessian; the first attempt is not committed). The frozen wording said 'local relative residual <= 1e-8'; the implemented criterion is max|F|/s <= 1e-8 against a static scale s (the strict form hits rounding where t is about 1e5). Neumann was declared only as attack A6; the full N runs are post hoc.

**H3 and the attacks.** *Which layers set mu_uni:* in every scanned row the 1e12 alt-footing layer at one of four redshifts (z = 4 for m2 >= 3.2e-12, z = 2.5 at 1e-13 to 1e-12, z = 1 at 3.2e-15 to 3.2e-14, z = 0.25 at 1e-15 and below); the canonical footing is within 5-7%. *Which edges fail at 1e-16 (N):* 16 layers (all six at z = 4, all six at z = 2.5, and z = 1 at 1e11 and 1e12), all outward, +14% to about +960%. *Tolerance:* with 10% or 20%, 24/24 needs m2 >= 1e-12; with 30% it is 3e-13, 50% 1e-13, 100% 3e-14; the baryon UV speed is 0.73 c at 1e-13, so a 50% tolerance makes it subluminal, but the cost bars still fail there (flagship -4.0 v_f^2, Sun +1.8e7 v_f^2). *Bars against tracking:* the costs scale as m2 (log-log slope 0.95-1.0 below 1e-16); the flagship bars are met only at m2 <= 1e-17 and the Sun bar only at m2 <= about 1e-22, while tracking needs m2 >= 1e-12; where the bars pass, 0/24 edges are within 10% and mu_uni is at the bisection floor of 1e20, so the gate is decoupled rather than repaired. **Any passing point? No.** The extended plane (m2 from 1e-30 to 1e-8, mu = 1, 2, 5, 10, 100 times mu_uni) has 0 of 115 points passing stable and T and F and S in both boundary conditions; the post-hoc fine scan (61 m2 values x 8 mu factors x both boundary conditions, 976 points) has 0 passing F, 0 passing S and 0 passing any pair including F or S (stable and T holds for 210 points D and 241 N); under N the signed flagship potential, flagship force and Sun potential change sign at different m2 (1.4-1.9e-14, 3.4-4.6e-14, 2.2-2.9e-11), none coinciding with another or with tracking. *chi0 = t versus consistent chi0 (A2):* mu_uni(slaved)/mu_uni(consistent) is 2.62 at m2 = 1e-12, 1.47 at 3.2e-12, 1.12 at 1e-11, 1.01 at 1e-10 and 8 to 113 below 1e-12, so CFG49's exploratory '1.00-1.06' holds only for m2 of about 3e-11 and above. *H2 ('finite m2 does not lower the stiffness') is refuted, as CFG49 also found:* 130 of 381 pairs are below 0.98 (lowest ratio about 0.05 at m2 = 1e-12 for the z = 4, 1e12 alt layer), from chi lagging t, which smooths the gate profile.

**What the agent would attack.** (1) Non-monotone stability at small m2: the bisection assumes stability is monotone in mu, which fails for m2 below about 3e-13 (31 probe violations, none at or above 3.2e-13), so there mu_uni is one edge of an island, not a minimum; CFG49's small-m2 rows use the same algorithm and inherit this. (2) Windows: the stiffness comes from DE13's lenient half-layer windows in t; on the whole domain 8 of 24 layers are unstable at the declared mu_uni and the minimum mu is 1.8e29 at m2 = 1e-12 (12x higher) and 1.4e28 at 1e-16 (Dirichlet; about 1.8e28 under N); windows in chi0 give 1.33e28 (0.9x); so mu_uni is a lower bound. (3) The Sun number at ell comparable to r is a truncation artefact; any Sun-cost claim needs a physical inner-boundary prescription. (4) The scoped no-go rests on a frozen spherical fluid background: it leaves out gas self-gravity, the T3/T4 cross terms and nonlinear chi evolution, and the UV speed is a frozen-background principal-symbol statement. It is not a closure of anything.
