# CFG796 FROZEN CRITERIA: where can a cold-energy converter live? (theory only, committed before any script)

CFG793 found that the committed action conserves cold mass: no term releases its rest mass. CFG794 found that no known
enhancement closes the gap. This lane maps every remaining place a converter could live and grades each one. It is
theory only. External numbers are quoted from memory (provisional).

## Branches
1. **Polarization identity** (cold mass = bound charge −∇·P, the field reading). Releasing it means depolarizing.
   - Energy available: the field energy u_F = g²/8πG, at most u_F/ρc² of the rest-mass reading (CFG792 F: 1/2.6e6).
   - Cost of depolarizing over a device scale ℓ: the stiffness penalty (L*/ℓ)², at ℓ = 1 m, 1 km and 1 AU, with
     L* = 5.75 pc.
   - Class: DEVICE-REACHABLE if the penalty is < 1 at ℓ ≤ 1 AU; otherwise FORBIDDEN BY STIFFNESS.
2. **Medium identity with a new local sink** Γ in ordinary matter. CFG792 requires it to be ≥ 2e5 off in natural matter,
   and CFG794 says it cannot be stimulated by known means. Class: UNMOTIVATED (no term in the record) unless the record
   holds one.
3. **Horizon sink.** The record's only sink is black holes converting cold mass to dark energy (PAPER50: the cold-only
   partition is consistent with FRBs). A horizon swallows cold mass; Hawking radiation turns a small hole's mass into
   light. For M = 1e8, 1e9 and 1e10 kg, report:
   - the Hawking power ħc⁶/(15360πG²M²) and the lifetime 5120πG²M³/(ħc⁴);
   - the Schwarzschild radius and the formation energy Mc²;
   - the cold-energy intake 16πG²M²ρ/(c²v), at ρ = 0.01 M☉ pc⁻³ and v = 230 km/s, versus the Hawking mass loss P/c².

   Class: CONVERTER EXISTS IN PRINCIPLE (a horizon). Its cold fraction is the ratio intake/(P/c²).
