## Limits and disclosures
- Author decompositions: RC100 f_DM and CRISTAL M_bary come from the authors' dynamical fits (halo priors, prior-anchored baryon masses); an implied a₀ here is the a₀ scale that makes the authors' decomposition match the kernel, not a direct measurement; earlier lanes called the a₀(z) confrontation non-diagnostic and this figure does not change that.
- Not blind: the RC100 slopes and CRISTAL medians of the flat, H(z) and proxy laws were seen in CFG213 / 216 / 220 / 222; the implied-a₀ estimator, the quartile bins, the bands, the DESI law and every number above are new in this lane.
- The ratio is relative to the footing's local a₀; there is no z ≈ 0 point, and flat predicts 1 only if the baryon calibration that set the local a₀ applies at every z, which is what the bands probe. A uniform baryon shift is not a z-dependent tilt (CFG217's axis).
- The caption is generated by rule from the flags (`cfg223_results.json`, `caption`), not hand-edited; the orchestrator's requested sentence "consistent with a constant a₀" was made conditional in the frozen criteria and the rule did not release it (one point, 0.065 dex).
- The 95% intervals are percentile bootstrap intervals of a skewed estimator at n = 6 to 27 (coverage measured in C4); the lever diagnostic is post hoc and reported only.
