## 3. The estimator (new)
For a set of rows, kernel ν, footing constant a₀,loc: **δ_i(s) = log₁₀[ D_i / ν( g_bar,i / (a₀,loc · s) ) ]**; the **implied a₀ ratio s\*** is the root of **median_i δ_i(s) = 0** for log₁₀ s in [−3, +3], found by a vectorised bisection (80 halvings). δ_i(s) is non-increasing in s (a larger a₀ needs a smaller D), so the root is unique; if the bracket holds no sign change the point is flagged UNBOUNDED (the count of unbounded resamples is reported; if over 5% the interval is drawn as an arrow, not a bar).
- **Primary cell:** ν_mono, canonical footing (a₀,loc = 9.3603e-11). The other three cells (ν_mono alt = 1.1312e-10; P2 canonical; P2 alt) are reported in the table as point estimates.
- **Bootstrap:** galaxies resampled with replacement within the point's own rows; **B = 10,000**; the index matrix from `default_rng(223·1000 + n).integers(0, n, size=(B, n))`; percentile intervals **16–84% (thick bar, "68%") and 2.5–97.5% (thin bar, "95%")** of the resampled s\*; the bootstrap sd of log₁₀ s\* is reported.
- **Expected implied ratio under each law T** (table only): the point's galaxies placed ON law T through their g_obs (g_bar,T = the inversion of g_obs = g_bar ν(g_bar/(a₀,loc F_T(z_i))) at each galaxy, D_T = g_obs/g_bar,T, as CFG216 / 217), then the same estimator: s\*_T. **Pull_L = (log₁₀ s\* − log₁₀ s\*_L)/sd**, with the per-resample expectation (CFG233): s\*_L recomputed in every bootstrap resample and sd the bootstrap sd of the difference. Pulls describe where a point sits against a law's expectation given the gas route; **they are statistics-only, not a verdict.**

## 4. The gas-calibration bands
For each point, **baryon-mass shifts τ_b ∈ {−0.30, −0.15, +0.15, +0.30} dex applied to ALL baryons**: g_bar → g_bar 10^τ_b at fixed g_obs (so D → D 10^(−τ_b), CFG217's convention); s\* recomputed at each (point estimates, no bootstrap). The figure draws, behind each marker, a dark bar over the range of s\* for |τ_b| ≤ 0.15 (the CFG221 requirement on the shared calibration) and a light bar for |τ_b| ≤ 0.30. **For the two independent-route CRISTAL points the table also reports the gas-only shifts** (gas mass × 10^τ, τ ∈ {±0.15, ±0.30}, the CFG220 machinery). A uniform shift is not a z-dependent tilt (CFG217's axis, which moves the points relative to each other); a tilt is not in this figure. The fit-route baryon masses carry a 1-dex Gaussian prior in the authors' fits, so the ±0.30 dex band UNDERSTATES their systematic.
- **Intervals used by the caption rule:** the statistical interval is the 95% bootstrap interval; the band-widened interval at level τ is [min(lo95, s\*(+τ), s\*(−τ)), max(hi95, s\*(+τ), s\*(−τ))], a union, not a quadrature.

## 8. Reading rules and limits
- Author decompositions: RC100 f_DM and CRISTAL M_bary come from the authors' dynamical fits (halo priors, prior-anchored baryon masses), so an implied a₀ here is "the a₀ scale that makes the authors' decomposition match the kernel", not a direct measurement; earlier lanes called the a₀(z) confrontation non-diagnostic and this figure does not change that.
- The implied ratio is relative to the footing's local a₀; no z ≈ 0 point is drawn; a flat law predicts 1 only if the baryon calibration that set the local a₀ applies at every z, which is what the bands probe.
- No knob is scanned: one estimator, one B, one seed scheme, the orchestrator's band sizes. No sentence says the data favour flat, the proxy, H(z), the dark-energy law or any framework.
