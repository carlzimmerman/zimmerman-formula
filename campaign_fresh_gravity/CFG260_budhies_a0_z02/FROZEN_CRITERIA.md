## 1. Laws and what the point is compared with

FLAT: a₀(z) = a₀. RIVAL: a₀(z) = a₀ E(z), E = √(0.315 (1+z)³ + 0.685) (as CFG223), E(0.2) = 1.109 (+0.045 dex). The measured point is the sample's **s\*** (CFG223's definition): the multiplicative a₀ scale (relative to the footing's local a₀) that sets the median over the galaxies of δ_i(s) = log₁₀[D_i / ν_mono(g_bar,i / (a₀ s))] to zero, D_i = g_obs,i / g_bar,i; the implied absolute a₀ = s\* a₀(footing) does not depend on the footing. Expected s\* of each law for the same galaxies: FLAT 1, RIVAL ≈ 1.1 (per-galaxy E(z_i), through the same estimator, as CFG223's `expectations`).

## 3. The estimator (frozen exactly; every constant is a declared input, UNVERIFIED where marked)

Per galaxy of the set:
1. **Tabulated width** W = `w50_kms` (the 50 % width; `w20_kms` is not used).
2. **Frame and corrections:** W_c = (W − δ) / (1+z)^k with **δ = 28 km/s** (19 instrumental, Bottinelli-style λ₅₀ = 0.5 for both sides, plus 9 turbulent; UNVERIFIED) and **k = 1 (branch O, primary: widths in observed-frame velocity units) or k = 0 (branch R: rest-frame)**; both scored in every row. A W − δ ≤ 0 galaxy is dropped and counted.
3. **Inclination:** not in the tables (only PA). **sin i_eff = sin 60° = 0.8660** for every galaxy (the median of isotropic orientations), so V = W_c / (2 sin i_eff). A faint-end selection toward face-on and non-isotropic samples are band terms (below), not a fit.
4. **Gas:** M_gas = 1.33 M_HI (He); **H₂ ignored** (a one-sided band term).
5. **Stars (the colour recipe, UNVERIFIED):** (B−R)_rest = (B−R)_obs − (K_B − K_R), K_B = 2.75 z, K_R = 1.00 z (mag; linear in z, 0.55 and 0.20 at z = 0.2); M_R = R_obs − 5 log₁₀(D_L / 10 pc) − K_R − 0.03 (Galactic extinction); L_R = 10^(−0.4 (M_R − 4.61)); log₁₀(M\*/L_R) = −0.523 + 0.683 (B−R)_rest (Bell & de Jong 2001 form, Kroupa-like scale; no internal extinction); M\* = L_R × M/L_R. The eight `*`-flagged magnitudes (converted from SDSS) are used as given.
6. **Baryons:** M_b = M_gas + M\*. **Radius:** D_HI [kpc] = 10^(0.506 log₁₀ M_HI[M☉] − 3.293) (the HI size–mass relation, Wang+2016 form, 0.06 dex scatter; UNVERIFIED, from memory), R = D_HI/2; **all baryons are taken inside R** (an overestimate of a few per cent).
7. **Accelerations:** g_bar = G M_b / R²; g_obs = V² / R; D = g_obs / g_bar. G = 6.6743e-11, M☉ = 1.98847e30 kg, 1 kpc = 3.0857e19 m.
8. **Regime check (reported):** y = g_bar / a₀ at R; the fraction below 0.3 (the weak-field regime where the slope-4 baryonic Tully–Fisher form holds to 20 %).
9. **s\* and the interval:** CFG223's solver (vectorised bisection on log₁₀ s in [−3, 3], 64 iterations; no root flagged), the galaxy bootstrap with B = 10,000 (seed from `SeedSequence([260, n])`), percentile 68 % and 95 % of s\*.
10. **The BTFR route (reported beside it):** a₀,i = V_i⁴ / (G M_b,i) (the deep-regime limit, no radius), s\*_BTFR = median_i a₀,i / a₀; it overestimates a₀ by a factor y ν² ≈ 1.1 to 1.4 at y = 0.05 to 0.2.
11. **Bands:** (a) **baryon bands**, as every other point of the chart: s\* after a shift of ALL baryon masses (gas and stars; R fixed) by ±0.15 dex (inner) and ±0.30 dex (outer), with a no-root flag; (b) **recipe band**, one knob at a time, the two brackets in parentheses, the band half-width the quadrature sum of the half-differences in log₁₀ s\*: width correction δ (16, 40 km/s); sin i_eff (0.80, 0.92); M\* zero point (±0.25 dex on M\* only); K-correction (×0.5, ×1.5); R scale (±0.15 dex); distance scale (H₀ = 67.4 and 73.0: D_L, M_HI and M\* scale as D²); **reported singly, outside the quadrature:** H₂ (M_H2 = 0.3 M_HI added), the P2 kernel, the other frame, the alt footing, the BTFR route.
12. **Outputs:** `cfg260_results.json` and `cfg260_points.csv` in the shape of `CHART_a0z_combined_2026-09-30/chart_a0z_points.csv` (columns lane, object, gas_class, z, z_shown, no_root, s_star, a0_1e-10_m_s2, stat68/95, inner/outer bands), with extra columns for the recipe band; `gas_class` = "HI" (HI measured; H₂ neglected; stars from colours).

## 4. The pre-flight (mocks; reads NO width) — decision lines

The pre-flight uses the real non-width inputs of the primary set (z, D_L, M_HI, S_int, B, R, membership, R_proj) and MOCK widths:
- **Mock truth:** M_gas,true = 1.33 M_HI 10^τ_gas; M\*,true = M\*(recipe) 10^(τ_M\* + ε₁), ε₁ ~ N(0, 0.15) per galaxy (colour-M/L scatter); R_true = R(recipe) 10^(τ_R + ε₂), ε₂ ~ N(0, 0.06); g_bar from them; g_obs = g_bar ν(g_bar / (a₀ s_true F)), F = 1 or E(z_i); V_true = √(g_obs R) 10^ε₃, ε₃ ~ N(0, 0.03) (the BTFR scatter); random inclination, cos i uniform, **conditioned on detection**: W_obs = [(2 V_true sin i) + δ + τ_W] (1+z)^k_true + N(0, 20 km/s), accepted iff S_int / W_obs ≥ S_lim(f), S_lim(f) = f × [2e9 M☉ / (2.356e5 D_L²/(1+z))] / 150 km/s, f = 1 (field centre), 1.5, 2 (beam attenuation); the analyst then applies section 3 with nominal recipe values.
- **Shared-systematics budget** (each knob drawn once per mock, Gaussian with SD = half the half-range; the CFG219 / CFG258 lesson: the decision statistic is the TOTAL scatter over mocks, never the bootstrap alone): level A / level B half-ranges: τ_M\* 0.10 / 0.25 dex; τ_gas 0.05 / 0.15 dex; τ_W 5 / 12 km/s; τ_i (sin i_eff of the truth against 0.866) 0.02 / 0.06; τ_R 0.05 / 0.15 dex; τ_D 2 / 6 %. The frame k is a branch, not a draw. UNVERIFIED declared scenarios.
- **Quantities:** per mock the analyst's s\*; over M = 2,000 mocks: the median bias log₁₀ s\*_hat − log₁₀ s_true (SD, 5th and 95th percentiles) with no shared draw and at levels A and B, for s_true ∈ {1, 2, 0.5} (F = 1) and the RIVAL (F = E(z_i)); coverage of the within-mock bootstrap (K = 300 mocks, B = 1,000); the noiseless levers d log₁₀ s\*/d(knob) and the aligned worst case S_joint (CFG258's definition); the frame lever; the detection-selection bias for f = 1, 1.5, 2; the regime fractions.
- **PF-D1 (can this sample separate FLAT from the rival?):** POSSIBLE iff at level B both (R1) P5[RIVAL] > P95[FLAT] of the mocks' log₁₀ s\* and (R2) the noiseless RIVAL − FLAT shift Δ > S_joint,B + 2 SD_stat; otherwise NOT POSSIBLE; also reported at level A and the required total precision Δ/3.29.
- **PF-D2 (is the point drawable?):** DRAWABLE iff N(P) ≥ 60, |median bias| ≤ 0.10 dex (no shared draw, f = 1.5, s_true = 1), SD_stat ≤ 0.12 dex, and the fraction of P with y < 0.3 is ≥ 0.80; otherwise NOT DRAWABLE (the measurement is then run only as a clearly labelled lower-quality row).
- **PF-D3 (the frame):** the frame lever (−4 to −4.7 × log₁₀(1+z̄) according to the regime, noiseless, at the sample's own y) is reported; **the measurement reports both branches whatever the pre-flight says.** The truth frame of the mocks is k_true = 1 unless MUTATE=2.

## 6. Controls (those that can fail)

- **C1** the membership counts of CFG212 (94 / 8 / 30 and 24 / 3 / 8). **C2** D_L against flat ΛCDM (0.5 %). **C3** the HI-mass formula ratio × (1+z) within [0.99, 1.01] for every galaxy (the frame evidence; a failure would void the primary choice). **C4** noiseless identity: with no scatter, known inclination, no selection and nominal recipe, the estimator returns s_true to 1e-6 for F = 1; for a z-dependent F (the RIVAL) it returns a value inside [min F, max F] that equals an independent scipy `brentq` recomputation of the median condition to 1e-9. **C5** the solver equals CFG223's `implied` on a test vector to 1e-12. **C6 (blind loader)** the pre-flight's loader returns no key containing "w20" or "w50", and the pre-flight source contains those strings only inside the forbidden-column list. **C7** bootstrap coverage on mocks (HE5). **C8 (reactivity, the CFG219 / CFG258 lesson)** a planted shared baryon offset of +0.15 dex in the mock truth moves s\* by the lever times 0.15 (within 20 %), is contained by the ±0.15 dex baryon band of the same mock, and the level-B total SD of log₁₀ s\* exceeds the median within-mock bootstrap SD by a factor ≥ 1.5; a statistic that read the bootstrap alone would fail this.
- **MUTATE=1:** a planted s_true = 2 (0.30 dex) must be recovered (|median log₁₀ s\*_hat − 0.301| ≤ 0.10, no shared draw) and the pre-flight's PF-D1 must still say NOT POSSIBLE at 0.045 dex. **MUTATE=2:** the truth in the rest-frame (k_true = 0), the analyst in the observed-frame (k = 1): s\* must fall by 0.36 ± 0.06 dex (the deep-limit value is 0.32; at the sample's y the lever is 4.7). **MUTATE=3:** the analyst's sin i_eff 0.70 instead of 0.866: s\* must rise by 0.43 ± 0.07 dex (4.7 × log₁₀(0.866/0.70)). Each bites (exit 1) only if its stated response is seen.

## 7. The measurement (run once, only after the pre-flight and this file are committed and the measurement script is committed; no width is read before)

Reads `w50_kms` (and `e_w50` for a reported comparison only), builds P and the subsets, and reports for P, in order: N (and widths dropped); branch O (primary) and branch R: s\* with the 68 / 95 % bootstrap intervals, the implied a₀, the regime fractions, the baryon bands, the recipe band (with each knob's row), the singly-reported rows; pulls against FLAT and the RIVAL (statistics only, and widened by the recipe band in quadrature and by the ±0.15 dex baryon band), the CFG223-style counts; then S1 to S8 as separate rows. **The first run is the result; nothing is re-run to change it.** Reading rule (no verdict words): the numbers as printed; "consistent with" is released only if the frozen flags (in the 95 % interval; in the recipe-widened interval) say so for that law, as CFG223's caption rule did.

## 8. What this does NOT cover

No per-galaxy inclination (only a statistical correction); no radius-resolved velocity (a global profile width at an HI-size radius); no H₂; the colour M/L and K-corrections are a declared recipe, not a fit; the HI size–mass relation is from memory; the cluster constants are the data chat's summariser reads (UNVERIFIED); the environment (gas content in clusters, stripping beyond 2 Mpc, the external field on the primary set) is a systematic left in, not modelled; no local same-pipeline control sample; the width frame is unresolved in the repository. The point therefore says what this declared recipe gives, with the bands drawn, and no more.

## Addendum 1 (2026-10-01, before any pre-flight script or number exists; committed with the core module)

A smoke test of the core module (`cfg260_core.py`, non-width inputs only) built noiseless mock widths with the formula of section 4 and returned log₁₀ s\* = +0.034 instead of 0. **Cause: section 4's mock width W = [(2 V sin i) + δ + τ_W](1+z)^k_true applies the frame factor to δ, while the section 3 estimator (W_c = (W − δ)/(1+z)^k) subtracts δ before dividing; the nominal analyst is therefore biased by δ z ≈ 6 km/s by construction.** The estimator is unchanged (δ is a broadening in the units of the tabulated width). **The mock-truth width is corrected to W_obs = 2 V_true sin i (1 + τ_i) (1+z)^k_true + δ + τ_W + N(0, 20 km/s)**, with τ_i a multiplicative factor on the true sin i (the truth-side counterpart of the analyst's sin i_eff bracket) and τ_W in km/s; the detection test uses W before the noise. The distance-scale knob τ_D is a fractional error: the true M_HI and M\* are the tabulated ones × (1 + τ_D)² and R follows the true M_HI (the analyst uses the table). Nothing else in the criteria changes; HE3 and C4 are unaffected in their wording (C4's identity is exactly what caught this).
