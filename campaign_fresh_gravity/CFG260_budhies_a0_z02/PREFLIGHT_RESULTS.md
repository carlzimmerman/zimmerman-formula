# CFG260 stage 1 — the blind pre-flight: BUDHIES widths cannot separate FLAT from a₀ ∝ H(z), and a clean a₀ point is NOT DRAWABLE from them (the survey's peak-flux limit pins the implied a₀)

> **κ = ½ FITTED. Mocks only; no HI line width was read (`w20`, `w50` and their errors were never displayed or computed on). A forecast, not a measurement. No sentence says the data favour a law.**
> Criteria `FROZEN_CRITERIA.md` (**00f20121d**) + Addendum 1 (**2b8a8e4c7**) + Addendum 2 (**b6cd28eb8**, after the FIRST run). Scripts: `cfg260_core.py`, `cfg260_preflight_v1.py` (the first run, kept), `cfg260_preflight.py` (v2: `CFG260_SET=PC|PC125|PC15|P`, `MUTATE=1|2|3`).

## Bottom line
1. **Separating FLAT from a₀ ∝ H(z) at z ≈ 0.2: NOT POSSIBLE** in every set. The rival's expected shift is **+0.043 dex** (s\* = 1.105) against a total precision of 0.13–0.32 dex statistical alone (N = 32 to 7), an aligned systematic worst case of **0.22 dex (level A) to 0.63 dex (level B)**, and, decisively, the selection effect below. (R2 in the code used the f = 1.25 mock difference, +0.003, not the noiseless +0.043; against S_joint,B = 0.63 the verdict is the same.)
2. **A clean implied-a₀ point is NOT DRAWABLE.** The survey's mass limit is a PEAK-flux limit, so near the limit a galaxy is detected only if it is nearly face-on; with no inclinations (the tables carry only position angles) the unknown-inclination correction (sin i = 0.866) then fails, always in the same direction: **s\* is biased LOW, and at the strongest selection it does not depend on the true a₀ at all.** The frozen primary set P (N = 110) is selection-dominated even for the field-centre threshold once the WSRT primary beam is included (bias −1.24 dex at f = 1; f is the factor on the position-dependent threshold). Restricting to galaxies detectable at every inclination (Addendum 2's PC, completeness ratio c ≥ 1, N = 32) is unbiased only if the survey is as sensitive as the field-centre limit scaled by the beam: bias **−0.07 (f = 1), −0.35 (f = 1.25), −0.82 (f = 1.5)**. The size of f is not known from the repository.

5. **The regime is deep:** the fraction of PC with y = g_bar/a₀ < 0.3 at R_HI is 1.00 (median y 0.068; P-all 0.93); PC is gas-richer than P-all (M\*/M_gas median 1.9 against 2.8).

## What this means for the measurement (frozen protocol, section 7 and Addendum 2)
The measurement is run once, after this stage and its script are committed, **as a clearly labelled lower-quality row**: primary PC, both frame branches, the ladder P-all / PC / PC125 / PC15 and S1 to S8 beside it, the mock biases above as the interpretive table. **The selection bias is one-directional (low): a measured s\* is, within the other systematics, a LOWER bound on the implied a₀**, and a plateau of s\* with the completeness threshold would be the least biased value. Reported after the widths are read, as diagnostics only (no decision): the empirical detection ratio S_int / (W S_thr) of every galaxy, whose lower envelope estimates f, and the measured-to-predicted edge-on width ratio.
