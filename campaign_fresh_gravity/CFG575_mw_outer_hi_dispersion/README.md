# CFG575: the outer Milky Way HI dispersion. NO QUALIFYING MEASUREMENT; CFG574 stays NOT DIAGNOSTIC

- **Criteria:** `FROZEN_CRITERIA.md`, committed alone before any value was read (c4a967741). (owner chat 10-09)
- **Requirements from CFG574:** round rule (RM) 6.9, phantom disc (PD) 9.1, Newtonian baryons alone 5.8 km/s.

## What the literature has (read from source TeX, arXiv:0704.3925, Kalberla, Dedes, Kerp & Haud 2007)
- No published HI velocity dispersion MEASURED at R ≈ 10–30 kpc was found. The values used for the outer
  disc are assumptions carried out from the solar neighbourhood or the inner Galaxy:
  - Kalberla et al. 2007: a two-phase CNM/WNM model plus magnetic support (factor ≈ 1.15), "close to a
    single-component model with an effective constant velocity dispersion of 8.3 km/s", held constant with R
    by assumption ("the turbulent velocity dispersion in the ISM appears to be independent of distance").
  - They quote the "conventional approach" as single-phase σ ≈ 9.2 km/s (Olling & Merrifield 2000/2001).
  - They state a constant σ is hard to justify and that matching the flaring could need σ changes of 25–40%
    at R ≈ 17 and 35 kpc.
- Kalberla & Dedes 2008: σ = 74 km/s for the turbulent extra-planar/outskirts phase (R ≳ 40 kpc), not the disc.

## Verdict
**NO QUALIFYING MEASUREMENT.** CFG574 remains NOT DIAGNOSTIC. The assumed values sit between the two
requirements or on the PD side; they are not evidence either way, because they were never measured at
10–30 kpc. Never cite this lane as a PD win.
