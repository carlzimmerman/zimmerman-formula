## 5. Definitions frozen (the README/criteria do not fully fix these; declared before any number)

1. **Class assignment rule (scope item 1), implemented as a decision table on per-(galaxy, route) records.** Universe U: every galaxy in any table on disk with 2 <= z <= 5.7 (CRISTAL reaches 5.69: CFG227 includes it; I report U for z <= 5 and for z <= 5.7 separately) that has (a) a tabulated velocity V (single characteristic or ring) and (b) at least one baryon mass column; the pre-filter counts of every other set (no gas: SINS 35, ZFIRE 44, Danhaive 33, Jones 3; gas only, no M*: Roman-Oliveira 4) are listed as "no class" with the reason. Attributes: K1 = SED stellar mass on disk not obtained from the dynamical fit; K2 = V at a stated radius or a curve with radii on disk (numbers, not a description); K3 = number of independent gas-mass tracers with stated conversions; K4 = conversion route in {jointly optimised per galaxy (Dunne+22 opt_*), stated per source, single local prescription}; K5 = baryon normalisation derived from or prior-anchored to the dynamical fit. **Order of rules:** D if K5 (fitted with the dynamics or a scaling prior; includes RC100, the CRISTAL fit route, Lelli+23 free mass models); else M if K1 and K2 and K3 >= 2 and K4 = jointly optimised; else S if K3 = 1, a conversion stated and justified for that sample, and not K5; else L if K3 = 1 with a local-calibration prescription applied as published; else "no class" (stars-only, gas luminosity without conversion, or gas absent). **Cross-match for M (frozen):** every z >= 2 row of the four Dunne+22 optimisation tables and of the master table (Name, OName, SimbadName, RA, Dec), ACE, the four singles, Kirkpatrick+19 and the SMG/SPT tables is matched to every kinematic-table row by (i) normalised name (strip spaces, hyphens, case, leading catalogue prefixes) and (ii) position within 2.0 arcsec and |dz| < 0.02 where both tables carry RA, Dec (Dunne master, ALPAKA sample, CRISTAL sample, SINS table 1); a match counts only if K1, K2 also hold. **Named readings tried beside the literal one (each reports N per class):** R0 literal; R1 S1 split by the TeX footnotes (049.1, 075.1 -> "gas from another paper", 122.1 likewise); R2 S1 as D (alpha_CO justified by the paper's dynamics); R3 CRISTAL counted as galaxies (6 + 6 fit-only) instead of rows; R4 window z <= 5 (CRISTAL class L reduces to one disc, CRISTAL-11 at 4.44); R5 M without the K2 requirement (Dunne+22 galaxies at z >= 2 with optimised conversions: counted, no points).
2. **Point:** g_obs = V^2/r; r = 2 r_e (kpc) for S1 with D_A from flat LCDM (H0 67.4, Om 0.315, quadrature); V = `vcirc_2re_kms`; log10 error 2 x 0.4343 sigma_V/V (mean of the asymmetric errors).
3. **Baryon model (S1, as frozen):** M* and M_gas in ONE thin exponential disc, R_d = R_e/1.678 (1.67835), Freeman: V^2(r) = (2GM/R_d) y^2 [I0(y)K0(y) - I1(y)K1(y)], y = r/(2 R_d); g_bar = V^2/r (radial, in-plane). **Algebra first:** at r = 2 R_e, y = 1.678 is the same for every source, so g_bar = c0 G M_bar/R_e^2 with c0 a universal number, and D = g_obs/g_bar = V^2 R_e/(2 c0 G M_bar). Consequences: D scales linearly with R_e at fixed V and M_bar; as 1/sin^2 i through V = V_obs/sin i; inversely with M_bar; and d log g_bar/d log alpha_CO = gas share of g_bar.
4. **Laws:** F_flat = 1; F_H = E(z); F_proxy and F_MDEC as section 1.3. nu = nu_mono (primary), P2, canonical and alt footings (P2 and alt reported beside, never substituted).
5. **Statistic:** median delta per group; 95% CI = 2.5/97.5 percentiles of 10,000 bootstrap medians, `numpy.random.default_rng`, seed 237 (stability 238 and 100,000 draws). Class labels by whether 0 is in the CI are NOT used by CFG227 (descriptive); I report the CI only.
6. **Gas shift:** g_bar(tau) = g_* + g_gas 10^tau (gas mass moved by tau dex, stars fixed). Inner band tau = +-0.213, outer tau = +-0.671 for S and L (+-0.03, +-0.093 for M). **The band is treated as a half-width on the gas mass, asymmetric in its effect on delta** (stars-only floor on the minus side).
