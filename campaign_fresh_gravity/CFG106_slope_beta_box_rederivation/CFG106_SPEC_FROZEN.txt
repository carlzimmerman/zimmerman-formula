MODEL
  Pipeline (reused from CFG76's independently written estimator, disclosed): h50-style GC dispersion bins from sluggs_forbes2017_*.tsv (3-sigma-clipped MLE, equal-number bins,
  outer bins R > max(1 R_e, 2 kpc)), ATLAS3D JAM calibration (half the stars inside r_1/2, total model mass there = M_JAM/2), the rule = law + collapse debris (conservation form,
  x_e = 0.40, Mandelbaum red masses).  Configuration = CFG76's reproduction of CFG55 to 2e-6: plain RAR kernel nu = 1/(1-exp(-sqrt y)) everywhere, M_JAM ~ D (dist_mode 'dyn'),
  Hernquist stars a_h = R_e/1.8153, hot gas omitted.  Both footings: a0 = 9.36e-11 (canonical), 1.13e-10 (alt).  Sample: 16 galaxies (h50-faithful 19 intersect ATLAS3D qual >= 1).
  NEW CODE (mine, not in CFG76): the Jeans solver.
    Spherical Jeans with tracer nu(r) = r^-gamma and constant beta:  d(nu s_r^2)/dr + 2 beta nu s_r^2 / r = -nu g,
      => s_r^2(r) = r^(gamma-2beta) INT_r^inf r'^(2beta-gamma) g(r') dr'.
    LOS projection: s_los^2(R) = INT_R^inf (1 - beta R^2/r^2) nu s_r^2 r dr/sqrt(r^2-R^2) / INT_R^inf nu r dr/sqrt(r^2-R^2)  (the Binney-Mamon kernel, constant beta),
      evaluated at r = R cosh u by composite Gauss-Legendre (segments u in [0,1],[1,3],[3,8],[8,22], 24 nodes each).
    Radial integral: log-r grid (4000 points, 0.03 - 1e5 kpc), trapezoid in ln r, PLUS an analytic tail beyond the grid edge (g -> C/r, C = g r at the edge:
      tail = C R^(2beta-gamma)/(gamma-2beta)); s_r^2 beyond the grid = its edge value (the exact deep-MOND asymptote).
    Aperture convention: sigma_los evaluated at the median projected radius of each equal-number bin (point aperture, as in the h50/CFG76 baseline); no bin averaging;
      predictions and bins are in kpc at the SLUGGS distance.
  Slopes: gamma_i = clip(-0.63 log10 M*_SLUGGS,i + 9.81, 2, 4) (Alabi+2017 relation, as CFG111), then gamma_i + delta with NO re-clip (shifted values stay in [2.09, 3.83], inside 2..4).
  Anisotropy: one constant beta for every GC of every galaxy.
  BOX: delta in {-0.4,-0.2,0,+0.2,+0.4}  x  beta in {-0.5,-0.25,0,+0.25,+0.5}; 25 cells per footing.
  STATISTIC: o_i = mean over the outer bins of log10(sigma_obs/sigma_pred) (galaxy i); m = mean_i o_i; err = std(o_i, ddof=1)/sqrt(N); z = m/err (N=16 law; rule N = number of galaxies
    whose rule calibration has a root, expected 16).  'Above 2 sigma' = z > 2 strictly; 'rule fits' = |z| < 2 strictly.  Error = galaxy-to-galaxy SEM, ignores bin errors (as CFG55).
