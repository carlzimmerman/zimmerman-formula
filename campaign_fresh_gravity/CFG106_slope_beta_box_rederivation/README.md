After the runs, all 100 cell means and errors (law and rule, both footings) were compared with CFG114's results JSON: the largest differences are 1.7e-4 dex in a mean, 3e-5 in an error and 0.0073 in z. Sources of the small differences: CFG114's solver uses h50's Jeans code, which truncates the LOS integral at u = 6 and drops the radial tail beyond 3e4 kpc; at the corner (gamma about 2.1-3, beta = +0.5) this biases sigma_pred by up to 0.57% in a bin (measured against a verbatim copy of that solver, R8), an effect of at most 1.7e-4 dex on the box mean; SLUGGS population masses at the corner give -0.24 sigma in CFG114 and -0.25 sigma here (rounding-level, not gated).

**The solver (new code, not from CFG76 or CFG114).** Jeans equation for a tracer nu = r^-gamma with constant beta: sigma_r^2(r) = r^(gamma - 2 beta) int_r^inf r'^(2 beta - gamma) g dr'; radial integral on a log-r grid of 4000 points from 0.03 to 1e5 kpc, trapezoid in ln r, with an analytic tail beyond the grid edge (g -> C/r, so the tail equals C r_edge^(2 beta - gamma)/(gamma - 2 beta)) and sigma_r^2 held at its edge value (the deep-MOND asymptote). Line of sight: sigma_los^2(R) = int (1 - beta R^2/r^2) nu sigma_r^2 r dr/sqrt(r^2 - R^2) divided by int nu r dr/sqrt(r^2 - R^2), with r = R cosh u and composite Gauss-Legendre on segments [0, 1], [1, 3], [3, 8], [8, 22], 24 nodes each (reaching r about 1e9 R, sigma_r^2 held constant beyond the grid). Aperture: a point aperture at each equal-number bin's median projected radius at the SLUGGS distance; the offset is the mean of log10(sigma_obs/sigma_pred) over the outer bins (R > max(R_e, 2 kpc)), then over galaxies; z = mean/(sd with ddof = 1/sqrt(16)). Closed-form checks at four (gamma, beta) pairs (2.5, -0.5), (3, 0), (2.09, 0.5), (3.83, 0.25): Newtonian point mass sigma_r^2 = GM/((gamma + 1 - 2 beta) r) to 6.7e-5; a flat rotation curve sigma_r^2 = v^2/(gamma - 2 beta) and sigma_los^2 = v^2 (gamma - beta (gamma - 1))/(gamma (gamma - 2 beta)) to 1.4e-5 and 7.2e-6; the point-mass sigma_los(R) from Gamma functions to 1.3e-5; beta = 0 constant sigma_r gives sigma_los = sigma_r, and the projection denominator matches its Gamma-function form to 5e-11; doubling the grid to 8000 points and the nodes to 48 moves every box mean by at most 3.6e-6 dex. **Reused from CFG76:** the GC-bin estimator, the JAM calibration and the rule debris machinery (plain RAR kernel and M_JAM proportional to D, which CFG76 showed reproduces CFG55 to 2e-6); the slopes are gamma_i = clip(-0.63 log M*_SLUGGS + 9.81, 2, 4) (no re-clip needed: shifted values stay within 2.09-3.83). So CFG76's data-side pipeline and CFG114's solver are not fully independent of each other here: only the Jeans/anisotropy solver is new.

**What the agent would attack** (numbers from the frozen reported rows; rows marked post hoc were not computed by the script). (1) **The slopes are not measurements:** CFG111's Table 1 values are the Alabi relation evaluated at the SLUGGS masses and the box is a coherent shift of that relation; the coefficients are -0.63 +/- 0.17 and 9.81 +/- 1.94 and their correlation is not on disk; the 1 sigma of the 16-galaxy mean slope is 0.27 for rho = -0.99, 0.86 for rho = -0.9 and 2.7 for rho = 0, so +-0.4 is 1.5, 0.5 or 0.1 sigma; with 1000 draws of (a, b) the fraction with law z < 2 is 4.6% / 21% (canonical, beta = 0 / +0.5) at rho = -0.99 and about 43-47% at rho = 0, so the law's 2 sigma failure is only as firm as the unknown correlation; independent per-galaxy scatter around the relation does not help the law (delta_i ~ N(0, 0.4^2), beta_i uniform in +-0.5: 0% canonical and 0.8% alt with z < 2), so the corner needs a coherent, relation-level offset. (2) beta = +0.5 for every GC at every radius is more radial than the measured systems by CFG113/114's own account (literature not checked). (3) **The corner is a mean cancellation, not a fit:** at the canonical corner 6 galaxies are still under-predicted by more than 0.05 dex (M87 +0.137, NGC 7457 +0.120, NGC 5846 +0.087, NGC 4365 +0.076, NGC 4278 +0.074, NGC 4374 +0.067) and four over-predicted by more than 0.05 dex (NGC 4494 -0.174, NGC 2768 -0.093, NGC 4459 -0.089, NGC 3607 -0.080); the scatter barely changes (sd 0.086 against 0.091 at the published cell); nulling each galaxy alone at beta = +0.5 needs a shift inside +-0.4 for only 5 of 16 galaxies (7 on alt); M87 would need gamma about 1.6 and NGC 4365, 4374, 5846 about 1.85-1.9, below the 2-4 validity range of the relation itself. (4) **Two tuned parameters on the box boundary:** z^2 falls from 13.0 to 0.34 canonical (10.4 to 0.01 alt); the law's zero lies outside the box in every beta row scanned except beta = 0.25 (delta* = -0.69) and 0.5 (delta* = -0.47); at beta = 0 the zero needs delta* = -0.96 (CFG76's gamma = 1.83); at delta = 0 no beta below 0.995 nulls it; the rule's zero sits inside the box (delta* between -0.07 at beta = +0.5 and -0.32 at beta = 0); only 2 of 25 canonical cells (4 of 25 alt) are below 2 sigma, all at the shallow-slope, radial-orbit end. (5) The error model (post hoc arithmetic on the printed tables): the SEM ignores bin errors and common-mode terms; inflating the error by 20% takes the law's count of cells above 2 sigma to about 21 canonical and 19 alt, so '23/25' is a property of that SEM. (6) Calibration convention: the Hernquist enclosed fraction instead of 1/2 makes the corner law +0.17 sigma canonical (-0.34 alt) and the rule at the corner -2.87 (-3.02); with SLUGGS's own masses the corner law is -0.25 (-0.96) and the rule -3.79 (-4.13); the law's z > 2 count falls to 17/25 canonical and 10/25 alt with SLUGGS masses but is 21/25 and 19/25 with the Hernquist fraction. (7) **Framing:** a statement about a box edge; the rule over-predicts at the same corner (-2.59 sigma), so it does not favour either model.
