But the gas is class S/L, whose bands are ±0.213 (inner) and ±0.671 dex (outer; the record's conversion-drift budget, `cfg224b_conversion_drift_results.json`), and the ALPINE inclination errors are 4° to 31°. That is what the class-M programme (CFG229) targets; here the gas is class S/L.

## 9. What would make a z ≈ 4 to 5 ALMA test informative (post hoc, arithmetic)
Baryons known to about 0.10 dex or better (Q3), which for the gas means a per-galaxy optimised multi-tracer mass with the class-M band (0.03 / 0.093: CFG229's route) and stellar masses with a measured error; inclinations known to 5° or better (here 4° to 31°); a rotation curve resolved to beyond 2 R_e with σ(R) measured (R_t is unconstrained at 1″ beams; the pressure term decides the sign of every δ); the intrinsic scatter below about 0.05 dex; and radii at which y is 1 to 3 (the ALPINE six already sit there at 2 R_e, SPT0418-47 at y ≈ 10 needs 1.8 × its outer radius). Higher-resolution ALMA of the brighter ALPINE rotators (DC494057, DC552206, VC5110377875 at S/N 6.7 to 10.6) is the natural next step; the data chat holds the availability.

## Limits
One radius per galaxy and one thin exponential disc for stars and gas (the sizes differ in reality); a constant-σ pressure term; the ALPINE [CII] maps are faint (S/N 4 to 10.6) and the flux estimator is biased high at low S/N (C2); α_[CII] = 30 has a ±0.3 dex scatter inside the class S band and the dust route (α_850, T_d) is a bracket of a factor of about 4; the SPT0418-47 curve and lens model are the authors' (the lens systematic is not quantified by them); HI is not separated (the [CII] and dust masses are total gas); the floor (D ≥ 1) presumes ν ≥ 1 and a thin-disc g_bar; the pooled median rule over six or seven galaxies returns discrete values (the bootstrap quantiles of ALL7 coincide with individual galaxies' s\*).
