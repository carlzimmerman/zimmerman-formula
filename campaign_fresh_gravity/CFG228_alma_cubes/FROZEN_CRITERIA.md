# CFG228 — FROZEN CRITERIA: the ALMA cubes (six ALPINE [CII] rotators and SPT0418-47): two-tracer gas ([CII] line and dust continuum), rotation from the cubes or the authors' curve, the implied a₀ with a full error budget, and δ against FLAT / PROXY / H(z) / M-DEC

> **Compilation of ALMA products with literature stellar masses; conversions as published (from memory where marked); calibration-limited; NOT a direct a₀ measurement; not a detection; not a verdict. ΛCDM has no a₀: the proxy is an effective-a₀ PROXY. κ = ½ is FITTED. No sentence of this lane says the data favour a law.** A two-tracer agreement between [CII] and dust is a check on two UN-optimised conversions that were calibrated on local and CO-anchored samples; it does not certify the absolute gas scale (a factor common to both is invisible, CFG237).

Written 2026-09-30 and committed BEFORE any flux, size, velocity, g_bar, g_obs, D, δ or implied a₀ of this lane is computed from the ALMA products. Requested by the orchestrator (proposal CFG228) with the implied-a₀ block added on the owner's question "can we calculate a₀ from the high-z data". Full rigor protocol (frozen criteria first; velocity-free stage first; a blind pre-flight committed BEFORE any velocity is extracted; hand estimates frozen below; controls that can fail; MUTATE; first runs kept; wording restrictions). Conventions, function and control names follow CFG229 (`FROZEN_CRITERIA.md` 5c131c037 + Addendum 1 7e3b14d20), whose machinery is reused read-only.

## 2. Stage 1 — velocity-free measurements (`cfg228_stage1.py`; outputs committed BEFORE the pre-flight)
**ALPINE, per galaxy (algorithms fixed here):**
- **Beam and pixel:** from the header (BMAJ, BMIN in degrees, pixel |CDELT1|); Ω_beam = π/(4 ln 2) · BMAJ · BMIN; N_beam = Ω_beam/Ω_pix. The PI primary-beam response (`*.flux.fits.gz`) divides every image pixelwise before summation.
- **Centre:** the peak pixel of the PI moment-0 map (`CIImom0.image`, Jy/beam km/s) after a 3-pixel boxcar smoothing, within 3″ of the map centre.
- **[CII] flux S_CII (Jy km/s):** the growth curve of the pb-corrected moment-0 map in circular apertures (radius 0.5″ to 4.0″ in 0.25″ steps, sum divided by N_beam); adopted flux = the value at the smallest radius that reaches 95% of the curve's maximum inside 4″; **noise** = the standard deviation of the same aperture placed at 200 random positions outside 6″ from the centre (inside the pb > 0.5 region); the half-light radius R_half = the radius enclosing 50% of the adopted flux. **Cross-check (reported, not substituted):** the same aperture integrated over the channels around the line in our own cube (contiguous channels above 2σ around the integrated-spectrum peak).
- **L_[CII] (L☉) = 1.04 × 10⁻³ S_CII ν_obs D_L²** (S in Jy km/s, ν_obs in GHz = RESTFRQ, D_L in Mpc; Solomon+92 form; `from memory`). **M_gas,[CII] = α_[CII] L_[CII], α_[CII] = 30 M☉/L☉ (Zanella+18's mean, 0.3 dex scatter; the value and its ±3σ range 3.8 to 238 are quoted in Rizzo+20's Methods, on disk)**, taken to include helium; class S.
- **Continuum:** the PI continuum image (`continuum.image`, Jy/beam, collapsed over the line-free channels of the four spectral windows; effective frequency = the header's CRVAL3), pb-corrected; aperture flux S_ν in the SAME aperture as the [CII] flux, noise by the same 200 random apertures; **detected if the aperture S/N ≥ 3**, otherwise a 3σ upper limit. Rest wavelength 155 to 184 μm.
- **Dust-based gas (class L), a bracket, not a measurement:** modified black body with β = 1.8, **T_d = 35 K primary, 25 K and 45 K beside**, the CMB contrast correction at the observed rest-frame frequency (T_CMB = 2.725 (1 + z)), extrapolated to rest 850 μm; **M_gas,dust = L_850/α_850 with α_850 = 6.9 × 10¹² W Hz⁻¹ M☉⁻¹ (Dunne+22 Table 7, log L_IR > 11, including helium; the record's CFG224 tabulation)**; the luminosity is matched to the L_IR > 11 bin, L_IR ≈ SFR/1.48 × 10⁻¹⁰ with the corpus SFR. The T_d bracket (factor ~4) and the ACE offset are the class-L band (§4).
- **d_AB = log₁₀(M_gas,[CII]/M_gas,dust)** per galaxy with both tracers detected; over those galaxies the mean μ_d, the SD, SE = SD/√N and **K_AB = √(SE² + (|μ_d|/2)²)** (the CFG224 definition). **Class rule (fixed here): "M2" (two-tracer agreement) iff N_both ≥ 5 and K_AB ≤ 0.10 and every |d_i − μ_d| ≤ 2 max(SD, 0.10); its gas = the log-mean of the two tracers, inner band max(K_AB, 0.05) and outer band ±0.21. Otherwise the report says "tracer disagreement, class downgraded": the gas is the log-mean of the two (class S/L) where both exist, the [CII] mass (class S) otherwise, with the class S/L bands (inner ±0.213, outer ±0.671 dex, from `cfg224b_conversion_drift_results.json`); both single-tracer masses are reported beside.** The class-M band (0.03 / 0.093) is NEVER used here (no per-galaxy optimised conversion exists for [CII]).
- **Size (baryon geometry):** R_e,[CII] = √(R_half² − (½ √(BMAJ BMIN))²) when R_half exceeds the beam HWHM, else the unresolved value ½ × the beam HWHM (flagged); the measurement radius is R_out = 2 R_e,[CII] (declared; 1.5 and 3 R_e beside).
- **Stellar mass:** the corpus `log_mstar_msun` (Faisst+20 SED; route stated in the corpus note; **UNVERIFIED, no errors tabulated**); bands inner ±0.20, outer ±0.30 dex (declared).

**SPT0418-47 (the authors' lens model; NO lens modelling here):**
- **Rotation:** Rizzo+20's source-plane curve V_rot(R) = V_t (1 + R_t/R)^β/[1 + (R_t/R)^ξ]^{1/ξ} with V_t = 245.1 ± 0.8 km/s, R_t = 0.14 ± 0.03 kpc, β = 0.80 ± 0.02, ξ = 2.0 ± 0.4, i = 54 ± 2°, σ₀ = 58.1 ± 1.8 km/s with σ(R) exponential of scale R₀ = 1.7 ± 0.1 kpc (Extended Data Table 1); the asymmetric-drift term is below 1% (their text). Errors by Monte Carlo over the quoted parameter errors; **the lens-model systematic is not quantified by the authors and is flagged.** R_out = 4 R_gas = 3.6 kpc with R_gas = 0.9 kpc (declared a priori as the regime y ~ 3 to 6; 2 R_gas and 5 R_gas beside).
- **Gas:** [CII] — the authors' de-lensed L_[CII] = 1.8 × 10⁹ L☉ with α_[CII] = 30 (class S); dust — the image-plane continuum flux of the collapsed 357.7 GHz image (`cont.I.pbcor`, rest 160.4 μm) in a circular aperture of 2.2″ around the ring centre, noise by 200 random apertures in 4 to 10″, divided by the authors' μ = 32.3 ± 2.5, then the same T_d / β / α_850 bracket as above (class L). **The image-plane [CII] flux from spw25 + spw27 (aperture 2.2″; the line straddles the two windows) divided by the authors' L_[CII] gives an implied [CII] magnification, reported beside the dust μ as a check on differential lensing.** d_AB and the class rule as above (N = 1: no class M2).
- **Stellar mass:** the De Breuck+19 SED value 9.5 ± 3.0 × 10⁹ M☉ as QUOTED in Rizzo+20's Methods (independent of the dynamics; route not verified by reading De Breuck+19), primary; Rizzo+20's own 1.2 × 10¹⁰ (from the curve decomposition: circular) beside; bands inner ±0.14 (the quoted 1σ), outer ±0.30.
- **Baryon geometry:** stars = a spherical point mass (Sérsic R_e,* = 0.22 kpc ≪ R_out), gas = a thin exponential disc with R_d = R_gas = 0.9 kpc.

## 3. Baryon model, kinematics and g_obs (ALPINE)
**Baryons:** M* and M_gas in ONE thin exponential disc, R_d = R_e,[CII]/1.678, g_bar = the Freeman disc force at R_out (CFG229's `disc_v2`); variants: stars at 0.5 R_e,gas (stellar discs are more compact), R_e × 1.5 and ÷ 1.5, the spherical enclosed-mass form.
**Rotation, Stage 3 (`cfg228_stage3.py`; run ONLY after the pre-flight is committed):** per galaxy a thin-disc forward model of the sub-cube (48 × 48 pixels, channels within ±700 km/s of the integrated-spectrum peak): exponential [CII] surface brightness with R_d,c = R_e,[CII]/1.678 fixed from Stage 1 and the total flux a free scale; rotation curve **V(R) = (2/π) V_a arctan(R/R_t)** (V_a free, R_t free in [0.2, 3] kpc); constant σ₀ free; systemic velocity and PA free; **inclination fixed at the corpus `inc_kin_deg` and marginalised by Monte Carlo with its corpus error**; each channel model convolved with the header's Gaussian beam (elliptical, PA) through FFT; χ² over the voxels inside the mask (pixels with moment-0 S/N > 2, channels within ±2 FWHM of the systemic velocity), per-voxel noise from the line-free channels of the same cube, inflated by √N_beam for the beam-correlated noise; `scipy.optimize.least_squares` with bounds; parameter errors from the inflated (JᵀJ)⁻¹ and a 50-draw inclination Monte Carlo; **V_rot(R_out)** and its error; **g_obs = V_c²/R_out with V_c² = V_rot² + α(R_out) σ₀², α(R) = 2 R/R_d (the asymmetric-drift term of an exponential isothermal disc) primary; α = 0 and α = 3.36 beside.** A fit that fails or whose V_a sits on a bound is reported as UNCONSTRAINED (no g_obs, counted, excluded from pooled statistics). **Also reported beside (literature check, not a control):** the published Jones+21 outer-ring V_rot and its difference from ours.

## 4. Laws, kernels, footings, δ, the floor, the implied a₀ (CFG229 conventions)
FLAT, PROXY (Z1 `lcdm_native`), H(z) (E(z), Ω_m = 0.315), M-DEC (√ρ_DE, (w₀, w_a) = (−0.838, −0.62), the record's values); **δ_L = log₁₀[g_obs/(g_bar ν_mono(g_bar/(a₀ F_L(z))))]**, ν_mono, canonical a₀ = 9.3603e-11, alt 1.1312e-10 and P2 beside. **Newtonian floor** (D < 1 = a baryon-model inconsistency for ANY of these laws): PASS / BAND-DEPENDENT / ROBUST FAIL as CFG229 §10, over the declared bands and variants. **Implied a₀:** s\* = the root of median_i log₁₀[D_i/ν_mono(g_bar,i/(a₀ s))] = 0 (`a0implied.implied`, CFG223's estimator, verbatim), a₀,implied = s\* × 9.3603e-11 m/s², the ratio to the alt footing s\*/1.2085, NO ROOT where D ≤ 1; per galaxy and pooled (ALPINE six; all seven; the floor-PASS subset). **Error budget, each line separate:** (i) statistical — per galaxy a 10,000-draw Monte Carlo over the flux errors (→ gas), the velocity fit error, the inclination, and the random stellar error (inner band); pooled, the galaxy bootstrap of CFG223 (seed 228); (ii) gas class band (S/L ±0.213 inner / ±0.671 outer, or M2's if the rule passes); (iii) M\* band (inner ±0.20, outer ±0.30; SPT0418 inner ±0.14); (iv) joint corners; (v) inclination (corpus error; SPT0418 ±2°); (vi) pressure α(R) / 0 / 3.36; (vii) geometry (R_e × 1.5, ÷ 1.5, stars at 0.5 R_e,gas, spherical), T_d 25 and 45 K, the no-He run, the own-cube [CII] flux, R_out at 1.5 and 3 R_e (SPT0418: 2 and 5 R_gas), the P2 kernel; (viii) the kernel lever λ = d log₁₀ s\*/d(baryon dex) at each galaxy's own (g_obs, g_bar) with the kernel slope. **UNINFORMATIVE if (a) no root, or (b) the joint OUTER band moves log₁₀ s\* by more than 1 dex or removes the root, or (c) |λ| > 10** (the rule of CFG229 A3). The z ≈ 0 reference is QUOTED: both footings and the record's 1.37e-10 (ν_mono at Υ = 0.50, degenerate with Υ), no SPARC analysis. **The statistical and the systematic bars are separate styles in the figure and the README.**

## 5. The PRE-FLIGHT (`cfg228_preflight.py`; committed BEFORE `cfg228_stage3.py` exists; blind to every velocity)
It reads ONLY the static file written by Stage 1 (z, R_out, R_e, stellar and gas masses with their bands, inclination and its corpus error, the published velocity ERRORS as the expected statistical precision: Jones+21's outer-ring `e_Vrot` for ALPINE, Rizzo+20's parameter errors for SPT0418, and σ_int = 0.15 dex declared); the extraction's precision is taken from the literature's, not assumed better. Mocks on the ACTUAL galaxies (1,000 per set and truth, seed 228; 20,000 marginalised): truth T ∈ {FLAT, H(z), PROXY}, g_obs,true = g_bar ν(g_bar/(a₀ F_T)); random baryon errors = the inner bands, systematics = the outer bands at the joint corners (S_max) and gas-only; **the rule of CFG229 §9: a separation is POSSIBLE only if signal (median over mocks) > S_max + 2 SD; the same at the gas band alone, at the inner band and marginalised are printed.** Sets: each galaxy, ALPINE6, ALL7. **Implied-a₀ block** (blind): per galaxy and set under truth FLAT the median and 16 to 84% range of log₁₀ s\* (stat-only and with σ_int), the no-root fraction, the lever at the nominal g_bar, the outer-band shifts, the A3 verdict, the expected s\* under each law. Post hoc (labelled): the radius at which y = 3. **Gate:** no README sentence may say a law is separated, preferred or disfavoured unless the pre-flight rule said POSSIBLE for the set discussed.

## 7. Controls (all reported; a failure is reported plainly; none is an identity)
- **Stage 1:** C1 **unit identity** — a uniform 1 Jy/beam image over N_beam pixels sums to 1 Jy in the aperture code; C2 **planted source** — a synthetic Gaussian source of known total flux (1 Jy km/s) of the size of the real sources injected into a noise-only region of each moment map is recovered within 15% and the random-aperture noise matches the scatter of 50 recoveries to 30%; C3 **the L_[CII] formula** reproduces a hand computation (closed form from ν, D_L, S) to 1e-9 and the published de-lensed L_[CII] of SPT0418-47 is reproduced within a factor 2 from our image-plane flux and the dust μ (reported; a departure is the differential-lensing flag, not a failure); C4 **the T_d bracket is monotone** (M_gas,dust falls as T_d rises) and the CMB correction lowers S_ν; C5 **the class rule** returns "downgraded" on a planted sample with d_i scatter 0.5 dex and "M2" on one with 0.05 dex.
- **Extraction:** **C-ext planted-rotation recovery** — for each galaxy 50 mocks: the fitted model with V_a = 150 km/s, R_t = 1.0 kpc, the corpus inclination, σ₀ = 40 km/s and the galaxy's own beam, geometry and PSF, added to noise cut from the line-free channels of the SAME cube, refitted; **the recovered V(R_out) must lie within 15% of the input in ≥ 90% of the mocks and its mean bias must be below 10%** (this can fail at 1″ resolution: failures are reported and the galaxy's V is flagged); **C-beam** a point source through the model convolution returns the header's beam FWHM to 3%; **MUTATE** — planted V × 1.5 returns V(R_out) × 1.5 within the errors.
- **Scoring:** as CFG229 (C3 disc function; C4 independent Hankel disc force; C5 directions; C6 / C6n planted-law recovery; C8 blindness — the pre-flight output is bit-identical with the Stage-3 outputs removed; C9 mock SD against finite-difference error propagation; C10 the implied-a₀ estimator bit-equal to CFG223's; C11 one galaxy recomputed by hand with g_bar from the Hankel integral). **MUTATE** (`MUTATE=1`: g_obs × 1.5 shifts every δ by +0.17609; `=2`: +0.30 dex on both baryon components; `=3`: no helium; `=4`: flux × 2 at Stage 1 raises both gas masses by 0.301 dex and leaves d_AB unchanged).

## 8. Limits (written before the run)
ALPINE's 1″ beam is about 6 kpc: R_out ~ 4 to 6 kpc is one to two beam radii, the forward model is an extrapolation of a parametric curve, and Jones+21 fitted only two to three rings; the inclinations (corpus `inc_kin`, 46° ± 4° to 57° ± 31°) are the dominant velocity systematic and are marginalised, not measured here; the stellar masses are single literature SED values without errors (unverified); the [CII] and dust conversions are single-tracer prescriptions, one from memory (Solomon+92 luminosity form) and one quoted from a TeX footnote (α_[CII] = 30), applied outside their calibration samples; the dust extrapolation from rest 155 to 184 μm to 850 μm is temperature-dependent (the bracket is the class-L band); ALPINE's collapsed continuum is faint and is an upper limit where S/N < 3; the thin exponential disc with one R_e for stars and gas is ours; for SPT0418-47 the curve and the de-lensed L_[CII] are the authors' lens-model outputs, differential magnification between dust and [CII] is unquantified, and the stellar mass is a quoted value; the floor presumes ν ≥ 1 and a thin-disc g_bar; the implied a₀ is lever-amplified wherever it exists and omits the intrinsic scatter in its statistical interval.

## 9. Wording restrictions
"Compilation; two un-optimised tracers; calibration-limited; not blind to the published kinematics; descriptive, not a verdict." No "favours", "prefers", "disfavours", "confirms" or "detects"; the PROXY is always "the ΛCDM effective-a₀ proxy"; κ = ½ is FITTED; a lean is not a detection; a floor failure is a statement about the baryon model, never about a₀; "class M" is never used for these gas masses (M2 at most, by the rule above).
