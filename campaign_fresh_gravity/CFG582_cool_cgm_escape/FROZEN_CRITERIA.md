# CFG582 FROZEN CRITERIA — can cool (~10^4 K) circumgalactic gas supply the KiDS early-type shortfall?

(owner chat 10-09, "yes do the cool gas check"). Committed alone before any literature value is read.
After CFG580 + CFG581 the hot-gas escape is closed; this tests the cool-gas escape.

Requirement (CFG531 template): extra baryons f·M* inside ≈ 100 kpc, f = 0.8 (canonical) / 0.55 (alt), for KiDS
early types at log M* ≈ 10.7–10.9 (required mass at log M* 10.8: 5.0e10 / 3.5e10 M☉).

Qualifying measurement: a published cool-phase CGM mass (total hydrogen + helium, or hydrogen with a stated
conversion; from UV/optical absorption with ionisation modelling) around QUIESCENT / passive / early-type galaxies
with stellar mass log M* 10.5–11.5, inside a stated projected or 3-D radius ≤ 300 kpc, read from the source text or
tables (arXiv TeX/HTML; never recalled). Star-forming samples are reported as context, not scored.
Conversion to 100 kpc: M(<100 kpc) = M(<R) × (100 kpc / R) for R > 100 kpc (ρ ∝ r^-2, M ∝ r; declared now); for
R ≤ 100 kpc, M(<R) is a lower bound on M(<100 kpc) and is scored as given. If a sample's median M* differs from
10.8, the requirement is scaled to that M* (f × M*_median).
Scoring per measurement: take the GENEROUS end (upper end of the quoted range, or central value + 2σ, or the
stated upper limit; a quoted lower limit scores as unbounded above → cannot rule out).
 RULED OUT for a measurement iff its generous value < required for BOTH f = 0.8 and f = 0.55.
 VIABLE iff its central value ≥ required for f = 0.55.
 UNDETERMINED otherwise.
Overall: COOL-GAS ESCAPE RULED OUT iff every qualifying measurement is RULED OUT; VIABLE if any is VIABLE;
 else UNDETERMINED. NO QUALIFYING MEASUREMENT if none is found.
Disclosed: absorption probes sightline samples, not individual galaxies; ionisation-model uncertainties
(often ≥ 0.5 dex); cool gas need not be in equilibrium (inflow/outflow); κ fitted; cold energy's mass required;
not theory closed.
