## 1. Points (fixed here)
A point is one galaxy at one stated radius r: **g_obs = V²/r** (V the published circular or rotation speed at r), **g_bar** from the baryons; **D = g_obs/g_bar**; log₁₀ errors σ_log g_obs = 2·0.4343·σ_V/V (mean of the asymmetric errors), σ_log g_bar from the published mass errors through the declared model.
- **S1 Amvrosiadis+25 (`amvrosiadis_bestfit.csv`, `_parent.csv`), the modelled sources with 2 ≤ z ≤ 5** (nine: 007.1, 022.1, 041.1, 049.1, 065.1, 066.1, 071.1, 075.1, 122.1; the three at z < 2 are excluded). r = 2 r_e with r_e from `re_arcsec` at the source's redshift (flat ΛCDM, H₀ = 67.4, Ω_m = 0.315), V = `vcirc_2re_kms`. **Baryon model (ours, declared): stars M* = 10^logMstar and gas M_gas = 10^logMgas (α_CO = 0.92 as published) in ONE thin exponential disc with R_e as tabulated (R_d = R_e/1.678); g_bar = the Freeman disc acceleration at r** (the function of CFG216 / CFG219, `disc_v2`).
- **S2 CRISTAL (z 4.4 to 5.7; eight of the fourteen discs are above z = 5):** the rows of CFG213 (R_e: the twelve discs, fit route; the six class-A, independent route) and CFG220 (R_out: six, both routes), through the committed machinery (`rows_Re`, `rows_Rout`), g_obs = g_bar D. Authors' baryon geometry (fit route) or authors' geometry with independent masses (route).

## 2. Laws and the statistic
As CFG223: FLAT (F = 1); the ΛCDM effective-a₀ PROXY (Z1 `lcdm_native`); a₀ ∝ H(z) (E(z), Ω_m = 0.315); a₀ ∝ √ρ_DE (the record's M-DEC with (w₀, w_a) = (−0.838, −0.62), values from the record). **δ_L = log₁₀[g_obs / (g_bar ν_mono(g_bar/(a₀ F_L(z))))]**, ν_mono, canonical footing 9.3603e-11 (alt footing 1.1312e-10 and P2 reported). Per set (S1, S2 by radius and route, S4 at z ≥ 2): the median δ_L with a 10,000-resample galaxy bootstrap (seed 227) 95% interval, **descriptive only**; S3 lower limits enter no median (a lower limit on g_bar is a lower limit on g_bar ν(g_bar/a₀) and so bounds δ from above by an amount that depends on the regime; they are drawn as limits).

## 3. The gas-calibration band (the headline caveat)
For every point with a gas component: g_bar(τ) = g_stars + g_gas 10^τ, the gas mass shifted by τ. **Inner band τ = ±K_local of α_CO in the point's z bin from CFG224b (`cfg224b_conversion_drift_results.json`: B3 z 1.6 to 3.0, B4 z ≥ 3.0; the multi-tracer optimised route) and outer band τ = ±K_ACE (the un-optimised single-tracer route: ACE against Stripe82, CFG224b).** Each point is drawn with both bands as horizontal bars in g_bar; each median δ_L is recomputed at τ = ±inner and ±outer and reported with the point estimate. The CRISTAL fit-route baryon masses carry a 1-dex prior in the authors' fits, so the bands UNDERSTATE their systematic (as CFG223).

## 6. Limits (written before the run)
The S1 and S3 baryon models are OURS (thin exponential discs with the tabulated half-light radius as R_e for both stars and gas, which is an assumption: CO and optical sizes differ); V_circ at 2 r_e (Amvrosiadis) carries the authors' dynamical model; ALPAKA's V_ext is a rotation speed not corrected for pressure support (V/σ of 10 to 20, a few per cent); the S1 gas masses use α_CO = 0.92 justified by the same paper's dynamics (partly circular: the conversion is tuned to the dynamics being tested); the nine S1 galaxies are massive submm-selected discs at g_bar of a few a₀, the near-Newtonian regime where the a₀ scale is weakly constrained (CFG223's lever); z 2 to 5 has no low-mass, low-acceleration galaxy in these tables; the bands are the record's two measured systematics, not an error budget; "first compiled" refers to the tables the record holds, not to the literature.
