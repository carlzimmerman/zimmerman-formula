# CFG227 — FROZEN CRITERIA: the first compiled radial-acceleration relation (RAR) at z = 2–5 from tabulated kinematics and baryons

> **Compilation; conversions as published; calibration-limited; author decompositions and tables, not a direct a₀ measurement; not a detection. ΛCDM has no a₀: the proxy is an effective-a₀ PROXY. κ = ½ is FITTED. No sentence says the data favour a law.** The data chat (`data_assembly/Z2_Z5_UNCOMPUTED_RAR_DATA_2026-09-30.md`, fbf0c8290) finds no published RAR at z = 2–5, only dark-matter fractions and one MOND mass-model test on two discs (Lelli+23). This lane plots what the record's tables already allow.

Written 2026-09-30 and committed BEFORE any g_obs, g_bar or δ of this lane is computed. Requested by the orchestrator. **Not blind:** the CRISTAL rows (g_bar, D) and the RC100 rows were scored in CFG213 / CFG216 / CFG220 / CFG222 / CFG223; the Amvrosiadis and ALPAKA values have not been turned into accelerations before this freeze.

## 1. Points (fixed here)
A point is one galaxy at one stated radius r: **g_obs = V²/r** (V the published circular or rotation speed at r), **g_bar** from the baryons; **D = g_obs/g_bar**; log₁₀ errors σ_log g_obs = 2·0.4343·σ_V/V (mean of the asymmetric errors), σ_log g_bar from the published mass errors through the declared model.
- **S1 Amvrosiadis+25 (`amvrosiadis_bestfit.csv`, `_parent.csv`), the modelled sources with 2 ≤ z ≤ 5** (nine: 007.1, 022.1, 041.1, 049.1, 065.1, 066.1, 071.1, 075.1, 122.1; the three at z < 2 are excluded). r = 2 r_e with r_e from `re_arcsec` at the source's redshift (flat ΛCDM, H₀ = 67.4, Ω_m = 0.315), V = `vcirc_2re_kms`. **Baryon model (ours, declared): stars M* = 10^logMstar and gas M_gas = 10^logMgas (α_CO = 0.92 as published) in ONE thin exponential disc with R_e as tabulated (R_d = R_e/1.678); g_bar = the Freeman disc acceleration at r** (the function of CFG216 / CFG219, `disc_v2`).
- **S2 CRISTAL (z 4.4 to 5.7; eight of the fourteen discs are above z = 5):** the rows of CFG213 (R_e: the twelve discs, fit route; the six class-A, independent route) and CFG220 (R_out: six, both routes), through the committed machinery (`rows_Re`, `rows_Rout`), g_obs = g_bar D. Authors' baryon geometry (fit route) or authors' geometry with independent masses (route).
- **S3 ALPAKA I (`alpaka1_*` and `alpaka1_digitised/`), the ten discs with z ≥ 2 (ids 13, 15, 18, 19, 20, 22, 23, 24, 25, 28):** r = R_ext (the outermost digitised ring, kpc), V = V_ext (table). **STARS ONLY: g_bar is a LOWER LIMIT** (the published line luminosity has no published conversion, so no gas mass is adopted); the stars are an exponential disc with R_e = the dashed optical R_e where the paper drew one, else R_ext/1.2 (the median R_ext/R_e of the galaxies with both). id 24 has no M* and is listed without a point. The gas-inclusive extent (α_CO L′ with α_CO = 0.8 and 4.36, r_J1 = 1) is tabulated only.
- **S4 RC100, corrected table, the rows with z ≥ 2** (the repo's loader: g_obs = V_c²/R_e, g_bar = (1 − f_DM) g_obs; authors' three-method decomposition with an SED + scaling-gas prior): a larger LOW-INFORMATION set, drawn in grey; single radius per galaxy.
- **Listed, no point:** Roman-Oliveira+23 (four at z 4.3 to 4.4: V_max and V_ext, no ring radius, gas-only masses, no M*), Lelli+23 (two discs: one at z = 2.24; only a mean V_rot, no radius-resolved curve in the tables), J081740, PKS 0529-549, GN20 (single discs without a tabulated radius or stellar mass in the tables), SINS and ZFIRE (single velocities, no measured gas), Danhaive+25 (no gas), Jones+21 ALPINE (no baryons in the tables).

## 2. Laws and the statistic
As CFG223: FLAT (F = 1); the ΛCDM effective-a₀ PROXY (Z1 `lcdm_native`); a₀ ∝ H(z) (E(z), Ω_m = 0.315); a₀ ∝ √ρ_DE (the record's M-DEC with (w₀, w_a) = (−0.838, −0.62), values from the record). **δ_L = log₁₀[g_obs / (g_bar ν_mono(g_bar/(a₀ F_L(z))))]**, ν_mono, canonical footing 9.3603e-11 (alt footing 1.1312e-10 and P2 reported). Per set (S1, S2 by radius and route, S4 at z ≥ 2): the median δ_L with a 10,000-resample galaxy bootstrap (seed 227) 95% interval, **descriptive only**; S3 lower limits enter no median (a lower limit on g_bar is a lower limit on g_bar ν(g_bar/a₀) and so bounds δ from above by an amount that depends on the regime; they are drawn as limits).

## 3. The gas-calibration band (the headline caveat)
For every point with a gas component: g_bar(τ) = g_stars + g_gas 10^τ, the gas mass shifted by τ. **Inner band τ = ±K_local of α_CO in the point's z bin from CFG224b (`cfg224b_conversion_drift_results.json`: B3 z 1.6 to 3.0, B4 z ≥ 3.0; the multi-tracer optimised route) and outer band τ = ±K_ACE (the un-optimised single-tracer route: ACE against Stripe82, CFG224b).** Each point is drawn with both bands as horizontal bars in g_bar; each median δ_L is recomputed at τ = ±inner and ±outer and reported with the point estimate. The CRISTAL fit-route baryon masses carry a 1-dex prior in the authors' fits, so the bands UNDERSTATE their systematic (as CFG223).

## 5. Controls (all reported; a failure is reported plainly)
- **C1** counts: S1 nine, S3 ten discs of which nine with M*, S4 the number of RC100 corrected rows at z ≥ 2 (reported; the data chat quotes 41), the CRISTAL rows equal CFG213's and CFG220's (C5). **C2** non-vacuous placement: synthetic galaxies with the S1 masses and radii whose V is set so that g_obs lies exactly on law L (by inverting g_obs = g_bar ν(g_bar/(a₀F_L))) return δ_L = 0 to 1e-9 for each law and a non-zero δ under the other laws. **C3** the disc function: Freeman's peak V² = 0.3872 G M/R_d to 0.003 and g → GM/r² far from a compact mass to 1e-3. **C4** band direction: raising the gas mass (τ > 0) raises g_bar and lowers every δ_L at every S1 point. **C5** the CRISTAL rows reproduce CFG213 / CFG220's committed medians of δ_FLAT and δ_H(z) to 1e-9, and the RC100 rows reproduce CFG216's loader. **MUTATE** (`MUTATE=1`, outputs `*_MUTATE`): g_obs × 1.5 at every point shifts every δ_L by +0.176 to 1e-9 and leaves g_bar unchanged.

## 6. Limits (written before the run)
The S1 and S3 baryon models are OURS (thin exponential discs with the tabulated half-light radius as R_e for both stars and gas, which is an assumption: CO and optical sizes differ); V_circ at 2 r_e (Amvrosiadis) carries the authors' dynamical model; ALPAKA's V_ext is a rotation speed not corrected for pressure support (V/σ of 10 to 20, a few per cent); the S1 gas masses use α_CO = 0.92 justified by the same paper's dynamics (partly circular: the conversion is tuned to the dynamics being tested); the nine S1 galaxies are massive submm-selected discs at g_bar of a few a₀, the near-Newtonian regime where the a₀ scale is weakly constrained (CFG223's lever); z 2 to 5 has no low-mass, low-acceleration galaxy in these tables; the bands are the record's two measured systematics, not an error budget; "first compiled" refers to the tables the record holds, not to the literature.

## A1. Gas-route class per galaxy (rule-based, fixed here) and its band
- **M** multi-tracer gas mass, luminosity-matched optimised conversions (CFG224b); **S** a single tracer with a conversion the source states and justifies for that sample; **L** a single tracer with a local-calibration prescription applied as published (for example a dust-continuum conversion calibrated on local or ULIRG samples); **D** baryon mass derived from the dynamical fit or a fitted scaling prior (flagged, NEVER pooled with M, S or L, and no law statement is made from it).
- **Assignment:** S1 Amvrosiadis = **S** (CO only, α_CO = 0.92 stated; flagged: the same paper justifies it with its own dynamics, so it is partly circular); CRISTAL independent route = **L** (dust-continuum gas at T_d = 50 K, Scoville+16); CRISTAL fit route = **D**; RC100 (M_baryon fitted with an SED + scaling-gas prior, f_DM from the three-method average) = **D**; ALPAKA = stars only (no gas mass adopted; limits). **No galaxy of this compilation is class M** (no z ≥ 2 galaxy with a radius-resolved curve, a stellar mass and a multi-tracer gas mass is tabulated): **N_M = 0, so the pooled class-M result is reported as "insufficient class-M"** and the compilation makes no class-M statement.
- **Bands** (on the gas mass; each point and each group median carries its class band): **M: ±0.03 (inner) and ±0.10 dex (outer)** (CFG224b: K_direct 0.021 to 0.032 and K_local 0.093 (B3) / 0.067 (B4) of α_CO); **S and L: ±0.21 (inner) and ±0.67 dex (outer)** (CFG224b's ACE offset against Stripe82: −0.213 with the metallicity slope fixed at +1, −0.520 with no slope, −0.671 with the OLS slope; the nearest frozen bracket); **D: no band, flagged.** These replace the K_IN / K_OUT of section 3 (which were 0.093 / 0.067 and 0.340).
- **No law verdict from classes L or D** (and none from S either while N_M = 0): every δ is descriptive.

## A2. Independence and pressure support
The primary scored groups are the baryon-INDEPENDENT ones: S1 and CRISTAL independent (R_e and R_out). The D groups (CRISTAL fit route, RC100) are drawn in grey and listed, never scored as evidence. Pressure support follows each source's convention (Amvrosiadis V_circ at 2 r_e as published; CRISTAL's V_circ with α = 3.36 as CFG213); **sensitivity variants:** the CRISTAL rows with α = 1.68 and 0 (CFG213's own alternatives, the Burkert-type and no-correction limits), ALPAKA's V_ext with V_c² = V_ext² + α σ_ext² for α ∈ {0, 1.68, 3.36}, and S1 with V_c² = V_circ² + 3.36 σ² (`sigma_kms`) in case the published V_circ is not pressure-corrected. **Geometry sensitivity for S1 (post hoc, motivated by the first run):** the stellar disc with R_e,* = 2 R_e,CO (stars are typically more extended than CO) besides the frozen R_e,* = R_e,CO.

## A3. The statistic
Per galaxy: δ_L for FLAT, the PROXY, H(z) and M-DEC with the class band extents; per group: the median with the 95% bootstrap interval (as above) and the medians at the inner and outer band shifts. A pooled class-M result is computed only if N_M ≥ 5; otherwise the compilation states "insufficient class-M".

## A4. Power pre-flight (run and reported BEFORE the scored table is presented)
For each scored group (S1; CRISTAL R_e independent; CRISTAL R_out independent): mocks on the ACTUAL galaxies (z, g_bar, the stars/gas split through f_gas, the class band): truth T ∈ {FLAT, H(z), PROXY}, g_obs,true = g_bar ν(g_bar/(a₀F_T(z))) at a gas shift τ_true; observed log g_obs = log g_obs,true + N(0, σ_i²) with σ_i² = σ_int² + σ_meas,i², **σ_int = 0.15 dex (declared)**, σ_meas,i = the propagated velocity error for S1 and 0.08 dex (declared; errors are not propagated in the CFG213 / 220 rows) for CRISTAL; 1,000 mocks per (group, truth), seed 227. Reported per group: (i) **signal** = |median over mocks of the group-median δ_FLAT when T = H(z)| and likewise for T = PROXY; (ii) **statistical SD** of the group-median δ_FLAT when T = FLAT; (iii) **calibration systematic** = the shift of the noise-free group-median δ_FLAT when the gas shifts by the outer band. **A separation of flat from H(z) in that group is called "possible" only if signal > (iii) + 2 × (ii); otherwise "not possible", and likewise for the proxy.** The pre-flight is descriptive of THIS compilation, not a statement about any real sample.

## A5. Additional controls
- **C6 planted-law recovery with noise:** in the pre-flight mocks, the group median of δ under the TRUE law is within 2 statistical SD of 0 in at least 90% of the mocks, and under each other law it is offset by the noise-free amount to within 3 SD (reported per group). **C7 common-mode bias is invisible (the CFG224b caveat):** a synthetic class-M set of 20 galaxies with three tracers (CO, [CI], dust) whose conversions all share a bias τ_c = +0.30 dex: the tracer-agreement diagnostic d_AB = log₁₀(M_A/M_B) is unchanged to 1e-9 while the group-median δ_FLAT shifts (reported); this shows what the class-M band cannot exclude. **MUTATE:** as above.
