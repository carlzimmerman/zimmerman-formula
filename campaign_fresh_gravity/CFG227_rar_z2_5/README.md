## What is in, by gas-route class (Addendum 1)
- **Class S: Amvrosiadis+25, nine modelled sources at z 2.02–4.45, V_circ at 2 r_e**, SED M* plus CO gas at α_CO = 0.92 (the same paper justifies that with its own dynamics: flagged partly circular), our thin-exponential-disc baryons with the tabulated R_e for stars and gas.
- **Class L: CRISTAL independent route (R_e and R_out, six class-A discs, z 4.4 to 5.7)**: SED M* plus dust-continuum gas at T_d = 50 K (Scoville+16), the committed CFG213 / CFG220 rows.
- **Class D, FLAGGED and never scored: CRISTAL fit route (R_e 12, R_out 6) and RC100 at z ≥ 2 (41)**: baryon masses fitted with the dynamics or a scaling prior. ALPAKA (9 discs at z ≥ 2 with M*): **stars-only lower limits on g_bar**, no gas adopted.
- **Class M: none. N_M = 0, so the pooled class-M result is: insufficient class-M.** Listed without a point: Roman-Oliveira+23 (no ring radius, no M*), Lelli+23 (one disc at z = 2.24, only a mean V_rot), J081740, PKS 0529-549, GN20, SINS, ZFIRE, Danhaive+25, Jones+21.

## What the points say (descriptive; medians of δ with 95% bootstrap intervals, ν_mono at the canonical footing)
- **S1 cannot test any law: the modelled baryons exceed the observed accelerations.** 7 of the 9 have g_obs below g_bar (median D = g_obs/g_bar = 0.52); median δ_FLAT −0.310 [−0.664, +0.171], PROXY −0.338, H(z) −0.373, M-DEC −0.306; with the gas shifted by the outer band the flat median still runs −0.24 to −0.70. Sensitivities (beside, never substituted): V_c² = V_circ² + 3.36σ² gives −0.241 (H(z) −0.295); stars at R_e,* = 2 R_e,CO (post hoc, motivated by the first run) gives −0.216 and a median D of 0.62. The baryon model (massive gas-rich discs at compact CO radii) or the masses (α_CO = 0.92, SED M*) overshoot the dynamics; this says something about the compilation's baryons, not about a₀.
- **CRISTAL independent (class L):** R_e: flat +0.117 [−0.060, +0.384], PROXY −0.085 [−0.253, +0.207], H(z) −0.144 [−0.319, +0.141]; R_out: flat +0.106 [−0.138, +0.343], PROXY −0.136, H(z) −0.203 [−0.477, +0.029] (these are CFG222's numbers, reproduced exactly). **Moving the gas by the inner band (±0.213 dex) shifts the flat median by about ±0.1 and by the outer band (±0.671) from +0.26 to −0.20 (R_e) and +0.24 to −0.16 (R_out): every law's median crosses zero inside the band**, which is as wide as the laws are apart (0.14 to 0.34).
- **Class D (flagged, not scored):** RC100 at z ≥ 2 (41): flat +0.008 [−0.010, +0.064], H(z) −0.105 [−0.147, −0.053]; CRISTAL fit route as CFG222. **ALPAKA (9, stars-only lower limits on g_bar):** δ_FLAT computed with g_bar,* is an upper bound on the true δ, median −0.08 (range −0.59 to +0.22), consistent with anything.
- **A common-mode tracer bias is invisible (C7, the CFG224b caveat):** +0.30 dex on all three tracers of a synthetic class-M set leaves the tracer-agreement diagnostic d_AB unchanged to 1e-9 while the group median δ_FLAT moves by about −0.07 in the S1-like regime.
- **What would make a z = 2–5 RAR informative** (from the pre-flight and the band numbers): radius-resolved curves (several radii per galaxy; the KMOS3D and SINS cubes on disk are unprocessed), gas from several tracers with luminosity-matched optimised conversions (class M), stellar masses that do not overshoot the dynamics, and galaxies below g_bar ≈ a₀ (none in these tables). With the class bands, none of the present groups can separate flat from a₀ ∝ E(z).

## Limits

## CORRECTION (appended 2026-09-30; the original text above is kept unchanged except for the banner)

The CFG237 referee (criteria 6ad68510 committed first; result commit 53f6517a3; `campaign_fresh_gravity/CFG237_rar_z2_5_referee/README.md`) reproduced every number this README prints, and found the following sentences wrong or over-read. **I checked the three that can be checked from the tables on disk myself (items 1, 2 and 3, scratch scripts; they are folded into CFG229 as committed controls); items 4 to 8 are the referee's findings and I accept them.**

1. **“Class M: none. N_M = 0 … insufficient class-M” is wrong under the lane’s own frozen rule.** The class rule was never implemented: `CLASS` in `cfg227_rar_z2_5.py` is a hard-coded dict and “N_M = 0” was a string literal, not a computed result (my process failure: a claim with no script behind it). Cross-matching the Dunne+22 per-galaxy optimised multi-tracer masses (`opt_ad`, `opt_dax`, `opt_xa`, `opt_xd`) to every kinematic table with a name or position gives **seven z ≥ 2 galaxies with a class-M gas mass, a stellar mass and a velocity at a stated radius: ALPAKA 15, 18, 19, 20, 22, SINS Q2343-BX610 and ALESS 122.1** (referee counts under the literal rule: M 7, S 8, L 6, D 60; other readings move them, CFG237 §3 item 1). I re-matched the five ALPAKA positions to the Dunne master table independently: separations 0.08 to 1.28 arcsec with |Δz| ≤ 0.015, next-nearest Dunne source more than 3 arcsec away. CFG227 used other gas for these (ALPAKA stars-only; 122.1 α_CO = 0.92 from another paper’s footnote). The class is a statement that a per-galaxy optimised multi-tracer gas mass exists; **Dunne’s `logMH2` is M_H2 without helium (the paper’s M_mol columns carry a factor 1.36), so any use needs ×1.36 (+0.134 dex) — this is checked numerically in CFG229, not assumed.** N_M = 7 ≥ 5, so the pooled class-M statistic this lane’s own rule (Addendum 1, A3) calls for is now owed: **CFG229 scores the seven** (criteria frozen first).
2. **The S1 anomaly is not an α_CO effect.** The sentence “the baryon model (massive gas-rich discs at compact CO radii) or the masses (α_CO = 0.92, SED M*) overshoot the dynamics” is half right. Independent recomputation (same thin exponential disc, same radii): median D = 0.519 as adopted and **0.653 with α_CO = 0 (stars only)**, 5 of 9 still below 1; the five stellar-dominated sources (007.1, 022.1, 041.1, 066.1, 071.1; gas share of g_bar 0.05 to 0.20) carry D < 1 through their SED M*; four of the nine have M* above the authors’ own M_dyn(< 10 kpc) (referee); only 049.1, 065.1, 075.1 and 122.1 are the “gas-rich compact” discs, two of which have D > 1. The anomaly needs a joint move in the stellar mass, the stellar disc size and the inclination (referee grid: JOINT / NOT DECIDABLE, minimum cost 1.74 declared-sigma units) and says something about the SED masses and the geometry, not about α_CO and not about a₀. Stands: “S1 cannot test any law”.
3. **“Every law’s median crosses zero inside the band” is false for H(z) and for the proxy.** From `cfg227_rar_z2_5_results.json`, CRISTAL independent, outer band (−outer to +outer): R_e FLAT +0.255 to −0.195 (crosses), M-DEC +0.313 to −0.190 (crosses), **PROXY −0.013 to −0.285 and H(z) −0.075 to −0.336 (neither crosses)**; R_out FLAT +0.238 to −0.157, M-DEC +0.302 to −0.142 (cross), PROXY −0.051 to −0.328, H(z) −0.129 to −0.387 (neither). The gas band does not remove the lean of these six class-L discs away from H(z) (every bootstrap interval still contains 0: a lean is not a detection, five of the six are above z = 5, and dropping any one disc moves the R_e FLAT median from +0.117 to +0.010 or +0.225, the third and fourth of six being 0.215 dex apart).
4. **C7 is an algebraic identity, not a finding.** d_AB = log(M_A/M_B) is a difference in which a factor common to all tracers cancels, so “unchanged to 1e-9” holds by construction. C7 can detect a tracer-specific bias (only CO +0.30 moves d_AB by exactly +0.30) and cannot detect a common-mode bias of any size, constant or redshift-dependent (CFG237: a τ_c(z) = b_z log10((1+z)/3.5) with b_z = −1.12 gives flat-true mocks the z slope of H(z)-true mocks, d_AB 1.8e-15, outside the class-M band). So **the class-M band is a band on tracer AGREEMENT, not on the absolute gas scale.** The quoted “−0.07” is the regime of the S1-like gas share 0.2 (−0.155 at gas share 0.5), not a general number.
5. **C5’s “equal to 1e-16” is an identity of a shared code path** (the CRISTAL rows come from `exec_lane` of the CFG213 / CFG220 scripts and are compared with CFG222’s output from the same machinery): it shows the import works and the rows were not altered, nothing about their correctness. An independent code path (curves + interpolation) agrees to 1e-3 (R_e) and 5e-4 (R_out).
6. **Power table, two definitions.** The “calibration systematic” in the table is S_max (the larger of the two noise-free shifts at the outer band, asymmetric: S1 +B −0.234, −B +0.072), not the half-range (S_half 0.153 / 0.256 / 0.228); the “signal” is the noise-free median, while the frozen text (A4 i) says the median over mocks (0.054 / 0.251 / 0.317 against 0.050 / 0.275 / 0.340, within 9%). “Not separable in any group” holds under every rule the referee tried (R-lin with S_half or S_max, R-marg) except one marginal cell: CRISTAL R_out against H(z) under S_half with the quadrature rule (POSSIBLE by a small margin).
7. **Scope of the title.** “z = 2–5”: the class-L CRISTAL discs lie at z 4.4 to 5.7, **five of six above z = 5** (only CRISTAL-11, z = 4.44, is inside the window); S1 is at 2.0 to 4.4.
8. **Class S and the bands.** Three of the nine S1 gas masses (049.1 and 075.1 from Wardlow+18, 122.1 from Calistro Rivera+18) and two M* come from other papers (TeX footnotes the CSV did not carry); the two S1 sources with D > 1 are footnote sources. The outer class band was widened from ±0.340 (first run) to ±0.671 after the S1 medians were seen (disclosed in Addendum 1, but post hoc).

**What this changes.** The CFG227 verdicts “each scored group not separable” and “S1 cannot test any law” stand. “N_M = 0” does not, and the statement “the gas is the wall” must not be read as the reason for S1’s D < 1. The class-M galaxies and the stellar masses are taken up in CFG229; the ALMA cubes (z 4.2 to 5.5) in CFG228.
