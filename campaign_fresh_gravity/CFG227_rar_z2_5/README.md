## What is in, by gas-route class (Addendum 1)
- **Class S: Amvrosiadis+25, nine modelled sources at z 2.02–4.45, V_circ at 2 r_e**, SED M* plus CO gas at α_CO = 0.92 (the same paper justifies that with its own dynamics: flagged partly circular), our thin-exponential-disc baryons with the tabulated R_e for stars and gas.
- **Class L: CRISTAL independent route (R_e and R_out, six class-A discs, z 4.4 to 5.7)**: SED M* plus dust-continuum gas at T_d = 50 K (Scoville+16), the committed CFG213 / CFG220 rows.
- **Class D, FLAGGED and never scored: CRISTAL fit route (R_e 12, R_out 6) and RC100 at z ≥ 2 (41)**: baryon masses fitted with the dynamics or a scaling prior. ALPAKA (9 discs at z ≥ 2 with M*): **stars-only lower limits on g_bar**, no gas adopted.
- **Class M: none. N_M = 0, so the pooled class-M result is: insufficient class-M.** Listed without a point: Roman-Oliveira+23 (no ring radius, no M*), Lelli+23 (one disc at z = 2.24, only a mean V_rot), J081740, PKS 0529-549, GN20, SINS, ZFIRE, Danhaive+25, Jones+21.
- **Bands on the gas mass (from CFG224b):** S and L ±0.213 (inner) and ±0.671 dex (outer) (the ACE offset against Stripe82 with the metallicity slope fixed at +1 and with the OLS slope); class M would be ±0.03 and ±0.093 (none present); D has no band.

## What the points say (descriptive; medians of δ with 95% bootstrap intervals, ν_mono at the canonical footing)
- **S1 cannot test any law: the modelled baryons exceed the observed accelerations.** 7 of the 9 have g_obs below g_bar (median D = g_obs/g_bar = 0.52); median δ_FLAT −0.310 [−0.664, +0.171], PROXY −0.338, H(z) −0.373, M-DEC −0.306; with the gas shifted by the outer band the flat median still runs −0.24 to −0.70. Sensitivities (beside, never substituted): V_c² = V_circ² + 3.36σ² gives −0.241 (H(z) −0.295); stars at R_e,* = 2 R_e,CO (post hoc, motivated by the first run) gives −0.216 and a median D of 0.62. The baryon model (massive gas-rich discs at compact CO radii) or the masses (α_CO = 0.92, SED M*) overshoot the dynamics; this says something about the compilation's baryons, not about a₀.
- **CRISTAL independent (class L):** R_e: flat +0.117 [−0.060, +0.384], PROXY −0.085 [−0.253, +0.207], H(z) −0.144 [−0.319, +0.141]; R_out: flat +0.106 [−0.138, +0.343], PROXY −0.136, H(z) −0.203 [−0.477, +0.029] (these are CFG222's numbers, reproduced exactly). **Moving the gas by the inner band (±0.213 dex) shifts the flat median by about ±0.1 and by the outer band (±0.671) from +0.26 to −0.20 (R_e) and +0.24 to −0.16 (R_out): every law's median crosses zero inside the band**, which is as wide as the laws are apart (0.14 to 0.34).
- **Class D (flagged, not scored):** RC100 at z ≥ 2 (41): flat +0.008 [−0.010, +0.064], H(z) −0.105 [−0.147, −0.053]; CRISTAL fit route as CFG222. **ALPAKA (9, stars-only lower limits on g_bar):** δ_FLAT computed with g_bar,* is an upper bound on the true δ, median −0.08 (range −0.59 to +0.22), consistent with anything.
- **A common-mode tracer bias is invisible (C7, the CFG224b caveat):** +0.30 dex on all three tracers of a synthetic class-M set leaves the tracer-agreement diagnostic d_AB unchanged to 1e-9 while the group median δ_FLAT moves by about −0.07 in the S1-like regime.
- **What would make a z = 2–5 RAR informative** (from the pre-flight and the band numbers): radius-resolved curves (several radii per galaxy; the KMOS3D and SINS cubes on disk are unprocessed), gas from several tracers with luminosity-matched optimised conversions (class M), stellar masses that do not overshoot the dynamics, and galaxies below g_bar ≈ a₀ (none in these tables). With the class bands, none of the present groups can separate flat from a₀ ∝ E(z).

## Limits
The S1 and ALPAKA baryon models are ours (thin exponential discs with the tabulated R_e for stars and gas; CO and optical sizes differ); Amvrosiadis's V_circ at 2 r_e carries the authors' dynamical model; ALPAKA's V_ext is not pressure-corrected (α variants 0, 1.68, 3.36 move the upper bound by 0.03); the band numbers are the record's measured gas systematics, not an error budget; class D fit-route baryon masses carry the authors' priors; the z = 2–5 window contains no low-acceleration galaxy; "first compiled" refers to the tables the record holds, not to the literature.
