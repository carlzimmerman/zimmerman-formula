## The screen, exactly as frozen cut 11
Both components carry finite Gaia DR3 `radial_velocity`; **flagged if |rv₁ − rv₂| > max(3 σ_dRV, 3 v_c(s_proj))**, σ_dRV = hypot(`radial_velocity_error`₁, `radial_velocity_error`₂) and v_c = the circular speed at the projected separation from the builder's own masses (the expression in `frozen_cuts`, evaluated on the BEFORE table). The flags are computed post hoc on the BEFORE table so that AFTER is nested inside BEFORE and the two share one Monte Carlo stream (the WP2 noise floor showed that two separate builds differ in about 2.8% of pairs by reseeding alone).

## What is computed
- **S_RV = the BEFORE pairs with both RVs** (the RV-matched subsample, so selection by RV coverage is separated from the screen's effect). Reported: N(S_RV), the number and fraction flagged; the median σ_dRV, v_c and |dRV| of S_RV; N of the BEFORE set.
- **γ̂_before** = the pipeline's own estimator on S_RV; **γ̂_after** = the same estimator on S_RV minus the flagged pairs; **Δγ̂ = γ̂_after − γ̂_before**, with the SD of Δγ̂ from a **paired bootstrap** (B = 100 resamples of the pairs of S_RV; both estimators on the same resample). Both footings. The estimator is exactly the validation's (`validate_dr3.v4_gamma`: `ingest_csv`; the DR3-noise forward model, population 3,000,000, seed 20261216, `dr4 = False`; `run_fit`; fit seed 20261217 + j); σ_fit as returned. NON-SCORING: no comparison with any prediction.
- **Reference rows:** γ̂ on the whole BEFORE set (all pairs, no screen) and on the whole AFTER set (screen applied where both RVs exist); γ̂ of the flagged pairs alone if at least 50 are flagged.

## Controls (all reported; a failure is reported plainly)
- **C1** the post hoc flags reproduce the builder's own cut: `frozen_cuts` with the RV columns enabled, run on the same BEFORE table, keeps exactly the pairs this lane leaves unflagged. **C2** planted triples: a random 10% of the unflagged S_RV pairs get a dRV offset ~ N(0, 3 km/s) in the screen's inputs only; the empirical flagged fraction of the planted pairs equals the analytic mean of Φ((−T_i − d_i)/3) + 1 − Φ((T_i − d_i)/3) (T_i the pair's threshold, d_i its dRV) within 3 binomial standard errors, and the un-planted pairs' flags are unchanged. **C3** planted contamination of γ̂: the same planted pairs also get a plane-of-sky velocity offset (two components ~ N(0, 3 km/s) added to the relative velocity vector at a random angle); over 20 repeats the planted γ̂ shift (contaminated minus original) and the fraction of it the screen removes are reported, and the screened shift is smaller than the contaminated shift in at least 90% of the repeats. **MUTATE** (`MUTATE=1`, outputs `*_MUTATE`): both thresholds set to infinity: zero pairs flagged and Δγ̂ = 0 exactly.

## Limits (written before the run)
Gaia DR3 RV errors of about 1 to 5 km/s against an orbital ceiling of about 1.4 km/s at s ≥ 2 kAU mean the screen catches only triples with km/s-scale inner-orbit dRV, mostly in the brighter stars (RVs need G_RVS ≲ 14); the RV-matched subsample (60% of the pairs) is brighter and not representative of the whole sample; the screen cannot measure an orbit; γ̂ here is the pipeline's γ_inf against the DR3-noise forward model on a few thousand pairs, so σ_fit is large and a change of a fraction of σ_fit is not resolved by one sample; the paired bootstrap measures the estimator's noise in Δγ̂, not any systematic. The DESI top-up and the El-Badry variant are separate later steps.
