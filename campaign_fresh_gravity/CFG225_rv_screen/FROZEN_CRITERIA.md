## The pair set
- **PRIMARY:** the in-repo build of the frozen builder at its REAL settings (all twelve chunks, `N_SHIFT = 30`, the builder's frozen seeds: the WP2-γ seed set 0 work directory, whose stage-E and stage-F caches are reused), with the **RV screen (cut 11) disabled in `frozen_cuts`** by a read-only in-memory patch (RV columns set to non-finite in a copy), so every pair that passes the other frozen cuts is present: **the BEFORE set**. `build_catalog.py`, `wide_binary_pipeline.py`, the pre-registration and every `*_HASH.txt` are not edited.
- **VARIANT (only if `catalog_builder/validate_dr3.py::v4_gamma`'s El-Badry route can be reused offline without editing any frozen file):** El-Badry's in-volume pairs through the same frozen cuts with the RV screen disabled. If it cannot, it is reported as not run.

## The screen, exactly as frozen cut 11
Both components carry finite Gaia DR3 `radial_velocity`; **flagged if |rv₁ − rv₂| > max(3 σ_dRV, 3 v_c(s_proj))**, σ_dRV = hypot(`radial_velocity_error`₁, `radial_velocity_error`₂) and v_c = the circular speed at the projected separation from the builder's own masses (the expression in `frozen_cuts`, evaluated on the BEFORE table). The flags are computed post hoc on the BEFORE table so that AFTER is nested inside BEFORE and the two share one Monte Carlo stream (the WP2 noise floor showed that two separate builds differ in about 2.8% of pairs by reseeding alone).

## What is computed
- **S_RV = the BEFORE pairs with both RVs** (the RV-matched subsample, so selection by RV coverage is separated from the screen's effect). Reported: N(S_RV), the number and fraction flagged; the median σ_dRV, v_c and |dRV| of S_RV; N of the BEFORE set.
- **γ̂_before** = the pipeline's own estimator on S_RV; **γ̂_after** = the same estimator on S_RV minus the flagged pairs; **Δγ̂ = γ̂_after − γ̂_before**, with the SD of Δγ̂ from a **paired bootstrap** (B = 100 resamples of the pairs of S_RV; both estimators on the same resample). Both footings. The estimator is exactly the validation's (`validate_dr3.v4_gamma`: `ingest_csv`; the DR3-noise forward model, population 3,000,000, seed 20261216, `dr4 = False`; `run_fit`; fit seed 20261217 + j); σ_fit as returned. NON-SCORING: no comparison with any prediction.
- **Reference rows:** γ̂ on the whole BEFORE set (all pairs, no screen) and on the whole AFTER set (screen applied where both RVs exist); γ̂ of the flagged pairs alone if at least 50 are flagged.

## Controls (all reported; a failure is reported plainly)
- **C1** the post hoc flags reproduce the builder's own cut: `frozen_cuts` with the RV columns enabled, run on the same BEFORE table, keeps exactly the pairs this lane leaves unflagged. **C2** planted triples: a random 10% of the unflagged S_RV pairs get a dRV offset ~ N(0, 3 km/s) in the screen's inputs only; the empirical flagged fraction of the planted pairs equals the analytic mean of Φ((−T_i − d_i)/3) + 1 − Φ((T_i − d_i)/3) (T_i the pair's threshold, d_i its dRV) within 3 binomial standard errors, and the un-planted pairs' flags are unchanged. **C3** planted contamination of γ̂: the same planted pairs also get a plane-of-sky velocity offset (two components ~ N(0, 3 km/s) added to the relative velocity vector at a random angle); over 20 repeats the planted γ̂ shift (contaminated minus original) and the fraction of it the screen removes are reported, and the screened shift is smaller than the contaminated shift in at least 90% of the repeats. **MUTATE** (`MUTATE=1`, outputs `*_MUTATE`): both thresholds set to infinity: zero pairs flagged and Δγ̂ = 0 exactly.

## Limits (written before the run)
Gaia DR3 RV errors of about 1 to 5 km/s against an orbital ceiling of about 1.4 km/s at s ≥ 2 kAU mean the screen catches only triples with km/s-scale inner-orbit dRV, mostly in the brighter stars (RVs need G_RVS ≲ 14); the RV-matched subsample (60% of the pairs) is brighter and not representative of the whole sample; the screen cannot measure an orbit; γ̂ here is the pipeline's γ_inf against the DR3-noise forward model on a few thousand pairs, so σ_fit is large and a change of a fraction of σ_fit is not resolved by one sample; the paired bootstrap measures the estimator's noise in Δγ̂, not any systematic. The DESI top-up and the El-Badry variant are separate later steps.

# ADDENDUM 1 (2026-09-30, after the primary result, written BEFORE any DESI radial velocity is combined with any pair): the DESI DR1 MWS variant

Requested by the orchestrator after the data chat's DESI cross-match (`data_assembly/DESI_MWS_CROSSMATCH_2026-09-30.md`, 2390b7409; the per-pixel files are downloaded, `desi_mws/desi_mws_matches.csv` and `desi_mws_epochs.csv`). **What has been seen:** the data chat's summary numbers (331 DESI rows match 317 components; both components with a good DESI RV in 18 pairs; a gain of 92 pairs with RVs on both components, 3,838 against 3,746 from Gaia alone; 22 of 131 multi-epoch stars with χ²/dof > 3 at a 1 km/s floor); no per-pair flag, no γ̂ with DESI RVs. Still EXPLORATORY: the DR3 γ̂ was seen; Amendment 7(e); no verdict words; not a test of any law; DESI RV precision (random 0.1 to 0.3 km/s, systematic floor 1 to 2 km/s) cannot measure orbits, only screen triples.

- **RV per component for the variant:** Gaia DR3 `radial_velocity` with its error where finite (as the primary); otherwise the DESI coadd RV of the match in `desi_mws_matches.csv` that passes the data chat's recommended cuts (`success` = 1, `rvs_warn` = 0, `rr_spectype` = STAR, `vsini` < 30; the best-S/N row if several), with error √(`vrad_err`² + floor²), **floor = 1 km/s for the `bright` and `main` programs and 2 km/s for `backup`** (the documented systematic floors; 2 km/s for everything as a sensitivity). The screen is cut 11's own expression on these RVs (the primary's flags are unchanged where both components have Gaia RVs: control C1).
- **Variable flag (a second, separate screen):** a component is an RV-variable candidate if it has at least two good single-epoch rows in `desi_mws_epochs.csv` (`success` = 1, `rvs_warn` = 0) and χ²/dof > 3 about its error-weighted mean RV with each epoch's error √(`vrad_err`² + 1²) (dof = epochs − 1). A pair is variable-flagged if either component is.
- **S_RV+ = the BEFORE pairs with an RV (Gaia or DESI) on both components.** Reported: N(S_RV+) and the gain over S_RV; the pairs flagged by the extended RV screen (of which from DESI RVs), the pairs variable-flagged (of which also RV-flagged), and γ̂ (the pipeline's estimator exactly as the primary) **before, after the extended RV screen, and after the extended screen plus the variable flag, all on S_RV+**, with the paired bootstrap SD of each change (B = 100) and both footings; plus the floor sensitivity (2 km/s for all DESI RVs).
- **Controls:** **C1** on the pairs with Gaia RVs on both components the variant's flags equal the primary's exactly; **C2** planted triples among DESI-RV components (dRV offset N(0, 3 km/s) on 10% of the unflagged S_RV+ pairs, 20 repeats): the empirical flagged fraction equals the analytic mean within 3 binomial SE; **C3** the variable rule on synthetic constant-RV stars (errors as tabulated, N = 2,000, 2 to 5 epochs) flags fewer than 5%, and on synthetic stars with a planted 5 km/s RV semi-amplitude flags at least 90% when at least 3 epochs are drawn at random phases; **MUTATE** (`MUTATE=1`): thresholds to infinity and the variable cut to infinity flag nothing and change γ̂ by exactly 0.
- **Limits:** the DESI sample is small (317 components, 92 extra pairs, 18 pairs with both DESI RVs, whose median |ΔRV| of 1.6 km/s is of the size of the systematic floors); single-epoch variability needs a floor assumption; the variable flag is a candidate list, not a triple identification.
