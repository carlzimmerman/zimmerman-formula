## 1. The question and the decision
Can a lens-redshift split of the weak-lensing RAR within ONE survey separate FLAT (zero shift of log g_obs at fixed g_bar) from a₀ ∝ H(z)? The rival's shift at fixed g_bar in the deep regime is Δlog g_obs = ½ log₁₀[E(z_B)/E(z_A)] (flat ΛCDM, Ω_m = 0.3, as in the proposal); a stellar-mass calibration offset COMMON to both bins cancels, only its DRIFT with z survives (a mass bias of Δ dex moves log g_obs at fixed inferred g_bar by ½ Δ in the deep regime). **Decision per (scenario, bin pair, budget level): POSSIBLE / NOT POSSIBLE for FLAT against a₀ ∝ H(z), the required per-bin precision, and the drift ceiling.** NOT POSSIBLE is a valid, likely answer. FLAT against the ΛCDM proxy is NOT decided here; the lane reports the minimum detectable shift per cell so that phase 1c's proxy shift can be compared.

## 2. Inputs allowed and forbidden (a rule of this lane, checked in the script)
- **Allowed:** lens redshifts, stellar masses and classes (`lr_lenses.npz`: `z`, `logM`, `typ`); the per-lens, per-g_bar-bin lensing weights and pair counts `WW`, `NN` and `gbar_edges` of `cfg110_perlens.npz` (**never `WG`**, the weighted-signal sum); the FULL-sample jackknife patch sums `wgE`, `W` of `lr_esd_jackknife.npz` (50 patches, 2 classes, 15 bins; the full sample, summed over classes, is not a z split); the published full-sample ESD of Brouwer+21 (`Fig-4-5-C1_RAR-KiDS-isolated_Nobins.txt`) only as a cross-check of the amplitude.

## 3. The signal
- **Bins:** K1 = the seven June g_bar bins 8 to 14 (centres 10^−12.90 to 10^−11.42 m/s², all ≪ a₀; fixed by the lens distribution in CFG61 before any comparison).
- **Per-bin shift under law L:** δ_j^L = log₁₀ ν(g_bar,j / (a₀ s_B^L)) − log₁₀ ν(g_bar,j / (a₀ s_A^L)), with the committed monotone kernel ν_mono (`CFG4_common.nu_mono`), a₀ canonical 9.3603e-11 m/s², g_bar,j the geometric bin centre, s^FLAT = 1, s^H(z) = E(z) = √(0.3 (1+z)³ + 0.7). In the deep limit δ_j → ½ log₁₀(E_B/E_A).
- **Bin pairs (nominal centres; ±0.05 in z windows):** P1 0.15 / 0.35 (deep-limit 0.024 dex), P2 0.2 / 0.5 (0.037), P3 0.2 / 0.7 (0.063), P4 0.3 / 1.0 (0.090). The lever L = (z_B − z_A)/0.3.
- **Statistic:** T = the generalised-least-squares common shift over the seven bins, T = aᵀ δ with a = C_Δ⁻¹ 1 / (1ᵀ C_Δ⁻¹ 1), C_Δ = C_A + C_B; its expected value under H(z) is signal_T = aᵀ δ^H, under FLAT 0; σ_stat = (1ᵀ C_Δ⁻¹ 1)^−½.

## 4. The statistical noise model (from the record's own weights; not a z-split signal)
- Full-sample jackknife (50 patches, both classes summed) → the ESD amplitude and covariance C_F (7×7) at K1; σ_rel,j = √C_F,jj / ESD_F,j; the correlation matrix R_F; log₁₀ errors σ_F,j = σ_rel,j / ln 10.
- For a lens-redshift window Z: W_Z,j = Σ over lenses with z in Z of `WW[l, j]`; the window's log₁₀ errors σ_Z,j = σ_F,j √(W_F,j / W_Z,j) (shape-noise scaling, error ∝ 1/√W); C_Z = D_Z R_F D_Z, D_Z = diag(σ_Z,j). The two windows of a pair are independent (disjoint lenses; sample variance between redshift windows neglected: declared). No Hartlap factor (the forecast's covariance is the truth by construction; declared).
- **Windows:** [z_c − 0.05, z_c + 0.05]; for the KiDS-bright sample (0.1 < z < 0.5, record) the windows are clipped to the sample, and the signal and the lever of a clipped window use the lens-mean redshift of the window.

## 7. The statistic, the rules and the outputs (frozen)
- **Mocks:** 200,000 draws per (scenario, pair, level), seed 2551: T = signal_T^L (1 − d_ℓ if L = H(z)) + N(0, σ_stat²) + Σ_t N(0, (b_t L / 2)²). The systematic draws are SHARED by the whole mock (the CFG219 lesson: the statistic sees them).
- **R1 (total scatter, marginalised):** separable iff P5[T | H(z)] > P95[T | FLAT]. Analytically signal_eff > 3.29 √(σ_stat² + σ_sys²); the Monte Carlo is the frozen statistic and must agree with the analytic value (control C6).
- **R2 (worst case, as CFG228 / CFG229):** separable iff signal_eff > S_joint + 2 σ_stat, S_joint = Σ_t b_t L (aligned), signal_eff = signal_T (1 − d_ℓ).
- **Decision:** **POSSIBLE iff R1 AND R2 hold**; a cell where R1 holds and R2 fails is printed 'NOT POSSIBLE (R1 only: marginalised, not robust)'; a cell where even σ_stat → 0 fails is 'UNREACHABLE at any precision'.
- **Per cell the lane also reports:** σ_stat; signal, signal_eff; S_joint; σ_sys; the **required precision** σ_T,req (R1: √(max(0, (signal_eff / 3.29)² − σ_sys²)); R2: (signal_eff − S_joint) / 2; the stricter) and the equivalent UNIFORM per-bin precision on log₁₀ g_obs σ_b,req = σ_T,req √(n / 2) with n = 7 (each K1 bin of each z window); **μ_req** = μ (σ_stat / σ_T,req)²; the **drift ceiling** (largest S_joint allowed: signal_eff − 2 σ_stat; largest σ_sys allowed: √((signal_eff / 3.29)² − σ_stat²)); the **minimum detectable shift** MDS_R1 = 3.29 √(σ_stat² + σ_sys²) / (1 − d), MDS_R2 = (S_joint + 2 σ_stat) / (1 − d) (for phase 1c's proxy).

## 9. Controls and MUTATE (all reported; a failure is reported plainly; first runs kept)
- **C1 identities.** The deep-limit signals (g_bar → 10⁻¹⁸) equal ½ log₁₀(E_B/E_A) to 1e-9 and the proposal's table (0.024, 0.037, 0.063, 0.090) to 3 decimals; the K1 exact-kernel signals lie in [0.90, 1.00] × the deep values.
- **C2 weights tie to the estimator.** Σ over all lenses of `WW[:, j]` equals Σ over patches and classes of `W[:, :, j]` of the jackknife file to 1e-9 for every K1 bin; 200 random halvings of the lenses give W_half / W_full = 0.5 ± 0.01.
- **C3 planted-law recovery (statistical noise only, budget off).** In the H(z)-true mocks the mean of T equals signal_T within 3 σ_stat / √N_mock and 90% (± 3%) of draws lie within ± 1.645 σ_stat of it; the FLAT-true mocks centre on 0 likewise.
- **C4 reactivity (the CFG219 lesson; a control that can fail).** With the systematic amplitude τ ∈ {0, 1, 3} the total-scatter statistic Z_tot = |⟨T_H⟩ − ⟨T_F⟩| / sd(T over all mocks) must fall by more than 30% from τ = 0 to τ = 3; the 'blind' statistic Z_blind = |median T_H − median T_F| / σ_stat (a median over mocks that ignores shared systematics) is reported beside and must stay within 3% (it is blind by construction: its flatness is what makes the control discriminating).
- **C5 planted drift (inside the run).** For the best cell (DE-30, P4, level A with the systematic terms set to zero) the decision is POSSIBLE; with one term equal to the rival's shift (b = signal_T / L) it must be NOT POSSIBLE.
- **C6 Monte Carlo against analytic.** The Monte Carlo margin P5[T | H] − P95[T | FLAT] equals signal_eff − 3.29 √(σ_stat² + σ_sys²) within 4 Monte Carlo standard errors for every cell.
- **C7 scaling identities.** σ_stat ∝ 1/√μ exactly (DE-30 against DE-3: √10 to 1e-9); halving every weight multiplies the variance by 2 (planted weights).
- **MUTATE=1:** an unbudgeted drift equal to the rival's shift (per pair) is added to the FLAT-true mocks only; every cell must come out not separable (H10). **MUTATE=2:** the shared systematic draws are replaced by independent per-bin draws that average away; C4 must FAIL (the control bites).

## 10. Limits (written before the run)
The budget is declared, not measured: a systematic that is not in it is invisible to the forecast (e.g. the stellar-mass mix at fixed g_bar changing with z, the ESD-to-g_obs profile-shape assumption, baryonic profile evolution); linear-in-z drifts; shape-noise scaling of the error with the record's weights (the jackknife covariance already contains the correlated parts of the full sample only); no sample variance between redshift windows; the DE scenarios are parameterised by μ, not by a survey specification; the K1 bins are the June machinery's (the record's lens sample, not Brouwer's 259,383); a₀ at its canonical footing. A POSSIBLE here would mean only that the declared budget and precision leave room, never that the measurement favours a law.
