# CFG255 phase 1a: data scoping for a lens-redshift split of the weak-lensing RAR (data front, 2026-09-30)

Request: the orchestrator's proposal `PROPOSAL.md` (00ee88eec). For each candidate: lens-z range and N, isolation criterion, stellar-mass method and its consistency across z, whether a z-split ESD/RAR is already released (with covariance), product sizes, and whether a z-split can be built from public catalogues and at what download size.
**Nothing was downloaded into the repository and no z-split number was looked at.** Only paper and release pages were read. The fetch tool cached five PDFs outside the repo (Brouwer+21 1.8 MB, Mistele & McGaugh 1.5 MB, Mistele+24 0.81 MB, DES Y3 MagLim 2.2 MB, HSC PDR3 photo-z note 0.87 MB), which I read as text. "How read" is marked per source: **text** = full text read; **abstract** = abstract page only; **page** = a web page through a summariser; **snippet** = a search snippet only (not citable).

## Bottom line
1. **No released product is z-split.** The only released lensing-RAR products (Brouwer+21, a 2.3 MB tarball of ESD text files) are split by stellar mass, Sersic index and colour, not by lens redshift. I found no paper that reports a lensing RAR or an isolated-galaxy ESD in lens-redshift bins at fixed M* or g_bar (a search, not an exhaustive survey).
2. **The only candidate with an existing isolation machinery and stellar masses is KiDS-bright + KiDS-1000**, but its lens range is only 0.1 < z < 0.5 (mean 0.2-0.25), so the rival's reachable shift inside one survey is the low end of the proposal's table, about 0.02-0.03 dex, against a stated stellar-mass uncertainty of 0.29 dex per galaxy and a 0.2 dex method-to-method systematic.
3. **A z-split from public catalogues is buildable for KiDS-bright**: lens catalogues 85 MB + 246 MB, shear catalogue 16 GB (21,262,011 sources), n(z) tarball (size not listed). The released ESD products cannot be re-split. The KiDS-GGL pipeline page says access requires contacting a named person; I have not and will not (an owner decision).
4. **DES Y3 and HSC Y3 reach z ~ 1** (MagLim 0.20-1.05; HSC deeper) but I found **no isolated-lens/lensing-RAR analysis and no stellar-mass product** in the DES Y3 cosmology products I read; HSC has stellar masses (Mizuki) but a small area (433.48 deg2). Neither is a ready route.

## 2. Quantities the proposal needs, from the sources
- **Rival size inside C1/C2.** Brouwer+21 compute that an evolving H(z) would change their ESD by only ~5% at the mean lens redshift ~0.2 (in the Emergent-Gravity context, whose scale also goes as H0). With the proposal's table (a0 ratio 1.115 between z=0.15 and 0.35, 0.024 dex) the order of magnitude is the same: a 5% ESD change is 0.021 dex. **That lever arm is small against the stellar-mass terms below.**
- **Stellar-mass budget, as stated by the papers:** 0.29 dex per-galaxy statistical (Brouwer), 0.2 dex method-to-method systematic, 0.056 dex offset to GAMA, morphology-dependent factor 1.0 (LTG) / 1.4 (ETG) between two analyses of the same KiDS photometry (Mistele). What none of the papers quantify, and what 1b must forecast: the drift of the mass scale with redshift inside 0.1-0.5 (rest-frame bands, k-corrections in the LePhare fit, photo-z scatter growing as 0.02(1+z), the flux limit r < 20 changing the mass-completeness and type mix with z).
- **Isolation across z:** the criterion uses 3-D distances from photo-z (sigma_z about 0.026) and a flux-limited sample; Mistele & McGaugh (2310.15248) say the isolation criterion "becomes less reliable at large radii" and use 4 Mpc/h70. A depth-dependent change of the neighbour census with z is not quantified in the sources.
- **Source-lens geometry:** KiDS sources 0.1 < zB < 1.2 (SOM-gold n(z) in 5 tomographic bins); DES sources and HSC sources reach behind z > 1 lenses; no source-geometry study for z-split was found.
