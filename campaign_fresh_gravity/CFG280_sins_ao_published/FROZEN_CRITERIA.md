## 4. Estimator and statistics (the programme's)
- **s\*** = CFG223's median-residual root (CFG229's a0implied, ν_mono, canonical 9.3603e-11; solver bracket log₁₀ s ∈ [−3, +3]); **status ROOT / FLOOR (median D ≤ 1) / CEILING (median D > 1 but s\* > 1000)** (`hzq_core.s_status`). - **Per-galaxy Monte Carlo (B = 10,000, seed = crc32(row label) mod 100000):** V_c and R_e by split normals with their published asymmetric errors (the table's V_c errors already carry the inclination, beam-smearing and σ₀ uncertainties; R_e is drawn once per sample for both g_obs and g_bar), log M\* by a normal with the declared σ = 0.20 dex (no error is tabulated), the PHIBSS M_mol by a log-normal with its CO flux error; percentiles over the draws with a root; the fraction without a root reported. **Pooled rows:** galaxy bootstrap, B = 10,000, central values; full-distribution percentiles.
