  not measured galaxy a₀ values.**
- A 3σ separation needs galaxy a₀ to 0.055–0.084 dex at z 1.0–2.33. No public sample reaches it (CFG685: best 0.4–0.5 dex).
- The one in-regime test, KURVS at z ≈ 1.5 (CFG160, ff3d9ea7d1), is "a conditional lean toward a₀ ~ H(z); not robust, not
  a kill" (governing wording, 74a55e85c). It flips with the unmeasured gas.

So "a₀ follows dark energy, not the expansion rate" is the law's claim. It is testable (CFG689 P6, one clean deep-MOND
point at z ≈ 2.5 to ≈0.10 dex), and untested.
