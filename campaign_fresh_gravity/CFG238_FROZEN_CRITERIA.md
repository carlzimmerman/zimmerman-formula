## 9. What would count as disagreement with CFG224 / CFG224b

- **Numbers:** any frozen pass line (section 4) missed after a second seed, other than the DISCRETENESS edges, is a disagreement and is reported with a diagnosis (table cell, definition, join, code); a count that differs by one row is a disagreement of N even if the statistics agree.
- **The z slopes:** the overlap or sample-flag estimator leaving 2 SE from zero in a case the README reports as consistent with zero; a selection mock with a plausible input producing a spurious slope larger than the README SE (then the claim is conditional); fewer than two overlap bins (the "fixed-luminosity" contrast is an extrapolation).
- **Circularity:** D10 not holding (then the catalogue masses do not agree exactly and the README's "by construction" is wrong in detail); a raw-ratio slope that the factor slopes hide; the ratio scatter not below the shuffled one; a median per-galaxy e_logMH2 above 0.10 in a bin while the README or a downstream lane quotes 0.03 to 0.10 as a per-galaxy mass error.
- **ACE:** the ACE-minus-Stripe82 offset moving by more than 0.3 dex across the declared prescription swaps, or the OLS extrapolation differing from the +1 to +1.7 slopes by more than 4 sigma, or the conversion-free ratio within 0.25 dex of the local one while the mass offset is larger: each is scored "the 0.2 to 0.7 dex figure is a prescription difference, not a measured z-evolution of a local prescription error".
- **The hat:** a quote of sigma_CO or sigma_dust without the N = 5 interval.
- **Wording:** any README sentence that (i) reads agreement as accuracy; (ii) reads K (an error of a mean scale) as a per-galaxy gas-mass error; (iii) reads "consistent with zero slope" as "no drift"; (iv) presents a K(z) comparison across different tracer pairs as a redshift trend; (v) treats the ACE bracket as an error bar; (vi) says the data favour a law.
