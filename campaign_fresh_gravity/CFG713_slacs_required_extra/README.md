**Reading.** A heavier IMF cannot fix SLACS: more stars steepen the profile, and the law already predicts slopes too steep (CFG712).
What the lenses require is an extra, extended cold component holding ~1/3 of the mass inside θE — about **3× the cold mass the law
supplies there** — after which the stellar masses are ordinary (Chabrier). The requirement scatters lens to lens (some need less than
the law; 14 need a cored, shallower-than-isothermal component). Corroborated independently by weak lensing: the KiDS early-type excess
at matched baryonic mass is robust on near-IR masses (owner chat's CFG669 / near-IR rescore). **Target for the settling/capture lanes:**
early-type galaxies carry ~2–3× the law's cold mass near R_e/2, with no clear dependence on size or stellar mass in this sample.
Limits: isothermal shape assumed for the extra; de Vaucouleurs light for all; γ is Etherington's global power-law fit.
