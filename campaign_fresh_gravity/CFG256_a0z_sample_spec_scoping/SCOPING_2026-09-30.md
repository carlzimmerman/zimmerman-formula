# CFG256: does any existing or near-term sample meet the requirement for measuring a0 at z >~ 1? (data-front scoping, 2026-09-30)

Request: the orchestrator's CFG240 requirement (`../CFG240_calibration_wall/README.md`, 7b73ef6a0; PAPER38 DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23073072). **Nothing was downloaded. No data were opened.** Only paper and release pages were read. "How read": **text** = full text read from a PDF that the fetch tool cached outside the repo; **abstract** = abstract page only; **page** = a web page through a summariser (wording is the summariser's); **snippet** = a search snippet only (not citable).

## The requirement, as written in CFG240 (checked against the README table)
With an unknown multiplicative baryon calibration f free, sigma(log a0) >= 3 sigma / sqrt(N) for any design (T4). At sigma = 0.1 dex, the target 0.1 dex needs N > 9 (N = 20 satisfies it). For the P2 kernel with N = 20, sigma = 0.1 dex: **y_min = 0.01 needs y_max* >~ 7.9; y_min = 0.001 needs y_max* >~ 5.5; y_min = 0.1 never reaches 0.1 dex (best about 0.117)**. A prior of 0.15 dex on log f lowers y_max* to 5.5 (y_min = 0.01). For nu_mono, y_min = 0.01, N = 20, sigma = 0.1: no stable break-even; y_min = 0.001 needs y >~ 17. N = 20 at sigma = 0.2 dex never reaches 0.1 dex (floor 0.134). (y = f g_bar / a0 at the true f and a0.)

## Bottom line
1. **No existing or near-term sample I could verify reaches both ends of the requirement (y <~ 0.01-0.1 and y >~ 5-8) at z >~ 1.** What exists splits cleanly: HI reaches the deep regime but only at z <~ 0.09 in the resolved samples; the z ~ 1-4 samples (Halpha, CO, [CII], lensed CO) are baryon-dominated where measured, i.e. y >~ 1, and none reports a deep-regime point.
2. **The best resolved low-z deep sample does not discriminate flat from H(z).** MIGHTEE-HI + LADUMA (130 galaxies, z <~ 0.09) finds a0 = (1.50 +- 0.05)e-10 m/s2, and on its own a1 = (-1.60 +- 2.33)e-10 m/s2 "consistent with no evolution". The H(z) rival has da0/dz = a0 dE/dz = 0.45 a0 = about 0.7e-10 per unit z at z = 0 for Omega_m = 0.3 (my arithmetic), 0.3 sigma of that a1 error. Adding SPARC as a z = 0 anchor gives a1 = (5.23 +- 1.05)e-10 (formal 5.0 sigma), which the authors themselves attribute "largely" to the different sample selection of the anchor (the MIGHTEE-only and SPARC-anchored fits differ at 2.5 sigma).
3. **High-z a0(z) from kinematics exists but is not the requirement's design**: MUSE-DARK III (79 galaxies, z 0.33-1.44) reports a1 = 1.59 +- 0.10 (e-10 m/s2) with a global a0(z~1) = 2.38 +- 0.1 (abstract); the sample is built from forward-modelled MUSE kinematics, with the stellar/gas decomposition inside the model, and this repository already carries a separate audit of the route dependence of that rise (see the CFG198/199 entries of the campaign record; not re-verified here).
4. **Near-term HI at z ~ 1 is a forecast only**: the SKA-Mid AA4 science chapter says a very deep (about 10,000 h) Band-1 survey would be needed to spatially resolve M_HI ~ 1e10 disks at z ~ 1, at about 1 arcsec; it gives no RAR or a0(z) forecast. ngVLA CO(1-0) work (Casey+18) uses a mock z ~ 4.5 galaxy, not a sample forecast.
5. **An external calibration prior of <~ 0.15 dex is the other route to shorten the design; it is not available at z >= 1.6** (see `data_assembly/GAS_ANCHOR_SCOPING_2026-09-30.md`: 0.35-0.7 dex per object by my arithmetic).

## Reading against the requirement
- The composition that the Fisher design wants (deep points and y >~ 8 in the same z bin) exists at **z ~ 0 only**: SPARC (up to y >> 8) plus MIGHTEE/LADUMA/WALLABY for the deep end. At z > 0.5 the available resolved samples supply only the high-y end.
- The only sample-level a0(z) results at z ~ 0.3-1.4 (MUSE-DARK III) and at z < 0.1 with a SPARC anchor (MIGHTEE) both mix different sample selections between z ends; Varasteanu+26 say so about their own anchored fit (2.5 sigma between anchored and un-anchored).
- By the CFG240 table, a sample of N = 20 at 0.1 dex needs y_max* >~ 8 together with y_min <~ 0.01 in each z bin (or a 0.15 dex prior and y_max* >~ 5.5). **No candidate above has a verified per-z-bin design that does this at z >~ 1.**
- Cross-reference: CFG255 (lensing RAR z-split) is the route that reaches deep points at z < 0.5 in an ensemble, not per galaxy.
