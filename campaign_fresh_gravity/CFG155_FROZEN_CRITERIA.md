- **Anisotropy.** For a point mass ρ_b = 0 at r > 0, so β = −(3/2) ρ_b/ρ̄_b = −½ dln M_b/dln r = 0. The ergodic inversion is the whole question at r > 0.
- **Formula.** CFG130 M1: f(E) = (1/(√2 π²)) ∫₀^∞ ρ″(Φ = E + t²) dt, with ρ″ = d²ρ_c/dΦ² analytic. No boundary term is written.

- **Zero point of Φ.** Not stated. The README's range matches Φ = asinh x − √(1+x²)/x in units √(G M_b a₀): Φ(1e-5) = −1.0e5, Φ(1e5) = 11.2 (a zero at r_M would give 11.7). A shift only translates E; it cannot change a sign.
- **Quoted values.** f/∫|integrand| = 1 (the integrand is positive at every point); the Kepler-cusp limit (8√2 π³)⁻¹ (−E)^(−1/2) "to 4 digits"; a round trip to ρ_c "to 4e-4"; no region with f < 0. Item 4 quotes d ln f/dE "from +0.3 to −2.0". The README quotes no minimum of f.

## Method (declared)

Units G = M_b = a₀ = 1: r_M = 1, speeds in v_M = (G M_b a₀)^(1/4), energies in v_M², f in M_b r_M⁻³ v_M⁻³. The problem depends on x and E/v_M² only, so the sign is the same on both footings and at every mass (C6). No constant is introduced.
- **Target functions** (derived by hand; C5 verifies them): g = √(1+x²)/x²; Φ = asinh x − √(1+x²)/x, so Φ → −1/x at the centre (the point-mass zero) and Φ ≈ ln 2x − 1 outside; ρ_c = 1/(4π x √(1+x²)); dρ_c/dΦ = −(1+2x²)/(4π(1+x²)²); d²ρ_c/dΦ² = x⁵/(π (1+x²)^(7/2)).
- **Every profile** (target, controls, MUTATE) is declared by ρ(x) and g(x); dρ/dΦ = (dρ/dx)/g and d²ρ/dΦ² are generated by sympy, so all profiles share one code path.
- **ρ_c as a function of Φ.** x(Φ) by a safeguarded Newton inversion of the monotone Φ(x) in ln x; ρ_c(Φ) = ρ_c(x(Φ)).
- **Eddington's formula** (Binney & Tremaine 2008, §4.3.1, eq. 4.46; recalled). Relative convention Ψ = −Φ, ℰ = −E, with Φ₀ = 0 because the potential has no finite value at infinity: f(ℰ) = (1/(√8 π²)) d/dℰ ∫ (dρ/dΨ) dΨ/√(ℰ − Ψ), from Ψ_min = −∞ to ℰ. Equivalently f(E) = (1/(√8 π²)) d/dE ∫ from E to Φ_max of (dρ/dΦ) dΦ/√(Φ − E), with Φ_max = +∞.
- **Boundary term.** The second form adds −(1/(√8 π²)) (dρ/dΦ at Φ_max)/√(Φ_max − E). It is zero here: dρ_c/dΦ → −1/(2πx²) → 0. Its value is printed for every profile.
- **The logarithmic divergence.** Φ grows as ln x, so every orbit is bound and there is no escape energy. No truncation radius is introduced; the integrals run to Φ = +∞ and converge because ρ_c ∝ e^(−2Φ) outside. The x-integral stops at x_max = max(1e14, 1e8 x_E); the dropped tail is below (1/(2√2π³)) / (2 x_max² √(Φ(x_max) − E)), which must be under 1e-10 f(E) at every grid energy (printed).
- **Three routes to f** (the same formula, different numerics):
  - A: the second form in t (Φ = E + t²); 64-node Gauss–Legendre on segments ending at x_E × 10^(k/4). No singularity.
  - B: the second form in x, f = (1/(2√2π³)) ∫ from x_E to ∞ of x³ dx / ((1+x²)³ √(Φ(x) − E)) for the target. QUADPACK with the weight (x − x_E)^(−1/2) on [x_E, 2x_E], the ratio (Φ(x) − E)/(x − x_E) formed without cancellation, then half-decade segments to x_max; epsabs = 0, epsrel = 1e-11.
  - C: the first form: A(E) = 2 ∫₀^∞ (dρ/dΦ)(E + t²) dt by Route A's quadrature, then f = (1/(√8π²)) dA/dE by a 5-point central difference with step 1e-3 × s(E), s(E) = min(max(1, |E|), E − Φ(0), Φ_max − E). It never uses d²ρ/dΦ².
- **Grid.** E_i = Φ(x_i), x_i log-spaced at 100 per decade on [1e-6, 1e6] (1201 energies): Route B at all, Routes A and C at every 10th. The sign of d²ρ_c/dΦ² is checked at 16001 points on x in [1e-8, 1e8].
- Tolerances and settings are frozen here and are not changed after a result is seen.

## MUTATE

MUTATE=1 replaces ρ_c by a cored tracer in the unchanged target potential: ρ_mut = 1/(4π √(x² + x_core²) √(1+x²)), x_core = 0.1. Same code, grid and criteria; its derivatives come from sympy like every profile's.
- Declared expectation. ρ_mut falls outward, so the simple necessary condition dρ/dΦ ≤ 0 holds. But its inner slope is 0 in a potential that is Keplerian at the centre. For ρ ∝ Ψ^γ with Ψ = GM/r, Eddington gives f ∝ Γ(γ+1)/Γ(γ−½) ℰ^(γ−3/2), negative for 0 ≤ γ < ½. So f < 0 at the most bound energies (x_E below roughly x_core), with f → −ρ₀/(4√2π²) (−E)^(−3/2), ρ₀ = 1/(4π x_core). The crossing energy is not predicted.
- The MUTATE bites only if Route B returns f < −1e-6 × ∫|integrand| at some grid energy, so that H1(b) fails and the run exits 1. H1(a) also fails (ρ_mut″ < 0 in the core); that alone does not count. The deep asymptote is checked within 5% at x_E ≤ 1e-4.
- C1, C2, C3, C5 and C6 do not use ρ_mut and must still pass. The round trip is an identity for either sign of f and must still hold. The Kepler limit, the σ² = V_c²/2 identity and Q2–Q3 are expected to fail and are reported.
