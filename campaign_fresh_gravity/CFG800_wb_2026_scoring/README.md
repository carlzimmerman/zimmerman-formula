The prediction is CFG740, sealed 2026-10-13 and unedited: the stiffness gives essentially no boost, with velocity boost ≤ 3.2e-4
at 30 kAU. Its falsifier is a boost ≥ 1.01 at ≤ 50 kAU.

**Disclosed:** Cookson et al. report that extending their sample to 150 pc without the ipd cut makes MOND 12× preferred. With
the cut, Newton is 88× preferred.

**Reading:** the framework sides firmly with the no-boost camp. It agrees with neither standard MOND (20%) nor ΛCDM-with-nothing
by accident: its "no boost" comes from the Cassini-forced stiffness.

**Gaia DR4 decision rule** (frozen before 2026-12-02). R = median ṽ beyond the MOND radius (≤ 50 kAU) ÷ the Newtonian-regime
normalization, measured with the Cookson quality checklist and a calibrated triple fraction.
- **FRAMEWORK KILLED** if R − 1 ≥ 0.01 at ≥ 3σ.
- **CONSISTENT** if |R − 1| < 0.01 at 2σ. Standard MOND is then excluded.
- **UNDECIDED** otherwise.
