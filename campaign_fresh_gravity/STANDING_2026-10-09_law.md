# Standing of the gravity law, 2026-10-09 (evening)

Candidate B: the law switched off outside bound halos, plus cold energy. κ = ½ is fitted (current estimate
0.42 ± 0.10, CFG493). The footings 9.36e-11 and 1.13e-10 m/s² are never pooled. The cold energy's mass is still
required. This is not a closed theory. Every line cites a committed lane; numbers are canonical / alt.

## Verdict in one sentence
The law works for disc galaxies, the Milky Way's vertical force, clusters, satellites and large-scale growth; it
fails around massive early-type galaxies at ≈ 50–100 kpc by ≈ 30–40% in mass, no measured baryons close the gap,
and the cause is not yet found.
