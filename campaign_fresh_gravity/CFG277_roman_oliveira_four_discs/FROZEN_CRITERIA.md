## 3. g_obs, g_bar and the gas route
- **g_obs (headline, pressure-corrected):** V_c² = V_ext² + 3.36 σ_ext² (the SINS convention; the disc scale is not tabulated, so the Burkert 2σ²R/r_d term cannot be formed), g_obs = V_c²/r_ext. **The rotation-only reading (α = 0, CFG197's primary) is a knob and a reported column.** V_ext is the authors' inclination-corrected rotation speed; the inclination errors are not on disk (named systematic).
- **Baryons (CFG197's geometry, kept for comparability):** g_bar = f_enc G (M_gas [+ M\*])/r_ext², a point mass with the fraction **f_enc = 0.5** of the baryons enclosed (CFG197's primary; knobs 0.25 and 1.0). **M_gas = the CO-based M_mol of the tables with no helium factor** (the α_CO of the papers includes helium; a ×1.36 knob). **Class S: the gas mass is not a limit** (α_CO / α_[CII] / α₈₅₀ vary), so a root is not automatically an upper bound; **where the stars are missing the baryon side is a lower limit on the baryons only if the gas mass is right**, so each row's status is read with the gas bands of §5.
