## What this lane cannot say
It does not measure a₀ at z = 4.4. The rows are near-Newtonian (CFG240 T3), the pair is a close pair (tides) with blended photometry, the SED masses are of unequal reliability (a preprint with a deferred method; a blended two-band fit; an AGN-contaminated fit), the two SGP-1 masses differ by 0.48 dex, the SGP gas masses are "approx", and the geometry is a point mass with half the baryons enclosed (a ±0.3 dex lever on g_bar). A floor with the lowest independent baryon set is robust against any higher set; a root is an upper bound only if the gas mass and the SED mass are right; class S carries no law statement.
