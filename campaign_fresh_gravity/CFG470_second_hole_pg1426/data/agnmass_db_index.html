Description of the Black Hole Mass Calculations

Black hole masses are calculated as:

MBH = < f > R V2 / G

R is taken to be c τcent, the speed of light times the emission-line time delay measured from the centroid of the cross-correlation function around the peak, which is a measure of the light-crossing time between the broad emission line region and the (much smaller) accretion disk. V is taken to be σline,rms, the second moment of the emission line profile in the variable part of the spectrum, which is a measure of the line-of-sight velocity of the gas responding to the continuum variations. G is the gravitational constant.

The scale factor < f > is the population-average multiplicative scaling factor required to bring the M-σ* relationship for AGNs into agreement with the relationship for quiescent galaxies. Details can be found in the references provided for each determination of < f > above. The uncertainty on < f > is not included in the determination of the final mass uncertainty.

The final black hole masses provided here are the weighted averages of the individual masses determined from all the emission lines from all reverberation experiments for a specific object. The user is also presented with the black hole mass based only on measurements of Hβ on the detailed page for each object. Masses based on other emission lines or combinations of emission lines may be calculated by the user from the reverberation measurements included on the detailed page for each object. Please note that other combinations of reverberation measurements may require the adoption of different values of < f >

Please refer to the PASP article describing the database for more information.
