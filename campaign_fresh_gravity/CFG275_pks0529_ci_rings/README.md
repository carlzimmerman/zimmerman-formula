# CFG275 — PKS 0529-549 (z = 2.5706): seven of eight independent-baryon rows sit at the baryon floor; the eighth is a vacuous bound; the curve cannot constrain a₀

**Bottom line.** The five-ring [CI](2-1) circular-velocity curve of Lin et al. (arXiv:2411.08958) has V_c ≈ 335 km/s at R ≈ 1.1 kpc and ≈ 260–270 km/s from 1.9 kpc outwards, so the dynamical mass inside 3.3 kpc is about 5.6 × 10¹⁰ M☉. **Every independent baryon set that includes gas, or the SED stellar mass, has more Newtonian pull than that at the median ring**: the four SED-stellar-mass rows have median D = g_obs/g_bar of 0.13–0.27, and three of the four preliminary-CIGALE rows (with gas) 0.24–0.56; **only the lowest set, CIGALE stars with no gas (a lower limit on the baryons), has a root, s\* ≤ 1.85, and it is ILL-CONDITIONED (recipe half-width 1.6 dex; 44 % of Monte Carlo draws have no root)**. The accelerations are high at every ring (y = g_bar/a₀ = 5.8–560 over the rows), so there is no lever on a₀ at all (CFG240 T3). This is the same tension the authors discuss (SED mass 3× their dynamical upper limit; three gas tracers 4× apart), not a statement about the gravity law. κ = ½ is FITTED. No law statement is made.

**Chart row (fixed by the frozen rule): `PKS0529 [CIG; CO]` = FLOOR, median D = 0.555, z = 2.5706** (89 % of the Monte Carlo draws have no root; the 11 % that do give a conditional 68 % interval [1.09, 13.2], which is not a bound on the row; the baryons would have to fall by 0.28 dex for s\* = 1). **How strong is a floor here?** The SELFTEST (a world on the law at s_true = 2 with 0.15 dex scatter) puts the SED rows at the floor in 37–50 % of 100 worlds, because y = 25–560 leaves ν − 1 ≈ 0.001–0.02: **a floor with median D near 1 would not be informative. The real medians are 0.13–0.56, i.e. 0.25–0.9 dex below 1 (against a 0.15 dex scatter), so they are floors in the sense of an over-counted baryon budget.** The kinematic readings (V_c constant σ, varying σ, V_rot) move the median g_obs by +0.001 / +0.008 / −0.015 dex (HE7); the stellar geometry is the largest baryon-side lever at the inner rings (an exponential disc of R_e = 4.7 kpc instead of the MaxDisk bulge + disc lowers g_bar by 1.8 dex at the innermost ring). No knob turns a FLOOR row into a rooted one (0 of 9–12 knobs rooted for the seven floor rows).

## What this lane cannot say
It does not measure a₀ at z = 2.57. The accelerations are high at every ring; the stellar size and B/D are unmeasured; the SED mass (an elliptical template for a starburst AGN host) is 3× the authors' dynamical upper limit and the CIGALE re-fit is preliminary; the three gas tracers disagree by a factor 4; the five rings are correlated; the disc may be out of equilibrium after a merger, or two galaxies may be aligned (the [OIII] redshift differs by ~350 km/s), in which case V_c is not a potential tracer; the gas profile was fitted only at R < 5.8 kpc and the finite gas thickness is ignored. A floor with the lowest independent baryon set is robust against any higher set; a root with stars only is an upper bound; class S/L carries no law statement.
