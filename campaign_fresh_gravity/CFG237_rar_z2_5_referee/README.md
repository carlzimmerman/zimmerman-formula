**(3) S1 g_obs < g_bar: geometry, mass or alpha_CO (algebra, then the 9,800-cell frozen grid).**
- **Algebra (verified to 1e-9):** at r = 2 R_e, y = r/(2 R_d) = 1.678 for every source, so g_bar = c0 G M_bar/R_e^2 with c0 = 0.2836 universal; **D scales exactly as R_e at fixed V and M, as (sin i / sin i_new)^2, and as 1/(1 + gas share x (alpha/0.92 - 1)) in alpha_CO.**
- **Per galaxy:** D = 0.36, 0.31, 0.22, 0.65, 0.57, 0.52, 0.095, 1.64, 2.23 (007.1, 022.1, 041.1, 049.1, 065.1, 066.1, 071.1, 075.1, 122.1). The five stellar-dominated sources (007.1, 022.1, 041.1, 066.1, 071.1; gas share 0.05-0.20) have stars-only D 0.10-0.65: **with alpha_CO = 0 the median D is only 0.65, so alpha_CO cannot produce, or repair, the anomaly**; the gas-dominated four (049.1, 065.1, 075.1, 122.1; gas share 0.67-0.79) have stars-only D 2.0-8.0 and end at D 0.65, 0.57, 1.64, 2.23. Four sources have M* above the authors' M_dyn(<10 kpc) (007.1, 022.1, 041.1, 071.1; M*/M_dyn 1.7-5.7; 071.1 is the AGN-flagged SED, log M* 12.31); M_bar/M_dyn(<10 kpc) >= 1 in 4 of 9 (median 0.87; below the frozen >= 5 of 9 line for "MASS-LEVEL"); enclosed baryons exceed V^2 (2 r_e)/G in 7 of 9.
- **Single axes needed for median D = 1 (others at baseline): R_e x 1.93, or M* -0.71 dex on all nine, or an inclination 17 degrees lower; alpha_CO: not reachable (alpha = 0 gives 0.653).** Inside the ordinary ranges no single axis restores (alpha_CO 0.6-1.5, |b| <= 0.2, |di| <= 5, s <= 1.33). 3,238 of 9,800 cells restore (median D >= 1 and >= 5 of 9 with D >= 1); the lowest-cost restoring cell (cost 1.74 in declared 1-sigma units) is alpha_CO 0.60, inclination -5 degrees, stellar R_e,* = 2 R_e; 69 joint cells inside the ordinary ranges restore (they use the stellar-disc size or the pressure term, m* = 2 or k = 3.36).
- **Verdict by the frozen rule: JOINT / NOT DECIDABLE (minimum cost 1.74).** Plainly: alpha_CO is excluded as the cause; geometry and stellar mass are not separable with these tables, and the anomaly needs a modest joint move (a larger stellar disc, a few degrees of inclination, a lower alpha_CO or M* zero point) or a single large one (R_e x 1.9, M* -0.7 dex, i -17 degrees). D >= 1 is only the Newtonian floor: the cells with |median delta_FLAT| < 0.1 number 2,227 (lowest cost 1.39). **My frozen estimates here were wrong three times and are kept:** alpha_CO "about 0.3" (phase-1 guess from three gas-rich rows I had seen), the mass-level line (4 of 9, not >= 5), and my coded "no joint ordinary-range cell" line (69 cells restore; the single-axis wording held).
- Wording consequence for CFG227: "the baryon model (massive gas-rich discs at compact CO radii) or the masses (alpha_CO = 0.92, SED M*) overshoot" is half right: the overshoot sits in the SED M* (and geometry), not in the gas; it "says something about the compilation's baryons", not about a0, stands.

**(4) CRISTAL independent-route medians and the 1e-16.** From the tables with my own code (route g_bar,ind = rho g_bar,fit, rho = M*/((1 - f_molgas) M_fit); R_e: V_circ^2 = V_rot^2 + 3.36 sigma_0^2; R_out: V_tot^2/R and V_bary^2/R from the vector summary) the class-L medians reproduce CFG227's JSON to 1e-9 (R_e flat +0.1175, proxy -0.0853, H(z) -0.1443; R_out +0.1058, -0.1359, -0.2030). **An independent code path (curves + PCHIP/linear interpolation at Rout/Re x Re; R_e from the curves) agrees to 1e-3 (R_e) and 5e-4 (R_out), so a code path that does not share the row builder lands at 1e-3 to 1e-4, not 1e-16.** What the 1e-16 agreement shows: CFG227's rows come from `exec_lane` of the CFG213 and CFG220 scripts and C5 compares them with CFG222's JSON (same machinery, same tables, same estimator): it shows the import works and the rows were not altered. **What it does not show:** that the rows are right (an error in a table cell or in the row builder would reproduce perfectly), anything about the class-L label, or an independent confirmation (scored IDENTITY). What my agreement shows: the tables and the route definition reproduce the numbers with different code; it shares the tables, the route definition, the kernel and the authors' fit. Extra facts: the median of six is the midpoint of the 3rd and 4th deltas (R_e flat +0.010 and +0.225, a 0.215 dex gap): dropping any one disc moves the flat median to +0.010 or +0.225 (R_out -0.021 or +0.232); a +0.05 change in one disc's f_molgas moves it by 0.015 at most; rho spans 0.34-1.22 (two discs, 02 and 03, have D_ind about 0.97 at R_e); 5 of 6 discs are above z = 5.

**(5) What C7 can and cannot detect.** It is an identity: a factor common to all tracers cancels in every tracer ratio, so "d_AB unchanged to 1e-9" (CFG227 gets 0.0: its d_AB is (a + 0.3) - (b + 0.3)) is not a finding. **It CAN detect a tracer-specific bias** (only CO +0.30: d_AB and d_AC move by exactly +0.30). **It CANNOT detect a common-mode bias of any size, constant or z-dependent.** My mocks (20 galaxies, 2,000 mocks, three tracers with 0.08 dex scatter): the group-median level moves by the lever (-0.155 at +0.30 for gas share 0.5; -0.069 at gas share 0.2, which is the regime of CFG227's "-0.07" (its C7 set is the S1 list repeated three times, first 20 rows, placed on the flat law at the true gas mass); the S1 data themselves move by -0.12 at +0.213); **within-class slope vs z is unchanged (-0.0001 per unit z) for a z-independent gas share and moves by -0.0044 per unit z (s.e. 0.0001) when the gas share rises with z**. **A z-dependent common-mode bias tau_c(z) = b_z log10((1+z)/3.5) with b_z = -1.12 (+0.075 dex at z = 2, -0.26 dex at z = 5) gives flat-true mocks the slope of H(z)-true mocks (0.0419 per unit z) and d_AB does not see it (1.8e-15); it lies outside the class-M band (+-0.03 / +-0.093)** (the level differs: +0.047 against +0.154 in the H(z)-true set). So the class-M band is a band on tracer AGREEMENT, not on the absolute gas scale.
