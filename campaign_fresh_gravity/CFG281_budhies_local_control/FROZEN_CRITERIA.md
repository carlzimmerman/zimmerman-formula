**This file is committed before any CFG281 script exists and before any local width has been loaded.** The BUDHIES chain (CFG260) gave s\* = 0.260 (68 % 0.215–0.397; 95 % 0.109–0.520) for the primary set PC (N = 32, z̄ = 0.196, rest-frame widths, the frame now resolved: `CFG260_budhies_a0_z02/README.md`, Addendum 2026-10-01) — 3.8 times below the canonical scale and 4.9 times below the local value — and was labelled "not a calibrated point". > **κ = ½ FITTED. a₀(z) FLAT is the framework's distinctive law, a₀ ∝ H(z) the rival. This lane measures what the CHAIN returns on a local sample whose a₀ is known empirically (the SPARC RAR value 1.20 × 10⁻¹⁰ m s⁻², s_ref = 1.20/0.93603 = 1.282 on the canonical footing, in the deep regime where ν_mono equals the exponential RAR kernel), and so the chain's bias; it does NOT test a law, and the BUDHIES/local ratio it reports is a pure ratio of two chain outputs. ## 3. The chain (identical to CFG260 §3 where the inputs exist; the sample-specific inputs are declared here)
Per galaxy:
1. **Width** W = `w50_kms`. 2. **Frame and corrections:** W_c = (W − δ)/(1 + z)^k with **δ_L = 9 km s⁻¹ (the turbulent part of CFG260's 28 = 19 instrumental + 9 turbulent; the ALFALFA W50 is already corrected for instrumental broadening and smoothing, the BUDHIES R4 widths are not: UNVERIFIED reading of the catalogue's definitions) and k = 1 (the ALFALFA W50 carries no (1 + z) correction, so the rest-frame width is W50/(1 + z), the frame of the BUDHIES widths; z = `vhel_kms`/c)**; knobs below. 3. **Inclination:** sin i_eff = sin 60° = 0.8660 for every galaxy (identical to CFG260), V = W_c/(2 sin i_eff). 4. **Gas:** M_gas = 1.33 M_HI, M_HI = 10^`logmhi` (the catalogue's distances, H₀ = 70; H₂ ignored). 5. **Stars (the sample-specific step):** M\* = 10^`logms_taylor`, the colour-based stellar mass of the catalogue (Taylor+11 form: g − i and the absolute i magnitude, Chabrier IMF; the BUDHIES B − R / Bell & de Jong step has no counterpart because the local photometry is SDSS g − i and i; the IMF scales differ by ≈ 0.05–0.10 dex, covered by the zero-point knob). 6. **Radius:** D_HI = 10^(0.506 log₁₀ M_HI − 3.293) kpc, R = D_HI/2, all baryons inside R (identical). 7. **Accelerations:** g_bar = G M_b/R², g_obs = V²/R, D = g_obs/g_bar. 8. **Regime:** y = g_bar/a₀ at R; the fraction below 0.3. 9. **s\*:** CFG223's solver (bisection on log₁₀ s ∈ [−3, 3], 64 iterations; ν_mono; canonical 9.3603e-11). 10. **BTFR route (reported):** s\*_BTFR = median_i V_i⁴/(G M_b,i)/a₀. 11. **Bands:** baryon bands ± 0.15 and ± 0.30 dex; **recipe knobs, one at a time, half-width = quadrature** (the local analogues of CFG260's): δ (0, 18 km s⁻¹), sin i_eff (0.80, 0.92), M\* zero point (±0.25 dex on M\* only), R scale (±0.15 dex), distance scale (H₀ = 67.4, 73.0; M_HI and M\* scale as D²); the K-correction knob has no local analogue.

## 7. What this lane cannot say
It does not say a₀(z) evolves or not: it measures the chain's output on a local sample. The stellar step, δ, the selection and the survey sensitivity differ between the two samples; the local galaxies are field and group spirals while the BUDHIES PC is a cluster-outskirts, strongly selected set of 32; the isotropic inclination assumption is common to both (its cost is measured locally by V5, not for BUDHIES); the HI size–mass relation and the colour recipe are declared inputs; ALFALFA widths and BUDHIES widths are measured on different profiles with different resolutions.
