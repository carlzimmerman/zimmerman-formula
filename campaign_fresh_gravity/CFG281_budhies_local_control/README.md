# CFG281 — the BUDHIES chain on a same-pipeline local HI sample: it recovers the local a₀ (s\* = 1.45, +0.05 dex from the SPARC scale) but is NOT CALIBRATED by the frozen precision rule, and BUDHIES's s\* sits 5.6× below the local control

**Bottom line.** The identical width → V → baryon chain of CFG260, run on 1,370 ALFALFA code-1 detections (M_HI ≥ 3 × 10⁹ M☉, 20–85 Mpc, SDSS-based stellar masses) and **mass-matched to the BUDHIES primary set PC** by its log M_HI quartiles, returns **s\* = 1.449 (68 % 1.288–1.524, 95 % 1.210–1.661)**, i.e. a₀ = 1.36 × 10⁻¹⁰ m s⁻²: **+0.053 dex above the SPARC-empirical scale (s = 1.282), so the chain is right locally to about 13 %.** The frozen calibration rule nevertheless says **NOT CALIBRATED** because two precision clauses fail by small margins (recipe half-width 0.216 against ≤ 0.20 dex; distance-window drift 0.105 against ≤ 0.10 dex); the level clause passes. **BUDHIES/local: ρ = s\*_B(PC, rest 0.2602) / s\*_LC = 0.180 (log₁₀ ρ = −0.746 ± 0.166 from the two bootstraps, ± 0.391 with both recipe widths 0.28 and 0.216): a pull of −4.5σ against ρ = 1 (FLAT) and −4.7σ against H(z) on statistics alone, −1.9σ and −2.0σ with the recipe widths.** ρ is a ratio of two chain outputs, meaningful only if the chain's biases are the same in the two samples (not tested: ALFALFA and BUDHIES widths, selections and stellar-mass steps differ), so it is not an a₀(z) measurement. **By the owner's rule the BUDHIES row stays OFF the chart** (the chain is NOT CALIBRATED), and the file `cfg281_points_stageB.csv` contains only the two local-control rows (matched and unmatched), labelled "local control of the BUDHIES chain, NOT a high-z point". κ = ½ is FITTED. No law statement is made.

## What this does and does not say
- **The chain works locally.** At the PC's own M_HI distribution the chain reproduces the local a₀ to +0.053 dex; the unmatched sample gives +0.017 dex. The SELFTEST (fabricated widths on the law at s = 2 with isotropic inclinations) reads the chain's own estimator 0.023 dex low (+0.278 against +0.301; coverage 90 % of 100 worlds), so the estimator's median-of-sin²i argument holds to a few per cent.
- **Why "NOT CALIBRATED".** Clause (ii): the recipe half-width is dominated by the stellar-mass zero point (0.149) and sin i_eff (0.133); the local set has a stars-to-gas mass ratio of 0.75, so a ±0.25 dex stellar-mass change is worth 0.15 dex. Clause (iii): the 20–50 Mpc window (N = 256) reads 1.61 and the 50–85 Mpc window (N = 1,116) 1.26: a decline of 0.105 dex with distance, **not explained by the mass (V2: M_HI ≥ 5 × 10⁹ gives 1.38 against 1.33 unmatched)**. **If that trend continued log-linearly (slope ≈ −0.37 per dex of distance) to the BUDHIES distance it would account for roughly 0.4–0.5 dex of the 0.75 dex deficit; this is an observation on two windows, not a result: the nearer window is small, flow-model distances are least certain there, and the extrapolation over a factor of ten in distance is not validated.** The frozen rule does not allow me to call the chain calibrated; the level and the numbers above are what the reader needs.
- **What ρ is.** BUDHIES's s\* = 0.260 (rest-frame, N = 32, bootstrap SD 0.162 dex, recipe half-width 0.28) against the identically built local value 1.449: a factor 5.6 (0.75 dex). **No recipe knob of the local chain moves s\* by more than 0.15 dex; the BUDHIES deficit is therefore NOT a bias of the chain at local masses.** The sample-specific inputs that differ (the instrumental correction δ = 28 against 9 km s⁻¹ because the ALFALFA W50 is already instrument-corrected and the BUDHIES R4 widths are not; the stellar-mass step B − R against g − i; the detection selection, the survey sensitivity, the profile S/N, a cluster-outskirts sample against field and group spirals) are the candidates, and none is tested here. **The reading "a₀ at z = 0.2 is 5.6 times lower" is not made: the recipe widths alone put the ratio at −1.9σ from 1 and the same-pipeline assumption is untested.**
- **The M5 control is exact:** this script's chain core, fed the BUDHIES PC arrays with CFG260's recipe, reproduces CFG260's s\* = 0.260210 to 1e-6, so the control implements the same chain.

## Provenance and process (see `FROZEN_CRITERIA.md`)
- **Data.** ALFALFA–SDSS (Durbala+2020, AJ 160, 271; VizieR J/AJ/160/271) joined to the ALFALFA α.100 catalogue (Haynes+2018, ApJ 861, 49; J/ApJ/861/49): the owner-approved download of 2026-10-01, 11.7 MB, logged with sha256 in `data_assembly/FETCH_MANIFEST_2026-10-01.jsonl`; the parsed CSV (31,503 rows, 49 columns, sha256 `c790a7ec…9ed2`, rebuilt byte-identically by its parser) is in `data_assembly/alfalfa_sdss_local_control/`. The widths were loaded only at stage B.
- **Selection (frozen, no width cut):** in both catalogues 31,500 → HI code 1 25,432 → M_HI ≥ 3 × 10⁹ 15,935 → 20–85 Mpc 1,518 → complete photometry and a valid flux 1,370 (bins of the PC's quartiles: 642 / 141 / 154 / 60 galaxies; 370 below the PC minimum, 3 above its maximum). Chain: W_c = (W50 − 9)/(1 + z) (δ_L = 9 km s⁻¹ because ALFALFA's W50 is already instrument-corrected; k = 1 because it carries no (1 + z) correction); V = W_c/(2 sin 60°); M_gas = 1.33 M_HI; M\* = the catalogue's Taylor+11 colour mass; R = D_HI/2 from CFG260's HI size–mass relation; CFG223's s\*.
- **Process (everything kept).** Criteria b766cf43e (with the data files); stage A + script bb6956707 (5/5 controls; matched median y 0.048, gas fraction 0.48; HE1, HE2 hit); SELFTEST 1eed4e943; measurement (once), MUTATE=1, MUTATE=2 and this README: see the commit that adds this file. **Disclosure:** while setting the thresholds I counted rows on the non-width columns (1,518 / 1,370 and the bin counts above); no width was seen before the measurement.

## Controls and hand estimates, as they fell
- **Pass:** C1–C5 (stage A: loader, the catalogue's M_HI identity to 0.0059 dex, estimators equal to CFG223's and cfg260_core's, the noiseless world to 2e-16 dex on L and 200 matched resamples, the matched bins' weights), M1, **M5**, M3 (reported: matched and unmatched differ by 0.036 dex, inside the 0.216 band), M4; **MUTATE=1** (widths × 1.1892: +0.3225 dex against 0.30 ± 0.05) and **MUTATE=2** (sin i_eff 0.70: +0.3957 against 0.37 ± 0.06).
- **Hand estimates (§8):** HE1 hit, HE2 hit (stage A); HE3 hit (s\*_LC 1.449 in [0.6, 2.0]); **HE4 MISS** (CALIBRATED: the two precision clauses fail); HE5 hit (the b/a inclination variant moves s\* by 0.070 dex); **HE6 MISS** (the McGaugh stellar masses move it by 0.133 dex, GSWLC by 0.078, against ≤ 0.08); HE7 hit (ρ = 0.180 in [0.10, 0.40]); **HE8 MISS** (drift 0.105 > 0.10); HE9 hit (M5 and both MUTATE responses). My expectation that the chain would calibrate was wrong on the precision clauses and right on the level.

## What this lane cannot say
It does not say a₀(z) evolves or not. The local sample is field and group spirals at 20–85 Mpc; the BUDHIES PC is 32 cluster-outskirts galaxies at z = 0.2 with HI widths from a different instrument and S/N, a different stellar-mass step and a detection selection that the local control does not reproduce; the isotropic inclination is common to both (its cost is measured locally by V5, +0.07 dex, not for BUDHIES); ALFALFA's distances come from flow models without a method flag; the GSWLC stellar masses exist for only 406 of the 1,370 galaxies. The distance trend is two windows. No chart row for BUDHIES is written.
