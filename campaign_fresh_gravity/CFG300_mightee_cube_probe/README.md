# CFG300 — the MIGHTEE-HI DR1 cube probe: can resolved rotation curves be extracted from the L2 cubes at z 0.02–0.09, or only HI widths?

*A data-feasibility probe, not an a₀ measurement: at z ≤ 0.093 the rival law a₀ ∝ H(z) differs from FLAT by at most 4.5 %, so nothing here can separate the two laws. κ = ½ is FITTED. No verdict words beyond the frozen map entries. Run under the owner's "probe, cap 10 GB" (2026-10-02) and finished here as chained work after the instruction to consolidate the calculation thread into the orchestrator.*

## Bottom line (the frozen map, first run, as it fell)
1. **Resolved rotation curves: 1 of 3 targets at r0p0 — only the nearest massive disc.** T1 (z 0.027, log M_HI 9.93) is 10.3 beams across at the 3σ level (126″ deconvolved), integrated S/N 66, and its position–velocity cut has four beam-wide points on each side that rise over the first two to three beams to a plateau near ±170 km s⁻¹ (Spearman ρ = −1.00). T2 (z 0.044, log M_HI 9.45, the median-type source) is 3.4 beams across with one PV point per side, and T3 (z 0.025, log M_HI 8.81) is 3.9 beams with one per side: neither is resolved at 12.3″. At r0p5 (16.3″) T1 is 12.9 beams across with S/N 92 but is NOT RC-capable by the frozen rule (|ρ| = 0.60). So the cubes give resolved curves only for the nearest massive discs and widths for the rest.
2. **Widths: the frozen rule passes T1 only** (T2 and T3 miss the W50 tolerance max(3σ_cat, 25 km s⁻¹) by 1.45 km s⁻¹ each: 140.5 and 74.6 against the catalogue 167 and 101). Post hoc, W50 of the unmasked aperture spectrum is inside the tolerance for both (145.9 / 157.0 and 86.3 / 81.9), so that miss is a property of the frozen mask.
3. **Controls: the fetch passes 8/8; stage B passes 9/16 and the failures are kept.** C2 (integrated flux within 25 % of the catalogue S_HI) fails at r0p0 for all three (S_int/S_cat 0.57, 0.24, 0.59; T1 r0p5 0.76 passes); C5 (no ≥ 5σ seed in the line-free channels or the off-source ring) fails for both T1 cutouts; C4 fails for T2 and T3; C3, C0, C1 pass; MUTATE=1 passes; MUTATE=2's check fails on a tolerance that was too tight (below). Post hoc: the unmasked aperture flux plateaus at 0.75 / 0.84 / 0.70 of the catalogue for T1 r0p0 / T1 r0p5 / T3, but only 0.36 ± 0.07–0.10 for T2 — the flux-scale question for T2 is OPEN; and the ≥ 5σ noise tails are not Gaussian (T1: 35 positive and 27 negative voxels in the line-free channels against 0.63 expected per tail), so a "5σ seed" in these cubes is not a 5σ event.
4. **For the programme:** the cubes can supply a few resolved curves for nearby massive discs (T1 at z 0.027 is the one example here; local calibration, not a₀(z)) and widths for the rest; the widths for the full 293-source sample come from the catalogue, not from these cubes (CFG301's draft). Nothing in this probe can discriminate FLAT from a₀ ∝ H(z) at z ≤ 0.093.

Over the whole line-free channel range: T1 35 positive and 27 negative voxels (Gaussian expectation 0.63 per tail), T2 2 / 5 (0.16), T3 11 / 1 (0.13). T1 r0p0's ring seeds in the line window are +9 / −18 (read as heavy-tailed noise). The frozen ring (100″) was set from a predicted HI radius of 50″; the 3σ radius at r0p5 is 105″.

## What this does not say
Three galaxies were chosen by rule from the eight public example rows of the catalogue paper (disclosed in the criteria); a null for T2 or T3 says nothing about the 293-source catalogue. The PV points are beam-smeared intensity-weighted velocities, not rotation curves: turning them into V(R) needs the inclination, a beam-smearing correction or a 3D forward model, and T1's flux shortfall, the non-Gaussian noise tails and the unresolved T2 flux question limit the extraction fidelity. The r0p0 and r0p5 cubes carry no BUNIT (Jy/beam assumed; the 0.75–0.85 plateau for T1 and T3 supports the scale to about 25 %, T2 does not). The r0p0 and r0p5 cutouts share the same visibilities and are not independent. **No a₀ number is computed; nothing here separates FLAT from the rival.**
