# CFG788 FROZEN CRITERIA: can cold energy couple to ordinary matter, and what is the propulsion ceiling?

## Question
The action has no matter–P or matter–cold-energy coupling beyond gravity. Add the most general weak contact coupling
allowed, and compute three things:
- (A) what existing experiments allow;
- (B) the maximum acceleration a device could get from it;
- (C) whether a closed device can exhaust momentum into the P field.

## A. Allowed coupling window (declared inputs, quoted from memory, PROVISIONAL)
- A coupling acts on a test body as a composition-dependent acceleration toward or along the cold-energy flow. The Sun
  moves at about 230 km/s through the Galaxy's cold energy, so the flow is a wind.
- **A1, MICROSCOPE** (Touboul et al. 2022): Eötvös |η| ≲ 3e-15 in Earth's field. Limit on a differential acceleration:
  Δa ≲ 3e-15 × 9.8 × (h_orbit factor ~0.8) ≈ 2.4e-14 m s⁻².
- **A2, Eöt-Wash galactic-source test** (Wagner et al. 2012): differential acceleration toward the Galactic centre
  ≲ ~1e-15 m s⁻².
- **A3, composition-independent coupling:** indistinguishable from gravity locally. Bounded by the record's ephemeris gate
  (sunward anomaly at 1 AU < 3.66e-14 m s⁻²) and by CFG770/774 (Newtonian locally).
- Ceiling a_max = min(A1, A2, A3), from whichever applies to a coupling a device could exploit.

## B. Propulsion ceiling
- With a_max as above, report the time to reach Δv = 1 m/s, 1 km/s, and 0.01c.
- Report the thrust on a 1000 kg craft.
- Compare with sunlight pressure at 1 AU on 1 m² per tonne (~9 × 10⁻⁹ m s⁻²).

## C. Momentum exhaust into P (the "push on the vacuum" route)
- The P field around baryons is gapped (CFG760/761). Perturbations radiate only above ω_g, with mass term 2|v₀|/s from the
  law's curvature.
- For a device-sized source (r ~ 1–10 m) in the Solar neighbourhood, compute the radiation threshold from the record's
  formula and the stiffness length (λ_e ≈ 2.1–2.4 pc, CFG770).
- A source smaller than λ_e couples to P only through the stiffness-screened static response (CFG770). Report the order
  of magnitude of the momentum it can transfer per second for a 1 kg-scale mass oscillating at 1 kHz.
- **Symmetry theorem (analytic):** a source invariant under the full rotation group exerts no net force on itself in a
  uniform background. A perfect sphere therefore cannot propel itself by any field coupling; propulsion needs a dipole
  (asymmetry) in the source.

## Classes
- **NO PRACTICAL PROPULSION** if a_max ≤ 1e-12 m s⁻² (reaching 1 km/s takes more than 30,000 years).
- **WINDOW** if a coupling allowed by A gives a_max > 1e-12. Report the experiment that would test it.
- C is reported separately as P EXHAUST CLOSED / OPEN.
