## Q-swirl

**W1 (sympy).** The river law of M2 with vorticity: a body moving at w relative to the flow feels −w × (∇ × v). Check: a rigidly rotating flow reproduces the rotating-frame Coriolis and centrifugal terms exactly.

**W2 (sympy).** A steady swirl v = λ_c V(R) φ̂ (λ_c the drag fraction, s = d ln V/d ln R) gives a tracer moving at u φ̂ the radial acceleration a_R = −λ_c² V²/R − (u − λ_c V) λ_c (V/R)(1 + s). Prograde (u = V) and retrograde (u = −V) tracers differ by 2λ_c(1 + s)V²/R; for s = 0 prograde gets −λ_c V²/R (inward) and retrograde +λ_c V²/R (outward). A vertical vorticity exerts no force on vertical motion. λ_c = 1 carries the stars (a prescribed swirl is a restatement, as door 11 warns).

**W3 (sympy, GR's swirl).** From the 1PN metric g₀ⱼ = −4Uⱼ/c³ the Lagrangian carries −(4/c²)U·v, so a_GM = −(4/c²) u × (∇ × U), Uⱼ = G ∫ρ vⱼ/|x − x′| d³x′; the river's GR swirl is the shift v_GR = 4U/c².
