# CFG226 — the record's nonlocal point-source relation against the SPARC baryonic Tully–Fisher relation

> **The relation** G M = v⁴/a₀ + (πβ/2) v² (equivalently A²/a₀ + (πβ/2)A = GM, v² = A; β a length) is the exact far-field amplitude of an ideal point source in the declared nonlocal model of `sol61_push/CORE_REVIEW_2026-09-30.md` §3. That record proves its limits (slope 4 at large M, slope 2 at small M, a₀,inf/a₀ = (√(1+η²) − η)², η = πβ√a₀/(4√(GM))) and never fits it to data. **This lane fits it to SPARC's BTFR. κ = ½ is FITTED; no law verdict; it tests the far-field amplitude relation only.** Criteria `FROZEN_CRITERIA.md` (893dbe507), committed before the script.

## Bottom line
- **With a₀ fixed at the framework's footings (9.36e-11, 1.13e-10) the best β is 0** (95% upper limit 0.052 and 0.068 kpc, Υ = 0.5), **and the fit is poor** (intrinsic scatter 0.28 and 0.22 dex against 0.15 with a₀ free): the plain BTFR wants a₀ ≈ 1.6e-10, above both footings, and a positive β only adds mass at low speed, so it cannot repair the normalisation.
- **With a₀ free, the frozen fit (M given v) prefers β = 0.26 kpc** (profile 68% [0.19, 0.33], one-sided 95% upper limit 0.39 kpc; a₀ = 1.95e-10; β = 0 is worse by Δ(−2 ln L) = 18.8; Υ = 0.35: β = 0.33, Δ = 34.5; Υ = 0.70: β = 0.19, Δ = 7.8; gas-dominated: β = 0.22, Δ = 5.0). Crossover speed v_c = √((π/2) β a₀) ≈ 50 km/s (M_c ≈ 5e8 M☉); the predicted a₀,inf/a₀ at M_bar = 1e7, 1e8, 1e9, 1e10, 1e11 M☉ is 0.04, 0.24, 0.62, 0.86, 0.95. The review's conditional sheet dictionary β a₀ = π/(4y) would put y = π c²/(4 β a₀) near 4.5e7.
- **That preference is a regression-direction effect, shown post hoc** (`cfg226_posthoc.py`, `cfg226_posthoc2.py`, reported only): the plain slope of this sample is 3.60 fitting M given v and 3.83 fitting v given M, and β mimics a shallower slope. **Fitting v given M (the tighter direction, 0.04 dex scatter in v): β = 0.099 kpc (95% upper 0.196, improvement 2.6) for Υ = 0.5, 0.009 kpc (upper 0.12, improvement 0.01) for Υ = 0.70, 0 (upper 0.10) for the gas-dominated galaxies.** Across all variants and both directions the one-sided 95% upper limit on β is 0.10 to 0.44 kpc, i.e. a crossover below about 30 to 60 km/s.
- **The shallow slope comes from the HIGH-mass end, where this relation's curvature goes the wrong way:** the plain slope is 3.51, 3.21, 2.84 above log M_bar = 9, 9.5, 10, and the M-given-v β̂ rises to 0.47, 1.1, 2.6 kpc with a₀ up to 4.2e-10 while the improvement stays 17 to 24. So the β̂ > 0 in the frozen fit does not show low-mass bending; it is the overall slope being below 4.
- **What would decide it:** the lowest-mass gas-rich dwarfs (v < 40 km/s, M_bar down to 1e6 to 1e7 M☉: LITTLE THINGS, Lelli+2019), where the relation predicts a₀,inf/a₀ of 0.04 to 0.3. SPARC has only a handful below 50 km/s (the left edge of the figure). Those tables are not on disk; fetching them needs the owner's go.

## Controls (`cfg226_nonlocal_btfr.out`: 3 of 4 pass) and two failures, stated plainly
- **C4 algebra PASS** (A solves the quadratic, a₀,inf/a₀ equals the record's closed form, written in its identical stable form 1/(√(1+η²)+η)², to 7e-16; the slope matches its numerical derivative to 1.3e-9). **C3 PASS** (five starts agree to 1.4e-9). **C2 PASS** (200 mocks: the true β = 0.30 kpc lies in the 68% profile interval 69% of the time; with β = 0 the 95% limit covers 0 in 100%).
- **C1 FAIL, narrowly:** the plain slope of this sample is 3.597 against the frozen window [3.6, 4.2] (literature 3.85 ± 0.09; the inverse regression gives 3.83, which is how the estimator direction matters).
- **MUTATE FAIL:** the noise-free mock (β = 0.50 kpc) returns β̂ = 0.524 and the plain BTFR is worse by 77.7 against the frozen bound of 100. The cause is the frozen likelihood's parameter-dependent effective variance (its ln σ² term is biased on noise-free data). The post hoc variance with a fixed slope S₀ = 3.7 returns β̂ = 0.50000 in both directions (plain BTFR worse by 73.9 and 64.5: the real errors set that size, the frozen 100 was a guess) and reproduces the real-data fit (M given v: β̂ = 0.263, a₀ = 1.955e-10, improvement 19.05), so the bias is small on the real data. The first runs are kept as `*_firstrun.out` (the SPARC parse read fixed columns that the file does not follow, N = 0, now whitespace tokens; C4's threshold hit the closed form's cancellation at large η).

## Limits
The relation is the far-field amplitude of an IDEAL POINT SOURCE; SPARC discs are extended and the review says finite boundaries change a nonlocal operator globally. Υ, distances and gas masses carry systematics the declared bands only sample; the M-given-v and v-given-M fits bracket the regression-direction effect. A slope below 4 can have other causes (Υ, distances, inclination, pressure support in dwarfs), so a β > 0 would not single out this model; the data here neither need nor exclude a β up to about 0.4 kpc.
