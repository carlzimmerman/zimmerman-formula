# CFG224b — the redshift dependence of the gas-mass conversion factors (Dunne+22 per galaxy) and ACE's dust-to-CO ratio

> Addendum to CFG224 (`README.md`: K = 0.038 at z < 0.6, 0.128 at z ≈ 1.2, nothing above z 1.6 from three galaxies). **Dunne+22's optimised conversions make the tracer masses agree galaxy by galaxy BY CONSTRUCTION**, so the informative content is how the conversion factors themselves vary with z. Conversions as optimised or adopted by each source; author tables, no law verdict. κ = ½ FITTED. Criteria `FROZEN_CRITERIA_B.md` (3b1c554e0), committed before any factor statistic. Figure: `cfg224b_conversion_drift.png`.

## Bottom line
- **The bin means of the optimised factors differ between the local bin and z ≥ 1.6 by up to about 0.1 to 0.17 dex, but that is mostly the luminosity dependence.** α_CO (ad table, N = 196 / 16 / 74 / 32 in B1 to B4): log₁₀ 0.528 → 0.427 → 0.438 → 0.470 (factor 3.37 → 2.67 → 2.74 → 2.95); drifts against B1 −0.101 ± 0.028, −0.090 ± 0.022, −0.058 ± 0.033; K_direct 0.02 to 0.03 (below the CFG223 range), K_local 0.105, 0.093, 0.067. The three tables disagree in the sign of the z ≥ 3 α_CO drift (ad −0.058 ± 0.033, daX +0.064 ± 0.043, xa +0.104 ± 0.041: a 0.16 dex spread between tables). X_CI drifts up with z: daX −4.876 → −4.741 (+0.135 ± 0.040 at z ≥ 3), xa +0.094, xd +0.051. The dust factors (κ_H, GDR) rise by 0.06 to 0.17 dex at z ≥ 0.6 (ad κ_H +0.084, +0.085, +0.060; daX GDR +0.146, +0.170, +0.091; xd GDR +0.070, +0.128, +0.072).
- **At fixed L_IR the z slope is consistent with zero in every factor:** slope per unit log₁₀(1+z) with the L_IR term: α_CO ad +0.056 ± 0.050, daX +0.004 ± 0.072, xa −0.002 ± 0.066; X_CI daX −0.039 ± 0.080, xa −0.026 ± 0.064, xd −0.104 ± 0.066; κ_H ad −0.056 ± 0.042; GDR daX −0.025 ± 0.078, xd −0.084 ± 0.066. **Without the L_IR term the same slopes are 2 to 4σ** (ad α_CO −0.124 ± 0.032, ad κ_H +0.117 ± 0.028, daX X_CI +0.181 ± 0.043, daX GDR +0.216 ± 0.052). The L_IR coefficients are α_CO −0.058 ± 0.012 per dex (ad), X_CI +0.04 to +0.08, κ_H and GDR +0.06 to +0.09. **So the apparent drift with z is the luminosity dependence of the conversions plus the luminosity–redshift correlation of the samples.** Across z = 0 → 2.5 (Δlog₁₀(1+z) = 0.54) the fixed-luminosity change is 0.00 to 0.06 dex (1σ about 0.03 to 0.05) in each factor; to z = 5 about ±0.04 to 0.08.
- **ACE shows the un-optimised prescriptions disagree at z ≈ 2.2.** ACE's dust-to-CO-gas ratio R = log(M_dust/M_mol,CO) = −2.37 (SD 0.18, N = 15, 12+log(O/H) 8.29 to 8.61) lies **0.67 dex below Stripe82's OLS relation at the same metallicity (frozen; K = 0.34, at or above the CFG221 requirement), 0.52 dex below Stripe82's mean with no slope, and 0.21 dex below with the slope fixed at +1 (dust-to-gas proportional to metallicity)** (post hoc, beside the frozen number: Stripe82's own OLS slope is −0.49 ± 0.33 over only 0.21 dex, and ACE's metallicities lie BELOW Stripe82's, so the frozen offset extrapolates). K is therefore between about 0.11 and 0.34 depending on the metallicity slope. The two sources adopt different prescriptions (ACE's α_CO(Z) and single-band Band 7 dust at β = 2.08, κ = 0.4 m²/kg at 250 µm; Stripe82's Milky Way α_CO times a metallicity factor and Herschel SED dust with Leroy+11 dust-to-gas).
- **What it means for the calibration wall, plainly:** tracer conversions that are optimised galaxy by galaxy across three tracers are stable in z at fixed luminosity to about ±0.05 dex, so a multi-tracer gas mass for a galaxy in Dunne+22's regime is known to about 0.03 to 0.10 dex (K_direct 0.02 to 0.03 for the mean, K_local 0.07 to 0.10 if the local value is assumed) — **but a single pre-adopted tracer with local prescriptions can be 0.2 to 0.7 dex off at z ≈ 2** (ACE), which is the size of the wall in CFG219 to CFG223. The optimised conversions are anchored to Dunne+22's normalisation (κ_H = 1884, X_CI = 1.6e-5) and to the tracers' mutual consistency, so a common-mode z-dependent bias shared by all three tracers is invisible here.
- **K needed against measured (B3 and B4, from CFG224 §6, f_gas 0.5):** RC100 quartile 3 needs 0.149 (0.107 to 0.249), quartile 4 0.110 (0.079 to 0.184); CRISTAL 0.20 to 0.30 (0.14 to 0.64). Measured: α_CO (ad) K_direct 0.021 (B3) and 0.032 (B4), K_local 0.093 and 0.067; ACE K 0.34 (0.11 to 0.34). The gas-calibration requirement is reachable at z ≥ 1.6 **if the a₀ lane's gas masses come from per-galaxy multi-tracer optimisation and luminosity is matched**, not if one tracer with local conversions is used.

## Limits
Optimised conversions inherit Dunne+22's priors and normalisation and its lensing corrections; selection differs with z (luminous, dusty, submm-selected galaxies at high z against local discs), which is why the L_IR term matters; the high-z SMGs' CO(1-0) luminosities are often estimated from mid-J lines; a z-independent conversion is not a z-independent true gas mass; the z ≥ 1.6 bins hold 12 to 74 galaxies per table; 15 to 17 rows per table have numeric H-ATLAS-style names that do not join to z and are excluded; ACE is one survey with its own prescriptions and N = 15; the ACE offset depends on Stripe82's poorly determined metallicity slope (the post hoc numbers bracket it).
