## 1. Pair offsets (primary: independent tracer pairs)
Tracer families in the canonical order CO, CI, CII, DUST. For a galaxy with masses from tracers A before B in that order, **d = log₁₀(M_A / M_B)** (dex), masses as the source states them. Pair types: CO–CI, CO–CII, CO–DUST, CI–CII, CI–DUST, CII–DUST.
- **Stripe82** (Bertemes+18; 78; z 0.03 to 0.20): CO–DUST from `logMgas_CO`, `logMgas_dust` (α_CO from the Milky Way value times a metallicity factor; dust from Leroy+2011 dust-to-gas).
- **Bourne+19** (9 with both masses; z 0.84 to 1.22): CI–DUST from `Mmol_CI_1e9` ([CI] with Q₁₀ = 0.35, X_CI = 3e-5) and `Mmol_cont_1e9` (continuum, Scoville-type).
- **NOEMA3D** (z 1.12 to 1.25): the **five whose CO control passes** (G4_38065, G4_38232, G4_20371, GN4_24517, GN4_18574) for CO–DUST and CO–CI and CI–DUST from the data chat's reconstruction (`logMmol_CO_table1`, `logMmol_CI_reconstructed`, `logMmol_dust_reconstructed`); the five whose control fails (G4_23011 and the four CO(3-2) galaxies) are a SENSITIVITY row only, with the CO from the reconstruction and from Table 1.
- **Singles** (stated values at the stated default conversions; errors as stated): PKS 0529-549 (z 2.5725; CI 3.1±0.6e11, CO 0.73±0.09e11, DUST 2.2±0.2e11); Q1700-MD94 (z 2.333; CO 1.7e11, DUST ~7e10, no errors stated); J081740 (z 4.2603; CO 8.8±2.6e10, FIR-DUST 5.7±0.7e10, CII 9.8±0.6e10); D49 (z ≈ 3; DUST log M = 11.19±0.03 and a [CI] UPPER LIMIT log M < 11.22: reported as a bound on CI–DUST, never in a mean).
- **EXCLUDED from the primary (independence guards, reported beside):** Kirkpatrick+19 (12; RJ dust is calibrated to CO with the same α_CO = 6.5: the ratio is reported only as the paper's own consistency check); H-ATLAS z = 0.35 (12; the three conversions are solved jointly per galaxy so the masses agree by construction).

## 2. Bins and per-bin statistics
Bins fixed here: **B1 z < 0.6; B2 0.6 ≤ z < 1.6; B3 1.6 ≤ z < 3.0; B4 z ≥ 3.0.** For each (pair type, bin): N, mean μ, SD s (ddof = 1), standard error SE = s/√N, median, 1.4826 × MAD, and, for N ≥ 6, a 10,000-resample bootstrap 68% interval of the mean (seed 224). Where every galaxy of the pair has stated errors, the **intrinsic scatter** s_int = √(max(0, s² − mean(σ_i²))) with σ_i² = (σ_A,i² + σ_B,i²) in dex; otherwise only the observed s is given. **Pair types with N < 3 in a bin are listed per galaxy and get no statistics.**
- **The bin's headline pair type** is the one with the most galaxies in the bin (ties: the one listed first in the order above).
- **Calibration knowledge K of a (pair type, bin) = √(SE² + (|μ|/2)²)** (dex): the error of a two-tracer pooled mass scale if either tracer is equally likely to be right. **Class (the quoted requirements, no law verdict): K < 0.05 "below the CFG223 range"; 0.05 ≤ K < 0.10 "inside the CFG223 range (±0.05 to ±0.10 dex)"; 0.10 ≤ K < 0.15 "between the CFG223 and CFG221 requirements"; K ≥ 0.15 "at or above the CFG221 requirement (±0.15 dex)".**
- **z dependence:** the CO–DUST mean offset in B1, B2, B3, B4 (where N ≥ 3 or as singles) is tabulated and plotted as it is; no trend significance is claimed with these N.

## 3. Three-cornered hat (where three tracers measure the same galaxies)
For the five validated NOEMA3D galaxies (CO, CI, DUST): v_AB = the sample variance of d_AB; the per-tracer scatter σ_A² = (v_AB + v_AC − v_BC)/2 (and cyclically), jackknife errors over the five galaxies; a negative variance is reported as negative (not clipped) and flagged "not constrained". Same for PKS 0529-549 and J081740 as single-galaxy lines (no statistics).

## 4. Secondary: conversion-free luminosity ratios at z > 2
- **SMGs (arXiv:2404.05596; 20; z 2.26 to 4.78):** per galaxy r = log₁₀(L'_CI / L'_CO(1-0)) from `LpCI_1e10`, `LpCO10_1e10` as tabulated (the CO(1-0) value may be estimated from mid-J CO; the observed `Jup` is recorded and the statistics are given for all and for each Jup); mean, SD, SE. **Implied α_CO** per galaxy = `Mgas_CI_1e10` / `LpCO10_1e10` (X_[CI] = 5.1e-5 as the paper uses): median and SD of its log; and the offset log₁₀(α_assumed L'_CO / M_gas,[CI]) for α_assumed in {0.8, 3.6, 4.36}: mean and SD. The SD of r is conversion-independent; the offsets are not.
- **SPT DSFGs (arXiv:2306.03153; 29; z 1.87 to 4.80; flux table only):** L' ratios from fluxes, L'_A/L'_B = (S_A/S_B)(ν_rest,B/ν_rest,A)² with ν_rest = 492.1607 ([CI]1-0), 809.3446 ([CI]2-1), 806.6518 (CO7-6), 1900.5369 ([CII]) GHz: SD of log L'_CI(2-1)/L'_CI(1-0) and of log L'_[CII]/L'_CI(1-0) where both are measured. Luminosity-level, excitation- and conversion-dependent: NOT a mass test.

## 5. Literature input: Dunne+22 (arXiv:2208.01622), parsed from the TeX tables
From the table "Mean optimised conversion factors" and the table "Empirical calibration factors": per (sample, luminosity class) the half-width of the 16 to 84% range in dex for X_CI, α_CO and κ_H (the per-galaxy scatter of each conversion factor); the error on the mean in dex (σ/mean/ln 10); the spread of the mean α_CO, κ_H(α_850) and α_CI between samples (max minus min, dex) within a luminosity class and in the empirical table. Rows flagged CIcor or lo-VALES are not used (the paper removes them). **These calibrate tracers against each other in mostly local and luminous samples; they do not measure a redshift evolution.**

## 6. What it implies for the flat-versus-H(z) test (arithmetic with declared inputs, not a forecast)
For each CFG223 figure point: the lever L = d log₁₀ s*/d(baryon dex) from `CFG223_a0_over_cosmic_time/cfg223_lever.out` (−2.5 to −4.8), the gas fraction f_gas = 0.5 (declared; 0.3 and 0.7 as sensitivities), and the law separation Δ = log₁₀ E(z_med) (H(z) against flat) from `cfg223_results.json`. A gas-mass calibration error K moves log s* by |L| f_gas K, so **K_needed = (Δ/2) / (|L| f_gas)** (half the separation). Tabulated beside the measured K of the bin containing z_med (B2 for the RC100 quartiles, B4 for CRISTAL). Point-wise, no pooling; the CFG221 operating characteristics are quoted, not re-run.

## 7. Controls (all reported; a failure is reported plainly)
- **C1** data counts equal the data chat's `checks_multitracer.txt` (Stripe82 78, Bourne 9 with both masses, Kirkpatrick 12, SMG 20, SPT 29) and the NOEMA3D validated set is exactly the five with control pass; no non-finite value enters a statistic. **C2** estimator identity on synthetic pairs (N = 500, μ = 0.10, s = 0.15): μ, s and SE recovered to 3 SE, and the bootstrap interval within 10% of ±SE. **C3** bootstrap coverage of the 68% interval of the mean on Gaussian mocks, N = 9 and 78, K = 2,000: coverage in [0.60, 0.76]. **C4** three-cornered hat: synthetic three-tracer data with known (σ_A, σ_B, σ_C), N = 300: each recovered to 15%; at N = 5 the fraction of negative variances is REPORTED. **C5** independence guard: no Kirkpatrick or H-ATLAS row in any primary statistic. **C6** the TeX parse reproduces the orchestrator's quoted values (α_CO = 2.66 +0.96/−0.70 with N = 90; 3.08 +1.32/−0.81 with N = 240; 3.52 +0.95/−0.84 with N = 88).
