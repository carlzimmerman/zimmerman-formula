# CFG224b — FROZEN CRITERIA: the redshift dependence of the gas-mass conversion factors from Dunne+22's per-galaxy tables, and ACE's dust-to-CO ratio at z ≈ 2.2

> Addendum to CFG224 (`FROZEN_CRITERIA.md`, 99f62160b; result 26a38f428: K = 0.038 at z < 0.6, 0.128 at z ≈ 1.2, not estimated above z 1.6 from three galaxies). The data chat has since parsed Dunne+22's per-galaxy CDS catalogue (408 sources) and the ACE survey (26 galaxies, z 2.09–2.49) into `data_assembly/multitracer_gas/` (6c18c4204). **Written 2026-09-30 and committed BEFORE any conversion-factor statistic is computed.** Conversions are as the sources state or optimise them; **Dunne+22's optimised conversions make the tracer masses agree galaxy by galaxy BY CONSTRUCTION, so the informative content is how the conversion factors themselves vary with z**, not a tracer offset. A conversion that drifts with z is exactly a z-dependent gas-mass calibration error if the local value is used. κ = ½ is FITTED; no law verdict.

What has been seen before the freeze: the column names and first rows of every table; the counts that join to z (names matched to the master table: daX 99 of 114, ad 318 of 335, xa 106 of 121, xd 137 of 152; the unmatched rows carry numeric H-ATLAS-style names and are excluded and counted); the ACE list of 26 rows with its detection flags; Dunne+22's own mean-conversion table (CFG224 §5). No per-galaxy factor has been binned or fitted.

## 1. Data
- **Dunne+22** (`multitracer_gas/dunne2022/`): `dunne2022_master.csv` (z, log L_IR) joined by exact name over the master's `Name`, `OName` and `SimbadName` columns to the four optimisation tables: **daX** (dust + CO + [CI]: `aCO`, `XCI`, `GDR`, `aCI`, `a850`), **ad** (CO + dust: `aCO`, `kappaH`, `loga850`), **xa** (CO + [CI]: `aCO`, `XCI`), **xd** (dust + [CI]: `XCI`, `GDR`). Per galaxy y = log₁₀ of the factor; the factors used are **α_CO (aCO), X_CI (XCI), κ_H (kappaH, ad only) and GDR (daX, xd; not compared across tables)**. Per-galaxy errors (`e_aCO`, `e_XCI`, `e_kappaH`, `e_GDR`) give σ_dex = e / (value ln 10). Non-finite, non-positive or unmatched rows are excluded and counted.
- **ACE** (`ace_merged_per_galaxy.csv`): galaxies with both a CO(3-2)-based M_mol (`Mmol_CO_1e10`, flag not '<') and a dust mass (`logMdust_2609_20926`, flag not '<'), with 12 + log(O/H). **Local comparison:** Stripe82 (`stripe82_z_lt0.3_CO_dust.csv`: `logMdust`, `logMgas_CO`, `Z_12logOH`).

## 2. Statistics (fixed here)
- **Bins** as CFG224: **B1 z < 0.6; B2 0.6 ≤ z < 1.6; B3 1.6 ≤ z < 3.0; B4 z ≥ 3.0.** Per (table, factor, bin): N, mean μ, SD (ddof = 1), SE = SD/√N, the intrinsic SD √(max(0, SD² − mean σ_dex²)), and for N ≥ 6 a 10,000-resample bootstrap 68% interval of the mean (seed 224). Bins with N < 3 get no statistics.
- **Drift against the local bin:** Δ_b = μ_b − μ_B1 with SE_Δ = √(SE_b² + SE_B1²). **Two knowledge numbers per (table, factor, bin ≥ B2):** K_direct = SE_b (the error of the measured mean in that bin) and **K_local = √(Δ_b² + SE_Δ²)** (the error of assuming the local conversion there). Classes as CFG224 (K < 0.05 "below the CFG223 range"; 0.05 to 0.10 "inside the CFG223 range"; 0.10 to 0.15 "between"; ≥ 0.15 "at or above the CFG221 requirement").
- **z slope at fixed luminosity:** OLS of y on log₁₀(1+z) and (log L_IR − 11.7) for each (table, factor) with at least 30 joined galaxies: b (per unit log₁₀(1+z)) and c, with bootstrap SDs (10,000 resamples of galaxies); Dunne+22 report shallow correlations of the factors with luminosity, so the z-slope is reported with and without the L_IR term.
- **ACE:** R = log₁₀(M_dust/M_mol,CO) against 12 + log(O/H). The local relation R_S82(Z) = a + bZ from an OLS fit of the 78 Stripe82 galaxies (log M_dust − log M_gas,CO against `Z_12logOH`, residual SD reported). **Δ_ACE = mean over the ACE galaxies of [R_ACE − R_S82(Z_ACE)]**, SE = residual SD/√N (ACE's own scatter and Stripe82's combined in quadrature), **K = √(SE² + (|Δ|/2)²)** and its class. The two sources use their own α_CO(Z) and dust-to-gas prescriptions, so Δ_ACE is an offset at the conversions each source adopts.

## 4. Controls (all reported; failures stated plainly)
- **C1** the joins reproduce the counts seen before the freeze (daX 99, ad 318, xa 106, xd 137) and the ACE set is 15. **C2** estimator identity on synthetic factors (N = 500, μ = 0.10, SD = 0.15): mean, SD and SE to 3 SE. **C3** regression recovery: synthetic log factors with known (b, c) on the real (z, L_IR) of the ad table: the OLS recovers b and c to 3 bootstrap SD. **C4** bootstrap coverage of the 68% interval of a bin mean on Gaussian mocks at N = 9 and 40 (K = 2,000): in [0.60, 0.76]. **MUTATE** (`MUTATE=1`): +0.30 dex injected into every log α_CO at z ≥ 1.6: the B3 and B4 drifts of α_CO shift by +0.300 to 1e-9, the B1 and B2 statistics are unchanged, the z-slope b rises, and K_local in B3 and B4 rises.

## 5. Limits (written before the run)
Optimised conversions inherit Dunne+22's priors and normalisation (κ_H^N = 1884, X_CI^N = 1.6e-5) and the catalogue's lensing corrections for the high-z SMGs; selection differs with z (luminous, dusty, submm-selected galaxies at high z against local discs), which is why the L_IR term is reported; the high-z SMGs' CO(1-0) luminosities are often estimated from mid-J lines with excitation corrections; a z-independent conversion is not a z-independent true gas mass; ACE is one survey with two sets of prescriptions (its own α_CO(Z), single-band Band 7 dust at β = 2.08, κ = 0.4 m²/kg at 250 µm) against Stripe82's; the ACE N is 15.
