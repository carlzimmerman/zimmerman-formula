## 10. DATA NOTE (dated 2026-09-29; folded in before hashing, on the coordinator's message): what the KURVS paper says about σ(R) and pressure support, and how it changes attack (a)

Source: `data_assembly/arxiv_tables/KURVS_SIGMA_METHODS_NOTE.md` (commit 093fa9733), a data-front note from the paper's TeX (Puglisi+2023); it reports what the authors state and marks its own inferences "(mine)". I read it after freezing sections 1–9, so the additions below are dated additions, not part of the original frozen text; nothing in sections 1–9 is changed except the two pointers to this section. I take its statements as given (unverified against the TeX by me). Facts used:

1. **Not in the tabulated outer velocities:** no pressure-support (asymmetric-drift) correction. The maximal-extent and R′6D velocities are inclination-corrected only; R′3D is inclination- and beam-smearing-corrected. The Burkert+10 pressure correction (one constant σ0 per galaxy) is applied only in the authors' inner-kinematics f_DM analysis, and the authors call large-radius corrections "poorly constrained by data" (the correction grows with radius; constant-σ and vertical-geometry assumptions untested).
2. **The outer σ(R) is the OBSERVED line width:** instrumental broadening removed; beam smearing corrected only for the single σ0 (Johnson+18, within 3.4 R_d) and assumed negligible beyond; unresolved bulk motion, non-circular motion, clumps and outflows are NOT removed. Outer-to-full σ ratio 1.13 ± 0.26 (beyond 2.2–3.4 R_d); no S/N or PSF statement specific to 4–7 R_d; asymmetric or irregular σ profiles are flagged for KURVS 4, 5, 10, 12, 14, 15, 17, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, and outer broad-component pixels for 6, 12, 16, 19 (KURVS-13's outer σ is consistent with flat; KURVS-17's outer pixels have large uncertainties).

**How this changes the meaning of attack (a).**
- **P0 and P4 are both models of an unmeasured quantity, and neither is "already in the data".** The tabulated v_last that the pipeline uses carries no pressure correction, so P0 is exactly "the tabulated V read as V_c" (i.e. the outer σ carries no radial support), while P4 (Kretschmer's α σ_out²) adds a simulation-calibrated term. There is no double-counting risk for P4 (no correction sits in the table), and P0 is not a physically neutral default: it asserts zero support from a gas whose measured line width is ≈ the central value. This weakens CFG175's phrase that P0 is "no pressure correction" as though that were the null; it is the uncorrected table, which the authors themselves regard as needing a correction.
- **Contamination pushes the effective s DOWN, i.e. toward P0, and is one-sided.** Because σ_out is an upper bound on the turbulent dispersion at these radii (uncorrected smearing, unresolved and non-circular motion all add width), applying K21's α to the observed σ over-corrects if part of the width is not pressure-supporting. Define s_eff = s_phys × (σ_supp/σ_obs)², where σ_supp is the supporting radial dispersion. The T law fits with s_eff ≈ 0 at μ ≈ 1.0 (P0) and s_eff ≈ 0.75 at the ceiling μ = 3.47 (hand estimate, section 4); so the physical statement needed for T at PHIBSS-median gas is (σ_supp/σ_obs)² ≲ s_up ≈ 0.3, i.e. at least ~70% of σ_obs² non-supporting, against a measured outer/full ratio of 1.13 ± 0.26 that shows the outer dispersion is not smaller than the beam-corrected profile. Flat and the rival need the same reduction only in the other direction (they need MORE s than P0).
- **What the note cannot say:** no test of contamination beyond 3.4 R_d, none at 4–7 R_d, no measurement of anisotropy (σ_R/σ_los) or vertical geometry. So P0 cannot be excluded by the repo, and K21 (VELA, with dominant spheroids, observed-σ inputs) cannot be shown to apply either.
