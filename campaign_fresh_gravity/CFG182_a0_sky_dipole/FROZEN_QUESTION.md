# CFG182 — does SPARC's acceleration scale depend on direction on the sky? (frozen 2026-09-29, before any script or number)

**Why.** Door 11, Addenda 1–2 (`closure_map/DOOR11_FLOWING_VACUUM_GATES_2026-09-29.md`): the owner's dark-energy flow comes from ONE direction ("the top") and is massless. If the flow is spatially directional (variant 11B′), the galaxy acceleration scale should depend on where a galaxy sits on the sky relative to the flow (a dipole a₀(n) = ā₀(1 + A n·d)). The flat, constant-a₀ law predicts A = 0. Two published papers claim a dipole in SPARC's a₀: hemisphere anisotropy 0.37 ± 0.04 with maximum toward (l, b) = (175.5°, −6.5°) (arXiv:1707.00417, Zhou, Zhao & Chang 2017) and dipole amplitude 0.25 ± 0.04 toward (171.30° ± 7.18°, −15.41° ± 4.87°) (arXiv:1803.08344, Chang, Lin, Zhao & Zhou 2018), with the authors' own caution ("premature to claim that the Universe is anisotropic"); both from abstracts only (`data_assembly/DOOR11_LITERATURE_DATAFRONT_2026-09-29.md`, item 2). This lane re-tests the claim on the repo's own copy of SPARC with the per-galaxy nuisances marginalised and a permutation null that absorbs the look-elsewhere effect of a free direction. A null is valid and informative: it bounds the owner's directional flow (door-11 gate G8). Nothing is assumed about the answer. κ = ½ stays FITTED; a₀ is fitted here (ā₀ free), so κ does not enter any pass line.

## Sample and model (PRIMARY)
- Clean sample: Q ≤ 2 and Inc ≥ 30° (the Li, McGaugh & Lelli 2018 cuts; CFG4 uses Q ≤ 2), and at least 5 usable points. Points used: V_obs > 0, e_V > 0, and fiducial V_bar² > 0 (g_bar > 0 at Υ_disk = 0.5, Υ_bul = 0.7). No velocity-error cut (LML 2018 kept all points).
- Law: g_obs = ν(g_bar/a₀) g_bar. Kernel: ν_mono (the 09-26 decision; FP1's committed definition, loaded read-only through CFG4_common). Reported kernel variants: P2 as CFG4 defines it, ν = √(1 + 1/y), and the "simple" ν = ½ + √(¼ + 1/y) that the door-11 text calls P2 (the P2 definition conflict flagged in CFG181 is not resolved here; with a₀ fitted the kernel only changes the transition shape).
- Per-galaxy nuisances (LML 2018 priors): Υ_disk log-normal about 0.5, σ = 0.1 dex; Υ_bul log-normal about 0.7, σ = 0.1 dex; distance Gaussian about D_SPARC with σ = e_D; inclination Gaussian about Inc with σ = e_Inc. Distance leaves g_bar invariant and scales the predicted observed velocity as √(D′/D); inclination scales it as sin i′/sin i. χ² in velocity space with the published e_V, plus the four prior terms.
- Profile likelihood: for each galaxy the nuisances are PROFILED (minimised) at each value of log₁₀ a₀ on a fixed grid (−11.3 to −9.0, step 0.01), giving a curve χ²_i(log a₀). The dipole acts on a galaxy only through its own a₀_i = ā₀(1 + D·n_i), so the global point-level fit is exactly Σ_i χ²_i(log ā₀ + log₁₀(1 + D·n_i)), with D = A d a free 3-vector (Galactic Cartesian) and |D| ≤ 0.95.
- Per-galaxy error scaling (declared): each galaxy's curve is divided by s_i = max(1, χ²_min,i/(N_i − 1)) (the Birge ratio), so galaxies with non-circular motions or wiggles far beyond their formal errors do not dominate. Reported variant: no scaling.
- Headline numbers: Â = |D̂|, its direction (l, b), ā₀; errors from 2000 galaxy bootstrap resamples (positions travel with galaxies); the Fisher (curvature) error is reported for reference only.

## The null
- **Permutation null (primary, N = 5000):** reassign the sky positions among the galaxies of the fitted sample, keeping everything else (curves, distances, methods) attached to each galaxy; refit the free-direction dipole each time. p_A = (1 + #{Â_perm ≥ Â_obs})/(1 + N). Two schemes, both run: (a) simple (each galaxy one unit); (b) block: the Ursa Major members (f_D = 4) share one sky position and one distance scale, so they are permuted as ONE unit carrying their mean position. **The pass line uses the larger of the two p values.** The free direction is refit in every permutation, so the look-elsewhere effect is inside p. The likelihood-ratio statistic Δχ² = χ²(A = 0) − χ²_min is reported with its own permutation p (not a pass line).
- **Gaussian-noise null (reported):** (i) curve level, N = 5000: each galaxy's scaled curve is shifted so its minimum sits at log ā₀ + ε_i, ε_i ~ N(0, σ_i² + τ²), σ_i the curve's Δχ² = 1 half-width, τ ≥ 0 the maximum-likelihood extra inter-galaxy scatter at A = 0; (ii) point level, N = 100 generative mocks (separate script, grid step 0.02): true nuisances drawn from the priors, no dipole, velocities = law prediction + Gaussian noise with the Birge-scaled e_V, full profile pipeline rerun. Their Â distributions and p values are reported next to the permutation p.

## Systematics
- **Distance-method split:** H = Hubble flow (f_D = 1); I = redshift-independent individual distances (f_D ∈ {2, 3, 5}: TRGB, Cepheid, SNIa). Ursa Major (f_D = 4; one position, one distance) is reported as a third group, not in I. Each subsample gets its own free-direction fit, bootstrap covariance, and the fixed-direction amplitude along the full-sample direction.
- **Inclination split** at the sample's median Inc (reported).
- **Footprint:** the survey's own anisotropy is the mean position vector f = ⟨n_i⟩ of the fitted sample; its axis ±f̂ is compared with the fitted direction. Also reported: the least-constrained direction of the dipole (the smallest-eigenvalue eigenvector of the Fisher matrix of D).
- **Other directions (reported):** angle between the fitted direction and the CMB dipole (l, b) = (264.0°, 48.3°); the local bulk flow (Carrick et al. 2015 2M++ external flow, (304°, 6°), FROM MEMORY, UNVERIFIED); Chang et al. 2018 (171.30°, −15.41°); Zhou et al. 2017 (175.5°, −6.5°); the Ursa Major cluster (mean member position); Virgo (283.8°, 74.5°, from memory). For each: whether it lies inside the 68% and 95% bootstrap cones of the fitted direction, and the fixed-direction amplitude A_fix ± σ along it.
- **Peculiar-velocity template (reported):** a coherent bulk motion W of the Hubble-flow galaxies relative to the frame of their distances biases the fitted a₀ as a₀_i = ā₀(1 − 2 W·n_i/(H₀ D_i)) (f_D = 1 only, H₀ = 73, SPARC's convention). This 3-parameter template is fitted in place of the dipole; its Δχ² is compared with the dipole's. A real flow-induced dipole is distance-independent and present in every distance method; a peculiar-velocity artefact scales as 1/D and lives in the Hubble-flow galaxies.
- **Distance trend (reported):** the dipole refit with an added term β log₁₀(D_i/10 Mpc) in log a₀_i.

## Pass lines (declared before data)
A **directional-flow signal** is declared only if ALL of:
- **S1** p_A < 0.003 against the permutation null (the larger of the simple and block p), primary sample, primary kernel, Birge scaling on.
- **S2** stable across the distance-method split: (a) the H and I dipole vectors agree, χ² = ΔDᵀ(Σ_H + Σ_I)⁻¹ΔD with 3 dof gives p > 0.05 (bootstrap covariances), and (b) the fixed-direction amplitude along the full-sample direction is positive at ≥ 1σ in both H and I.
- **S3** not aligned with the footprint: the footprint axis ±f̂ lies OUTSIDE the 95% bootstrap cone of the fitted direction (the cone = the angle containing 95% of bootstrap directions about the best fit).

Otherwise the result is a **NULL**, reported as:
- **A₉₅**, the 95% upper limit on A for a random flow direction, by Neyman construction: dipoles of amplitude A_inj (0 to 0.5, step 0.025) in random directions are injected into position-permuted data (curve level: galaxy i's preferred a₀ moved by the factor 1 + D_inj·n_i); A₉₅ is the smallest A_inj for which Â ≥ Â_obs in ≥ 95% of 300 trials. The same machinery reports the recovery bias ⟨Â⟩ versus A_inj.
- Fixed-direction 95% intervals A_fix ± 1.96σ along the CMB, bulk-flow, Chang and Zhou directions.
- **Comparison with the published claims (reported, declared tests):** C1: along Chang's direction, is A = 0.25 outside our 95% interval? C2: at Zhou's axis, our hemisphere anisotropy H = 2(a_N − a_S)/(a_N + a_S) (a₀ fitted separately in the two hemispheres from the same curves) versus 0.37 ± 0.04; plus the hemisphere scan (HEALPix nside 16, ≥ 10 galaxies per hemisphere) giving H_max, its direction, and its permutation p. (For a dipole sampled uniformly, H = A, so 0.37 and 0.25 are directly comparable with A.)
- **G8 translation:** a sky-direction modulation a₀(1 + ε f̂·n) of the owner's flow must have ε < A₉₅ (random direction) or < the fixed-direction bound for a named direction; in the deep-MOND regime the force goes as √a₀, so the force anisotropy is bounded by ε/2. A flow whose effect depends instead on each disk's orientation relative to the flow (even in the angle) predicts no dipole but a quadrupole in n weighted by P₂(cos i); a reported fit of that model, a₀_i = ā₀(1 + ε[½ sin² i_i − ⅓ + P₂(cos i_i)(n_i·f̂)²]) with (ε, f̂) free and its own permutation p, bounds that reading (not a pass line).

## MUTATE control (must bite)
MUTATE=1 injects a known dipole A = 0.20 in a random direction (seed 182, drawn uniformly on the sphere, printed) into the DATA at the point level, before the profiles are built: V_obs → V_obs √(ν(y_i′)/ν(y)), y = g_bar,fid/a₀,ref, y_i′ = g_bar,fid/(a₀,ref(1 + D_inj·n_i)), a₀,ref = 1.2 × 10⁻¹⁰ m/s² (declared), e_V unchanged. The whole pipeline reruns and writes separate outputs (`*_MUTATE.*`). **M1 (load-bearing):** the change of the fitted dipole vector, D̂_mut − D̂_obs, has amplitude 0.20 ± 0.05 and points within 25° of the injected direction. **M2 (reported):** p_A of the mutated data; it is required to be < 0.003 only if 0.20 ≥ 4σ_A(boot) of the real data; otherwise it is a statement of power, not a pipeline failure. The systematics are rerun on the mutated data to show that the pass lines CAN be passed by a real sky dipole.

## Expectations (hand estimates, written down so they can be wrong)
Per-galaxy log a₀ is uncertain by ~0.1–0.2 dex once distance is marginalised (a₀ ∝ D² in the deep regime), so σ_A ~ 0.06–0.12 for ~150 northern-heavy galaxies; the published ±0.04 probably reflects fixed distances. A coherent ~150 km/s peculiar-velocity error at ~20 Mpc biases Hubble-flow a₀ by ~20%, i.e. the size of the claimed dipoles, which is why the distance split is binding. Expected: no signal passing S1–S3 (P ≈ 0.75), A₉₅ ≈ 0.2–0.35, the published amplitude along Chang's direction not excluded at 95% (P ≈ 0.5).
