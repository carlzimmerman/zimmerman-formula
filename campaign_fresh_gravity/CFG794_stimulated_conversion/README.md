# CFG794: can any known enhancement close the conversion gap? (theory only)

**Label: NOT CLOSABLE.**

| class | best enhancement | note |
|---|---|---|
| (a) resonant cavity | ≤ 1.7 × 10⁶ | Limited by the ambient field's own bandwidth, (c/σ)², not by the cavity Q. |
| (b) stimulated (laser) | below threshold by **4 × 10²⁷** | Even with Q = 10¹¹, the cavity loses photons far faster than cold energy adds them. Seeding then costs more energy than it extracts. |
| (c) coherent volume | ≤ 4 × 10⁴ | Coherence length 0.1 mm at a 2.5 eV portal scale (conditional on that scale). |
| (d) parametric pumping | reduces to (b) | The pump supplies the photons. |

The best passive product, (a) × (c), is 7 × 10¹⁰. The gap needs 6.4 × 10³⁴, so it falls short by about 24 orders of magnitude.

A cold-energy laser would reach threshold only if the spontaneous lifetime were about 10²⁷× shorter than the sky allows
(CFG789). That is, the coupling would already have to make the sky glow.

Controls: K1 (Planck mode count) and K2 (S) pass. MUTATE (τ = 1 μs) crosses threshold, so the test bites.
