# CFG789: cold energy → light

**Label: DATA-CLOSED for ambient fuel.**
- The cosmic optical background caps cold energy's spontaneous conversion to light at a lifetime τ ≥ 4.4 × 10¹⁵ yr.
- Ambient cold energy in 1 km³ near the Sun can therefore give at most 4 × 10⁻¹⁹ W of light.

  mechanism for that is in the record.
