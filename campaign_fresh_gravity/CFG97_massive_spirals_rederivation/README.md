# CFG97 -- independent re-derivation of CFG41 (Di Teodoro+2023 massive spirals) and of its selection-bias Monte Carlo

**Question (frozen: the docstrings of `cfg97_massive_spirals_hi.py` and `cfg97_selbias_mc.py`, hashes in `FROZEN_HASHES.txt`, written before any run).** Does CFG41's headline reproduce from independent code: over the 15 Di Teodoro+2023 disks (canonical footing, B = 0.076 +/- 0.030) the law's corrected offset is -0.028 +/- 0.066 dex (-0.43 sigma), the four S0/S0a +0.004 +/- 0.071 (+0.05 sigma), and the rule's -0.105 +/- 0.131 (-0.80 sigma) on the S0/S0a; and does the selection-bias Monte Carlo (CFG41_selbias_mc) reproduce? The agent read only the three READMEs, the two CFG41 docstrings (ast.get_docstring) and the data TSVs; it opened the CFG41/CFG53 scripts, .out and JSON only after its frozen main and MUTATE runs (a grep of CFG41's a0 and Lelli lines and a read of both .out files; the script bodies were not read). CFG36's machinery was forbidden, so **the rule side could not be reconstructed.** Assumptions it had to make: nu_mono := the exponential RAR form (CFG64: agrees to 3e-9 for y <= 0.1); canonical a0 = 9.36e-11 and alt 1.13e-10 (from the standing note and the README's alt-minus-canonical law shift of -0.017 dex); the Lelli+2016 flat-part algorithm is the agent's own; B = 0.076 +/- 0.030 taken from the README for the headline (B_mine reported separately).

**The rule side and the shape statement.** The rule depends on Mandelbaum's masses, the Dutton-Maccio NFW, the conservation form at x_e = 0.40 and the definition of f_ex, all inside CFG36's machinery; a consistency-only test using the README's f_ex as an input finds that neither v^2 = v_law^2 (1+f) nor v_law^2/(1-f) gives the README's rule-minus-law shift of 0.109 dex (they give 0.082 and 0.159), so f_ex is not a simple velocity-squared fraction; this is not a reproduction. CFG53's law-side shape numbers: the frozen run pooled unweighted (red +0.033 +/- 0.131, blue +0.042 +/- 0.047) and got them DIFFERENT from CFG53's (+0.014 +/- 0.124, +0.052 +/- 0.041); CFG53 uses inverse-variance pooling with the error scaled by sqrt(chi2/dof) (learned after the frozen runs, from the CFG53 docstring); the post-hoc rerun (`posthoc_shape_weighting.py`) gives red +0.0135 +/- 0.1241 and blue +0.0517 +/- 0.0406, a three-decimal match, and the per-galaxy slopes match; CFG53's rule-shape numbers (+0.155, the power, -1.22 sigma) are not reproduced.

**Failed controls (kept).** C2: the Lelli algorithm reproduces the tabulated v_flat within 3 km/s for 10 of 15, against the declared 12 (the same count as CFG41, a somewhat different set: NGC1324, NGC2599, NGC5790, UGC02885, UGC12811); the tolerance is an absolute 3 km/s, so it is not scale-invariant (under MUTATE velocities x0.5 the same algorithm passes 12/15). C3a: the frozen Freeman-against-ring-integral check returned inf (a numerical fault in the agent's control); the post-hoc repair (`c3a_repair.py`) gives a maximum relative difference of 4e-8; the C3b peak check passed (0.6221 sqrt(GM/Rd) at 2.150 R_d). C5 failed because the MC's own M3 control failed. **M3 (injected offset) is a real result:** the recovered-minus-injected error is +0.018 at delta = -0.05, -0.018 at +0.05 and -0.035 at +0.10, so B is not additive: it shrinks as the true offset grows, by about 0.35 x delta (CFG41's own single-measurement Table 1b shows the same); solving raw = delta + B(delta), the S0 corrected offset moves from +0.003 to about +0.019 (+0.27 sigma) and the 15 from -0.028 to about -0.029. MUTATE (velocities x0.5): H1 and H2 both fail (-5.0 and -4.2 sigma), exit 1. The MC MUTATE run fails the R1 reproduction check only because no rows match when the selection is decoupled (B = nan), a weak control.

**What the agent would attack.** (1) B is not fixed by the data: correcting with B = 0, 0.058, 0.076, 0.081 gives corrected 15-galaxy means +0.048, -0.010, -0.028, -0.033 and S0 means +0.079 (+1.12 sigma), +0.021, +0.003, -0.002. The referee's 'about 0.02 too large' is partly reproduced: at fixed (s_int, s_w), adding 0.2-dex M* errors lowers the matched-row bias by 0.011 (0.0668 to 0.0566) and 0.016 (0.0845 to 0.0684); re-matching on measured mass gives 0.068-0.094 (centre 0.081), so the claim depends on which rows count as matched. The mock uses a0 = 1.2e-10 while the law test uses 9.36e-11 (an internal inconsistency; each a0 gives only one matched row): with the mock a0 = 9.36e-11 the matched B is 0.119, giving 15 at -0.071 (-1.08 sigma) and S0 at -0.040 (-0.56); with 1.13e-10, B = 0.087, giving -0.039 and -0.008. No verdict flips, but the sign of the S0 offset does. (2) A galaxy-specific bias lowers the S0 correction: the S0 four sit higher in mass (conditional bias 0.060 against 0.078 for the 15), so the corrected S0 becomes +0.019 (+0.27 sigma). (3) The HI-to-baryon conversion is negligible (gas x0 to x2 moves the 15-galaxy mean by +0.016 to -0.014; M* from the rotation-curve decomposition -0.004; Upsilon 0.5 and 0.7 give +0.021 and -0.018); stellar mass is what matters. (4) Inclination is not in the tables: a common +3 degree error moves v by -0.023, -0.013 and -0.006 dex at 45, 60 and 75 degrees. (5) Distance is small (random per-galaxy SD 0.007, common +-5% scale +-0.011; with inclination the floor rises only from 0.066 to 0.068; CFG41's floor omits both). (6) Leave-one-out: the 15-galaxy mean ranges -0.037 to -0.018 (jackknife SE 0.017), no sign flip; the S0 four range -0.013 to +0.014 and the sign flips: the S0 mean is statistically zero, dominated by the 0.053-0.059 M* systematic and the 0.030 B error. (7) The P2 kernel gives the 15 at -0.005 (-0.07 sigma) and the S0 at +0.023 (+0.33 sigma), moving the law offset by about 0.023 dex (CFG64 reported only the rule numbers); a0 = 1.2e-10 gives -0.050 (-0.77 sigma); the Freeman and sphere brackets move the raw 15-galaxy mean to +0.034 and +0.058 against +0.048; an inverse-variance weighted raw mean is +0.073 +/- 0.009. (8) The a0 footing was inferred from the README and memory and should be confirmed in CFG36's code.

The law fitting these 15 says nothing about the rule. Nothing here says the data favour the framework; kappa = 1/2 is FITTED.
