# astra crispy ideas

Forty proposed calculations in two sets of twenty, prepared on 2026-09-26 from the repository's current research frontier. These are assignments with falsifiable outcomes, not completed calculations or a claim that the theory of gravity has been established.

- [Detailed calculation cards](docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-26-astra-crispy-ideas.md): inputs, first calculation, completion criterion and controls.
- [Machine-readable task queue](docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-26-astra-crispy-ideas.tasks.json): dependencies, source keys and disjoint output directories.
- [Source snapshot inventory](docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-26-astra-crispy-ideas.sources.json): exact working-tree hashes and availability on the publication base.

Start in parallel with **C01, C02, C05 and C07** for the common-action construction. **C13** tests a fragile numerical merger margin. **C24 and C27** test whether the proposed inverse and pressure relations can become measurements. Existing L388/L389 work is a prerequisite to reconcile, not a job to duplicate.

The operative repo target is **nu_mono with causality criterion B**. The user's request to explore both exponential laws is retained as separately named exact-RAR and exact-AQUAL comparison branches. Results from these branches, CA5, the separate spectral action and the phenomenological carrier simulations cannot be combined into a certification of one theory.

**Source availability:** this documentation branch is based on remote main at `ecffd2af3`. The research snapshot was read from local HEAD `fef4cfd8b` plus working-tree changes. Of 45 cited files, 16 match the publication base, 2 differ, and 27 are absent there. They are fingerprinted, not bundled. Local agents can resolve them in the shared checkout; remote agents must acquire the exact prerequisite sources before dependent execution.

To claim work, choose one card and create its `CLAIM.json` under `real_research/astra_crispy_ideas/Cxx/`. Record the owner, branch, frozen action/kernel, source hashes and bounded scope; report the claim to the coordinator. Keep code, derivations, evidence and the final `RESULT.md` in that directory. Use a separate output directory for each task and explicitly label inconclusive or failed results.
