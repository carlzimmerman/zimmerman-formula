**What "bounded" means.** The record can now say, without another lane, what the form is, which of its numbers are
fitted, where it was tested and holds, where it fails, what it is silent on, and which frozen lines would kill it. It
cannot say that the form is derived or that it passes. Section 6 lists what a derived closure would need; none of it
exists.

## 1. The form

- **The law.** g^2 / (a0 + g) = g_Newton, with a0 = kappa c sqrt(G rho_DE) and kappa = 1/2.
- **Cold dark energy, counted once.** The universe carries X = 5.41 times the baryons in a cold component. Around
  condensed baryons the law describes how that component sits; it is not a second force on top of it. A hot system
  holds 5.41 times the baryons that fell into it. This is form B, rules 1 to 5 of PAPER72 (version 1.5, DOI
  "10.5281/zenodo.24231666"). The owner's decision of 2026-11-09: "Form B is the form the record closes on." and "It is
  chosen as a form, not as a pass." (`closure_2026_11_09/OWNER_DECISION_2026-11-09_FINAL_FORM_B.md`).
- **Two fitted constants.** kappa: "KAPPA NOT DERIVED" (KAP1), "KAPPA NOT DERIVED (FITTED)" (OUT4). Both: "kappa = 1/2
  and X = 5.41 are fitted." (`COMPLETED_FORM.md`). The share is fitted to the CMB.
- **One rule that is not a solution.** "(The hot branch is a rule, not a solution of the action.)" (`COMPLETED_FORM.md`,
  rule 5).
- **One rule that is missing.** With the law's layout at every radius the form fails the Lyman-alpha forest: "FAILS at
  the forest's own redshifts" and "an independent blind build FAILS (8142/7686)" (PAPER72, the forest row); at the level
  of the transmitted flux, "The full law layout is dead at flux level." (row 22). So the form needs a sixth rule that
  says where the law's layout stops. Two candidates are on the record and neither is adopted (Section 3).

## 2. Where it holds

**With power, as the law's own passes** (none is evidence for the cold component or for a sixth rule):

- Rotation curves of 175 nearby galaxies (row 1): "PASS: rms 0.0563 (line 0.0660)" (PD1), with the stellar
  mass-to-light ratio fitted and no galaxy faster than 332 km/s. "A pass of the law, not of rule 6."
- The Solar System (row 2): carried, not recomputed for form B.
- The Milky Way's enclosed-mass profile from DESI DR2 (row 31): "9 of 12" (DW1); "The extrapolated M200c fails."
- Lensing equals dynamics, one of the three frozen kill tests (Section 5): measured, passes, "Power: weak."

**By construction, so not an independent test:** CMB peaks, CMB lensing and the growth rate (row 23). "Linear content
is the cold reference; the share is fitted to the CMB."

**Equal to the cold reference, so not a test of the law or of a sixth rule:** the richest clusters' amount on its
one-sided gate, their radial shape (only under a post hoc amendment), and the forest at matter level under the first
candidate (rows 14, 16, 21).

**With fragile power only.** The scorecard lists KiDS early-type lensing (row 11, a gate a blind build found cannot see
the settled radius), nine massive spirals on canonical (row 4) and two Milky Way rows (29, 30) under "With fragile
power only, and none confirmed by an independent blind re-grade". Its other two rows that read PASSES are the forest
holds of Section 3 (row 22) and two dwarf classes that sit inside their interval only with the systematics budget
(row 37).

**The count** (the scorecard, the same on both footings): "Canonical: EQUALS THE LAW 14; NOT GRADED FOR THIS FORM 6; NO
POWER 8; FAILS 2; PASSES TRIVIALLY 5; DEPENDS ON AN UNTESTED ASSUMPTION 6; PASSES 3; FAILS ON COVERAGE ONLY 1. Total
45."

So what holds is the law on today's rotating galaxies and in the Solar System, with one fitted constant, and a cold
component that reproduces the linear cosmology because it is fitted to it. Outside galaxies the form equals the cold
reference or has no power in most rows.

## 3. Where it fails

**Standing fails, both footings:**

- **Outer cluster shear (row 20).** Carried from PAPER72 as a fail near 3 sigma with systematics. Counting the cold
  share out to turnaround closes about a third of the miss: "STILL FAILS / STILL FAILS (canonical / alt)" (OTA1, not
  blind).
- **DF2, DF4, NGC 147 and IC 1613 (row 36).** They sit below the form's law by more than twice their uncertainty, DF2
  and DF4 by "0.743 / 0.781 and 0.943 / 0.981 dex below"; tides cannot reach them; a second, blind build reads "LOW ROWS
  COUNT AGAINST B" (RB2R). "Rule 6 does not touch them: form B's fail stays."

**The forest, for the form as adopted** (Section 1). The two candidate sixth rules:

- *The crossing-speed joint with the record's junction* (CJ1; rows 21, 22, 44). It held the forest at flux level in one
  build, and the blind build read no verdict. That junction rescales the law's halo, so the layout that held is not the
  law inside: "THE HOLD DOES NOT SURVIVE THE EXACT JUNCTION" (XJ1). It also fails its own coverage line on massive discs
  at redshift 2.1 to 2.5: "JOINT FAILS ON G2 (coverage)" (JNT2 and the blind JNT2R).
- *The law unscaled inside the second or third caustic of the clock* (XJ1, post hoc). A blind build confirms the forest
  holds as labels, fragile: "CAUSTIC 3 HOLDS, BLIND, FRAGILE (lam_x0.5)" (XJ1R). But it is a drawn profile. The clock's
  own flow does not deliver the charge the law's halo needs there, "SHORT AT CAUSTIC 2 / SHORT AT CAUSTIC 3" (CSD1), and
  the layout that does conserve charge reads "FAILS THE FOREST (caustic 3)" (BJ1). On the weak-lensing amplitude "the
  exact junction WORSENS it, on both footings" (WLJ2, not blind).

PAPER72 put it as the lead's reading, not a lane's label: "So far no layout graded here meets all three of" the law
unscaled inside, charge conserved at the junction, and the forest. BJ1's flux-level run has since failed, so that still
stands.

**The weak-lensing amplitude (row 25).** For the form as adopted: "AMPLITUDE EXCLUDED BY RAW KiDS AT 3 SIGMA" as frozen
(CO2), a tension once the reference run's noise is corrected. Raw KiDS sits about 5.4 of its own error widths below the
amplitude the CMB pins, for the cold reference as well: "The tension is the one any cold component fitted to the CMB
carries, and the split does not relieve it." On the surveys' current releases the gates read no verdict as frozen, and
consistent, fragile, post hoc and in one blind re-grade; PAPER72 calls these "a consistency check, not a pass". With
the exact junction, by the earlier lane's gate rule: "ROW FAILS WITH THE EXACT JUNCTION" (WLJ2). The scorecard's word
for the row is NO POWER, placed for the record's junction.

**Predictions.** "No registered prediction has come true." Of 115 entries: "41 not yet tested, 20 post-dictions, 14
retired or withdrawn, 17 undecided, 12 failed, 11 consistent but not distinctive, 0 came true"
(`predictions_2026/PREDICTIONS_SCORECARD_2026-11-12.md`). For form B itself, "DPR1 found no dated distinctive
prediction for form B as adopted" (`COMPLETED_FORM.md`).

**Against it unless an untested assumption holds:** Euclid's wide lenses break the law's ceiling on the stars alone
(row 40; the data word is confirmed blind). The form then needs group mass along the sightline, which turns on gas
masses and source redshifts that are not measured lens by lens.

**High, without power to call a fail:** the ultra-faint dwarfs and M31's bright dwarfs (row 35). Their medians stay
above the form; no class fails under a full systematics budget and none could detect the frozen offset.

## 4. What it does not say

- **The two constants.** No lane derives kappa or the share. The crossing speed that the candidate rule uses carries
  both: "This is an algebraic speed intersection, not a derivation of a transition" (`COMPLETED_FORM.md`).
- **How a falling clock becomes a standing one.** Collapse computed with the record's own equations folds at the centre
  and "the equations give no answer beyond that moment" (PAPER72, on COL1). "The standing state exists." (PAPER72, on
  CSD1); what is missing is the transport into it: "the flow brings one to twelve per cent of the charge, and the action
  has no other channel in the weak field" (PAPER72).
- **What clusters are.** Their non-baryonic mass under form B "reads NO CANDIDATE on both footings" (PAPER72, on CLS2).
  No single dust term serves the CMB, clusters and galaxies together.
- **Why the boundary is sharp, what the medium is, and the kinetic function.** Not derived, not identified, not fixed
  (`COMPLETED_FORM.md`, "What is not derived").
- **Stability beyond a point mass.** A disc in an external field has no verdict, with stable excluded.
- **Six rows turn on an untested assumption** (8, 15, 19, 27, 28, 40): how many points count as independent on resolved
  high-redshift discs, the hydrostatic mass of the richest clusters to about 10 per cent, the Bullet's stripped gas,
  the Milky Way halo's outer star-count slope and anisotropy, and the wide lenses' group gas and source redshifts.
  **Eight rows have no power** (6, 7, 9, 10, 17, 18, 25, 35).

## 6. What a derived closure would need

None of these exists, and no lane on the record points at one.

- An equation for the clock behind a fold: "What would decide the joint now is an equation for what the clock is
  behind a fold" (PAPER72).
- A flow of the action that carries the charge into the standing state, across a transition of finite width, with the
  conserved current's interface condition met (PAPER72, version 1.5).
- A derivation of kappa and of the share: "no data row can grade them; a derivation would." (the scorecard's last list).

## 7. The statement

The record closes, bounded, on this. The law with a0 = (1/2) c sqrt(G rho_DE) describes today's rotating galaxies and
the Solar System from their baryons with one fitted constant. A cold dark energy component at 5.41 times the baryons,
fitted to the CMB and counted once, carries the linear cosmology by construction and gives the richest clusters'
amount at its ceiling. As adopted the form fails the Lyman-alpha forest, the outer cluster shear and four low dwarfs,
and its weak-lensing amplitude is in tension with raw KiDS (excluded at 3 sigma as frozen). A sixth rule removes the
forest fail only as a drawn profile that the clock's own flow does not supply. Both constants are fitted, the hot
branch and the joint are rules and not solutions, and no registered prediction has come true. Gaia DR4 on 2026-12-02
can kill it and cannot confirm it. In PAPER72's words: "The candidate is NOT ADOPTED. The theory is not closed."
