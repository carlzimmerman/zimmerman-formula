\begin{abstract}
We present a relativistic completion of the Zimmerman acceleration scale
$a_0 = \tfrac12 c\sqrt{G\rho_\Lambda}$ as an Aether--Scalar--Tensor theory
whose free function is not free.  Requiring the MOND interpolating function
to be the \emph{derivative} of the Lagrangian determines it in closed form
from the measured mode count $n=2$:
\[
f(\mathcal{X})=\mathcal{X}-2\ln\!\bigl(1+\sqrt{\mathcal{X}}\bigr)
-\frac{2}{1+\sqrt{\mathcal{X}}}+1,\qquad
f'(\mathcal{X})=\mu_2(\sqrt{\mathcal{X}})=\frac{u(2+u)}{(1+u)^2},
\]
with $\mathcal{X}$ the invariant built from the spatial projector of a unit
timelike aether.  Three sectors then follow from the one function with no new
parameters.  (i)~\emph{Dark energy:} FRW homogeneity forces
$\mathcal{X}=0$ identically, and $f(0)=-1$ gives $w=-1$ exactly --- the
cosmological constant is the value of the MOND function at its non-analytic
point, so the background is exactly $\Lambda$CDM.  (ii)~\emph{MOND:} the
static sourced equation $\nabla\!\cdot\![f'\nabla\phi]=4\pi G\rho$ yields
$g^2=a_0g_N$ in the deep limit and $g\to g_N$ in the Newtonian limit; the
observed radial acceleration relation is reproduced to $5.9$ per cent with
nothing fitted.  (iii)~\emph{Cold sector:} the shift symmetry's Noether
charge is exactly conserved, so its density scales as $a^{-3}$ and is cold by
construction --- it is not a particle species, and the theory predicts no
direct-detection signal.  The theory is healthy on the entire branch:
$f'>0$ (no ghost), and
$c_s^2=(u^2+3u+2)/(u^2+3u+4)\in[\tfrac12,1)$ (no gradient instability and
subluminal), with the non-analytic point regular.  We state the open
problems honestly: the linear perturbation spectrum, the preferred-frame
parameters $\alpha_{1,2}$ and the aether's own dynamics are not settled here,
and the integer $n=2$ remains a measurement.  We give four pre-registered
falsification tests.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

The radial acceleration relation (RAR; \citealt{McGaugh2016}) correlates the
observed centripetal acceleration in galaxies with that predicted by their
baryons with a scatter of $\sim0.1$~dex over five decades.  Two readings
compete: a modification of the gravitational law (MOND;
\citealt{Milgrom1983}) or the response of a dark halo that correlates with
baryons.  Both need a relativistic completion.  Relativistic MOND
completions (TeVeS, Einstein-Aether, BIMOND, and more recently
Aether-Scalar-Tensor, AeST; \citealt{SkordisZlosnik2021}) carry a
\emph{free function} that is fitted to the rotation-curve data.

In this paper the free function is not free.  The Zimmerman programme
\citep{ZimmermanProgramme} identifies the acceleration scale as
$a_0=\tfrac12 c\sqrt{G\rho_\Lambda}$ and, from SPARC data, fixes the
interpolating function's \emph{slope} to the integer mode count $n=2$.  We
show that these two inputs determine the Lagrangian up to an irrelevant
constant, and that the resulting action produces dark energy, the MOND law
and a cold sector from a single expression.

\section{The action}

We take the AeST-type action
\begin{equation}
S=\int\!\sqrt{-g}\left[\frac{M_P^2}{2}R-\Lambda^4 f(\mathcal{X})\right]+S_m[g,\psi],
\end{equation}
with
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{X}=\frac{h^{\mu\nu}\partial_\mu\phi\,\partial_\nu\phi}{2\Lambda^4},
\qquad
h^{\mu\nu}=g^{\mu\nu}-\frac{u^\mu u^\nu}{u^2},
\end{equation}
where $u^\mu$ is a unit timelike aether, hypersurface-orthogonal, aligned
with the cosmic frame in FRW.

The spatial projector is not optional.  Because $\mu_2$ depends on
$\sqrt{\mathcal{X}}$, the function $f$ is non-analytic at
$\mathcal{X}=0$.  In a pure $k$-essence construction the galactic branch is
spacelike and the cosmological branch timelike, so $\sqrt{\mathcal{X}}$ is
imaginary on one of them.  The projector makes $\mathcal{X}\ge0$ in both, so
one real function covers galaxy and cosmos.

\subsection{The function is fixed}

Requiring $f'(\mathcal{X})=\mu_2(\sqrt{\mathcal{X}})$ and $f(0)=-1$ gives
equation~(1) uniquely up to an additive constant, which is physically
irrelevant (it shifts $\Lambda$ only).  We verified the derivative identity
symbolically.

\section{Three sectors}

\subsection{Dark energy}

In FRW with $u^\mu$ aligned to the cosmic frame, a homogeneous $\phi(t)$ has
a purely timelike gradient, so $h^{00}=g^{00}+u^0u^0=-1+1=0$ and
$\mathcal{X}=0$ \emph{identically}.  Then
\begin{equation}
\rho=2\mathcal{X}f'-f=+1,\qquad p=f=-1,\qquad w=-1,
\end{equation}
exactly.  The cosmological constant is the value of the MOND function at its
non-analytic point.  This is why the CMB background is exactly $\Lambda$CDM:
there is no freedom for it to be otherwise.

\subsection{MOND}

The static, weak-field, sourced equation is
\begin{equation}
\nabla\!\cdot\!\left[f'(\mathcal{X})\nabla\phi\right]=4\pi G\rho,
\end{equation}
i.e.\ AQUAL with $\mu=f'$.  In spherical symmetry
$\mu(g/a_0)\,g=g_N$.  We solved this numerically: the deep limit returns
$g^2=a_0g_N$ with the deviation falling linearly in $u=g/s$ over five
decades ($+1.24\times10^{-2}$ at $u=1.65\times10^{-2}$ down to
$+1.23\times10^{-4}$ at $u=1.63\times10^{-4}$), and $g\to g_N$ for
$g_N\gg a_0$.  Against the observed RAR the theory deviates by at most
$5.9$ per cent across the transition --- with $a_0$ taken from
$(c,G,\rho_\Lambda)$ and the shape from the measured $n=2$; nothing was
fitted.

We emphasise that equation~(4) is \emph{sourced}.  The sourceless
self-gravitating $k$-essence equation gives $\rho\propto r^{-3}$; that is a
different equation and is not this theory's.

\subsection{The cold sector}

The action is invariant under $\phi\to\phi+c$, so the Noether current is
exactly conserved and its charge density obeys $\dot n+3Hn=0$, i.e.\
$n\propto a^{-3}$ (verified numerically to $9.3\times10^{-5}$).  Being a
charge, it carries no pressure perturbation: it is exactly cold.  This is
the dark sector, and it is a conservation law rather than a particle
hypothesis.  It produces no signal in direct-detection experiments.

\section{Health}

For $u>0$: $f'=\mu_2>0$, so no ghost.  The sound speed is
\begin{equation}
c_s^2=\frac{f'}{f'+2\mathcal{X}f''}=\frac{u^2+3u+2}{u^2+3u+4},
\end{equation}
so $\tfrac12\le c_s^2<1$ on the whole branch: no gradient instability and
subluminal.  As $u\to0$, $c_s^2\to\tfrac12$ --- the non-analytic point is
regular.

\section{What is not settled}

We state these plainly.  (i)~The linear perturbation spectrum, the CMB
acoustic amplitudes and the growth rate are not computed here.
(ii)~The preferred-frame parameters $\alpha_1,\alpha_2$ depend on whether
the aether is dynamical; if it carries Einstein-Aether kinetic terms they are
of order the coefficients $c_i$ and are bounded by
$|\alpha_1|<10^{-4}$ and $|\alpha_2|<1.6\times10^{-9}$.  AeST is known to
admit a healthy corner \citep{SkordisZlosnik2021}, but it must be
re-verified with this function.  (iii)~The aether's own dynamics is
inherited, not derived.  (iv)~$n=2$ is a measurement; we have closed the
derivation routes we could construct, not proved its necessity.

\section{Falsification}

\begin{enumerate}
\item If $c_s^2$ leaves $[0,1]$ anywhere on the branch, the theory is dead
(checked here on the full branch: it does not).
\item If the perturbation spectrum fails the CMB acoustic peaks at the
sub-per-cent level, the completion is dead.
\item If $a_0$ differs from the measured value by more than the
$\rho_\Lambda$ systematics, the identification is dead.  We obtain
$a_0=9.36\times10^{-11}\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$ against a measured
$\sim1.2\times10^{-10}$: a 22 per cent discrepancy that is within the
historical spread of the MOND value but must be sharpened.
\item If a direct-detection experiment claims a WIMP, this theory is dead:
it predicts none.
\end{enumerate}
