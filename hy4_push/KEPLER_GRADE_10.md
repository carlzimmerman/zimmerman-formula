# TEN KEPLER-GRADE PREDICTIONS
### Quantitative, falsifiable, and made by no other framework

Each prediction is a **specific number or curve**, derived from the framework
with zero free parameters beyond the measured scale (Ω_Λ ≡ a₀) and the measured
integer n = 2. Each states the instrument that can kill it.

Reference values: **a₀ = 9.3624×10⁻¹¹ m/s²** (canonical footing),
n = 2, Λ = 2.2404 meV, Z = 5.7888, cH₀/a₀ = 6.994.

---

## P1. THE UNIVERSAL SURFACE DENSITY
**Prediction:** the phantom's mean surface density within r_M is the same for
every galaxy, independent of mass:

$$\Sigma_{\rm ph} = \frac{a_0}{\pi G} = 213.8\ M_\odot\,{\rm pc}^{-2}\quad(\log_{10} = 2.330)$$

**Derivation:** M_ph(<r_M) = M_b (amplitude law, coefficient 1) and
r_M² = GM_b/a₀ ⟹ Σ = M_b/(πr_M²) = a₀/(πG). No M_b survives.

**Kills it:** measure μ_0D = ρ_0r_0 across a mass range; any systematic trend
with mass kills the lemma. Observed: log₁₀ = 2.15 ± 0.05 (Donato+2009) — our
range 106.9–213.8 brackets it; the prefactor convention is being fixed.

## P2. THE ACCELERATION AT THE MOND RADIUS IS EXACTLY a₀
**Prediction:** g(r_M) = a₀ = 9.3624×10⁻¹¹ m/s², **for every galaxy, at every mass.**

**Derivation:** g = √(GM_b a₀)/r and r_M = √(GM_b/a₀) ⟹ g(r_M) = a₀ identically.

**Kills it:** measure the acceleration at the radius where the dark and baryonic
masses are equal. If it is not a₀, the amplitude law is wrong.

**Kills it:** any systematic deviation from unity at r_M.

## P5. THE DISPERSION–VELOCITY RELATION
**Prediction:** σ/v_c = 1/√2 = **0.707107**, at all masses.

**Derivation:** σ² = ½√(GM_b a₀) (G046) and v_c² = √(GM_b a₀) ⟹ σ = v_c/√2.

**Kills it:** measure the halo velocity dispersion and the asymptotic circular
velocity in the same system; any ratio other than 0.7071 kills the
equilibrium temperature (G046's central result).

## P6. THE BARYONIC TULLY–FISHER ZERO POINT
**Prediction:** M_b = A v⁴ with **A = 1/(G a₀) = 80.46 M⊙ (km/s)⁻⁴**,
i.e. v⁴ = GM_b a₀, giving:

| M_b | 10¹⁰ | 6×10¹⁰ |
|---|---|---|
| v_c | 105.6 km/s | 165.2 km/s |

**Kills it:** the zero point is fixed by Λ and n (A = n M_Pl/(G Λ²)).
A measured zero point disagreeing beyond systematics is the same 22% tension
as P11 below, seen in a different observable.

## P7. THE FULL RAR CURVE, ZERO PARAMETERS
**Prediction:** given g_N/a₀, the ratio g_obs/g_N is fixed by μ₂:

| g_N/a₀ | 0.01 | 0.10 | 0.50 | 1.00 | 2.00 | 10.0 | 100 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **g_obs/g_N** | 10.385 | 3.571 | 1.868 | 1.489 | 1.247 | 1.027 | 1.000 |

This is the **shape** prediction — it is the curve, not just its asymptote.
Measured on 155 SPARC curves: 0.150 dex, zero free parameters.

**Kills it:** any systematic deviation of the *shape* (not just the asymptote)
from this curve.

## P8. THE EFE TRUNCATION RADIUS
**Prediction:** the halo is truncated where the internal field meets the
external one:

$$r_{\rm cap} = \frac{a_0}{g_{\rm ext}}\,r_M$$

For the Milky Way (M_b = 6×10¹⁰, g_ext ≈ 1.7 a₀): **r_cap = 5.56 kpc**, i.e.
0.588 r_M. Field galaxies (low g_ext) extend much further; satellites and
cluster members truncate early.

**Kills it:** measure halo sizes vs environment. A satellite galaxy whose halo
extends beyond its predicted r_cap kills the EFE cap.

## P9. THE PHANTOM SHARE DECLINES WITH REDSHIFT
**Prediction:** the baryon-tied fraction of the dark sector falls as g_ext
grows, so **the RAR must weaken with z**: its scatter grows as
σ(z) = σ₀·s(0)/s(z), from 0.064 dex at z = 0 to ~0.29 dex by z = 2.

| z | 0 | 0.3 | 0.6 | 1.0 | 1.5 | 2.0 | 3.0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| phantom share | 11.8% | 8.3% | 6.2% | 4.5% | 3.3% | 2.5% | 1.6% |

**No other framework predicts this** (ΛCDM: same substance at all epochs;
MOND: fixed force-law shape).

**Kills it:** H026's pre-registered test. Current status: **NOT ESTABLISHED**
(MSA-3D spans too narrow a baseline). A wide-baseline sample showing flat
scatter kills it.

## P10. GRAVITATIONAL WAVES AND BLACK HOLES SEE EXACTLY GR
**Predictions:** (a) c_T = c **exactly** — no Horndeski or disformal coupling
exists in the action to generate α_T; (b) black holes carry **no independent
scalar charge** (the profile is secondary hair fixed by M); (c) compact objects
obey the **same phantom law** as galaxies, M_ph/M = r/r_M with
r_M = √(GM/a₀) — e.g. r_M(10 M⊙) = 0.122 pc, r_M(10⁹ M⊙) = 1.22 kpc.

**Kills it:** any tensor-speed deviation, any BH scalar charge, or any
deviation of compact-object environments from the r/r_M law.

## THE META-PREDICTION (P11): the 22%
Every prediction above inherits one liability. The postulate gives
a₀ = 9.3624×10⁻¹¹; rotation-curve fits prefer ~1.2×10⁻¹⁰ — a **22% gap**.
It appears in P1 (surface density), P4 (r_M), P6 (BTFR zero point).

**This is the single test that decides the framework.** Either the measured a₀
carries ~20–30% systematics (its literature values do vary that widely), or the
postulate needs correcting. **Unresolved.**
