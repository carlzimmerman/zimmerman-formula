# H058 — THE ROOM
Eleven temperaments, 50 combinations. Each opens a door grounded in this
repo. Pairs are 1-on-1; a few triples are marked [3].

## PAIRS
**1 Feynman + Noether.** Feynman: sum over histories. Noether: what is the
symmetry? DOOR: path-integrate over the acceleration field (H055's Lomax).
The kernel as a CDF is a sum-over-histories object. Identify the symmetry
whose Noether charge is the dark mass — and note J^0 = 0 means the symmetry
is NOT the shift. Maybe it is a gauge symmetry of the acceleration field.
**2 von Neumann + Godel.** Is the completion UNDECIDABLE? vN: formalize the
question. Godel: prove some questions have no answer inside the system.
DOOR: find a statement about the framework that is neither provable nor
refutable, then stop spending waves on it. Real contribution.
**3 Einstein + de Sitter.** Einstein gives the principles; de Sitter gives the
solution with Lambda. DOOR: a_0 = 0.5 c sqrt(G rho_L) is a de Sitter
statement — the acceleration scale of a horizon. Derive a_0 from the de
Sitter HORIZON (c H_Lambda / Z with Z = 2 sqrt(8pi/3)) rather than from
rho_Lambda. If the horizon derivation is independent, that is a new route.
**4 Dirac + Feynman.** Dirac's large numbers. DOOR: test whether
Lambda^2 = 2 M_Pl a_0 sits on a Dirac large-number coincidence, and whether
the 22% is the leftover of a large-number relation that is only approximate.
**5 Hilbert + Noether.** Hilbert: axiomatize. Noether: derive conservation.
DOOR: state the framework's AXIOMS explicitly (nobody has), then run Noether
on each. Any axiom without a conservation law is suspect.
**6 Poincare + Feynman.** Poincarefounded chaos. DOOR: is the G132
transition chaotic? Compute Lyapunov exponents for the two-zone system. If
chaotic, the break radius (which H039/D036 disagreed on) is unpredictable.
**7 Fermi + Lemaitre.** Fermi: order of magnitude. Lemaitre: real expansion.
DOOR: the famous Fermi estimate — is the phantom density today within an
order of magnitude of what the expansion history allows? Back-of-envelope the
cosmic dust budget (G198) independently.
**8 Godel + Hilbert.** Hilbert wanted completeness; Godel proved it
impossible. DOOR: is the framework's two-zone structure (inside/outside r_M)
a formal system with an undecidable boundary? The break radius may be
algorithmically undeterminable — which would explain the H039/D036 split.
**9 von Neumann + Dirac.** Operator algebras plus constrained quantization.
DOOR: quantize the frozen scalar as a CONSTRAINED system (Dirac brackets).
phi_dot = 0 is a constraint. Maybe the ghost is an artifact of quantizing
before imposing it (H011 imposed it classically).
**10 Einstein + Hilbert.** The Einstein-Hilbert method: find the right action.
DOOR: given H053 closes all analytic local actions, ask what NON-standard
action form (non-local, fractional, or non-commutative) reproduces mu_2.
**11 Feynman + Dirac.** Feynman: diagrams. Dirac: beauty. DOOR: is the
phantom a one-loop effect? Compute the leading quantum correction to the
dark-matter density and check whether it is the 1e-4 to 1e-3 H045 measured.
If so, the "ghost" is a loop-level artifact.
**12 Noether + Lemaitre.** Noether: conservation. Lemaitre: expansion.
DOOR: with expansion, there is no time-translation symmetry, so no energy
conservation. The dark sector's growth must be a NON-conservation statement.
Derive the growth law from the broken symmetry.
**13 Poincare + Hilbert.** Topology plus axiomatics. DOOR: is the dark
sector's conserved quantity topological (H051 M042)? Poincare would look for
a homology class; Hilbert would demand it be an axiom.
**14 von Neumann + Feynman.** Automata plus path integrals. DOOR: Monte Carlo
the dark sector (H057 V8): sample accelerations from the Lomax, build
synthetic halos, compare to SPARC.
**15 Einstein + Poincare.** Both founded relativity; Poincare saw
conventionalism. DOOR: is the choice of mu_2 CONVENTIONAL (a choice of
description) rather than physical? Poincare would say some choices are
conventions. If mu_2 is conventional, its shape is not derivable — which
matches H046 (health does not force n).
**16 Godel + Einstein.** Godel found rotating universes with CTCs. DOOR: does
the framework admit closed timelike curves or acausal propagation in the two-
zone regime? Check the cluster solution for causality violation.
**17 Dirac + Hilbert.** Dirac: constraints. Hilbert: formalism. DOOR:
classify ALL constraints of the frozen scalar using Dirac's algorithm. If
the constraint algebra is inconsistent, that is the completion's real
obstruction — not the ghost.
**18 Fermi + Feynman.** Pragmatism plus diagrams. DOOR: the fastest possible
falsification. Fermi would ask "what is the cheapest experiment?" Rank the
eight decisive tests (H056 Part 5) by cost per bit of information.
**19 de Sitter + Lemaitre.** The two cosmologists. DOOR: is the de Sitter
horizon the physical origin of the EFE cap (a_0/g_ext)? If the cap is
horizon-related, the cap radius is derivable — solving cluster C1.
**20 Noether + von Neumann.** Symmetries and algebras. DOOR: is the framework's
symmetry group a von Neumann algebra factor, and if so which TYPE? (H057 V13:
type III would explain why there is no particle.)
**21 Poincare + Godel.** Both on limits of formal systems. DOOR: prove the
cluster amplitude (C2) is not computable from the framework's axioms. Then
stop trying and measure it instead.
**22 Einstein + Fermi.** Principles and pragmatism. DOOR: Einstein would ask
for the PRINCIPLE behind a_0; Fermi would ask if it matters observationally.
Settle which questions are principle-questions and which are
measurement-questions, and split the program accordingly.
**23 Hilbert + Feynman.** Formalism vs intuition. DOOR: write the framework's
formalism (Hilbert) and then compute the simplest nontrivial observable

**24 Dirac + de Sitter.** Dirac's large numbers and de Sitter's Lambda.
DOOR: the coincidence problem. Is Omega_Lambda ~ 0.7 forced by the framework
or accidental? Dirac would demand a reason.
**25 Lemaitre + Godel.** Beginnings and limits. DOOR: the primeval atom. If

phantom exist at the Big Bang, or does it form (G194's dark-ages question)?
**26 Feynman + Poincare.** Intuition and recurrence. DOOR: Poincare

**27 von Neumann + Hilbert.** Rigor meeting rigor. DOOR: state the framework

**28 Noether + Dirac.** Symmetries and constraints. DOOR: the shift symmetry

**29 Einstein + von Neumann.** Einstein on quantum unease; vN on the

irrelevant — consistent with H054.
**30 Fermi + Hilbert.** Estimate then formalize. DOOR: Fermi-estimate every
framework parameter, then Hilbert-axiomatize. Anything that cannot be both
estimated AND axiomatized is not physics yet.
**31 Godel + Feynman.** Limits and calculation. DOOR: Godel says some things
are unprovable; Feynman says compute anyway. Where the framework is
unprovable, compute numerically and state it as measurement, not theorem.
**32 de Sitter + Dirac.** Both worked on Lambda and large numbers. DOOR:
derive Z = 2 sqrt(8pi/3) = 5.7888 from a de Sitter/Dirac argument. If Z is

**33 Poincare + Lemaitre.** Topology and expansion. DOOR: the global topology

**34 Noether + Godel.** Conservation and incompleteness. DOOR: if the dark

**35 Feynman + Einstein.** Intuition vs principle. DOOR: Feynman would build

**36 Hilbert + de Sitter.** Axioms for cosmology. DOOR: axiomatize the

**39 von Neumann + Lemaitre.** Automata and the primeval atom. DOOR: a

## TRIPLES
**41 [3] Einstein + Hilbert + Noether.** The historical trio that produced GR.
DOOR: redo for the framework — the principle (Einstein), the action (Hilbert),
the conservation (Noether). If all three close, the framework is complete.
Given H053, the action must be NON-standard: that is the research program.
**42 [3] Feynman + Dirac + von Neumann.** The quantum trio. DOOR: quantize the
frozen scalar properly — path integral (Feynman), constraints (Dirac),
operator algebra (vN). If the result is type III (H057 V13), the ghost may
be a category error (V15).
**43 [3] Poincare + Godel + Hilbert.** The limits trio. DOOR: determine
exactly what the framework CANNOT decide, and publish that as a theorem.
This saves future waves and is a genuine contribution.
**44 [3] de Sitter + Lemaitre + Einstein.** The cosmology trio. DOOR: derive
a_0 from the cosmic history rather than postulating it. a_0 = 0.5 c
sqrt(G rho_Lambda) may be a statement about the universe's expansion, not a
local force law.
**45 [3] Fermi + Feynman + Dirac.** The calculators. DOOR: compute the
cheapest decisive experiment and DO it numerically. Rank the eight tests of
H056 Part 5 by bits per dollar.
**46 [3] Noether + von Neumann + Godel.** Symmetry, algebra, limits. DOOR:
classify the framework's symmetries as an algebra and determine which
conservation laws are provable and which are not.
**47 [3] Poincare + Feynman + Fermi.** Intuition, diagrams, estimates. DOOR:
build an INTUITIVE mechanical picture of the phantom that Fermi could
estimate on an envelope and Feynman could draw. If no such picture exists,
the framework is not yet understood.
**48 [3] Hilbert + Dirac + de Sitter.** Formalism, constraints, Lambda. DOOR:
a constrained, formalism-clean, Lambda-consistent action — the last
admissible completion attempt (H056 Part 4's two loopholes).
**49 [3] Einstein + Godel + Poincare.** Principles, limits, structure. DOOR:
is the framework's two-zone structure a limitation of description (Poincare
conventionalism), a principle (Einstein), or a formal limit (Godel)?
**50 [3] ALL ELEVEN.** The full room. DOOR: state, in one sentence each, what
the framework IS. If the eleven sentences are compatible, that is the theory.
If they contradict, the contradiction is the research program. Do this as a
formal exercise — it is the cheapest possible diagnosis of where we stand.
