## What the change buys and what it costs

**Buys.**
- Dark energy becomes dynamical, and a0 follows V through a term of the action.
- There is no dust term, so MK1's and MK2's one-constant problem does not arise.
- In a potential that has always been static the clock is the cosmological one exactly (AC8).
- AC1's independent-clock caustic branch is removed (AC2's own statement).

**Costs.**
- The clock still carries the law's halo as a transported charge (AC6).

- With the record's switch on D.P, perturbations about FRW are not smooth (AC7 M-b). The record repaired that with AC2q.
- It has no cold component, so the CMB, growth and clusters are untouched. It fixes no constant: xi, and so kappa,
  stay by hand.

   the lead's expectation that item 2 passes is wrong for any galaxy that forms. It holds only in a potential that has
   always been static.

   switch (AC7 M-b). The repair was AC2q. Reading B, the 11-07 Pi^6 blend at b = rho_DE, is smooth there. But its
   quadratic stiffness is zero at Pi = 0, so FRW perturbations sit on the Lstar = 0 branch, where the phase speed goes

5. **"Item 4 is the unknown."** The record ran the moving Sun on AC2q and v3s through MS3: PASS (scoped), FINAL per
