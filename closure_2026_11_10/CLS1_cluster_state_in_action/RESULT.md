## What was tested, in one paragraph

Action (A) is QX17's, with the adopted Pi^6 blend. Its static weak-field cluster states are S1: tau = Q_inf t, and the
dust charge is n = mu_K^2 (Q - 1) / K. That is exactly linear in x = Q - 1, with no free offset. The clock fluid has
e = n (1 + x/2), p = n x/2 and enthalpy per charge Q, so hydrostatic balance is N Q = Q_inf (Tolman). The law's phantom
sits inside the action-exact inverse F(M, r) = M^2 / (M + a0 r^2 / G). The dust's active mass n (1 + 2x) is inverted
exactly.

Two necessary conditions follow from monotonicity alone. Both hold for any interior profile, any Q_inf and any cap:
- **UP** (DT3's): ln[(1 + x(n_i,max)) / (1 + x(n_j,min))] >= D_min / c^2. As mu_K grows the dust gets colder, the span
  falls as 1/mu_K^2, and UP fails.
- **LOW** (new): ln[(1 + x(n_i,min)) / (1 + x(n_k,max))] <= D_max / c^2. As mu_K falls the dust gets stiffer. The
  measured density contrast then forces a larger potential span than the measured field allows, and LOW fails.

## What this lane does not settle

- **Moving configurations.** A non-stationary, non-spherical or shell-crossed clock is outside the static test, as in
  DT3. Whether a falling clock forms anything is COL1's question (BREAKS DOWN there).
- **Exactness of the construction miss.** The windows are proofs (Lean-checked algebra plus floating-point margins).
  The miss inside the window is a dense scan of a two-parameter family, not a proof.
- **Data.** Hydrostatic masses with their EM_FORW errors and the gas and star profiles are taken as given, as in DT2 and
  DT3. Inside 0.1 R500 the baryons are extrapolated with the first two points' log slope (construction only). HeCS
  masses use H0 = 70 and are spectroscopic; they enter only the reported HSE+HeCS family.
- **The share.** The dust / M_b printed for the best profiles (3.5-8.6 at 0.7 R500) is reported, not graded. XCV1 grades
  form B's ceiling.
