## 1. The theory in six lines

1. **Fields.** The metric, one clock field tau (a preferred slicing with unit normal n), and a polarization vector P on
   the clock's slices. Matter couples to the metric only. The action is QX17's (A).
2. **One conserved charge.** The clock equation is a conserved current. Its charge N is the clock's dust plus the
   polarization term D.P. The universe carries 5.41 units of it per unit of baryons (fitted to the acoustic peaks).
3. **Falling clock = cold dust.** Where the clock free-falls with the expansion, P = 0 and N is pressureless. This is the
   cold component of cosmology.
4. **Standing clock = the law.** Where the clock stands still around baryons, its own equations give
   div[ mu(|grad Phi| / a0) grad Phi ] = 4 pi G rho_b with mu(x) = x / (1 + x), and the halo is the charge itself:
   an isothermal halo with sigma^2 = (1/2) sqrt(G M_b a0), gravitating and lensing like mass.
   a0 = (1/2) c sqrt(G rho_DE), which is Lambda = 32 pi a0^2 / c^4.
5. **Counted once.** A halo is made of the charge its region brought: for a galaxy the dust inside the Lagrangian sphere of
   its turnaround shell, 59.5 M_b at every mass (PD1); for a hot system 5.41 times the baryons that fell in (RB1). Around
   a galaxy the charge follows the law until it is used up. Above v_vir = 265.8 / 288.1 km/s part of it sits in a core
   instead (PD1 A1; its one half and its square are form choices, not derived). A body that brought no charge has no
   halo, and real mass feels no external field effect.
6. **The joint.** How a falling clock becomes a standing one is NOT derived. Section 4 lists what the record already
   forces on it.

Constants beyond G and c: kappa (fitted), the 5.41 (fitted), the screening constants of v3s, and the form choices of the
hot branch. Nothing else enters a prediction below.

## 3. What fails or is missing, stated once

- **The joint is not in the action.** A clock that starts as dust falls onto baryons, nothing removes its infall energy,
  and it folds at the centre in 0.06 to 0.15 Gyr; the equations give no answer after the fold (COL1: BREAKS DOWN). The
  folded region today (62 to 197 kpc) covers every rotation curve and stops 1.1 to 5.4 times short of the KiDS windows
  and of the cap (FLD1: DOES NOT).
- **No dust term that depends on the clock's offset alone** serves the CMB, clusters and galaxies: the offset is about
  1e-6 both in the background at z 3400 and in a galaxy well today (MK1, MK2: NO VALUE).
- **No cluster state has been solved in the action.** Rule 5's hot branch is a rule, not a solution (FB1 note, item 3).
- **Closed exits:** a wall between a law region and a dust region (HE1 and the switch class); the law on the clock's
  acceleration relative to the baryons (REL1: NOT WELL-DEFINED, the baryons' equation becomes fourth order).

## 4. What the record forces on the joint

Any completion has to do all of these at once. Each line is a landed verdict, not a wish.

1. It acts where the flow stops expanding and folds, not at a fixed place (HE1) and not at a fixed potential depth (MK1).
2. It makes the clock stand still behind the fold and leaves the dust inert there (COL1; SCS1 and SCS2 say the standing
   state then holds).
3. It disposes of the infall energy, or carries it as heat: galaxies end at the law's dispersion, and clusters hold more
   than the law gives (the law alone is 0.47 / 0.43 dex short on X-COP, QX2).
4. It conserves the charge through the change (rule 5). In COL1's law-dominated limit the dust that has arrived is 1 to
   4 % of the law's halo, so a halo of advected dust is short by 25 to 100 there (a diagnostic, no label).
5. It reaches at least the cap radius by today, not 0.52 of the turnaround radius (FLD1).
6. It keeps matter's equations second order (REL1).

**Updated the same evening.** A dust stiffness keyed on the clock's expansion is dead: TK1, BREAKS AT TURNAROUND. A
collapsing region passes through zero expansion while its clock is still falling, and there short waves grow without
bound and the region never turns around. That adds a seventh condition:

7. It cannot key on the expansion alone (TK1).

Of the clock's first-order invariants that left the acceleration, which is zero for a falling clock anywhere and equals
the local field for a standing one. It was tested the same evening and is dead too: AKD1, BREAKS AT A1. A stiffness that
falls with the clock's acceleration is a negative copy of the law's own term. Around the expanding background it gives
the clock negative inertia on every sub-horizon scale at z 3400 and 1100, and a shape that is flat at zero acceleration
has the same sign problem in the outskirts of static wells. That adds an eighth condition:

8. It cannot be a dust stiffness that falls with the clock's acceleration (AKD1).

So no dust term built from the clock's own first-order kinematics, its offset, its expansion or its acceleration, joins
the two states. Still alive and untested as a result: one kinetic function with a narrow interval of changed
compressibility (DT2: an engineered counterexample to MK1's breadth, with nonlinear reachability open).
