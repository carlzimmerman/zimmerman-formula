## What this adds to R541D

R541D's failure (sphalerons answer a B bias) is real and specific to B: moving the bias to B - L removes it exactly,
as the lead expected. But the escape needs three things R541D did not: Dirac neutrinos (a Majorana mass turns the
bias into a vacuum that makes neutrino pairs at 2|eps|, 34 orders over the line), a new B - L sector that acts above
2.5e9 GeV (below that, the bias's own washout pulls B - L negative by more than 10 %), and a dust term that keeps Q
near 1 at 1e9 GeV, which action (A) does not. Dirac leptogenesis does not help: it keeps total B - L = 0 (including
nu_R), so the clock's charge, and R, would be 0 (argued, not computed).

## Not checked, and weaknesses

- Equilibrium coefficients are symmetric-phase, flavour-universal, all Yukawas in equilibrium; nu_R treated as either
  fully out of or fully in the bath. O(1) effects; GE3's T_f line moves by such factors, GE5 does not care.
- The pair-production rates are first-order, order-of-magnitude, computed in the rephased frame; the Majorana rate is
  for one mass eigenstate at m = 0.05 eV; Pauli blocking is handled only as a cap. Antinucleon annihilation (a gamma-ray
  line far stronger than the 1 % energy line) was not used; it would only tighten the requirements.
- The nucleon-decay chiral formula (D+F, f_pi) and the n-nbar treatment are order-of-magnitude.
- The GE3(c) sector is a placeholder; no model was built, and its T = 0 remnant may come at higher dimension or with
  small couplings. Whether such a sector exists with Dirac neutrinos and a remnant below the 1 % lines was not
  decided; that is why the pressureless label is only reported.
- GE5 uses the record's mu_K lines (MK1, KD1); it does not re-derive them. It treats the neutral start as exact.
- No perturbation solve for isocurvature; inflationary fluctuations of tau carrying charge were not checked.
- The 79-entry native list is R541D's; a 1.2 % window over 79 candidates is not a strong test either way.
- Look-ups (T_sph, Y_p, Davidson-Ibarra, z_f, KamLAND-Zen, Super-K, Delta m^2_atm, Heeck-Rodejohann) are PROVISIONAL.
