- Five match within 1.4 to 4.1 arcmin. **A85 does not: the HeCS-omnibus centre (RA 10.4400, Dec -9.4596) is 9.47 arcmin from X-COP's
  X-ray centre**, about 0.6 Mpc at z 0.055, about a third of R200. The name match is unique and the redshifts agree (0.0553 / 0.0555).
- So this is not a parser error. It is the same cluster, but the caustic mass is measured around a different centre. A85 has a known
  southern subcluster and filament, and the HeCS centre lies toward them. A caustic profile about an offset centre is not the mass
  inside a sphere about the X-ray centre, where the gas and stars are measured. The size and sign of that error are not computed here.
