## The mass used, and what it assumes

- HeCS-omnibus (Sohn et al. 2020, ApJ 891, 129; arXiv:1910.11192), table 2. **M200 and R200 are caustic masses** (section 3.2 of the
  paper; table note: "R200 and M200 based on the caustic technique"). They are not virial masses and not a sigma scaling. The frame is
  H0 70, Omega_m 0.3, the same as X-COP's.
- The caustic mass assumes no hydrostatic equilibrium and no gas physics. It does assume a **filling factor F_beta calibrated on
  N-body simulations of LCDM haloes**. Sohn+2020 do not print it; F_beta = 0.7 (Serra+2011) is assumed. This assumption and Serra's
  numbers come from a WebFetch read and are PROVISIONAL.
- **How far the calibration would have to move:**
  - the class median reaches 1 at **F_beta = 0.906**, i.e. caustic masses 29 % higher;
  - Serra's quoted accuracy (x0.9 to x1.1) leaves the label AT THE CEILING (median 1.565 / 1.217);
  - the superseded F_beta = 0.5 gives ABOVE THE CEILING (2.133 [1.088, 4.548]).
- Whether simulated LCDM haloes' anisotropy applies to form B's cold component is not known.

## What was not graded, and limits

1. **Six of 12 clusters.** The graded class has five, the minimum the frozen rule allows. The bootstrap median of five is coarse:
   here its interval runs from the lowest cluster (ZW1215, 0.735) to the highest (A2142, 2.619).
2. **Item 11 is not graded.** There is no raw shear profile beyond R500.
3. **The caustic mass is model-calibrated.** F_beta comes from LCDM N-body haloes. The per-cluster scatter (40-80 %) is from the
   same simulations. The lower end was used, which makes a per-cluster ABOVE easiest to reach; none reached it.
4. **Centring.** HeCS-omnibus centres A85 0.6 Mpc from the X-ray centre. A2255 (372 kpc) and ZW1215 (253 kpc) are also offset. The
   effect of an offset centre on M(<R200) about the X-ray centre is not computed.
5. **Same-radius only for the caustic mass.** The caustic mass is measured at the HeCS R200 only. Rows at RB1's R500 and last radius
   are transfers (Q-rescaled hydrostatic shape, or NFW-DM14) and are reported, not graded.
6. **One-sided, as RB1.** f_ret below 1 is read as baryons lost, and is not tested here.
7. **Not separating.** At this aperture the ceiling equals the cosmic baryon fraction (see the top).
