1. **At the forest's redshifts the redistribution goes the other way, and it is much larger.**
   - At z = 0, CO1's layout raises the power slightly: R_cold 1.006 / 1.010 at k = 1 h/Mpc.
   - At z = 3 it removes power: R_cold at k = 1 / 3 / 5 / 10 h/Mpc is 0.992 / 0.845 / 0.665 / 0.564 (canonical) and
     0.992 / 0.831 / 0.669 / 0.613 (alt).
2. **The reason is the law's fixed physical scale.**
   - a0 does not change with z under form B's rules. So the law's halo has a fixed physical size for given baryons: its
     dispersion is sigma^2 = (1/2) sqrt(G M_b a0).
   - A halo at z 3 is about four times smaller in physical size than a z = 0 halo of the same comoving extent.
   - So the law's phantom reaches its whole share only near the turnaround radius, about four times further out relative
     to the halo than at z = 0. Galaxies' cold dark energy is then laid out flatter than the cold reference's NFW.
   - The one-halo power of galaxy-mass halos falls, and those halos carry the forest's scales at z 3.
3. **The forest sees it at every redshift bin.**
   - The projected 1D power falls by 8 to 51 % (canonical) and 7 to 48 % (alt).
   - Each of the 12 bins fails on its own (Delta chi^2 110 to 1379), and the pull peaks at z 3.0 to 3.4.
4. **The fail is not an extrapolation artefact.**
   - Setting R = 1 above 10 h/Mpc still gives 2784 / 2824.
   - The gentlest reading, rule 5 applied only to the charge inside the virial radius (reading V), still gives 495 / 292.

## Verifying the fail

- The z-code is CO1's layout with only the changes the criteria name. At z = 0 it agrees with CO1 to 1e-15. At z = 0 its
  forest gate reproduces HLF1's held reading exactly.
- The size of the effect follows from a hand check of rule 4's scale. For M_ta = 1e11 Msun/h at z 3, the condensed
  baryons are about 1.5e9 Msun/h. Then r_M is about 0.05 Mpc physical and about 0.2 Mpc/h comoving. The isothermal phantom
  reaches the whole cold share near the turnaround radius, not at 0.25 to 0.5 of it as at z = 0.
- Under no bracket and under neither reading of rule 5 does the row pass. With every power above 10 h/Mpc reset to the
  reference, it still fails by a factor of about 700.
- What could still save the row lies outside this lane's frozen rules:
  - a0 evolving with z (MZ3 shows the sign turns over);
  - a layout at z ~ 3 different from rule 5's (the joint is not derived: the theory's open item 1);
  - an argument that the forest's gas does not trace the total matter's projected power inside halo outskirts. The
    record's pipeline assumes it does, and so does every reading here.
