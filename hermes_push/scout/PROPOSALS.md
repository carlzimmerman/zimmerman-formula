# SCOUT output log

Append-only. Two kinds of entry and nothing else.

**CERTIFIED** — a Lean theorem that compiled with no `sorry`, with its axiom list. These are results.
**PROPOSED** — a structured suggestion awaiting triage. These are questions, never claims.

A human triages proposals by changing STATUS to `taken`, `rejected: <reason>`, or `duplicate of <entry>`. Nothing else in this file
is ever edited.
