# QX5 iter c: key = matter velocity relative to the khronon frame

Common protocol: ../FROZEN_CRITERIA.md. Kappa = 1/2 FITTED; not theory closed.

The khronon frame u is the cosmic frame. A key built from u has three readings:
1. the clock rate u.d tau -> Q - 1 = iter a (not regraded);
2. internal (thermal/dispersion) velocity of matter relative to u -> coarse-grained pressure = iter b (not regraded);
3. bulk velocity of the system relative to u, k_c = |v_bulk|^2/c^2, term F(k_c) on the phantom.

Reading 3 is closed analytically: it needs clusters to move faster through the cosmic frame than galaxies that obey
the law. Inputs (literature, flagged provisional, not graded data): the Local Group moves at 627 +- 22 km/s relative
to the CMB, and the nearby SPARC galaxies share that bulk flow; X-COP-like clusters' peculiar velocities from kSZ are
consistent with a few hundred km/s and are not larger. If cluster bulk speeds lie within the galaxy range, no
monotone F separates them: DEAD (same sandwich lemma as Lean QX5_monotone_sandwich). If they do not, the reading is
reported OPEN (no disk data to grade).
