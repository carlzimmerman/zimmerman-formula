# QX5 iter b: key = coarse-grained pressure of the source matter, w = p/(rho c^2)

Common protocol: ../FROZEN_CRITERIA.md. Kappa = 1/2 FITTED; not theory closed.

Covariant term: the law's auxiliary (QUMOND-type) potential couples to matter through rho F(w), w = p/(rho c^2)
built from T^{mu nu} (w = (T^mu_mu + rho c^2)/(3 rho c^2) up to sign convention); the metric couples minimally.
Static effect at leading order: phantom x F(w), w mass-weighted over the source components at r.

Key k_b per component: disc gas (10 km/s)^2/c^2; stellar disc (0.2 V_obs)^2/c^2; bulges, elliptical stars and
cluster/group member stars V_c^2/(2 c^2) (isothermal); X-COP hot gas V_c^2/(2 c^2) from M_HSE; group gas
kT/(mu m_p c^2), mu = 0.6, measured kT (Lovisari), and its V_c^2/2 estimate reported as a check of the
isothermal proxy. Lensing not used (CGM state unknown).
Direction: F non-decreasing in k_b (hot matter, more boost).
Analytic rechecks: phantom-only, so the solar core (w ~ 2e-6) and neutron stars (w ~ 0.1) do not change their
Newtonian/PPN mass. A source-level (non-phantom) version is excluded in advance by the triple-pulsar SEP bound and is
not graded.
