# QX4: data the owner must approve (nothing was downloaded)

Iteration 2 found that the external field effect rescues AGC 114905 if its external field is g_e = 0.076-0.29 a0
(both footings). With the tidal condition for a regular disc, that needs a host of baryonic mass at least
3.4e9 Msun (canonical) or 6.4e9 Msun (alt) at d >= 30-37 kpc. The required mass grows as d^2: a Milky-Way-mass
host (about 6e10 Msun) would have to lie within about 130 kpc. A large-scale field of that size would also do.
That makes the label CONDITIONAL. To decide it:

1. A neighbour census around AGC 114905 (position and distance from Mancera Pina+2022/2024): every galaxy with
   cz within +-500 km/s and projected separation below 1 Mpc. Sources: NED, HyperLEDA, ALFALFA a.100, and the
   2MPZ cone used in NV2 (ssa.roe.ac.uk; 2MPZ alone is too shallow at 78.7 Mpc for an LMC-mass host).
2. A large-scale g_e estimate for the same position, by the method the record already uses for SPARC (sum over a
   catalogue within about 5 Mpc).

The galaxy's EFE label is frozen as CONDITIONAL until those data are graded under a new frozen criteria file. Malin 1
needs nothing: EFE does not rescue it for any g_e.
