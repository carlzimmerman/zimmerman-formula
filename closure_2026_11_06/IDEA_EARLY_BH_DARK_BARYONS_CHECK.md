# Idea check (not a frozen lane): early-collapse black holes as the hot systems' missing mass

2026-11-06. An analytic reading of numbers already on the record. It was written after EB1 and grades nothing new.

## The idea

The a0 law fits cold discs, but hot systems sit high: ellipticals +0.21, groups +0.26, X-COP +0.47 and Coma UDGs
+1.1 dex (QX18, QX20). No present-day variable keys that step (QX20-QX25). A formation-history cause would explain
why. The proposal: hot, early-assembled systems keep a population of 1e2-1e7 Msun black holes. Those holes formed
from ordinary baryons when, under the R1 QUMOND rule, 1e6 Msun clumps collapse at z 11.3-12.4 (EB1 fbb755861e).
Late-built discs have few. DB1 (40e2665f48) found that holes in this mass range are the only class that survives
its physical gates, but it had no way to form them. EB1 supplies one, conditionally.

## Why it does not survive the record

1. **The amount per star is not universal.** A population born at early collapse should scale with the old
   stellar mass.
   - **Ellipticals:** QX18's D = +0.210 is log10(M_obs/M_law) at 2-8 Re. That needs extra baryons of 0.6x (Newtonian
     regime) to 1.6x (deep regime, M_law ~ sqrt(M_b)) the stars.
   - **Clusters:** X-COP needs 4.4-5.4x the hot gas (DB1). With gas/star 5.8-16.7 (DB1's Bullet subclusters), that
     is about 25-90x the stars.
   - **Result:** the per-star yield differs by a factor of at least 15 between ellipticals and clusters. A single
     formation channel does not do that.
2. **The amount per gas fails the Bullet.** DB1's P3 arm gives the dark baryons exactly the amount and shape the
   requirement asks for (keys 14/15, X-COP and groups pass), and the Bullet still fails. The main subcluster comes
   out 4.84e14 against 3.5e14, and the bullet can't pull its lens peak off its gas. No gas-proportional amount fits
   both subclusters, so an origin story cannot rescue this arm.
3. **Budget.** In clusters alone, the dark baryons are 19-54 % of the BBN baryons (DB1 gate e, PROVISIONAL census),
   which passes only within FRB errors of about +-40 %. Groups and ellipticals add more.
4. **Origin is conditional twice.** EB1's collapse needs R1, which fails the CMB-lensing shape (WG1-WG6). It also
   dies under any frozen external field of 0.03 a0 or more. EB1b, now running, settles WG2's own field, which sits
   between those values.
5. **It does not touch the CMB.** The holes form after recombination, so the primary peaks and the CMB-lensing
   shape (QX3, QX15) are unchanged.

**Reading.** Dead on items 1 and 2 by numbers already on the record, whatever EB1b says. EB1b decides only whether
the R1 rule can form early seeds at all (the A2744-QSO1 timing gate). Do not re-propose this idea without a channel
whose yield per star rises about 15-fold from ellipticals to clusters and that also splits the Bullet's two
subclusters correctly.
