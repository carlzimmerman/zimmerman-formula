# QX25: data the owner must approve (nothing was downloaded)

T2 cannot test the KiDS early/late split at fixed stellar mass. Brouwer+2021 released colour bins and Sersic bins, but none
crossed with stellar-mass bins.

Deciding whether the lensing excess follows type at fixed M* needs the KiDS-bright isolated lens catalogue, with u-r colour,
Sersic n and stellar mass per lens, and the lensing ESD per lens or the KiDS-1000 shear catalogue to re-stack.

Source: the KiDS DR4 bright sample (Bilicki+2021) and the KiDS-1000 shear catalogue (Giblin+2021), from the KiDS
public data portal.

Until then, T2 is reported at fixed g_bar only, as B21 binned it.
