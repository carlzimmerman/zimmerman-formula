- At L* = 5.75 pc, these diffuse, low-acceleration clusters (r_h 16–28 pc) would be mostly unscreened, and the law with
  the Milky Way's external field (boost 1.6–6.4) would raise their dispersions ×1.3–2.5. They are Newtonian, so L* must
  be large enough to screen them.
- **New window: L* ∈ [24 pc, ≳ 1 kpc]** (canonical, primary). Before it was [5.75 pc, ≳ 1 kpc] (CFG691).

**Consequences (reported, no verdict).**
- **Gaia DR4:** CFG740's sealed wide-binary envelope γ_v − 1 = ½(s/L*)² was computed at L* = 5.75 pc. With L* ≥ 24 pc the
  envelope is ≥ 17× smaller (∝ L*^−2). The sealed file is unchanged; this is a sharper reading of it.
- **Ultra-faint dwarfs:** at L* = 24 pc, systems of 10³–10⁴ M☉ with r_h 20–40 pc have S = 0.06–0.30, so they are largely
  screened and nearly Newtonian. Their observed dispersions, already +0.33 dex above the unscreened law, then need **real
  bound mass**, i.e. a cold component, not a gravity boost. That sharpens the record's two-population picture (S4).

at all, so the test bites. The statistic is a log-χ² with a 0.15 dex Υ width (CFG332's), not CFG332's combined-p T, which
was not recomputed (disclosed). Υ_V = 1.6 (SPS, CFG332 route 1).
