# K006 — addition at the Sun does not die on the gate that was set

The deep-branch formula is the wrong test here. For \(M_b = 6.5\times10^{10}\,M_\odot\), \(r_M = 9.84\) kpc canonical / \(8.96\) kpc alt, so at 8.2 kpc \(M_S = 0\). The pre-registered addition kill (ratio \(> 1.5\)) does not fire: deep-formula addition is \(0.94\).

On \(\mu_2\), at 233 km/s:

| reading | canonical | alt |
|---|---|---|
| kernel alone / \(M_{\mathrm{dyn}}\) | 0.85 | 0.89 |
| kernel + \(10^{12}\,M_\odot\) NFW, \(c=10\) | 1.16 | 1.20 |

The kernel alone is inside the factor-1.5 band. Adding that halo is 16–20% high and does **not** clear 1.5. The gate was too coarse to kill addition at the Sun. That is the result. The threshold is not moved after the fact.
