# Primary sources consulted

- [UKRmol-in3.3.0 release](https://zenodo.org/records/18340340): downloaded source,
  included GBTOlib, distributed tests/templates. Source hash in SOFTWARE.json.
- [UKRmol-out3.3.0.1 release](https://zenodo.org/records/18538198): downloaded outer
  propagation/eigenphase source; archive extracts under3.3.0 directory name.
- [UKRmol+ suite paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.03018): continuum methodology;
  does not establish convergence of this new calculation.
- [Hvizdos et al., PRA97,022704](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/5qw494pp): exact
  two-coordinate DR model and frame-transformation benchmark. Its model potential
  is not the full two-electron Coulomb parent used here.
- [BO approximation in a two-dimensional R-matrix treatment](https://journals.aps.org/pra/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevA.98.062706): methodological reference;
  not an imported physical rate table.
- [Competing ionization and dissociation in a gerade model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.10820): two-dimensional model, not a substitute for this nuclear calculation.
- [HD+ dissociative recombination and ion-pair calculations](https://journals.aps.org/pra/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevA.109.052806): physical need for potentials,
  resonance couplings and nuclear channels. Ordinary molecular masses differ.

No published reaction coefficient was substituted for a computed mass-ratio100
coefficient. Literature supports the route and its limitations, not the requested
astrophysical separation. Local source/test inspection established executable
input conventions and the Rydberg K-file energy convention.
