# FT-I2: formation before dark self-binding

Evidence-driven continuation of FT-I1, declared before this run. The first
28 pilot trajectories have no persistent T; the homology envelope puts the
T endpoint and high-coherence barrier above the initial total energy for
every numeric profile. The envelope is restricted, not a universal theorem.

Change the first uncertain implication: assemble the phase before the free
component has acquired strong negative binding energy. Keep baryon initial
radius and every equation/parameter prescription. Increase only the initial
dark-to-baryon Plummer scale ratio from 3 to 10 or 30. Recompute velocities
and angular momenta from the actual all-F initial force, as in FT-I1. This
tests an initial formation ordering, not a new force or imposed state seed.
No initial RAR profile is used. A cosmological origin for that ordering is
not established, and larger free-streaming scales are not declared viable.

Pilot profile: 1 (Mb=1e8 solar masses, b=1 kpc), f=.95, alpha=1,3,10,
iota=.01,.1, scale=10,30; 12 trajectories. Six shells/species, nq=256,
duration=12 baryon dynamical times, same 18 second cap. Any high-q candidate
must be checked at f=.3, other mass profiles, longer time and finer grids.
Near candidates may discriminate formation failure from persistence failure.
No success is inferred from a transient crossing of q=.95.
