Does cold energy relax onto the law? (CFG748)

Collisionless shells starting at rest. m(<r)/r = 1 is the law. The mass-starved start stays short at 50 r_M. The cold-infall start failed its energy-conservation control (3.2e-3 against 1e-3), so treat its curve with that caveat.

Different starting states (CFG747, t = 2000–3000)

Every start lands within 2% of the law out to 5 r_M, but not beyond. This lane failed its frozen outer-radius criterion (R1 FAIL) and its force-check bar (1e-6) sat below floating-point noise. The result is inner halo only.

Orbiting cold energy is unbound (CFG749)

Total initial energy, in the same units. Binding is the baryons' alone, −1.50. Any orbital speed of 30% of the law's circular speed pushes the total positive, so the mechanism needs cold energy nearly at rest.

Settling time of the cold medium (CFG743, CFG699)

Solid: oscillation period at the cold medium's own halo density (CFG743). Dashed: period if the medium sits at the cosmic mean density (CFG699), 36–113× too slow. Bar: 1.38 Gyr, ten periods in a Hubble time. A short period is not settling, since CFG744 shows settling also needs damping.

Damping needs a light independent field (CFG744)

One-crossing time r/c_P, the exponent-free lower bound. If the polarization is the cold medium (waves at 20–220 m/s) it fails by orders of magnitude. An independent field at c_P = c relaxes within a Hubble time. That leaves the cold energy as a separate component, with baryon-state dependence as the open selector.

The FRB baryon census against black-hole dark energy (CFG779)

114 localized FRBs see the full Big Bang baryon density (B = 1.064 [1.010, 1.122]). Converting baryons is excluded. Converting only the cold component is not (0.9σ with the floor). The first three bars switch with the control above. The partition bars always carry the 5% floor, as scored in the lane. Partition is a post-hoc reading, reported without verdict weight.

How robust is that exclusion? (CFG786)

The Croker-like case (30% of baryons eaten) under each systematic variant. Only the declared stellar-channel escape route at a condensed budget of 0.24 brings it below 3σ (2.48). That escape is the remaining loophole, reported in the paper.

Structure growth if cold mass converts (CFG780)

χ² against raw KiDS shear (lower is better). The dashed line is the control, 144.26, which is the unconverted baseline both runs are compared with. Converting the cold mass costs about 60% of the T1x gain but T1x stays better than the control. T12 goes into tension (+8.30). The branch is tolerated, not favoured.

A surplus of tethered cold energy does not stay (CFG776)

After baryons leave, the surplus is pushed out within 50–100 dynamical times and overshoots into a deficit. m/r = 1 is the law. The relic hypothesis therefore predicts early types 0.08 dex below the law against an observed +0.28 ± 0.083 (Z 4.3, wrong sign). A gradual loss at τ = 50 with p = 1.5 leaves a partial relic of +0.31 at r = 8 (CFG787).

The relic in groups and clusters (CFG777)

Predicted amplitude against 1 (the data), Tacconi p = 1.873. It overshoots in eRASS1 (mid radii) and ACCEPT (outer radii). eFEDS also fails (χ² 22–23 on 8 dof, p ≈ 0.004). Even p = 1 overshoots ACCEPT. Fails 0 of 3 tests.

Other tests in the block

Top: the photo-evaporation unbinding selector flags 100% of Milky Way ultra-faint dwarfs, as designed, but also about 50% of M31 satellites against a 10% ceiling, so it is dead and its follow-up (CFG784) was not run. Bottom: black-hole masses in 51 LIGO–Virgo events show a change with redshift of −0.012 ± 0.050 dex. Both k = 0 (no change) and k = 3 (−0.104) survive, so the test is not diagnostic. The sigmas are computed here from the quoted numbers.

Can the coupling give propulsion? (CFG788)

Time to gain 1 km/s at a constant acceleration, against the age of the universe (13.8 Gyr). At the experimental ceilings it takes 0.9–32 Gyr. Sunlight on a sail is about 1e5 times stronger, so there is no practical propulsion, and the stiffness closes the P-exhaust channel.

Can cold energy be burned as fuel? (CFG789)

Ambient cold energy in 1 km³ near the Sun can give at most 4e-19 W of light, capped by the cosmic optical background (τ ≥ 4.4e15 yr). A 1 N light drive at 300 MW would need conversion stimulated by a factor of 7e26. No mechanism for that is in the record. Inputs are from memory and provisional.
