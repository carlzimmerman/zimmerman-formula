RK03: nonlocal self-repulsion, not density-gradient pressure

The following is a proposed extension; none of its new parameters is derived
from the original v3s action or from kappa. In first-order clock variables,
normalizations suppressed and c=1 in this paragraph,

 Lnew = rho Q - U_quad(rho) - s2 rho[ln(rho/rho_star)-1]
        - F_mu_nu F^mu_nu/4 -(m0^2+eta rho) B_mu B^mu/2
        + e rho u^mu B_mu.

u is the original khronon unit normal, rho its conjugate density, and B is a
classical vector field. There is no new particle mass inventory, but there
ARE new field degrees of freedom. Full relativistic constraint/health and
cosmological perturbation analyses remain open. The logarithmic bulk term
is an explicit constitutive addition to K, not something inferred from it.
Matter is not assigned vector charge; it continues to couple to the metric.

STATIC ACTION AND FORCE

In the static weak-field sector B_i=0, b=B_0. In physical units the reduced
interaction Lagrangian is
 Lb = |grad b|^2/2 +(m0^2+eta rho)b^2/2 - e rho b.
Varying b yields D_rho b=e rho, D_rho=-Delta+m0^2+eta rho.
Eliminating the constrained b yields the POSITIVE interaction energy
 E_V[rho]=1/2 integral e rho D_rho^(-1)(e rho) d^3x.
Its variational derivative is NOT e b alone:
 h_V=delta E_V/delta rho=e b-eta b^2/2.
The mass derivative is forced by the action and was checked symbolically.

Let rho_t=m0^2/eta, v2=e^2/eta, u=eta b/e, p=1-u. Then
 [-Delta+m0^2(1+rho/rho_t)]u=m0^2 rho/rho_t,
 h_V=v2(u-u^2/2)=v2(1-p^2)/2.
For decreasing spherical density, p generally increases outward and the
outward repulsive support is g_V=v2*p*p'. It is computed by solving the
spatial boundary-value problem, not by substituting a local density switch.

The bulk logarithm supplies h_bulk=s2 ln(rho/rho_star), hence
 g_support=-s2 (ln rho)' - v2(1-u)u'.
On an extended cored profile rho=rho_c/[1+(r/rc)^2],
 g_bulk=2s2 r/(r^2+rc^2), tending to 2s2/r. Thus this construction has
extended isothermal support rather than the r^-3/r^-5 terms rejected in
RK01/RK02. It does not thereby establish an exact global equilibrium.

NONLOCAL RESPONSE KERNEL

Around a homogeneous rho_bar, b_bar=e rho_bar/(m0^2+eta rho_bar).
Linearizing BOTH the vector equation and its chemical potential gives
  (q^2+m_eff^2)delta b=(e-eta b_bar)delta rho,
  delta h_V=(e-eta b_bar)delta b,
where
  m_eff^2=m0^2(1+rho_bar/rho_t),
  e_eff=e/(1+rho_bar/rho_t).
Therefore the STATIC kernel is
  delta h/delta rho = s2/rho_bar + e_eff^2/(q^2+m_eff^2).
Its density dependence is jointly forced by the mass coupling. Keeping the
mass while omitting the screened effective charge is an incorrect kernel.

The self-gravitating quasistatic longitudinal response can be written
  omega^2 = s2 q^2 - 4piG rho_bar K(q,rho_bar),
  K(q,rho_bar)=1-alpha_eff q^2/(q^2+m_eff^2),
  alpha_eff=e_eff^2/(4piG).
This uses Newtonian self-gravity for the CMB prescreen and the native
nonlinear acceleration law for the cluster/galaxy static tests. It is NOT
the complete metric-coupled, time-dependent FRW transfer function. Eliminating
the static vector field does not certify its retarded longitudinal dynamics.

POSITIVITY AND HIGH-DENSITY LIMIT

For arbitrary density variations,
  delta^2 E_V = integral [(e-eta b)delta rho]
                    D_rho^(-1)[(e-eta b)delta rho] d^3x >=0.
The logarithmic bulk contribution is also strictly convex for s2>0.
Consequently, in a specified external potential and fixed reservoir, the
static density problem is convex. This is reduced static positivity, not a
full covariant no-ghost proof with gravity and clock constraints included.

In the homogeneous limit,
 E_V/V=e^2 rho^2/[2(m0^2+eta rho)]
      =v2 rho^2/[2(rho+rho_t)].
It approaches v2*rho/2 at high density, rather than a fixed-mass vector's
stiff rho^2 energy. Its homogeneous second density derivative is
 e^2 m0^4/(m0^2+eta rho)^3>0. The new pressure term can therefore be very
small at recombination even if repulsion matters today.

GALAXY NECESSARY EQUILIBRIUM TEST

For cosmic mean rho_inf, p_inf=rho_t/(rho_inf+rho_t). Chemical equilibrium
in a specified native potential depth Psi is
 s2 ln(rho/rho_inf) + v2(p_inf^2-p^2)/2 = Psi.
Together with the vector equation this is a nonlinear boundary-value problem.
Since 0<=p<=1, the maximum positive repulsive barrier is v2*p_inf^2/2,
giving a conservative density lower bound. Passing that bound is inconclusive.
The actual nonlinear equation, not just this bound, was solved in refinement.

Use z=r p. The nonlinear field equation is
 -z''+m0^2[1+rho(z)/rho_t]z=m0^2 r,
 rho(z)=rho_inf exp{[Psi-v2(p_inf^2-p^2)/2]/s2}.
Its Newton diagonal contains the positive correction
 m0^2 rho/rho_t * v2 p^2/s2. Stopping on a finite-difference residual alone
is unsafe on a stretched grid. The corrected solver checks the Newton change
to ln rho and re-solves the independent linear vector equation at the final
density. Only that defect-checked refinement is credited.

SCOPE OF REJECTION

The observed potential is held fixed in the galaxy test, with an intentionally
conservative truncation at 300 kpc and unresolved central depth omitted.
The new vector energy is a small mass correction at fitted v/c, so it cannot
remove a leading positive clock mass of order 0.7--0.8 baryon masses by itself.
The resulting excess violates the requested one-percent ceiling in the
tested equilibrium branch. This is not a proof of an inevitable late-time
state from all primordial initial conditions. Nonequilibrium formation,
the boundary reservoir, and direct isolated-lens selection require further
analysis before any universal physical exclusion of the action family.
