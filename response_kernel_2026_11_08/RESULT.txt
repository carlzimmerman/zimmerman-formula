Main physical result
--------------------
The Legendre density of the original clock admits a positive gradient energy
without adding a particle. For constant zeta, its homogeneous longitudinal
response in the frozen, weak-field approximation is

  chi_deltaPhi = -q^2 / [rho_bar A q^2 + rho_bar zeta q^4 - (omega+i0)^2].

A=4*pi*G/mu_K^2, q is physical. With self-gravity, subtract
4*pi*G*rho_bar from the denominator. This is derived from the first-order
clock-density action, not imposed as a multiplier of the galaxy law.

The static chemical potential becomes A*rho-zeta*Delta rho, defeating the
premise of a purely local-density constitutive reduction. Nevertheless its
pressure/gravity ratio is Jcap=zeta*q^4/(4*pi*G)=(ell*q)^4. Density cancels.

Second extension: forced coefficient from an auxiliary interaction
----------------------------------------------------------------
Adding one algebraic spatial covector B, with u.B=0,
  L_B=(m_B^2+g*n)*B^2/2-lambda*B.Dn
gives, after exact elimination,
  L_eff=-lambda^2*(Dn)^2/[2*(m_B^2+g*n)].
Thus zeta(rho)=zeta0/(1+rho/rho_t), not an inserted phenomenological switch.
The remaining parameters are independent extension couplings; the original
action does NOT predict them. The reduced gradient energy is jointly convex.
Its high-density Fisher/quantum-pressure limit is known, not a discovery or
an automatic escape from the excluded superfluid/wave routes. What is tested
here is the explicit regularized coefficient and its full variational force.

The full force includes zeta' and zeta''; replacing constant zeta by zeta(rho)
in the old force would give an incorrect result. With p=rho', L=Delta rho:
  g_cap=zeta*L' + zeta'*p*(L+rho'') + (zeta''/2)*p^3.
The linear q^4 coefficient becomes rho_bar*zeta(rho_bar), so early density
can suppress it. The constant-coefficient CMB obstruction does not alone
close this changed action.
