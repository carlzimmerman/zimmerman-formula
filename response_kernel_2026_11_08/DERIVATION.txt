RK01: derivation before numerical testing

1. Minimal action change

Suppress the pre-existing overall normalization of the clock Lagrangian and
use c=1 here. For K(Q)=mu_K^2 (Q-1)^2 set
  n = dK/dQ = 2 mu_K^2 (Q-1),
  U(n) = n + n^2/(4 mu_K^2).
Then K(Q)=ext_n[n Q-U(n)]. Thus replacing K by
  L_K = n Q - U(n) - zeta_n/2 h^{mu nu} nabla_mu n nabla_nu n
adds one spatial-gradient coefficient. h^{mu nu}=g^{mu nu}+u^mu u^nu,
u_mu=-nabla_mu tau/Q. The same original khronon defines the foliation.
The gradient vanishes on a homogeneous background and the zeta_n=0 action
is the original K(Q). Neither abundance nor kappa is selected by this term.

Varying n at fixed tau gives
  Q-U'(n)+zeta_n nabla_mu(h^{mu nu} nabla_nu n)=0.
In a static weak field the last term reduces to the spatial Laplacian.
The covariant divergence generally also contains lapse factors; do not
replace it by a flat Laplacian in a full spacetime proof.

On the nonrelativistic branch, after the fixed conversion from n to mass
density rho and subtracting the rest-energy term, the first-order action is
  L_NR = rho [dot pi - Phi - |grad pi|^2/2]
         - A rho^2/2 - zeta |grad rho|^2/2.
Here velocity is v=-grad pi. A=4*pi*G/mu_K^2 in the record's normalization.
Its positive internal/gradient Hamiltonian is
  H_int = integral [A rho^2/2 + zeta |grad rho|^2/2] d^3x.
The density is the canonical momentum of pi, not an independent particle.
This NR reduction does not establish the full gravitational constraint count.

2. Forced response kernel

Chemical potential: h = A rho - zeta Delta rho.
Continuity and Euler linearized about a homogeneous rho_bar give
  dot delta = -div v,
  dot v = -grad[Phi + A rho_bar delta - zeta rho_bar Delta delta].
At frozen background, proper q and retarded frequency omega,
  chi_deltaPhi(q,omega)
    = -q^2/[rho_bar A q^2 + rho_bar zeta q^4-(omega+i0)^2].
The density susceptibility is rho_bar times this expression.
The sign is such that a negative gravitational potential attracts density.
With Newtonian self-gravity and an external baryon contrast delta_b,
  [-(omega+i0)^2 + rho_bar A q^2 + rho_bar zeta q^4
    -4*pi*G*rho_bar] delta = 4*pi*G*rho_b delta_b.
The FRW leading nonrelativistic subhorizon equation adds 2H dot delta,
and q=k_com/a; a frequency-local kernel is a frozen-background diagnostic,
not an exact Fourier representation of an expanding universe.

Positive zeta stabilizes high q. This is a density-gradient pressure kernel,
not the prior higher-gradient acceleration correction to J(Y). In particular
the static chemical potential is not a function of density alone, so the
old convex-K local-density reduction does not dispose of this construction.

Define ell^4=zeta/(4*pi*G). Omitting ordinary pressure conservatively,
  Jcap = omega_cap^2/omega_grav^2 = ell^4 q^4.
rho_bar cancels EXACTLY. High early mean density does not save this kernel.
For a constant coefficient the length is physical at every epoch.
At z=1100, q=(1+z)k_com, not k_com.

3. Static cluster target and aperture convention

At equilibrium: A rho-zeta Delta rho+Phi=C, hence
  g = -A rho' + zeta (Delta rho)'.
For rho=rho0/(1+x^2), x=r/rc,
  M_X(<r)=4*pi*rho0*rc^3 [x-arctan x],
  -rho' = 2*rho0*r/rc^2 /(1+x^2)^2,
  (Delta rho)' = 8*rho0*x*(5-x^2)/[rc^3*(1+x^2)^4].
The capillary term supports against inward gravity only for r<sqrt(5) rc;
it changes sign outside. A core length is not an outer mass aperture.
At large r ordinary pressure support decays as r^-3, the capillary term
as -r^-5, while an extended approximately flat-curve field falls as r^-1.
Therefore this constant-coefficient term cannot supply the missing outer
isothermal support asymptotically. Finite observed profiles are tested,
not assumed to be excluded by that asymptotic statement.

Use native mass profiles: at the last common radius r_last, g_obs=G M_hse/r^2.
Invert g_N=g_obs^2/(g_obs+a0); then
  M_X,last = r_last^2*g_N/G - M_b,last.
Normalize rho0 using M_X(<r_last)=M_X,last. This is an observationally fixed
budget for a necessary profile test, not a formation or capture calculation.

4. Limitations and continuation criterion

The new term does not derive the initial 5.4 baryon weight. Neither an
equilibrium reconstructed from a chosen target nor a dispersion test is a
full cosmological solution. The no-go claim, if obtained, is limited to the
constant-coefficient density-gradient construction and stated coldness test.
For zeta(rho), the linear capillary ratio becomes zeta(rho_bar) q^4/(4*pi*G).
Quantify the suppression required at recombination before considering any
state-dependent completion. An arbitrary chosen power is not a derivation.
