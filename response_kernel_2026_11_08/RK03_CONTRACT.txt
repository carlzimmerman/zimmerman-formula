Objective: move beyond gradient energy and test a nonlocal interaction that
can supply an extended halo's force. This is a proposed extension, not an
adopted action or a discovered universal mechanism. No new particle carrier.

Changed action in first-order clock variables, c=1:
 L = rho Q - U_quad(rho)
     -s2*rho[ln(rho/rho_star)-1]
     -F_mu_nu F^mu_nu/4 -(m0^2+eta*rho) B_mu B^mu/2
     +e*rho*u^mu B_mu.
u_mu=-partial_mu tau/Q is the pre-existing khronon normal. B is a new
classical vector field; it has field degrees of freedom, no particle mass
inventory is introduced. rho is the existing conjugate density. The
covariant full constraint/health analysis is unperformed; test the static
weak-field and quasistatic longitudinal reduction explicitly, not full GR.
New independent couplings: s2>=0, m0>0, eta>0, e^2>=0. Nothing selects them
from a0 or derives kappa. The local logarithmic bulk energy is also an
explicit constitutive addition, NOT a deduction from the quadratic K(Q).
The vector interaction is the new forced kernel beyond a local convex K.

Prior route distinction: X57 M2 repelled the clock FROM BARYONS, sourcing
the mediator with baryonic density and constant mass. Here baryons have no
vector charge, the source is the clock density itself, the mass is affine
in that density, and its derivative alters the effective charge. Do not
reuse X57 M2's transfer formula. No novelty claim over vector-fluid theory.

Static reduction, b=B0, u=eta*b/e, rho_t=m0^2/eta, v2=e^2/eta:
 (-Delta + m0^2*(1+rho/rho_t)) u = m0^2*rho/rho_t,
 h_V=v2*(u-u^2/2),
 g_support= -s2*(ln rho)' - v2*(1-u)*u'.
Include the mass derivative term -eta*b^2/2 in h_V. The isothermal term has
outer force 2*s2/r on rho~r^-2, unlike the rejected gradient terms.
Original quadratic pressure is neglected in the cluster force to favor the
candidate (it is tiny at the recorded BBN-compatible mu_K=8.89e6/Mpc).

On a homogeneous density, u_bar=rho/(rho+rho_t),
 e_eff=e*(1-u_bar), m_eff^2=m0^2*(1+rho/rho_t).
The STATIC density/chemical susceptibility is
 dh/d rho(q)=s2/rho + e_eff^2/(q^2+m_eff^2).
Hence the leading quasistatic longitudinal frequency is
 omega^2=s2*q^2+rho*e_eff^2*q^2/(q^2+m_eff^2)-4*pi*G*rho.
The retarded vector/light-cone completion is not inferred from this static
formula. The homogeneous energy E_V=v2*rho^2/[2(rho+rho_t)] saturates to a
matterlike term rather than the stiff rho^2 of a fixed-mass charged fluid.
