# Known issues: dr1_catalogue_v5 (full tier)

## Orientation and coordinate frames

- **Per-lens figures are north up, east left.** Every per-lens PNG was redrawn from the stored
  best-fit model with north up and east left. The RGB panel of `rgb.png` has an N/E compass, and
  every figure's axis labels (arcsec) increase to the north and west. The figures were redrawn,
  not flipped as pixels, so
  critical curves, caustics, positions and source planes are mirrored with the images. No model
  was refitted. Each redrawn fit was checked against the original: the model image, chi-squared
  and source reconstruction agree to within 1e-4 (VIS lens models within 1e-8).
- **FITS, RA/Dec and sky-frame columns are in the sky frame.** This covers the cut-out WCS, the
  WCS on the model FITS, `wcs_vis_*.json`, `positions*.json`, `wcs.csv`, `coolest*.json` and the
  `*_east_offset`, `*_north_offset` and `*_pa_east_of_north` columns.
- **The fitted model parameters are in the pipeline frame**, with +y pointing south and +x west.
  This covers `centre`, `ell_comps`, `gamma_1`/`gamma_2` and their CSV columns. The sky-frame
  columns above give the same quantities on the sky. To convert a fitted angle φ (degrees,
  counter-clockwise from +x), the position angle east of north is (90° − φ) mod 180°.

### Figures still in the pipeline frame (north down)

For 193 lenses, one or more figures could not be redrawn north up and keep the original
pipeline-frame (north-down) version. In each case the mirrored fit did not
match the original within tolerance, or its pixelized source could not be solved again, so the
figure was not redrawn rather than redrawn wrongly:

- `vis_pix_fit.png`: 127 lenses
- `fit_sersic.png`: 8 lenses
- `fit_multi_wavelength.png`: 66 lenses (at least one band did not match)

All other figures of these lenses are north up. Every other figure in the release is north up.
