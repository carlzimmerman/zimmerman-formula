## Limits

  cells EXPLAINED. Under SE2's rule these two FAILS might not survive. That was not computed here, because it would be a
  change after the freeze.

  instantaneous tidal cut with no heating or re-virialisation. Reaching a LOW row with r_J inside r_1/2 means stars outside
  the tidal radius, which the equilibrium estimator does not describe.
- The host masses are the lanes' conventions, not new measurements: flat V_c 200 / 230 km/s, Coma's hydrostatic beta
  model, NGC 1052's baryons under the law. The pericentres use LVD kinematics, a flat 200 km/s host and astropy's
  default Galactocentric frame. They are reported edges, not graded. M31's distance (776.2 kpc) is the consensus root of
  32 LVD rows.
- The graded law is the brief's algebraic form, with no screening. The screened reading is reported, from D10's iso_1d at
  central values.
- No LCDM-calibrated quantity enters any row.
- Open item 6 of the owner decision (Coma UDGs not graded with their own share) is now graded. The hosted rows add two
  open items under B: the ultra-faints and bright M31 dwarfs sit +0.6 and +0.25 dex above B, and DF2 / DF4 need a host
