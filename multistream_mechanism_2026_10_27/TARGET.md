# MS27: energy transfer and a stable Lambda-linked core

User authorization: continue the multistream idea into a mechanism that transfers
energy and stops at the right core density. Start date 2026-10-27, base revision
`e6d278b747` (repository is concurrently active).

The target is the core surface density `Sigma=rho0*r_quarter`, with
`rho(r_quarter)=rho0/4`, and desired `Sigma=a0/(2*pi*G)` on the canonical and
alternative acceleration footings separately. A universal volume density is
not the proposed law. Baryons, halo envelopes and cosmological evolution are
additional obligations, not assumed successes.

Success requires: an explicitly conserving parent or controlled effective
model; actual energy transfer; a stable endpoint or precisely scoped stopping
bound; a derived connection of its coefficient with Lambda; and an activation
regime that preserves permitted cold cosmological evolution. A model with the
target coefficient inserted is conditional progress, not completion.

halo fit, shared theory edits, or long simulation is authorized by this record.

request. No claim of new literature novelty is intended. Closure of a failed
