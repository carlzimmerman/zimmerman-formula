# Exact source scope

Checked 2026-10-27 with the web tool. No paper was downloaded into this project.

- Pierre-Henri Chavanis, *Predictions from the logotropic model: the universal
  surface density of dark matter halos and the present proportion of dark
  matter and dark energy*, arXiv:2201.05903 (submitted 2022-01-15), equations
  145-156. Primary PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2201.05903 . It supplies the known
  EOS, Lane-Emden scaling and quarter-density core convention. These were
  independently rederived and recomputed here. Its global cosmological model
  and abundance claims were not adopted or validated. Our A_req is a matching
  condition, not that paper's prediction.
- Chavanis, *A new logotropic model based on a complex scalar field with a
  logarithmic potential*, Phys Rev D106,063525 (2022),
  https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.106.063525 and
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/2201.05908 . Appendix F discusses a core plus finite
  envelope, and stability below a boundary-dependent critical contrast. It
  does not derive our multistream activation, reciprocal mode source or
  stopping coefficient. Our moving-fixed-pressure eigenproblem was checked
  independently of its qualitative stability discussion.
- V.M.C. Ferreira and P.P. Avelino, *New limit on logotropic unified dark energy
  models*, Phys Lett B770 (2017),213-216,
  https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physletb.2017.03.075 . Primary open full text was
  read from https://s3.cern.ch/inspire-prod-files-5/5eaa265b0de950f963bf065e2341659f .
  It bounds the original globally active perfect-fluid model using linear
  growth, quoting B<6e-7. This excludes simply applying the halo-sized A
  universally in that model. It is not a direct exclusion or validation of an
  unbuilt phase-dependent kinetic extension.

Literature application verdict: the equilibrium EOS/scaling is known and
applicable in the declared active barotropic phase. Transfer to an actual
multistream cold-field phase, a causal mediator coupling and the required
cosmological history remains conditional. No global novelty claim is made.
