## What this does to the crossing-speed joint

**It neither kills the joint nor gives it real support.** On canonical, the three resolved discs above v*(z) take a best
halo multiple of 0.67, with a 95 % interval [0.25, 1.18]. That contains the record's share, so as frozen the joint's statement
holds, a hit pending a blind re-grade. But that word flips to FAILS if the pressure correction is dropped or the baryon prior
is moved up by 1 sigma. The outer shapes at 1.8 to 2.5 R_1/2 cannot tell the cold-dust layout from the law or from baryons
alone. On alt only two discs are above, so it cannot say.

Below the boundary the joint's other half does worse. The four discs it assigns to the law prefer the reference halo at
A ~ 1.4 over the law: chi2 224 against 281 on canonical. This is also FRAGILE, on the pressure bracket.

So the resolved Genzel / Lelli curves do not decide the joint. A decision needs three things:
- curves reaching beyond about 3 R_1/2, where the layouts' shapes separate;
- measured gas profiles, which fix M_b to better than the 0.2 to 0.3 dex that now trades one-for-one with A;
- a beam-forward model in place of the FWHM cut.

The ALPAKA rings already on disk are the nearest candidate set.
