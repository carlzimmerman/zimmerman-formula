# Primary-source checks for MV28

Checked 2026-10-28; no external full text is retained. No novelty claim.

David Shih, *Spontaneous R-Symmetry Breaking in O'Raifeartaigh Models*,
[arXiv:hep-th/0703196v3, 19 January 2012](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/0703196v3).
Sections 2 and 3, especially (3.1)-(3.10), give the model, transverse bound,
and small-y origin mass. Figure 3 specifies r=4,y=0.2,lambda=1 with a
minimum near x=1.3. The vacuum is metastable and has a runaway; the phase
breaks R symmetry spontaneously. These are benchmark/source assumptions,
not claims of a new model. Our matrices, their determinants, pressure
mapping and radial stiffness dictionary are reconstructed from W. The
source does not identify this sector with cosmological vacuum or derive
the relative-motion portal.

Ryuichiro Kitano, *Gravitational Gauge Mediation*,
[arXiv:hep-ph/0607090v3, 18 August 2006](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-ph/0607090v3).
Equations (1),(2),(4) and the surrounding discussion were checked for the
supergravity potential, Kahler corrections and superpotential constant.
The constant affects gravity and vacuum cancellation, while its absence
permits the declared R symmetry. We apply the general potential to the
different W and K specified here; we do not import Kitano's messenger
model, vacuum solution or phenomenological scales. Exact R symmetry of
quantum gravity and missing UV Wilson coefficients remain unproved.

S. P. Martin, *Two-loop effective potential for a general renormalizable
theory and softly broken supersymmetry*,
[hep-ph/0111209v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-ph/0111209v2), equations
(1.1),(3.2),(3.3), checked in the preceding VR28 source audit. They fix
the scalar/Weyl counts and the 3/2 subtraction convention used here.
One-loop formulas are used; no two-loop result is claimed.
