# Primary-source transfers for the RG continuation

Nathan Seiberg, *Exact Results on the Space of Vacua of Four Dimensional
SUSY Gauge Theories*,
[hep-th/9402044v1, 8 February 1994](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/9402044v1).
Section 3, equations (3.1)-(3.4), was read for exact massive meson responses
and branch conventions. Integrating those responses gives the displayed
mass-dependent superpotential. Promoting masses to lambda_X X is a
conditional adiabatic effective-theory use. Canonical Kahler metric, SUSY
splitting control and gravitational energy are not proved by holomorphy.

S. P. Martin and M. T. Vaughn, *Two-Loop Renormalization Group Equations
for Soft Supersymmetry-Breaking Couplings*,
[hep-ph/9311340v4, 24 July 2008](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-ph/9311340v4).
Equations (2.7)-(2.11), especially the one-loop gamma in (2.10), were
checked for sign and loop normalization. Only the one-loop formula is
used. The singlet sign obstruction follows from its Yukawa Gram matrix.
Identifying a threshold-dependent Wilsonian metric with a particular
gamma requires the stated dictionary; it is not automatic in a multi-field
breaking sector.

The sign convention for the metric response is calibrated against Kitano's
explicit one-loop chiral threshold, equation (3), in the exact version
already recorded in SOURCES.md. It is not applied to an unspecified charged
field while retaining an illegal gauge-noninvariant linear superpotential.

These are known microscopic ingredients and a local algebraic combination.
No worldwide novelty claim or complete microscopic normalization is made.
