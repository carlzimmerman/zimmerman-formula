Outcome
With ordinary matter minimally coupled to the Jordan metric, the proposed
tracking scalar produces an attractive Yukawa correction in the local,
linear, weak-source limit. Its enhancement is less than one third of the
tensor force. More decisively, the source-induced force falls at least as
fast as inverse square throughout this approximation. It does not produce
the desired inverse-radius low-acceleration force in that regime. Thus the
identification of the vacuum field with the operational a0 is not established.
This is not a nonlinear or all-matter-couplings impossibility theorem.

1. Declared extension and approximation
Add S_m[g_J, matter] to the parent's action, with canonical scalar kinetic
term, F=M^2+xi phi^2 and V=E+lambda phi^4/4. Use its positive constant-field
de Sitter vacuum, M,xi,lambda,E>0. Write z=phi_v^2.
Linearize about that vacuum, use an unscreened weak point source with small
scalar excursion, and work on scales r much smaller than H^-1 so that local
static flat-space propagation is a valid leading approximation. The source
acceleration below excludes the homogeneous de Sitter acceleration.
The results describe leading linear source response, not exact force bounds
including post-Newtonian corrections, finite-source effects or cosmological
curvature corrections.

2. Matter coupling after diagonalizing scalar-gravity mixing
The Einstein metric is g_E=(F/M^2)g_J. Thus matter sees A(phi)^2 g_E with
 A=M/sqrt(F),
 (dchi/dphi)^2=K=M^2(F+6xi^2 phi^2)/F^2.
The dimensionless canonical coupling is alpha=M d(log A)/dchi, hence
 alpha^2=xi^2 z/[F+6xi^2 z].
The scalar-to-tensor potential coefficient is
 B=2alpha^2=2xi^2 z/[F+6xi^2 z].
It satisfies
 0<B<1/3,
 1/3-B=F/[3(F+6xi^2 z)]>0.
This includes the scalar-metric mixing; treating phi as already canonical
would give the wrong coupling.

3. Local scalar equation and source force
At leading order in the Einstein frame the perturbation obeys
 (nabla^2-m_E^2) delta chi = alpha rho_E/M,
with decaying point-source solution
 delta chi = -alpha M_b,E exp(-m_E r_E)/(4pi M r_E).
The matter potential contribution is alpha delta chi/M. Adding the tensor
potential gives -G_tensor M_b/r times [1+B exp(-m_s r)].
In Jordan local physical units,
 G_tensor=1/(8pi F_v), m_s=sqrt(F_v)/M times m_E,
 r_E=sqrt(F_v)/M times r_J.
Thus m_E r_E=m_s r_J, and the same dimensionless force expression holds:
 g_source(r)=G_tensor M_b/r^2 [1+B(1+m_s r)exp(-m_s r)].
G_tensor is the tensor coefficient, not a claim that a locally measured
Cavendish constant excludes the scalar contribution.

For x=m_s r>=0 define y=(1+x)exp(-x). Since y(0)=1 and
y'=-x exp(-x)<=0, one has 0<y<=1. Consequently
 1<=g_source/g_tensor<4/3.
The exact logarithmic slope of the leading source expression is
 d log g_source/d log r
 = -2 - B x^2 exp(-x)/[1+B(1+x)exp(-x)] <= -2.
By contrast, the target low-acceleration law g=sqrt(G M_b a0)/r has slope
-1 and source-mass scaling sqrt(M_b). Our linear expression is proportional
to M_b and has the wrong radial slope, independently of the numerical
choice of lambda/xi. Calibration of Newton's constant cannot change either
of those exponents. The Yukawa range alone therefore cannot define the
required universal MOND-like acceleration threshold.
These slope statements apply only where the stated local linear expansion
is valid; no extrapolation to distances near the de Sitter horizon is made.

4. A further exact relation in the tracking vacuum
From the parent calculation,
 m_E^2=2lambda M^4 z/[F(F+6xi^2 z)],
 H_E^2=M^2 lambda z/(12xi F).
Therefore
 m_E^2/H_E^2=24xi M^2/[F+6xi^2 z],
 m_E^2/H_E^2 + 12(1+6xi)B = 24xi.
Scalar range and force strength cannot be adjusted independently at fixed
xi within this particular vacuum family. This does not impose an empirical
constraint here, and xi itself remains free. If the scalar range is of
horizon size, the static Yukawa crossover is outside the local approximation;
its short-distance limit still gives an inverse-square source force.

5. Research decision
The quartic curvature tracker can hold Lambda/phi_v^2 constant but, with
this simplest matter coupling, fails to supply the desired force law in
its controlled weak-source regime. Setting lambda=128pi xi cannot repair
that mismatch. A nonlinear regime, extra response field, or different matter
coupling would be a materially new mechanism requiring its own matching
and stability calculation. No such repair is established in this artifact.
Do not report the tracking field as an experimentally defined a0.
