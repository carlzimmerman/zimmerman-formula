LOCAL TWO-FIELD KINETIC RECEIVER — DERIVATION
12 October 2026. Distinct constructive continuation of DERIVATION.txt.

1. Assumptions and change of receiver

In one spatial dimension, take four populations f_i(x,t) of mass per length
with fixed velocities v_i, and TWO independent real massless scalar fields:
Pi_A=partial_t phi_A, g_A=partial_x phi_A, A=1,2. These are two physical fields,
not the sine/cosine amplitudes of the previous single global Fourier mode.
Assume free canonical energy/momentum densities
 e_R=(|Pi|^2+c^2|g|^2)/2, p_R=-Pi.g, c>0.
Gravity, baryon recoil, microscopic rates and a coupled parent action are absent.
This is a classical constitutive prototype in a declared frame. Fixed velocity
bins do not establish Lorentz/Galilean covariance or a complete kinetic kernel.

2. Restriction for the simplest one-local-scalar source

Let J be a cold reaction rate with energy cost D and momentum change p_b. For
one scalar source s added to Pi_t=c^2*g_x+s, cancellation requires
 Pi*s=-D*J, g*s=p_b*J.
Eliminating s gives (D*g+p_b*Pi)*J=0. Generic nonzero reactions therefore fail
this compatibility condition. It is necessary, not sufficient at Pi=g=0.
It applies to this source ansatz. Interaction energy/flux, additional reactions,
or different field content can change the question.

3. Regular two-field reaction

Let b=(-1,-1,1,1), R=f1*f2-f3*f4,
 D=sum_i b_i*v_i^2/2, p_b=sum_i b_i*v_i,
 h=Pi2*g1-Pi1*g2,
 r=(D*g2+p_b*Pi2, -D*g1-p_b*Pi1).
Direct contractions give Pi.r=-D*h and g.r=p_b*h. For gamma>=0 define
J=gamma*h^2*R and S=gamma*h*r*R:
 f_i,t+v_i*f_i,x=b_i*J,
 Pi_t=c^2*g_x+S, g_t=Pi_x.
Rates are polynomial, with no division by h at degenerate states. The cold
sources D*J and p_b*J are canceled by the field sources pointwise. sum_i b_i=0
cancels mass production. Same-position mass-neutral collisions leave density
unchanged; gravitational dynamics are not included in this isolated prototype.

The receiver content, collision shape and rate are constructed inputs, not
derived microscopic physics. No acceleration target, profile or a0 is inserted.
In this one-dimensional normalization gamma has units L*T/M^3; gamma=.1 is a
dimensionless diagnostic, not the earlier integrated-mode coefficient.

4. Local stress and actual kinetic current

Define rho=sum_i f_i, j=sum_i v_i*f_i,
e=sum_i (v_i^2/2)*f_i+e_R and p=sum_i v_i*f_i+p_R. The equations imply
 rho_t+partial_x j=0,
 e_t+partial_x [sum_i (v_i^3/2)*f_i-c^2*Pi.g]=0,
 p_t+partial_x [sum_i v_i^2*f_i+e_R]=0.
Density moves through the moment of the same velocity populations. The field
has explicit energy flux and momentum stress. Periodic boundaries conserve
integrated mass, energy and momentum. These are local flat-space identities,
not a gravity coupling, covariant action or observed lensing metric.

5. Positivity, entropy and inactive states

Along each cold characteristic the reaction is gain minus loss with coefficient
gamma*h^2>=0. At f1=0 the reaction is gamma*h^2*f3*f4>=0, similarly for f2;
at f3=0 it is gamma*h^2*f1*f2>=0, similarly for f4. Smooth solutions therefore
remain nonnegative while coefficients remain finite. Conservative upwind
semidiscretization also gives nonnegative inflow at an empty bin. DOP853 has no
general positivity guarantee; reported numerical positivity is at stored times.

For positive populations and a common reference density, entropy density
-sum_i f_i*(log(f_i/f_ref)-1) has reaction production
 gamma*h^2*(a-b_star)*log(a/b_star)>=0,
 a=f1*f2, b_star=f3*f4.
Streaming gives its entropy flux. At empty bins the logarithmic gradient may
diverge, although the polynomial state equations remain finite.

Zero receiver data remain zero. Fixed proportionality Pi2=lambda*Pi1 and
g2=lambda*g1 stays proportional under free waves, has h=0 and is inactive.
Variable local proportionality need not persist. If Pi is initially zero,
 h_t=c^2*(g1*g2_x-g2*g1_x).
This is not the old oscillator onset formula. Appropriate spatial/phase data
are needed for activation; the physical initial receiver state is undetermined.

6. Principal propagation and frozen growth estimate

In U=(f1,...,f4,Pi1,Pi2,g1,g2), write U_t+A*U_x=Q(U). A is constant: diag(v_i)
for matter and [[0,-c^2*I],[-I,0]] for the fields. H=diag(I4,I2,c^2*I2)>0
symmetrizes A, giving characteristic speeds v_i and +/-c. Requiring all matter
speeds within c is an additional assumption; the diagnostic velocities satisfy it.

Freeze a finite state and write B=Q'(U). In H variables the Fourier generator
is B_tilde-ik*A_tilde, with A_tilde symmetric. Its Hermitian part sym(B_tilde)
is independent of k. Every eigenvalue satisfies
 Re(lambda)<=lambda_max(sym(B_tilde)).
The frozen source cannot produce growth unbounded with k. This permits finite
positive growth. It is not stability about physical backgrounds, global
continuum existence, gravitational health or a three-dimensional completion.

7. Computation and its limits

LOCAL_CRITERIA.txt fixes grids, states, controls, tolerances and resource limits
before execution. local_checks.py checks source and field-flux identities and
rejects source-sign/missing-recoil mutations. Cold fluxes follow by fixed velocity
moments. Central skew wave derivatives and conservative upwind matter advection
preserve integrated semidiscrete invariants; time integration adds reported errors.

The initial empty-product fixture gives a strictly positive source for each
product bin on an open spatial set. The numeric assertion tests aggregate final
product mass. Both initial gain terms follow from the exact equations. Positivity
was sampled at 201 times; complete trajectories were not saved. Two-grid RMS
difference is a diagnostic, without an accuracy/convergence claim. Density changes
also when gamma=0 due to actual streaming; energy exchange and collision rates
distinguish reaction activity.

Fresh review found the original single-scalar omission fixture insensitive to
deleting p_b*Pi. local_control.py closes that exact frozen criterion separately:
D=0, g=p_b=Pi=J=1 makes the truncated condition zero but the full residual one.
The original source and completed run are preserved; no trajectories are rerun.

8. Remaining physical derivation

An admissible model must justify the two fields, initial states, interaction and
coefficient, or replace them with derived degrees of freedom. It must derive
three-dimensional recoil, live baryon reaction where needed, and consistent
metric/action/stress coupling. Pointwise e_R>=c*|p_R| follows by Cauchy-Schwarz
and AM-GM. A local control volume exchanges flux; its initial pointwise energy
is not a closed capacity budget. No halo selecting principle follows from these
identities alone. CFG679 admission and the full TOE remain open.
