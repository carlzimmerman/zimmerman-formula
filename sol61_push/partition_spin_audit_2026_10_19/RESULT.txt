2. What the analytic partition actually selects
With the source's zero-at-infinity potentials,
Phi_t-Phi_f = integral_r^infinity [Mt(s)/s^2+Mt(s)^2/s^4]ds >0
for nonzero positive tethered mass. Derivative is negative outward. This
follows by subtracting the two functional derivatives, not by the sampled grid.
At positive beta, common chemical potential, equal carrier mass and equal
label degeneracy,
 rho_t/rho_f=exp[-beta(Phi_t-Phi_f)],
 f_free=1/[1+exp(-beta(Phi_t-Phi_f))] >1/2.
It rises inward. These statements select a label preference in given potentials;
they do NOT determine enough inner total mass. Density normalization, temperature,
energy, boundary and self-consistent potentials remain decisive.
Unequal conserved populations, degeneracies or internal chemical offsets change
the ratio. None can be added afterward as an inferred physical receiver without
an action/kinetic justification. The underlying separate-population functional
explicitly says automatic conversion is not licensed by its mass conservation.

3. Why this is not a low-spin mechanism, and what rotation changes
CFG848 imposes spherical isotropy, no ordered rotation, and no total-J constraint.
A zero-mean-spin trial family is an INITIAL RESTRICTION, not a prediction that
assembly lost angular momentum. Low spin of the actual cold component is also
not measured by the stars' pressure-supported classification.

For a formal rotating maximum-entropy extremum with energy, mass and total
angular momentum fixed, variation gives
 f_i(x,v) proportional to exp{-beta[v^2/2+Phi_i-Omega Lz-mu]}.
Complete the square in azimuthal velocity:
 vphi^2/2 - Omega Rperp vphi = (vphi-Omega Rperp)^2/2
                                      -Omega^2 Rperp^2/2.
After integrating velocities,
 rho_i proportional to exp{-beta[Phi_i-Omega^2 Rperp^2/2-mu]}.
The SAME centrifugal term cancels from rho_t/rho_f. Mean streaming is
<vphi>=Omega Rperp, with Omega set by the SUPPLIED total J. This is an identity
for given potentials/common temperature and chemical balance, not a newly
solved nonspherical equilibrium of the radial functional. Adding Omega alone
neither predicts different progenitor spins nor inserts a selective core sink.

The whole isolated cloud cannot lose total angular momentum through internal
label changes. For the core,
 dJ_core/dt = external torque - net angular-momentum flux through its boundary
             + any explicitly modelled receiver torque.
Internal conversion source terms cancel in J_t+J_f if the complete action is
rotationally invariant and its receiver is included. In a fixed spherical
collisionless potential each particle's angular momentum is itself conserved.
Collisions can transfer J outward; they do not make it disappear. A core could
therefore lose J while the outer cloud retains it, but that needs a transport
kernel and an outer mass/energy/torque budget. Equilibrium free fraction alone
is not that calculation.
