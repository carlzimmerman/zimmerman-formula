2. The exact missing number

If a covariant completion interprets W_full(0) as the TOTAL vacuum energy,
with no additional bare or other-sector vacuum contribution, then

 epsilon_Lambda = W_full(0) = C a^2/(4 pi G),
 rho_Lambda = epsilon_Lambda/c^2,
 Lambda = 8 pi G epsilon_Lambda/c^4 = 2 C a^2/c^4,
 kappa^2 = a^2/(G epsilon_Lambda) = 4 pi/C,
 A Lambda = 2 pi C.

Therefore
 A Lambda = 32 pi^2 <=> C=16 pi <=> kappa=1/2.

This is an explicit target for the microscopic vacuum normalization, not a
selection of C. For example C=8 pi instead gives kappa=1/sqrt(2) and
A Lambda=16 pi^2 with exactly the same g_N(g), potential derivatives and
saturation acceleration a. Both C choices give positive vacuum energy.
They differ in cosmology; they are not claimed to fit the same measured
Lambda and a. This counterfamily refutes the claim that saturation,
criticality and positivity alone force the coefficient.

Extra vacuum contributions strengthen, rather than remove, the missing
matching problem. One must use the total gravitating vacuum energy.

The integral is independent of L; chi(S4)=2, not 1. It is not A Lambda.
