# Explicit production methods

This appendix applies to run_production.py. It leaves the original experiment and frozen run_resolution.py pilots unchanged. The new choices alter numerical sampling/precision, not the physical mass, gravitational law or elastic cross section.

The admitted Metal direct backend computes every ordered off-diagonal pair in full3D. Raw binary64 positions are encoded in three FP32 residual components so close separations remain accurate even when a compact dwarf and distant tidal tails share a large coordinate origin. Displacement arithmetic, forces and particle potentials use compensated high/low pairs with safe compiler math. The separately tested one-Newton FMA-residual variant is explicitly compiled and identified by backend metadata and library hash. It is not nativeGPUfloat64 or rawFP32. No spherical or tree approximation enters.

Admission requires nativeCPUfloat64 agreement within1e-11for force/potential in the declared tests, including a two-pc cluster far from a first particle in a500-kpc tail. Independent momentum/torque, unequal-mass, self-exclusion, translation and concurrency tests also apply. The production wrapper repeats CPU comparisons on evolved states at declared gravity-call checkpoints, and aborts if the gate fails. These empirical checks are not an a priori uniform precision certificate. Time-integration, finite-particle and support convergence remain separate requirements.

The optional CTMC sampler integrates the SAME frozen-position finite-h central elastic jump generator as the historical kernel's first-order Bernoulli approximation. It chooses unordered graph edges uniformly through degree-weighted oriented-edge tickets, uses a global relative-speed thinning envelope, draws exponential positive waiting times, and updates the bound after accepted velocity jumps. Boundary and coincident proposals have zero acceptance. Equal-mass central reflections conserve pairenergy, linear momentum and vectorangularmomentum. The sigma0/m and documented below-resonance velocity dependence are unchanged.

CTMC allows multiple jump opportunities within the collision substep. Its recorded max_prob is an upper per-pair HAZARDtimesdt, not a Bernoulli probability; the historical .05probability rejection does not apply to this method. Proposal/event caps fail closed rather than truncating time. The original Bernoulli method and its guards remain available through --collision-methodbernoulli as independent discretization controls. Both methods keep positive collision time outside the signed Hamiltonian composition and require timestep/support controls. Exact frozen-position sampling does not make the finite-h or kinetic-splitting approximation exact.

The new quiet initial family samples the same continuous position/velocity distributions as the original prepared scenario, with nested raw coordinates and explicit per-level moment normalization. Same-level isolated/host and elastic/collisionless initial states match exactly before bulk orbit translation. An alternative initial scramble/IID sampling check is needed when sampling spread matters. A converged result must compare resolved radial observables and remnant mass, not merely conservation defects or operation counts.
