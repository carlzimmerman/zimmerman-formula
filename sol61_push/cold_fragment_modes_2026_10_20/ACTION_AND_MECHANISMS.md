20 October 2026. Exploratory **alternative common matter action**. The full
cold-energy theory remains open. These calculations establish a conditional
classical mechanism in this specified model; they do not adopt it as the
repository's canonical theory or derive the observed dwarf galaxies.

The new result is a natural radiation channel. A localized quadrupole mode

external drive. Actual merger excitation and galactic orbital transport
