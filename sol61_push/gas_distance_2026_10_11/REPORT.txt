DERIVATION AND ITS LIMITS
At fixed redshift, observed X-ray surface brightness is proportional to
integral n_e^2 Lambda(T,Z) dl, with fixed spectral emissivity/composition.
Retain the same angular 3D model and clumping. A distance change lambda
scales all physical lengths by lambda. To preserve brightness, density
must change by lambda^-1/2. Hence gas column scales as lambda^1/2, and mass
inside the SAME ANGULAR aperture scales as lambda^(5/2).

For an unreduced thin lens, alpha_infinity is proportional to projected
mass/angular-impact-physical-radius, or equivalently Dl times the angular
convolution of the surface density. Thus alpha_infinity and kappa_infinity
scale as lambda^(3/2). For each actual source, also multiply by its new
Dls/Ds. It is incorrect to keep the physical gas mass fixed, or change only
the source efficiency while leaving an X-ray-calibrated map unchanged.
These statements hold at corresponding angular positions; they do NOT
rescale a fixed 250-kpc aperture mass without re-evaluating its boundary.

This derivation does not establish the real line-of-sight gas structure.
An extra depth elongation eta at fixed transverse angular shape instead
requires density eta^-1/2 and gives column/deflection sqrt(eta), while
preserving X-ray brightness. The toy eta={.5,2} tests this degeneracy;
it is NOT an observational interval for Bullet and is not fitted here.
This extends the existing baryon-depth warning to a normalization ambiguity.
