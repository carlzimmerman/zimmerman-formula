Conditional X-ray gas distance conversion; frozen before calculation.

Hold redshift, observed angular X-ray morphology, spectral emissivity,
composition, angular 3D geometry and clumping prescription fixed. The gas
is optically thin and surface brightness is proportional to integral n^2 dl.
For distance ratio lambda=Dl_new/Dl_old, derive density lambda^-1/2,
column lambda^1/2, fixed-angular-aperture mass lambda^5/2 and unreduced
lensing alpha and kappa lambda^3/2. Source-reduced alpha additionally
multiplies (Dls/Ds)_new/(Dls/Ds)_old. These are conditional transformations,
not inference that the actual gas has the assumed depth or emissivity.

Use author flat H0=70, Om=.3 and existing PM flat H0=67.36,
Om=(.02237+.1200)/.6736^2 at zl=.296. Use the prior observed image operator
and source redshifts. No DESI fit, halo prescription or a0-dependent
normalization. Save two explicitly named footing copies of the same gas
input; gas lensing is not a function of a0 in this fixed-background step.

Controls: numerical LOS integration of a Gaussian toy under lambda
{.5,1,2} reproduces brightness invariance, column and angular-mass scaling
at 1e-8. Independent distance integrals agree with Astropy at 1e-8 relative.
Identity and round trip map conversion return input at 1e-12 relative.
MUTATE fixing physical density when changing distance fails brightness
invariance at lambda=2 by >.1. MUTATE treating physical gas aperture mass
as fixed gives alpha exponent -1 rather than 3/2 and fails toy oracle.
Verify constant-source gauge and finite arrays. Report actual difference
from a distance-factor-only rescaling, without using it as fit evidence.

A separate analytic elongation eta of LOS depth at fixed angular shape
preserves brightness with n -> eta^-1/2 n but changes gas column and
alpha by sqrt(eta). Demonstrate with eta {0.5,2}; do not assign that bracket
to Bullet or fit eta. No numerical threshold can validate actual 3D gas
geometry from this experiment. No theory pass/fail, cold mass, or action.
One process, one thread, CPU30s, wall60s. Preserve failures.
