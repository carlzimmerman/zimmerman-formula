1. INDEPENDENT EXACT STRESS, CURRENT AND ODES

Write
 S=f^2+4h^2,
 T=Omega^2 S,
 K=N(f'^2+h'^2),
 V=m^2 f^2+M^2 h^2-2ghf^2+lambda f^4.

Canonical stress in a local orthonormal frame gives exactly
 rho=T+K+V,
 p_r=T+K-V,
 p_t=T-K-V,
 p_t-p_r=-2K<=0.

There is no factor 1/2 in these expressions: each canonical COMPLEX scalar
has L=|dPhi|^2, not one real-field kinetic term. The positive-charge current
convention is j^mu=i(Phi* d^mu Phi-Phi d^mu Phi*) plus twice the analogous
X current. Hence
 q_local=2Omega(f^2+4h^2),
 Q=4pi integral_0^infinity r^2 q_local/sqrt(N) dr.
The ADM gravitational mass is mu(infinity), not the integral of a separate
number-density rest mass. Its local constraint is

 mu'=4pi r^2 rho,
 alpha'/alpha=G[mu+4pi r^3 p_r]/(r^2 N).

Let B=2/r+alpha'/alpha+N'/(2N). Variation of the original action gives

 f''+B f'=[m^2-Omega^2-2gh+2lambda f^2]f/N,
 h''+B h'=[(M^2-4Omega^2)h-gf^2]/N.

The relative minus sign X=-h is already incorporated. In particular the
inhomogeneous h source is -g f^2 on the right, not +g f^2. At the center,

 f''(0)=[m^2-Omega_c^2-2g h_c+2lambda f_c^2]f_c/3,
 h''(0)=[(M^2-4Omega_c^2)h_c-g f_c^2]/3,

The factor 1/3 comes from f''+2f'/r ->3 f''(0). Both gradient terms,
metric constraints and asymptotic lapse normalization are required in a
full-field equilibrium. The formulas above were derived BEFORE reading
the equilibrium owner's code or profiles.

2. EXACT PRESSURE SIGN AND THE LOCATION OF THE LOCAL FREQUENCY MAXIMUM

Stress conservation gives the anisotropic equilibrium identity
 p_r'=-(rho+p_r)alpha'/alpha+2(p_t-p_r)/r
      =-2(T+K)alpha'/alpha-4K/r.

The positive-square form

   +lambda_rep f^4
shows V>=0, rho>=0, and rho+p_r=2(T+K)>=0. A nontrivial regular localized
solution has positive enclosed mass at every r>0 in its occupied interior.

For the stipulated decaying massive tails, p_r->0 faster than r^(-3),
mu->M_ADM>0 and alpha'/alpha=G M_ADM/r^2+o(r^(-2))>0 far out. Thus
p_r'<=0 there and p_r(infinity)=0 implies p_r>=0 sufficiently far out.
At ANY finite zero p_r=0 in a nontrivial occupied solution,

 p_r'=-rho G mu/(r^2 N)-4K/r<0.

A negative interior p_r would have to cross from negative to nonnegative
in reaching the positive outer region; its first such zero would have
nonnegative derivative, contradicting this formula. Therefore p_r>=0
throughout (strictly positive at every finite occupied radius). This proof
uses the stated asymptotic/regularity conditions, not an assumed EOS or
monotone rho. It also shows alpha'>0 and Omega'<0 throughout r>0, so the
maximum local chemical frequency is at the center. The energy density
itself is not thereby proved monotone.

5. PURE-X CONVERSION-SECTOR QUADRATIC FUNCTIONAL

The action permits X->Phi Phi, so stability of the single-field mother
sector is not sufficient. Linearize around Phi=0, X=-h exp(-2i omega t).
Since the Phi contribution to stress and to the X equation begins at
quadratic order, the daughter perturbation decouples from metric/X
perturbations at first order. Write

           +r^(-2)Delta_(S^2),

The exact linear equations are
 W xi_tt+2omega W eta_t+K_- xi=0,
 W eta_tt-2omega W xi_t+K_+ eta=0.
For exp(s t), the off-diagonal pencil entries are +2omega s W and
-2omega s W. The gyroscopic sign matters. With inner-product measure
alpha sqrt(gamma) d^3x, K_+/- are self-adjoint for regular decaying data.
The conserved rotating quadratic energy is

 E_2=integral alpha sqrt(gamma) [W(xi_t^2+eta_t^2)
                               +xi K_-xi+eta K_+eta] d^3x.

Strict positivity/coercivity of K_- gives a positive conserved norm and
excludes exponentially growing finite-energy daughter modes, since
K_+>=K_-. It is a sufficient conversion-sector stability condition. A
negative direction of K_- alone is NOT a proof of dynamical instability:
the gyroscopic terms can matter. Positivity must hold on the infinite
domain or with a controlled finite-boundary error. The l>0 sectors add a
positive angular gradient term, so the l=0 lower bound suffices for this
particular positive-energy criterion in all angular daughter sectors.

At g=0 the criterion is that omega lie below the lowest test-Phi bound
frequency nu_0 in the stellar metric. Merely checking omega<m excludes
free-particle pair decay at infinity but does not exclude daughter bound
modes. The lambda|Phi|^4 term vanishes from the linearized sector.
