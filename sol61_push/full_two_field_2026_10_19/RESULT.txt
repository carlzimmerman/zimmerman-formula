FULL TWO-FIELD GRAVITY: EQUILIBRIA, STABILITY AND THE FLUID BOUND
Checkpoint EKG-2026-10-19-C; parent NTS-2026-10-19-B; 19 October 2026.

The full static spherical Einstein--two-complex-scalar equations have been
solved numerically with both gradients, the metric and the conserved charge
retained. Localized nodeless solutions with BOTH fields populated exist in
the declared parameter regimes. A diffuse mixed solution passes the tested
full constrained radial energy criterion. A more compact mixed solution
has a resolved slow growing mode in the finite Galerkin dynamics.

The old homogeneous chemical ceiling is not a universal full-field ceiling.
Its finite-surface TF compactness theorem remains valid with its original
hypotheses. A separately declared R99 extension fails for full-field
equilibria, but the compact mixed witness is not a demonstrated stable
counterexample. A universal compactness theorem restricted to stable MIXED
states remains open. All stability statements below have an explicit finite
radial scope; none is a quantum/nonradial/nonlinear stability theorem.

1. EXACT EQUATIONS USED

The original minimally Einstein-coupled action is unchanged:
 L_matter=|dPhi|^2+|dX|^2-V,
 V=m^2|Phi|^2+M^2|X|^2+g(X*Phi^2+X Phi*^2)+lambda|Phi|^4,
 M>2m, Delta=M^2-4m^2,
 lambda=lambda_rep+g^2/Delta, lambda_rep>0.
Natural units c=hbar=1, signature +---, charge weights1 and2. Parameters
remain inputs of the action. No equation of state, galaxy law, baryonic
portal, new interaction or fitted acceleration scale is inserted.

Set ds^2=alpha^2dt^2-N^(-1)dr^2-r^2dOmega^2,
N=1-2G Menc(r)/r, Phi=f exp(-iomega t), X=-h exp(-2iomega t),
Omega=omega/alpha, T=Omega^2(f^2+4h^2), K=N(f'^2+h'^2).
Then, exactly,
 rho=T+K+V, p_r=T+K-V, p_t=p_r-2K,
 Menc'=4pi r^2rho,
 alpha'/alpha=G(Menc+4pi r^3p_r)/(r^2N),
 f''+B f'=[m^2-Omega^2-2gh+2lambda f^2]f/N,
 h''+B h'=[(M^2-4Omega^2)h-gf^2]/N,
 B=2/r+alpha'/alpha+N'/(2N),
 Q'=8pi omega r^2(f^2+4h^2)/(alpha sqrt(N)).
Regular centers and asymptotically flat, decaying massive tails close the
boundary problem. Detailed derivation: equilibria/DERIVATION.txt and
gradient_analysis/DERIVATION.txt, independently reconstructed before the
crosscheck. This is a numerical solution of the spherical stationary system,
not a closed-form solution or classification of every initial condition.

The full radial perturbation problem uses the constrained ADM Hamiltonian,
not a fluid perturbation equation. With y=sqrt(4piG)(Phi,X),
Pi=a/alpha ydot, a=N^(-1/2), S=sum(|Pi|^2+|y'|^2), U=4piG V,
 mu'+2rSmu=r^2(S+U), mu=G Menc,
 qbar=GQ=-2 integral r^2 Im(y1*Pi1+2y2*Pi2)dr.
The second variation of mu_infinity-omega qbar is restricted to deltaQ=0
and quotiented by the common phase. Both fields' real/imaginary amplitudes
AND canonical momenta are retained; the metric is recomputed under every
variation. HAMILTONIAN_DICTIONARY.txt gives the full normalization.

3. WHY THE OLD BOUND CHANGES SCOPE

The old finite-surface isotropic TF theorem used the algebraic local
minimum and zeta=u_star^2 r_lambda/(1+r_lambda):
 C_TF<[1-(1+zeta)^(-2)]/2=.000266560037913255.
Gradients turn that algebraic problem into differential equations and
produce p_t-p_r=-2K. At a central maximum one obtains inequalities, not
h=gf^2/(M^2-4Omega^2). The old local chemical ceiling therefore cannot
be imported into the full equations.

A regular nontrivial smooth massive scalar equilibrium also has a tail,
not an exact finite zero-pressure edge. R99 here is explicitly the areal
radius enclosing99% of ADM mass; C99 uses TOTAL ADM mass. Our compact
mixed equilibrium has C99 about1.475 times the old number. This refutes
the separately stated universal R99 extension. It is not a same-radius
contradiction of the actual TF theorem. Stability may still restrict the
mixed branch; the compact witness's slow instability makes this distinction
essential.

There is also an exact full-action pure-X sector, Phi=0. Its integrated
KG identity and monotone lapse imply Omega_center>M/2, strictly above the
TF ceiling sqrt(M^2-g^2/lambda)/2. This independently proves that the local
ceiling is not universal, even before inspecting mixed numerical profiles.

A much weaker known full-field bound survives. The positive-square
potential implies V>=0 and the dominant energy condition. Exact stress
conservation with regular decaying tails gives p_r>=0, and
p_r+2p_t<=3rho. Andreasson's Theorem1 therefore yields
 sup_r G Menc(r)/r<=24/49.
For the total-mass/R99 convention it gives C99<=24/(49*.99).
This is a known anisotropic-gravity corollary, not a new zeta-linked or
scalar-sharp theorem. Primary source, checked exact version:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/gr-qc/0702137v2 . Hypotheses and notation translation
are recorded in gradient_analysis/DERIVATION.txt.

5. PREDICTION, LIMITS AND CONTINUATION

The directly reproducible dynamical predictions at the declared couplings
are a slow growing radial mode on the compact mixed branch and a positive
lowest radial oscillation frequency on the diffuse mixed branch. They
follow from the action, solved background and constrained Hamiltonian;
they were not read off a galaxy relation. They can be tested by independent
full-field spectral calculations and appropriately long dynamical runs.
An observational prediction needs physical parameter selection, formation,
occupation/lifetime, abundance and coupling to a measurable channel.

The original protected nonthermal quantum-state objective remains open.
Exact charge conservation protects total charge, not arbitrary coherence.
No global novelty, Nobel-level result, quantum lifetime, nonradial stability,
cosmological population or galaxy law has been established here.

The next sharp obligation is continuum radial spectral/error control and
nonradial stability of the diffuse mixed candidate, followed by continuation
toward the actual weak-gravity benchmark. For stable-mixed compactness,
continue a fixed-coupling family through the slow-mode crossing and locate
the stable endpoint with a declared mass-containment radius. Do not turn
this one unstable compact profile into a universal stable-state bound.

All scripts, failures, bounded runner records and independent checks remain
in their owner directories. reference/RESULT.txt explains its initial failed
continuation; equilibria/METHODS_AND_LIMITS.txt preserves nodeful and failed
mixed routes. A completed manifest authenticates the declared execution,
not a proof of every attempted mathematical assertion. checkpoint.json,
HANDOFF.json and SELF_REVIEW.txt record the final reconciliation and pins.
