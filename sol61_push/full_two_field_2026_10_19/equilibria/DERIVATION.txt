ACTION AND CONVENTIONS
Natural units c=hbar=1. The original carrier is minimally coupled to Einstein gravity, with
 V=m^2|Phi|^2+M^2|X|^2+g(X*Phi^2+X Phi*^2)+lambda|Phi|^4,
 Delta=M^2-4m^2>0,
 lambda=g^2/Delta+lambda_rep, lambda_rep>0.
The gravitational choice is ordinary minimal Einstein coupling. No MOND law, a0, extra U(S), source gate or new scalar interaction is inserted.

Use polar-areal coordinates
 ds^2=alpha(r)^2 dt^2-a(r)^2 dr^2-r^2dOmega^2,
 N=1/a^2=1-2G mathcal M(r)/r,
 Phi=f(r)exp(-i omega t), X=-h(r)exp(-2i omega t).
The frequency is positive and lapse-normalized at infinity. It is not a position-dependent fitted parameter. Define S=f^2+4h^2, K=N(f'^2+h'^2), T=omega^2S/alpha^2. The potential on the ansatz is V=m^2f^2+M^2h^2-2g h f^2+lambda f^4. Its exact energy density and pressures are
 rho=T+K+V,
 p_r=T+K-V,
 p_t=T-K-V.
Thus p_t-p_r=-2K. Scalar spatial gradients create anisotropic stress and were not discarded.

EXACT RADIAL FIELD EQUATIONS
 mathcal M'=4pi r^2 rho,
 alpha'/alpha=G(mathcal M/r^2+4pi r p_r)/N,
 a'/a=(G mathcal M'/r-G mathcal M/r^2)/N,
 B=2/r+alpha'/alpha-a'/a,
 f''+B f'=[(m^2-omega^2/alpha^2)f-2g h f+2lambda f^3]/N,
 h''+B h'=[(M^2-4omega^2/alpha^2)h-g f^2]/N.
The minus sign in X=-h is essential for both cubic terms. Both gradients, both conversion equations, and their stress contribution are retained. The same equations follow directly from the original complex Euler equations and the Einstein tt/rr equations. The separate compactness audit reconstructed the signs and normalizations before inspecting this implementation.

The exact conserved charge is
 Q=8pi omega integral_0^infinity a r^2(f^2+4h^2)/alpha dr.
It is not inferred from an assumed mass density. M_ADM=mathcal M(infinity), while M in the potential remains the source particle mass. Complete dispersion into free positive charge-one particles would require energy at least mQ. Finding M_ADM<mQ is a binding diagnostic, not a proof of radial, angular or quantum stability.

CENTER AND TAIL BOUNDARIES
Regularity gives f'(0)=h'(0)=0, mathcal M(0)=0 and finite alpha(0). The code starts at small r0 using
 f(r0)=f0+f2 r0^2/2, f'(r0)=f2 r0,
 h(r0)=h0+h2 r0^2/2, h'(r0)=h2 r0,
 f2=[(m^2-omega^2/alpha0^2)f0-2g h0 f0+2lambda f0^3]/3,
 h2=[(M^2-4omega^2/alpha0^2)h0-g f0^2]/3,
 mathcal M(r0)=4pi rho0 r0^3/3.
A central amplitude is fixed and omega is the BVP eigenparameter. The lapse is not fixed at the center.

Localization requires omega<m and 2omega<M in the asymptotically normalized time. The Phi homogeneous tail has exponential rate sqrt(m^2-omega^2), with Schwarzschild power corrections. X has its massive homogeneous tail and a forced tail sourced by f^2. Because sqrt(M^2-4omega^2)>2sqrt(m^2-omega^2), the forced X tail can decay more slowly than its homogeneous tail. A naive free-X Robin condition is therefore not an exact general outer condition for this mixed branch.

The accepted solver uses f=h=0 at a large finite outer radius, with alpha(Rbox)=sqrt(N(Rbox)), the vacuum Schwarzschild normalization. This is approximate tail truncation, not an assertion of an exact finite material surface. Expanding Rbox and examining outer mass and both field tails control it numerically. Neither a rigorous infinity error bound nor an analytic existence theorem is claimed.

SOLVER COORDINATES AND FILE DICTIONARY
For m=lambda=1 and variable dimensionless G, the TF-seeded full solver uses z=sqrt(G)r and v=G^(3/2)mathcal M. Then N=1-2v/z. Its BVP states are
 (f,f_z,h,h_z,v,b=log alpha).
The source terms in both KG equations are divided by G N; the full energy includes G N(f_z^2+h_z^2). Its Einstein equations are
 v_z=4pi z^2rho,
 b_z=(v/z^2+4pi z p_r)/N.
This rescaling reduces numerical scale separation; it does not remove gradients.

NPZ profiles contain physical r, f,fp=df/dr,h,hp=dh/dr,alpha,a,Q(r), and z,fz,hz,v,b. They also contain G,m,g,lambda_total,M,omega, the frequency logit, and the original collocation nodes/y/cubic-polynomial coefficients. The scalar NPZ key M is the SOURCE particle mass. ADM mass is stored explicitly in the companion JSON. For evolution, X=-h, Pi_Phi=-i omega a f/alpha, Pi_X=+i2omega a h/alpha in the standard Pi=a/alpha times field time derivative convention. The rescaled evolution fields sqrt(4piG)Phi and sqrt(4piG)X must be constructed explicitly; saved f and h are not rescaled that way.
