FRESH CONTINUUM RECONSTRUCTION

 mu'=r^2[(1-2mu/r)S+U], mu=G M_enclosed,
 or mu'+2rS mu=r^2(S+U).
The integrating-factor solution is exactly
 mu(R)=exp[-I(R)] integral_0^R exp[I(s)]s^2[S(s)+U(s)]ds,
 I(r)=integral_0^r2sS(s)ds.
This is what functional() computes. The source profile's stored v=G^(3/2)M is correctly ignored; the metric constraint is recomputed from the perturbed fields and Pi. Thus varying a or the source mass profile independently is neither needed nor permitted in this reduced functional.
