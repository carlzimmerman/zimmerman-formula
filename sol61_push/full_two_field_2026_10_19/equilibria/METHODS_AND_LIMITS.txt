NUMERICAL METHODS, CONTROLS AND UNSUCCESSFUL CONTINUATIONS

The full solver uses scipy.solve_bvp collocation with a positive frequency parametrization omega=expit(p). Central regularity is implemented through quadratic Taylor matching at a nonzero start; lapse is normalized to the asymptotic Schwarzschild exterior at the finite outer box. Both amplitudes satisfy the full radial Klein-Gordon equations. TF profiles are initial guesses only; no fluid EOS appears in the accepted residual function.

Accepted reproducible run_001 uses exactly the five cases in contract.json: main mixed branch at G8.5e-5,h0.02; tolerance1e-8 then1e-10; outer z10->14; centerz0 1e-6->5e-7; one TF-like G1e-8 point. No adaptive scan is part of this fixed run. Runtime is bounded by runner timeout/CPU/log caps and each child solver timeout. No randomness. Source, contract, seeds and canonical carrier input hashes are recorded before/after. A completed validated manifest records execution/provenance, not a theorem.

verify_profiles.py does not merely inspect solver success. It reconstructs the exact physical stress and Klein-Gordon equations from saved collocation polynomials at off-grid8-point Gauss nodes per cell; compares mass/lapse/KG residuals and radial stress conservation; independently integrates mass and proper-volume charge; checks both fields for resolved sign changes, no horizon, tail mass and sub-free-particle energy. R99 is a root of the saved mass interpolant, not an arbitrary grid bin. It uses total ADM mass in C99; mass enclosed there is.99M.

The source-continuation seed solve_ekg.py first solved a single-Phi g=0 problem at G=m=lambda1 and central rescaledF=.05. Restoring the original g yielded a stationary mixed solution with a NODE in h. It is not the requested nodeless branch and not primary evidence of a condensate ground state. The g=0 seed is a DIFFERENT action/ratio; current code labels its actual r_lambda undefined and preserves target-ratio metadata separately. Earlier seed JSON target-ratio labels are historical diagnostics, not physical input claims.

The TF-seeded full solver then produced nodeless mixed points at G1e-8,1e-7,1e-6,1e-5,3e-5,5e-5,7e-5,8e-5,8.5e-5 while keeping the original couplings and a fixed central f. Those failed method attempts do not prove a physical nonexistence or instability. Replacing fixed central f by fixed central h avoided that continuation bottleneck, yielding accepted h0.008,.012,.02 profiles. These exploratory profiles are preserved; the fixed bounded run reproduces only the final witness and its declared controls.

An early TF initial-guess calculation emitted an invalid-log RuntimeWarning because numpy.where evaluated a discarded exterior Schwarzschild expression at interior points. The returned initial-guess values were finite and the accepted BVP converged. The current script computes the exterior expression only on exterior points; the bounded run has no such warning. This was initial-guess implementation cleanup, not a change in the action or acceptance conditions.

Local node check: exact far-tail values can change sign at numerical magnitudes many orders below resolved amplitudes. The nodeless gate requires any negative value to be smaller than10^-10 of the field peak. Final f is positive on Gauss nodes; h negativity is at~10^-38 relative. No physical node is inferred from double-roundoff values at the truncated boundary.

Tail control: finite Dirichlet boundaries are an approximation. Positive asymptotic mass gaps imply exponential homogeneous tails; h also has a forced f^2 tail. Domain enlargement and negligible mass in the outer20% support localization. There is no rigorous interval certificate, uniqueness result, exact infinite-domain error bound or proof for every coupling. Eigenfrequency and metric normalize the same coordinate time; center lapse is not independently reset.

Limitations: dimensionless action-family parameters differ from the physical MeV benchmark's Gm^2/lambda. All calculations are classical/tree-level minimal Einstein gravity. Radiative corrections, additional allowed interactions, quantum occupation, nonthermal formation, radial/angular dynamics, cosmology, lensing populations and lifetime require separate evidence.
