# Nonlinear polarization on an expanding background

An explicit preferred-frame reduced cosmological action has now been constructed. Its matter forces, field equations, Hubble damping and expansion-work identity follow from one variational principle. A dynamical homogeneous-background extension is also written down. This is progress beyond prescribing unrelated friction terms, but it is not a unique fully covariant parent theory or a completed cosmological fit.

The first action-based nonlinear tests do not establish core formation or a selected population. They find sensitivity to the initial field state and mild suppression from the logarithmic polarization nonlinearity in the cases that permit a same-epoch comparison. The large-scale growth diagnostic still fails the inherited99% target at k=1/Mpc.

## What was derived

Conserved comoving material masses imply the cold reference density scales as A^-3. With physical orthonormal P components and the explicitly chosen kinetic term, variation gives

 xddot+2H xdot=-A^-2 grad(Phi+h),
 eta(Pddot+3H Pdot)=-partial_P W-A^-1 grad h,
 b(chiddot+3H chidot)=e²/(2chi²)-alpha(chi-1)+d A^-2 Laplacian chi.

The same action gives the energy exchanged with expansion, including the changing reference density and physical wavenumber. In the48 baseline runs, the largest normalized energy/work discrepancy is8.58e-7. Separate finite-difference Hamiltonian checks test the coordinate forces and explicit expansion derivative. Expansion work is not radiation from a core.

DERIVATION.md contains the reduced action, equations and conventions. MINISUPERSPACE.md adds a lapse and homogeneous gravitational action, obtaining a Friedmann constraint and a pressure consistent with the work identity. That coupled-background system was not numerically evolved: the runs below use its separately declared, imposed EdS benchmark. Full inhomogeneous relativistic constraints and the primordial state remain unresolved.

## Nonlinear evolution from small matter perturbations

The test retains the exact spatially averaged logarithmic W and finite field inertia, with a homogeneous dynamical phase field and one matter/polarization spatial mode. Four characteristic mass scales, three initial contrasts and two field preparations were compared against quadratic-W controls with identical initial data:48 runs. Six canonical tracking-state comparisons were added. The approximation retains matter only to quadratic order and stops when either matter contrast reaches magnitude .3. It cannot identify bound three-dimensional cores or fragmentation.

At the canonical1.095e7-solar-mass scale, with initial matter contrast .001 at z1100, all three field preparations reach z10:

| Initial fields | Cold contrast at z10 | Cold amplification |
|---|---:|---:|
| Unpolarized, zero field velocities | .01860 | 18.60 |
| Joint stationary fields, zero field velocities | .04997 | 49.97 |
| Joint stationary fields, derivatives tracking that branch initially | .03611 | 36.11 |

No case in this table becomes a core or reaches the material stopping threshold. The same density perturbation is not a complete cosmological initial condition: field coordinates and momenta alter its evolution.

At initial contrast .01, the canonical unpolarized case reaches z10 with cold contrast .1764. Its quadratic-W control reaches .1876, so the logarithmic potential reduces cold growth by about6% in that comparison. The stationary and tracking cases instead encounter the baryon .3 limit at z26.32 and z18.27 respectively, with cold contrasts .1851 and .1894. Those are limits of the calculation, not collapse detections.

Of the48 baseline runs,36 reach z10 and12 hit the density limit. No run hits the polarization or positive-chi safety limits. In all18 paired baseline cases reaching z10 in both models, the logarithmic potential yields less cold growth than the quadratic control. This is a finite result, not a nonlinear no-collapse theorem. Ratios across different stopping epochs are not treated as growth comparisons.

Some stationary preparations at larger masses start with chi between roughly7 and9 despite only1% matter contrast. This is initial stored field excitation, not a phase transition generated by cosmological evolution. It cannot be counted as successful precursor production. The unpolarized preparation avoids that assumption but introduces its own field-adjustment transient. Tracking the instantaneous equilibrium derivative reduces another arbitrary choice but is not a derivation of the primordial field state.

Dwarf fits and rotation curves were not recomputed: no core population, occupation law or self-consistent galactic baryon solution was generated. The flat static scalar-sector action is retained, but that alone does not certify those observables after cosmological assembly. No cluster density/lensing profile or remnant mass distribution is predicted.

## Research decision

The route has advanced to an explicit expanding reduced action and tested nonlinear initial-value problem. It has not reached the user's full goal. There is no demonstrated universal mass selector or common early precursor attractor in these tests.

Two dependencies now control the next step: evolve a spatially resolved matter/field system beyond the one-mode restriction, and determine physically justified field initial data from a consistent relativistic background/primordial state. The homogeneous-background extension is available for that work, but its constraint system and field stress must be evolved together before claiming a self-consistent cosmology. Any eventual cores must be detected as persistent bound three-dimensional objects with masses independent of resolution and initial preparation; none has been established here.
