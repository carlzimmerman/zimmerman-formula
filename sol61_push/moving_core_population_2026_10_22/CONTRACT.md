Target: derive effective core dynamics and determine whether abundance/distribution are selected, then test necessary cluster, dwarf/rotation, and growth compatibility.

Three routes: collective-coordinate action and kinetic nonselection; an explicit positive distribution function plus held-out cluster shape tests; growth/packing/encounter and galactic consequences. Core formation, merger kernel, cosmological initial data, field inertias, and halo transport are not given. Do not convert fitted mass/scale into a prediction.

A zero-inertia dust limit is a limit, not automatically the given action. Derive Vlasov only conditional on survival and negligible collisions/overlap interactions. Core production rate and initial mass function are not specified by Vlasov.
