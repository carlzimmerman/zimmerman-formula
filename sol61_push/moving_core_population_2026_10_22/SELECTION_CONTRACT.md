# Same-mass/same-energy nonselection witness
Construct an isotropic n=3 polytropic Vlasov sphere f proportional to (Ecut-E)^(3/2) on E<Ecut, and the analytically verified Plummer sphere. Integrate dimensionless Lane-Emden n3 to its first zero, compute normalized gravitational binding energy independently as integral m(x)x theta³ dx. Rescale n3 to M=G=1 and the Plummer b=1 energy E=-3pi/64, using virial T=-U/2. This is a positive collisionless stationary DF and finite-mass equilibrium, not a time-evolved attractor or stability proof. Compare supports at identical M,E; repeat two solver tolerances. Their different supports demonstrate that conserved mass and energy alone do not select the spatial profile, even after those inputs are supplied. No cosmological or field-core formation inference.
