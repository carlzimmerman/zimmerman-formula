# Self-audit: CFT-20261022

This is a self-review, not an independent referee assessment.

- Rechecked the fixed-mass scaling: U scales as t, V as t³, Z as t^-3, J as t^-1, T as t³; the logarithmic potential contributes 2D-3B to the first variation. These yield the displayed virial identity.
- Eliminating U with that identity gives mu_c M-E=-2V+2Z-2T+2B-D. The scalar identity V=2J+2Z+H/2 then yields the strictly negative expression in RESULT.md. The sign of 2W-wW_w follows from its zero at w=0 and negative derivative for 0<w<1.
- The first law is conditional on a differentiable stationary branch and fixed couplings. It is not a formation rate, global existence theorem or full stability theorem. The doubling comparison requires a connected increasing-mass branch. Positive-gradient terms are covered algebraically; their stationary profiles were not constructed numerically.
- All physical energy comparisons include exterior fields. The deliberate omission control exited 1: all 12 equilibrium checks and the finite-difference first law failed (13 failures). Thus a plausible-looking truncated energy is detected by independent stationarity identities.
- The ordinary runs contain 18 energy, 6 transport and 6 feedback checks, all passing. Those checks establish the declared finite diagnostics, not cosmological or observational validity of the theory.
- Friction examples require a light bath. Equal-mass diffusion, gas pressure and finite-size core effects are not optional corrections to a universal drag law. A full-core/half-light-mass ratio diagnoses breakdown of the point-perturber approximation, not a static enclosed-mass exclusion.
- The stellar heating ratios are illustrative energy budgets, not disruption predictions. Neither starting radius nor stellar deposition fraction was derived.
- Contact escape and capture estimates use Newtonian point gravity at the declared contact radius. Field overlap, inelastic scattering and merger rates remain uncomputed. The finite-N relaxation estimate does not exclude violent relaxation.
- The radiation statement concerns the fixed-chi, vacuum, linear channel. A subgap characteristic frequency does not exclude high-frequency pulse tails, nonlinear emission, other fields or material ejection.
- One actual mass doubling is not decisively ruled out by the inherited coarse statistics. These remain phenomenological law-plus-core sensitivity tests, with no refit and no self-consistent baryonic response. Growth is an imposed conversion-history comparison, not a covariant cosmological calculation.
- Corrected the inherited calibration label: the ten-object MW sample includes Leo IV and Canes Venatici II. No sample was changed. The separate UFD comparison remains disjoint.

Verdict: the passive binding-energy selector is obstructed on the stated stationary branches. A common derived formation and transport mechanism is still unresolved; the surviving route requires dynamical arrest, supply and reciprocal energy export.
