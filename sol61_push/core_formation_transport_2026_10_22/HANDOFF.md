## What survives
The doubled core is not decisively excluded by the coarse inherited dwarf and SPARC summaries. Gravitational drag can be faster in some dwarf outskirts than in larger galaxies/clusters. Neither observation chooses the stopping mass or central occupation. Central dwarf drag is outside the declared weak-perturber regime; illustrative orbital energy transfers exceed stellar kinetic energy in all nine defined paths. Slow linear vacuum polarization radiation is gapped. Pure-gravity cluster two-body relaxation is too slow to select its distribution over a cosmic age, while assembly remains possible.
