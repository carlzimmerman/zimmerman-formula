# Early dense precursor checkpoint

The existing action has not produced an action-derived early core population or passed the joint dwarf/rotation-curve/cluster/growth test. This turn establishes a local growing-mode obstruction and a new limitation of the cosmological linear approximation: polarization can become nonlinear while matter density contrasts are still small. This identifies a concrete missing calculation, rather than demonstrating precursors.

## Results

**Finite field inertia does not accelerate the passive local growing mode.** For one cold fluid, x=s² obeys

 eta x²+[1+q²-eta J(1-q²)]x-J=0,
 0<x<=J/(1+q²).

Its positive root decreases monotonically with q². The corresponding two-fluid growing mode is also no faster than the quasistatic mode. Thus this finite-response branch has no preferred nonzero wavenumber that could select a core mass. This is a frozen-background theorem, not a full expanding-universe no-go theorem.

**The canonical core scale is strongly suppressed in the inherited expanding linear diagnostic.** With the declared mass-to-wavenumber convention,1.095e7 solar masses corresponds to k=24.77/Mpc. The following are linear extrapolations from equal matter contrasts and dust-like velocities at z1100:

| Target epoch | Cold amplification | Fraction of dust amplification | Initial contrast for extrapolated cold delta=1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| z100 | 4.067 | .373 | .246 |
| z30 | 10.807 | .304 | .0925 |
| z20 | 15.881 | .303 | .0630 |
| z10 | 30.733 | .307 | .0325 |

These are not physical collapse thresholds. Baryons become nonlinear earlier in these extrapolations, and the field approximation can fail much earlier still. The actual primordial density spectrum is not derived or inferred from CMB temperature amplitude here.

**The phase-trigger loophole fails its perturbative validity test.** An explicitly conditional FRW scalar-response calculation includes both the mean and twice-wavenumber phase forcing generated by h². Some larger-scale modes seem to develop phase shifts before density becomes nonlinear. However, the full amplitude audit shows that every sampled10% phase trigger has already violated the conservative polarization or matter-linearity limits. Across14 mass scales and3001 sampled times, the smallest combined violation factor exceeds18. The apparent trigger is not a controlled result.

**Small density perturbations can already require nonlinear polarization.** On the canonical scale, a1% initial contrast gives a quasistatic polarization amplitude .3045, outside the conservative |P|<.1 range. A .1% initial contrast gives .03045 and only .03073 cold density contrast by z10. The corresponding second-order phase shift for the1% case peaks at just .000175. Thus polarization nonlinearity precedes the proposed phase transition in this benchmark. Exact nonlinear evolution might change the conclusion; the perturbative extrapolation cannot determine how.

## What is and is not preserved

No action coefficient or existing phenomenological fit was altered. That does not establish preservation of dwarf dispersions or rotation curves after population formation. No bound core catalogue, central occupation, cluster spatial profile or lensing prediction was obtained. The inherited linear growth problem persists in its regime of validity; its extrapolation to finite early perturbations is now more tightly qualified by the field-amplitude check. No CMB or large-scale-structure likelihood was run.

The reference mean density uses H0=67.4 and Omega_m=.315 from the base-LambdaCDM inputs documented in the previous checkpoint. The growth equations remain imposed EdS. A primordial density spectrum requires a transfer calculation with consistent metric, radiation and matter equations; the standard treatment exemplified by [Ma & Bertschinger](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/9506072) does not supply those equations for this modified action.

## Decision

Do not start another compact-seed collapse calculation and label it cosmological formation. The next substantive obligation is a consistent expanding-background reduction retaining the exact logarithmic polarization potential, finite field inertia, the homogeneous reference density and reciprocal matter/field energy exchange. It must specify its initial statistical state. The present reduced action leaves the parent clock/metric sector and allowed curvature couplings unspecified, so a unique action-derived nonlinear cosmology cannot yet be asserted.

The remaining early-precursor route is open at that dependency. The new hierarchy to investigate is nonlinear polarization at small density contrast, not a demonstrated linear instability or a selected mass spectrum. Derivations, all numerical outputs and the failed interpretation gate are retained alongside this report.
