# Conditional compact-core shape test
Use cached CFG687 per-cluster D,E,PL,PN,mg,ra for 318 eRASS1 objects, eight geometric-centre radial bins 0.15--3 Mpc, surface density Msun/pc^2. Verify PL baseline MID/OUTER against stored canonical b0.67 rc0.15 f0.20 amplitudes before interpreting.

Test two baselines: PN (Newtonian baryons, appropriate to finite cold core with vacuum exterior in this action), and PL (generous phenomenological preserved-law baseline plus core, NOT a solution of this action). For each test a common mass and a mass proportional to cached gas mass. Fit only MID bins 3,4,5 by the existing inverse-variance band-moment convention. Predict INNER bins 0,1,2 and OUTER 6,7. Thirty RA jackknife groups; refit MID parameter on every deletion, report scatter and band residual Z as descriptive diagnostics, not an independent discovery significance or a full covariance likelihood. No per-object masses. All fits are conditional on centred unresolved cores and GR-like lensing; bin-centre templates inherit existing pipeline approximation.

Controls: reproduce baseline, inject known compact mass on both baselines and recover it, verify unit conversion. Report both positive and negative held-out outcomes. Physical unit conversion uses ell0=.4404221297174536 kpc, a0=9.3603e-11 and 1.1312e-10 SI separately. Local rotation tolerance illustration v0=200 km/s at r=10 kpc, 10% speed increase, neglect mediator cancellation; this is not a SPARC fit.
