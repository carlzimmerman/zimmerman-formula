1. A FAST SIGNAL IS NOT AUTOMATICALLY AN EFFICIENT ENERGY SINK
Consider the explicit local linear mediator action
 L_chi=gamma_f chi_dot^2/(2c_f^2)-gamma_f|grad chi|^2/2+g chi rho,
with gamma_f>0, c_f>0. rho is the stipulated source density. Matter in this
comparison has the reciprocal interaction potential -g chi per unit mass;
varying both matter and field accounts for the work. Here the breathing source
trajectory is prescribed, not derived from a particular fluid EOS.
Define alpha=g^2/(4pi gamma_f G), the static scalar force strength relative to
Newtonian gravity. This is an assigned candidate coupling, not a measured
portal or a prediction of the retained action.

For a uniform sphere of conserved mass M and radius
 R(t)=R[1+epsilon cos(Omega t)], epsilon<<1,
the spatial form factor is F(z)=3(sin z-z cos z)/z^3, z=Omega R/c_f.
The harmonic radiative charge is epsilon M z F'(z), with
 z F'(z)=-z^2/5+z^4/70-z^6/2520+O(z^8).
The leading constant-charge term cancels. Retardation resolves the changing
size only at O(z^2). Independent radial quadratures include both the interior
density change and the moving surface contribution; dropping the latter
would incorrectly make the total source charge change.

The far-zone outgoing field has energy flux
 P_rad=g^2 Omega^2 |epsilon M z F'(z)|^2/(8pi gamma_f c_f).
For z<<1, P_rad=alpha G M^2 epsilon^2 Omega^2 z^4/(50c_f).
The stipulated homologous harmonic mode has reference energy
 E_mode=3MR^2 epsilon^2 Omega^2/10.
With the additional scale comparison Omega^2=GM/R^3 and v=Omega R,
              t_E/t_dyn=(15/alpha)(c_f/v)^5.
At assigned alpha=1, c_f=c and v=200 km/s this ratio is 1.135e17.
This is a prescribed-source, weak-radiation estimate, not a galaxy cooling
likelihood, an on-shell halo eigenmode, or an exclusion of all mediators.
Varying scalar charges, strong nonlinear response, pressure-dependent source
terms, nonsphericity, other source couplings or a slow material mode require
their own power calculation and can change the coefficient or suppression.
The vector gauge counterpart with conserved spherical charge has no ordinary
transverse monopole radiation; that earlier source constraint remains intact.

2. SAME FIELD CAN TRANSPORT MASS AND ENERGY WITHOUT A NEW RECEIVER
Return to the earlier canonical complex scalar candidate, whose potential is
 V=rho_v+m^2|Psi|^2+lambda|Psi|^4.
Its lambda=0 dilute, positive-frequency, weak-gravity limit is
 i hbar psi_dot=-hbar^2 laplacian psi/(2m)+m Phi psi,
 laplacian Phi=4piG m|psi|^2.
The conserved number is integral |psi|^2; cold rest mass is m times number.
Its nonrelativistic energy is gradient kinetic plus half the gravitational
self-interaction energy. No artificial cooling or damping is in this action.
The vacuum offset remains present in the relativistic parent. It is neglected
in this local Newtonian comparison, valid only on appropriate subcosmological
scales. No vacuum energy is extracted. Initial field occupation is supplied;
particle production and relic abundance remain independent missing steps.

This branch has a global U(1), with no dark gauge field or critical charge.
It is not the neutral two-sign plasma/demixing candidate, and not the S2
critical self-gravity-canceling medium. The candidate-to-result distinction
is essential: restoring ordinary self-gravity is what permits this test.

3. ACTUAL SPHERICAL EVOLUTION
Use hbar=m=G=1 and u=r psi. Evolve
 i u_dot=-u_rr/2+Phi u, u(0)=u(Rbox)=0.
The initial state is a normalized Gaussian of mass 1:
 u=A r exp[-r^2/(2*5^2)] exp[-i*.03*r^2/2].
The Gaussian radius and inward velocity chirp are specified inputs, not a
law-shaped halo, temperature or fitted equilibrium. There are no baryons in
this comparison; its object is self-gravitating rather than a galaxy model.

Sine-transform kinetic evolution and exact potential substeps give a
reversible split-step Hamiltonian method. The discrete Newtonian potential
uses the symmetric shell Green kernel -G/max(r_i,r_j), including the complete
outer-shell contribution. No field is reset to a target and no absorber is
used. Mass remains in the computed domain; the outer boundary is placed
far enough away and separately enlarged to test its influence.

Runs through t=200:
 baseline: N=2047, Rbox=200, dt=.02;
 refinement: N=4095, Rbox=200, dt=.01;
 larger box: N=4095, Rbox=400, dt=.02;
 gravity-off control: N=4095, Rbox=400, dt=.02, G=0.
The refined run conserves total mass to 1.35e-11 and total energy to a
maximum relative error 3.18e-7. The larger-box mass beyond .8 Rbox is
<4.55e-14. The baseline/refined final masses outside radius ~20 differ by
9.40e-6 of the total; enlarging the box changes that mass by <7.6e-11.
Radial flux independently accounts for the change of enclosed mass to
3.1e-8 in the refined run. This is genuine redistribution, not mass creation.

The peak central density is ~10.1 times its initial value. It subsequently
oscillates; at t=200 it is only ~1.36 times its initial value. Thus a highly
concentrated episode is verified, but a stationary final core or an attractor
is NOT claimed. About 8.57% of mass is outside radius ~20 at the endpoint,
versus ~94.83% in the gravity-off control. Those are finite-radius transport
fractions, not all proven escaping or permanently unbound mass.

4. THE ENERGY-EXIT CHECK, INCLUDING A NEGATIVE RESULT
After seeing that radius-20 mass moves both inward and outward, a POST-HOC
diagnostic added energy partitions at radii 20,50,100. The original evolution
was rerun unchanged with these measurements, retaining all controls.
No observational target was fitted; these diagnostics are nevertheless
post-hoc, not pre-registered evidence of a chosen cooling fraction.

Define E_core=K_core+W_core,self. K_core uses the physical spherical field
gradient, not a velocity inferred by dividing through a wave node. Assign
all exterior self-gravity and core-exterior cross gravity to
 E_ext=E_total-E_core.
This is an explicit Hamiltonian partition; it does not pretend gravitational
energy has a unique local density. Nor is positive E_ext a theorem that
every exterior parcel escapes in a changing potential.

At t=200 in the refined run:
 cut 20: exterior mass .08578, exterior energy -.0014164;
 cut 50: exterior mass .0174251, exterior energy +.000359038;
 cut100: exterior mass .0000457912, exterior energy +.00000881086.
Radius-20 material is therefore NOT all escaping positive-energy exhaust.
The sign check prevents interpreting its entire 8.6% mass as cooling loss.

At radius50, core energy changes from -.03291409 initially to -.03327313.
Its exterior carries positive energy including the attractive cross term.
The export is ~1.09% of the initial binding-energy magnitude, carried by
~1.74% of the mass at the endpoint. The exterior mass grows from .0000289
at t100 to .005216 at t150 and .017425 at t200. Some matter really travels
outward while the retained interior becomes more negative in this partition.
Baseline/refinement exterior energies at50 differ by ~.021%; doubling the
box at fixed grid spacing changes that energy by <8.2e-11 absolute.

This verifies a finite-time positive-energy tail, not complete irreversible
settling, escape to infinity, a universal ejected fraction or a cosmological
formation history. A conservative whole system can retain energy while an
open interior loses energy to its exterior. This is the available mechanism
that the earlier closed-mode/no-attractor argument did not forbid.

5. A SUCCESSFUL CORE MECHANISM STILL DOES NOT SELECT THE GALAXY LAW
The unchanged Schroedinger-Poisson equations have the exact scaling
 psi_lambda(r,t)=lambda^2 psi(lambda r,lambda^2 t),
 Phi_lambda(r,t)=lambda^2 Phi(lambda r,lambda^2 t).
Thus M->lambda M, R->R/lambda, E->lambda^3 E and v->lambda v.
A homologous coherent-core family therefore has MR fixed at fixed particle
mass; its scale is set by hbar^2/(Gm^2M). It does not pick a universal a0.
If one additionally assigns M proportional to baryonic mass, that one-scale
core family does not reproduce the baryonic Tully-Fisher scaling. This is
not a no-go theorem for multiscale cosmological wave halos; no baryons,
nonlinear S2 interaction or halo population were evolved here.

Likewise, transferring ordinary gravitational cooling into the critical
tether phase would change the force balance: critical repulsion cancels
the material's self-gravity. Its assembly and energy export must be solved
again on its own action. Calling the scalar-wave result a successful
formation result for S2 would combine incompatible candidates.

This advances the assembly/energy-budget piece of the earlier shared-field
candidate. It does not identify the substance in nature, derive its initial
population, explain the vacuum value or join it to the galaxy response.

The next unproved arrow is a physical state/interaction that gives the
