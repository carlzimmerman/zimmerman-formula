Original target
Determine which baryonic state physically couples to the proposed cold-energy
tether, and derive why stars plus HI/H2 would couple while hot gas does not.
A result must distinguish an action-derived coupling from an imposed source
label, retain ordinary gravity of all mass, identify reciprocal momentum and
energy exchange, and survive galaxies with few stars and old stripped debris.
The investigation may falsify that source rule instead of assuming it true.
No claim to solve all gravity/cosmology, carrier existence or fitted kappa.

What counts as substantive progress
An exact obstruction to a specified mechanism; an explicit healthy coupling
that derives the phase distinction; a justified medium-state alternative with
different predictions; or a necessary observational discriminator. A chosen
profile, tunable phase label, or equilibrium witness alone does not solve the
target. The previous warm-tether witness remains feasibility, not selection.
