The physical meaning of "tether"
The identified medium responds collectively to a gravitational potential.
Nothing in the stated action attaches a carrier to a particular atom or star.
The restoring force is radial/mean-field in the spherical comparison, and
depends on the mass displacement. A basin/frame anchor remains a separate
missing reciprocal interaction. Matter composition, cooling history and
carrier supply cannot be inferred from the word polarization alone.

1. Which baryons the action actually reads — route G
With K=4piG, the corrected unsquared potential part is
  L=-|gradPhi|^2/(2K)+P.gradPhi-W_full(u)
    -(rho_cond+rho_hot+rho_free)Phi, u=K|P|.
All three ordinary source vertices have the same derivative with respect to
their density. Swapping equal condensed and hot densities leaves this action
unchanged. In the spherical zero-stiffness cold comparison,
  g-u=b_cond+b_hot+b_free,
  g=K W_full,u=u+R(u), R=u^2/(a0-u).
Therefore R=b_cond+b_hot+b_free. A baryon-only template R=b_cond leaves
inward carrier acceleration -(b_hot+b_free), unless stress or another
interaction supplies support. This is the real meaning of the source gap.

Newtonian gravity has no temperature label. The schematic relativistic
3p/(rho c^2) diagnostic for assigned 0.1,1,8keV gas is at most 4.3e-5 and
adds rather than removes active source strength in that diagnostic. It is
not an exact nonlinear-gravity calculation or a substitute for full stress.
A linear frequency filter does not solve this: a stationary smooth hot gas
distribution has a zero-frequency mean gravitational source, just like a
stationary coarse stellar distribution. Suppressing microscopic hot-particle
fluctuations does not suppress that mean source. Spatial stiffness and star
granularity already have wrong-sign/small-size constraints in CFG722 B7.

Strongest restriction: within this universal-density action or a linear
filter reading only that same density/current, hot/cold labels cannot be
recovered from equal input fields. Additional matter-state variables and an
interaction depending on them are required. This is not a no-go for every
nonlocal, history-dependent or nongravitational theory.

3. Ordinary baryon cooling is not the attachment mechanism — route K
For zero-drift Maxwell gases with a passive elastic interaction,
  Qchi = positive_prefactor * (Tb-Tchi).
Temperature equilibration gives kTchi=mchi sigma_chi^2. At sigma_chi=100km/s,
the tested rest energies .62,16,143eV give kinetic temperatures below 0.19K.
Even assigned 10K gas heats all those carriers. Cooling at that speed instead
requires mchi c^2>7.745keV; 100K requires >77.45keV, 10^4K requires >7.745MeV.
These are necessary heat-sign conditions, not cross-section/rate exclusions.
They concern direct thermal contact with a light carrier; heavy carriers,
inelastic or collective channels are not excluded by that statement.

Momentum is a second, independent problem. A pressureless ordinary circular
tracer obeys v_b^2/r=b_total+u. The carrier feels inward b_total+u and outward
full tether u+R. If drag brings that carrier into the same circular motion,
the outward residual in its radial equation is u+R>0. It cannot cold-corotate
at that radius with a nonzero tether. A drag mechanism must solve tangential
momentum, baryon reaction, pressure and escape, not only cool an energy scalar.
At g_b/a0=1e-4 the static comparison's absolute binding ceiling is already
only about0.10 of the baryon circular speed. This ceiling is not an exact
time-dependent trajectory; the quoted drag time uses a held-radius toy flow.

Physical stellar encounters are too sparse in the declared simple example:
1e10 solar-size stars across a 5kpc disc, 200km/s encounters, point-mass
gravitational focusing and1000 crossings yield a probability bound2.14e-9.
This is an assigned opaque-star example, not a measured galaxy constraint.
It excludes explaining a broadly distributed halo by those literal encounters
without enhanced dark density or a long-range/collective interaction.
The primary heating literature also identifies this coarse-graining issue;
it does not supply the missing tether vertex.

6. The simulation's source is an irreversible memory — route S
The actual update_tags functions in CFG640 and CFG650 perform
  f_d <- max(f_d, f_condensed_of_current_catchment).
The source deposits f_d. It is never lowered; field particles retain tags.
AST-isolated runs of those exact functions on two co-located particles give
.10 and .80 after the same current catchment, reflecting different histories.
Leaving the catchment erases neither tag. This is a specific history-variable
model, not a present-temperature classifier or microscopic coupling proof.
The record explicitly omits feedback/reheating and W_int conversion energy.

If ordinary baryons obey continuity, a weighted source rho_eff=eta rho_b obeys
  d_t rho_eff+div(rho_eff v_b)=rho_b D_t eta.
Changing the selected source requires a state-transition term. The interaction
energy has a corresponding state-work term; it cannot disappear by changing
a force-source array while claiming an action-derived phase conversion.
No overall PM energy audit is claimed from this isolated identity.

A simple phase-dependent full-tether coefficient is also not a free fix.
For W_alpha=alpha W_full in G=a0=1, the reduced energy derivative is
  F_alpha=(alpha-1)u+alpha u^2/(1-u).
The zero-field curvature is alpha-1. alpha=1 is the critical deep-law point;
alpha>1 has a finite weak-source linear response, while0<alpha<1 has an
unsourced polarized stationary root u=1-alpha in this comparison. A real
phase transition may have a different action; this does not exclude one.
It shows why toggling a constitutive coefficient requires rederiving the law
and stability, with energy exchange W_full d(alpha)/dt accounted for.

Directly canceling extra-source gravity has a different cost. In a static
position-only pair model, compensating the gravitational hot/tether cross
energy cancels both directions of that interaction. Hot gas would then fail
to accelerate under the tether mass in the same way as an ordinary metric
tracer. Hydrostatic and lensing comparisons would have to be redone with the
new nongravitational gas force. One cannot retain both the old universal
material-force pipeline and a one-way cancellation. This restricted
reciprocity observation does not exclude mediators/anchors carrying stress.
