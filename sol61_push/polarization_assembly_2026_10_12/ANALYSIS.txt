Analytic source-work diagnostic — 12 October 2026

Scope: the existing radial effective two-field response model, positive
constitutive slope, fixed outer Phi=0, regular origin, units C=a0=Z=I=1.
This is a derivation within an assumed model, not a microscopic theory.

Let D=grad Phi-P and W'(P)=b(P), with the signed radial P convention.
H = integral [Phi_t^2/2 + rho(t) Phi] r^2 dr
  + integral [P_t^2/2 + D^2/2 + W(P)] r^2 dr.
The equations Phi_tt=div D-rho and P_tt=D-b(P) imply
 dH/dt = integral rho_t Phi r^2 dr
when boundary power vanishes. The staggered discretisation preserves the
integration-by-parts identity with cell volumes and face dual volumes;
its continuous-time energy identity is the identical finite-dimensional one.
Velocity Verlet and numerical work quadrature introduce the checked error.

For rho=lambda(t) rho0, this becomes Hdot=lambda_dot <rho0,Phi>.
The quintic ramp used here is lambda=10s^3-15s^4+6s^5, s=t/T on [0,1].
It has zero first and second derivatives at both ends. A sudden source
starts with H=0; Phi=0 makes its instantaneous source-coupling work zero.
A gradual source can remove interaction energy; its work cannot be omitted.

Let Phi_s,P_s be the static solution for the instantaneous source and let
D_s=grad Phi_s-P_s=b(P_s), div D_s=rho. Expanding the energy and using
stationarity gives the exact identity
 H-H_s = integral [Phi_t^2/2 + P_t^2/2 + (D-D_s)^2/2
                 + W(P)-W(P_s)-b(P_s)(P-P_s)] r^2 dr >= 0.
In discrete form the Phi terms use cell volumes and P,D terms face weights.
The inequality uses convexity of W. It assumes the fields stay in the
interpolant support where its force and primitive agree. That is checked.
This is a global energy statement; excess energy can remain outside the
force-evaluation window even when the interior agrees with the target.
The derivative relative to the instantaneous minimum is
 d(H-H_s)/dt = integral rho_t (Phi-Phi_s) r^2 dr.
Consequently ramping is a driving protocol, not a theorem of attraction.

For comparison, a single linear mode x_tt+omega^2 x=f lambda(t), omega>0,
initially at rest at lambda=0, has residual oscillation amplitude
 |f|/omega^2 * |integral_0^T lambda_dot exp(i omega t)dt|
after the ramp. This follows by integrating the forced-oscillator Green
function by parts. Since lambda_dot is nonnegative and integrates to one,
the filter modulus is <=1. Twice integrating by parts makes it the
Fourier transform of lambda triple-dot divided by (i omega)^2; one more
integration, including endpoint terms, gives O((omega T)^(-3)) for this
quintic ramp. The filter tends to one as omega T tends to zero.
Thus slow switching suppresses modes only relative to their own periods.
The model's vacuum null branch prevents a uniform spectral-gap argument.
This diagnostic is not a proof for the nonlinear inhomogeneous calculation.

Continuity qualification: lambda(t) times a fixed compact baryon profile
is a prescribed source, not a closed baryon flow. Conserved spherical
assembly would require rho_t + r^(-2) d_r(r^2 rho v)=0, hence
 rho v = -lambda_dot M0(<r)/(4 pi r^2) in conventional mass units.
Outside a sharply compact profile rho=0 but the required flux is nonzero.
An exterior reservoir/inflow must be supplied; the numerical ramp does not
supply it. This is why a passing ramp test alone cannot establish formation
from a complete matter action. A fixed-mass homologous contraction gives
a cleaner next test: rho(r,t)=a(t)^(-3) rho0(r/a(t)), v=(a_dot/a)r,
which satisfies continuity including the advected compact edge. Its driving
still needs an external force or a baryon equation, but it creates no mass.

Self-review: signs checked by the equations and discrete adjoint identity;
energy gap assumes a stationary solution with the same boundary data;
convexity is not convergence; zero-frequency modes are excluded only from
the single-oscillator formula, not silently from the field theory.
No external theorem is invoked, and no novelty claim is made.

For a conserved compact source the same work has a mechanical meaning:
 integral rho_t Phi dV = integral rho v_b dot grad Phi dV,
using continuity and vanishing surface mass flux at the fixed outer wall.
For homologous contraction v_b points inward and grad Phi outward, so this
power is negative for an attractive field. The field can lose energy to
baryon motion. If baryons obeyed their own equation with force -grad Phi,
the gravitational part of their kinetic-energy change would be the opposite
power. Holding a prescribed slow trajectory still requires accounting for
baryon stresses, cooling or external control. No such equation is supplied.
This identifies a possible receiver for this protocol, not its microphysics.
