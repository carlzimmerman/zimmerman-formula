# Radiation scope clarification

The `directed_radiation_J_branch` numbers in root run_001 are **particle-like orbital-packet** figures. They are not universal minimum energies for angular momentum radiated by a compact emitter. The raw completed numerical record is retained; this note and RESULT.md supply its precise interpretation.

For an outgoing scalar angular mode psi=A(r,theta) exp[i(m phi-omega t)], the common radial Noether current gives

    energy flux = omega F_r,
    axial-J flux = m F_r,
    Jdot_z/Edot = m/omega.

A massive mode's radial wave number cancels in the ratio. A compact emitter may lie inside the angular-mode near/turning region; its radius need not bound this ratio by r/c. Emission may be strongly barrier-suppressed, but that needs a mode/rate calculation. The instantaneous positive-energy size bound on a localized object's spin is a different statement from a bound on flux ratios through a near-zone surface.

Particle-like on-shell packets with orbital J assigned inside radius r do obey J_orb<=rE/c. Intrinsic photon spin, coherent multipole J and extended near fields require separate accounting. Radiation that has zero net momentum AND zero intrinsic J in an emitter COM advects only its boosted orbital contribution; isotropic energy intensity alone does not establish those hypotheses.

ARGON's exterior field spectrum and the actual merger's mode amplitudes were not computed in this packet. The positive stationary-state gap does not fix them. No all-radiation no-go is claimed. This correction was independently derived by the merger-bounds reviewer before final reporting.
