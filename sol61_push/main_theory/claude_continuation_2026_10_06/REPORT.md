## New declared bridge, varied before using it as a mass

Use signature (−,+,+,+), natural units, Ricci convention R=6(Hdot+2H²), and a complex scalar (two real canonical scalars):

S=∫√−g [M R/2−Λ_v−∇χ*·∇χ−ξR|χ|²]+S_ordinary[g].

M>0, ξ>0, with no independent bare scalar mass. This is our candidate repair, not an action specified by Claude. The effective scalar frequency term is m_eff²=ξR. Metric variation gives

(□−ξR)χ=0,
Tχμν=∂μχ*∂νχ+∂νχ*∂μχ−gμν|∂χ|²
 +2ξ[|χ|²Gμν+(gμν□−∇μ∇ν)|χ|²].

Writing f=|χ|², homogeneous components are

ρ=|χdot|²+2ξ(3H²f+3H fdot),
p=|χdot|²+2ξ[−(2Hdot+3H²)f−fddot−2H fdot].

The coefficient 2ξ is required by the complex normalization; it is not a second mass term. One may move 2ξfG to the geometric side, obtaining M_eff=M−2ξf. Both conventions give the same Einstein equations. For any finite compact interval an amplitude can be chosen small enough to keep M_eff>0; this is not a proof of all perturbative health. Setting R to its background value *before metric variation* omits these terms and does not define this covariant bridge.

## Exact matter-era result: a conserved carrier charge with zero source stress

Take exact EdS H=2/(3t), R=4/(3t²), Λ_v=0, ordinary dust sourcing the metric. The scalar equation is

χddot+2χdot/t+4ξχ/(3t²)=0.

For ξ>3/16 define ω²=4ξ/3−1/4 and take

χ=√A t^(−1/2) exp[iω ln(t/t0)], A>0.

Then f=A/t, |χdot|²=4ξA/(3t³). The improvement density is

2ξ[(4/3)−2]A/t³=−4ξA/(3t³),

and the pressure improvement is the same. Therefore **ρ=p=0 exactly**, not merely averaged. The metric remains an exact ordinary-dust EdS solution in this test action. The U(1) number charge a³f phase_dot is nonzero and conserved (a³∝t², phase_dot=ω/t), but it is not positive gravitating cold density. A conserved particle label/charge alone cannot supply the missing cosmic cold share.

For one canonically normalized real mode ϕ=√2 Re χ=Ct^s, with real action −(∂ϕ)²/2−ξRϕ²/2 and s²+s+4ξ/3=0, the full density coefficient is

ρ=−s(3s+2)(2s+1)C²t^(2s−2)/2,
p=−sρ.

An individual nonzero real-power mode could scale as dust t^−2 only at s=0, which forces ξ=0 and ρ=0. In the oscillatory regime the real solutions have sign-changing log-periodic stress whose phase average vanishes; the two-real-field circular solution above supplies an exact, stronger control. This does not classify all inhomogeneous states, self-interacting potentials, source formations or jointly backreacted cosmologies.

Indeed, the cancellation extends to any power-law a∝t^v admitting a circular oscillatory root: Re(s)=(1−3v)/2, ω²=6ξv(2v−1)−Re(s)². Its exact ρ and p both vanish. The existence condition ω²>0 is essential: plugging imaginary frequencies into the formal identity does not provide a physical oscillating radiation solution. Exact de Sitter has the analogous χ∝exp[−3Ht/2+iω_dt], ω_d²=(12ξ−9/4)H², again with zero full stress.

## Radiation control and amplitude freedom

As a prescribed exact-radiation metric control, H=1/(2t), R=0. Nonzero stiff probe stress generally requires backreaction; this is not asserted to remain an exact radiation-only Einstein solution. The scalar is massless regardless of ξ; homogeneous solutions are χ=C0+C1t^−1/2. With complex constants, the cross terms cancel from the full stress:

ρ=(3ξ/2)|C0|²t^−2+(1/4−3ξ/2)|C1|²t^−3,
p=(ξ/2)|C0|²t^−2+(1/4−3ξ/2)|C1|²t^−3.

The constant branch is radiation-like and amounts to a Planck-coefficient shift. The decaying branch is stiff, ρ=p∝a^−6, rather than dust. For ξ>1/6 its RHS energy is negative in the chosen split. That sign alone is not a physical-ghost theorem; the nonminimal constraint system would need reduction. Neither branch is a positive homogeneous dust source. In an actual radiation+dust universe the trace of the subdominant dust is nonzero, so R need not vanish exactly; the exact-radiation control is not a claim about every mixed history.

For illustration only, if one equates the *de Sitter* effective mass √(12ξ)ℏH with Claude's quoted carrier scale, using declared H=1.8e−18 s^−1 gives ξ≈3.11e25. This merely moves the free mass into a huge dimensionless coupling; it does not justify applying Claude's constant-mass transfer fit to this evolving, nonminimally coupled field. Finite nonzero modes and altered metric response remain untested. The standard positive control is a separately specified constant-mass carrier with conserved number density and adiabatic ρ≈mn∝a^−3; its mass/amount must be fixed by further physics.

## What this does and does not decide

The cheapest curvature-only replacement for the undeclared carrier mass does not yield the needed homogeneous gravitating dust on the exact matter/radiation controls. Full action variation produces an exact stress cancellation on its oscillating branch and radiation/stiff behavior on the radiation branches. It does not select the vacuum coefficient, carrier abundance, or 32π. Adding a bare mass, self interaction, nonzero gradients, additional curvature operators or another matter coupling changes the test and may evade it; every such change must rederive background stress, source dictionary and transfer function. No general no-go for cold matter, BIMOND, QUMOND, or all curvature couplings is claimed. No prior same-action cosmology/MOND passes are transferred.

The next precise implication would be a covariant mass-generation mechanism whose *full varied* homogeneous stress is positive dust through radiation/matter eras, while its mass and abundance follow independently of the demanded coefficient. This note rejects only the explicit ξR-only route, before any large numerical galaxy run.
