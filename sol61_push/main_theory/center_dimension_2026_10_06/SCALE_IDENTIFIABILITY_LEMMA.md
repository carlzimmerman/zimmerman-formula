# Vacuum symmetry, quadratic cosmology and regular-center conditions cannot select A/H

This is an analytic identifiability statement for the specified logarithmic KGB plus normalized-clock response family. It does not assume that every member has a completed stable galaxy solution. The original32pi goal remains open.

Fix n>=3, K_E,c,H, the selected vacuum parameter, and epsilon in[0,1). Let A vary over positive values in the same covariant action, with

`Lresp=K_E lambda_n[(1-epsilon)a²-2W(a;A)]`,

`W_a=sqrt(a²+A²/4)-A/2`, `W(0)=0`,

where a is the magnitude of the normalized-clock acceleration. Its local expansion is

`W(a;A)=a³/(3A)+O(a^5/A³)`.

Therefore W and its first two variations vanish on every geodesic clock background. The term |a_vector|³ is C² at zero; no ordinary third Frechet derivative there is assumed. Its directional cubic coefficient depends on A.

For every A>0 the following evidence is identical:

1. The exact classical vacuum-rescaling map: normalized clock, a and the entire response are invariant under positive scalar rescaling. The logarithmic shift absorbs the same constant vacuum offset.
2. The homogeneous rolling geodesic vacuum solution and its metric H. Lresp and its first variation vanish there.
3. The complete quadratic response action about that geodesic background: it is K_E lambda_n(1-epsilon)(delta a)², independent of A. Any exact quadratic metric/clock constraint reduction or linear cosmological observable therefore has the same A dependence, namely none.
4. The regular-center leading necessary equations with a=O(r): only the coefficient of a² contributes to the leading lapse/radial equations. All the center discriminants/leading branch equations derived here are independent of A.

It follows that **no condition built exclusively from those four inputs can force a particular A/H**. If such a condition accepts one positive A, replacing it by any other positive A leaves those inputs and its truth value unchanged. This is an exact parameter-family argument, not absence of a discovered numerical selector.

The family is physically distinguishable in its nonlinear response: the positive-a cubic term is proportional to1/A. In the declared weak aligned-clock radial dictionary, elimination of the Einstein metric yields

`epsilon a+W_a(a;A)=mu/r^(n-1)`,

`mu=Msource/[2K_E lambda_n S_(n-1)]`.

Here S_(n-1) is unit-sphere area. In a controlled intermediate regime epsilon A<<a<<A, this becomes `a²≈A mu/r^(n-1)`. At n=3 it has the MOND1/r scaling with amplitude varying as sqrt(A). This is a formal weak-response prediction; a full sourced flowing metric must still establish its applicability and measured Newton normalization. The family argument does not borrow the independent BIMOND kernel-vacuum integral into KGB.

Thus the proposed vacuum adjustment, linear health and source-center regularity do not identify the MOND scale even if a detuned regular core survives. A further nonlinear/global consistency principle, new coupling/symmetry with actual action restrictions, or independent microphysics is needed. A finite-gradient health or boundary-matching condition might supply new information; it is deliberately outside this no-selection theorem and must be executed rather than presumed to fix the desired coefficient.

Dimensions remain consistent: A and H both have inverse-length units with c_light=1, so A/H is dimensionless for every n. No hbar is used. Identifying A with the observed a0 additionally requires the physical metric and measuredG_N from the same completed source solution; no such identification is asserted here.
