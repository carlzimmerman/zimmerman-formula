EXACT SUPPORT BOUND
Let g(r)>0 and r^3 g(r) nondecreasing on [r_in,R]. At a natural pericentre
r_p, radial acceleration satisfies
  rddot = j^2/r_p^3 - g(r_p) >=0,
hence j^2>=r_p^3 g(r_p)>=r_in^3 g(r_in)=j_min^2>0.
Thus EVERY natural-pericentre orbit in that annulus has j>=j_min.
This applies to a continuum library as well as a finite one.

For the benchmark target g=M(r)r/(r^2+eps^2)^(3/2), M(r)>=0 and
M'(r)>=0 on this interval. The geometric factor in r^3g is
  r^4/(r^2+eps^2)^(3/2),
whose logarithmic derivative is
  (r^2+4eps^2)/[r(r^2+eps^2)]>0.
The assumption follows from the target's nonnegative spherical baryon and
cold densities, with its inner cold mass subtracted consistently.

The benchmark initial velocities have two tangential Gaussian components
of width sigma=.05. At ANY positive initial radius r,
  Pr(j<j_min | r)=1-exp[-j_min^2/(2 sigma^2 r^2)]>0.
Averaging over either IC-B or IC-C gives positive mass below j_min.
No orbit mixture in the natural-pericentre annulus can preserve that EXACT
initial marginal, regardless of grid refinement. This says nothing about
whether the missing fraction is observationally important. Coarse j bins
can hide it; thus a finite binned feasibility pass is compatible with this
exact support obstruction. The obstruction is to this restricted orbit set,
not to spherical collisionless motion with the benchmark's reflecting wall,
and not to real halos that extend below the numerical cutoff.

THE MISSING FAMILY, EXPLICITLY
For an apocentre r_a>r_in, set
  E=Phi(r_a)+j^2/(2 r_a^2),
  j_cross^2 = 2[Phi(r_a)-Phi(r_in)]/(1/r_in^2-1/r_a^2).
If 0<=j^2<j_cross^2, then radial speed at the wall is nonzero:
  v_r(r_in)^2=2[Phi(r_a)-Phi(r_in)]
                  -j^2(1/r_in^2-1/r_a^2)>0.
These orbits bounce at the wall, rather than turn naturally before it.
Under the monotone r^3g condition, the effective potential has at most one
minimum; the interval between wall and outer turning point is accessible.
Their radial period and mass residence fractions are
  T_r=2 integral_(r_in)^(r_a) dr/sqrt(2[E-Phi(r)]-j^2/r^2),
  P_k=(2/T_r) integral_(bin k intersect [r_in,r_a]) dr/|v_r|.
The change r=r_a-(r_a-r_in)u^2, u in[0,1], removes the simple outer-turning
point square-root singularity. The degenerate j=j_cross boundary needs its
own limiting treatment. The existing natural-orbit family plus this wall
family supplies the missing support. A zero-j radial wall orbit is an
immediate analytic control; the existing library cannot represent it.
