## Load-bearing checks

1. The numerator slope is `-hc*(delta+x)<0`. Thus the beta ceiling is
   necessary in the inherited positive-kinetic perpendicular sector; the
   no-go is stronger than merely failing a chosen sufficient interpolation.
   The explicit path `delta=x^2` stays strictly above the density threshold.
2. A limit with beta approaching one requires `delta/x` tending to infinity.
   This condition does not say delta must grow. For example fixed positive
   delta is sufficient as x tends to zero. The regular-core tail is admitted.
3. The new `beta_star` differs from the earlier `R/(R+1)` parent reduction.
   Its exact multiplier `1/(1+x)` both keeps strict cone margin and removes
   that extra finite-R vacuum deficit. It remains non-smooth as an electric
   vector coefficient at fixed density until the separate central blend.
4. The blend scale is proportional to eta, rather than its square root. This
   makes the endpoint margin `95*eta/256` positive while the center endpoint
   itself tends to one. Although the margin tends to zero in vacuum, the
   claim requires strict positivity only at occupied positive density.
5. Numerator affinity proves the entire blend, including its actual smooth
   cutoff values, from its two endpoints. The 36 interpolation controls
   check rational convex weights; they do not implement or approximate the
   exponential cutoff. No sampled cutoff value is used as a uniform proof.
6. The Lorentz invariant control selects the electric-parallel polarization
   with delta Y zero. In a parity-even action L_XY vanishes at Y=0; L_YY
   cannot change this polarization's `t/p` speed. Derivative interactions,
   non-parity-even mixing and additional fields are outside this control.
7. The spherical traction sign follows independently from fixed-charge
   field energy and fixed-particle material work. Positive wall tension
   raises the pressure requirement. It cannot repair a zero-pressure edge.
8. The local interface witness has nonzero pressure and density on the
   occupied side. It deliberately changes the hypothesis of the old smooth
   taper obstruction. It has no uncounted normal charge or particle flux.
9. Its logarithmic pressure is positive without a numerical logarithm:
   `log(2)=2*atanh(1/3)>2/3>5/8`. The full Gamma=6/5 material effective
   inertia is `rho+24P/5`, rather than bare rho or bare enthalpy.

## Remaining decisive work

Maxwell phase is not a solved global free-boundary equilibrium. The next
physical discriminator is simultaneous central-species, neutral-fluid, gauge
and interface force balance, followed by one constrained quadratic form that
includes the moving interface. Surface energy, gravity and any surface charge
must be included if present. Coefficient limits and separate bulk principal
