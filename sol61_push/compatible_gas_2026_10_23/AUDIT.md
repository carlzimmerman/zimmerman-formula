# Computation audit
Self-review, not independent certification. This is a finite numerical research phase, not a completion of the theory.

- The first `check` failed because an editing substitution produced np.np.sum. Its original source and failure are retained. `gas_v2.py` fixes that typo; no failed run is counted as physical evidence.
- `check_v2` passes the directional total energy identity (~5e-11 relative) and cooling asymptotes (~1e-11), but its central force finite difference fails the requested tolerance. Subtraction of large total energies loses precision in the tiny central force. `check_v3` uses the quadratic gravitational-energy difference and expm1 thermal difference and a step ladder. Relative force discrepancy decreases quadratically to8.1e-7 or better for all four faces. Both records are retained.
- The closed compressive gas exercise preserves positive thermal energy, nondecreasing shell entropy (roundoff ~2e-13), and energy to2.4e-14 or better at three timesteps. It is a finite smooth/compressive artificial-viscosity exercise, not a validated strong-shock Riemann convergence campaign.
- `check_final` verifies homogeneous pressure balance/adiabatic thermal derivative and the exact signed-coordinate parity of the inherited cold deposition/force (energy difference zero, force mismatch4.4e-16). v2 incorrectly stopped when a cold radial orbit reached the origin. v3 removes that origin guard, retaining outer-boundary and gas-crossing guards. v4 adds exact-state restart with parameter consistency checks; it otherwise keeps the v3 equations. Cold signed passage is permitted, gas shell interpenetration is not.
- `base`, `fine`, `time` end at the v2 central-orbit guard; they are accepted pre-passage states, not destruction results. `crossing_fine` and `crossing_time` resume their exact saved arrays, accumulated work and heat. `crossing_base` starts afresh with v3. They are not independent physical realizations. All cold/field/gas integrals and initialized field tangent conditions are inherited explicitly.
- Time refinement changes the z8 n128 aperture masses by only a few parts per million, but spatial refinement strongly changes the central profile. Central gas widths are far smaller than the fixed field cell. No whole-core resolution claim is justified.
- Molecular abundance is prescribed, gas initial seed is imposed, EdS is imposed. No entropy floors, imposed remnant mass, sinks, source clipping beyond the explicitly specified CMB approximation, energy redistribution or thermostat is used. Gas internal energy is evolved as its own variable; positivity failure aborts a step. The accepted state is committed only after a successful complete RK4 step.
- Molecular losses are tracked as heat. Bulk viscosity's background work is separately included in expansion work. Residual energy error is reported in absolute units and normalized units; agreement of the global budget cannot prove a local object is bound. Positive field energy, cosmic environment and surface fluxes remain relevant.

This is a predeclared refinement budget, not an extension after inspecting its result. One numerical thread per run. Inputs/outputs and exact argv are hashed by the experiment runner. Failed and timed-out records, if present, remain part of the audit.

Within the adopted CMB-floor model the molecular source is inactive in a neighborhood of the homogeneous background, leaving the previous linear result unchanged. This is an analytical conditional comparison, not a preservation certificate.
