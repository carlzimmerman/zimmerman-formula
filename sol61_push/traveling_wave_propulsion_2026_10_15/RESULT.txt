TRAVELING-WAVE PROPULSION: A CONDITIONAL DESIGN, WITH ITS FAILURE POINTS
15 October 2026. Self-review; no independent referee.

ANSWER
No established human method activates dark energy or the proposed cold medium
for useful propulsion. An ambitious coherent research concept is an externally
powered traveling electromagnetic pattern that scatters existing cold particles
or launches outgoing collective waves. Momentum leaves in that medium; the
generator recoils. This needs a new measured interaction, absent from the frozen
S2/S3 action. It is a research hypothesis, not a demonstrated engineering route.

WHY THIS DIFFERS FROM THE EXISTING CONVERTER PROGRAM
CFG793 found no useful built-in photon portal or cold-rest-mass conversion.
CFG795's material/conversion cases and ARGON06's model-specific dark-photon
audit do not prove that every imaginable momentum-transfer interaction is
impossible. Conversely, they do not establish the interaction proposed here.
We do not reuse their assumed 2.24 meV mass as a particle identification.

This route supplies energy from the craft and exports momentum. It need not
destroy cold particles or violate their conserved number. A |Psi|^2 coupling
preserves complex-field U(1); it can scatter occupied states while conserving
net charge. Pair production, when kinematically available, is a separate process.

CONCRETE CONDITIONAL INTERACTION
For the earlier canonical complex scalar candidate, add in natural units
 L_portal = - g2 |Psi|^2 F_mu_nu F^mu_nu / 4.
g2 has mass dimension -2. This is a proposed operator, not a consequence of
the Zimmerman static gravity law, and not a measured constant. It locally
changes scalar mass squared by delta(m^2)=g2 F^2/4. In the dilute positive-
frequency, nonrelativistic limit, the particle potential is U=delta(m^2)/(2m).
Signs and normalization here use the canonical -|partial Psi|^2-m^2|Psi|^2
convention. Large changes, instability and EFT breakdown require a full model.

Human-accessible control would be two coherent counterpropagating RF fields
or an array of driven electric/magnetic near fields. After carrier averaging,
their invariant can contain a component U=U0 cos(kx-omega t).
For two collinear opposing vacuum beams with common linear polarization,
 k=k1+k2 and omega=omega1-omega2 for the slow component.
Roughly 150 MHz carriers give a roughly 1 m spatial period; an 84 Hz difference
would move that pattern at 84 m/s. This illustrates controllable source geometry,
NOT an experimentally selected dark resonance. Two feeds/open interaction
geometry are implied; no assertion that one cavity supports arbitrary modes.

A single ideal vacuum plane EM wave has F^2=0. Thus simply increasing the
brightness of one beam does not implement this scalar portal. Cross fields,
counterpropagating fields or appropriate near fields are essential. Scalar
couplings and field-overlap experiments have prior literature; this is not a
newly discovered operator. Existing constraints must be applied to a specified
mass, abundance, sign, coupling and screening model. No allowed useful window
has been established here.

WHAT EXCITATION MEANS
For scattering a free nonrelativistic particle of initial velocity v,
 omega = k.v + hbar |k|^2/(2m).
For a collective mode, the drive must instead overlap its dispersion and
the occupied distribution's Doppler shifts. A resonance does not supply a
coupling, unlimited bandwidth or unlimited amplitude. The prior frozen static
action does not determine these dynamics.

A directed traveling pattern selects a momentum direction. The apparatus
must include its generators and supports in the force budget. Momentum must
actually leave into an outgoing field, wake or scattered particles. Merely
oscillating an internal field and recovering it each cycle gives no continuing
net thrust to an isolated complete system.

POWER / FORCE IDENTITY AND ITS PRECISE LIMITS
For H_int=integral n U dV, force on medium Fx=-integral n partial_x U dV,
and source work P=integral n partial_t U dV. Since partial_t U=-(omega/k)
partial_x U, P=(omega/k) Fx for the monochromatic traveling component.
This identifies the direct source force and source work, not merely an
unexamined quasiparticle momentum. Finite source envelopes add edge terms;
stored energy, transients and bidirectional emission must be separately counted.
In steady directed radiation, |F_craft|=P_out/v_phase. Both are in the medium
rest frame. In a moving frame frequency and mechanical work change together;
one cannot transfer a rest-frame efficiency unchanged to a moving spacecraft.

For input power P_in, directed coupling eta, and directionality factor D<=1,
 |F|=D eta P_in/v_phase in this ideal limit.
At v_phase=84.09 m/s, 1 N needs 84.09 W of outgoing wave power. This is NOT
84 W electrical input, and does not establish that a device can launch it.
Beating ideal directed photon thrust requires D eta>v_phase/c=2.805e-7,
plus actual wave momentum capacity and an accessible source solution.

Producing ordinary free massive particles from vacuum instead gives E/p>=c
including their rest energy, so cannot beat photon thrust per supplied energy
by merely making massive 'dark exhaust'. Slow collective modes require an
occupied medium; accelerating existing particles has a different energy budget.
A Lorentz-invariant cosmological-constant vacuum provides no material rest
frame or ordinary wake. No vacuum-energy extraction or warp mechanism follows.

THE DILUTENESS CHECK THAT PREVENTS A FALSE BREAKTHROUGH
Conditional illustration only: homogeneous stationary positive mass medium,
 rho=0.3 GeV/c^2/cm^3=5.348e-22 kg/m^3,
 Lstar=5.7543993733715695 pc,
 dynamic lift L=dot(P)^2/(2rho)-gamma |grad P|^2/2,
 gamma=2piG Lstar^2, cP=sqrt(gamma rho)=84.0889 m/s.
This is a specified gapless longitudinal linear benchmark, not a measured
fluid or a full local completion of S2. Background flow, kinetic dispersion,
restoring gaps and nonlinearities can invalidate its use for a real device.

For a plane wave with fractional density amplitude delta=k P0/rho,
 I= rho cP^3 delta^2/2; momentum flux=I/cP.
For delta=0.1 and effective wave area=100 m^2:
 I=1.5899e-18 W/m^2,
 force=1.8908e-18 N,
 outgoing power=1.5899e-16 W.
Keeping that amplitude, 1 N would require effective outgoing wave area
5.2889e19 m^2, equivalent to a disk radius 4.103e9 m. A 1 nN target needs
radius 129.75 km. These are flux-carrying areas in this model, not a universal
physical-antenna-size theorem. Focusing, nonlinear states and other modes
require separate solutions, and do not inherit these numbers as proven bounds.

The earlier static meter-scale suppression estimate is 3.17e-35 at this Lstar.
It obstructs an ordinary static gravity actuator. Do not apply that static
factor as a proven bound on every resonantly forced dynamic device; that
requires the source, losses and dynamics. No such successful device is solved.

NEXT PHYSICAL DISCRIMINATOR
Before thrust engineering, measure whether a driven pattern creates a response
that propagates to a separate receiver with reproducible dispersion and coupling.
Vary k and omega independently; reversing k should reverse measured momentum.
Electromagnetic leakage itself can produce directional force and receiver
signals: shielding, direct RF accounting and mechanical isolation are essential
to interpret such a result. No anomaly or sensitivity forecast is claimed here.

The missing quantities are a real dark coupling, a stable accessible mode,
momentum capacity and a complete reciprocal generator/medium solution.
Our numerical benchmark currently supplies an obstacle, not a propulsion result.
