ORBIT MIXING: A DISTINCT CLOCK, NOT A COMPLETE CAPTURE MECHANISM
2026-10-11. Analytic research checkpoint; no new numerical experiment.
Framework unchanged: repaired kernel supplied; kappa=1/2 fitted; cold mass
real; both a0 footings separate. No fitted damping coefficient introduced.
Status: exact conditional derivation, self-reviewed; framework application open.

1. THE EXECUTED ANALYTIC STEP
Assume a stationary, integrable spherical Hamiltonian H(J). Actions J have
conjugate angles theta and frequencies Omega(J)=dH/dJ. Ignore the perturbed
self-gravity in this first diagnostic (the fixed-background approximation).
The collisionless equation is
  partial_t f + Omega(J).partial_theta f = 0.
Its exact solution for any Fourier mode n is
  delta f_n(J,t) = delta f_n(J,0) exp[-i n.Omega(J)t].
The angle average f_0(J) is invariant. A linear observable has response
  A_n(t) = integral dJ W_n(J) delta f_n(J,0) exp[-i n.Omega(J)t].
Thus its decay, if any, follows from frequency spread AND the initial
perturbation/observable. A finite orbit collection can recur; decay is not
assured. No universal exponential or universal alpha follows from H alone.

For the explicitly narrower case where normalized mode weights form a
nonnegative probability distribution p(omega), define
  C(t)=integral p(omega) exp(-i omega t) d omega.
With finite second moment and mean mu, variance sigma_omega^2,
  C(t)=1-i mu t-<omega^2>t^2/2+o(t^2),
  |C(t)|^2=1-sigma_omega^2 t^2+o(t^2).
The initial loss of coherence is quadratic. Exact exp(-gamma t) in modulus
from t=0 with gamma>0 is incompatible with this class. A Gaussian frequency
distribution gives |C|=exp(-sigma_omega^2 t^2/2), so the initial coherence
scale 1/sigma_omega is computable once weights and frequencies are fixed.
This is a scale, not a proved halo settling time or a selected rate constant.

Self-review/countercontrols (analytic, not numerical test counts):
- A single frequency gives sigma_omega=0 and NO decay in |C|.
- Two equally weighted frequencies mu +/- Delta give
  C=exp(-i mu t) cos(Delta t): initial decay followed by exact recurrence.
- Signed/complex observable weights are not probabilities; the variance
  argument above must not be silently applied to them.
- Infinite-variance spectra evade the second-order expansion. Effective
  late-time exponential damping is not excluded by a short-time theorem.

2. WHAT THIS CAN AND CANNOT DO FOR COLD ENERGY
For stationary H, each orbit's H and J are constant. Phase mixing can erase
visible coherent structure without changing action populations or the total
energy integral integral H f dJ dtheta. Angle-averaged fine-grained populations
are not driven toward the maximum-entropy target by this transport operator.
In particular, E>0 unbound orbits in an isolated potential with Phi(infinity)=0
cannot become E<0 bound orbits by stationary phase mixing. Nor can this
operator repair an incorrect distribution over actions. Smoothing a density
movie is therefore insufficient evidence for capture, target selection, or
energy transfer to dark energy.

This suggests a hybrid decomposition worth testing: reversible orbital mixing
may supply some apparent relaxation, while a separately derived exchange
changes actions/energy when required. It does NOT prove that the exchange is
small or remove the framework's receiver requirement. For self-consistent or
time-dependent fields, individual actions/energies can change, and the full
field-plus-matter energy ledger must be used. Fixed-background arguments do
not forbid violent relaxation or collective damping.

3. THREE DISTINCT CONTINUATIONS AND FIRST GATES
A. ORBIT SPECTRUM (closest cheap diagnostic).
CFG620 Library already exposes Tr, E, L, phase-volume weights and bin residence
fractions. For a declared radial-angle perturbation, omega_n=2*pi*n/Tr.
Use its actual target occupation weights, NOT uniform orbit-cell weights.
Compute the predicted response separately for each system and a0 footing.
A phase-space evolution with BGK and dissipative drift disabled must reproduce
that response before any decay is interpreted as physical. One-frequency
control must not damp; phase reversal must recover coherence in the
fixed-field diagnostic. This tests mixing, not capture or full self-gravity.
No benchmark run launched here: production jobs remain separately owned.

B. COLLECTIVE RESPONSE (harder, genuinely additional physics).
Linearize Vlasov-Poisson about the repaired-kernel-supported f0(J), retaining
perturbed self-gravity. Locate growing modes and compute the initial-value
response, including the continuum. This can reject an equilibrium that merely
passes a static Jeans check, or produce a mode-dependent damping prediction.
Do not insert a desired pole or identify 1/t_dyn with a derived decay rate.
First unresolved leaf: a justified continuum f0 and consistent gravitational
response operator; a finite maximum-entropy orbit fit is not this proof.

C. CAPTURE/EXCHANGE (still essential).
Track initially unbound versus bound material and actual boundary fluxes.
For a time-independent isolated potential, crossing from E_i>0 to E_f<0
requires energy removal Delta E=E_i-E_f>0 for that material. Identify where
that energy goes; collective redistribution into escaping material differs
from transfer into a vacuum receiver. A measurable decrease in a coarse
observable alone establishes neither. Full self-gravitating energies must
include field energy and avoid double-counting pair potential energy.
First gate: one conservative energy/mass ledger through a capture event,
followed by a regular receiver action if a vacuum-transfer channel is claimed.

4. RELATION TO PRIOR WORK AND LITERATURE
This does not claim discovery of phase mixing. Repository search found old
CFG5 phase-mixed-collapse discussions and CFG70 memory-exchange constraints;
those are not being relabeled as new results. The additional proposed test is
to extract a perturbation-specific response from the existing CFG620 orbit
library, and to prevent phase smoothing from being counted as capture.
The analytic proof above is self-contained. External background consulted:
Jacob Bedrossian, arXiv:2211.13707v1 (2022), abstract verified 2026-10-11,
https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.13707v1 . Its advertised Vlasov-Poisson damping
results concern specified phase-space settings, principally a spatial torus;
no theorem from it is asserted to cover these inhomogeneous finite halos.
No exhaustive novelty search, data exclusion, derived alpha, or theory closure.

DECISION
Keep the repaired empirical target. Test whether orbit dynamics supplies a
predictive part of the relaxation before adding a new dissipative interaction.
If it only mixes phases while leaving incorrect action populations intact,
record that failure and move to the explicit capture/receiver problem.
