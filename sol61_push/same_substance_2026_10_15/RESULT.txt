1. ONE ACTION, BOTH STATES
Use hbar=c=1, signature (-+++), and the reduced Planck mass Mpl:
 S=int sqrt(-g)[Mpl^2 R/2 - g^{mu nu}d_mu Psi* d_nu Psi
                -rho_v-m^2|Psi|^2-lambda|Psi|^4] + S_b[g].
Here rho_v>0, m>0, lambda>=0. Psi is a proposed complex scalar, two real
canonical degrees of freedom; baryons couple minimally to the same metric.
No extra phenomenological MOND force or S2 action has been inserted.
In polar coordinates Psi=r exp(-i theta)/sqrt(2),
 V(r)=rho_v+m^2 r^2/2+lambda r^4/4.

The vacuum Psi=0 is an exact field solution with
 T_mu_nu=-rho_v g_mu_nu, rho=rho_v, p=-rho_v.
It is a stable potential minimum; both real perturbations have mass m.
This field need not roll slowly to maintain the constant vacuum term.
Consequently m does NOT need to be <=H. The slow-roll mass argument in
CFG635 is not a no-go theorem for this constant-minimum construction.
CFG288 already contains its lambda=0 version; that earlier partial result
is acknowledged rather than being rediscovered as a new theory.

The exact homogeneous equations in flat FLRW are
 Q=a^3 r^2 theta_dot=constant,
 r_ddot+3H r_dot-r theta_dot^2+m^2 r+lambda r^3=0,
 rho=r_dot^2/2+r^2 theta_dot^2/2+V,
 p=r_dot^2/2+r^2 theta_dot^2/2-V,
 3 Mpl^2 H^2=rho (when this field is the sole simulated source).
The Einstein and scalar equations conserve the total stress. Different
vacuum offsets change H and are NOT claimed to give identical cosmologies.

2. THE COLD STATE IS THE OCCUPIED FIELD
In Minkowski space there is an exact uniform rotating state r=constant,
 theta_dot=mu, mu^2=m^2+lambda r^2.
Its positive charge density is n=mu r^2. Subtracting the vacuum contribution,
 rho_c=m^2 r^2+3lambda r^4/4,
 p_c=lambda r^4/4,
 rho_c+p_c=mu n.
For lambda r^2<<m^2 this is massive low-pressure matter, rho_c~mn.
In slowly expanding space, charge conservation gives n=Q/a^3. Adiabatic
radial following therefore gives rho_c~mQ/a^3, with expansion and interaction
corrections. The approximation requires slow background evolution compared
with the radial gap; it is not asserted at arbitrary early times or density.
Keeping the full Cartesian wave field avoids declaring a phase-only fluid
valid at nodes or after multistreaming. No nonlinear halo theorem follows.

At fixed geometry the energy above the vacuum is bounded below by m|Q|:
complete |Psi_dot +/- i m Psi|^2 in the Hamiltonian, retain positive
|grad Psi|^2 and lambda|Psi|^4, and choose the sign matching Q.
For infinite homogeneous backgrounds apply this to a finite periodic volume.
With gravity this is not an ADM energy or self-gravitating positivity theorem.

3. BOTH FINITE-DENSITY MODES ARE HEALTHY IN THIS LOCAL COMPARISON
Expand the exact rotating Minkowski state. With z=omega^2, k the spatial
wavenumber, the coupled radial/phase determinant is
 (z-k^2-2lambda r^2)(z-k^2)-4mu^2 z=0.
Writing D=2m^2+3lambda r^2>0 gives
 omega_+/-^2=k^2+D +/- sqrt(D^2+4mu^2 k^2).
The smaller root is nonnegative because
 (k^2+D)^2-[D^2+4mu^2 k^2]=k^2(k^2+2lambda r^2)>=0.
The Cartesian kinetic form is positive and its principal characteristics
are the metric light cone. These statements concern local matter modes on
fixed flat geometry, not the gravity-coupled Jeans problem or all backgrounds.
The gapless mode has
 cs^2=dp_c/d rho_c=lambda r^2/(2m^2+3lambda r^2),
with 0<=cs^2<1/3. For lambda=0 the leading gapless dispersion is quadratic,
not relativistic sound: omega_-^2~k^4/(4m^2).

4. A NONTRIVIAL FAILED ATTEMPT TO TIE THE TWO STATES
Try V=rho_v(1+r^2/f^2)^2, with f>0. This fixes
 m^2=4rho_v/f^2, lambda=4rho_v/f^4,
 beta=lambda rho_v/m^4=1/4.
Let u=lambda r^2/m^2 and q=rho_c/rho_v. Then
 q=(u+3u^2/4)/beta, cs^2=u/(2+3u).
For beta=1/4, q=4u+3u^2. Changing f cannot change cs at fixed q.
Illustrative q=.4,1,100000 gives cs^2=.0409807,.0813570,.3321162.
These q values are test inputs, not a new observational likelihood.
The dense state approaches radiation-like pressure, not cold matter.

For a general independent quartic, imposing cs^2<=epsilon<1/3 at density
ratio q>0 is equivalent to
 beta <= (2epsilon-3epsilon^2)/[q(1-3epsilon)^2].
For the illustrative coldness gate epsilon=1e-6 at q=1e5, beta must be
<=2.000009e-11. The linked square potential has beta=.25, so fails that gate
by over ten orders. This is a necessary local EOS bound in the rotating-state
class, not an empirical limit on all dark matter and not a cosmological test.
The lambda=0 free-field endpoint evades this pressure obstruction but leaves
the vacuum height and particle mass independent. Other potential shapes may
evade it and are not excluded by this calculation.

5. WHAT STILL PREVENTS IDENTIFICATION WITH THE ZIMMERMAN MEDIUM
The canonical scalar field equation is
 Box Psi-m^2 Psi-2lambda|Psi|^2 Psi=0 in this signature.
There is no baryonic scalar monopole source: baryons affect it through the
metric. Psi=0 remains an exact solution even in a baryon-generated metric.
Therefore the model cannot force a nonzero cold halo from an exactly empty
classical field merely because baryons exist. Gravitational collapse of
pre-existing excitations is possible, but requires initial mass/charge.
This is a counterexample to automatic halo manufacture, not a prohibition
of quantum gravitational particle production or a prepared cold population.

The nonrelativistic occupied field obeys a Schrödinger-type equation with
self-gravity and contact repulsion, not the S2 constitutive pole. It has no
derived universal g_b=g^2/(g+a0), halo abundance, source-phase switch, or
finite-stiffness matching. Importing the successful S2 static formulas would
be joining different actions without a derivation.

rho_v is a potential HEIGHT; m^2 is its CURVATURE at the minimum. They are
independent in this action. Consequently rho_v^(1/4) is an energy-density
scale, not a derived particle mass, gap, or photon line. The 2.24 meV/542 GHz
identification requires extra assumptions about a species and photon coupling.
The two states are consistent in a specified model; their actual existence
and the required relation a0=(c/2)sqrt(G rho_DE,mass) remain unestablished.
