RESULT
The minimal unit-vector sigma-model lift of the parent scalar action is
linearly unstable on every nonzero uniform static scalar-gradient background
when A>S>0. The parent eta=9/8 construction satisfies this inequality.
This is a failure of this dynamical-frame completion, not a contradiction of
the fixed-frame calculation and not a no-go for all gravitational theories.

1. Declared action, background, and exact perturbation sector
Use fixed Minkowski metric (-+++), c=1, u.u=-1 enforced exactly, and
 z=u^a partial_a chi, X=partial_a chi partial^a chi, s=sqrt(X+z^2).
 L_chi=A z^2/2-E(s),
 L_u=-B/2 partial_a u_b partial^a u^b, B>0.
Here A=K_N eta is constant for the parent construction. E is its spatial
energy. No metric fluctuations or matter sources are included in this test.
The background u=(1,0,0,0), chi=v z_coordinate, v>0 is an exact solution
of this flat-space source-free system. Its infinite total energy is not
claimed to describe an isolated galaxy; it defines a uniform-background
linear stability test. The vector background equation vanishes because
u.grad chi=0. The normalization multiplier can be zero on this background.

Take chi=v z_coordinate+pi(t,x), u=(sqrt(1+b(t,x)^2),0,0,b(t,x)).
Thus wave vector is perpendicular to background gradient and b is a physical
transverse vector polarization, not a normalization or gauge mode.
Set S=E'(v)/v=K_N mu(v/v0)>0 and d=A-S.
Expanding to quadratic order, including both scalar and vector variations:
 L2= A pi_dot^2/2 - S pi_x^2/2
     + B(b_dot^2-b_x^2)/2 + d v b pi_dot + d v^2 b^2/2.
The vector free kinetic term is positive. Nevertheless its induced tilt
potential has the wrong sign for d>0. One must retain the first-derivative
mixing d v b pi_dot rather than conclude instability from that sign alone.

2. Coupled mode calculation
For exp(-i omega t+i k x), write w=omega^2. The determinant is
 D(w,k)=(A w-S k^2)(B w-B k^2+d v^2)-d^2 v^2 w.
At k=0 it factors exactly:
 D(w,0)=w[AB w+d S v^2].
For A>S>0, B,v>0 there is a negative squared frequency
 w_grow= - (A-S) S v^2/(AB) < 0,
 gamma=sqrt((A-S) S v^2/(AB)).
The zero root is not the growing mode. The negative root corresponds to
an exponentially growing solution, not an imaginary propagation speed in
the high-frequency principal symbol.

The instability is not restricted to a homogeneous mode:
 D(0,k)=S k^2[B k^2-d v^2] <0 for 0<k^2<d v^2/B.
The leading coefficient in w is AB>0. A real quadratic with negative
constant/leading-coefficient ratio has two real roots of opposite signs
(the discriminant is positive). Thus this entire open band contains growth.

Independent Hamiltonian check at k=0: P=A pi_dot+d v b, Q=B b_dot gives
 H2=(P-d v b)^2/(2A)+Q^2/(2B)-d v^2 b^2/2.
On the conserved P=0 subspace, H2=Q^2/(2B)-d S v^2 b^2/(2A).
The same negative oscillator potential and growth rate follow. This avoids
mistaking derivative mixing for a stabilizing effect.

Exact witness, in units K_N=B=v=1, v0=4/3:
 mu(3/4)=3/5, A=9/8, S=3/5, d=21/40.
 At k=0: D=9 w(25 w+7)/200; growing root w=-7/25.
 At k=1/2: D=3(4w-1)(75w+11)/800; growing root w=-11/75.
All numbers here are exact rational values, not numerical searches.

3. Structural conflict, not a special eta accident
For the parent standard interpolation,
 F=E''(v)/K_N=t(t^2+2)/(1+t^2)^(3/2),
 mu=t/sqrt(1+t^2), F-mu=t/(1+t^2)^(3/2)>0.
Requiring the fixed-frame radial scalar speed to be <=1 demands
 A>=K_N F>K_N mu=S, precisely the tilt-unstable regime of this lift.
Setting A=S instead removes the induced tilt terms but gives radial
speed squared F/mu=(t^2+2)/(1+t^2)>1. A<S still cannot meet the radial
speed condition. Merely retuning eta therefore cannot fix this combination.
Allowing A=A(s) alone does not change this quadratic conclusion: the
background z=0 makes derivatives of A multiply terms of at least third
order, so the same local A and S control this sector.
This conclusion is limited to the specified derivative-free scalar/vector
coupling and sigma-model frame dynamics. It is not a general aether theorem.

4. Tested escapes and remaining routes
(a) Increase B: gamma and k_critical scale as B^(-1/2). This delays growth
and pushes it to longer wavelengths, but every finite B retains an unstable
band on the infinite uniform background. B=infinity freezes the frame and
returns the parent's restricted model. No numerical galaxy timescale follows
without physical B, v, boundary conditions and a background solution.
(b) Add an external tilt pinning term -m b^2/2: replacing d v^2 by
 d v^2-m in the second determinant factor shows what is required.
For m>d v^2 the quadratic Hamiltonian is positive definite for nonzero k
in this two-field sector: its scalar momentum square and both spatial/pinning
potentials are positive. This is a diagnostic repair, not a dynamical-frame
completion: a preferred reference for the pinning has been reintroduced.
(c) Finite-domain spectral gap: if allowed modes have k^2>d v^2/B, the
unpinned Hamiltonian is positive in this sector. This cannot be assumed for
a galaxy or cosmology; their actual background and boundary spectrum are
uncomputed. It is a conditional escape, not a universal stability claim.
(d) Metric constraints, derivative scalar-vector couplings, or a constrained
clock in place of an independent vector change the calculation. They remain
open routes, not excluded by this result. In particular the transverse
polarization used here is not an independent mode in a hypersurface-
orthogonal clock theory; this argument must not be copied into that theory.
