CLOCK FOLLOW-UP DF-002 — 2026-10-15
Parent DF-001; same action and background, now impose the clock representation
before variation: u_a=-partial_a T/sqrt(-partial T.partial T), T=t+tau.
This is a distinct, hypersurface-orthogonal theory, not merely a gauge choice.
Original coefficient-selection objective remains unresolved.

RESULT
Removing the independent transverse vector does not remove the instability
in this particular sigma-model lift. Nonzero wave vectors with a component
along the scalar background gradient have a finite unstable band.

Derivation
To first order u^i=-partial_i tau; the exact normalization gives u^0=1+
|grad tau|^2/2+O(perturbation^3). No linear tau_dot term remains in u^0.
Since the background scalar has zero time derivative, higher-order corrections
to u do not affect the quadratic scalar/vector mixing below.
For chi=v z_coordinate+pi, let A=K_N eta, S=K_N mu, R=K_N F, d=A-S.
Substitution in the quadratic action, with all scalar gradient directions,
gives
 L2=A pi_dot^2/2-[S |grad_perp pi|^2+R pi_z^2]/2
   +B[|grad tau_dot|^2-sum_ij(tau_ij)^2]/2
   -d v tau_z pi_dot +d v^2 tau_z^2/2.
These statements are restricted to linear perturbations about T=t; they do
not count nonlinear higher-derivative degrees of freedom of the clock action.

For k>0, define c=k_z/k and C=S(1-c^2)+R c^2>0. The determinant, divided
by the nonzero overall k^2 factor, is
 D_clock=(A w-C k^2)(B w-B k^2+d v^2 c^2)-d^2 v^2 c^2 w,
 w=omega^2.
Its constant term is C k^2(B k^2-d v^2 c^2). Therefore for A>S>0 and c!=0,
 0<k^2<d v^2 c^2/B
 gives one negative real w root. At exactly c=0, the mixing and induced
negative mass vanish and the two displayed modes decouple. For c!=0,
the nonzero root tends to -d S v^2 c^2/(AB) as k tends to zero, but the
strictly k=0 clock mode is degenerate and must not be inferred by dividing
its determinant by k^2. The finite band, not the k=0 limit, proves growth.

Exact witness
K_N=B=v=1, v0=4/3, A=9/8, S=3/5, R=123/125, k=1/2, c=1.
The exact polynomial printed in run/stdout.txt has negative constant term
and positive leading coefficient. This is an explicit finite-wavelength
counterexample to stability of this clock completion.

Self-review
Passed: clock unit constraint expansion; substitution into quadratic action;
Fourier symbol including mixed time/space term; nonzero k restriction;
negative constant term and positive leading coefficient imply opposite-sign
real roots. Six exact symbolic checks corroborate determinant reduction,
limits, band, perpendicular decoupling, and the rational witness. This is
not a full formal Lean derivation; the sibling TiltBound.lean formalizes

Decision
DF-001's proposed clock escape has now been tested and fails for this same
minimal sigma-model action. This does not exclude other clock operators,
metric constraints, or actual finite/inhomogeneous backgrounds. A useful
next test requires changing the action or solving a finite-background
spectral problem, not simply relabeling the preferred vector as a clock.
Larger B can delay growth but no physical value or timescale is selected.
Neither this calculation nor its parent links a0 to the arbitrary E0.
