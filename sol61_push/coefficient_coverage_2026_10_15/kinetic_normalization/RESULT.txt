KINETIC NORMALIZATION ESCAPE — 2026-10-15
Status: explicit fixed-frame scalar construction; not a gravity completion.

Research decision
The constant high-acceleration force offset found in the relaxing-auxiliary
model is avoidable if time kinetic normalization is allowed to exceed the
asymptotic Newtonian spatial normalization. This respects the old conditional
no-go theorem: it changes an assumption, rather than disproving that theorem.
The interpolation used here is known, and no global novelty is claimed.

1. Explicit action and conventions
Fix flat spacetime and a preferred inertial frame, reference light speed c=1.
Let K_N,v0,q,M>0, eta=9/8, v=|grad chi|, t=v/v0, and
 L = K_N eta (dot chi)^2/2 - E(v) - q rho chi/M - E0,
 E(v) = K_N v0^2 [t sqrt(1+t^2) - asinh(t)]/2.
The matter source is prescribed; the frame has no dynamical degrees of freedom.
The scalar models the entire nonrelativistic force potential q chi/M; it is
not an extra force added on top of a separate Newtonian field.

E'(v)=J(v)=K_N v mu(t), mu(t)=t/sqrt(1+t^2).
Field equation: K_N eta ddot chi - div[ K_N mu grad chi ] = -q rho/M.
Static equation: div[ K_N mu grad chi ] = q rho/M.
E(0)=0, E' >=0. The source-free Hamiltonian density, after subtracting E0,
is K_N eta (dot chi)^2/2+E(v)>=0. This does not assert positivity for the
source-coupled system or prove nonlinear global regularity.

2. Exact local scalar characteristic bound
For a background with v>0, the spatial Hessian of E has eigenvalues
 K_N mu(t) (twice), K_N F(t),
 F(t)=t(t^2+2)/(1+t^2)^(3/2).
The time Hessian is K_N eta, with no mixed time-space block. Thus the frozen
principal symbol has squared speeds mu/eta and F/eta, including on
nonstationary scalar backgrounds. The frame and metric remain fixed.
F'(t)=(2-t^2)/(1+t^2)^(5/2), hence
 max F = F(sqrt(2)) = 4 sqrt(6)/9 = sqrt(32/27).
It suffices to choose eta >= sqrt(32/27) for non-superluminal propagation;
strict inequality gives strict subluminality at every v>0.
The minimum increase over equal normalization is approximately 8.866%.
Our convenient eta=9/8 is a freely chosen parameter, not selected physics.

Independent exact algebra check with x=t^2:
 32(1+x)^3 - 27x(x+2)^2 = (x-2)^2(5x+8) >= 0 for x>=0.
Also 32/27 < (9/8)^2. Positivity of mu,F for t>0 turns these squared
bounds into the speed bounds. Lean proves the polynomial and rational
inequalities; the action differentiation and principal-symbol interpretation
are documented analytical reductions, not fully formalized in Lean.

At v=0 the spatial quadratic form vanishes. Time kinetic remains positive,
but strict spatial hyperbolicity is lost: this is not a uniform stability
proof through zero-gradient regions. At high gradient both squared speeds
tend to 8/9, not 1. This slower scalar cone is the explicit tradeoff.

3. Force law and exact high-acceleration recovery
Outside a positive spherical mass M_b, the integrated flux is
 r^2 J = q M_b/(4 pi M).
Define g=qv/M, a0=qv0/M, G_N=q^2/(4 pi K_N M^2), g_N=G_N M_b/r^2.
Then g mu(g/a0)=g_N, with unique positive solution
 g^2 = [g_N^2 + sqrt(g_N^4+4g_N^2 a0^2)]/2.
Deep acceleration: g/sqrt(a0 g_N) -> 1 as g_N/a0 -> 0.
High acceleration: g-g_N = a0^2/(2g_N)+O(a0^4/g_N^3) -> 0.
Thus the previous constant a0/3 offset is absent in this model. This is a
spherical isolated-source result, not a general algebraic solution for disks
or an external-field calculation.

The old obstruction used D=K_N v-J and J'<=K_N, implying D'>=0.
Here the characteristic condition is only J'<=eta K_N. Indeed F>1 in
part of the transition, so D can decrease to zero while scalar waves remain
slower than the reference light speed. The deficit eta K_N v-J remains
nondecreasing but is not the deficit relative to the measured Newtonian law.
This distinction between the two coefficients is the mechanism.

4. Relation to existing physics and limits
The standard MOND mu=x/sqrt(1+x^2) is explicitly given in Zhao & Famaey,
Refining MOND interpolating function and TeVeS Lagrangian, arXiv
astro-ph/0512425v3, Eq. (6), https://arxiv.org/html/astro-ph/0512425.
Their Eq. (2) and discussion of Eq. (4) distinguish total force from an
additional TeVeS scalar force. We cannot automatically insert this scalar
as an additive TeVeS component: that changes the force relation and can
produce a multivalued scalar interpolation. No current observational
assessment is inferred from that historical paper.

The work here establishes a construction and an exact coefficient bound,
not a new MOND interpolation or an observationally viable theory. The
preferred frame is imposed. Lensing, matter dynamics, tensor modes,
cosmological equations, radiative stability, external-field constraints,
and the dynamics that generate a preferred frame are not supplied.
E0 is arbitrary and drops out of the scalar equation. a0 is also an input.
Consequently this does not select Lambda/a0^2=32 pi, or 3/128. The 32 in
32/27 is a property of this chosen interpolation's maximum and provides
no connection to the target vacuum coefficient.
