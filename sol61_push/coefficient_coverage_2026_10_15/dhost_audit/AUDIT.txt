1. The failed implication
The historical script defines its target as deltaPhi=0 with deltaPsi nonzero: ordinary matter should retain its Newtonian potential while light responds differently. Its Section2 then replaces this with G_Phi=0, so that matter does not source the TOTAL temporal potential at all. This is not the stated target. For the same source and boundary reference, deltaPhi=Phi-Phi_N=0 means Phi=Phi_N and G_Phi=G_N, not zero.
The relation Psi=(1+alpha_H)Phi at alpha_T=0 implies
 deltaPsi=alpha_H Phi_N when deltaPhi=0.
Thus the algebra used in the old proof does not by itself exclude a nonzero spatial-potential modification with unchanged temporal potential. If delta was intended as a cosmological perturbation rather than a change from Newtonian gravity, the no-go tests a different target: zero temporal gravity, not Newtonian temporal gravity.

3. Exact coefficient counterexample to the algebraic inference
Set beta1=b, alpha_L=alpha_T=0, alpha_H=-2b,
 beta2=-6b^2, beta3=-2b[2(1-2b)+b]=-4b+6b^2.
The static Poisson bracket is
 A=(1+alpha_H)^2-beta3/2=(1-b)^2.
For b!=1, choose the positive tensor coefficient M^2=M_ref^2/(1-b)^2.
Then M^2 A=M_ref^2, preserving the static Poisson normalization.
Choose the Poisson regime with no retained mass term and common isolated-source boundary conditions. At the level of these reduced coefficient equations,
 Phi=Phi_N, Psi=(1-2b)Phi_N.
For the explicit rational witness b=-1/4:
 alpha_H=1/2, beta2=-3/8, beta3=11/8, M^2=(16/25)M_ref^2,
 deltaPhi=0, deltaPsi=Phi_N/2.
This satisfies the displayed static, degeneracy, and nondecay COEFFICIENT relations. It is an algebraic countermodel to the inference, not a constructed action and background satisfying all field equations. Changing M^2 is allowed in this diagnostic family; no claim that the bare tensor coefficient is independently measured and fixed to M_ref^2 is made. With that extra restriction the result must be reassessed.

4. Why this is not a recovered physical solution
Degeneracy removes an extra mode; it does not ensure positive kinetic and gradient coefficients of all remaining modes. No scalar stability, background admission, covariant coefficient realization, or source matching has been supplied for the rational witness. Nonzero metric slip is not by itself Cassini-safe: light and matter are both tested. The historical conflation of pure-slip and Cassini-safe should not be retained.
For constant coefficients a linear static response scales as source mass M/r^2. The desired deep-MOND force scales as sqrt(M)/r. Therefore this witness cannot reproduce the universal deep-MOND force or its lensing counterpart. A nonlinear, background-dependent source solution is essential. This scaling obstruction is to the fixed-coefficient linear construction, not every nonlinear DHOST theory.
Neither beta1 nor the amplitude cp in the channel-count argument is selected. Repairing this proof gap does not derive kappa=1/2 or Lambda/a0^2=32pi.
