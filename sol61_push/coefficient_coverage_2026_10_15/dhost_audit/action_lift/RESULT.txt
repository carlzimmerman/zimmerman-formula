1. Specify the action before matching any force
Let physical matter couple minimally to g, signature -+++, and X=g^(mu nu)phi_mu phi_nu<0. Define
 C(X)=(-X/q^2)^b, gtilde=C(X)g, Y=gtilde^(mu nu)phi_mu phi_nu=X/C(X).
Take q>0, b!=1 and a positive constant M2. Start with
 S=integral sqrt(-gtilde)[M2 Rtilde/2+P(Y)]+S_m[g,matter],
 P(Y)=-V0+A(Y+q^2)^2/2, A,V0>0.
This is a constructed diagnostic model, not a microscopic selection or novel theory claim. Matter is NOT assumed minimally coupled to gtilde. On X<0 the conformal factor is positive; d log(-Y)/d log(-X)=1-b, so the map is locally invertible when b!=1.

Up to a boundary term the gravitational/scalar action in physical variables is
 integral sqrt(-g)[M2 C R/2 + 3M2 (partial C)^2/(4C) + C^2 P(X/C)].
Here (partial C)^2 uses g; C contains derivatives of phi. The derivative term is not an independent ordinary scalar kinetic term whose sign may be read in isolation. This is an invertible derivative-dependent conformal image of the seed gravity/scalar system in vacuum. Its degeneracy does not prove that the remaining modes have positive gradient energy, or prove health with physical matter included.
Primary class reference: Creminelli et al., arXiv:1809.03484v2, Section5 Eq72, describes the derivative-dependent conformal class surviving its stated wave-speed/decay analysis. The present P and C are explicit choices, not a result selected by that paper.

2. An actual vacuum with a nonzero clock
In gtilde FLRW choose phi=q ttilde. At Y=-q^2, P_Y=0, P=-V0 and rho=2Y P_Y-P=V0. The scalar current vanishes and Einstein equations admit exact de Sitter with 3M2 Htilde^2=V0. At this branch X=Y=-q^2 and C=1, so the physical background metric equals gtilde.
On fixed local gtilde, phi=q t+pi gives
 P^(2)=2Aq^2 (pi_dot)^2,
with no quadratic spatial-gradient term. Positive time kinetic is therefore only a restricted scalar-sector result. Spatial degeneracy and gravitational mixing need a full constraint calculation; no full stability claim is made. This ghost-condensate-type boundary is already familiar in the repository's ROLLING_KESSENCE_BASELINE.md, with different X convention. We are not rediscovering it as a new mechanism.
V0 is arbitrary; changing it changes H. This model supplies no protection against additive vacuum corrections and no physical acceleration a0.

3. Leading static, unitary-clock calculation
Work in a weak-field local patch, fixed clock phi=q t, near-zone spatial momenta large compared with H, neglecting background-curvature corrections. This is a constrained branch/approximation, not a proof that every static galaxy has this clock. Physical potentials obey g00=-(1+2Phi), gij=(1-2Psi)deltaij.
At C=1, deltaX=-2X Phi, delta ln C=-2b Phi. Hence
 Phitilde=(1-b)Phi, Psitilde=Psi+b Phi.
The leading Einstein spatial-gradient Lagrangian is
 M2[(grad Psitilde)^2-2 grad Phitilde dot grad Psitilde].
With isolated/matched boundary data eliminating the harmonic difference, the spatial constraint gives Psitilde=Phitilde and Psi=(1-2b)Phi.
The scalar lapse perturbation is deltaY=2q^2 Phitilde. Its quadratic energy contribution to L is +2Aq^4 Phitilde^2. Retaining physical matter gives
 L_static^(2)=-M2(1-b)^2 |grad Phi|^2+2Aq^4(1-b)^2 Phi^2-rho_b Phi.
Therefore
 [Delta+k_*^2]Phi=rho_b/[2M2(1-b)^2],
 k_*^2=2Aq^4/M2>0.
This reduced functional has not been shown to be a consistent asymptotic solution of all cosmological momentum/clock equations with a source. Background-suppressed constraints may still be load-bearing when their leading static term vanishes; the full lapse/shift/source matching remains an obligation. The positive sign of k_*^2 is important: this is a Helmholtz-type static operator, not a Yukawa screening proof. Boundary resonances and full dynamical signs remain separate. Background H corrections were neglected; retaining k_* while dropping them requires a scale hierarchy, e.g. k_*>>H, obtainable only as an input choice here.
For k>>max(H,k_*), the leading Poisson calibration is
 G_N=1/[8pi M2(1-b)^2].
Exact Newton response over all distances does not follow. In particular the prior algebraic witness's omitted mass term is restored by this chosen vacuum-supporting scalar.

4. Light and massive matter in the same metric
The leading lensing potential is (Phi+Psi)/2=(1-b)Phi. At b=-1/4 and M2=(16/25)Mref2, the short-distance Poisson coefficient equals 1/(8pi Mref2), while the lensing potential is 5Phi/4. This is a conditional physical-metric prediction, not a Cassini pass or a fit to lensing data. No independent lensing law is added by hand.

5. Why the construction does not supply the missing galaxy law
For fixed coefficients, domain and admissible boundary conditions, the static equation is linear in rho_b. Wherever its inverse exists, scaling source mass by t scales Phi and g by t. It cannot yield a nonzero universal g proportional to sqrt(M)/r on that branch.
Moreover M2(1-b)^2 is strictly positive for finite M2>0 and b!=1. Making this reduced static gradient coefficient vanish by b=1 destroys invertibility of the conformal transformation. The singular/mimetic class requires a new degree-of-freedom and source analysis; it cannot be obtained by importing this invertible branch's health claims. This excludes that simple criticality maneuver, not all nonlinear DHOST responses.
The repository already proves a more general conditional regular-response result in sol61_push/puzzle_32pi/dynamical_sector_2026_10_06/REGULAR_RESPONSE_SCREEN.md. Its bounded-inverse hypotheses must not be assumed for the full ghost-condensate/gravity problem merely because this reduced branch is linear. Nonuniform limits, different clock profiles and nonlinear branches remain distinct.

6. What is connected, and what remains independent
One chosen action now connects vacuum stress V0, clock dynamics A,q, tensor normalization M2, matter force normalization and the light/matter potential ratio b. It exposes their relations rather than combining results from different models. But V0, A, q and b remain independent input constants. There is no derived a0, particle spectrum, gauge interaction or account of all physics. Even solving this gravity-sector model would not establish those larger claims.
The next meaningful extension must change the sourced nonlinear dynamics, not just tune b or set V0 to match32pi. It must retain the lapse/shift equations, physical matter coupling and boundary conditions, and explain how the required critical response coexists with a healthy time evolution.
