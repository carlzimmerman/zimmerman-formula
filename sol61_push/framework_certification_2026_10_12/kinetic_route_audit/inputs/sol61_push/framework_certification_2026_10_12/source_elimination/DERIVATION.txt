CAUSAL SOURCE RESPONSE IN THE EXISTING NR ACTION
No extra eta field, no added damping, no halo profile.

Use the supplied approximation
 i psi_t=-laplacian psi/(2m)+2c3 b psi*+2k |psi|^2 psi,
 i b_t=(-laplacian/(2M)+delta)b+c3 psi^2,
 delta=M-2m>0, c3=g/(2m sqrt(2M)).
This is the source's leading positive-frequency NR Hamiltonian, not the full
relativistic field theory or an established quantum kinetic reduction.

At source momentum P let a=delta+|P|^2/(2M) and F_P(t)=(psi^2)_P(t).
The exact linear-source solution for a given forcing history is
 b_P(t)=exp(-i a t)b_P(0)-i c3 int_0^t exp[-i a(t-s)] F_P(s) ds.
Substitution into the psi equation retains the convolution and the initial
source field. For nonzero P it also retains spatial propagation; it is not a
local friction coefficient. The operator version uses the source propagator
exp[-i(-laplacian/(2M)+delta)t] with specified domain/boundaries.

For F_P(t)=F exp(-i omega t), a!=omega and zero initial source,
 b_P(t)=-c3 F/(a-omega)[exp(-i omega t)-exp(-i a t)].
At resonance a=omega,
 b_P(t)=-i c3 F t exp(-i a t).
For a well-prepared initial source b_P(0)=-c3 F/(a-omega), only the particular
response remains. A generic initial mismatch has constant-amplitude oscillatory
memory in this single-mode equation; it does not decay by assumption.

For P=0 and this well-prepared monochromatic reduction,
 k_eff(omega)=k-c3^2/(delta-omega).
Zero-frequency k_eff(0)=k-c3^2/delta has the positive coefficient proved in
nr_carrier_energy/. Its replacement at nonzero omega differs by
 -c3^2 omega/[delta(delta-omega)]. Thus a slow/source-gap expansion requires
small actual frequency relative to the gap AND absence/control of initial
transients. At resonance that expansion fails. No sign of damping follows
from a real off-resonant susceptibility alone.

For psi=A exp(-i omega t/2), |psi|^2=n=A^2, P=0, the particular source
weighted-charge fraction relative to the daughter is
 2|b|^2/n=2c3^2 n/(delta-omega)^2.
An interpretation as a negligible slaved source requires this quantity small;
that is a state-dependent admission condition, not a derived universal bound.
For zero initial b and constant forcing F, source storage is
 |b|^2=2c3^2 F^2[1-cos(delta t)]/delta^2.
Its derivative has both signs. The diagnostic keeps psi forcing prescribed;
it does not assert conservation of a backreacted combined energy for that
externally driven trajectory. The full source/psi Hamiltonian is conservative.

The supplied near-threshold production shell is precisely the problematic
region: pair excess frequency p^2/m in leading NR units equals delta on shell.
NR p^2=m delta differs from the exact p_*^2=Delta/4 by delta^2/4.
The relative momentum-squared error is delta/(4m), small only with the actual
near-threshold hierarchy. Being cold alone does not authorize eliminating a
source whose pair detuning is zero. A slow decay rate may coexist with small
gap, but does not move the resonant pair off its shell.

Constructive consequence: retain psi and b as coupled degrees of freedom in
the resonant sector; use conservative contact matching only in a separately
admitted off-resonant sector. A collision closure must then derive its actual
memory/dephasing/state-preparation assumptions and count conversion and elastic
processes consistently. This advances the common-action route; it does not
derive a target-free halo selector or add a hidden irreversible reservoir.
