Framework C2 receiver and whole-goal audit, 13 October 2026
Independent audit of existing statements; original sources are unchanged.
Primary verdict: incomplete, with a concrete common-coupling/local-dynamics
implication missing. The ghost receiver is a valid conditional building block,
not subject to a blanket two-sign impossibility theorem.

1. Exact candidate and domain
Use signature (-,+,+,+), X=-g^{mu nu} theta_mu theta_nu/2, and the supplied
P(X)=-rho0+c(X-X0)^2/2, rho0,c,X0>0. Work first on a homogeneous timelike
background with theta_dot>0. Put d=X-X0. The exact two-derivative quantities are
p=-rho0+c d^2/2,
rho=2X P_X-P=rho0+2cX0 d+3c d^2/2,
rho+p=2c(X0+d)d,
pi=P_X theta_dot=c d sqrt(2(X0+d)).
The rest-frame principal coefficients are A=P_X+2X P_XX=c(2X0+3d),
B=P_X=c d, so c_s^2=d/(2X0+3d) wherever A is nonzero. Expanding the action
gives (A eta_dot^2-B|grad eta|^2)/2 for scalar fluctuations. This is the
fixed-metric two-derivative block, not the metric-constrained full operator.

At d=0, w=-1, pi=0, but theta_dot=sqrt(2X0) is nonzero. The Legendre map is
regular since d pi/d theta_dot=A=2cX0>0. Thus dH/dpi=theta_dot has a nonzero
value at pi=0. Near ground the local homogeneous Hamiltonian is
H=rho0+sqrt(2X0)pi+pi^2/(4cX0)+higher terms.
Calling pi=0 'receiver at rest' must not erase this rolling background.
The gravitating density shift starts at first order, while delta p starts at
second order. The leading deposit has dust-like pressure, not exactly w=-1.
This conclusion is a perturbative stress statement; no nonlinear dust fluid
or absence of all higher-gradient flux follows from it.

2. Two signs, health and capacity
For d>0 both A,B are positive and 0<c_s^2<1/3. A prepared positive d_i buffer
can either receive energy or give some back while staying at d>=0. Its
homogeneous healthy-branch available density above ground is exactly
2cX0 d_i+3c d_i^2/2. The local floor on this branch is rho0. Hence the existing
CFG635 language 'both signs cannot be had' needs the restriction 'net withdrawal
from the exact ground state crosses onto the negative-gradient side.' It does
not exclude withdrawal from an already prepared healthy buffer or replenishing
boundary flux. Preparation, accessible volume, replenishment and coupling
rates need explicit equations; a huge constant rho0 is not itself the buffer.

For -2X0/3<d<0, the time coefficient remains positive but B<0. This is a
gradient instability, not automatically a wrong-sign time-kinetic ghost.
At d=-2X0/3 the Legendre/principal block degenerates. Assertions outside the
timelike/regular branch require a new analysis. A healthy-background statement
does not prove global nonlinear evolution stays on that branch.

For the stipulated extra dispersion omega^2=c_s^2 k^2+k^4/Mk^2, Mk>0,
a negative c_s^2=-a gives an unstable band 0<k<Mk sqrt(a), a maximally growing
k=Mk sqrt(a/2), and gamma_max=a Mk/2 if that mode is in the retained domain.
The k4 term bounds the ultraviolet growth; instability and UV ill-posedness
are different statements. A finite IR box or EFT cutoff can restrict available
modes, so the unconstrained maximum is conditional on mode coverage.
At ground omega=k^2/Mk and group speed=2k/Mk for k>0. Continuing this formula
to arbitrary k would exceed c=1, but an EFT does not license that continuation.
A cutoff, metric mixing and UV/front-propagation contract are needed to infer
physical causality. No numerical H0-based instability/length bound is used here.

3. Local current, stress and reciprocal force
For the P(X) block, T^{mu nu}=P_X theta^mu theta^nu+P g^{mu nu} and
j^mu=-P_X theta^mu. In a flat frame j0=pi, j_space=-P_X grad theta;
energy density is P_X theta_dot^2-P, energy flux=-pi grad theta, momentum
density is -pi grad theta under the contravariant T^{0i} convention, and
T^{ij}=P_X theta_i theta_j+P delta^{ij}. The homogeneous rho formula above
is the scalar-rest-frame specialization. Signs depend on using contravariant
momentum/energy flux consistently. We use T^{0i} throughout this audit.

Define a source F by pi_dot-div(P_X grad theta)=F, equivalently div j=F.
Then div T^{mu nu}=-F theta^nu. Its energy component is +F theta_dot and its
spatial component -F grad theta. The cold sector and interaction stress must
supply the opposite four-force. Naming a current is not the same as supplying
its source, reciprocal cold force or total local conservation identity.
For a stipulated heat power Q, F=Q/theta_dot is locally regular near the rolling
ghost ground, unlike a zero-velocity canonical ground. The associated receiver
momentum transfer is -Q grad theta/theta_dot; deleting it is not licensed by
the regular heat division. Supplying Q still does not derive a common coupling.
If the k4 correction is retained, its action and additional Noether current,
stress and metric variation must also be included; the bare P(X) tensor alone
cannot certify the full higher-derivative model.

4. Regular bracket and what it actually proves
For an exchange coordinate q, D=dE_c/dq, s=dS/dq, h=dH_rec/dpi,
w=(h,-D), M=mu w w^T, mu>=0, the regular rates are
q_dot=mu h^2 s, pi_dot=-mu h D s.
They satisfy E_dot=D q_dot+h pi_dot=0 and S_dot=mu h^2 s^2>=0 exactly.
At the ghost ground h=sqrt(2X0), the bracket has no canonical stall.
The receiver power -mu h^2 D s may have either sign; entropy positivity does
not determine that sign. An already one-sided S or D cannot be made two-sided
merely by replacing the receiver. A chosen drift requires a constitutive
mobility; rescaling mu changes all exchange times with the same algebraic
invariants. No time scale or admissible prepared buffer is thereby derived.

The source CFG542 explicitly reports that the full reversible Vlasov bracket
fails L delta S=0 for its deficit functional. The pairwise energy-degenerate
bracket is not by itself a full GENERIC system or a common covariant action.
CFG635 supplies the receiver P(X) and this constitutive bracket, but no actual
common coupling yielding that dissipative transfer and its local reciprocal
stress. This is the smallest current mechanism gap. It is not an assertion
that no such coupling can exist. The older homogeneous no-secular-transfer
claim was already restricted by spatial_exchange_scope: spatial modes carry
real exchange and flux, so that blanket exclusion cannot be reused here.

Regularity alone also fails to preserve the stipulated healthy branch. At pi=0,
h>0, the displayed bracket gives pi_dot=-mu h D s. If mu>0 and D s>0,
it points into pi<0, hence d<0 on the local positive-velocity Legendre branch.
A positive buffer's finite budget therefore does not prove the unrestricted
bracket stops at exhaustion. A boundary condition, vanishing drive/mobility or
replenishing flux must be demonstrated rather than inferred from conservation.
The separated H_rec(pi)+E_c(q) drift also changes ghost shift charge unless
its current/source exchange is specified. This criticism is confined to that
separated construction. An actual conservative P(X,r) cross-dependence can
exchange radial/phase energy while preserving phase shift charge; its stress
divergence contains an additional P_r grad r term. Such an action is not
the displayed separated dissipative bracket and needs its own equations.

5. Whole-goal completion, beyond theorem counts
A framework-only axiomatic certificate may legitimately take the owner's
declared kernel, vacuum-scale bridge, fitted kappa and cold abundance as inputs.
Logical proof does not require deriving every fundamental postulate. These
inputs must be listed separately from conclusions. A static law copied into
a target does not demonstrate that target is selected by dynamics. If the
frozen CFG679 mechanism contract is the intended stronger closure, its own
R0/R1/R3 and M/S/data requirements still apply: no copied profile in the mechanism,
its explicitly bounded universal parameter count rather than per-system tuning,
common action, attractor from the specified initial states, settling time,
well-posed dynamics, signed exchange, full momentum ledger, metric lensing,
GW/Solar-System behavior, selector/catchment and independent observational
tests. Current bounded proofs cannot substitute for those distinct statements.

The main particle route now has conditional 3D local streaming and completed
event stress, as well as original-NR coherent exchange; the missing implication
must not be described as 'no 3D transport.' Its state-dependent quantum/field
to kinetic closure remains conditional. The ghost route adds a possible
rolling smooth receiver with a real healthy buffer, but does not identify it
with either charged phi/chi carrier or the constructed neutral eta postulate.
Any identification requires explicit field content and coupling, with charge,
energy and momentum accounted for. Separate constructions cannot silently
be composed into one complete theory.

The candidate THEORY_v2 openly labels PM evidence as conditional on inherited
Lambda-CDM initial conditions/background. Those outputs do not qualify as
framework-only empirical certification unless the borrowed inputs are removed
or justified independently within the stated framework. No standard MOND
interpolating equation is imported in this audit: the owner's kernel is a
declared axiom, while relation to other models is not evidence for its mechanism.
Whole-goal closure requires an authoritative final model, a dependency-complete
statement inventory, exact hypotheses and excluded borrowed inputs, and
evidence for each intended physical prediction. A dated reproducible archive
establishes what was demonstrated; it cannot make open physical implications
complete or guarantee that no future contribution remains possible.
