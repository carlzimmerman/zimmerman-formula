source, so totalenergy AND all spatialmomentum must balance.

Changing signature without changing the raised derivative/source sign would
create an apparent contradiction. This is a convention clarification, not a
new physical coupling or a correction to the accepted component computations.

INTERIOR F requires a displayed matter/receiver interaction or explicit source.

divergence and must not be relabelled an Euler source. A GENERIC energy
cancellation alone does not supply the cold reaction or phase-charge source.
No new source function is defined as a physical hypothesis here.
