Exact check: shared inputs do not cancel a likelihood difference
-------------------------------------------------------------
Let d be the measured vector, C positive definite, s(theta) the control,
f(theta)=s(theta)+e(theta) the framework and r=s-d. At fixed nuisances,
 Delta chi^2 = e^T C^-1 e + 2 r^T C^-1 e.
Both r and e can depend on the shared cosmological inputs theta. Equal
inputs reduce some numerical/systematic differences but cannot remove
this dependence. Even with fixed e it remains through the second term.
Illustrative exact one-component counterexample (not survey data):
 C=1, d=1, e=1. For s=0, Delta=-1; for s=1, Delta=+1.
Nuisance minimisation does not supply a general cancellation identity.
Therefore refitting/deriving shared cosmology requires a fresh, declared
analysis, not subtracting a correction from the existing +68.7.
