WHY A REDUCTION IS STILL REQUIRED
Under standard real-field time reversal Pi->-Pi, g fixed and v->-v,
D is unchanged, p_b changes sign, h changes sign, and r is unchanged. The rate
J=gamma*h^2*R is even and S=gamma*h*r*R is odd. Actual reversed equations require
the matter collision rate to be odd and the Pi-equation source to be even.
Thus the nonzero prototype reaction is not an exact closed time-reversal-invariant
equation for these displayed variables. A microscopic reversible parent may
yield irreversible reduced equations after coarse-graining, but that reduction,
state preparation and operational regime must be established. Extra variables,
different symmetries, derivative/interaction stress or more general action
formalisms are outside this test; this is not a universal action impossibility.
