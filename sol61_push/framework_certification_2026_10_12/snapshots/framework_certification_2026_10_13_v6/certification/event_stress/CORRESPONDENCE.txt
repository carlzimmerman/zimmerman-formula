For a completed event with source energy Es=E1+E2>0 and momentum Ps=p1+p2,
particle kinetic spatial stress is p_i*p_j/E. In any chosen spatial projection z,
let a=z.p1, b=z.p2. The jump is
 a²/E1+b²/E2−(a+b)²/(E1+E2)
 =E1E2/(E1+E2)*(a/E1−b/E2)².
This is precisely stressJump, with the energy/momentum addition built into its
definition. Physical interpretation requires positive energies and the displayed
particle-stress dictionary. The theorem does not derive that dictionary from
quantization or certify the coherent interaction stress during conversion.
