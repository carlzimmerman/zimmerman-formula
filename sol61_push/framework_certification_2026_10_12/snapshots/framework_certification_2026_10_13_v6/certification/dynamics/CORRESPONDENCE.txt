Exact mathematical objects
Real q, target A, total mass B, signed specific transfer cost D and receiver H.
Declared vector field qdot = -rate*(q-A); other mass derivative = -qdot;
declared receiver reaction Hdot = -D*qdot. Hence the two mass weights sum to B
and the energy derivative of D*q+H is zero at each state. No spatial kinetic
continuity or field stress tensor is supplied by this toy transfer model.

Claim-to-theorem correspondence
shared_equilibrium: q=A is stationary for every real rate.
mass_ledger: the two declared component derivatives cancel for every rate.
energy_ledger: the declared receiver reaction cancels D*qdot for every rate.
squared_error_rate: the chain-rule expression 2*(q-A)*qdot equals
  -2*rate*(q-A)^2. Lean certifies this expression, not the chain rule applied
  to a separately constructed ODE solution.
positive_rate_decreases: rate>0 and q!=A make the error derivative negative.
negative_rate_increases: rate<0 and q!=A make it positive.
admissible_unstable_state: A>0, epsilon>0, A+epsilon<B supply two positive
  masses in the interior. The negative-rate field shares the target and
  conserves mass but has positive squared-error derivative there. No assertion
  that the unstable flow stays in the allowed mass interval forever is made.

Independent analytic witness

q(t)=4+exp(t), H(t)=12-2*exp(t), energy=20 and total mass=10.

This explicit trajectory is an analytic review witness, not formalized here.
It disproves the inference from static equilibrium plus reciprocal conservation
to local contraction. It does not refute any fully specified physical theory.
