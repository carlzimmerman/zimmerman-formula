1. MATTER ENERGY AND ITS FULL CHANNEL COST

and u_beta. Let rho=C_c f+C_b B be the source mass vector, with cell-volume

2. A MOMENTUM-CARRYING RECEIVER

Represent one real massless scalar Fourier mode by sine/cosine amplitudes phi
and canonical Pi. In a fixed periodic 1D geometry,

The common frequency is essential: the canonical motion

conserves both E_R and P_R by antisymmetry of G. The constant canonical Poisson
block has Jacobi. This is a mode-level Hamiltonian, not a local tensor/action proof.

These instantaneous identities hold with state-dependent D. They require the
complete E_Pi=Pi; additional momentum-dependent interactions change the formula.

A single wavevector closes one momentum direction. In 3D either the cold
