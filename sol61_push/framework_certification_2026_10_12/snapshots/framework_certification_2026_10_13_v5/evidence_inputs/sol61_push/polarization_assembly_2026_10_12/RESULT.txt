SOURCE ASSEMBLY OPENS A FINITE FORMATION ROUTE
12 October 2026. New evidence within the existing effective polarization model.

The theory is still open. The concrete advance is that the prescribed static
force profile CAN be formed approximately in a tested, mass-conserving baryon
assembly history, even when both response fields initially vanish. No drag or
direct relaxation toward a target was inserted. The strongest run has about
0.03% late RMS force error, against the existing 10% gate.

This corrects an overly broad reading of the preceding formation failure:
the abrupt compact-source initial condition failed; the response model does
not fail every formation history tested. The old result remains valid for
its stated initial condition and is preserved unchanged.

WHAT CHANGED
------------
All runs use the existing exact-kernel effective equations in units
C=a0=Z=I=1:
  Phi_tt = div(grad Phi-P) - rho_b(t),
  P_tt   = grad Phi-P-b(P).
The potential W has W'=b. The signed inverse-deficit table is extracted by
AST whitelist from CFG596; the large PM engine is not imported or run.
The constitutive kernel, response coefficients, final baryon profile and
force-error threshold were not fitted to the result.

1. The first experiment smoothly increases the fixed compact source with
   lambda(t)=10s^3-15s^4+6s^5, s=t/T, clipped to [0,1]. It helps substantially,
   but does not provide a closed mass-conserving baryon flow.

2. The stronger experiment keeps the entire baryon mass fixed. A diffuse
   cloud contracts homologously, rho(r,t)=a^-3 rho0(r/a), with
   a=16-15 lambda(t), T=320. The final source is the same exp(-r^2/8) profile,
   cut at r=8, with integral rho*r^2 dr=1. The initial cloud has radius 128.
   The continuum velocity v_b=(a_dot/a)r obeys mass continuity, including
   the moving compact edge. Each sampled mesh density is normalized to
   exactly unit discrete mass. This is a prescribed moving source; a baryon
   continuity/momentum solver is not being claimed.

3. Prepared initial fields were then removed as a falsification test. The
   zero-field run starts with Phi=P=all velocities=0 and diffuse baryons
   already present. Its initial force error is 100%. The successful final
   profile is not supplied as an initial field or a relaxation target.
   Initial diffuse baryons and their prescribed contraction remain inputs.

ENERGY IS ACCOUNTED FOR
----------------------
With source energy +rho_b Phi, the exact model identity is
  dH/dt = integral rho_b,t Phi dV.
For a conserved source this equals integral rho_b v_b.grad(Phi) dV.
Thus a contracting baryon source can take energy out of the response field;
the opposite gravitational work would enter its mechanical energy if its
own equation were evolved. This is a concrete energy-exchange channel, not
unexplained field damping. It does not identify the cooling or stresses
needed to sustain the imposed baryon trajectory.

For the fine zero-field contraction run, initial field Hamiltonian H=0;
final H=-1.27897152; integrated source work=-1.27897148. The largest sampled
balance error is 1.006e-8 times |H_final_static|. Dividing by the initial
energy would be undefined and is not done. Total field-plus-source energy
is not conserved during prescribed assembly; the recorded external work
is necessary. After assembly the source is fixed.

The field is NOT globally at rest. The fine zero-field run retains excess
energy (H-H_final_static)/|H_final_static|=0.65289 at t=960 despite the small
interior force error. The prepared-field case retains 0.08074. These numbers
depend on the box and reference static energy. Local force formation does
not imply global relaxation. No spatial attribution of all residual energy
or infinite-time attractor is claimed.

VALIDATION
----------
Nine simulations in four bounded runs; all grouped numerical controls pass.
Screen: R=768, N=6144, dt=.02, T=0/80/320, end=T+640.
Growth verification: T=320; (R,N,dt)=(768,12288,.01) and (1536,12288,.02).
Each contraction family: T=320, end=960; R=768; (N,dt)=(6144,.02),
(12288,.01). Fine resolution dr=.0625. All jobs use a cooperative one-thread
cap, bounded wall/CPU time and the installed Python/NumPy/SciPy runtime.

* Growth: worst late force-profile refinement difference 0.3271% of target
  L2 norm; doubled-domain interior profiles agree exactly at stored precision.
* Prepared contraction: worst late refinement difference 0.01028% of target.
* Zero-field contraction: worst late refinement difference 0.009988% of target;
  its coarse/fine worst RMS errors are 0.02949% / 0.02925%.
* Contraction mass error at every time step is <=4.45e-16 in unit-mass units.
* Initial/final discrete equilibria satisfy the declared residual check;
  source-free zero RHS, discrete integration by parts and interpolation
  support checks cover the applicable code paths.
* Source-work errors pass the 0.2% tolerance by a wide margin. Dropping the
  work term for the fine growth run would give a 37.31% residual instead
  of 1.28e-8, so the energy check does distinguish incorrect bookkeeping.

The doubled-domain numerical check was performed for source growth only.
For contraction, changes start inside r<=128; the continuum principal
characteristic speeds are 0 and +/-1. A reflected disturbance cannot return
to r<=20 by t=960 (earliest path 768-128+768-20=1388). This explains the chosen
box but is not a rigorous error bound for the semidiscrete scheme. Simultaneous
space/time refinement is a bounded diagnostic, not a convergence theorem;
the quoted ~0.03% is a computed error, not a certified continuum error bar.

All four manifests validate input/output hashes. Their exact commands,
versions, actual Git revisions, dirty status, limits and timings are saved.
The shared checkout advanced during this work; actual input hashes are the
authoritative execution ancestry. No existing research lane was modified.
