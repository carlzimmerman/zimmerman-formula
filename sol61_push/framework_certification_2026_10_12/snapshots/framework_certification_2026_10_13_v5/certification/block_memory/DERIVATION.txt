BACKREACTED FINITE QUANTUM BLOCK MEMORY
The supplied regulated Hamiltonian, split into retained source+daughter states
and eliminated three-daughter states, has H=[[A,B],[Bdagger,C]], H=Hdagger.
The split retains the ORIGINAL quartic in C and the original conversion in B.
It is not a golden-rule width matched star Hamiltonian or a new receiver vertex.

For state vector (u,v), i udot=A u+B v and i vdot=Bdagger u+C v.
Exact elimination gives
 v(t)=exp(-iCt)v0-i int_0^t exp[-iC(t-s)]Bdagger u(s) ds,
 i udot=A u+B exp(-iCt)v0-i int_0^t B exp[-iC(t-s)]Bdagger u(s) ds.
This includes backreaction through u(s), and initial eliminated-state amplitude
v0. A populated three-daughter sector adds an inhomogeneous initial-memory
term. The full system is unitary; discarded amplitudes are not a fictitious
energy sink. Deriving a reduced density-matrix collision law requires a state
and correlation/approximation contract beyond this exact state-vector equation.

For stationary E away from spectrum(C),
 v=(E-C)^-1 Bdagger u,
 [E-A-B(E-C)^-1 Bdagger]u=0.
The same Schur complement gives the retained resolvent. The actual kernel
contains the full C propagator and all its quartic mixing, not merely free
daughter denominators. If E lies in spectrum(C), this particular elimination
formula is undefined; the full finite Hermitian eigenproblem remains regular.
No bare denominator is by itself an on-shell collision rate.

At the BARE eliminated energy E=E0 it is zero, while the full matrix inverse

calling that divergence an infinite physical rate loses the actual full model.

v=-i exp(-iE0t)sin(ht). Exact norm=1, energy=E0, complete return after pi/|h|.
These statements are a bounded diagnostic, not a theorem excluding continuum

The complete source/daughter Q3 construction is a separate computation with
