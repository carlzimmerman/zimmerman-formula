This repository uses a two-part licensing structure:

## 1. Code — AGPL-3.0-or-later

All source code in this repository (every `.py` script, tooling, and pipelines) is licensed
under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later**. The full text is in
[LICENSE](LICENSE).

## 2. Prose, papers, figures, and scientific content — CC-BY-4.0

All documentation, papers, markdown prose, figures, and general scientific information in
this repository are licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
(CC-BY-4.0)**: share and adapt freely, with attribution to Carl P. Zimmerman and, where a
paper carries one, its Zenodo DOI. The Zenodo depositions of the papers carry CC-BY-4.0
explicitly.

## Attribution and prior art

The scientific content builds on published work that is credited in place, in every paper,
and in [CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff) — in particular Milgrom (1983), Milgrom & Sanders
(2008, the interpolation kernel), McGaugh, Lelli & Schombert (2016), and Skordis & Złośnik
(2021, the relativistic scaffold). Withdrawn claims are recorded in
[RETRACTIONS.md](RETRACTIONS.md).
