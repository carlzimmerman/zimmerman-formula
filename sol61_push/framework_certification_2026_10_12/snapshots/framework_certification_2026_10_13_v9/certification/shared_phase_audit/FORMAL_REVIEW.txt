Independent SharedPhase.lean review, 13 October 2026
Primary verdict: proved as written. This is the explicitly prospective action's
mathematical certificate, not its admission as the framework's cold component.

The definitions and all10 raw theorem statements/proofs were independently read.
full_action_expansion is an exact quartic polynomial identity, including its
linear background residual and cubic/quartic remainder. It does not assume the
radial stationary equation; that equation must be separately imposed to cancel
the linear sigma term. The linear phase term is a homogeneous-background total
derivative, not a hidden zero-charge assumption. Gradients are scalar projected
coordinates, so full geometric tensor/metric definitions are not formalized.

canonical_momenta proves actual derivatives of quadraticL. legendre_transform
assumes K nonzero and solves peta=K eta_dot+C sigma exactly. The positive
Hamiltonian theorem requires K>0, mass-square mu>=0 and B>=0; it certifies the
displayed rotating-frame quadratic form, not physical T00 or nonlinear energy.
The all-parameter dispersion/discriminant dictionary is a polynomial identity.
Nonnegative discriminant follows for K,B,mu nonnegative. The frequency theorem
quantifies arbitrary REAL s and assumes only that s is a polynomial root,
K>0,B>=0,mu>0. It does not assume s=omega^2>=0 and is not vacuous for that
reason. A negative s would make every polynomial term nonnegative with a
strictly positive middle term. The source separately reconstructs the real
Fourier determinant; this Lean polynomial theorem is not a Fourier-transform
or differential-equation spectral theorem.

The B0 theorem gives the exact two roots for every real wave coordinate and
K nonzero:0 and wave^2+mu+C^2/K. It does not hide the zero-gradient branch.
background_enthalpy uses the actual homogeneous source pressure and density
definitions and gives u^2 B. pure_ghost_point_not_total_de has explicit nonzero
R,u and positive density, then proves w+1>0 when u^2/2=X0. Positivity of
the offset or a stationary/root-existence theorem is not silently assumed or
proved. These definitions derive the declared polynomial dictionary; they
do not perform full tensor metric variation or prove cold particle-number
conservation, physical rest-preserving exchange, gravity or halo selection.

The clean main log reports all10 names with only propext, Classical.choice,
Quot.sound. Neither sorryAx nor custom physical axioms occurs in the final
reports. FalseUnmixedGap intentionally treats the gap as mu when mixing is
nonzero; the fixture polynomial is -1, so its zero claim fails on goal False.
FalseBoundaryNoZero asserts the B0 zero root disappears at wave2; the
polynomial is0, so the nonzero claim also fails on goal False. Both logs
compile the correct declarations before the semantic failures. The first
failed development draft, including parser/proof errors and resulting temporary
sorryAx reports, is preserved in build_history and excluded from accepted
evidence. It cannot be used as a passing certificate for the final source.
