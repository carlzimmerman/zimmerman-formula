# Review of the recent work and the path toward completion

Assessment date: September 29, 2026, America/New_York. Inventory checkpoint is recorded in `review_scope.json`.

## Verdict

**Incomplete, with the smallest central gap being dynamical selection of the galaxy dark component and its environmental/history dependence.** Candidate B is an effective law plus an ownership prescription. The first-principles chain is a different action, with different couplings and predictions. Neither is a completed realization of the other.

Claude's strongest recent contribution is the obstruction map and its increasingly careful corrections. The recent work gives substantially better reasons to reject particular mechanisms. It does not establish that all mechanisms are impossible or that a completion is one small algebraic step away.

This review inventories the whole committed September 26–29 window and follows the principal workstreams through current summaries and selected raw derivations/code. It is **not a line-by-line audit or rerun of every computation**. Git author names do not authenticate which AI wrote a file; attribution to Claude follows the user's description and the lane records. Uncommitted work by other sessions is excluded from the verdict. ## What the recent work earned

1. **A more precise action problem.** `campaign_fresh_gravity/closure_map/GAPS_1_2_JOINT_STATUS.md` identifies the enclosed-baryon closure and the legal ownership switch. Its identification of these gaps is explicitly not a theorem. The no-go hypotheses are now visible, which makes them usable.
2. **A distinction between model failure and pipeline failure.** The KiDS–Local Group and budget machinery also misfits standard halos. These results should not be treated as clean exclusions of B. Conversely, the early-minus-late KiDS difference remains a serious constraint on models predicting negligible type dependence at fixed baryonic acceleration.
3. **Better controls and provenance.** The kernel mislabels, silent sample omission, projection error, spectrum units error, and Lean verifier error were found and recorded. `closure_map/OVERNIGHT_LESSONS_2026-09-29.md` is particularly valuable. Correcting these errors is scientific progress, although an unchanged headline after rerunning establishes reproducibility rather than full physical validity.
4. **An honest formal core.** `fable_independent_2026/lean_2026/ChainCert/README.md` separates conditional algebra from empirical facts and action derivations. Its ownership hierarchy is an input. Its fluid-cap and point-mass-fluid modules are not automatically one fluid.
5. **A useful negative coefficient result.** `sonnet55_push/puzzle_32pi/README.md` and its coefficient-evidence lane find no derivation of the coefficient. The factorization of 32 pi squared does not select a physical scale. The fitted normalization remains sensitive to interpolation, stellar masses, distances, and covariance.

## Two assumptions worth reopening, narrowly

**The exact target depends on the chosen law.** CFG44's enclosed-charge closure equals the P2 phantom for a point mass. For extended baryons it is an ODE closure, not exactly the algebraic P2 law; the raw B1 script already checks the additional density term. It is also not the exact nu_mono phantom. A summary that calls all of these the same target silently narrows the search. This is a known distinction in the raw code, not a discovery of an error in that code.

**The exchange-energy exclusion is not a universal energy exclusion.** CFG48 G4 and CFG70 explicitly posit a thermal-store coupling and compare its required energy with baryon orbital energy. Those calculations constrain that coupling. They do not show that a collisionless halo cannot be supported by its own gravitational binding energy. CFG118 already allows ordinary gravitational formation, but tests a static baryon seed and a few angular-momentum prescriptions. Neither result closes general assembly histories. A new mechanism must still account for total energy and reciprocity; dropping a physical reaction by assertion is not a repair.

## A better path

1. **Choose a single target before more scans.** Preserve the measured galaxy relation and the vacuum-scale hypothesis, but keep P2, nu_mono, the enclosed-charge ODE and the cold-debris rule distinct. Use one candidate for each joint prediction. Keep the intended final target of one action explicit.
2. **Study reachability and history response.** First construct a genuinely self-gravitating state with the required density, then perturb it by baryon assembly. Determine whether it retains the relation or requires a mass-dependent transfer of angular momentum, energy or dark mass. This turns “formation history” into a calculable obligation.
3. **Exploit the existing environmental constraint.** Any completion should make a prediction for the KiDS early/late difference and for embedded systems. An exact universal present-baryon law cannot acquire type dependence without additional physics or different baryonic inputs. Measure or bound that dependence jointly, rather than adding a separate post-hoc escape for each population.
4. **Consolidate observations into a joint generative inference.** For high-z discs, carry measured gas, stellar mass, inclination, beam smearing, pressure support and selection together. Correlated calibration errors must be shared across galaxies and epochs. Prefer a small sample with independent gas/kinematic information to another large table of model-derived masses. Investigate gas-rich local versus star-dominated samples as a normalization check before spending effort on coefficient numerology.
5. **Use decision-based checks.** Require a new route to remove an identified assumption or predict an observable. Additional Lean identities, repeated symbolic reductions, and renamed gate scans are worthwhile only if they close a missing arrow. Continue retaining failed controls.

The first investigation was **anisotropic gravitational support plus an explicit assembly-response test**. It survives scoped support checks but fails to maintain the target under ordinary adiabatic assembly. Carl subsequently clarified that the intended intuition is a stream passing through. The updated recommendation is **a dynamically driven internal response of a passing medium**, with amplitude selection and coherence as the first decisive obligations. Passive focusing fails in the tested limit. The published dipole example supplies a concrete quadratic response, but our independent audit exposes rather than closes its initial-state assumptions. See `RESULTS_2026-09-29.md` for the derivations and the narrower research decision. No claim that this direction must succeed is justified.
