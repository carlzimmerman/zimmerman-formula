# Research target and first probes

User authorization: review recent work, investigate a possible overlooked breakthrough, save meaningful chunks in `sol61_push`, commit and push them. Original goal: a completed theory. Status: OPEN.

## Success criterion

A completion must derive the galaxy behavior, environmental/ownership behavior and dark-sector dynamics from one specified action or an explicit dynamical reduction of it; conserve energy/momentum; have the required healthy evolution; and confront the relevant data using that same construction. A fitted kappa is allowed by the existing requirements. A selected equilibrium, a formal identity, or a surviving observational bound is partial progress only.

## Portfolio

1. **Target audit.** Independently differentiate the exact P2 mass profile and distinguish its real-density target from CFG44's enclosed-charge ODE. Check point mass and an extended analytic sphere. Success is a correct exact target dictionary. This distinction is already recognized in CFG44's raw code; no novelty claimed.
2. **Alternative support and energy bookkeeping.** Construct a circular-orbit collisionless halo in ordinary Newtonian gravity, with spherical orientation averaging. Check anisotropic Jeans balance, the gravity-plus-particle action, self-gravitating radial perturbations and total binding-energy bookkeeping. Success establishes realizability and scoped radial health, not formation or a new theory. Test both footings. General Einstein-cluster ideas are known and must be attributed.
3. **Adiabatic baryon-assembly falsification.** At fixed dark shell mass and conserved specific angular momentum, vary a central baryon mass slowly. Compare the resulting circular radii with the point-mass P2 target family. Success as a proposed mechanism would require that the law stays invariant without prescribed torques or shell reassignment; failure yields a precise missing transfer law. This is an analytic and bounded numerical probe, not a cosmological simulation.

## Limits

Spherical weak-field systems; positive density; fixed baryon background for the radial stability calculation; no crossing of Lagrangian dark shells; circular-orbit distribution is measure-valued and tangential, not CFG44's isotropic or finite-beta distribution. No inference to non-spherical, fully coupled baryon-dark, relativistic, cosmological or nonlinear stability. A point-mass cusp cannot be extended to r = 0 in GR; finite inner radius and outer cutoff must be stated. A finite halo's radius and abundance remain initial data.

No change to another session's files, main scientific ledger, manuscripts or deposited artifacts.
