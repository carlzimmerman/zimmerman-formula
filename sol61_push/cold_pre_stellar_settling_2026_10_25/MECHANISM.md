# CCT020: what a pre-stellar wave collapse would have to accomplish

This packet tests a spherical conservative complex cold-field proxy, not a derived Khronon/AeST action, SCT-M, or Claude's collisionless clump models. The field interpretation does not establish that an experimentally verified new substance exists.

In physical units the tested cold equations are

\[
i\eta\partial_t\psi=\left[-{\eta^2\over2}\nabla^2+\Phi_c+\Phi_{b,N}\right]\psi,\quad
\nabla^2\Phi_c=4\pi G|\psi|^2,\quad \rho_c=|\psi|^2.
\]

The conserved isolated fixed-source energy is

\[
E=K+W+U_b,\quad K={\eta^2\over2}\int|\nabla\psi|^2dV,
\quad W={1\over2}\int\rho_c\Phi_c dV,\quad U_b=\int\rho_c\Phi_{b,N}dV.
\]

The mass current is \(\boldsymbol j=\eta\,\mathrm{Im}(\psi^*\nabla\psi)\). Interference and gravity can redistribute energy and mass without particle collisions. A dense inner configuration must leave compensating energy in the outer field or a real outward flux. This calculation has reflecting boundaries and measures finite inventories; it has not demonstrated radiation to infinity. Spherical symmetry sets spin transport to zero, rather than solving it.

The baryonic Newtonian potential in the field equation is supplied once. The later passive stellar force uses the existing baryon-only nonlinear extra law plus the cold Newtonian force once; it does not apply that extra law to the combined cold+baryon source. This is a selective coupling prescription, not a reciprocity/action derivation. Before stellar insertion the same fixed baryon profile is only an externally supported gas proxy. Gas equilibrium, its cooling and star formation are not simulated.

## A conditional energy obstruction, not a universal no-go

For a stationary-density isolated full target with negligible surface stress,

\[
2K+W+W_b=0,\quad W_b=-\int\rho_c\,r\,g_{b,N}(r)dV,
\quad E_{\rm vir}={W\over2}+U_b-{W_b\over2}.
\]

For the fitted full-supply Plummer candidates, \(W=-3\pi GM_c^2/(32b)\). Pure loss of nonnegative energy requires \(E_{\rm vir}\leq E_0\). Under the stationary-density assumption, the original initial state fails this necessary screen for Sextans, Fornax and Leo I. Draco passes the necessary screen only; it still needs an actual relaxation mechanism and stellar compatibility. An additional tightly bound component, a different permitted mass/profile, source work or failure of isolation can change this conditional conclusion.

A regular spherical stationary zero-flux single smooth positive mode cannot hide virial support in radial phase flow. The fitted smooth Plummer amplitudes supply far too little gradient kinetic energy by themselves. Excited nodes, a mixture of frequencies, nonspherical modes or another supported density are distinct possibilities requiring their own dynamics; they are not established by fitting a smooth density.

## Separate shared assembly-energy hypothesis

`ENERGETIC_CONTRACT.md` changes the common initial infall to \(v_{\rm in}/V=0.8\) for all four galaxies, keeping mass, density and eta fixed. The threshold was selected after the energy calculation and is explicitly exploratory. It supplies coherent initial energy rather than inventing a radiative sink. It is not evidence that actual assembly produces that infall, nor permission to tune each dwarf separately.

The first registered density-settling window determines passive star insertion independently of measured velocities. No qualifying window means no tested stellar survival. Even a qualifying interval is finite stationarity evidence only. Relating the collapse clock to the actual onset of star formation additionally requires a cosmological start time and a gas/star-formation history, which are absent here. Waiting to insert stars by construction cannot prove that real stars waited.

For a fluctuating density, use the correction in `ENERGY_SCOPE_CORRECTION.md`: computing the binding energy of a smooth mean density omits negative fluctuation binding. The conditional stationary screen cannot exclude such fluctuating envelopes.
