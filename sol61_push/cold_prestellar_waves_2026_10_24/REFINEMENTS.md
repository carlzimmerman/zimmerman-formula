# Recorded extensions and failed implications

The original four nr=256 log-grid pilots ended at T=60. Their tiny absorber losses cannot establish absence of escaping waves: propagation to the boundary was not complete, and the outer log grid is too coarse for a 30-km/s wave in several physical scalings. They are retained. Their source revision is archived byte-for-byte as wave_pilot.py; old receipts name wave.py at that revision. Reproduction uses the archive, not the changed current default.

Hybrid radial grids use a logarithmic inner third below r=1 and a uniform outer grid; smaller absorber onset=16, outer wall=28, and T=160 allow outgoing waves to reach the sink. Same physical initial harmonic cloud, eta and gas history. Compare L=2 vs L=4 angular closure, then radial/time refinement and boundary sensitivity as resources warrant. The initial wave state has only l=0 and l=2, identically represented at both cutoffs; higher angular content is dynamically generated. Initial masses differ only by radial quadrature/normalization precision, not post-run rescaling.

wave_controls.py is a separate frozen-source copy with a gas=none switch. The source-free run distinguishes spontaneous self-gravitating wave emission from emission driven/enhanced by the prescribed gas cycle. All initial clouds are collectively bound; neither case demonstrates creation of the initial cold inventory or capture of a previously unbound scattering cloud.

Stellar-birth acceptance is NOT granted to a nonstationary, incompatible remnant. An additional offline diagnostic may insert passive circular-orbit cohorts into the saved time-dependent MONOPOLE mass profiles at t=30 or 80, comparing to a frozen-profile control. These are explicitly counterfactual stress tests even if the birth gate fails. They ignore nonmonopole forces, a stellar DF and cold backreaction, and cannot prove protection if a monopole response is small. Do not call them a live-star simulation or observed stellar heating.

The coarse dt=0.04 source-free run stopped at t=104 on its >5% energy-error guard and is rejected. Only the dt=0.01 rerun supports interpretation of source-free transport. The actual metadata/failed field are retained.

A combined resolution/boundary check uses nr=768,dt=0.02,absorber onset=20,wall=40,strength=1 rather than nr=512,dt=0.04,onset=16,wall=28,strength=0.3. The stronger/wider sink reduces possible outer-wall reflections. Its raw cumulative loss occurs farther out and must not be compared as if travel time were identical. Code before the gamma option is archived as wave_v2.py; all earlier hashes remain auditable. This is a combined sensitivity, not a clean attribution of each numerical change.

The seed-free branch wave_initial.py uses initially only l=2,m=1 and l=2,m=2, no l=0 or m=0 wave. It is a separate mechanism stress test, not a galaxy-selected initial condition. Its net angular-momentum vector is tilted: initial Jx is nonzero. Production histories ledger Jz, not all three components, so its Jz export cannot be called a validated total-spin export. Analyze the rotationally invariant l=0 population and the final core J-vector norm instead. A growing m=0 coefficient alone could be axis-dependent; growing l=0 is not. The aligned base runs have only even m and vanishing Jx/Jy, for which the Jz ledger suffices. These distinctions prevent a tilted-axis or counterrotation artefact from being labeled a low-spin core.
