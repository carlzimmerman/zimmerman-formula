# CCT-007: pre-stellar nonspherical collapse

Question: does a common pre-stellar collapse prescription actually produce positive-energy escaping field waves and spin export, then a remnant compatible with Draco, Sextans, Fornax and Leo I? This is an exploratory finite-mode simulation of the NEW CCT-003 selective-coupling wave candidate, not current v3s/AeST/SCT-M. Stellar survival is gated on an acceptable settled remnant; stars are not declared protected merely because their birth was postponed.

Equations: i eta psi_t=-eta^2 Laplacian psi/2+(Phi_c+Phi_g)psi, Laplacian Phi_c=4 pi G |psi|^2, isolated multipole boundary; spherical prescribed gas Phi_g=-Mg(t)/sqrt(r^2+ag(t)^2). Cold feels no Xi. Source work is explicit. The initial cloud can already be bound; its collapse is not newly captured unbound mass.

One shared physical prescription: eta=3000 pc km/s (a disclosed Draco-scale benchmark choice, not derived); cold initial mass=5.364*200*Mformed from CCT-006; length unit=catalogue stellar projected spherical half-light radius; gas mass=Mc_initial/5.364; initial gas scale=5 length units, contracts to 1 over t=4..10 dynamical units, holds until 16, disappears over 16..24. Remnant then evolves without gas to t=60. This is one imposed history, not an SFH-inferred gas trajectory or a free four-dwarf fit. Incoming radial speed=10 km/s. Finite initial harmonics are l=0,m=0 plus l=2,m=0,+2,-2 with unequal +- amplitudes so the field has shape and nonzero spin. Initial angular state is defined identically in all refinements. Catchment normalization and its conditionality are inherited explicitly from CCT-006.

Numerics: angular Galerkin wave expansion through l=L; density/isolated potential multipoles through 2L; dealiased angular quadrature. Logarithmic radial grid with a weighted self-adjoint Laplacian, Crank-Nicolson radial kinetic steps. Nonlinear angular potential uses a unitary Hermitian matrix exponential with a midpoint predictor, checked by energy/J and dt/L refinements. Outer absorbing layer has separate mass, total Hamiltonian-energy and J removal ledgers. Absorption alone is not asymptotic escape: require outward mass flux, positive removal energy and boundary sensitivity. No wraparound periodic gravity or renormalization after mass loss.

First bounded runs: nr=256,L=2,dt=0.02,T=60 for the four physical scalings, then radial/dt/angular/boundary refinements where warranted. Per-process memory cap below 1 GB; one numerical thread; no shared jobs changed. Initial and final fields, all parameters, source hashes, radial profiles, energetics and failed gates are retained. Numerical gates: mass residual<1e-8, energy residual<1%, J residual<1%; any failure makes a physical claim provisional. Final-quarter stationarity and model/data compatibility are checked separately. Angular truncation is not a full 3-D PDE result without convergence.

Run: OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 sol61_push/cold_prestellar_waves_2026_10_24/wave.py --galaxy Draco --tag draco_L2 --nr 256 --lmax 2 --dt .02 --T 60

No full-theory completion, no cosmological inventory derivation, no claim that the field has no particle interpretation, no unaccounted source work, and no observational exclusion probability from this finite history.
