Carrier route admissibility under the fixed goal
2026-10-11. Read-only review of source proposals; source hashes accompany it.

DECISION
The current microscopic-field/production proposals are conditional model
extensions, not solutions within the active no-new-knobs/no-new-species
framework. This does not refute their finite calculations or all possible
field descriptions of cold energy. It changes what computation is worth
undertaking for this particular goal: do not promote their production
benchmarks to evidence of a derived cold-energy theory.

WHAT THE RECORD ACTUALLY PROVIDES
cold_production's two-field construction adds a source field and independently
specified masses and couplings. Its one-complex-field construction reduces
the field count but still specifies lambda, f, daughter mass and radial
source amplitude. LINEAR_BAND explicitly says band location/width remain
adjustable physical inputs. Its cold linear band is not an abundance,
nonlinear endpoint, halo law, vacuum coupling or settling-rate derivation.
The microscopic carrier report explicitly lacks a vacuum-dependent
selector of the target density and leaves microscopic parameters free.
Both reports disclose these limits. They should remain optional extensions;
this review neither changes nor absorbs that separately evolving lane.

REQUIRED ACTION GATE
A candidate action needs to supply four links before its numerical halo
profile can count toward closure:
1. Identify which degrees of freedom carry the stipulated mass, with every
   additional dimensional and dimensionless parameter either derived from
   allowed inputs or declared as a relaxation of the fixed objective.
2. Derive evolution and a regular energy/momentum receiver from the same
   action. A homogeneous energy ledger alone does not supply a local receiver.
3. Encode the stipulated repaired kernel consistently and demonstrate that
   the resulting evolution realizes its profile in the declared regime.
   A target functional is allowed: the fixed goal supplies the empirical law.
   Derive the kinetic response and audit conservation/stability rather than
   treating a prescribed target as proof that the dynamics reaches it.
4. Derive the common timescale and supply ownership without refitting them
   for each system. Equilibrium selection alone cannot fix mobility/time.

This does not ban testing numerical targets: CFG626 remains a legitimate
kinetic-consistency test. It distinguishes its evidential role from a
microscopic origin. Even a full CFG626 pass would establish the tested
phenomenological evolution, not independently close the missing rate,
receiver, supply and action requirements.

NEXT DISCRIMINATING DOOR
For any admissible action proposed next, perform tests of the derived
dynamics before a cosmological simulation: use the same parameters and independently
declared initial conditions across distinct baryon masses and profiles.
Do not calibrate a separate initial energy/mode population/boundary for each
halo. Check whether the resulting acceleration curves follow the repaired
kernel on each fixed a0 footing, and whether the timescale follows without
an alpha fit. These would be new frozen experiments only after a concrete
action is supplied; no such run or new successful action is claimed here.

Meanwhile the continuum reservoir bounds answer a separate placement
question and cannot manufacture that action. The unfinished native growth
and repaired-kernel tests remain necessary constraints on any proposal.
No theory closure or general impossibility theorem follows from this audit.
No Claude file, optional production lane, or sealed prediction was edited.

CORRECTION TO c97a01d45 (2026-10-11)
The earlier wording of item 3 wrongly imposed a target-free microscopic
derivation of the empirical kernel as a closure requirement. The active
goal fixes that kernel and explicitly allows CFG620 as a candidate velocity
target. Encoding the supplied law in an action or constitutive functional
is therefore allowed. Explaining its deeper origin would be additional
work, not a prerequisite added by this audit. The change above removes
that extra restriction; it does not waive deriving a common rate, valid
receiver and consistent dynamics or passing the full frozen data tests.

The useful remaining action route is therefore to complete the existing
phenomenological variational principle, not necessarily to introduce a new
microscopic production field. CFG542 ACTION sections 1, 2 and 5 already
separate the supplied deficit functional from the undetermined mobility.
Neither energy conservation nor equilibrium alone selects alpha; setting
alpha=1 by convention would still not constitute the requested derivation.
The optional production models' independently specified physical parameters
remain an unresolved mismatch to the fixed no-new-knobs goal.
