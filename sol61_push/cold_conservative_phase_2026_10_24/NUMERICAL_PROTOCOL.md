# Executed scope and interpretation

Commands are `python3 run_matrix.py` followed sequentially by `python3 controls.py`, from this packet or by absolute path. Each subprocess sets the three numerical-library thread variables to one. Matrix: incoming Draco/Sextans/Fornax/Leo I and late Draco/Sextans, N=64,96; B=28, stretch=3.5, dt=.02, requested T=10, soft wall budget80s checked every five steps. A preliminary incoming Draco N48 run requests T=1 and stops on initial mass transfer. Controls: incoming all four, T=.5, (N,dt,B)=(64,.02,28),(96,.02,28),(96,.01,28),(96,.02,20). No old run is overwritten.

The executable adds an initial mass transfer gate of1% and continuum-generator mismatch gate of10%, before evolving; these numbers were in the producer before its first execution. The contract requires those comparisons but does not specify their numeric thresholds. There is no post-hoc target tuning. The density-weighted adjacent residual-amplitude phase gate is5% above pi/3. It is a warning about resolution, not a proof that every accepted step is spatially accurate; the short controls give additional finite evidence.

There is no absorber in these runs. This is closed finite-box conservative evolution, so total mass retention alone is automatic and cannot establish a bound remnant. Escape, retained cold core mass, angular-momentum export, stellar survival and no-overboost all require further physical evidence. Aperture masses in outputs are cell-centre sums, not a bound-state decomposition or observational fit. Tags start inside one parent stellar radius; they identify only that initial subset, not all subsequently captured mass.

Late initial fields use S=0 as contracted. Their initial resolution gates stop them before any stellar advance; their stored initial passive cohorts are not new stellar-survival results. The dormant control implementation holds the initial cold potential fixed in the inertial grid, rather than advecting it with the bulk flow. It must be replaced by a rigidly advected control before any future late cohort is evolved and interpreted. That approximation has no effect on the present, zero-step late results. No live-star likelihood or baryonic backreaction is supplied.

Incoming cases are precisely the prior saved incoming fields demodulated by their l=0 radial phase; no inventory reset or fitted density is introduced. D/S parents are CCT009, F/Leo parents CCT011, with identical physical assembly prescription and galaxy-specific units. They are not all the same dimensionless phase because eta_code and speed/V vary. Static phase factoring is a discretization of the continuum carrier identity, not an exact change of variables in the old angular Galerkin engine. Therefore differing later outcomes cannot yet be assigned solely to repair of old transport.

Provenance limitations: galaxy pins are sampled after input deserialization/initial setup and before evolution, then rechecked after evolution, rather than before the first input read. The recorded revision is sampled at process start/end. The separately stored source hashes identify the uncommitted producer. RSS is the process high-water mark sampled after compressed-array serialization, not an enforced memory cap. The80s wall budget is a soft loop check, not a hard process timeout. The bounded audit has separate enforced wall/CPU limits. The original benchmark was run without a runner; its deterministic result is independently rerun under the bounded audit runner. Neither numeric conservation nor finite convergence proves a continuum formation mechanism.
