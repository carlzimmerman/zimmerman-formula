1. Paolo Facchi, Marilena Ligabo, Davide Lonigro, *Spectral properties of the
   singular Friedrichs-Lee Hamiltonian*,
   [arXiv:1910.05957v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1910.05957v2), version dated
   18 December 2019; later J. Math. Phys. 62, 032102 (2021),
   [DOI 10.1063/5.0013032](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0013032).
   Authentication: author/title/version checked in primary PDF. Extraction:
   Eqs. (11)-(12) give the regular source-plus-continuum block operator;
   Theorem 3.2 gives the subtracted singular resolvent; Remark 3.3 distinguishes
   finite and infinite mean energy of the bare-source vector. Translation:
   their resolvent uses `(H-z)^-1` and the opposite self-energy convention;
   our displayed regular model uses `(z-H)^-1` and `Sigma=integral J/(z-x)`.
   Project implication: **verified for the declared regulated star model**,
   not for the full interacting scalar QFT. Our finite-regulator resolvent,
   source occupation and explicit threshold control are derived directly.

2. R. Chang, Q. Bouton, H. Cayla, C. Qu, A. Aspect, C. I. Westbrook,
   D. Clement, *Momentum-resolved observation of thermal and quantum
   depletion in an interacting Bose gas*,
   [arXiv:1608.04693v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1608.04693v2), version dated
   30 September 2016; later Phys. Rev. Lett. 117, 235303 (2016),
   [DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.117.235303](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.117.235303).
   Authentication: primary PDF title/authors/version checked. Extraction:
   Eqs. (2), (3), (5) distinguish Bose quasiparticle occupation, atom
   occupation including quantum depletion, and homogeneous Bogoliubov
   depletion. Translation: their excitation energy is our `Omega_k`;
   their `mc^2` is our `eta=g2 n0`. Project implication: **conditional** on
   a weakly interacting, approximately equilibrated scalar condensate.
   The paper does not prove that this project's cosmology reaches it.
   Our raw-energy exponents are derived from the displayed DOS Jacobian.
