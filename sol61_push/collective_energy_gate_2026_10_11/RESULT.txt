MEANING
Even a feasible angular-momentum-preserving orbit mixture cannot be reached
from these IC-B reservoirs by closed conservative collective evolution with
static baryons and the same retained cohort. At least the listed energy must
leave the cold component. This is stronger than testing the energy of one
chosen entropy-maximizing DF: it bounds ALL stationary velocity structures
with the exact specified density and boundary assumptions.
The numerical table uses the benchmark's softened field and baryon potential
quadrature. It is not an independently calibrated energy measurement.

DERIVATION
Let P_r=rho <v_r^2> and P_t=rho <v_theta^2+v_phi^2>. Spherical steady Jeans:
  dP_r/dr +(2P_r-P_t)/r = -rho g.
Multiply by4pi r^3 and integrate over[r_in,R]:
  2K = integral r g dM +4pi[R^3 P_r(R)-r_in^3 P_r(r_in)].
A natural outer cutoff of the target has P_r(R)=0. The elastic inner wall
has nonnegative pressure, hence
  K <= (1/2) integral r g dM.
At fixed density and static baryons, W is fixed. Therefore
  E_final <= Emax = W +(1/2) integral r g dM.
Closed energy conservation requires E_initial<=Emax. If not,
  Q_export >= E_initial-Emax >0.
This retains the inner-wall virial term; using the zero-boundary identity as
an equality after adding wall-impact orbits would be wrong.

For the benchmark softened shell force, the symmetric pair potential is
  u(r,s)=-1/sqrt(max(r,s)^2+eps^2).
For a continuous spherical distribution this gives
  W_cc=-integral M(<r)/sqrt(r^2+eps^2) dM
      =(1/2) integral Phi_c dM.
The baryonic interaction is W_cb=integral Phi_b dM, counted once.
The code checks pair-force differentiation, the independent half-potential
sum, and the unsoftened uniform-sphere value-3M^2/(5R). Doubling self-energy
and reversing the force sign fail their mutation checks.

LIMITS AND NEXT PHYSICAL OBLIGATION
IC-B is a prescribed benchmark reservoir, not a cosmologically derived
initial condition. These results do not prove candidate B fails, nor do they
identify the energy destination. Options still requiring actual derivation:
- transfer to responsive baryons or an external gravitational field;
- energy exported by escaping mass, with the retained mass/supply recalculated;
- a regular dark-energy receiver with complete local conservation.
Letting mass escape changes the cohort and is outside this closed test.
Time-dependent baryons exchange energy; they cannot be treated as a static
potential while also serving as an uncounted receiver.

IC-C has E_initial-Emax<0 (about-.50 to-.64 M V_f^2). It is NOT excluded by
this upper-bound test, but neither is it shown to settle. Its lower energy,
full invariants, wall pressure and kinetic support still need joint testing.
No claim that the saved finite orbit witnesses exactly realize the continuum
density; this energy bound applies to the intended exact target. Density
relaxations could change the bound and would require a separate assessment.
The baryon potential is inherited CFG544 tabulation with its finite tail;
refinement here tests mass quadrature only, not that tabulation's error.
The large positive margins are numerical evidence within that benchmark,
not interval-certified statements about every physical halo.

DECISION
Spherical collective relaxation remains a possible internal process, but
cannot REPLACE the energy-export mechanism for the benchmark diffuse supply.
Prioritize a conservative receiver/escape calculation before a long evolution
claiming dissipation-free formation. This restores the original framework's
missing energy channel as an essential obligation rather than an optional
addition. No alpha derived, no raw-data verdict, kappa remains fitted.
