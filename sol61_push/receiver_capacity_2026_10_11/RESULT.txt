REGULARITY IS NOT REACHABILITY: EXACT RECEIVER-CAPACITY TEST
2026-10-11. Base9c7e7cb2e. Analytic continuation, self-reviewed.
No numerical run, new fitted constant, raw-data verdict or closure claim.

QUESTION
Can the regular pair bracket actually finish a transfer requiring energy
from its canonical receiver? Solve its simplest channel exactly before
promoting the construction into a physical model.

DECLARED CHANNEL
Take E_c(q)=Dq, D>0, receiver potential constant, E_receiver=pi^2/2+V0,
S(q)=-(q-q_target)^2/2, and constant m>0. These are a diagnostic channel,
NOT a proposed physical action or a choice of the theory's parameters.
Let q0<q_target, A=q_target-q0>0, and pi0!=0. The previously constructed
bracket yields
  qdot=m pi^2(q_target-q),
  pidot=-m pi D(q_target-q).
Total energy is conserved exactly, so
  pi^2=pi0^2-2D(q-q0).
Write x=q-q0, L=pi0^2/(2D). Then
  xdot=2mD(L-x)(A-x).
Thus the receiver has enough available kinetic energy to reach the desired
endpoint only if L>=A, or equivalently pi0^2/2>=D(q_target-q0).

EXACT SOLUTION AND THREE OUTCOMES
For A!=L, integration gives
  (A-x)/(L-x)=(A/L) exp[2mD(A-L)t].
This equation, its derivative and its t=0 value recover the ODE and IC.
For A=L,
  x=A-A/(1+2mDA t).
Consequently:
- L>A: x approaches A, with late exponential rate2mD(L-A).
- L<A: x approaches L instead. The receiver momentum tends to zero;
  the requested target is never reached, despite smooth equations and
  positive entropy production at every finite time.
- L=A: x approaches A only algebraically, with remaining deficit~1/t.
For pi0=0 the exchange is stationary from the outset in this channel.
There is no finite-time crossing of the energy-exhaustion boundary.
The asymptotic rate depends on receiver energy above the required threshold,
not just the cold gravitational dynamical time. Rescaling m rescales time
without changing any invariant or endpoint, so normalization is still free.

SELF-CHECKS AND SCOPE
At x=0 the integrated ratio is A/L as required. Differentiating its logarithm
gives2mD(A-L), including both orderings of A and L. At equal A,L the separate
rational solution has xdot=2mD(A-x)^2. In all cases0<=x<=min(A,L), pi^2>=0,
and Edot=D qdot+pi pidot=0. The exact mathematical threshold is not based on
numerical labels. For D<0, advancing q releases cold energy into the receiver;
the positive-D capacity obstruction does not transfer unchanged.
A varying potential V(phi), spatial energy influx, additional exchange modes,
and cold Hamiltonian transport are outside this reduced channel. They may
replenish capacity, but must be accounted for explicitly. The flat receiver
potential allows canonical phidot=pi without altering this energy balance;
there is no suppressed source of potential energy in the calculation.

IMPLICATION FOR THE BENCHMARKS
The IC-C heating requirement established in collective_energy_lower is
.496-.638 M_target V_f^2. If the receiver is flat-potential, local and has
no replenishing flux, at least that much available receiver energy is needed.
The enormous constant vacuum offset V0 does NOT count as available kinetic
energy: it cancels from energy differences and supplies no force. A receiver
at rest with only a constant vacuum offset cannot heat the cold halo through
this regular bracket. Setting a large V0 does not fix that failure.
No numerical statement about the actual receiver capacity is possible until
its state, potential and accessible volume are derived. No effective-w
measurement is silently converted into local scalar kinetic energy.

DECISION
The regular bracket is a valid structural building block, but no full
settling solution follows from it. A useful next model must derive both
available energy and replenishment, and then calculate the transfer history.
One may obtain different rates in different systems from different receiver
states, but assigning those states separately to match data would add knobs.
This route remains conditional, with precisely identified missing physics:
receiver dynamics and coupling normalization, together with kinetic target,
supply, local conservation and the required framework-native data tests.
