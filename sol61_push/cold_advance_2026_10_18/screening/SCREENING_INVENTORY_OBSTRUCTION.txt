Normalized claim and verdict
The proposed rule is to multiply the canonical retained draw by CFG691's
S(2 rM r/L^2), while retaining a conserved cold stock that can also populate
a free component. As an exact field-law identity of the current full-W
action, this rule is REFUTED: both the constitutive law and the source
geometry differ from the point-source cubic problem defining S. A value at
one aperture may coincide accidentally; this audit does not rule out every
phenomenological multiplier or every occupancy/stress extension.

A second, restricted result is proved below: the full-W static branch,
active at every exterior radius with nonnegative settled density, cannot
carry globally finite total settled cold mass in the gravitational field of
a nonzero finite baryonic source. Finite stiffness does not remove this
finite-stock obstruction. A physical occupancy/interface/stress rule remains
necessary. This is not a no-go for all versions of the original theory.

1. Objects, conventions and dependency graph
K=4 pi G; P=P_rad(r) rhat, u=K P_rad>=0, g=Phi'(r)>=0.
Mt(r)=r^2 u(r)/G is the identified settled enclosed mass; this identification
is CONDITIONAL on the project's carrier dictionary, not a particle derivation.
Mb(r), Mf(r) are baryonic and free enclosed masses; b=G Mb/r^2,
f=G Mf/r^2. h=gamma/K=L^2/2>0. The domain is 0<=u<a0.
Delta_v u=u''+2u'/r-2u/r^2 is the radial VECTOR Laplacian.

Relevant static action, with W_full written as a function of u:
 Lstat= -|grad Phi|^2/(2K) + P.grad Phi - W_full(u)
        -gamma |grad P|^2/2 -(rho_b+rho_f) Phi,
 K dW_full/du = a0 u/(a0-u).
This is the corrected full-W branch recorded in TWO_POPULATION_RESULT.txt;
the original CFG694 completed-square cubic is a different constitutive choice.
Free sources are held fixed in the field variation. Their stationary phase
space distribution and exchange with the settled population are not supplied.

Variation in Phi, with isolated spherical flux and no extra central charge:
 g-u=b+f. Variation in P gives
 g=a0 u/(a0-u)-h Delta_v u.
Therefore the coupled field equation is
 (1) -h Delta_v u+R(u)=b+f,  R(u)=u^2/(a0-u).
The ordinary gravitational acceleration is g=b+f+u. Adding Mt as a second
ordinary source on top of this already identified polarization charge would
double count it.

Dependency graph:

2. What CFG691 actually solves
The cubic completed-square action has
 K W_cubic,u=u+u^2/a0, hence R_cubic(u)=u^2/a0.
For an isolated POINT mass M and no free source its equation is
 (2) -h Delta_v u+u^2/a0=GM/r^2.
Let rM=sqrt(GM/a0), ell=h/rM=L^2/(2rM), s=r/ell,
u=sqrt(GMa0) q(s)/ell. Then equation (2) reduces exactly to
 -[q''+2q'/s-2q/s^2]+q|q|=1/s^2,
with inner q=1/2+A s+O(s^2), outer q~1/s. CFG691 defines S(s)=s q(s),
so its screened acceleration is sqrt(GMa0) S(s)/r.
There is no canonical full-W interpolation or extended Plummer source in this
reduction. Using the same numerical S does not change its defining equation.

Even reusing the cubic field u itself in (1) leaves the extra positive
constitutive residual
 R_full(u)-R_cubic(u)=u^3/[a0(a0-u)]>0 for 0<u<a0.
The specific proposed multiplication uses u_can S, which is a different field
again; it is tested directly next.

3. Exact local obstructions to the radial multiplier
For every real k, Delta_v r^k=(k+2)(k-1)r^(k-2).

POINT source, no free component: the zero-stiffness canonical field is
 u_can=a0*2/[1+sqrt(1+4r^2/rM^2)] -> a0 as r->0.
Since S(s)=s/2+O(s^2), the multiplied field obeys
 u_proxy=u_can S(2rM r/L^2)
        =(a0 rM/L^2) r+O(r^2).
Its vector Laplacian has no r^-2 term: the leading r term is annihilated.
The left side of (1) is bounded at the origin, whereas the required right
side is GM/r^2. Thus this proxy cannot solve (1) for any fixed L>0.
An actual bounded solution to the point-source equation must instead have
u(0)=GM/L^2 if its inner expansion is constant+O(r). Compatibility with the
full-W domain then additionally requires L>rM for this bounded expansion.
No assertion about a singular saturated alternative is made here.

REGULAR PLUMMER source of total baryonic mass Mb and scale a:
 b=G Mb r/(r^2+a^2)^(3/2)=b1 r+O(r^3), b1=G Mb/a^3>0.
The zero-gradient canonical u_can~sqrt(a0 b1) r^(1/2).
The proposed S, with any fixed positive total or aperture rM, scales as r.
Therefore u_proxy=C r^(3/2)+higher powers, C>0, and
 -h Delta_v u_proxy~-(7/4)h C r^(-1/2)<0,
while R(u_proxy)=O(r^3) and the required source is +b1 r.
The signs and powers disagree. The smooth finite-stiffness interior instead
admits u=A r-[b1/(10h)]r^3+O(r^4); A is selected by the full boundary problem.
This is a direct obstruction to the radial proxy, independent of data scores.
It does not prohibit an accidental equality at one measurement aperture.

4. Conserved stock is not an independent additional force
Assume the identified-carrier dictionary, ordinary Poisson gravity, and a
fixed original cold stock C0. A complete inventory has
 C0=Mt(infinity)+Mf(infinity)+M_other+M_lost,
where every additional category must be defined and counted once.
Nonnegative Mt alone is insufficient: rho_t>=0 requires Mt'>=0; similarly
Mf>=0 and Mf'>=0 are separate obligations.

In the optimistic one-aperture bookkeeping of CE-FM-077, write C0=t0+F0,
where t0 is the counted settled draw and F0 the complementary stock. If an
actual exchange changes t0 to t_lambda=lambda t0, exact conservation demands
 F_lambda=F0+(1-lambda)t0
or an explicit remaining-stock category receiving that difference. If eta is
the enclosed fraction of the new free stock, the aperture cold mass is
 t_lambda+eta[C0-t_lambda].
This formula is a bookkeeping identity, not a field solution or a transport
law. That free mass also changes f in (1), so lambda cannot remain a fixed
baryons-only response without a further coupling derivation.

Keeping the old physical settled mass t0 but suppressing only its gravity
conflicts with Mt=r^2u/G in this branch. Keeping the old free stock F0 and
also adding the removed draw as a fresh mass source while retaining t0 in
the gravitational source counts the same stock twice. If the removed draw
is instead merely unoccupied/diffuse, its location and gravitational source
must be tracked; it cannot be silently deleted.

For a prescribed TOTAL enclosed cold mass Mc(r), set s=G Mc/r^2 and f=s-u.
Equation (1) becomes
 (3) -h Delta_vu+a0u/(a0-u)=b+s.
It still needs Mt<=Mc and Mf'=Mc'-Mt'>=0. Equation (3) is a useful inverse
static discriminator given independently prescribed total profiles; it is
not a mechanism selecting those profiles.

5. Finite-stock obstruction, including noncompact tails
Precise hypotheses:
 - equation (1) holds for all r>=r0, h>0 and a0>0;
 - u is C^2 there, 0<=u<a0, and m(r)=r^2u/G;
 - settled density is nonnegative: m'(r)>=0;
 - settled mass is bounded: m(r)<=C0<infinity;
 - enclosed free mass Mf(r)>=0;
 - baryonic enclosed mass Mb(r)>=M_*>0 beyond r0.
No derivative limit at infinity is assumed. No finite-support assumption is
needed. These hypotheses are narrower than every possible cold theory.

Direct differentiation gives
 Delta_vu=(G/r^2)[m''-2m'/r].
Multiply (1) by r^2/G:
 (4) -h[m''-2m'/r]+Gm^2/(a0r^2-Gm)=Mb+Mf.
Bounded m makes the nonlinear term tend uniformly to zero:
 0<=Gm^2/(a0r^2-Gm)<=GC0^2/(a0r^2-GC0)->0.
Choose r1 large enough that the denominator is positive and this term is
at most M_*/2. Equation (4) then implies, for all r>=r1,
 m''-2m'/r<=-M_*/(2h).
Set w=m'/r^2>=0. Therefore
 w'<=-M_*/(2h r^2).
For any R>r>=r1 integrate this inequality:
 w(r)>=w(R)+(M_*/2h)(1/r-1/R)
      >=(M_*/2h)(1/r-1/R).
Let R->infinity in this LOWER BOUND (no limit of w(R) is needed):
 w(r)>=M_*/(2h r), hence m'(r)>=M_* r/(2h).
Integrating this last inequality forces m(r) to grow at least quadratically,
contradicting m<=C0. Thus no solution satisfies all stated hypotheses.

The argument's quadratic growth is a contradiction consequence of assuming
bounded m; it is not the asymptotic prediction of the unconstrained branch.
The actual unsupplied isolated response has u~sqrt(GMb a0)/r and m~r.
Stiffness only changes its subleading terms.

For the simple finite-support exterior, m and Mf are constant and
Delta_vu=0 exactly. Equation (4) reduces to
 Gm^2/(a0r^2-Gm)=Mb+Mf,
which cannot hold on an interval with Mb+Mf>0. This elementary special case
is consistent with the general tail proof.

Consequences: a finite-stock model must leave at least one of these explicit
hypotheses, for example via an occupancy/interface term, additional material
stress, a different mass dictionary, or a different exterior constitutive
law. Naming such a possibility does not derive or validate it. Abruptly
truncating P also needs care: its divergence includes boundary contributions,
so positive enclosed mass cannot simply disappear without a counted interface.

6. Boundary and comparison facts for a bounded response calculation
For an UNSUPPLIED isolated full-W source with total mass M, let
A=sqrt(GMa0). The large-r formal expansion is
 u=A/r-[GM+a0 L^2]/(2r^2)+O(r^-3).
This follows by matching the r^-2 and r^-3 coefficients in (1), f=0.
It is a useful artificial outer boundary for local response studies; its
Mt grows linearly and it cannot certify a finite physical stock.

On a finite interval with fixed admissible boundary values and 0<=u<a0,
(1) is the EL equation of the normalized functional
 J[u]=integral dr r^2 {h/2[(u')^2+2u^2/r^2]+V(u)-(b+f)u},
 V(u)=-u^2/2-a0u-a0^2 log(1-u/a0),
 V'=R, V''=u(2a0-u)/(a0-u)^2>=0.
The positive h quadratic gives strict convexity on a fixed-BC admissible
domain and therefore at most one minimizer/regular solution. Existence,
endpoint singularities and density positivity are separate checks.

With the SAME fixed endpoint values, adding nonnegative free source cannot
lower the interior u. For two solutions the difference delta satisfies
 -h[delta''+2delta'/r-2delta/r^2]+c(r)delta=f2-f1>=0,
where c is a nonnegative secant of increasing R. A negative interior minimum
would have delta'=0, delta''>=0, making the left side strictly negative due
to +2h delta/r^2. This contradicts its nonnegative right side. Thus delta>=0.
The argument requires comparison-compatible boundary conditions; arbitrary
different finite-stock outer conditions do not inherit this comparison.
