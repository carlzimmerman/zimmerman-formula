2. The missing scattering dictionary is concrete
Use kappa_T=sigma_T/m with sigma_T=int(1-cos theta) d sigma/d Omega d Omega.
Heat transport may use sigma_V=int sin^2(theta) d sigma/d Omega d Omega,
and identical-particle treatments can use int(1-|cos theta|)d sigma.

2sigma/3 and sigma/2. A classical perfectly reflecting hard sphere does give

3. Analytic fixed-stock transport bound
Set M=C0=53.64 Mb, use no extra halo stock, and choose stationary Newtonian
Plummer cold and stellar components with scales b and a=.75r12 respectively:
 Phi(r)=-GM/sqrt(r^2+b^2)-G Mb/sqrt(r^2+a^2),
 rho(r)=3M b^2/[4pi(r^2+b^2)^(5/2)],
 M(<r)=M r^3/(r^2+b^2)^(3/2).
The cold terms satisfy dPhi_cold/dr=GM(<r)/r^2 and dM/dr=4pi r^2 rho.
The stars obey the same identities with M->Mb, b->a, and Mstar(<r12)=.512Mb,
matching the source aperture convention. Include their compact potential even
though Mb is much smaller than the full cold stock: at b=10r12 their central
potential depth is .24857 times the cold depth, increasing maximum speed by
sqrt(1+.24857)=1.11740. Parent review identified this correction before the
final report. The cold-only run001 is preserved and remains separately valid.
For particles individually bound in this potential, speed<sqrt(-2Phi).
Thus every pair obeys v_rel<=2v_esc(0)=sqrt[8G(M/b+Mb/a)], and
rho<=rho0=3M/(4pi b^3).
Grant a constant monodisperse transport kappa equal to the geometric estimate.
The collision hazard anywhere is bounded by
 Gamma <= Gamma_max = kappa [3M/(4pi b^3)] sqrt[8G(M/b+Mb/a)].
Over a fixed age T, maximum exposure is Lambda_max=Gamma_max T. Therefore a
NECESSARY, deliberately generous condition for one collision exposure is
 b <= b1, where kappa [3M/(4pi b1^3)] sqrt[8G(M/b1+Mb/a)] T=1.
The left side decreases strictly with b1>0; run002 uses a bounded 180-step
log-space bisection to invert it for exposures1,10,100. This is an upper bound
on collision exposure, NOT a collapse-time formula. Many collisions can still
produce expansion rather than central collapse. Initial profile, orbit and
conductive boundary determine the direction of energy flow.

4. Why current dense endpoints do not establish a formation route
For a homogeneous global-virial snapshot, taking a sphere radius a and
sigma_1D^2=GM/(5a), a Maxwell velocity proxy gives

It has the same M^(3/2)a^(-7/2) scaling as the COLD-ONLY Plummer bound, but
a rate smaller than that bound by sqrt(5pi/2)=2.8025. This is a secondary
cold-only scale estimate, not
a homogeneous sphere equilibrium claim. Draco at a=10r12 then needs139.45
cm2/g for one expected collision per particle in10Gyr.

Using instead the present REQUIRED cold aperture mass, its mean density inside

sigma, gives exposures at the .5041 cap of5.208/2.482/.945/.471 for

this calculation. Fornax/LeoI endpoint exposures are.659/2.591; neither those
numbers nor the fixed-b ranking selects the required collapse/expansion phases.
