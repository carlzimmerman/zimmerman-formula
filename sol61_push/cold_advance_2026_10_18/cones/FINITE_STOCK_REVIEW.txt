Claim card
Let G,a0,h>0. On an unbounded exterior interval [r0,infinity), with r0 chosen
positive if necessary, let u be C2, 0<=u<a0, and set m=r^2u/G. Assume m'>=0
and m<=C0<infinity. Suppose nonnegative enclosed free mass Mf and a baryonic
enclosed mass Mb>=M_*>0 obey the corrected full-W equation at every exterior
radius:

 -h[u''+2u'/r-2u/r^2]+u^2/(a0-u)=G[Mb(r)+Mf(r)]/r^2.       (A)

Then no such u exists. The conclusion covers noncompact settled tails. It
assumes neither a limit of m' nor a limit of m'/r^2 at infinity.

Actual source definition
The source in (A) is Mb+Mf. The polarization/settled quantity m is already
represented by u in the Poisson flux relation

 g-u=G(Mb+Mf)/r^2.

It is not an independent additional ordinary source in this same equation.
Adding m to Mb+Mf would silently change the mathematical problem and double
count the identified polarization charge under this action's dictionary.
Conversely, equating m to an inertial conserved cold population is a separate
physical dictionary assumption. The theorem is a conditional consequence of
that assumption, not a derivation of the carrier's stress or microscopic mass.
The older cubic law has a different nonlinear residual; this audit is of the
full-W residual u^2/(a0-u), exactly as requested.

Independent derivation of the mass equation
From u=Gm/r^2, direct differentiation gives

 u'=G(m'/r^2-2m/r^3),
 u''=G(m''/r^2-4m'/r^3+6m/r^4),
 u''+2u'/r-2u/r^2=G(m''-2m'/r)/r^2.

Also

 (r^2/G) u^2/(a0-u)=Gm^2/(a0r^2-Gm).

Thus (A) is exactly

 -h(m''-2m'/r)+N(r)=Mb(r)+Mf(r),
 N(r)=Gm(r)^2/[a0r^2-Gm(r)].                              (B)

This matches equation (4) of the raw source, including the vector-Laplacian
-2u/r^2 term and both normalizations. Using a scalar radial Laplacian instead
would give a different mass equation and would not audit this claim.

Independent decisive implication
Nonnegative u and G give m>=0. With m<=C0, for all sufficiently large r,

 0<=N(r)<=GC0^2/(a0r^2-GC0) -> 0.

Choose r1 so that a0r1^2>GC0 and N(r)<=M_*/2 for r>=r1. The right side of
(B) is at least M_*, so, writing c=M_*/(2h)>0,

 m''-2m'/r <= -c.

The exact derivative identity

 [m'/r^2]'=(m''-2m'/r)/r^2

then gives w'= [m'/r^2]' <=-c/r^2. Since m'>=0, w(R)>=0 for every R. For
R>r>=r1 integration therefore yields

 w(r)>=w(R)+c(1/r-1/R)>=c(1/r-1/R).

The left side is fixed as R varies. Taking the supremum of the rightmost
lower bound over R, without assuming convergence of w(R), gives w(r)>=c/r.
Consequently

 m'(r)>=cr,
 m(R)>=m(r1)+c(R^2-r1^2)/2.

This contradicts m<=C0. The limit step and sign of the reverse integration
are correct. The quadratic lower bound is a contradiction under the bounded-m
hypothesis, not an unconditional asymptotic prediction of (A).

Restrictions and strongest safe statement
Only this unbounded-radius static branch with the stipulated source and
positive settled-density dictionary is excluded. A finite-radius occupied
model is not ruled out, but it must specify where equation (A), the dictionary,
or another explicit hypothesis changes, and account for its interface stress
and stock. Simply truncating a solution and continuing to impose all the
same exterior hypotheses cannot evade the contradiction. This audit does not
assert that changing any named hypothesis supplies a consistent completion.
No finite upper bound on Mf was needed; nonnegative free mass only strengthens
the contradiction. Likewise finite total baryonic mass is not used once the
positive exterior lower bound M_* is assumed; it is a sufficient physical
scenario for that lower bound, not an extra theorem conclusion.

Remaining gap
There is no internal proof gap in the audited theorem. The separate physical
gap is a finite-stock action/interface/phase whose equations cease to obey at
least one audited hypothesis and whose sources are counted once. That is the
cheapest next discriminating theoretical obligation; a baryons-only response
plus a freely added cold reservoir does not resolve it in this same action.
