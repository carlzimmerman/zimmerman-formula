1. A finite cold stock requires a real exterior change

The full rational constitutive law and the pure cubic approximation differ.
Writing u=4piG P_rad, h=L^2/2, b=GMb(<r)/r^2 and f=GMf(<r)/r^2 gives

 -h [u''+2u'/r-2u/r^2] + u^2/(a0-u) = b+f,  0<=u<a0.        (1)

The cubic point-source factor from CFG691 is not an identity for this law or
an extended Plummer source. In particular, its naive radial multiplication
has the wrong regular-origin power and residual sign. Equality at a single
aperture would not fix that equation-level mismatch.

A precise obstruction is now proved: assume (1) holds at every sufficiently
large radius, identify settled mass m=r^2u/G, require m'>=0 and m<=C0<infinity,
and retain nonnegative enclosed free mass plus a positive baryonic enclosed
mass. No solution satisfies all these hypotheses. This includes noncompact
finite-mass tails and does not assume a limit of the density derivative.

The key mass equation is

 -h(m''-2m'/r) + Gm^2/(a0r^2-Gm) = Mb+Mf.

Bounded m makes the nonlinear term tend to zero. Positive exterior source then
forces [m'/r^2]'<=-c/r^2 for c>0. Nonnegative m' gives m'>=cr at large r,
contradicting bounded mass. The apparent quadratic growth is a contradiction
under the bounded-m assumption, not the predicted unsupplied asymptotic tail.

Consequently, stiffness alone cannot provide a finite physical halo by cutting
off the usual response. A proposed occupied phase/interface, additional stress
or changed exterior law must show explicitly which hypothesis changes and how
stock and boundary terms are conserved. This does not exclude such extensions.

Inventory is also reciprocal: reducing a settled draw t to lambda*t requires
the complementary stock to change from F to F+(1-lambda)t, or another counted
category. Its gravity then feeds back into (1). A freely added reservoir and
an unchanged original response are not a coupled field solution.

 -d [U_xx+2U_x/x-2U/x^2] + U^2/(1-U)
       = R^2 x/(1+x^2)^(3/2).

4. A bounded causal effective response can be constructed explicitly

For a varied timelike clock theta and scalar response phi on a fixed matter
metric, define X=-dtheta.dtheta>0, u^mu=-d^mu theta/sqrt(X),
y^2=(g^{mu nu}+u^mu u^nu)d_mu phi d_nu phi, and V=u.dphi.
Consider the separately proposed effective action

 L=P(X)+(Z0+2gamma*y)V^2/2-gamma*y^3/3,
 P(X)=kappa*X/2+Delta*(X-X0)^2/(8X0).

The background is theta=sqrt(X0)*t, phi=y*x, with gamma,Z0,X0,y>0.
The exact necessary and sufficient local conditions for positive quadratic
derivative energy and all-direction characteristic speeds 0<c^2<=1 are

 kappa > (Z0+gamma*y)y^2/X0,
 Delta >= gamma*(Z0+gamma*y)y^3/(Z0*X0).

The radial direction is proved to be the worst direction. The new clock
compressibility Delta can satisfy these conditions on any fixed compact
nonzero gradient band; increasing only the response temporal coefficient
cannot cure the canonical Delta=0 obstruction in this class.

An exact example is gamma=Z0=X0=1, kappa=Delta=3, 0<y<=1. At y=1 the radial
speed squares are (19+-sqrt(217))/36, both strictly between zero and one.
The added operator and its first derivative vanish at X=X0, preserving the
background clock stress/current and static cubic response on that branch.

This is a constructive effective-model result with a new independent
coefficient. It does not derive that coefficient from the actual carrier,
extend through the degenerate cubic vacuum, control arbitrary gradients,
prove nonlinear/global causality, or supply a gravitational metric/lensing
solution. The separate scalar-function rational-W extension in the note is
also conditional and does not inherit the original polarization/stock action.
