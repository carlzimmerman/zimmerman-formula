A continuum test for distant-reservoir explanations
2026-10-11. Frozen criteria b181a252d; 207/207 checks passed.

This is a new route relative to the examined local Bullet operator work,
not a claim of global mathematical novelty. It avoids choosing a halo
profile or discretizing an arbitrary exterior density into a finite basis.

PROOF
Let K(x)=x/|x|^2 in the angular lens plane. For x,y nonzero,
|K(x)-K(y)| = |x-y|/(|x||y|).
Squaring and expanding proves the identity; the symbolic check is exact.
If a positive mass coefficient measure db(z) is supported outside a disk
of angular radius R, and B=integral db, then for images theta_i,theta_j
inside the disk, triangle inequality gives

 |alpha_i-alpha_j| <= B |theta_i-theta_j|/[(R-ri)(R-rj)],
 ri=|theta_i|, rj=|theta_j|, with coordinates about the disk centre.

Indeed |theta_i-z|>=R-ri at every allowed mass point; integrate the kernel
identity against the positive measure. This covers arbitrary exterior
azimuthal structure and arbitrarily fine positive mass distributions.
Finite B and strictly positive separations ensure the integrals are bounded.
The disk bound is generally conservative; the exact single-point distance
identity can be saturated, which does not establish sharpness for all disks.

For a same-feature image pair with source factor w, let delta be its
observed separation minus the deflection difference of all fixed interior
and other declared components. The necessary angular mass bound is

 B >= |delta| (R-ri)(R-rj)/(w |theta_i-theta_j|).

Here B=4GM/(c^2 Dl) in radians squared; angular arcsecond calculations
include the square of arcseconds per radian. Maximize over image pairs.
This is an exterior-support test, not a density or formation prescription.
Unrestricted mass near an image evades it, consistent with the earlier
positive-clump construction. Positivity and excluded support are essential.

HOW THIS OPENS A DOOR
For each proposed derived reservoir: specify its excluded region and total
available mass. Subtract the footing-specific baryon-plus-settled prediction
in the same operator. Apply the inequality before expensive forward modelling.
If the resulting necessary bound exceeds the independently accounted shared
budget, that support proposal cannot work. Passing remains merely necessary.
This generalizes the earlier spherical aperture projection bound to image-pair
deflections and arbitrary positive exterior structure. It is useful even
when no radial reservoir profile has been derived. Interior component
uncertainties and image-position errors must be bounded before a robust veto;
the present demonstration has not done that minimization.
