NONLINEAR ACTIVATION: SPECULATIVE RESEARCH TARGET, NOT AN ENGINE
15 October 2026. Self-review only.

The ambitious target is a controllable local dark-field phase, rather than
a weak propeller operating at unchanged ambient density. Two distinct routes
must not be conflated: (A) parametric amplification of a preexisting occupied
collective mode; (B) a locally field-induced change of scalar ground state.
Neither has a demonstrated electromagnetic coupling or human-feasible window.

A. PARAMETRIC ACTIVATION
Assume a genuine dark mode, confinement/feedback, and a portal allowing an
external EM pump to modulate its restoring coefficient:
 a_ddot+(omega0/Qd) a_dot+omega0^2[1+h cos(2omega0 t)]a=0.
For small h and high Qd near exact resonance, amplitude growth
 s approximately omega0[h/4-1/(2Qd)]. Threshold h Qd>2.
The pump supplies energy. Qd is the DARK mode quality factor including escape,
dephasing and absorption; it cannot be replaced by an EM resonator's Q.

checks.py integrates a full fundamental matrix over one pump period and
computes its Floquet eigenvalues for Qd=1000 and h=0,.001,.002,.02.
The zero and .001 cases decay; .02 grows consistently with the approximation.
Liouville determinant exp(-pi/Qd) checked in all four cases. The .002 example
lies near the leading-order threshold; the approximation is not exact there.
At ASSIGNED f0=84 Hz, h=.02, Qd=1000, amplitude e-folding time is about .421 s.
These are normalized oscillator examples, not measured or predicted dark
parameters. No nonlinear saturation, mode identification or density increase
has been established. Parametric growth does not remove the earlier weak-wave
momentum-capacity obstruction at fixed background density.

B. SWITCHABLE PHASE AS A MORE RADICAL TARGET
For a canonical scalar candidate one may postulate, in natural units,
 V_eff(r;I)=rho_v+[m0^2-chi I(x,t)] r^2/2+lambda r^4/4, lambda>0.
I denotes a controlled signed EM invariant, not automatically beam intensity;
chi has the units required to shift m^2. This is a hypothesis to constrain.
At infinite homogeneous volume, a negative quadratic coefficient changes the
minimum to r^2=(chi I-m0^2)/lambda. That condensate need NOT be the conserved,
rotating cold phase in our earlier construction. Its charge, equation of state,
gradients and actual material mass content must be computed independently.

Finite size matters: inside an ideal radius-R sphere with Dirichlet boundary,
the lowest linear relativistic mode has omega^2=m_eff^2+(pi/R)^2 in natural
units. Actual instability needs chi I>m0^2+(pi/R)^2, not merely m_eff^2<0.
The corresponding one-metre gradient energy is pi hbar c/R=6.20e-7 eV.
Actual boundaries are not known dark mirrors; different leakage or external
matching changes this spectrum. This is a conditional finite-domain example.
Wall energy, switching work, pump backreaction and restoring the apparatus
must be included. A local reduction of the potential does not give a free
cyclic vacuum-energy source. No coefficients or threshold fields derived here.

C. PROPULSION REQUIRES A SEPARATE MOMENTUM SOLUTION
An eventual system would prepare a state, pump energy into it, and release a
directed wake or packet. All outgoing components, including idlers and pump
radiation, must be counted. Uniform parametric production of opposite momenta
alone gives zero net thrust. A directional release needs an actual solved
coupling/boundary mechanism; asymmetric openings alone are not proof.

Collective waves in an existing medium may have P_out/F=v_phase<c. Newly
created free massive particles obey total E/p>=c and cannot beat photon thrust
per supplied energy when their rest-mass preparation cost is included. Captured
preexisting dark propellant would instead need real capture, cooling, storage
and a mass-throughput budget. No one of these capabilities follows from gain.

For nonlinear-state proposal B the concrete theoretical target is one set of
coefficients simultaneously giving an allowed portal, finite-size activation,
a stable controllable state and useful total outgoing momentum. Assigning a
convenient negative mass or high Q is not a solution of that target.
