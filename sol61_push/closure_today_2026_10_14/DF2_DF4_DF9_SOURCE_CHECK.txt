Observed result versus interpretation
Keim et al., A Third Galaxy Missing Dark Matter along a Trail of Galaxies
in the NGC 1052 Field, arXiv:2603.15860v2, revised 15 June 2026, ApJ 1004,
210: https://arxiv.org/html/2603.15860v2
The paper reports DF9 sigma=6.5(+3.9,-4.3) km/s versus stars-only
8.3(+0.9,-1.4), and its abstract compares with a standard-halo estimate
24+-3. DF2/DF4/DF9 have dynamical masses consistent with their stellar
masses. This constrains mass within the measured region, not an exact
proof of zero dark matter at every radius. DF9's deficiency was a prior
prediction of the bullet-dwarf scenario. It is not unique evidence for
the Zimmerman framework. The inspected v1 differs numerically; use v2.

van Dokkum et al., Nature 605,435–439 (2022), DOI
10.1038/s41586-022-04665-6, https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-022-04665-6
The proposed collision separates shocked gas from collisionless dark mass;
the gas forms new dwarfs. Its approximately 8-Gyr reconstruction uses the
assumed three-dimensional geometry and average post-collision velocity.
It is a model-dependent formation history, not a measured lower bound on
the cold-population clock for every trail member. Stellar age alone does
not prove the same duration of assembly or availability of external supply.

Tang et al., New Measurements of Distances to Galaxies in the NGC 1052
Field with the Hubble and James Webb Space Telescopes: Testing the
Bullet-Dwarf Origin of the Trail, arXiv:2606.05144v1, 3 June 2026:
https://arxiv.org/html/2606.05144v1
Table 1 and sections IV/V give DF2 JWST TRGB 17.6+-0.6 Mpc, compared with
the earlier HST 21.7+-1.2; DF4 HST 20.0+-1.6, SBF18.8+-1.7;
DF9 SBF18.7+-1.5. The relative-distance ordering remains uncertain.
If the high-radial-velocity DF2 is closer than DF4/DF9, straightforward
backtracking gives increasing past separation, challenging a simple common
collision unless later gravitational deflections matter. Alternatively a
robust relative HST offset could shift the entire trail to the near side.
The authors retain dark-matter deficiency but request uniform precise
distances. Therefore the old collision geometry and >=6-Gyr clock start
are conditional and should be bracketed before a global assembly exclusion.

What these galaxies add to the next mechanism
An elapsed-time rule alone cannot explain halo availability. The proposed
collision points to separable baryons and a cold reservoir, with inherited
supply plus later capture/transport setting the halo. This is an inference
to test, not a derived law or unique explanation. A rule M_supply=5.364 M_b
for every newly formed bound dwarf is particularly suspect after material
separation; supply must be measured or computed from its own assembly.
