2. Mathematical comparison
Use the conditional carrier force from CE-FM-019/021, units G=a0=1,
initial baryon mass Q0=1, supplied finite tethered-background mass S.
Its initial ground profile is m0=(sqrt(1+4r^2)-1)/2 capped at S,
edge Re=sqrt(S+S^2), outward test-carrier tether R(m/r^2),
R(u)=u^2/(1-u). Inside Re the residual force cancels Q0/r^2.
Outside, the radial acceleration is -Q0/r^2+R(S/r^2).
Its potential, normalized to zero at infinity, is
 U0(r)=-(1+S)/rr+sqrt(S)atanh(sqrt(S)/rr), rr=max(r,Re).
For S=0 use U0=-1/r. The potential is flat inside Re and continuous with
continuous first derivative at Re; outside its derivative is Q0/r^2-R.

Now prescribe positive baryon growth DeltaQ(t), keep the background fixed,
and give a negligible-mass carrier the Hamiltonian
 H=|v|^2/2+U0(r)-DeltaQ(t)/r.
Its exact source work is dH/dt=-DeltaQdot/r. Spherical growth preserves
angular momentum. If DeltaQ becomes constant, H<0 means bound motion in the
final frozen potential, which approaches zero at infinity. A time-independent
host with H>0 cannot permanently bind this same incoming test orbit.
No force proportional to velocity, radiation, deletion, reflecting core,
or fitted drift coefficient is present.

4. What this DOES NOT close
The varying source is prescribed, not obtained by varying live baryons and
their reservoir. An energy receiver can be BOOKKEPT with dE_receiver=-dH,
but that identity is not the receiver's EoM or action. Background cold carriers
were frozen although Q changes; their real response can alter the capture.
The captured mass is negligible; finite captured supply changes the tether,
edge and energy minimum. Bound motion need not cool to the exact law and
does not establish long-term collective stability. Basins, precursor charge,
hot/condensed selection, abundance, metric and field constraints stay open.

5. Reciprocal capture ledger and the cold-profile gap
From CE-FM-021/022, for point Q and supplied equilibrium S,
 dE_min/dS=-B(S,Q),
 B=(Q+S)/Re-sqrt(S)atanh(sqrt(S)/Re)>0,
 Re=sqrt(S+S^2/Q).
For a finite retained mass d arriving from infinity with total kinetic K_in,
at fixed Q, no radiation and initial equilibrium, a final no-escape state
must have excess energy
 E_exc,final=K_in+E_min(S)-E_min(S+d)
            =K_in+integral_S^(S+d)B(s,Q)ds>0.
This is the reverse bookkeeping of the prior escape result. Capturing mass
releases binding energy relative to the new minimum; it does not make that
energy disappear. Conservative energy transfer can bind a subset while
heating/rearranging the remainder. An exact cold ground profile still needs
export, an additional receiver, or non-ground kinetic/stress support.
Changing Q adds its reciprocal source work and modifies this fixed-Q ledger.
