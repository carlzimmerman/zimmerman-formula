1. Exact claim and hypotheses
Work on a spherical interval with r>0, positive differentiable baryon mass
B(r), cold target mass M0(r)>0 and no stiffness/pressure/boundary-force terms.
The full saturating static law is
 M0(M0+B) = a0 r^2 B/G.
Use gamma(r) = V0(r)/(2 pi k r),
 V0^2 = G(B+M0)/r, k>0 supplied.
The requested enclosed clock is
 Mc(r,t) = M0(r)[1-exp(-gamma(r)t)].
Available local mass density is rho0=M0'/(4pi r^2), supplied initially; it
is not a derived abundance or preparation. Conversion preserves that total
local mass. Initially untethered precursors are massive and gravitate. Their
coupling is a specified comparison, not an interaction derived from the action.

2. Full-law local clock: constructive result
For any differentiable M0 and gamma with M0'>0, define
 z=gamma t, q=-M0 gamma'/(M0' gamma).
Direct radial differentiation gives the NECESSARY local fraction
 H=Mc'/M0'=1-(1+qz)exp(-z).
Consequently an enclosed exponential is not a local exponential.
For all t>=0, a monotone fraction 0<=H<=1 exists iff 0<=q<=1:
 H_z=exp(-z)(1-q+qz).
If q>1, H_z(0)<0 and initial cold density is negative. If q<0,
H>1 for z>-1/q, exceeding local supply. For 0<=q<=1, H is a convex mixture
 (1-q)[1-exp(-z)] + q[1-(1+z)exp(-z)].
Thus split the supplied reservoir into weights 1-q and q; the first part
undergoes one conversion, the second two equal-rate conversions. Each branch
has nonnegative populations and locally conserved mass. This is an explicit
kinematic realization, not a selected branching fraction or microscopic action.

For the full law, let mu=M0/B>0 and n=r B'/B. Its logarithmic slopes are
 nc=r M0'/M0 = [(n+2)mu+2]/(2mu+1),
 nt=r(B+M0)'/(B+M0) = [n(mu+1)+2mu]/(2mu+1).
Since gamma is proportional to sqrt((B+M0)/r^3),
 q=(3-nt)/(2nc) = [(4-n)mu+3-n]/[2(n+2)mu+4].
For 0<=n<=3 its numerator is mu+(3-n)(mu+1)>0; denominator minus
numerator is 3n mu+n+1>0. Hence 0<q<1. A nonnegative nonincreasing
spherical baryon density has 0<=n<=3 because its local density does not
exceed its interior average. This covers regular cores as well as exterior
point-source intervals, without using a singular deep-law reservoir at r=0.
It does not cover all positive baryon profiles: n=4,mu=1 gives q=-1/16,
an admissible local rising-density-shell example but an invalid all-age clock.

Special cases: n=0 gives q=(4mu+3)/(4mu+4), rising from 3/4 to 1;
n=1 gives 1/2; n=2 gives 1/4. The old pure two-stage deep exterior clock
is the n=0,mu->infinity limit, not the exact full-law formula.
With a supplied cutoff Rmax the total mass is S=M0(Rmax), and
Mc(total,t)=S[1-exp(-gamma(Rmax)t)]. All other states hold the remainder.
The cutoff, source profile, k and physical state identities remain inputs.
A coupled evolution can change B, gamma and the actual available density;
this construction assumes they are fixed and is not that evolution.

3. Shared gravity: exact momentum obstruction to conversion at rest
At a fixed radius let u0=GM0/r^2, x=u0/a0 in (0,1), and
 gb=R(u0), R(u)=u^2/(a0-u), gtotal=gb+u0=u0/(1-x).
Write f=Mc/M0=1-exp(-z). The cold self-gravity cancels in the stated
carrier comparison, leaving outward residual tether R(fu0). The massive
neutral precursor's gravity is gfree=(1-f)u0. Therefore the cold inward
acceleration is exactly
 a_in=gb+gfree-R(fu0)
     =u0(1-f)/[(1-x)(1-fx)]
     =gtotal(1-f)/(1-fx)>0 for every f<1.
For x=f=1/2, a_in/gb=4/3. The earlier baryon-only at-rest comparison gave
5/6; including the precursor adds the missing inward attraction.
This is a structural spherical identity, not just the rational test grid.

Let outward velocity be positive and let source S inject cold mass with
mean outward velocity v_in. The pressure-free momentum equation is
 rhoC Dv/Dt = -rhoC a_in + S(v_in-v) + F_interaction.
An exactly at-rest cold profile requires
 S v_in + F_interaction = rhoC a_in.
Birth at rest or an isotropic decay of resting parents has v_in=0; without
an extra force/stress, it cannot maintain the specified at-rest profile.
The recoil/interaction partner must carry the opposite momentum; an omitted
partner is not reciprocal closure. Ordinary nonzero velocities/pressure,
different precursor tether couplings, evolving gravity, stiffness and boundary
stresses remain outside this obstruction.

For the fixed local network above,
 S/rho0=gamma exp(-z)(1-q+qz), rhoC/rho0=H.
If support is attributed solely to incoming momentum, its exact required
mean injection velocity is
 v_in,required = gtotal H /
 [gamma(1-q+qz)(1-x+x exp(-z))].
This is a required momentum ledger entry, not a derived source velocity.
Assigning that velocity by hand would impose the desired solution. It also
requires a kinetic-energy reservoir and changes the assumed precursor flow.

4. Existing source-action comparison: what is actually missing
CFG722's ID selector is a velocity-independent Vlasov force
 C_sel=-div_v(a_T f_c). Its velocity integral is zero for decaying velocity
tails. It conserves carrier number and supplies no population-conversion rate.
The current carrier notes separately contain X -> Phi+Phi, with charge
conservation and a supplied dilute spontaneous width Gamma_c. They explicitly
state that S_bridge to the polarization sector is unspecified. These are
different mechanisms; neither may inherit the other's missing terms.

Even within a prescribed local population-decay approximation, the full-law
clock requires a nonconstant hazard:
 lambda(t)=-d/dt log(1-H)
          =gamma(1-q+qz)/(1+qz),
 lambda_dot=gamma^2 q^2/(1+qz)^2>0 for q>0.
It starts at gamma(1-q) and tends to gamma. A single constant Gamma cannot
match both. The exceptional q=0 requires n=(4mu+3)/(mu+1)>3, outside the
nonincreasing-density full-law hypotheses at finite mu. The two-stage mixture
provides age memory algebraically, but the source action does not specify that
intermediate or equal-rate interaction. Spatial transport, stimulated/coherent
conversion and a changing source state are not excluded by this comparison.
No inference is made that every possible physical formation mechanism fails.
