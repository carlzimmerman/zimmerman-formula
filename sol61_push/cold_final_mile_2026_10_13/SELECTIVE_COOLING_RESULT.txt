CE-FM-008 — selective capture must remove EXCESS energy, not merely release binding energy
Primary verdict: the mass-retaining sink does not cool the stated near-equilibrium halo sufficiently by removing a small frozen Maxwellian tail. Collective redistribution, different prepared populations and mass-preserving energy export remain open; no general sink no-go is claimed.

Full residual-field functional in G=a0=1:
 U[M;M_N]=integral_0^infinity [r^2 w(M/r^2)-M_N M/r^2]dr,
 w(u)=-u^2/2-u-log(1-u), w'=R(u).
For fixed nonnegative total cold massQ, its pointwise constrained minimum is M=min(M0(M_N,r),Q), with M0=[sqrt(M_N^2+4M_N r^2)-M_N]/2. Monotonicity of this profile makes it an admissible cumulative mass. This is the same canonical residual action distinguished in ACTION_CORRECTION.txt.

Let R be the truncation radius, B=integral dM0/r and mu=-M_N/R+integral_R^infinity R(Q/r^2)dr. At the minimum,
 partial Umin/partial M_N=-B,
 partial Umin/partial Q=mu.
An infinitesimal captured massd increases sourceM_N by d and decreasesQ by d, so dUmin=-(B+mu)d. A particle removed from the exact force-free density releases that same baseline binding energy plus its kinetic energy. Therefore, to first order, only the particle's kinetic energy reduces energy ABOVE THE NEW minimum. Large receiver energy that originates in deeper central binding is not automatically cooling of the retained population.

This first variation is local to compact positive capture radius and smooth/thin removal directions. A capture radius shrinking to0 with packet mass encounters the point-source cusp and cannot inherit this differentiability statement without an additional limiting analysis.

Remaining mechanism needed
To cool most halo kinetic energy while keeping captured mass small, a dynamical process must first concentrate excess energy in an exceptionally small escaping/captured subset, or export energy without consuming rest mass. A thermal-tail label alone supplies neither. Need an actual conserving collective/collision kernel and angular-momentum transport, plus physical sink capture conditions. Time-dependent rethermalization or coherent sources may change the budget and are explicitly untested here.
