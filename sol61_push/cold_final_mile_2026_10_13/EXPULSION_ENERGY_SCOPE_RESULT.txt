EXACT HAMILTONIAN OF THIS SHELL ODE (not a new full physical action)
For each sorted shell interval [m_i,m_i+dm], define
 c_i=[(m_i+dm)^3-m_i^3]/(3dm)
    =m_i^2+m_i dm+dm^2/3.
The actual source acceleration is
 rddot_i=-Mb(t)/r_i^2+c_rank(i)/r_i^4+j_i^2/r_i^3.
Between crossings it follows from
 E=sum_i dm[v_i^2/2-Mb(t)/r_i+c_rank(i)/(3r_i^3)+j_i^2/(2r_i^2)].
The sorted interaction potential is continuous at equal-radius crossings;
j stays attached to each particle. A perfectly elastic fixed inner wall
does no net work; its discrete implementation still requires a drift check.
For smooth intervals,
 dE/dt=-dotMb sum_i dm/r_i.
Gradual baryon loss therefore injects positive energy in this prescribed
external-potential model. An instantaneous p->1 jump changes energy by
 (p-1)sum_i dm/r_i
at unchanged positions/velocities. These identities and finite-shell
coefficient algebra were independently checked. They supply an executable
K2/work ledger but do not measure its discretization error retroactively.
