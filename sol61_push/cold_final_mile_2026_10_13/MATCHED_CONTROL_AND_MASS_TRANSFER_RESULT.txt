Residual integration normalization is not fully certified
Source run_system rescales settled/reservoir cold masses by
s=supply/I.supply_turned while returning I.M_core_g unscaled. derive builds
core shapes with a predicted turnaround radius and evaluates them on the actual
turnaround radius. In the continuum interpolation idealization this gives
 Mc_final(r)=t Mobs(k r),

Thus residual core-amplitude and size errors need independent inspection even
when the same-model aperture diagnostic passes. The saved data do not expose

against Mobs(r), and refine the turned-mass and radius-anchor fixed points.

inconsistent; a real correction requires a consistent solver rerun.

2. A common normalization cannot solve the dwarf gap
Let A>=0 multiply ALL saved free aperture profiles, and allow even an
independently chosen retained settled contribution Si for every galaxy with
0<=Si<=c=5.364, keeping observed Plummer stellar fraction p=.512.
For each observed target Di, the necessary interval is
 max[0,(Di-p-c)/Fi] <= A <= (Di-p)/Fi.
This gives canonical A>=9.08968 from Sextans but A<=1.074735 from Fornax.
Alt gives A>=9.006705 and A<=.994712. The intervals are empty. This is an
algebraic obstruction in the specified fixed-shape family, stronger than merely
failing a one-parameter fit: it grants each galaxy arbitrary Si within its cap.
It is not a no-go for different profiles, stellar inputs or transport phases.
A shared scalar normalization, or a shared increase in source mass with the
same profiles, cannot be the proposed cure. The concentration change must be
selective by galaxy; the canonical Sextans/Fornax amplification selectivity
must be at least8.457599 (the exact bound ratio, not a new parameter).

3. Concrete stock-conserving transfer targets
At the saved base, infer T=Di*(sigma_model/sigma_obs)^2. The required minimum
net inward cold transfer in units of total stellar Mb is Delta=Di-T.
A mass-only constructive redistribution can take positive Delta from outer
FREE stock. For negative Delta, remove inner free first and then inner settled
stock. Under the explicit observed stellar aperture convention, canonical:
 Draco: move18.41970Mb inward, using40.524% of available outer free stock.
 Sextans: move42.30306Mb inward, using98.686% of outer free stock.
 Fornax: move1.79254Mb inner free outward AND.62953Mb inner settled outward;
         the latter is24.63% of its inferred inner settled component.
 LeoI: move.55446Mb free outward AND.16655Mb settled outward;
       the latter is10.00% of its inferred inner settled component.
All four transfers are nonnegative and fit within their category stocks.
Across all11, AntliaII is still infeasible at the fixed central-value stock;
this is the prior inventory shortfall, not a new claim of observational exclusion.

This construction demonstrates only a mass-budget path. It does not implement
energy transport, guarantee equilibrium or preserve the field solution after
mass moves. In particular, the settled component is tied to the response law:
releasing it must update the complementary stock, its gravity, constitutive
response and any boundary stress in ONE action/solver. No negative mass,
extra phantom force or duplicated cold supply may be used to hide overboosts.
The populated finite-stock interface problem from CE-FM-079 remains essential.

4. Mechanism decision
The model must make Draco/Sextans inward transport and Fornax/LeoI outward
transport emerge from independent halo/formation/orbit conditions under one
carrier law. A gravothermal phase difference is a candidate from CE-FM-078;
it is not established for this carrier. In particular the CE-FM-079 dilute
transport benchmark found too few collisions for an initially extended halo at
the selected geometric fragment estimate: a compact initial population or a
newly derived transport kernel remains needed. Repeating the same diffuse
constant-rate calculation does not advance that bottleneck.
