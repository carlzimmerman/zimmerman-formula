3. New check: mass just outside R is not invisible to outer stars
For a normalized spherical Plummer tracer of scale a, the whole-population
virial contribution of a spherical mass shell at radius r is exactly

 delta sigma_los^2 = GM/(3a) [1+(r/a)^2]^(-3/2).

Thus 'outside aperture R' does not imply 'no stellar-kinematic effect'. This
uses the same complete-tracer assumption as our earlier stock check, not a
reconstructed finite-aperture selection likelihood. It is a reported-only
additional guard, NOT an amendment/regrade of CFG918's frozen test.

Two selected source capacity-maximizing endpoints (canonical B0, G1, RE,
cored core, phiK=1, lambda_c=1) place their circular shell A at R. Even ignoring
the core and receiver B, the static baseline+A whole-tracer lower dispersions
are19.399km/s Fornax and14.146km/s LeoI, versus12.10 and9.29. Those particular
capacity optima would overboost the assumed stellar tracer. This is a
conditional central-value incompatibility, not a statistical exclusion.

4. Implementation and interpretation limits that remain material
- The receiver family is restricted to two spherical shell components. With
  self-gravity it is not proved to bound EVERY possible receiver. EXCLUDED
  means excluded in that declared family/core grid, not an all-profile theorem.
- Jcap>=Jreq is an optimistic necessary capacity comparison, not a constructed
  dynamical endpoint with exact vector J or a torque/capture rate. Maximally
  coherent planar motions and spherical self-gravity are separate idealizations.
- K7 recomputes energies with KB chosen as Einit-Epart. This checks consistent
  integrals/bookkeeping, not dynamic conservation in a formation trajectory.
- Read-only review found a boundary inconsistency: if rA=rB with both masses
  nonzero, A's circular speed omits B's half-shell force while B includes A's
  half-force. Likewise core-shell coincidences need consistent treatment.
  None of our1,352 saved endpoint optima has nonzero A at rA=rB, so this did
  not invalidate the audited examples. Between-endpoint optimizers or all
  classes were not independently checked for impact. No source file edited.
