NEW PROFILE/APERTURE CONSISTENCY OBLIGATION
CFG815 f_eff_sys uses numerator Mgas_beta(<3Mpc)+Mcond. For its denominator
it constructs a NEW profile: beta gas to Rm=min(2.2R500,2.8Mpc), then slope-3
(and F returns to slope-2 beyond2Rm), integrates it to rta and adds Mcond.
Since Rm<3Mpc, that modification also changes the profile INSIDE the same
3Mpc aperture. The old numerator and new denominator are therefore not two
enclosed masses of a single continuous gas profile. This is implemented as
frozen; it is a physical dictionary/normalization issue, not a silent regrade
or an accusation that the script disobeys its declared numerator.

For continuum beta rho=A/(r^2+rc^2), rc=.15R500, write
 B(r)=r-rc atan(r/rc), M_beta(r)=4pi A B(r),
 H=Rm^3/(Rm^2+rc^2).
The continuous slope-3 extension has, for R>=Rm,
 M_new,P(R)=4pi A [B(Rm)+H ln(R/Rm)].
For F beyond2Rm replace the last term by
 H ln2 + H(R-2Rm)/(2Rm).
These expressions follow by shell integration and agree with direct
quadrature at five actual catalogue radii for each profile. They audit the
continuum shapes specified by the source, not its finite gradient/interpolation
errors or a new fitted gas model.

EXACT STACK INFLUENCE (new diagnostic)
For outer-band per-cluster n_i=sum_j W_ij D_ij and d_i=sum_j W_ij P_ij,
 A=sum n_i / sum d_i, A_i=n_i/d_i, q_i=d_i/sum d_i,
 A=sum_i q_i A_i,
 A_(-i)-A=q_i(A-A_i)/(1-q_i).

set by MODEL-WEIGHTED denominator shares q_i and residuals, not inverse-

FAILED CAUSAL IMPLICATION
A nonmonotonic trend of stacked observed/model ratios does not prove no
smooth retention prescription can work, nor identify a selection effect.
Stellar calibration, gas-aperture definitions, redshift, covariance and
heterogeneous radial coverage all enter these ratios. No forward selection
likelihood or population-conditioned test is supplied by the new diagnostic.
The model's failure stands; it is not exonerated by proposing sample effects.
Likewise a76-object aggregate pass with an admitted failed K1 is conditional,
not a derivation of carrier dynamics or a robust full-population success.
