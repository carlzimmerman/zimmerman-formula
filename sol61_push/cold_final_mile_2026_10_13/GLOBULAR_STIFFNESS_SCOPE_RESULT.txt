SMALL S DOES NOT IMPLY A NEAR-NEWTONIAN TOTAL BOOST
The source formula is
 sigma_pred^2/sigma_N^2 = B_eff = 1+S(B_law-1).
S is the RETAINED FRACTION OF THE UNSCREENED EXCESS, not the total boost.
Near-Newtonian motion requires S(B_law-1)<<1, not merely S<<1.
For any specified tolerance delta on the variance boost, it requires
 S <= delta/(B_law-1).

At the source L23.95730pc, using its same isolated algebraic law and
half-light estimator, with NO host EFE:

These are positive algebraic countercontrols to inferring 'nearly Newtonian'
from S=.06–.30 alone. They are not real observed-dwarf fits. An adopted host
external field changes B_law and must be specified per system; it cannot be
silently omitted or transferred between the old EFE template and an EFE-blind
same-action prediction. No UFD total mass requirement is newly measured here.

Because0<=S<=1, B_eff stays between1 and B_law. Thus if a particular dwarf
