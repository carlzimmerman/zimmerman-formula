NEW UNIT TRANSLATION
CFG416 FROZEN_CRITERIA explicitly defines f_ret thresholds in Msun/h.
Its cfg416_pm.py x_supply computes Mta in Msun/h, calls fret_of(log10 Mta),
then divides the retained baryon mass by .674 to obtain physical Msun.
CFG804 instead uses physical lens masses in Msun (Gpc, pc and acceleration)
and calls the identical numerical rule with log10(Mta_physical/Msun).
Those dictionaries differ. The omission is invisible in the low-mass flat
f_ret=.10 control, so K1 logMb=10.5 cannot detect it.

Let q=log10(Mta_physical/Msun). For a retention function F tabulated in
Msun/h, the translated equation is
  Mb = f_b Mta_physical F(q+log10 h),  N=1/F(q+log10 h).
CFG804 solves the same equation with h=1. Our dictionary calculation uses
h=.6736 carried by CFG805/T1x. This is a controlled unit translation, not
an observational re-calibration of F. B21 h70=1 radii/photometric masses and
any adopted physical-H0 changes must also be treated consistently before
new empirical fits. Msun/h and Msun do not become identical at h70=1.

FINITE INVENTORY AND BARYON PHASE OBLIGATION
For the adopted spherical law Mc(<x)/Mb=(sqrt(1+4x^2)-1)/2, integrating to
x_edge=sqrt[(s N)(1+s N)] gives EXACTLY Mc=s N Mb, s=5.364. Independent
aperture integration respects this and the exterior point-mass tail.
Increasing N genuinely changes the available mass relative to CE-FM-061's
N=1 ceiling; this is not a redistribution of the same stock.
However Mta=(1+s)N Mb includes N Mb of catchment baryons. The model's
central baryon point accounts for Mb; the other (N-1)Mb requires a spatial
inventory. At translated primary bins 2-4 it is 9.0,8.3849,5.9815 Mb.
It can be outside the modeled region or diffuse; this calculation does not
choose where, how it lenses/sources the medium, or whether catchments overlap.
The F1 phase/source problem and F3 unique mass allocation/free dynamics remain.
Nor does a budget establish that the live medium settles into this exact law.
