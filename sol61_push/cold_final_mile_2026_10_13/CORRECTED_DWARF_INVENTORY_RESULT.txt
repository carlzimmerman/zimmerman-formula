Answer
The corrections remove an erroneous group/cluster negative-mass argument and
leave an inventory-based dwarf route open. They do not close the dwarf mass
gap. Under the already assumed 10% baryon retention, the total cold stock can
cover 10/11 central dwarf aperture mass estimates if sufficiently concentrated.
That concentration is not derived, and the same prescription must avoid adding
too much mass to dwarfs whose baseline already overpredicts their dispersion.
Neither changing deficit units nor counting a global stock places it inside a
stellar aperture. No cold particle, production or normalization is derived.

1. Exact dictionary and raw accounting check
The corrected definitions are xT=(Mtot-Mb)/Mb and
xA=(Mtot-Mb-Mphantom)/(5.364 Mb). Thus
 xT=5.364 xA + Mphantom/Mb.
They are different quantities. Reading xA as xT loses both the scale factor
and the phantom contribution, causing the earlier overdraft. No new mass is
created by repairing the dictionary. The source's kernel/settled contribution
and free cold stock must be counted once, not added as independent copies.

3. Separate optimistic retained law from free mass
For a bounded inverse problem use CFG822/809's s kernel nu=.5+sqrt(.25+1/y),
y=G p Mb/(r12^2 a0), and optimistic fully settled retained draw
 S=min[(nu-1)p,c] in units of total stellar Mb.
Free stock is Ftot=c/f-S, including expelled cold AND undrawn retained cold;
this preserves S+Ftot=c/f. Required signed inner free mass is D-p-S.
Negative values mean this selected fully settled baseline overpredicts, not
that a negative cold component exists. Canonical required free fractions:
 AntliaII1.10710 (insufficient), CVnI.57314, Carina.08405, CraterII.22580,

positive free mass cannot match their central values on this fixed baseline.
Suppressing settlement/screening could change that baseline; it is another
coupled model decision, not a subtraction of positive stock.

The code supplies an executable inverse target for any0<eta<1: a Plummer
free-mass profile with scale b=r12 sqrt(eta^(-2/3)-1) encloses eta of Ftot.
Canonical targets: Draco b219.819pc, UMi250.902pc, CVnI291.754pc,
Sextans56.899pc. These scales are algebraically inferred targets with an
independent reconstruction check, NOT predicted halos, equilibrium proofs,
measured cold profiles or a fit with no new parameters. All low-retention
systems, including globulars, need a physically derived population rule.
