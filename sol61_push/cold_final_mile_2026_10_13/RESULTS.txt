Cubic radial model in CFG747/748/749 instead has M_c(x)=x, because it substitutes R(u)=u^2. Route B: exact orbital alternatives, executed
Choose 0<=alpha<1 and give each carrier circular orbit tangential speed
  v_t^2=alpha G M_b/r, L^2=alpha G M_b r.
Use randomly oriented planes, so the ensemble is spherical and has no net rotation. Circular radial balance requires
  -g_N + R(u) + v_t^2/r=0,
  R(u_alpha)=(1-alpha)g_N,
  u_alpha=[sqrt(q^2+4a0 q)-q]/2, q=(1-alpha)g_N,
  M_alpha(r)=r^2 u_alpha/G.
All expressions are the full saturating force, not a cubic approximation. On any finite annulus the density is positive, u_alpha<a0, and the annulus can be matched to a prescribed core of mass M_alpha(r_inner); truncate the carrier outside a finite r_outer. This is a velocity-space measure supported on circular orbits, not a smooth distribution function or a cosmological assembly result. A fixed baryonic point source and conditional basin anchor remain assumptions.

For positivity write M_alpha^2=k[r^2-G M_alpha/a0] with positive constant k=(1-alpha)M_b a0/G (or use units G=a0=M_b=1: M^2=(1-alpha)(r^2-M)); differentiate to obtain M'>0. Outside the outer population spherical enclosed mass stays fixed; interior orbits are unchanged.

Deep limit:
  M_alpha/M_0 -> sqrt(1-alpha).
At alpha=.6 and r=20 r_M, full ratio is .638292; deep ratio .632456. This is a non-law stationary halo despite cold nonrelativistic orbital motion and exact conservative dynamics. Arbitrary initial-state convergence to the law cannot follow from the collisionless selector.

Spherical laminar shell stability: linearize at fixed enclosed mass and each shell's fixed L, before substituting equilibrium. The radial frequency satisfies
  omega_r^2=(g_N/r)[2(1-alpha)(2a0-u)/(a0-u)-2+3alpha]
           =(g_N/r)[2-alpha+2(1-alpha)u/(a0-u)]>0.
This supports radial perturbations that preserve shell ordering. It does not establish nonspherical collective stability, finite-width phase-space stability or global continuation after crossings. Do not label this a globally stable halo.

CFG749 predictor correction

  (M_c/r)^2=1-v_t^2/(r g_N),
not 1-v_t^2/v_c,law^2. In its units r g_N=1/r while v_c,law^2=1+1/r. The correct local ratio is therefore (r+1) times the ratio used in cfg749.py. alpha=.6,r=20 gives true .632456 and recorded predictor .985611. This counterexample is exactly stationary, so no infall pressure or averaging ambiguity supplies the discrepancy.

Stress and virial: why at-rest algebra is not a warm equilibrium theorem
Let p_r=rho<v_r^2>, p_t=rho<v_theta^2>=rho<v_phi^2> in a stationary spherical nonstreaming state. The first Vlasov moment is
  dp_r/dr + 2(p_r-p_t)/r = rho[-g_N+R(u)].
The same moment equation follows by integrating the specified carrier Vlasov equation against v; no gravitational Poisson theorem is needed. On the exact law the right side vanishes. Locally, this permits radial stress p_r proportional to r^-2 with p_t=0; this explains why radial-orbit local agreement alone does not force vanishing dispersion.

For a finite isolated stationary carrier with vanishing surface stress and no flux, multiply the tensor equation by position and integrate:
  2K= integral rho r[g_N-R(u)] dV.
If the full law holds on the entire carrier support, K=0. A nonzero radial stress construction must pay boundary stress/flux somewhere, or depart from the law somewhere. The reflecting inner wall in CFG747/748/749 supplies an external boundary impulse and must be included in such a global balance. This conditional virial statement is not an exclusion of finite-time, open, pressure-supported or externally confined halos.

Surviving statement and quantitative obligation
The exact law is the force-free at-rest member of a larger equilibrium family. In the deep circular family, a mass-profile tolerance delta requires alpha<=2delta-delta^2. Five-percent mass tolerance means v_t^2<=.0975 G M_b/r, considerably smaller than .0975 times the law speed squared at large radius. The full saturating version for k=M_alpha/M_0 is
  alpha=1-k^2(a0-u0)/(a0-k u0).
This is a necessary preparation/transport requirement for this family, not a population bound or an observational scatter inference.

Route C: imperfect criticality comparison, executed
Explicitly modify the model by leaving inward self-gravity epsilon u:
  R(u)=g_N+epsilon u.
Then
  (1+epsilon)u^2+(g_N-epsilon a0)u-a0 g_N=0.
epsilon>0 gives u->epsilon a0/(1+epsilon) as g_N->0; epsilon<0 gives u~g_N/|epsilon|. Thus the deep 1/r extra force is structurally tied to exact criticality. At x=1000 a 1% detuning gives mass ratios 10.0040 or .09907 relative to the declared exact theory. These are sensitivity examples in a different action, not proof of physical detuning. CFG694 chooses exact cancellation and claims no independent P mass term; a derivation or symmetry protecting that relation in the carrier completion is not supplied here.
