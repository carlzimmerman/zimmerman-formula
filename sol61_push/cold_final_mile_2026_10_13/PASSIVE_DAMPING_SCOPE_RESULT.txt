Claim card
For the isothermal spherical surrogate rho0=A/r^2, Phi0'=C/r, P=sigma^2 rho,
with sigma^2=C/2 and frozen inner mass, independently reconstruct the radial
operator. Then ask whether an unchanged negative conservative restoring mode
can become linearly stable solely by adding ordinary passive linear drag.
Scalar claim: Omega>0, gamma>=0 finite, qddot+gamma qdot-Omega^2 q=0 always
has an exponentially growing solution. Matrix extension: M positive definite,
D positive semidefinite, K symmetric with a negative direction, all real
finite matrices; M qddot+D qdot+K q=0 has a real positive growth exponent.
This excludes pure passive damping as the repair in this declared class.
It is not a no-go for all open systems, feedback, modified sources or media.

1. Reconstructing the corrected radial force
Continuity gives delta_rho=-(r^2 rho0 xi)'/r^2=-rho0 xi'.
For xi(inner)=0 with frozen enclosed inner mass, Poisson gives
 delta_g=-C xi/r^2, C=4 pi G A.
Euler requires ALL three terms
 rho0 xi_ddot=-(delta_P)'-delta_rho Phi0'-rho0 delta_g.
Dividing gives
 xi_ddot=sigma^2 xi''+(C-2 sigma^2)xi'/r+C xi/r^2.
At closure the drift cancels:
 xi_ddot=sigma^2 (xi''+2 xi/r^2),
 xi''+(omega^2/sigma^2+2/r^2)xi=0 for exp(-i omega t).
Thus AG1's corrected operator survives this reconstruction. If the inner
interface moves or its mass perturbation is not frozen, the Poisson
integration constant changes; the displayed expression must be rederived.
No claim for all boundary pairs is obtained by silently keeping it unchanged.

2. A direct DD negative-direction witness
On an annulus a<r<b with xi(a)=xi(b)=0, the conservative restoring operator
is K=sigma^2[-d^2/dr^2-2/r^2]. Its kinetic weight is positive and constant
because4 pi r^2 rho0=4 pi A. Set L=ln(b/a), k=pi/L and trial
 xi(r)=sqrt(r) sin(k ln(r/a)).
The numerator divided by sigma^2 is EXACTLY
 integral_a^b [(xi')^2-2 xi^2/r^2] dr
   = pi^2/(2L)-7L/8.
The denominator integral xi^2 dr is positive. Hence b/a>exp(2pi/sqrt7)
=10.74908702916351 gives a strictly negative Rayleigh quotient and at least
one negative restoring eigenvalue on this regular finite DD annulus. The
zero-frequency log-sine and log-cosine bases also check exactly. This is an
analytic negative-direction argument, not a finite scan or new galaxy test.
It agrees with the AG1 DD ladder. The DF threshold and observed widths have
not been independently re-scored here.

3. Why ordinary damping fails to repair the unstable mode
Let omega0^2=-Omega^2<0. Adding scalar linear drag while preserving that
mode gives qddot+gamma qdot-Omega^2 q=0. Its growing exponent is
 lambda_plus=(sqrt(gamma^2+4 Omega^2)-gamma)/2
            =2 Omega^2/[sqrt(gamma^2+4 Omega^2)+gamma] >0.
This is positive for EVERY finite gamma>=0. At large gamma it approaches
Omega^2/gamma from above. Strong damping can delay growth, not remove it.
The saddle energy E=(qdot^2-Omega^2 q^2)/2 satisfies
 Edot=-gamma qdot^2<=0. Energy decreases even while the amplitude grows;
energy dissipation is not stability when the equilibrium is a saddle.

This does not rely on the damping commuting with the restoring matrix.
For the finite matrix system, consider the symmetric pencil
 P(lambda)=lambda^2 M+lambda D+K, lambda>=0.
At lambda=0 its minimum eigenvalue is negative. At sufficiently large
lambda its minimum eigenvalue is positive, since M>0 dominates. Continuity
therefore gives some lambda*>0 and nonzero v with P(lambda*)v=0.
Then q=v exp(lambda*t) grows. D>=0 expresses passivity; the continuity proof
also makes clear the finite symmetric-matrix assumptions. An exact2x2
noncommuting control has M=diag(1,2), K=diag(-1,3), D=[[2,1],[1,2]], all
required signs, and det P=2lambda^4+6lambda^3+4lambda^2+4lambda-3: negative
at0 and positive at1. Mixing the modes with positive damping does not rescue
this control. No infinite-dimensional unbounded-damping theorem is claimed.

For uniform bounded drag on the source's increasingly singular negative
modes, increasing Omega also increases lambda_plus without bound. Such drag
does not by itself regularize the singular-center limit. A regular core or
another changed operator/domain still needs to be specified and checked.

4. Strongest safe consequence and remaining gap
AG1's omission correction is a substantive result, and its algebraic
sigma^2=C/2 equilibrium relation is unaffected. The original G081 neutral
family is not recovered by simply invoking energy export or relaxation.
A physical dissipative completion could change the EOS, background, mass
flux, effective stiffness or boundary dynamics. Those are different models
and can have different spectra; they are not excluded by this calculation.
The full W_rem/current-frame/stiffness tether medium was NOT the surrogate
analyzed by AG1 and is not refuted by this fluid instability. Its actual
occupied state, perturbation forces and reciprocal export channel remain
missing. A cutoff solution likewise requires a physical core/matching rule,
not a chosen numerical excision alone. Neither the half coefficient nor a
static profile proves a dynamical attractor or selects the cosmological kappa.
