Exact scope and usable next equation
For homogeneous optically thin emission, observed bolometric energy density
u0 = integral dt a(t)^4 epsilon_phys(t), with a(today)=1.
With conserved comoving cold stock rho_phys=rho0/a^3 and constant conversion
rate Gamma, the optical contribution is
u_opt = rho0 c^2 Gamma integral dt a(t) eta_obs(t).
Here eta_obs includes the emitted spectrum, redshift into the observed band,
and propagation/reprocessing. For the source's effective time-averaged
parameterization integral a eta_obs dt = tH fz eta_cos, its bound is
Gamma eta_cos <= Gamma_code, not generally Gamma <= Gamma_code.
For event-removal Gamma=1/tau_particle, tau_particle >= eta_cos tau_code.
Rapid stock depletion requires solving rho(t); this fixed-history identity
does not certify a short-lifetime cosmology.

Local power obeys
P_opt,local <= P_code * (Gamma_local/Gamma_cos) * (eta_local/eta_cos)
when the specified common-history rate comparison is applicable and eta_cos>0.
Identical local/cosmic efficiency and rate CANCEL the eta factors: the tiny
optical ambient-power ceiling survives. Thus a small optical branching ratio
alone does not rescue ambient optical fuel. If eta_cos=0, the optical datum
places no rate bound. The quoted stimulation factor constrains the product
of environmental rate enhancement and relative optical efficiency; a
microscopic coupling enhancement has not been derived from it.

A binary portal has epsilon proportional to n_chi n_partner <sigma v>
E_visible, rather than a supplied universal one-body Gamma rho c^2.
The previously audited chi+antiS -> n+gamma channel has about 0.499867 MeV
photons. It needs a photon spectrum/transfer and partner-history calculation
before borrowing an optical-band limit. This observation neither excludes
nor rescues that hypothetical portal and gives no engine mechanism.
