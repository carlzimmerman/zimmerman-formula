Open flow differs from the closed-halo virial case
For a pressureless inward radial stream of positive speed v and constant mass flux F,
  M'(r)=F/v(r),
  v dv/dr = -G M_N/r^2 + R(u), u=G M(r)/r^2,
  R(u)=u^2/(a0-u).
The incoming surface stress/flux is essential: the zero-surface-stress virial obstruction in CE-FM-003 does not apply. This stream is a singular cold velocity distribution, not an ordinary bounded-f halo. These equations still require the conditional carrier/displacement identification and a spherical Newtonian background; they do not generate initial conditions or the capture radius.

In the deep cubic limit M=A r and constant v solve the equations when A=sqrt(M_N a0/G); continuity then demands F=A v. Thus a nonzero streaming state can hold the deep profile, but its inflow conditions must satisfy that relation. It is not a prediction of arbitrary boundary flux.

Full saturating correction, G=a0=M_N=1
Eliminate v to obtain
  R(M/r^2)-1/r^2 + F^2 M''/(M')^3=0.
The force-free mass M0=(sqrt(1+4r^2)-1)/2 has M0''>0 and cannot be an exact finite-F solution. A formal small-flux branch is
  M=M0+F^2 mu+O(F^4),
  mu=-r^2 M0''/[R'(u0)(M0')^3]
    =-1/[4r R'(u0)]
    =-(sqrt(1+4r^2)-1)^2/[16r sqrt(1+4r^2)].
mu<0 and tends to-1/8 at large r. Thus the leading full-law correction is a negative, approximately constant enclosed-mass offset F^2/8 in the outer deep regime, not a changed leading flat-curve coefficient. This is a formal expansion on a compact positive-radius interval; convergence or uniformity at r=0/infinity is not proved.

Central mass growth invalidates a sustained fixed-source black-hole reading

  T_growth/T_residence=M_N/Delta M,

Conditional saturation flux aperture bound

  M(R)<a0 R^2/G,

CE-FM-007 concrete receiver correction
conservative_receiver.py supports variable mass packets, exact cubic or quadrature full saturating field energy, and an actually empty remaining halo. It solves the following stated particle/receiver/radiation energy model at a capture event. Let d be captured packet rest mass; E_before is pre-capture Newtonian modeled carrier energy; E_ref is post-removal carrier energy with central receiver increased by d; B=G sum_retained dm/r. For additional receiver mass correction x, E_after=E_ref-B x. Conservation of particle/receiver rest energy plus modeled Newtonian energy and prescribed exported radiation yields
  x=(E_before-E_ref-E_radiated)/(c^2-B),
  Delta M_receiver=d+x.
This is a complete algebraic solution for that declared instantaneous budget, not a physical capture or radiation law. It requires the weak-gravity denominator c^2>B and nonnegative receiver increment. c^2 and radiation are stated inputs. Fixed baryon/source self-energy, baryon-receiver interaction, anisotropic recoil/spin and covariant metric dynamics are excluded; the update cannot be called a complete physical action.
