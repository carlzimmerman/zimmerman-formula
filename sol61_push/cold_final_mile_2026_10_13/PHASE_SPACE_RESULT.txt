CE-FM-003: collisionless preparation has a nonzero kinetic floor for bounded phase density
Primary verdict: conditional analytical obstruction to exact force-free settling for smooth bounded phase-density data, plus numerical necessary conditions for near-law states. Self-review; not a general cold-energy no-go.

Mass phase-space distribution f satisfies rho(x)=integral f(x,v)d^3v, f>=0, f<=F<infinity. The specified smooth velocity-independent conservative Vlasov flow preserves this upper bound, since phase-space divergence is zero and f is constant on characteristics. Coarse averaging cannot increase the same bound. Spherical forces also preserve each particle's individual angular momentum. Escape can remove particles but cannot lower a retained particle's angular momentum without an added torque.

Elementary velocity-ball inequality, proved without a literature leaf:
Let v_*=(3rho/(4piF))^(1/3) and f_*=F 1_{|v|<v_*}. At fixed rho both f and f_* have the same integral. Therefore
 integral (v^2-v_*^2)(f-f_*)d^3v>=0:
 inside the ball both factors are nonpositive; outside both are nonnegative.
Cancel the equal-mass term to obtain
 tau(x)=(1/2)integral v^2 f d^3v >= C F^(-2/3)rho^(5/3),
 C=(3/10)(3/(4pi))^(2/3),
 K>=C F^(-2/3)integral rho^(5/3)d^3x.
It holds for ordinary bounded functions in velocity space with the stated finite moments. It does not apply to a delta-sheet cold distribution, a condensate wavefunction or an externally cooled collision operator without an appropriate distribution bound.

Combine with the CE-FM-001 stationary virial identity for a finite isolated carrier with zero surface stress/flux:
 2K=integral rho r[g_N-R(u)]dV.
Exact force-free law R(u)=g_N on all occupied points gives K=0. Nonzero mass with finite F gives K>0 by the inequality. Hence a smooth bounded collisionless carrier cannot form that exact finite stationary law under these assumptions. This does not exclude approximate agreement, open boundary flux, cosmological time dependence, primordial singular sheets or additional reciprocal cooling. It makes a required preparation assumption explicit.

Quantitative necessary preparation in G=a0=M_b=1:
Take M(r)=k M0(r), 0<k<1, 0<r<R, M0=(sqrt(1+4r^2)-1)/2, rho0=1/[2pi r sqrt(1+4r^2)]. Set u0=M0/r^2. The full saturating virial kinetic budget is
 K_vir=(k/2)integral_0^R [M0'(r)/r][1-k^2(1-u0)/(1-k u0)]dr.
The phase-density bound requires
 F >= [C integral (k rho0)^(5/3)dV / K_vir]^(3/2).
This is necessary, not sufficient: no positive stationary distribution function or physically justified outer boundary is constructed. Dimensionless F converts with rho_unit/v_unit^3, rho_unit=M_b/r_M^3 and v_unit=(G M_b a0)^(1/4). No cosmological or measured F has been supplied; these tables do not exclude an observed population.
