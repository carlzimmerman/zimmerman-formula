F5 reduction: a vacuum-related carrier mass is not yet a vacuum-selected abundance
2026-10-13, self-reviewed analytical checkpoint. Based on CFG723_carrier_mass_origin/README.md and the actual two-field carrier NR_QUANTUM_BOUND.md and COLD_ENERGY_MECHANISM.md. No new action is assumed.

The source and daughter mass terms in the existing carrier action contain free m and M. The U(1) symmetry protects charge; it does not calculate m or a cosmological initial state. The prepared source number N gives Q=2N, so even at identical action parameters and vacuum density distinct prepared N give distinct cold-energy amounts. The finite regulated nonrelativistic Hamiltonian preserves each of these charge sectors. Its empty particle-sector vacuum is also invariant: every interaction annihilates that vacuum. Hence this Hamiltonian alone does not fill the empty sector with a uniquely chosen charge/abundance. This is a statement about its particle-only regulated approximation; relativistic particle-antiparticle production or a new cosmological source could conserve net charge while producing mass and requires separate calculation.

Dimensional reduction if mass is to use only hbar,c,G,rho_DE (rho_DE is mass-equivalent density):
 m_P=sqrt(hbar c/G), zeta=hbar G^2 rho_DE/c^5,
 m=m_P F(zeta).
F is an arbitrary dimensionless function until dynamics selects it. In particular m_DE=(hbar^3 rho_DE/c^3)^(1/4)=m_P zeta^(1/4) is a possible dimensional scale, not a prediction. Any dimensionless prefactor, exponent or other functional choice is still an input without an action calculation. Dimensional consistency does not show that this scale is compatible with the carrier's required cold production, interaction and phase-density regimes.

Even fixing a constant m through a verified vacuum mechanism leaves the comoving particle/charge number as separate information. Late pure daughter rest density obeys rho_c approximately m n with n a^3 conserved under the particle-only number-conserving dilute evolution. To predict Omega_c/Omega_b requires a source/initial-state or production mechanism, not just m(rho_DE).

If m is allowed to vary with a time-dependent rho_DE, n a^3 conserved and pressure negligible give
 dot rho_c + 3H rho_c = (dot m/m)rho_c.
For m proportional to rho_DE^p this is p rho_c dot rho_DE/rho_DE. A reciprocal receiver must absorb the opposite energy exchange. A strictly constant vacuum has zero divergence and cannot accept a nonzero exchange while remaining that same constant vacuum. If rho_DE and m are constant, the exchange vanishes but initial abundance remains unconstrained. This is a necessary conservation accounting requirement, not a derivation of the receiver or of a specific varying-mass model.

Strongest surviving F5 route: derive the carrier mass spectrum and source state from an explicitly specified vacuum-coupled action, then compute conserved amount/yield, momentum distribution and production epoch. Use CE-FM-003 kinetic/phase-space preparation as a downstream gate. No universal impossibility follows from the present action's unselected inputs.
