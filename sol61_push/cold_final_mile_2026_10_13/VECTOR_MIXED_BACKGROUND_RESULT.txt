Result
A positive Maxwell term does not make the proposed L(F) attachment hyperbolic
with the matter metric on every background. An explicit parallel electric and
magnetic field has an imaginary-frequency transverse mode. This is a local
classical obstruction for unrestricted backgrounds of this single-invariant
action; it does not exclude a dynamical medium, a restricted phase, or L(F,G).
No new empirical claim or external theorem is used below.

Derivation
Use F=(|E|^2-|B|^2)/2, A=L_F, C=L_FF, D=A E, H=A B. Set background E and B
parallel to z and wave vector k along x. Faraday gives perturbations
 delta B_y=-k e_z/omega, delta B_z=k e_y/omega,
 delta F=E e_z-B k e_y/omega.
Multiplying the two transverse Ampere equations by omega yields the matrix
 [A omega^2-beta k^2,       -C E B k omega]
 [-C E B k omega,      alpha omega^2-A k^2],
where alpha=A+C E^2 and beta=A-C B^2.
Its determinant factors exactly as
 A(omega^2-k^2)(alpha omega^2-beta k^2).
The ordinary mode is luminal; the other has omega^2/k^2=beta/alpha.
This independently derives both the pure electric result A/(A+C E^2)
and the missing mixed-field restriction beta>0 (when alpha>0).

Exact counterexample for the proposed repair
In a=1 units take L_F=epsilon+sqrt(2F)/(1+sqrt(2F)), on its F>0 branch.
At F=1/2, epsilon=1/10, A=3/5 and C=1/4. Choose E^2=5, B^2=4;
F remains 1/2 but alpha=37/20, beta=-2/5, so
 omega_extra^2/k^2=-8/37.
The negative value is not a small rounding or threshold ambiguity. A Fourier
perturbation grows with rate sqrt(8/37)|k| in the linearized equations.
The unbounded high-k growth excludes ordinary well-posed evolution on the
matter time slices for this unrestricted effective action. An added cutoff
or higher-derivative term would change the model and must be analyzed.

No common hyperbolicity covector for this example
The extraordinary quadratic form is proportional to
 -alpha t_cov^2+beta(p_x^2+p_y^2)+alpha z_cov^2.
When alpha>0,beta<0 its unique-sign direction is z: a hyperbolicity covector
requires z_cov^2>t_cov^2+(-beta/alpha)(p_x^2+p_y^2). Matter metric time instead
requires t_cov^2>z_cov^2+p_x^2+p_y^2. These inequalities contradict each other,
including the zero transverse component case. Merely checking Gtt<0 without
tracking the signature would miss this obstruction.

Structural scope and surviving design constraint
At any fixed F>0 with finite A>0,C>0, the family B^2=t, E^2=t+2F keeps A,C
fixed but has beta=A-Ct<0 for t>A/C. Thus every finite positive Maxwell
coefficient in this G-independent repair leaves some such background.
In the parallel-field frame, a necessary allowed-domain condition is
 B^2<A/C. In terms of G=E dot B, it is
 G^2<(A/C)^2+2F A/C (for F>0,A>0,C>0).
The explicit example has G^2=20 vs allowed boundary204/25=8.16.
Pure Maxwell C=0 restores the common cone exactly, providing a mutation
control, but removes the specified nonlinear electric response.
This is not a universal no-go for nonlinear electrodynamics or cold energy.
A full L(F,G), extra fields/medium or an invariantly enforced field domain
can change the determinant and must be checked with the same static law.
The earlier CE-FM-074 vacuum/energy-tail analysis remains separate and valid.
