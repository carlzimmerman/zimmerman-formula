CE-FM-002: the full saturating shell force now has an admissible, conserving finite implementation.

Primary verdict: implementation and finite assertions verified in the stated local range, conditional on double arithmetic, quadrature and the declared Newtonian carrier identification. This is not a formation result.

Discrete model, G=a0=M_b=1:
  w(u)=-u^2/2-u-log(1-u), w'(u)=u^2/(1-u), 0<=u<1.
  E = sum_i dm v_i^2/2 - sum_i dm/r_i + integral_{r_min}^infinity r^2 w(m(<r)/r^2) dr.
For equal-mass shells sorted by radius, enclosed masses m_i^- and m_i^+=m_i^-+dm are fixed between crossings. Moving a shell changes the adjacent mass step. Differentiating the energy gives exactly
  a_i = -1/r_i^2 + r_i^2 [w(m_i^+/r_i^2)-w(m_i^-/r_i^2)]/dm.
Thus the residual is the finite-shell average of R(u), not R evaluated at an arbitrarily chosen shell midpoint. The cubic limit w=u^3/3 recovers CFG747's discrete force. The fixed core/inner boundary are comparison inputs, not derived formation data.

Implemented in check_full_shells.py. Uses stable series evaluation for small u, exact logarithmic expression otherwise. Field-energy integration uses q=1/r and Gauss-Legendre quadrature on each enclosed-mass interval, including the tail to infinity and the core segment. Total particle rest energy and the fixed core's constant self-energy are omitted consistently; this is a Newtonian dynamical energy, not a relativistic stress tensor.

The two short perturbation runs have dt=.004 and .002 and duration20. No inner-wall collision occurs; no saturation violation is permitted.
