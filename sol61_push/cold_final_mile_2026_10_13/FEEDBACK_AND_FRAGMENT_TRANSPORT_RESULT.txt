1. Exact gravitational gas-cycle benchmark
Let a tracer start on a circular orbit of radius R in a fixed central potential
of mass M0. Slowly add central gas Mg=g M0, then remove it impulsively, ending
with the same gas-free potential M0. Spherical symmetry conserves specific
angular momentum; adiabatic inflow conserves the orbital action. Just before
outflow the orbit is circular at R/(1+g). After the instantaneous loss,
 E_final/E_initial=1-g^2, a_final/R=1/(1-g^2), e_final=g,   0<g<1.
Thus DeltaE/|E_initial|=g^2>0. The gain comes from gas-induced potential work,
not an uncounted cold-energy source. The slow-inflow contraction alone is NOT
permanent when the entire cycle is adiabatic: that null control restores the
initial energy and orbit. Mass redistribution requires a nonadiabatic step.

Use the source's nine expelled baryon masses per current stellar Mb as a
budget, and bring a shared illustrative fraction zeta=.1 of that gas inside
the aperture: Mg=.9Mb. This is an EXPLICIT ASSUMPTION, not a measured gas
history, a new source criterion or a fitted per-galaxy parameter.
M0 is taken from the saved CFG822 aperture mass, NOT the desired endpoint:

This is selective orbital heating, not a proof that the required inner cold

2. An inward channel needs escaping energy, not merely exothermic lumps
For a conditional thin-wall droplet family, take
 E_rest(Q)=omega Q+alpha Q^(2/3), alpha>0.
Fusion of charges Q1,Q2 releases rest binding
 DeltaS=alpha[Q1^(2/3)+Q2^(2/3)-(Q1+Q2)^(2/3)]>0.
Concavity/subadditivity for exponent2/3 gives the sign. Equal lumps release
20.62995% of their INITIAL SURFACE ENERGY; this is not20.6% of total mass.
This formula assumes the thin-wall family. No tension from the separate
sextic phase-interface model was transferred to the actual M=100 mediator.
Actual fragment shapes/walls and collision dynamics need their own treatment.

Energy conservation is restrictive. In the pair CM, if merged droplets settle
to their ground state and escaped radiation carries Eesc, then
 K_final=K_initial+DeltaS-Eesc.
If Eesc=DeltaS only, orbital energy has NOT cooled. If Eesc<DeltaS, the
remaining population can heat. Orbital cooling requires Eesc>DeltaS; complete
capture in this simplified bookkeeping requires Eesc=DeltaS+K_initial.
Energy outside a droplet but still trapped/absorbed in the halo is not escaped
cooling energy. Charge, actual droplet rest energy, random/CM kinetic energy
and escaping radiation must be separate counters. A non-lump energy fraction
or a decreasing lump count cannot establish orbital cooling by itself.
The actual vacuum radiation spectrum, charge-neutral emission channel, escape
probability and collision rate remain unknown. The previous dilute-halo rate
obstruction is not removed: any efficient early coalescence must occur while
the carrier is genuinely dense, or derive a different transport kernel.
