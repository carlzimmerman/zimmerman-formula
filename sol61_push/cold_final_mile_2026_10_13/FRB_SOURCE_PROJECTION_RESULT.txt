Exact counterexample to the general median projection:

y_i=a_i B_i, the best constant amplitude is
 B_hat=sum(a_i y_i)/sum(a_i^2)=sum(a_i^2 B_i)/sum(a_i^2),
not the sample median of B_i. Take a=(1,100),B=(.45,.70): weighted projection
140009/200020=.699975..., median23/40=.575. The correct varying model has
zero residual, while the median scalar surrogate has chi2=10001/64=156.265625.
This is a structural counterexample using a different likelihood, not a mock
of the actual FRB host/scatter distribution and not a new observational gate.
Actual marginal likelihood can have still different weights and skewness.
