1. What the source-side construction establishes
For a>0, E>0, D=E^2/(E+a), the electric action with L(0)=0 is
 L_e(E)=E^2/2-aE+a^2 log(1+E/a).
A=D/E=E/(E+a), B=D'=E(E+2a)/(E+a)^2 are positive away from vacuum.
Scalar c_parallel^2=B/A=(E+2a)/(E+a); vector extraordinary v_perp^2=A/B.
With the background axis fixed, their dispersions instead read
 omega_s^2=k_perp^2+c_parallel^2 k_parallel^2,
 omega_v^2=k_parallel^2+v_perp^2 k_perp^2.
Thus the quoted reciprocal numbers compare different propagation directions;
same-direction speeds are not generally reciprocals. The vector extraordinary
cone is subluminal, ordinary cone luminal, on this nonzero electric background.
This does not settle full magnetic backgrounds, coupling signs, attraction,
lensing, a dynamical medium, or the GR initial-value problem.

2. Vacuum obstruction to a positive quadratic linearization
Both A and B tend to zero as E tends to zero, while v_perp^2 tends to 1/2.
L_e=E^3/(3a)+O(E^4), so the finite ratio of principal coefficients supplies
no nondegenerate quadratic vacuum kinetic term. An L(F) description with
F=(E^2-Bmag^2)/2 also needs an extension across F=0 and to magnetic fields;
the positive electric branch behaves as F^(3/2) and is not C2 there.
This is not a universal ill-posedness theorem for degenerate nonlinear PDEs.
It leaves uniform vacuum energy estimates/linearized strong hyperbolicity
unestablished for the proposed unregulated L(F) attachment.

3. Isolated positive-energy exterior has a logarithmic tail
For nonzero net positive Gauss charge Q and a flat asymptotic exterior,
 D=Q/(4pi r^2), E=(D+sqrt(D^2+4aD))/2 ~ sqrt(aQ/(4pi))/r.
Hamiltonian density H=ED-L_e has H'=E D'>0 and
 H~2E^3/(3a); hence
 lim_(r->infinity) 4pi r^3 H = sqrt(a) Q^(3/2)/(3 sqrt(pi)) > 0.
The exterior energy integral therefore diverges as log r. For a=1,Q=4pi,
energy per logarithmic radius at r=100,1000,10000 is respectively
8.409059,8.380723,8.377895, approaching 8pi/3=8.377580.
Consequently the globally exact unregulated law does not supply a finite
isolated gauge-energy body; an asymptotically flat finite-ADM completion
with this positive asymptotic tail is not obtained. This conclusion assumes
that tail persists. Screening, compensation, finite basins, cosmological
boundaries or another exterior phase can change the premise and must be
specified and budgeted. The earlier round4 Maxwell exterior is a distinct
action/phase; its results are not invalidated or donated to this attachment.
