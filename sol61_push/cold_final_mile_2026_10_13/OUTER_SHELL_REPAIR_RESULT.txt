EXACT EXTERNAL-SHELL TRANSFER (new necessary condition)
Keep the current scalar spherical lensing relation and a fixed baseline.
A thin spherical shell of mass dM at radius a has, for 0<R<a,
 y=sqrt(1-(R/a)^2),
 Sigma(R)=dM/(2 pi a^2 y),
 M_cyl(<R)=dM(1-y),
 DeltaSigma_shell/dM = -(1-y)^2/(2 pi R^2 y) = K(R,a) < 0.
For R>a it is +1/(pi R^2). The ideal shell is singular exactly at R=a;
no assertion or evaluation at that boundary is used. At R<<a,
 K=-R^2/(8 pi a^4)+O(R^4/a^6).
Thus it would be WRONG to say all added positive mass increases the inner
ESD: a hollow/exterior reservoir decreases it through projection.

Nevertheless the inner response is strongly suppressed. For fixed R<a_min,
y increases with a and (1-y)^2/y=1/y-2+y decreases with y. Therefore
 K(R,a_min) <= K(R,a) < 0 for a>=a_min.
Any nonnegative spherical density supported outside a_min, total mass M_ext,
is a positive mixture of shells, so
 DeltaSigma_ext(R) >= M_ext K(R,a_min).
To lower the fixed baseline by d>0 requires
 M_ext >= d/|K(R,a_min)|.
This bound is independent of the exterior radial profile. It does not
apply to a component extending inside a_min, baseline mass re-fitting,
redistribution/removal of existing material, nonspherical projections,
negative density contrasts, or changes to the metric/lensing dictionary.
A nonnegative nonincreasing projected Sigma instead has DeltaSigma>=0
and cannot by simple addition lower a fixed inner baseline.

FAILED IMPLICATION / PRACTICAL CONSEQUENCE
An outer-only spherical positive addition within the current census budget
cannot remove this nominal inner residual at the existing diagnostic masses.
Adding it may still improve outer lensing. A joint repair must reprofile the
central baryonic mass, move existing inner mass, extend a physically derived
component inward, revise the inventory, or justify another lensing response.
That is a necessary route distinction, not a no-go theorem for cold energy,
for two-halo structure, or for the live gas experiment. The no-edge baseline
itself is not a finite stock solution; it is the source's stated diagnostic.
