1. Dimensional scan: exact countercheck
Use mass density rho, and dimensions in order M,L,T:
 a0=(0,1,-2), G=(-1,3,-2), c=(0,1,-1),
 hbar=(1,2,-1), rho=(1,-3,0).
For a monomial a0^a G^g c^v hbar^h rho^d to be a mass, the mass equation is
 -g+h+d=1, not g+h+d=1 as implemented/commented in ARGON03.
The script assigns a=4g+4h-3, v=6-10g-9h, d=1-g-h.
Its resulting dimension is M^(1-2g), with L,T exponents zero. Except g=0,
these are not masses and cannot be compared numerically with kilograms.
The corrected exponent family is
 d=1+g-h, a=-3-2g+4h, v=6+2g-9h.
A bounded integer scan, even corrected, could not establish the unrestricted
claim that no dimensionally consistent combination reaches an energy band.
The source's negative dimensional-search conclusion is therefore not proved.
This does NOT derive an optical mass; that mass is still an unspecified input.

2. The vacuum scale does not fix the carrier's mass
With the source's supplied density rho_DE=4a0^2/(G c^2), the quantity
 m_DE=(rho_DE hbar^3/c^3)^(1/4)
is dimensionally a mass, m_DE c^2=0.002240117016591203 eV with the displayed
SI constants. Source value differs by4.36e-7 fraction solely through its
kg-to-eV conversion constant; no physical discrepancy follows.
But m_chi=C_m m_DE, with unspecified positive dimensionless C_m, has the same
dimensions. Dimensional analysis supplies no equation fixing C_m=1, identifying
chi with a dark photon, or selecting its population. More generally
 Pi=a0 sqrt(hbar G/c^7) is dimensionless, and
 m=sqrt(hbar c/G) F(Pi)
is a dimensionally valid family for arbitrary dimensionless F. The supplied
rho_DE relation does not make a particular F a dynamical prediction.
The original CFG723 handoff says the mass VALUE MAY be set by rho_DE (F5);
a pole mass/spectrum calculation and abundance history are still missing.
Neither an integer scan nor a fourth-root energy-density scale supplies them.

3. Stored lower-coupling photon rate is 100 times too large
Under the source's own fixed-density, fixed-mass response, N scales as eps^2.
The stored values are N(1e-12)=43935358.97297008/day and
N(1e-14)=439353.5897297008/day. The latter uses division by100.
Correct division is10000, giving4393.535897297009/day. The same wrong /100
appears in the print statement. The headline eps=1e-12 rate itself is not
changed by this correction. ARGON06's separate eps=1e-13 scaling to about
4.4e5/day is arithmetically consistent; it must not be mistaken for eps=1e-14.
No actual detection significance follows from a photon count alone.

4. Smallest missing inference in the proposed kill test
The specified detector model is P=eps^2 f_chi rho_local,E c A C_response,
where f_chi is the fraction in the assigned species and C_response counts the
assumed polarization, material conversion, geometry and efficiency. For an
assigned mass/spectrum a null bounds the product eps^2 f_chi C_response.
It bounds eps alone only after those inputs have been fixed. A selected
frequency does not identify an unknown line with the theory's gravitating
carrier. Conversely, a verified non-gravitational signal from that actual
carrier would contradict the strict no-signal E6 hypothesis. This latter
conditional implication survives; an arbitrary 542GHz line or a finite
sensitivity null is not by itself a complete framework decision.
The core theory does not require the newly supplied dark-photon portal,
its coupling, its mass coefficient or its assumed population. A null also
leaves the zero-coupling limit and other masses untested. These are dependency
requirements for interpreting the proposed test, not changes to frozen E6.
No original frozen criterion, publication or portal action was modified.
