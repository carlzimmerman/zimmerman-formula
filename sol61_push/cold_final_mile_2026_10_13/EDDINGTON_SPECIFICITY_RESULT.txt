THE LOGNORMAL CORRECTION IS NOT INHERENTLY A SMALL-SIGMA SERIES
Let x=ln M_true, y=ln M_obs, likelihood y|x~N(x,sigma^2) with known fixed
sigma, and an idealized exponential log-mass prior proportional exp(-alpha x).
Completing the square gives EXACTLY
 x|y ~ N(y-alpha sigma^2, sigma^2).
This formal unbounded prior gives a proper posterior; physical mass bounds
or an actual selected population require their own normalization/likelihood.
Under these assumptions -alpha sigma^2 is the exact posterior log-mean/mode
shift for ANY sigma. The source README's claim that this shift itself is
only a small-sigma approximation is too broad. Large errors instead amplify
sensitivity to the PRIOR, error distribution, boundaries and selection,
none of which this diagnostic authenticates.

Mass and lensing summaries are different:
 median(M|y)=M_obs exp(-alpha sigma^2),
 E[M|y]=M_obs exp[-alpha sigma^2+sigma^2/2],
 E[M^s|y]=M_obs^s exp[-s alpha sigma^2+s^2 sigma^2/2].
For a local prediction DeltaSigma proportional M^s, plugging in the
corrected median (source's s=.47) omits the last logscatter factor.
The formula and three Gaussian quadrature checks pass. It assumes the
same local slope, error likelihood and prior; it is NOT a full halo model.
