2. Proper-time production versus log-scale integration
For N=ln a, dN/dt=H, so dt=dN/H=da/(aH). With a comoving production density
psi(t), converted comoving mass per dN is proportional to psi/H.
CFG780 defines dt=psi/(a E_ref), calls it 'psi dt per dln a', then integrates
it using diff(log(AA)). That extra1/a is appropriate to a da integration,
not to the dln a integration performed. It shifts the normalized source
history toward earlier epochs even after fixing total mass loss at z=0.
The predecessor conserved-history source uses j=Xi psi/(rho_ref H_ref E)
with derivatives in N, consistent with dt=dN/H. Thus this is a mismatch with
the actual intended MD14 proper-time rate, not merely an alternative variable
name. A new definition psi_per_log_time could define a different history,
but would not implement the stated source without an explicit translation.

4. Perturbations need the interaction, not just the background
Even after a consistent background, the standard growth ODE is conditional
on the perturbation and momentum-transfer closure. For one pressureless stock
in the leading subhorizon Newtonian comparison, write
 rho_dot+3H rho=-Q, q=Q/rho, S=deltaQ/rho,
 delta_dot=-theta+q delta-S,
 theta_dot+2H theta=-Agrav delta
assuming no additional momentum-transfer acceleration. Eliminating theta:
 delta_ddot+(2H-q)delta_dot-(Agrav+q_dot+2Hq)delta
   =-S_dot-2H S.
For local per-mass removal with unperturbed rate, deltaQ=Q delta, hence
S=q delta, and the extra source terms cancel, recovering the ordinary growth
form. For spatially smooth absolute removal, deltaQ=0, they do not cancel.
Thus identical background Q(t) does not uniquely specify the growth law.
Actual baryons plus converting cold stock require the corresponding coupled
species and transfer equations; a black-hole-biased source is not selected
by either closure without derivation. A pure vacuum stress also imposes its
own exchange-gradient constraint already preserved in the earlier source
audit. This note derives no new healthy interacting perturbation model.

The source explicitly discloses its use of a linear growth multiplier on the
nonlinear gravitating P(k). Its unchanged scorer also retains the original
projection/background conventions. These are frozen approximation choices,
not secretly a fully recomputed interacting-cosmology likelihood. They must
remain visible when claiming the branch is empirically tolerated. The earlier
CE046 FRB source-projection concern is also not resolved by calling this the
'FRB surviving branch'. No new FRB or shear exclusion is assigned here.
