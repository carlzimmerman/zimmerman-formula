NEW EXACT CALIBRATION RESULT (extends CE-FM-058 reference subtraction)
Write s=N_sky/tS, H and C as load count rates, DeltaJ=JH-JC:
  T=JC+DeltaJ*(s-C)/(H-C).
For a line u in count-rate units entering sky/hot/cold with coefficients
ks, kh, kc (all evaluated at the relevant channel/LO/polarization):
  T(u)=JC+DeltaJ*[s-C+(ks-kc)u]/[H-C+(kh-kc)u].
Its derivative at zero is
  dT/du=G*[ks-kc - (s-C)/(H-C)*(kh-kc)], G=DeltaJ/(H-C).
This follows by substitution and the quotient rule. It includes changes
in the gain denominator, not just subtraction of the cold load.

If ks=kh=kc, T(u)=T(0) EXACTLY at all finite u with nonzero H-C.
Apply the same sky increment to ON and REF: their difference is unchanged,
so the source L1 timing anchor is also unchanged. Thus the calibration
has a common additive count-rate null direction invisible to that anchor.
This is a mathematical counterexample to inferring end-to-end response
from timing agreement or postcalibration injection alone. It does NOT
establish that a physical mirror conversion signal has this phase response.
For conversion upstream of the load switch, a sky-only response can be
appropriate; it needs an optical/material transfer argument. Signals in
zero/dark pixels or unequal optical/load coupling add further responses
not modeled or inferred here.
