3. CONDITIONAL CURVATURE TRACKER
Jordan action: F(phi)R/2 - (partial phi)^2/2 - V(phi), ordinary matter
minimally coupled to the Jordan metric, with
 F=M^2+xi phi^2,
 V=E+h phi^2/2+lambda phi^4/4,
 M,xi,lambda,E>0.
h denotes a mass-squared coefficient. At nonzero constant phi,
 4xi Lambda=h+lambda phi^2,
 (lambda M^2-xi h)phi^2=4xi E-h M^2.
For h=0,
 phi_v^2=4xi E/(lambda M^2), Lambda=E/M^2,
 Lambda/phi_v^2=lambda/(4xi).
The vacuum ratio tracks changes of E, but its value is a free coupling ratio.
32pi requires lambda=128pi xi. This has not been independently selected.

Including scalar-metric mixing, set F_v=F(phi_v) and D_h=lambda M^2-xi h.
 K=M^2(F+6xi^2 phi^2)/F^2>0,
 m_E^2=2M^2 phi_v^2 D_h/[F_v(F_v+6xi^2 phi_v^2)].
A branch with D_h>0 and 4xi E-hM^2>0 passes these local kinetic/mass checks.
For h!=0 the ratio changes with E:
 d(Lambda/phi_v^2)/dE=-h D_h/(4xi E-hM^2)^2.
No protection of h=0 has been demonstrated. No solution to the smallness of
Lambda is claimed: for h=0 it still grows linearly with E.
