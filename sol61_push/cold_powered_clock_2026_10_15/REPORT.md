# Controller calculation and handoff

The powered clock equation is now explicit for the proper-source F(B,C) action.
The controller contributes an extra current that the stationary analysis did
not include. A direct uniform speed ramp fails that equation. An explicit,
compact shift correction cancels the extra current exactly under a stated
invertibility condition.

This solves the clock equation only. The correction changes the stress and
leaves independent controller and Einstein equations. A spatially uniform
controller ramp also has infinite energy, so finite-energy operation needs a
localized evolution. No working cold-energy drive has been established.
