# Four-dwarf gates for a fragment-merger mechanism

The scalar reference branches contain enough candidate binding energy that a small processed fraction could meet the **old conditional wind energy lower bounds**. A nonzero merger-excited mode would establish a microscopic channel, but would not establish the mass distribution, charge retention, galactic torque, low stellar heating, population rate, or four-dwarf selectivity. Sextans also has an unresolved observational-aperture/stock gate that precedes a formation claim.

This is a read-only reconciliation of saved evidence. The accompanying `sources_four_dwarf_gates.json` records 20 passing bounded arithmetic checks, exact hashes for 20 unchanged inputs, and measured RSS of 13.8 MB. No field, merger, eigenvalue, BVP, or formation experiment was rerun; no physical scalar mass or overall action normalization was selected.

## 2. Necessary transfer and heating fractions

Let beta be the reference stationary pair-gap fraction, f_proc the fraction of initial cold stock processed once through equivalent mergers, f_mode the fraction of the released binding reservoir deposited into the measured mode family, R(T) the fraction of that mode excitation radiated by the required time, and eta_orb the useful coupling to the specified **galaxy-centred cold matter wind**. Under the still-unproved identification of the galaxy cold stock with the scalar reference branches,

    f_proc f_mode R(T) eta_orb >= epsilon_i / beta,
    epsilon_i = delta_ideal,i / M_free,i.

Here delta_ideal c^2 is the old compact, optimally aligned matter-wind lower bound. It is not a universal bound on coherent multipolar radiation or a different endpoint. This inequality is optimistic: inefficient conversion, changed rest loss and reduced wind mass must ultimately be solved together in the nonlinear mass/J ledger.

The historical two-field beta is 0.0195503316358. The later **three-field modified-action** reference beta is 0.0195579700089. The following arithmetic uses the latter explicitly; it does not identify the two actions or establish the physical bridge.

For the last column, write E_exc for the energy in the relevant excitation, with useful wind fraction eta and directly deposited stellar fraction h. The simultaneous conditions eta E_exc >= E_wind,min and h E_exc <= H_allowed imply

The small Fornax/Leo I margins follow from the fixed old global tracer guards. They are bookkeeping requirements of those endpoints, not a universal stellar-heating law; potential changes, member selection and dynamical stellar response remain to be calculated. A universal microscopic merger law must produce different **histories and spatial deposition**, or a derived environment dependence, rather than supplying four independently fitted efficiencies.

## 4. What the measured weak-mode outlet actually exports

The modified three-field action has saved natural weak-mode radiation coefficients, with K the excitation above the stationary state at the **current bound charge**, K=E_bound-E_stat(Q_bound). These coefficients do not constitute a merger branching measurement.

| Bound Q | omega | Omega | nu=omega+2 Omega | gamma, chiral | gamma, specific standing pattern |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 80,000 | 0.921787530 | 0.075467655 | 1.072722840 | 3.80035e-6 | 1.83105e-5 |
| 160,000 | 0.909516399 | 0.055984807 | 1.021486013 | 1.67733e-7 | 1.44105e-5 |

The standing pattern is specifically H=(Y22+Y2,-2)/sqrt(2), with zero total intrinsic J and a natural monopole second harmonic. Its Q160 coefficient is about 85.9 times the chiral coefficient. It must not be assigned without calculation to a generic head-on m=0/Y20 merger or a different mode mixture.

Only the positive-Psi channel is open at these second harmonics. Consequently

    Q_out / K_out = 1/(2 Omega),
    E_out / K_out = (omega+2 Omega)/(2 Omega),
    K_out = (1-omega) Q_out + (nu-1) Q_out.

The first term in the last equation is charge unbinding energy; the second is the free-Psi kinetic energy. At Q160, Q_out/K_out=8.930994472, E_out/K_out=9.122885933, and free-wave kinetic/K_out=0.191891461; the remaining 0.808108539 unbinds charge. At Q80 the kinetic fraction is 0.481814626. The group speeds are about 0.204 c and 0.362 c respectively. These waves have not become the old approximately 128 km/s Sextans cold matter wind.

Counting 9.12 K as all useful wind kinetic energy while retaining the same core charge/stock is inconsistent. Absorption, redistribution or rebinding could give a different energy ledger, but needs an explicit destination and matching charge/mass accounting. Frozen-background extrapolation of the full Q80+Q80 pair gap through the Q160 outlet would export about 16.63% of initial charge and 17.84% of initial lab energy; it is not a small correction or an actual evolved solution.

The conditional weak-mode law is

    dK/dtau = -gamma K^2,
    R(T) = gamma K0 tau / (1+gamma K0 tau),
    tau >= R/[gamma K0 (1-R)]  for a requested 0<R<1.

Physical tau would be m_Psi c^2 T/hbar, and an overall action scale also enters physical energies/charges and quantum occupation. Neither scale is chosen here. The full reference gap gives gamma_standing Delta/Omega about 0.72 and 0.77, so the weak, slowly varying single-mode law cannot be extrapolated to that amplitude. A formation rate further needs n times the velocity/impact-parameter/phase averaged capture cross section, the merger branching distribution, and a density/assembly/return/survival history. Gamma or a geometric area alone supplies none of those population inputs.

## 5. Dwarf-speed orbital-energy precision

The same-action true-origin Q80 fragment energy is 76,151.17723273078; the pair energy is 152,302.35446546157 and its reference stationary gap is 2,978.72488092358. No physical mass conversion is needed for the following ratios.

For equal fragments with **relative** speed v_rel=2.3e-5, each COM speed is v_rel/2, and

    K_orb = M_fragment v_rel^2/4 = 1.007099319e-5,
    K_orb/E_pair = 6.6125e-11,
    K_orb/Delta = 3.380974609e-9.

The gap is about 2.958e8 times the orbital kinetic energy. A total-energy subtraction/conservation claim resolving 10% of K_orb needs relative accuracy about 6.6125e-12; 1% needs 6.6125e-13. If the speed parameter instead means each fragment has COM speed +/-2.3e-5, K_orb is four times larger: 4.028397276e-5, with 10% and 1% thresholds 2.645e-11 and 2.645e-12. State the speed convention.

The continuum true-origin and matched fine-grid gaps differ by 0.076568307, about 7,603 times the first K_orb. This is a difference of discretizations, **not measured drift**, and does not prove matched differences impossible: shared systematic errors may cancel. A dwarf-speed capture/velocity-cutoff claim must demonstrate that cancellation, separation convergence, matched v=0 controls, and E/Q/J drift relative to K_orb. Percent-level accuracy relative to the binding gap is insufficient. Phase/tail and finite-separation interaction energies can also exceed K_orb. Double precision by itself is not a no-go.

For illustration only, b=20 and that v_rel give J_orb=17.514770764. A Q160 chiral mode would need K=Omega J_orb/2=0.490280529 to carry this intrinsic J, much greater than the incoming K_orb but small relative to the binding gap. This requires a demonstrated, correctly oriented transfer from binding/internal energy; it is not a capture calculation.

## 6. Decisive next measurements

1. Measure merger capture versus separation, velocity, impact parameter and relative U1 phase with one action and conservative E/Q/J accounting. A fast head-on axisymmetric probe tests an m=0 sector at zero orbital J; it does not measure the chiral m=2 branching or nonzero-impact-parameter torque.
2. Project the remnant onto the matching charge-corrected bound modes; measure excitation K and the actual radiated E, Q and vector J. Separate core charge loss, retained envelope, escaped charge, and reabsorbed flux. Reconcile mode/continuum projection with same-grid stationary energies rather than treating total excess energy as the tested mode.
3. Derive a mass/action scale and population history, then evaluate f_proc, f_mode and R(T). Run a field/particle/stellar transport bridge that measures eta_orb, stellar heating, and where the last torque occurs. Preserve the wind/core mass constraint while varying efficiency.
4. Resolve the aperture/member definition and regenerate endpoints if required. Test one shared mechanism against all four mass and stellar constraints, with predictions for its environmental dependence. A matched Draco/Sextans result does not grant the Fornax/Leo I guard.

These are necessary closure gates. The saved stationary branches and weak radiating modes establish a candidate reservoir and outlet in the modified matter action; they do not yet establish a galaxy formation mechanism or complete the cold-energy theory.
