## Three concrete next physics steps

### 1. Select one galactic candidate and write its complete action/source table

Use the canonical finite-stiffness critical-current mechanics as the proposed
galactic target, with the confined radial body retained as a boundary control
unless its chosen phase/EOS can actually be connected to galaxies. If instead
the confined phase is selected as the physical candidate, require that
connection explicitly before inheriting the canonical law. This is a work
priority recommendation, not a claim that either extension already exists.

**Acceptance:** one dated candidate lists every species, mass, charge, current,
frame membership, EOS, stiffness, phase/wall variable, outer population and
free parameter. Vary every dynamical stock and field. Derive Gauss, force,
continuity and total stress-energy exchange equations; recover the intended
static law with the corrected remainder potential rather than double-counting
the completed square. Explain the positive density/source mapping for general
geometry. Count remnant charge and precursor gravity, and connect the derived
outer stock to the empirical assessment in the companion reports. A chosen
tail, wall, rate or fitted census prescription must be labeled as an input.
If no such joined candidate can be written, report the exact missing term or
incompatible premise and stop downstream validation claims.

### 2. Attack that candidate's angular/interface and metric problem

The cheapest decisive falsifier is a physical negative angular/interface mode
or an ill-posed matching condition. The positive radial operator, moving Gauss
trace, and separate bulk occupied/Maxwell controls already exist; another
radial-only success is lower priority.

**Acceptance:** derive a quadratic action after eliminating the actual gauge,
gravitational and material constraints; retain tangential, relative-current,
surface and outgoing-field degrees of freedom. Derive the boundary principal
symbol, traction/flux and a specified well-posed domain. Either exhibit a
counterexample or establish an energy estimate for a declared nonspherical
domain. Vary the same metric to derive motion and lensing potentials and
identify its scalar/vector/tensor modes. Include the full frozen nonzero
stiffness; an effective Gamma=2 radial pressure does not alone determine its
transverse or relativistic dynamics. A local bulk cone or tensor-only result
does not pass this step.

### 3. Derive the physical transition and evolving production budget before large formation runs

Identify an interaction/state that produces the EOS or phase, permeability,
conversion and source selection used by step 1. The charge-receiver witnesses
in `sol61_push/multicurrent_continuation_2026_10_15/RESULT.md` are reversible
conditional alternatives; renaming a charged population “free” does not remove
it from Gauss law, and a light remnant's charge cannot be deleted.

**Acceptance:** derive forward/inverse rates or explicitly retain them as
phenomenological inputs; give reciprocal energy/momentum/charge fluxes and
determine whether receivers/remnants stay, escape or screen. Show where exported
energy goes and what physical limit suppresses recapture. Derive the same
species' homogeneous expanding-background budget, including precursor and
vacuum evolution. State the resulting abundance/yield/momentum inputs. Only
after these equations and applicable health checks exist should assembly runs
start from independent finite-supply states, continue through multistreaming,
and test a declared basin and convergence tolerance. Native cosmology remains
open until background and linear perturbation/transfer equations, native
initial conditions and CMB/growth likelihoods exist; inherited T1x initial
conditions or a sigma8 adjustment do not meet it.

Parameter-origin analysis may proceed in parallel on the selected action.
Selected `kappa`, abundance and stiffness stay explicit inputs until a
mechanism removes their freedom. Candidate-matched empirical rescoring follows
actual predictions, not a successful score from a different parent.
