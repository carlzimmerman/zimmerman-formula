## 1. The exact target is more specific than an enclosed-charge closure

For P2, let b = GM_b(<r)/r² and g = sqrt(b² + a0 b). Differentiating the mass inferred from g gives

    rho_c = a0 b/(4 pi G r g) + [(b + a0/2 - g)/g] rho_b.

The second term is nonnegative and disappears outside a point source. CFG44's charge closure includes the first term only. Its raw code already recognizes this distinction; no new-error or novelty claim is made. Independent symbolic differentiation and three Plummer profiles agree. The diffuse test reaches a density ratio 2.403 between exact P2 and the first-term target.

## 2. Support can exist without deriving selection

A spherical orientation average of circular collisionless orbits has radial pressure zero and each tangential pressure rho r g/2. This satisfies anisotropic Jeans balance. The general construction is an Einstein cluster, not a new idea. [Boehmer and Harko, arXiv:0705.1756](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0705.1756) provides the relativistic context; [Acharyya et al., arXiv:2311.18622](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.18622) discusses scoped perturbation conditions. Our calculation is Newtonian and does not import a global relativistic stability verdict.

For ordered spherical dark shells with fixed mass m_i and specific angular momentum j_i, define A_i = M_b + sum of interior shell masses + m_i/2. In the noncrossing sector the shell potential is

    V_i = -G m_i A_i/r_i + m_i j_i²/(2 r_i²).

At equilibrium j_i² = G A_i R_i and V_i'' = G m_i A_i/R_i³ > 0. This is a self-gravitating radial-shell calculation with a fixed central baryon mass. With an extended fixed baryon profile the frequency gains the nonnegative term G M_b'(r)/r². Collective shell frequencies differ from frozen-field test-particle frequencies; the code checks that distinction.

Eight bounded orbit integrations retain energy and the specified turning points. A control dropping the centrifugal force fails all eight. For a point-mass P2 halo restricted to [r_inner,R], K = a0 M_b(R-r_inner)/2 and the active gravitational energy is -a0 M_b(R-r_inner), giving 2K+U = 0. This excludes the inference that support universally requires a baryonic thermal energy store. It does **not** supply energy-conserving formation: interior mass is held fixed, and a finite outer cutoff is initial data. Nonradial, shell-crossing, fully coupled and relativistic stability remain open.

## 3. Assembly supplies a sharp necessary transfer law

For the point-mass target, a dark enclosed mass label m has

    r_target = sqrt(G/a0) sqrt(m²/M_b + 2m),
    j_target² = G(M_b+m) r_target.

If this same shell must remain on the target as M_b changes, its angular momentum must obey

    d ln(j_target²)/d ln M_b = M_b/(M_b+m) - m/[2(m+2M_b)].

The right side vanishes only at m/M_b = (1+sqrt(17))/2. An entire profile cannot satisfy angular-momentum conservation while tracking the target at fixed shell labels. Under a slow factor-two baryon increase, the inferred a0/a0_initial spans 3.55785 at m/M_b = 0.1 to 0.56608 at m/M_b = 30. The required correction even changes direction across the profile.

This is an analytic falsification of this assembly prescription, not all formation histories. Allowing transfer of shell mass, non-spherical torques, dissipation or a response field changes the premises. A proposed completion now has a precise transfer obligation instead of an unspecified appeal to history. We do not claim global novelty of this elementary shell calculation.

## 4. A passive stream passing through fails in its simplest limit

Take a uniform cold collisionless beam of density rho_infinity and speed V_infinity through a fixed baryonic point mass. No absorption, time-dependent potential, interactions or dark self-gravity. Conservation of incident flux and residence time gives the exact spherical average

    rho_stream(r) = rho_infinity sqrt(1 + 2GM_b/(r V_infinity²)).

For example, integrate 2 pi b rho_infinity V_infinity db over incident impact parameter, multiply by the two shell crossings dr/|v_r|, and divide by 4 pi r² dr. Here v_r² = V_infinity² + 2GM_b/r - b² V_infinity²/r² and the largest reaching impact parameter makes v_r = 0. Numerical quadrature independently checks seven focusing strengths.

The excess density's logarithmic slope is

    -[1 + 1/sqrt(1+z)]/2,  z = 2GM_b/(r V_infinity²),

between -1 and -1/2. It never reaches the deep-P2 halo's r^-2 profile. The beam's energy at infinity is positive; static conservative gravity does not capture tracer particles. Its focusing radius GM_b/V_infinity² scales with M_b, whereas r_M scales with sqrt(M_b). Matching them by choosing V_infinity⁴ = GM_b a0 inserts the desired BTFR into the external stream speed.

This closes the passive ballistic model only. A time-dependent, self-gravitating or interacting stream is not covered. Nor is this the earlier CFG171 assumption that bodies are carried by a postulated flow. Real mass must source real gravity here. A mutation erasing focusing fails all seven quadrature checks.

## 5. Internal motion offers a concrete opening, with two hidden requirements

Blanchet and Seraille give local dipole dynamics in [arXiv:2502.14686v2, Eq. (3.22)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.14686) and [arXiv:2507.02563v1, Eq. (9)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.02563). The longer paper explicitly leaves formation and universality of the initial state unresolved. These papers were already cited in the repository; the new work here tests dynamics rather than claiming discovery of the literature.

Our illustrative dimensionless choice is eta_a = sqrt(3), so sum eta_a^-2 = 1, omega = (0.3,1,1.002), alpha_bar = 1, and constant g along x. This tunes away the linear response. It does not derive that tuning. With z_a = omega_a² xi_a/(2g), the translated local equation is

    z_a'' = omega_a² [e_x - z_a - (sqrt(3) g/2)(z_b cross z_c)],

cyclic in the three species. The induction is D/g = 1 - sum z_ax/3. No spatial equations or gravitational backreaction are solved.

Parallel dipoles give zero response. Transverse coherent oscillations give nonzero mean D proportional to g² over the tested window, and reversing one orientation flips its sign. At g = 0.0002 the numerical D/g² is 0.0264238902. The independent forced-oscillator solution gives 0.0264190815, agreeing within 0.019%. Doubling one initial amplitude gives response ratios 2.0473, 2.0944 and 2.1871. The original assertion of factor two within 0.1 fails at the highest forcing; the failure is retained. The quadratic expansion and long integration make a perturbative correction plausible, but no separate nonlinear error analysis certifies that explanation.

Two stronger conditions emerge from the independent calculation:

1. **Amplitude selection.** Fixed z oscillation amplitudes mean physical xi oscillation amplitudes proportional to g. This scaling was an input to the apparent D proportional to g². At fixed physical transverse displacements, the leading local correction is independent of g instead. A real transport or formation mechanism must select the scaling; the response equation alone does not.
2. **Maintained coherence.** With omega_3 - omega_2 = 0.002, the leading response averaged from t = 100 to 600 is 0.0264191. Extending to 600000 gives -0.00000869908. For unequal fixed frequencies the infinite-time mean vanishes at this order. Exact equal frequencies retain a response, but that equality or phase locking needs physical justification. This is local leading-order analysis; nonlinear locking and transport could alter it.

For clarity, the analytic longitudinal perturbation is proportional to the sum over Omega = omega_3 - omega_2 and omega_3 + omega_2 of

    omega_1²/(omega_1²-Omega²) [cos(Omega t) - cos(omega_1 t)].

The retained coefficient multiplies the two transverse amplitudes and their oriented correlation. None of the tested cases is resonant with omega_1. A favorable finite averaging window cannot certify a stationary constitutive law.

## Research decision

The best direction identified in this review is **a passing medium with a driven internal response**, rather than passive focusing or another equilibrium fit. This is a candidate route, not an established physical mechanism. The decisive next construction needs transport and an energy budget that predict (i) transverse amplitude proportional to local g, (ii) persistent orientation/phase correlation with the attractive sign, and (iii) environmental/history dependence. It must do this without setting those outputs in initial conditions or in the driving term.

A minimal honest target would evolve incident dark material, its internal energy and correlations, and the gravitational field together. Start with external conditions independent of galaxy mass; perturb baryon mass and stream conditions; determine whether a local response proportional to g²/a0 is an attractor. Check energy reciprocity before interpreting a fit. Any successful local response must subsequently reproduce the chosen full galaxy law, ownership behavior, cosmology and health conditions from the same action. The KiDS early/late difference is a useful discriminating constraint on the added history dependence.

The currently missing ingredient is **selection and maintenance of the response state**. Neither this session nor the existing papers have derived it. That narrower problem is more productive than more coefficient factorizations or conditional algebra, and it is the substantive route change delivered here.
