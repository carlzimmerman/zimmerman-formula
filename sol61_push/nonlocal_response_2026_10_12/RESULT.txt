NONLOCAL RESPONSE: A CONDITIONAL EXIT FROM THE LOCAL EXTERNAL-FIELD OBSTRUCTION
2026-10-12 research continuation; self-audited, not independently reviewed.

Outcome
-------
A variational, spatially nonlocal response can be exactly insensitive to an
added uniform gravitational gradient without becoming Newtonian at all scales.
In one periodic Gaussian-source experiment it differs by 8.3933% in radial-force
L2 from the local-response comparator; matched-point grid refinement changes
the filtered force by 0.6682%. This evades the earlier LOCAL-action obstruction.
It does NOT establish an isolated galaxy fit, observed accuracy, cold matter,
or a complete theory. The kernel is inverse-designed from the existing force
law, and the filter length is an extra undetermined input.

Construction and exact variation
--------------------------------
Let C=4 pi G, q=grad Phi, and B(g) be the baryonic acceleration required by the
existing repaired monotone response at total acceleration g. Assume
0 <= B'(g) <= 1 and 0 <= B(g)/g <= 1. Define

  ec'(g) = [B(g)-g]/C, ec(0)=0,
  P_L(k) = H(k) = 1-exp(-k^2 L^2/2),
  E[Phi] = integral [q^2/(2C) + ec(|P_L q|) + rho_b Phi] d^3x.

P_L acts componentwise, is self-adjoint, is a contraction, and annihilates
constants. With z=P_L q, stationarity gives

  div {q/C + P_L[ec'(|z|) z/|z|]} = rho_b.

The OUTER P_L is required by variation. Dropping it destroys this derivation.
The response is continuous at z=0 using its limiting vector value.
For q -> q+a with constant a, z is unchanged and div(a)=0. Thus the internal
equation is exactly invariant under a uniform external gradient. This statement
concerns the equation with an affine boundary component, not a finite total
energy for a uniform field on all of R^3. Curved external fields remain coupled.

Convexity, not a completed causal theory
--------------------------------------
The eigenvalues of Hessian ec(|z|) are (B'-1)/C in the radial direction and
(B/g-1)/C in transverse directions, lying in [-1/C,0]. Therefore

  delta^2 E >= [||delta q||^2 - ||P_L delta q||^2]/C >= 0.

This is a conditional convexity proof for the static functional. It does not
prove uniform coercivity as arbitrarily high frequencies are admitted. Adding
a positive time-kinetic term would give nonnegative linear stiffness, but the
instantaneous spatial filter is not thereby made relativistically causal.
No mass transport, metric lensing, or physical formation time is derived here.

What scale separation would cost
--------------------------------
As a CONSTANT-COEFFICIENT diagnostic only, take an ec curvature -(1-b)/C with
0<b<1. A Fourier mode then has stiffness

  K(k) = [1-(1-b) H(k)^2]/C.

The local comparator stiffness is b/C. Keeping the inverse-stiffness response
within a fractional suppression epsilon requires

  1-H^2 <= epsilon b / [(1-epsilon)(1-b)].

For b=.01 and epsilon=.1 this requires k_g L >= 3.8692. Requiring the filter
amplitude of an environmental mode H(k_e)<=.1 requires k_e L<=.45904.
Together these require k_g/k_e >= 8.4287. This illustrates an actual restriction:
one fixed length cannot strongly separate overlapping environmental and internal
spectra. It is not a theorem about the spatially varying nonlinear solution or
a measured environmental-force suppression. Gaussian filtering of a uniform
field is much weaker evidence than full own-host independence in a real group.

Cold-mass accounting
--------------------
Define p(z)=[|z|-B(|z|)] z/(C|z|). The equation reads

  div q/C = rho_b + rho_eff,  rho_eff = div P_L p.

In a periodic box, integral rho_eff=0 exactly. A nonzero continuous rho_eff
therefore has both signs. It describes a density CONTRAST, and cannot by itself
be an everywhere nonnegative additional mass density on this box. This does
not exclude a real medium with a positive background; that background, its
conserved current, and its sourcing must be supplied separately. On R^3 a
zero-total-effective-mass conclusion additionally requires vanishing boundary
flux; periodic cancellation alone does not prove it for an isolated system.

Physical interpretation and next discriminant
---------------------------------------------
Candidate interpretation: a collective response to the departure of a field
from its spatially smoothed environment. This is a proposed effective mechanism,
not an identified substance. It preserves reciprocity through a single energy.
It removes one obstruction at the price of a nonlocal length and still lacks
the independent mass required by the project.

One fixed-L box doubling now passes as recorded above. Before escalating this
to galaxy claims, add a displaced finite source and measure internal-force
changes. That discriminates realistic environmental coupling. A physical completion must derive the filtering from dynamical fields
and give a conserved cold-mass current with metric lensing. Relabeling rho_eff
as cold energy would not provide either.

Neither kappa=1/2 nor 32pi^2 is derived. The coefficient identity under the
existing mass-density convention remains Lambda c^4/a0^2=8pi/kappa^2=32pi.
