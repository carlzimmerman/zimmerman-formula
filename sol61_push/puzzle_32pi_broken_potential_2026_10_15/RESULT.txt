Main result
A negative scalar mass-squared term can reverse the combination W=U+M f U_chi
that blocked the previous construction, while the Jordan potential remains
strictly positive and the constant-field vacuum has positive canonical scalar
mass-squared. Exact local Einstein-scalar data then yield a stationary physical
circular speed at nonrelativistic speed and weak local metric curvature.
This is a new counterexample to extending our earlier sign obstruction to
negative mass-squared potentials; it does not contradict its stated h>=0 scope.
It is new to this investigation, not a claimed new result in the literature.

Following this local data reveals a limitation and another exact result:
when the scalar gradient vanishes at the stationary-speed point, the physical
logarithmic curvature is 2/N>0. It is a minimum, not a broad flat plateau.
Thus the local sign reversal is real, but this tested example is NOT a
successful galaxy force law or a derivation of 32pi.

3. Full coupled local equations, no frozen metric
Use the parent's metric ds_E^2=-exp(2nu)dt^2+dr^2/N+r^2dOmega^2.
Let x=phi^2 (xi=M=1 here), u=r^2 U, w=r(log A)',

 U(x)=(13/50-x/2+x^2/4)/(1+x)^2,

A dot means d/d(log r). The local system is
 dot(x)=-2(1+x)w,

Physical circular speed y=V_c^2=(a+w)/(1+w) has exact derivative

this expression independently.

5. Why this does not yet give a flat rotation curve
At any zero-gradient point w=0 with dot(y)=0 in this smooth system, c_dot=0
and u_dot=2u. Differentiating once more, imposing the radial constraint, yields
 d^2 log(y)/d(log R_J)^2 = 2/N >0.
At the chosen point its exact value is
 3163642000000/1581817539393 = 2.000004375481892 approximately.
The identity uses no special choice of c beyond the stationary-point and
nondegeneracy assumptions. It applies to this conformally coupled canonical
scalar system at such a point; it is not a statement about arbitrary matter
or time-dependent configurations. Since w=0 and dot(y)=0, conversion from
log r to log R_J introduces no extra term in this second derivative.
The initial scalar is a local maximum below its vacuum value, not the
vacuum itself. The quadratic rise of y on both sides is unavoidable here.

Numerical continuation over Einstein radius ratios 1/2 to 2 confirms the
turnover rather than an extended plateau. At twice the starting radius,
R_J/R_J,* approximately 2.00000106 and y/y_* approximately 1.66666591,
with logarithmic slope approximately +1.39999. These are finite numerical
results, not existence or asymptotic theorems. Two tolerance/step settings
agree on sampled y to about 3.71e-10 of the initial y. The independent exact
curvature identity, not grid agreement, decides the turning-point question.

6. What still prevents calling this a physical solution
No baryonic interior or external medium has been matched to these local data.
The profile near the point lies below the stable vacuum on both sides and
does not automatically approach that vacuum at large radius. The previous
maximum-principle obstruction still constrains any attempted return to a
higher positive vacuum through a source-free static region. An environmental
boundary that maintains the lower field is an additional physical input.
No inhomogeneous spectrum, observational fit, source-mass scaling, global
regularity, or radiative protection has been established.
The arbitrary action coefficients still do not select 32pi. The mass term
also destroys the earlier exact vacuum-energy tracking.

7. Actual new clue and remaining test
A stable vacuum and positive potential do NOT force W>=0. A symmetry-breaking
potential is a concrete way to evade that particular sign obstruction.
However, tuning a zero-gradient cancellation merely produces a narrow
turnover with curvature 2/N. To pursue a broad approximately flat profile,
one must investigate a nonzero scalar-gradient region and a sustained
negative-W balance, then supply a physical environment/source match and
check stability. This is a defined candidate route, not a claimed repair.
An exact extended flat interval and a numerical fitting scan were not tried
or implied by this calculation.
