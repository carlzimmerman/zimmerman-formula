# Dynamical susceptibility discriminator

Follow-up to the executed constant and material-compliance calculations. Test a positive independent scalar susceptibility chi, with local potential

 V_total(e,chi)=e^2/(2chi)+m_chi^2(chi-chi0)^2/2, chi>0,

and positive canonical kinetic and gradient energies. This is an explicitly declared reduced-model extension, not an established covariant completion. e=rho-rhobar-divP. A=1/chi is the matching stiffness. All new parameters are inputs; none is fitted.

First uncertainty: can a passive internal scalar, initially at its vacuum minimum, make matching stronger when the mismatch appears? Check the joint potential Hessian, equilibrium equation, and stiffness ordering. Generalize only by a written minimizer comparison for E(e,q)=V(q)+A(q)e^2/2, A(q)>0 and q0 a global minimizer of V. A global equilibrium statement is not a no-go for driven, metastable or explicitly baryon-coupled phases.

Checks before execution: symbolic Hessian determinant; implicit sign dchi/de for e>=0; bounded dimensionless e=0,.1,1,10,100 roots with chi0=m_chi=1; a reversed stiffness ordering must fail as a negative control. No observed galaxy classification is tested. If the scalar softens, specify the missing independent source required for a viable stiffening phase rather than tuning V to a desired class label.
