CURVATURE TRACKING: A CONDITIONAL ESCAPE, WITH AN EXACT FAILURE TEST
2026-10-15

Outcome
A classical scalar-tensor model has a nonzero constant-scalar de Sitter
branch on which Lambda/phi^2 is independent of an additive vacuum offset.
The branch has positive tensor and scalar kinetic terms and positive scalar
mass squared. The ratio is lambda/(4xi), still an unconstrained coupling
ratio. A scalar mass term generally destroys the offset independence.
This advances the stability and deformation analysis of Route C in
../puzzle_32pi_dynamic_2026_10_14/RESULT.txt, rather than claiming a new
scalar-tensor theory. It changes the model from the preceding fixed-background
elastic medium; it does not inherit that medium's force law.

1. Action and conventions
Signature -+++, metric connection, four dimensions, natural units:
 S = integral sqrt(-g) [F(phi) R/2 - (partial phi)^2/2 - V(phi)],
 F = M^2+xi phi^2,
 V = E + h phi^2/2 + lambda phi^4/4.
M,xi,lambda,E>0. h is a mass-squared coefficient (not assumed positive).
M is the constant coefficient in the action, not automatically the measured
Planck mass. F at the vacuum determines the tensor normalization.
No ordinary matter, no additional response sector, no higher derivatives.
At constant phi and R_mn=Lambda g_mn,
 Lambda=V/F,                    V'=2 Lambda F'.
For phi!=0 these imply
 4xi Lambda=h+lambda phi^2,
 (lambda M^2-xi h) phi^2=4xi E-h M^2.
Define D=lambda M^2-xi h and N=4xi E-h M^2. D>0,N>0 are sufficient
for the positive nonzero branch phi^2=N/D.

2. Include scalar-metric mixing when testing stability
Use the nonsingular transformation g_E=(F/M^2) g. The Einstein-frame
potential and scalar kinetic coefficient are
 U=M^4 V/F^2,
 K=M^2/F + (3/2)M^2(F'/F)^2
  =M^2(F+6xi^2 phi^2)/F^2 >0.
The stationary equation is
 U'=M^4 phi [D phi^2+h M^2-4xi E]/F^3.
At the nonzero stationary point the canonically normalized scalar mass is
 m_E^2=U''/K=2 M^2 phi^2 D/[F(F+6xi^2 phi^2)] >0.
The tensor kinetic coefficient F is positive. Thus the classical vacuum
passes the local linear no-ghost and no-tachyon checks. In the Einstein
frame its linear scalar obeys the canonical massive wave equation; around
an expanding de Sitter vacuum, homogeneous perturbations damp for m_E^2>0.
No global nonlinear stability or observational viability theorem is claimed.

3. Exact tracking in the quartic case
Set h=0. Then
 phi_v^2=4xi E/(lambda M^2),
 Lambda=E/M^2,
 Lambda/phi_v^2=lambda/(4xi).
Positive changes of E move the scalar vacuum and curvature together; the
ratio is exactly unchanged for fixed positive xi,lambda,M.
This escapes the previous non-selection argument's fixed-response-scale
assumption. It does NOT keep Lambda small when E is large, and therefore
does not solve the usual vacuum-energy magnitude problem.
The desired 32pi requires lambda=128pi xi. Neither stationary equations nor
stability select that coupling relation.

4. A necessary shape condition, not just a convenient example
Keep F=M^2+xi phi^2 but allow V=E+W(phi). Suppose a continuously varying
branch spans an open positive-phi interval as E varies, and demand
Lambda/phi^2=k throughout that interval. The scalar equation requires
 W'(phi)=2 k phi^2 F'(phi)=4 k xi phi^3.
Integrating on that interval forces
 W(phi)=k xi phi^4+constant.
Conversely, the quartic family above realizes this ratio on its positive
branch when k>0. Thus for this F and these branch assumptions, exact tracking
requires a quartic potential on the sampled interval. This is a local
functional classification, not a classification of all scalar-tensor models.
Its integration step is a prose proof, not a Lean theorem in this artifact.
Every positive k remains possible: tracking itself provides no special 32pi.

5. Mass deformation: explicit robustness test
For h!=0 on the positive stable branch,
 Lambda/phi^2=lambda/(4xi)+h/(4xi phi^2),
 d(Lambda/phi^2)/dE= -h D/(4xi E-h M^2)^2.
For D>0 this derivative is nonzero whenever h!=0. A mass term is permitted
by the action's phi->-phi symmetry and diffeomorphism invariance. These
symmetries alone therefore do not protect exact tracking. This is an allowed
operator test, not a computed quantum correction or a claim that every UV
completion generates h. M and E already introduce dimensionful parameters;
no exact scale symmetry has been supplied.
Exact example M=xi=1, lambda=4:
 h=0, E=1 or 2: ratio=1 in both cases.
 h=1/10, E=1: phi^2=1 and ratio=41/40.
 h=1/10, E=2: phi^2=79/39 and ratio=3199/3160.
Both deformed examples still satisfy D>0,N>0 and pass the local mass test.
Thus breaking the tracking need not introduce an instability.

6. Physical gap and next discriminating question
phi has acceleration units in natural units, but that is not a derivation
of a0=phi. An operational matter-force coupling must establish that dictionary.
Even a0=c phi with constant c would change the target to lambda/(4xi c^2).
Adding a response sector can also alter the vacuum equations and stability.
The new concrete requirements are therefore: a mechanism protecting the
quartic form, an independent determination of lambda/xi, and a matter-response
matching calculation. Tuning h=0 and lambda=128pi xi is not a prediction.
