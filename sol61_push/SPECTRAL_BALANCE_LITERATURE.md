## Singh, A Relativistic MOND

Primary version: [arXiv:2601.04290v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.04290v1), 7 January 2026, Tejinder P. Singh. Checked Sections 4–5, Eqs. (5),(9)–(14). Eq. (5) relates a0 to a de Sitter radius through an unspecified matching coefficient xi. With Lambda=3/ell², its dictionary gives Lambda c⁴/a0²=3xi². The target requires xi=sqrt(32pi/3); the inspected derivation does not calculate this number. The conformal argument fixes the cubic static power up to normalization. An additive static constant is not force-selected. Classification: adjacent result; no verified coefficient selection. The lapse-based invariant also requires a foliation/covariance analysis before it can establish a complete metric theory. We do not rely on this paper's claimed health.

## Visser, Lorentz invariance and the zero-point stress-energy tensor

Primary version: [arXiv:1610.07264v3](https://arxiv.org/html/1610.07264v3), 17 November 2016, Matt Visser. Checked Sections 1–3, Eqs. (1)–(16). The source uses relativistic sqrt(k²+m²) modes, weighted sums of masses to powers zero, two and four, and the full zero-point stress. Its finiteness/Lorentz-invariance implication depends on those assumptions. Our z=3/2 spectrum has different ultraviolet powers: equal counts and squared masses cancel its energy divergences. We separately derive a rest-frame pressure boundary identity; Visser's Lorentz theorem is not applicable to that dispersion. Classification: related method after translation, with a failed theorem transfer. Finite energy does not determine its amplitude or a MOND coupling.
