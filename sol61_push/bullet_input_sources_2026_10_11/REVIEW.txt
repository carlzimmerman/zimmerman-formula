PARAFICZ PRIMARY SOURCE
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1209.0384v2
The cached PDF identifies v2, 31 Aug 2016 in its arXiv margin; its manuscript
header says 15 Oct 2018. This date discrepancy is recorded, not resolved.
Section 5 conclusion 1 gives projected M(<250 kpc): main (2.5 +/- 0.1)e14
and sub (2.0 +/- 0.2)e14 solar masses. Thus the two CFG625 anchors are
source-verified as published reconstruction outputs. The source adopts
Om=.3, OL=.7, H0=70 and z=.296; uncertainties are ordinarily 1 sigma.
They are not independent raw aperture measurements or a cold-only budget.
Section 4.2 explicitly includes stellar AND dark mass in the galaxy
component. Consequently Figure 11's galaxy fraction 11 +/- 5 percent
cannot be used as stellar mass. Its 9 +/- 3 percent gas fraction is also
relative to the reconstructed total. Keep both as source context.
