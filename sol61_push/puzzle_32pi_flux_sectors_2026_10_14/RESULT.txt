Goal and what changed
Determine whether a mechanism that removes the freely gravitating vacuum
offset can independently select Lambda_geom/a0^2=32 pi. This is a different
question from reproducing the local response or protecting a chosen value.
The tests below concern a specified compact Euclidean gravitational saddle.
They do not construct a healthy Lorentzian MOND theory.

The project already contains horizon obstructions, ordinary two-form
sequestering, and the fixed-volume equivalence Vol*a0^4=3/128. Those are NOT
new discoveries here. The added calculation tests the Gauss-Bonnet version
with an explicit integer flux pair, then tries selection by relative action,
and finally tries compensating boundary weights. All three tests were executed.

1. Source-dependent starting point and declared extension
Coltman, Li and Padilla, arXiv:1903.02829v1, section 2, Eqs. 2.1–2.7 gives
the Omnia Sequestra action, rigid on-shell vacuum and Gauss-Bonnet multipliers,
and curvature-average constraints from two four-form fluxes. Its introduction
explicitly leaves flux data empirical. Kaloper and Padilla, arXiv:1606.04958v1,
supplies the earlier graviton-loop extension. Neither source predicts MOND.
This calculation tests a particular classical subclass and an explicitly
declared Euclidean continuation, not the papers' complete quantum mechanism.
Both sources were checked 2026-10-14; no local binary source copy is claimed.

Use c=hbar=1, M2=M_Pl^2>0, mu4=mu^4>0. Write lambda for the bare vacuum
counterterm, V for a constant matter vacuum contribution, theta for the
Gauss-Bonnet multiplier, and Q,Qhat for four-form fluxes. Choose sigma(z)=z,
sigmahat(theta)=theta^2/2; the two functions are not both linear. Define

 I_E = integral sqrt(g)[-M2 R/2 + lambda + V - theta E4]
       -lambda Q/mu4 -theta^2 Qhat/2.

This fixes our Euclidean signs and real-variable saddle prescription. No
integration contour, physical quantum measure, or preferred state follows
from it. Positive quantized Q=n q, n a positive integer, is an EXTRA hypothesis
used to strengthen the test; quantization is not derived from those sources.
On a closed S4, nonzero flux requires a topologically nontrivial gauge sector
with patchwise potentials. A globally defined exact form dA would integrate
to zero and is not the assumption here. Take Qhat nonzero.

Variation gives, on the round sphere of radius ell,

The radius equation is explicitly checked by varying ell, not just assumed.
Therefore Lambda_geom^2 Omega=24 pi^2 and

The topological constraint fixes theta for given Qhat, while the volume flux

All roots discussed here are positive-curvature round-sphere stationary

3. Attempted selector: choose a sector by Euclidean action
Under V -> V+d and lambda -> lambda-d, the bulk sum lambda+V is unchanged.
But the topological term changes. Exactly,
 I_E,new-I_E,old=d Q/mu4=d Omega,
and the change in the difference between two sector actions is
 d(Omega_1-Omega_2).
Fixed-sector equations therefore remain unchanged while relative actions
do not. A sector-dependent constant cannot be discarded when comparing
sectors. This conclusion follows from the displayed action itself.

On the saddle,
 I_E=-2 M2 Lambda_geom Omega + V Omega +(64 pi^2)^2/(2 Qhat).
For the two sectors above, Omega_2=4 Omega_1 and Lambda_2=Lambda_1/2:
 I_2-I_1=Omega_1(3V-2 M2 Lambda_1).
At V=0 this is negative: the larger-volume alternative has lower action.
At V=M2 Lambda_1 it is positive: the target has lower action.
The geometries have not changed. Only the vacuum offset has changed.
Consequently fixed sector weights combined with exp(-I_E) do not provide a
vacuum-shift-invariant selector. This is a conditional action-comparison
result, NOT an assertion that exp(-I_E) is a valid cosmological probability.

Allowing Omega to vary continuously is a useful diagnostic, not variation
of an integer flux. Let B=sqrt(24 pi^2), z=sqrt(Omega). Dropping the same
topological constant in every sector, for V>0,
 I_E=V z^2-2 M2 B z
    =V(z-M2 B/V)^2-M2^2 B^2/V.
The continuous minimum has Lambda_geom=V/M2 and lambda=0. It restores
dependence on the original vacuum energy. For V<=0 the displayed action
decreases without a finite minimum as volume grows. Thus this elementary
action minimization does not derive a robust finite target sector.

4. Executed repair: compensate the sector weights
Suppose an additional state prescription supplies weights w_n(V). In the
formal positive-real expression w_n exp(-I_n), relative invariance requires
 w_n(V+d)=exp(d Omega_n) w_n(V),
up to one common normalization. Its general solution is
 w_n(V)=exp(V Omega_n) f_n,
with arbitrary vacuum-independent sector weights f_n. This cancels the
offset sensitivity but leaves the relative f_n unspecified.

For f_n constant, the effective action is -2 M2 B sqrt(Omega_n)+constant,
which has no finite-volume minimizer along unbounded positive n. Trying a
damping choice f_n=exp(-alpha Omega_n), alpha>0, produces precisely the
same completed square with V replaced by alpha. Its continuous optimum
has Lambda_geom=alpha/M2. The target now requires alpha=32 pi M2 a0^2.
This is another input. The repair transfers the selection problem to the
boundary state; it does not solve it. More general states remain possible,
but none is derived by this calculation. The required compensating law is
an exact algebraic condition, not a demonstrated physical state.

5. Decision, evidence and resumption boundary
Topology plus the tested quantized flux hypothesis does not select the
coefficient. Fixed-weight action comparison fails vacuum-shift invariance.
Compensating weights restore invariance at the price of an arbitrary sector
distribution; the simple uniform and exponential choices were tested above.
This closes those specified selectors, not every quantum boundary-state or
UV-completion route. The original prediction remains open.

The next necessary input is a derived sector state/measure or superselection
principle, together with the actual MOND response coupling. It must explain
both the offset transformation of relative sector weights and the numerical
volume ratio 3/128. No such physical state is presently specified. Boundary
state/UV routes are deferred and open, not claimed impossible or solved.
