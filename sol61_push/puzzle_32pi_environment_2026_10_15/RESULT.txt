Outcome
Changing the scalar boundary values does not rescue an exactly flat circular
speed profile for this particular static scalar-tensor action, on the radial
branch where physical areal radius increases with Einstein areal radius.
An exact positive combination of potential and conformal coupling removes
the parent's assumptions phi>=v, phi>0, and vacuum outer boundary. No scalar
monotonicity or sign of its radial derivative is required.
This does not address extended ordinary matter in the exterior, time-dependent
scalar fields, different potentials/couplings, or radial branches on which
physical areal radius fails to increase. It does not select 32pi.

1. Action and hypotheses retained
F=M^2+xi phi^2, V=E+lambda phi^4/4 with M,xi,lambda,E>0.
Ordinary test matter follows the Jordan metric g_J=A^2 g_E, A=M/sqrt(F).
The source-free exterior contains only the static real canonical Einstein
scalar chi and its potential U. Use
 ds_E^2=-exp(2nu)dt^2+dr^2/N+r^2 dOmega^2, N>0, exp(nu)>0,
 K=(dchi/dphi)^2=M^2 D/F^2, D=F+6xi^2 phi^2,
 U=M^4 V/F^2.
Smooth solutions and a source-free open annulus are assumed. No existence or
source-matching claim is made. phi may be any real number, including zero.
Choose the smooth canonical coordinate with dchi/dphi=sqrt(K)>0.
Define signed coupling f=-M d(log A)/dchi=xi phi/sqrt(D).
It is no longer assumed that f, Q, or w is nonnegative.

2. The exact quantity whose sign matters
Use the parent's definitions
 Q=-exp(nu) r^2 sqrt(N) chi', B=f Q/M,
 a=r nu', w=r(log A)'=B/[r exp(nu) sqrt(N)],
 u=r^2 U/M^2, S=-B'/[exp(nu) sqrt(N)].
Without imposing any boundary sign, the scalar equation implies
 S=(r^2/M)[f_chi chi'^2+f U_chi/N].
Consequently the combination appearing in the orbit derivative is
 T=u/N+S
  =r^2 f_chi chi'^2/M + r^2 [U+M f U_chi]/(M^2 N).
It is T, not S separately, that must become sufficiently negative for a
flat circular-speed profile to be possible. The apparent negative U_chi
below the vacuum is therefore not by itself an escape.

3. Positivity holds for every real phi
Direct canonical differentiation gives
 f_chi=xi M F/D^2>0.
Moreover, writing z=phi^2>=0,
 U+M f U_chi = M^4 P/(F^2 D),
 P=E[M^2+xi(1+2xi)z]
   +(lambda z^2/4)[M^2(1+4xi)+xi(1+6xi)z].
Every term is nonnegative and the E M^2 term is strictly positive. Thus
U+M f U_chi>0 and T>0 everywhere in the assumed static region. This also
holds at phi=0: no division by f or phi is used.
This is an exact algebraic property of this action, not an approximation
about phi near the vacuum.

4. Physical circular speed and sign
The coupled equations from the parent yield, with dot=d/d(log r),
 V_c^2=(a+w)/(1+w),
 dot(V_c^2)=-[(a+w)/N+T(1-a)]/(1+w)^2.
Assume R_J=A r increases with r: 1+w>0. For a nonzero timelike circular
orbit, 0<V_c^2<1, which implies a+w>0 and a<1. Therefore
 dot(V_c^2)<0 and d(V_c^2)/dR_J<0.
The quantitative bound remains
 d log(V_c^2)/d log R_J <= -1/[N(1+w)^2] <0.
No positive-scalar branch, vacuum outer condition, sign of chi', or small
curvature was needed. The maximum principle from the parent is no longer
required for this result.

An exactly flat interval is excluded. In weak metric/conformal gradients
N approximately 1 and |w| much less than 1, the bound is near -1, not 0.
As before, strict decrease alone does not exclude all approximately flat
segments when N or w is large. Circular-orbit existence and radial stability
are separate questions; the inequality holds wherever the required timelike
orbit exists.

5. Rejected alternative and error record
The attempted new mechanism was a non-vacuum environment maintaining phi
below its vacuum value, so that U_chi<0 might produce a growing scalar charge.
An initial hand reduction omitted a factor of xi in the E-dependent part
of U+M f U_chi, incorrectly suggesting a sign reversal. The first executable
symbolic identity rejected that reduction before any integration ran.
The failed candidate is retained as rejected_candidate.py, not evidence for
a solution. Correct differentiation in the original phi variables and an
independent dimensionless reduction agree on the positive polynomial above.

For x=xi phi^2/M^2 and e=4xi^2 E/(lambda M^4),
 (U+M f U_chi)/U
 = [e(1+(1+2xi)x)+x^2(1+4xi+(1+6xi)x)]
   /[(e+x^2)(1+(1+6xi)x)].
At the proposed example xi=1/10, x=1, e=10, this equals 125/143>0.
There is no local zero-slope example or numerical IVP result to report.
This is an algebraic rejection of the proposed mechanism, not evidence of
instability and not a conclusion from a failed numerical solver.

7. Research decision
The boundary-only repair is closed for this action and the stated radial
branch: modifying environmental scalar values alone does not change the
positive sign. Extended matter within the annulus changes the lapse source
and is a different proposed mechanism, not covered by this result. A time-
dependent stress or a different action could also change the argument.
The original task still requires a physical response law and a derivation
of its relation to vacuum energy; neither is provided by this scalar tracker.
A future repair must explicitly alter the sign combination T or the field
content, rather than repeat static boundary searches inside this class.
