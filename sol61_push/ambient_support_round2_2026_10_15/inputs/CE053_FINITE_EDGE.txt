1. A noncompact tail need not have infinite integrated enthalpy
Write A=G M_Q/a0 (length squared). The necessary enthalpy resource outside
R>sqrt(A), expressed as mass equivalent, has infimum
 B(R) = integral_R^infinity 4 pi r^2 hc(r)/c^2 dr
      = a0^2/(2 G c^2) [A^2 R/(R^2-A)
                         - A^(3/2) atanh(sqrt(A)/R)].
Differentiating gives -4 pi R^2 hc(R)/c^2, and B(infinity)=0.
The integrand is positive, hc~D0^2 A^3/r^6, so B is positive and finite.
For a strictly passing gate, total enthalpy mass equivalent is > B, not = B.
Assigning h*=lambda hc, lambda>1, passes the SCALAR gate with finite integral.
This is a profile-level countercontrol to any inference that noncompact
support necessarily needs an infinite mass inventory; the original package
did not make that inference. It preserves the compact-edge obstruction.

In G=a0=Mb=1, c^-2=epsilon=V_f^2/c^2, at the original pure-chi finite-supply
radius Re=sqrt(s+s^2),
 B(Re)/Mb = epsilon/2 [Re - s^(3/2) atanh(1/sqrt(1+s))].
For s=5.364 the coefficient is 0.3163537598728310: epsilon=1e-6 gives a
threshold 3.16354e-7 Mb. This is a rest-mass budget only in the dust limit;
otherwise it is integrated enthalpy/c^2. If frame weights are not dust mass
fractions, h* can be below total enthalpy and more support may be required.
The strict gap and admitted sound response must also be specified.

Critical missing implication
Fixed exterior charge is compatible with no exterior charge density. A
neutral supporting stock would change frame membership; the explicit chi/S
candidate has no such neutral member. Extending positive charged chi/S instead
changes enclosed charge and invalidates the fixed-Q field used above.
Furthermore neutral pressureless matter cannot simply remain statically at
rest under gravity. Stress, flow or additional interactions, the varied frame,
source backreaction and actual Einstein equations must be solved. Small
integrated enthalpy alone does not construct a supported halo, a regular
vacuum action, uniform hyperbolicity at infinity or a physical core. The
material frame becomes dilute only at infinite radius; its off-shell limit
still needs analysis. Any such support also contributes to gravity/lensing.

2. ARGON02's untruncated curve is not its finite-supply curve
The raw Sigma_c function projects rho_c out to z=200 rM at EVERY projected
radius. Sigma_bar uses that function. Re is separately computed and printed;
it is never used as a cutoff in the actual projected density. Thus the finite
edge and its projected curve were not computed together. Several displayed
symbolic/deep checks are unconditional True entries; the raw formulas, not
those labels, support any exact claim.

For the specified spherical toy, rho=Mb/[2 pi rM^2 r sqrt(1+4r^2/rM^2)]
inside Re and zero outside. Then for R>=Re exactly:
 Sigma_c(R)=0,
 M_c,projected(<R)=s Mb,
 DeltaSigma_c(R)=s Mb/(pi R^2), with log slope -2.
The SETTLED MASS still contributes exterior shear. A free component is not
needed to make that settled contribution persist. Add baryonic projected
mass and any actual free component separately; total exterior point-mass
coefficient would be (s+1) Mb if all baryons are interior and no free matter.
No empirical inference is made from this identity.

For R<Re an independent shell representation is
 Mproj(<R)=M(<R)+integral_R^Re dM/dr [1-sqrt(1-R^2/r^2)] dr,
 Sigma(R)=2 integral_0^sqrt(Re^2-R^2) rho(sqrt(R^2+z^2)) dz.
The projected-mass formula was also checked via the complementary cylinder
mass s Mb - integral_R^Re dM/dr sqrt(1-R^2/r^2) dr.
