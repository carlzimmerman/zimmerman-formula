# Recent work used to choose this round

Review scope: the prior multicurrent checkpoint and the eight files copied in
[the source manifest](inputs/source_manifest.json), at initial Git
`283acbcffe1c6c4a5e8f9860f66e43c4e3808ab3` and snapshot Git
`880c56463f022a2a38bd63692971c8a16403b22c`. This is a bounded local review,
not a complete repository-wide empirical regrade. Untracked CE source reports
are explicitly identified in the manifest; the actual inspected bytes are
included here so this packet is reproducible.

1. The [previous boundary proof](inputs/MC_BOUNDARY_PROOF.md) excludes the
   unchanged smooth nonscreened taper to empty vacuum in its declared family.
   [CE053](inputs/CE053_FINITE_EDGE.txt) shows that a noncompact support requirement
   can still have a finite integrated resource. This selects the present
   question: can pressure and gravity hold an actual finite-mass neutral stock,
   rather than merely assign its enthalpy?
2. [CE055](inputs/CE055_PASSIVE_DAMPING.txt) retains the corrected isothermal
   surrogate operator and proves that passive drag cannot stabilize an
   unchanged negative restoring direction in the stated scalar/finite-matrix
   class. A different EOS, equilibrium and boundary must be derived together.
   The present neutral polytrope is such a changed comparison, not a repair
   achieved by applying damping to that old operator.
3. [CE057](inputs/CE057_LAW_DICTIONARY.txt) distinguishes canonical
   `gb=g^2/(g+a0)` from AG3's quadratic `g^2=gb^2+a0 gb`. It also distinguishes
   an acceleration-proxy band from a literal material shell. The copied
   [AG3 handoff](inputs/AG3_HANDOFF.md) carries the corrected interpretation.
   The failed K2b gate remains failed; this round neither reruns SPARC nor
   claims a new fit or a full-medium spectrum from the proxy widths.
4. The same CE057 report rejects a deep-regime force extrapolation into the
   opposite high-acceleration regime and preserves the finite-source/stiffness
   obligation. A formal cold supply radius does not establish an actual
   laboratory reservoir. The finite atmosphere below is a conditional
   equilibrium construction, not an experimental inventory or device.
5. Existing cosmological, detector and observational lanes retain their own
   criteria and ownership. No shared production run was restarted here, no
   native initial conditions were supplied by this construction, and no
   empirical result was transferred to this new neutral-support candidate.

The resulting research choice is narrower than whole-theory closure: vary a
new neutral current, build its equilibrium including its own rest-mass gravity,
and test its actual quadratic energy and interfaces. The exact regular-core
example is a useful witness in that problem; the charged galaxy-producing
species, formation mechanism and full metric remain distinct open items.
