QUESTION AND PRIMARY VERDICT
Is the galaxy response the motion of the cold mass itself, or a faster
collective degree of freedom carried by that mass?

Verdict: the current frozen S3 identification makes the halo-density response
mass transport. It can be collective mass motion, but that does not remove
its material inertia. The static S2 action alone does not select a dynamics.
A separate fast mode is possible; the explicit positive two-field lift
tested here does not turn the retained mass mode into that fast mode.
Actual microscopic identity and a successful fast-mediated galaxy theory
remain OPEN. No universal impossibility theorem for modified gravity,
entrainment, phase conversion or all coupled fields is claimed.

ROUTES AND EVIDENCE

B. Construct and solve a positive massive-displacement/light-field model.

1. WHAT THE FROZEN SOURCE EQUATIONS ACTUALLY SAY
Canonical October 14 S2 is a spatial static action with no time derivatives:
 L=-|grad Phi-KP|^2/(2K)-rho_b Phi-W_rem(K|P|)
   -gamma|grad P|^2/2, K=4piG, gamma=K Lstar^2/2.
S3 identifies div P=rho_c-rho_bar and P_dot=-j_c. Its rho_c is actual
positive cold rest mass. The static Newtonian constraint is
 laplacian Phi=K[rho_b+div P].
Only the longitudinal part of P contributes to this source at linear order.
A divergence-free internal mode does not supply this extra mass density.
Its own field energy can gravitate in a larger relativistic model, which
is a different source contribution and must be derived there.

Continuity alone is slightly weaker than S3: it gives
 div(j_c+P_dot)=0, not necessarily vector equality. Thus adding a curl to
the current is possible, but cannot remove the longitudinal transport.
For homogeneous rho0>0 and a nonzero Fourier wave vector k,
 j_L=-P_dot,L, delta rho=i k.P_L.
Ordinary cold-particle kinetic energy satisfies, by velocity-space Cauchy,
 T_density >= |j|^2/(2rho0) >= |delta rho_dot|^2/(2rho0 k^2).
Random streaming adds kinetic energy; it does not erase the mean-current
cost. This is a bound on the ordinary kinetic contribution, not a universal
bound on all possible interacting total energies or relativistic vacua.

On a finite periodic domain with smooth positive bounded rho(x), the
minimum-current formulation is also explicit. Minimize integral j^2/(2rho)
subject to div j=-rho_dot and zero net mass change. A multiplier gives
 j=rho grad lambda, div(rho grad lambda)=-rho_dot,
 Tmin=integral rho|grad lambda|^2/2.
This assumes the usual elliptic solution and admissible boundary conditions;
it is not applied across vacuum edges. A mediator cannot eliminate this
specified positive material kinetic term while keeping the same current.

For the linear full S3 identification the material coefficient is Im=1/rho0.
Consequently c_mass^2=gamma/Im=2piG rho0 Lstar^2 for the bare gradient term.
This is not a universal bound on gravitational signal speed, phase-mixing
time, local force-driven response, or the velocities of every coupled mode.
Local restoring curvature supplies a frequency gap; it is not a new small
material inertia. The numerical radiative-attractor time cannot be freely
set by c if the same P is still the displacement of this cold mass.

2. EXPLICIT LIGHT-MODE TEST, WITH RECIPROCAL COUPLING
For one longitudinal Fourier component let P be physical mass displacement,
Q an auxiliary response coordinate, with positive Im,If,kappa,gamma and W>=0:
 L2=Im P_dot^2/2+If Q_dot^2/2
    -W P^2/2-kappa(P-Q)^2/2-gamma k^2 Q^2/2+J P.
J is an external probe of the mass response; energy supplied by the probe
must be counted. With J=0 the Hamiltonian is a sum of positive kinetic,
locking, gradient and curvature energies. Both equations are varied;
the force on P is reciprocal to the force on Q.

Static elimination gives
 Q=kappa P/(kappa+gamma k^2),
 V_eff=[W+kappa gamma k^2/(kappa+gamma k^2)]P^2/2.
Thus the original W+gamma k^2 is recovered only to leading order when
gamma k^2/kappa<<1. This is an explicit new quadratic comparison, not an
exact finite-k replacement for the full S2 action.
Adiabatic following retains
 I_eff=Im+If[kappa/(kappa+gamma k^2)]^2 >= Im.
This is a low-frequency derivative expansion. At general frequency one
must use the exact two poles, not this effective coefficient.

The exact squared-frequency matrix is V-z M with
 M=diag(Im,If), V=[[W+kappa,-kappa],[-kappa,kappa+gamma k^2]].
Its determinant is
 Im If z^2-[If(W+kappa)+Im(kappa+gamma k^2)]z
             +kappa gamma k^2+W(kappa+gamma k^2).
It has nonnegative eigenvalues from the positive energy, with the uniform
zero mode allowed when W=k=0. No negative physical kinetic direction is
introduced by this particular construction.

3. THE FAST MODE DOES NOT BECOME THE MASS MODE
The clearest exact comparison is W=0 at small k. At k=0 the two modes are
 common: (P,Q) proportional to (1,1), omega^2=0;
 relative: (P,Q) proportional to (-If/Im,1),
           omega_h^2=kappa(Im+If)/(Im If).
Their long-wavelength common-mode slope is
             c_common^2=gamma/(Im+If) <= gamma/Im.
The fast relative mode mainly moves Q when If<<Im. It can signal or carry
energy without displacing much halo mass. Uniform k=0 P itself has zero
density contrast; these formulas describe the finite-k longitudinal limit.

This is quantitative in the susceptibility to the mass probe J:
 chi_PP(omega)=A/(-omega^2)+B/(omega_h^2-omega^2),
 A=1/(Im+If), B=If/[Im(Im+If)], A+B=1/Im.
Hence the fast pole's fraction of this inertial spectral coefficient is
                  B/(A+B)=If/(Im+If).
It is not a fraction of all dark matter, a cross-section, a photon
conversion efficiency, or a general observed spectral measurement.

Using the illustrative prior bare mass speed 84.09 m/s and assigning
sqrt(gamma/If)=c gives epsilon=If/Im=(84.09/c)^2=7.8675e-14.
The fast mass-response fraction in the displayed limit is then ~7.87e-14;
the common mode stays at ~84.09 m/s. These inputs demonstrate the mechanism,
not an empirical constraint on every light mediator. Resonant driving can
still move P with supplied energy. Altered interactions or current maps
must be evaluated through their own equations.

4. WHY FIELD-ONLY POSITIVE HALOS ARE NOT AUTOMATICALLY POSITIVE MATTER
For a localized P with vanishing boundary flux,
 integral delta rho d^3x=integral div P d^3x=0.
A positive effective halo region therefore needs compensating depletion,
boundary inflow, or a true production source if rho is to be actual mass.
If rho_bar=0 everywhere initially, a positive halo cannot grow as conserved
positive matter from an empty isolated domain through polarization alone.

An exact smooth witness is P=A r exp(-r^2) rhat, A>0. Its contrast is
 delta rho=A(3-2r^2)exp(-r^2).
The total signed mass is exactly zero; the central contrast is positive
and the outer contrast is negative. Its minimum is -2A exp(-5/2).
A background rho_bar>=2A exp(-5/2) allows a nonnegative physical density;
rho_bar=0 makes the negative outer region unphysical as actual cold matter.
This is a static admissibility witness, not a solution of the S2 equations.
It shows why effective signed polarization and counted positive cold matter
need separate audits. An infinite reservoir or an open boundary is a physical
input, not mass created by a fast wave.

5. LOOPS THAT REMAIN OPEN AND WHAT THEY MUST CHANGE
Entrainment or derivative-current mixing: the earlier reciprocal-anchor
clock calculation actually changes the current/inertia relation. Its
restricted positive-energy branch has wavelength-dependent inertia and
formal quartic dispersion; it has no inherited full causal certificate.
It is not covered by our position-locking two-field restriction. Moreover,
the actual restricted clock block does not provide a fast longitudinal
rest-mass mode. Its first-order current is
 delta K=-p_dot+P0 laplacian(pi)-(P0.grad)grad(pi).
In Fourier space the clock term is
 [-k^2 P0+(P0.k)k]pi, whose dot product with k is exactly zero.
That report selects p along P0_transverse and a transverse reference
displacement. Thus div(delta K)=0 and its first-order rest-density
perturbation is zero in that held homogeneous block. This is the block
with the reported derivative-inertia/quartic dispersion. It cannot be
promoted to a fast halo REST-MASS response. Full background gradients,
metric mixing and field/interaction energy can change gravitational
sources; those were not included in that block and remain uncomputed.
Its full inhomogeneous metric/current analysis remains open, so we neither
reject every such route nor silently call that candidate healthy.

An internal charge/spin-like mode can have little total-density motion.
The new neutral-pair comparison already exhibits the distinction. To
explain the observed extra gravitational pull it must either rearrange
the actual mass later or have its additional force/metric source derived.
It cannot be inserted as positive halo mass solely through a field label.
Binary-superfluid literature likewise distinguishes density and relative
modes. This is an analogy and a method, not evidence for a dark superfluid.

Fast mediator plus real cold matter: physically possible in principle.
It changes the microscopic content/dynamics, while preserving the need
to count material mass and reciprocal exchange. It must derive baryon
coupling, static galaxy limit, physical metric/lensing and relaxation.
Our test says that appending positive light-field inertia and a locking
spring is not sufficient to speed the retained mass displacement.

Effective modified gravity: if P is an independent fast field whose div P
is an effective signed source instead of actual conserved mass, a small
I may be a legitimate new choice. Then S3's mass interpretation and its
cosmological abundance cannot be inherited unchanged. Its lensing theory
must be derived rather than identified with a separately counted cold halo.
