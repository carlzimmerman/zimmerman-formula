Result and scope
For the specified static, positive-field, source-free exterior of the
quartic curvature tracker, the coupled Einstein-scalar equations imply that
the physical matter-frame circular speed strictly decreases with areal radius
wherever a timelike circular orbit exists. No weak-field approximation is
used. This rules out an exactly flat rotation curve on that branch. In the
weak-metric and weak-conformal-gradient limit the bound approaches the
Keplerian slope, ruling out a nearly flat curve in that controlled limit.
Strict monotonicity alone does not exclude every approximately flat segment
in a strongly curved or strongly conformally varying solution.
No source-matched solution, universal modified-gravity impossibility theorem,
or coefficient selection is claimed. The original 32pi target is unresolved.

1. Model and hypotheses
The Jordan action is F(phi)R_J/2 - (partial phi)^2/2 - V(phi), with
 F=M^2+xi phi^2,
 V=E+lambda phi^4/4,
 M,xi,lambda,E>0,
 E=lambda M^2 v^2/(4xi), v>0.
Matter is minimally coupled to g_J. In a source-free annulus there is only
the scalar stress. In the Einstein frame g_E=(F/M^2)g_J,
 A=M/sqrt(F), g_J=A^2 g_E,
 dchi/dphi=sqrt(K)>0,
 K=M^2(F+6xi^2 phi^2)/F^2,
 U=M^4 V/F^2>0.
Use the exact static spherical metric
 ds_E^2=-exp(2nu(r))dt^2+dr^2/N(r)+r^2 dOmega^2,
where N>0 and exp(nu)>0 on the closed annulus r_in<=r<=R, r_in>0.
Assume a smooth strictly positive scalar solution, phi(r_in)>=v and phi(R)=v.
This is a boundary-value hypothesis, not proof of source or cosmological
matching. In particular, a Dirichlet vacuum value at R is NOT a claim that
the field is smoothly constant outside R; that would impose extra data.

2. Curved scalar equation preserves the charge sign
The exact scalar equation is
 [exp(nu) r^2 sqrt(N) chi']'=exp(nu) r^2 U_chi/sqrt(N).
The potential obeys
 U_phi=lambda M^6 phi(phi^2-v^2)/F^3.
The maximum-principle argument from the parent still applies: at an interior
minimum with 0<phi<v the scalar equation demands N chi''=U_chi<0, a
contradiction. Thus phi>=v. Define
 Q=-exp(nu) r^2 sqrt(N) chi'.
The outer minimum implies chi'(R)<=0. The scalar equation and U_chi>=0 then
give Q>=0, Q'<=0 and phi'<=0 throughout the annulus.
Define the positive coupling magnitude
 f=-M d(log A)/dchi=xi phi/sqrt(F+6xi^2 phi^2).
Since df/dphi>0, f'(r)<=0. Therefore B=f Q/M>=0 and B'<=0.
Here B is a radial flux product, not the constant Yukawa coefficient used
in the earlier linear-force report.

3. Include the scalar's exact gravitational stress
The canonical static scalar has
 rho=N chi'^2/2+U,
 p_r=N chi'^2/2-U,
 p_t=-N chi'^2/2-U.
The Einstein equations, normalized by M^2, give
 N'=(1-N)/r-r rho/M^2,
 nu'=(1-N)/(2rN)+r p_r/(2M^2 N).
Their lapse-divergence consequence is
 [r^2 exp(nu) sqrt(N) nu']'
   =exp(nu) r^2 (rho+p_r+2p_t)/(2M^2 sqrt(N))
   =-exp(nu) r^2 U/(M^2 sqrt(N)).
The scalar gradient contributions cancel in rho+p_r+2p_t. They still
contribute to N and must not be omitted from the metric equations.

4. Reduce to physical circular-orbit quantities
Let
 a=r nu',
 w=r(log A)'=B/[r exp(nu) sqrt(N)]>=0,
 u=r^2 U/M^2>=0,
 D=1+r nu'+r N'/(2N)=(1-u)/N,
 S=-B'/[exp(nu) sqrt(N)]>=0.
A dot below means d/d(log r), not a time derivative. The exact equations are
 dot(a)=-D a-u/N,
 dot(w)=-D w-S.
The physical Jordan areal radius is R_J=A r and
 d(log R_J)/d(log r)=1+w>0.
For a circular geodesic, the speed measured by a static Jordan observer is
 V_c^2=(a+w)/(1+w).
This follows directly from the temporal and angular metric coefficients:
V_c^2=(d log(A^2 exp(2nu))/dr)/(d log(A^2 r^2)/dr).
A nonzero timelike circular orbit requires 0<V_c^2<1, hence a+w>0 and a<1.
This does not assert that every radius admits a circular orbit, or that such
an orbit is radially stable.

5. Cancellation giving the exact obstruction
Differentiate the orbit expression and substitute the equations above:
 dot(V_c^2)=[-D(a+w)-(u/N)(1+w)-S(1-a)]/(1+w)^2.
Substituting D=(1-u)/N cancels the potentially adverse potential term:
 dot(V_c^2)=-[(a+w)/N+(u/N+S)(1-a)]/(1+w)^2 <0.
Every term in the square bracket is nonnegative and its first term is
strictly positive. No assumption u<1 is required. Since R_J increases with r,
the physical circular speed is strictly decreasing with physical radius.
More quantitatively,
 d log(V_c^2)/d log R_J
 = -1/[N(1+w)^2]
   -(u/N+S)(1-a)/[(a+w)(1+w)^2]
 <= -1/[N(1+w)^2] <0.
When N is close to 1 and w is small, the upper bound is close to -1,
whereas a flat rotation curve has logarithmic slope 0.
This includes the full scalar stress and metric response; it does not reuse
the fixed-Minkowski force formula outside that formula's validity range.

6. Independent checks and adversarial self-review
- Einstein components agree with the sourced static anisotropic equations.
- The lapse-divergence identity was checked from all three independent
  Einstein tensor components, not merely assumed from the radial equations.
- For Schwarzschild-de Sitter, N=1-2mu/r-H^2 r^2 and exp(nu)=sqrt(N), the
  lapse flux is mu-H^2 r^3 and its derivative is -3H^2 r^2, exactly matching
  U=3M^2 H^2. The zero-H limit also gives the Schwarzschild circular-speed
  derivative with the expected sign.
- The potentially dangerous a<0 case is allowed: the proof only needs
  a+w>0 and a<1, which follow from a timelike circular orbit.
- Large positive u does not invalidate the sign after the cancellation.
- A horizon, phi crossing zero, ordinary exterior matter, alternate boundary
  values or time dependence is outside the proof.
- The proof is conditional on existence; it does not manufacture a solution
  by choosing the metric and scalar independently.
- This was self-review, not independent external refereeing. No novelty claim.

8. Current decision and remaining routes
The proposed escape 'include backreaction in the same positive-branch static
quartic tracker with these boundaries' cannot yield an exactly flat curve.
Its weak-metric regime also fails the near-flat target. This removes the
last explicitly proposed approximation loophole for that scoped construction.
The broader program remains open: alternate exterior matter/response fields,
non-vacuum environment, time dependence, different scalar branches and
strong-field approximately flat segments were not excluded. Each requires
new equations or boundary data, and none currently selects lambda/xi.
Next discriminating work should supply a concrete response mechanism or
boundary condition that changes a sign used above, then test its health and
source matching. Repeating parameter scans within the proved class cannot
produce an exactly flat interval.
