# Source scope

Checked 2026-10-13 against primary arXiv PDFs, no local copies retained.

1. E. Timmermans, Phase Separation of Bose-Einstein Condensates, cond-mat/9709301v2 (21 October 1997 identifier; PDF rendering also carries a 2018 typesetting date). https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/9709301 . Eqs. 1–6: coupled GP equations and homogeneous two-component stability. Map equal masses and densities, lambda_1=lambda_2=g_s and lambda=g_x. Then the difference branch has omega²=epsilon[epsilon+2n(g_s-g_x)]. Does not validate this repo's scalar-field matching or its existence.

2. Controlling phase separation of a two-component Bose-Einstein condensate by confinement, arXiv:1204.1256. https://arxiv.org/pdf/1204.1256 . Opened official PDF. Its confinement/kinetic-energy qualification motivates explicitly checking the finite-box mode band instead of asserting separation from the interaction inequality alone. No numerical coefficient in this package is imported from that paper.

This was a targeted check of known mixture physics and applicability, not a novelty search or empirical validation. The carrier reduction and equal-mass matrix were calculated separately in this package.
