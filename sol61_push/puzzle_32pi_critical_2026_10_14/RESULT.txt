CRITICAL32PI-001 — change the question to a critical amplitude ratio
2026-10-14. New research direction; original physics goal remains OPEN.

Previous work reconstructed a local statistical response but could not fix
its absolute vacuum offset. The new angle is criticality: ask whether energy
and response are controlled by one scaling function whose amplitude ratios
are independent of normalization. This is a mechanism class, not a claim that
our vacuum belongs to a known universality class.

1. A precise, normalization-independent target

For the corrected potential the physical matching requirement is
 R_vac=epsilon_Lambda W3²/W2³=64pi.
W2,W3 are derivatives with respect to the SAME polarization variable at its
vacuum. If a critical effective potential takes the scaling form
 W(P)=B Psi(P/D),
then W2=B Psi2/D², W3=B Psi3/D³. If its relevant energy is B Psi0, the ratio is
 R=Psi0 Psi3²/Psi2³.
Both B and D cancel. Critical exponents alone do not determine this ratio:
the actual scaling function, operator dictionary and state are required.
Cancellation under rescaling alone also does not prove RG universality.

A phase-energy difference can remove a shared additive background, but total
gravitating vacuum energy cannot be silently replaced by that difference.
For epsilon_total=epsilon_background+Delta epsilon the extra contribution is
 epsilon_background W3²/W2³.
Thus this route has TWO distinct obligations: a critical response ratio and a
principle tying its energy to the total gravitational source. This is a more
restrictive, testable question than hunting for appearances of32pi².

2. A concrete non-topological source of pi²

Consider a leading-large-N, three-dimensional O(N) scalar composite model.
Conventions: per-component density; g rho³/6 interaction; renormalized rho
relative to its critical value; disordered branch; scalar mass m>=0. No
identification of rho or m with the MOND polarization is assumed.

We independently evaluate the UV-subtracted real-scalar Gaussian determinant:
 J(m;L)=1/(4pi²) int_0^L k²[log(1+m²/k²)-m²/k²] dk.
Then
 dJ/dm=-m² atan(L/m)/(2pi²) -> -m²/(4pi),
 J(m;infinity)=-m³/(12pi), J(0)=0.
The subtraction fixes a reference and a relevant counterterm; it does not
fix gravity's absolute cosmological term. The leading auxiliary potential is
 Vaux=g rho³/6-m²rho/2+J(m).
The m saddle gives rho=-m/(4pi) on the disordered branch (rho here is a
renormalized composite, not an unrenormalized positive squared field).
Eliminating rho yields
 V(m)=m³/(24pi) [1-g/(16pi²)].
The independent rho saddle gives m²=g rho². Thus a nonzero mass at vanishing
relevant deformations requires
 g_c=16pi².
This derives a pi²-containing critical coupling from a momentum integral;
it is not topology or a number selected by fitting the target. Its numerical
value is convention dependent through the normalization of g. Crucially,
V(m)=0 for EVERY m at g=g_c: the critical branch does not fix a mass or supply
positive vacuum energy. Writing32pi²=2g_c would add an unexplained dictionary.

3. Move off the flat point and test an actual amplitude ratio

Set delta=1-g/g_c>0 and add a relevant deformation -t m/(8pi), t>0:
 E(m)=delta m³/(24pi)-t m/(8pi).
Its stable positive stationary point satisfies t=delta m0². The endpoint
m=0 has E=0; the stationary energy is negative. Define the positive
condensation depth Delta epsilon=E(0)-E(m0)=delta m0³/(12pi).
This is an energy difference from an endpoint, NOT a pair of proved physical
vacua and NOT the observed positive dark energy.
At the stationary point,
 E''=delta m0/(4pi)>0, E'''=delta/(4pi),
 Delta epsilon (E''')²/(E'')³=1/3.
The ratio is exactly independent of delta,m0, component multiplicity, and
common energy/field normalizations. Even granting the most favorable mapping
of this depth and composite response onto the physical target, it gives1/3,
not64pi (a factor192pi apart). An arbitrary background would tune the result,
but would forfeit the amplitude-ratio selection mechanism.

This is a constructive calculation of a parameter-independent ratio, not a
universal no-go. The first model does not match. Finite-N corrections, another
operator, a different critical theory or dimension are different hypotheses;
none is a justified numerical correction to the result computed here.
