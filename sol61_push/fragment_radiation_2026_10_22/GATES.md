# Interpretation gates, declared before the repeats are read

The finite-duration question is whether mass partition robustly forgets initial inventory while allowing spatial fragmentation and actual field-energy export. A numerical detector cannot establish this by naming every density peak a core.

Numerical gates:
- Energy plus absorbed energy relative drift below1e-3.
- Frozen-source field loss negligible compared with moving heavy cloud (less than1%).
- Material retained inside boundary; no periodic wrap treated as ejecta.
- Particle/grid/box repeats of radiation within20%, and material CDF within0.05 absolute, before claiming a converged cooling or profile result. Failure does not falsify the action; it blocks that quantitative interpretation.
- Field-inclusive angular-momentum balance is separate from material spin. Cartesian-grid/periodic boundary artifacts remain explicit.

Selection gates:
- A remnant must be persistent and demonstrably bound, not merely connected. FoF links .25,.5,1ell0 provide sensitivity checks, not physical radii.
- A mass selector must show a reduced dependence of bound remnant masses on initial mass across the declared grid; fixed total mass cannot supply this by construction. The new particle model permits redistribution and escape and therefore does not impose one-remnant inventory.
- Similar half-mass radii at one time do not establish an attractor. Inspect last-third time variation and resolution dependence.
- Positive outgoing chi flux and field absorption do not imply positive outward P flux or permanent loss to infinity. Keep signed currents.
- No preserved dwarf, rotation or cluster fit is claimed without converged formed profiles, live baryonic response and a predicted population. An unchanged unpumped linear kernel is not a full growth pass.
