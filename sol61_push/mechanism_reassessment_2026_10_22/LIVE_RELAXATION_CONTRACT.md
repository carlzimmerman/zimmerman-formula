# Live spherical particle response, before computation

Test a local basin near the finite A10,ra1,gamma0.01,mass1 equilibrium, not cosmological formation or a selector. Sample its positive distribution deterministically with a Sobol sequence. Evolve spherical representative orbits under a radial particle-mesh Hamiltonian. Deposit cell masses by linear interpolation on positive radial nodes; q_j=cumulative mass/r_j². Discrete potential U=sum dr r_j² V(q_j)+gamma/2 sum m_j²/(r_j² dr), with V=-q-ln(1-q)-q²/2. Its exact cell-mass derivative is the reverse cumulative R(q)dr plus gamma rho_j; interpolate that chemical potential to derive radial force. Add analytic Plummer baryonic potential. No friction or sink.

First verify the discrete force by finite differences of energy for particle radii away from cell boundaries. Then compare unperturbed equilibrium sampling with a canonical radial dilation r->1.05r, vr->vr/1.05 (tangential velocity->vt/1.05 to preserve angular momentum). Use dt0.01, duration40, N32768, grid1024, radius10 initially. Report energy drift, total mass, maximum q, outer excursions and radial mass/size oscillations. Passing stability or relaxation requires converged controls/refinement; an exploratory run alone is not evidence. Abort at q>=0.98 or escape beyond grid. The finite mesh's core/boundary treatment and finite sampling are approximations, not full3D dynamics.
