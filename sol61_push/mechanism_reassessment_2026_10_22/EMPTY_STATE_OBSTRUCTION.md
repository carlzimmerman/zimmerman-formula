# What can actually originate the carrier population?

This is a classical derivation from the existing charged-carrier potential. It is not a theorem about the separate identified-medium action, nor a nonperturbative quantum vacuum proof.

With y=|Phi|² and M²=4m²+Delta, complete the square in X:

    V = M² |X + g Phi²/M²|² + m² y + lambda_eff y²,
    lambda_eff = lambda_rep + g²(1/Delta - 1/M²)
               = lambda_rep + 4m² g²/(Delta M²) > 0.

For each Phi the unique minimum in X is X=-g Phi²/M². The remaining potential is strictly increasing in y>=0. Consequently the only homogeneous stationary vacuum is Phi=X=0. There is no hidden nonzero local minimum, false-vacuum reservoir, or classical symmetry-breaking transition in this potential with the stated positive parameters. The minimization is an exact algebraic statement about the potential; it is not a dynamical elimination of X and does not omit its kinetic terms when evolving the fields.

This strengthens the previous linear statement that the empty state has positive masses: a nonlinear homogeneous phase does not rescue spontaneous classical seeding either. At nonzero charge or imposed density one may obtain other states, but that reintroduces a population as an input.

For minimally coupled homogeneous classical fields in an expanding FLRW background,

    rho = |dot Phi|² + |dot X|² + V,
    dot rho = -6 H (|dot Phi|² + |dot X|²).

For H>=0 and zero initial fields and derivatives, rho begins at zero, cannot increase, and is nonnegative. Therefore it remains zero. The same conclusion follows from local uniqueness of the field equations, even without the monotonic-energy argument. For a periodic spatial average the additional gradient term contributes -2H times the nonnegative physical gradient-energy density. Expansion does not classically populate exactly empty fields.

Quantum particle production on a time-dependent geometry evades this classical obstruction. The preceding vacuum_seed experiment demonstrates that possibility in a prescribed scattering geometry. However, the flat-space carrier action does not supply its expansion history, initial quantum state, or curvature couplings. With the convention that conformal coupling is xi=1/6, the linear mode equation is

    u_k'' + [k² + m² a² + (6 xi - 1) a''/a] u_k = 0.

Thus two curved-space completions agreeing with the same flat-space action can predict different production. At m=0, xi=1/6 the equation is a constant-frequency oscillator and produces no particles from its conformal vacuum; this is an exact negative control, not an approximation. Massive production generally remains sensitive to the background and xi. The previous calculation fixed xi=0 explicitly; it did not derive that choice.

The practical implication is precise: the existing positive carrier potential cannot select a nonzero cold seed by classical relaxation from empty initial data. To pursue the quantum route honestly, the next physical input must be a specified gravitational completion and cosmological state, followed by a full spectrum, abundance and free-streaming calculation. Merely choosing a small daughter momentum in X decay still leaves the original source preparation unexplained.

Gravitational production itself is already studied, including scalar production and structure constraints: [Garcia, Pierre and Verner, arXiv:2206.08940](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.08940). This source's abstract supports attribution and identifies abundance and structure constraints as necessary tests; none of its numerical bounds are imported into our distinct interacting carrier model. This note's algebra is derived directly above. No novelty claim is made.
