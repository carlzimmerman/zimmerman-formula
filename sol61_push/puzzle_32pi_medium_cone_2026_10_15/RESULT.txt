1. What is proved
For the constant-gradient quadratic system of the parent calculation, an
all-direction bound on characteristic normal speeds reduces to one scalar
inequality. This replaces a finite angle scan with an analytic test. Units
set the comparison speed to 1; the underlying elastic toy theory is
nonrelativistic. This is not a statement about a relativistic UV completion,
group velocities of individual branches, or nonlinear/global stability.

Let v,A,b,m,rho > 0 and 0 < cN < 1. Write c for the cosine of the angle
between wavevector and background gradient. The coupled pencil is
 Q(s,c) = K s^2 - 2 C(c) s - G(c),
 K = [[A-b/v, A v], [A v, rho+A v^2]],
 C = diag(b c/m, 0),
 G = diag(b v(1+c^2)/m^2, rho cN^2).
The other two polarizations have speeds +/- cN.

For K positive definite and G positive definite, all pencil roots are real.
Indeed, for any nonzero complex null vector u, multiplying by its conjugate
transpose gives k s^2 - 2 d s - g = 0 with k=u*Ku>0, g=u*Gu>0 and
real d=u*Cu. Its discriminant is strictly positive.

The following is necessary and sufficient for every root to lie in [-1,1]:
 Q(1,c) >= 0 and Q(-1,c) >= 0 (positive semidefinite).
Sufficiency: apply the scalar endpoint inequalities k-2d-g>=0 and
k+2d-g>=0 to the same null vector. For s>1,
 k s^2-2 d s-g = s(k-2d-g)+k s(s-1)+g(s-1)>0,
a contradiction. Replace s by -s and d by -d for s<-1.
Necessity: if Q(1,c) has a negative direction, its least eigenvalue is
negative. For sufficiently large positive s, Q(s,c) is positive definite
because K is. Continuity gives a singular Q at s>1. The negative endpoint
is identical with s -> -s. This matrix-continuity argument is written here,
not formalized in Lean.

2. Eliminate the angle analytically
Q(-1,c)=Q(1,-c). Off-diagonal and lower diagonal entries of Q(1,c) are
independent of c, while
 Q11(1,c)-Q11(1,1) = b(1-c)[2m+v(1+c)]/m^2 >= 0
for -1<=c<=1. Hence the worst endpoint is c=1, s=1.
Set r=rho(1-cN^2)>0. Its lower diagonal is r+A v^2>0.
The Schur complement gives the single exact criterion
 A-b/v-2b/m-2b v/m^2 - A^2 v^2/(r+A v^2) >= 0.       (*)
Moreover (*) itself implies K positive definite, since
 K=Q(1,1)+2C(1)+G(1),
and the last two terms sum to a positive definite diagonal matrix.
Thus no extra kinetic-positivity assumption is needed when applying (*).
Equality permits a characteristic speed exactly 1; strict inequality gives
a strict bound. K remains strictly positive at these cone endpoints.

3. Previous numerical example, now with exact bounds
A=rho=100, b=m=1, cN=1/10. Multiplication of the endpoint determinant
by positive v gives
 p(v)=-200v^4-200v^3-298v^2+9702v-99.
Criterion (*) is exactly p(v)>=0 for v>0.
p''(v)=-2400v^2-1200v-596<0 on v>=0. Since p(0)<0, p(1)>0,
and p(v)->-infinity, there are exactly two positive roots; the admissible
set is the closed interval between them.

Root isolation with exact rational endpoints:
 72533/7105990 < v_low < 2101/205833,
 1797083/558975 < v_high < 5839585/1816378.
Numerically:
 v_low  ~= 0.0102073039791,
 v_high ~= 3.21496131312.
These numbers use the parent's fixed parameter normalization and are not
measured physical accelerations. Positive-energy window alone was about
(0.0100010002,99.9899990). Both ends become stricter under the speed test.
For example v=0.0101 has positive kinetic energy but maximum sampled phase
speed about 2.05113; v=3.3 gives 1.03920; v=10 gives 5.99611.

For the parent's ideal static branch v=Csrc/r, the local admissible radial
band becomes Csrc/v_high <= r <= Csrc/v_low, with width ratio about 315.
This is only a local-background diagnostic along that ideal branch; it
neither constructs nor matches a global spherical solution.

4. Verification and limits
check.py verifies five symbolic identities, isolates roots with exact
rational arithmetic, and cross-checks eigenvalues at nine gradients over
401 angles. The numerical grid is not used as proof of the angular bound.
The run/manifest.json records inputs, hashes, environment and execution.
Cone.lean formalizes the angular numerator inequality, positive and negative
scalar root bounds, and negativity of the displayed second derivative.
It does not formalize the matrix reduction, spectral continuity, root
isolation, variational physics, or PDE well-posedness.

5. Relation to the original 32pi / 3/128 question
This is an admissibility condition, not a coefficient selection rule.
E0 remains an independent additive constant in the action. Changing E0
changes the putative cosmological constant while leaving K,C,G and (*)
unchanged. Consequently the present speed and stability tests cannot select
Lambda/a0^2=32pi, nor the equivalent round-S4 quantity a0^4 Omega=3/128.
The next physical gap is a microscopic matching/protection mechanism for
the response coefficients and vacuum energy, or an independent dynamical
constraint relating them. Saturating (*) by hand does not supply that gap.
