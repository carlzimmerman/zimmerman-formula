Spherical exterior-reservoir bound; no projected-disk shortcut.

A uniform 3D thin shell of angular radius a has projected enclosed fraction
1-sqrt(1-s^2/a^2) at s<a. For unit angular mass coefficient its deflection
is theta/[a^2(1+q)], q=sqrt(1-|theta|^2/a^2).
Verify radial Jacobian eigenvalue 1/[a^2 q(1+q)] and tangential eigenvalue
1/[a^2(1+q)]. On a convex disk |theta|<=m<R, every shell a>=R has derivative
norm <=1/[R^2 q_R(1+q_R)], q_R=sqrt(1-m^2/R^2).
Integrate over nonnegative shell masses to derive a profile-independent
image-pair bound for a spherically symmetric reservoir with support r>=R.
This does not constrain arbitrary non-spherical 3D distributions.
