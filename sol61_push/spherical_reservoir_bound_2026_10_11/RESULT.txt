A bound for spherical 3D outer reservoirs
2026-10-11; frozen criteria 4086dd855; 405/405 checks passed.

IMPORTANT DISTINCTION
The preceding exterior_deflection result requires a projected disk free of
the mass being bounded. A reservoir outside a 3D sphere does not satisfy
that condition: its near/far portions project inside the disk. Do not apply
that earlier disk bound just because a catchment has an inner 3D radius.
The bound below instead assumes a spherically symmetric positive reservoir.
Roundness is required for settled cold mass by the framework; it is NOT
here inferred for its unsettled reservoir. This test is conditional on that
additional support/symmetry proposal, not a new framework assumption.

CONTINUUM DERIVATION
A uniform thin shell of radius a, measured in angular coordinates, has
projected enclosed fraction 1-sqrt(1-s^2/a^2) inside projected radius s<a.
For angular mass coefficient B its deflection vector is
 alpha(theta)= B theta/[a^2(1+q)], q=sqrt(1-|theta|^2/a^2).
Its Jacobian eigenvalues are
 tangential: B/[a^2(1+q)], radial: B/[a^2 q(1+q)].
The latter is largest. On a convex disk |theta|<=m<R, for every a>=R,
it is at most B/[R^2 q_R(1+q_R)], q_R=sqrt(1-m^2/R^2).
This follows since increasing a increases a^2, q and 1+q at fixed m;
increasing image radius increases the eigenvalue at fixed a.
Integrate the Jacobian along the image-pair segment, which lies inside
that disk. Then integrate over arbitrary nonnegative shell masses. Thus

 |alpha_i-alpha_j| <= B_total |theta_i-theta_j| L,
 L=1/[R^2 q_R(1+q_R)], m=max(|theta_i|,|theta_j|)<R.

This proves the continuum bound for every positive spherical shell mixture,
not only the numerical examples. No radial profile or shell discretization
is needed in the proof. At the centre L approaches 1/(2R^2); near the shell
boundary it diverges, so extrapolating the central approximation is unsafe.

For several declared centres and exclusion radii, use m_k and L_k separately:
 |delta_ij| <= w_s |theta_i-theta_j| sum_k B_k L_k.
Here delta subtracts all fixed baryonic and settled components. The B_k are
nonnegative and must obey the shared available-mass budget. These linear
necessary inequalities can reject some allocations before a radial fit.
They do not establish feasibility, dynamics, ownership or a common rate.
Arbitrary non-spherical exterior 3D mass is outside this proof's domain.

GAS-ONLY ILLUSTRATION (NOT A RESERVOIR MEASUREMENT)
All non-gas mass is hypothetically spherical about the catalogue tangent
origin and exterior to the selected radius; real interior stars/settled
mass invalidate this premise. Applying the formula to the gas-only
remainder gives minimum total non-gas masses:
 R=674.555 kpc: 6.24547e15 solar masses;
 R=919.847 kpc: 1.29326e16;
 R=1226.463 kpc: 2.41630e16;

The limiting pair is N11.1/N11.2 in all four illustrative cases.
These are NOT Bullet mass estimates, cold-reservoir requirements, measured
support radii or exclusions. They demonstrate evaluation of a conditional
bound. Their difference from the projected-disk examples compares different
support/symmetry classes and does not rank either as a universally tighter
bound. No observational covariance or complete image likelihood is included.
