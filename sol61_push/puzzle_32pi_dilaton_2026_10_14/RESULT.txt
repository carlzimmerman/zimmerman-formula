This continuation tests radiative/approximate-scale breaking and then expands
the test to arbitrary finite positive mixtures of a specified potential family.
It is a new scoped calculation, not a proof that all collective vacua fail.

1. Fixed physical target and dictionary

Use the two independent fingerprints of the corrected local potential:
 R=epsilon_Lambda W3²/W2³=64pi,
 S=W4 W2/W3²=3/2.
They are invariant under constant linear field rescaling and overall common
energy rescaling. They are NOT invariant under arbitrary nonlinear field
redefinition; a physical source/operator dictionary must be specified. Here
we provisionally identify polarization linearly with chi-f and evaluate the
potential derivatives at chi=f. This identification is a test hypothesis.

2. Single-deformation model, including the logarithmic radiative limit

With A,f>0, chi>0, Delta=4+e>0, take
 V_e(chi)=A f^4/[e(e+4)] [(chi/f)^(e+4)-(e+4)(chi/f)^4/4].
The e=0 value is defined by continuity. This is the leading single-deformation
dilaton potential; A=m_d²/f². Its derivative at f vanishes and
 V(f)=-A f^4/[4(e+4)], V2=A f²,
 V3=A f(e+5), V4=A(e²+6e+11).
V(0)=0 for Delta>0. The positive condensation depth is -V(f). With that
reference the vacuum energy is NEGATIVE, so identifying the positive depth
with observed dark energy is only a generous matching test, not a gravity
prediction. A separate positive uplift would introduce another vacuum input.

The depth-response and shape invariants are
 R_depth=(e+5)²/[4(e+4)]=(Delta+1)²/(4Delta),
 S=(e²+6e+11)/(e+5)²=(Delta²-2Delta+3)/(Delta+1)².
The radiative/logarithmic limit is
 V_0=A chi^4/4 [log(chi/f)-1/4],
 R_depth=25/16, S=11/25.
This reproduces the Coleman-Weinberg-type shape, but neither target ratio.

Solving R_depth=64pi gives Delta approximately .0012464997 or802.2464728.
If Delta is the dimension of a nonidentity scalar primary in a unitary4D CFT,
the small root violates Delta>=1. The large root is not a nearly marginal or
relevant deformation. Neither reproduces S=3/2.
Solving S=3/2 instead gives Delta=-5+2sqrt7~.2915 or -5-2sqrt7<0.
These are likewise outside Delta>=1. More directly, for every Delta>=1,
 1-S=(4Delta-2)/(Delta+1)²>0,
whereas the target S=1.5. This is a leading single-deformation obstruction;
it does not constrain arbitrary EFT fit exponents without a CFT dictionary.

3. Stronger test: any positive common-minimum mixture

Now allow ANY finite sum of those potentials with a common f, positive
amplitudes A_i, and scalar dimensions1<=Delta_i<=4 (relevant or marginal
nonidentity scalar deformations). Set A=sum A_i and w_i=A_i/A. Define
 mu=sum w_i Delta_i, q=sum w_i Delta_i², h=sum w_i/Delta_i.
Direct differentiation of the sum gives
 depth=A f^4 h/4, W2=A f², W3=A f(mu+1), W4=A(q-2mu+3).
Therefore
 R_depth=h(mu+1)²/4, S=(q-2mu+3)/(mu+1)².
This is not the average of the individual invariants; cross terms are retained.

For each1<=D<=4,
 5D-4-D²=(D-1)(4-D)>=0,
 (5-D)/4-1/D=(D-1)(4-D)/(4D)>=0.
Averaging gives q<=5mu-4 and h<=(5-mu)/4, with1<=mu<=4. Consequently
 S <= (3mu-1)/(mu+1)² <= 9/16,
 R_depth <= (5-mu)(mu+1)²/16 <= 2.
The final inequalities follow from exact factorizations
 9(mu+1)²-16(3mu-1)=(3mu-5)²,
 32-(5-mu)(mu+1)²=(mu-3)²(mu+3).
Both bounds are sharp: mixtures supported at Delta1 and4 attain S=9/16
with weight2/9 at4, and R=2 with weight2/3 at4 (different mixtures).
Thus increasing the number of such independently stable deformations CANNOT
reach either required fingerprint. This is an analytic finite-mixture theorem,
not a parameter scan. Arbitrary signs, different minima, irrelevant operators,
extra fields, nonlinear source dictionaries or large EFT corrections leave
the tested class and require fresh analysis. No universal QFT no-go is claimed.

4. What changes in the research direction

This rules out trying more positive superpositions with the same minimum as
a way to obtain the coefficient. A candidate in this general direction must
change at least one explicit assumption above. Coupled fields with source-
dependent equilibrium, or cancellations between operators with different
minima, are concrete possibilities; neither has been derived or validated.
They also need an independent calculation of the absolute vacuum stress.
A new tunable coupling chosen to make R=64pi would not solve selection.

The most informative next object would be a symmetry-constrained coupled
microscopic action, with the physical polarization operator fixed before
computing its effective potential. Compute the relaxed response (integrate
out other fields at their shifted equilibrium), not a fixed-field slice.
Compare both R and S, and separately check the sign/reference of vacuum
energy. No such action is supplied by this calculation, so this is an open
route rather than a completed theory or an assertion that it must succeed.
