2. Static reciprocal force balance
In a static comoving frame of lapse N, normal actual density R=n+div P. The static matter integrand is -N[n+div P+E(n)+W_full]. At fixed P, a conserved reference displacement gives delta n=-div(n eta). Integrating by parts gives the reference equation
  n grad[N(1+E'(n))]=0.
For n>0 this replaces the failed dust equation grad N=0. With E''>0 and 1+E'>0,
  E'' grad n=-(1+E') grad ln N.
The pressure p=n E'-E satisfies grad p=-n(1+E') grad ln N. The carrier polarization variation still gives grad N=N dW_full/dP in this static fixed-clock comparison, because E has no explicit P dependence. Thus an internal reference stress can balance the anchor without freezing it externally. Boundary matching and coupled metric/clock equations have not been solved.

3. Positive local inertia and the changed carrier coefficient
At fixed Eulerian densities and small velocities, covariant -E(sqrt(n_normal^2-jbar^2)) contributes n E' vbar^2/2. The physical-current rest action supplies (n vbar-Pdot)^2/(2R). Define h=n E'. Then
  Lkin=(n vbar-Pdot)^2/(2R)+h vbar^2/2,
  Hessian=[[n^2/R+h,-n/R],[-n/R,1/R]], det=h/R.
R>0 and E'>0 make this block positive. With zero conserved reference momentum in the homogeneous comparison, eliminating vbar gives
  Lkin,red=I_eff Pdot^2/2, I_eff=h/(n^2+R h).
This reduction is a selected momentum sector, not algebraic elimination of a propagating anchor at every frequency. It exposes the inertia change. For R=n, I_eff/(1/R)=E'/(1+E'); a small reference enthalpy gives a small reduced coefficient. At E'=0 the free reference can cancel the physical current and the reduced coefficient vanishes. Freezing vbar instead would falsely retain 1/R.
In homogeneous displacement variables P=-n xi, the block is n[(etadot+xidot)^2+E' etadot^2]/2. An additional positive reference compressibility and positive polarization curvature make the corresponding nongravitating frozen quadratic potential positive at k>0. This is only a local comparison: gravity, the critical cancellation, clock constraints and inhomogeneous backgrounds are not included.

With n=n0 at N=1, C=1+epsilon+b n0 and

Both increments are positive for 0<N<1. Subtracting the homogeneous boundary source leaves the susceptibility C^2/b. If this branch is continued to infinity with nonzero homogeneous background and an imposed Newtonian potential tail 1-N~M/r, the reference excess has a 1/r tail and divergent enclosed excess ~r^2. 6. Approximate invisibility survives locally, but supply matters

  I_eff=b(epsilon+nu)/[R b(epsilon+nu)+nu] ->1/R.
So small source does NOT by itself force the actual carrier inertia to vanish. This is a countercontrol against an overly broad obstruction. The limit is singular in the reference sector; uniform coercivity has not been established.
However the exact conserved-current identity on a fixed isolated catchment with zero integrated P flux is

If nu<=nu_max and catchment volume V is fixed, Q<=V nu_max/b ->0. Keeping Q>0 requires b<=V nu_max/Q in this bounded-rescaling family. Enlarging the catchment, allowing boundary flux, or changing the density scaling avoids this particular argument and remains open. No physical b, catchment size or numerical data gate was derived.

6b. Compact free-surface continuation, RA-003

If the lapse increases outward under inward gravity, density decreases outward. Let Q_A be the total integrated reference density on its connected spherical support. Comparing inner and outer average densities proves

The integrated reference lapse source is at least (1+epsilon)Q_A. Thus a stipulated enclosed reference-density budget Q_A(<r)<=delta M_b requires

Disconnected supports, anisotropic stress or a different EOS are not covered. No physical R_A or delta was selected.
