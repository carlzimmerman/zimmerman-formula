2. Why the clock contribution cannot be omitted
Let the clock normal have small velocity v=-grad pi. In a local orthonormal frame,
  n_clock^0=1+v^2/2,
  P^0=v dotP0+..., B^{0i}=P_i+v^2 P_i/2-v_i(v dotP)+...
The norm changes by P^2->P_spatial^2-(v dotP0)^2. The quadratic W correction is +κperp(v dotP0)^2/2 in L. One must also retain the lapse-gradient term g dotB^{0i} coming from the physical rest-current action. On the algebraic background these combine to
  L_clock,potential=κperp[P0^2 v^2-(P0 dotv)^2]/2.
Keeping only W would give a wrong angle dependence. RA-011 checks both contributions from the same source/action convention.
The principal spatial current perturbation is
  deltaK=-pdot+P0 Delta pi-(P0 dotgrad)grad pi.
For Fourier k this clock term is -k^2 P0_transverse pi and is transverse to k. Choose p along that transverse projection, with k not parallelP0; let eta be the transverse reference displacement. The restricted Lagrangian block is
  [(n etadot-pdot)-k^2 Pperp pi]^2/(2R)+h etadot^2/2
  +κperp k^2 Pperp^2 pi^2/2-(κp+gamma k^2)p^2/2.
There is no independent pidot in this block. Eliminating this auxiliary pi and then selecting zero conserved transverse-reference momentum gives
  I_eff=1/[n^2/h+R+k^2/κperp],
  omega^2=(κp+gamma k^2)[n^2/h+R+k^2/κperp].
The kinetic block and constrained finite Hamiltonian are positive. For gamma>0, omega^2/k^4->gamma/κperp. Holding the clock fixed misses the k-dependent inertia. At exactly parallel k,P0 the pi-dependent block vanishes rather than giving this formula; there is no uniform angle-limit certificate. The reference momentum reduction is a specified local sector, not elimination of all propagating fluid modes on an inhomogeneous background.

4. Include one principal ADM metric constraint
For the same reference-rest-frame alternative, take P0,k alongx and eta_y,p_y odd under y and even underz. Fix the spatial vector gauge h_xy=0. Scalar/lapse perturbations have the opposite y parity and h_yz the opposite z parity; the principal sector retains transverse shift N_y. Coordinate reference velocity is etadot, Eulerian velocity u=etadot+N. The physical normal current is

The leading Einstein-Hilbert ADM vector term is Mpl^2 k^2|N|^2/4, from K_xy=K_yx=-N_yprime/2. Define A=Mpl^2 k^2/2. The quadratic velocity/constraint block is
  |C u-pdot+D N|^2/(2R)+Href|u|^2/2+A|N|^2/2.
Eliminating the nondynamical shift and then the zero reference-momentum sector gives

The shift adds a finite leading inverse-inertia correction; it does not remove k^2P0^2/Href. Therefore the formal quartic branch survives this principal odd-sector metric constraint. 5. Positive energy and wellposed linear evolution: a separate conclusion
For either constant-coefficient reduced comparison in the appropriate single wave direction, the branch can be written with positive C0,C1,K0,gamma as
  H=integral[C0 Pi^2+C1(Pi_x)^2+K0 p^2+gamma(p_x)^2]dx/2,
  C(k)=C0+C1 k^2, K(k)=K0+gamma k^2,
  pdot=C(k)Pi, Pidot=-K(k)p, omega^2=C(k)K(k).
At each Fourier k, weighted variables (sqrtK p,sqrtC Pi) rotate at real frequency sqrt(CK). The rotation preserves the energy exactly. This explicit multiplication operator is norm-preserving on the weighted L2 finite-energy space. It is strongly continuous: for any fixed finite k the rotation tends to the identity as t->0, while its change is bounded by twice the initial norm; dominated convergence applies. Its inverse is rotation by -t, giving existence, uniqueness and continuous dependence in this reduced energy space. No external PDE theorem is imported.
For positive C1,gamma that energy space is equivalent to H1 x H1 in the single spatial coordinate. The generator is unbounded, which is compatible with a strongly continuous evolution. This proof is constant-coefficient and does not establish nonlinear variable-coefficient evolution, current interfaces, uniformly controlled angle changes or all GR constraints. Large-k phase/group speeds are unbounded in the formal quartic model, so a metric-cone certificate does not follow. A preferred-clock causal prescription and a microscopic cutoff would be additional inputs, not automatically a contradiction or an adopted repair.
