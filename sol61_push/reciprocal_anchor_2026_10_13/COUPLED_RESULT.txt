1. Coupled radial energy: account for reference and actual mass together
Use G=a0=Mb_total=1, Mb(r)=min(r^3,1) for a supplied compact baryonic sphere, reference density n=q'/r^2 (radial 4pi absorbed), physical enclosed carrier m, and u=(m-q)/r^2. Both m,q are nondecreasing and approach the supplied S=5.364; 0<=q<=m<=S and 0<=u<1. Then zero net polarization flux at infinity follows from m-q->0. The conditional NR energy is
  H=integral[-(Mb*m+m^2/2)/r^2+r^2*w_full(u)+r^2*e(n)]dr,
  w_full=-u-log(1-u).
The baryon-only constant field term is omitted. This is the expanded FULL convention; using w_rem here would count the cancellation incorrectly. Reference internal energy's explicit gravitational source is higher NR order and omitted in this comparison. Actual cold mass gravitation is already m; do not add q as a separate rest-mass source to it.

At fixed q, carrier variation gives w_full'(u)-(Mb+m)/r^2. Its second derivative is [(1-u)^(-2)-1]/r^2>0. The pointwise minimum under the supply cap is
  mstar=min(q+root(Mb+q,r),S),
  root(Q,r)=[sqrt(Q^2+4Q*r^2)-Q]/2.
It is monotone whenever q and Mb are monotone. Within positive actual-carrier support, g=(Mb+m)/r^2=w_full'(u); outside, m=S and the capped variational derivative is nonpositive. Arbitrary independent-P vacuum variation is not the carrier variation.

Reference variation of the same energy, after eliminating m, gives
  [e'(n)]'=-w_full'(u).
This equals ordinary hydrostatic gravity only below the carrier cap. Beyond the finite actual-carrier edge the density obstacle supplies a reaction and the reference sees w_full'(u)<g. This distinction is decisive for finite support.

2. Quadratic reference: actual carrier edge plus exponential charge tail
For e=B n^2/2, q'=r^2 n and B n'=-w_full'(u). In carrier vacuum let d=S-q; then

The linear decaying asymptotic is d=A(r+sqrtB)exp(-r/sqrtB), n=A exp(-r/sqrtB)/(sqrtB*r). The reference has finite total charge while extending beyond the compact actual carrier.

Proof: apply L_k=d''-2d'/r-k^2d and the maximum principle with equal edge deficit and zero at infinity. Derivatives at the equal edge bound n_edge between d_edge*k0^2/[r_edge(k0*r_edge+1)] and the corresponding k1 expression. This comparison assumes the decaying branch; it is not global existence proof.

5. Local matter Hamiltonian and metric-cone cost
On flat homogeneous populated rho>0,P0=0, mu0=1+beta*rho, holding the clock fixed and integrating a boundary term,
  L2=phidot^2/(2beta)+beta*Pdot^2/(2mu0)-gradphi dot Pdot/mu0

Its Hamiltonian is beta*p_phi^2/2+mu0*(p_P+gradphi/mu0)^2/(2beta)+rho|gradphi|^2/(2mu0)+curvature*P^2/2, positive for beta,rho,curvature>0. Bare longitudinal frequencies obey
  omega^4-[k^2+mu0*curvature/beta]omega^2+rho*curvature*k^2=0.
Both roots are positive; bare high-frequency speeds are c_metric and0. This is a matter block, not a full Cauchy certificate.
Restoring positive isotropic P stiffness gamma changes the principal speed polynomial to
  z^2-[1+mu0*gamma/beta]z+rho*gamma.
At z=1 it equals -gamma/beta<0; a principal root lies above the metric cone for every gamma>0. Vacuum longitudinal speed squared is 1+gamma/beta and has a gap sqrt(curvature/beta); transverse speed squared is gamma/beta. Therefore the quadratic EOS does not satisfy a metric-causal criterion with the existing stiffness. Preferred-frame theories can choose another causal standard, but none is adopted here. Exact clock/metric constraints and background validity may change the block and are not resolved by this comparison.
