MATTER RESPONSE: THE FORCE LAW FIXES ITS CHARACTERISTIC CONE
2026-10-14. Parent: puzzle_32pi_wz_kinetic_2026_10_14/RESULT.txt.

Result: a concrete matter-response construction makes a0 an emergent quantity,
but it does not fix Lambda/a0^2. It also gives a sharp force/propagation identity
that any repair within this action class must satisfy. These are conditional
results for a familiar scalar-gravity class, not claims of new physics.

1. Declare the response sector before assigning a0

Take signature (-+++), reduced Planck mass M, and
 S = integral sqrt(-g)[M^2 R/2 - mu^4 F(X)]
       + S_m[exp(2 alpha chi/M) g_mn, matter],
 X=(partial chi)^2/mu^4, alpha>0, mu>0.
The former anomaly scalar phi may set mu through unspecified microscopic
dynamics, but no such matching is claimed here. chi is the response field;
it is not silently identified with phi. A potential is neglected over the
source scale. Work in a static spherical weak-field region, with negligible
scalar backreaction on the Einstein-frame Newtonian metric and small conformal
perturbation. The calculation concerns the spacelike branch X>0.

The scalar equation is 2 div(F_X grad chi)=alpha rho/M. Outside a source of
mass m_s, spherical integration gives F_X chi'=alpha M g_N, where
g_N=G m_s/r^2, G=1/(8 pi M^2). The scalar acceleration magnitude is
u=alpha chi'/M. Consequently
  g_N=F_X u/alpha^2,  X=M^2 u^2/(alpha^2 mu^4).
This derives the source-to-acceleration dictionary from the matter coupling.

2. Exact reconstruction of the requested interpolation

The requested total force law is g_N=g^2/(a0+g), with g=g_N+u. Eliminating g
gives g_N=u^2/(a0-u), 0<u<a0. Therefore this response sector must have
  F_X=alpha^2 u/(a0-u).
Put y=sqrt(X), q=a0 M/(alpha mu^2), so u/a0=y/q. An antiderivative is
  F(X)=F(0)+alpha^2[-y^2-2qy-2q^2 log(1-y/q)].
It is defined on 0<=y<q. Near zero,
  F(X)-F(0)=[2 alpha^2/(3q)]X^(3/2)+O(X^2), F_X(0)=0.
Thus the exact low-acceleration law demands a degenerate quadratic kinetic
term at the zero-gradient vacuum in this model. Nonanalytic collective
physics or a different background is needed to interpret that limit; the
ordinary canonical small-fluctuation vacuum is not supplied by this action.

3. The main structural discriminator

The scalar principal tensor, up to an irrelevant common factor, is
  K^{mn}=F_X g^{mn}+2F_XX partial^m chi partial^n chi/mu^4.
On a static radial background the time coefficient is -F_X, the radial
coefficient F_X+2X F_XX, and transverse coefficients F_X. Hence
  c_parallel^2=1+2X F_XX/F_X
              =d log(g_N)/d log(u).
Speeds here are measured relative to the Einstein metric null cone. The
conformal matter metric has the same null cone. The exact target implies
  c_parallel^2=(2a0-u)/(a0-u)>2,
with limit 2 as u tends to zero from above, and divergence as u approaches a0.
The transverse speed squared is one. For 0<u<a0 both kinetic and spatial
coefficients are positive, so this superluminal cone is not by itself a ghost
or proof of a closed causal curve. The result is nevertheless a constraint on
causality and on any Lorentz-invariant UV completion; it cannot be removed by
rescaling alpha, mu or chi while keeping this exact force law and action class.
Global causality and the validity of the high-gradient limit are not settled.

4. A healthy vacuum regulator changes the asymptotic force law

For an illustrative regulated deep-response kernel F_X=k0+kappa sqrt(X),
k0>0, the static law becomes
  g_N=(k0/alpha^2)u+u^2/a0,
  a0=alpha^3 mu^2/(kappa M).
As u approaches zero the linear term dominates: u/g_N tends to alpha^2/k0.
Thus the total force returns to a constant multiple of g_N rather than the
square-root law. The two terms are equal at u=a0 k0/alpha^2 and
g_N=2a0(k0/alpha^2)^2. A small k0 permits an intermediate MOND-like window;
it does not retain exact deep-MOND asymptotics down to zero acceleration.

5. The absolute coefficient is still independent

If the same sector has F(0)=lambda>0 and no other vacuum stress, then
Lambda=lambda mu^4/M^2. The emergent scale above yields
  Lambda/a0^2=lambda kappa^2/alpha^6.
The dynamical mass scale mu cancels. The target 32 pi therefore requires
lambda kappa^2=32 pi alpha^6. This is an additional matching condition, not
an output of the response construction. A constant shift of F changes lambda
without changing F_X or the force law. Even if phi fixes mu, that alone cannot
select the dimensionless ratio. The combination is invariant under a constant
field redefinition chi_new=s chi, alpha_new=alpha/s, kappa_new=kappa/s^3.
It is a physical matching freedom rather than a mere coordinate convention.

6. Lensing check

At leading weak-field order the matter potentials are
Psi_m=Psi_E+alpha chi/M and Phi_m=Phi_E-alpha chi/M. Their sum, which controls
lensing, is unchanged by the direct conformal scalar contribution. Scalar
stress can still affect the Einstein metric; that effect is outside the
negligible-backreaction approximation. This simple response construction
therefore does not automatically produce the lensing associated with its
extra orbital acceleration.
