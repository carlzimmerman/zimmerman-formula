## Continuum identities

Translation of the square gives the bound at every centre. The double integral carries G/2, not G, before minimizing over A. This gives E>=-G²M³/(8 eta²). It is non-sharp and star-free. A numerical Hamiltonian input to it is not a continuum-convergence certificate. No no-go for arbitrary assembly/negative-energy ejecta is claimed.

For a self-adjoint H[Gamma] along a solution, the nonlinear commutator remains an isospectral evolution: cyclicity makes d Tr Gamma^n/dt zero. Pure-state rank cannot grow. A deterministic norm sink also leaves a single vector outer product rank one, although traces change. This rules out literal manufacture of the high-rank statistical endpoint from this pure-state equation, not every acceptable coherent density. The ensemble covariance commutator and gravitational correlation energy cannot be silently discarded. The isolated Poisson integration-by-parts boundary term vanishes even when finite-time samples have different monopole masses.
