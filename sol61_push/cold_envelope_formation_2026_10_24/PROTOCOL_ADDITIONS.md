# Recorded exploratory additions, not changed acceptance criteria

The baseline contract and all failed histories remain intact. The following were introduced sequentially after inspecting evidence; they are posthoc explorations, not preregistered confirmations:

- Near-marginal total binding E=-0.001 |W_self| after finding the old history's energy deficit.
- A local escape-speed WKB pulse after the near-marginal cases still overconcentrated. `--vinfinity` in that producer is a LOCAL test-potential reference. Its actual collective initial energy is measured separately; it is not a common far-field 10km/s budget.
- Per-radius Chebyshev shifts, Parseval energy evaluation and pi-periodic Fourier transforms preserve the same finite even-m Hamiltonian. Independent dense/Green/gradient/torque checks, a direct full/half Fourier comparison and a ten-step identical-state restart comparison are preserved.
- Sextans bound-history continuation uses dt=.04 after t=60, with a parallel dt=.02 continuation through t=80. Segment metadata/source pins identify this change. No claim of uniform dt is made.
- The mmax=6 test changes the angular cutoff, not the source. Its large occupation sensitivity is retained as an unresolved numerical limitation.
- A shell CDF is interpreted at finite-volume OUTER CELL FACES, with zero mass at the origin, for aperture/stellar-force interpolation. The producer's raw `M_inner` (nodes r<1) is kept unchanged. Derived aperture values are not interchangeable with that raw node sum or earlier centre-CDF interpolation. Independent thin-shell virial diagnostics in diagnostic_history.json use their explicitly stated quadrature.
- Pure-state rank and coarse-graining energy identities expose a bridge obligation to the high-rank statistical endpoint. Late time averaging is explored separately. It does NOT turn failed original instantaneous profile/stationarity gates into passes, and does not establish protection of stars.
- `stellar_robustness.json` separately tests the two pre-existing stellar anisotropies beta=0,-0.5 and both a0 footings, with corresponding references. It is a posthoc diagnostic; no cold profile is fitted or changed and footings are never pooled.

No existing published/frozen criterion, theory action, shared engine, automation or remote repository was modified. The endpoints and source inventories remain conditional inputs. A force law, wave parameter and classical representation are not a proof that nature has no particle interpretation.

A final, explicitly separate **mean-force approximation stress test** was added under the user's request to test stellar disturbance. It does not launch or score a live DF as a purported acceptable remnant. Passive circular probes are initialized in the future-window mean force and evolved in the recorded varying MONOPOLE with the baryonic potential held fixed. This counterfactual experiment can expose orbital excitation missed by averaging; it cannot certify real-star heating, survival, a birth history, or a passed original remnant gate. Both birth times and dt refinement are retained. `stellar_probes_v1.py/json` preserve the first producer before adding orientation-averaged velocity diagnostics; no force or trajectory was changed by that addition.

The final **centering audit** corrects a physical interpretation limitation, not a frozen criterion. The old cloud origin is not automatically the galaxy/fragment centre. Draco develops off-axis symmetry-related wells; origin-centred aperture angular momentum contains orbital motion, and origin-fixed circular probes cannot establish heating of stars following a fragment. The original outputs/failures are preserved, and instantaneous well-centred profiles are re-scored as a separate posthoc diagnostic. The free translating-Gaussian benchmark checks this coordinate representation against an analytic solution. New nonlinear fragmentation/recoil and co-moving stellar tests remain required.
