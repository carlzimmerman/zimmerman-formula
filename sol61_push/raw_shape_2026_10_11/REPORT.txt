RESULT
S = A(K-in) - A(K-out) = 0.10999268 dex.
Joint 50-patch jackknife sigma = 0.16638770 dex; S/sigma = 0.6611.
Ignoring band covariance would give 0.17767112 dex, not the adopted error.
200 paired mass-stratified relabellings give sigma = 0.18376401 dex,
S/sigma = 0.5986, and plus-one two-sided tail fraction 111/201 = 0.55224.
Their mean is -0.03921253 dex: the nonlinear ratio statistic's null is
not exactly centered at zero. The tail above is the frozen absolute-S
calculation, not a centered or asymptotic significance claim.

Verdict: shape unresolved by this diagnostic. This does not disprove the
existing amplitude association. It does not exclude every possible shape
statistic. It does not identify cold energy, age, a stellar-mass bias or
any settling law. The relabelling null is no association, NOT a fitted
nonzero-amplitude-only null; its p-value must not be sold as a formal test
of shape at arbitrary fixed amplitude. The paired jackknife remains the
separate uncertainty on the measured shape contrast.

These stacks use acceleration-indexed bins with lens-dependent physical
radii. A 'radial shape' interpretation requires forward modelling of that
binning and selection. Present-baryon equality is not established simply
by matching catalogue stellar masses and their medians.
