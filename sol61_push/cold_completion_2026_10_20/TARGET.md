# Cold-energy completion contract — 20 October 2026

User request: advance the theory with all its equations, mechanisms and actions.
Base observed HEAD: dce2a2e3d. Shared navigation remains CE-FM-093; existing
publications and frozen criteria are read-only. This is a new exploratory packet.

Completion means one specified action and state that yields production,
abundance, force normalization, transport, stable dwarf profiles, lensing and
cosmological observables, with energy/charge/momentum/angular-momentum accounting
and independent empirical tests. A consistent candidate action, a stationary
solution or a capacity bound is partial progress.

Three routes are pursued in parallel:

1. Reconcile the exact carrier and fragment actions, including their incompatible
   rest-energy bounds. Test an explicitly changed common matter action, rather
   than transfer an ARGON result to the protected carrier by identification.
2. Derive the gravity/polarization source dictionary and identify double counting
   and the missing normalization/metric closure.
3. Derive the real asymptotic energy/charge/spin channels of the fragment action.
   A positive stationary binding gap is insufficient if the gap cannot escape.

The root computation tests a proposed, classical, minimally gravitationally
coupled matter extension. It includes a charge-2 source X, charge-1 daughter
Psi and neutral real mediator chi. Its dimensionless coefficients are exploratory
and chosen to retain an ARGON-like branch; they are not fitted or independently
predicted. No old pressure, cross-section or galaxy-law claim transfers.

Success of this computation: an exact polynomial positivity certificate gives
one uniform classical energy/charge floor for the extension, and the full
three-field stationary equations have numerically resolved candidate bound
profiles at Q=80000 and 160000, with an explicit convergence comparison and
orthogonal charge/virial/chemical-potential checks. Failure is retained as failure
of this chosen extension or implementation, not a no-go for cold matter.

Caps: single thread, 120 s wall time and 90 s CPU per run, logs below 1 MiB,
measured RSS below 1 GB. No large grid, Claude experiment, publication or frozen
test is changed. A future matched collision must measure escaped flux; it is
not replaced by the stationary test. CFG932's settling-energy test is Claude's
live scope and is not duplicated.
