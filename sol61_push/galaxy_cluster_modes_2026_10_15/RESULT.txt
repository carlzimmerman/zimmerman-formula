2. NEUTRALITY FORCES A SOURCE DICTIONARY
For an ordinary massless U(1) gauge field in an exactly homogeneous,
isotropic classical background, no nonzero spatial electric vector is
allowed. The Gauss equation then requires zero homogeneous net gauge charge.
This repeats the already recorded GAUGE_PARENT neutrality obligation; it is
not a new theorem excluding all cosmologies, massive vectors or charge domains.

As the minimal LOCAL neutral comparison, take two equal-mass populations,
m>0, charges +/-q, and equal background number density n per sign.
Their rest density and gauge charge density are
 rho=m(n_+ + n_-), rho_Q=q(n_+ - n_-).
Let delta n_M=delta n_+ + delta n_- and
delta n_Q=delta n_+ - delta n_-.
The first mode changes mass, the second changes charge. A pure linear charge
mode has delta rho=0 although it has a nonzero dark electric field. Thus the
single-sign identity div(P)=positive carrier mass cannot be imported into
a neutral two-sign mixture. Nonlinear charge separation can subsequently
redistribute mass, as the spatial example shows.

Neutral baryonic gravity accelerates both signs equally. With equal masses,
equal pressure coefficients and initially zero charge difference/current,
it has no linear source for delta n_Q. Exact symmetric nonlinear classical
initial data remain symmetric by the exchange symmetry. An instability can
amplify an existing fluctuation; it does not supply a seed or create particles.
Mass/temperature asymmetry or other interactions change these hypotheses.

3. ONE EXACT CLOCK RELATION
Use rationalized gauge normalization div E=rho_Q. For the weak-field local
two-sign cold medium the longitudinal plasma scale is
 Omega_p^2=sum(q_a^2 n_a/m_a)=2q^2 n/m=(q/m)^2 rho.
The retained critical assignment q/m=sqrt(4piG) therefore implies
             Omega_p^2=4piG rho=Omega_g^2.
Omega_g is the local Jeans coefficient, not a growing mode at every k and
not an exact finite-halo crossing frequency. This equality holds at Maxwell
weak field and rest-mass inertia. Nonlinear constitutive susceptibility,
relativistic enthalpy, magnetic mixing and extra species modify it.

Illustrative densities .3 and .035 GeV/cm^3 give respectively
 Omega_p^-1=.04731 and .13852 Gyr;
 periods 2pi/Omega_p=.29728 and .87036 Gyr.
The first number is an illustrative galactic density. The second is the
fiducial density in DeRocco/Giffin Eq. (2), not our inferred cluster density.
An inverse rate is not a period or a relaxation time. The result makes
instantaneous response an unjustified assumption and leaves open a long-age
galaxy/short-passage cluster separation without asserting that it succeeds.

For ordinary collisionless plasma Debye length scales as sigma/Omega_p;
here it is comparable to a gravitational support scale rather than a tiny
electromagnetic screening length. A local kinetic approximation therefore
needs a finite-system check at gravitational-strength charge.

4. THE CHANGED HYPOTHESIS: EXPLICIT SEPARATION-FAVORING INTERACTION
The old charge_demixing_2026_10_13 packet derives g_x>g_s from a particular
two-frequency reduction of its scalar carrier. That calculation has no
long-range dark gauge cost. We now specify a DIFFERENT candidate adding
dark gauge charge and self-gravity; its gauge coupling is new input, not
claimed already derived from that carrier action.

Take two nonrelativistic complex wave fields with n_+=|psi_+|^2 and
n_-=|psi_-|^2, canonical Schroedinger symplectic terms and kinetic energy
 hbar^2(|grad psi_+|^2+|grad psi_-|^2)/(2m).
Use contact energy
 U=g_s(n_+^2+n_-^2)/2+g_x n_+ n_-, g_x>g_s>=0.
For nonnegative densities U is bounded below. Its negative relative-density
curvature at fixed total density permits demixing; it is not a kinetic ghost.
Vary gravitational Phi and dark electrostatic phi with
 laplacian Phi=4piG[m(n_++n_-)-rho_background],
 -laplacian phi=q(n_+-n_-).
The wave equations have potentials
 m Phi +/-q phi+g_s n_+/-+g_x n_-/+.
These are reciprocal Newtonian electrostatic interactions, with no frozen
opposite-charge background. Periodic mean-subtracted gravity is a local
comparison, not a cosmological solution. A gauge-invariant spatial parent
replaces derivatives by covariant derivatives; only its electrostatic
longitudinal limit is evolved. No transverse or relativistic health result
is supplied. Rest energies and a constant vacuum term do not affect these
fixed-inventory local equations.

Linearizing the actual two-wave equations about n_+=n_-=n gives
 omega_M^2=hbar^2 k^4/(4m^2)+n(g_s+g_x)k^2/m-4piG rho,
 omega_Q^2=hbar^2 k^4/(4m^2)+n(g_s-g_x)k^2/m+Omega_p^2.
An explicit 2x2 squared-frequency matrix is diagonalized in check_modes.py.
The mass branch includes gravity; the charge branch includes electric
restoring force. Gravity does not directly source charge separation here.

Let d=g_x-g_s>0 and v_d^2=nd/m. Completing the charge-mode square in k^2
gives the minimum at k_*^2=2mnd/hbar^2 and
 min omega_Q^2=Omega_p^2-n^2 d^2/hbar^2.
Charge separation grows only if nd>hbar Omega_p. Both long wavelengths
(electric cost) and short wavelengths (gradient cost) are then stable.
The finite growing band is
 k_+/-^2=(2m^2/hbar^2)[v_d^2 +/-sqrt(v_d^4-hbar^2 Omega_p^2/m^2)].
At critical q/m the density threshold becomes
              rho > rho_crit=16piG m^2 hbar^2/d^2.
Hence gravitational compression CAN in principle take a prepared neutral
mixture across an internal instability threshold, if the stipulated
condensate and interactions apply. This is a state rearrangement of existing
matter. Threshold crossing, seed generation and condensation in a galaxy
have not been evolved or derived.

An illustrative assignment m=1e-22 eV/c^2, v_d=100 km/s and rho=.3 GeV/cm^3
gives lambda_*=852 pc and inverse peak growth 1.876 Myr. The threshold speed
sqrt(hbar Omega_p/m) is 19.90 km/s. These mass/coupling choices are knobs,
not parameter-free predictions, allowed-particle claims or a galaxy fit.
No finite-temperature normal component is included. Higher cluster velocity
dispersion alone does not establish which phase is occupied.

7. IMPLICATION FOR THE DARK-ENERGY/COLD-ENERGY QUESTION
The coherent picture worth testing now has three distinct physical roles:
vacuum stress, mass-carrying excitations, and an internal charge/response
mode of those excitations. They can belong to one interacting dark sector,
but a spatial polarization is not automatically the mass itself.
The new computation demonstrates the third role in a stipulated candidate.
It supplies no selection of the vacuum energy, particle mass, cosmic amount,
a0, or constitutive pole. A constant rho_v can be added without changing
this local mode analysis; it still changes the cosmological background.
Thus galaxies/clusters constrain the occupied state's dynamics strongly,
but these local tests cannot establish the microscopic identity of dark
energy or derive its value. No 542 GHz photon signal follows.
