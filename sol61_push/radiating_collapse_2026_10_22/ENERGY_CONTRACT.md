# Endpoint energy feasibility
Use the canonical lambda=0 cold density shapes as explicitly specified candidate endpoints, but re-minimize their field energy with the collapse model's finite-chi coefficients and grid regulators. This is not a construction of finite-chi equilibria. At each fixed density the field energy is convex (quadratic-over-positive-chi perspective, convex W, positive phase/gradient energies), so its stationary minimum supplies the least field energy for that density in the discrete model. Zero spin minimizes endpoint rotational energy.

For isolated evolution to one such endpoint plus mutually separated nonnegative-energy ejecta and outgoing waves, E_initial >= E_endpoint,min is necessary. If it fails, that endpoint is inaccessible without external work or a further negative-energy bound component. Cooling makes the constraint stronger, not weaker. Bound material outside the selected centre is not ejecta and retains inventory dependence. Do not promote a scan of specified endpoint shapes into a global ground-state lower bound over all densities.

Compute matched cold shapes of masses .5,1,2,4 times canonical, using the inherited ODE and an independent quantile sampling. Target energy at N=256/128 shells and N=512/256 shells; report numerical regulator sensitivity. Seed energies for all initial masses/spins. Domain, force and mass checks retain the main contract. 90 second single-thread cap.
