# Local fragmentation discriminator
Homogeneous unpumped background e=P=0, chi=chi0, X=0; subtract background gravity and linearize in a static patch. Pure cold material, no velocity dispersion, rotation/shear, expansion or baryons. Dimensionless density rho0 and P inertia eps are positive. Compute the longitudinal dispersion polynomial and check its growing branch against the original density/P equations. A monotonically decreasing growth rate with wave number excludes a fastest finite nonzero k in this specific linear regime; it does not exclude nonlinear fragmentation or spectra imposed by initial conditions. The phase perturbation is unsourced at linear order because its source is quadratic. Scan eps=.001,.005,.02 and rho0=.01,.1,1 over k=1e-3..1e3 (300 points). No random input; 30-second cap.
