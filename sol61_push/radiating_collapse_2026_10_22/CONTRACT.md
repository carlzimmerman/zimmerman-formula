# RC-20261022: finite-chi radial collapse and a remnant-energy bound
Base revision 08daade3f873ad4fa6dfbf31b9be950a833d9072. Continue dynamical_arrest_2026_10_22. Own directory only. The target is a remnant mass insensitive to initial inventory and modest spin, with actual energy export and retained phenomenological fits/growth. A stationary family, damping inserted into matter, or a fixed-mass homology is not evidence for that target.

Three discriminators: (1) conservative radial-shell collapse coupled to dynamical polarization and phase fields with an exterior absorber; (2) a remnant binding-energy inequality independent of radiation efficiency; (3) finite-profile observational/growth sensitivity if simulations are numerically usable. No claim of full 3D formation or common cosmology.

The finite model is a variational radial projection of the reduced action, with multiple cold shell currents permitting crossing. Each shell is constrained to spherical shape and given fixed axial angular momentum. Rotational energy is L_i^2/(2I_i), I_i=2m_i r_i^2/3. This is not a solved axisymmetric rotating fluid: angular shape changes, torques between shells and fragmentation are excluded. Zero-spin runs are spherical shell-current dynamics. Initial baryons and X are zero; this isolates formation via the existing phase channel. Dwarf/rotation tests remain phenomenological and cannot demonstrate baryonic heating is absent.

Units: length ell0=.4404221297174536 kpc, acceleration a0=9.3603e-11 m/s^2, time sqrt(ell0/a0), mass a0 ell0^2/G, energy a0^2 ell0^3/G. Radial volume weight is integral r^2 dr. e=rho-div w, h=e/z, z=chi/chi0. Energy includes W(w)=-|w|-ln(1-|w|), e^2/(2z), phase a(z-1)^2/2, b z_dot^2/2, d|grad z|^2/2 and polarization eps w_dot^2/2. a=1/(2lambda), b=a/gap^2, d=b c_phase^2; eps=.005. Positive z is required.

Field grid: staggered finite-volume divergence and adjoint gradient, radial symmetry at zero, fixed vacuum values at outer edge. TSC shell deposition and its exact derivative provide reciprocal material force. Softened shell gravitational potential uses 1/sqrt(max(r_i,r_j)^2+soft^2), soft=dr; rotational inertia also uses r_i^2+soft^2. These are explicit regulators, to be varied with resolution. No physical mass scale may be inferred from them. Matter is never damped or removed. Field momenta are damped only in the exterior absorber; removed kinetic energy is tallied exactly and must balance total energy loss. Radiation/absorber activity does not count as material ejection. Matter reaching the grid edge invalidates the run.

Initial smooth Gaussian radial mass distribution, quantile shells, scale .6*(M/Mref)^(1/3), Mref=.08408132497418408. Fixed-density initial family. Initial fields minimize the discrete static field energy with matter held fixed. Initial radial velocity zero; spin kinetic fraction 0,.02,.05 of initial gravitational binding. Survey M/Mref=.5,1,2,4, lambda=1, gap=.5, phase speed=1. Parameter sensitivity lambda=10 and gap=.2 only after baseline verification. Numerical pilot N=256, shell count=128, Rmax=16, absorber starts r=10, dt=.001, time=30; repeat dt and spatial resolution before interpreting remnant trends. Longer runs only if informative.

Required checks: force versus energy finite differences, static field gradient residual, finite energy/mass balance, fields within domain, absorber bookkeeping, refinement. Inner mass at radii 1.5,2,3 and late temporal variation are diagnostics; no stable remnant is declared solely from a snapshot. Far outward shells are only candidate ejecta until field forces are negligible and energy/trajectory support escape. No random inputs. Bounded runs with observed provenance. An integration/regularization failure remains a numerical failure, not a theory exclusion.
