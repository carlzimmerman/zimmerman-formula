Result: the previous incompatibility is specific to the Lorentz-invariant
P(X) response class. In a fixed preferred frame, a positive higher-spatial-
derivative energy can stabilize nonzero-wavenumber vacuum modes without
changing the leading large-radius MOND force. This is a constructive scalar
effective-theory example, not a full gravity theory or a coefficient selection.

1. Explicit changed model

In a fixed locally flat rest frame, use
 L = K chi_dot^2/2 - 2 kappa |grad chi|^3/(3 mu^2)
     - (Laplacian chi)^2/(2 S^2) - alpha rho chi/M - lambda mu^4,
with K,kappa,mu,S,alpha,M positive. S is a mass scale, not an action.
The source is static, and its Newtonian gravitational field is treated
separately with G=1/(8 pi M^2). The weak-force dictionary is u=alpha chi'/M.
The rest frame is assumed rather than dynamically derived. A covariant
embedding would need a timelike field or a physical medium and its equations.
The preferred-frame kinetic coefficient K is independent of the static
response stiffness. This is the precise assumption changed from P(X).

2. Vacuum and finite-gradient perturbations

The source-free energy, apart from the constant offset, is nonnegative:
 K chi_dot^2/2 + 2 kappa |grad chi|^3/(3 mu^2)
 + (Laplacian chi)^2/(2 S^2).
At zero gradient, the quadratic dispersion is
 omega^2=k^4/(K S^2)>0 for k>0.
The constant k=0 shift mode remains gapless. The equation has two time
derivatives, not four; the additional derivatives are spatial. This verifies
linear scalar stability in the prescribed frame, not gravitational stability
or quantum perturbative control.

About a static locally constant gradient of magnitude v, the frozen-coefficient
dispersion is
 omega^2 = [ (4 kappa v/mu^2) k_parallel^2
              +(2 kappa v/mu^2) k_perp^2 + k^4/S^2 ]/K.
All displayed coefficients are positive for v>0. Unlike the P(X) construction,
K can lower the two-derivative wave speeds without changing the static force.
For a bounded gradient range v<=vmax, choosing K>=16 kappa vmax/mu^2 gives
maximum two-derivative speed squared <=1/4.
The fourth-derivative term still makes group speeds grow with k. In the
zero-gradient vacuum, v_group=2k/(sqrt(K) S); thus there is no claim of
subluminality at arbitrary momentum. A cutoff and UV completion are necessary.
For the frozen-coefficient dispersion one can bound group speed squared by
cmax^2+4k^2/(K S^2). The additional restriction k<=sqrt(K)S/4, with the
previous cmax^2<=1/4, therefore gives group speed squared <=1/2. This is a
local low-energy bound in the fixed frame, not a global causality theorem.

3. Static source equation and asymptotic force

Variation gives the static equation
 div[(2 kappa/mu^2)|grad chi| grad chi]
       - Laplacian^2 chi/S^2 = alpha rho/M.
For a positive radial gradient g=chi', outside a spherical source of mass m_s,
 2 kappa r^2 g^2/mu^2
   - r^2[g''+2g'/r-2g/r^2]/S^2 = Q,
 Q=alpha m_s/(4 pi M).
Define C=sqrt(alpha m_s mu^2/(8 pi kappa M)). A formal large-radius expansion is
 g=C/r + A/r^2 + B/r^3 + ...,
 A=-mu^2/(2 kappa S^2), B=-A^2/(2C).
Substitution cancels the flux residual at orders 1/r and 1/r^2. The retained
truncated series has a flux residual of order 1/r^3, explicitly saved by check.py.
This checks the expansion; it does not prove existence or uniqueness of a
global solution satisfying source-interior and outer boundary conditions.

The leading scalar acceleration is still
 u^2=a0 g_N, a0=alpha^3 mu^2/(kappa M).
The relative first correction is -ell/r, ell=mu^2/(2 kappa S^2 C).
Unlike a positive ordinary |grad chi|^2 term, this regulator is subleading
at large radius and does not force asymptotically linear response in g_N.
Use the expansion for r>>ell inside the region where the medium, weak fields,
and negligible cosmological curvature apply. The logarithmic leading scalar
potential cannot be extrapolated to arbitrarily large physical radius while
retaining those assumptions. Halo/cosmological matching remains necessary.
Only the deep-force law is reconstructed here, not the full interpolation.

4. What is gained and what remains free

This is a counterexample to the inference that stabilizing the vacuum must
always destroy the deep-MOND force. That inference held for the ordinary
two-spatial-derivative regulator tested in the parent; it is not universal.
The separate kinetic and spatial operators also remove the former identity
between static force slope and the wave speed.

If lambda mu^4 is conditionally the total vacuum energy, then
 Lambda/a0^2=lambda kappa^2/alpha^6, as before. Neither K nor S fixes this
ratio, and an arbitrary constant energy offset still leaves the force intact.
No value 32 pi or 3/128 is selected by this construction.

The important new obstacle is technical naturalness: a |grad chi|^2 term is
allowed by the simple shift and rotation symmetries of this action. It was
set to zero, not prohibited by a demonstrated symmetry or renormalization
result. If it is generated with positive coefficient, the parent's very-low-
acceleration crossover returns. The nonanalytic cubic interaction also needs
a microscopic explanation. No quantum strong-coupling scale was computed.
