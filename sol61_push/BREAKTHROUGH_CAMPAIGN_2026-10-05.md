## Integration requirements

Each returned result must identify its exact model, action or distribution,
normalizations, boundary/initial data, allowed parameters and claimed range.
The coordinator reconstructs the critical implication from the raw artifact;
independent calculations or controls must test the claim, not merely reproduce
the implementation. Computation provenance and failed routes remain saved.

Cross-lane transfer requires an explicit dictionary. A switch acting on a
phenomenological force term does not automatically act on the cold field's
stress; a vacuum integral selected in a static reduction does not fix the
cosmological offset; phantom Poisson density does not by itself define a
material density or transport law. A result without these maps stays local.

constructing explicit static actions. Its new conditional orbital identity is

    C = (1/4) integral_0^infinity y nu(y)

It requires an isolated spherical compact source, monotone response, vanishing
integration boundary term, and the specified sector-only additive vacuum

that dictionary. A measured partial integral above 32pi would refute that
restricted target; a smaller integral would not establish it.
A finite-radius body's exterior samples only a finite upper acceleration;
the full integral is an identity of the response function, not a claim that
one body's exterior orbits cover all positive y. Its sampled windows retain
the one-sided bound.
