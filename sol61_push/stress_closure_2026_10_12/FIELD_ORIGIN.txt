Conservative origin of the CFG660 equilibrium stress
2026-10-12. Base 3c1c5a42fd4c56c1b85c0171cb8a24b02b08f3c3.
Exact analytic construction, self-reviewed; no numerical run, no novelty claim.

Let g=|grad Phi|>0, C=4*pi*G and B(g)=F^{-1}(g), the inverse of the
prescribed spherical acceleration law. Hold a0 fixed in space and time.
Assume smooth fields and fixed boundary data (or vanishing boundary variations).
Define the stationary potential functional

 I[Phi]=int [ e(g)+rho_b Phi ] d^3x,
 e(g)=(1/C) int_0^g B(s) ds.

Its Euler equation is
 div[(B(g)/g) grad Phi]=C rho_b.

In spherical symmetry, with the usual central flux condition, integration
gives B(g)=GM_b(<r)/r^2, hence the exact prescribed law. Other boundary
fluxes must be included explicitly. This is a field equation in general
3D; the spherical algebraic acceleration relation does NOT hold generally.

Split e into the Newtonian variational density and a correction:
 e_N=g^2/(2C),
 e_c=e-e_N=-(1/C) int_0^g [s-B(s)] ds.

The correction's canonical spatial stress is
 Pi_ij=(e_c'(g)/g) partial_i Phi partial_j Phi - e_c(g) delta_ij.
Its eigenvalues along/across grad Phi are
 P_parallel=g e_c'-e_c, P_perp=-e_c.
Therefore
 dP_parallel/dg=g[B'(g)-1]/C,
 P_perp-P_parallel=g[g-B(g)]/C.
These are exactly CFG660's equations, up to the common pressure constant.

There is also an exact 3D divergence identity:
 partial_j Pi_ij=partial_i Phi div[(e_c'/g) grad Phi].
Define rho_eff=laplacian(Phi)/C-rho_b. Using the field equation,
 div Pi=-rho_eff grad Phi.
Thus this stress balances the apparent extra gravitational density in
any smooth solution of THIS nonlinear potential theory. This extends the
stress identity beyond the spherical derivation; it does not extend the
algebraic spherical law, positivity of rho_eff, or the no-EFE round rule.

Checks and physical interpretation
----------------------------------
B=g gives e_c=0 and ordinary Poisson. B=g^2/a0 gives
 e_c=-g^2/(2C)+g^3/(3C*a0), and the deep stresses in CFG660.
Where B>0 and B'>0, the Hessian of e as a function of grad Phi has
eigenvalues B/gC and B'/C: the stationary operator is locally elliptic.
At g=0 the deep case is degenerate; no uniform ellipticity is claimed.

I is a stationary field functional, NOT an independently positive energy
reservoir for cold material. The correction e_c can be negative. Adding
the conventional matter kinetic term to the action with field term -e
and coupling -rho_b Phi gives a nonrelativistic conservative effective
gravity model for fixed a0. It does not provide an independent cold-mass
current, material abundance, heat capacity, settling rate or relativistic
lensing law. rho_eff is inferred from Phi, not an independent substance.

Consequently the derivation solves the conservative FIELD representation
of the stress, not the requested independent-material mechanism. Adding
independent cold matter as a source changes the field equation; retaining
the baryon-only law then requires another demonstrated mechanism. Adding
viscosity to the apparent density is not justified by this action.
Making a0 depend on a dynamical DE field requires varying that field and
including the associated forces and energy exchange, not substituting a0(t).

Provenance and boundary of novelty
----------------------------------
The nonlinear Poisson/action construction belongs to the established
Bekenstein-Milgrom AQUAL family, with mu(g/a0)=B(g)/g. This identification
is mathematical, not an instruction to adopt standard MOND phenomenology.
The exact inverse kernel can be the project's own prescribed kernel;
its physical origin and a0=kappa*c*sqrt(G*rho_DE) remain unproved.
