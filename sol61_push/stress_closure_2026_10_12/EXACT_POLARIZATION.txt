2026-10-12. Continuation of FIELD_ORIGIN.txt and the earlier
cold_production_2026_10_11/POSITIVE_POLARIZATION.md (read only).

Question: replace the earlier cubic toy response with the exact prescribed
kernel while retaining a nonnegative source-free energy and local linear
stability at nonzero background field. This is an effective construction,
not a derivation of that kernel from microscopic physics.

Assumptions
-----------
Let g=F(b), b=B(g)=F^{-1}(g), C=4*pi*G. On the smooth branch require
 B(0)=0, 0<B(g)<g, 0<B'(g)<1 for g>0.
The repaired monotone-deficit kernel is intended to have F'>1; the
construction applies on its smooth branches satisfying these inequalities,
not automatically to numerical interpolation knots or arbitrary kernels.
Assume the integrals below converge and the branch includes zero.

Define the magnitude of an internal vector field P through
 p(g)=[g-B(g)]/C.
Since p'=(1-B')/C>0 this relation has a unique inverse g=g(p).
Define U(0)=0 and U'(p)=g(p), equivalently
 U(p(g))=g*p(g)-int_0^g p(s) ds.

Let q=grad Phi. The source-free energy density is
 E = Z/2*(dot Phi)^2 + I/2*|dot P|^2 + V(q,P),
 V(q,P)=|q|^2/(2C)+U(|P|)-P.dot(q), Z,I>0.                 (1)
Z and I are new, UNDETERMINED response coefficients, with the dimensions
needed in (1). Positivity does not determine their values or physical origin.
They are not claimed to be allowed knob-free predictions of the project.

Static response: exact, within the constitutive branch
-----------------------------------------------------
Minimising V over P gives U'(p) P/p=q, so P=p(g) q/g.
Then V_min(g)=g^2/(2C)-int_0^g p(s)ds=int_0^g B(s)ds/C.
With source coupling +rho_b Phi in the stationary functional,
 div(q/C-P)=rho_b, i.e. div[(B(g)/g)grad Phi]=C rho_b.
Thus the prescribed spherical law is recovered exactly, with the central
flux condition of FIELD_ORIGIN.txt. General 3D predictions are this field
equation, not the spherical algebraic rule or the project's round/no-EFE rule.

Energy and linear stability: analytic check
-------------------------------------------
U'(p)>0, U''(p)=C/(1-B')>C. Its transverse vector Hessian eigenvalue is
 U'/p=C*g/(g-B)>C. Hence the block Hessian of V in (q,P) has q block I_3/C
and cross block -I_3; its Schur complement is Hess(U)-C*I_3, positive
on a nonzero background. Equivalently, the reduced q Hessian has radial
B'/C and transverse B/(C*g), both positive.
Also V(q,P)>=V_min(|q|)>=0 wherever the global constitutive minimisation
is defined, because B>=0. With Z,I>0, source-free kinetic energies are positive.

On a uniform nonzero background, substitute delta q=i*k*delta Phi.
The positive quadratic form above plus positive kinetic terms implies
nonnegative squared mode frequencies for the frozen-coefficient linear
system. This covers all wave directions, not just a sampled k grid.
At k=0, the constant Phi mode is unconstrained; no restoring force is claimed.
At vacuum g=0 in the deep branch B~g^2/a0, the Schur complement becomes
degenerate. There is no uniform stability/coercivity estimate there.
No nonlinear/global well-posedness or relativistic causality theorem follows.

Dynamics and physical reading
----------------------------
The nonrelativistic action with L=kinetic-V-rho_b Phi gives
 Z*ddot Phi=div(q/C-P)-rho_b,
 I*ddot P=q-U'(|P|)P/|P|.
The internal vector responds to the force-potential gradient and changes
the flux sourcing that potential. Both fields store energy and exchange it
reversibly. This is a concrete conservative RESPONSE model, not viscosity.
It has no intrinsic relaxation: it can oscillate. Outgoing waves can export
energy only with specified boundary conditions; they are not automatically
a cosmological vacuum receiver. Coupled dynamical baryons must be included
for a closed sourced energy budget. Frozen external sources are not a free bath.

What has advanced, and what has not
----------------------------------
The earlier cubic toy admitted a positive energy but only a particular
approximate force law. Here the exact inverse kernel determines U and an
analytic Hessian criterion proves local source-free mode stability on the
nonzero branch. This answers a limited effective-model existence question.
It does NOT derive the kernel: U was inverse-designed from it.

There is still no independent conserved cold monopole mass, cosmic abundance,
microphysical identity for P, predicted Z/I, autonomous settling, relativistic
lensing prescription, or joint observation fit. Adding an independent mass
source changes the equilibrium and requires a further derivation. Ordinary
positive-mass molecules do not automatically realise this P coupling (see
the earlier POLARIZATION_SELECTOR.md dipole-cancellation argument).
An additive vacuum offset does not affect U', the field response or a0;
therefore this construction cannot fix kappa=1/2 or tie a0 to that offset.
