Target contract
Derive the total gravitating vacuum normalization, without inserting the
desired constant, while reproducing the corrected local response law.
In the existing conventions K=4 pi G, x=u/a and W=(a^2/K)L,
L=C-x-log(1-x). The two derivative fingerprints are R=4C and S=3/2.
The target R=64 pi requires C=16 pi. If this is the total vacuum energy,
Lambda=2 C a^2, and the constructed area pi/a^2 gives area*Lambda=2 pi C.
These gravitational and area identifications are premises retained from the
previous program; the area is not established to be a physical horizon.

1. Executed route: coupled fields can repair the shape
Take k,M>0, b nonzero, real g, and arbitrary real E. Define
 W_E(x)=E+k x^2/2+b x^3/6+b^2 x^4/(16k),
 V_E(x,y)=W_E(x)+(M/2)(y+g x^2/(2M))^2.
At fixed x, the unique minimizing hidden field is y*=-g x^2/(2M).
The relaxed potential is exactly W_E. Its origin derivatives are
 W2=k, W3=b, W4=3 b^2/(2k),
so S=W4 W2/W3^2=3/2 exactly. The fixed-y quartic derivative instead is
3 b^2/(2k)+3g^2/M: relaxation really changes the response shape.
This leaves R=E b^2/k^3 completely arbitrary.

This is a globally stable static example, not just a stationary expansion:
 W_E-E=x^2[(b x+4k/3)^2/(16k)+7k/18]>=0.
Equality requires x=0; the auxiliary square then requires y=0. The vacuum
Hessian is diag(k,M), positive definite. E changes neither fact.
Thus the earlier positive-mixture obstruction does not extend to arbitrary
coupled fields. But overcoming that obstruction does not select the energy.
The selected quartic was engineered to match S; it is not a microscopic
derivation of that value. This polynomial only matches low derivatives.

2. Executed route: exact full-response construction, not a Taylor fit
For x<1 and M>0, define
 U_C(x,y)=C-x-log(1-x)+(M/2)(y+g x^2)^2.
Its unique fixed-x minimizer is y*=-g x^2; eliminating y gives the exact
logarithmic potential L_C(x)=C-x-log(1-x).
 L'_C=x/(1-x), L''_C=1/(1-x)^2,
 L_C(0)=C, L2=1, L3=2, L4=6.
For the physical interval 0<=x<1, the response rises from zero to infinity.
Returning to u=a x gives g_response=a x/(1-x)=a u/(a-u).
Here g_response is the acceleration, distinct from auxiliary coupling g.

The global minimum on x<1 is (0,0), with value C: L'_C is negative for
x<0 and positive for 0<x<1, and the square is nonnegative. On the relaxed
valley, writing h(x)=g x^2, the Hessian is
 [[L''+M h'^2, M h'],[M h',M]],
whose determinant is M/(1-x)^2>0 and trace is positive. This establishes
static local stability on that valley, not a covariant perturbation analysis.

Every member has the SAME complete response and S=3/2, yet R=4C.
C=16 pi and C=8 pi are both positive, statically stable choices. They yield
area*Lambda=32 pi^2 and 16 pi^2 respectively under the retained dictionary.
Consequently these local-response and static-stability premises cannot imply
the desired coefficient. The logarithmic potential was prescribed, so this
construction is a counterfamily to inference, not a new microscopic theory.

3. Executed route: the shift survives elimination
For any potential V(x,y), adding a field-independent c preserves all
fixed-x minimizers: V(x,y*)<=V(x,y) iff V(x,y*)+c<=V(x,y)+c.
Whenever a minimum exists, the minimized potential shifts by c exactly.
There is no assumption about the number of hidden fields in this statement.
For a convergent finite-dimensional integral with fixed beta>0 and measure,
 Z_c(x)=integral exp[-beta(V(x,y)+c)]dy=exp[-beta c] Z_0(x).
Thus -log Z_c/beta=-log Z_0/beta+c, with all x derivatives unchanged.
In the explicit quadratic auxiliary model, its integral supplies only an
x-independent Gaussian factor. This is not a calculation of a dynamical
field theory's path integral; kinetic terms and regulators need their own
analysis. The constant-factor identity requires no perturbative expansion.

A constant in the matter potential is not invisible to gravity. In the
action term -integral sqrt(-g) V, adding c adds -c integral sqrt(-g),
and the stress contribution is -c g_{mu nu}. So the family members are
different gravitational theories, even when their nongravitational local
response agrees. The counterexample does NOT say gravity cannot measure
vacuum energy or that every specified UV theory allows arbitrary shifts.
It says the response data used here do not determine that gravitational term.

Decision and handoff
The coupled-field shape route was executed successfully as an engineered
static construction. The claim that this alone determines 32 pi^2 fails.
Prescribing C=16 pi would reproduce the target but would assume its answer.
The conditional non-identifiability result is complete; the physical goal
remains open. It cannot be closed honestly by more algebra on these premises.

To reopen physical selection requires an additional specified mechanism that
fixes or forbids the additive gravitational vacuum term, together with its
physical source dictionary and boundary/renormalization prescription. A
fully specified UV action or a derived gravitational boundary condition could
provide such an input; neither has been supplied or derived in this work.
No universal impossibility theorem for those mechanisms is claimed. They are
deferred open routes, not exhausted or validated candidates. The discriminating
check is whether two different C values remain allowed by that new mechanism.
