## What this changes physically

The concrete route is now narrower: redistribute the excessive Draco central concentration into a broad reservoir, retain an extended Sextans profile, and prevent the much larger Fornax/Leo supply from concentrating similarly. This route need not rely on particle collisions or expel all excess mass. It still needs a same-action field solution that supports those envelopes, selects their different concentrations, conserves mass/energy/vector angular momentum and keeps the actual stellar DF quiet. The present oversized coherent collapse does not do that. A shell's deeper potential can help confinement without producing inner force, but an extended halo cannot undo an already excessive central acceleration.
