Use ONLY fit_002 canonical beta=0, half-supply smooth Plummer inverse envelopes. Test all four galaxies with the same prescribed rule, not tuned amplitudes per galaxy. Potential on stars is the CCT baryonic law plus Newtonian cold gravity once. Stellar cohorts are passive, circular initially, spherical orientation-averaged, deterministic Plummer radial quantiles (128 per tracer, cumulative fraction 0.98). Sextans populations reported separately. These are stationary null ensembles, NOT observed stellar phase-space DFs. Birth distributions and live stellar gravity are not inferred.

Full cold mass stays constant; b(t)=b0[1+epsilon(1-cos(2pi t/P))/2] for four cycles, then b=b0 for two further P. epsilon=0 (static null),0.03,0.1,0.3; P/t_dyn=2,6,20 with t_dyn=sqrt(a/g_total(a)), a the catalogue circularized half-light radius. No hidden stellar force/extra dark source. This is a prescribed homogeneous envelope expansion/contraction, NOT a solution of the cold field or a measure of its actual fluctuation amplitude. It quantifies sensitivity, not stability or lasting material capture.

Integrate dr/dt=vr, dvr/dt=L^2/r^3-g_total, and per-star source work dW/dt=G Mc b bdot/(r^2+b^2)^(3/2). Track E_star(t)-E_star(0)-W(t). Use DOP853 with rtol1e-8, atol1e-10; independent tighter rtol1e-10, atol1e-12 for one epsilon0.1,P/t_dyn6 case per galaxy. Require normalized star work-ledger residual<1e-5, refinement in radius/variance summaries<1e-4. Static null must keep initial circular radii and velocity trace to1e-7 relative. Raw aggregate histories and worst star ledger retained, no discarded failed runs.

Report late radius and velocity-variance changes, minimum enclosed cold mass at a, period in Myr, work residual and comparison to catalogue error scales. Catalogue errors are sensitivity yardsticks, not an observational exclusion of unknown past stellar sizes/velocities. A quiet response in this restricted drive family DOES NOT establish coherent-wave stability, support, cold-mass retention or four-dwarf closure. Nonspherical forcing, resonances not sampled, host tides and actual assembly remain separate obligations.
