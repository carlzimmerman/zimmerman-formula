# Sources, exact releases and selection

* Walker et al.2023, ApJS268,19, DOI[10.3847/1538-4365/acdd79](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4365/acdd79); [CDS J/ApJS/268/19](https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/viz-bin/cat/J/ApJS/268/19), author release [Zenodo7837922](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7837922). Existing local ReadMe, hectocat.dat.gz and m2fshi.dat.gz are pinned byte-for-byte in each run manifest. Use Hectochelle Draco_1/Leo_1 and M2FS_HiRes Fornax_1. The local files preserve the fixed byte-column positions with separators; the parser follows the ReadMe, not guessed field order. Use published inverse-variance good-observation mean velocities/errors, one Obs=1 record per Gaia ID. No duplicate spectral epochs are counted as independent stars. All selected Gaia IDs, source line numbers, positions, velocities, errors and cut counts are saved in fit_002/result.json. Counts after the registered cuts: D458, F807, Leo299. These are OUR explicit subsets, not the paper's official final membership samples. Flags/velocity-window selection can bias variance; sensitivities are retained.
* Tolstoy et al.2025, A&A698,A53, [10.1051/0004-6361/202554176](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202554176), [arXiv2504.02787v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.02787v1), [CDS catalogue](https://doi.org/10.26093/cds/vizier.36980053). Use the unchanged CCT006 FLAMES subset:298 stars, actual coverage, error cuts, metallicities and two-population tracer assumptions. No errorless Walker note-field velocity is promoted into this fit. See the earlier packet's SOURCES.md for online table/publication count and note-field discrepancies. Its selected membership is not the complete published quality pipeline.
* Geometry and error yardsticks: pinned real_research/data/dsph/lvd_dwarf_mw.csv from [Local Volume Database](https://github.com/apace7/local_volume_database), [Pace overview](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.07424). This work acknowledges the Local Volume Database and its upstream source references. Current analysis uses the LOCAL SNAPSHOT, not an unpinned live query. Distances, PMs, centre, ellipticity, PA and circularized half-light radii are fixed. The catalogue global dispersions are NOT substituted for our selected-sample velocity likelihood. Population-based stellar/formed masses come from CCT004 results.json; Sextans keeps CCT006's assumed500000Msun and formed=surviving/.59. Stellar-mass, distance, photometric-shape and SFH covariance are not propagated; initial baryon catchment is not observed.
* Held-out Draco PM data: Vitral et al.2024, ApJ970,1, [10.3847/1538-4357/ad571c](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/ad571c), [arXiv2407.07769](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.07769), author data [Zenodo11111113](https://zenodo.org/records/11111113). DRACO_BINS.dat and README.txt downloaded intact to hst_data/. MD5s match the author release:1e5fd634386465f9d3992cf7f9d4a134 and35e33df9bafb2c91bd7ccc52bec2ed47. Only five rows with PM information are used, in the original mas/yr units; missing=-9999 rows are excluded. Ten dispersion-component values are compared. The overlapping LOS bins are not counted twice. No HST parameter refit, bin covariance, bin averaging, axisymmetric/rotation fit or formal significance is claimed. LVD distance81.55kpc is kept consistently, rather than mixing this geometry with a different fitted dynamical distance.

All input SHA256s, software, execution revisions and results are in the bounded run manifests. Raw survey files are already tracked in the repository; this packet adds only the small HST files and selected-row/fit results. Old CCT model proxies are not current v3s or AeST data-model closure.
