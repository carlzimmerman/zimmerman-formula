## Geometric transport in the test-payload limit

For b = Vσ(r), σ = 0 inside r≤R/2 and σ = 1 outside r≥R, the central coordinates have flat cabin metric. A payload at x = y = z = 0 follows a geodesic and has proper time t. Outside, the inertial coordinate is Z = z+Vt. Thus the same cabin worldline has Z = Vt relative to that distant inertial chart. The wall's directional clock/material stress supplies the curvature connecting those regions. It is not curvature of a two-dimensional sphere acting as an independent thrust source.

If one merely prescribes a trajectory X(t), the corresponding flat interior written in lab coordinates is

\[
ds^2=-dt^2+dx^2+dy^2+[dZ-\dot X(t)dt]^2.
\]

The worldline Z = X(t) is geodesic: its coordinate acceleration cancels the interior connection. C3W-003 verifies this directly for arbitrary formal X(t). This is kinematics of an imposed interior. It does **not** show that the wall field equations, control energy and radiation support that acceleration. The static proof cannot be applied to an arbitrary time-dependent trajectory without a new argument.

For example m = m0 exp(gλΛ) gives `a^μ=−gλ(g^{μν}+u^μu^ν)∂νΛ`. This is a scalar-gradient force paid for by the controller field, not a reactionless force or a demonstrated baryon tether. No such material mass coupling has been measured or connected to the proposed cold carrier.

It then relies on the actual geometry evolving, not on changing its label V. A finite massive craft also backreacts on the source. Adding its stress changes the very background for which stability was proved. The present geometry omits that backreaction and includes no nozzle, reactor or manufactured material.

## Energy and momentum accounting

The target spatial metric is exactly δij and its extrinsic curvature vanishes outside the transition. Accordingly its **GR geometric ADM surface integrals** vanish. The relevant definitions are [Gourgoulhon, gr-qc/0703035v1, equations 7.15 and 7.56](https://arxiv.org/pdf/gr-qc/0703035v1). This calculation does not evaluate the modified theory's full canonical charges with its material asymptotics and background subtraction. It certainly does not demonstrate zero fuel cost, a supplied payload mass or arbitrary momentum production.

The positive quantity in the wall theorem is the energy of small perturbations about an assigned background. The background still has negative normal stress energy in parts of the wall. A preparation process, energy source, and conserved total craft–field–radiation budget have not been derived from those mathematical coefficients.

The new controller current removes a precise field-equation obstruction to varying the stationary speed state. What remains mechanically decisive is a **localized, finite-energy launch solution in that fixed action with payload backreaction**, plus a demonstrated way of supplying the assumed medium and controller. Uniformly changing the controller across infinite space is excluded by its infinite kinetic cost.

Outgoing light or matter from an independently supplied conversion reaction still gives ordinary recoil according to the previously derived exhaust budgets. No derived interaction routes that hypothetical reaction into the geometric controller. Gravity also acts on other baryons that encounter the wall; this construction establishes no bystander immunity.

The current result is therefore an engineered, positive-energy steady geometric transport background with a specified theoretical controller coupling. It is not evidence that cold energy exists, that it can be manufactured, or that an autonomous drive has been built.
