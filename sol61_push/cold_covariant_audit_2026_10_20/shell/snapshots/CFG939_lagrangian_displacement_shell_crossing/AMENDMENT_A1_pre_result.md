## The identity

In the static law (single stream, at rest) the net force on a cold element vanishes at every radius: −g_Nb(r) + R(u_law(r)) = 0.
The frozen-configuration effective potential μ(r) = Φ_b(r) + ∫_r^∞ R(G M(<r′)/r′²) dr′ is therefore FLAT inside the supplied
region. For point-mass baryons and a finite supply ending at R_e in the deep regime, μ = −(2/3) G M_b / R_e (the push beyond the
edge is (G M_e)²/(3 a0 R_e³) = G M_b/(3 R_e)). So the frozen E_tf of §4 C2 equals ½v² + μ_edge for any element of a tethered
medium near the law: it is a test-particle escape energy in a frozen field, not a conserved quantity of the dynamics. Every
moving element of a relaxed tethered medium can have E_tf > 0 while it stays bound by the evolving field.
