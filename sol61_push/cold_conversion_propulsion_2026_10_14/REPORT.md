## 1. A precise interpretation of the desired mechanism

There are two different possible goals:

* Confine cold-to-baryon conversion to an engine, use light as exhaust and
  keep effects on nearby matter small. This has a conditional conventional
  momentum-conserving description below.
* Deliberately shape spacetime to propel the craft while leaving every other
  baryon unaffected. This needs an actual metric, stress tensor, field
  equations and geodesic analysis. The existing conversion reaction does
  not provide those controls or a solution.

The first goal is the working engine interpretation. Both interpretations
are audited so that radiation recoil and a geometric force are not counted
twice. Exactly zero influence is stronger than negligible influence in a
specified region. No quantitative bystander tolerance was supplied, and none
is invented here.

\[

\[
