# Vacuum scalar spectra do not supply a comoving mean dust population at second order

This is a conditional second-order abundance obstruction in the SAME shear-repaired action, not a theorem excluding dark matter, matter-era scalar response or nonlinear condensates. For arbitrary real scalar spectra about coincident empty de Sitter, the actual interaction-plus-shear normal stress averaged with each metric's proper spatial volume has only a^-4,a^-5,a^-6 corrections. There is no a^-3 density term. Vanishing mean pressure on an open time interval forces the nonzero-mode scalar data to vanish. A dust-like term in the average expansion alone is therefore insufficient.

## Scope and full stress variation

Use n3,K,H,a0>0,0<eta<1,b=3(1-eta)/eta>0, a=exp(Ht), shared theta=t and the retained envelope -A+I/2-I^(3/2)/12+... with controlled Euler jets. The only added operator is -eta K sum(V sigma²). Ordinary matter is absent. First-order common fields and homogeneous relative data are zero; second-order homogeneous relative particular data are zero. Consider nonzero scalar modes, either finite Fourier support or convergent spectral moments stated below, conditional on their second-order field coefficients being admitted with the required regularity.

Define Dlog=ln(Vg/Vhat), Vg=N sqrt(gamma). Actual relative shift and shear rows give Dlog1=Dlog2=0 in the exponential chart. Smooth cubic pure-relative terms vanish by exchange; the retained norm-cube has a second-order relative lapse divergence source, but no leading relative shear source. Thus the constraint is not discarded because of that term. The vacuum volume ratio v/Vg=v/Vhat is one through the order considered.

In unitary foliation I=[(gamma_inv+hatgamma_inv)/2]grad r grad r/a0², r=ln(N/L). Independent log-lapse variation gives

E_logN,int=K a0² v M-2K div[v M_I(gamma_inv+hatgamma_inv)grad r],

At this order the direct I/2 term contributes -K|grad nu|²/(2a²). The leading divergence contributes zero to its unweighted average, but its proper spatial-volume average carries 1/N: integration by parts gives +K|grad nu|²/a². The sum is therefore

average_gamma(rho_int-rho_Lambda)=K average(|grad nu|²)/(2a²), rho_Lambda=K a0² A.

The norm-cube divergence has zero average at this order; multiplication by the first-order volume weight starts at the next order. Linear second-order relative Laplacians likewise integrate to zero. Common metric corrections do not change this quadratic term or the vacuum ratio. Independent conformal spatial variation gives average_gamma(p_int+rho_Lambda)=K average(|grad nu|²)/(6a²). Here p is one third the preferred-normal spatial trace, not an equation of state assumed from time scaling.

The shear action gives rho_shear=-eta K sigma1² and p_shear=-eta K sigma1² by direct lapse and conformal spatial variation. This negative bare operator contribution is not a negative physical reduced kinetic conclusion; the full constrained vacuum scalar has the positive kinetic coefficient already derived in the parent. Stress splitting and canonical energy are different calculations.

## Arbitrary scalar Fourier data and exact powers

On a flat periodic torus write real fields as sums over all nonzero lattice vectors k, including the conjugate pair, with average(exp(i(k+l)x))=delta(k+l,0). Reality means D_-k=conjugate(D_k), C_-k=conjugate(C_k). Let k² denote the coordinate Laplace eigenvalue. The complete first-order scalar branches are

d_k=D_k/a, u_k=C_k k²/(bH²a²), nu_k=d_k+u_k,
z_k=C_k-d_k-u_k, e_k=2(d_k+u_k)-3C_k.

They follow from q=az and 2K a Kr qdot=constant, Kr=bP/(bH²+P), P=k²/a²; C_k and D_k are independent time-constant data, not chosen vacuum coefficients. The pure-decay branch has zero first shear. The momentum branch has f_k=H u_k and relative t_k=-3H u_k/eta. Each individual metric's tracefree extrinsic jet is half that relative value times (khat_i khat_j-deltaij/3). The projector's squared trace is 2/3, so orthogonality gives

average(sigma1²)=3H²/(2eta²) sum_k |u_k|².

This includes arbitrary phases, orientations and coherent decay/momentum interference. No finite-mode sample is used as a proof for all spectra.

Consequently, subtracting the baseline vacuum,

rho_bar=K/(2a²) sum_k k²|d_k+u_k|²-3KH²/(2eta) sum_k|u_k|²,
p_bar=K/(6a²) sum_k k²|d_k+u_k|²-3KH²/(2eta) sum_k|u_k|².

For a single cosine of amplitude d+u this reproduces K P(d+u)²/4-3K H²u²/(4eta). Expanding the sums yields

rho_bar=R4 a^-4+R5 a^-5+R6 a^-6,
R4=K/2 sum k²|D|²-3K/(2eta b²H²) sum k^4|C|²,
R5=K/(bH²) sum k^4 Re(D conjugate(C)),
R6=K/(2b²H^4) sum k^6|C|².

The pressure has the same three powers, with coefficients
P4=K/6 sum k²|D|²-3K/(2eta b²H²) sum k^4|C|²,
P5=R5/3, P6=R6/3.

All sums are finite or absolutely convergent if sum k²|D|² and sum k^6|C|² are finite; the cross moment is controlled by Cauchy-Schwarz. These summability conditions justify exchanging the sums and the displayed averages. They do not establish field-equation regularity; the latter remains a distinct hypothesis. The prepared smooth profiles meet stronger conditions when their coefficients are used consistently.

## Dust obstruction and the apparent expansion clue

Since H>0, an open time interval is an open interval in a. The powers a^-4,a^-5,a^-6 are linearly independent there. Thus no nonzero rho_bar can equal a fixed positive comoving dust density times a^-3 on an open interval. Independently, p_bar identically zero requires P6=0; positivity forces every C_k=0. Then P4=K sum k²|D_k|²/6 forces every D_k=0. A homogeneous comoving pressureless population is absent in this second-order scalar preparation. One-time cancellations do not satisfy this interval statement.

This is not inference of w from a power law: the actual pressure trace was varied separately. It does not assume the split pieces are separately conserved homogeneous fluids. Inhomogeneous lapse, geometric correlations and energy exchange matter in their averaged evolution.

For a single-shell mixed branch the actual preferred-normal mean expansion has an a^-3 interference term. The intrinsic-curvature average contributes an equal and opposite term in the exact averaged Hamiltonian constraint. The direct normal stress above has interference proportional to P d u, hence a^-5. The expansion clue is real geometry but is not a gravitating homogeneous dust source. Its phase-dependent sign is also not a positive abundance selector.

Other backgrounds, first-order common/tensor/vector sectors, ordinary matter coupling, homogeneous relative data, higher orders, singular/nonperturbative branches and altered operators remain outside this result. The theorem does not rule out cold matter in general. Neither A nor eta is selected; H²=A a0²/6 remains a continuous vacuum family. The next physical obligation is a mechanism producing a positive ordinary-matter-era background and its transfer, not identification of a coordinate or canonical-energy term as dust.
