# Why the infrared sign needs a separate physical diagnosis

Primary source checked on 2026-10-06: A. Emir Gümrükçüoğlu, Shinji Mukohyama and Thomas P. Sotiriou, *Jeans’ Ghost*, arXiv:1606.00618v1 (2016), [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/1606.00618v1), especially sections IV–V, equations 29–35; journal reference Physical Review D 94, 064001.

The source constructs a canonical transformation that exchanges an infrared negative kinetic term for positive kinetic terms with a Jeans-like unstable potential. Its example has a minimally coupled canonical scalar with a **spacelike** background gradient, anisotropic Bianchi I geometry and a negative cosmological constant branch. This is a counterexample to inferring catastrophic vacuum decay from an infrared kinetic sign alone. It is not a health theorem for our timelike logKGB clock, acceleration response, or dust history.

The current independent audit proves a reduced velocity coefficient and its sign. The full dust action includes lower derivative mixing and potentials, including the matter tadpole `+3 rho_d zeta nu`. Those terms cannot be discarded in the actual mode diagnosis. The physical meaning of the coefficient's zero requires the unreduced constraints or an explicitly regular canonical description. Neither this source nor a pass count supplies that missing calculation.
