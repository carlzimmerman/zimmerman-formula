# Source authentication and translation

Checked 2026-10-06. Primary sources were read through the web tool; no full-paper source was copied into this folder. This is a bounded formula/procedure check, not a novelty search.

1. Kobayashi, Yamaguchi, Yokoyama, *Generalized G-inflation: Inflation with the most general second-order field equations*, arXiv:1105.5723v4, https://arxiv.org/html/1105.5723v4, §4.2 Eqs.55–60. Raw lapse/shift quadratic action and Sigma/Theta definitions authenticated. Translate their constant tensor coefficient G_T to M, scalar G3 to this G, lapse alpha to nu, shift Laplacian to t. Their scalar-only background must not silently stand in for a dust background: the report derives the extra coordinate-density tadpole explicitly.

2. De Felice, Frusciante, Papadomanolakis, *On the stability conditions for theories of modified gravity in presence of matter fields*, arXiv:1609.03599v2 (13 March 2017), https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.03599 and https://arxiv.org/pdf/1609.03599, §III Eqs.III.1–III.12. Schutz-Sorkin action, dust limit and velocity/density variables authenticated. Their physical density perturbation differs from our coordinate-density pi by 3rho zeta. Our direct J^i elimination supplies the raw dust calculation below, including its lapse/shift reactions. The paper's UV criteria are not used as an IR catastrophe theorem.

3. Gumrukcuoglu, Mukohyama, Sotiriou, *Jeans' Ghost*, arXiv:1606.00618v1, https://arxiv.org/html/1606.00618v1, §§II,IV,V. Verified canonical-change interpretation of an IR negative kinetic sign. Its canonical spacelike scalar, Bianchi-I background and negative cosmological constant differ from the timelike FRW logKGB here. It supplies a counterexample to a general implication from IR kinetic sign to catastrophic vacuum decay, not a theorem that this particular clock is harmless.
