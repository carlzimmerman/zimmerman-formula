# Independent root audit of the six-state clock–dust–radiation crossing

Accepted as a conditional local classical linear theorem. I independently expanded the radiation action and reconstructed the canonical equations; the result is not inferred from the author's check count or the dust-only four-state theorem.

For P(Y)=lambda Y², homogeneous current conservation gives chidot proportional to a^-1. Thus delta chidot/chidot=vr_dot-Hvr. At quadratic homogeneous order, `N exp(3zeta)P(Y)=P0 exp(3zeta)N^-3(1+sr)^4` gives

`6P0(sr-nu)²+12P0 zeta sr-9P0 zeta nu+(9/2)P0 zeta²`.

Since rho_r=3P0, R_r=4P0 and C_r=6P0, these are precisely the reported radiation terms. In particular the zeta*nu term is -3rho_r, not -3R_r. The actual gravity tadpole is 3(rho_d+rho_r); their cancellation leaves 3rho_d. The actual pressure-dependent gravity mass cancels the remaining radiation zeta². Omitting either term would corrupt the complete mode equations even though the highest-velocity Schur sign might survive.

Using the shift relation and `Pr=2Cr(sr-nu)+3Rr zeta`, the clock lapse reaction becomes `Rnu=2Snu+6Theta zd+Tzeta-pi-Pr`, with T=2Mp²+3rho_d+3Rr. Its momentum reduces to

`Pz=2Ac zd+(Ac/M)(rho_d vd+Rr vr)+dTzeta-dpi-dPr`.

Direct variation gives `Pr_dot+4HPr=er Rnu-Rr p²vr` and `Pz_dot+3HPz=Tnu+(2Mp²-3Rr)zeta+Pr`. The extra H in the radiation equation follows from the -Hvr normalization; the last Pr and -3Rr terms in the clock equation follow from the retained radiation tadpole. These lower derivative terms have therefore been checked independently, not discarded before diagonalization.

The matter-momentum zd coefficients are rho_d Ac/M and Rr Ac/M, so they are regular after the clock momentum is inverted. In the exact six-state system the simple-zero residue is consequently the outer product of `(1,d,d,0,0,Td)` and `(-dT,0,0,d,d,1)/(2alpha)`. Their contraction vanishes. The nonzero rank-one nilpotent residue, analytic normal form and clock-norm pole follow exactly as stated; the radiation momentum changes the compatibility condition and cannot be deleted. Nonzero k, q and Theta make the gauge fixing and lapse/shift elimination legitimate. The six equations represent three canonical scalar pairs away from the crossing; matter conservation is already included, not an extra amplitude restriction.

The analytic reduction proof uses ad_N³=0, convergent series with inverse coefficients bounded by a constant divided by their order, and an analytic scalar majorant. It establishes a codimension-one regular amplitude subspace, not dynamical selection of it. The scalar deltaX on constant-clock surfaces is -q²nu in unitary gauge, giving a genuine generic linear pole. Neither a UV ghost claim nor a nonlinear continuation follows.

The numerical root is a bounded illustration, not certified interval evidence. A separate root proof in ../RADIATION_CROSSING_EXISTENCE.md supplies actual transverse crossing modes for almost every level in a nonempty comoving interval without using that numeric root. No statement is made about whether a prescribed finite-box spectrum contains one, nor about measured CMB initial data. Radiation here is an irrotational perfect fluid; photon collisions, anisotropic stress, entropy and atomic recombination are separate missing physics.
