# An open level interval contains actual simple radiation-history crossings

This root derivation supplies an existence link to the separately reconstructed six-state perturbation theorem. It uses the exact tuned background, not an extrapolated dust-only mode equation. Assume D,R>0, 0<eta<1, kappa>0. Normalize x=a/a_f, d_f=1-4r_f/3>0 and kbar=k/(H* a_f). On 0<x<1 the background is analytic, h>1+eta, and its kinetic sign is determined by

`Kc/M = kappa x^-2 [kbar²-S(x)]/(h-eta)²`,

`S(x)=3eta[h(x)-1-eta]x²/kappa`.

In normalized variables the actual early background expansion has `h=alpha x^-2+beta x^-1+O(1)`, with `alpha=sqrt(2eta r_f)>0`, `beta=eta d_f/alpha>0`. Consequently

`S(x)=S0+(3eta beta/kappa)x+O(x²)`, `S0=3eta alpha/kappa>0`.

Thus S increases above S0 at sufficiently small positive x. At the background fold S(1)=0. Continuity and its finite endpoint extension imply an attained interior maximum Smax>S0. For every level kbar² in (S0,Smax), sufficiently early S is below that level, some interior S is above it, and S near the fold is below it. The corresponding fixed comoving mode therefore has at least two zeros and a negative interval between some zero pair, while remaining positive in the sufficiently early limit and at the fold. No unimodality or exact global zero count is needed.

Moreover, S is analytic and nonconstant on (0,1). Its critical points are isolated on that interval, so there are at most countably many critical values. Choose kbar² from (S0,Smax) outside those values. Every crossing of that level has S'(x)!=0. Since xdot=Hx>0, the zero of Kc is simple in physical time. Positive q,rho_d,rho_r and nonzero Theta hold at every finite x in the background. These are actual modes satisfying the simple-zero hypotheses of the full clock/dust/radiation theorem, not merely prospective conditional events. Removing at most countably many levels from a nonempty interval also shows that the transverse-level choices have full Lebesgue measure within that interval.

This argument does not choose a measured cosmological mode, prove independence of the regularity conditions at the two crossings, evolve through nonlinear breakdown, or guarantee that every finite-box Fourier spectrum contains a selected mode. It establishes an existence obstruction for a continuous perturbation spectrum on the retained radiation history. The response scale A remains absent throughout; changing it does not change this quadratic crossing structure.
