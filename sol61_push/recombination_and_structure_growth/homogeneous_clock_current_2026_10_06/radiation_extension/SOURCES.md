The exact ordinary-dust action remains the coordinate-density Schutz-Sorkin action in the fold-health report; it is not replaced by a singular P(Y) approximation. No atom/photon collision, recombination-rate or observed CMB formula is being imported. The primary Jeans' Ghost arXiv:1606.00618v1 interpretation caveat in fold_health/SOURCES.md continues to apply: an IR kinetic sign is not a UV vacuum-decay theorem, nor does that different spacelike-background example prove this clock benign.
