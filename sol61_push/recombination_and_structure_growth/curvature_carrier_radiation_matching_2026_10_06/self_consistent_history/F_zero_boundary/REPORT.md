# Finite homogeneous continuation is not tensor health at F=0

For the retained canonical curvature-carrier action, a finite expanding homogeneous solution can extend mathematically through a simple F=M−2ξ|χ|² zero. The Friedmann root, scalar equations and curvature need not diverge there. Nevertheless the physical transverse-traceless graviton kinetic coefficient vanishes; a generic unsourced tensor mode develops a logarithmic singularity, and the formal F<0 side has negative physical tensor kinetic energy. A regular homogeneous trajectory therefore does not establish healthy continuation.

This is a conditional local theorem, not a proof that the parent stopped trajectories attain F=0. The recorded guard data have positive Ḟ and finite H and are compatible with the regular root regime. The projected F=0 examples below are new local constraint-compatible data, not certified continuations of the original trajectories. No new ξ grid or long integration is performed.

## Regular homogeneous equations through the zero

The Friedmann constraint at F=0 reads

3vH₀=ρ_d0+ρ_r0+E₀.

The right-hand side is positive, so finite H₀>0 requires v>0. Also B₀=6ξM>0. The expanding root can be evaluated as

H=2(ρ_d+ρ_r+E)/[3(√(Ḟ²+4F(ρ_d+ρ_r+E)/3)+Ḟ)].

At the finite point the square-root argument is v²>0 and the denominator is 6v>0. The function is analytic in a neighborhood, including sufficiently small negative F. Its limit is

H₀=(ρ_d0+ρ_r0+E₀)/(3v),
R₀=[ρ_d0+2(6ξ−1)E₀]/(6ξM).

The coupled proper-time equations ȧ=Ha, χ̇=q, q̇=−3Hq−ξRχ and conserved fluid densities are consequently analytic at these declared data. The constraint derivative coefficient 3(2FH+Ḟ)=3v is nonzero. Standard local analytic ODE existence supplies a unique finite homogeneous continuation. This statement is about the homogeneous equations, including their Einstein spatial equation via the parent trace/constraint identity; it supplies no healthy perturbation theory across F=0.

Keeping coefficients other than F fixed, the root has expansion

H(F)=T/(3v)−F T²/(9v³)+O(F²), T=ρ_d+ρ_r+E.

This coefficient-jet expansion is not the time expansion of an actual trajectory; the other variables evolve too.

The actual scalar equations give F̈=−4ξ[E−3HD−ξRf]. Substituting F=0, 3Hv=T and B₀=6ξM yields the exact trajectory jet

F(t)=vτ+½F̈₀τ²+O(τ³), τ=t−t₀.

Thus the local continuation is transverse: F>0 immediately to the future and F<0 immediately to the past. This is not an asymptotic conclusion about every history. An approach with divergent H, velocities or vanishing a violates the declared finite-limit hypotheses; a point with v=0 cannot obey finite expanding H and positive total energy. Those cases are not classified here.

The indicial equation is r²=0. The analytic branch has γ̇₀=0 and

γ_reg=γ₀[1−p₀²τ²/4+O(τ³)].

     =[1/(a₀³v)]ln|τ|+terms less singular than ln|τ|.
