# Cuscuton with a varied galaxy bridge: regular vacuum obstruction and degeneracy cost

## Restricted result

The original positive cuscuton action cannot sustain exact rolling de Sitter with only a constant vacuum source. This follows from its metric stress, independently of potential tuning. A quadratic potential changes the cosmological Friedmann coefficient along a regular matter-tracking history, but does not cancel vacuum sensitivity. Linking the MOND scale to that field keeps the normalized coefficient constant only by allowing both H and a0 to change. A declared acceleration response using the same foliation adds a scalar time-derivative term at finite spatial momentum; treating the coupled action as the original nondynamical cuscuton would omit a variation.

This is a changed-premise examination, not a repetition of freely adding a vacuum constant. It tests an actual constrained field, its quadratic tracking potential, an explicit varied response, and a zero-gradient auxiliary completion. It neither establishes a healthy complete theory nor selects 32π.

## Action, dimensions, metric and regular branch

Use signature (−,+,…,+), c=ℏ=1, d=n+1, n≥2, and minimally coupled matter with the same physical metric that defines test-body trajectories. Write

S=∫√−g [K R/2 + Q v − V(φ) − ρv] + Sm,
v=√(−g^{μν}∂μφ∂νφ)>0, Q=μ²>0, K>0.

Take φ of mass dimension 1, [K]=[Q]=mass^{d−2}, [V]=[ρv]=mass^d. For V=V0+m2 φ²/2, [m2]=mass^{d−2}; the symbol m2 denotes a positive coefficient, not the square of a dimension-one parameter in every dimension. All variations below stay on a connected timelike branch with fixed s=sign φdot. The original action is not differentiable at v=0.

Direct metric and scalar variation gives

Tφ_{μν}=Q ∂μφ∂νφ/v +(Qv−V)g_{μν},
Q∇μ(∂^μφ/v)−V′=0.

For homogeneous flat FRW, ρφ=V, pφ=Q|φdot|−V, and

V′=−s nQH,
A_n K H²=ρm+ρv+V, A_n=n(n−1)/2,
−(n−1)K Hdot=ρm+pm+Qv.

Consequently pure constant vacuum plus the regular cuscuton has Hdot<0. Exact Hdot=0 requires v=0. This excludes an exact rolling de Sitter branch for every differentiable potential, assuming no additional negative-enthalpy sector. It is not a statement about exotic matter, negative Q, spatially inhomogeneous solutions, or nonsmooth completions.

The gravitational source is the full Tm+Tφ−ρv g. Bare minimally coupled cuscuton does not directly change the measured short-distance Newton constant. For d≥4, with Newton's Gauss convention ∇²Ψ=Ω_{d−2} G_N ρ, κ=1/K=[(d−2)Ω_{d−2}/(d−3)]G_N. The d=3 Newton/Tangherlini calibration is exceptional. General-n homogeneous equations above do not require that calibration.

## Quadratic tracking, critical branch and vacuum response

Choose V=V0+m2φ²/2 and s=+1. Then

φ=−nQH/m2, φdot=−nQ Hdot/m2,
Kcos=K−nQ²/[(n−1)m2],
A_n Kcos H²=ρm+ρv+V0,
Hdot=−(ρm+pm)/[(n−1)Kcos].

For ordinary positive-enthalpy matter a consistent decreasing-H tracking solution has Kcos>0, hence φdot>0. At Kcos=0 the algebraic Friedmann equation requires ρm+ρv+V0=0; this is a compatibility condition, not arbitrary vacuum self-adjustment. Exact constant H on the quadratic branch sets φdot=0 and leaves the domain of the original action. A dust-plus-positive-vacuum history may approach that endpoint asymptotically; a regular finite-time pure-vacuum rolling solution does not follow from the algebraic Friedmann relation.

The limiting H² response is dH²/dρv=1/(A_n Kcos), larger than the bare positive-K response. Now declare, rather than assign silently, a0=A(φ)=−γφ with γ>0 on φ<0. The tracking relation implies

a0=γ nQH/m2,
C=Λgeom/a0²=A_nH²/a0²=(n−1)m2²/(2nγ²Q²).

Thus varying vacuum changes the nonzero a0 together with H. Constant C in this limited ratio does not keep a0 fixed or select C: the remaining parameter combination is explicit. The ratio uses the geometrical de Sitter normalization of the limiting history, not an assertion that the regular branch reaches an exact vacuum de Sitter state.

## A concrete same-foliation response and all scalar variations

Set uμ=−∂μφ/v on the positive branch and aμ=uν∇νuμ, g_c=√(aμaμ). Add

Lresp=−2K[W(g_c;A(φ))−g_c²/2],
W(g;A)=½[g√(g²+A²/4)+(A²/4)asinh(2g/A)]−Ag/2.

On FRW, aμ=0 and this term and its first variation vanish. The homogeneous obstruction therefore persists. In a leading static weak-field, single-potential reduction with Ψ the physical test potential, the Einstein term −K|∇Ψ|² combines with this term into −2K W. Its flux is W_g=√(g²+A²/4)−A/2, so g²=W_g²+A W_g. In four dimensions the usual spherical baryon-only dictionary W_g=G_N M_b/r² would yield the P2 law. This dictionary requires neglecting the cuscuton perturbation stress; in the coupled theory δρφ=V′δφ, response gradient stress and metric constraints must instead be included. No baryon-only globally matched force law is proved here.

The explicit scale derivative is

W_A=A asinh(2g/A)/4−g/2<0,
∂Lresp/∂φ=2Kγ W_A.

There is also a foliation variation. If E_{u,μ} denotes the Euler derivative of Lresp with respect to an independently varied uμ, including its ∇u dependence, P^{μν}=g^{μν}+uμuν, then δuμ=−P^{μν}∂νδφ/v. The complete scalar equation is

Q∇μ(∂^μφ/v)−V′+2KγW_A+∇ν(P^{νμ}E_{u,μ}/v)=0.

Dropping the final term does not describe this action. In particular near a geodesic background, on a fixed metric with coefficients frozen, δa_i=−∂iδφdot/v and Lresp=+K a²+O(|a|³/A). The bare cuscuton has no quadratic δφdot², but the added term contributes +K|∇δφdot|²/v². For a quadratic potential the resulting fixed-metric instantaneous scalar frequency at k>0 is

ω²=Qv/(2K)+m2 v²/(2Kk²)>0.

This is a diagnostic of changed kinetic structure, not a count of physical modes after gravitational lapse/shift/fluid constraints. The k=0 mode remains exceptional; freezing the background and ignoring H terms restricts the approximation. The fully constrained spectrum and degeneracy are the next health obligation.

## Local elliptic check and bounded scale control

For the bare cuscuton the regular scalar perturbation in the two-potential metric obeys

[−Δ+v V″/Q]δφ=nφdot(Φdot+HΨ)+(v/Q)δLφ,
where v V″/Q=−nHdot on the quadratic tracking background.

The response adds foliation terms to this equation. In the frozen static approximation its explicit scale term shifts the mass to m2+2Kγ²W_AA>0. Time-dependent foliation and metric terms cannot be inferred from this static check.

For g>0 let x=2g/A and s=√(1+x²). Then

W_gg=x/s>0, W_g/g>0,
W_AA=(asinh x−x/s)/4>0,
W_gA=(1/s−1)/2,
det D²W=[x asinh x−2(s−1)]/(4s)>0.

The last numerator vanishes at x=0 and has derivative asinh x−x/s>0. Thus the local static response has positive radial/transverse stiffness and a positive joint (g,A) Hessian away from zero. At g=0 its gravitational stiffness vanishes; uniform ellipticity is not asserted. Low-order V′δφ Ψ mixing and the global metric constraints remain outside this principal/static result.

For a linear frozen Dirichlet ball of radius R, the positive-mass torsion estimate gives ||(−Δ+mass²)^{-1}||∞≤R²/(2n). The response-only fractional scale bound is

|δa0|/a0 ≤ [Kγ² R²v/(nQ)] |W_A|/A
= θ ε_H(HR)² |W_A|/A,
θ=Kγ²/m2, ε_H=−Hdot/H².

The static metric-source contribution is at most ε_H(HR)²||Ψ||∞/2. These are bounds on a0 variation, not complete force fidelity. The timelike condition |∇δφ|≪v, spatial matching, and response stress must also be checked. Controls use θ=10^{-4}, HR=10^{-3}, ||Ψ||∞=10^{-6}, g/A∈{.01,.1,1,10}; all four response bounds are below 10^{-8}. Such small scale perturbations do not prove that the omitted source terms are small enough for a galaxy fit.

## Changed zero-gradient completion: self-adjustment loses fixed scale

An explicit alternative auxiliary action is Laux=Q uμ∂μφ−V+λ(u²+1). Varying u gives Q∂μφ+2λuμ=0; on the regular branch λ=Qv/2 and elimination recovers Qv. At ∂φ=0 it instead permits λ=0 with an independent unit u, which the original square-root action does not define.

Choose the linear potential V=V0−nQHcφ, with Hc an input. The φ constraint −Q∇u−V′=0 fixes homogeneous H=Hc. On the constant-field, λ=0 branch the stress is −Vg and

φ*=(ρv+V0−A_n K Hc²)/(nQHc).

This completion can hold H fixed while vacuum changes by shifting φ*. But the declared bridge shifts da0/dρv=−γ/(nQHc). A locally constant plateau A(φ)=A* could avoid that shift while its variation vanishes, at the price of supplying both A* and Hc externally. Neither coefficient selection nor health at the degenerate λ=0 point follows. This constructive exception makes the missing implication precise: vacuum self-adjustment alone cannot provide a fixed independently measured MOND scale through the chosen rolling-field bridge.
