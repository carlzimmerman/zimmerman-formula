# Root continuation: the physical radial force tail

This is an analytic consequence of the frozen compact-spherical-source, static, leading-large-Z action in REPORT.md. It does not assume a finite-Z nonlinear existence theorem or import a covariant vacuum map.

The actual varied equation is DeltaPhi=div[nu(y,T(r)) gradPhi_N]. In spherical symmetry, multiply by r^(n−1) and integrate radially. Regularity at the center of a smooth compact source sets the integration constant to zero, giving Phi'=nu(y,T(r)) Phi_N'. Thus gradients of T are accounted for by the exact flux identity; they are not dropped by using a fixed-kernel equation.

Outside the source, y=(rM/r)^(n−1). Let kappa=a0²/(2S G_n). At fixed m>0, the accepted leading stiffness response is deltaT=8kappa y^(7/2)/(7Z m² T0³) plus the stated faster far-field corrections. The physical extra acceleration relative to the same source at the vacuum cutoff T0 is therefore

Delta g = a0 y partial_T chi(y,T0) deltaT + higher stiffness order
        = 8a0³ y^6/(7S G_n Z m² T0^6) + o(y^6).

Its sign is positive. In n=3, S=4pi, so Delta g=2a0³ rM^12/[7pi G Z m² T0^6 r^12] at this leading order. The cutoff itself has an r^−7 tail, while the measured spherical force correction has an r^−12 tail. These are different quantities. The coefficient has acceleration dimensions: [G_n Z m²]=L²/time^4.

This follows first in the linear 1/Z response, then in its large-r asymptotic; the orders are explicit. It is not a uniform statement for arbitrary nonlinear stiffness or a prediction for nonspherical galaxies. The background kernel's MOND enhancement remains far larger at sufficiently small y, so this new tail does not fix its amplitude or select its cutoff.

For the massless leading control, deltaT~Q_J/[Z(n−2)S r^(n−2)] gives Delta g~2a0 Q_J y^(5/2)/[Z(n−2)S T0³ r^(n−2)], hence r^−((7n−9)/2), or r^−6 in n=3. The massless boundary T0 remains free and is not a selected vacuum minimum. Again the actual force dictionary, not the modulus falloff alone, defines this consequence.

No hbar, target coefficient or astrophysical fit enters this analytic control. It is a conditional extra prediction of the declared action, not fulfillment of the original MOND–Lambda objective.
