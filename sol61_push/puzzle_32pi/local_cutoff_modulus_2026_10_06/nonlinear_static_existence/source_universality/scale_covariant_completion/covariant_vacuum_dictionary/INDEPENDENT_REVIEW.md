## Source normalization and spatial branch
In n spatial dimensions, the trace-reversed Einstein 00 equation for nonrelativistic matter gives Delta phi=8pi G_EH (n−2)rho/(n−1). Thus Omega G_n has exactly that coefficient. With chi_n=(n−1)/(2(n−2)), the covariant interaction coefficient 2K chi_n a0^2 equals a0^2/(2 Omega G_n), with K=1/(16pi G_EH). No equality of G_n and G_EH is assumed outside n=3.

For p_i=partial_i phi*, the declared weak scalar metrics give C^0_0i=p_i, C^i_00=p_i, C^i_jk=−(delta_ik p_j+delta_ij p_k−delta_jk p_i)/(n−2). Contracting produces Upsilon=−(n−1)p^2/(n−2), so z=p^2/a0^2. There is a genuinely symbolic spatial compatibility check, stronger than the finite dimension samples: the background contracted connection Cbar_i is zero and its trace C^lambda_i lambda is −2p_i/(n−2). An independent spatial perturbation H has delta Cbar_i=partial_j H_ij−partial_i H_jj/2. The first product's cross term is −2p_i partial_j H_ij/(n−2)+p_i partial_i H_jj/(n−2), canceled exactly by −delta Cbar_i Ctrace_i. Hence no phi*–H bilinear remains, even for spatially varying M_z. This admits H=0 on the NR interaction branch; it does not prove global uniqueness or physical health.

Variation of the actual NR action gives beta Delta phi−div(m grad phi*)=Omega G_n rho and alpha Delta phihat+div(m grad phi*)=0. For beta=1,alpha=−1, Delta phi* is Newtonian and m=nu−1 implements the direct QUMOND lift. For alpha=beta=1, the equations are instead div[(1−2m)grad phi*]=Omega G_n rho and Delta phi=Omega G_n rho+div(m grad phi*). Only regular spherical flux integration yields y=(1−2m)x and g/a0=(1−m)x. In a nonspherical field, the weighted star gradient need not be curl-free; matching its divergence does not turn it into grad phi_N. ## Parametric reconstruction and its domain
Let d=y e(y,T), x=y+2d and M=q+2d^2−lambda T^2/2−A. At fixed T, q_y=2d, so M_y=2d(1+2d_y)=2d x_y and m=M_z=M_y/(2x x_y)=d/x. This gives the desired spherical force g/a0=y(1+e).

At fixed x, x_T=2d_T, and the full derivative is M_T=q_T+4d d_T−(M_y/x_y)x_T−lambda T=q_T−lambda T. The cancellation is exact; auxiliary stationarity is not a post-variation prescription. The stated sufficient interval lambda<5/192 guarantees fixed-T D>1/2 on the stationary source branch, hence x_y=2D−1>0 and a local inverse around each finite positive source point. It does not guarantee a single off-shell inverse on the whole (z,T) plane or at the vacuum, and it leaves negative z unspecified. These omissions are correctly retained as action-admission obligations.

After elimination, the envelope identity qeff_y=2y e follows from stationarity. The extra 2d^2 tends to zero both at y=0 and infinity, yielding M_eff(0)=−A and M_eff(infinity)=2Cresp−A. A bounded source-branch m approaching 1/2 does not by itself prove a regular second variation at the coincident vacuum. Only a covariant extension with the declared vanishing first interaction-derivative stress there licenses the vacuum equations. The final report states this conditional premise explicitly.
