# Root review of the EFE/vacuum-moment independence

Reviewed the actual QUMOND action, source projection, quadrupole functional and compact-bump argument. The decisive result is a local nonidentifiability theorem for the specified observables, not preservation of every Solar System test or a relativistic vacuum prediction.

The action variations fix `Delta PhiN=4piG rho` and `Delta Phi=div(nu grad PhiN)`. Linearizing around a constant external field gives a tensor before gradient projection, while a Fourier potential retains its longitudinal projection `nu_e[1+K_e k_parallel^2/k^2]PhiN`. Thus replacing that operator with the algebraic acceleration vector can change transverse components and is not a field-equation implementation. This independently supports Claude's withdrawal of the earlier algebraic EFE route.

A Green-function inner Hessian followed by integration by parts makes the quadrupole a linear functional of nu-1 at fixed external Newtonian field. The report explicitly distinguishes its acceleration-potential quadrupole from the opposite-sign gravitational potential Hessian, retaining the cited convention. The factor a0/rM has dimension inverse time squared; the vacuum moment C and both dimensionless kernel weights have the stated different scaling. The actual primitive and finite field value are supported by the authored symbolic checks/high-precision integration and agreement with Claude's separate multipole discretization; root does not represent that agreement as a new particle-orbit rerun or an interval-certified integral.

For exact bump functionals q1,q2 and c1,c2, `delta nu=t(b1-q1 b2/q2)` annihilates the quadrupole exactly, while `delta C=t(c1-q1 c2/q2)`. Because the ratio of weights w_e(y)/y is nonconstant, supports can make this latter coefficient nonzero. Bumps away from the external root preserve both nu_e and its derivative and hence the declared far-field response. Strict constitutive inequalities have positive margins on compact supports, so a sufficiently small deformation preserves them. This proof establishes actual freedom even if numerical cancellation is only approximate. It does not establish arbitrarily large moment changes under fixed margins.

The target-shaped p35 kernel retaining its cited Cassini tension and the illustrative Cassini-margin mixture are separate examples. The small null direction demonstrates nonuniqueness around either base; it does not make the target kernel Cassini-admissible or show that the mixture fits rotation curves. Higher multipoles and finite-radius source response generally change. The historical2014 estimate is a specified comparison, not an authenticated current ephemeris limit.

Finally the moment-to-Lambda dictionary is additional relativistic physics. The NR QUMOND action alone leaves an additive Qfun constant and does not derive its vacuum coupling; its moment cannot be assigned to the distinct logKGB clock action. The scoped theorem therefore eliminates quadrupole-plus-far-field constitutive selection as a sufficient route, while leaving a more restrictive action or further functionals open.
