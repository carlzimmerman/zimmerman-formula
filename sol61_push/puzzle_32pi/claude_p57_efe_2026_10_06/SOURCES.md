# Primary-source authentication and notation

Checked2026-10-06 through official arXiv full HTML. Repository citation entries and older Cassini calculations were discovery aids; no matching local authenticated full copies of these exact versions were found by filename search. Source versions and equation identifiers below are primary-source dependencies. No global novelty search was performed.

- Mordehai Milgrom, *Quasi-linear formulation of MOND*, arXiv:0911.5464v2 (2010-03-02), [full version](https://arxiv.org/html/0911.5464v2). Equations3,5,6 give the two-potential action and field equations; Eq8 describes the gradient projection. SecV.1 Eqs57–60 give the external-field monopole and anisotropic asymptotic potential. The paper's nu argument is Newtonian gN/a0; hat-nu is its logarithmic derivative. These are translated to e,nu_e,K_e in the report. The source supports the NR QUMOND field law, not a relativistic KGB/BIMOND vacuum coefficient.
- Mordehai Milgrom, *MOND effects in the inner solar system*, arXiv:0906.4817v2 (2009-08-24), [full version](https://arxiv.org/html/0906.4817v2). Eqs4–6 give the axisymmetric trace-free inner quadrupole and scale a0/rM. Its main calculation is the nonlinear modified-Poisson theory; the surrogate treatment later identified with QUMOND is distinguished. This report derives its QUMOND kernel functional independently from Green's function, rather than importing its numerical AQUAL values.
- A. Hees, W.M. Folkner, R.A. Jacobson, R.S. Park, *Constraints on MOND theory from radio tracking data of the Cassini spacecraft*, arXiv:1402.6950v2 (2014-04-29), [full version](https://arxiv.org/html/1402.6950v2). Eq3 states QUMOND; Eq6 defines PhiQ=−Q2 xixj(eiej−deltaij/3)/2; Eq8 translates q=−2Q2 rM/(3a0). Its Cassini fit is Q2=(3±3)10^-27 s^-2. The illustrative positive two-sigma ceiling9e−27 used here is this specific2014 estimate, not a new ephemeris fit or a claim about every MOND formulation.

Internal new work: exact change-of-variable reduction of the QUMOND quadrupole to a one-dimensional kernel weight; its asymptotic weight; the two-bump null direction and moment distinction. These are proved in REPORT.md and checked by checks.py. No source attribution to an external novelty theorem is claimed.
