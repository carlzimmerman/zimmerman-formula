# The conformal scalar-curvature value stabilizes only a small cutoff

This is a further independently specified curvature-coupling test, not a derivation of32pi. Take the classical improved-stress value xi_c=(d-2)/[4(d-1)], and use its conventional negative scalar-curvature sign in the already declared nonminimal cutoff action. Every canonical common vacuum equilibrium then has T<1, a unique strict minimum, and a scalar mass between (d-2)/2 and d-2 times the squared de-Sitter Hubble rate. The dimensionless kinetic normalization remains free. In d=4 this mechanism stabilizes a light scalar, but the action-parameter coefficient Lambda/a0² is below pi/4, far from32pi.

## Independent coupling convention and exact action scope

Use the frozen nonminimal-cutoff action and notation M0=4K, gamma=f²/M0>0, u=lnT and canonical Jordan scalar phi=f u. Set

xi_c=(d-2)/[4(d-1)], alpha=xi_c gamma,
F(u)=1-alpha u², |u|<1/sqrt(alpha), d>=4.

The negative sign follows the scalar term -xi_c R phi²/2. Its value can be derived classically: in flat space the improved stress trace for a massless free scalar on shell is [- (d-2)/2+2xi(d-1)](partial phi)², which vanishes precisely at xi_c. This fixes the improvement value without a target coefficient or hbar. It does NOT make the full cutoff action Weyl invariant: the constant Einstein term, dimensionful a0, canonical logarithmic field and retained kernel potential break that premise. We test this independently specified value; it is not a symmetry proof for the complete theory, nor a unique microscopic reason to choose F.

As in the parent, multiply BOTH Einstein and projected interaction terms by F to retain critical leading MOND cancellation. This additional common multiplier is a declared model choice, beyond a free conformal scalar action. The admissible domain F>0 is essential. No continuation across F=0, negative Einstein coefficient or ghost compensator is inferred. The two-metric action is exchange symmetric; common vacuum reduction retains both metric equations and does not decide relative/clock health.

## Unique finite minimum, at the wrong scale

Write beta=2/(d-2), p=A_u/A and p'=d p/du. The frozen all-finite-u theorem gives 1<p<3/2 and -1/4<p'<0. The parent action yields

U=V0 A F^-beta, Z=f²/F+M0(d-1)/(d-2)q²>0,
q=F_u/F=-2alpha u/(1-alpha u²),
q'=-2alpha(1+alpha u²)/(1-alpha u²)².

The constant common scalar equation is p=beta q. Since p>0, a finite equilibrium must have u<0; hence T<1. The log-potential slope tends to minus infinity at the left F=0 boundary, while it is positive at u=0 and everywhere on the positive-u half-domain. A finite equilibrium therefore exists in the negative-u domain.

At a critical point put z=alpha u² in(0,1). Then q=p/beta and

q'=-p²(1+z)/(2beta²z),
C=(logU)''=p'+p²(1+z)/(2beta z)>0.

Strict positivity follows from p'>-1/4, p>1 and beta<=1. Every root crosses the log-slope from negative to positive. Two such roots would require an intervening zero crossing with nonpositive derivative, impossible by the same exact identity. Thus there is exactly one root. U diverges at both finite F=0 endpoints; the root is its unique strict global minimum on this connected domain. This is a common canonical vacuum minimum, not an all-sector stable theory or cosmological attractor theorem.

The same constant-u Jordan metric equation gives Lambda=chi_n a0² A/2. Since T<1 and A(T)<pi T/2,

Lambda/a0²<chi_n pi/4.

In d=4 chi_n=1, so Lambda/a0²<pi/4. The a0 in this statement is the geometric action parameter. Operational ordinary-source force calibration belongs to the separate source package, not an imported equality. No shifted field origin is allowed in this unshifted test; such a shift would add another freely chosen cutoff scale.

## Exact mass prediction from the fixed improvement coefficient

Using xi_c and gamma in Z gives at the stationary point

Z/M0=(d-1)/(d-2) q²/z.

Let HE²=2U/[M0(d-1)(d-2)] and m²=U C/Z. Therefore

m²/HE²=2z p'/p²+(1+z)/beta.

The strict bounds on p' and p yield

(d-2)/2 < m²/HE² < d-2.

Indeed the upper bound follows from p'<0 and z<1; the lower bound follows from p'>-1/4 and p>1, giving more than 1/beta+z(1/beta-1/2)>1/beta. In d=4, 1<m²/HE²<2. This stationary scalar decouples from linear common metric perturbations because U_u=0 and the background scalar velocity vanishes. Its canonical principal sound speed is one. A mass of order H does not justify rapid coherent oscillations as cold matter. This is different from both the rolling tracking curvature and the positive-quadratic family's m<H result.

## Remaining implication

The result supplies a classical dimension-dependent coupling value and an extra scalar-mass prediction, but neither the correct cutoff nor a parameter-free coefficient. The complete potential is not conformally invariant and gamma is not fixed. Ordinary-source scalar feedback, noncoincident constraints and the physical Newton/MOND dictionary must still be solved in this action.
