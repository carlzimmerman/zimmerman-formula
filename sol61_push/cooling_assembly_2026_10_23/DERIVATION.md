# Cosmological assembly and conditional cooling

Units and action are inherited from ../fragment_radiation_2026_10_22/DERIVATION.md. This calculation is a weak-source diagnostic of that passive action, not a cosmological completion. Dimensionless mass is m=M/M0, length is in ell0, and time in t0. Physical angular frequencies are in Gyr^-1, without an extra 2pi.

## Density clock

For the characteristic radius containing M at mean density Delta times critical,

R_Delta^3 = 2GM/(Delta H^2),
Omega_dyn^2 = GM/R_Delta^3 = Delta H^2/2.

Consequently Delta=200 gives Omega_dyn=10H, independently of M. A prescribed breathing frequency omega=n Omega_dyn propagates in the inherited massive phase channel only when omega > omega_gap = 0.2/t0. Equivalently, for a cloud of mean density rho,

rho > 3 omega_gap^2/(4pi G n^2).

This is a density condition. In the illustrative cosmological clock it is an EARLY epoch condition: z exceeds 11.2338 for n=1 or 6.69765 for n=2. The channel closes for characteristic virial clouds as the universe dilutes. It can remain open for denser substructures at later times. The numbers n=1,2 are assumptions about forcing, not eigenfrequencies obtained from the action. Transient high-frequency spectral tails can radiate even when a dominant frequency is below threshold.

A fundamental frequency n=2 is not the order-epsilon-squared second harmonic generated by a source oscillating at n=1. These are separate assumed source histories in this scan. Nonlinear harmonics and P-wave emission are not computed here.

## Mass-conserving source and phase radiation

At quadratic order W(P)=P^2/2 and chi=1, the quasistatic reciprocal field obeys P=-grad h and (1-Laplacian)h=rho. For a Gaussian of mass m and width R with integral rho d^3x=4pi m,

h_tilde(k)=4pi m exp(-k^2 R^2/2)/(1+k^2).

Our Gaussian width equals the characteristic R200; this is a size convention, not a literal Gaussian mass enclosure definition. For R(t)=R[1+epsilon cos(omega t)] with fixed m, epsilon=.1, the fundamental component of the phase source h^2/2 is

S_omega(x)=epsilon h(x) R partial_R h(x),
S_tilde_omega(q)=(epsilon/2) R partial_R Fourier[h^2](q).

With s=R^2+2u and v=R^2+2w, the Schwinger representation of (1+k^2)^-1 gives

Fourier[h^2](q) = (4pi m)^2/(2pi)^(3/2)
  integral_0^infinity du dw exp(-u-w) (s+v)^(-3/2)
  exp[-q^2 s v/(2(s+v))].

Multiplying this integrand by

-epsilon R^2 [3/(s+v) + q^2(s^2+v^2)/(2(s+v)^2)]

gives S_tilde_omega. The derivative was checked by centered finite differences in log R.

The linear phase equation is

b delta_chi_tt - d Laplacian delta_chi + a delta_chi = S_omega cos(omega t).

Here b=d=.125, a=.005, so q=sqrt(omega^2-.2^2). An outgoing spherical wave has amplitude S_tilde_omega(q)/(4pi d r). Averaging the phase current -(d/4pi) chi_t grad chi over one period and integrating over the sphere gives

P_rad = omega q |S_tilde_omega(q)|^2/(32pi^2 d).

The power vanishes below the monochromatic propagation threshold. It scales as m^4 at FIXED R and frequency. It does not do so along a fixed-density cosmological family because R changes with mass.

Spatial phase cancellation becomes important at qR of order unity. The corresponding dimensional mass scale would be

M_coherence ~ 100 H^2 (c_chi/sqrt(omega_phys^2-omega_gap^2))^3/G.

This is only a coherence estimate. The exact efficiency also includes the reciprocal-field kernel and binding proxy. It varies with epoch, density convention and assumed mode frequency; it is not a universal remnant mass.

## Interpretation of efficiency and validity

We report t_eff = |U_Gaussian,Newton|/P_rad, with |U|=m^2/(2 sqrt(pi) R), converted by t0. This is the time needed to radiate that energy at the prescribed steady amplitude and frequency. It is NOT a collapse solution, a measured remnant formation time, or a calculation of total action binding energy. A real source can damp, accrete, fragment, or require much less than this energy to cross a threshold. The earlier moving-particle runs demonstrate damping of export, not a sustained engine.

The retained weak cases require max|P|<.1 and lambda h_max^2<.1, lambda=100. The latter bounds the static phase shift using positivity of the Yukawa kernel, since delta_chi <= h_max^2/(2a). It does not by itself bound a driven resonant phase amplitude. The quadrature, small amplitude and quasistatic P assumptions limit this calculation. It is not a rigorous bound on the full nonlinear radiation rate. At the largest tested driving frequency eta omega^2 is about .013, with eta=.005, supporting the low-frequency P approximation but not proving the entire nonlinear model valid.

## Supply and population

For comoving number density n(m,t), available cold matter only constrains

integral m n(m,t) dm = F(t) rho_c,0.

It does not determine F, the number moment integral n dm, the spectrum, or spatial bias. For example, monodisperse populations obey N=F rho_c,0/M for every selected M. A formation law additionally needs a collapse criterion, accretion and merger histories, transport and survival; schematic mass-space continuity is partial_t n + partial_m(dot m n) = formation + mergers - disruption, supplemented by spatial transport. None of those kernels follows from a total mass budget.

The same density condition can hold inside a dwarf or a cluster progenitor. Central occupation requires transport and capture, while an extended cluster profile requires survival and orbital dynamics. A radiation clock alone supplies neither.

## Growth diagnostic

The inherited benchmark is an imposed Einstein-de Sitter background, with N=ln a, fb=1/6.364, fc=1-fb and initial growing dust amplitudes delta_b=delta_c=delta_b'=delta_c'=a_i at z_i=1100:

 delta_b''+.5 delta_b'=1.5(fb delta_b+fc delta_c),
 delta_c''+.5 delta_c'=1.5(fb delta_b+fc S delta_c),
 S=1/[1+(k ell0/a)^2].

At a chosen formation epoch we replace S by 1, granting complete conversion to optimally clustering pressureless cores, with no delay, recoil, radiation loss or inertial penalty. The final weighted amplitude is normalized to the exact dust solution. This is deliberately optimistic. The threshold redshifts label possible conversion times; they do not make this EdS diagnostic consistent with the separate LambdaCDM clock. A single covariant cosmology and transfer-function fit remain missing.
