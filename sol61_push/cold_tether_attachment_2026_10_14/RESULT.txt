u for tether mass acceleration, and R=u^2/(a0-u). The identified-carrier
force dictionary gives a_r=-b_c-h+R. If the target is R=b_c, a_r=-h.
For a spherical stationary carrier the moment equation is
  d(rho sigma_r^2)/dr + 2 beta rho sigma_r^2/r = -rho h,
  beta=1-(sigma_theta^2+sigma_phi^2)/(2 sigma_r^2).
Thus at rest is impossible when h>0, but positive velocity stress need not
be. For the isotropic case with specified outer pressure,
  p(r)=p(r_out)+integral_r^r_out rho(s) h(s) ds.
Positive pressure is only a moment feasibility condition; it does not alone
prove a nonnegative stationary distribution. That is why the next step
constructs and checks distributions rather than stopping at Jeans balance.
