Nonlinear vacuum continuation, 2026-10-12
Finite numerical evidence; self-reviewed. Original theory goal remains open.

The linear-vacuum null packet is NOT stationary under the full constitutive
force in the tested cases. Nonlinear transport exports most of its energy
from the central region for two of the three tested amplitudes by t=300.
This narrows the earlier linear-vacuum obstruction: linear neutrality did
not establish nonlinear trapping, and nonlinear trapping is not observed
for these two cases on this time interval.

Fine-grid results (N=1024, dt=0.075, dimensionless units):
 Initial peak |grad Phi|/a0    central energy / initial total at t=300
 0.01                         0.506639
 0.03                         0.064121
 0.10                         0.022877

All three gradient profiles change substantially (relative L2 differences
1.27-1.56). Dropping the nonlinear force leaves the same prepared null
packet exactly stationary in the linear control. The frozen <10% central
energy gate passes at 0.03 and 0.10, fails at 0.01 at this end time.
Failure of that finite-time gate is not permanent trapping.

Validation
----------
Two grids/timesteps: (512,0.15) and (1024,0.075). Final central fractions
differ by at most 0.000735, below the frozen 0.03 tolerance. Maximum sampled
energy drift on the fine grid is 0.00018865 relative (0.018865%), below
the 0.2% frozen threshold. Drift decreases on refinement. The run's
manifest validates with input/output hashes; raw samples and controls are
in nonlinear_run/results.json. No random sampling or data fitting.

Equations and boundaries
-----------------------
 C=a0=Z=I=1; q=D Phi, with the spectral derivative D.
 Phi_tt=D(q-P), P_tt=q-P-sign(P)b(|P|).
 Here p=g-B(g), and b(p) is the inverse CFG596 table's baryonic field.
 Energy density = (Phi_t^2+P_t^2+(q-P)^2)/2 + int_0^|P| b(s) ds.
The potential integral is evaluated exactly for the piecewise-linear inverse
interpolant used in the force, so force and monitored energy are consistent.
The small interval below the first tabulated p is joined linearly to zero;
this is a disclosed finite-table approximation near the vacuum.

Initial Phi is a width-8 Gaussian scaled to the declared peak gradient,
P=q, both velocities zero. Periodic length 512; central window |x|<24.
There is no damping, absorbing boundary, mass source or gradient regulator.
Energy leaving the central window remains elsewhere in the periodic box;
recurrence is possible. No strict monotonic decay or infinite-time
attractor is claimed. The Nyquist derivative is zero to preserve real
fields. Two-resolution agreement is a bounded check, not proof of spectral
convergence, aliasing control at arbitrary times or PDE well-posedness.

Physical meaning
----------------
The effective field has a conservative energy-transport mechanism even
around vacuum at finite amplitude: nonlinear waves. That is stronger than
the previous nonzero-background linear propagation result. It does not
identify microscopic polarization degrees of freedom, assign physical
values to Z/I, transport conserved cold monopole mass, or demonstrate
settling around a baryonic source. No formation time in Gyr is available.
The model still encodes the desired kernel through its potential rather
than deriving the kernel or kappa=1/2. It has no relativistic lensing
completion. The next discriminating physical calculation is a sourced,
energy-accounted response and its coupling to the existing cold carrier;
this vacuum packet result alone cannot fill either obligation.
