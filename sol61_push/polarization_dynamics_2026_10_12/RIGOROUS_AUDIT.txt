2. Exact structural external-field calculation
----------------------------------------------
Eliminating the static polarization from the constructed action gives
 div[mu(g) grad Phi]=C rho_b, mu(g)=B(g)/g, C=4*pi*G,
 B the inverse prescribed spherical force law. For a uniform external
gradient q_e of magnitude g_e, write grad Phi=q_e+grad phi, where the
internal perturbation is small. Linearising gives

 div[K_e grad phi]=C rho_b,
 K_e=mu_e(I-nn)+B'_e nn, n=q_e/g_e.                         (1)

This follows by differentiating mu(|q|)q:
 d(mu q)/dq=mu I+(B'-mu)nn. Both eigenvalues are positive on the branch.
For the compact-source far-field monopole of mass m,

 phi=-Gm/[sqrt(det K_e)*sqrt(x^T K_e^-1 x)].                (2)

One can verify (2) by the coordinate change y=K_e^-1/2 x in the
constant-coefficient Poisson equation, with the transformed delta-source
Jacobian sqrt(det K_e). Along the external-field axis and an equatorial
ray at radius r this gives internal acceleration magnitudes

 g_parallel=(Gm/r^2)/mu_e,
 g_equatorial=(Gm/r^2)/sqrt(mu_e B'_e).                    (3)

These scale linearly with the weak internal Newtonian field, while the
isolated deep relation scales as its square root. The mismatch persists
as that internal field goes to zero: a structural external-field effect.
Equations (1)-(3) describe the external-dominated far field, not the
nonlinear source core. They are not a numerical full-3D galaxy solution.

4. Why an assembly-time adjustment cannot cure this
--------------------------------------------------
The response tensor K_e depends only on the stationary constitutive action,
not on the time used to prepare it. If exact independence from every
uniform external field and orientation is required within this local,
isotropic scalar-field class, K_e must be one constant isotropic tensor.
Its eigenvalues then require B'(g)=B(g)/g=k constant, hence B(g)=k*g.
That is a linear force law, not the prescribed nonlinear relation.
This restricted no-go argument applies to local static energy e(|grad Phi|)
and any locally eliminated auxiliaries producing that same equation. It
does not cover nonlocal selection, history-dependent nonequilibrium states,
additional metric fields, or relaxed empirical requirements. It does not
prove that any of those alternatives works.
