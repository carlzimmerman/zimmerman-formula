Why the weak-field limitation is structural
-----------------------------------------
The mode equation in these units is
 omega^4-(k^2+a)omega^2+k^2(a-1)=0.
At long wavelength, omega_slow^2=B'(g) k^2+O(k^4).
The measured dimensionless long-wave speeds are 0.04469, 0.14037,
0.41687 and 0.88120 for the four backgrounds, respectively.

With general positive Z,I and C, the long-wave speed squared is
 v_slow^2=B'(g)/(C Z). I affects the finite-frequency branch but cancels
from this leading low-frequency speed. On the deep branch B~g^2/a0,
 v_slow^2~2g/(C Z a0).
Thus the long-wave crossing-time estimate for a fixed region grows like
 g^(-1/2) for fixed Z. This is an asymptotic wave timescale, not a measured
galaxy settling time or a rigorous bound on every packet.

At g=0, a=1 and H(k)*(1,ik)=0. A nonzero perturbation on this null branch
remains stationary in the LINEARISED system at every k. Cubic and higher
terms can alter its nonlinear evolution; this calculation does not prove
nonlinear trapping or nonlinear instability. It does prove that a claim of
strict linear attraction at vacuum would be false for this model.
