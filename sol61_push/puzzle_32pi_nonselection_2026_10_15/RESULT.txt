A CONDITIONAL NON-SELECTION THEOREM FOR THE PRESENT RESPONSE MODEL
2026-10-15

Question: Do the current response, positive-energy, and characteristic-speed
conditions force Lambda/a0^2=32pi, or equivalently a0^4 Omega=3/128 on S4?
Answer: No. Within the declared fixed-background model these conditions
leave the ratio arbitrary. This is a logical obstruction, not a numerical
failure to find the right parameters.

Assumptions and scope
1. The response action contains an independent additive vacuum energy E0.
2. The response coefficients and reference geometry are held fixed while E0
   varies. The tests are precisely the local response and quadratic tests
   already derived, not the full coupled Einstein-matter field equations.
3. To discuss the cosmological ratio, use the conditional identification
   Lambda=E0/M_Pl^2 and keep the derived positive a0 fixed.
Set D=M_Pl^2 a0^2>0. Then R=Lambda/a0^2=E0/D.

Proof
All first and second field variations of the constant -E0 vanish on the
fixed reference geometry. Thus the static current, K, C, G, and the
characteristic bound are independent of E0. For any target t>0, choose
E0=tD. Then R=t, while every one of those tests has the same outcome.
Consequently those tests cannot single out any positive target ratio.

Concrete counterexample to selection by these tests
Use A=rho=100, b=m=1, cN=1/10 and reference gradient v=1, as in the parent
calculation. The all-direction criterion is p(1)=8905>0, so this example
passes that criterion strictly. Keep these response parameters, q, and
M_Pl fixed, and choose either
 E0_1=32pi D, giving R1=32pi,
 E0_2=64pi D, giving R2=64pi.
Both choices pass exactly the same declared local tests. If interpreted as
positive vacuum energies, both give positive Lambda under assumption 3.
This does not construct two complete self-consistent cosmological solutions:
the elastic medium's gravitational stress and background matching have not
been solved. The counterexample concerns what follows from our actual tests.

Round-sphere conversion (conditional geometry, not a new dynamical law)
For a round Euclidean S4 with Lambda>0, Lambda^2 Omega=24pi^2, hence
 a0^4 Omega=24pi^2/R^2.
The two choices above yield respectively 3/128 and 3/512. The same local
response tests therefore do not distinguish even these two dimensionless
geometric values when combined with the stated conditional dictionary.

What a missing selector must accomplish
An additional equation must remove the E0 freedom and relate it to the
response sector. Algebraically, obtaining the desired ratio requires
 E0 = 32pi M_Pl^2 a0^2
    = 128pi q^6 m^4 M_Pl^6/b^2
using the parent a0=2q^3 m^2 M_Pl^2/b.
This equality is the missing requirement, not a derived result. Imposing it
as a definition, fitting it to observations, or demanding it as a boundary
condition would not constitute an independent prediction.
An actual proposed selector must also explain its robustness under allowed
vacuum-energy shifts and solve the relevant gravitational background.
It is possible for such extra dynamics to change the present conclusion;
no universal impossibility theorem for physics is claimed.

Formal verification boundary
Nonselection.lean proves five statements using Mathlib:
- Any positive target ratio has a positive energy realizing it.
- A test independent of energy admits distinct positive ratios whenever it
  accepts a parameter choice. Independence is explicit in the theorem type.
- The explicit 32pi and 64pi ratio witnesses are different.
- The corresponding rational S4 expressions are 3/128 and 3/512.
- The chosen reference gradient satisfies the polynomial criterion.
The action-to-diagnostic derivation and S4 geometric identity are not
formalized here. The geometric theorem checks the algebra after that identity
is assumed. No claim of formalizing the full physical theory is made.
