## What we were missing in the review

1. **The switch must gravitate.** Turning its MOND multiplier off does not turn
   off its kinetic/potential energy and trigger pressure. The new homogeneous
   derivation exposes this omitted arrow in the exact-growth claim.
2. **Trigger health is a subsystem result.** The full switch adds f'' curvature
   and f' constitutive mixing. A positive-root certificate for the free
   trigger does not certify the assembled transition operator.
3. **A finite vacuum integral can still have a free coefficient.** The cutoff
   root fits the target; a compact monotone deformation preserves the finite
   galaxy law and changes the ratio. A principle selecting the whole response
   function is required.
4. **The data programme needs a shared-error model.** With the stated p44 floor,
   the total/vacuum comparison needs about 445 galaxies at 2 sigma, rather than
   the 41-galaxy zero-floor estimate. Closer coefficients cannot be separated
   merely by expanding that sample.
5. **Some documentary caveats need narrowing.** Correa's analytic EPS model
   is not N-body calibrated; its formulas match the implementation's shape.
   Their use for a different cold component still needs a spectrum/collapse
   dictionary. Conversely, historical NOT RUN gates are now partly addressed
   by CFG320/329; retain those gains at their actual scope.

## Work order and stopping rules

**First priority: a complete switch/cosmology discriminator.** For any retained
dynamical switch, keep the new FRW stress, solve the background and derive the
coupled perturbation matrix. Test that candidate against bound-system response
and its transition before expensive source or cosmology runs. The existing
theta_b class has failed fidelity/reach; rerunning its same optimum is not a
new attempt. A revised mechanism must explicitly change that obstruction.
Success is a common parameter domain satisfying all three sectors, not a
FRW-only or interior-only pass. Failure closes the specified construction only.

**Second priority: source and cold-history closure.** Carry independent
formation/infall/retention inputs into one conserved baryon/cold evolution.
Authenticate units and collapse/source transfer before relying on narrow UFD
windows. Make the simultaneous test on spirals, UFDs and lensing mandatory:
the earlier broad cold-share fix already failed this test. A label or
per-population fitted mass is a diagnostic, not the desired dynamical result.

**Parallel scientific question, executed sequentially here: coefficient
selection.** For any proposed UV or self-tuning selector, vary every surviving
physical coupling and additive vacuum offset while holding the defining
principle fixed. Compute C across that family before choosing a target root.
If C varies, save the counterfamily and retain the strongest partial result.
Only if the freedom disappears should we pursue a full 32pi proof/audit.

**Data priority:** independent distances for gas-rich discs, object-level
history/tracer inputs for dwarfs/ellipticals and adequate isolated-lensing
power. Derive their required precision from the covariance model. Existing
large-data requests remain other sessions' work; this checkpoint launches no
downloads or external jobs.

**Final integration:** reconstruct one complete dependency chain and audit
every external leaf, source map, boundary, stability regime and held-out
prediction. A discovery in one lane is not grounds to mark the other closed.
New source data, a different admissible action or a constraint that removes a
recorded counterfamily are concrete reasons to reopen a route. Additional
equivalent identities and target-fitted scans are not.
