## 1. A reciprocal maximum is constructible, with physical costs

[Cold action report](cold_component/new_doors_2026_10_05/reciprocal_max/REPORT.md).

For spherical baryon and cold Newtonian fluxes b,d>=0, define p(b)=sqrt(b^2+a0 b)-b and

H(b,d)=(b+d)^2/2 + integral_0^b [p(s)-d]_+ ds.

The baryon response is exactly b+max(d,p(b)). In the MOND-active branch the reciprocal cold response is d+b_t(d), with b_t=d^2/(a0-2d). This closes the action-integrability gap in a static two-potential spherical construction. It does not preserve the old cold force: as d tends to zero at fixed b, the cold tracer force vanishes. Reciprocal max gravity therefore cannot retain ordinary baryon capture of dilute cold tracers in that branch.

The norm-based full vector completion is convex but changes nonspherical Newtonian response. The tested Newton-vector alternative has negative transverse curvature. More generally, opposite-flux midpoint convexity proves that any finite globally convex energy with the exact aligned maximum on an unrestricted, unbounded flux domain cannot retain the full Newton OFF vector law; arbitrary finite boundary offsets do not remove this contradiction. A kappa>1 cold self-coupling makes the norm completion strongly convex while changing cold self-gravity. These are explicit prices, not interchangeable versions of an unchanged theory.

For compact sources the cold radial orbital stiffness has numerator

kappa d - d^2(a0+2d)/(a0-2d)^2.

It becomes negative for some baryon-dominated source ratios even when the static energy is convex. A sufficient ratio window is eta=d/b >= [1+sqrt(1+16 kappa)]/(2 kappa); eta=5.36 passes at kappa=1. Chosen finite positive coincident Plummer profiles admit stable radial tracer and shell support in the tested range. Neither selected support nor elliptic uniqueness proves formation or nonradial stability.

**Next decisive test:** evolve a specified cold distribution and baryon accretion through the active branch, retaining both reciprocal forces. Test whether the missing dilute-tracer capture rules out the candidate before building a covariant completion. Do not infer halo formation from the existence of circular shells.

## 4. An analytic control exposes the sensitivity and degeneracy

[Analytic pressure kernel](recombination_and_structure_growth/new_doors_2026_10_05/analytic_pressure_kernel/REPORT.md).

In the EdS Born limit with delta_c=A(k)a, wave pressure Q=hbar^2/(4m^2) gives

U(a)-U(a_d)=2Q k^4 A(sqrt(a)-sqrt(a_d))/H_m.

A cooled gas c_b^2=v0^2/a^2 has the same time kernel with coefficient -v0^2 k^2. A two-epoch difference removes initial U; normalization by A removes primordial amplitude; several k values separate the restricted wave slope from the gas constant. A single k cannot do so even with many epochs.

At the declared illustrative mass 2e-20 eV/c^2 and a_d=.02, the Born frequency ratio is 8.0e-15 at k=.1/Mpc, .0080 at k=100/Mpc, and 80 at k=1000/Mpc. The last point is outside the approximation. The small-scale leverage and large-scale sensitivity ceiling are therefore quantitative, not a qualitative claim that any wave pressure must be detectable in recombination.
