# What this candidate mechanism actually is

This packet tests a conditional coherent cold field. It does not derive that field from the literal Khronon/AeST action or establish a particle/nonparticle ontology. No cross-model transfer of cosmological or cluster results is justified by these computations.

For this candidate, the physical equations are

    i eta d_t psi = -(eta^2/2) Laplacian psi + Phi psi
    Laplacian Phi = 4 pi G |psi|^2
    rho = |psi|^2; v = eta grad arg(psi)
    d_t rho + div(rho v) = 0
    (d_t + v.grad)v = -grad Phi + (eta^2/2)grad(Laplacian sqrt(rho)/sqrt(rho)).

The last fluid equation holds away from nodes/vortices. The regular wave equation handles those regions. Self-gravity amplifies density; gradient stress opposes arbitrarily narrow concentration. Nonlinear time-dependent collapse can scatter coherent waves into an extended envelope and outgoing modes without binary collisions. Core binding energy pays for exported energy. These waves carry the same field's mass and angular momentum; they are not a second freely available cooling bath.

    M = integral rho
    E = (eta^2/2) integral |grad psi|^2 + (1/2) integral rho Phi
    J = integral psi* [-i eta x cross grad] psi.

Phi is sourced once. Half the self-interaction belongs in the global energy. Auxiliary density partitions are not independent waves, and their gradient energies cannot be summed without their interface correction. Newtonian Phi is elliptic and supplies no independent gravitational-wave reservoir.

Use the canonical local energy density and physical surface current together:

    e_can = eta^2 |grad psi|^2/2 + rho Phi + |grad Phi|^2/(8 pi G)
    F_E = -eta^2 Re(psi_t grad psi*) - Phi_t grad Phi/(4 pi G)
    F_J through sphere = x cross [eta^2 Re(grad psi* d_r psi)
                                 + grad Phi d_r Phi/(4 pi G)].

For a finite ball, E_can = K_ball + half integral_ball rho Phi + surface integral Phi d_r Phi/(8 pi G). Omitting that boundary term corrupts a cooling budget. A finite outward mass pulse is not enough: the wave may be bound, carry negative energy, or import spin through another part of the surface. Integrate signed physical currents, and compare changes in the corresponding inventories at each radius. Negative Yoshida time stages are numerical stages, not physical ejecta.

At asymptotically negligible Phi, a free outgoing mode proportional to exp(i k r + i m phi - i omega t) has omega=eta k^2/2 and

    F_E / F_M = eta omega; F_Jz / F_M = eta m;
    F_E / F_Jz = omega/m (m nonzero).

This ties cooling and spin loss to actual ejected cold mass. Our free l=2,m=2 Gaussian control checks the Jz/mass flux relation to roundoff and energy current against its analytic solution. It does not prove collapse efficiently populates the needed escaping modes.
