## The physical distinction now fixed

The [source and force dictionary](SOURCE_FORCE_DICTIONARY.md) separates actual rest mass, gauge charge, constitutive frame and polarization. In spherical leading order,

\[
 g_c={G\over r^2}\sum_aM_a,\qquad
 u_Q={G\over r^2}\sum_a{q_a/m_a\over\zeta}M_a,
 \qquad\zeta=\sqrt{4\pi G}.
\]

Gravity responds to the first rest-mass sum, in addition to field/stress contributions in the full metric. The electric field responds to the charge-weighted sum. They agree structurally only for the specified common critical charge-to-mass ratio and species membership. A neutral population, opposite-charge stock or light equal-charge remnant changes that identification. The actual illustrative portal assigns a remnant ratio 939.06542194 times the original value.

This dictionary describes an explicit conditional gauge candidate. It does not establish that the canonical nonlocal source/host prescription or a baryon-only nonspherical flux is the density of that candidate.
