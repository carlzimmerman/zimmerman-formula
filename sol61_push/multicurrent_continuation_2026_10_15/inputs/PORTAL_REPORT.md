**The cleanest charge-complete reaction is**

\[
 \boxed{\chi+\overline S\longrightarrow n+\gamma.}
\]

A dark fermion chi carries the transferable baryon number. An oppositely
dark-charged scalar antiparticle Sbar cancels its dark charge. The final neutron

The same interaction also permits photon-triggered conversion and nuclear

This is baryon-number **transfer**. It cannot create a net baryon excess from

curvature scalar or fluid action established a hidden baryon-number reservoir.

## 1. Conservation decides the necessary new ingredients

The retained [reciprocal-medium construction](../reciprocal_tether_medium_2026_10_14/REPORT.md)

Introduce these **new, assumed microscopic degrees of freedom**:

| Particle | Spin | Ordinary electric charge | Dark U(1) charge | Generalized baryon number |

| Complex scalar S | 0 | 0 | +1 | 0 |
| Antiparticle Sbar | 0 | 0 | -1 | 0 |

The actual gauge coupling multiplies the dark charge units. It may be chosen

a chiral gauge anomaly. Baryon number here is a global charge conserved by
the displayed perturbative portal, not a claim that Standard Model baryon
number is nonanomalous at electroweak energies.

The low-energy neutron portal is

\[
 \boxed{\mathcal L_{\rm tr}=yS^\dagger\bar nP_L\chi+\mathrm{h.c.}}
 \qquad P_L=(1-\gamma_5)/2.

The vertex has dark charge -1+1=0 and baryon number -1+1=0. Fermion parity
and electric charge are respected. It transfers chi's baryon number to a

construction does not resolve genuine baryogenesis from zero initial total B.

Neutron/dark-sector portals and colored-mediator realizations have substantial

## 2. A Standard Model gauge invariant parent exists

The neutron operator is a hadronic description, not an elementary quark-level

\[

A renormalizable tree parent uses a scalar Phi in (bar-3,1,+1/3), B=2/3,

\[
 \lambda_q\epsilon^{abc}\Phi_a(u_b^cd_c^c)
 +\lambda_N\Phi^{\dagger a}(N_Ld_a^c)
 +y_D S^\dagger(\chi_LN_R^c)

Include ordinary positive scalar masses and Dirac masses. Each displayed

For examples only take beta_had=0.0144 GeV³, the representative lattice

## 3. A mass ordering that preserves a cold population in vacuum

Use hbar=c=1 in particle formulas and +--- for the explicit spin calculation;
this is a convention change from the gravity calculation, not a change of
physics. [PDG2025 constants](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2025/reviews/rpp2025-rev-phys-constants.pdf)
supply mn=939.56542194, mp=938.27208943 and me=0.51099895069 MeV.
Choose the **illustrative**, unmeasured dark masses

\[
 m_\chi=m_n-0.5\ \mathrm{MeV},\qquad m_S=1\ \mathrm{MeV}.
\]

Then all of these free, zero-temperature channels are kinematically closed:

* chi -> n+S, because mchi<mn+mS;
* chi -> p+electron+antineutrino+S, because mchi<mp+me+mS;
* chi -> hydrogen+antineutrino+S, even allowing the atomic binding energy;
* n -> chi+Sbar, because mn<mchi+mS.

This is vacuum kinematic stability against these baryon-transfer channels,
not a statement of nuclear, thermal or full-model stability. In particular,
nuclear binding can reopen conversion, as shown below. A spontaneous-decay
candidate with mchi>mn+mS is also possible, but would decay everywhere unless
its coupling is tiny or another physical gate is supplied.

## 4. Opposite-charge recombination: both dark particles disappear

If an Sbar reservoir is physically available, chi+Sbar can turn into a neutron
and an ordinary photon. Total initial dark charge is zero and B=1. The same
charges hold in the final state, with no remaining charged dark particle.
At negligible incoming relative velocity W=mchi+mS, two-body kinematics gives

\[
 E_\gamma=\frac{W^2-m_n^2}{2W}=0.4998670305\ \mathrm{MeV},\qquad
 T_n=0.1329695\ \mathrm{keV}.
\]

The photon removes almost all the 0.5-MeV released rest energy. Nearly all
the initial rest mass becomes neutron rest mass. This is not energy generated
from nothing: chi and Sbar are consumed. The neutron can subsequently be
captured by a nucleus or undergo n -> p+electron+antineutrino. Formation of
neutral hydrogen would additionally involve electron capture and cooling.

An explicit nonzero amplitude is supplied by the portal and the measured
neutron magnetic moment. Write
L_Pauli=-(mu_n/2) nbar sigma^{mu nu} F_{mu nu} n, with
mu_n=-1.91304273 e/(2mp) in natural units, using the
[PDG neutron magnetic moment](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2025/listings/rpp2025-list-n.pdf).
For P=pchi+pSbar and an outgoing real photon k,

\[
 \mathcal M=y\mu_n\bar u_n
 \sigma^{\mu\nu}\epsilon^*_\mu k_\nu
 \frac{\slashed P+m_n}{P^2-m_n^2}P_Lu_\chi .
\]

Replacing epsilon by k gives zero, so the chosen Pauli diagram is photon-gauge
invariant. With the chi spin averaged and final spins/polarizations summed,

\[
 \sigma v_{\rm rel}\big|_{v\to0}=
 \frac{E_\gamma}{16\pi Wm_\chi m_S}
 \overline{|\mathcal M|^2}.
\]

For this chosen leading low-energy EFT and the example masses,

\[
 \sigma v_{\rm rel}/|y|^2=2.2114862\times10^{-20}
                      \ \mathrm{cm^3\,s^{-1}}.
\]

There is no rate without specifying the interaction and partner density:
R=nchi nSbar <sigma v>. For 1-TeV heavy mediators and unit heavy vertices,
y=1.44e-11 and sigma v=4.58574e-42 cm³/s. This is extremely slow at dilute
densities. It is a theoretical channel, not a practical manufacturing method.
Neutron form factors, additional EFT contact terms, radiative corrections and
medium effects are not included in this leading calculation.

The opposite reservoir is an indispensable input. We did not derive its
production, abundance, capture or delivery into a chi population. If it is
co-located with the original charged medium it can screen the field needed
for tethering; separate supply and subsequent charge/current evolution must
be solved rather than assumed. This reaction could deplete a tether, but no
joint halo conversion history has been calculated.

## 5. A photon can activate conversion without an opposite reservoir

The same vertex permits chi+gamma -> n+S. The emitted S retains the initial
dark charge; it is not discarded from the mass, charge or energy budget.
For chi initially at rest, the exact photon threshold is

\[
 E_{\gamma,\rm th}=\frac{(m_n+m_S)^2-m_\chi^2}{2m_\chi}
                  =1.5011979996\ \mathrm{MeV}.
\]

The incoming photon now supplies energy instead of carrying it away. Using
the same Pauli vertex, the internal neutron momentum is **Q=pn-k**. It is
not pchi+k, since the scalar is emitted at the portal vertex. The amplitude is

\[
 \mathcal M=y\mu_n\bar u_n\sigma^{\mu\nu}\epsilon_\mu k_\nu
       \frac{\slashed Q+m_n}{Q^2-m_n^2}P_Lu_\chi .
\]

After averaging initial chi spin and photon polarizations,

\[
 \frac{d\sigma}{d\cos\theta}
 =\frac{p_f}{32\pi s p_i}\overline{|\mathcal M|^2}.
\]

At photon energy 3.002396 MeV the result is
sigma/|y|²=5.60534e-31 cm². With y=1.44e-11 this is 1.16232e-52 cm².
An ideal single-pass independent-particle fluence for half conversion,
ln(2)/sigma, would be 5.96e51 photons/cm². This estimate omits competing
absorption and therefore already makes the small rate explicit. It is not
an equipment recommendation. Crossing and the Hermitian conjugate also
allow n+S -> chi+gamma; inverse reactions must enter a full kinetic model.

## 6. Nuclear binding supplies a material trigger

For the very same stable-vacuum masses, a nucleus can receive chi's baryon
number as a bound neutron:

\[
 \chi+(A,Z)\longrightarrow(A+1,Z)+S.
\]

If Sn is the neutron separation energy in the final nucleus, the available
rest energy is exactly

\[
 Q_{\rm capture}=S_n-(m_n+m_S-m_\chi)=S_n-1.5\ \mathrm{MeV}.
\]

For a free proton target, the final nucleus can be a deuteron:

\[
 \boxed{\chi+p\longrightarrow d+S.}
\]

Using md=1875.612945 MeV from the same constants table gives
Bd=2.22456637 MeV and Qcapture=0.72456637 MeV. Exact final kinematics
gives scalar momentum 1.40439167 MeV, deuteron recoil 0.525779 keV and
S kinetic energy 0.72404059 MeV. All electric charge, dark charge, baryon
number and four-momentum can be conserved. The deuteron now contains one
additional ordinary baryon; it is not a proton created from nothing.

Spin does not forbid the reaction: two spin-1/2 incoming particles have a
triplet component, which can feed the spin-1 deuteron plus scalar. In a
defined zero-range S-wave bound-state approximation,

\[
 \widetilde\psi_d(q)=\frac{\sqrt{8\pi\gamma_d}}{q^2+\gamma_d^2},
 \quad\gamma_d=\sqrt{2\mu_{np}B_d}=45.7021\ \mathrm{MeV},
 \quad\int\frac{d^3q}{(2\pi)^3}|\widetilde\psi_d|^2=1.
\]

Its overlap with a plane-wave neutron source is nonzero. This checks an
allowed overlap in that approximation; it is not a matched nuclear capture
rate. A real calculation requires <d|nbar|p>, effective-range/two-body-current
corrections and the chosen portal coefficient. We have not replaced those
inputs by an arbitrary fitted cross section.

Related baryonic dark-matter capture is already studied in
[Fornal–Grinstein–Zhao, Phys. Lett. B811 (2020) 135869, §§2–3](https://scoap3-prod-backend.s3.cern.ch/media/files/57567/10.1016/j.physletb.2020.135869_a.pdf).
Their direct neutron-mixing model emits photons and has no conserved dark
charge requiring our S. Its capture cross sections and allowed mass window
cannot simply be reused for this charge-preserving reaction. It supplies
precedent for the role of binding, not a rate or exclusion certificate here.

## 7. Why a curvature switch is an extra problem

A curvature-dependent mass term is possible as a new covariant operator,
for example -[mS0²+kappa G/LambdaC²]|S|², where G is the Gauss–Bonnet
invariant. It would change thresholds if its induced mass shift were large
enough, with the metric varied and its energy exchange included. It does not
by itself create the baryon-transfer portal.

Two pitfalls are quantitatively exposed by the calculation. First, shutting
chi -> nS does not shut chi -> S+p+electron+antineutrino. For the separate
spontaneous-decay witness mchi=mn+100 MeV+1 keV, the S masses needed to close
the channels are 100.001 MeV versus 100.78333356 MeV for the free continuum.
Bound hydrogen raises the latter S-mass threshold by another 13.6 eV in the
assigned atomic-binding approximation. Nuclear targets change it further.
The original run key named complete_weak_threshold_GeV denotes the continuum
only; the follow-through preserves that record and corrects its scope.

Second, ordinary stellar black-hole curvature is tiny in GeV units. At a
10-solar-mass Schwarzschild horizon G=12/rh^4=2.39e-80 GeV^4. Closing that
example's continuum channel by shifting a 100-MeV S requires delta mS²
approximately 1.57e-4 GeV². With kappa=1 the coefficient would need
LambdaC approximately 1.23e-38 GeV, far below the curvature energy
G^(1/4)=1.24e-20 GeV. It cannot be interpreted as a controlled ordinary
weak-coupling curvature expansion with that cutoff. This excludes that
simple order-one-coefficient gate interpretation, not every possible light
order parameter, nonperturbative gate or curvature theory.

The conversion channels above need no curvature switch. Their direct gates
are masses, collision partners, incoming energy and nuclear binding.
