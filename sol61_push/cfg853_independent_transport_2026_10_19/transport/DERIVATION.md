## 1. The result actually established

**Conditional theorem (finite-stock virial/export bound).** Let a finite spherical
cold cloud have phase-space kinetic energy K, squared mass moment
I=integral r^2 dM, and the exact effective energy of ZAR-K1 / CFG848:

    E = K + U_N + U_C + U_b,
    U_N = -(G/2) integral_0^infinity [M_f^2+2 M_t M_f]/r^2 dr,
    U_C = (G^2/(3 a0)) integral_0^infinity M_t^3/r^4 dr >= 0,
    U_b = integral Phi_b(r) d(M_t+M_f),
    Phi_b = -G M_b/(r+a), a >= 0.

Assume finite integrals, valid virial integrations/differentiations, a radial
reciprocal evolution generated by this functional, and no extra energy stores.
Let B be the outward surface stress contribution, with the convention
B=integral_boundary x dot Pi n dS (B>=0 for an ordinary spherical confining
pressure). For an isolated complete cloud B=0. Suppose at the instant of interest
the free mass inside R_c is at least m. Then

    I'' + 2 B >= 4 E + G m^2/R_c.                         (1)

In particular a stationary or virial-time-averaged finite isolated endpoint has

    E <= -G m^2/(4 R_c).                                 (2)

Here an average requires a bounded moment so that the averaged I'' vanishes,
and m must be an instantaneous lower bound over the averaging interval (or use
the averaged m(t)^2/R_c). A merely stationary density in a finite reflecting
box is not the isolated case.

If a whole initial cloud has energy E_i and produces an isolated virialized
bound remnant plus a positive-energy outgoing receiver, with no external work,
then its exported mechanical energy obeys

    E_out >= E_i + G m^2/(4 R_c).                         (3)

Use max(0,right side) when additionally E_out>=0. This is a necessary endpoint
bound, not a transport rate or a sufficient construction. If some free mass
has been renamed tethered, a charge/interaction receiver and its energy must
be included before applying the theorem. The theorem bounds FREE core mass;
it does not automatically bound a tethered core or total mass.

### Proof

Under a uniform spatial dilation r -> lambda r, U_N has degree -1 and U_C
degree -3. The exact scalar virial identity is

    I''/2 = 2K + U_N + 3U_C + W_b - B,
    W_b = -integral r Phi_b'(r) dM.

Eliminating K gives

    E = I''/4 + B/2 + U_N/2 - U_C/2 + U_b - W_b/2.

For Hernquist/point baryons,

    U_b-W_b/2 = -G M_b integral (r+2a)/[2(r+a)^2] dM <= 0.

Also U_N <= U_ff, because its tether/free cross term is negative. Since
M_f(r)>=m for every r>=R_c,

    U_ff = -(G/2) integral_0^infinity M_f(r)^2/r^2 dr
          <= -(G/2) integral_R_c^infinity m^2/r^2 dr
          = -G m^2/(2R_c).

Discarding only nonpositive terms proves (1); (2) sets I''=B=0. Apply (2) to
E_remnant=E_i-E_out for (3). Equalities are limiting bounds: a thin free shell,
no baryonic contribution, no tethered material, no cubic term. A usual smooth
core is more tightly bound, so the inequalities are intentionally permissive.

For a pure free Newtonian cloud these identities reduce to the ordinary
Lagrange-Jacobi identity. The mass-concentration estimate proved here retains
the stated spherical hypothesis.

## 2. An angular receiver bound independent of entropy maximization

Let point carriers with no uncounted intrinsic spin form a final stationary
spherical cloud with total gravitating cold mass <=M
and fixed baryon mass M_b. In the effective action the zero-at-infinity label
potentials satisfy Phi_t>=Phi_f>=-G(M+M_b)/r. If every envelope particle has
negative specific orbital energy in its own stationary label potential, and
the envelope of mass M_e is contained inside R_out, then

    |J_e| <= M_e sqrt[2G(M+M_b) R_out].                  (6)

Indeed v^2<-2Phi_i<=2G(M+M_b)/r, hence |x cross v|<=sqrt[2G(M+M_b)r]; integrate
and use the triangle inequality. This conditional bound does NOT assume that
negative individual orbital energies imply negative total envelope energy.

If total angular momentum is conserved and the core is required to obey
|J_c|<=J_c,max, then with DeltaJ=max(0,|J_i|-J_c,max),

    R_out >= DeltaJ^2/[2G(M+M_b) M_e^2].                (7)

With M_e<=(1-q)M and a nearly zero-spin core this gives, for the uniform initial
cloud spin convention lambda=J_i sqrt(|E_i|)/(G M^(5/2)),

    R_out/R_i >= (5/3) [lambda/(1-q)]^2 M/(M+M_b).       (8)

At q=.9885403 and M_b negligible, lambda=.03 requires R_out>=11.422 R_i;
lambda=.05 requires >=31.728 R_i. A tiny bound outer stock can carry J by
moving very far out. Low total initial spin is another escape. External tidal
torques or a radiation receiver change the budget. None is produced by merely
choosing a rotational Lagrange multiplier in an entropy functional.

If DeltaJ=0 there is no angular lower bound on R_out. If M_e=0 and DeltaJ>0,
this receiver is impossible; division by M_e in (7)-(8) is then prohibited.
If both vanish, angular conservation is already satisfied. Energy and stock
constraints still apply even when the angular inequality is vacuous.

## 3. Joint energy/J export by the permitted small ejecta

A constructive receiver state exists in the ordinary gravitational budget:
a small positive-energy tail can carry the required J at a sufficient lever arm.
Here is an exact necessary test in a clearly narrower asymptotic receiver model.

Assume an outgoing free-particle receiver of total mass M_e>0 crosses a common
sphere R_L enclosing a STATIC, nonrecoiling spherical remnant M_c>0. All J is
measured about that fixed inertial origin. Assume negligible receiver
self-gravity, no exterior baryons, and no subsequent interactions. Let Q>=0 be the
total kinetic energy at infinity. At the crossing sphere,

    K_L = Q + G M_c M_e/R_L,
    |J_e|^2 <= 2 M_e R_L^2 K_L,

where the second line is Cauchy-Schwarz. Thus carrying DeltaJ requires

    Q >= DeltaJ^2/(2 M_e R_L^2) - G M_c M_e/R_L,         (9)

or

    R_L >= [sqrt((G M_c M_e^2)^2+2 M_e Q DeltaJ^2)
             -G M_c M_e^2]/(2 M_e Q), Q>0.              (10)

Use its numerically stable rationalized form in the receipt. At Q=0,
R_L>=DeltaJ^2/(2 G M_c M_e^2), for a parabolic receiver. These are phase-space
capacity bounds, not claims that the requisite scattering distribution exists.
They distinguish carrying enough energy from carrying enough J.
For DeltaJ=0, (9) has no positive angular-energy requirement; R_L must still
enclose the core. M_e=0 with DeltaJ>0 is impossible in this particle-receiver
model. Negative Q describes a different, bound receiver and is excluded here.

For any R_L strictly above (10), its capacity is realized in the stated
test-particle approximation by two antipodal outgoing packets of mass M_e/2,
at positions +/-R_L xhat, with velocities

    +/-[v_r xhat + v_t yhat],
    v_t=DeltaJ/(M_e R_L),
    v_r=sqrt(2Q/M_e+2GM_c/R_L-v_t^2)>0.

Their net linear momentum is zero, their J vectors add to DeltaJ zhat, and
their asymptotic energy is Q. Thus the joint conservation budgets are
compatible once the lever threshold is passed; a small stock loss is not an
absolute conservation prohibition. This construction does not show that the
actual action produces those packets from the native initial cloud, nor that
the test-particle approximation and source rate suffice. It is an endpoint
receiver construction, not a formation mechanism.

An endpoint illustration uses the previous Sextans inventory M=2.53958e7Msun,
q=.9885403, R_i=5*647.853pc, uniform core R_c=25.21pc, all (1-q)M escaping, and
E_i=E_core+Q. Then v_infinity,rms is approximately469km/s. At lambda=.03,
if the core has zero net J, the receiver must cross a sphere of radius at
least 191.22 pc; if the core uses the maximum J allowed by a uniform virial sphere,
the remaining J still needs a crossing lever arm 42.55 pc. Values are in the
machine-readable receipt. The ejection can carry the entire energy budget yet
fail the angular budget at the core surface. In the stated static-remnant model
the free-flight receiver branch cannot cross inward of that necessary radius
while carrying the stated energy and J. Torque/heat exchange at larger radii,
time-dependent work after the last close passage, or a core retaining more
rotation are concrete continuations; each changes or uses an explicit hypothesis.
No actual trajectory is demonstrated.

A uniform virial core's permissive J bound used here is

    J_c,max = sqrt(6/25) sqrt(G M_c^3 R_c),

from I_c=2M_c R_c^2/5, K_c=3GM_c^2/(10R_c), J_c^2<=2I_cK_c. This is a necessary
kinematic bound; saturating it consumes all kinetic energy in ordered rotation
and need not be a realizable spherical equilibrium. For random kinetic energy
fraction 1-eta, replace J_c,max by sqrt(eta) J_c,max. The joint receiver calculation
therefore remains deliberately generous.

## 4. Interaction dictionary and the first uncertain implication

* ZAR-K1 / CFG848: the functional above, separate populations in the original
  candidate, deep-cubic/zero-stiffness and spherical. It licenses conservative
  mean forces. It does not supply collisions, diffusion, t/f conversion or a
  receiver. In a strictly spherical collisionless history individual L is
  conserved even if the mean potential changes with time. Energy can change in
  a time-dependent potential; this does not automatically remove the angular
  barrier. CFG848 adds common-beta chemical conversion and a reflecting wall.
* Original charged Phi/X theory: charges one and two, canonical minimal gravity,
  V=m^2|Phi|^2+M_X^2|X|^2+g(X*Phi^2+c.c.)+lambda_tot|Phi|^4,
  lambda_tot=lambda_rep+g^2/(M_X^2-4m^2), M_X>2m, lambda_rep>0. Its matched
  daughter contact is repulsive. Charge-preserving X<->2Phi does not by itself
  delete net charge or J. Gravitational torques/scattering and allowed field
  waves can in principle be receivers; their rate/kinematics must be calculated
  with this action. No real-mediator force is added to it here.
* ARGON11 / M100: separate complex Psi + real chi action,
  L=-|dPsi|^2-(dchi)^2/2-[I+Lq I^2+a_chi chi I+U(chi)], I=|Psi|^2,
  U=M_chi^2 chi^2/2-M_chi chi^3/8+chi^4/4. Its existing coherent fragment
  simulations are periodic and resolution-unconverged. Real chi radiation and
  fragment recoil are possible channels in this action, but neither geometric
  area nor box outside-lump energy measures Q or DeltaJ from (9).

The first uncertain physical implication is that the ACTUAL fragment or native
assembly dynamics can populate a receiver with the required joint (E,J) flux,
at adequate rate and without exceeding the <=1.146% Sextans stock margin, while
the same law also respects Fornax/LeoI. The derived inequalities provide direct
diagnostics for a kinetic experiment; they are not a no-go for all interactions.
