number-density fits and Gaussian widths of a selected sample, not certified

For consistently weighted, whole spherical stationary Plummer tracers in a
common Newtonian potential, an exact projected-virial identity is

  y_i=3 a_i <v_los^2>_i/G
     =integral[1+(s/a_i)^2]^(-3/2) dM(s).

For h=a2/a1>1,R=y2/y1, any finite nonnegative radial mass measure requires
1<=R<h^3. When1<R<h^3, concavity gives the sharp positive-measure bound

  M>=y1[(h^2-1)/(h^2/R^(2/3)-1)]^(3/2).

The proof does not assume isotropic stellar velocities. Equality is a single
spherical mass shell; a physical tracer DF/smooth halo need not attain it.
It is a necessary lower bound, not an equilibrium-construction theorem.
