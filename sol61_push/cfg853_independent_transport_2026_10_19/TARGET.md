# CFG853 independent transport checkpoint A

User request: independently attack cold-core transport as an explanation of the
extra gravitating mass inferred in faint dwarf galaxies, subject to the existing
constraints. Started at Git `42b43a572c921d2894ad475a0969a0078c89b286`.

No existing CFG853 implementation or authoritative numbered list of fourteen
constraints was found in this checkout. This packet uses a source-linked
reconstruction, explicitly distinguished from an originally frozen test. It
does not claim ownership of a campaign ID or modify campaign frozen criteria.

The target is a single physical transport law, using identified framework
degrees of freedom and common parameters, that supplies the needed aperture
mass, exports its energy and angular momentum to an accounted receiver, obeys
finite stock and survival constraints, fits multiple stellar tracers, and does
not overfill the comparison dwarfs or rotating galaxies. Initial conditions,
capture rates and a new interaction cannot be fitted separately to each dwarf
and called a prediction. A fit, equilibrium free fraction, conservation-allowed
endpoint or finite computation is partial progress only.

Routes executed in this packet:

1. Coordinator: reconstruct four-dwarf stock with consistent normalization;
   test binary-shift transfers and all nonnegative velocity-only Maxwellian
   capture kernels; derive a two-tracer projected-virial mass discriminator.
2. `constraint_audit`: reconstruct observational, conditional stock and
   mechanism obligations, including later CFG860/861 updates.
3. `transport_routes`: derive whole-system endpoint and receiver restrictions
   for gravothermal concentration from mass, energy and angular momentum.
4. `kinetic_candidate`: test a normalized gravothermal core/envelope route
   against primary-source kinetic results and the aperture-mass requirements.

Writes are disjoint under this folder. No manuscript, shared ledger, live job,
queue, frozen criterion or publication is edited. All experiments are small,
single-process calculations. Process inspection via `ps` was denied by the
sandbox; no field or PM evolution is launched. The current action's missing
capture kernel cannot be replaced by geometric scattering area.

Completion requires the original physical mechanism, not merely success on
these reduced tests. Results that exclude a restricted model do not exclude
cold matter, all transport laws, or the dwarf observations.
