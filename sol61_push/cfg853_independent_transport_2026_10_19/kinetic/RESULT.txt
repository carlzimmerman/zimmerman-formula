PROOF AND EXACT DOMAIN
Let q=3-alpha>0 and h(r)=r^alpha rho(r) be nondecreasing. For0<r<R,
rho(r)<=rho(R)(r/R)^(-alpha); for R<r<Rt the reverse inequality holds.
Thus A=M(<R)<=C=4pi rho(R)R^3/q, while B=M(R<r<Rt)>=C[(Rt/R)^q-1].
Since A/(A+B) increases with A and decreases with B, F<=C/(C+C[k^q-1])=k^-q.
The proof also extends to nonnegative h with suitable almost-everywhere
monotonicity. It excludes a central atom/point mass and any profile whose
logarithmic density slope exceeds alpha on the relevant interval.
No gravity law, equilibrium or collision-rate assumption enters this bound.
It is a mass-distribution discriminator, not a transport impossibility.

The finite check uses an exactly normalized continuous flat core plus power
envelope: rho=rho_c for r<=rc, rho=rho_c(rc/r)^alpha outside, truncated at Rt.
For rc<=R, its unnormalized mass is
M(R)/(4pi rho_c)=rc^3/3+rc^alpha[R^(3-alpha)-rc^(3-alpha)]/(3-alpha).
For R<rc use R^3/3. It lies below the bound for every0<rc<=Rt, approaching
the pure-cusp bound as rc/Rt->0. Its asymptotic envelope amplitude held fixed
gives Mc proportional to rc^(3-alpha), while central velocity dispersion
squared scales as rc^(2-alpha). Therefore a shrinking gravothermal core has
Mc decreasing: dlnMc/dln(vc^2)=(3-alpha)/(2-alpha)=-4.26316 at alpha2.190.
Tenfold radius contraction retains only.154882 of the preceding core mass.
This is the source's selfsimilar direction, not an accretion model.
