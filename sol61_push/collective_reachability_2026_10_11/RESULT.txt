EXACT NECESSARY CONDITION FOR THE PROPOSED COLLECTIVE ROUTE
Take a fixed closed material cohort with constant mass M and trajectories
  xdot=v, vdot=-grad Phi(r,t).
Allow arbitrary time dependence and spherical self-gravity. Then specific
angular momentum vector ell=x cross v satisfies
  elldot=x cross [-grad Phi]=0.
Hence j=|ell| is constant for each element, and for every bounded measurable
function h the quantity integral h(j) dM is invariant. Equivalently, the
entire normalized marginal p(j) is conserved, not only total vector spin.
The theorem includes a changing spherical potential and therefore goes
beyond the earlier fixed-H phase-mixing diagnostic. It is only a necessary
condition: satisfying it does not prove capture, mixing, or target selection.

Countercontrol: two equal-mass populations with ell=+ell0 and -ell0 have
zero net spin; two populations with ell=+2ell0 and -2ell0 also have zero net
spin, but their j distributions differ. A resampler conserving total spin
can therefore violate the invariant required of a spherical Hamiltonian.
CFG620's reported K6 net-J conservation does not establish this stronger law.
Its code's mode A resamples tangential speeds. Its position drift updates
Lm=rn*vt at fixed vt. These operations generally change individual j.
That is not an implementation bug in its declared dissipative model. It
means its success cannot be attributed to spherical gravitational mixing
without an additional angular-momentum exchange mechanism.

A COMPUTABLE LOWER BOUND ON THE MISSING MECHANISM
For the same cohort, now allow a noncentral acceleration a_perp and finite
first moments of initial/final p(j). Along each trajectory,
  |j_f-j_i| <= integral |elldot| dt
             <= integral r |a_perp| dt.
The trajectories induce a coupling of initial and final marginals. Define
W1(p_i,p_f) as the minimum of mean |j_f-j_i| over ALL such couplings. Taking
mass averages in the previous inequality immediately proves
  W1(p_i,p_f) <= (1/M) integral dt integral r |a_perp| dM.
This proof uses the definition of the transport distance, not an imported
halo model. Both sides have units of specific angular momentum. It gives a
lower bound on the integrated torque needed by ANY proposed mechanism for
this cohort; it is not a lower bound on its net signed angular momentum.

Two checks of scope: for p_i=delta(j-j0), p_f=delta(j-2j0), W1=j0, even if
net spin is zero throughout. For identical marginals W1=0, which proves
neither dynamical accessibility nor absence of torques. The converse fails.
Central forces give a zero right side and necessarily identical marginals.
Finite-radius boundary samples require an explicit entering/leaving flux
ledger. Changing which particles are counted can change p(j) without torque;
apply the bound to a tracked cohort, not a changing aperture population.
Non-spherical collective gravity is a possible source of torque, not ruled
out by this result. Coarse sampling errors must not be counted as torque.

DIRECT NEXT DISCRIMINATOR
For a declared initial cohort and the existing orbit library, assign target
cell masses y_c and j_c=lib.L[c]. Compare the initial j marginal with
sum_c y_c delta(j-j_c), normalized to the SAME mass. No tuning of the initial
marginal after seeing the target is allowed. A spherical candidate must
match it. In a binned feasibility test, add rows
  sum_{c in j-bin b} y_c = M_initial,b
alongside the existing spatial density rows sum_c P_ck y_c=M_target,k,
with y_c>=0. This checks whether ANY supported orbit mixture matches both,
not merely whether CFG620's unconstrained entropy maximizer does.

An infeasible finite orbit library alone is NOT a continuum no-go: refine
resolution/support and seek a dual inequality valid for all admissible
orbits before claiming one. A feasible mixture alone is NOT formation.
For a nonspherical candidate, compute its actual integrated torque and
compare to W1; include reciprocal baryon/cold/field angular momentum and
energy accounting. This can falsify an underpowered mechanism without
fitting alpha. A new numerical experiment requires criteria committed alone
first, mutation controls (wrong cohort, net-spin-only and removed torque),
and bounded resource use. None was launched in this checkpoint.
