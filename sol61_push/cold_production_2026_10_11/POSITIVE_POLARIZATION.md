# Positive scalar-polarization extension: an effective construction only

This exploratory branch was computed before the user redirected attention
to Claude's latest work. It is saved for reproducibility, not promoted to
the current carrier or framework. It adds degrees of freedom and assumptions.

Let phi be a scalar force potential and P a three-component polarization.
Choose positive time-kinetic coefficients Z and I, and internal energy

    E_int = a/2 |grad phi-P/a|^2 + beta/3 |P|^3
            + gamma/2 |grad P|^2, a,beta,gamma>0.

Together with Z dot(phi)^2/2 and I |dot(P)|^2/2 this has nonnegative
source-free energy. It is independent of the protected carrier phase, so
adding it alone does not break that phase's U(1). This is not a microscopic
identification of P with the scalar carrier. Baryonic coupling adds energy
alpha rho_b phi, which is not nonnegative; the source-free coldness theorem
cannot be transferred to that sourced system without energy accounting.

Neglect gamma in a slowly varying static constitutive limit and couple
baryons through the stated scalar source. Set D=a grad phi-P. Then

    div D=alpha rho_b,
    grad phi=P/a+beta |P|P, D=a beta |P|P.

Outside a spherical source of mass M_b, D=alpha M_b/(4 pi r^2) in magnitude.
For positive alpha the attractive scalar force magnitude is

    g_s=G_s M_b/r^2 + sqrt(G_s a_s M_b)/r,
    G_s=alpha^2/(4 pi a), a_s=alpha/(beta a^2).

If ordinary gravity is also present, the asymptotic coefficient is G_s a_s,
not automatically the measured G_N times the repo's a0. The coefficient
matching is an additional constraint. A vacuum offset is independent and
does not determine it.

The cubic norm and critical quadratic square are explicit phenomenological
choices. They were not generated by integrating out the previous fields.
Without gamma, the vacuum has an exactly zero quadratic frequency branch
at every wavenumber: a positive energy does not imply a well-conditioned
linear response. For dimensionless a=Z=I=beta=1 and gamma>0, the longitudinal
vacuum squared-frequency roots obey

    omega^4-[1+(1+gamma)k^2]omega^2+gamma k^4=0.

The low root is positive and tends to gamma k^4 as k approaches zero.
For a background polarization P0 along z, the polarization Hessian is
(1+|P0|+gamma k^2) times the identity plus |P0| zz^T. The full coupled
four-by-four matrix remains nonnegative because it is the Hessian of the
sum of the squared gradient term, convex cubic norm and regulator term.
Positive time kinetic terms avoid a linear negative-kinetic mode in this
nonrelativistic model; this is not a causal relativistic completion.

`positive_polarization.py` tests 60 background/wave/angle combinations,
checks the vacuum dispersion and static constitutive law, and detects the
instability induced by a negative quadratic mismatch. All 84 checks pass.
The negative mismatch example gives omega^2=-8.9902e-5 at k=0.1. A positive
mismatch instead restores a linear far-weak-field response, as in the
preceding polarization-selector audit. Critical tuning remains unexplained.

The exact static formula above uses gamma=0. For P=B/r radially,
laplacian P=-2B/r^3, so the regulator correction relative to beta P^2
falls as 2 gamma/(beta B r). The finite-gamma nonlinear radial solution was
not computed; sampled mode stability does not establish it or nonlinear
stability. No microscopic emergence, quantum renormalization, universal
vacuum normalization, cold-species unification or observational viability
has been proved.
