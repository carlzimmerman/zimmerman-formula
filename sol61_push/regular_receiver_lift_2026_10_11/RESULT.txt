A REGULAR CANONICAL LIFT OF THE PAIRWISE DISSIPATIVE BRACKET
2026-10-11. Analytic construction, self-reviewed; no simulation or data verdict.
Base b330f87026. Source: CFG542 ACTION sections1,2,5 and prior signed energy
bounds b330f87026/f67c3e9ca. Kappa fitted, law and footings unchanged.

RESULT
The existing dissipative pair bracket can be lifted to canonical receiver
momentum without1/pi singularities, but a bounded lift cannot retain the
original receiver-independent settling mobility at pi=0. This provides a
specific completion route and a specific remaining dynamical obligation.
It is not a new particle, a derived alpha, or a completed covariant action.

DERIVATION: ONE EXCHANGE CHANNEL
Let q transfer a conserved amount of cold mass between two cells; the pair's
mass vector is implicitly(-q,+q). Work at fixed receiver field coordinate
phi and other cold coordinates. Let
  E(q,phi,pi)=E_c(q)+pi^2/2+V(phi),
  D=partial E_c/partial q,
  S=S(q), s=partial S/partial q.
Canonical field normalization is used here; pi^2/2 is an energy in the
chosen cell normalization, not a new physical scale. Rest mass is transferred,
not created. D is the FULL instantaneous energy derivative of the selected
channel, including kinetic terms when they change; using only DeltaPhi is
valid only for the position-only prototype.
Define w=(pi,-D) in coordinates(q,pi), and
  M=m w w^T, m>=0 finite.
For arbitrary test covector x,
  x^T M x=m(pi*x_q-D*x_pi)^2>=0.
Since w dot grad_(q,pi) E=pi*D-D*pi=0, M grad E=0.
The dissipative equations are therefore
  qdot=m pi^2 s,
  pidot=-m pi D s.
Direct multiplication verifies
  Edot=D qdot+pi pidot=0,
  Sdot=m pi^2 s^2>=0,
  Q_receiver=pi pidot=-m pi^2 D s=-Edot_cold.
Every expression is regular at pi=0 for finite m,D,s. Q can have either sign
if D*s has either sign. Positivity of M fixes entropy production, not the
sign of the receiver's energy change. However, the particular one-sided
position-deficit functional in CFG542 can still enforce one sign. This
construction does NOT automatically repair that functional's kinetic limits.

WHY THE RATE MUST CHANGE IN THIS CLASS
For D!=0, any symmetric2x2 matrix satisfying M(D,pi)^T=0 has the above form,
with m=M_pi,pi/D^2. If M is bounded near pi=0, m is bounded locally, and
M_q,q=m pi^2 vanishes quadratically. With S_pi=0, qdot vanishes quadratically.
To preserve a prescribed nonzero mobility Gamma in qdot=Gamma*s for all pi,
one must set m=Gamma/pi^2. Then M_pi,pi=Gamma D^2/pi^2 diverges. Thus a
regular bracket and the original receiver-independent nonzero drift cannot
both hold through receiver rest in this two-coordinate channel.
This is a scoped algebraic result. At D=0 there is no first-order energy
cost in q and the division argument does not apply. Additional channels,
spatial transport, or nonzero receiver entropy require a larger analysis.
The smooth matrix above remains a construction even at D=0.

WHAT HAPPENS AT REST
The dissipative q and pi rates both vanish at pi=0. The canonical Hamiltonian
part may still accelerate the field if V'(phi)!=0, so this is not a general
claim that the full solution freezes forever. A receiver initially at a
stationary potential point with zero momentum is not set in motion by this
exchange channel alone. The existing prescribed-Q completion would force
Q/pi there instead. A real completion must derive initial receiver dynamics,
not insert a convenient nonzero pi to evade the issue.

SELF-REVIEW AND PHYSICAL LIMITS
1. This is an energy-degenerate positive dissipative bracket, not by itself
   a single ordinary action or full GENERIC system. CFG542's separate
   L grad S degeneracy problem remains. Spatial stress, flux, covariance,
   momentum exchange, kinetic support, catchment and stability are unproved.
2. m is an unspecified constitutive mobility with appropriate units. Nothing
   here licenses choosing its magnitude as1 or fitting it. Deriving m from
   existing physics remains essential to the no-new-knobs goal.
3. The signed energy requirements from the previous calculations must be
   met over the FULL evolution. A sign-capable algebraic expression does
   not establish sufficient receiver capacity or either endpoint outcome.
4. For a canonical homogeneous field with positive energy density,
   rho_phi+p_phi=pi^2>=0 in c=1 normalization. A regular coupling does not
   permit its intrinsic w to cross below-1. An effective equation of state
   inferred after moving Q to a continuity-equation term is a different
   quantity. Do not equate pi=0 with a demonstrated phantom crossing.
5. Changing pi's normalization without transforming m changes the physics.
   Any proposed rate must be written in invariant physical energy/momentum
   units before comparing systems or the two a0 footings.
6. Original framework allows empirical kernel in its functional. No new
   requirement to rederive that kernel is imposed by this construction.

DECISION
A regular receiver does not require insertingQ/pi by hand. It does require
coupling the cold drift to receiver state (or a more general exchange sector)
and deriving the mobility. The next meaningful completion must give the
FULL kinetic bracket with its reciprocal energy transfer, show how both
benchmark energy signs occur, and derive its time scale. Do not claim that
this elementary algebra identifies cold energy or completes the theory.
