RESULT

The immediate local-source obstacle can be removed exactly on Einstein
vacuum exteriors by replacing f(phi)E4 with f(phi)(E4-C^2). This preserves
the cosmological source. Equal Euler and Weyl anomaly coefficients offer a
possible microscopic reason for this relative coefficient. This is a useful
new direction for this candidate, not a claim of literature novelty or a
derivation of the target absolute coefficient.

1. Exact curvature identity in four spacetime dimensions

E4 = Riemann^2 - 4 Ricci^2 + R^2,
C^2 = Riemann^2 - 2 Ricci^2 + R^2/3,
J = E4-C^2 = 2R^2/3 - 2 Ricci^2.
On any Einstein metric R_mn=(R/4)g_mn, J=R^2/6. In particular, a
Schwarzschild-de Sitter exterior has R=12H^2, so J=24H^4, independently
of the mass m and radius r. Individually,
E4=24H^4+48m^2/r^6 and C^2=48m^2/r^6.
Thus the troublesome mass-dependent scalar source cancels identically.

More generally J_t=E4-t C^2 has source 24H^4+(1-t)48m^2/r^6.
For nonzero Weyl curvature, exact cancellation within this one-parameter
family selects t=1. This selects a relative operator coefficient, not beta.
The result is local to Einstein vacuum regions; matter interiors generally
have a different Ricci tensor and are outside this claim.

2. Changed classical action and an exact branch

S = integral sqrt(-g)[M^2 R/2 - (partial phi)^2/2 - U(phi)+f(phi)J],
U=U0 exp(phi^2/F^2), f=beta log(phi/F), all scales positive.
For constant phi=phi_v on an Einstein metric, the scalar equation is
U'(phi_v)=24H^4 f'(phi_v), precisely as in the original de Sitter model.
The curvature corrections to the metric equation vanish on this branch:
constant-coefficient E4 is topological in four dimensions; the Weyl-squared
variation is proportional to the Bach tensor, which vanishes on Einstein
metrics. Equivalently the R^2 and Ricci^2 variations vanish there, since
their derivative terms vanish and their algebraic terms cancel.
The remaining equation is U(phi_v)=3M^2 H^2.

Consequently each admitted constant-scalar de Sitter vacuum also admits the
same constant scalar on a Schwarzschild-de Sitter exterior with the same H.
This is an exact branch of the declared classical equations, not merely a
gradient-neglect approximation. It does not prove uniqueness, stability,
formation, interior matching, a MOND force law, or observational viability.

On conformally flat FLRW backgrounds C=0, and the first variation of the
added f C^2 term also vanishes. The old homogeneous equations and their
homogeneous perturbations therefore survive in this declared toy action.
General inhomogeneous perturbations do not share this simplification.

3. Literature clue and conventions

Komargodski–Schwimmer, arXiv:1107.3987v2, Eq. (2.13), gives a dilaton
anomaly action containing -a tau E4+c tau C^2 and derivative terms.
Its RG application uses anomaly differences with the IR spectrum accounted
for. With tau=-log(phi/F), the linear curvature terms have the required
relative sign when Delta a=Delta c. In the convention
<T^mu_mu>=(c C^2-a E4)/(16 pi^2), the candidate identification is
beta=Delta a/(16 pi^2). This identification is conditional, not a UV model.
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1107.3987 (Eq. 2.13 and Sec. 3).

Shapere–Tachikawa, arXiv:0804.1957v2, fixes this anomaly normalization in
Eq. (2.1) and obtains a=c=(N^2-1)/4 for N=4 SU(N) in Eq. (4.4).
This establishes an example of equal anomaly coefficients. It does not
establish an applicable flow, an IR spectrum, or our scalar potential.
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/0804.1957 (Eqs. 2.1 and 4.4).

A UV theory with a=c alone is insufficient: the relevant anomaly differences
must agree, including every light field. The dilaton derivative terms and
any conformal curvature coupling must also be retained when matching an
actual microscopic theory. They can change the cosmological dynamics and
stability. The exponential potential has not been derived from such a flow.

4. Attempt to select 32 pi: remaining normalization exposed

For the declared toy action the same vacuum relation holds:
Lambda/phi_v^2=3/(4 gamma), gamma=beta F^2/M^2.
Even granting the conditional anomaly identification above, it becomes
Lambda/phi_v^2=12 pi^2/[Delta a (F^2/M^2)].
The target requires Delta a (F^2/M^2)=3 pi/8.
Anomaly equality does not fix the continuous ratio F^2/M^2. Thus this clue
does not select 3/128. Its contribution is to remove a specific local-source
obstruction while pointing to a possible reason for the operator combination.

5. Adversarial check: additional gravitational modes

At constant scalar, the added -f(phi_v) C^2 term is a curvature-squared
gravitational operator. Taken as an exact finite-derivative gravitational
theory, a generic nonzero coefficient introduces an additional spin-two
sector with a ghost/tachyon problem. An EFT treatment requires a justified
cutoff and treatment of higher-derivative operators; it is not an automatic
cure. See arXiv:1806.10016 Sec. 2.1, Eqs. (2.1)-(2.8) and mode discussion:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1806.10016

Setting f(phi_v)=0 by choosing a constant counterterm is not a proof of
health; scalar-dependent interactions remain. Moreover, an additive constant
in f is no longer purely topological after adding -f C^2. A change of the
logarithm's reference scale therefore changes a physical Weyl-squared
coupling unless a matching counterterm is specified. This is an additional
matching datum, not an innocuous normalization choice.
