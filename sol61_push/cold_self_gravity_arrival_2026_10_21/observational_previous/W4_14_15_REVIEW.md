# W4-14/15 observational clue: source and raw-evidence review

Read-only review; no original script imports, fits, bootstrap/permutation runs, or model experiments. `raw_cached_audit.json` records 54/54 checks, comprising raw sample/median extraction, cached arithmetic, and exact revision-byte checks. This is an audit of the saved estimates, not a replication of their uncertainty estimates.

W4-14 revision `a4b55c5ca09ed189a7cdad36301e5bfee88ab7a4` matches its local source/results/output bytes. Criteria `9ffc65ac7a2eaa6d6b63f10a8c70918cdb6e48c4` precede its implementation. The primary estimate is correctly reconstructed from the saved inverse-variance bin weights: Delta_match=-0.0964123819 +/- 0.1111025272 dex, z=-0.868. Four bins satisfy the declared at-least-three-galaxies-per-class rule, so the **frozen label ACCELERATION is arithmetically correct**. It means this pooled test did not detect a class difference beyond 2 sigma; it does not establish equivalence or erase a causal baryon-type dependence.

Raw SPARC extraction confirms 153 quality/inclination-qualified galaxies and the four usable bin counts: gas/star 7/8, 21/12, 29/28, 12/59, for log g_bar bins spanning [-12,-11). All four saved bin medians are reproduced. These are cross-galaxy medians of within-galaxy-bin medians, not matched measurements of both classes in each identical galaxy at identical acceleration. The restricted within-galaxy estimate is -0.4406768899 +/- 0.0587615765 dex (11 galaxies). Those 11 are **not p64's original 11**: nine overlap; the new cohort adds DDO064 and UGC12632 and omits UGC05721 and UGC07125. Code uses >=2 valid points per class in the restricted range, whereas p64's original selection requires >=3 per class over the full range. The two statistics therefore change cohort/weighting as well as matching. A nominal 2.74-sigma difference obtained by adding their variances assumes independence; paired covariance is not saved and this is not a calibrated causal test.

**Frozen M2 remains a failed discriminator.** The known acceleration-only plant is assigned BARYON-TYPE by the same classifier: matched Delta=-0.0099899280 +/- 0.0027918823 dex (z=-3.58), while its restricted within-galaxy gap is only 1.31 sigma. Finite bin width can leave a residual even in a pure acceleration world. Calling this failure a control-design problem explains it; it does not turn the frozen control into a pass. The post-hoc noisy/full-range version is useful exploratory evidence, with no frozen verdict weight. M1's saved point estimate is near zero, but code changes original star galaxy IDs to ID+1000000 before cluster resampling, so that control's bootstrap does not preserve original cross-class galaxy identity. It is not a validation of the primary test's full clustering calibration.

Dependencies are narrower than their prose can suggest. W4-13 revision `cb7136f0712ba443c80fb3f52a15987bfff9e804` reproduces p64's fiducial simple and RAR values, and none of its five declared kernels closes the fiducial gap. This rules out closure by that menu, not every possible law or systematic. Its fixed-M/L global chi-square fit has severe lack of fit, already disclosed. The noiseless planted-world strict-inequality failure is retained and its comparison tolerance was repaired; this does not change the primary measurements. p64 revision `9b8efc6f7b6a13800cd2224781eb4c561f01bec3` has **UNDETERMINED**, not CONTRAST, as its frozen grid-wide A verdict. Its fiducial negative gap is real as an estimator conditional on adopted masses, kinematics, kernel and cuts; it is not a cold-energy detection.

W4-15 revision `3964e41784a64962e8957cbe61955dcb3dbcde7a` tests a different, broader cohort. Its 76 cached names exactly match raw quality/inclination, >=8 valid point and environment-availability selection; cached environmental values match the local table. Its primary rho=-0.2501794, permutation p=0.0297970 is correctly **INTERMEDIATE** under frozen criteria. The sign opposes the proposed positive environmental-EFE relation, but this is not an exclusion of every EFE model. Frozen planted-power M1 fails (rho=+0.07149, p=0.54365); M2's shuffle is null at p=0.06619. The later shuffled-baseline power exercise is post-hoc, not a successful frozen power calibration. No permutation inference was rerun in this audit.

The useful cold-source implication is a research priority: disentangle galaxy offsets, radius/acceleration, selection and baryonic mass/kinematic systematics before assigning a gas/star selector. Neither W4-14's pooled non-detection nor W4-15's opposite-sign intermediate environmental result derives a source term, binding phase, phase transition, transport kernel, or dwarf allocation. Gas versus stellar dynamical dominance is not a measurement of hot versus condensed cold medium. Draco/Sextans concentration, Fornax/Leo I protection, and their energy/charge/angular-momentum budgets remain independent unresolved gates. The red/blue lensing assertion was not audited here.
