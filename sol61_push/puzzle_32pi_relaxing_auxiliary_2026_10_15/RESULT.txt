RELAXING AUXILIARY: GENERATED INTERPOLATION AND A FORCE-DEFICIT OBSTRUCTION
2026-10-15. Candidate construction in this investigation; literature originality NOT established.

1. Model and assumptions
Use a fixed preferred time and flat spatial metric, c=1:
 L = K0 (d_t chi)^2/2 - E(|grad chi|,psi) - q rho chi/M - E0,
 E(v,psi) = B v^3/psi + h (psi-psi0)^2/2.
B,h,psi0,q,M are positive. psi>0 is an EXACT algebraic auxiliary, with no derivatives. E0 is independent. This is a scalar force model; no Einstein/aether equations or relativistic matter completion have been supplied. In the force calculation the scalar supplies the entire modeled force, not an extra force added on top of a separate Newtonian force.

2. Elimination generates the transition
Stationarity gives h(psi-psi0)psi^2=Bv^3. At v>0 the unique positive root is above psi0: the left side is negative below psi0, and its derivative is h psi(3psi-2psi0)>0 above psi0. E diverges as psi tends to zero or infinity, so this root is the global minimum. At v=0 the minimum is psi0.
Define v0=psi0(h/B)^(1/3), s=psi/psi0 and t=v/v0.
Then s^2(s-1)=t^3, s>=1.
The envelope flux is J=dE_eff/dv=3Bv^2/psi.
Choose K0=3B^(2/3)h^(1/3) and define mu=J/(K0 v)=t/s.
Eliminating s gives exactly:
 t = mu/(1-mu^3), 0<=mu<1.
This function is unique and increasing: dt/dmu=(1+2mu^3)/(1-mu^3)^2>0.
Its low-gradient limit is mu~t; its high-gradient limit is mu->1.

3. Quadratic stability and characteristic speeds
The auxiliary stiffness on the branch is E_psipsi=h(3-2/s)>0.
Eliminating its fluctuation gives the Schur complement
 E_eff''=E_vv-E_vpsi^2/E_psipsi.
Writing p=d log J/d log v gives
 p=(3s-1)/(3s-2)=(2+mu^3)/(1+2mu^3).
Radial and transverse spatial stiffnesses are K0 mu p and K0 mu.
With time kinetic K0, on every static finite nonzero gradient background,
 c_parallel^2=mu(2+mu^3)/(1+2mu^3), c_perp^2=mu.
The exact identity
 1-c_parallel^2=(1-mu)^3(1+mu)/(1+2mu^3)>0
proves both positive stiffness and speeds strictly between zero and one.
Intermediate propagation directions interpolate between these two eigenvalues.
This is a local principal-symbol and quadratic-energy statement, not a global nonlinear stability or full relativistic causality theorem.
At v=0 the time kinetic remains K0>0 but the spatial quadratic stiffness vanishes. Nonlinear control/regularization at this limit is unresolved.

4. Spherical force and a falsifiable residual
Outside a spherical source, r^2 J=q Mb/(4 pi M). Define g=q v/M,
 G_eff=q^2/(4 pi K0 M^2), g_N=G_eff Mb/r^2 and a0=q v0/M.
Then g mu=g_N and g/a0=mu/(1-mu^3).
For small g, g^2/a0~g_N: hence g proportional to sqrt(Mb)/r.
For large g, g/g_N->1. But the absolute residual does NOT vanish:
 (g-g_N)/a0=t(1-mu)=mu/(1+mu+mu^2) -> 1/3.
No observational fit or solar-system compatibility is claimed.
If G is independently defined as 1/(8 pi M^2), matching requires K0=2q^2; this is a calibration, not a derived value.

5. General obstruction discovered by examining that residual
Consider ANY twice differentiable isotropic reduced energy E_eff(v), v>0,
with constant positive time kinetic K0, J=E_eff', and a force normalized as above.
Suppose J(v)/(K0 v)->0 at small v (the deep cubic branch satisfies this).
Suppose also 0<J'(v)<=K0 for all v>0, i.e. positive radial stiffness and a radial characteristic speed no greater than the chosen unit speed.
Define D(v)=K0 v-J(v). For some small v_*>0, D(v_*)>0 by the deep-limit hypothesis.
Since D'=K0-J'>=0, the fundamental theorem of calculus gives
 D(v)=D(v_*)+integral_[v_*,v](K0-J'(u))du >= D(v_*)>0
for every v>=v_* (or use the mean value theorem).
Therefore absolute Newtonian recovery J-K0 v->0 is IMPOSSIBLE under these hypotheses, even when relative recovery J/(K0 v)->1 holds.
In force variables g-g_N=q D/(M K0), so the absolute force residual cannot tend to zero.
The explicit candidate realizes a finite positive limit q v0/(3M)=a0/3.
This elementary conditional obstruction is analytically proved here; its full calculus statement is not formalized in the accompanying Lean file. No novelty claim is made.
A candidate with a vanishing absolute residual must relax at least one hypothesis: for example, change the constant time-kinetic assumption, permit a speed above one somewhere, or change the single-scalar force identification. This does not itself establish that any such extension works.

6. Do not promote the auxiliary without rechecking
Adding a finite positive kinetic term to psi makes it a propagating field. At sufficiently high frequency the chi stiffness is unrelaxed:
 E_vv/K0=2mu.
Thus the chi characteristic speed exceeds one when mu>1/2. The algebraic-auxiliary result is not a proof for a finite-rate dynamical medium. A dynamical preferred frame would add further modes requiring separate analysis.

7. Original numerical puzzle remains open
If a future gravitational completion identifies Lambda=E0/M^2, then

The free additive E0 leaves this ratio arbitrary, including after K0=2q^2 normalization. Nothing here selects 32pi, 64pi^2, or 3/128.
