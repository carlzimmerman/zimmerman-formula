# Prescribed molecular-rich sensitivity
`hydro_cooling.py` adds a diagnostic radiative branch to corrected `hydro_v2.py`. For z<=100 take n(H2)/n(H)=1e-3, fixed; above z100 this source is off. This is deliberately imposed, NOT derived chemistry or a predicted primordial abundance. Hydrogen mass fraction=.76, neutral mu1.22 retained. Use Galli & Palla1998 arXiv:astro-ph/9803315v1 AppendixA eqA7 low-density H-H2 coefficient (10–10000K). Reject the active branch at nH>100cm^-3 or T>10000K. Below10K use coefficient at10K in the negligible tail; CMB temperature exceeds10K throughout these collapse runs.
Cooling per volume=fH2*nH^2*max[Lambda(T)-Lambda(Tcmb),0]. The CMB subtraction is a prescribed floor approximation, not detailed radiative level balance. No HD, atomic line cooling, dissociation, formation heat, radiation transport, self-shielding or abundance evolution. The timestep additionally resolves 0.1 of local cooling time. Net Compton-minus-molecular heat is integrated in the same energy identity. The inherited result key `compton_heat` stores NET heat on this branch and must be read accordingly.
This branch can show whether a molecular-rich cooling choice materially changes the nonradiative result. It cannot establish that this abundance is produced, or infer a mass/population. The first cooling-base manifest inherits generic hydro metadata; the executed `hydro_cooling.py` hash fixes these extra choices. Preserve that record and this explicit scope correction.
