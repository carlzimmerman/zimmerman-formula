# Density-weighted reciprocal repair, pre-run

The constant-g transverse speed is (DX+g^2/rho_b)/MX. Test g(rho_b)=g0 rho_b^alpha, including all density variations. If the power is required to remain bounded both as rho_b tends to zero and infinity with fixed coefficients, alpha=1/2 is the unique power. This is a regularity criterion for this reduced family, not a microscopic derivation.

The interaction is g(rho_b) X.curl v_b, not X.curl[g(rho_b)v_b]. Derive m_b=rho_b v_b+curl(gX) and delta L/delta rho_b=v_b^2/2-U_b'-Phi+g'(rho_b) X.curl v_b. Omitting the last term is an invalid nonreciprocal variant.

For locally constant chi, neglect mediator gradients only as an explicitly adiabatic approximation, X=g curl v_b/chi. Test a cylindrical power-law disc v=V r^p, rho=rho0 r^(-n). Compute its circular force independently with Cartesian material variations. For alpha=1/2 the expected flat-curve correction is n g0^2/(chi r^2); rigid rotation should have no local correction in this limit. The zero-mean-vorticity static cold branch and its radius bound are unchanged.

Frozen tests: symbolic equality with the material variation; scalar-source absence at linear order unchanged; density-independent transverse speed for alpha=1/2; explicitly omit the g' density force in the control. No claim of nonlinear stability, covariant completion, realistic disc boundary conditions or successful galaxy fitting.
