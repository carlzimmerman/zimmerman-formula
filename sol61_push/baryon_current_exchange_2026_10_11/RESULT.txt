1. Domain and action

Use signature (-,+,+,+), c=1. Cold elements have fixed masses m_a and
worldlines x_a. They represent the proposed material cold component, not an
additional particle species. Let B(x) be a smooth prescribed baryonic scalar
gate, independent of the cold worldlines in this conditional calculation.
Let theta be a canonical receiver field; lambda is an UNFIXED coupling.

 S = -sum_a m_a int ds_a
     -1/2 int sqrt(-g) (grad theta)^2 d^4x
     +lambda sum_a int B(x_a) partial_mu theta(x_a) dx_a^mu.

A constant receiver potential may be added, but it does not supply usable
exchange energy. The theta field is a candidate receiver, not an identified
physical dark-energy field. The action's interaction is exactly shift invariant.

Writing J^mu=sum_a int u_a^mu delta_g(x-x_a) ds_a gives

 S_int=lambda int sqrt(-g) B J^mu grad_mu theta,
 grad_mu J^mu=0.

For constant B this is a boundary term and produces no bulk interaction.
For variable B, integration by parts gives

 S_int=-lambda int sqrt(-g) theta J^mu grad_mu B + boundary.

The latter expression is shift invariant modulo the boundary, not an explicit
theta potential. It must not be interpreted as destruction of cold charge.

2. Exact field and worldline equations

 Box theta=lambda grad_mu(B J^mu)=lambda J^mu grad_mu B.

The worldline interaction is a one-form A_mu=lambda B partial_mu theta.
Its curvature is

 F_mu_nu=lambda[(partial_mu B)(partial_nu theta)
                    -(partial_nu B)(partial_mu theta)].

Variation of the worldline gives

 m_a a_mu=F_mu_nu u_a^nu.

Antisymmetry implies u^mu F_mu_nu u^nu=0. The mass-shell normalization and
fixed m_a are preserved. This is mechanical acceleration, not a variable-mass
coupling m(theta). Multistream worldlines are allowed; no single dust velocity
is required. Singular point self-forces are NOT solved by writing this action;
the equations for self-interaction need a continuum limit or regularization.

Both gradients matter: F=0 if B is constant, or if dB and dtheta are parallel.
Consequently simply multiplying a current interaction by an 'on' value cannot
produce uniform bulk damping. The interaction is sensitive to gate gradients.

3. Explicit energy balance in a controlled limit

Take flat space, a smooth continuum number density n, number flux j, and
static B(x). Continuity gives dot n+div j=0. The scalar equation becomes

 thetaddot-Laplacian theta=-lambda j dot grad B.

With e_theta=(thetadot^2+|grad theta|^2)/2 and
F_theta=-thetadot grad theta, direct multiplication by thetadot yields

 dot e_theta+div F_theta=-lambda thetadot j dot grad B.

The nonrelativistic particle force is

 m dv/dt=lambda[grad B(thetadot+v dot grad theta)
                        -grad theta(v dot grad B)].

Its mechanical power is EXACTLY, at this nonrelativistic order,

 v dot (m dv/dt)=lambda thetadot v dot grad B.

Summing over the smooth distribution cancels the scalar source above. Thus
total mechanical plus scalar energy is conserved, with scalar boundary flux
explicit. A fixed Newtonian gravitational potential adds its usual conservative
force and potential energy without spoiling this cancellation. This permits
binding/kinetic energy exchange while retaining cold rest mass. It does not
prove a nonzero outgoing luminosity for a specified halo or guarantee its sign.

For time-dependent B an additional power term appears. It must be assigned to
the baryonic gate's dynamics; treating B as an external input then ceases to
be a closed energy ledger. Even a static externally prescribed B can exchange
momentum with the cold-plus-scalar subsystem. A full action must vary baryons
and the metric and include their reaction. The present action is conditional.

4. Eliminate theta: the rate is not automatically a local alpha

At fixed background metric and specified boundary/initial data, formally

 theta=theta_hom + lambda G_ret * (J^mu grad_mu B).

Substitution in F gives a retarded, generally nonlocal mechanical response:
an O(lambda) response to theta_hom and an O(lambda^2) self-response. No
Markov approximation, universal friction coefficient, positive settling rate,
or target density follows just from this representation. G_ret fixes causal
propagation only after its operator and boundary conditions are supplied.
Using a retarded solution in a conservative action is not, by itself, a
derivation of a dissipative single-history variational principle.

In a stationary continuum with zero spatial number flux and static B, the
source is zero. An initially empty free wave field then stays empty. Radiation
from individual fluctuating elements would require microscopic information
and correlations absent from that smooth mean-field description. It cannot
be obtained by silently replacing the continuum with arbitrary massive tracers.

5. What this does and does not resolve

Resolved conditionally: rest-mass conservation and mechanical exchange are
compatible with a shift-symmetric derivative action and a baryon-only gate.
This advances beyond radial-to-phase conversion that consumes cold rest energy.

Unresolved, required for the user's law:
 - lambda and the normalization/functional form of B are not derived;
 - the canonical waves are not a w=-1 energy deposit;
 - an incoming state/import capacity is not selected;
 - full baryon reaction, self-force/continuum closure and stability are open;
 - neither nu_mono nor the reservoir profile or its supply edge is obtained;
 - no settling time, cosmological growth or observational gate is evaluated;
 - kappa=1/2 and the cold abundance remain inputs.

Do not adopt this as a finished model under the no-new-free-constant rule.
The next useful discriminator is whether the EXISTING theory's gate/action
fixes this interaction and its normalization. If it cannot, a radiation test
with freely chosen lambda would merely test an enlarged theory, not close this
one. A different receiver kinetic structure would require a new health audit.
