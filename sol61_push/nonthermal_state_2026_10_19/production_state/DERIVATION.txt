Outcome
The action supports a conditional, dilute nonthermal pair-production state.
It does NOT select a unique source phase, density or preparation, nor does
its U(1) protect the opposite-momentum pair correlations against all of its
own interactions. Exact charge conservation, a squeezed Gaussian state and
long-lived finite-density pair coherence are three different statements.
The useful same-action signal is a momentum/energy pair-correlation ridge
and its number-difference noise. Their existence and loss criteria are
derived below without inserting a galaxy law, bath, portal or selected BEC.
These are project-specific consequences of well-known parametric dissociation
physics, not claimed new fundamental physics.

1. Actual action and preparation dictionary

Set hbar=c=1 except when restoring times. Use signature +--- and
 L=|partial Phi|^2+|partial X|^2-V,
 V=m^2|Phi|^2+M^2|X|^2+g(X*Phi^2+X Phi*^2)+lambda_tot|Phi|^4,
 M>2m, Delta_c=M^2-4m^2,
 lambda_tot=g^2/Delta_c+lambda_rep, lambda_rep>0.
This is the expanded existing positive-square action; g can be made real
by field phases. No neutral real mediator, TLS medium or baryonic portal
is added. Phi and X have signed charges 1 and 2.

A prepared X+ quantum decays into two SAME-charge Phi+ quanta. Its
antiparticle X- decays into two Phi- quanta. A neutral stock needs both
source signs, or another specified zero-charge preparation. X+ decay alone
is not neutral pair creation Phi+ Phi-. The earlier real-source model and
optical neutral-source notes have different vertices and cannot be imported.

Required independent preparation inputs are: the initial density operator
of X+/X-, their momentum/wavepacket distribution, their correlations/phases,
their total energy/number, volume and epoch, and the initial daughter state.
An initially empty daughter sector and an occupied source sector is one
admissible preparation, not a state uniquely chosen by the action. In
particular an action plus the same mean source energy/number does not
distinguish a coherent source from its phase-averaged mixture.

The resting narrow-source kinematics are
 E*=M/2, p*=sqrt(M^2/4-m^2)=m u*,
 v*=p*/E*=u*/sqrt(1+u*^2),
 Gamma_dec=g^2 sqrt(1-4m^2/M^2)/(8 pi M).
Gamma_dec is an energy width; the vacuum lifetime is hbar/Gamma_dec.
M, m, g, lambda_rep and source preparation are inputs. Small v* is selected
by a near-threshold mass splitting, not derived by symmetry.

2. What exact U(1) does and does not protect

Let Q=N_Phi+ -N_Phi- +2N_X+ -2N_X- in a particle description of the
exact signed Noether charge. If [rho_initial,Q]=0, then [rho(t),Q]=0,
since [H,Q]=0. A same-charge anomalous pair operator a_k a_q has charge -2,
so
 <a_k a_q>_t=exp(-2i theta)<a_k a_q>_t=0 for every t.                    (1)
This applies in particular to an exact Q=0 neutral stock and to a source
number-diagonal ensemble. It does not forbid a neutral higher-point
correlation, pair-number correlation or a relational expectation
<b_X+^dagger a_k a_q>. The latter carries total charge zero and can encode
source-daughter phase information. A coherent charged source with a phase
reference is a different allowed preparation; it need not satisfy (1).

For a neutral stock, E>=m|Q| is only E>=0. It does not give a nonzero
neutral particle-number lower bound or protect its soft rest-mass abundance.
The separate positive-number NR bound omits the hard relativistic channels
and cannot be promoted to an exact neutral-stock theorem.

3. Exact pair-number invariant in a clearly restricted production model

In a finite box, retain only a source b_0 with zero momentum and distinct
unordered daughter momentum pairs {k,-k}, with k!=0. The leading resonant
Hamiltonian of this action has
 H_pair=M b_0^dagger b_0+sum_k E_k N_k
      +sum_{k in half-space} h_k[b_0 a_k^dagger a_-k^dagger+h.c.].
The coupling for distinct modes is h_k=g/[sqrt(2M V) E_k]. Its NR limit
is 2c_3/sqrt(V), where c_3=g/(2m sqrt(2M)) in the existing notes.

Every I_k=N_k-N_-k commutes with H_pair, for arbitrary b_0 depletion,
detuning and initial source number statistics. From daughter vacuum,
 I_k|Psi(t)>=0 and <(N_k-N_-k)^2>=0.                                    (2)
This is an exact invariant of this truncated Hamiltonian. It is not an
extra charge symmetry of the complete spatial field theory.

Replacing b_0 by an undepleted coherent amplitude makes the daughter
Hamiltonian quadratic. Its vacuum solution is a two-mode squeezed state
 a_k(t)=alpha_k a_k(0)+beta_k a_-k^dagger(0),
 |alpha_k|^2-|beta_k|^2=1,
 n_k=|beta_k|^2, m_k=<a_k a_-k>, |m_k|^2=n_k(n_k+1).
For k and -k distinct,
 g2(k,k)=2, g2(k,-k)=2+1/n_k,
 Cov(N_k,N_-k)=n_k(n_k+1).                                             (3)
These formulas require the stated Gaussian/coherent-pump assumptions.

The difference matters even without interactions. With ONE retained pair
channel and an initial source number state |N>_b, the exact state is
 sum_{n=0}^N c_n(t)|N-n>_b |n>_k |n>_-k.
Tracing the pump gives sum |c_n|^2 |n,n><n,n|: it has exact pair-number
matching but is separable across these two daughter modes and has m_k=0.
In a multimode continuum there may be additional within-fixed-number
entanglement; the one-channel example does not deny it.

An especially sharp counterexample to inferring phase coherence from
occupation data is the phase-twirled squeezed state
 rho_twirl=(1-q)sum_{n>=0}q^n |n,n><n,n|, 0<q<1.
It has ALL the same joint number statistics as a pure two-mode squeezed
state with the same q, including (2)-(3)'s number correlations, but m_k=0
and is separable. The pure squeezed state has m_k!=0 and is entangled.
Thus pair counting alone cannot establish that production selected a
protected phase-coherent state. Both are compatible with the same action;
source preparation determines which reduction is appropriate.

4. The full action already breaks the putative pair protection

The full cubic includes all moving sources,
 sum_{P,k} h(P,k) b_P^dagger a_k a_{P-k}+h.c.,
and the daughter quartic includes
 H4=(k_tot/V)sum_{p1+p2=p3+p4}
        a_p1^dagger a_p2^dagger a_p3 a_p4,
 k_tot=lambda_tot/(4m^2) in its leading NR particle reduction.
Neither generally commutes with I_k. For the quartic monomial its
commutator is multiplied by
 s(p1)+s(p2)-s(p3)-s(p4),
 s(p)=1_{p=k}-1_{p=-k},
which need not vanish. U(1), total momentum and total energy remain conserved.

There is no all-state kinematic prohibition on ELASTIC cross-pair scattering.
Take two daughters with |k|=|p|=p*, E*=M/2, and choose
 cos(angle(k,p))=(E*-m)/(E*+m).
Then the exact relativistic reaction
 Phi(k)+Phi(p) -> Phi(0)+Phi(k+p)                                     (4)
conserves energy and momentum:
 |k+p|^2=4E*(E*-m), E_0+E_{k+p}=m+(2E*-m)=2E*.
If the initial cloud also contains the partners -k and -p, this event
unpairs their individual opposite-momentum occupations. It needs no bath.

The existing same-charge tree amplitude is
 M(s)=-4[lambda_tot+g^2/(s-M^2)].
For (4), s=4mE*=2mM and
 lambda_eff=lambda_min(r_lambda-2m/M),
 lambda_min=g^2/(M^2-4m^2), r_lambda=lambda_rep/lambda_min.              (5)
At the repository's r_lambda=2 benchmark this is nonzero. A special value
r_lambda=2m/M cancels this particular invariant energy at tree level,
but neighboring incoming angles change s. It cannot cancel the complete
elastic continuum when lambda_rep>0. The example is away from the source
pole, so this nonzero amplitude does not invoke a divergent bare resonance.

For any finite-regulator state initially supported on I_k=0,
 <I_k^2>(t)=(t^2/hbar^2)<[I_k,H]^dagger[I_k,H]>_initial+O(t^3).          (6)
This is the norm of I_k exp(-iHt/hbar)|Psi_initial> expanded at t=0.
The coefficient is positive whenever the commutator acts nontrivially.
A two-pair Fock component with momenta k,-k,p,-p has a nonzero matrix
element of H4 to the unpaired configuration of (4), so pair matching is
not an exact invariant even of the regulated production carrier model.
Equation (6) is a short-time statement, not a secular decoherence rate.

Moving-source formation also breaks individual I_k algebraically. Its
on-shell phase space must be checked: two daughters on an EXACT infinitely
thin resting-decay shell both have energy M/2, so producing an on-shell
moving X with nonzero P is impossible from that exact shell. Finite line
width, broadened populations and off-shell coherent mixing change this.
The commutator alone must not be presented as an unsuppressed on-shell rate.
Connected return of an original pair is part of source decay's quantum
memory, not an independent factorized f_k f_q inverse-collision process.

For a neutral source stock the already derived same-action hard process
 X+ X- -> Phi+ Phi-
has sigma v=beta_f g^4/(2 pi M^6), beta_f=sqrt(1-m^2/M^2), at source CM
threshold. It produces hard daughters with momentum sqrt(M^2-m^2), preserves
signed charge, and reduces the soft positive weighted stock. This is an
existing result, not rederived as a new production protection mechanism.
Pure-daughter 4->2 and other allowed channels must also be included in a
full neutral-population lifetime; no all-channel hierarchy is established here.

5. Conditional prediction from the same vertex, without a galaxy law

Let an independently specified coherent source profile be X_c(x,t). Define
its box Fourier average X_P(t)=V^(-1)integral X_c(x,t) exp(-iP.x)d^3x.
To first order in the production interaction, the distinct-mode pair
amplitude is
 beta_kq(t)=-i g/sqrt(E_k E_q) integral_0^t
             X_{k+q}(t') exp[i(E_k+E_q)t']dt'.                         (7)
For a homogeneous resting source this is supported on q=-k. A finite
source gives a total-pair-momentum width set by its actual Fourier profile,
not an arbitrary independently selected correlation length.

In the narrow-pole vacuum-decay approximation X_0(t)=A exp(-iMt-Gamma t/2),
 (Gamma is an energy width, natural-unit time), equation (7) gives
 n_k~(gA/E_k)^2/[Gamma^2/4+(2E_k-M)^2] after the source has depleted.     (8)
Here the weak-source/dilute approximation and the pole reduction must hold;
the existing finite-band unitary calculation supports that reduction only
in its stated window, not at the actual far threshold or all late times.
Equation (7), with the real preparation history, is the more general result.

The prediction is a joint momentum/energy correlation ridge
 k+q approximately P_source, E_k+E_q approximately E_X(P_source),
with SAME-charge daughter pairs for each source sign. For a resting source
the narrow-line radius is p*, and a width Gamma in the pair-energy detuning
corresponds locally to momentum width of order Gamma/(2v*). This distinction
matters near threshold, where the radial momentum shell need not remain
narrow if Gamma is comparable with M-2m.

A phase-insensitive observable, for matched opposite counting bins A,-A,
is R=Var(N_A-N_-A)/<N_A+N_-A>. In the ideal paired model R=0; independent
Poisson counts give R=1. For balanced independent one-particle survival
probability s and no incoming background, conditioning on any pair number
and applying binomial counting gives exactly
 R=1-s.                                                               (9)
This relation does not require a coherent pump or a Gaussian state. The
loss model describes a restricted bin-removal approximation. Correlated
two-particle losses, return into bins, finite acceptance and additional
populations change it; it is not an exact collision theorem for the action.

These are testable correlation observables of a declared production event.
They distinguish independent thermal mode occupations from pair-correlated
emission, but by the twirling counterexample do not alone prove entanglement
or persistent anomalous phase coherence. The existing dark action supplies
no ordinary-matter detector portal. No currently accessible laboratory count
rate, photon signal or particular astronomical survey prediction is claimed.

6. Lifetime/prethermal regime and the missing calculation

In a dilute, factorized quasiparticle regime with known daughter distribution
f(p,t), elastic bin removal is computed from THIS action's differential
cross section and actual bin acceptance. Schematically
 Gamma_out(k,t)=integral d^3p/(2pi)^3 f(p,t) v_rel
       integral dOmega (d sigma/dOmega)[1-1_A(k_out)],
with other charge/source species, stimulated final factors and gain terms
added when present. Away from the source pole,
 sigma(s)=lambda_eff(s)^2/(2 pi s)
for identical same-charge daughters in the stated convention. Near the
pole one must use a width/medium treatment and avoid counting real X
formation twice. Replacing this with the threshold sigma0 at every produced
momentum is not an admitted lifetime estimate.

If independent removal is justified, s=exp[-integral Gamma_out dt] in (9).
A desired R<=epsilon therefore needs
 integral Gamma_out dt<=-log(1-epsilon).                               (10)
This is a conditional, directly evaluable lifetime criterion once the source,
distribution and counting geometry are specified. It is not derived from
the words 'cold', 'charged', 'squeezed', or 'weak coupling'.

A parametric prethermal possibility is
 hbar/Gamma_dec << t_obs,
 integral_0^{t_obs} Gamma_out dt <<1,
 hard/inverse-channel defects <<1,
 and production mean-field/occupation corrections small.
At fixed masses/couplings and small prepared source density, ordinary
factorized two-body collision rates scale with density while the single-X
vacuum width does not. This can separate production from later unrelated
collisions. It is a dilute PREPARATION REGIME, not a selected finite-density
state or symmetry protection. Original-pair coherent return and threshold
memory need the quantum continuum treatment rather than this density scaling.

No numerical physical lifetime is predicted: n_X, source state, momentum
width, epoch, expansion and the postproduction spectrum were not supplied
by the action. A full correlation-inclusive Phi/X quantum evolution with
the actual threshold, quartic scattering and both signs is the smallest
remaining calculation for a quantitative coherence/survival claim. A
Boltzmann equation obtained by simply deleting anomalous correlations
would assume away the question it needs to answer.
