1. WHAT THE ORIGINAL ACTION PROTECTS

 V=m^2|Phi|^2+M^2|X|^2+g(X*Phi^2+c.c.)+lambda_tot|Phi|^4,
 M>2m, lambda_tot=lambda_rep+g^2/(M^2-4m^2), lambda_rep>0.

It is invariant under Phi->exp(i alpha)Phi, X->exp(2i alpha)X.

cannot evolve into a net-positive-Q universe under this closed action.
A positive-charge region can exist with compensating negative charge

follow merely from the single-sign branch solved here.

2. FINITE FIXED-Q QUANTUM MODEL: NONTRIVIAL DARK MANIFOLD, NO PROTECTION

Inclusion of the pinned negative-frequency threshold matching correction
changes kappa to lambda_rep/(4m^2)>0 in the matched dilute Hamiltonian.
These are different controlled approximations and are not silently equated.
The following algebra applies to either declared kappa>0 Hamiltonian.

Define F=sqrt(delta)b+c a^2/sqrt(delta V). Then EXACTLY within this
finite-mode normal-ordered model,

The annihilator F lowers Q by two. In the complete Q sector use
|j>=|N_a=Q-2j,N_b=j>, j=0,...,floor(Q/2). For Q>=2 its matrix maps
dimension floor(Q/2)+1 to dimension floor(Q/2), and its diagonal band has
nonzero entries. Thus rank F=floor(Q/2), and dim ker F=1.
Writing |D_Q>=sum_j u_j |j>, F|D_Q>=0 is equivalent to

 u_(j+1)=-(c/(delta sqrt(V)))
          sqrt[(Q-2j)(Q-2j-1)/(j+1)] u_j,
 u_j=u_0[-c/(delta sqrt(V))]^j
          sqrt[Q!/((Q-2j)!j!)].                            (2)

For c>0 EVERY allowed u_j is nonzero. This is a nontrivial atom-molecule
superposition, not a pure-species state. But it is not stationary under
the existing Hamiltonian: by (1), H|D_Q>=mQ|D_Q>+(kappa/V)A|D_Q>, where
A=a^dagger^2a^2 has eigenvalues (Q-2j)(Q-2j-1). These eigenvalues are
strictly distinct along j for Q>=2, and |D_Q> has support on every j.
Therefore A|D_Q> is not proportional to |D_Q>. Equivalently its contact
variance is strictly positive. No F-dark stationary state exists for

At the extra tuning kappa=0 the same |D_Q> WOULD have H=mQ, but that
tuning is absent for the specified lambda_rep>0 action, and F is not a

The exact same conclusion follows for a proposed pure-atom dark state:
b^dagger a^2 acts nontrivially whenever Q>=2. A pure-molecule state likewise

There IS a real finite-mode quantum ground state. H_Q is a Hermitian

3. STABLE POSITIVE-CHARGE HOMOGENEOUS BRANCH

beta<=cn/delta and mu>=2(k-c^2/delta)n=2kappa n>0, a contradiction.

 s^2-[J L+z t]s+det(A)z[Jd+z d_a d_b]=0.                   (7)

5. LIFETIME: CHARGE DOES NOT PROTECT THE CONVERSION MODE

The cubic allows X_+->Phi_+ Phi_+ and conserves Q=2. The exact tree vacuum
decay width from the original action, including identical daughters, is

 Gamma_X=g^2/(8piM) sqrt(1-4m^2/M^2),
 p_*=sqrt(M^2/4-m^2).                                    (12)

For delta<<m and the same NR vertex c,

 Gamma_X=c^2 m^(3/2) sqrt(delta)/pi[1+O(delta/m)].          (13)

Direct derivation: at zero total momentum the pair creation matrix element
is 2c; the identical-state factor is 1/2. Golden rule gives
2pi(1/2)(2c)^2 integral d^3p/(2pi)^3 delta(delta-p^2/m),
which evaluates to (13). The conversion-mode eigenvector tends to the
molecule-like excitation and Omega_g tends to delta as n tends to zero.
The two-daughter continuum remains open, so this mode has a nonzero
perturbative width approaching (12) in the dilute limit, provided the
resonance is narrow and the thermodynamic/continuum decay limit applies.

At finite condensate density the final modes are Bogoliubov excitations,
the resonant momentum and vertices shift, and Bose occupation can enhance
decay. No all-density linewidth or non-Markovian time profile was derived
here. The primary Pimenov/Mueller (2024) paper already identifies this
pair-production damping mechanism in atom-molecule condensates.
Finite boxes instead have discrete exact eigenstates and recurrences;
zero width of a closed-box exact eigenstate is not symmetry protection
of the prepared mean-field oscillation.

The gapless phonon is a distinct case: it is protected against a mass gap
by U(1), but not against allowed interactions/damping at nonzero momentum.
No exact coherent-state lifetime or condensation rate follows merely from
Q conservation. Small g makes (12) small; that is a free coupling hierarchy,
not a new conserved quantum number.
