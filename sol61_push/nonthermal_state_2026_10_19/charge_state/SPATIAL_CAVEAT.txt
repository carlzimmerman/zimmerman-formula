NQ-2026-10-19-1 continuation; coordinator-requested short discriminator.

1. HIGHER PARTIAL WAVES

For two identical spin-zero Phi particles in their center-of-mass frame,
bosonic symmetry allows even orbital angular momentum l. A regular relative
wavefunction behaves as r^l Y_lm near coincidence. The local leading NR
quartic contact and local Phi-Phi-X conversion sample its value at r=0;
therefore l>=2 states have zero overlap with both at that order. Total
angular momentum also forbids an isolated J>=2 two-body state converting
into a single spin-zero X in its rest frame. This is a genuine spatial
selection rule absent from the common-mode model, not an evasion of its
proof within that model.

Without a trap or gravity these are scattering states, not localized bound
objects or an automatically prepared quantum state. Full relativistic loop
matching can generate finite-range/derivative elastic amplitudes in higher
partial waves. Thus zero response to a leading delta contact is not an
all-order protection theorem for the complete interacting spatial theory.

2. EXISTING GRAVITY DOES GIVE A KNOWN CHARGE-PROTECTED DIMER GROUND STATE

Including ordinary Newton gravity already present in the larger framework,
the relative two-Phi Hamiltonian in the weak-gravity, low-momentum regime is

 H_rel=p^2/(2mu_red)-Gm^2/r+g2 delta^3(r)+higher EFT terms,
 mu_red=m/2, g2=lambda_rep/(2m^2), hbar=c_light=1.           (S1)

The contact is the fully threshold-matched dilute interaction; replacing
it by the older uncorrected RWA contact would be a different approximation.
For alpha_G=Gm^2<<1 and a_B much larger than the source interaction range,
the leading gravitational Coulomb problem has

 a_B=2/(Gm^3), E_n=-G^2m^5/(4n^2)=-E_B/n^2,
 E_B=G^2m^5/4.                                            (S2)

Finite-range repulsion does not remove all these levels: high enough
radial/orbital states live far from its support while the attractive 1/r
tail remains. In the weak-contact regime even the ground state has the
ordinary small positive contact shift discussed below. This is an actual
internal bound-state construction from existing gravity, rather than merely
asserting eigenstate stationarity.

The same-charge ground state has Q=2 and total energy below 2m. Charge
forbids annihilation into neutral gravitons/ordinary products; energy forbids
ionization into two free Phi and conversion into the heavier M>2m scalar X.
Subject to the stated weak-gravity EFT and isolated-system assumptions,
its internal ground state is stable. Uniform gravity translates its COM;
its internal gravitational response is tidal. This does not supply a
uniform-acceleration dipole or a MOND constitutive interaction.

The lowest d-wave has principal n=3. Its leading contact shift vanishes,
but it can radiate a quadrupole graviton and settle to the 1s state:

 omega_(3d,1s)=(E_3-E_1)=8E_B/9=2G^2m^5/9.               (S3)

The transition is allowed by angular momentum. Simple quadrupole power
counting gives Gamma~G omega^5 m^2 a_B^4~G^7m^15; no exact coefficient
or numerical lifetime is asserted here. The neutral-real-scalar atom
annihilation rates in the literature cannot be imported for a same-charge
Phi-Phi ground state. The source's radiative transition and binding-energy
mechanisms, rather than neutral annihilation, are the relevant comparison.

At first order in the matched short-range contact,

 delta E_nS=g2 |psi_nS(0)|^2=g2/(pi n^3 a_B^3),
 delta E_1S/E_B=lambda_rep alpha_G/(4pi).                   (S4)

This applies when the renormalized contact pseudopotential approximation,
small scattering-length/a_B and binding energy small relative to detuning
are controlled. It is not a short-distance nonperturbative theorem. A
3d-to-1s line receives the negative shift -delta E_1S because its final
ground-state energy is raised. Mass, coupling and an actual prepared
abundance remain free inputs; the line is known Coulomb/contact physics.

These are enormous internal length/time scales, not a useful present
spectroscopic signal at that mass. No cosmic Hubble value, formation yield,
ambient tidal field or thermal population was assumed. Their applicability
to an isolated object in an actual cosmological environment remains open;
an enormous ideal Coulomb radius cannot be treated as a realized halo atom.

Preparation has an allowed channel: two scattering Phi can radiatively
capture into a bound state while emitting a graviton. Pure unitary closed
two-body dynamics without an energy receiver cannot capture. The capture
matrix element, state abundance and survival in the environment were not
calculated here. Existence/protection is distinct from production.
