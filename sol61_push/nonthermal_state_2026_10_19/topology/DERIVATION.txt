1. WHAT THE SYMMETRY ACTUALLY PROTECTS

The exact global symmetry is (Phi,X)->(e^{i theta}Phi,e^{2i theta}X), conserving Q. Because the original potential is a sum of positive mass terms and nonnegative squares, its zero-density vacuum is uniquely Phi=X=0. The vacuum manifold is a point. There are no vacuum S1 windings or vacuum domain-wall sectors protecting a finite isolated lump.

At prescribed nonzero mixed amplitudes, the conversion energy is 2g|X||Phi|^2 cos(theta_X-2theta_Phi). For g>0 it fixes theta_X-2theta_Phi=pi modulo2pi. A loop entirely in an occupied bulk with both fields nonzero must therefore have integer windings N_X=2N_Phi. The mixed-phase manifold is S1, and bulk vortices are possible. A charge-two-only condensate has the familiar residual Z2 and permits a molecular vortex corresponding to a half winding of the common charge-one phase; in the mixed phase a lone such half vortex needs a zero-amplitude wall or relative-phase defect. These are known atom-molecule superfluid facts, not a new astrophysical prediction.

For FINITE isolated stock the amplitudes vanish at the outer boundary/infinity. A vortex can move through that zero-amplitude region without changing Q. The nonvanishing-bulk boundary condition required for a conserved winding is absent. Specifying a vortex initially is therefore not deriving a permanently protected nonthermal state. Charge conservation does not imply phase coherence, a thermalization barrier, vortex lifetime or an occupied many-body ground state.

For the phase-locked axisymmetric ansatz Phi=f(r,theta)e^{i(N phi-omega t)}, X=-h(r,theta)e^{2i(N phi-omega t)}, canonical stress and current give the usual J_z=N Q (restore hbar when Q counts quanta). This remains a known spinning-boson relation. It fixes neither Q nor the metric multipoles, and does not by itself yield an observable population or a new gravitational force law.

2. LOCAL HOMOGENEOUS MIXED BRANCH OF THE ACTUAL POTENTIAL

Set Phi=sqrt(n)e^{-i omega tau}, X=-h e^{-2i omega tau}, n,h>0. tau is local proper time, so omega is the local charge chemical potential. Define

Stationarity gives

 [[8lambda n,-4g sqrt n],[-4g sqrt n,2D]],

Writing r=lambda_rep/(g^2/Delta), eta=r/(1+r), M=2m sqrt(1+u_star^2),

The same branch's charge, pressure and energy density are

Stationarity/envelope differentiation gives dp/domega=q, depsilon/domega=omega dq/domega>0, and

Therefore 0<c_s^2<1 on the finite branch. This is the coherent hydrodynamic sound speed at leading derivative order; it is not the full frequency-dependent two-field Bogoliubov spectrum or the gas's kinetic velocity dispersion.

At low density,

Pressure stays positive and grows with density. This is not the usual generalized Chaplygin gas p=-A/rho^alpha; the sign and density power differ. No Chaplygin identification is used.
