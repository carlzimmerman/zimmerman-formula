Claim 2: exact homogeneous equation of state of that mean-field branch

Along this branch dp/domega=q and depsilon/domega=omega dq/domega>0.
The positivity follows because n and h increase with omega. In particular
dp/(epsilon+p)=domega/omega and the sound speed is q/(omega q')>0.
This is a barotropic coherent EOS, not the independently postulated galaxy
medium and not a claimed exact quantum effective potential at arbitrary density.
