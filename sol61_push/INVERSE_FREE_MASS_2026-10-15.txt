CLAIM AND HYPOTHESES
Consider static spherical aligned fields, g_b>0, a0>0, outside stiffness-
dominated regions. Let g_f=G M_f(<r)/r^2 be an additional gravitating source
which does not itself carry the tether response. Assume the SAME active
medium responds to the combined ordinary source A=g_b+g_f, so that
    A = g^2/(g+a0).
This is a conditional extension, not an established consequence of the
currently unspecified S4 phase. Charged remnants, phase boundaries, finite
stock constraints and material-frame changes can invalidate this premise.

Define the acceleration scale inferred using observed baryons alone:
    a_app(r) = g(r) [g(r)-g_b(r)] / g_b(r).
Then the exact inverse equation is
    M_f(<r)/M_b(<r) = [a_app(r)-a0]/[g(r)+a0].

PROOF
The assumed law gives g^2=(g_b+g_f)(g+a0). Subtract g_b*g:
    g(g-g_b) = a0*g_b + g_f*(g+a0).
Divide by g_b, subtract a0, and divide by positive g+a0.
Spherical Newtonian source fields give g_f/g_b=M_f/M_b.
Verdict: proved under the displayed conditional source/response assignment.
The proof is universal in its stated positive scalar domain; finite checks
below are supporting arithmetic only.

POSITIVE-DENSITY TEST
Define F(r)=M_b(<r)[a_app(r)-a0]/[g(r)+a0]. An actual positive free
population requires both F(r)>=0 and F'(r)>=0, wherever differentiable:
    rho_f(r) = F'(r)/(4*pi*r^2).
Thus positive residuals alone are insufficient: their inferred enclosed
mass must also increase with radius. Negative a_app-a0 cannot be attributed
to positive extra source mass under this assignment.

CONSEQUENCES
1. Exact adherence to the baryon-only canonical law implies M_f=0 inside
   that radius while this same response branch remains active.
2. If 0<=a_app-a0<=delta*a0, then
       M_f/M_b <= delta/(1+g/a0) <= delta.
   Delta is a stipulated tolerance in acceleration scale, not measured
   acceleration scatter or an empirical confidence interval.
3. A substantial free population cannot simply be placed inside the same
   responding phase while preserving the original baryon-only curve.
   A replacement requires an explicit changed phase, response suppression,
   altered coupling, or a prediction which departs from that curve.
4. An exterior spherical shell has g_f=0 inside it; the theorem does not
   exclude such shells, nor identify a projected lensing excess with a
   local three-dimensional acceleration. Projection must be calculated.

SIMPLE INVERSE ALREADY CONTAINED IN THE CANONICAL LAW
With no additional source, define M_t=r^2*(g-g_b)/G. Then
    (g-g_b)/a0 = M_b/(M_b+M_t).
The tether acceleration in units of a0 equals the enclosed baryon fraction.
Equivalently M_t*(M_b+M_t)=a0*M_b*r^2/G. For a point baryon mass,
M_t/M_b=(sqrt(1+4*x^2)-1)/2, x=r/r_M. This is a rearrangement of ARGON02,
not a newly independent prediction or a derivation of abundance.
At x=10,20,30 the required ratios are 9.51249,19.50625,29.50417.
They exceed the assigned 5.364 stock by 4.14849,14.14225,24.14017 Mb.
Those differences are budget deficits of that profile, not measured debris.

CHECKS AND LIMITS

a0 in {1/2,1,3}; g in {1/100,1/10,1,10,100}; g_f/A in
{0,1/100,1/10,1/2,9/10}. Exact inversion, sign and tether-fraction checks
all passed. This was a self-check, not independent review or an empirical fit.
No full S4 action, relativistic lensing calculation or observational inversion
was executed. Baryon errors, external fields, nonspherical geometry and
nonequilibrium can imitate violations; this formula alone identifies no species.
