# Review record

Self-review, not independent review. CCT-005 is preserved unchanged.

Load-bearing controls: analytic matched-Plummer projected dispersion (maximum relative error 1.64e-4); integrated virial for beta=0/-0.5 (errors <8.4e-5); direct refined cold kernel at the winning scales; absolute normalization rejects doubled gravity; all selected IDs and source hashes match; all reported best fits/profile tests converged; post-impulse orbit energy errors <3e-8. RSS remains below 100 MB.

Corrections during development: the first taper test used untapered surface-density population weights; corrected to the projected tapered density before interpreting that case. Added explicit broad velocity-window normalization after perspective correction. A 31-scale interpolation had ~0.8% kernel error; refined to 121 scales, directly verified <0.1%. Coarse-grid-only mass profiles had artificial likelihood bumps; continuous scale minimization replaces them. Initial-baryon bookkeeping originally compared total formed stellar mass to a one-time reservoir; corrected to account for the possibility of recycling. Earlier intermediate outputs were overwritten by final verified runs; no broad claims were published from them.

Finite scope: stars have a spatially irregular, preselected, membership-filtered sampling; conditioning on positions does not remove velocity-dependent targeting or population-dependent magnitude selection. Radius/covariance, metallicity-distribution uncertainty, field calibration, binaries, tidal/external-field terms, and a dynamically positive stellar DF are not fully fitted. Constant tangential anisotropy is one sensitivity, not arbitrary anisotropy coverage. CDS and paper sample counts/quality wording are not exactly reconciled; SOURCES.md preserves that issue. Do not call this the published authors' fit or an observational exclusion probability.

Capture inequalities use prescribed favorable timing, assumed primordial ratio and catchments; no capture probability, coherent-field bound-mode transfer, gas feedback source, phase support, angular-momentum export or tidal survival has been computed. Stellar growth tests are counterfactual circular ensembles. Neither their contraction nor an adiabatic action argument determines the unknown stellar birth sizes. All positive-mechanism claims remain open.

The final energy extension includes the incoming energy of the escaped mass: q=(e_in-e_bound)/(e_out-e_in), not (e_in-e_bound)/e_out. A deliberate omission mutation fails the full initial/final energy ledger. The virial energy uses the Newtonian stellar potential for cold material, with its external virial term; it does not apply the baryonic Xi force to cold mass. A stationary target energy is only a necessary condition; existence/stability of that target for one universal eta is not verified.
