IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION LIMITS
The rows use absolute fraction-of-total mass error. As radial bins narrow,
the same density discrepancy can produce smaller per-bin errors. Halving t
is NOT evidence of continuum convergence by itself. The next discriminator
must fix physical cumulative-mass test radii while refining the orbit set,
and preferably bound a separating dual functional over continuous orbits.
Likewise the quadrature condition1e-5 is looser than the LP gate1e-6; the
freeze is preserved and those marginal IC-B failures are INCONCLUSIVE.

The orbit set excludes pericentres below .001r_* and apocentres beyond r_*.
J is binned, and finite sampling of E,L is not all possible stationary DFs.
A feasible binned marginal need not reproduce the exact continuous marginal.
No inference that non-spherical torques are required in the actual theory.
Target mass/Mcat is1.000067 to1.000186. Shape normalization was declared in
advance; supply and energy are NOT tested. Failed LP solutions can change
their total mass slightly as part of the minimax residual; those solutions
are not physical candidates, and their reported mass is retained.
