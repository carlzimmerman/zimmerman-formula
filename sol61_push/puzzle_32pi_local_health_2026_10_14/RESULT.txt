1. New local-source obligation

Use c=hbar=1, M^2=(8piG)^-1, V=2CM^2a^2,
f=-3M^2/(16Ca^2), C>0, a>0. The scalar equation at constant a is
 V'(a)=f'(a) E4,
with f'=3M^2/(8Ca^3)>0. At two points with distinct E4 this equation cannot
hold at the same a unless another varying term compensates. Subtracting the
two equations proves it. A P-gradient stiffness term by itself cannot cancel
an Euler-curvature source when P=0. Consequently CFG770's screened P field
does not, by itself, certify screening of this new scalar. A full common
metric/clock/P action remains necessary.

For an illustrative Schwarzschild-de Sitter metric, with central mass m,
 E4 = 24 H_Lambda^4/c^4 + 48(Gm/c^2)^2/r^6,
 excess/background = 2[ Gm/(r^3 H_Lambda^2) ]^2.
At 1 AU from the Sun the ratio is 2.94e44 canonical / 1.40e44 alternative.
At 10 kpc from a hypothetical 1e11 solar-mass point source it is 3.82e10 /
1.81e10. These are fixed-metric diagnostics; the galactic example is not a
realistic galaxy profile or an observational score. Both footings are kept
separate. H_Lambda^2=32pi a0^2/(3c^2) is imposed for this candidate test.

Ignoring scalar gradients and backreaction, the local equilibrium would obey
 a_local^4=3 E4/(32C^2),
hence a_local/a_background=(1+excess/background)^(1/4): about 1.31e11 at
1 AU on the canonical footing, or 442 for that galaxy illustration.
Such large changes invalidate an expansion about constant a on that fixed
metric. They are not predictions of a self-consistent solution and are not
an exclusion of nonlinear screening. The exact constant-scalar obstruction
is stronger evidence than extrapolating these numbers into a false solution.

2. Repair attempt: remove sensitivity to tidal/Weyl curvature

In four dimensions, writing W2 for the Weyl square,
 E4-W2=(2/3)R^2-2 Ricci^2.
This removes the central mass's Weyl contribution in an Einstein exterior.
But the metric action now contains a nonzero curvature-squared spin-2
operator: at constant a, f E4 is topological and -f W2 remains. A fourth-order
spin-2 propagator generically has opposite residues, as checked by
 1/[z(A+Bz)] = (1/A)[1/z-1/(z+A/B)].
At special curved-background critical parameters the poles can degenerate;
this identity is not a calculation of that curved spectrum. No healthy
repair is established by merely subtracting W2. Standard quadratic-gravity
mode analysis, rather than the original scalar-Euler health, applies.

The simplest alternative avoiding Ricci-squared is I=R^2/6. It equals E4
on de Sitter but is blind to Weyl curvature. For
 P(R,a)=M^2R/2 -2CM^2a^2 +f(a) R^2/6,
both constant-field equations still hold at R=8Ca^2. However the ordinary
tensor kinetic coefficient is
 2 P_R = M^2+(2/3)fR = 0.
This loses the usual graviton quadratic kinetic term; it is not a viable
Einstein-like vacuum.

The obstruction is not specific to R^2. For the entire restricted metric
class P=M^2 a^2 F(x), x=R/a^2, with a constant scalar, nonzero constant R,
no matter, and no extra derivative terms, the two stationary conditions are
 F-xF'=0,             [scalar variation]
 xF'-2F=0.            [metric trace]
They force F=F'=0. Thus P_R=0. This rules out a positive tensor coefficient
in this scale-only Ricci class. Extra scales, non-Ricci curvature couplings,
additional fields, varying backgrounds or derivative terms change the class;
there is no universal modified-gravity no-go here.

3. Actual repair retained for further testing: stabilize the scale

Add to the ORIGINAL scalar-Euler action, not the failed Ricci replacement,
 U_stab=(mu2/2)(a-b)^2, mu2>0, b>0.
It vanishes with its first derivative at a=b, so the chosen de Sitter branch
and beta=-3/(16C) are unchanged there. These are new inputs: b sets the
chosen scale and mu2 sets restoring strength. Neither is derived.

The coupled homogeneous restoring coefficient becomes exactly mu2, while
 D=1+6M^2/b^2 remains positive. Hence
 D(delta a_ddot+3H delta a_dot)+mu2 delta a=0,
 m_eff^2=mu2/D>0.
The neutral mode is removed. This is a real local stability improvement,
not a coefficient derivation.

A self-review of the full quadratic unpolarized de Sitter scalar-metric
block gives the same result in regular scalar variables. At constant a=b,
the scalar-Euler mixing is equivalent at this order to scalar-tensor mixing
 F1 delta a delta R/2, F1=8H^2 f'=2M^2/b.
Using delta E4=4H^2 delta R on a maximally symmetric background and including
the volume and potential variations gives a quadratic scalar-tensor surrogate
 F_eff=M^2+F1 delta a,
 U_eff=V0+2V0' delta a+(V0''-24H^4f0''+mu2)(delta a)^2/2.
Its Einstein-frame kinetic coefficient is D and its mass numerator is
 U_eff''-2F1^2 V0/M^4=mu2. The tensor coefficient at the EXACT constant
vacuum remains M^2. This is a restricted vacuum quadratic derivation, not
a certificate for occupied P, arbitrary curvature or time-dependent states.
The next calculation shows why that distinction matters.

An independent constant vacuum shift still changes the ratio:
 Lambda/a^2=2C+[mu2(a-b)^2/2+Delta]/(M^2a^2).
For Delta>0 it exceeds 2C at every finite positive a, even after adjusting
the scalar. At first order the stationary scalar shifts by
 delta a=4 Delta/(b mu2),
so stabilization permits response but does not protect the coefficient.

4. Coupled nonlinear homogeneous evolution, with tensor checks

Dimensionless variables: x=a/b, tau=H_* t, h=H/H_*, u=dx/dtau,
eps=b^2/M^2>0, nu=m_eff^2/H_*^2, H_*^2=2Cb^2/3.
Define
 U=V_total/(M^2 H_*^2)=3x^2+(eps+6)nu(x-1)^2/2,
 F=f H_*^2/M^2=-1/(8x^2), Fx=1/(4x^3), Fxx=-3/(4x^4).
The actual equations integrated in evolve.py are
 [eps, -24Fx h^2; 8Fx h^2, 2+16hFx u] [u';h']
   =[-3eps h u-Ux+24Fx h^4;
     -eps u^2+8h^3Fx u-8h^2Fxx u^2],
 x'=u.
The independent Friedmann constraint monitored is
 3h^2=eps u^2/2+U-24h^3Fx u.
The tensor kinetic and gradient coefficients, divided by M^2, are
 Q_T=1+8hFx u,
 F_T=1+8(Fx u'+Fxx u^2), c_T^2=F_T/Q_T.

Eight trajectories: eps=1e-120 or 1e-6; nu=1 or 10; initial x=.9 or 1.1,
u=0, h=sqrt(U/3); tau in [0,30]. No source data fitted.
All eight homogeneous trajectories converge to x=h=1, u=0 within 2.4e-6.
Their maximum relative Friedmann residual is below 1.0e-9. This alone
would have produced a false broad 'healthy' conclusion.

For nu=1, all four sampled trajectories retain Q_T>=0.959 and F_T>=0.853.
Their speed-squared deviations reach 0.265: this is NOT a present-day
multi-messenger pass. Exact late-time equilibrium has c_T=1; whether the
universe is close enough to it is untested. These trajectories are finite
evidence for homogeneous relaxation and tensor signs, not a proven basin
or general spatial health certificate; the scalar check below adds a finite
principal-part test.

For nu=10, all four have negative F_T during their evolution, with Q_T>0.
Minimum F_T is about -1.307 for x0=.9 and -0.2575 for x0=1.1. This is a
tensor gradient instability on those homogeneous backgrounds, despite their
successful relaxation. It is a physical failed gate, not a solver failure.
The eight integrations were not pre-labelled as health passes; the script's
assertions check relaxation and constraint control, and the raw tensor
failures are retained explicitly.

Controls: nu=0 leaves x=1.1 unchanged (the old neutral family); nu=-1 moves
away from the chosen x=1 vacuum (it approaches a different stationary state,
not claimed to run to infinity). A tighter integration at nu=10,x0=1.1
agrees in final x to <1e-7. No larger scan was used to hide the failed cases.

5. Exact tensor counterexample and a sharper admissibility gate

At initial rest u=0 the constraint yields h^2=U/3 and Q_T=1. Solving the
two evolution equations algebraically gives
 F_T=[eps x^6+2U^2-2Ux x^3]/[eps x^6+(2/3)U^2].
For eps=0, nu=10, x=11/10 this is EXACTLY -4419/17161 <0.
It remains negative at eps=1/1,000,000, proved by exact rational arithmetic
and in Lean. Thus this failure does not rely on setting eps to zero or on
an ODE tolerance. For eps=0 near x=1,
 F_T=1-2nu(x-1)+O((x-1)^2).
Making the restoring force arbitrarily stiff does not preserve a uniformly
healthy neighbourhood in this parameterization. The admissibility test at
rest is explicitly
 eps x^6+2U^2-2Ux x^3 >0.
Positivity must also be monitored throughout evolution, not only initially.
This is the new load-bearing gate for any continuation of this repair.

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.10022v2
No slow-roll approximation is used in these tests.

scalar speed squared stay positive at all sampled points in all four runs.

the tensor failure remains. eps=1e-6 produces essentially identical signs.

Thus the gentle candidate survives BOTH sampled principal-part checks.
This is not a proof of positivity between samples or throughout a basin.
Some scalar speeds exceed one; tensor speeds also differ from one during
