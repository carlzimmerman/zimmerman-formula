# A concrete remaining channel: work and torque from a live source

The tests do not prove that a coherent field cannot form a core. They show that the present isolated-wave trial has not removed enough internal energy, and a common slowdown does not supply the desired initial-energy selector. A static elastic filter supplies no absorptive receiver in its tested approximation. A useful next mechanism must therefore specify an actual energy/angular-momentum ledger, not an arbitrary damping or retention factor.

One conditional route is pre-stellar capture during nonaxisymmetric gas assembly. In a Newtonian wave-plus-live-matter extension, write

    i eta psi_t = -(eta²/2) Laplacian psi + (Phi_c+Phi_b) psi,
    Laplacian Phi_c = 4 pi G rho_c,
    Laplacian Phi_b = 4 pi G rho_b.

There is one total potential and each physical density is sourced once. For isolated all-space integrals, assign the interaction energy once:

    E_c = K_c + (1/2) integral rho_c Phi_c + integral rho_c Phi_b,
    E_b = K_b + U_b + (1/2) integral rho_b Phi_b.

Then, without radiation or boundary flux,

    dE_c/dt = integral rho_c partial_t Phi_b,
    dE_b/dt = -integral rho_c partial_t Phi_b,
    dJ_c,z/dt = -integral rho_c partial_phi Phi_b,
    dJ_b,z/dt = +integral rho_c partial_phi Phi_b.

The energy relation follows from the cold wave equation and Poisson reciprocity: integral rho_c Phi_b,t = integral Phi_c rho_b,t = integral j_b dot grad Phi_c, opposite the work of the cold force on matter. These are bookkeeping identities for this conditional extension, not a derived current-theory coupling or a successful history. Gas internal energy, radiation and outflow must be carried in the matter ledger. For finite apertures, derive the matching surface/cross-field flux rather than transplanting the self-only current unchanged.

A deepening source potential can do negative work on the cross-inclusive cold energy. That can reflect external confinement while internal phase motion actually increases; it is not by itself independent self-binding or cooling. Keep self-energy, phase motion and interaction energy separate. A spherical source cannot provide angular torque: source asymmetry/mergers and an identified receiver are needed for net spin transfer. Subsequent gas removal can undo capture; the entire assembly/removal history and outgoing waves must be followed. Scheduling the change before star formation could avoid some stellar heating, but this is an obligation to test against actual histories, not a guarantee.

The next bounded experiment should freeze a live-source model and source budget FIRST. Check equal-and-opposite work/torque, recoil, radiation/outflow and delayed recapture. Use the same derived source/selection rule in all four dwarfs, including their observed history/profile uncertainties. A prescribed potential can test response but cannot establish receiver capacity; its integrated work has to be charged to real gas/envelope mass. The current cut-window results specify an energetic deficit, not a permission to change the observed mass budget.

Alternatively, the isolated field could still emit enough positive-energy/spin-carrying waves to leave a bound subset; the present data have not certified that channel. Any proposed receiver must be evaluated in the SAME action. The owner's Palatini filter, current gravity model and conditional CCT Schrodinger-Poisson action are not interchangeable without a derivation. No particle collision, extra cold force, deleted wave mass or ad hoc damping is introduced by these equations.
