1. ODE normalization and regular-singular endpoint: passed

In G=a0=Mb_total=1 units, Plummer enclosed baryonic mass and acceleration are
 Q(r)=r^3/(r^2+A^2)^(3/2), b(r)=Q/r^2=r/(r^2+A^2)^(3/2).
With u=r v, m=r^2u=r^3v and r=E x, z=dv/dx,
 Delta_vector u=r v_rr+4v_r,
 -H Delta_vector u+u^2/(1-u)=b
is exactly equivalent to
 v_x=z,
 z_x=E^2/H [r v^2/(1-rv)-(r^2+A^2)^(-3/2)]-4z/x.
The nonsingular part is the implementation's fun; solve_bvp's S[1,1]=-4
supplies the singular part. Its endpoint condition z(0)=0 is consistent with
the explicitly listed first boundary condition and leaves v(0) free.
There is no division by r at the numerical origin and no missing angular
-2u/r^2 term. The regular local expansion gives
 v=v0-r^2/(10 H A^3)+O(r^3),
 z_x(0)=-E^2/(5 H A^3),
confirming the factor five induced by the regular singular equation.

The logarithmic edge parameter enforces E>0. The linear hydrostatic seed solves
the R(u)=0 equation exactly and is used only for initialization; all accepted
rows solve the nonlinear rational R. Its small-r J expansion has the correct
coefficients through r^9. Thus the seed is not being mistaken for evidence
that the nonlinear equation is solved.

3. Zero-stiffness edge and vacuum chemical inequality: passed

At a smooth taper the necessary inequality is
 R(S/E^2)<=Q(E)/E^2,
equivalently Q(E)(E^2-S)-S^2>=0. zero_edge solves precisely the equality on
E>sqrt(S). The function is strictly increasing there because Q'>0 and Q>0,
so the equality has a unique root for the positive Plummer source. The accepted
E>E0 ensures a strictly inward exterior test-material drive at the edge.

For every r>=E the code's force_mass is r^2 times that drive:
 F_mass(r)=S^2/(r^2-S)-Q(r).
Its derivative is
 F_mass'=-2rS^2/(r^2-S)^2-Q'(r)<0.
Thus the edge sign analytically extends to all exterior radii, not merely the
500 points between E and 100E. Plummer's noncompact positive baryonic tail
strengthens this inequality. The exterior is vacuum for the settled material,
not an exterior freely varied independent polarization EL. This is exactly
the changed admissibility premise required to escape CE079's obstruction.

4. Integrated field equation and tail energy identity: passed

The regular-singular equation implies
 (r^4 v_r)'=r^3/H [R(rv)-b(r)].
The code integrates the right side and compares it with E^3 z(1), which equals
E^4 v_r(E). Both factors and signs are correct. The largest recorded absolute
integrated-equation discrepancy over all 94 solves is 5.8146153137e-7, below
the specified relative/absolute gate. This is a useful orthogonal integral
consistency check of the computed solution, though it uses the same spline.

The exact identity underlying the two quadratures is
 r^2 u_r^2+2u^2=m_r^2/r^2-2(m^2/r^3)'.
For a regular center and finite stock,
 H/2 int_0^infinity [r^2u_r^2+2u^2]dr
  =H/2 int_0^E m_r^2/r^2 dr
  =int_0^E 4pi r^2 (2pi H rho^2)dr.
For u=S/r^2 the exterior gradient contribution is exactly H S^2/E^3.
The implementation includes that tail in eg. Without it, the equality would
fail by the edge term H S^2/E^3, so its inclusion is material rather than a
cosmetic normalization. The two independently written quadrature integrands
agree to a largest saved absolute error 1.4311817287e-9 over the 90 continuation
profiles, within the stated gate. This verifies the global radial identity;
it does not create material density in the exterior or derive the microscopic
inertia of the original independent-P action.
