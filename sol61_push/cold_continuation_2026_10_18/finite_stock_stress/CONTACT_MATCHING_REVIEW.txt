The factors of 2,4pi,G, hbar and c in the displayed contact matching and

Exact natural-unit derivation, hbar=c=1
Take the assumed leading homogeneous mean-field contact energy
 e= lambda_NR n^2/(4m^2), rho=mn.
Then
 e=lambda_NR rho^2/(4m^4)=kappa rho^2/2,
 kappa=lambda_NR/(2m^4),
 p=rho de/drho-e=kappa rho^2/2.

The separately derived physical radial stiffness identity is
 E_H=(H/(2G)) int[r^2u'^2+2u^2]dr
    =int 2pi G H rho^2 d^3x,
with u=Gm_enclosed/r^2 and regular-center/finite-stock endpoint terms zero.
Equating the *internal-energy density*, rather than its derivative or twice
its pressure, gives
 2pi G H_contact=kappa/2,
 H_contact=kappa/(4piG)=lambda_NR/(8piGm^4),
 L_contact^2=2H_contact=lambda_NR/(4piGm^4).
These are exactly the reviewed formulas. If an independent field stiffness h
also remains present, the total longitudinal coefficient is h+H_contact;
the scalar contact term alone is not the complete L unless h=0.

Natural dimensions provide a separate check:
 [n]=E^3, [m]=E, [rho]=[e]=E^4, [lambda_NR]=1,
 [kappa]=E^-4, [G]=E^-2, [H]=[L^2]=E^-2.

SI restoration and independent conversion
Here rho_SI is rest mass per volume, m_kg is the particle rest mass in kg,
and e_SI is energy per volume. Then
 kappa_SI=lambda_NR hbar^3/(2m_kg^4 c),
 [kappa_SI]=m^5/(kg s^2),
 H_SI=kappa_SI/(4piG_SI),
 L_SI^2=lambda_NR hbar^3/(4piG_SI m_kg^4 c).

If particle mass is entered as its rest energy in GeV, the reviewed code uses
 G_GeV^-2=G_SI (1GeV_J)^2/(hbar c^5),
 (1GeV^-1)_metres=hbar c/(1GeV_J).
Both conversions are correct. The c^5 in the first is needed because the
mass input is energy, not kg. No reduced-Planck-mass/8pi substitution occurs.

Microscopic coefficient scope
The pinned MASTER source lines101–117 have
 kc=lambda_tot/(4m^2), c3=g_c/(2m sqrt(2M)), delta=M-2m,
and the positive source-eliminated contact coefficient
 kc-c3^2/delta.
Consequently, when the source is consistently integrated out in the
low-frequency/low-momentum, dilute expansion,
 lambda_NR=4m^2(kc-c3^2/delta)
 =lambda_rep+g_c^2/[2M(M+2m)] >0
under that source's parameter hypotheses. Replacing this by lambda_tot
without specifying the source state/elimination would be unwarranted.
Finite source occupation contributes conserved charge and rest mass; rho=mn
is the leading one-species effective description, not an exact identity for
arbitrary two-field states. Likewise normal-ordered psi-dagger-squared psi-
squared has expectation n^2 only in the stated mean-field/coherent closure;
arbitrary correlations or mixed charge populations can change the EOS.

The benchmark lambda_NR<=1 is a chosen weak-contact regime. The saved
mass ceilings at fixed L follow only within that regime and this phase:
L scales as sqrt(lambda_NR)/m^2, so m<=m_at_lambda1. They are not a universal
unitarity, scattering, experimental or all-phase mass constraint. Inverting
to huge lambda_NR for MeV particles is an exclusion of that weak-contact
explanation of the chosen length, not certification of a strong-coupling
EOS or an allowed alternate particle model. Other stiffness, fragments,
correlations, quantum-pressure gradients and relativistic/in-medium terms
require their own comparison. None is supplied by this coefficient match.
