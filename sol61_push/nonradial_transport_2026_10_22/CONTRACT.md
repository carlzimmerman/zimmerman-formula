# NT-20261022: nonradial fragmentation and transport
Base b0ced915c8a722595eedef88ac8d63c57a0208ca; predecessor radiating_collapse_2026_10_22. Own directory only. Goal: derive selected core masses/distributions with preserved dwarf, rotation and growth behavior. A fastest linear wavelength, a conserved transport experiment, or an imposed surface density is not completion.

Routes: (1) exact linear response of flattened cold matter, finite thickness, rotation and shearing-wave amplification; (2) angular-momentum and geometry budget for supplying a fragmenting sheet from prior modest-spin seeds; (3) conservative nonradial particle evolution in the action's quadratic, quasistatic field limit to measure torque and mass-scaling dependence. This is not a full nonlinear 3D field simulation. Earlier cold_fragment_modes_2026_10_20 uses a different scalar action and physical normalization; do not transfer its fragment masses or cooling rates.

Units inherited: ell0=.4404221297174536 kpc, M0=a0 ell0^2/G, time sqrt(ell0/a0). rho unit=a0/(4piG ell0), surface-density unit=M0/(4pi ell0^2). Canonical mass mstar=.08408132497418408 M0. Sheet: vertical density Sigma*f_H(z), f_H=exp(-abs(z)/H)/(2H), fixed normalized vertical profile and common in-plane displacement. Use vertically averaged, not midplane, force. H=0 is a delta sheet limit. Shape form factor T(a)=(2+a)/(2(1+a)^2). Axisymmetric cold growth gamma^2=Sigma/2*[k*T(kH)-k^2*T(sqrt(k^2+1)*H)/sqrt(k^2+1)]-kappa^2. Ignore pressure/dispersion only as a declared favorable cold limit; a collapsed kinetic sheet needs a new closure. Sigma, thickness and rotation are environmental inputs. Scan H=0,.1,.3,1,3,10 and surface densities .25,.5,1,2,4 times the value giving the canonical razor-thin footprint mass. Footprint mass convention: circular patch radius pi/k, not a derived nonlinear bound-core mass.

Scan H=0,1; kappa^2/Amax=.3,.9,1.2; ky/k_peak=.25,.5,1,2,4. This is a finite amplification calculation, not a fragment mass function.

Numerical bounds/provenance recorded per run. No user data refit, manuscript edits, commits, or subagents. Research and computation-audit skills applied. All conclusions conditional on exact stated regimes; no globally universal mass claim from a linear calculation.
