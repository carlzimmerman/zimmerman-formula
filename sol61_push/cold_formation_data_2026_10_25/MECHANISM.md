# What this experiment actually evolves

This is a finite branch of the existing CCT selectively coupled field proxy. It is not a derived bridge to Claude's current v3s/AeST action or to retired SCT-M. None of these runs establishes that the proposed astronomical component exists or settles its microscopic interpretation.

In physical units the assumed cold field obeys

    i eta partial_t Psi = [-eta^2 Laplacian/2 + Phi_c + Phi_b,N] Psi,
    rho_c = |Psi|^2,    Laplacian Phi_c = 4 pi G rho_c.

The fixed baryonic Plummer source enters the cold Hamiltonian once. The stellar acceleration is prescribed separately:

    g_star = (g_b,N + sqrt(g_b,N^2 + 4 a0 g_b,N))/2 + g_c,N.

The cold mass is NOT also inserted into the nonlinear baryonic term. The selectively coupled proxy and passive stars do not constitute a reciprocal full action or a live-system energy theorem.

The cold field can clump without a collision term. Its conserved current is j=eta Im(Psi* grad Psi). Where phase/density variables are regular, v=eta grad arg(Psi), Q=-eta^2 Laplacian sqrt(rho)/(2 sqrt(rho)), and

    partial_t rho + div(rho v)=0,
    partial_t v + (v dot grad)v = -grad(Phi_c + Phi_b,N + Q).

Q and interference are field stresses, not a postulated collision/cooling rate. Concentration releases gravitational binding energy, but this Hamiltonian cannot delete it: other field modes/envelope/outgoing material must receive it. Conserved cold energy is

    E_c = integral [eta^2 |grad Psi|^2/2 + rho Phi_c/2 + rho Phi_b,N] dV.

For the passive stellar orbits in this fixed baryonic model,

    Delta E_star = integral partial_t Phi_c(x_star,t) dt

up to the measured orbit integration residual. Checking this work, the paired frozen control, radius and velocity structure prevents confusing integrator drift, adiabatic compression, and actual time-dependent potential response. The cold field does not receive the reciprocal work of changing stellar density here; source-change diagnostics explicitly expose that approximation.

All initial spin is zero by imposed spherical symmetry. These runs cannot derive torque, angular-momentum removal, nonspherical waves, or a spin selector between galaxies. A reflecting outer wave boundary and finite radius do not measure physical escape at infinity. No numerical damping, absorber, profile reset or post-initialization mass renormalization supplies free cooling.

The Gaussian supply/acceleration rule is common to all galaxies, deliberately independent of the separate inverse final halo scales. Its incoming speed, eta, acceleration threshold and hypothetical catchment supply remain physical assumptions. A four-dwarf prediction requires their derivation from one assembly model, converged evolution, observed stellar profiles/kinematics, and a reciprocal source treatment. A transient matching enclosed mass or Jeans moment cannot substitute for these obligations.
