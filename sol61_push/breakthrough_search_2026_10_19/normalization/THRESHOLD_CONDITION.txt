SUPPLEMENTARY MATCHING CONDITION, 2026-10-19
Self-review; necessary Wilson-coefficient condition, not a new theory.

This was derived while checking technically natural coefficient selection.
It is independent of the sibling gap-spectrum response route.

In c=hbar=1 units use the low-a expansion of the declared foliation action
with independent Wilson coefficients:
 L = sqrt(-g)[Q R/2 - V + C2 a^2 - C3 a^3 + ...] + Lm[g,Psi],
 Q>0, V>0, C3>0. The stationary reduced gravitational action is
 L_NR=-(Q-C2)|grad Phi|^2-C3|grad Phi|^3-rho_b Phi+... .
Here G=1/(8 pi Q) is calibrated in the high-acceleration regime after the
completed response turns off. On the critical surface C2=Q, the deep
coefficient is a0=2Q/(3C3), and rho_DE=V (energy equals mass in these units).

Consequently
 kappa^2=a0^2/(G V)=32 pi Q^3/(9 V C3^2),
 kappa=1/2 iff V C3^2=(128 pi/9)Q^3.

A scale-independent selection mechanism expressed as these leading Wilson
relations must preserve TWO surfaces under running/matching:
 beta_C2=beta_Q,
 beta_V/V+2 beta_C3/C3-3 beta_Q/Q=0.
Finite matching preserving the second relation must instead obey
 (V_new/V)(C3_new/C3)^2=(Q_new/Q)^3.
This is a diagnostic for the claimed simple relation in this operator basis.
It is not a claim that arbitrary running Wilson coefficients are observables.
The full quantum effective action, including logarithms and higher operators,
must be used for an observable statement; different schemes can relocate
finite terms and quantum corrections to the simple tree relation.

If matter is integrated out with the metric held fixed and its microscopic
action/measure do not involve tau, its determinant is exactly a functional
Gamma_m[g], independent of tau. Direct matter loops therefore do not generate
the independent foliation operators a^2 or a^3 at this step. They do generate
metric vacuum/curvature operators. In particular for a massive scalar with
curvature coupling xi, the local heat-kernel terms proportional to m^4 and
m^2(xi-1/6)R are verified in Franchino-Vinas, de Paula Netto and Zanusso,
arXiv:1902.03167v2, section V.A, equations (48)-(49). At xi=1/6 the latter
mass-squared coefficient vanishes while the former does not. The simple
tree matching surface is therefore not automatically protected by universal
metric coupling, even in that particularly clean example.

This is not an unavoidable physical jump in an observed cosmological
constant upon changing renormalization scale. A renormalized vacuum
counterterm and consistent high/low-energy matching must be included.
It says that keeping a fixed numerical kappa through particle thresholds
needs a specified correlated subtraction, mixed portal, or symmetry identity.
Neither the equivalence principle nor a matter-only determinant supplies it.
Graviton/clock loops and added interactions could change the matching; no
calculation excluding such a protected completion is supplied here.
