Target and scope
Can de Sitter background data, universal matter coupling, gravitational criticality,
and positive static response fix kappa=1/2 in a0=kappa c sqrt(G rho_DE)?
Here rho_DE is MASS density, so Lambda=8 pi G rho_DE/c^2.
The result below concerns the single-function preferred-foliation action. It is
not a no-go for all MOND theories, all horizon thermodynamics, or UV completions.
There is no derivation of kappa=1/2. There is a precise consistency relation
between the interpolation and the two constant endpoints of this action.

1. Exact action, conventions and needed static reduction

Use c=1 until section 3. Let Q=1/(8 pi G)>0. Define a scalar clock tau with
timelike gradient, n_mu=-partial_mu tau/sqrt(-g^{ab}partial_a tau partial_b tau),
a_mu=n^nu nabla_nu n_mu, and a=sqrt(g^{mu nu}a_mu a_nu)>=0. Consider

 S = integral sqrt(-g) [Q R/2 - Q f(a)] d^4x + S_m[g,Psi].                 (1)

Matter has no direct tau dependence. The scaffold is the known Blanchet-Marsat
action, arXiv:1107.5264v1, equations (2.1)-(2.4), (4.5)-(4.8).
Their universal matter metric and the stationary weak-field reduction are
inputs, rather than a newly proposed relativistic theory. In its stationary
branch tau=t, a=g=|grad Phi| at leading nonrelativistic order, and

 div[mu(g) grad Phi]=4 pi G rho_b,   mu(a)=1+f'(a)/(2a),  a>0.            (2)

The ordinary cosmological constant force is subleading in this local galaxy
reduction and is kept separately in the exact covariant background equations.
In spherical isolation, where the curl correction vanishes, g mu(g)=g_N.
No algebraic isolated relation is asserted for a general disc or external field.
For homogeneous FLRW with tau=cosmic time, a=0. If f(0)=Lambda_0 then this
sector's background stress is -Q Lambda_0 g_mu_nu, independent of higher
coefficients of f. No full cosmological perturbation or horizon entropy claim
is made. In particular a unit timelike foliation need not be regular and
boost-invariant on a bifurcate Killing horizon; a naive Wald-area argument
would need an additional field-regularity analysis.

2. Endpoint/area identity, including unequal endpoints

Assume f is locally absolutely continuous on [0,infinity), (2) holds almost
everywhere, and f has finite endpoint values Lambda_0=f(0), Lambda_inf=f(infinity).
Then for every finite b>=0, the fundamental theorem of calculus gives

 f(b)-Lambda_0 = 2 integral_0^b a[mu(a)-1] da.                            (3)

Consequently the improper signed integral exists and

 Lambda_inf-Lambda_0 = 2 integral_0^infinity a[mu(a)-1] da.               (4)

If integral a|mu-1| da is finite, define I_plus=integral a[mu-1]_+ da and
I_minus=integral a[1-mu]_+ da. Both are nonnegative and

 Lambda_inf-Lambda_0=2(I_plus-I_minus),
 total_variation(f)=2(I_plus+I_minus).                                  (5)

Absolute convergence is needed for the separated areas and total-variation
claim; the finite signed endpoint identity alone does not imply it.

Suppose mu(a)=a/A+o(a/A) as a tends to zero, A>0. Then mu<1 on a nonzero
interval near zero. If Lambda_inf=Lambda_0, (4) implies mu>1 on a set of
positive weighted measure. For continuous mu this includes an open interval.
Thus exact equality of the two vacuum endpoints plus deep MOND requires a
regime where the spherical force is smaller than g_N. It does not require
negative force, negative static ellipticity, or a repulsive acceleration.

The equality Lambda_inf=Lambda_0 is an EXTRA PHYSICAL ASSUMPTION. The ordinary
always-enhanced branch 0<mu<=1 can consistently have Lambda_inf<Lambda_0.
If mu<=1 everywhere and is not identically one, finite endpoints imply

 Lambda_0-Lambda_inf=2 integral_0^infinity a[1-mu(a)] da>0.               (6)

For a single change of sign at a_star, from mu<1 to mu>1, f has its minimum
there, and its actual downward/upward excursions satisfy

 Lambda_0-f_min=2 integral_0^{a_star} a(1-mu) da,
 Lambda_inf-f_min=2 integral_{a_star}^infinity a(mu-1) da.                (7)

These are exact amplitude/area relations, not merely a sign argument.

3. SI normalization and a constraint on the high-acceleration tail

The covariant norm a has dimension inverse length. Write the physical
acceleration g=c^2 a and x=g/a0. Restoring units in (4) gives

 (Lambda_0-Lambda_inf)c^4/a0^2
       = 2 integral_0^infinity x[1-mu(x)] dx.                            (8)

If Lambda_inf=0, and if kappa is defined against Lambda_0 as above, then

 8 pi/kappa^2 = 2 integral x(1-mu) dx,
 kappa^2 = 4 pi / integral x(1-mu) dx.                                  (9)

This is a derived coefficient/shape relation conditional on the high-field
vacuum subtraction Lambda_inf=0. It selects no numerical kappa until the
shape and subtraction are independently selected. The target kappa=1/2
would require integral x(1-mu) dx=16 pi. Choosing this area is another input.

For an eventually always-enhanced tail with 1-mu(x) asymptotic to B/x^p,
B>0, the existence of a finite Lambda_inf requires p>2. At p=2 the endpoint
diverges logarithmically; at p<2 it diverges by a power. This excludes the
literal all-the-way standard (p=2) and simple (p=1) interpolations IF one
also demands a finite f(infinity) in (1). Their local force approaching
Newtonian does not by itself imply a finite vacuum endpoint. It does not
exclude either interpolation as a nonrelativistic phenomenological law or
as the finite-range limit of a larger theory.

As a check, take mu_n(x)=x/(1+x^n)^(1/n), n>2. Integration by parts and
t=x^n give exactly

 2 integral_0^infinity x(1-mu_n) dx
       = (1/n) Beta(3/n,1-2/n).                                        (10)

The endpoint term x^2(1-mu_n) vanishes precisely because n>2. These known
interpolation shapes illustrate that the endpoint difference is a shape
moment. For n=3 the right side is 1, while n tending to infinity gives
1/3, and n tending to 2 from above makes it diverge. No n value is selected.

4. Explicit fixed-Lambda family with positive static Hessian

Fix Lambda>0 and Q>0. For every A>0 let

 f_A(a)=Lambda-a^2 exp[-2a/(3A)],   s=2a/(3A).                          (11)

All these actions have exactly the SAME Q, f_A(0)=Lambda, f_A(infinity)=Lambda,
the same homogeneous de Sitter solution H^2=Lambda/3, and the same universal
matter coupling. They differ in their MOND scale, not their additive vacuum.
Expansion at a=0 gives

 f_A=Lambda-a^2+(2/(3A))a^3+O(a^4/A^2),
 mu_A=1+(s/2-1)e^(-s),
 mu_A=a/A+O(a^2/A^2),   mu_A(infinity)=1.                              (12)

Thus the physical a0=c^2 A and kappa=sqrt(8 pi)A/sqrt(Lambda) are arbitrary.
In particular kappa=1/2 and kappa=1 occur at the same positive Lambda and G.
If one additionally demands f_A>=0 everywhere, it suffices and is necessary
that Lambda>=9 A^2 e^(-2), equivalently kappa<=sqrt(8 pi)e/3. This still
admits a continuum containing both 1/2 and 1.

Here is a global analytic static-ellipticity check, rather than a scan.
For s>0, the transverse Hessian factor is

 mu_A=1-e^(-s)+(s/2)e^(-s)>0.

The longitudinal factor is

 F_A=mu_A+a dmu_A/da=1+(-1+2s-s^2/2)e^(-s)>0.                         (13)

Indeed e^s>=1+s+s^2/2, so e^s F_A>=3s>0. Hence the stationary scalar flux
map has positive eigenvalues mu_A,mu_A,F_A for all a>0. Its source-free
static energy, after subtracting Q Lambda, is

 E_A(g)=Q g^2[1-exp(-2g/(3A))]>=0,   c=1,
 E_A'=2Q g mu_A.                                                       (14)

This is positivity/strict convexity of the reduced static gradient energy.
At g=0 its Hessian vanishes, as in ordinary deep MOND. It is NOT a proof
of full covariant mode health, nonlinear evolution, cold-carrier matching,
cosmological perturbation viability, lensing on every background, or a
bounded energy after adding a prescribed attractive source term.

There is a concrete known health limitation, not merely a generic caveat.
Flanagan, arXiv:2302.14846v3, equations (42)-(43), gives the stationary
khronon quadratic kinetic form and sufficient conditions f'<=0, f''<=0
for its nonnegativity. Equation (54) and the following discussion already
identify a low/high-acceleration transition obstruction to their stronger
symmetric-configuration stability conditions. Our witness has f'>0 for
g>3a0 and therefore violates even the transverse sign condition there.
Static ellipticity must not be promoted to healthy clock dynamics. This
packet supplies no fully viable covariant counterfamily and claims none.
Whether a constrained dynamics calculation yields a necessary ghost
statement, and whether added clock/metric operators repair it, remain open
continuations. They are not executed in this bounded normalization lane.

The forced compensation is explicit:
 mu_A=1 at g=3a0,
 max(mu_A-1)=e^(-3)/2 at g=9a0/2,
 f_min=Lambda-9 e^(-2)a0^2/c^4 at g=3a0.                              (15)

The maximum spherical fractional force deficit relative to g_N is
1-1/[1+e^(-3)/2], approximately 2.429 percent. This is a mathematical
feature of this unfit witness, not a claimed observational prediction of
the frozen framework. Different witness shapes move the compensation.

5. What the result settles, and what it does not

The declared background, universal coupling, critical quadratic coefficient,
positive reduced static Hessian, finite high-field endpoint, and even equal
endpoints do not determine kappa: (11) is a direct counterfamily at fixed
Lambda and G. This is distinct from the earlier additive-offset counterfamily.
The endpoint identity and the finite-endpoint tail condition are reusable
tests of any proposed single-f(a) completion.

The entire covariant scaffold and its nonrelativistic MOND limit are known
literature. (4) is a direct integral corollary of its known relation (2).
Blanchet-Skordis, arXiv:2404.06584, section 3.2.7, equations (3.22)-(3.26),
also distinguishes the high-field constant from the cosmological one.
No major-new-physics or global novelty claim follows. The particular witness
is engineered to establish underdetermination and must not be called a
microscopic explanation. Full dynamics could reject individual members or
the scaffold, but no supplied health theorem singles out A at fixed Lambda.

Smallest remaining leaf for coefficient selection: a specified symmetry,
quantum state or UV matching equation that forbids (11)'s A-deformation and
fixes its independently normalized response at the desired value. Another
static matching or horizon-radius identity cannot supply this missing leaf.
