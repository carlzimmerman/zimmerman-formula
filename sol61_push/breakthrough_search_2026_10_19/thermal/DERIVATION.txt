Thermal response of the rational two-level ensemble
Checkpoint BZS-2026-10-19-A; coordinator derivation, initially unaudited.

Premises and scope
K=4 pi G, a>0. Independent supported quantum two-level sites have
H_Delta(E)=d[Delta I+Delta sigma_x-E sigma_z], common d>0,
w(Delta)=p_sat a Delta/(a^2+Delta^2)^(3/2), p_sat=a/K.
w is site density times dipole d, per reduced-gap interval. Delta and E
have acceleration units, d has mass*length units, and d Delta is energy.
The source E is the conjugate gravitational acceleration ONLY under the
additional dipole/source dictionary of this model. This is not derived from
the original minimally coupled Phi/X particles. Set t=k_B T/d and theta=t/a.
Assume a canonical Gibbs state, the same fixed site inventory at each T,
no interaction-induced spectrum changes, and local zero-stiffness spherical
force matching b=E-K p_T(E). Finite L* and external fields are not included.

Exact static result
Diagonalization and Z=2 exp(-beta d Delta) cosh(beta d R),
R=sqrt(Delta^2+E^2), give

p_T(E)=integral_0^infinity w(Delta) E/R tanh(R/t) dDelta.

At T=0 the parent proves p_0(E)=p_sat E/(a+E), E>=0.
For every T>0, differentiation at E=0 by dominated convergence gives

K chi_T(0)=integral_0^infinity (1+x^2)^(-3/2) tanh(x/theta) dx,
delta_T=1-K chi_T(0)
       =theta integral_0^infinity [1-tanh y]/(1+theta^2 y^2)^(3/2) dy.

The integrands prove 0<delta_T<1, delta_T increases strictly with T,
and delta_T<=theta log 2. At low temperature,

delta_T=theta log 2-(9/16) zeta(3) theta^3+O(theta^5).

The cubic coefficient uses integral y^2[1-tanh y]dy=3 zeta(3)/8.
Consequently b(E,T)/E tends to delta_T>0 as E tends to zero, whereas at
T=0 b/E=E/(a+E) tends to zero. With these fixed inputs the exact deep-MOND
asymptote is lost at every positive temperature. An isolated point-source
far field instead has E~G M/(delta_T r^2), where the stated local matching
applies. Temperature, inventory readjustment, external field, finite supply,
and stiffness must be solved before this becomes a halo prediction.

Uniform low-field thermal scaling, and its prior overlap
Write D_T=p_0-p_T. Since w(Delta)/R equals
p_sat a Delta/[R(a^2+Delta^2)^(3/2)], substitute R in D_T to obtain

K D_T = a^2 E integral_E^infinity
 [1-tanh(R/t)]/[a^2+R^2-E^2]^(3/2) dR.

For E,t much smaller than a its leading term is
(E/a) integral_E^infinity [1-tanh(R/t)]dR
=(E t/a) log(1+exp(-2E/t)). Therefore

b(E,T)=(E t/a) log[2 cosh(E/t)]
        +O(E^3/a^2)+O(E t^3/a^3).

For fixed E/t the errors are relatively small as t/a tends to zero.
The first error also reflects the full rational zero-T law rather than
only its leading E^2/a term. The thermal crossover is of order E~t;
equating the two asymptotes gives the convention-dependent estimate
E_cross~t log 2. It is not an exact transition or sharp boundary.

This log-cosh scaling is ALREADY present, for a different planar Dirac
microscopic model, in sol61_push/FINAL_5_PERCENT_SPRINT.md. The finite-T
rounding mechanism and log 2 are not newly discovered here. Their appearance
in the rational two-level construction identifies overlapping infrared
behavior under different microscopic assumptions, rather than independent
physical confirmation.

Connection to the sibling zero-temperature dynamic calculation
For a common d, Omega_a=2 d a/hbar. Direct transition matrix elements give

K Im chi_R(omega;0,0)= (pi/2) x/(1+x^2)^(3/2), x=omega/Omega_a>0.
tau_0=lim_(omega->0+) K Im chi_R(omega;0,0)/omega
     =pi/(2 Omega_a)=pi hbar/(4 d a).

Eliminating d and a from the leading thermal deficit gives

lim_(T->0+) delta_T/(T tau_0) = (4 log 2/pi) k_B/hbar.

This is a conditional temperature/absorption consistency relation of the
specified ensemble. tau_0 is a zero-T, zero-field absorption slope, not an
independently established halo equilibration time or an arbitrary finite-T
relaxation time. At T>0 the zero-field resonant absorption is multiplied by
tanh[hbar omega/(2 k_B T)]; its strict low-frequency slope vanishes. The
frequency and temperature limits cannot be exchanged. A bath introduces
additional population-relaxation channels not fixed by these Hamiltonians.

The static gap distribution alone does not determine this relation when
d may depend on Delta. Such a change preserves the ground-state static
law while changing raw gaps, thermal response and absorption. The common-d
assumption is essential. No value of d, a, T, kappa or G was derived.
