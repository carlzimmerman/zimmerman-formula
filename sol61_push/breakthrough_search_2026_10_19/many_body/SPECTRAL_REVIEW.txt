FRESH DERIVATION OF THE LOAD-BEARING STEPS

1. Canonical source and double commutator.
Since [V(x),D]=0 and [p_i^2,x_i]=-2 i hbar p_i,
 [H0,D]=-i hbar sum_i (q_i/m_i) p_i.
A second commutator gives
 [D,[H0,D]]=hbar^2 sum_i q_i^2/m_i.
In a ground eigenstate, direct insertion of a complete energy eigenbasis independently gives
 <[D,[H0,D]]>=2 sum_n (E_n-E_0)|D_0n|^2
 =2 hbar sum_n omega_n |D_0n|^2.
Thus the first moment is 2 sum_n omega_n |D_0n|^2/(V hbar), exactly matching the proof. There is no missing factor of two or hbar. Completeness and nonnegative excitation energies imply a true ground-state subspace consumes at most the full oscillator budget. This is not automatically a statement about a thermally truncated multilevel theory.

Units also agree: for mass charges and acceleration source, chi has units kg s^2/m^3, 1/K has the same units, M1 and rho_D have units kg/m^3, and K rho_D has units s^-2. No density is obtained from the static chi0 condition alone.

2. Static spectral probability and Markov.
The unsubtracted zero-frequency dispersion relation is
 chi0=(2/pi) integral_0^infinity Im chi_R(omega)/omega domega.
Positivity makes the measure defined in the source a probability measure. Its second frequency moment is
 integral omega^2 dP=M1/chi0<=K rho_D.
On {omega>=Omega}, Omega^2 <= omega^2. Therefore
 Omega^2 P(omega>=Omega)<=integral_{omega>=Omega}omega^2dP
 <=integral omega^2dP<=K rho_D.
Combine with P<=1. The event includes equality at the threshold, so the result is still correct for discrete atoms at Omega. The excluded zero-frequency and contact terms are material: without the specified complete static representation, the chosen measure need not account for chi0.

3. Gibbs TLS response, reconstructed without assuming the tanh factor.
For a single TLS choose H0=(Delta/2) sigma_z, Delta=hbar omega>0, and rotate the transverse source phase so D=d sigma_x. The perturbed levels are +/-R(E), with
 R(E)=sqrt(Delta^2/4+d^2 E^2).
The Gibbs free energy is F(E)=-kBT log[2 cosh(R(E)/(kBT))]. The source expectation is -F'(E), giving at E=0
 chi_j,T=2d^2/Delta *tanh(Delta/(2kBT)),
 chi_j,0=2d^2/Delta.
The same formula follows from the population difference p_ground-p_excited. Divide summed per-volume susceptibilities by their zero-T sum. The resulting weights are exactly dP_j=chi_j,0/chi0, with no missing occupation factor, extra omega or extra volume. A purely off-diagonal source has zero diagonal covariance contribution at zero field. For a general biased source that contribution can exist and destroys the claimed universal thermal weighting; the source expressly excludes it.

4. Jensen.
For c>0 define f(z)=tanh(c sqrt z), z>=0. It is continuous at zero with f(0)=0, increasing, and strictly concave on z>0. Direct differentiation gives exactly the source's
 f''=-c sech^2(c sqrt z)/(4 z^(3/2))
     -c^2 sech^2(c sqrt z)tanh(c sqrt z)/(2z)<0.
The continuous extension is concave on the whole nonnegative half-line; an infinite right derivative at zero is not a failure of Jensen. The finite second frequency moment provides the required finite E[z]. Jensen and monotonicity then give
 E_P[tanh(c omega)]=E_P[f(omega^2)]
 <=f(E_P[omega^2])<=f(K rho_D).
For a single frequency saturating the oscillator budget the abstract TLS model attains equality. This does not assert that an exact finite-inertia canonical particle Hamiltonian with a physical gravitational source realizes such an ideal inventory.

5. Inversion and illustration.
Because chi0=1/K, deltaT=1-chiT/chi0. If deltaT<=epsilon, then 1-epsilon<=chiT/chi0<=tanh(A/T), A=hbar sqrt(K rho_D)/(2kB)>0. Monotonic atanh on (0,1) gives T<=A/atanh(1-epsilon). No inequality reverses incorrectly.
Independent direct numerical check of the constants gives

These reproduce the illustration. The chosen rho_D remains a reference input, not a measured halo value or a bound on actual bath temperature.
