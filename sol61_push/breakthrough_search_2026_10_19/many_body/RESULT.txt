PRIMARY RESULT, WITH ITS EXACT DOMAIN

In the long-wavelength linear response of the frozen S2 action, a small baryonic source in a nonzero constant external field has negative inferred Newtonian mass density in an equatorial cone. Finite stiffness does not remove its leading r^-3 density tail. If the source is a satellite in the slowly varying exterior field of a spherical host, its leading negative equatorial cold-source perturbation and the host's leading positive cold-source density satisfy

  -delta rho_c,s / rho_c,H = (g_s/g_e) D/(2r).

Here g_e is the host acceleration at host-satellite separation D, r is distance from the satellite in the plane perpendicular to the host field, and g_s is the magnitude of the satellite's internal acceleration there, with the host acceleration subtracted. The same MOND response mu(g) is used for both configurations. The response and its derivative cancel. The density quantities are Poisson-inferred sources, and equal the material cold-density contrast only if literal S3 is adopted together with S2.

Consequently, in the ordered regime

  lambda_response << r << (g_s/g_e) D << D,

and outside the satellite baryons, the leading satellite perturbation is negative and exceeds the positive host density. An explicitly supplied additional positive background can avoid negative TOTAL material density only if it exceeds the deficit:

  rho_extra >= [g_s - 2g_e r/D] L_e/(4 pi G Gamma r),
  Gamma=1+L_e,  L_e=d log mu/d log g evaluated at g_e.

This inequality is exact within the displayed linear-response / local-host approximation; it is not a quantitative error bound for the full nonlinear two-source action. For Gamma=1 (Newtonian constitutive response), numerator-density amplitudes vanish and the ratio has no sign interpretation.

WHAT IT CHANGES

The finite-L action can screen Solar System EFE tests while still requiring signed Poisson density on scales larger than its response length. Thus a claim that stiffness alone makes a literal positive-mass displacement medium realize the exact field action for arbitrary hosts has not been established and faces this explicit obstruction. The remaining choice is whether div P is a signed constitutive source, whether a sufficiently large positive carrier baseline is actually present, or whether the action/source/domain is changed. No such repair is silently adopted here.

The ideal uniform-field, zero-background linear-response theorem is proved by direct derivation. Application to a finite host with a positive cosmological mean is CONDITIONAL on quantitative control of the finite-source, nonlinear and slowly varying host errors. It is not a whole-framework falsification. The exact cancellation ratio is a useful way to state that missing test, rather than a new MOND phenomenon.
