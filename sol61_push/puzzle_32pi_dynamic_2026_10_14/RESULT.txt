1. Route A: promote the saturation scale while preserving the local force

Set c=hbar=1; K=4piG. For the corrected zero-offset polar potential
 W_pol(u,a)=(-au-a^2 log(1-u/a))/K,
the exact mixed derivative is
 d/du [partial W_pol/partial a] = -u^2/[K(a-u)^2] <0, 0<u<a.
At u=0, W_pol,a=0, hence W_pol,a<0 for every nonzero u.
A local potential U(a) with vacuum U'(a0)=0 cannot keep a=a0 stationary
for every polarization amplitude. Local promotion changes the force law
unless another term cancels this source or the field is effectively frozen.
This statement holds on the fixed background with no compensating coupling;
it is not a no-go for nonlocal, constrained or curvature-coupled models.

For a heavy auxiliary scale with U''(a0)=m2>0, keeping u fixed and neglecting
its gradients, the small-u expansion gives
 delta a = u^3/(3 K a0^2 m2)+O(u^4),
 W_eff = U(a0)+W_pol(u,a0)-u^6/(18 K^2 a0^4 m2)+O(u^7).
Thus the deep cubic survives to leading order but the exact interpolation
changes. These formulas require small delta a and positive finite stiffness;
no astrophysical tolerance or full field-theory health follows from them.

2. Route B: a minimally specified scalar-Euler construction

Let M^2=(8piG)^(-1), and promote a>0 to a canonical scalar. In the homogeneous
unpolarized sector consider
 S=integral sqrt(-g)[ M^2 R/2 -(partial a)^2/2 -V(a)+f(a) E4 ],
 V(a)=2 C M^2 a^2,
 f(a)=beta M^2/a^2,
 E4=Riem^2-4 Ric^2+R^2.
Here V is exactly the constant part C a^2/K of the prior potential. The
identification of this scalar with the MOND saturation scale is an explicit
model-building hypothesis. A covariant polar sector, its clocks and matter
couplings are NOT constructed by this action. beta and C are dimensionless.
The inverse-square f is a chosen mechanism, not derived microphysics.

With b(t) the FRW scale factor (distinct from scalar a), H=dot b/b,
 3M^2 H^2 = dot a^2/2+V-24H^3 f' dot a,
 ddot a+3H dot a+V'-24 f'H^2(H^2+dot H)=0.
An independent lapse variation uses the homogeneous action
 L/b^3=-3M^2 h^2/N+dot a^2/(2N)-NV-8 f' dot a h^3/N^3,
where h=dot b/b before dividing by lapse. This reproduces the constraint.

For a constant positive a and de Sitter H>0,
 H^2=2Ca^2/3,
 0=4CM^2 a [1+(16/3)beta C].
Thus a nonzero positive-energy branch exists precisely when
 C=-3/(16 beta), beta<0,
 Lambda/a^2=2C=-3/(8 beta).

This is a dynamical relation, unlike defining a horizon radius. It gives
Lambda/a0^2=32pi only if beta=-3/(256pi), equivalently C=16pi. Nothing in
this construction selects beta. Two parameters are related; their required
numerical value is not predicted. The result is conditional on the scalar
being the locally measured response scale and the completion preserving G.

3. Coupled homogeneous perturbations: positive coefficient, neutral mode

Linearize around one such de Sitter vacuum. The lapse constraint gives
 delta H=A delta a+B delta dot a,
 A=V'/(6M^2 H), B=-4f'H^2/M^2.
Substitution in the scalar equation gives
 D delta ddot a+3HD delta dot a+0 delta a=0,
 D=1+6M^2/a^2>0.
Therefore delta a=constant + decaying exp(-3Ht) mode. The constant mode
changes which member of the continuous de Sitter family is occupied.
This is not a unique attractor or a full scalar/tensor/no-ghost certificate.
Inhomogeneous perturbations and nonlinear evolution were not tested.
Treating curvature as externally frozen would misleadingly leave a positive
restoring term; the metric constraint cancels it exactly.

The mechanism behind the cancellation is general. For constant M and any
nonzero differentiable V, the stationary scalar/de Sitter equations require
 V'=(8/(3M^4)) f' V^2.
If this holds over an interval, integration gives
 f=f0-3M^4/(8V).
Our reciprocal quadratic pair is a special case. It constructs a continuum
of stationary vacua, not a selector of a unique vacuum. An arbitrary V can
be paired this way; the identity supplies no preferred 32pi.

4. Vacuum-shift discriminator

Keep the calibrated f and replace V by 2CM^2a^2+Delta. Eliminating H gives
the exact scalar residual
 -Delta(4CM^2a^2+Delta)/(CM^2a^3).
For Delta>0 this cannot vanish at finite a>0. For Delta<0 the formal nonzero
root gives V=-2CM^2a^2<0, so it is not this de Sitter branch. Consequently
the model does not dynamically absorb an independently added constant
vacuum energy. Retuning f to the new V restores the designer relation and
puts the missing selection back in by hand. This is a classical sensitivity
test, not a computed quantum-loop correction.

5. Could topology quantize beta?

A tempting redefinition is theta=M^2/a^2, so f=beta theta. If, additionally,
theta were a compact field of period 2pi and the normalized Euler charge
chi could range over integers of gcd one, invariance of exp(iS) under its
period would require beta*32pi^2 to be an integer. The desired value gives
-3pi/8, not an integer. More decisively V=2CM^4/theta is not periodic, and
neither the original scalar domain a>0 nor its kinetic term defines that
compact axion. This particular quantization proposal is inapplicable; it
cannot supply beta. No blanket claim about all topological completions is
made. Changing the period to fit beta would be another input.

6. Route C: ordinary nonminimal curvature does not automatically help

As a comparison choose F(a)=M^2+xi a^2 and
U(a)=V0+lambda(a^2-v^2)^2/4 in an F R/2 action. Constant-scalar de Sitter
stationarity is F U'-2F' U=0. Explicitly it is
 a[lambda(a^2-v^2)(M^2+xi v^2)-4xi V0]=0.
The nonzero root depends on V0, xi, lambda and v. It supplies a stationary
scale but no parameter-free Lambda/a^2. This exact branch equation was
checked; it is not a completed or empirically tested alternative.

Decision and continuation

The curvature construction is a conditional bridge with a free coupling,
not a solution. It does not meet the original coefficient target or exact
local-law requirement. Minimal scalar promotion fails exact law preservation;
reciprocal Euler selection is neutral and vacuum-shift sensitive; ordinary
nonminimal curvature retains free parameters. These scoped mechanisms have
been executed, not merely proposed.

Open continuations require a physically specified relation selecting beta
and protecting it against independent vacuum terms, plus a common polar/
metric action. Testing scalar-Euler inhomogeneous health is deferred: passing
it cannot remove the demonstrated coefficient freedom, so it is downstream
of the current bottleneck. A concrete microscopic beta prediction or a
non-adjustable symmetry would justify reopening this branch. Arbitrarily
adding a stabilizing function does not address that bottleneck. Original
research goal remains OPEN; no claim of general impossibility or full
programme exhaustion.
