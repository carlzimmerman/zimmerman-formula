CFG631 age interpretation: bounded self-review, 2026-10-11

CLAIM AND VERDICT
Claim audited: the new KiDS result establishes formation time as the
physical cause of extra cold mass. Verdict: incomplete, with the smallest
missing implication being colour association -> age-driven cold mass.
This is a source/code review, not an independent rerun or new experiment.

DEPENDENCIES AND OBLIGATIONS
1. A colour association under the implemented estimator: computational
   evidence reported by CFG631; not independently rerun here.
2. Its frozen positive-control gate: FAILED in the source results.
3. Colour uniquely measures age: NOT ESTABLISHED. The frozen inventory
   explicitly says ages and metallicities are absent.
4. The inferred excess isolates cold mass from stellar mass/environment:
   CONDITIONAL on inherited estimator machinery; not a native verdict.
5. Formation time yields a settling rate or carrier: NOT ADDRESSED.

DECISIVE EVIDENCE
FROZEN_CRITERIA section 7 requires positive D and Z >= 2 in all four
raw stellar-mass-tertile cells. It says: 'If this fails, the KiDS part
has no teeth.' The saved results explicitly contain a false check for
that requirement. README discloses the failure and then treats null
calibration as operative. That is a post-result change of interpretation,
not satisfaction of the original control. A calibrated null and positive
signal recovery test different properties; one cannot replace the other.
The code checks recovery at lines 94-97, then assigns its age label
separately at lines 135-139. Thus AGE CONFIRMED is a sub-statistic label,
not a declaration that every frozen gate passed.

This does NOT demonstrate that the colour association is false. It also
does NOT demonstrate the estimator is generally powerless: the chosen
positive control may genuinely have been too weak. Preserve the association
as a conditional lead and the control failure as a failure. Do not promote
it to a derived formation mechanism or silently replace the frozen gate.

STRONGEST SAFE STATEMENT
The existing KiDS estimator reports a positive u-r association after
specified covariate controls, on both footings. It has a failed predeclared
recovery control and does not isolate age or identify the extra mass.
Disc non-detections are sample/property-specific limits, not a universal
absence of any second parameter. No new physical inference is established.

NEXT DECISIVE CHECK

First inventory existing CFG700 work so this does not repeat its raw-ESD
age check. Declare separate stellar-population, environment and framework
cold-profile responses, with observational covariance and distance inputs

outside the nuisance-response span. If it does not, stop: these data cannot
identify cold mass separately under those assumptions. If it does, freeze

on a genuinely held-out sample or clearly label reused data. Keep both a0
footings separate and supply/settling parameters derived, not fitted away.
This proposed diagnostic is not yet run, not a promised detection, and not
a new action. The current raw-image Bullet gas-input work remains useful.
