\begin{abstract}
We examine gravitational wave predictions from the $T^3/\mathbb{Z}_2$ orbifold framework. The tensor-to-scalar ratio $r \approx 0.015$ is derived via $\alpha$-attractor inflation theory, testable by LiteBIRD. We clarify that both GW polarizations ($h_+$ and $h_\times$) survive the orbifold projection, correcting earlier claims. The framework predicts specific features in the stochastic GW background from phase transitions and cosmic strings, distinguishable from standard $\Lambda$CDM predictions.
\end{abstract}

\section{Gravitational Wave Polarizations}

\subsection{Standard GR: Two Polarizations}

In 4D general relativity, GWs have two polarizations:
\begin{align}
h_+ &: \text{plus polarization} \\
h_\times &: \text{cross polarization}
\end{align}

Both are transverse and traceless.

\subsection{Earlier Claim (RETRACTED)}

An earlier version of the framework claimed:
\begin{quote}
``The $\mathbb{Z}_2$ orbifold projects out $h_\times$, leaving only $h_+$.''
\end{quote}

\textbf{This was WRONG.}

\subsection{Correct Analysis}

The $\mathbb{Z}_2$ acts on extra dimensions:
\begin{equation}
\mathbb{Z}_2: y^i \mapsto -y^i \quad (i = 1, 2, 3)
\end{equation}

The 4D metric perturbation $h_{\mu\nu}$ has indices $\mu, \nu \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$---\textbf{no $y$-indices}.

Under the orbifold action:
\begin{align}
h_+(x, y) &\xrightarrow{\mathbb{Z}_2} h_+(x, -y) = h_+(x, y) \quad \text{(even)} \\
h_\times(x, y) &\xrightarrow{\mathbb{Z}_2} h_\times(x, -y) = h_\times(x, y) \quad \text{(even)}
\end{align}

\textbf{Both polarizations are $\mathbb{Z}_2$-even and survive.}

\subsection{What Would Project Out $h_\times$?}

To eliminate one polarization, you would need:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item A symmetry acting on 4D spacetime (not extra dimensions)
    \item Parity violation in the gravitational sector
    \item Chern-Simons modification to GR
\end{enumerate}

The $T^3/\mathbb{Z}_2$ orbifold does none of these.

\subsection{Revised Prediction}

\begin{equation}
\boxed{\text{Both } h_+ \text{ and } h_\times \text{ polarizations present (standard GR)}}
\end{equation}
