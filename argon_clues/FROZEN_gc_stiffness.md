# Frozen criteria: outer-halo globulars under the STIFFENED tether (written before the run)
Question: does the framework's own stiffness screening (L* = 5.75 pc, the CFG691 minimum; r_s = L*^2/r_M) make the four outer-halo globulars (NGC 2419, Pal 3, Pal 4, Pal 14) Newtonian-ish WITHOUT the record's hand-written 'class E: formed embedded' ownership rule (CFG332 route 1a), while leaving the unscreened law for the larger ones?
Inputs: CFG332's own data and conventions (Baumgardt GC tables, Upsilon_V = 1.6 fixed, M/L width 0.15 dex added in quadrature, Wolf-type sigma, isolated/no-EFE reading), both footings, never pooled.
Predictions: sigma_N (Newton), sigma_iso (algebraic law, no stiffness), sigma_stiff (Plummer baryons, 3D half-mass radius (4/3) r_half, stiffened ODE, g_stiff/g_N as the boost).
Score per cluster: z = (log10 sigma_pred - log10 sigma_obs)/sqrt((eso/so/ln10)^2 + 0.15^2); statistic X2 = sum z^2 over the four.
Pass: X2(stiff) < X2(iso) and X2(stiff) <= X2(Newton) + 1 on both footings. Fail: X2(stiff) >= X2(iso). Ambiguous otherwise. Mutation: L* = 0.05 pc must reproduce sigma_iso (X2 identical).
Disclosed: Plummer is a model; no external-field effect (consistent with CFG693 as the record reads it, and with the stiffness screening making it moot for small systems); Pal 3's dispersion may be binary-inflated (CFG334) so it is scored but flagged; only four clusters.
