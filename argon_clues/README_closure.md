**What it would buy:** nothing for the open problems. It lowers the cold supply (R0) that the free-component fork needs, and the data allow at most 2% of it. It is a closed-off direction unless a specific localised channel with its own observational signature (gamma/X-ray line or UV excess) is proposed. The census sign (B slightly > 1, 1.9 sigma-ish at the central value but P0 consistent) is not evidence for it.
