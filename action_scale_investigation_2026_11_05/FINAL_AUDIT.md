## The precise missing equation

In the stated c=1, leading weak-field unscreened response regime, write

    W(p)=p^2/(2K)+C3 p^3/3+..., K=4pi G.

Then a_MOND=1/(K C3). The target coefficient is equivalent to

    K^2 C3^2 G rho_DE = 4.

The equations tested here do not impose that invariant relation. Their
existing symmetries allow the cubic response to change without changing
the homogeneous vacuum. They also allow a vacuum constant to change without
fixing the response. A new microscopic matching equation, genuine symmetry
identity or dynamical selector would have to connect these two quantities
and justify the normalization. Choosing that equation, a potential minimum,
or the desired boundary value by hand is a new assumption.

This explains the original delta S=0 question: stationarity gives equations
for the fields an action already contains. It does not create an extra
relation between two independent couplings. Promoting a coupling to a field
changes the theory and requires new dynamics; the minimal promotions were
explicitly tested rather than presumed to solve the problem.
