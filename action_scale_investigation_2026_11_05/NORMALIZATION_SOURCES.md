supply an equation fixing the response coefficient from the vacuum term.

## Weyl scalar-field approach (published2026-07-23)

[Primary article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1140/epjc/s10052-026-16091-3),
equations(9)-(10). The source defines a1 as an inverse-length MOND scale and
gives Lambda=lambda*a1^2. Translating to SI physical acceleration yields
a_phys=c^2 sqrt(Lambda/lambda), hence kappa=sqrt(8pi/lambda). Its hierarchy
convention relates field scales to the empirical MOND scale, and lambda
is estimated using cosmological quantities. The checked equations do not
derive the required fixed lambda. Verdict: adjacent connection with an
unfixed normalization, not the missing matching principle.
