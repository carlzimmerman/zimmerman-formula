# Shared condensate and gravitational coupling: frozen matching test

Base bddd5251c29f3689d544baed6e6a0f08cf09f42b. This tests a changed action,
not a rerun of the metric-only gapped model in K778. The gapless cubic sector
is retained explicitly; the amplitude has an independent positive canonical
kinetic coefficient Z. No graviton-loop derivation is assumed.

Use c=hbar=1, sigma>0, p>=0 and positive A,B,t,h,xi,Z. The hypotheses are

    F(sigma,p)=xi(sigma^2+zeta p^2),
    U=U0-t(sigma^2+p^2)/2+A h^3(sigma^2+p^2)^(3/2)/3
      +B h^3 p^3/3.

The scalar-tensor terms are F R/2-Z(grad sigma)^2/2-U, with matter minimally
coupled to the Jordan metric. Polarization is spatial relative to a clock.
In the local held-background response take the same leading polarization
coupling as before with K=1/(2F0). Compare zeta=0 and zeta=1, declared before
computation. zeta=1 places the radial condensate in the curvature coupling;
its radiative protection is not assumed. The B sector still distinguishes
p and sigma. U0 is retained. This is not asserted to be the protected
sequestering embedding; mixing these prescriptions without a new action
would be invalid.

At homogeneous p=0, constant sigma, derive the scalar and Einstein vacuum
equations, and the Einstein-frame amplitude kinetic and mass. Parameterize
x=A h^3 sigma0/t. Classify stable positive scalar-tensor vacua, without
claiming the untested clock/polarization sector healthy. Compute the induced
quadratic p response at fixed background curvature and the unchanged cubic
coefficient after stationary amplitude elimination. Examine the scalar
Compton/Hubble hierarchy before using a held-amplitude local response.

On the zeta=1 critical tree-level slice, calculate the operational intermediate
weak-field a_MOND, using the long-range tensor Newton coupling G=1/(8pi F0)
only where scalar exchange is suppressed and r<<H^-1. Keep B/A free and also
report the stronger equal-amplitude hypothesis B=A. Express kappa in terms
of x, t/F0 and B/A, without inserting a target value.

Investigate the small positive vacuum ratio epsilon=U/F0^2 symbolically:
does fixed positive finite dimensionless microscopic data give a finite
kappa, and what scaling would be necessary? This is a conditional EFT
hierarchy test, not an empirical cosmology fit or a selected parameter scan.

Exact checks: constant-vacuum equations and U0 relation; vacuum energy;
Einstein-frame kinetic and mass; curvature-induced quadratic response for
both zeta choices; absence of a new cubic from a regular amplitude shift;
normalization and equal-amplitude limit; small-vacuum scaling identity.
Mutation omits the nonminimal curvature contribution to the p response and
must fail the full coefficient and radial-curvature cancellation checks.
No full covariant MOND completion, criticality protection or new coefficient
candidate is claimed. Additional source/clock constraints remain mandatory.
