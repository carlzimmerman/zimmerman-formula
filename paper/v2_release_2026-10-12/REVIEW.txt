ENERGY-SIGN GAP
The v2 statement includes Q = rho_c alpha tau_ff grad(psi).grad(Phi) >= 0.
The inequality does not follow from the displayed local equations.
For a,b>0 and nonzero vector u, take locally
psi(x) = (a/2)|x|^2 + u.x,
Phi(x) = (b/2)|x|^2 - u.x.
Both Laplacians are positive (3a and 3b); at x=0 the gradient dot product is
-|u|^2. Thus positivity of the two Poisson sources alone cannot prove Q>=0.
This is a counterexample to that local inference, not a constructed global
solution of the full model. A valid global positivity argument would need the
missing boundary/geometry/target constraints. The review draft states the gap
and does not alter the original equations or assert a replacement mechanism.
