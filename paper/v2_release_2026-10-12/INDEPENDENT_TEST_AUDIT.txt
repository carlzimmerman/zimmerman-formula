DIRECT AUDIT OF THE V2 TEST — October 12, 2026

Primary verdict: correct only after a stated restriction.
Restriction: the existing scores compare specified numerical constructions at
inherited cosmological inputs. They are not an independent test of the whole
Zimmerman framework, nor a demonstration that Lambda-CDM is correct.
Fresh source inspection and executable checks by the same reviewer; not an
independent human referee, full likelihood reproduction or exhaustive audit.

1. DECISIVE FINDING: THE LABEL IS RELATIVE, NOT ABSOLUTE

CFG650 imports klass from CFG592 (cfg592_native.py:288). Its inputs are only
delta chi-square, robustness deltas and a data-support flag. It has no absolute
candidate residual as an input. The actual function gives CONSISTENT when both
models have chi-square 10,000 and their difference is zero.

Exact stronger result, independent of cosmological assumptions:
Whitened data d=(0,0), C=I, control s=(M,0), candidate f=(M,1).
chi_s^2=M^2, chi_f^2=M^2+1, delta=1 for every real M.
If the robustness deltas are also 1 and the support gate passes, the source
classifier says CONSISTENT for every M. Thus no finite upper bound on absolute
disagreement follows from that label. The algebra proves the family statement;
the script checks the extracted source function at five integer M values.

This refutes the interpretation 'CONSISTENT implies an adequate absolute fit'.
It does not refute legitimate use of this rule as a declared relative comparison.
Likewise its EXCLUDED label is not, by itself, a calibrated absolute rejection
or a general three-sigma statement. No result of this audit says the candidate
fits well; the control's inadequacy cannot make an inadequate candidate adequate.

2. ACTIVE INHERITED ASSUMPTIONS

Current CFG650 PM code fixes h, omega_b, omega_c, n_s and sigma8; defines the
flat-Lambda expansion; initializes from a CDM transfer/growth prescription;
and imports a turnaround/census construction. The inherited omega_c/omega_b
also supplies the 5.364 abundance. These are inputs to predictions.

Current CFG592 scorer fixes Om and distances (lines 31, 47-71); includes Om
in lensing and alignment kernels (around 122-142); continues low-k power
with its inherited linear spectrum (214-223); and extends beyond z=1 with
the inherited growth (224 onward). Its DESI option changes distances, while
the lensing prefactor and DZG still use the original Om/growth: it is a distance
sensitivity variant, not a complete alternative cosmological solution.

Measured angular shear correlations are different from these modeling inputs.
Published calibration, redshift distributions, covariance, scale cuts and
intrinsic-alignment prescriptions also require assumptions. 'Raw data' does
not mean assumption-free inference. Atomic physics and conservation identities
are not Lambda-CDM-specific merely because this pipeline uses them.

The scalar identity delta = e^T C^-1 e + 2(s-d)^T C^-1 e shows why shared inputs
do not cancel generally. With d=1,C=1,e=1, changing s from 0 to 1 changes delta
from -1 to +1. This was checked with exact integer arithmetic. No claim is made
about how much of the actual survey residual these dependencies explain.

3. FITTING AND SIGNIFICANCE

CFG592 fit (163-174) minimizes r^T C^-1 r + prior(x) with L-BFGS-B. That profiles
penalized nuisances; it does not marginalize by integration. It also returns the
best objective without recording optimizer success, projected gradients or
boundary diagnostics. Source inspection identifies this missing diagnostic;
it does not show that any stored optimization failed.

CFG650 around 193-195 computes chi-square survival at N_kept from chi2_data,
after nuisance fitting. This is not automatically its true sampling law.
Counterexample to the general inference: with one Gaussian observation and a
free fitted mean, minimized data chi-square is identically zero, not chi-square
with one degree of freedom. Priors/bounds here make an automatic N-p replacement
unjustified too. Calibrate the full fitting statistic on model-generated mocks;
do not reinterpret the stored p-values as exact exclusion probabilities.

5. REQUIREMENTS FOR AN INDEPENDENT TEST

A. Specify one candidate forward model: conserved cold-mass current, source and
   catchment dynamics, reciprocal energy transfer, background expansion and
   perturbations. Derive its early-time equivalence to any borrowed solution,
   or explicitly fit/condition the independent initial-condition parameters.
B. Use survey observables with explicit calibration/covariance assumptions.
   Make absolute candidate residuals primary. Report nuisance penalties apart
   from data residuals, optimizer diagnostics, priors and fit boundaries.
C. Calibrate the chosen discrepancy statistic under the candidate with complete
   nuisance refitting, and under alternatives for power. Freeze support and
   decision rules before new test results. Disclose already-seen data and model
   selection; existing previews cannot become a blind test retrospectively.
D. Infer model-dependent abundance, background and initial amplitude jointly
   or disclose conditioning; a fixed Planck-derived value is not an independent
   measurement for every alternative theory. Do not add unrelated per-galaxy
   parameters to hide residuals. Compare predictive performance separately.
E. Use fresh/held-out observables to test the specified construction. Numerical
   equivalence to S0 remains a code/control check, never a substitute for fit.

These are missing mathematical and inference ingredients, not demands that
the candidate resemble Lambda-CDM. Replacing inherited functions with unnamed
free functions would leave the observable prediction unspecified.

7. DEEPER SOURCE CHECKS (v0.3)

Support: CFG592 support_mask (249-259) explicitly uses S0, nominal redshift
nuisances and no IA. CFG650 passes PS0 into it and retains those bins for both
fits. The actual function, executed on a one-redshift/two-multipole fixture,
gives 0.95 for positive contributions (19 inside, 1 outside) and 19/119 for
(19 inside, 100 outside). Thus the rule does not certify candidate support.
It may still be adequate for the actual spectra; that quantitative check is
not performed here. The issue is a missing guarantee, not a demonstrated bias.
With positive weights, f_candidate = b_in f_control /
[b_in f_control + b_out (1-f_control)] in a two-region constant-ratio case.
Additionally, an absolute-support fraction does not control relative error of
a signed integral near cancellation: supported terms +100,-99 and omitted -1
give 99.5% absolute support but full signed integral zero versus truncated one.
This is an algebraic example, not a constructed KiDS bin or convergence verdict.

Gate: extracting the actual CFG650 T-OWN/status source fragments reproduces
missing -> CONSISTENT + 'PASS ... [PROVISIONAL: T-OWN missing...]',
failed -> NOT DIAGNOSTIC, passed -> PASS.
The missing state is warned about, not concealed. It is nevertheless not a
validated PASS and should be kept distinct in downstream interpretation.
No historical run is alleged to have a missing gate. The primary scorer also
reads T_OWN from its mutation JSON without enforcing that JSON's NZ1_all_bite
flag or binding the cache to input hashes; manual run discipline can satisfy
the criteria, but the scorer alone does not enforce the entire prerequisite.
No production scorer or frozen criterion was edited by this audit.

Reproduction: check_support_and_gates.py; support_gate_contract.json;
support_gate_run/result.json and manifest.json. Runner and validator succeeded.
The projection kernels are deliberately synthetic positive unit weights; only
the function and branch logic are production source. This distinction matters.

8. DIRECT PHYSICAL CONSEQUENCES (NO CONTROL COSMOLOGY)

Finite-supply bound: assume isolated spherical Newtonian gravity of real mass,
total condensed baryons <=M_d, total available cold mass <=S, and no other mass
or external-force contribution. Then v_c^2(r)<=G(M_d+S)/r. A required flat
speed v_f can hold only for r<=G(M_d+S)/v_f^2. Under the ideal deep-law
v_f^4=G M_d a0, this becomes r<=(1+S/M_d)sqrt(G M_d/a0).
This is an elementary necessary bound, not a novelty claim, measured edge,
proof of the model, or assumption-free statement about real nonspherical halos.
It turns finite supply into an explicit prediction obligation: give the outer
transition and its lensing/dynamical consequences rather than imposing an
infinite target on a finite reservoir. No Planck ratio is needed for the bound.

Receiver identity: define epsilon_DE=rho_DE c^2 and homogeneous received energy
Q_DE. From dot epsilon +3H(1+w)epsilon=Q_DE and a0 proportional to sqrt(epsilon),
dot a0/a0=Q_DE/(2epsilon)-3H(1+w)/2 for constant kappa. A constant exact vacuum
(w=-1, dot epsilon=0) implies Q_DE=0. A nonzero net homogeneous sink requires
evolution or another receiver. Local/global averaging, perturbations and
reciprocal dynamics remain missing. This is conservation algebra, not an
assumption that Lambda-CDM is observationally correct or an exclusion of a
dynamical receiver. An approximately constant background could remain usable
after a quantitative error bound; this pass does not compute that bound.
