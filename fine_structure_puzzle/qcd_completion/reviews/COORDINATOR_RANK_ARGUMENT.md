# Candidate rank argument for the static J1 asymptotic connection

Coordinator research argument, 2026-10-19 UTC, after seeing the angular author's conditional asymptotic calculation. This is not a blinded independent derivation. Submitted separately to angular_completion and the fresh kernel_rank_audit reviewer. It is a candidate sufficient argument, not a numerical result or a claimed full-QCD coefficient.

Hypotheses to establish: the canonical real ten-channel operator is symmetric; the smooth-origin regular zero-solution space L is ten-dimensional and Wronskian-isotropic; the stated asymptotic charged/neutral growing and non-growing bases exist with a nondegenerate limiting symplectic pairing; its L² kernel at J1 has dimension one per magnetic quantum number, spanned by translation; and a regular gauge-gradient g0 has C0 neutral growth proportional to r with no C0 constant part, C6 decay proportional to r^-2, no other neutral growing part and no charged growing part. The author's actual operator and boundary problem must supply these objects, not a renamed scalar model.

1. In the twenty-dimensional zero-solution space let S be the non-growing asymptotic Lagrangian: six exponentially decaying charged modes, one C0 neutral constant, two C2 neutral r^-1 modes, and one C6 neutral r^-2 mode. Neutral C0 constant is not L² and must not be counted as an index zero mode.
2. For s in L intersection S, W(g0,s)=0 by origin regularity. Since s has no growing part, the only nonvanishing asymptotic pairing is g0's C0 growth against s's C0 constant. Thus that constant vanishes. All remaining components of s are L². Under the kernel hypothesis, L intersection S is the one-dimensional translation line.
3. Project L onto the ten asymptotic growing coefficients. Its kernel is L intersection S, so its rank is nine. Because both L and S are Lagrangian, the annihilator of this growing image is the Wronskian pairing with L intersection S. This is a one-dimensional linear relation.
4. Translation has a nonzero neutral C2 r^-1 tail. Its pairing with neutral C2 r² growth is nonzero. Therefore the unique annihilating relation necessarily has a neutral C2 coefficient. No relation can be supported solely on the six charged growing rows. Those six rows have full rank. After eliminating them, the neutral growing projection has rank three and the fourth residual regular column is translation.

This would close the connection-rank hypothesis without fitting finite-radius determinant values. It does not by itself prove the leading mixed-wall determinant is nonzero; the actual boundary matrix on these four neutral directions and six charged directions must also be evaluated. Nor does it establish that every inverse-radius correction is universal. For that step the precise Coulomb asymptotic series, the g0/Wronskian constraints on neutral scattering, and exponentially small charged-neutral mixing must be controlled.
