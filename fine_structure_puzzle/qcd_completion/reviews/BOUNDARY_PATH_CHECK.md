# Conditional J1 boundary-path transfer

Coordinator algebraic check. This is conditional on the J1 large-x expansion in the full-vector report, not evidence that establishes that expansion. It evaluates that same dimensionless auxiliary comparison on a different path. It does not provide a dimensionful determinant measure or physical vacuum subtraction.

Let x=m_W R and suppose

    log Z_J1(x)=-3x+15 log x+C+3/x+O(x^-2).

If the physical wall Higgs magnitude b=m_W h(m_W R) is held fixed, rather than the asymptotic m_W, then with y=bR,

    y=x coth x-1,
    x=y+1+O(y exp(-2y)).

Substitution, without fitting or changing the function, gives

    log Z_J1=-3y+15 log y+(C-3)+18/y+O(y^-2).

Thus even this calculated partial-wave auxiliary term changes its inverse-radius coefficient from 3 at fixed asymptotic scale to 18 at fixed wall scale. This is the next-sector analogue of the frozen FSP-003 spherical path check. It reinforces the need to specify the complete metric/boundary comparison, including any dimensionful measure and reference-state transport, before interpreting a coefficient as a physical QCD vacuum response. It does not turn either coefficient into A_rho or electromagnetic-alpha dependence.
