# Primary-source check of the leading screened-cloud action

This check was requested by the coordinator after the independent exterior/core calculation had been saved and executed. It supplies an external benchmark and **no novelty claim**. The executed scripts, contracts and source-equations input are unchanged.

Forgacs–Obadia–Reuillon, [hep-th/0412057v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0412057v2), is the 24 March 2005 arXiv revision of *Numerical and asymptotic analysis of the 't Hooft–Polyakov magnetic monopole*, Phys. Rev. D 71, 035002. The action and normalization are equations (1), (4)–(7); the small-beta matched asymptotics are Section 5, especially equations (38)–(40). Its leading expansion is

\[
M(\beta)=M_0[1+\beta/2+O(\beta^2\log\beta)],
\quad M_0=4\pi v_F/e,
\quad \beta=M_H/M_W,
\quad M_W=e v_F.
\]

Only the linear coefficient and its conventions are being checked. The source analyzes an isolated classical monopole on infinite space with the ordinary adjoint-Higgs polynomial potential; it is not a deformed-Yang–Mills determinant or a finite-wall calculation.

For the static compactified conventions of REPORT M6–M9, identify q=e Phi, e=g, v=q(infinity)=e v_F, m=M_H, and multiply the classical mass by the circle length L. With kappa=L/g^2,

\[
L M_0=4\pi\kappa v,
\quad \beta=m/v,
\quad L M_0\,\beta/2=2\pi\kappa m.
\]

Thus M9 is **known after normalization translation** in this classical benchmark. Applying that leading correction to the specified double-trace theory remains conditional on the core/overlap assumptions already stated, since its potential is different. This check does not upgrade M9 to a complete normalized fugacity and does not alter the vacuum-stress verdict.

The [publisher's note, Phys. Rev. D 71, 119902](https://journals.aps.org/prd/pdf/10.1103/PhysRevD.71.119902), was also downloaded and read. It corrects a Table I entry at beta=0 to unity. It does not change the linear small-beta coefficient. No higher-order coefficient or numerical-fit value from this source is used.
