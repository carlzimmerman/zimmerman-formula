                                                           The electromagnetic form factors of the proton and the neutron are computed within lattice QCD
                                                        using simulations with quarks masses fixed to their physical values. Both connected and disconnected
                                                        contributions are computed. We analyze two new ensembles of Nf = 2 and Nf = 2 + 1 + 1 twisted
                                                        mass clover-improved fermions and determine the proton and neutron form factors, the electric and




arXiv:1812.10311v2 [hep-lat] 27 Aug 2019
                                                        magnetic radii, and the magnetic moments. We use several values of the sink-source time separation
                                                        in the range of 1.0 fm to 1.6 fm to ensure ground state identification. Disconnected contributions are
                                                        calculated to an unprecedented accuracy at the physical point. Although they constitute a small
                                                        correction, they are non-negligible and contribute up to 15% for the case of the neutron electric
                                                        charge radius.
