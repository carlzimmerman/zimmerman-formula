# FSP-008: exact flavor completion of the sea insertion

The question is whether the sea contractions omitted because they vanish at the
three-flavor symmetric point can be recovered by exact identities, with the
normalization and current contacts required by a proton or curved-vacuum
observable. The working claim is a finite-regulator identity, not a numerical
QCD prediction or a convergence theorem.

Fix a flavor-blind additive-mass Dirac regulator on one specified smooth
background, a photon inverse with specified gauge and zero-mode prescription,
and normalized QCD gauge averaging at e=0. Keep the same regulator for the
proton current insertion and the unconditioned vacuum insertion. The three
sea masses are (ml,ml,ms); charges are (2/3,-1/3,-1/3).

Derive the full normalized second source derivative, including valence,
same-loop sea, two separate sea loops, mixed valence-sea, and all second-source
vertices. Apply the exact light-minus-strange resolvent identity before noisy
trace estimation. Keep parameter matching, metric differentiation, contacts,
and the H-to-zero limit separate. Do not assume the flavor-singlet insertion is
a pure change of the strong scale.

An authorized bounded exact-arithmetic benchmark compares the decomposition
against differentiation of a finite determinant ensemble. It checks arbitrary
noncommuting vertices, nonzero source contacts and nonzero mean one-current
loops, as well as independent-noise products. The benchmark has no spacetime,
QCD dynamics, physical proton, curvature limit or numerical alpha. A pass can
validate the algebra only. A failure must be retained and diagnosed before a
revised run is interpreted.

The strongest practical source overlap is checked against Harris et al.,
arXiv:2301.03995v2, revised 1 May 2026 (equation 8 corrected), and RC*,
arXiv:2506.19770v1. Their flat-volume algorithms are not curved-space QCD data.
