# Global U(1) quotient and source-only normalization

This supplements the pretarget derivation without reading the new author package.

In Hopf coordinates for the constant SU(2) field, the U(1) gauge action shifts the charged angle psi, while s and the neutral angle phi remain invariant. The S3 metric is

`ds_S3²=ds²/[4s(1-s)]+s dpsi²+(1-s)dphi²`.

Its volume density is `ds dpsi dphi/2`. Dividing the integral by a fixed coordinate-Haar global U(1) volume removes the psi integral and leaves the same uniform s density. It does not add a new s-dependent mass determinant.

Equivalently, fixing a2=0 with a1>=0 gives a global Faddeev–Popov factor sqrt(s). The coordinate change a1=sqrt(s) contributes the inverse sqrt(s); they cancel. The stabilizer endpoint s=0 is a coordinate degeneracy of measure zero, and the source determinant has a continuous m²=uF²s→0 limit there.

The L2 normalization of the constant gauge parameter supplies a volume factor in the global gauge-group convention, as in the primary gauge-sphere source. It is independent of the orientation s and must remain with the absolute Maxwell normalization. It is not an extra source-dependent contribution to the compact photon ratio. Ghost scalar constants are omitted because d(constant)=0; no longitudinal vector is paired to them.

A free Maxwell determinant/anomaly, a coupling-normalization term such as −log e, and a matched U-independent uR contact are three separate objects. The first is an absolute known gauge contribution, the second has zero fixed-u radius derivative when H independent, and the third changes the physical charge response unless independently matched. None can be inferred by normalizing the Haar measure or by dropping the U-independent part of a curvature counterterm. The photon-only beta15 comparison in the pretarget note concerns the s-dependent pole alone.
