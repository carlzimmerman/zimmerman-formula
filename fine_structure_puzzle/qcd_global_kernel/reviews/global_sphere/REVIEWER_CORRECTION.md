# Correction to the preserved pretarget derivation

The independent pretarget note's **free anomaly sign** is wrong. This is a reviewer error; no author artifact was changed. The scalar and interaction formulas in that note are unaffected.

For the source's convention `W=-log Z`, dimensional regularization gives the radius-independent free pole `-A/(2epsilon)` in `d=4+2epsilon`. The curvature counterterm is proportional to `(mu R)^(2epsilon)` with pole `+A/(2epsilon)`. Its finite logarithm is therefore `+A log(mu R)`, not `-A log(mu R)`.

Thus `A=4a=31 dG/45+11 N_Dirac/90`, `H partial_H W_free=-4a`, and `rho_free=a/V`. The frozen UV target has this correct sign. An independent check is the conformal scalar: the integrated heat coefficient `a2(xi=1/6)=-1/90` gives `W=-(1/90)log H+constant`, consistent with `a_scalar=1/360` and the same convention.

For nine massless vectors and nine four-component Dirac components, `a=73/40`; hence `rho_free/H^4=219/(320pi^2)`. This correction preserves the original pretarget note so the independence record is not rewritten after seeing the target.
