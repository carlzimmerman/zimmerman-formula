# Scope and implementation review

The 18 exact identities and the 72 numerical cases at each of 60 and 90 digits passed in run_001. The manifest validates as a completed v2 evidence record. This is a self-review, not an independent proof certificate or QCD validation.

The numeric iterator is the full Cartesian product declared in INPUTS.json: 2 tensions, 2 prefactor constants, 3 powers, 3 radii and 2 charge parameters, for 72 cases. Each precision compares forced metric, forced mixed charge/metric, unconditioned metric and unconditioned mixed charge/metric derivatives. Direct differentiation of log1p(exp(-W_forced)) avoids loss of the exponentially small partition correction. All four formula values are also compared between precisions. The dimensionless fixtures have no calibration to physical QCD or alpha. The finite grid neither tests nor establishes the H->0 theorem.

The finite-sector covariance identity is checked using independent rational weights and their independent H/u derivatives, orthogonal to the exponential single-wall implementation. Pure volume/stress normalization is checked against rho=A H and against the intentionally different W=V A H. The equatorial negative area mode and the exactly critical derivative are checked separately. The universal conclusion rests on the explicit elementary exponential bound, with its derivative hypotheses exposed.

For precision about the phrase "controlled polynomial" in DERIVATION.md Sections 4–6: the nonzero forced/negative-effective-tension coefficient formulas use a prefactor of the form specified in Section 2, or at least H³ D log(kappa_poly)->0 and H³ D partial_u log(kappa_poly)->0 with sufficient charge-derivative control. The broader bound (5) alone deliberately does not imply those forced coefficient limits; Section 3 states that distinction. For a negative effective tension, one additionally needs a lower bound on kappa_poly (e.g. a nonzero positive k(u) on the compact interval in the displayed power-law form) to conclude p->1. A one-sided upper bound on an arbitrary prefactor is insufficient. A prefactor hiding exp(-constant/H³) must be included in the effective tension in (8), not called a polynomial measure. This paragraph makes the intended prefactor class explicit without changing frozen run inputs or its checked formulas.

All positivity and sign statements use the same curved-background Z0 that occurs in the declared partition ansatz. They constrain an added population and say nothing about a term already present in W0, a cancellation or phase interference between sectors, a nonpositive measure, or a different state prescription. The pure-area equator is an unstable stationary defect; the conditional partition formula does not promote it to an actual semiclassical QCD vacuum saddle. The physical missing leaf is a microscopic global state/measure and its matched charge response.
