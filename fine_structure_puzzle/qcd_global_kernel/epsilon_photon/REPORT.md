# A new gauge-complete photon contribution at epsilon² order

For a constant SU(2) orientation, s=a1²+a2², the LO gauged kinetic
action gives a photon mass insertion mA²=uF²s and a correlated
photon-pion derivative mixing. On closed S4 the mixing with transverse
photons vanishes by integration by parts. In an arbitrary covariant
gauge the longitudinal seagull and the two mixing insertions cancel:
their nonconstant-mode block has determinant lambda²/alpha_g,
independent of mA. Ghost constant modes and the global U(1) quotient
are kept separate. This establishes why the retained transverse
seagull is a gauge-complete orientation correction at this order.

The transverse Hodge spectrum yields

    Res zeta_T(1)=-1,       FP zeta_T(1)=2/3-2 EulerGamma.

Its curvature tadpole pole is -R/(32pi²eps). The resulting orientation
pole is canceled by delta k15=-1/(64pi²eps) in d=4-2eps, matching
the PHOTON-ONLY beta15=1/2 from the primary chiral source. This does
not inherit or resolve the separate pion-Z counterterm-basis question.

In a declared common zeta scheme, with the finite curvature LEC left
unfitted, the compact correction is

    delta W_gamma=t <s>,
    t=uF²/(2H²)[-log(mu²/H²)+2/3-2gamma-64pi²k15,zeta^r].

The explicit photon running cancels the scale dependence. DERIVATION.md
calculates its fixed-parameter metric and charge response, including
the required Haar covariances and third cumulants. The inherited Haar
integral is used as a definition, without repeating its old grids.

The absolute Maxwell/gauge-volume normalization and a pion-independent
curvature/EM contact remain separate. Finite scheme conversion, the
pion contribution, other NLO matching and the full QCD remainder are
not supplied. No alpha or cosmic target is fitted, and no complete
