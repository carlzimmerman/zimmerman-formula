# Sea-loop OPE and an evaluated ultraviolet photon band

## Scope and conventions

This calculation advances the missing individual-proton sea response by evaluating a controlled ultraviolet quantity. It does not evaluate its infrared completion. Write \(M_p(e^2)=m+e^2D_p+O(e^4)\), \(\alpha=e^2/(4\pi)\), and remove the vacuum expectation of every insertion. States have relativistic normalization and are spin averaged. The reference path for the complete response holds \(\overline{\rm MS}\) quark masses and strong coupling fixed at a declared scale \(\mu\). No numerical electromagnetic coupling is used.

Use the parent Compton convention

\[
W^{\mu\nu}=i\int d^4x\,e^{iqx}\langle p|Tj^\mu(x)j^\nu(0)|p\rangle,
\quad j^\mu=\sum_f Q_f\bar q_f\gamma^\mu q_f,
\quad Q^2=-q^2,
\]

where the notation includes the spin average. With \(P^{\mu\nu}=-g^{\mu\nu}+q^\mu q^\nu/q^2\), the first amplitude is \(P^{\mu\nu}W_1\), exactly the parent's \(T_1\). Hill–Paz uses \(\nu=2p\cdot q\); this does not change \(W_1(0,Q^2)\). Their second amplitude multiplies \(p_\perp^\mu p_\perp^\nu\), without a factor \(m^{-2}\). These distinctions prevent extra factors of two or of the proton mass.

For a finite four-dimensional Euclidean band \(Q_0<|Q|<Q_1\), define the actual zero-charge insertion

\[
\frac{D_{\rm band}}m
=\frac{i}{4m^2(2\pi)^4}\int_{\rm band}d^4q\,\frac{W^\mu{}_{\mu}(p,q)}{q^2+i0},
\tag{1}
\]

with the indicated Wick rotation understood. The microscopic modification here is a photon covariance restricted to that band and no local retuning. This finite kernel defines a different, controlled response functional from the complete QED mass derivative. Equation (1) uses the full tensor, including dispersive and subtraction contributions.

## Contractions and operator dictionary

At three degenerate sea masses, a single-current sea loop carries \({\rm Tr}Q\), two such loops carry \(({m Tr}Q)^2\), and a loop containing both currents carries \(S_Q={\rm Tr}Q^2\). For \(Q=(2/3,-1/3,-1/3)\), these are respectively \(0,0,2/3\). Vacuum subtraction leaves the proton–loop covariance. Its nonzero charge coefficient establishes survival of the topology, not the sign or nonvanishing of its full nonperturbative amplitude.

For a hard closed loop containing both currents, one external gluon gives a zero color trace. Two gluon attachments give a coefficient proportional to \(g_s^2/(16\pi^2)=a\), where \(a=\alpha_s/(4\pi)\). Thus the leading hard sea loop matches onto scalar and spin-two gluon operators at order \(\alpha_s\), carrying \(S_Q\). The external gluons can connect this local operator to the proton.

This is a short-distance topology statement. It is not a decomposition of the complete sea response into gluon operators alone: a soft segment of the same full loop can be represented by a disconnected quark-operator matrix element. Singlet quark and gluon operators mix. Full scalar quark matrix elements include disconnected contractions, and parton moments include sea quarks. The complete sea-loop observable therefore needs more information than the displayed gluon coefficient.

Use canonical field strength, \({\cal L}_g=-G^2/4\), and the dimension-four basis

\[
O_f^{(0)}=m_f\bar q_fq_f,\quad O_g^{(0)}=G^A_{\rho\sigma}G^{A\rho\sigma},
\]
\[
O_f^{(2)\alpha\beta}=\bar q_f\left(iD^{\{\alpha}\gamma^{\beta\}}-g^{\alpha\beta}i\!\not D/d\right)q_f,
\quad O_g^{(2)\alpha\beta}=-G^{A\alpha\lambda}G^{A\beta}{}_{\lambda}+g^{\alpha\beta}G^2/d.
\]

Both spin-two operators are traceless in \(d=4-2\epsilon\). The identity drops out of the hadronic covariance. Contact terms are retained through operator renormalization and the mass/coupling counterterms.

## Authenticated Wilson coefficients and mixing

The primary coefficient input is [Hill–Paz, arXiv:1611.09917v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.09917v2), equations (6),(7),(9),(18),(19). The exact version's rendered PDF pages 4 and 7 were checked because text extraction contains overlapping obsolete layers with wrong signs. The visible equations give

\[
T^{\mu\nu}=\frac1{q^2}\left[P^{\mu\nu}S_0+
P^{\mu\nu}\frac{q_\alpha q_\beta}{q^2}S_2^{\alpha\beta}
+P^\mu{}_{\alpha}P^\nu{}_{\beta}S_3^{\alpha\beta}\right]+\text{higher dimension},
\tag{2}
\]

where \(S_0=\sum_f c_{1f}O_f^{(0)}+c_{1g}O_g^{(0)}\) and \(S_i^{\alpha\beta}=\sum_f c_{if}O_f^{(2)\alpha\beta}+c_{ig}O_g^{(2)\alpha\beta}\). At the first nonzero order for each operator,

\[
c_{1f}=2Q_f^2,\quad c_{2f}=4Q_f^2,\quad c_{3f}=-4Q_f^2,
\]
\[
c_{1g}=aS_Q/3,\qquad
c_{2g}=aS_Q\left(-\frac{14}3+\frac83 L\right),\qquad
c_{3g}=aS_Q\left(2-\frac83 L\right),\quad L=\log(Q^2/\mu^2).
\tag{3}
\]

Quark coefficients have omitted \(O(a)\) corrections; gluon coefficients have omitted \(O(a^2)\) corrections. The bare spin-two gluon coefficients contain respectively \(-8aS_Q/(3\epsilon)\) and \(+8aS_Q/(3\epsilon)\), multiplied by \((Q^2/\mu^2)^{-\epsilon}\). The \(\overline{\rm MS}\) mixing term \(Z_{qg}^{(2)}=2a/(3\epsilon)\), multiplied by tree coefficients \(4Q_f^2\) and \(-4Q_f^2\), cancels those poles separately. Expansion of the same factor produces the two logs in (3). This explicitly retains the mixing/contact subtraction.

The mixing normalization is independently pinned by [Hill–Solon, arXiv:1409.8290v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.8290v1), equations (15),(20),(22), Tables 3–5. In their convention \(dO/d\log\mu=-\gamma O\), its spin-two entries are

\[
\gamma_{qq}=64a/9,\quad \gamma_{qg}=-4a/3,\quad
\gamma_{gq}=-64a/9,\quad\gamma_{gg}=4n_fa/3.
\tag{4}
\]

For example, the gluon-fed term in \(d[2\sum Q_f^2 f_f^{(2)}]/d\log\mu\) is \(+8aS_Q f_g^{(2)}/3\). It cancels the derivative \(-8aS_Q f_g^{(2)}/3\) of the explicit gluon log in \((c_{2g}-c_{3g})f_g^{(2)}/4\). Cancellation of the remaining quark-fed \(O(a)\) scale dependence requires the omitted one-loop quark coefficients. Thus a standalone gluon term at a finite scale is not a scheme independent full response.

## Scalar matrix elements and the subtraction tail

Define

\[
\langle O_f^{(0)}\rangle_p=2m^2 f_f^{(0)},\quad
\langle O_g^{(0)}\rangle_p=-2m^2\widetilde f_g^{(0)},\quad
\langle O_i^{(2)\alpha\beta}\rangle_p=2(p^\alpha p^\beta-g^{\alpha\beta}m^2/4)f_i^{(2)}.
\]

The trace and momentum sum rules yield

\[
1=(1-\gamma_m)\sum_f f_f^{(0)}-\frac{\widetilde\beta}{2}\widetilde f_g^{(0)},
\quad\widetilde\beta=\beta_s/g_s=-\beta_0a+O(a^2),
\quad\gamma_m=d\log m_f/d\log\mu,
\quad\beta_0=11-2n_f/3,
\tag{5}
\]
\[
\sum_f f_f^{(2)}+f_g^{(2)}=1.
\]

Consequently \(\widetilde f_g^{(0)}=2[1-(1-\gamma_m)\sum f_f^{(0)}]/(\beta_0a)+O(1)\). The apparent \(1/\alpha_s\) in the canonical gluon matrix element cancels the explicit \(\alpha_s\) in \(c_{1g}\). Equation (5) fixes the scalar gluon matrix element once the sigma fractions and coupling are supplied. It does not fix the gluon momentum fraction at a finite scale.

Taking the \(W_1\) projection of (2) at \(\nu=0\) gives

\[
\frac{Q^2W_1(0,Q^2)}{2m^2}
=-2\sum_fQ_f^2 f_f^{(0)}+\frac{aS_Q}{3}\widetilde f_g^{(0)}
+2\sum_fQ_f^2 f_f^{(2)}
+aS_Q\left(-\frac53+\frac43 L\right)f_g^{(2)}+\cdots.
\tag{6}
\]

In particular the hard-loop gluon subtraction coefficient is \(aS_Q[\widetilde f_g^{(0)}/3+(-5/3+4L/3)f_g^{(2)}]\). Formula (6) is not integrated down to small \(Q\). If used in the parent subtraction functional alone, its spin-two terms produce ultraviolet logs that must not be called the mass divergence.

## Exact full-tensor angular cancellation

Taking the Lorentz trace of (2), before taking \(d\to4\), gives

\[
T^\mu{}_{\mu}=\frac1{q^2}\left[-(d-1)S_0
-\frac{q_\alpha q_\beta}{q^2}\{(d-1)S_2^{\alpha\beta}+S_3^{\alpha\beta}\}\right].
\tag{7}
\]

Under an \(O(d)\)-invariant Euclidean photon-shell integral, analytically continued back to Lorentz indices,

\[
\left\langle q_\alpha q_\beta/q^2\right\rangle_\Omega=g_{\alpha\beta}/d.
\]

Every spin-two term in (7) therefore integrates to zero. This holds separately for each scalar function \(c_i(Q^2)\), including its logarithms, and before removal of the dimensional regulator. It cannot leave a pole-times-evanescent-trace remainder. This is a cancellation in the complete traced tensor, not in \(W_1(0,Q^2)\) alone. It uses a rotationally invariant photon regulator; a different regulator needs its matching terms.

Since \(d^4Q=\pi^2Q^2dQ^2\), the surviving scalar contribution to (1) is

\[
\frac{D_{\rm band}^{(0)}}m
=\frac{3}{64\pi^2m^2}\int_{Q_0^2}^{Q_1^2}\frac{dt}{t}
\left[\sum_fc_{1f}(t)\langle O_f^{(0)}\rangle_p+c_{1g}(t)\langle O_g^{(0)}\rangle_p\right].
\tag{8}
\]

At fixed leading order, define its logarithmic slope

\[
B_0=\frac3{16\pi^2}\sum_fQ_f^2 f_f^{(0)}
-\frac{3c_{1g}\widetilde f_g^{(0)}}{32\pi^2},
\qquad \frac{D_{\rm band}^{(0)}}m=B_0\log(Q_1^2/Q_0^2).
\tag{9}
\]

For the hard-loop gluon component alone, (5) gives

\[
B_g=-\frac{S_Q}{16\pi^2\beta_0}\left(1-\sum_f f_f^{(0)}\right)+O(\alpha_s).
\tag{10}
\]

The negative sign is checked against the parent subtraction prefactor: its scalar piece is \(W_1^{(0)}=-\langle S_0\rangle_p/Q^2\), so \(-3\int W_1^{(0)}dQ^2/(64\pi^2m^2)\) agrees with (8).

## Coupling and mass counterterms

The independent primary matching input is [Gasser et al., corrected arXiv:1506.06747v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.06747v3), equations (56),(57),(64), rendered PDF pages 16–17. Its leading logarithms are

\[
\delta m_f=\frac{3e^2Q_f^2m_f}{16\pi^2}\log(\Lambda^2/\mu^2),
\quad\delta g_s=-\frac{e^2g_s^3S_Q}{256\pi^4}\log(\Lambda^2/\mu^2).
\tag{11}
\]

Field normalization must be translated explicitly. Introduce \({\cal G}=g_sG\), so \({\cal L}_g=-{\cal G}^2/(4g_s^2)\). The coupling-variation insertion in the source is proportional to \(\delta g_s\,{\cal G}^2/g_s^3\); its canonical form contains \(\delta g_sG^2/g_s\). This rescaling follows by differentiating that kinetic term and is independently checked by the cancellation below. Treating the source's \(1/g_s^3\) formula as if its operator were canonical \(G^2\) gives a spurious factor \(g_s^{-2}\).

In the present normalization the counterterm contribution is

\[
\frac{D_p^{\rm ct}}m
=-\sum_f\frac{\delta m_f/e^2}{2m^2}\langle\bar q_fq_f\rangle_p
+\frac{\delta g_s/e^2}{4m^2g_s}\langle G^2\rangle_p
=-B_0\log(\Lambda^2/\mu^2).
\tag{12}
\]

The source symbols \(\delta m_f,\delta g_s\) are counterterm parameters in its displayed Lagrangian. Their mass insertion is \(-\delta m_f\,\partial M/\partial m_f\), and their coupling insertion is \(-\delta g_s\,\partial M/\partial g_s\). In particular, with rescaled fields \(\partial M/\partial g_s=-\langle{\cal G}^2\rangle_p/(4mg_s^3)\). Thus the source's negative \(\delta g_s\) in (11) is opposite to an actual positive bare-coupling increment used to implement the counterterm. Confusing that convention with a boundary tangent \(dg_s/de^2\) reverses the matching sign.

The gluon equality can be checked directly without the anomaly:

\[
\frac{3c_{1g}}{64\pi^2m^2}\langle G^2\rangle_p
=\frac{g_s^2S_Q}{1024\pi^4m^2}\langle G^2\rangle_p
=-\frac{\delta g_s/e^2}{4m^2g_s\log(\Lambda^2/\mu^2)}\langle G^2\rangle_p.
\tag{13}
\]

Thus the leading scalar ultraviolet divergence cancels for the individual proton, including the isoscalar gluon piece. The cancellation is not restricted to \(p-n\). The identity term is removed by vacuum subtraction, while quark and gluon insertions remain essential.

With a sharp photon cutoff and subtraction of the displayed logarithm, the logarithmic part of the renormalized tail is \(B_0\log(\mu^2/Q_0^2)\). This does not specify the finite cutoff-to-\(\overline{\rm MS}\) mass/coupling matching constant. Taking \(\mu=Q_0\) makes this chosen logarithmic part zero; it proves neither a zero finite tail nor a zero sea mass. A change of fixed-parameter path changes the full derivative by the mass and coupling Jacobians in the parent derivation.

## Evaluated alpha-free asymptotic quantity

Choose an explicit model: exactly three massless fundamental quark flavors in SU(3) QCD, rational charges as above, fixed flavor content at every scale, and a baryon of charge +1 whose QCD mass is \(m\). This is a declared microscopic theory, not physical QCD with finite strange/charm thresholds. All scalar quark operators vanish. Equations (5),(8)–(10) then imply the controlled limit

\[
\boxed{\displaystyle
\lim_{Q_0/m\to\infty}\frac{D_{[Q_0,2Q_0]}}m
=-\frac{\log4}{216\pi^2}
=-0.0006502823404878045\ldots .}
\tag{14}
\]

Here \(D\) is the coefficient of \(e^2\), not of \(\alpha\). Its equivalent coefficient per \(\alpha\) is \(-\log4/(54\pi)\). Perturbative corrections vanish with \(\alpha_s(Q_0)\); dimension-six and higher terms vanish with powers of \(m/Q_0\), subject to the usual asymptotic OPE expansion. The finite band response is nonzero. The complete renormalized ultraviolet mass tail still includes (12). No measured alpha, proton mass in energy units, empirical sigma term, fitted cosmological target, or lattice spacing enters (14).

For an orthogonal subtraction-function check, the LO matrix (4) and momentum conservation imply \(f_f^{(2)}\to3/(16+3n_f)\) and \(f_g^{(2)}\to16/(16+3n_f)\). The nonsinglet eigenvalues damp flavor differences, and the singlet nonzero eigenvalue is \(-(64+12n_f)a/9\). Thus in the same massless three-flavor theory

\[
\lim_{Q/m\to\infty}\frac{Q^2W_1(0,Q^2)}{2m^2}
=\frac4{81}+\frac4{25}=\frac{424}{2025}.
\tag{15}
\]

The \(4/81\) term is scalar gluon; \(4/25\) is the quark spin-two term after singlet evolution. The explicit gluon spin-two Wilson term vanishes as \(\alpha_s(Q)\to0\) at \(\mu=Q\), while sea content remains in the evolved quark moments. Integrating (15) as subtraction alone would retain a false full-mass spin-two logarithm; (7) proves its cancellation against the other tensor contribution. Equation (15) is not a complete sea-topology observable.

## Limits and continuation

The general finite-mass scalar coefficient is controlled by the sigma fractions in (9),(10). At a finite subtraction scale the twist-two fractions are independent inputs. Neither sum rule gives the nonperturbative same-loop Compton covariance at low/intermediate Q. The finite local matching constants, higher operator matrix elements and complete IR photon integral are absent here. The curved-space coefficient \(A_\rho\) is also independent of this flat-space calculation. Therefore neither (14) nor (15) evaluates \(R(\alpha)=A_\rho/m_p^3\), its stationary point, or its finite-alpha continuation. The next physical calculation needs the sea covariance with a declared current/contact prescription and the same mass/coupling path, or nonperturbative matching for that complete response.

Exact finite checks of the charge factors, all nine traceless four-dimensional matrix directions, pole cancellations, scalar counterterm coefficients, singlet fixed point and rational asymptotic coefficients are in compute_uv.py and run_001/. Those checks validate the finite algebra; they do not prove convergence of the OPE or compute an infrared hadronic matrix element.
