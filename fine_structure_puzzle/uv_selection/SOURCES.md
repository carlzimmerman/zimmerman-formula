## U-S1: coupled two-U(1) completion

- Bibliography: Christopher D. Carone, *Implications of asymptotic safety in two minimal Z-prime models*.
- Exact primary version: [arXiv:2512.12044v1, 2025-12-12](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.12044v1). Publication authenticated at [Phys. Rev. D 113, 035027, 2026-02-23](https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/yrr4-vhdw). Calculations here pin the preprint equations, not an assumed identical published layout.
- Locators: Tables I-II; Eqs. (1)-(5), (8)-(18); Sec. IV algorithm and Appendix A, Eqs. (20)-(22).
- Source status: derives/states one-loop matter fixed-point and correlation results in a parameterized-gravity model; no first-principles gravity coefficient calculation.
- Hypotheses: minimal SM + three right-handed neutrinos + one complex phi; B-L or chi charges; universal gravity correction above a Planck step; one-loop MSbar matter running; real top and bottom Yukawas.
- Translation: g1=sqrt(5/3) gY, source g is our gx, source tilde-g is our gt; fhat=16 pi^2 f_g. The declared covariant derivative here removes ambiguity in the source's shorthand matrix kinetic notation.
- Exact project arrow: published triangular beta system -> matrix flow in REPORT.md. **Verified within stated truncation** by independent charge sums and symbolic identity. Full physical UV completion -> these beta functions: **conditional**, requiring a controlled gravity calculation, consistent symmetry identities, and higher-operator closure.
- Calibration distinction: Eq. (8) sets fhat using hypercharge at the Planck scale; Appendix A supplies measured gauge and Yukawa boundary values. These numerical examples do not independently predict alpha.
- Additional source result, not rerun here: Sec. IV reports the nonzero-hypercharge branch gives a top Yukawa 3%-8% above its quoted 2-sigma upper bound; that exclusion is conditional on its assumptions and is not used as a significance claim for our calculation.
- Checks performed: exact-version metadata/history and publication authentication; equations and charge tables extracted; normalization translated; matrix/gauge/Yukawa implications computed. Anomaly cancellation and the source's numerical scans were not independently reproduced.
- Overlap: fixed points and ellipse are known verbatim; invariant matrix reformulation/stability/matching here are formal consequences after notation translation, not claimed novel.

## U-S2: original coefficient-free mass correlation

- Bibliography: Astrid Eichhorn and Aaron Held, *Mass difference for charged quarks from asymptotically safe quantum gravity*.
- Exact primary version: [arXiv:1803.04027v2, 2018-10-23](https://arxiv.org/html/1803.04027v2); [Phys. Rev. Lett. 121, 151302](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.121.151302). arXiv metadata states v2 is identical with publication.
- Locators: Eqs. (1)-(5), Fig. 1, paragraphs following Eq. (5), discussion of gravity universality and crossover.
- Extracted implication: nonzero top and bottom fixed points, common f_y and the stated third-generation truncation imply yt*^2-yb*^2=gY*^2/3. **Verified after translation**, also rederived by subtracting the two beta functions.
- Our inverse experiment: an authorized new calculation using this structure and newer explicitly declared reference boundaries. It is not a reproduction of the source's IR benchmark or a first-principles gravity prediction.
- Physical application: **conditional** on branch, flavor truncation, crossover, relevant non-Abelian inputs and matching. The source itself keeps f_g,f_y parameterized.
- Checks performed: version authentication; fixed-point/Yukawa formulas read and subtracted; project inverse application executed. Full source gravitational or two-loop calculations were not reproduced.
- Overlap: known coefficient-free relation; inverse numerical test here is an application, with no novelty assertion.

## U-S3: gravity-coefficient uncertainty with realistic matter

- Bibliography: Maksym Riabokon, Marc Schiffer and Fabian Wagner, *Regulator and gauge dependence of the Abelian gauge coupling in asymptotically safe quantum gravity*.
- Exact primary version: [arXiv:2508.03563v1, 2025-08-05](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.03563v1). The arXiv history was checked; no v2 exists there at this check.
- Locators: Sec. III Eqs. (8)-(24); Sec. IV; Appendix C, Figs. 11-13.
- Setup: Einstein-Hilbert/background-field approximation, linear metric split, Landau gauge limits, two regulator shape families; anomalous dimensions in regulator insertions neglected.
- Implication: the tested SM-matter truncation does not supply a regulator-stationary f_g. **Verified as a source statement**, not recomputed. Appendix C nevertheless finds partial optimization through the cosmological-constant exponent; hence a blanket claim that there is no useful optimization is too strong.
- Source caveat retained: large anomalous dimensions/background-field approximation make the SM extrapolation unreliable. This is uncertainty in the checked truncation, not a theorem that the exact physical coefficient is arbitrary.
- Checks: metadata authentication, technical approximation extraction, Appendix C conclusion read. No independent FRG integration.
- Overlap: known adjacent result.

## U-S4: current full-flavor branch, exact latest version

- Bibliography: Astrid Eichhorn, Zois Gyftopoulos and Aaron Held, *Quark and lepton mixing in the asymptotically safe Standard Model*.
- Exact primary version: [arXiv:2507.18304v3, 2026-10-09](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.18304v3), submission history authenticated at [arXiv abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.18304). v1 was discovery material, then the actual latest v3 was checked. v3 includes corrected appendix equations and PMNS convention; our claims below use v3.
- Locators: Sec. 2, Sec. 2.1, Sec. 2.2; Appendix C Table 1; Appendix D matching prescriptions; Appendix E Table 2.
- Source result: parameterized one-loop full-flavor trajectories support a bottom-dominated UV fixed point and an intermediate top-dominated regime. fg=9.749e-3 and fy=-3.27e-4 are calibrated parameters. Table 1 gives theta_t,UV=+0.0015, theta_b,UV=-0.00053, theta_gY,UV=-0.019; weak/strong gauge couplings are fixed in the IR.
- Exact application: the two-nonzero-Yukawa UV subtraction used in U-S2 is **inapplicable to this branch**. The top Yukawa is a relevant UV direction. Treating intermediate top focusing as an exact UV boundary would require an additional argument controlling the duration/cascade amplitude.
- Checks: latest-version metadata authenticated; the calibrated parameters, branch, critical exponents and matching prescriptions extracted. Ancillary Mathematica notebook and complete trajectories were not rerun.
- Physical accuracy: no new significance or uncertainty claim is inferred from the source table. This report does not audit its complete flavor phenomenology.
- Overlap: known adjacent result, with the current version changing the assumptions needed by an inverse mass-to-hypercharge test.
