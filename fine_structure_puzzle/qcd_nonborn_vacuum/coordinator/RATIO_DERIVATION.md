# What the new observables would calculate in the ratio

FSP-009 coordinator synthesis; this algebra is not a numerical QCD evaluation.
Use the original physical target and its fixed renormalized parameter family.
Let M(u)>0 be the flat, infinite-volume proton mass, u=e^2, and let
E(H,u)=W_tt-4W_t for t=log(H^-1). The new contact-complete vacuum derivation
expresses E and E_u as actual unconditioned sphere expectation values.

Define the finite-radius quantity

    R_H(u)=-H^3 E(H,u)/(4 Omega M(u)^3), Omega=8pi^2/3.

Its exact derivative at fixed H and on the declared common path is

    partial_u R_H=-H^3/[4 Omega M^3]
                   [E_u-3(M_u/M)E].

If the differentiated remainder and limit controls specified by the vacuum
derivation hold, these converge to

    R=A/M^3,
    R_alpha=4pi [A_u/M^3-3 A M_u/M^4].

M_u here is the complete renormalized proton response, including elastic,
non-Born, all sea contractions and finite parameter matching. A sharp soft
photon band defines only one component before the full matching insertions.
Its computed pi-N contribution cannot be substituted for M_u in this formula
while claiming a physical ratio. The analogous warning applies to replacing
E by a normalized topology ratio or a low-energy orientation correction.

The fixed-proton calibration path is different: its tangent derivative must
be used consistently in E_u and the action contacts, and then M_u=0 along
that path. The earlier calibration Jacobian already describes this projection;
it is inherited and is not a new way to cancel unknown nonlocal responses.

These equations identify two jointly required physical measurements rather
than a relation that eliminates one of them. The charge-exchange analysis
tests a proposed Ward closure in a channel where the vacuum is a singlet and
the proton two-current response is not. A measured flat condensate or bag
energy contributes E=0 for its constant-density piece and does not supply
the missing curvature remainder. No numerical value of A, R or alpha follows
without evaluating the remaining complete expectation values.

The new proton current-convolution calculations are coefficients at u=0.
Their completion would give the corresponding initial slope only. Full
finite-alpha dependence needs higher charge responses or a justified finite-u
solution, and selection of alpha needs an additional predictive physical
equation. An identity for R_alpha is not that selection equation.
