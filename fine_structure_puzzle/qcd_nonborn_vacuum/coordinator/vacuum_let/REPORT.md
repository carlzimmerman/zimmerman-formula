# A new flat-vacuum source and its connected-response obligation

**The 2026 source does not determine the sphere coefficient A_rho.**
[Bai-Chen2502.20241v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.20241v2) concerns condensate/bag
energy in flat QCD and thermal/dense information. Its lattice equation-of-state
analysis includes a fitted parameter. No such fit is imported as an unfitted
prediction in this programme. Exact source metadata, version, hash, extraction
and visual equation checks are in [SOURCES.json](SOURCES.json).

There is also a distinct admission question for the low-energy-theorem bound:
does the pointwise bare scalar/topological ordering survive the connected,
contact-complete prescription required by a normalized vacuum response? The
[raw derivation](DERIVATION.md) separates these operations. Configurationwise
S>=|Q| implies a raw second-moment inequality; it does not alone order their
variances. A strictly positive, CP-symmetric unconditioned finite product
ensemble supplies an exact counterexample to that abstract inference.

The [bounded run](run_001/manifest.json) completed and its current-input v2
manifest validates. It checks four exact partition/metric identities and
24 complete finite cases, summing 4,368 configurations with 4,588 assertions.
The cases are logical controls, not QCD samples. They preserve the true raw
inequality and refute only the stronger connected inference from those
premises alone. They neither implement QCD's anomalous scale Ward identity
nor establish that the source's physical numerical bound is false. A separate
QCD-specific connected/contact argument could provide additional premises.

For any flat constant density E0(u), W=E0(u)V gives rho=E0(u); its contribution
to rho(H)-rho(0), A and A_u is zero. This normalization identity does not
constrain the independent curved/global remainder. Even an exact improved
flat condensate value would therefore leave the requested coefficient open.

Independent review of the source application is recorded separately in the
phase review directory. The code, pre-run derivation and inputs remain frozen;
later corrections must be companion records. No global novelty claim, QCD
counterexample, alpha fit or author message is made.
