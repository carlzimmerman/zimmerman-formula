# Physical target and measurement baseline

Checked 2026-10-18, America/New_York. These are source checks, not a new global fit or a complete experimental review.

The target is the on-shell electromagnetic coupling in the Thomson limit, in rationalized units alpha(0)=e_phys^2/(4 pi). The programme uses the [NIST CODATA 2022 table](https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/pdf/all.pdf) as a reference: alpha(0)^-1=137.035999177 with standard uncertainty 0.000000021. This is an adjusted recommended value, not a statistically independent extra experiment. [NIST's database history](https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/Reference/versioncon.shtml) identifies the currently displayed adjustment as 2022.

Keep the independent atom-recoil determinations visible: [Morel et al., Nature 588, 61-65 (2020), abstract](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-2964-7) gives 137.035999206(11); [Parker et al., Science 360, 191-195 (2018), author preprint abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.04130) gives 137.035999046(27). Their difference is about 5.5 combined quoted standard deviations if correlations are neglected. This discrepancy does not provide evidence for a new theory by itself. A candidate should expose its theoretical uncertainty and the likelihood assumptions, rather than select whichever determination is closest. Demanding agreement with every quoted 1-sigma interval would be an impossible admission rule for these two data points.

The [PDG Electroweak Model review, Section 10.3.1](https://pdg.lbl.gov/2025/reviews/rpp2025-rev-standard-model.pdf) distinguishes the on-shell and MS-bar couplings and explains the hadronic contribution to evolution up to electroweak energies. Its preferred numerical alpha baseline differs from CODATA 2022. The source is a mutable PDF URL; values must be quoted with their source, not silently combined. The [Gabrielse-Venanzoni review, arXiv:2507.11268v2 (15 February 2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.11268v2), available from the authors, was also checked for recent experimental context; it is not used here to infer a new alpha value.

Only the numerical reference and the definition of the target are taken from these sources. No benchmark value appears in FSP-N's algebraic experiment. A value of a gauge coupling at a UV fixed point is a different observable until scale evolution, symmetry breaking and threshold/scheme matching have been supplied.
