# FSP-011 needs matrix: data required for M_u^p on the fixed path, and what exists

Rows follow the decomposition M_u^p = Born^(0) + (1/2) M_u^(p-n) + N0(mu0) of [DERIVATION.md](DERIVATION.md) eqs. (4)-(5). The status entries mean:

* **EXISTS**: published and usable as stated.
* **PARTIAL**: published but missing a required class, range, mass or normalisation.
* **ABSENT**: not found in the inspected sources.

Absence statements concern the sources inspected here and in FSP-006 to FSP-010. They are not global claims. "Alpha-normalised" means the input rests on cross sections divided by a measured alpha^2, so a per-e^2 coefficient built from it inherits the measured alpha.

**What the stuck proton step needs, minimally:** N3 + N4 + N5, at physical light and strange masses, for the u,d,s current, combined into the single functional N0(mu0) of DERIVATION eq. (5), with N8/N9 inside its matching. Everything else in M_u^p is now assembled (N1, N2) or reduced to existing local data (N8-N10).
