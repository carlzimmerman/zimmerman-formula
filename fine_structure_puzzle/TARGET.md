Base Git revision: 3c01aa2861bf576f4c2d942251eb9d2f89cae108. The surrounding repository has pre-existing uncommitted work; it is not an assumed clean baseline. Existing files are read-only inputs for this programme.

## Requested goal
Derive the physical Thomson-limit fine structure constant alpha(0) = e_phys^2/(4 pi epsilon_0 hbar c) from an explicit physical mechanism whose premises and parameter choices do not encode the measured answer. Identify a genuinely useful overlooked mechanism or obstruction if the full goal remains open. New exploratory particle/UV models are authorized; the historical wall around the Standard Model is not imposed as a restriction on this separate programme.

## Success and evidence
A derivation must identify a theory, normalize electric charge using a stated physical charge lattice, identify which UV data are fixed, demonstrate uniqueness/predictivity rather than merely a bound or a chosen boundary condition, run and match to alpha(0), expose all free real and discrete choices, and confront independent observations. A numerical coincidence, low-order truncation, fit, measured-alpha extraction or consistency band is partial evidence at most. Precision claims must include theoretical and experimental uncertainty. Do not treat any conventional significance cutoff from the old programme as a theorem.

## Initial portfolio and scope
- FSP-A: reconstruct the nearest failed routes and identify an actual change in premise or method required to reopen them.
- FSP-U: check current primary literature on predictive UV/fixed-point mechanisms and execute a bounded discriminating derivation.
- FSP-S: examine induced/emergent gauge dynamics through a dispersion/subtraction and charge-normalization audit, including whether a new physical boundary principle removes the free coefficient.
- FSP-N: examine whether canonical normalization, multiple U(1) factors and threshold matching hide free data behind geometric or topological coefficients; produce exact counterfamilies and a candidate admission gate.

Each route reports its first uncertain implication, decisive check, strongest surviving claim, external assumptions, actual inputs/hashes, and a concrete continuation. None is declared a solution because agents agree.
