# Common-regulator local bulk plus outer collar contract

Established before calculation or reading any new root synthesis. All previous
boundary and far-field evidence remains frozen. Writes for this task are
restricted to this directory.

The target is a local charged one-loop benchmark, not a full monopole action.
Use B_R^3 x S_L^1, tr(TaTb)=delta_ab/2, background Feynman gauge, relative
one-form wall and framed Dirichlet ghosts exactly as in the frozen boundary
lane. Compare h=v-a/r, B=n_mag rhat/r^2, v=pi/L, with h=v and F=0 on the
same geometry and domain. a and n_mag are fixed dimensionless tail
amplitudes. Keep a smooth fixed interior cutoff r_c and a uniformly gapped
outer region; any interior/core completion is outside the benchmark.

Use one four-dimensional PV sum before removing the regulator. Common
mass shifts M_j^2 and coefficients c_j act on the vector and ghost operators
with identical domains at every circle frequency. c_0=1, M_0=0, fixed
heavy-mass ratios, and sum c_j M_j^(2k)=0 for k=0,1,2 are a sufficient
regulator choice. The magnetic harmonic finite scheme is fixed at
mu=2pi/L; changing its finite coupling must remain explicit.

The output is the coefficient of 1/R in Gamma and in logweight -Gamma for
the local bulk derivative/potential terms plus outer boundary collar.
The collar must be matched to the chosen bulk density representative.
Specifically verify whether the frozen Duhamel mass-jet collar belongs to
the all-IBP bulk representative or the unintegrated local diagonal one.
Keep every compensating surface term in either convention.

Retain quartic potential a^4, electric a^2, magnetic n_mag^2, lower wall
mass-function contributions after the common PV limit, odd center sums,
and allowed finite boundary matching. No fitting, alpha input, full angular
determinant, core, neutral, lifted-mode, deformation, saddle, or stress
claim is authorized by this finite local benchmark. Hand derivations and
finite arithmetic checks are separate evidence. Any finite check requires
a pre-execution contract and a new bounded validated v2 run.
