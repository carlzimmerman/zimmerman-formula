## What changed

Handoff item 1 asked for all-current, non-Born two-current proton data across scales, including disconnected insertions, matching contacts and finite-mass dependence. That requirement is now reduced as follows.

* **An exact first-order isospin identity on the fixed MS-bar path:** M_u^p = Born^(0) + (1/2) M_u^(p-n) + N0(mu0).
* **The critical step.** The isoscalar response depends only on reduced amplitudes that do not occur in p-n. The same-loop sea (Tr Q^2 singlet), the separate-loop sea and the connected majority-minority exchange are absent from p-n. Hence no isovector datum, at any precision, fixes N0. This was re-derived by a separate method (placement enumeration with symbolic charges; isospin reduced amplitudes), run blind before comparison; it agrees class by class.
* **Charm reduction.** Charm sea classes reduce to existing local matching plus power corrections: the photon plus two-gluon quark-loop coupling vanishes by Furry's theorem, and the same-loop charm class is a threshold shift. Nonperturbative data are therefore needed only for the u,d,s current.
* **EFT image.** In the chiral EFT the isoscalar piece corresponds to the f_1 + f_3 combination. The f_3 operator is pion independent, so f_3 appears only in the isoscalar mass shift, consistent with Mueller-Meissner eq. (3.8), where f_2 alone enters m_p - m_n.

## What was assembled (MeV per e^2)

* **Born isoscalar.** Over all Q^2, Born^(0) = 1.8673 with BBBA05 form factors and 2.0184 with dipole-Galster. Born_p is 6.0713 and 6.3711 respectively.
* **Convention check.** The full-Born p-n reproduces the published elastic values: 0.771 and 0.798 MeV, against 0.75(2) MeV (Cao-Dai-Guo 2026) and 0.77 MeV (WLCM). Its normalization reproduces the point-Dirac sharp-cutoff constant 3/2.
* **Isovector total.** (1/2) M_u^(p-n) = 3.871 (+0.164/-0.327) from Cao-Dai-Guo [2609.15154v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15154v1) and 3.162(872) from GLR [2003.13612v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.13612v3). This conversion only undoes the sources' alpha prefactor; 6% of the CDG value rests on alpha^2-normalized cross sections.
* **Partial sum.** Born^(0) + (1/2) M_u^(p-n) lies between 5.03 and 5.89 over the choices. **It is not M_u^p.** The isoscalar local quark conversion, 0.8140 at mu0 = Lambda (71% from charm), belongs inside the missing N0 and was not summed.

## Remaining observable

N0(mu0) is the isoscalar, Born-subtracted, Feynman-gauge Cottingham integral of the u,d,s two-current proton tensor, with every contraction class and its local matching (exact definition in DERIVATION eq. (5)). It is not in any inspected publication or public dataset. Acquiring it requires e=0 lattice QCD:

* physical light and strange masses, at least three lattice spacings;
* full nucleon four-point functions including same-loop and split-even sea contractions;
* an infinite-volume photon kernel;
* OPE and sigma-term matching to the fixed path.

The nearest resources are the near-physical domain-wall ensembles already used for the published subtraction-function and disconnected-moment studies. That is a production job; none was started.
