# Review restriction on the common UV ratio note

This companion restricts COMMON_UV_RATIO.md without changing that posttarget
candidate's bytes. Its un-divided RG identities are valid under their stated
hypotheses. Equation(2), which writes c_g/beta_s, additionally assumes beta_s is
nonzero at the reference point, or requires a separately proved finite limiting
ratio. No beta-function-zero limit is established here. The strict massless
endpoint statement still requires existence/regularity, no extra scale and
actual decoupling/uniform limits. It is not a physical-QCD value.

The same independent review finds a separate wording issue in the frozen
hard_bridge/DERIVATION.md: O(s^1/2) control of f(s)-f(0) alone does not imply
control of its derivatives. The theorem is admitted using its explicitly stated
endpoint assumptions s^k f^(k)(s)->0 for1<=k<n, in addition to continuity and
integrability. These stronger assumptions hold in the benchmark and must be
checked for any physical transfer. The reconstruction identity is not claimed
from a big-O bound alone. The review records an oscillatory counterexample.
