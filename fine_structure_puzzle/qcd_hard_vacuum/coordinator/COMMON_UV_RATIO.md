# Common ultraviolet matching in the dimensionless ratio

FSP-010 coordinator analytic synthesis. This is a conditional consequence of
renormalization and the new all-photon-scale reconstruction, not a new physical
QCD coefficient. It changes no frozen worker input. The scale-projection logic
is inherited; the application here identifies which new heavy-photon endpoint
terms survive in the requested ratio.

Assume A exists as the nonanalytic H-linear stress coefficient of the common
unconditioned QCD theory, M>0, and both are differentiable RG-invariant functions
of renormalized g,m_f,mu with dimensions3,1. Analytic local gravity terms do not
shift A. Exclude independent extra background/state scales and control all
metric/mass/charge limit exchanges. Write gamma_f=-dlog m_f/dlogmu and
D_m=sum_f(1+gamma_f)m_f partial_(m_f). Dimensional analysis plus the QCD RGE gives

 beta_s partial_g A=-3A+D_m A,
 beta_s partial_g M=-M+D_m M,
 beta_s partial_g R=D_m R,  R=A/M^3.                      (1)

These are identities along a specified fixed-parameter family, not values of
A or its scalar derivatives. They do not follow from a vacuum trace expectation
alone without the existence and differentiability assumptions above.

Suppose the hard matching of a very heavy canonical photon shifts QCD parameters
per u by c_g(s),c_f(s), so O_u^hard=c_g partial_g O+sum c_f partial_(m_f)O,
up to higher-dimension operators. The SAME Wilson coefficients enter A and M.
The identity operator and analytic gravitational terms drop out of A; proton
matrix elements are vacuum-subtracted. Combining the matching before assigning
any unknown number gives

 R_u^hard=sum_f [c_f+(c_g/beta_s)(1+gamma_f)m_f]
                    partial_(m_f)R + higher-dimension terms.   (2)

Thus a universal strong-scale shift cancels between A and M^3. A gluon threshold
is not automatically an independent additive constant of the ratio. At physical
nonzero quark masses its effect remains through the actual mass derivatives of
R, which have NOT been calculated. The new leading quark finite coefficient
likewise multiplies m_f partial_(m_f)R. The proton sigma terms alone do not
supply partial_(m_f)A and cannot close (2).

In a strictly massless pure-QCD family, if R exists, no extra scale enters, and
the local scalar insertions have a regular vanishing-mass limit, the dimension4
heavy-photon endpoint of R_u vanishes. A matched all-scale reconstruction could
then have a zero UV endpoint for THIS dimensionless response while the separate
MSbar proton mass response has its nonzero threshold. Higher operators must
actually decouple and differentiated limits must be uniform. A formal massless
argument is not a physical-QCD prediction, and neither A=0 nor A nonzero is
established. If A=0 the displayed linear ratio identity still applies; no log A
is used.

This clarifies two requirements. Matching A and M separately and using different
parameter paths could spoil a real cancellation. Conversely, canceling their
universal scale piece does not calculate the remaining all-scale current and
unconditioned vacuum observables. The original alpha selection target remains
open even if these conditional endpoint identities hold.
