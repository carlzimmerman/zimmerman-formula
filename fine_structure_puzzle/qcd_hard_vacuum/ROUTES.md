# FSP-010 route index

Base FSP-009, [ancestry](BASELINE.json), [contract](CONTRACT.md). The full physical ratio and alpha goal remain open. Each partial package addresses a different missing physical step; their values cannot be added as if they were a complete mass.

The [common UV ratio note](coordinator/COMMON_UV_RATIO.md) uses inherited RG/scale projection to combine matching before assigning a physical number. A universal strong-scale shift cancels from A/M^3; finite physical quark masses leave mass derivatives of A as well as M. These are not supplied by proton sigma terms alone. A massless conditional limit is not physical QCD.

Source-discovery limitations are retained in the packages and [coordinator scan](coordinator/SOURCE_SCAN.json). Earlier soft polarizabilities, photon filters, finite sea moments, full Born tensors, topology, scalar OPE, pion/photon sphere and boundary routes retain their restrictions through the [FSP-009 handoff snapshot](../checkpoints/FSP-009_navigation/HANDOFF.md.snapshot.txt). A route requiring new data is deferred, not a general impossibility result.
