# Physical photon in the mixed-Abelian UV route

Base FSP-000; coordinator's independent application of the pole formula in CLAIM.md. This is conditional on FSP-U's one-loop beta functions and scalar charges, not a second proof of a quantum-gravity completion.

Let the connection basis be (W3, A_Y, A_X), with covariant derivative coupling charges (T3,Y,X). In the two models under study phi has (T3,Y,X)=(0,0,1); the neutral Standard Model Higgs component has (-1/2,1/2,X_H), where X_H differs between the models. With nonzero phi and Higgs expectation values, the unique unbroken connection direction is n=(1,1,0). A charged electron has q^T n=-1, for either chirality.

At tree level the neutral kinetic matrix is block diagonal between W3 and the Abelian 2x2 matrix K_A, with K_33=1/g2^2. Therefore

alpha_EM^-1=4 pi [1/g2^2+(K_A)_YY].

The kinetic mixing entry of K_A does not enter this expression directly. It affects trajectories and matching; radiative corrections remain necessary. This result does not require taking the heavy phi mass to infinity, as long as the mass kernel is exactly one-dimensional with the stated charges.

In FSP-U let G=[[gY,gt],[0,gX]], H=G G^T and K_A=H^-1. Direct inversion gives (K_A)_YY=1/gY^2. For B the charge-trace matrix and F=16 pi^2 f_g, a full-rank fixed point satisfying H B H=F H obeys H*=F B^-1 and hence K_A*=B/F. Both models have B_YY=41/6, giving

alpha_EM^-1 at tree-level matching = 4 pi/g2^2 + (41/6)/(4 pi f_g),

if these fixed-point couplings are the couplings supplied at that matching boundary. This is not alpha(0) until RG transport and scheme/threshold matching are done. It exposes the residual f_g and g2 boundary data. It must never be read as an electroweak formula evaluated in a physically broken vacuum at arbitrarily high UV energy; the relation is a boundary-condition dictionary followed by a low-energy matching problem.

The dependence on f_g is nonzero with other data held fixed. Whether such a variation is allowed in a full theory must be decided using that theory's gravitational critical surface. A local F^2 counterterm or a freely varied phenomenological f_g does not by itself refute a UV completion that actually fixes the relevant combination.
