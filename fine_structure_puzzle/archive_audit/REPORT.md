## Executed bounded discriminator: quantum lifting of a flat direction

The archive leaves Q3 quantum lifting uncomputed. To avoid mistaking “a minimum exists” for a prediction, this audit checked the explicitly *restricted hypothetical* form

`V(t)=M0^4 t^p [A+B log t]`, with `t>0`, `p>0`, `M0>0`, real A and nonzero real B.

This is **not claimed to be the Q3 effective potential**. Whether that model generates B at all, given its supersymmetry and spectrum, is an earlier physical question. It is a minimal scale-anomaly ansatz used only to expose the missing finite datum. With Q3's *tree-level dictionary* `alpha_SU2=t^2/pi^2`, exact differentiation yields

`t*=exp(-A/B-1/p)`,

`V''(t*)=M0^4 p B t*^(p-2)`,

`V(t*)=-(B/p) M0^4 t*^p`,

`d log(alpha_SU2*)/dA=-2/B`.

For this ansatz a stable finite extremum requires B>0 and is AdS. Every positive target t can be selected by setting `A=-B log t-B/p`. A finite renormalized A therefore permits arbitrary coupling unless UV matching fixes it. This is a physical free Wilson coefficient when the low-energy theory leaves it unspecified, **not** a claim that a mere renormalization-scheme change can alter observables with matched coefficients held consistent. Adding a constant uplift changes the vacuum energy but does not fix t; a modulus-dependent uplift can shift t and brings its own coefficients.

The first discriminating calculation for the Q3 continuation is therefore: determine whether the complete anomaly-consistent theory actually lifts the flat direction; derive the full finite effective potential and kinetic functions with UV matching; count every remaining coefficient; establish vacuum uniqueness/selection, stability and vacuum energy; identify a massless photon/SM charge lattice; then run and match. Since the archived Q3 U(1) is massive and `g'/g` remains free for added groups, even a unique t would not yet derive alpha(0).

All **16** new exact algebra checks passed. AH5's **4** and Q3 q1's **14** archived checks reproduced. Each of the three v2 manifests has `run.status=completed`, unchanged declared inputs and a successful validator result. These counts are finite implementation checks; the formulas above supply the actual analytic reason.

## Concrete continuations, in discriminating order

1. **A genuinely predictive gauge-gravity or gauge-Yukawa fixed point.** The archive's failure of two truncations and selected SM-desert rules leaves this open. Demand a zero-dimensional admissible critical surface for the relevant coupling combination (or a separately fixed relevant datum), normalized physical charges, all mass/crossover ratios and threshold matching. Stop if one free relevant parameter shifts alpha. This aligns with FSP-U, without reopening numerical boundary menus.
2. **A physically fixed induced gauge coefficient.** The existing one-loop logs are useful but do not remove a finite local coefficient. A proposed new UV boundary/state principle must constrain the subtraction term and the charged spectrum independently. This aligns with FSP-S and is a new premise, not a better fit to N_eff or a cutoff.
3. **Q3 quantum stabilization as a bounded alternative.** First compute whether the symmetry permits a nonzero lifting potential in a fully specified spectrum; only then compute finite matching. The exact result above is a cheap admission gate that prevents promoting an arbitrary A or uplift into a derivation. This is overlooked *in this archive*; no literature novelty is asserted.
4. **Fully dynamical de Sitter EM with genuinely new light states, only if independently motivated.** The AH3 rescaling obstruction does not cover it: under `e->k e`, `E->E/k`, lambda stays fixed but Maxwell energy scales as k^-2 and current as k. The coupled system can distinguish e. That observation is a scope correction, not evidence for selection of e. A successful calculation must also eliminate initial/state/spectrum data and satisfy independent constraints. Known massive-particle linear response is already a poor lane.
5. **MPP normalization/continuum audit before more lattice time.** B4 explicitly suggests an unverified 9/8 normalization discrepancy; the raw report also exposes convention uncertainty in SU2. Authenticate source action conventions and match a physical continuum observable first. Raw MC alone cannot fix the continuum mapping or the UV criticality postulate.
