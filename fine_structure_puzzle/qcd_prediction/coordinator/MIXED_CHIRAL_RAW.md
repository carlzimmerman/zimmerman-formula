# Raw coordinator derivation: a mixed chiral electromagnetic response relation

FSP-003, parent FSP-002. Written before reading the electromagnetic agent's follow-up result. This candidate is an infrared correlation test, not the requested cosmological ratio or an alpha value.

Use two degenerate light quarks with positive reference e=0 pion mass M, fixed strange data and strong scale, a fixed mass-independent QCD+QED matching convention, and e^2 as a continuous perturbative action parameter. Take the derivative in e^2 at e^2=0 first. Define

E_chi(M)=partial_(e^2) log chi(M,e^2)|_(e^2=0),
E_p(M)=partial_(e^2) m_p(M,e^2)|_(e^2=0).

The current leading chiral formulas have

E_chi(M)=K_chi^r-Z/(8 pi^2)[1+log(M^2/mu_chi^2)]+higher chiral terms,

E_p(M)= -F^2(f1+f3+f2)/2 -3 g_A^2 Z M/(16 pi)+higher chiral terms.

At the displayed order F,Z,g_A and the renormalized matching constants are fixed chiral-limit quantities. Thus

D_chi=partial_(log M^2) E_chi=-Z/(8 pi^2),
D_p=partial_M E_p=-3 g_A^2 Z/(16 pi),

and

D_p/D_chi=(3 pi/2) g_A^2.

Equivalently the normalized cross-sector response 2 D_p/[3 pi g_A^2 D_chi] is one at this order, without assigning Z, F, meson EM LECs or the baryon isoscalar EM constant. This is a proposed formal consequence of already calculated chiral terms; it is not being claimed as previously unknown physics.

For a claim about the complete theory, the safe candidate is the sequential chiral limit M->0 after the e^2=0 derivative, with g_A evaluated in that same zero-charge chiral limit, Z nonzero and higher-order terms controlled. Finite-e^2 and zero-mass limits do not commute because Delta_pi^2/M^2 ceases to be small. The leading analytic local mass terms disappear under the indicated derivatives; higher e^2 M^2 logarithms should vanish in D_chi as M->0 and in D_p as M->0, but all operators at the same order and mass-scheme conversions must be checked before claiming a full-QCD low-energy theorem. This path varies proton mass with the quark mass and charge. It is not the fixed-proton-mass cosmological inverse path.
