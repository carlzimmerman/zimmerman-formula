GROUNDING
The interp asserts ONE fixed dimensionless number theta* (= phi = 1.618034, or
1/phi = 0.618034) is the shared kernel predicting BOTH:
  -- the CKM CP phase  delta_CP = 1.14 rad  (flavor physics), and
  -- the spectral index  n_s = 0.9649       (cosmology),
via "pi-free" maps F(phi) -> delta_CP and G(phi) -> n_s, with theta* fixed in
advance (no per-bullet refit).

ARITHMETIC (2 min, no refit)
Natural pi-free combinations of phi (no free parameters, no pi):
  phi = 1.618034
  1/phi = 0.618034
  sqrt(phi) = 1.272020
  phi - 1/phi = 1.000
- delta_CP band [1.11, 1.17] (1.14 +/- 0.03): nearest natural value is
  sqrt(phi) = 1.272 -> 11% off; far outside the 2x window [1.08, 1.20].
  phi and 1/phi are further off. No pi-free phi expression lands in band.
- n_s = 0.9649 (1 sigma ~ 0.0005): no natural phi combination lands; closest is
  the CONTRIVED 1 - 1/phi^7 = 0.96556, off by ~0.0007 (outside 1 sigma), and the
  exponent "7" is unprincipled (no target-independent reason for 7).

FATAL FLAW: F and G are UNDEFINED
The hypothesis specifies only "theta* enters through phi" and "and/or the ratio
n_s:phi". That is not a function. With F, G free, the single-number claim is
UNDERDETERMINED / TAUTOLOGICAL: for any target you can define F(phi)=1.14 and
G(phi)=0.9649 by fiat, so the hypothesis cannot be falsified as written. This is
exactly the per-bullet overfit the idea's own "WHAT KILLS IT" clause rejects
("a per-bullet fit is two free parameters ... => DISCARD").

NEAR-MISS CHECK: NOT available
NEAR-MISS requires the miss on a SPECIFIED function to be within 2x of a stated
tolerance, or ONE principled target-independent fix. Neither holds:
- the delta_CP miss (~11% for the nearest natural phi value) is ~4x the 0.03
  band -- far outside the 2x window;
- there is no specified F/G to take a miss against, and naming a fix would mean
  inventing F, which IS the overfit and has no target-independent justification.
The two bullets (flavor CP phase, cosmological spectral index) are unrelated
domains with no mechanism for a shared golden-ratio origin.

GRADE: DISCARD
REASON: F and G are unspecified, so the "single golden-ratio kernel" is
underdetermined/tautological (the per-bullet overfit the idea itself forbids);
and no natural pi-free phi combination lands delta_CP=1.14 rad (nearest 11% off,
~4x the band) or n_s=0.9649 (only a contrived exponent, outside 1 sigma).
Neither a PURSUE (not falsifiable) nor a NEAR-MISS (no specified function; miss
far outside 2x) is warranted.
