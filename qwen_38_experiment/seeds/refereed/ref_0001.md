REASON (one sentence): The hypothesis is structurally unfalsifiable as stated — its
defining functional forms f_wall and f_Q0 are explicitly deferred to "separate seeds"

for delta_pred) cannot be executed in-repo without the very functions the interp withholds.

  dimensional normalization is fixed (f_wall/f_Q0 undefined).

the test is unrunnable and the "shared C" is tautological with free functional forms.
