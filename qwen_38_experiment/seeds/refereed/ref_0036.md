   convergent auto-satisfies cannot falsify anything -- the "recovery" is post-hoc
   selection among many functionals, not a genuine prediction.
3. The torsion readout (test 3) is explicitly future/unmeasured, so it contributes
   nothing now.

Net: a ratio coincidence dressed as a single-knob channel, whose only checkable core
(CF recovery) is degenerate/tautological. Not falsifiable in any meaningful sense.
