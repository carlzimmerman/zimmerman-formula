# INTERP 0033 -- one dimensionless number shared by lepton-mass bound & recombination off-switch

SEED: holonomy angle R_dm=0.387 bounds m_mu/m_e=206.77; pi-free part of alpha^-1=137.036
selects the recombination off-switch; wildcard = what ONE number would both bullets share.

## Charitable reading
The two bullets are two readouts of ONE geometric quantity: the dark-matter-sector
holonomy angle R_dm. It is UNIVERSAL. It bounds the lepton mass ladder (a geometric bound
on m_mu/m_e), and because the fine structure constant enters recombination physics, the
same angle also fixes the recombination "off-switch" epoch. The "pi-free" 137.036 is the
RAW inverse coupling; its role is that recombination is governed by the GEOMETRIC part of
alpha (the holonomy), not by the bare number 137. The wildcard answer is therefore R_dm
itself: one angle, two consequences.

  (b) F_rec(R)  reproduces the recombination off-switch, z_rec = 1089 (Planck) /
      T_rec ~ 0.26 eV, within 2x,
using the SAME R and the SAME family, with R NOT fitted to either target.

- NULL: hits one target, misses the other (the two bullets are independent, not one angle).
- DISCARD: reproducing both needs two different N's, or R must be fit to a target value
  (that is p-hacking, not a discovery).
