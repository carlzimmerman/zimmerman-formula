WHAT THE SEED SAYS (paraphrased)
- A "duality exchanging the drain flow" is measured by the Koide relation Q_k = 2/3
  (Koide quark-mass sum rule: Q = (m_u+m_c+m_t)/(m_d+m_s+m_b) = 2/3).

CHARITABLE UNIFICATION
The shared number is the Koide constant Q_k = 2/3. A drain-flow duality partitions a
flow F into a drained part F_d and a remnant F_r with the fixed ratio F_d : F_r = 2 : 1,
i.e. F_r/F = 1/3 and F_d/F = 2/3 -- the 2/3 of the Koide bullet IS the partition ratio.
Applying it to 1/alpha = 137.036:
  F_r = (1/3) * 137.036 = 45.6787   (the "drained remnant")
  F_d = (2/3) * 137.036 = 91.3573
The duality then claims F_r equals the pressure-promotion A(Q) = kappa^2 * G(-K).
So the single shared number 2/3 must reappear INSIDE kappa^2*G(-K), i.e.
  G(-K) = F_r / kappa^2 = 45.6787 / kappa^2.
  with kappa = 1/2 exactly:  G(-K) = 4 * 45.6787 = 182.715
  with kappa = 0.551 (fitted central): G(-K) = 45.6787 / 0.3036 = 150.46
Both are quoted; the test must not lean on the fitted kappa.

HYPOTHESIS (H-0069, one falsifiable claim)

of 1/alpha), equals 150.46 (kappa=0.551 footing) -- equivalently 182.72 at the
kappa=1/2 footing -- AND this equality arises because G(-K) contains the Koide ratio
2/3 as a structural constant, not as a fitted one. The two bullets share exactly one
number: 2/3.
