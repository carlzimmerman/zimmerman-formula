# INTERP 0065 -- the aether-tilt common-shadow hypothesis

SEED 0065 (three bullets):
  B1: a polyhedral solid angle of the pressure promotion A(Q)=kappa^2 G(-K)
      is measurable by the (neutrino) Koide number 2/3.
  B2: the pi-free part of m_W/m_Z (=0.8814, seed value) is the shadow of the
      "aether tilt".
  B3 (wildcard): one dimensionless number that BOTH bullets share, if true.

## Charitable deciphering
Both B1 and B2 are proposed as two independent shadows of ONE "aether tilt"
number tau.  B1 says the normalized polyhedral solid angle G(-K) entering the
pressure promotion is pinned by the neutrino Koide relation to 2/3; B2 says
the pi-stripped weak-mass ratio is the same tilt; B3 asks for the single tau.

## The hypothesis (one concrete object)
H-0065: there is a single dimensionless aether-tilt tau such that
  (i)   G(-K) = Q_nu = 2/3   (B1, via neutrino Koide), and
  (ii)  (m_W/m_Z)_{pi-free} = tau   (B2), with pi-dependence stripped by
        removing the explicit one-loop ~pi^2 electroweak radiative terms,
  and therefore the two estimates of tau must COINCIDE: 2/3 == 0.8814 (mod pi).
The shared wildcard number is proposed to be tau = 2/3 = 0.6667 (equivalently
the seed's 0.8814 once its pi-part is subtracted).  If kappa is the tilt,
A(Q)=kappa^2*(2/3) with kappa=0.551 gives A(Q)=0.203; with kappa=1/2 gives 1/3.

## Exact test (what a referee runs)
  tau_1 = 2/3 = 0.6667  (predicted) vs measured neutrino Koide Q_nu = 0.6594+/-0.001.
  tau_2 = 0.8814        (B2, pi-stripped m_W/m_Z, PDG-based).
  PASS criterion: |tau_1 - tau_2| < combined 1-sigma error on both.
  Dimensional guard: any dimensional A(Q) cross-check must hold on BOTH
  footings a0 = 9.3619e-11 and 1.1279e-10.

## What kills it
  - 2/3 (0.6667) vs 0.8814 differ by 0.215 (~32%): orders of magnitude beyond
    any error => REFUTED/NULL if the two are not identified after pi-stripping.
  - Even tau_1 vs measured Q_nu is a ~1% near-miss (0.6667 vs 0.6594): at best
    a near-miss, NOT a hit; do not launder it as agreement.
  - kappa=1/2 is EXCLUDED (fitted 0.551+/-0.043); the 1/3 variant of A(Q) is
    already off by ~21% from the 0.551 footing => REFUTED for that branch.

## Verdict expectation
Most likely REFUTED or a documented near-miss (2/3 vs 0.6594). Honest grading
refers this to the blind referee; a CONVENTION-grade match is not a hit.
