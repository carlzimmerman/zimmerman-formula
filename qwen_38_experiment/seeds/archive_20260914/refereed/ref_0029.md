# REF 0029 -- blind referee verdict

Seed: interp_0029 ("single shared dimensionless number links Cabibbo boundary
term and Koide y-gate"). Blind check: read ONLY the interp.

## Grade: DISCARD

## Reason
Not falsifiable in-repo, and the "one number, two extractions" is a p-hacking
coincidence search, not a free prediction.

1. Undefined test: the interp gives no concrete renormalization map f, no numeric
   boundary/bulk ratio of A(Q)=kappa^2 G(-K), and no y-gate extraction. Neither
   the Cabibbo side (f(r)->0.2250) nor the Koide side (g_y->0.551) can be
   computed from in-repo data/scripts. A test that cannot be evaluated is not
   falsifiable.
2. Trivially satisfiable: if X=kappa=0.551, consistency forces r=0.551 and forces
   f(0.551)=0.2250. Two free knobs (r and f) are then made to hit two targets
   (0.551, 0.2250) -- satisfiable by construction, no free prediction left.
3. Coincidence search: the wildcard asks for the ONE dimensionless number shared
   by two unrelated constants (Cabibbo sin(theta_C)=0.2250, kappa=0.551). With no
   a-priori coupling and f free, |X_cab - X_koide|<tol can always be engineered.
   This is exactly the dial/p-hacking structure the PROTOCOL bars.

## Near-miss? No.
Not a fail-within-2x-tolerance (the test is undefined, not a numeric miss), and
there is no single principled fix whose justification does not depend on making the
number come out right (fixing f means tuning to 0.2250).

## Footings
All quantities dimensionless (Cabibbo, Koide, kappa); dimensional footings
(9.3619e-11 / 1.1279e-10) do not apply.
