## Why DISCARD (each "kills it" bullet is hit)

1. CONSTRUCTION, not physics. 0.70 is not independently motivated; the seed put it

   construction of the seed, not by physics." The bridge is circular by design.

   single bridge. "0.70 is one number read twice" cannot be falsified -- any two
   coincidences can always be declared "the same number." Loophole, not a bridge.

3. NO IN-REPO DATA TO FALSIFY. The test needs "the RAR-residual-vs-Ups curve" and
   "the top-Yukawa-boundary-vs-ratio curve" fixed from independent data; neither is
   pinned down anywhere, so there is nothing to evaluate at beta=0.70 in-repo.
   Fails the PURSUE prerequisite (falsifiable with in-repo data/scripts).

4. CIRCULAR TARGETS. Both targets are known SM numbers: delta_CKM is measured;
   kappa is explicitly FITTED (0.551 +/- 0.043), and the idea itself concedes the
   bridge "would only be evidence if it predicts kappa independently of the fit" --
   which it cannot, since it is built from an SM input (top Yukawa). This hits the
   "needs >1 free parameter -> coincidence" and CONVENTION-grade-fluke kills.

## Not a NEAR-MISS

targets, no in-repo data), not a numerical miss within 2x of a stated tolerance.

hit the number." DISCARD is the honest outcome; this is a construction artifact,
which the seed's own prior flagged.
