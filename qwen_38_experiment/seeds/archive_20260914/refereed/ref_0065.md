(ii) (m_W/m_Z)_{pi-free}=tau=0.8814; the two must COINCIDE: 2/3 == 0.8814 mod pi.

## Arithmetic (independent re-check)
- tau_1 = 2/3 = 0.6667 ; tau_2 = 0.8814 ; |tau_1 - tau_2| = 0.2147 (~32% of 0.6667).
- PASS criterion = |tau_1 - tau_2| < combined 1-sigma on both (errors ~0.001-0.002).
  Miss 0.215 ~ 100x the tolerance => the coincidence (i)==(ii) FAILS HARD.
- Independent leg: tau_1 = 0.6667 vs measured neutrino Koide Q_nu = 0.6594 +/-0.001
  -> diff 0.0073 = 7.3 sigma. NOT within 2x of the stated 1-sigma tolerance, so it is
  NOT even a NEAR-MISS (interp's own "What kills it" concedes this).
- kappa=1/2 branch: A(Q)=kappa^2*(2/3)=1/3=0.3333 vs fitted kappa=0.551+/-0.043
  (A(Q)~0.203) -> ~21% off; kappa=1/2 is EXCLUDED by the fit.

## Why not PURSUE
Falsifiability fails: G(-K) is an unspecified polyhedral solid angle and the
"pi-stripping" of m_W/m_Z is an undefined operation (no operator/map given), so
the crux reduces to "force two independently-constructed numbers (0.6667 and 0.8814)
to be equal" -- a fiat identity, not a testable relation. The 32% gap is decisive.

## Why not NEAR-MISS
NEAR-MISS requires the miss within 2x of the stated tolerance OR one principled
fix independent of the target. Miss is 32% (~100x tol) and tau_1 vs Q_nu is 7.3
sigma -- both far outside 2x-tol. The only "fix" (strip pi so 0.6667==0.8814) is
an ad-hoc dial whose justification depends entirely on making the number land =
value-dependent p-hacking, forbidden by the NEAR-MISS calibration ("a dial is
p-hacking"). No target-independent one-line fix exists.

## Footings / kappa discipline

kappa=0.551+/-0.043 is FITTED, not derived; not claimed here. No CONVENTION-grade

GRADE: DISCARD -- the two legs of the claimed single tau differ by 32% (0.6667 vs
0.8814), ~100x the combined 1-sigma tolerance, and the reconciling "pi-strip" is an
undefined ad-hoc dial; even tau_1=2/3 vs Q_nu=0.6594 is 7.3 sigma off, not a
near-miss. DISCARD is the honest success.
