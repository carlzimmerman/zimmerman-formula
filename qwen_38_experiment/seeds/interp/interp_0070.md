## The wildcard answer (single shared dimensionless number)
The single number both bullets share is the golden ratio phi = 1.6180339887
(and its nesting phi^(1/2)=1.2720, phi-1=0.6180). Both bullets are claimed to be
two readings of the SAME phi-based map on the framework's a0-line, so the
hypothesis is that phi -- not m_p/m_e or 0.2312 -- is the binding constant.

## Bullet 1 (charitably restated, concrete)
The a0-line map I(y), pinned so that g/g_N = phi at y = 1, has its "golden-ratio
point of the golden-ratio point" = I iterated through the golden-ratio
substitution twice. Claim: that nested value equals m_p/m_e = 1836.152673.
All quantities here are dimensionless (ratios), so NO a0 footing is involved --
both footings (9.3619e-11 / 1.1279e-10) are irrelevant to this test.

## Bullet 2 (charitably restated, concrete)
The "drained remnant" of the weak mixing angle, R = 1 - sin^2(theta_W)
= 1 - 0.2312 = 0.7688 (or sin^2 itself, referee to pick one, pinned a-priori),
at the SAME nesting depth as Bullet 1 yields the dark-energy equation of state
w = -1 EXACT (cosmological constant; "the vacuum never rolls", no quintessence).
Again dimensionless, no footing.

2. Compute nested-phi output for Bullet 1; compare to 1836.152673 (2 sigma).
3. Compute nested output for Bullet 2; compare to w = -1.000 (Planck 2018
