## Results already checked

1. **Evolving dark energy does not guarantee distinguishable couplings.** With different fitted matter abundances and constant coupling coefficients, density and canonical-potential portals can share exactly the same expansion and acceleration histories. A positive, evolving-w counterexample is explicit. The pressure portal can be separated from that pair by a derived interval condition. [Portal derivation and 41 symbolic checks](portals/REPORT.md).
2. **Positive baryons impose a force-shape condition independent of arbitrary stellar gradients.** We derived exact local and finite-shell bounds for isolated spherical simple-μ and quadratic laws. The DESI Milky Way central proxy profile violates positivity at the usual acceleration footings in some shells, but positive profiles exist inside the quoted marginal intervals. This is not a statistical exclusion. [Bounds and feasibility witnesses](positivity/REPORT.md), [independent audit](portals/POSITIVITY_AUDIT.md).

3. **A distance-free acceleration/gas quotient removes each galaxy's velocity-amplitude inclination mode.** S=T/A_gas=a₀/γ_gas gives an acceleration-history ratio if gas conversion is relatively calibrated. Its exact invariance does not guarantee precision: nonlinear profiles remain broad on all three development templates. The missing design information is gas-force-dominated transition shape. [Inverse report](inverse/REPORT.md), [independent quotient audit](inverse/QUOTIENT_ROOT_AUDIT.md). The 342 full synthetic cases include a preserved failed unregularized DDO154 block; none is an observed gravity residual fit.
4. **Even a fixed identical coupling coefficient cannot separate density and potential from background histories alone.** An explicit positive family assigns different dust-like amounts to matter and the scalar while preserving both histories. [Fixed-coefficient counterexample](portals/FIXED_COEFFICIENT_ADDENDUM.md).
5. **Growth supplies a conditional third observable.** Under a specified GR + smooth-dark-energy perturbation closure, a derivative-free integral relation identifies the matter coefficient while profiling the unknown initial growth amplitude. It breaks that background ambiguity. It requires latent between-bin functions, measurement windows and joint covariance; no DESI growth pass has been established. [Derivation and independent symbolic audit](portals/GROWTH_IDENTIFIABILITY_AUDIT.md).
