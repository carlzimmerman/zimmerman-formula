The DESI Milky Way central proxy profile violates positivity at the usual acceleration footings in some shells, but positive profiles exist inside the quoted marginal intervals. This is not a statistical exclusion. [Bounds and feasibility witnesses](positivity/REPORT.md), [independent audit](portals/POSITIVITY_AUDIT.md).
