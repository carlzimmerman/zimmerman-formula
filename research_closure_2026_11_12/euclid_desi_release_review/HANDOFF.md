## Open experimental obligations

For the strongest route, obtain a constrained two-dimensional dynamical inverse problem: a specified physical kernel/field solver, independent velocities, stellar colour/IMF-gradient information, gas shape, inclination/thickness, PSF and correlated errors. Fit A and T without imposing the cosmological relation; predict held-out resolved information. The old-SPARC calculations are development evidence for identifiability only.

For lensing, retrieve vetted source-redshift links and resolve radius/SED conventions, then use actual image/PSF/noise/mask products. Freeze separate absolute-mass, profile-shape and independent-dynamics gates. Model or condition on successful-SIE selection. Do not substitute an SIE velocity scale for stellar spectroscopy.

For spectral extraction, confirm same-line target coverage in opposite orientations and validate line blends and astrometry against suitable external kinematics or injection tests. The formula alone does not turn a line displacement into a circular speed, gas mass or flat rotation curve.

For the joint BAO analysis, carry full covariance and use independent q=a₀r_d/c². Test background column rank and coupling convention before reporting κ. Compare density, pressure and potential portals as separate forward hypotheses; constructing a density history from one hypothesis and then confirming that hypothesis is circular.

These are open continuation conditions, not exhausted routes or a claimed impossibility theorem. The work has not resolved a healthy common action, cosmological growth or every historical failure.
