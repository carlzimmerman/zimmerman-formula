# Final bounded injection / holdout review

12 November 2026. Read-only inspection of v2 script, selection, contract and completed run02 results. No independent nonlinear refit or extra experiment performed.

## Outcome: ready with narrow scope preserved

The 12 inverse cases are consistent with the stated contract: one preselected NGC2403 development template x two pointwise algebraic laws x three deterministic gamma injections x two inverse models. Each case uses three fixed optimizer starts (36 optimizer calls in total). All stored best optimizations succeeded; reported multistart and interpolation checks passed. No new core algebra or units error was found.

This is useful evidence that an omitted radial source gradient can strongly bias T while this particular internal holdout does not reliably flag it. Allowing the exact injected gradient family substantially restores T in this synthetic model. It is not an observed fit, independent gravity validation, nuisance-complete precision result or calibrated predictive test.

## Selection, truth and held-out data

The v2 selection declares the target was chosen after the information preflight for useful sampling; it is a development case. Training excludes exactly sorted zero-based indices 12–17 and 44–49: 61 training rows and 12 held-out rows, corresponding to 1.36–1.87 and 6.96–9.44kpc. These are two interpolation annuli inside the measured range, not an independent target, outer extrapolation test or held-out observing campaign.

Holdout velocities are excluded from least-squares residuals. Published velocity/error values supply radial availability and error weights only: the fitted truth is generated by the chosen law, not the observed V curve. The stellar light template is available at all radii, including held-out velocity radii, which is appropriate for holding out kinematics conditional on imaging. Holding out those velocity points does not make the light model itself independent.

Source truth directly recomputes the softened-ring force with Sigma(r) multiplied by (r/Rd)^gamma for gamma=-.1,0,+.1. This is a genuine source perturbation within the surrogate ring geometry rather than merely multiplying the local force by a radial factor. The inverse uses the same ring geometry, calibrated published disk baseline and same power-gradient family, via interpolation. Direct-versus-interpolated predictions are checked, but truth and inverse are not independent physical source models. Missing thickness, source-tail and disk-field validity uncertainties from the earlier preflight remain missing.

The first launch is retained as failed; the v2 notice says it stopped before fitting on a nonnegative outer-SB slope caused by rounded equal bins. The fallback -1/Rd is the preceding preflight policy and occurs before the optimizer in v2. This review did not infer science results from run01 or independently establish historical selection timing beyond the preserved contract/manifest record.

## Result interpretation

With gamma fixed to zero, nonzero injected gradients produce T biases:

- simple-mu: -0.4221 and +0.6338 dex;
- quadratic: -0.3047 and +0.3647 dex.

With gamma profiled and its Gaussian 0+/-0.1 exponent prior, recovered gradients are approximately -.095 to +.098 and T biases are -.0199 to +.0130dex (simple-mu), -.0150 to +.0109dex (quadratic). These remaining offsets include shrinkage toward the zero-gradient prior: the injected nonzero cases are one prior standard deviation from its centre. They are not noise realizations or independently measured galaxy biases. No claim of unbiased recovery across arbitrary gradients is justified.

Fixed-gradient held-out residual power using local parameter-predictive covariance is 3.39–7.72, below the frozen illustrative threshold 9. Thus this particular check does not reach its own optimistic separation marker even when T bias exceeds .6dex. This is a useful negative result: passing a small internal holdout is not sufficient to certify acceleration-scale robustness. Profiled-gradient powers .0037–.0121 show within-family predictive recovery, not likelihood preference for a physical gravity theory.

The statistic r^T[diag(errorV_hold^2)+J_hold Cov_train J_hold^T]^-1 r uses train-only local Gaussian posterior curvature including priors. This is a reasonable design approximation. Because held-out means are noiseless, the model may be misspecified and covariance is linearized, it is not a realized chi-square draw, detection significance, posterior predictive p-value or demonstrated coverage. The threshold 9 must not be described as a calibrated 3-sigma test for these 12 correlated predictions.

## Inclination and priors

Both inverse models include an inclination prior exactly centred on the injected 63deg truth. The source geometry is fixed while only sin(i) velocity projection varies. In this submodel, the exact homogeneity degeneracy permits any small i shift to be absorbed into global star/gas/T rescaling without changing predicted velocities. Accordingly the MAP inclination remains at its prior centre in every result. This confirms prior anchoring; it does not show that the data constrained i or that a biased inclination prior is harmless. The experiment injects gradients, not an inclination mismatch, warp or light-deprojection error.

Gamma sigma=.1 is the dimensionless exponent uncertainty, not .1dex in M/L. On the full 0.16–20.87kpc grid, gamma=+/-.1 corresponds to +/-0.21154dex of differential log10 M/L from inner to outer radius, apart from free global amplitude. Global stellar and angular-gas amplitudes are freely profiled within the stated positive log bounds. Stored optima are interior. Reporting recovery should mention the shared injected/fit family and true-centred inclination prior.

## Publication wording

Safe: 'On one old SPARC radial grid, source-gradient injection can bias a local algebraic-law T inference by 0.3–0.6dex when gradients are omitted. The predefined internal holdout did not reliably reject those biased solutions. Profiling the injected gradient family substantially recovered T under optimistic geometry/covariance assumptions.'

Unsafe: 'The T observable is validated', 'Euclid can now measure kappa', 'the theory passed independent data', 'inclination uncertainty was removed', or 'the holdout rejects wrong models at three sigma'.

No additional experiment is required for reporting this bounded result.
