# External normalization checks

Checked 2026-11-12 against primary papers, with no source code imported:

- Juan Maldacena, *Non-Gaussian features of primordial fluctuations in single field inflationary models*, [arXiv:astro-ph/0210603v5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/astro-ph/0210603), equations (2.10)–(2.14). In the homogeneous canonical-scalar limit its lapse constraint and curvature action agree with the lapse/shift normalization used here. Its derivation retains the background equations and integration by parts; it does not prove the finite-Pi extension in this lane.
- Jaume Garriga and V. F. Mukhanov, *Perturbations in k-inflation*, [arXiv:hep-th/9904176v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/9904176), equations (25)–(28). Translating their kinetic variable to Q and their signature conventions, the no-polarization flat-FRW limit has the curvature kinetic coefficient `q² H_QQ/h²` and gradient coefficient `q H_Q/h²`. The source supports that limiting comparison, not a formula for a divergence-gated polarization action.

Both PDFs were opened directly. The archival version identifiers above, rather than a search-result date, identify the sources. The internal ADM action, finite-density constraints and principal symbol are independently derived in this lane and remain subject to the independent audit.
