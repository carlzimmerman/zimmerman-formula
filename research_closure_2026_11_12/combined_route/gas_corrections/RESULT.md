# FINAL5 forward correction

The numerical toy model is repaired. The proposed universal observational decision is withdrawn; no new gas observation has been made. Original scripts, outputs, criteria and documents are archived with hashes in `originals/`. The live historical entry point dispatches to `corrected_signal.py`; it never overwrites the frozen old result files.

For the particular *saturated* enclosed-mass example Mbar(<r)=pi Sigma0 r², a consistent compact-baryon plus gas-shell realization has Rin=sqrt(Mb/(pi Sigma0)), rho_gas=Sigma0/(2r) on Rin<r<Rout, and zero gas below Rin. Its gas mass is pi Sigma0(R²−Rin²). This avoids counting the compact baryons a second time. A lower bound on enclosed mass does not uniquely force this example or exclude every other profile.

For b>=Rin, Sigma_proj(b)=Sigma0 acosh(Rout/b). At Rout=2.6 Mpc, the factors at b=0.3,1,2 Mpc are 2.84929,1.60944,0.756433; the column vanishes at the edge. For b<Rin subtract Sigma0 acosh(Rin/b), with finite central limit Sigma0 ln(Rout/Rin). The emission-measure integral involves arccos(b/R), not arccosh/b. Both expressions have been verified against independent line-of-sight integrals; their projected area integrals recover the 3D mass and emission measure.

The circular Gaussian beam kernel is b/s² exp[−(R−b)²/(2s²)] i0e(Rb/s²). The previous normalized 1D radial Gaussian was not a 2D convolution. The repaired kernel reproduces the analytic convolution of two circular Gaussians, including its central amplitude. The default now projects the sphere; the flat-sheet default was not a lower bound near the outer edge.

The X-ray luminosity-distance denominator uses centimeters when the luminosity is in erg/s: the old flux labeled erg/s/cm² was too large by 10,000. The corrected shell also removes the gas luminosity previously assigned inside Rin. The cooling function is an illustrative supplied coefficient, not an authenticated plasma calculation; these values are not a detection forecast.

tSZ measures integral ne kT dl. Doubling T doubles y at fixed column; an inferred column therefore needs temperature information. GO1's gas-mass error was already conditional on a conversion model and cannot yield a temperature-independent column error for a different lens population. No corrected significance is assigned without the actual map transfer/filtering, lens selection, temperature model, correlated structures and covariance. The original frozen shape criterion cannot be used as a test of the corrected spherical projection: a falling projected column is expected even in this toy model. New criteria would need a separately preregistered observable pipeline.

Comparisons with cosmic density must specify epoch: rho_b(z)=rho_b0(1+z)^3; the original z=0 denominators are not overdensities at the quoted z=.308. M200 is mass inside its own radius, not a universal total-baryon cap out to 2.6 Mpc. The universal “no third shape” and “no outcome in between” assertions are unsupported. The old KA3 values 3.234e13 and 3.186e13 differ by about 1.5%, rather than agreeing to their printed precision. No historical observation or frozen decision label is silently relabeled here.

Validation is in `before_fix_regressions.json` and the audited forward run. The old default projection fails the independent LOS check; the old beam fails the analytic Gaussian benchmark. The repaired functions pass LOS, projected mass, projected emission measure, beam, unit and temperature-mutation checks. The corrected script intentionally gives no KILL/KEEP decision for the theory.
