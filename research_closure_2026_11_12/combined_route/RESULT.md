## What survived the attempted falsifications

The AQUAL effective density has negative equatorial lobes, so it cannot itself be identified everywhere with nonnegative cold rest density. However, signed polarization and a density contrast on a positive background remain possible. In the tested far-field regime, a cosmic positive background can cover the negative contrast. The numerical spherical enclosed mass is monotone over the controlled sampled range; an apparent outer decline disappeared under boundary controls. There is no universal sign-based no-go here.

A separate analytic comparison supplies a family of positive finite-mass cold equilibria with an inner deep-law profile and a smooth outer taper. Its isotropic phase-space distribution is positive by an exact convexity argument, independently reviewed; Jeans and mass integrals also pass. This is useful partial progress: finite positive equilibrium mass is possible in a standard Newtonian phase-space description. It does not establish stability, a relativistic field completion, formation or selection of the required mass. In fact, this family exists for every positive mass ratio. Moreover, a spatially uniform external acceleration can be removed exactly from ordinary gravitational matter dynamics by an accelerating-frame transformation. A true AQUAL-style external-field effect therefore needs a specified additional field/clock coupling; merely supplying conserved cold matter does not derive it.
