# Route priority after combining failures

## Decision and meaning of “strongest”

The primary objective is fundamental mechanism progress: a formed cold-field state, physical response and common normalization, not one more fitted observable. Under that objective, **the highest-value integration task is to derive or reject a conserving history-dependent stress/transport realization from one specified parent.** The X48 example makes the effective target concrete; PN28 makes one candidate parent concrete. There is no demonstrated compatible merger and no evidence-based probability that PN28 will ultimately win.

This ordering uses unmet dependencies and test readiness, not a weighted count of passes. A route that starts with more arbitrary extra functions is not promoted merely because fewer tests have been run. The twelve obligations are not claimed to be a minimal necessary-and-sufficient theorem. The priority would change if the objective were solely the next implementable PN28 calculation.

## What the failures actually combine into

1. **Memory and transport must be physical.** A collision-rate stop does not stop free-streaming flux (MS27); ordinary conduction does not preserve the MC27 closure; single-barotrope cold turn-on is obstructed in BOLTZ1. These scoped results motivate independent state/history variables and a conserving transport derivation. They do not prove that the exact X48 gate is unique. [FND01](LANE_MATRIX.md#fnd01), [FND12](LANE_MATRIX.md#fnd12), [FND44](LANE_MATRIX.md#fnd44).
2. **Energy received must change the relevant mass distribution.** CM1/BH1/CL5 distinguish available energy, channel cost, transient softening and retained change. DR01 now establishes finite reciprocal work in a different parent; it does not close the next spherical-response implication. [SYN05–08](LANE_MATRIX.md#syn05), [FND25–27](LANE_MATRIX.md#fnd25).
3. **Selection must survive population and radial constraints.** RM2 is a fitted requirement map, not a selector. Its weak high-mass response is in tension with LC1 on the native chain, but that quantitative target does not transfer unchanged to PN28 or X48. CSM1 already attempted internally consistent switching and loses joint calibration at its registered levels; SL1/SL2 add no accepted algebraic repair. [RM2](LANE_MATRIX.md#obs-rm2), [LC1](LANE_MATRIX.md#obs-lc1), [CSM1](LANE_MATRIX.md#syn01), [SL1/SL2](LANE_MATRIX.md#syn03).
4. **One parent must account for total stress, scales and formation.** PN28 leaves its potential and B/V relation open; X48 leaves material scales and microscopic closure open; TP04 supplies real cold-state progress only under its own prepared state and vacuum assumptions. These gaps do not cancel when the models are placed next to one another. [FND17–20](LANE_MATRIX.md#fnd17), [FND53](LANE_MATRIX.md#fnd53), [FND39–41](LANE_MATRIX.md#fnd39).

## A concrete obstacle to the preferred integration task

X48 has active fixed-memory pressure `P=K rho^2`. PN28's selected equilibrium relation has a dilute `rho^(5/3)` regime and approaches a logarithmic total-pressure form. The held-fixed variables and domains must be matched before comparing derivatives; these displayed relations cannot simply be identified. Moreover, reversible Hamiltonian phase mixing does not by itself establish a positive irreversible entropy-production law. Any coarse-graining needs its regime, retained variables and discarded correlations specified.

Therefore the target is the **needed conserving mechanism**, not an exact copy of X48's successful EOS or numerical coefficients. A parent yielding a different admissible constitutive law must earn its own formation and response evidence. A failed mapping rejects that realization, not every history-dependent model. [PN28 equilibrium](LANE_MATRIX.md#fnd19), [X48 material model](LANE_MATRIX.md#fnd48), [matching obligation](LANE_MATRIX.md#aud12).

## What would change this priority

- A completed common-parent derivation with cold total stress and formed-state selection would outrank the current proposed integration task.
- A finalized data or validity audit may alter an observational requirement; it does not by itself supply missing stress or state dynamics.
- A failed controlled PN28 transport mapping would end that particular matching attempt. Do not compensate by fitting new gates or absorbing the discrepancy into B/V.
- If the task is narrowed to testing the already-calculated PN28 energy transfer, priority 2 is the correct immediate experiment. It remains a distinct, useful partial question.

No new physical run was executed to produce this ranking. It is a transparent recommendation from the captured evidence, not an optimality proof, all-model exclusion or theory-closure claim.
