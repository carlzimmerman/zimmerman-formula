# Evidence coverage

This matrix covers all major mechanism families identified in the current failure inventory and current response/microscopic/entropy-transport continuations. It does **not** represent a fresh audit of every experiment in the repository. Direct evidence means a report or result was read and pinned, not that its science was rerun. Full-path source records disambiguate colliding lane names.

- 131 scoped lane/claim records, including 11 expressly summary-only historical family records.
- 138 pinned captures (135 distinct original paths).
- Rebuild directory inventory: 350 immediate directories; 58 have direct source captures, 132 additional directories map to indexed families, 160 are not individually assessed. Directory counts include support/audit areas and are not a count of independent mechanisms.

Unassessed directories carry no failure or success weight. The old inventory itself had about 90 opened reports among about 330 lanes, with further title-only/brief entries; its assertions retain those limits. Current directly captured reports supersede stale titles and the inventory where identified. Claims of global uniqueness or a mathematically optimal winner would require broader review and are not made.

## Concurrent evidence

HB1, M3A1, FG1, PZ1 and DR8-B are pending as of the pinned evidence. A filename or a passing initializer is not a final scientific grade. Original source drift is reported by validate.py; captures are never silently refreshed during validation.

No external novelty claim, full mathematical-proof audit, new likelihood comparison, new simulation, or exhaustive review of unrelated Standard Model/particle experiments is part of CM01. The route ranking is a reasoned priority for this core-mechanism objective, not a probability of ultimate success.
