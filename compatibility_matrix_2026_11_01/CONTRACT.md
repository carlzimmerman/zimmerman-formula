# CM01: failure-driven compatibility matrix

The user requests a synthesis, a strongest-surviving-route assessment, and
publication. This is evidence reconciliation and route selection, not a new
physical simulation or automatic reopening of a closed research program.
The checkpoint and scope are in CHECKPOINT.json. Concurrent shared work is
read through immutable snapshots and is never edited.

## Deliverables

1. An explicit inventory of relevant lane families, with source hashes and
   coverage gaps. Use current authoritative indexes and reports before old
   narratives. No claim to have audited every unrelated repository experiment.
2. A machine-readable lane matrix and readable report. Separate finite failure,
   scoped analytical obstruction, fitted partial success, conditional positive
   result, uncomputed obligation, in-progress work and owner-closed work.
3. A compatibility matrix of candidate architectures against common obligations.
   An untested cell is never a pass. Observational data and conservation laws
   transfer; passes, fitted requirements and no-go theorems transfer only with
   their hypotheses. A mixed assembly needs an explicit same-action matching
   argument; it cannot inherit its components' grades.
4. A failure-driven comparison of materially different surviving continuations,
   including the possibility that none is currently jointly viable. Distinguish
   best next bounded test from greatest unproved chance of ultimate success.
   No fabricated probabilities or weighted pass-count leaderboard.
5. A non-repetition register: exact completed/stalled operation, obstruction,
   and the changed premise or uncomputed implication required for continuation.
6. Independent adversarial review, link/schema/hash validation, and a dedicated
   codex/ branch containing this package only. Push and verify remote commit.

## Common obligations

- C01: one specified action, gravitational source/force ledger, no double count;
- C02: physical state selection, production, abundance and lifetime;
- C03: health, relevant stability, multistream and vacuum-domain completion;
- C04: cold total stress and stated cosmological constraints;
- C05: claimed scales/normalizations derived rather than inserted or fitted
  (a basin threshold is an obligation only for candidates using that switch);
- C06: galaxy normalization and mass/brightness trends together;
- C07: dwarf constraints and required response ordering;
- C08: cluster radial structure with measured gas;
- C09: independent lensing, Milky Way and DiskMass constraints;
- C10: sustained radial mass redistribution under baryonic evolution;
- C11: physically derived selection across states/environments, not labels;
- C12: reciprocal energy/angular-momentum exchange and finite source budgets.

These are a shared specification, not a theorem that their intersection exists.
"No known compatible completion" is different from a proved impossibility.
Negative-energy directions do not alone prove kinetic exponential growth.
Small pressure or finite work does not establish a stable formed halo.
Computational/code failures are not physical failures.

## Lane-record schema

Each row: id, name, family, parent, status, result, obstruction, scope,
requirements (C IDs), nonrepeat, next_new_step, sources (snapshot IDs), aliases.
Optional fields may add measured numbers, caveats and hypothesis changes.
status is one of scoped_obstruction, finite_failure, fitted_partial,
conditional_positive, open, in_progress, owner_closed, inconclusive,
data_audit, corrected. Conflicting interpretations remain explicit.

Candidate judgments must cite row IDs and identify changed assumptions. A
candidate that needs a new state, coupling or vacuum relation has an open
matching obligation, not a completed repair. No science results are retuned.
