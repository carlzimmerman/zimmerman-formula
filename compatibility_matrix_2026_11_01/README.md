# CM01 — what survives the combined failures?

**No reviewed lane yet satisfies the full intersection. The strongest justified integration task is a same-parent realization of conserving, history-dependent stress and transport.** That is an open route, not a discovered working mechanism or a guarantee that PN28 realizes it.

The comparison changes the earlier emphasis. **X48 entropy-memory transport has the strongest reviewed finite core-formation evidence:** a specified initially cold sphere settles at fixed coefficients across 32–256 shells. **PN28/DR01 has the most developed explicit reciprocal kinetic-response calculation:** finite quadrupole forcing transfers energy and angular momentum. These are different models. Neither result supplies the other's state, pressure law, scales, stability, cosmology or observational grades. [Evidence](LANE_MATRIX.md#fnd50), [response](LANE_MATRIX.md#fnd25), [matching gap](LANE_MATRIX.md#aud12).

The new step is to test whether a single parent can generate the needed memory, stress and transport with an honest energy ledger and physical activation conditions. PN28 is a concrete candidate to test, not a presumed winner. **Rejecting that mapping is a useful outcome.** Copying X48's successful closure into PN28 would leave the fundamental problem unsolved. The scoped work is defined in [NEXT_STEP_CONTRACT.md](NEXT_STEP_CONTRACT.md); this synthesis does not execute it.

| What the failures jointly require | Why the old repairs do not meet it |
|---|---|
| A formed, retained core state | Static equilibria and finite linear work do not show formation or sustained radial regulation. |
| One source/force/stress ledger | Galaxy-law fits, native zooms, PN28 response and X48 entropy cores have different parents. |
| State-dependent response with the right population ordering | Constant response, fitted switches, common clocks and simple feedback proxies fail their tested combinations or only prescribe the needed ordering. |
| Radial control across galaxies, dwarfs and clusters | Weak spiral contraction alone does not repair dwarf mass assignment, outer-cluster structure or local/lensing constraints. |
| Cold total stress with a physical quiet phase | A small pressure term or inactive toy background is not a cosmological/forest result; retained internal and field energy still counts. |
| Derived scales and finite reservoirs | Available energy is not a working transfer channel; fitted thresholds and independent pressure/vacuum scales do not explain the normalization. |

These are constraints on the next model, not a proof that their intersection exists. The inference toward history-dependent transport is a research priority, not a theorem excluding every alternative.

The already-executed work is explicitly fenced off: consistent core/switch CSM1, symbolic SL1, inverse-response SL2, constant/mass-falling response fits, budget-only feedback calculations, homogeneous wave response, static FR01, linear DR01, TP04 masses and leading lifetime channels, and X48 finite collapse/conditional energy estimates. HB1, M3A1, FG1, PZ1 and DR8-B remain pending in the captured record. The forest audit also weakens an earlier claimed kill.
