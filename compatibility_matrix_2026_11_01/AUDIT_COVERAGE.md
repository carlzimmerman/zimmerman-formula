## Interpretation boundaries

- AUD02 reports the dated forest audit and its scope; it does not independently re-run the September proxy or authenticate its external literature claims.
- AUD03 captures an HB1 partial output with only `EM1|off` and `EM1|blum` baselines. Its criteria/amendments expose branch, universal-EOS and extrapolation questions. No hydrostatic outcome is assigned.
- AUD04 captures M3A1 criteria, preparation and factor files. No final combined audit or regraded cluster result was available in this capture. The factors are provenance inputs, not evidence that any correction passes.
- AUD05 checks the actual FG1 law output’s `checks 6/7` and `K0x38 FAIL`, alongside the queue’s unresolved-mode caveat. This does not claim that all raw harness numbers are wrong.
- AUD06 uses the final TP01 dictionary. Universal algebra, restricted PDE reduction, finite implementation checks and physical validation remain separate.
- AUD07 reads the frozen v3s parent/closure, the dated corrections and relevant later raw-data qualifications. It does not freshly audit all historical v3s leaf calculations; its old sweeping wording is not promoted into a universal exclusion.
- AUD08 preserves the owner-counted TD1 cap and the fact that scale failures apply only to architectures using those scales. The queue’s kappa reopening condition is documentary, not an independent audit of 537 attempts.
- AUD09–AUD11 retain each older polarization parent’s action/state, fixed-source or imposed-background scope. They do not become PN28 passes or exclusions by sharing field terminology.
- AUD12 is an integration obligation, not an additional mechanism or a completed matching proof. It cannot establish a nonempty common solution set.

## Ranking cautions delivered to coordinator

There is no justified single winner by pass count. An observable target is transferable; a fitted repair magnitude or no-go result transfers only with its premises. The forest downgrade is not a rescue, pending M3A1 is not an artefact verdict, and HB1 expectations are not results. The corrected CSM1 consistent two-state switch must be recognized as already tried (coordinator owns its exact rows), rather than suggesting the same switch as new. Concurrent X48 entropy-transport evidence belongs to the foundational fragment and must be compared by criterion: its core-formation evidence can exceed PN28’s even while its phenomenological transport gates and a0 normalization prevent same-parent grade transfer. No independent final ranking review is recorded by this fragment until the coordinator’s completed matrix exists.
