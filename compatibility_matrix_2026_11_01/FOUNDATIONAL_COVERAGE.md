## Comparative judgment

There is no evidenced candidate here that satisfies all common obligations,
and no defensible numerical probability ranking of eventual success.
The strongest **bounded continuation of demonstrated reciprocal kinetic
response** is PN28 dilute-state radial redistribution beyond DR01: compute the
actual second-order spherical response, or a declared finite-amplitude
experiment with matched baryon growth and a finite live driver's budget.
Measure the same enclosed cold mass in matched dynamic controls. A positive
small-amplitude coefficient is not prevention of the 31.4% static contraction;
positive work is not retained heat; driver reversal after contraction is not
prevention during growth. PN28 retains its unselected B/V, phase population,
EOS protection and cross-system state/history issues (FND16–FND27).

X48's entropy transport is the stronger **existing effective demonstration
of virgin spherical core formation**, because one unchanged coefficient set
settles four refinements and has reciprocal energy accounting, entropy
production and a conditional floor mechanism. Its static smooth-loading
resistance is also direct, although contraction remains substantial and it
has not evolved responsive baryons. That makes it a useful target for a
microscopic effective limit, not a derived dark-energy mechanism. Its many
material scales, inactive dust completion, actual forest histories and
nonradial domain prevent treating it as a microscopic prediction. PN28 also remains a
phenomenological parent; its advantage for a bounded response calculation
is the already specified reciprocal kinetic dynamics (FND48–FND53).

For the user's **whole mechanism** question, the strongest unfilled
intersection is therefore a same-parent matching and state-selection gate:
can a specified healthy, cosmologically cold parent produce a history-dependent
stress/transport response with reciprocal work, physically determined scales,
and the relevant baryonic response? X48 supplies a concrete successful
phenomenological behavior to explain; PN28 supplies an explicit reciprocal
kinetic candidate to test. Their separate successes cannot be combined by
assertion. Deriving an X48-like limit from PN28 or another viable parent has
not been demonstrated. The exact X48 functional gates are not universal
requirements, and a different conservative kinetic mechanism could satisfy
the observational obligations without reproducing that transport law.

Thus common-parent matching is logically prior to claiming a joint solution,
while PN28 radial response is the clearest immediate discriminator inside an
already specified parent. It would be misleading to label either the
unspecified matched architecture or PN28 alone the proven most promising
whole theory. TP04's surviving conditional particle route is a distinct
architecture, outside a field-only requirement if that requirement is
retained; neither its coldness nor its bounded leading lifetime supplies a
galaxy a0 or cluster-response mechanism (FND37–FND42).
