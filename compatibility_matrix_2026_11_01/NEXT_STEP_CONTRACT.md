## Gate 1: state and energy map

Name the parent and regime. PN28 is the first concrete parent to examine, not an assumption of success. Write the independent variables retained after averaging, and show how density, pressure/stress, the memory/entropy coordinate, heat flux and dormant energy are obtained. Define which variables are held fixed in each constitutive derivative. Include scalar/gradient/reservoir contributions to both total energy and the gravitational source.

**Reject a claimed match** if the same variable is counted twice, if required energy disappears when the pressure gate closes, or if it relies on using one pressure law for equilibrium and another for perturbations without a derived state change. PN28 and X48 EOS differences must be addressed explicitly.

## Gate 2: controlled transport and quiet domain

Derive the transport and production signs and coefficients in a named limit, with an error/regime statement. State which additional degrees of freedom or initial statistical assumptions are required. Reversible phase mixing alone is insufficient to assert a local irreversible entropy law. A free relaxation rate, inserted threshold, label-based halo choice or imported X48 coefficient is an extra assumption, not a passed matching gate.

Map inactive and active domains to actual density/compression/phase-space histories. Check the same parameters admit the intended cold expanding state and a core-forming regime. This is an initial admissibility check, not a substitute for eventual forest/CMB and population calculations. If a density-only reduction loses multistream information, retain the necessary variables instead of declaring closure.

## Gate 3: common conservation and cold stress

Establish reciprocal energy and momentum balance, including any boundary flux and driver reservoir. Compute coldness from the total averaged stress and energy, not one kinetic pressure. Identify all independent scales, including the pressure/vacuum relation, a0 normalization if present, and transport scales. No observational calibration may be reported as a derivation.

At minimum check an equilibrium/inactive limit and one nontrivial exchange configuration using the same equations. These checks test the mapping; they do not establish a cosmic population, core stability or observational fit.

## Stop and promotion conditions

- **Scoped no matching:** a required relation cannot hold under the stated parent/limit. Preserve the failed implication and identify the exact additional premise a different model would need.
- **Conditional matching only:** the law follows only after a new state, bath, portal or material coefficient is assumed. Report that assumption and keep its origin open.
- **Controlled effective matching:** the stated reduction, stress, source and transport balances work with a bounded domain/error and independently identified coefficients. Only then is a new common-parent formation/response test justified. It earns no inherited N/X48 observational pass.

No outcome may be upgraded to “the full mechanism works” without formation/state selection, relevant stability, cosmology and joint galaxy/dwarf/cluster/local regrading in that same parent.
