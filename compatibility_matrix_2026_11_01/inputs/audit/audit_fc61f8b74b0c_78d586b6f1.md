## Where the rows collide
- **Rows 3 and 4:** with the measured response on, Υ ≥ 0.50 comes with tilt ≥ +0.32, and |tilt| ≤ 0.15 comes with Υ ≤ 0.32 (RM1 on NR2, NO CELL). Clearing takes almost no response above lg M_b about 10, and then clusters drop to 12 of 15 (RM2).
- **Rows 4 and 11:** whatever is dust in the CMB falls into galaxies, and a live disc measures it contracting. This is the central conflict. The record has no process that undoes it at one coupling: ER1, PG1 and DR9 fail.
- **Rows 7 and 8:** systems of about the same mass want different things, so mass alone can't be the switch (X58: retain, exclude, retain is non-monotone). The basin split answers this only for T located, canonical, one object's margin.
- **Rows 1 to 3 and 10:** the amount needed runs from about 0.8 of native on SPARC, below 0.96 on groups, to 1.22–1.80 on clusters (SL2). One constant misses this.
- **Row 10 alone:** law, native and `max` all fall short in the heaviest bin. Outer shear rises with gas mass close to Newton's slope, and no rule on record reproduces this on measured gas.

## What this says
A rule passing all twelve would have to be absent from isolated discs while present in cluster outskirts in proportion to gas, be dust at the CMB and yet not contract in spirals, separate same-mass systems by something other than these selectors, and fix the Milky Way, where the law overshoots. No family on record does this, which is a statement about the families tried and not a no-go theorem. Inputs not settled, any of which could move a row: EM1's mass line, core shape chosen by hand, f_Q measured at one mass, and hydrostatic against shear at 0.12 dex.
