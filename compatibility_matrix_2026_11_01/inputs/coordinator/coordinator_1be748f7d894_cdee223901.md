## Reading
- Measured gas softens the cusp about 7 %, where 18 % is needed. Most of it arrives in the first 5 cycles (R 0.960), then
  it flattens. The channel moves η, as BOUND shows, but at measured gas it stops short.
- Energy is the second obstacle. Net work into the cold component is about 0.4 % of the gas lift energy (1.15e57 vs
  3.10e59 erg), and the lift alone takes 76 % of the supernova budget. BE1's 1.7 % counted only the energy the core must
  gain, so it does not measure the cost of this channel.
- BOUND reaches the target but needs 3.6× E_SN, so it is not a route.
- A 200 Myr ramp is not reversible here: A1 still softens by 4 %, about half of PRIMARY's work. Core-scale orbits take of
  order 900 Myr (estimate), so neither history is adiabatic at r0. PRIMARY's "rapid vs slow" contrast is modest.

## Limits
One galaxy, one seed, spherical. η sits on a grid and scatters about ±1 % (STATIC 23.98–24.52), so R is good to about
0.02. Gas mass is today's; a gas-richer past is untested and is the obvious way this could be too pessimistic. Gas
histories are prescribed, not generated by stars. Particle η is read at x_c 0.013, and R is applied to the x_c 0.001
value 25.94. Alt was not run (its SPARC line fails at every c_f, X78). Clusters untested.
