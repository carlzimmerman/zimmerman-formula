## Most informative intersection

The apparent galaxy rescue is a *requirement*, not a demonstrated architecture. RM2's six fitted survivors need almost no response above baryon mass approximately 1e10, below LC1's measured smooth-disc response range. They retain enough low-mass response to help the dwarf/gas-rich constraints, while their mass-only high-end suppression worsens cluster inner/shape grading. Thus a mass-only switch cannot inherit a joint pass from those six cells. This is a counterexample to ranking candidates solely by their SPARC median or number of cleared fitted gates.

A physically evolved orbital distribution or forcing history could distinguish galaxies of similar mass, and baryonic radial structure can already change the sign of UR1 response. Neither fact supplies the required magnitude, the dwarf ordering, the measured-gas outer cluster profile, or independent lensing and local-mass constraints. Gas versus stars as a name is not a force-law selector: their actual distributions and time histories must differ in the specified dynamics. Present-day bar labels have no demonstrated separation; the ELN proxy is only partially selective and its finite bar-region reservoir fails the existing budget test. A later finite resonant work result therefore cannot be promoted to sustained expansion or to a cluster solution.

The observational fragment identifies **no demonstrated jointly compatible completion**. It does not prove none can exist. Observational constraints and conserved budgets can be carried to a new candidate; the old architecture's fitted response strengths, halo profiles and pass labels cannot.
