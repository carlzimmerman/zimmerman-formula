# Which results can coexist?

A compatible assembly requires one specified action or effective limit, state variables, gravitational source, normalization and observable map. Sharing the word “cold” or an acceleration scale is not a matching argument. Observations transfer as measurements with their reduction assumptions; fitted repair sizes and model-specific grades require recalculation.

| Proposed combination | Current judgment | Missing compatibility check |
|---|---|---|
| Native N fits + PN28 response | Unmatched | Derive the native mass assignment/profile and baryon response from PN28; N's fitted M200 and chosen core do not follow from its waterbag |
| PN28 + X48 entropy transport | Unmatched; highest-value integration question | Different state/EOS/transport laws; derive memory, flux, total energy and scales or reject the mapping |
| Law-alone galaxy success + cold halo source | Unmatched | Specify whether the law sees baryons or total stress; derive the force/source ledger without counting the same response twice |
| Cold total stress from TP04 + PN28 logarithmic response | Unmatched | Different microscopic state, scale/vacuum assumptions and particle/field scope; explicit matching and production required |

| RM2 response fit + LC1 measured contraction | Tension on their shared native chain | A derived additional channel/history must change the response; f_hi<=.2 and f_Q~.872 are not simultaneous predictions of the unchanged model |

| Passed numerical controls + physical viability | Invalid inference | Controls establish that a calculation runs/tests its equations; physical criteria require completed observables |

The common obligations give a specification, not a constructive proof. “No currently demonstrated intersection” leaves both possibilities open: a future compatible completion, or a later obstruction. An untested union of models is not the strongest survivor merely because it has not yet failed a calculation.
