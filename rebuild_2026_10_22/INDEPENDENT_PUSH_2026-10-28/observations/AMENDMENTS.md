# Protocol notes

A1 (pre-computation; after source recovery): The new four-target independent gas diagnostic uses the measured Babyk et al. (2018) beta and core radius, not GT1's fixed beta=2/3 and rc=0.15R500. It solves the hydrostatic acceleration implied by the measurements and forwards it through measured GC tracer densities. This tests the shared-potential hypothesis conditionally, not the unchanged GT1 grade. No R500 is invented. Source apertures and core radii are rescaled by the ratio of SLUGGS to source angular-diameter distance. A separate beta=2/3 asymptotic speed is descriptive only.

A2 (pre-computation; source warning): Single-temperature spectral fit for M87 has statistic/dof=4.3; fitted surface-brightness beta models have chi2/dof about3 for three targets. Quoted small statistical temperature errors cannot be used as complete model errors. Emission-weighted temperatures are not radial profiles. No clean four-target PASS/FAIL is assigned to the pre-existing theory from this diagnostic, irrespective of the offsets it yields.

A3 (pre-computation): All four source apertures are integrated spectral apertures, not guaranteed detections to their edge; Babyk section4.4 explicitly allows extrapolation of mass/gas beyond detected surface-brightness radii. CGA independent Rmax checks are retained where available. Finite Jeans projections can depend on gravity outside a given measured projected aperture; report the variance fraction carried there by zeroing that force contribution only as an aperture sensitivity.

A4 (post-first-run discovery; before membership-label counterexample check): CLoGS identifies NGC4261 as a group-scale hot atmosphere inside the original HG footprint and it matches KT2017 principal group39659. Coordinator inspection found that the stored HG1/HG2 rows label it FAINT. Check that exact stored association and label in the new script; if confirmed, this is a physical-hot-atmosphere false negative under the original eRASS matching selector. It does not establish a catalogue extraction bug: FAINT means no selected eRASS match. Do not flip its label or change D without measured gas at the HG radius and sensitivity-comparable selection. Check shared-bin support explicitly from stored rows before describing its weight in D.

A5 (post-result precision caveat): Oh2015 Table2's V(Rmax)=16.9 km/s is asymmetric-drift corrected, rather than uncorrected projected rotation. Therefore the simple entire-speed 1/sin(i) scaling is an optimistic heuristic. For a fixed positive pressure-support contribution V_AD^2, the correct change is V_new^2=(V_old^2−V_AD^2)[sin48/sin(i)]^2+V_AD^2. At lower inclination this is bounded above by scaling all V_old^2; the resulting −0.299 canonical residual is a maximum possible correction under fixed pressure support, and the 22.06-degree zero-residual angle is an upper bound under that condition. A full gas-disk refit is still required; the delta-method uncertainty is illustrative only and is not a new Z grade.
