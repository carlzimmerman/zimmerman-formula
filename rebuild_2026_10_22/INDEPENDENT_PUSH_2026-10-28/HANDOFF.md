## IP28-BK: failed trigger, surviving mechanism obligation

The actual Local Group geometry rules out the >a0-at-turnaround proposal
under the tested mass identification. Finite-age and static radii are distinct;
the threshold depends on whether g_N, simple-law gravity, or net acceleration
is meant. The numerical toy calculations do not supply a covariant parent.

The smooth-annulus discriminator obstructs a universal local-force-only basin
flag. Dust scaling obstructs an absolute mass selector only at fixed scaled
initial histories. A mass-dependent P(k), boundary information, or new state
can evade those restrictions; none is silently ruled out.

Next theory obligation: specify how a physical state obtains the common
basin information and changes cold-sector stability, with a predictive field
equation and conservation laws. Test its rule on the annulus pair before an
expensive numerical run. No such surviving concrete field equation has been
established in this checkpoint. Kappa requires a physical normalization or
vacuum matching principle; field rescaling alone cannot determine it.
