# J1_potential_selector — AMENDMENT A1 (POST-RESULT; reporting only, no graded line changes)

Written after the first full run (all graded lines and the verdict unchanged by this amendment).

The reported "ε_need" of the monotone bound M came out as the search cap (1e8) for more than half of the satellites. Reason:
in the screened regime (r12 ≪ ℓ = L★²/2r_M) E3's B_scr − 1 = S(s)(ν − 1) ≈ (s/2)·√(a₀/g_b) with s ∝ r_M ∝ a₀^{-1/2}, so
the boost tends to a constant independent of a₀: **no a₀ multiplier of any size can close those systems.** The single median
of a capped quantity is not meaningful. From A1 on, the script reports instead:
- the number of satellites that no ε ≤ 1e8 can close (unreachable);
- the median ε_need of the reachable ones, and the Milky Way V(R₀) inflation at that ε;
- the median saturation log10[B_scr(ε = 1e8)/B_scr(ε = 1)].
