# HL2 AMENDMENT A1 (committed before any lensing and hydrostatic value has been paired)

**The frozen 10 % radius rule is wrong and is removed.** For two-paper pairs at each mass's own r500, radii within 10 % means masses within about 0.12 dex. That cuts clusters on the statistic being measured, at the size of the gap being tested. It would bias g toward zero.

**Replacement:** a pair from two papers takes each mass at its own Δ = 500 radius, no radius cut. A one-paper pair at a common radius (Mahdavi+13, Zhang+10) stays at that radius. g per source is reported, so the radius convention shows.

**Matching:** Okabe & Smith's and Martino's tables share LoCuSS names, so across sources I match normalised names with |Δz| ≤ 0.02, and list what fails. Position isn't tabulated in every source.

**Sources after reading table structures only:**
- Used: Mahdavi+13; Okabe & Smith 16 M_WL with Martino+14 M_hydro (Chandra where available, else XMM: the rule Smith+16 states); Zhang+10.
- Smith+16: same pair, no separate table.
- Rejected, reason: Hoekstra+15, no M_hydro of its own, and Mahdavi's M_hydro is read at Hoekstra+12's radius; Applegate+14, von der Linden+14, WtG masses at fixed aperture or against Planck; Mantz+16, r2500; Sereno & Ettori 15, compilation with no per-cluster table in source.
- K1 where a radius is tabulated: Martino, Mahdavi. Okabe's table has no radius at M500 and h⁻¹ units, converted at h 0.7, disclosed.

Everything else stands.
