# WF1 AMENDMENT A1

Part 1 written before any screen, fit or graded number. kappa = 1/2 FITTED. Not theory closed.

**1. CLASS scale controls were placed at the wrong masses.** At m 1e-23 and 1e-22 eV, k_J today is above CLASS's P_k_max of 30 h/Mpc (100 h/Mpc at 1e-22), so k_half can't be read and three frozen controls returned null: my mistake. They move to m 1e-25 and 1e-24 for the m^(1/2) ratio and the Jeans factor, and m x 16 runs from 1e-25. Lines unchanged. K_rf5_heavy passed as frozen (dplik -0.002). Seen so far: only those control lines, at RF5's fixed parameters.

**2. Build.** The first control pass ran an unpatched binary: make relinked without recompiling `perturbations`, and all the ratios came back exactly 1. Caught from that flat ratio, fixed by rebuilding the object. `K_rf5_heavy` above stays valid since the patch is off at that limit, and it passes again after the rebuild.

**3. K_jeans, post-control, disclosed.** As frozen it fails: k_half 0.81 h/Mpc against today's k_J 10.3, ratio 0.079, outside the factor of 3. By the frozen rule that alone reads NO VERDICT. My reference was wrong. The suppression is set when modes enter near equality, not today, since k_J grows only as a^(1/4). k_J at equality is 10.3 x (1/3400)^(1/4) = 1.35 h/Mpc, ratio 0.60. And Hu, Barkana and Gruzinov's fit, recalled, k_1/2 = 4.5 (m / 1e-22 eV)^(4/9) /Mpc, gives 0.86 and 0.31 h/Mpc at 1e-24 and 1e-25 against 0.81 and 0.31 measured. So K_jeans is replaced by those two comparisons, within a factor of 3 and 30 % respectively. The fit is used only as a numerics check, never in a grade. The verdict at gate 1 stands on this post-control replacement. Seen so far: only control lines at RF5's fixed parameters. K_sqrtm 2.67 and M_x16 3.91 passed as frozen.

**4. K_SP.** The first run failed at E -0.1640, r_half 3.878, because the control's own enclosed mass was off by half a cell, first order in the grid. Corrected to the trapezoid the solver itself uses; the control's lines are unchanged.

**5. M_s rule's sigma, before any graded number.** The frozen wording takes V_max of "the model" and also says the core is "as hot as what surrounds it". Those conflict. If V_max includes the core itself, a soliton's own V_max keeps its <v^2> below 1.5 V_max^2, so M_s runs away to the whole fair share: the control cluster at m 1e-23 eV put 1.6e15 Msun inside rms radius 1 kpc. That is the rule feeding itself, seen only in the control line. Primary now: sigma from V_max of baryons + envelope, excluding the core, which is what "surrounds it" means. The literal reading runs as a reported variant at the selected m (`Cfg.literal`).

**6. Envelope inside the core, before any graded number.** rho_env = rho_1 exp(-(Phi - Phi_ta)/sigma^2) with total rho = max(rho_s, rho_env) runs away too: the isothermal factor grows without limit inside the soliton's own well, so the envelope outweighs the core and sigma follows it (sigma 1e25 km/s at some m in the control test). Physically a wave envelope can't vary on scales below the core. Primary now: the exponent is held at its value at the core's rms radius, inside that radius. Seen only in control tests on one cluster and NGC 3198.

**7. Solver order, before any graded number.** Iterating core, envelope and sigma together doesn't converge: Control tests on one cluster and three discs gave non-monotonic M_s and unconverged rows, with sigma wandering. So: (a) envelope and sigma first, from HF1's equilibrium with no cap, on baryons + envelope; sigma is then fixed, which also settles part 5. (b) For that sigma, M_s by bisection in log M_s on <v^2> = 3 sigma^2, each trial a self-consistent ground state in baryons + envelope + core under the law. (c) rho_f = max(rho_s, rho_env), with rho_env's exponent from (a), held flat inside the core's rms radius (part 6), renormalised to the fair share. The envelope therefore doesn't feel the core's own potential: a stated approximation, worst where M_s is a large part of the share, and rows with M_s above 30 % of the fair share are counted. The literal variant of part 5 is dropped: under (a) it isn't defined, and part 5's runaway is on record.

**8. Unresolved core, before any graded number.** Where the ground state's rms radius falls under 3 inner grid points, the wall sets it and the M_s rule returns the full share spuriously (cluster at m 1e-22 eV, grid floor 1 kpc). These rows use the envelope alone, since the resolved trend puts such a core at about 1 % of the share (M_s 1.7e13 of 1.7e15 Msun at 1e-23). Counted in the flags.
