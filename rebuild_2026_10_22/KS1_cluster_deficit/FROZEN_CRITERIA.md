## Definitions, fixed
- **k3(x)** = [M_tot(<x R500) - M_law(<x R500)] / M_gas(<x R500), 3D. M_law = M_b nu(y), with M_b = gas (1 + f*), or measured stars where on disk.
- **Q(x)** the projected ratio, GM1's, only as a check.
- **Y(x)** = M_tot / M_b, footing-free. **A_F(x)** = (lensing) / (Newtonian baryon signal / f_b), projected, no deprojection.
- **f_b**, two values carried, neither is a direct measurement: 1/(1 + R_nat) from SPEC's native Omega_m 0.2976016, h 0.6856427, omega_b 0.0223081, and BK1's 0.15747. No Planck ratio. ## The f_b reading, a description
Y(x) and A_F(x) per set, band x 0.5-1, 1-1.4, 1.4-2; gas terciles for KiDS. Z = (Y - 1/f_b) / sigma. Implied k_F(x) = (1 + f*)/f_b - M_law/M_gas, against the measured k3(x).
- **Labels, per set at x >= 1:** **CONSISTENT** |Z| < 2 and sigma_Y/Y <= 0.15. **DISAGREES** |Z| >= 3. **UNDERPOWERED** otherwise. Overall takes the worst across sets if they differ, and says that they differ. Two-halo isn't subtracted, so KiDS beyond x about 1.5 is biased high by an unmeasured amount, and the band says so. Gas beyond R500 on the shear side is GS1's tail from X-COP, shared.
- **Power, stated.** Fractional shift in Y detectable at 3 sigma per band; P(|Z| >= 3) under each of (a) Y 20 % over 1/f_b, (b) constant k3 continuing from x 1, (c) k3 continuing its measured power law, all by mock from the measured covariance. If no rival gets over 0.8 in any band, the test is declared LOW POWER whatever Z says.
