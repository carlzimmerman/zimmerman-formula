arXiv:2305.01589v2 (2023-10-04), equations 6, 14, 16:

    x=|grad psi|^2/a0^2,
    z=x+ell0^2 ||Hess psi||_F^2/a0^2,
    P=x Q(z)/z,             Q'(z)=nu_simple(sqrt(z)).

psi is the ordinary, unfiltered Newtonian potential of the full density.
ell0 is one fixed physical length for all matter. There is no Gaussian
source filter and no object-mass width rule. The gravitational Lagrangian is

    Lg=-(1/(8piG))[2 grad phi.grad psi-a0^2 P].

unchanged discrete simple-QUMOND energy. Full variation includes BOTH the
first-gradient and second-gradient contributions. Dropping derivatives of
the curvature-dependent factor would define a different, unaccepted law.
