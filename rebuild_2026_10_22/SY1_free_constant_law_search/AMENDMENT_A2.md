3. **s and D, one formula.** s is dlog g_b/dlog r along strictly increasing runs of r, by np.gradient, on points with g_b > 0. A run of one point is a point system: s = -2. D = rho_b/rho_DE, with rho_b = g_b (2 + s)/(4 pi G r), the spherical-equivalent density, floored at 0, and rho_DE = 4 a0^2/(c^2 G) from a0 = kappa c sqrt(G rho_DE). So D = y (2 + s)/(16 pi ell), and it carries kappa = 1/2 FITTED. D isn't a free measurement: y, s and ell determine it.
   - **Weakness, stated:** many RQ3 rows give g_b at one radius only (A9, A10, A12, A13, A20, A23, A25, B2, gate 4, some others). For an expression reading s or D, these rows are reported as point-rule rows and aren't counted as a pass for s.

   - Clusters are told apart in RQ3 only by r falling back, never by r500, which RQ3 tags as a label. On X-COP train this equals grouping by cluster.
