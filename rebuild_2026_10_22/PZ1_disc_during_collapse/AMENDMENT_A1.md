# PZ1 AMENDMENT A1: a seed with no turnaround before z_f (before any disc response was read)

The criteria didn't say what happens if a seed's R_T has no maximum before z_f. It happened: on seed 3, R_T is still rising at t(z_f) (largest sample 0.2902 Mpc at 6.05 Gyr of 6.08), the run stopped as written, and no PZ1 disc run exists for it. Seed 6 is still in that phase as this is written.

**Rule.** No definition is changed and no new turnaround is chosen for such a seed. It counts as not finished: it isn't in the pairs, and the 6-seed line still applies. Starting its disc after z_f would be later than LC1's start, so it can't test the question.

**Disclosure.** The surviving seeds are a selection, by a property of the run, on seeds whose M/2 region turned around before z_f. So LC1's medians are also reported on the same subset, and the comparison is the same-seed pairs. Also on record: the median-radius turnaround, the frozen side check, often disagrees strongly with the mean-radius one (seed 1 1.76 against 2.97 Gyr, seed 5 1.75 against 4.63 Gyr), so "turnaround" of this lumpy region is not sharp, and t_ta runs from about 2.8 to 4.6 Gyr so far. No run with another definition is made here; this is reported as a limit.

Nothing at z_f or later on any disc run had been read when this was written: only `turnaround.json` files, the R_T history, and progress lines.
