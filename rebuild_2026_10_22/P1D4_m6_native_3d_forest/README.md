# P1D4_m6_native_3d_forest: X57's M6 against DESI DR1 P1D with a framework-native 3D forest model

## Provenance

- **Criteria:** `FROZEN_CRITERIA.md` together with `forest3d.py` (f05be7280b), committed alone before any data fit.
- **A1** (2a29ad2dff): the S0c range was set to k_f..6 h/Mpc and the stride to 2, after a synthetic-only S0c failure.
- **A2:** sharded table build, code only.

**Run conventions.** κ = ½ FITTED. Footings are never pooled. Cold dark energy is a gravitating w ≈ 0 field component, not
a particle. Not "theory closed". Nothing here says the data favour the framework.

**Data.** DESI DR1 P1D (Zenodo 16943723; owner-approved; untracked).

**The model.** It is built from native linear power, with:
- a fixed-amplitude 256³ box, L = 64 Mpc/h;
- lognormal baryons;
- FGPA;
- linear Kaiser redshift-space distortion from the same modes;
- thermal broadening;
- compensated 3D M6 spheres.

No ΛCDM hydrodynamic simulation is used.

## Verdict (frozen rule): **NOT DIAGNOSTIC (NATIVE 3D MODEL INADEQUATE): ROBUST on both footings**

**Large improvement on the linear proxy, still short of adequate.** P1D3's null χ² was 7017/719; here it is
**1864.2/690**. The per-z worst fits are at z 2.2–2.6. γ sits at its floor (1.0) and R_J at its ceiling (0.2 Mpc/h)
there, so the model wants less small-scale power at low z than lognormal + FGPA gives.

**Variants (canonical; alt is similar):**

| variant | null χ² / dof | p | Asimov Δχ² |
|---|---|---|---|
| primary | 1864 / 690 | — | 10.3 |
| V-stat | 2143 / 690 | — | 14.6 |
| V-floor2 (2 % model floor) | 1207 / 690 | — | 5.6 |
| V-hiz (z ≥ 3.6) | 433 / 327 | 7e-5 | 1.11 |

Model MC error is 0.7–1.4 % per bin.

**μ fits (reported only, since the model fails G0):**
- The primary pins μ̂ at the grid edge, 2. The template is absorbing misfit, as in P1D3.
- V-stat gives μ̂ = 0 with μ₉₅ = 0.04.
- V-hiz gives μ̂ = 0 with μ₉₅ ≈ 1.0, the same edge as P1D3's z ≥ 3.6 and P1D1's k ≤ 0.1 subsets.

## Checks: 4/6

- **Passing:** K1 (bins), K2 (μ = 0 slices identical across footings), and S0, which covers S0a–S0e. S0c is box Gaussian
  P1D against the analytic projection, 3.2 %. S0e is interpolation against direct evaluation.
- **K3 FAILS:** a planted μ = 1 (direct mocks with true positive patches, plus covariance noise) is recovered at μ̂ 0.68
  with Δχ²₀ 4.0, against the required ≥ 9 while G1 (10.3) passes. Recovery is biased low and under-powered relative to
  the Asimov.
- **K5 FAILS:** the main fit process peaked at 1.197 GB, from the interpolators and direct mocks in one process. Every
  table worker stayed below 0.95 GB.
- **Not yet run:** the twin and the rerun.

## What this means

The forest still cannot grade M6 with a native model. The remaining gap sits at z ≤ 2.6 small scales, where real IGM
physics (shock heating, pressure smoothing history and nonlinear velocities) matters most. The consistent pointer across
P1D1, P1D3 and P1D4 is that in the most adequate regime M6 sits at μ₉₅ ≈ 1, the edge of X57's bound.
