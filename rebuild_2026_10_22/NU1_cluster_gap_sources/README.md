## Verdict: PARTLY (frozen rule), and not a cure

The frozen rule gives PARTLY in all 4 cells. The allowed maximum removes 53–68 % of the all-cluster MID residual. But the same
mass breaks the outskirts badly: all-cluster Z_OUT,CAL falls from −0.7…−1.8 to −6.8…−9.0. Even then, the T2 and T3 terciles
stay 2.4–2.7σ high (canonical) and 1.9–2.1σ high (alt). So ordinary mass at its allowed maximum does not reconcile MID and
OUTER. It trades the core gap for an outskirts failure. Almost all of the lever is one ingredient: three degenerate 0.45 eV
neutrinos at the KATRIN limit, packed as tightly as Tremaine–Gunn allows.

## 1. Massive neutrinos (sourcing AQUAL; fair-share infall cap and fine-grained Tremaine–Gunn cap in the law's potential)

- **Oscillation minimum (Σ 0.059 NO / 0.100 IO) and the record's 0.0613 eV: nothing.** The fair-share cap is 1.8–3.0e12 M☉
  per cluster. The phase-space limit at 1 Mpc holds only 3–5e8 M☉, because ρ_TG ∝ m⁴ v_esc³. A_MID is unchanged to 0.001.
- **KATRIN maximum (3 × 0.45 eV, Σ 1.35 eV, Ω_ν/Ω_b 0.65).** The cap is 4.2e13 M☉ (median). The Tremaine–Gunn limit binds
  inside 1 Mpc: M_ν(<1 Mpc) = 8.1e12 M☉, which is 0.40 × the baryons there. v_esc(0.5 Mpc) is about 2000 km s⁻¹.
  - All-cluster A_MID,CAL becomes 1.49 / 1.53 (canonical) and 1.34 / 1.38 (alt), with Z +2.5 / +2.4 / +1.9 / +1.9.
  - OUTER drops to 0.67 / 0.60 (Z −4.7 to −6.6).
  - The limit depends on R_ta, because the AQUAL potential is logarithmic: 3 Mpc gives MID 1.56 / 1.42; 10 Mpc gives
    1.36 / 1.20, with OUTER at Z −6 to −7.8.
- **NK tracing the baryons (the more physical shape):** MID 1.47 / 1.32, OUTER 0.75 / 0.68.
- **Caveat.** Σ = 1.35 eV is allowed only by laboratory data. Every cosmological bound excludes it, but those bounds are
  ΛCDM-fitted and are not used here. The framework's own CMB/BAO analysis has not been checked against it. The record's own
  value (0.0613 eV) does nothing.
- **Sterile neutrino the gap would need (a NEW constant, not adopted).**
  - Amount: gas-tracing mass sourcing AQUAL with Ω_s/Ω_b ≥ 1.35 / 1.46 (canonical) or 1.04 / 1.14 (alt) for the full stack,
    and 1.4–2.1 for T2/T3. That is Ω_s h² ≈ 0.023–0.047.
  - Mass: Tremaine–Gunn (g f = 1, median cluster) needs m_s ≥ 1.3–1.5 eV. A thermal relic with that abundance would have
    m ≈ 2.2–4.3 eV and ΔN_eff ≈ 1.
  - Outskirts: it would still push OUTER to 0.56–0.58 (Z −6.9 to −7.8). A gas-tracing component cannot close MID without
    breaking OUTER, which repeats F1's and X2's finding.
  - Independent constraints, in words only (PROVISIONAL): BBN N_eff (a fully thermal extra species is disfavoured at several σ
    by D/He alone), laboratory sterile searches at 1–10 eV mixing, and dwarf-scale phase-space and small-scale-structure limits.

## 3. What the terciles say

- **T1** (median Mgas500 3.3e12) has no MID gap at R0, and any added mass pushes it below 1. Its OUTER is already 2.2–2.7σ
  below the law.
- **T2 and T3 carry the gap.** At the allowed maximum they keep +0.31 to +0.52. Closing them needs the abundance of a new
  species (Ω_s/Ω_b ≈ 1.4–2.1), not ordinary mass.
- **The required profile is the wrong shape.** Every allowed source that raises MID raises OUTER more in T1/T2. MID needs a
  rising mass trend while OUTER needs one that falls toward low mass (X7 V3), and no single added mass distribution does both.

C0 (F1 PRIMARY, statistical errors; reported): the allowed maximum gives all-cluster A_MID 1.32 / 1.36 (canonical, Z +3.3 /
+3.5) and 1.20 / 1.23 (alt, Z +2.2 / +2.5), with OUTER 0.53–0.59 (Z −7.5 to −9.7).

## What this does not show

- The ν profile is a maximal-concentration bound, not a dynamical solution. Real free-streaming capture would put less mass
  inside 1 Mpc.
- v_esc uses the isolated AQUAL g; the EFE would lower it.
- The external values (KATRIN, BBN Ω_b h², oscillation splittings, BCG H₂) are PROVISIONAL and were quoted without download.
- The asphericity and cross-shear caveats of F1/X7 are inherited.
