# M5_equilibrium_law: is the law the equilibrium of a normally self-gravitating cold-energy medium?

## Verdict: NO-GO for every candidate (theorem). No VIABLE route, no CONDITIONAL survivor.

The two premises exclude each other for a local medium that conserves momentum:
- "the law is the medium's equilibrium for every baryon distribution";
- "the medium self-gravitates normally, with no hold".

1. **The equilibrium forces the stress (S1, S2).** Exactly one local stress makes the law the hydrostatic equilibrium for
   every baryon profile: Π_ij = [χ ∂_iΦ∂_jΦ − K(s)δ_ij]/4πG, with χ = 1 − μ and K′ = χ/2. This is AQUAL's field stress
   minus Newton's, and it is unique up to a constant pressure.
   - It holds exactly in 3D. CFG660 had it only for spheres.
   - It cannot depend on ρ_c. This closes CFG660's open door ("can a two-variable Π(ρ_c, g) keep the law?").
   - The equilibrium is unique and fixed by the baryons (S3; P4c on 175 SPARC galaxies, 5e-8 dex). Requirement (i) is met.
2. **That stress cannot be the medium's dynamics.**
   - A density-blind support gives ξ̈ = g ∂ξ along the field: Hadamard ill-posed, |Re s| ∝ √k (S4; N2 grows with resolution).
   - Any along-field stiffness c² cures the modal growth but leaves convective amplification ln A = TV(ln ρ_c)/2δ, where δ
     is the law error the stiffness causes.
   - At a 0.02 dex law tolerance on SPARC, the median ln A is **59 / 67** (16–84 %: 31–100 / 36–116). 98–99 % of galaxies
     have ln A > 10. Even at 0.054 dex the median is 20 / 23.
   - The equilibrium is not an attractor. Requirement (ii) fails.
3. **In the deep regime the stress cancels the medium's own nonlinear self-gravity (S5).**
   - The force per unit mass is −[(ρ̄ − δρ_b + O(μ))/(ρ̄ + δρ_c)]∇δΦ.
   - Linear growth is exactly dust (S6). Collapse is not: a top-hat needs linear δ = **13.6** to collapse, against 1.686
     for dust (N1).
   - Keeping the μ-term, with physical masses of 1e11–1e15 M☉, overdensities that collapse at a = 1 as dust collapse only
     at **a = 4.2–6.9** (N1b, reported only).
   - The hold of TM1 and U1 comes back by necessity. Requirement (iii) fails.

κ = ½ FITTED. Footings never pooled. Cold energy is real rest mass, not a particle. Not "theory closed". Nothing here says
the data favour the framework.

## Scope and disclosures

- **S2 scope.** S2 covers local stresses Π(ρ_c, ∇Φ) for a medium that couples to the baryons only through gravity.
  - A direct local coupling to ρ_b moves force onto the baryons. That is the reaction failure of CFG121 G3 and
    CFG122 G3.1.
  - Strain-dependent (elastic) stresses make the equilibrium depend on a reference state. That loses (i), and is TM1's
    W(u).
  - Neither is re-scored here.
- **WKB in R2.** R2 uses WKB, so its numbers are order-of-magnitude.
  - The constant-δ choice is not the optimum. A δ(r) profile changes ln A by O(1) factors, not by the 20–60 needed.
  - The supply ratio uses a ΛCDM-processed Ω_c/Ω_b and is reported only.
- **N1 and N1b.** Both use EdS with Λ neglected, which delays both arms. Baryons are smooth, which is optimistic: per S5,
  clustered baryons weaken the medium's pull further.
  - N1b and its μ-term were added after freezing. They are reported only.
- **(c) BTFR line.** The canonical footing misses the frozen 3× line by a hair (2.94×). The class is set by the worse
  footing (alt 3.09×).
- **Not blind.** The R1, R3 and R5 numbers re-use verified pipelines (P4, Z2, F1) that I had read before freezing.
