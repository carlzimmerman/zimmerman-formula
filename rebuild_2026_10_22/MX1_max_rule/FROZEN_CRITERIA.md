## 1. The rule, fixed
g = max(L, C), L = law(g_b) = g_b/2 + sqrt(g_b^2/4 + a0 g_b), C = g_b + g_c. Where g_b <= 0, L = g_b, so g = g_b + g_c. No refit of anything against rotation curves, lensing or masses. Own implementation; no import or exec of DW2's code. Data read from its inputs and other lanes' JSON is allowed; RQ3's harness is run through a wrapper that only redirects outputs.

**Share.** M200 = (1 + 5.35) M_b(<R200), iterated. 5.35 is UX2's native output. EM1 line, for the EM1 cell only: M200 = 10^(11.18 + 0.9 (lg M_b - 10)). R200 at 200 rho_c, h 0.672, native. M_c(<r) = (5.35/6.35) M200 m(r/R200), m the native ZN2 shape at z 0 (`zn2_low_shapes.json`), interpolated linearly in lg M between tabulated masses, clamped at the ends, m ~ x^2 below the table.
**M_b, one order of rule for every system, no selector by name:** (i) where Q carries `lgMb`, a measured total, M_b = 10^lgMb, whatever the fitted Upsilon; (ii) where Q carries `r500` matching an X-COP cluster, M_b(<r) is that cluster's **measured** MGAS + MSTAR from its own FITS file, flat past the end of each profile, no extrapolation: this replaces DW2's linear extension, and gas reaches R200 in all 7 by the extents I read; (iii) otherwise the largest g_b r^2/G on the system's array, read at R200 with the array's own values, flat beyond its end. The array in (iii) gives contraction its M_b(r) in every case.
**Contraction.** Scaled by f: M_c = M_0 + f (M_contracted - M_0), Blumenthal-type, r_i M_0i(r_i) (1 + M_b,tot/M_c,tot) = r_f (M_b(r_f) + M_0i(r_i)), my own solver.

## 5. Physics, questions answered with a computation each
**P1, is a parent possible?** Two auxiliary Newtonian fields g1, g2 sourced by baryons and by cold; QUMOND-class Lagrangian with Q(g1, g2); baryons feel dQ/dg1, cold feels dQ/dg2. Requiring dQ/dg1 = max(L(g1), g1 + g2) on the aligned line, and Q continuous, I get by hand: law zone Q = A(g1) + h(g2), with the cold's force h'(g2) = a0 g2/(a0 - g2), its own field only and no baryons; cold zone Q = (g1 + g2)^2/2. Test: closed-loop integral of (dQ/dg1, dQ/dg2) on grids crossing the boundary, zero within 1e-6 relative to the loop's largest path term, both footings; twin with the cold's force set to g1 + g2 in the law zone must fail. Labels: **ALIGNED PARENT EXISTS** or NO PARENT. Then oblique g1, g2: Q_max = max(Q_L, Q_C), measure the difference between its zone and the rule's over angles. If it differs by more than 1 % of the (g1, g2, angle) cells on a stated grid, the vector rule itself isn't that parent's. Health is read from UX1's K7 (indefinite; READ, not re-derived). No covariant parent is attempted.
