## Is a field equation possible
**P1, ALIGNED PARENT EXISTS, VECTOR RULE HAS NONE.** QUMOND-class, auxiliary fields g1, g2 from baryons, cold; baryons feel dQ/dg1, cold feels dQ/dg2. On aligned fields:
- law zone: Q = A(g1) + h(g2); baryons feel L(g1); **cold feels a0 g2/(a0 - g2), its own field only;**
- cold zone: Q = (g1 + g2)^2/2.
The loop closes (K8, twin fails by 1e-2). The surfaces touch along the boundary, Q_L >= Q_C everywhere, 2e-13 on it (A1: my frozen "max of Q" was wrong for that reason, and reads cold zone 0 %, against the rule's 14 %).
**Oblique:** baryon force ghat_1 L(g1) forces Q_L = A(|g1|) + h(|g2|), while on the rule's boundary Q_C depends on g1 alone and |g2| ranges from L - g1 to L + g1. Jump Q_L/Q_C - 1 at g1 = a0: 0.7 % at 30 deg, 21 % at 60 deg, no finite value from 90 deg, where the boundary has |g2| > a0 and h diverges. So no continuous Q. This is an argument inside one class, not a no-go. Health of the class reads as UX1's K7 (indefinite, READ, not re-derived). So the sharp switch has no equation here, for the rule as written.
