No viable response was obtained with the fixed baryon sources and comparison
cuts. The useful outcome is a reproducible set of corrected equations,
identified failure mechanisms, and scoped exclusions. This does not establish
a universal no-go for modified gravity or every Gaussian/nonlocal action.

The constant isotropic quadratic Hessian repair is excluded analytically under
its stated boundary/matter assumptions: the sign that reduces the bad UV term
worsens both static-force failures. A completion preserving the same reduced
static response inherits them. No new fully specified dynamical parent was
derived in this investigation.

## Next steps, in order and with spending gates

1. **Park further width/exponent sweeps of these tested functionals.** Preserve
   the explicit unsampled-width and full-population caveats; the present data
   provide no evidence-based reason to fund another broad sweep.
2. **Require an explicit new action before another numerical program.** Check
   its exact functional against KT41's prior-art map. Derive its physical
   matter coupling and every reciprocal term. An extra field, memory time or
   cutoff name without a changed static functional is insufficient.
3. **Apply cheap analytic gates first.** Demonstrate adequate high-acceleration
   Solar recovery including the radial anomaly; derive the signed dwarf
   compression stress; identify which hypothesis of the KT40 obstruction is
   actually changed. Failure stops that candidate before galaxy scans.
4. **Only a survivor earns the fixed diagnostic suite:** complete MW vertical
   profiles, the frozen dwarf virial, regular Solar Q2, and full-return orbital
   monopole checks. Preserve the existing baryons and cuts. Do not accept a
   four-cut MW/Q2 pass as sufficient.
5. **Only after those pass, expand the expensive obligations:** the full1,375
   baryon population, a self-consistent Galactic-Hessian embedding, dynamical
   and relativistic health, and observational fits with appropriate covariance.

There is no authorized background run or pending computation. No new viable
candidate is claimed. A future continuation starts with the explicit action
and inexpensive gates above, rather than repeating the closed scans.
