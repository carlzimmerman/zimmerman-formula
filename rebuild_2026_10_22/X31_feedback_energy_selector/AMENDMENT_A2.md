binding, more extra.

best-threshold loop restricted to direction UP (`best_up`), with its offenders, and gives each hard case's side against that
UP threshold (`side_UP`, `side_UP_efe`). Both are reported only. The verdict still comes from X19's two-direction rule. It
was FEEDBACK FAILS before this addition and is FEEDBACK FAILS after it.
