# Sign-convention wording clarification

DERIVATION.md is frozen because it is an input to monopole_001,
monopole_low_001 and localize_001, and to the independent dynamics review.
Its line59 phrase "positive scalar binding virial" meant the convention
that an attractive contribution has positive W; it did not assume W>0.

DERIVATION_v2.md replaces that phrase by an explicit attractive-positive
sign convention and states that the sign of W is not assumed positive.
There is no change to any equation, boundary condition, numerical result,
configuration count or physical conclusion. The negative W results and
stationary-equilibrium obstruction retain their original meanings.
The original document and all computation manifests remain unchanged;
no experiment rerun is needed for this wording-only clarification.
