## 1. The law
g_N = g²/(g + a₀), with a₀(z) = κc√(Gρ_DE(z)), κ = ½ fitted. The relation is Milgrom's simple form. The nearness of a₀ to cH₀ is Milgrom's observation, and the tie to dark energy was proposed earlier by Limbach, Psaltis and Özel. Two footings, canonical 9.3603e-11 and alternative 1.1312e-10 m/s², are never pooled. SPARC doesn't single out κ = ½: Milgrom's cH₀/2π fits at least as well (Δχ² 5.34 against 63.90 from the free best fit). Of 550 derivation or selection attempts on record, none derives ½. They all reduce to one missing length, exactly c/√(Gρ), that no field equation on record supplies (KC1). Telling ½ from 0.461 needs a₀ to 2.8 %, and the shared galaxy-mass floor is 9.47 %.
