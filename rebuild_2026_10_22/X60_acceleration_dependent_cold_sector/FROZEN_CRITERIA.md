## 1. Hypothesis AN (acceleration-dependent nature)

Cold dark energy and dark energy are two phases of one field: dust (w ≈ 0) where the gravitational acceleration exceeds a
threshold g′ of order a₀, smooth vacuum energy (w ≈ −1) below it.

**Threshold.** Primary g′ = X47's g_T = 0.254484 a₀ (2.382e-11 / 2.879e-11 m s⁻²; the peak pull of an a₀/(2πG) Burkert
core). Variant g′ = a₀. For E1 the full window in g′ is scanned and reported; no value is fitted.

**Known obstructions (inputs, not re-derived):** X47 (local key kills linear growth; latching forms no floor), CFG944
Theorem G (local θ-keyed L(θ, u) blocked), the μ-pincer Part F (density-keyed pressure fails), X54 (no background
exchange, ξ ∈ [−0.0035, +0.0011]), X17 (acceleration-keyed sound speed inert in statics).

  E3-N (relocation: dust removed from galaxies reappears elsewhere).
