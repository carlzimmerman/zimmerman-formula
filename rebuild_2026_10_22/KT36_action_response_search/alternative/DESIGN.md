# Positive mixtures of reciprocal Gaussian action terms

This is an authorized bounded research experiment, not an adopted response law.
The baryon population, acceleration footings, simple nu, and observational cuts
remain frozen. The alternative is distinct from changing the local constitutive
nu function and from independently choosing an inner and outer Gaussian.

For fixed self-adjoint Gaussian operators S_j define the phantom energy
`E_ph,mix[rho] = sum_j w_j E_ph[S_j rho]`, with `w_j>=0` and `sum_j w_j=1`.
Its variational potential is
`Phi = N rho + sum_j w_j S_j Phi_ph[S_j rho]`.
Thus every action term contains its own matched source and outer operator.
An isolated two-scale mixture has three explicit shape parameters: two widths
and one independent weight. The widths are held fixed in a source variation,
as in KT33; this is not a derivation of the object-level width rule.

Normalization preserves the isolated far-field deep-MOND coefficient of the
original response. Positive weights cannot cancel same-sign Cassini signals.
The full potential is the convex combination of the component full potentials,
so squared circular speeds and signed vertical forces are affine in weights.
The actual speeds and chi-squared scores are not affine.

The first discriminator uses the stored 1,375-cell MW outputs at the fifteen
widths `0,.1,.15,.2,.25,.3,.35,.4,.45,.5,.55,.6,.75,1,2`, with fresh matching
Cassini calculations. For each cell, maximize local `vc^2` over all normalized
positive mixtures satisfying the signed Cassini cut. A linear functional on
this simplex with one additional active linear cut attains its maximum at a
single passing component or at a two-component edge crossing the cut. Exhaustive
vertex enumeration therefore covers arbitrarily many positive components
supported on these fifteen widths, without gridding their weights.

Stored low- and high-width MW arrays use different resolutions and some narrow
components failed their numerical angular-tail gate. The first run is therefore
an exploratory finite optimization, not a continuum or validated-model result.
It will identify the largest local-speed witness and the component widths for a
targeted same-resolution replay. Passing this necessary local-speed test would
require a separate full four-cut check; failure rejects only the tested support
and resolution. No claim about all real Gaussian widths is licensed.
