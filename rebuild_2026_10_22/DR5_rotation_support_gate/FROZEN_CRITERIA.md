**Stated before any number, as desk results.**
1. *Rule is posited, not derived.* No action gives S_rot. A gate on baryon V and σ is a non-gravitational coupling to baryon velocity moments. Any row from it is BY HAND.
2. *Vortices without quanta.* A shift-symmetric non-compact field has a single-valued phase: no vortices at all. Vortices need a compact phase, period Γ₁ (the classical constant that quantization would call h/m). No quanta are needed, but Γ₁ is one new constant, FITTED, and CR1 rows that have it either fail R5 (irrotational, no crossing, so the zooms' η isn't native) or carry quanta (R7).
3. *No torque.* Gravity is a gradient: ∮∇Φ·dl = 0 (Kelvin), so a barotropic field gets no circulation from a disc by gravity alone, bars included. Nucleation needs a further non-gravitational or dissipative ingredient.
