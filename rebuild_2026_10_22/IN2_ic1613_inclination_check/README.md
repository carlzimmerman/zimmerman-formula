## Verdict (by IN1's frozen falsifier): **IC 1613 REMAINS A FAILURE**

- The independent stellar-tracer central values (32°, 36°) and the photometric value (37°) are all ≥ 30°.
- **Statistically it is disfavoured, not excluded.** IN1's window edge of 26.5° lies 0.5σ below 32 ± 11 and 0.9σ below
  36 ± 11. Its centre, 22.5°, lies 0.9σ and 1.2σ below them.
- The MUSE authors themselves note that "a value as low as 15° is less favoured". They also note that an inclination
  near 35° would make IC 1613 an outlier of the baryonic Tully–Fisher relation **in their framework too**. Its HI
  curve rises unusually slowly, which they attribute to holes and depressions in the HI.

**Reading.** The data favour the moderate inclination at which IN1 showed the law over-predicts IC 1613. IC 1613
returns to the list of failures. It is a known outlier for other frameworks as well, which is context, not a rescue.
