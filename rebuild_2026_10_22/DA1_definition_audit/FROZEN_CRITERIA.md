## Method
For each item: (1) state the definition from the primary source, quoting under 15 words, with arXiv id or file sha256; (2) only then read the chain's code and state its definition; (3) compare; (4) where they differ, recompute the affected number on FG1's inputs, one thread, nice, under 1 GB RSS unless logged otherwise, and carry it to the graded line.

WebFetch look-ups are provisional: a difference found from a summarised page isn't a finding until checked against the full text or the raw table.

## Labels, per item
- **HOLDS:** chain's definition matches the source, or differs with no graded line moved by more than its own tolerance.
- **WRONG, GRADE CHANGES:** a real difference that flips a pass/fail on a graded line. State which, both numbers and both footings where they apply.
- **WRONG, NO FLIP:** a real difference, no pass/fail changed; number stated.
- **AMBIGUOUS:** the source allows more than one reading; every reading computed, and whether the grade depends on it.
- **CANNOT CHECK:** source or raw data not on disk or not reachable. Never counted as HOLDS. No downloads without the owner's approval.
