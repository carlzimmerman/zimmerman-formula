## Question
NP2's admissible L3 rule (leaves log y, log ell, f, mode F) passes gates 1-5 on both footings and RQ3_acceptance_v2 rejects it on rows that pull opposite ways at similar local leaves: A10 IC 1613 wants less gravity than the rule, A12/A13 dwarfs and ultra-faints and A30 KiDS lensing want more, and A5-A7 (Υ rows) fail. Does **one non-local variable E**, added as a fourth leaf (L3+E), remove these residual rows (A10, A12, A13, A30, A5, A6, A7) without breaking gates 1-5?

## Candidates (declared here; each one formula for every system, values only from files on disk; nothing downloaded)
Missing value = NOT COMPUTABLE for that system, never imputed. Each candidate's error σ_E is used only by the sign test.

- **E1, external Newtonian field from neighbours.** E1 = log10 e_N, e_N = |g_N,ext|/(1.2e-10 m s⁻²): the record's Chae-2021-style 2M++ + MCXC vector sum, `gext_vectors_2026/data/gext_vectors.csv`, value = mean of `log_eN_noclu` and `log_eN_maxclu` (X33's convention); σ_E = half the noclu-maxclu bracket per system. Rows exist for SPARC galaxies only (by name); LITTLE THINGS galaxies get it only through CX1's SPARC alias table. Not computable on disk for X-COP and KiDS lenses (2M++ ends at 200 Mpc; lens positions are not on disk; S2 records that no external field is measured for the KiDS lenses), nor inside the Local Group (2M++ velocity distances are not valid there and the Milky Way is not in the catalogue). Extending the estimator to Local-Volume sources would be a new estimator, not a record value; it is not built here.
- **E2, basin mass.** E2 = log10 M_basin, exactly CX1's E leaf (`CX1_leaf_collision/cx1_rows.py`, BS1 values), copied with attribution: SPARC: BS1 `A_c2b_refit` basin if listed, NOT COMPUTABLE if in BS1 `no_dist`, else EM1 lgM200(log10(0.5 L36 + 1.33 MHI)·1e9); LITTLE THINGS: Local Group members (CX1's LG_IDENT) → LG basin, SPARC alias → the SPARC rule, else NOT COMPUTABLE; dwarfs (X1 table): host MW or M31 → LG basin, field: BS1 field_R < R0 → LG basin, else EM1 of BS1's M_b(M★); X-COP: EM1(log10(M_gas,500 + M★,500)) (EM1 extrapolated, as CX1 flagged); groups: EM1(lgM_b); lensing: CX1's rows use CX1's E; in RQ3's A30 path the same formula for an isolated system, EM1(log10 M_b,total), with M_b,total = g_b r²/G at the outermost grid radius of the call (the grader's own baryon model; it can differ from CX1's 1.25 M★ by ~0.1 dex, reported); Milky Way and the planets: LG basin. σ_E = 0.15 dex (CX1's E_SD). For group members E2 is a neighbour quantity; for isolated systems it is a function of the system's own baryon mass. Both are BS1's definition, used as is.
- **E3, host potential depth.** E3 = log10(G M_host / ((d_host + 3 kpc) c²)), RQ3's host masses (MW 6e10, M31 1e11, field 6e10 M☉) and the X1 table's `d_host_kpc`. Defined only for systems with a recorded host distance (X1 dwarf table, which includes IC 1613). SPARC, X-COP, LITTLE THINGS other than through the X1 table, and lenses have no host distance on disk: NOT COMPUTABLE. σ_E = 0.1 dex (ASSUMED).
- **E4, tidal index.** E4 = Θ1 of the Updated Nearby Galaxy Catalogue (Karachentsev+ 2013, `real_research/data/ungc_karachentsev2013.tsv`, column Ti1), as catalogued; Θ5 (Ti5) reported only. Matching, frozen: names normalised as CX1's `norm` (upper case, spaces removed, leading zeros after letters removed), plus this alias table for the record's naming of the same objects: Andromeda N → And N; Ursa Major I/II → UMa I/II; Ursa Minor → UMin; Canes Venatici I/II → CVnI/CVnII; Coma Berenices → ComaI; Sextans → SexDSph; Sagittarius → Sag dSph; Sagittarius dIrr → Sag dIr; Pegasus dIrr and DDO 216 → Pegasus; Aquarius and DDO 210 → DDO210. SPARC galaxies by position: nearest UNGC galaxy within 0.1° of the `gext_vectors.csv` position. LITTLE THINGS: by name, else through CX1's SPARC alias and the SPARC match. X-COP, groups, lenses: beyond the catalogue, NOT COMPUTABLE. σ_E = 0.3 (ASSUMED).
- No other candidate is added. Considered and not used: X33's `e_ext` (different formulas by class: Chae table for SPARC, X1's host-only MOND-converted field for dwarfs); J1's |Φ| (own potential for SPARC, host-inclusive for satellites: not one formula); TH1 flow basins (13 centres, a subset of BS1's basins).

## Coverage (reported for every candidate before any fit)
Rows and systems with E, per graded line: train SPARC (odd), train X-COP (clusters 0, 2, 4, 6), SPARC even, X-COP other, LITTLE THINGS (gate 4), CX1 lines (G, L, Dw, Dw-UFD, IC1613), and per RQ3 block (SPARC A1-A10 incl. LT, dwarfs A11-A13, lensing A30, groups B1, X-COP B23-B25, MW A15-A17, DiskMass A18, planets C1, keys, Bullet, old gates).

**Fit-able rule:** a candidate is fitted only if it covers every train X-COP cluster and ≥ 90 % of train SPARC rows (gate 1 needs X-COP). Train rows without E are dropped from the fit (count reported). A candidate that is not fit-able reads **NOT COMPUTABLE** for the fit; its sign test is still run where computable.

## Step 1: sign test (cheap pre-check, before any fit), per candidate and footing
- Residual r = log10(g_obs / law(g_b, a0_foot)) − m(leaves), m = NP2's frozen `real_L3_F_<footing>` rule (read-only), on train rows (BF1) and on CX1's centre rows (`np1_core.cx_rows`).
- **Train trend:** per train system, mean r against E (systems with E), Spearman ρ; p from 2000 permutations of E across those systems (seed 20261107). s_train = sign(ρ) if p < 0.05, else 0.
- **Target groups:** T10 = IC 1613 (CX1's IC1613 LITTLE THINGS row), TUFD = CX1 line Dw-UFD, TDW = CX1 line Dw, T30 = CX1 line L. A5-A7 have no sign test (mode F targets are built at Υ 0.5; they are graded only through the fit).
  - want_T = sign(median r_T) if |median r_T| > 2 sd_T (sd_T = median CX1 row sd / √(number of systems)), else 0.
  - ΔE_T = median E of the group − median E of its 50 nearest train rows in NP2's kernel metric ((z − z′)/ℓ, standardised leaves), i.e. at fixed local leaves.
  - The group **separates** if want_T ≠ 0, s_train ≠ 0, |ΔE_T| > σ_E and sign(ΔE_T)·s_train = want_T.
- **Label per candidate and footing:** SEPARATES (every computable group with want ≠ 0 separates, and a computable pair with opposite wants exists); PARTIAL (some separate); DOES NOT SEPARATE; NOT COMPUTABLE (no target group computable, or no train trend computable).
- **Look-elsewhere:** E permuted across systems 1000 times (seed 20261108), the test re-run; the fraction reading SEPARATES is reported. A SEPARATES counts only if that fraction ≤ 0.05.
- The sign test decides no verdict label; it is reported beside the fit.

## Step 2: fit (fit-able candidates only)
- **Machinery:** NP2's admissible Gaussian-process rule (`../NP2_admissible_rule/np2_core.py`, imported read-only; extensions copied into this lane with attribution): same split, target, W3 wrapper, ARD kernel, class noises, step A (type-II ML, 4 starts), step B (4-fold grouped CV, λ grid {¼ … 32}, constrained fold fits, CV eligibility per NP2 A3), enforcement pool (NP2 C1 + A2), working-set caps, solve/check tolerances (A1), checker (C4 + A2), footprint metering (A3).
- **Arm L3+E only, mode F only,** both footings, each fitted separately and never pooled. (NP2's L5 could not be held admissible; NP2's mode U made A5-A7 worse and broke gates 4-5. Neither is rerun.)
- **E as a leaf:** standardised like the others. Admissibility directions (stars-only, gas-only, fixed share) hold E fixed: adding the system's own baryons does not move its environment. The constraint box pads E's train range by 3 frozen lengths; the checker's wide box takes E over [8, 16] (E2).
- **Candidate loader for RQ3:** g = law(g_b, a0_foot)·10^m(log y, log ell, f, E), E looked up by RQ3's call keys with the candidate's formula: `sample` and `name` (SPARC discs, LITTLE THINGS, dwarfs, with `host`), `r500` (X-COP rows, matched to the cluster's FITS R500), `lgMb` (groups), the grid (lensing, E2 only), `sample` (MW, planets). Anything else (DiskMass, keys' eRASS clusters, Bullet, old-gate samples) has no E: NaN, RQ3 reads NOT COMPUTABLE. Reading `name`, `sample`, `host`, `r500` puts RQ3's [LABEL] tag on the card; that is inherent to a non-local leaf (RQ3 row A29: "no environment input to g") and is reported, not hidden.

## Grading
- **Gates 1-4:** NP1/NP2's `gates14` on the rows that have E; lines recomputed on the same rows (gate 2/3 SPARC line = 1.05 × the real law's rms on those rows). Dropped rows counted per line.
- **Gate 5 and Stage 3:** RQ3 `acceptance.py` run unedited from its own source with this lane as working directory (NP2's `np2_stage3` pattern): the **unedited card (U)**.
- **SUBSET card (S), declared because one system without E makes a whole RQ3 block NOT COMPUTABLE:** the same `acceptance.py` text, executed unedited, with CRG1's `load_gal` and `load_lt` (and the X1 dwarf list, if needed) filtered to systems where E is computable. The S card is a different sample from RQ3's. NP2's frozen L3 F rule and the plain law are run through the same S card, so before/after compare like with like. S results never make RESOLVES; they can make PARTIAL.
- **Before/after table:** each target row (A10, A12, A13, A30, A5, A6, A7) per footing: NP2 L3 F on RQ3's card (from NP2's results), NP2 L3 F on the S card, NV1 on U and S, NV1 E-shuffle MUTATE on U and S.

## Labels (per candidate and footing; never pooled)
- **NOT COMPUTABLE:** the candidate is not fit-able, or its controls fail.
- **ONE VARIABLE RESOLVES (E):** controls pass; admissible (checker clean, fit converged); gates 1-4 pass; gate 5 passes on U; every target row is computable on U and passes (|Z| ≤ RQ3's line); no row that NP2 L3 F passes on RQ3's card fails on U; and the E-shuffle MUTATE stops resolving (at least one target row fails on U).
- **PARTIAL:** not RESOLVES, but at least one target row moves: on U or on S, |Z| falls by ≥ 1.0 against NP2 L3 F on the same card (or the row turns PASS), while the E-shuffle MUTATE on the same card does not reproduce at least half of that fall. Each moving row is listed with before/after Z; rows that get worse are listed too.
- **NO SINGLE NON-LOCAL VARIABLE ON RECORD RESOLVES:** the lane label when no candidate RESOLVES on a footing. It is printed with the PARTIAL rows if any.
- **Lane label NOT COMPUTABLE:** no candidate fit-able with passing controls and no sign test computable.
- Gate failures, checker violations and underfits are reported as in NP2 (UNDETERMINED (INADMISSIBLE / UNDERFIT) for that candidate's fit), and such a fit can't RESOLVE.

Tolerances are the existing gates' (BF1/SY1 gates 1-4, NP2's) and RQ3's (Z line, tolerances.json), unchanged, set before any fit.

## Controls, run and read before any real fit (mode `controls`; `real` refuses without its output)
1. **Known answer:** X-COP mean log B +0.4675, LITTLE THINGS median −0.0252, each within 5e-4.
2. **Zero-weight rule through NV1's E loader and RQ3 unedited** (`--no-keys --no-bullet`), both footings: every row computed on the zero card matches RQ3's `results/law.json` (label; Z and value to 1e-6 relative); rows NOT COMPUTABLE are listed with their reason (E coverage, or NP2's by-design skips). This list is RQ3's coverage for the candidate. On the S card, the zero rule must reproduce the plain law (`RQ3_acceptance_v2/candidates/law.py`) run through the same S card, row by row, to the same tolerance.
3. **Checker:** flags a planted −1.5 dex bump (NP2's, with width 1 in E) and passes the zero rule.
4. **Admissible plant** g = law(g_b, a0(1 + 0.5 f)) at SPARC's errors (NP1's draw), fitted in L3+E: converges, checker clean, gates 3-4 pass.
5. **Planted non-local boost:** g = law(g_b, a0(1 + 0.5 f))·10^(0.15 z_E), z_E = (E − train mean)/train sd per system, SPARC errors (NP1's draw), on train and holdouts with E. Fitted (a) with E, (b) with E permuted across systems (seed 20261108). Pass: (a) converges, checker clean, gates 3-4 pass; the SPARC-even rms of (planted − rule) for (a) ≤ 0.85 × that of (b); and (a)'s mean ∂m/∂E over train rows is within 0.5-1.5 × the planted 0.15/sd_E.
6. **MUTATE (E shuffle), run with the real fit:** E permuted across systems (seed 20261109, the same permutation of system E values for train and every holdout and RQ3 lookup), refit, graded on U and S. It is the look-elsewhere control: a RESOLVES or PARTIAL must not survive it (see labels).
