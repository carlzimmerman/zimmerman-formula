## Question
NV1's E1 (external Newtonian field from neighbours) was NOT COMPUTABLE for X-COP and the KiDS lenses because 2M++ ends at 200 Mpc. With a 2MPZ cone per X-COP cluster and the on-disk KiDS-bright DR4 sample as neighbour catalogues, is E1 computable for them with one formula for every system, does it separate the residual rows (NV1's sign test), and does NP2's admissible L3 rule with E1 as a fourth leaf (L3+E1) resolve NV1's residual rows (A10, A12, A13, A30, A5, A6, A7) without breaking gates 1-5?

## E1, one formula for every system (the record's convention, truncated identically everywhere)
E1 = ½ [log10 e_noclu + log10 e_maxclu], e = |Σ g_i| / (1.2e-10 m s⁻²): the record's Chae-2021 Sec. 3.1 vector sum (`gext_vectors_2026/src/gext_estimator.py`, reimplemented in this lane with attribution), with:
- **Sources:** galaxies as point masses M★ + M_gas, M_gas from Chae's Eqs. 7/8 deterministic expectation (record convention, f_early clipped to [0, 1]); MCXC clusters (`gext_vectors_2026/data/raw/mcxc.tsv`, all 1743 rows, no 200 Mpc cut) as M_MOND point masses outside R500 and (d/R500)³ inside, Chae Eqs. 3-4 as the record codes them (E(z) as written). Sources closer than 10 kpc are excluded (Chae).
- **noclu** = raw visible sum. **maxclu** = each galaxy mass divided by the catalogue's visible fraction f at its distance, then the whole field × 8 (record convention).
- **Distances:** comoving D_c(z) with H0 = 73 km s⁻¹ Mpc⁻¹ (Chae / SPARC), flat Ωm = 0.3, for every catalogue (2M++ z = V_cmb/c). Separations are comoving / (1 + z_test) (proper at the test point). SPARC test points sit at the SPARC distance (record).
- **Truncation (new, the same for every system):** only sources within a sphere of comoving radius **R_s = 30 Mpc** of the test point enter. SPARC E1 is recomputed with this truncation from the on-disk 2M++ (`gext_vectors_2026/data/raw/2mpp_vizier.tsv`, catalogue mode `full`, as the record), so NV2's E1 is NOT the record's full-sky value; the Spearman ρ and median offset between NV2's SPARC E1 and the record's (`gext_vectors.csv`, mean of the two logs) are reported, not gated.
- **Self exclusion ("the system itself"):** sources whose projected separation is below the system's outer radius r_out and whose redshift is consistent with the system's: SPARC galaxy: the record's rule (0.1° and |ΔD| < max(3 Mpc, 0.2 D)); X-COP cluster: r_out = 2 R500 (FITS header), consistent = |Δz| < 0.01 for spectroscopic sources, < 3 × 0.015 for photometric ones, and MCXC entries within 0.2° and |Δz| < 0.01 of the cluster; KiDS lens: r_out = 3 Mpc/h70 (B21's measurement extent), consistent = |Δz| < 3 × 0.02 (1 + z), plus the lens itself by ID.
- **Catalogues:**
  - SPARC: 2M++ (spectroscopic; record masses: Ks, M/L_Ks 0.64, Lavaux-Hudson k+e; visible fraction from the Kochanek+01 K-band LF at the record's region-dependent Ks limit). No photo-z MC.
  - X-COP: the 2MPZ cones (below). Masses as 2M++ (kCorr = extinction-corrected 20 mag/arcsec² isophotal Ks, the same family as 2M++'s K20), Lavaux-Hudson k+e, M/L 0.64; visible fraction from the same Kochanek LF at Ks = 13.9 (2MPZ limit). Redshift: zSpec when present (> −1), else zPhoto with σ_z = 0.015 (Bilicki+14).
  - KiDS lenses: KiDS-bright DR4 (`_external_data/lam1_kids_bright_2026_11_03/`, read-only, `python3 -I`), pool = `masked` = 0, MAG_AUTO_CALIB < 20, finite mass, any z. M★ = MASS_BEST + 0.15 + 0.056 dex (0.15: the record's stand-in for B21's fluxscale correction, whose MAG_GAAP_r is not on disk; 0.056: B21's normalisation to GAMA). σ_z = 0.02 (1 + z) (B21 Sec. 3.3). Visible fraction = share of the stellar-mass density above M_lim(z) for the Baldry+12 GAMA double Schechter SMF (log M* 10.66, φ1 3.96e-3, α1 −0.35, φ2 0.79e-3, α2 −1.47), M_lim(z) = the 5th percentile of the pool's log M★ in Δz = 0.02 bins; f clipped to [1e-3, 1] (record clip).
  - The three catalogues differ in depth and stellar-mass estimator. That is a disclosed systematic of E1 across classes; there is no on-disk system that two catalogues both cover to the sphere, so it is not calibrated. As a scale for it, the SPARC E1 difference between 2M++ modes `ks115` and `full` (depth only) is reported.
- **Photo-z error budget (set here):** Monte Carlo over redshift errors, every photometric source (and a KiDS lens's own z) redrawn from N(z_phot, σ_z), negative z reflected; X-COP 200 realisations (seed 20261110), lenses 20 (seed 20261111). E1 = median over realisations of ½[log e_noclu + log e_maxclu]; σ_E1 = √(h_b² + h_MC²), h_b = median half noclu-maxclu bracket, h_MC = half the 16-84 % range over realisations. SPARC: σ_E1 = h_b. The sign test uses one σ_E = the median σ_E1 over systems with E1 (as NV1 A1 did).
- **Footprint coverage:** fraction of the sphere's projected cap (radius asin(R_s / D_c)) inside the catalogue's footprint, from 2000 uniform points in the cap (seed 20261112); a point is covered if a catalogue source lies within 0.5° (2MPZ) or 0.06° (KiDS-bright pool). Coverage < 0.8 → E1 NOT COMPUTABLE for that system (X-COP) or the lens is dropped (lenses; counts reported per bin). SPARC/2M++: coverage 1 (record: zone-of-avoidance mock galaxies kept).

## Step 0: rebuild Brouwer+21's isolated lens sample (known answer before any lens E1)
- B21 Sec. 3.3 and App. A, as published: lenses with 0.1 < z_ANN < 0.5, M★ < 10¹¹ h70⁻² M☉, from the KiDS-bright sample (m_r,auto < 20, masked = 0); a lens is isolated if no galaxy with M★,sat > 0.1 M★,lens lies within a spherical radius r_sat = 3 h70⁻¹ Mpc, r_sat from the projected and redshift (photo-z) distances, comoving, H0 = 70, Ωm = 0.3 (the record's DC). Satellites from the whole pool. Mass bins log10(M★ / h70⁻² M☉) = [8.5, 10.3, 10.6, 10.8, 11.0].
- **Known answer:** B21 publishes the isolated total, **259,383**, and "∼1 million" KiDS-bright lenses; it publishes no per-bin counts and no exact isolated fraction (checked in the text). **Tolerance: the rebuilt isolated total within ±5 % of 259,383** ([246,414, 272,352]). Per-bin counts and the isolated fraction are reported, not gated.
- If it misses: reconstruction **FAILED**, lenses graded **NOT COMPUTABLE** (no lens E1 enters the sign test, the fit or A30). Lens E1 per bin is then still computed on the rebuilt sample and reported as **UNGRADED**.
- Lens E1 (either way): a subsample of 5000 lenses per bin (seed 20261113) among rebuilt isolated lenses with coverage ≥ 0.8. **Per-bin E1** = the lens-weighted mean of lens E1, weight w(z_l) = the record's mean WLENS in Δz = 0.02 bins of `lens_inventory.npz` (interpolated); the spread (weighted sd) and the error of the mean (bootstrap over lenses, 1000) are reported.
- **How per-bin E1 enters A30 (only if Step 0 passes):** RQ3's lens call carries no bin; the candidate reads the call's stellar mass lm = log10[(g_b − g_gas) r² / G] at the outermost grid radius and takes the per-bin E1 of B21's bin containing lm; lm outside [8.5, 11.0] takes the nearest bin's value (those grid points carry zero stacking weight in CRG1's Wg). CX1's line L rows bin1-bin4 take the same per-bin values in the sign test.

## Controls, run and read before any real result
1. **NV1 base controls:** known answer X-COP mean log B +0.4675, LITTLE THINGS median −0.0252 (each within 5e-4); zero-weight E1 rule through NV2's loader and RQ3 unedited reproduces RQ3's law card on every computed row (label; Z and value to 1e-6 relative), and the plain law on the S card (forward check, as NV1 A2); checker flags NV1's planted bump and passes the zero rule; NV1's admissible plant converges, checker clean, gates 3-4 pass.
2. **Planted non-local boost (NV1 control 5, with E1):** g = law(g_b, a0(1 + 0.5 f)) · 10^(0.15 z_E), fitted with true E1 and with E1 permuted across systems (seed 20261108). Pass as NV1: (a) converges, checker clean, gates 3-4; SPARC-even rms (a) ≤ 0.85 × (b); (a)'s mean ∂m/∂E within 0.5-1.5 × planted.
3. **E1 estimator known answers:** (a) one point mass: |g| and direction to 1e-10 relative; (b) uniform field: a single 1e15 M☉ point 10⁴ Mpc away, at 50 test points within 10 Mpc of the origin: |g| within 0.5 % of GM/d², directions within 0.01 rad of each other; (c) symmetric shell: 500 antipodal pairs around a test point: |g| / (G M_pair / r²) < 1e-10; (d) truncation: a source just inside R_s counts, just outside does not; (e) **record convention:** with no truncation, record distances (D = V_cmb/73) and record self rule, NV2's code reproduces `gext_vectors.csv` log_eN_noclu and log_eN_maxclu for all 175 SPARC galaxies to ≤ 2e-3 dex (the csv carries 6 significant figures).
4. **Photo-z fidelity (decides whether a photo-z catalogue's E1 is usable):** on the 175 SPARC test points with 2M++ sources (R_s = 30 Mpc), source distances redrawn from N(D, c σ_z / H0), 50 realisations (seed 20261114), E1 taken as the median, Spearman ρ against the noiseless E1. Model A (KiDS): every source perturbed, σ_z = 0.02. Model B (2MPZ): σ_z = 0.015 for each source with probability p_spec(Ks) of NOT being perturbed, p_spec = the fraction of 2MPZ cone sources with zSpec in 0.5-mag Ks bins (a catalogue property, measured from the download before any X-COP E1 is computed). **Pass: ρ ≥ 0.5.** Fail → that catalogue's E1 is NOISE-DOMINATED and NOT COMPUTABLE for its systems. Median bias (degraded − true) reported.
5. **MUTATE (run with the real fit):** E1 permuted across systems (seed 20261109), one permutation for train, holdouts and RQ3 lookups, per-bin lens values in the pool; on-the-fly values (lens grid) map through NV1 A1 item 5. A RESOLVES or PARTIAL must not survive it.
