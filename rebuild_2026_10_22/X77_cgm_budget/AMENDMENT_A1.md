# X77 AMENDMENT A1 (post-result, reported only; the frozen class is unchanged)

Written after the PRIMARY result was read.

**What happened.** The frozen rule returned "CGM BUDGET SUPPLIES THE MASS", but by runaway. With the measured β ≈ 0.43
profile extrapolated without a cut, the self-consistent r_ta reaches 4.5 Mpc (23 R_vir) and M_hot(<r_ta) ≈ 2×10¹³ M☉,
all of it beyond the measured R_vir. The resulting m = +2.37 overshoots the need (+1.25) by 1.1 dex. That would
over-predict σ_sat on the other side. The criteria only guarded the low side.

**Diagnostic.** Cut the hot-CGM profile at r_cut = k R_vir, i.e. no hot gas beyond. Report m_CGM against k, and the k
that gives exactly +0.85 (lensing) and +1.25 (satellites), at the central, generous and tight values. Compare the
result with Zhang et al.'s own closure radius, R_c ≈ 3 R_vir. Reported only: the cut is a free parameter that the data
beyond R_vir do not fix.
