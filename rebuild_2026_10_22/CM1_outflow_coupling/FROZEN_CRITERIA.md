# CM1_outflow_coupling: FROZEN CRITERIA (committed before any script or number)

**Question.** BE1 found that supernova energy is enough on energy grounds (median 1.7%) to soften the converged native
cusp (CE1 η 25.94 at 1e12) to the SPARC limit η 21.2. Can a gravity-only channel deliver it? The channel: impulsive
gas outflow, then slow return. The cold dark energy halo couples to baryons through gravity alone, so this is the only
coupling tested. The record's 10-12 forward test (sol61 FORWARD_FEEDBACK_RESULT.md) drove gas cycles DURING collapse
and found net energy loss. Its stated next step, run here, is to form the halo first and then drive the gas cycles.

**Representative galaxy (fixed before running).**
- Sample: the canonical-footing BE1 active galaxies (η-collapse branch active; X74 halos, read-only via BE1's loader).
- Take the medians of:
  - M_b/M200 (10^lMb);
  - M_gas/M200 (1.33 MHI);
  - R_d/R200 (SPARC Rdisk).
- Units: G = M200 = R200 = 1. The physical time unit is sqrt(R200³/G M200) at the median M200.

**Cold halo.**
- An NFW cusp, with c chosen so that X81's a_fit_profile at x_c 0.001 gives η = 25.94 (the CE1 1e12 median).
- Exponential taper beyond R200; isotropic Eddington DF in the total potential (cold + static baryons); N = 200000; seed 1.
- Self-gravity by spherical shells (rank-ordered enclosed mass); kick-drift-kick.

**Baryons.**
- Analytic Hernquist profiles, scale a_* = 0.70 R_d.
- Stars (M_b − M_gas) are static.
- The fluctuating gas starts at a_*. Each cycle:
  - it expands to 8 a_* with a cosine ramp over 5 Myr;
  - it returns with a cosine ramp over 200 Myr;
  - the period is 250 Myr; 40 cycles (10 Gyr).
- The final baryon profile equals the initial one.

**Runs.**
| run | what changes |
|---|---|
| STATIC | gas never moves |
| PRIMARY | the fluctuating mass is the measured gas, M_gas |
| BOUND | all baryons M_b fluctuate (a deliberately generous upper bound; unphysical) |
| MUTATE | PRIMARY masses with a slow 200 Myr out-ramp (reversibility control) |

**Statistic.**
- R = η(run)/η(STATIC) at equal time.
- η is from a_fit_profile on the particle cumulative mass, on [x_c, 1], with x_c the radius of the 500th particle (same estimator in every run).
- R is reported every 5 cycles; the graded value is R at 40 cycles.
- Needed: R ≤ 21.2/25.94 = 0.817.
- Energy: E_lift = Σ over cycles of the work done to expand the gas (driver work on gas in the total potential, rapid leg).
  ε_SN = E_lift / E_SN, with E_SN = (0.5 L36 / 100 Msun) 1e51 erg at the median active galaxy (BE1's assumptions).

**Verdicts (canonical footing; alt reported only, since its SPARC line fails at every c_f per X78).**
- **CHANNEL VIABLE:** PRIMARY reaches R ≤ 0.817 with ε_SN ≤ 1.
- **CHANNEL INSUFFICIENT AT MEASURED GAS:** PRIMARY fails, BOUND reaches R ≤ 0.817.
- **CHANNEL EXCLUDED IN THIS GEOMETRY:** BOUND fails too.
- **NOT DECIDED:** any check below fails.

**Checks.**
- **K1:** at t = 0, the particle η is within 5% of the analytic NFW fit at the same x_c. STATIC R(40 cycles) stays in [0.97, 1.03].
- **K2:** work balance |E − E0 − W|/|E0| < 1% in every run.
- **MUTATE:** the slow-out control must heat less than PRIMARY: |1 − R_MUT| < |1 − R_PRIM|. If PRIMARY moves R by < 0.03, MUTATE is NOT INFORMATIVE. BOUND moving R by > 0.03 shows the harness is sensitive.

**Scope.** Prescribed gas histories, not generated by stars. One representative galaxy, spherical, collisionless cold
component. Clusters untested. A pass is a gravity-only existence proof at SPARC mass, not a derivation of η. κ = ½
FITTED. Not theory closed. Cold dark energy is a gravitating field component, never a particle.
