# SC2_clone_rule_on_spec_lenses AMENDMENT A1 (POST-RESULT; changes no frozen line)

Written after the frozen runs (eda8433972), before any number from the runs below.

**The frozen comparison stands.** At the frozen counts, per-lens clones change 10 of 40 graded classes. That table is reported as frozen.

**Why it can't be read as the rule's effect on its own.** The frozen old and new columns each use one random I catalogue, and they differ by more than the rule.
- I's own noise in Δχ² at the frozen counts is about 5 to 6 on S1 and 3 to 4 on S3 with the covariance held fixed. The jackknife covariance also changes with each catalogue, and its 15 × 15 inverse from 36 patches is noisy.
- Three catalogues of S1 all lenses at R2 give −0.3 (old rule), −22.9 (new rule) and −15.8 (new rule, k20). The new rule's two values differ by 7, with no rule change.
- S3's route E χ² at R2 goes 8.7, 11.7, 28.4 across the same three.

So old against new at one seed mixes the rule with catalogue noise. This amendment separates them.

**Runs (reported only).**
- **Seeds at the frozen counts:** 5 more old-rule catalogues and 5 more new-rule catalogues (4 per lens on S3, 2 on S1). Seeds 20261031 + 11 to 15 and + 21 to 25.
- **Low noise:** the old rule at 20 N accepted points, two seeds (+ 31, + 32), and the new rule at 20 per lens, one more seed (+ 33), to go with the frozen k20.
- Same text, statistic, covariance, classes and combined reading as the frozen runs. Lens and R sums must stay equal to the frozen ones (K3), and the nulls are recomputed (K4). K2 and K5 are the new rule's checks and don't apply to old-rule catalogues.

**Outputs.**
- **Per line, rule and footing:** mean and standard deviation of Δχ² across seeds, tally of classes, and the same for the diagonal-only Δχ², which has no matrix inverse.
- **Combined reading:** tally per rule.
- **The rule's effect:** mean(new) − mean(old) with its standard error, at the frozen counts (6 catalogues per rule, counting the frozen ones) and at 20 per lens (2 per rule).
- **Weight ratios:** for each catalogue.

**How to read it.**
- The rule matters if its effect is clearly larger than its error. Otherwise the frozen CHANGED marks are catalogue noise, and the frozen classes were themselves one draw from a spread.
- The class tally shows how far any single frozen class can be trusted, under either rule.
- It yields readings, not frozen grades. SI1's and SI2's committed classes are not edited.
