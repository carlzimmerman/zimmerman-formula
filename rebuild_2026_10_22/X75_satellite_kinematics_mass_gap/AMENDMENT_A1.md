# X75 AMENDMENT A1 (post-result, reported only; the frozen class is unchanged)

Written after the PRIMARY result (common Δlog M200 ≈ +0.78) was read.

**Why.** The frozen sample is centrals, and it includes group centrals. The KiDS lenses in X53/X74 are B21-isolated.
Satellites of group centrals trace group potentials. At fixed M★ those are heavier than an isolated galaxy's halo, so the
frozen grade could overstate Δ for isolated lenses.

**What.** The same script and rules, restricted to hosts whose G3C FoF group has N_fof ≤ 3, or that are ungrouped
(`X75_MAXNFOF=3`). The rich-group hosts are dropped before satellites are assigned.
- This selects on the tracer itself, since a host with more satellites is in a richer group. It is therefore biased
  toward low halo masses and can only lower Δ.
- It is a bound, not a measurement of isolated halos. Reported only.
