Committed alone, before any script, and before any pulsar paper is opened for this lane. PA1 was free (ls, git log).

**Not blind.** Already read: UX5 README, LW1 README, UX5's scripts' outline. Known: AQUAL and QUMOND K(R0) at least 75.9 canonical and 76.2 alt with rotation in line, against 66.4 +- 2.2, line top 70.8; form R 21-23 % lower at median. From memory only: pulsar acceleration papers exist, with larger errors than kinematic K_z. I expect current values cannot decide (80 %).

kappa = 1/2 FITTED. Footings 9.3603e-11, 1.1312e-10 m/s^2, never pooled. Cold sector stays retired; nothing here reinstates it. Web values are PROVISIONAL. No downloads. Nothing here moves a line.

## C1. What counts
Admit a published value only if it is a direct vertical acceleration, slope, or K_z from timing, spectroscopic drift, or eclipse timing, with no Newtonian potential family needed for the value itself. Each row records: source, what is measured, heights, statistical error, stated systematics, and whether any Galactic model or Newtonian step enters. A value that needs Poisson (rho_0, Sigma converted) or a fitted potential is listed but tagged NOT POTENTIAL-FREE and can't decide. Where a paper's number can't be read from a page, the row says so; none is filled from memory without the tag FROM MEMORY, and those can't decide.

## C2. Comparison
Evaluate forms A, Q and R from UX5's solver at the measurement's own heights and quantity, on both footings, over the cells UX5 used. Forms are compared at the same baryons. If that cannot be done at a row's heights, the row says NOT COMPARABLE, rather than scaling from K(1.1 kpc), unless the scaling is labelled and tested against the solver.

## C3. Labels, for each admitted row and footing
sigma_tot = stat and stated systematics in quadrature.
- SEPARATES: |form A - form R|, same baryons, in-prior cells, minimum over cells, is at least 3 sigma_tot.
- EXCLUDES form X: every in-prior cell of X is at least 3 sigma_tot from the value.
- NO POWER: otherwise.
Overall: **CANNOT DECIDE** unless some admitted row SEPARATES on both footings or EXCLUDES a form on both. Also report the kinematic line against the same row, as NO TENSION, or TENSION at 3 sigma_tot.

## C4. Forecast
State sigma needed on K(R0, 1.1 kpc) and on the pulsar quantity for 3 sigma separation, at both the minimum and median gap, per footing, and against line top 70.8. Estimate the pulsar count needed with a stated scaling, labelled as judgment.

## C5. Files
List exact files for reanalysis: filename, URL, size in bytes. Sizes only from a server statement or page; otherwise say NOT VERIFIED. Nothing is downloaded. A HEAD request is not a download.

## C6. Checks
Rerun byte-identical. Recover UX5's K(R0) from its solver to 0.1 for a cell. MUTATE: with nu = 1 the form A - form R gap must change and K must differ from form A; an injected row with error divided by 20 must get SEPARATES, proving the labels can fire. Failures are published under these labels.
