# CX1 Amendment A1, 2026-11-03: pre-result, committed before any real-data collision number

**What had been seen when this was written.** The controls' machinery was smoke-tested on synthetic data only (the smooth null and the planted class, built on the real rows' leaf means and covariances). On real data, only per-class medians of log B and of the propagated errors were printed while checking the Monte Carlo (X-COP log B about +0.47, as the record's RS1 map already states). No real pair, near-pair count or collision was computed. kappa = 1/2 FITTED; not theory closed.

1. **Point-level gap: marginal, not conditional.** The frozen conditional gap m = Delta_B - Sigma_Bl Sigma_ll^-1 Delta_l tests "the two rows are the same true point". A near pair (d_L <= 1) need not be: its true leaves can differ by up to about 1 sigma, and the regression term then multiplies that real leaf difference by the error-regression slope (the Upsilon slope of B on y), which a smooth rule doesn't share. On the smooth null this gave 4,500 to 8,000 strong within-line collisions in 5 seeds of L3 and 1 to 16 strong cross-line ones: the statistic flags smooth data. **Primary gap is the marginal one:** m = Delta log B, v = Var_i(log B) + Var_j(log B), z = |m|/sqrt(v). Its variance includes the Upsilon, distance and inclination terms in full, so it is the more conservative test for a no-go. On the smooth null (5 seeds, all arms, canonical) it gives 0 strong cross-line and 0 to 3 strong within-line collisions. The conditional gap is still computed and reported (`m_other`, `z_other`), not graded. `CX1_GAP=conditional` swaps them for a reader.
2. **Line-level gap: regression intercept.** The frozen line statistic, the weighted mean gap over A-B near pairs, flagged smooth data when one line's rows sit systematically to one side of the other's in the leaf ellipse (gradient times offset). Replaced by the intercept of a weighted (1/v) least-squares fit of the A-minus-B boost gap on the A-minus-B leaf gaps over the near pairs: the offset between the lines at equal leaves, with a local linear rule's gradient fitted freely. Same bootstrap over systems (200, seed 20261104), same 3 sd and tolerance lines, same system minimum. It needs at least 2 x (leaves + 1) near pairs, and at least 21 bootstrap fits succeeding; otherwise its sd is infinite and the pair can't read collision. The plain weighted mean is reported beside it as `raw_mean`.
3. **F2 on the arm's leaves only.** The frozen F2 summed (log y, log ell, f, s) in every arm, so in L3 it depended on s, a variable L3 doesn't carry: LITTLE THINGS (s = -2 stand-in) against SPARC then reads a collision in L3, correctly, but that's not a smooth function of L3's leaves. F2 = 0.1 x the sum of the arm's own leaves.
4. **Invalid draws.** A draw is invalid when g_b <= 0 or g_obs <= 0 (lensing Delta Sigma can be drawn negative); the 10 % drop rule applies to invalid draws, not only g_b <= 0.

Everything else (leaves, error budgets, near-pair line d_L <= 1, z_LEE, tolerances, verdict labels, X-COP variants, the 1-in-20 rule for the nulls, planted deltas, MUTATE) is unchanged.

kappa = 1/2 FITTED; not theory closed.
