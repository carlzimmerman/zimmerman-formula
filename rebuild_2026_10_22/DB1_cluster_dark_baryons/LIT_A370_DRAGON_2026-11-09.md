# DB1 follow-up, 2026-11-09: the microlensing gate now has a number (Abell 370, Dragon arc)

**Result: compact cluster dark baryons from 0.1 to 100 Msun FAIL by 4x to 39x. DB1's PROVISIONAL row (d) now rests on a measured column.** Script `lit_a370_dragon.py`, output `lit_a370_dragon.json` / `.out`.

**Source.** Palencia et al. 2026, A&A, DOI 10.1051/0004-6361/202660640, arXiv:2604.22702v3. Read in full from the arXiv HTML (not a web summary). JWST NIRCam imaging from 2022 to 2024 finds 104 caustic-crossing events in the Dragon arc (z 0.725) behind Abell 370 (z 0.375). With the luminosity-function slope fixed at beta 2.5, the microlens column at the arc is **Sigma_* = 104 +- 21 Msun/pc^2**. The authors say this is twice the intracluster-light value, and that a Salpeter IMF would double the light-based mass. The paper names its microlens mass window as 1e-1 to 1e2 Msun. It quotes no PBH or compact dark matter fraction.

**Gate.** Sigma_crit = 3615 Msun/pc^2 in the paper's cosmology, so the measured microlens column is 2.9 % of critical. The room for non-stellar compact mass, at a 2-sigma upper bound after taking away the ICL stars, is 94 Msun/pc^2 (Chabrier reading) or 42 (Salpeter reading). DB1 needs CBDM at about 50 % of the core mass. Bracketing kappa at 0.4 to 0.9 and the CBDM share at 25 % or 50 %, the needed column is 360 to 1630 Msun/pc^2:

| kappa | CBDM share | needed (Msun/pc^2) | x room, Chabrier | x room, Salpeter |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.4 | 25 % | 362 | **3.9** | 8.6 |
| 0.65 | 50 % (DB1 central) | 1175 | **12.5** | 28.0 |
| 0.9 | 50 % | 1627 | 17.3 | 38.7 |

Even the most lenient row fails by about 4x. That closes CBDM as brown dwarfs, Jupiters, stellar remnants or super-critical clouds inside the 0.1-100 Msun window. A large compact column would also push events away from the critical curve (the "more is less" effect). The paper sees events piling up toward high magnification instead.

**What this does not cover.**
- Sigma_* is measured only at fixed beta = 2.5 and through one lens model (Diego+2025). With beta free, it is not separately constrained. A 4x margin survives a factor-2 shift, but not a factor-4 shift.
- kappa at the arc and the CBDM share there are bracketed, not computed. DB1 has no Abell 370 gas profile.
- Masses below 0.1 Msun, including DB1's 8e-4 Msun lower-edge cloud, lie outside the window the paper names. Oguri+2018 covers them qualitatively only.
- **DB1's one survivor, black holes of 1e2 to 1e7 Msun, sits above the window.** These would act as millilenses. The paper calls the many repeated events from single pixels "tantalising" for millilenses but needs more data. That is the only place this dataset touches the survivor. It is a pointer, not a measurement.
- The parity asymmetry (one hint of wave dark matter) is flagged by the authors as possibly a misplaced critical curve. Nothing here reads it.
