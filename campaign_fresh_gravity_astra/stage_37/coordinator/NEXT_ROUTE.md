# Proposed changed regime: bounded-speed edge strips

This is an unproved follow-up plan, not accepted evidence or an actual run.
Time powers below carry the fixed time unit L/v0: the comparison time is
B*(L/v0)N^-4 and the proposed local time is k_edge*(L/v0)N^-7/2.
It follows the checked initial relative-speed and acoustic intersections.
The global speed-separation shortcut fails, but this need not prevent local
regularity in the slow parts of the exact packet.

At either support edge x_e=x*+/-ell, take nested intervals with widths
constant times ell/sqrt(N)=constant times L N^-7/2. Constants can depend on
the fixed background and speeds, never retuned physical coefficients.
The exact polynomial vanishes quadratically at the edge. On such an interval,
v initially stays O(1) and |v_x| is O(N^7/2), with Lip(v_x)=O(N^7).
Choose fixed width constants to cover both initial v=b0 and v=b0+c roots,
with a larger outer buffer, and prove these statements from the exact profile.

A local blockwise characteristic construction might then supply an actual
uniform local time k_edge N^-7/2. If proved, it exceeds the requested fixed
B* N^-4 for all sufficiently large N, and a bounded local signal speed moves
O(N^-4), less than the O(N^-7/2) buffer. This changes the spatial and gradient
regime from FGF048's whole-packet N^4 gradient, instead of replacing its kappa.
It must retain field coupling, pressure, C1,1 joins, complete compatibility,
endpoint regularity and actual domain dependence. Matching to any global
classical branch requires local uniqueness on the overlapping causal domain.

No step here is yet a proof. A speed bound cannot simply be imposed by deleting
high-speed incoming data. Prove exterior independence using the actual nested
cone bootstrap. Derive density/field gradients and the integrated adverse
budget on the resulting INNER strips, retaining both field characteristics.
Prove which initial resonances are included; do not infer that all later
resonances stay there or that a local bound controls the supremum on the full
FGF049 cone. The high-speed core and any newly created interfaces remain
separate gaps. Failure of this construction is not a compression counterexample.
