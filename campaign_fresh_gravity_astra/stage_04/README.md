## Scale sector: conservation comes with backreaction

[SD1](scale_dynamics/DERIVATION.md) constructs an explicitly assumed positive
scale a=a_ref exp(chi). The scale field cancels the gravitational energy and
momentum exchange. With T=-aW_a=gb-2W and T_g=gb_g-b>0, however, an exactly
uniform static chi cannot satisfy its equation at unequal field magnitudes
without a compensating source. The environmental-scale construction changes
the literal universal constant-vacuum reading of the core relation.

A chosen cosh potential gives a positive mixed-field Schur quantity on a
source-free constant-gradient equilibrium. The
[independent audit](../../deepseek_push/astra_spawn_ideas/fresh_gravity_followups/results/FGF-010/fgf010_audit_001/DERIVATION.md)
checks that exact algebra. It also distinguishes functional equality between
scale and potential-derived vacuum density from equality at one selected
stationary state: the latter can be arranged by an additive constant and is a
weaker claim. No covariant scale sector or H(z) history has been derived.

## Matter sector: the background and walls determine the stability question

[MS1](matter_stability/DERIVATION.md) derives growing fluid-gravity modes in
an explicitly supported, mean-subtracted homogeneous model. K changes growth
rates but not its threshold. That background is not an isolated uniform
matter solution of the original equations.

For a genuine positive hydrostatic isothermal slab, the identity
kJ² Lrho Lg=1 rules out applying that growing-band calculation as a slowly
varying local approximation. The independent audit goes further. Writing
C=4piG and A=b'(g), it proves the complete finite-slab quadratic form is

    2V=integral[cs² rho xi'²+(A/C)(psi'+C rho xi/A)²]dx
       +[cs²rho'xi²-2rho xi psi]_left^right.

With positive smooth coefficients and xi=psi=0 at both walls, the boundary
term vanishes and the energy is strictly positive for every nonzero
longitudinal perturbation. This settles linear stability in that externally
supported one-dimensional boundary class. It does not settle free boundaries,
three-dimensional modes, nonlinear evolution or a metric theory.
See the [scoped reconciliation](../../deepseek_push/astra_spawn_ideas/fresh_gravity_followups/reviews/FGF-011_RECONCILIATION.md).
FGF-015 now tests the conforming numerical spectrum against this theorem.
