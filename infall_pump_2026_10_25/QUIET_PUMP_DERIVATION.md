# Separate infall pumping from an initial internal quench

Simply dilating a formed T/F profile conserves total and species mass, but does
not preserve stationarity of its internal composition. Minimizing only alpha
does not fix this: the two material components can start with different local
chemical potentials, creating conversion oscillations with no inward transport.
The conservative no-transport control demonstrates that ambiguity directly.

Prepare initial data at the same displaced TOTAL cell masses m_i. Write

 a_T=sqrt(m_i) sin(theta_i), a_F=sqrt(m_i) cos(theta_i),

with real amplitudes, chi=pi=p_alpha=0. Solve the ORIGINAL energy stationarity
in theta and alpha only, retaining both positive material gradients, the
carrier gradient, response, F pressure, gravity and alignment. The boundary
and T mass are not fixed. After this initial preparation release every field.
The energy decrease during preparation is NOT radiation or physical cooling.

With h=delta H/delta a*, theta stationarity says

 h_T cos(theta)-h_F sin(theta)=0,
 hence h_s(r)=mu(r) a_s(r) for both species.

The common mu need not be spatially constant: its gradient drives actual
infall. The relative composition and carrier have no initial relaxation kick.
In particular the conversion rate Gamma and its FIRST derivative vanish at
t=0 (up to the explicitly audited preparation residual), while the compression
source derivative generally does not.

At this initially real state, with all canonical momenta except the material
common phase stationary, H_dot(0)=0. Thus

 a_dot=-i mu a/sigma,
 a_ddot=-H_0(mu a)/sigma^2
        =-mu^2 a/sigma^2-[T_kin,mu]a/sigma^2.

The commutator is an additive transport forcing. In continuum notation,

 rho_ddot=div(rho grad(mu)),
 q_ddot=grad(q) dot grad(mu)

at the initial instant, before conversion enters at higher order. Spatial
transport changes density and local preferred composition; finite carrier
response and relative material phases must follow dynamically. Their conversion
current and the density-weighted compression current then source the scalar
with the reciprocal forces already in the action. This is different from
assuming that a homogeneous parametric oscillator populates itself from zero.

A sharp causal control adds ONLY a time-independent common potential
U_support(r)=-mu(r) to both species. This preserves all gradients and internal
composition, and makes the same initial state stationary to preparation/root
tolerance. No density constraint is used in either time evolution. The actual
infall run has NO support potential. A signal in actual infall but absent from
this control is generated by the released common material force, rather than
by the initial internal mismatch.

The prepared branch has exactly the same total-density law deficit and filled
profile. Thus this construction does not encode a corrected target density,
impose a finite radius, or supply a parent-mode population. It tests infall
of an already mixed state; exact all-F classical birth remains a separate
invariant-subspace/physical-fluctuation question.

Results must still distinguish near-field storage, transient radiation and a
sustained fast outlet. Initial quiet2Myr tests show substantial field work but
about100,000x less outward500kpc energy than the internally mismatched initial
state. Longer continuation and outgoing-characteristic analysis are required.
