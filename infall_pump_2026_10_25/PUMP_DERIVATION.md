# What an unseeded classical pump actually requires

Use the existing canonical CC Hamiltonian, not a prescribed occupation.
At rest the material amplitudes a_T,a_F are real, chi=pi=p_alpha=0.
The actual Hamiltonian material derivative is a_dot=-i H_a/sigma, including
response, gravity, F pressure, covariant gradient and alignment. Initially
rho_dot=0, alpha_dot=0 and chi_dot=0. Therefore S(0)=0, but generally

 S_dot(0)=g2 B[D_dot]+(g1+2g2 rho_T) Gamma_dot,
 D_dot_ij=kappa w_ij Im(conj(psi_dot_i) psi_j
                                  +conj(psi_i) psi_dot_j),
 Gamma_dot=(2J/sigma) Im(conj(a_dot_T) L_T(a)
                                  +conj(a_T) L_T(a_dot)),

where B[D] is exactly the existing discrete divergence in transfer(), and L
is the existing linear alignment operator at the initial alpha. This produces
chi_ttt(0)=-deposit(S_dot)/wave_volume without any supplied scalar or modal
population. It is deterministic acceleration of inhomogeneous material.
It is not proof of fast-mode occupation, a sustained cascade, or positive
net far export. A newly initialized nonequilibrium alignment can contribute
the conversion term even without ongoing infall; resting controls matter.

Reciprocal force is indispensable. With S the same source as in the full
material equation,

 dE_wave/dt + F_out = - integral chi_dot S,
 dE_material/dt = + integral chi_dot S

before exterior absorption. Finite carrier inertia includes its density force.
The quartic shift charge is integral pi+g1 M_T+g2 integral rho_T^2.
Neither wave-energy growth nor a spectrum alone measures cooling: near-field
storage can return energy to matter. Track signed work and actual flux.

For a retained oscillator, the homogeneous squeezing equation and cubic
three-wave terms preserve exactly zero b in the absence of an additive
inhomogeneous forcing. An autonomous infall pump requires the projection of
the full acceleration above, or a real instability with specified initial
perturbations. A time-varying frequency is not an additive classical source.

Likewise, exactly a_T=alpha=p_alpha=0 is an invariant all-F subspace of this
classical action: alignment cross terms, T source and carrier force vanish.
It can be linearly unstable yet remains there for exact zero perturbation.
This forbids claiming literal unseeded conversion birth from those exact data;
it does not forbid wave generation by an already mixed, falling profile.

The simultaneous sparse solve exposes the same enclosed-mass and reverse
potential recurrences as auxiliary constraints. It retains every density and
carrier variable and the free outer material mesh; no radial profile or
boundary is fixed. A new solver is valid only after old-root, directional
Jacobian, source/work, energy and charge checks. Numerical failure is not a
no-go for infall pumping.
