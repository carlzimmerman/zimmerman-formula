**Carl P. Zimmerman · Briar Creek Tech · 2026**

A dual-register textbook: the **main thread** of every chapter is written for a motivated high-schooler (calm, no equations, every term defined); clearly-boxed **Deeper Dive** sections carry the full rigor for a physicist. Read [the combined manuscript](A_BEAUTIFULLY_GEOMETRIC_UNIVERSE.md) or the per-chapter files below.

> **Honest framing (the book's own):** the galactic acceleration scale really does seem to be made of dark energy, and the theory costs *provably one* free number — but it does not derive that number's value, cannot yet do gravitational lensing honestly, leaves the Standard Model untouched, and is not confirmed against the mainstream picture. *Not a theory of everything yet, as frustrating as it may be.*
