# INTEGRITY BOARD — research-conduct rules (these have repeatedly caught real errors)
The institute's credibility rests on these. They are binding on every division and every audit.

1. **VERIFY A KILL AS HARD AS A WIN.** A "fails/deficit/dead" claim gets the same rigor as a "works".
   (Caught: the reviewer's n=3/2 slip → actually n=2 vs 3/2; my own PSD/NSD sign error in slip-lock.)
2. **HOLD, THEN AUDIT.** A promising positive result is HELD (never announced) until an independent
   hostile pass runs. (Caught: the tandem-μ "MOND inherited" overclaim — killed before it shipped.)
3. **DERIVE, DON'T ASSERT.** Every PASS must be backed by a printed residual/bound computed in-script.
   Asserting a limit you did not derive is a manufactured result. (The tandem retraction, logged.)
4. **NO FAILURE TRADING.** A ghost is not compensated by better phenomenology; each gate stands alone.
5. **LEARN FROM FINAL ADJUDICATION ONLY.** Intermediate "conditionally closed" labels are not knowledge
   (the MMG lesson: conditionally-closed then overturned).
6. **THEOREM SCOPE IS SACRED.** A gate applies only within its derivation's assumptions (slip-lock was
   Riemannian+propagating; it must NOT fire on teleparallel/nonmetricity/constrained sectors).
7. **THE VERIFIER IS GROUND TRUTH, NOT THE LLM.** Qwen proposes; deterministic gates judge; the model
   never grades its own theory. Survivors are PENDING_AUDIT until a human/Claude re-derives the gate.
8. **NULL IS A RESULT.** "NO VIABLE CANDIDATE after the trials correction" is a valid, publishable
   outcome (the atomos lesson). The institute is not rewarded for producing survivors.
9. **PROVENANCE.** Every load-bearing claim ties to a committed, runnable script. IDs are hash-bound;
   ledger integrity is swept at startup.
