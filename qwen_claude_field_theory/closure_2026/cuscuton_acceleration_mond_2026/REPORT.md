# Constrained cuscuton/acceleration MOND branch

## Candidate action

The new constructive door is the covariant single-metric action

\[
 S_{\rm CAM}=\int d^4x\sqrt{-g}\left[
 {M^2\over2}(R-2\Lambda)
 +2M^2a_0^2 Q\!\left({\sqrt{D_\mu uD^\mu u}\over a_0}\right)
 +\sqrt{X_\tau}\,\ell^\mu(D_\mu u-a_\mu)\right]+S_m[g,\psi],
\]

The factor \(\sqrt{X_\tau}\) is essential: in unitary gauge it converts
\(\sqrt{-g}\) to the spatial measure \(\sqrt h\), so the relation multiplier
does not introduce the spurious lapse-prefactor term found in rotated MMG.

where

\[
 n_\mu=-{\nabla_\mu\tau\over\sqrt{-g^{\alpha\beta}
\nabla_\alpha\tau\nabla_\beta\tau}},\quad
 h_{\mu\nu}=g_{\mu\nu}+n_\mu n_\nu,\quad
 D_\mu=h_\mu{}^\nu\nabla_\nu,\quad
 a_\mu=n^\nu\nabla_\nu n_\mu ,
\]

\(\ell^\mu n_\mu=0\), and

\[
 Q(y)=y^2+2(1+y)e^{-y}-2,\qquad
 {Q'(y)\over2y}=1-e^{-y}.
\]

The projected derivative \(D_\mu u\) contains no unitary-gauge time
derivative.  The relation multiplier makes \(D_i u=a_i=D_i\log N\) on a
static branch.  The residual \(u\mapsto u+f(\tau)\) is the homogeneous
zero-mode gauge freedom.

## Action-derived static equations

The companion SymPy calculation varies the reduced static density

\[
 {\cal L}_{\rm stat}=M^2(\Phi'-\Psi')^2
 +2M^2a_0^2Q(u'/a_0)+\ell(u'-\Phi')+\rho\Phi
\]

independently in \(\Phi,\Psi,u,\ell\), before making any branch
substitution.  It obtains

\[
 E_\Psi=2M^2(\Phi''-\Psi''),\qquad E_\ell=u'-\Phi',
\]

\[
 E_\Phi=-2M^2(\Phi''-\Psi'')+\rho+\ell',
\quad
 E_u=-\partial_x\!\left[4M^2(1-e^{-u'/a_0})u'+\ell\right].
\]

Therefore regular boundary data give \(\Phi=\Psi\), \(u'=\Phi'\), and the
sum of the independently varied \(\Phi\) and \(u\) equations is exactly

\[
 \partial_x\!\left[(1-e^{-\Phi'/a_0})\Phi'\right]
 ={\,\rho\over4M^2}.
\]

Thus the exponential interpolation is obtained from one displayed action,
not assigned after the fact.  In this reduced normalization the measured
high-field Newton coupling is \(G_{\rm eff}=1/(16\pi M^2)\); matching it to
the full \(3+1\) Einstein normalization is an explicit remaining
calibration, not an assumption.

Before setting \(N=1\), the unitary-gauge auxiliary density is
\[
 N\,2M^2a_0^2Q(u'/a_0)+\ell(u'-N'/N).
\]
Its independently varied lapse equation is
\[
 2M^2a_0^2Q(u'/a_0)+N^{-1}\ell'=0
\]
up to the Einstein and matter terms.  The \(u\) equation is the corresponding
lapse-weighted elliptic flux.  Both identities are generated by the gate.

## Auxiliary Dirac check

For a finite Fourier mode the generated auxiliary Hamiltonian contains the
spatial stiffness of \(u\), the relation multiplier \(\ell\), and the lapse
potential \(\Phi\).  The script constructs the primary constraints
\(p_u,p_\ell,p_\Phi\), generates secondary constraints by preservation, and
forms the actual Poisson matrix.  At a nonzero-mode witness it returns six
independent second-class constraints, PB rank six, and

\[
 (6-0-6)/2=0
\]

physical auxiliary configuration degrees of freedom.  The \(k=0\) matrix is
computed separately; its three primary constraints are first class and the
remaining \(u\) constant is the stated \(u\mapsto u+f(\tau)\) gauge/boundary
mode, not a propagating wave.

## FLRW and scope

On flat homogeneous FLRW with \(D_i u=a_i=0\), \(Q(0)=0\), the auxiliary
stress vanishes on \(u=\)constant and the Friedmann branch is

\[
 H^2={\rho+M^2\Lambda\over3M^2}>0.
\]

The current gate proves the exact static constitutive law, no-slip equation,
finite-\(k\) auxiliary zero-DOF result, separated \(k=0\) treatment, and an
expanding background.  It does **not** yet prove the full covariant
\(\tau\)-clock Dirac algebra, PPN \(\beta,\gamma,\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3\),
nonlinear FLRW perturbation stability, or the full \(3+1\) tensor/vector
constraint closure.  The branch status is therefore **OPEN**.
