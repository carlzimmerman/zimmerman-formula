## KGB background normalization

Deffayet, Pujolas, Sawicki and Vikman, *Imperfect Dark Energy from Kinetic Gravity
Braiding*, arXiv:1008.0048v2, 2010-09-24, JCAP 1010:026.
[Primary text](https://arxiv.org/html/1008.0048v2),
[PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1008.0048v2).
PDF SHA-256: `4d6e257206f28e107aa87137da061147035602d58e9c8d9affcddb0784d71cee`.

PDF equations (3.2)-(3.5) give the homogeneous current, energy and pressure;
(3.13) gives the kinetic coefficient. Its +--- signature and +G box term map
to this project's -+++ and -G box term, with the same positive homogeneous
kinetic invariant. Source K becomes EF P and its X becomes chi. The source's
unit reduced Planck normalization is restored as m here. Our homogeneous
identities are independently derived by variation. The paper's minimally
coupled external matter is not a license to omit the present transformed
matter contributions. Classification: known KGB identities after convention
translation; no imported matter-containing stability theorem.

## AeST and the L121 inference

Skordis and Zlosnik, *A new relativistic theory for Modified Newtonian Dynamics*,
arXiv:2007.00082v3, 2021-10-14, Phys. Rev. Lett. 127, 161302.
[Primary text](https://arxiv.org/html/2007.00082v3),
[PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2007.00082v3).
PDF SHA-256: `e2057c92c44fa10d36d9c77f532d610a815591bed2a329c2a39635d7dab34d66`.

Equations (7)-(12) and the discussion surrounding Figures 1-2 use perturbed
scalar/vector/metric equations. The text specifies numerical evolution of the
FLRW background and linear equations with a Boltzmann code, and states that a0
does not enter that model's linear cosmological regime. Its successful spectra
are therefore not evidence for L121's horizon-ratio test. Nor can its extra
vector and different functions be identified with this scalar-only action.
Classification: adjacent model, not a solution or stability certificate for the
present action. The source's physical fit is not independently rerun here.
