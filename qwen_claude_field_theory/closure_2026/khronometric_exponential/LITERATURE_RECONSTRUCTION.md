# KM-X1 — Literature chassis (verified against primary sources, not memory)

## The chassis: Bonetti & Barausse 2015 (arXiv:1502.05554)
"Post-Newtonian constraints on Lorentz-violating gravity theories with a MOND phenomenology."
**Verified abstract claim (WebFetch 2026-08-29):** a class reducing to *khronometric* theory (IR limit
of Hořava) at high acceleration and to MOND at low acceleration; MOND arises from action terms in the
**acceleration a_μ = u^ν∇_ν u_μ** of the congruence orthogonal to the khronon foliation. Their two
findings, quoted:
- "**if the theory is forced to reduce exactly to General Relativity ... in the high-acceleration
  regime, the post-Newtonian expansion breaks down at low accelerations, and the theory becomes
  strongly coupled.**"  → this is the P7 wound; letting high-a → *finite khronometric* (not GR) is the escape.
- "**we identify a sizeable region of the parameter space where the post-Newtonian expansion remains
  perturbative for all accelerations, and the theory passes both solar-system and pulsar gravity tests.**"

So the finite-α_∞ reading is ACCURATE — the finite-coupling escape is real *in their 2015 analysis*.

## The chassis couplings (khronometric, arXiv:1711.08845 Eq. 12)
Three couplings (α, β, λ): α on a_μa^μ, β the twist (sets c_T), λ on (∇·u)². PPN:
- α₁ = 4(α−2β)/(1−β)
- α₂ = [(α−2β)/(2−α)]·[1 − (α−2β)(1+β+2λ)/((1−β)(β+λ))]
- c_T² = 1/(1−β)     (spin-2 speed)
- c_s² = (2−α)(λ+β)/[α(1−β)(2+3λ+β)]   (khronon sound speed)
Scalar kinetic normalization K_s ∝ α; strong-coupling scale M_sc ∝ √α · (background) · c_s^{±3/2}.

"sizeable viable region" was mapped. The entire KM-X1 question is whether that region survives β=0.
