# Rotated-MMG lapse variation: exact action-level obstruction

This is a scoped check of the displayed first-order rotated-MMG action.  It
does not assert a no-go for every relativistic MOND action.

## Derivation

On a flat fixed spatial metric and the positive-gradient branch, set

\[
u=\log N,\qquad Y=\beta u',\qquad \beta=\frac{c^2}{2a_0}>0,
\qquad \mu(Y)=1-e^{-Y}.
\]

The advertised constraint is

\[
Q=\partial_x\!\left[\mu(Y)u'\right].
\]

The displayed action contains \(-NQ\), while \(Q\) itself depends on \(N\)
through \(u=\log N\).  Varying \(u\mapsto u+\varepsilon\) gives

\[
\delta Q=\partial_x\!\left[A(Y)\varepsilon'\right],
\qquad A(Y)=\mu(Y)+Y\mu'(Y).
\]

After one integration by parts, the lapse Euler equation is therefore

\[
Q+N^{-1}\partial_x\!\left[N,u' A(Y)\right]=0
\]

up to the common overall sign and the matter contribution.  In three
dimensions the second term is

\[
N^{-1}D_j\!\left[N(D_i u)A^{ij}\right],\qquad
A^{ij}=\mu h^{ij}+\frac{\mu_Y}{Y}D^iuD^ju.
\]

## Exact affine witness

Take \(u=kx\), \(N=e^{kx}\), \(k>0\).  Then \(Q=0\), but the additional term
is

\[
 k^2\,[\mu(Y)+Y\mu_Y(Y)]
 =k^2\left[1+(Y-1)e^{-Y}\right]
 =k^2e^{-Y}(e^Y+Y-1)>0,
\qquad Y=\beta k.
\]

Thus the affine branch that solves the advertised vacuum MOND constraint does
not solve the lapse equation of the displayed action.  The high-field limit
of the extra term divided by \(k^2\) tends to one, so this is not confined to
the deep-MOND regime.

The derivation, boundary-term identity, exact factorization and positivity
are generated by `rmmg_lapse_variation_gate.py`; no expected residual is
inserted into the computation.  The only immediate escape is to declare
\(u\) independent of \(N\), in which case the defining relation must be
added as a new constraint and its complete Dirac algebra recomputed.
