# Rotated-MMG lapse variation: the multiplier is not a multiplier

2026-09-09. This is a focused action-level audit of the rotated-MMG proposal
recorded in `../rotated_mmg_constitutive_2026/RMMG_ACTION.md`. It is a
conditional obstruction to that displayed action, not a universal no-go for
relativistic MOND.

## Literal action and independent variation

Hold a flat spatial metric fixed locally and take the positive-gradient
branch. Write

\[
 u=\log N,\qquad Y=\beta u',\qquad
 \beta=\frac{c^2}{2a_0}>0,
 \qquad \mu(Y)=1-e^{-Y}.
\]

The candidate writes the lapse-weighted constraint term as

\[
 I_\perp=-\int dx\,N Q[u],\qquad
 Q[u]=\partial_x\!\left[\mu(Y)u'\right].
\]

Because the same lapse defines \(u\), a lapse variation \(\delta N=N\epsilon\)
also gives \(\delta u=\epsilon\). The constitutive linearization obtained
directly from the chain rule is

\[
 \delta Q=\partial_x\!\left[A(Y)\epsilon'\right],\qquad
 A(Y)=\mu(Y)+Y\mu'(Y)
      =1+(Y-1)e^{-Y}.
\]

Integrating the first variation by parts (with the boundary variation
clamped, or on a closed one-dimensional leaf) gives

\[
 \delta I_\perp=-\int dx\,N\,E_N\,\epsilon,
 \qquad
 \boxed{E_N=Q+N^{-1}\partial_x\!\left[N u'A(Y)\right]}.
 \tag{1}
\]

The advertised lapse constraint is \(Q=0\), whereas the Euler equation of
the literal action is \(E_N=0\) (plus the matter contribution). The second
term in (1) is absent only if it is separately shown to vanish; it is not a
boundary term in the lapse equation.

The script independently checks the variation identity before specializing
to a background, including the exact total derivative generated by the
integration by parts. It also derives the full three-dimensional tensor form

\[
 E_N= D_i(\mu D^iu)
 +N^{-1}D_j\!\left[N(D_i u)A^{ij}\right],
 \quad
 A^{ij}=\mu h^{ij}+\frac{\mu_Y}{Y}D^iuD^ju,
\]

whose one-dimensional reduction is (1).

## Affine vacuum witness

Take a local affine branch

\[
 u=kx,\qquad N=e^{kx},\qquad k>0,
\]

so \(Y=\beta k>0\) is constant. Then the constraint written in the
candidate vanishes in vacuum,

\[
 Q=0,
\]

but the extra term generated by the actual lapse variation is

\[
 \boxed{E_N-Q=k^2\left[1+(Y-1)e^{-Y}\right]
       =k^2e^{-Y}(e^Y+Y-1)>0.}
 \tag{2}
\]

The final inequality follows because \(Y>0\) implies \(e^Y-1>0\). It also
shows the mismatch persists in the Newtonian/high-field limit, where the
extra term approaches \(k^2\), rather than being a deep-MOND artifact.
The affine profile is a local witness; it need not be globally identified
with a compact periodic scalar. A compactly supported patch can be chosen
with the same constant-gradient neighborhood.

## Interpretation and possible repair

There are only two immediate interpretations of the displayed candidate:

1. Treat \(u\) as the ADM combination of \(N\) and \(h\). Then the extra
   term (1) is compulsory, and the claimed MOND lapse constraint is not the
   Euler equation of the action.
2. Treat \(u\) as independent of \(N\). Then \(N\) can multiply \(Q\),
   but the defining relation \(u=\log N-\frac16\log h\) has been removed.
   Restoring it requires new relation constraints and a fresh nonlinear
   Dirac/HDA analysis; the existing finite scalar bracket witness does not
   apply automatically.

Thus the current rotated-MMG static/Dirac gates do not certify one action
giving both the geometric definition of \(u\) and the advertised MOND
constraint. This does not rule out a repaired action, but any repair must
vary the repaired action and rerun the constraint, Ward, PPN, FLRW and
stability gates.

## Reproduction

From the repository root:

```sh
python3 -B qwen_claude_field_theory/closure_2026/g03_covariant_action_2026/rmmg_lapse_variation_gate.py
python3 -B qwen_claude_field_theory/closure_2026/g03_covariant_action_2026/rmmg_lapse_variation_gate.py --require-advertised-lapse-constraint
python3 -B -m unittest discover -s qwen_claude_field_theory/closure_2026/g03_covariant_action_2026 -p 'test_rmmg_lapse_variation_gate.py' -v
```

The ordinary gate is an algebraic diagnostic and exits 0 when its derived
identities pass. The strict advertised-constraint mode exits 2 because the
literal action fails that requirement. No rank, sign or DOF count is supplied
as an input; all expressions in (1)--(2) are generated by SymPy.

The result is scoped to the displayed rotated-MMG lapse term on a fixed-
spatial-metric positive-gradient branch. It is not a claim about the separate
C-H covariant clock/heat action, arbitrary alternate actions, or universal
relativistic MOND impossibility.
