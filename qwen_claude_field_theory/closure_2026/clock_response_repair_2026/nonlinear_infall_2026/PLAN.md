# Nonlinear baryon/clock initial-value experiment

Approved scope: keep the existing reconstructed covariant action fixed; add a
conserved, minimally coupled baryon dust source; solve the constraints before
interpreting its force. No prescribed MOND force or spatially varying a0.

1. Independently derive the unrestricted clock Euler equation and its initial
   preservation, including the gradient generated by a nonuniform lapse.
2. Differentiate the existing reconstructed functions, not constraints, to
   obtain the background coefficient jets. Test against independent background
   evolution.
3. Test first, then solve the regular spherical Hamiltonian and lapse boundary
   problem for a localized Gaussian source. Compare two numerical methods,
   resolution, outer boundaries, zero-source and gamma=0 controls.
4. Compute action-derived initial scalar/metric/dust time derivatives and
   invariant areal acceleration. Do not identify the lapse with a Bardeen
   potential or label the normal-rest expanding slice as a stationary galaxy.
5. Attack small-source scaling analytically and computationally. Formalize only
   a precisely scoped algebraic implication in Lean, if feasible.
6. Review the new work, run existing action/matter/background tests, and commit
   and push only this directory. Record commands and exit codes.

Full nonlinear time evolution, weak-field potential reconstruction, and lensing
are still required after a successful initial-slice gate. No initial-slice result
or proof-assistant certificate will be called full theory closure.
