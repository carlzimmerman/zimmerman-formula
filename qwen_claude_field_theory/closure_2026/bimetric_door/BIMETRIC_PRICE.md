# Pricing the bimetric (Hassan-Rosen bigravity) door
**Status: OPEN-PRICED-PESSIMISTIC.** The first door that genuinely EXITS the now-closed single-metric
class — and the first to pass GW170817 — but carrying a structural anti-MOND problem. `price_bimetric.py`.

## Why it escapes what killed everything else
- Two dynamical metrics g (matter), f; HR interaction ⇒ 2 (massless) + 5 (massive graviton) = 7 DOF,
  ghost-free by construction (no Boulware-Deser). Lorentz-invariant — **NO preferred frame.**
- The g↔f mass term sources g's anisotropic sector through the f tensor structure, NOT locked to the
  δR direction (1,−2) that closed frame-free single-metric (DC-013). ⇒ **genuinely exits the slip-lock.**
- The massless graviton rides g's light cone ⇒ **c_T = 1, passes GW170817** (unlike the frame theories).

## The price
1. **7 DOF** — heavy field content.
2. **Higuchi tension.** MOND as a graviton mass gives m_g ~ a₀/c ~ H₀. The Higuchi bound (massive spin-2
   on dS) is m_g² ≥ 2H². With m_g ~ H₀ ~ H, the MOND mass sits ON the bound ⇒ the helicity-0 mode is a
   near-ghost on cosmological backgrounds.
3. **THE DECISIVE FORK (ghost-free XOR MOND).** A *linear* massive graviton gives a **Yukawa** force:
   vDVZ 4/3 enhancement at short range, exponential CUTOFF at long range (fixed Compton length 1/m_g).
   That is the WRONG scale-dependence for MOND — MOND enhances at LOW acceleration (large r, flat
   curves), Yukawa cuts off there. So HR-ghost-free ⇒ anti-MOND, no flat curves / BTFR. Forcing the a₀
   acceleration scale needs the nonlinear helicity-0 (Vainshtein) sector deformed off the HR ghost-free
   point — but Vainshtein SCREENS at short range (opposite of low-acceleration enhancement), and
   deforming off HR risks the Boulware-Deser ghost. Milgrom's BIMOND gives the MOND phenomenology but
   is NOT the HR ghost-free construction; its ghost-freedom is not established.

## Decisive open calc
Can a ghost-free HR-class potential reproduce μ(y)=1−e^{−y} (acceleration scale, flat rotation curves,
BTFR) in its nonlinear helicity-0 sector WITHOUT reintroducing the Boulware-Deser ghost or violating
Higuchi? Leans **pessimistic** — the Yukawa wrong-sign scale-dependence is structural, not a coefficient
choice — but it is NOT a clean no-go: the nonlinear helicity-0 sector is a genuine unknown.
