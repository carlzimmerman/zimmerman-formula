## The 13 requirements (all from ONE theory)

1. **Exact MOND phenomenology with the preferred interpolation** μ(y) = 1 − exp(−y), y = g/a₀.
   Weak-field quasistatic equation must reduce to ∇·[(1 − e^{−|∇Φ|/a₀})∇Φ] = 4πGρ_b. Spherical
   symmetry: μ(g/a₀)·g = g_N. Deep MOND: g² = a₀g_N ⇒ v⁴ = G a₀ M_b.
2. **Exactly two propagating gravitational DOF, N_grav = 2** — only the two tensor polarizations.
   No hidden scalar graviton, no propagating auxiliary disguised as a constraint, no ghost. A genuine
   matter or clock scalar is allowed ONLY if explicitly counted separately and shown healthy.
3. **Correct relativistic lensing / no slip.** In ds² = −(1+2Φ/c²)c²dt² + (1−2Ψ/c²)δ_ij dxⁱdxʲ require
   Φ = Ψ on the galactic branch ⇒ γ_PPN = 1 within bounds. **Φ and Ψ must BOTH be derived
   independently from the field equations — never derive one and assign the other.**
4. **Full acceptable PPN, DERIVED not assumed:** β≈1, γ≈1, α₁≈0, α₂≈0, α₃≈0 within current limits.
   Never hard-code these into a certification script.
5. **Ordinary matter conservation** ∇_μT^{μν} = 0 for minimally-coupled ordinary matter as a genuine
   consequence of the action / Noether–Ward identity. Newtonian continuity alone is insufficient.
   "Matter + auxiliary effective stress" conservation is insufficient if baryons feel an extra
   nonmetric force.
6. **Correct GW sector:** c_T = c, positive tensor kinetic energy, two standard polarizations,
   GW170817-type constraints satisfied.
7. **Stability:** derive the scalar/vector/tensor perturbation structure. No ghosts, no gradient
   instabilities, no pathological strong coupling, no hidden scalar pole, **no unacceptable
   superluminal or instantaneous physical channel.**
8. **Viable cosmology:** admits expanding FLRW, H(t) ≠ 0. Do NOT solve the local scalar problem by
   forcing K=0, p_q=0, or H=0. Treat homogeneous k=0 modes separately from local k≠0 constraints.
9. **Controlled zero-field limit:** μ(0)=0 makes y=0 possibly degenerate. Must have a mathematically
   controlled y→0 treatment; must not ignore loss of ellipticity or constraint rank.
10. **Correct Newtonian/GR recovery:** at high acceleration μ→1; recover Newton + GR to Solar-System
    precision. **Derive the measured Newton constant rather than assuming it equals the bare coupling.**
11. **Prefer one physical metric** for matter and photons (minimal coupling to the same g_μν). Avoid
    disformal dark-matter-emulator constructions that produce a photon/graviton speed mismatch.
12. **Preserve the exponential constitutive law if possible.** Useful exact primitive:
    G(y) = y² + 2(1+y)e^{−y} − 2, with G′(y)/(2y) = 1 − e^{−y}. Operator eigenvalues
    λ_⊥ = 1 − e^{−y}, λ_∥ = 1 + (y−1)e^{−y}, both positive for y>0. Prior work: the exponential
    kernel is NOT the main obstruction — failures have been architectural.
13. **Cosmological acceleration-scale relation** — preserve or derive if mathematically possible:
    a₀ = (c/2)√(Gρ_Λ) = c²√(Λ/32π) ≈ 9.4×10⁻¹¹ m/s². Broader falsifiable possibility:
    a₀(z) ∝ √ρ_DE(z). **DO NOT fake a derivation.** Constant-a₀-only is acceptable. If the a₀–Λ
    relation stays phenomenological input, say so explicitly.
