## Clock-current / nonlocal-MOND route

The live clock-current proposal derives an exponential AQUAL equation for a
dark mediator potential. Ordinary baryons remain minimally coupled to the
metric, whose leading source is \(\rho_b+n\). If the proposed ratio lock sets
\(n=\kappa\rho_b\), then under \(\rho_b\mapsto s\rho_b\),

\[
\rho_b+n\mapsto s(\rho_b+n).
\]

The physical metric acceleration therefore retains linear source scaling. In
deep MOND it must instead scale as \(\sqrt{s}\). The generated residual
\(s-\sqrt{s}\) is nonzero for generic \(s\). Thus putting the exact kernel in a
dark vector equation does not make minimally coupled baryons obey the MOND
metric equation.

The live localization diagnostics additionally report two transverse vector
modes for a healthy gauge localization and a homogeneous Gauss-law obstruction
for net clock charge. Those observations reinforce the result but are not
needed for the tracked scaling proof.

**Strict verdict: DEAD.**
