# Assumptions / Dependency Ledger

## A. Phenomenological target

\[
\mu(y)=1-e^{-y},
\qquad y=g/a_0.
\]

Therefore \(\mu(0)=0\) and \(\mu(y)\to1\) for \(y\to\infty\).

## B. Standard GEA reconstruction

Use the standard single-function GEA weak-field relation

\[
\mu=1-\frac{c_4-c_1}{2}F_{\mathcal K}.
\]

This is the critical model-specific input. If a different convention or a different GEA action is used, derive the relation again rather than importing this equation unchanged.

## C. Finite constitutive slope at the origin

Assume

\[
0<|F_{\mathcal K}(0)|<\infty.
\]

Then define

\[
\bar c_i=F_{\mathcal K}(0)c_i.
\]

If \(F_{\mathcal K}(0)=0\), divergent, or undefined, this proof does not apply.

## D. GW tensor-speed condition

In standard Einstein–æther normalization,

\[
c_T^2=\frac{1}{1-c_{13}},
\qquad c_{13}=c_1+c_3.
\]

Near-luminal propagation requires \(c_{13}\simeq0\). Exact \(c_{13}=0\) is used for the clean symbolic reduction; the small-nonzero case is a robustness check.

## E. Spin-1 sector

Use

\[
c_V^2=\frac{2c_1-c_{13}(2c_1-c_{13})}
{2c_{14}(1-c_{13})}.
\]

At \(c_{13}=0\), this becomes \(c_1/c_{14}\).

The package uses the conventional healthy-sign requirement \(c_{14}>0\) for this normalization.

## F. Logical conclusion

Combining B + C + A gives \(\bar c_4-\bar c_1=2\).

Combining that with \(\bar c_{14}=\epsilon>0\) gives

\[
\bar c_1=-1+\epsilon/2.
\]

Then D + E imply a negative spin-1 speed squared for \(0<\epsilon<2\).

The observationally relevant limit is much stronger: \(\epsilon\ll1\).

## G. What is NOT assumed

- No claim that all MOND theories are ruled out.
- No claim that all æther theories are ruled out.
- No claim that every multi-function extension fails.
- No claim that the phenomenological exponential \(\mu\) is inconsistent.
- No dependence on a black-hole coordinate pathology.
