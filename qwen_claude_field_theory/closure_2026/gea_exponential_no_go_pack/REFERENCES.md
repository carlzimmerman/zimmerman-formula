## Verification note

The GEA literature describes the theory as an Einstein–Hilbert action plus a unit-timelike vector with a free function of a scalar \(\mathcal K\), and reviews show how the weak-field limit yields MOND-like Poisson equations. The exact coefficient convention should always be re-derived from the action being used before importing a reconstruction formula.
