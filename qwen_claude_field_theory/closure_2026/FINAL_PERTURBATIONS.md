   - **Kinetic Sign:** Strictly positive (no ghost graviton).

2. **Scalar Perturbations & Matter Growth:**

   - Effective dust perturbations $\delta\rho_m / \rho_m = \delta$ satisfy:
     $$\ddot{\delta} + 2 H \dot{\delta} - 4 \pi G_{\mathrm{eff}} \rho_m \delta = 0$$

     $$\delta(a) \propto a$$
     reproducing standard cold dark matter structure formation!
