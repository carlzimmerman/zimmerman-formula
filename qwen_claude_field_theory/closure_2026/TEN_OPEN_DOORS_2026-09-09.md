# Ten open doors toward the full theory

Requested by Carl Zimmerman. Baseline: 57502f804, 2026-09-09.
These are research routes, not ten discoveries or promises of closure.
Novelty has not been established by a literature review. Carl supplied the
exponential-law framework, acceleration-scale relation, primordial-clock
direction, and the paddle/wake intuition motivating a possible memory
mechanism. Those contributions are hypotheses to test, not established laws.

## Non-negotiable destination

One explicit action must yield the exact physical-metric exponential MOND
equation, independently derived lensing potentials, two gravitational tensor
modes (any permitted genuine clock/matter field separately counted and
healthy), ordinary minimally coupled matter conservation, acceptable PPN,
luminal healthy tensors, viable expanding cosmology and controlled zero-field
and homogeneous sectors. No combining gates from different actions. Static
spatial Hessians are not kinetic Hessians or Poisson-bracket matrices.

## 1. Solve no-slip and zero flux together

**Opening:** We constructed zero-flux curved data, but initially matched
potentials subsequently separate. The scalar boundary condition alone is
insufficient; the joint compatibility problem is untested.

**First calculation:** On the calibrated action solve E_A=0, scalar flux=0,
P'+B'=0 and P''+B''=0 simultaneously, then differentiate the no-slip condition
along the actual equations. Vary admissible background data rather than
assigning one potential from the other. Restore a physical source action and
its boundary conditions before claiming a galaxy solution.

**Advance if:** No-slip is preserved on an interval and survives refinement
with the intended physical exponential force law. A single matching jet does
not pass. **Anchor:** USER_ACTION_ZERO_FLUX.md and USER_ACTION_KERNEL.md.

## 2. Make the clock–scalar sector genuinely degenerate

**Opening:** A working static sector does not remove extra propagating modes.
This is a decisive architectural gate for the submitted clock–scalar action.

**First calculation:** Derive its full ADM velocity Hessian, retaining lapse,
shift and scalar/clock mixing. Solve degeneracy conditions for the functions
and couplings, derive the corresponding primary constraints, and preserve
them through closure. Compute the actual Poisson-bracket operator, with
k=0 separated from k!=0. Check whether the degeneracy survives matter coupling.

**Advance if:** Two tensor modes remain and every allowed additional genuine
clock/matter mode is explicitly healthy; no hidden auxiliary mode or loss of
FLRW expansion. A singular Hessian alone does not pass.
**Anchor:** USER_ACTION_SHIFT.md; Fable's reported four-mode model is not a
two-mode certificate. **Priority: highest for architectural viability.**

## 3. Preserve MOND while regularizing the screened scalar

**Opening:** The xi operator changes the equations; the calibrated xi=0
kernel cannot automatically certify the finite-xi theory.

**First calculation:** Derive the complete quadratic response about an
on-shell curved background, including all metric connection terms. Search
for a degenerate combination of spatial operators whose contribution to the
physical MOND branch cancels while its auxiliary boundary problem is regular.
Vary the proposed combination before eliminating fields.

**Advance if:** Cancellation holds for arbitrary sources in the claimed
regime, not just one plane wave or a long-wavelength limit, without new poles.
**Anchor:** USER_ACTION_CONNECTION.md and USER_ACTION_CALIBRATED_BRANCH.md.

## 4. Complete the integrable-clock moving interface

**Opening:** IC49/IC50 substantially reduced roundoff and constructed longer
constraint-consistent local evolutions. This is a different action from the
submitted AeST-like action; its evidence must stay separate.

**First calculation:** Replace finite Taylor endpoint data with justified
physical boundary data. Solve the moving-interface compatibility conditions
without projection; test finite multiplier behavior as activation vanishes.
Run spatial, temporal and precision refinement at fixed physical duration.

**Advance if:** A convergent admissible interface solution exists, including
the limiting multiplier, followed by the full 3D constraint analysis.
**Anchor:** IC49_EVOLUTION_REPAIR.md and ACTION_AND_IC50_UPDATE.md.

## 5. Turn spatial nonlocality into an action with defined boundary data

**Opening:** Spatial smoothing can distinguish an extended host from compact
substructure. Fable's L57 report claims a conditional mechanism but explicitly
does not reach its target or predict its smoothing length. Its numerical
claims have not been independently reproduced in this handoff.

**First calculation:** Write the smoothing operator and its domain explicitly,
including the homogeneous mode and boundary conditions. Vary its metric and
matter dependence, including the inverse operator, then derive the auxiliary
constraints and physical stress tensor. Derive or independently calibrate
the length before evaluating held-out clusters.

**Advance if:** It preserves ordinary matter conservation and the required
mode count, and meets lensing/dynamics jointly without a fitted per-object
length. **Anchor (repository-relative):**
fable_independent_2026/L57_NONLOCAL_FUNCTIONAL.md.

## 6. Give the wake/memory idea a causal mathematical realization

**Opening:** Carl's paddle analogy suggests a response with memory. A physical
memory kernel is an alternative architecture, not evidence that gravity is
a fluid or that extra states can be ignored.

**First calculation:** Specify a covariant response kernel with initial data
and derive its equations from an appropriate variational formulation. Test
whether variation produces the intended causal kernel rather than its
time-symmetric counterpart. Compute the spectrum and all independent initial
data before claiming that memory carries no additional modes.

**Advance if:** The construction has the required action origin, conserved
matter, acceptable causal response and mode count, and yields the same MOND
and lensing equations. Prescribing a retarded Green function afterward is
not sufficient. **Priority: alternative if the local degeneracy route fails.**

## 7. Reconstruct a viable primordial-clock cosmology

**Opening:** A clock might provide cosmological structure while the gravity
sector retains only two tensor modes, under the user's explicit allowance
for a separately counted healthy genuine clock field.

**First calculation:** From a chosen surviving action derive the homogeneous
clock current, Friedmann equations, scalar quadratic action and constraints.
Evolve through radiation domination, recombination and late times, keeping
k=0 independent from the k!=0 analysis. Determine which integration constants
represent actual matter-like initial data rather than a prediction.

**Advance if:** Expansion, perturbation stability and structure formation
coexist with the same local MOND branch. Calling an integration constant
"geometric" does not remove its physical effects or its initial-data cost.

## 8. Solve clusters as a joint matter–metric boundary problem

**Opening:** Cluster lensing, dynamics and gas equilibrium constrain different
pieces of a relativistic model. Their joint fit can expose the missing stress
or rule out an otherwise attractive branch.

**First calculation:** For one surviving action, fit one declared common
parameter set to resolved baryons and solve both metric equations. Predict
shear and gas/dynamical observables with covariance, projection and nuisance
parameters included. Freeze choices before testing held-out systems.

**Advance if:** The same action explains all observables without inserting a
different phantom profile into each equation. Audit data and astrophysical
systematics explicitly; neither MOND nor LambdaCDM is assumed correct.
**Anchor:** Fable L57's reported residual discrepancy motivates this test,
but its numerical significance must be reproduced before relying on it.

## 9. Derive the external-field effect for binaries and galaxies

**Opening:** A nonlinear field equation can generate an environment-dependent
prediction beyond an algebraic spherical rotation law.

**First calculation:** Solve the selected action's nonspherical weak-field
equations with an independently specified external field. Derive the tensor
response and orbital observables; compare with an isolated-source control,
then account for selection effects and unresolved companions in binary data.

**Advance if:** One parameter set predicts both galaxy and binary behavior,
with the exponential law fixed in advance. This must be a field-equation
solution, not a fitted interpolation of a different theory's external-field
prescription. A new precise residual prediction is valuable even before
global theory closure, but must be labeled accordingly.

## 10. Derive—or honestly leave free—the a0–Lambda coefficient

**Opening:** Carl's relation a0=c^2 sqrt(Lambda/(32 pi)) is a possible structural
target. Dimensional analysis fixes a scale dependence, not its coefficient.

**First calculation:** Identify the canonically normalized couplings that set
the measured a0 in a surviving action. Derive the dimensionless ratio
a0/(c^2 sqrt(Lambda)) from a symmetry, constraint or dynamical selection
mechanism, tracking the measured versus bare Newton constant.

**Advance if:** The coefficient is fixed independently of fitting a0, remains
consistent with local/cosmological equations, and yields a falsifiable
prediction. If it remains a free coupling, record the relation as an input.
Do not sacrifice a viable construction to manufacture a coefficient proof.

## Efficient execution order and evidence rules

Start with door 2 and door 1: mode content and metric compatibility determine
whether more numerical work on the submitted action is worthwhile. Door 3
is its next operator-design calculation. Keep door 4 as the separate existing
construction. Doors 5–6 are architectural alternatives; doors 7–9 test a
survivor across regimes. Door 10 is not required to declare a theory viable
if its coefficient is honestly an input.

For each route, preserve the explicit action/version, assumptions, exact
commands, outputs, negative controls and remaining implications. Do not label
a route "closed" because its script exits zero. Use Mathbox-style bounded
computation and proof audits; formalize isolated algebraic/constraint lemmas
in Lean only when that strengthens an actual proof obligation. Lean cannot
supply missing physical assumptions or certify empirical adequacy.

No route here is guaranteed to lead to the full theory. Their value is that
each specifies a calculation that can change the next decision.
