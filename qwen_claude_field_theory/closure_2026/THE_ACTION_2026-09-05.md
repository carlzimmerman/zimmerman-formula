14. **γ_v recomputed at the corrected floors, with the carried kernel (g03y).** §5.13 found two errors in the standing wide-binary prediction: it was evaluated at ξ = 0.03/0.05 pc, the floors of the *unsaturated* inverse partner, and it solved with μ = 1 − e^{−y} everywhere although the wide-binary external field y_e = 1.9 lies **above** the saturation point y = 1, where the carried interpolation is μ = 1 − 1/(ey) instead. Both are corrected here, through g03g's own solver and the frozen pre-registered estimator, with a control confirming the estimator code reproduces the published 1.032/1.040 exactly when fed g03g's original tables.

    | kernel / footing | ξ (pc) | γ_v | published (stale) | registered band floor |
    |---|---|---|---|---|
    | exponential carrier / canonical | 0.07 | 1.0375 ± 0.0037 | 1.032 | 1.1614 |
    | exponential carrier / alt | 0.10 | 1.0275 ± 0.0037 | 1.040 | 1.1917 |
    | ν_RAR carried / canonical | 0.10 | 1.0450 ± 0.0037 | 1.032 | 1.1614 |
    | ν_RAR carried / alt | 0.15 | 1.0300 ± 0.0037 | 1.040 | 1.1917 |

    **The two corrections act in opposite directions and are of comparable size**, so the net change is not monotone in ξ and had to be computed rather than assumed: at fixed ξ the carried kernel *raises* the boost by +0.0405 (canonical) and +0.0225 (alt), because ν is 5.5% larger at y_e, while raising the floor *lowers* it by −0.0350 at both footings. Every value stays far below the registered band, by roughly 33σ of the estimator's own error, so **the prediction remains sharply distinguishable from standard MOND** — it is a Newton-like answer where the registered band expects 1.16–1.23. A second consequence: the anisotropy sign-flip radius s_× = 2.49ξ + 0.31 kAU moves from 15.7/26.0 kAU at the old floors to 36–77 kAU at the corrected ones, at or beyond the edge of DR4's usable 4–30 kAU range, so that prediction weakens as the first sharpens.
