# Nonlocal spin-2 state-space verdict — 2026-09-02

## Result

Two more realizations of the last nonlocal spin-2 residual are closed:

1. a non-rational retarded form factor generated by integrating out a healthy
   positive-spectral tensor bath; and
2. a pole-free, field-dependent universal metric form factor with a regular
   Lorentz-covariant quadratic expansion.

The first necessarily contains extra carrier states. The second loses both
tensor kinetic terms at the exact zero-field point because no slip forces the
spin-2 and scalar form factors to coincide, while exact MOND requires their
common coefficient to vanish there.

This is a scoped no-go, not a theorem about every formal nonlocal functional.
The residual would require a metric-only, non-universal covariant projector
with a controlled but non-regular zero-field limit. No such action is known or
built here.

## 1. Grant the strongest static construction

Define

\[
q={2\nabla\Phi\!\cdot\!\nabla\Psi-|\nabla\Psi|^2\over a_0^2},
\qquad
G(y)=y^2+2(1+y)e^{-y}-2 .
\]

The reduced static density is

\[
L_{\rm stat}=a_0^2G(\sqrt q).
\]

The executable variation gives

\[
{G'(y)\over2y}=1-e^{-y}\equiv\mu(y),
\]

and, on the branch \(\Phi=\Psi\),

\[
{\partial L_{\rm stat}\over\partial(\nabla\Phi)}
=2\mu\nabla\Phi,
\qquad
{\partial L_{\rm stat}\over\partial(\nabla\Psi)}=0.
\]

Thus the construction side is granted rather than assumed away: the same
static action can produce the exact exponential MOND flux and a no-slip branch.
The question is whether a covariant two-tensor completion can retain it.

## 2. No slip forces equality of the covariant form factors

For the most general gauge-independent Lorentz-covariant metric propagator
coupled to a conserved source, write

\[
\Pi={P^{(2)}\over a(k^2)k^2}-{P^{(0s)}\over2c(k^2)k^2}.
\]

The script constructs the Barnes--Rivers projectors, verifies their
idempotence and orthogonality, and contracts them with static dust
\(T^{00}=\rho\). For momentum along \(z\),

\[
h_{00}={\rho(-a+4c)\over6ac},
\qquad
h_{11}={\rho(a+2c)\over6ac},
\]

so

\[
\gamma={h_{11}\over h_{00}}={a+2c\over4c-a},
\qquad
\gamma-1={2(a-c)\over4c-a}.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{\gamma=1\quad\Longrightarrow\quad a=c.}
\]

On that branch the common response relative to GR is exactly \(1/a\). Hence a
MOND enhancement \(1/\mu\) fixes the common form factor to \(a=c=\mu\) in the
regular WKB/quadratic description. Universality is a consequence of no slip,
not an extra ansatz.

## 3. The zero-field rank obstruction

The two TT polarizations then have principal Lagrangian

\[
L_T={\mu(y)\over2}
\left(\dot h_+^2+\dot h_\times^2
-k^2h_+^2-k^2h_\times^2\right),
\]

and Hessian

\[
W_T(y)=\mu(y)I_2.
\]

For every \(y>0\), \(W_T\) is positive, rank two, and \(c_T^2=1\). At the
required exact point \(y=0\), however,

\[
\boxed{W_T(0)=0,\qquad \operatorname{rank}W_T(0)=0.}
\]

The quadratic Dirac chain has \(p_+\approx0\) and
\(p_\times\approx0\), zero preservation residuals, a zero Poisson matrix, two
first-class accidental linear constraints, and zero quadratic tensor degrees
of freedom. The rank loss is identical for \(k=0\) and \(k\ne0\). In the full
nonlinear theory this is a constraint bifurcation/strong-coupling point, not a
healthy disappearance of gravity:

\[
\lim_{y\to0^+}{1\over\mu(y)}=\infty,
\qquad
\mu(\epsilon)h^2=\epsilon h^2+O(\epsilon^2h^2),
\]

so the first tensor term begins at cubic field order instead of quadratic.

The obvious repair fails exactly. With
\(a=c=\epsilon_0+\mu(y)\), the tensor Hessian is regular, but the static law is
\(\mu_{\rm eff}=\epsilon_0+\mu\), and

\[
\lim_{y\to0^+}{\epsilon_0\over\mu(y)}=\infty.
\]

The Newtonian floor dominates the deep-MOND term, so the specified exact law
and BTFR limit are lost.

## 4. The non-rational positive-spectral fork

A genuinely non-rational retarded response can instead be generated by a
healthy bath. For two representative carrier modes,

\[
\Pi_E(z)={g_1^2\over m_1^2+z}+{g_2^2\over m_2^2+z},
\qquad
\Pi_E'(0)=-{g_1^2\over m_1^4}-{g_2^2\over m_2^4}<0.
\]

The carrier kinetic Hessian is \(I_3\) on one metric amplitude plus the two
carriers: rank three, no primary or secondary constraints, empty Poisson
matrix, and three configuration degrees of freedom. The positive residues are
the extra states.

For constant positive spectral density on
\([s_0,s_0+\Delta]\),

\[
\Pi_E(z)=\rho\log {s_0+\Delta+z\over s_0+z},
\qquad
\operatorname{Disc}\Pi=-2\pi i\rho.
\]

The branch cut is the continuum state space. Positive weight cannot cancel
it. Equal positive and negative contributions cancel only with kinetic matrix
\(\operatorname{diag}(1,1,-1)\), exposing a ghost.

In closed-time-path form a healthy oscillator produces both

\[
G_{\rm ret}(t,t')={g^2\over\Omega}\Theta(t-t')
\sin[\Omega(t-t')]
\]

and the vacuum noise kernel

\[
N(t,t')={g^2\over2\Omega}\cos[\Omega(t-t')].
\]

The first is nonreciprocal; the second is nonzero whenever the spectral weight
is nonzero. This is a quantum influence action, not a real one-copy
fundamental action. Restoring the explicit bath restores a closed action but
also restores its states.

This spectral statement deliberately excludes pole-free entire form factors:
such free theories can have the same pole spectrum as their local counterpart.
That escape is why the independent zero-field rank result in sections 2--3 is
needed.

## 5. Combined theorem and remaining residual

Under either of these standard realizations:

- **positive-spectral causal completion:** memory implies extra carrier states;
- **pole-free regular metric completion:** no slip implies \(a=c\), and exact
  \(\mu(0)=0\) removes the TT quadratic action.

Thus neither realization satisfies simultaneously exact exponential MOND,
\(\Phi=\Psi\), \(N_{\rm grav}=2\), and a controlled zero-field limit.

The only unclosed logical residue is a non-universal, metric-only, covariant
projector which:

1. modifies the static scalar constraint by the exact nonlinear MOND modulus;
2. leaves the TT Hessian equal to GR even at \(y=0\);
3. introduces no preferred frame, pole, cut, bath state, or auxiliary Cauchy
   data;
4. remains divergence-free after full nonlinear variation; and
5. preserves the ordinary matter Ward identity.

No explicit action satisfying these conditions is present. The next
unavoidable calculation is not another interpolation fit: it is the covariant
projector/Helmholtz problem. Either construct its second variation and prove
its scalar/TT separation on a general background, or prove that such a
projector requires additional state (a clock/aether), singular inverse
operators, or loss of the Bianchi identity.

## 6. TOE consequence

The same calculation is a state-space firewall for the repository's proposed
in-in TOE sector. A retarded influence equation can be a valid effective
equation, but it is not by itself a closed theory of everything. A fundamental
completion must display the environment whose spectral weight creates the
memory, count its states, include its noise/fluctuation sector, and derive the
total Ward identity. Omitting those objects is an effective open-system
description; including them defeats the claimed two-tensor-only state count
for a gravitational spin-2 bath. This is a new synthesis constraint, not a TOE
construction.
