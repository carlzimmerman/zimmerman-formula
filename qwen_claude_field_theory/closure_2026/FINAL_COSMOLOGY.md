## 1. Cosmological Structure of DW-MOND

On a spatially flat FLRW metric $ds^2 = -dt^2 + a(t)^2 \delta_{ij} dx^i dx^j$:
- Clock foliation: $\phi = t \implies u^\mu = (1, 0, 0, 0)$.
- Curvature source: $R_{uu} = R_{00} = -3 \frac{\ddot{a}}{a}$.
- Transport equation: $\frac{d}{dt}\left[ a^3 (M + f(Z)) \right] = 0 \implies M(t) = -f(Z(t)) + \frac{K}{a(t)^3}$.

The effective Friedmann equation becomes:
$$3 H^2 = \frac{8\pi G}{c^2} \left[ \rho_m(t) + \rho_{\mathrm{dust}}(t) + \rho_{\mathrm{DE}}(t) \right]$$
where $\rho_{\mathrm{dust}}(t) = \frac{c^4 a_0^2}{16\pi G}\frac{K}{a(t)^3} \propto a^{-3}$ acts as cold dark matter, and $\rho_{\mathrm{DE}}(t) = \frac{c^4 a_0^2}{16\pi G} |f(Z(t))|$.

$$\rho_{\mathrm{DE}} = \frac{c^4 a_0^2}{16\pi G} |f(Z_\infty)| = \frac{|f(Z_\infty)|}{16\pi} \frac{c^2 a_0^2}{G}$$
which naturally yields the cosmological expansion scale $H_0 \sim \frac{a_0}{c}$.

### Open Theorem (Dynamical Selection of $Z_\infty$ & $\kappa$):
The Zimmerman dark energy relation:
$$a_0^2 = \kappa^2 c^2 G \rho_{\mathrm{DE}}$$

$$\kappa = \sqrt{\frac{16\pi}{|f(Z_\infty)|}}$$
Determining $\kappa$ uniquely from first principles reduces to the dynamical selection of the cosmological attractor value $Z_\infty$.
