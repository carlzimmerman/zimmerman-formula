# Source scope and credit — checked 2026-09-10

Carl Zimmerman requested the primordial-clock direction, the fixed exponential
MOND target and the fitted acceleration/dark-energy relation. This calculation
pursues that direction. Kinetic gravity braiding itself is established work,
not an invention of this repository or a first-principles derivation of Carl's
coefficient. No global novelty claim is made for the solutions below.

1. Deffayet, Pujolas, Sawicki and Vikman, *Imperfect Dark Energy from Kinetic
   Gravity Braiding*, arXiv:1008.0048v2, JCAP 10 (2010) 026,
   [primary text](https://arxiv.org/html/1008.0048v2),
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1088/1475-7516/2010/10/026).
   Inspected sections 2.1–2.3 and the cosmological-current setup. This is the
   action-class, stress-tensor and effective-metric reference. Its (+---)
   convention uses K+G box(phi), X=+dphi^2/2. Changing metric signature gives
   our (-+++) convention P-G box(phi), X=-dphi^2/2. The scalar characteristic
   calculation must eliminate metric second derivatives using Einstein's
   equations; checking the uncoupled scalar equation would miss braiding.
   Our radial variation, explicit Einstein tensor and symbolic principal
   differentiation are implemented locally, not copied numerical certificates.
   The reference does not establish our MOND profile or its universality.

2. Nouri-Zonoz, Hassani, Adamek, Bellini and Kunz, *Signatures of kinetic gravity
   braiding in cosmological probes of the gravitational field*,
   [arXiv:2607.28207v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.28207v1), 2026-07-30.
   Inspected section 2, including its action and linearized stress components.
   It uses the same G2-G3 box(phi) convention. Its temporal potential Psi is
   our Phi; its spatial Phi is our Psi. Relevant recent context: braiding has
   density and metric responses even without a direct scalar–baryon coupling.
   No simulation, fitted EFT coefficient, or observational result from this
   paper is used as evidence for our candidate.

3. Gergely, *Fluid interpretation, Hawking–Ellis classification, and energy
   conditions of the proper kinetic gravity braiding stress tensor*,
   [arXiv:2608.15228v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15228), submitted 2026-08-15.
   Abstract/intro discovery only; its detailed energy-condition classifications
   have NOT been audited or applied here. This is adjacent recent work, not a
   theorem dependency for our computed signs.
