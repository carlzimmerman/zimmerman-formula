# Literature and novelty scope

Search date: 2026-09-04.

## Direct antecedents

- Bekenstein & Milgrom, *Does the missing mass problem signal the breakdown of
  Newtonian gravity?*, ApJ 286 (1984),
  <https://doi.org/10.1086/162570>, introduced the AQUAL action and nonlinear
  Poisson equation.  The field equation and its spherical first integral are
  not new here.
- Ciotti, Zhao & de Zeeuw, *Separable triaxial potential-density pairs in
  MOND*, <https://arxiv.org/abs/1202.1723>, prove that regular separable MOND
  potentials have vanishing density at the origin.  They also note that the
  central density vanishes linearly for a generic regular deep-MOND potential,
  and extend a related expansion to reflection-symmetric potentials.  This is
  a close mathematical antecedent to the incompatibility between a smooth
  MOND potential and nonzero central density.
- Hernandez, *Central MONDian spike in spherically symmetric systems*,
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.03473>, explicitly studies the inner low-field
  region of constant-density cores and the resulting square-root force.  The
  leading inner dynamical scaling and the existence of a central MOND region
  are therefore prior art, not claimed as discoveries.
- The base repository's committed
  exact_mond_regular_center_no_go_2026/REPORT.md already derives the exact
  exponential rank-zero \(C^2\) obstruction, the no-slip Ricci divergence, and
  a longer inner circular-orbit series.  Those results are prior repository
  work, not new content of this bundle.
- Sanders, *Cosmology with Modified Newtonian Dynamics (MOND)*,
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/9710335>, already uses the relativistic active
  density \(\rho+3p\) inside a heuristic deep-MOND cosmological spherical
  argument.  Accordingly, a bare pressure-completed square-root acceleration
  is not globally new.  The separate HPI-Delta action audit must distinguish
  its action-level center equations and curvature result from this precedent.

## Targeted search and classification

Primary-source/arXiv searches covered combinations of:

- MOND/AQUAL, regular center, force-free point, Hessian, Ricci scalar,
  Kretschmann invariant, and curvature singularity;
- deep-MOND or p-Laplacian radial regularity with nonzero source;
- relativistic MOND stars and central expansions;
- exact exponential interpolation near a constant-density center.

The checked sources establish the nonlinear Poisson structure, the central
square-root force, and incompatibility of certain regular MOND potentials with
nonzero central density.  No checked external source stated the full
linearized Kretschmann coefficient

\[
 R\sim5C/(c^2\sqrt r),\qquad
 R_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}R^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}\sim43C^2/(c^4r),
\]

the arbitrary-slip positive-square formula, or the source/kernel threshold
\(m\ge n\) in this spacetime-curvature form.  That search absence is **not**
proof of global novelty.  The defensible wording is:

> A repository-new arbitrary-slip and source/kernel-regularity extension of
> the already committed no-slip center obstruction, and an apparently
> unstated spacetime-curvature consequence of known central
> MOND/p-Laplacian behavior, under explicit one-metric weak-field assumptions.

The exact-exponential series in this bundle is shorter than the one already
committed in the repository and is retained only as an independent regression
control.

## Blind spots

The search was not exhaustive over paywalled full text, theses, books,
non-English literature, or every p-Laplacian regularity paper.  In particular,
the radial Hölder exponent is standard nonlinear-elliptic mathematics even if
its translation into a spacetime Kretschmann obstruction is not stated in the
sources checked here.
