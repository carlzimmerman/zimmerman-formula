## Contractions and boundary convention

Use signature `(-+++)`, future unit normal `n`, `phi_mu=-v n_mu`, `X=v^2/2>0`, `s=n^mu nabla_mu v`, and `K_ij=h_i^mu h_j^nu nabla_mu n_nu`. Thus `K_ij=(dot h_ij-Lie_shift h_ij)/(2N)` and `D_i v=-v a_i`, with `a_i` the normal acceleration. Direct differentiation yields

\[
\phi_{\mu\nu}=s n_\mu n_\nu+v(a_\mu n_\nu+n_\mu a_\nu)-vK_{\mu\nu},\quad
\Box\phi=-s-vK.
\]

The script constructs this complete symmetric tensor in a local orthonormal frame, with three acceleration components and all six independent `K_ij` entries, then contracts the raw source invariants:

\[
L_3=-v^2s^2-v^3Ks,\qquad
L_4=-v^2s^2+v^4a_i a^i=(\nabla X)^2,\qquad L_5=v^4s^2.
\]

After removing the usual Gauss–Codazzi total divergence, `F R` contributes

\[

\]

An independent flat-FLRW calculation checks the integration-by-parts sign: subtracting `d_t(6Fa^2 dot a)` from `6F(a^2 ddot a+a dot a^2)` gives `-6Fa dot a^2-6a^2 dot a fvs`. Acceleration terms are not discarded from the action; they do not enter this velocity Hessian. Their combined coefficient is `v^4 A4-2fv^2`.

## Trace/shear block and primary relation

Write `T=f+XD` (not a stress component), `sigma_ij=K_ij-h_ij K/3`, and assume `F!=0`. Substitution gives the exact completed square

\[
L_{\rm vel}=F\sigma_{ij}\sigma^{ij}
-\frac{2F}{3}\left(K+\frac{3vT}{2F}s\right)^2.
\]

The trace Hessian in `(K,s)` is

\[
H=\begin{pmatrix}-4F/3&-2vT\\-2vT&-3v^2T^2/F\end{pmatrix},
\quad \det H=0,\quad H\binom{-3vT}{2F}=0.
\]

Since `H_KK!=0`, this two-variable block has rank one. At `T=0` the null direction is simply the `s` direction; it is not an excluded case. The negative trace square is the usual gravitational trace sector and is not, by itself, a physical ghost diagnosis. Tensor shear and spatial curvature both have coefficient `F`; a positive tensor kinetic coefficient requires `F>0`. This establishes the displayed luminal tensor principal ratio, not scalar health.

For canonical density momenta, let `pi=h_ij pi^ij`. The quadratic sector gives

\[
\pi=\sqrt h(-2FK-3vTs),\qquad
p_v=\sqrt h(-2vTK-3v^2T^2s/F),\qquad
p_v-\frac{vT}{F}\pi=0.
\]

The last identity is a **kinetic primary relation**. The full action's momenta must include linear-velocity terms. With `-G box(phi)=G(s+vK)` left in that form, the corresponding relation is

\[
p_v-\frac{vT}{F}\pi-\sqrt h\,G\left(1-\frac{3v^2T}{2F}\right)=0.
\]

Alternatively, after integrating that term by parts to `-G_X v^2 s`, it becomes `p_v-(vT/F)pi+sqrt(h) G_X v^2=0`. Their difference is the canonical momentum shift generated by the boundary density `sqrt(h) G v`. Both expressions are checked; one must not mix their boundary conventions. `P(X)` adds no `K,s` velocity term in this independent-v formulation.

At `D=0`, `A4=3f^2/(2F)`, `A5=0`, and `T=f`: the previous action and kinetic relation are recovered exactly.
