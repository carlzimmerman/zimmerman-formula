Equations (1),(2),(5),(7) specify the quadratic invariants, tensor kinetic
terms, and luminal degenerate family. Their kinetic variable is
X_source = grad(phi)^2; ours is X=-X_source/2. Therefore F_source,X=-F_X/2.
Writing D=A3 and f=F_X gives

    A1=A2=0,
    A4=3f²/(2F)-(F-Xf)D/F-X²D²/(2F),
    A5=-(f+XD)D/F.

The applicable family assumes nonzero F and the stated degeneracy conditions.
The class and these coefficient relations are **known after notation
translation**, not new physics discovered here. The repository adds explicit
kinetic and radial checks in its own conventions.

The source's exterior GR result (14) uses its preceding large-gradient
Vainshtein truncation (12)-(13), including neglected lower powers. It is not
a general exclusion of the exponential weak-acceleration branch. Neither
this source nor a kinetic Hessian establishes our MOND, full stability,
matter-coupled constraints, PPN or cosmological requirements.
