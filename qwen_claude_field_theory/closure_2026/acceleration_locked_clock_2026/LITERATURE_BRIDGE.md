# Literature bridge for the ALC lane

The search was run on 2026-09-09 for recent work on relativistic MOND,
mimetic clocks, non-propagating scalar sectors, and lensing.  It did not find a
peer-reviewed six-month result that closes the requested ten-gate theory.  The
following papers are directly relevant inputs, not evidence that ALC works.

1. **Domènech & Ganz, “Connecting Relativistic MOND Theories with Mimetic
   Gravity,” arXiv:2503.11174 (2025).**  The paper constructs conformal/
   disformal-invariant scalar-vector-tensor building blocks and shows how
   unit-timelike constraints can be exchanged with mimetic constraints.  That
   motivates treating the primordial clock as an explicitly counted field in
   ALC, while warning that a non-invertible constraint is not automatically a
   two-tensor closure.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.11174

2. **Gomes & Guariento, “Hamiltonian analysis of the cuscuton,” Phys. Rev. D
   95, 104049 (2017).**  Their Dirac analysis shows that homogeneous and local
   limits of a nominally non-propagating scalar can have different constraint
   behavior.  This is the exact warning applied to ALC's (y\to0) and FLRW
   gates: a unitary-gauge absence of a time derivative is not a proof of no
   local scalar mode.
   https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevD.95.104049

3. **“The theoretical landscape of mimetic gravity,” Eur. Phys. J. C (2026
   review).**  The recent review summarizes how mimetic constructions can
   generate dust-like cosmology and modified-gravity phenomenology, but also
   catalogues the need for perturbative/stability checks.  It is useful for
   the requirement ledger, not a closure result.
   https://doi.org/10.1140/epjc/s10052-026-15958-9

4. **Bekenstein, “Relativistic gravitation theory for the MOND paradigm,”
   Phys. Rev. D 70, 083509 (2004).**  This is the historical action-level
   benchmark: relativistic MOND can obtain lensing and PPN agreement, but it
   uses dynamical scalar and vector fields.  ALC's novelty claim is narrower:
   it locks the exact exponential constitutive law to a clock foliation while
   retaining one physical metric.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0403694

The ALC calculation keeps these lessons as explicit gates.  In particular,
the new action is not called complete until its covariant clock Dirac chain,
preferred-frame PPN coefficients, scalar stability, and transfer functions
are derived.
