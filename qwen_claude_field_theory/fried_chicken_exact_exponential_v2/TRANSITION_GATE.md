# Decisive transition gate

The published 2026 architecture explicitly identifies three regimes:
1. Cosmology: Z < 0.
2. Deep MOND: 0 < Z approximately less than or equal to 1.
3. Newtonian: Z much greater than 1.

The authors propose solving a fictitious smooth spherically symmetric metric

 ds^2 = -B(t,r) dt^2 + A(t,r) d x . d x

for phi, then Z, then M, as a tractable two-dimensional transition study.

Our exact-exponential candidate must pass the following stronger version:

(A) Solve phi on the chosen smooth transition metric.
(B) Compute Z and its time/radial derivatives.
(C) Solve the first-order M transport equation with the cosmological initial data.
(D) Verify that M evolves continuously from the homogeneous cosmological branch toward -f(Z) where spatial transport dominates.
(E) Expand the complete action to second order in scalar, vector, and tensor perturbations.
(F) Check whether coefficients containing f_Z and f_ZZ remain finite after pullback through the actual background Z(t,r).
(G) Compute reduced kinetic/gradient matrices after all constraints.
(H) Reject any background or parameter point with Q<0, c_s^2<0, loss of hyperbolicity, or an uncontrolled singular coefficient.
