\begin{abstract}
If the galactic acceleration scale is locked to the dark-energy density, $a_0=\kappa c\sqrt{G\rho_\Lambda}$, and the MOND kernel on cosmological scales sees the Hubble-flow acceleration $cH(z)$ as its ambient field (the only prescription that survives a lensing test in the accompanying repository: a kernel acting on the peculiar field alone amplifies linear-scale potentials tenfold), then the effective gravitational coupling of linear structure is fixed with no new parameter: $G_{\rm eff}(z)/G=\nu\!\left(cH(z)/a_0\right)$ with $(cH_0/a_0)^2=8\pi/(3\kappa^2\Omega_\Lambda)=49$ for $\kappa=\tfrac12$, i.e.\ $cH_0/a_0=7.0$, and $\nu$ the radial-acceleration-relation function already used in galaxies. It predicts $G_{\rm eff}/G=1.077,\,1.050,\,1.030,\,1.004$ at $z=0,\,0.5,\,1,\,3$ (canonical footing), hence $\sigma_8$ raised by $1.1\%$ ($1.5\%$ on the alternative footing) over Planck-$\Lambda$CDM, $S_8=0.843$ ($0.847$), and $f\sigma_8$ raised by $2.7\%$, $1.9\%$, $1.1\%$ at $z=0.3,\,0.6,\,1.0$ ($3.8$, $2.6$, $1.6\%$). The sign is opposite to the reported $S_8$ tension; the amplitude is inside current redshift-space-distortion errors ($\sim5\%$) and at the reach of DESI's full sample ($1$--$2\%$). The identity is machine-checked (Lean~4); the growth computation is a committed script. This is a prediction of the framework's cosmology under the stated prescription, with the dark component clustering as in $\Lambda$CDM; it leaves the galaxy-scale phenomenology untouched and can be killed by a low-$S_8$ or low-$f\sigma_8$ result at the few-percent level.
\end{abstract}
\section{Derivation}
With $\rho_\Lambda=3\Omega_\Lambda H_0^2/(8\pi G)$ and $a_0=\kappa c\sqrt{G\rho_\Lambda}$,
\begin{equation}
\left(\frac{cH_0}{a_0}\right)^2=\frac{c^2H_0^2}{\kappa^2c^2G\rho_\Lambda}=\frac{8\pi}{3\kappa^2\Omega_\Lambda},
\end{equation}
a pure identity (Lean theorem \texttt{hubble\_kernel\_identity}). With $\kappa=\tfrac12$ and $\Omega_\Lambda=0.686$ this is $49.0$, so $cH_0/a_0=7.0$; the alternative footing ($a_0=1.13\times10^{-10}\,{\rm m\,s^{-2}}$) gives $5.8$. On cosmological scales the peculiar acceleration is far below $a_0$, so the kernel's behaviour is set by its ambient argument. Two prescriptions were computed with a QUMOND particle-mesh code validated against halofit: a kernel on the peculiar field alone overproduces the lensing (potential) power at $0.15$--$0.5\,h\,{\rm Mpc^{-1}}$ by $9$--$25\times$ at $z=0$ and is excluded; a kernel whose argument includes $cH(z)$ is Newtonian to within $\nu(cH/a_0)-1$. The surviving prescription therefore gives the scale-independent, redshift-dependent coupling
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\;\frac{G_{\rm eff}(z)}{G}=\nu\!\left(\frac{cH(z)}{a_0}\right)=\frac{1}{1-\exp\!\left[-\sqrt{7.0\,E(z)}\right]},\qquad E(z)=\frac{H(z)}{H_0},\;}
\end{equation}
using $\nu(x)=1/(1-e^{-\sqrt{x}})$, the radial-acceleration-relation function.
