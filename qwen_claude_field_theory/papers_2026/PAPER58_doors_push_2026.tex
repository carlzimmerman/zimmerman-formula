\begin{abstract}\noindent
The owner's programme studies a gravity framework whose galactic law is $g_N=g^2/(g+a_0)$ with $a_0=\kappa c\sqrt{G\rho_{\rm DE}}$ and $\kappa=\tfrac12$ fitted. Its extra gravitating mass is a collisionless ``cold energy'' medium, not a particle. On 2026-10-22 we made one rigorous pass over every door the record had left open (lanes D1--D9b), followed by an audit of hidden inputs (wave E1--E5). Each lane froze its criteria before computing and ran a mutation control. Both $a_0$ footings ($9.36\times10^{-11}$ and $1.13\times10^{-10}$\,m\,s$^{-2}$) are reported and never pooled. Most results are scoped no-gos and corrections to the record. A finding that changes several others: the framework's own action gives AQUAL with $\mu=x/(1+x)$, not the QUMOND that three lanes used (E2).
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Cassini splits the theory in two.} The unscreened canonical law fails Cassini $Q_2$ by 2.44--4.59$\times$ the Hees et al.\ (2014) $2\sigma$ line (canonical) and 2.85--5.19$\times$ (alternative) with the action's own field equation (QUMOND: 1.70--3.70$\times$ and 1.91--4.03$\times$). The strong-field turn-off cannot help: across its whole band it moves $Q_2$ by 0.052$\sigma$. This is the RAR--$Q_2$ class conflict of Desmond, Hees \& Famaey (2024). The screened theory (stiffness length $L_\star=24$--90\,pc) passes: its $Q_2$ is at most $2.5\times10^{-8}$ of the Hees line, because inside $L_\star$ the Sun's field is a hedgehog with no quadrupole; it passes for every $L_\star$ above a threshold that is at most 0.069\,pc (canonical) or 0.111\,pc (alternative), an upper bound on the threshold.
\item \textbf{The screened theory is the one version that passes both Cassini and the galactic law.} Its open items, stated plainly: the satellite dwarfs sit $+0.89\pm0.13$\,dex above its prediction, and the cold mass needed is supplied only through a $\Lambda$CDM-processed census with no binding mechanism in the record (E3); $L_\star$ is soft (native floor 28.85\,pc, 20.0--42.3\,pc over the stellar $M/L$ uncertainty) and $u$ is an $a_0$- and $h$-dependent number ($0.433$ at the galaxy-measured $a_0$; E1, E4); no covariant parent exists for the stated classes, with or without stiffness (D9, D9b, E5). Gaia DR4 is the decisive test: the screened theory predicts $\gamma_v-1\approx8.1\times10^{-6}$ for a 20\,kAU binary, the unscreened law-first reading $\gamma_v\approx1.17$.
\item \textbf{$\kappa$ is not derived.} No horizon-thermodynamic monomial gives $\tfrac12$ without square roots; with them, $\tfrac12$ appears only as $\sqrt{G\rho_\Lambda}$ times a half picked from a menu. The class's natural output is Milgrom's $a_0=cH_\Lambda/2\pi$, a postulate here. The data do not select $R=4$ ($\kappa=\tfrac12$) among natural caps: 5 of 7 grid values lie within $2\sigma$. The measured $\kappa$ is robust to the background (E4). On the background that BBN allows, the framework's native $H_0$ is 67.2--68.6\,km/s/Mpc, 4.4--5.8$\sigma$ below the local ladder: the ordinary Hubble tension (C2; corrected in v1.1).
\item \textbf{The unscreened law family.} No member passes both $Q_2$ and SPARC on both footings under AQUAL (E2). In an $a_0$ bracket around the canonical value, with QUMOND, some members passed (E1); AQUAL raises their $Q_2$, so their status is knife-edge, not settled. The turn-off has no mechanism in the action, and its scale $y_t$ is $\kappa$ relabelled.
\item \textbf{No covariant parent.} A U(1)-dielectric parent that enforces the law's Gauss identity is superluminal or needs a dark-sector Lorentz violation of $6.3\times10^{7}$ at 1\,AU; enforcing the identity in the record's healthy action brings back the no-self-gravity theorem; the stiffness changes none of this (E5).
\item \textbf{Tests, inputs and record.} The skew-sign test of the law's form is retired; the wide-binary fork is decided by systematics, not sample size; the sealed ALMA tests are expected to be unscorable; the native $1024^3$ shear run died of memory with no verdict. No tested mechanism gives the retained fraction $u\approx0.47$ ($13.0\sigma$ from the record's cluster model; inflow stream counting is rejected by the profile, $\chi^2=39.0$ for 8 degrees of freedom). Four earlier verdicts flip under clean inputs (E1; one of them, D7c, only in part, see v1.1 note). Six errata were written into the record.
\end{enumerate}
\end{abstract}

\section{Why $\kappa$? A no-go for the horizon class (D5)}
The law gives $\rho_\Lambda c^2/(a_0^2/G)=1/\kappa^2\equiv R$, so $\kappa=\tfrac12$ is $R=4$, $\Lambda=32\pi a_0^2/c^4$ and $a_0=cH_\Lambda/Z$.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{The class.} $a_0$ is any monomial in the de Sitter horizon quantities with their textbook coefficients (radius, area, volume, Gibbons--Hawking temperature, Bekenstein--Hawking entropy, Misner--Sharp, Komar and vacuum energies, surface gravity) times powers of $c,G,\hbar,k_B$, with the result free of $\hbar$ and $k_B$. Exponents are integers ($D=1$) or half-integers ($D=2$).
\item \textbf{Theorem (checked on all 3,900 enumerated monomials).} Every member is $a_0=q\pi^m cH_\Lambda$ with $q^2$ rational, so $\kappa^2=(8/3)q^2\pi^{2m+1}$. For $D=1$ the power of $\pi$ is odd, so $\tfrac12$ is impossible, and $Z/4\pi$ is the class's simplest member ($a_0=ck_BT_{\rm GH}/\hbar$). For $D=2$ all 16 constructions that give $\tfrac12$ have $m=-\tfrac12$ and $q^2=3/32$: the law's own $\sqrt{G\rho_\Lambda}$ times one of three rational halves (Misner--Sharp, Komar, $\sqrt{}$ of Bekenstein's $\tfrac14$).
\item \textbf{No evidential weight.} The class covers the data band with probability 1.0000, for both classes and both bands, and for the 11-value minimal subclass. No member fixes a second, coefficient-dependent number: it reads either $H_\Lambda$ (constant $a_0$) or $H(z)$, giving $a_0(2.5)/a_0(0)=3.7687$, the record's rival law. Nearest class values: 0.51824 (canonical) and 0.61421 (alternative) without square roots.
\item \textbf{Record error.} The record says the data admit only $R=4$ among natural caps. A19's own grid is $R\in\{1,2,3,4,8,12,16\}$. On the CFG493 band ($\kappa=0.417\pm0.095$), 5 of 7 lie within $2\sigma$, and $R=8$ ($-0.67\sigma$) is closer than $R=4$ ($+0.87\sigma$). Erratum e1fd7be4dc.
\item \textbf{Scope.} Classes outside this one (bare-$G$ portals, media, quantised couplings) stay open. The data cannot rank $\tfrac12$ against $Z/4\pi$.
\end{itemize}

\section{The strong-field turn-off and Cassini}\label{sec:cassini}
\subsection{The turn-off has no mechanism, and $y_t$ is $\kappa$ (D7)}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{The canonical law needs a turn-off.} Its tail is $y(\nu-1)\to1$, a constant anomaly $a_0$. At 1.5\,AU that is 2557.4 times the planetary bound $B_E$ on the canonical footing and 3087.4 times on the alternative. \item \textbf{$y_t$ is $\kappa$ relabelled.} The record's closure $I(y_t)=8\pi/\kappa^2$ is one-to-one: $y_t=66.2999$ (quoted $\Lambda$), 66.3803 ($32\pi$), 46.0148 (alternative), 77.876 (thermal). \section{The stiffness length $L_\star$ (D8, D8b, E4)}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Two objects, one length.} A76's sound speed (at the cosmic mean density) is exactly AG6D's free-medium equation of state ($0.720398$\,m/s at 24\,pc), so AG6D's bound applies: in the free medium $\sigma_c\le1.70$ (canonical) or 1.87\,km/s (alternative), below the $10^4$\,K width by 4.84 and 4.41 times. $L_\star=\sigma_c^2/a_0$ as a free-medium stiffness is excluded; it survives only as a property of a tethered state that no action term yet defines.
\item \textbf{Record error: the 24\,pc floor is a measurement.} It is CFG820's globular-cluster $\chi^2$ bound (fine-grid roots 23.641 / 24.476\,pc). It excludes the thermal $L_\star$: $\Delta\chi^2=3.913$ (canonical, by 0.07) and 5.992 (alternative). Erratum 9e6fbbbfcd.
\item \textbf{Measured dispersions.} Six tidal dwarfs give median $L_\star$ 42.2 / 35.0\,pc with an 84/16 spread of 4.45. Stilp et al.\ (2013), 23 dwarfs at a common 200\,pc beam, give median $\langle\sigma_{m2}\rangle=9.30$\,km/s, so $L_\star=29.95$ / 24.80\,pc, with 84/16 $=1.995$, a knife-edge pass of the frozen ``one constant'' line ($\le2$). The narrow-peak width gives 20.5 / 17.0\,pc, below the floor.
\item \textbf{But $\sigma$ is not universal (D8b).} Star formation does not drive it (FLOOR FAVOURED: $\rho=+0.474$, $p=0.023$ against $\alpha=0.0125$; slope $+0.088$, 95\% CI [$+0.026$, $+0.149$], excluding $1/3$). But its spread is real: $\chi^2=99.3$ for 22 degrees of freedom on tabulated errors, intrinsic scatter 0.96\,km/s, against an 84/16 of 1.28 from noise alone. It tracks the H\,\textsc{i} column ($\rho=+0.613$, $p=0.0024$). Read as a floor, the minimum $\sigma_{m2}$ gives 17.0 / 14.1\,pc, below the globular bound on both footings. So D8b does not strengthen the conditional prediction $L_\star\approx25$--30\,pc: it holds only if $\sigma_c$ is a typical, gas-set value, and then $L_\star$ depends on environment, in conflict with the record's use of one $L_\star$ for gas-free systems.
\item \textbf{The floor itself is soft (E4).} CFG820 built the 24\,pc floor with the RAR fitting function and an additive external-field recipe, neither of which is the screened theory's physics.
