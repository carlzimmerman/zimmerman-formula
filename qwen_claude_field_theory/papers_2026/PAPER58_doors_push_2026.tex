\begin{abstract}\noindent
The owner's programme studies a gravity framework whose galactic law is $g_N=g^2/(g+a_0)$ with $a_0=\kappa c\sqrt{G\rho_{\rm DE}}$ and $\kappa=\tfrac12$ fitted. Its extra gravitating mass is a collisionless ``cold energy'' medium, not a particle. On 2026-10-22 we made one rigorous pass over every door the record had left open. Each lane froze its criteria before computing and ran a mutation control. Both $a_0$ footings ($9.36\times10^{-11}$ and $1.13\times10^{-10}$\,m\,s$^{-2}$) are reported and never pooled. Most results are scoped no-gos and corrections to the record. None strengthens the framework.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{$\kappa$ is not derived.} No horizon-thermodynamic monomial gives $\tfrac12$ without square roots; with them, $\tfrac12$ appears only as $\sqrt{G\rho_\Lambda}$ times a half picked from a menu. The class's natural output is Milgrom's $a_0=cH_\Lambda/2\pi$, which is a postulate here, not a derivation. The record's claim that the data select $R=4$ (i.e.\ $\kappa=\tfrac12$) among natural caps is wrong: 5 of 7 grid values lie within $2\sigma$.
\item \textbf{The unscreened canonical law fails Cassini.} Its $Q_2$ exceeds the Hees et al.\ (2014) $2\sigma$ line by 1.70--3.70$\times$ (canonical) and 1.91--4.03$\times$ (alternative), and the strong-field turn-off cannot help: across its whole band it moves $Q_2$ by 0.052$\sigma$. No member of the record's unscreened law family passes both $Q_2$ and SPARC on both footings (weakest tension $2.6\sigma$). This is the RAR--$Q_2$ class conflict of Desmond, Hees \& Famaey (2024). The turn-off itself has no mechanism in the action, and its scale $y_t$ is $\kappa$ relabelled. The screened case is a separate lane (D7d), still running.
\item \textbf{The stiffness length $L_\star$} survives only as a property of a tethered state. Dwarf H\,\textsc{i} dispersions are not set by star formation, but they are not one universal number either; read as a floor they put $L_\star$ below the globular-cluster bound.
\item \textbf{No covariant parent.} A U(1)-dielectric parent that enforces the law's Gauss identity is superluminal or needs a dark-sector Lorentz violation of $6.3\times10^{7}$ at 1\,AU; enforcing the identity in the record's healthy action brings back the no-self-gravity theorem.
\item \textbf{Tests and their power.} The skew-sign test of the law's form is retired; the wide-binary fork is decided by systematics, not by sample size, and wide binaries shrink the joint $\kappa$ error from 0.121 to 0.077, not to 0.0605; the two sealed ALMA tests are expected to be unscorable; the native $1024^3$ shear run died of memory with no verdict.
\item \textbf{No tested mechanism gives the retained fraction $u\approx0.47$.} The record's cluster model gives $u=0$ ($13.0\sigma$ from 0.47); counting only inflowing streams gives $u\approx0.50$ with no constants but is rejected by the profile ($\chi^2=39.0$ for 8 degrees of freedom); isolated-galaxy lensing gives $u\ge0.660\pm0.028$, so $u\approx0.47$ is not universal.
\end{enumerate}
One lane had not committed a result when this draft was written and is marked pending: D7d (Cassini $Q_2$ of the screened theory).
\end{abstract}

\section{Why $\kappa$? A no-go for the horizon class (D5)}
The law gives $\rho_\Lambda c^2/(a_0^2/G)=1/\kappa^2\equiv R$, so $\kappa=\tfrac12$ is $R=4$, $\Lambda=32\pi a_0^2/c^4$ and $a_0=cH_\Lambda/Z$.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{The class.} $a_0$ is any monomial in the de Sitter horizon quantities with their textbook coefficients (radius, area, volume, Gibbons--Hawking temperature, Bekenstein--Hawking entropy, Misner--Sharp, Komar and vacuum energies, surface gravity) times powers of $c,G,\hbar,k_B$, with the result free of $\hbar$ and $k_B$. Exponents are integers ($D=1$) or half-integers ($D=2$).
\item \textbf{Theorem (checked on all 3,900 enumerated monomials).} Every member is $a_0=q\pi^m cH_\Lambda$ with $q^2$ rational, so $\kappa^2=(8/3)q^2\pi^{2m+1}$. For $D=1$ the power of $\pi$ is odd, so $\tfrac12$ is impossible, and $Z/4\pi$ is the class's simplest member ($a_0=ck_BT_{\rm GH}/\hbar$). For $D=2$ all 16 constructions that give $\tfrac12$ have $m=-\tfrac12$ and $q^2=3/32$: the law's own $\sqrt{G\rho_\Lambda}$ times one of three rational halves (Misner--Sharp, Komar, $\sqrt{}$ of Bekenstein's $\tfrac14$).
\item \textbf{No evidential weight.} The class covers the data band with probability 1.0000, for both classes and both bands, and for the 11-value minimal subclass. No member fixes a second, coefficient-dependent number: it reads either $H_\Lambda$ (constant $a_0$) or $H(z)$, giving $a_0(2.5)/a_0(0)=3.7687$, the record's rival law. Nearest class values: 0.51824 (canonical) and 0.61421 (alternative) without square roots.
\item \textbf{Record error.} The record says the data admit only $R=4$ among natural caps. A19's own grid is $R\in\{1,2,3,4,8,12,16\}$. On the CFG493 band ($\kappa=0.417\pm0.095$), 5 of 7 lie within $2\sigma$, and $R=8$ ($-0.67\sigma$) is closer than $R=4$ ($+0.87\sigma$). Erratum e1fd7be4dc.
\item \textbf{Scope.} Classes outside this one (bare-$G$ portals, media, quantised couplings) stay open. The data cannot rank $\tfrac12$ against $Z/4\pi$.
\end{itemize}
