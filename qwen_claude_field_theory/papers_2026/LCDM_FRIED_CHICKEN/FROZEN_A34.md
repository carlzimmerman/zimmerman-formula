## Papers (all summaries PROVISIONAL: arXiv abstract / ar5iv HTML through a summariser, 11-04; no PDFs, data or code downloaded; FROM MEMORY marked)

1. **Kaplinghat & Turner 2002** (ApJ 569 L19, astro-ph/0107284), "How Cold Dark Matter Theory Explains Milgrom's Law". As read: an SIS-like halo `rho_L(r) ~ beta^3 Omega_M rho_crit (1+z_c)^3 (r/ell)^-2`, ell = L/[beta (1+z_c)] the collapsed size of a comoving perturbation L, beta "of O(5)"; baryons (Omega_B/Omega_CDM ~ 1/7) dissipate and collapse by alpha ~ 10 > 7 so they sit inside r_DM = ell/7, where enclosed dark mass equals the baryon mass; `a_DM = G M(r_DM)/r_DM^2 = 4 pi G (ell/7) rho_L(ell/7)`; Eq. (4) `a_DM = cH0 [10 beta^2 Omega_M (eps/1e-5)^2] (H0 L0/c) (L/L0)^(-n_eff-2)` = O(1) cH0 (L/L0)^0.2, collapse when sigma_L ~ 1 with sigma_L(z) = (eps/1e-5)(1+z)^-1 (L/L0)^(-(n_eff+3)/2), L0 = 10/h Mpc, n_eff -2.2. NFW check quoted: `a_DM ~ 1e-8 (M200/1e12)^0.1 cm s^-2`. Stated: dark energy "not critical"; a_DM is fixed once galaxies are bound while cH falls, so a_DM ~ cH "only holds today". Milgrom's a0 quoted there as 2e-8 cm s^-2.
2. **van den Bosch & Dalcanton 2000** (ApJ 534, 146, astro-ph/9912004). As read: the paper does not derive a0 in CDM ("no obvious reason why the DM model would reveal a characteristic acceleration"); it is an MMW-type engine (NFW, adiabatic contraction, SN feedback mass loss, galaxy-formation efficiency eps_gf, f_bar 0.085, OL 0.7, h 0.7). Its Appendix eq. (B3): `xi_DM = sqrt(20 pi G H0 V200 Sigma0 / (a0^2 eps_gf f_bar))`, xi = V_flat^2/(a0 R_d).
3. **Mo, Mao & White 1998** (MNRAS 295, 319), the engine, FROM MEMORY: NFW halo, disc mass m_d M200, angular momentum j_d J, R_d = (1/sqrt 2)(j_d/m_d) lambda r200 f_c^(-1/2) f_R, f_c ~ 2/3 + (c/21.5)^0.7, fiducial m_d = j_d = 0.05, lambda log-normal median 0.05, Blumenthal adiabatic contraction.
4. **Di Cintio & Lelli 2016** (MNRAS 456 L127, 1511.06616). As read: DC14 profile (Di Cintio 2014a Eq. 3 in X = log M*/M_halo), NFW above 1e12 Msun, Kravtsov 2014 abundance matching 0.10 dex scatter, Dutton & Maccio 2014 c(M) 0.11 dex scatter, DM scaled by sqrt(1 - f_b) in velocity (f_b 0.158), contraction at MW mass "as found in simulations"; no a0 value or origin given ("driven by structural scaling relations").
5. **Desmond 2017** (MNRAS 464, 4160, 1607.01800). As read: fiducial = SHMR abundance matching with 0.16 dex scatter, pure NFW from darksky-400 (c scatter inherited), no contraction (nu = 0); nu = 1 Gnedin contraction variant; SPARC baryons Y 0.5/0.7. Characteristic-scale statistic: mean g_b where each galaxy's mass discrepancy D = g_obs/g_b crosses 3; AM shape reproduced, scatter and low-g normalisation overpredicted, "no evidence" the scale has less spread in data than mocks.
6. **Salucci / Donato et al. 2009 / Gentile et al. 2009** (FROM MEMORY): Burkert halo with constant central surface density log(rho0 r0) = 2.15 Msun pc^-2, so the halo acceleration at r0 is g(r0) = 2 pi [ln 2 + (1/2) ln 2 - pi/4] G rho0 r0 = 1.598 G rho0 r0 ~ 3e-11 m s^-2, mass-independent. Empirical, not derived in LCDM.
7. Recorded, not re-run: Kaplinghat, Keeley, Linden & Yu 2014 and Kaplinghat, Ren & Yu 2020 (SIDM isothermal cores tied to baryons): one-recipe and per-galaxy versions are A15 U3 and A19. Wu & Kroupa 2015 (critique that LCDM has no reason for a universal a0): an argument, no model to grade. Navarro et al. 2017 and Grudic 2020: A21.

## Implementations (equations as coded)

Common: lib.py SPARC (171 galaxies, 3373 points, Y 0.5/0.7), lib SHMR m200_of, Diemer19 c(M, z) 200c, cosmology H0 67.4, Om0 0.315, Ob0 0.0493, sigma8 0.811 (lib). f_b = Ob0/Om0 = 0.1565. Law-equivalent a0 of a curve = lib.fit_a pooled over all points.

- **KT eq. (4), written in physical form** (my algebra from Eq. 1 and the a_DM step, checked against Eq. 4's 10 beta^2): `a_DM = (3/2) beta^2 Omega_M H0^2 L (1+z_c)^2 / f`, f = Omega_B/Omega_CDM (1/7 in the paper; here 0.0493/0.2657 = 0.1856, and also 1/7), L = (3 M / (4 pi rho_m0))^(1/3) comoving, M = M200. Collapse: (KT-E) the paper's EdS reading 1 + z_c = sigma(L, 0) from colossus; (KT-G) LCDM growth, D(z_c) sigma(L, 0) = 1 by colossus growthFactor (z_c = 0 if sigma < 1). beta = 5 (paper "O(5)"), counted as 1 knob even when not tuned. Redshift reading (KT's "fixed since bound"): a galaxy at z has z_c,eff = max(z_c, z).
- **Mapping to the law.** At the radius where enclosed dark mass equals baryon mass, the law has g = 2 g_b, so g_b = a0/2 there. KT's a_DM is the dark (= baryonic) pull at that radius, so the primary law-equivalent is `a0_eq = 2 a_DM`. The paper compares a_DM to a0 directly; that reading (a0_eq = a_DM) is reported beside it, not used for the verdict.
- **KT NFW check (my reconstruction of their check):** NFW (1 - f_b) M200, Diemer19 c; r_DM solves M_NFW,DM(<r) = f_b M200 (all baryons inside); a_DM = G f_b M200 / r_DM^2. Variant KT-AM per SPARC galaxy: M200 from SHMR, baryon mass the galaxy's own M_b, r_DM from M_DM(<r) = M_b.
- **KT hand generalisations (not derived in the paper, each a hand choice):** H1 `a = C_KT c H_DE(z)`, H_DE = sqrt(8 pi G rho_DE(z)/3); H2 `a = C_KT c H(z_x)`, z_x where rho_DE(z_x) = rho_m(z_x) in that background, fixed for all observation z. C_KT = (KT-G a0_eq at 1e12, base) / (c H0), carried unchanged (KT's O(1)). Also reported with C = 1/(2 pi) and C = 1 for H2.
- **MMW engine on SPARC:** M200 = M_b / m_d, m_d = 0.05; NFW Diemer19 c; dark mass (1 - m_d) NFW; Blumenthal contraction (lib r1 iteration, f = 1); g = g_b + G M_DM,contracted/r^2. Scale: fit_a of the curves; analytic BTFR scale `a = V200^4/(G m_d M200) = 10 H V200 / m_d` at 1e12. Implied lambda = sqrt(2) R_d f_c^(1/2) / r200 (j_d = m_d, f_R = 1, stated), median against 0.05. Knob row: m_d one global knob.
- **vdBD on SPARC:** M200 from the SHMR (eps_gf f_bar = M_b/M200 per galaxy, the feedback-loss stand-in), NFW Diemer19, Blumenthal contraction. Scale from eq. (B3), my algebra: setting V_flat = V200 in B3 and comparing with the deep-law disc V^4 = G (2 pi Sigma0 R_d^2) a0 gives `a_vdBD = 10 H0 V200 / (eps_gf f_bar) = V200^4/(G M_b)` per galaxy; variant with V_max of the contracted curve at the last measured point replacing V200. Knob row: one global factor on M200.
- **DL16 on SPARC:** DC14 (recipe20's dc14_par and cum_mass, unchanged) at X = log M*/M200, lib SHMR (Kravtsov 2014 coefficients not recovered; deviation), DM mass (1 - 0.158) M_DC14 normalised at R200 as in A18, NFW above M200 1e12; c from Dutton & Maccio 2014, FROM MEMORY: log10 c200 = 0.520 + (0.905 - 0.520) exp(-0.617 z^1.21) + (-0.101 + 0.026 z) log10(M200 h / 1e12); Diemer19 row also. Contraction at MW mass not added (DC14 already contracts at high X). Knob row: global M200 shift and c shift (2).
- **Desmond 2017 on SPARC:** pure NFW at SHMR M200, Diemer19 c (nu = 0), and Blumenthal contraction (nu = 1, stand-in for Gnedin). Mean relation graded; scatter mocks: 40 seeds, per galaxy log M200 ~ N(0, 0.16/slope) mapped from 0.16 dex in M* at fixed halo through the local SHMR slope, log c ~ N(0, 0.11), independent. Scale statistic: per galaxy g_b where g/g_b = 3 by log interpolation; law gives g_b = a0/6 there, so `a0_eq,D3 = 6 g_b,cross`. Reported for the model, the law and the observed curves.
- **Salucci/Donato:** Burkert halo `rho = rho0 r0^3 / ((r + r0)(r^2 + r0^2))`, rho0 r0 = 10^2.15 Msun pc^-2, r0 = k R_d with one global k fitted (hand closure, 1 knob). Scale g(r0) = 1.598 G rho0 r0. LCDM reading (push): the NFW analogue g(r_s) = G M_DM(<r_s)/r_s^2 at 1e12, Diemer19, rebuilt per background.
