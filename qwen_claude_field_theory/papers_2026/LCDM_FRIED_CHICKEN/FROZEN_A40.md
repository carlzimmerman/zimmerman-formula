## (a) Correlated halo scatter (assembly bias) plus a systematic M/L trend

- Desmond 2017 (MNRAS 464, 4160; arXiv 1607.01800): abundance-matching mocks overpredict the MDAR scatter; the MDAR gives "2 sigma evidence for an anticorrelation of galaxy size and Hubble type with halo mass or concentration at fixed stellar mass" (search summary of the abstract). No correlation coefficient quoted.
- Matthee et al. 2017 (MNRAS 465, 2381), EAGLE centrals: at fixed halo mass a larger M* goes with a more concentrated halo; "up to 0.04 dex" of the M*-M200 scatter is due to concentration below 1e12.5 (search summary). My inference, not a quoted number: with a total SHMR scatter near 0.15-0.2 dex, removing 0.04 dex in quadrature or linearly means |rho(c, M* residual)| about 0.3 to 0.6.
