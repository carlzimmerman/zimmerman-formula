# ACDM flavored fried chicken version of Galaxy Dnynamics

Working note, v1.1, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23901396; v1.0 is DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23897582. How exactly LCDM's knobs can reproduce the law on real SPARC baryons, and what each match costs. Rival-flavoured exercise; adopts nothing and reinstates nothing. kappa = 1/2 FITTED; not theory closed.

- `lcdm_fried_chicken.tex` / `.pdf`: the paper. Rebuild: `tectonic lcdm_fried_chicken.tex`. Check: `python3 audit.py`.
- `FROZEN_RECIPE.md`: targets, recipes, Amendments A1 to A3, and the cosmology bug note.
- `scripts/recipe.py`, `recipe2.py`, `recipe3.py`, `recipe4.py`: run from `scripts/`, needs colossus. Outputs are the `.out` files. `recipe2_before_cosmology_fix.out` is the wrong run, kept on record.

**After v1.0, summarised in v1.1: Amendment A4, `scripts/recipe5.py`, `recipe5.out`.** T1: R11 and R11b are converged at 0.0415 and 0.0275 from their recorded knobs, a local statement, and neither reaches E1. R11b on unseen galaxies reads 0.0270 against the deposited 0.0326, but that restart began from knobs tuned on all galaxies, so it is not a clean holdout and v1.0's number stands. T2: gate S passes in all four rows, median rms to observed 0.1974 to 0.2032 against 0.205, while rms to law widens to 0.049 to 0.067. The gate is weak because measurement noise dominates that rms, and the draws are independent. T3: past the last measured point R11 runs +0.107 at log g_b -13, crosses zero, then -0.204 at -14.5 and -0.541 at -15.5; R11b stays +0.10 to +0.17 from -12.5 down. No two-halo term and no data graded, so this says where the two differ, not which is right.

**After v1.0, summarised in v1.1: Amendment A5, `scripts/recipe6.py`, `recipe6.out`, diagnostic `recipe6b.py`, `recipe6b.out`.** KiDS-1000 ESD, Brouwer+2021 Fig-3, four mass bins, 60 points, full covariance, nothing refitted, gate p >= 0.01. All fail: law 276.1, R11 277.9, R11b 242.1, base LCDM control 563.1. On R <= 1 Mpc, 44 points: law 211.4, R11 146.5, R11b 120.9, control 444.2. Amplitudes A: law 1.367, R11 1.179, R11b 1.140, control 0.809, each +-0.02 to 0.03. So the recipes do not lose to the law here, and two runs had bugs fixed before the first full table (two-halo grid range, a unit error and a name clash; the law row printed once before those fixes, unchanged by them). Post-result diagnostic, four extra parameters, not a grade: with a per-bin mass shift the law reaches 167.7/56 at +0.2 to +0.3 dex, R11 228.2 and R11b 206.0 at +0.1 to +0.2. The law's 167.7 agrees with CFG801's independently coded profiled no-edge 163.1. Limits: recipes built at z = 0 and applied mostly outside their tuned range, satellites and isolation not modelled, law row has no two-halo term.

**After v1.0, summarised in v1.1: Amendment A6, `scripts/recipe7.py`, `recipe7.out`.** R12 defines the CDM halo from the law with a = kappa c sqrt(G rho_DE), kappa = 1/2 by hand. E1 to E5 pass exactly, by construction, rms to observed 0.1951: that reports freedom, not evidence. Costs: implied M200 against the SHMR median +0.11 dex overall, -0.80 at log M_b 11-13; M_b/M200 below cosmic in all 171; rho_DE has to reach the halo by a term LCDM does not contain. KiDS, same grade as A5: untruncated control 276.1, reproduces the law; truncated at R200 plus two-halo 457.7, worse. Both fail.
