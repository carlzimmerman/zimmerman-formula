## Literature amplitudes (set now, before numbers; web look-ups 11-04, PROVISIONAL, summariser-read HTML/abstract pages, no PDF)

- SPARC itself (Lelli, McGaugh, Schombert 2016, ar5iv 1606.09251, summariser): no asymmetric-drift correction is described in the SPARC paper; pressure support said to matter only near V ~ 20 km/s with HI dispersion ~ 10 km/s. Stellar disc V_disk computed with an exponential vertical profile, z_d = 0.196 R_d^0.633 (Bershady et al. 2010); gas disc assumed thin. Q = 3 flags strong non-circular motions. A search summary of a NIHAO paper (1706.04202) says the original curves feeding SPARC were corrected for pressure support where needed, differently per sample. Reading fixed now: SPARC is PARTIALLY corrected for pressure support, unknown per galaxy; its V_disk already includes a thickness; its V_gas does not.
- Non-circular motions in dwarfs, Oman et al. 2019 (ar5iv 1706.07478, summariser): APOSTLE dwarfs with 60 < V_max < 120 km/s; tilted-ring curves "tend to underestimate" inner V_circ; worked example median 52.8 -> 46.7 km/s against true 52.0 at 2 kpc (about -10 %); orientation scatter about 15 % of V_circ; overestimates rare and milder. Used: mean -0.10, per-galaxy scatter 0.15 (fractional V), full amplitude inside 2 kpc.
- Bars: Randriamampandry et al. 2015 (MNRAS 454, 3743; search summary): tilted rings under/overestimate V_circ when the bar is parallel/perpendicular to the major axis; their 2017 thesis (search summary) about 40 % at alignment; Rhee et al. 2004 (cited in a 2025 paper, search summary) about 20 % central underestimate. Used: delta V/V = -A cos(2 phi), phi uniform random, A = 0.2 primary, 0.4 sensitivity; a worst-for-the-law variant with every bar aligned (delta V/V = -A). Bar region r <= 1.5 R_d (bar length about 1-1.5 R_d, FROM MEMORY), falling as exp(-(r - 1.5 R_d)/R_d) beyond. Bar flag: S4G CVRHS family index F >= 0.5 from Buta et al. 2015 (`real_research/data/s4g_morph_buta2015_cvrhs.tsv`, already in the repo); SPARC galaxies not matched there are treated as unbarred (stated).
- Pressure support: V_c^2 = V_rot^2 + sigma^2 (-d ln(Sigma sigma^2)/d ln r) (thin-disc asymmetric-drift form, isotropic, the brief's form with Sigma for rho). sigma constant per galaxy, so the term is sigma^2 (-d ln Sigma_gas/d ln r). Gas disc exponential, scale R_g solved per galaxy from the table: M_HI = 2 pi R_g^2 Sigma_0 and Sigma(R_HI) = 1 Msun/pc^2 (R_HI from the table); then -d ln Sigma/d ln r = r/R_g. sigma = 10 km/s primary, 8 and 12 sensitivity (HI dispersions 8-12 km/s, FROM MEMORY / brief). Galaxies with R_HI = 0 or no solution: term off, counted.
- Thickness: stars, the SPARC z_d already enters; residual true-thickness uncertainty taken as a further -5 % on V_disk^2, V_bul^2 untouched; gas thin in SPARC, true HI layer thick: -10 % on V_gas^2. Radial shape w_t(r) = 1/(1 + (r/(2 R_d))^2) (crude, chosen now; full Casertano integral not done). So g_b,true = g_b,cat - w_t (0.05 V_star^2 + 0.10 V_gas^2)/r. Amplitudes from the brief's 5-10 %, PROVISIONAL.

Dwarf definition (non-circular motions): SPARC V_flat < 120 km/s (V_flat 0 -> max V_obs). Primary applies to all such galaxies (extrapolating Oman's 60-120 range below 60, stated); variant: 60 <= V <= 120 only. Radial weight w_nc(r) = 1 for r <= 2 kpc, exp(-(r - 2 kpc)/2 kpc) beyond.
