## (a) Vainshtein: DGP normal branch and cubic Galileon

Force (DGP form, exact for spherical symmetry with enclosed mass; the cubic-Galileon decoupling-limit solution has the same form with B and r_V as constants, FROM MEMORY): g = g_N + y, y = 2 B g_N x^3 [sqrt(1 + x^-3) - 1], x = r/r_V, r_V^3 = 16 G M(<r) r_c^2/(9 beta^2 c^2), B = 1/(3 beta). Implemented with B and r_c as the two constants: r_V^3 = 16 G M r_c^2 (3B)^2/(9 c^2) = 16 G M r_c^2 B^2/c^2.
Analytic (written now): deep inside (x << 1) y -> 2 B g_N x^(3/2) = (c/(2 r_c)) sqrt(G M/r) = (c/(2 r_c)) sqrt(g_N r), independent of B. So y = sqrt(g_N a_V(r)) with a_V(r) = c^2 r/(4 r_c^2): a MOND-like square root but with a scale that grows linearly with r; v^2 from y grows as sqrt(r) (rising, not flat). The screening variable is the mean enclosed density against ~ c^2/(G r_c^2), not an acceleration. Natural r_c ~ c/H0 gives a_V(10 kpc) ~ H0^2 r/4 ~ 4e-16 m/s^2 (~1e-6 a0). The acceleration at the Vainshtein radius, g_N(r_V) = (G M)^(1/3) (c/(4 r_c B))^(4/3), goes as M^(1/3): fails G4.
