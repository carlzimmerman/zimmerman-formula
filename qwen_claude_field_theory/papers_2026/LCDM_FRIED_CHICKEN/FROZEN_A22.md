## Sources (look-ups PROVISIONAL, read from arXiv abstract / ar5iv HTML through a summariser, no PDF downloaded)
- Berezhiani & Khoury 2015, PRD 92, 103510, arXiv 1507.01019: abstract only read: axion-like particles of order eV, polytropic condensate, phonons mediate a MOND-like force. Values of alpha, Lambda NOT read from the page; BK 2015's "alpha ~ 0.86, Lambda ~ meV" is FROM MEMORY.
- Berezhiani, Famaey & Khoury 2018, JCAP 09 (2018) 021, arXiv 1711.05748, ar5iv HTML (provisional): fiducial m = 1 eV, Lambda m^3 = 0.05 meV eV^3 (Lambda = 0.05 meV), alpha = 5.7, beta = 2, sigma/m = 0.01 cm^2/g, a0 = 0.87e-8 cm/s^2; a0 = alpha^3 Lambda^2/M_Pl (M_Pl not stated as reduced; the quoted a0 is reproduced with the REDUCED M_Pl 2.435e18 GeV, so that is used); P = rho^3/(12 Lambda^2 m^6); total a = a_b + a_DM + a_phonon; a_phonon = alpha Lambda grad(phi)/M_Pl; phonon equation (integrated, curl term dropped)
  [(grad phi)^2 + 2m(2beta/3 - 1) muhat] / sqrt[(grad phi)^2 + 2m(beta - 1) muhat] * grad phi = alpha M_Pl a_b,
  rho_SF = 2 sqrt2 m^(5/2) Lambda [3(beta-1) muhat + (3-beta)(grad phi)^2/2m] / (3 sqrt[(beta-1) muhat + (grad phi)^2/2m]);
  core matched to NFW at R_NFW by continuity of density and pressure, P_NFW(R) = int_R^inf rho_NFW G M/r^2 dr; the MOND-like force "is limited to the superfluid region"; R_T from Gamma = (sigma/m) N v rho, N = (rho/m)(2 pi/(m v))^3, equal to the dynamical rate, agrees with R_NFW to ~30 %. The paper does not relate Lambda to the dark energy scale.
- Mistele, McGaugh & Hossenfelder 2022, A&A 664, A40, arXiv 2201.07282 (search-result summary, provisional): 169 SPARC galaxies with Upsilon free; best fits often NOT in the MOND-like regime, and forcing that regime conflicts with strong lensing. Not reproduced here (their per-galaxy Upsilon freedom is not used; Upsilon fixed 0.5/0.7 as in every lane).

## Routes (all on lib.load() SPARC, 171 galaxies, 3373 points, Upsilon 0.5/0.7, target = law curve; spherical baryons from v_bar, g_b inside the first point ~ r, outside the last point G M_b(last)/r^2)
- (a) Deep phonon force alone, BK sum: g = g_b + sqrt(a0_BK g_b). One global knob a0_BK, fitted to the law on all data; holdout tuned on GS[::2], unseen GS[1::2]. No condensate gravity (this is the "phonon supplies all" reading). Also printed at a0_BK = A0 (law's value, 0 knobs).
- (b) Hybrid with condensate gravity (the double counting), BFK 2018 equations above, beta = 2:
  - NFW envelope: M200 from lib SHMR (shift dn), Diemer19 c, mass (1 - f_b) as in lib.
  - Core: hydrostatic outward integration in w = muhat/m (velocity^2): dw/dr = -(g_b + G M_core(<r)/r^2), dM/dr = 4 pi r^2 rho_SF(w, A), with A = a_phonon solved locally from the phonon equation, written in accelerations as (A^2 + c1 K) A = a0 g_b sqrt(A^2 + c2 K), K = 2 (alpha Lambda m)^2 w/(M_Pl hbar)^2, c1 = 2beta/3 - 1, c2 = beta - 1. Core edge where w <= 0 (rho_SF set to 0 there). Dark matter feels only gravity, not the phonon.
  - Matching: for each w0 on a log grid 1e6 to 1e13 m^2/s^2 (36 values) find the outermost radius R where rho_SF falls through rho_NFW, residual log(P_SF(R)/P_NFW(R)) with P_SF = rho^3/(12 Lambda^2 m^6) and P_NFW from the NFW plus baryon mass (baryon mass held at M_b beyond the last point; the core mass inside R is replaced by the NFW mass in P_NFW, a small approximation, stated); root in w0 by bisection on the first sign change from the top. Outside R: M_DM = M_core(R) + NFW shell, no phonon force. A galaxy with no solution is graded with no superfluid (g = g_b + g_NFW) and counted.
  - (b1) BFK fiducial: alpha 5.7, Lambda 0.05 meV, m 1 eV, dn 0: 0 knobs tuned.
  - (b2) tuned: log10 alpha in [-1, 2], log10 Lambda/meV in [-3, 1], log10 m/eV in [-1, 1], SHMR dn in [-0.54, 0.54] (A3's bound): 4 global knobs, least_squares from the fiducial, max_nfev 40; on all data and on GS[::2] for the holdout.
- (c) Per-galaxy halo freedom on (b2)'s global knobs: per galaxy log M200 shift in [-0.6, 0.6] and log c shift in [-0.22, 0.22] (A2's 2 sigma row), core re-matched each time; 342 + 4 = 346 parameters. Holdout: unseen galaxies cannot be given per-galaxy values without fitting them to the target, so the unseen score is (b2)-on-GS[::2] at the default halo, i.e. the same number as (b2)'s holdout, stated. Knobs at bound counted.

## Expected in advance
(a) fails E1 at about 0.03-0.06: the sum g_b + sqrt(a0 g_b) overshoots the law in the transition (law -> g_b + a0 at high g_b, sum -> g_b + sqrt(a0 g_b)). (b1) worse than (a): condensate gravity on top of a full phonon MOND force double counts. (b2) pushes the dark mass down (dn to its lower bound or m, Lambda to kill the core), lands near (a), fails E1. (c) improves but stays above 0.02 because the phonon interpolation, not the halo, sets the transition shape. N1/N2 fail G1, N3 passes G1 by construction; G2 is a hand term in every case; N3 at m 1 eV puts a far denser condensate at galaxy radii and fails E1. No tie.

## AMENDMENT A22.1, after a first test evaluation, before any other number (disclosed)
Seen so far: one test evaluation of (b1) as frozen. With the NFW envelope fixed at the SHMR M200 and Diemer19 c, the density-and-pressure match has a solution in 0 of 171 galaxies: at every w0 the condensate is far colder than the envelope where their densities cross (e.g. NGC2998: P_SF/P_NFW e^-6.7 to e^-9.7 at the crossing), so every galaxy fell back to "no superfluid" and the test printed rms to law 0.1419 (that is NFW plus baryons). That stands as the as-frozen (b1) result and is printed again by the script. BFK let the envelope adjust; a fixed LCDM c(M) envelope does not admit their match. Added, frozen now, gates unchanged:
- Mode M (primary for (b1), (b2), (c), N3): envelope fixed at SHMR M200 and Diemer19 c (times the (c) shifts); core matched by continuity of density and ENCLOSED MASS at R (M_core(R) = (1 - f_b) M_NFW(R)); pressure continuity dropped, and log P_SF/P_NFW at R reported as a diagnostic so the cost is visible. Root in w0 as before.
- Mode BFK (b1 only, no tuning): density, pressure and mass continuity, with M200 fixed at the SHMR value and c free per galaxy, c offset scanned over -1 to +1 dex (17 values) from Diemer19, then interpolated. Reports how many galaxies admit it, the median c offset needed, and the grade.
Everything else as frozen.
