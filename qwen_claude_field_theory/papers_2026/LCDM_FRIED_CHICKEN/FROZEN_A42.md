## Common halo engine (my stand-in; nothing here is taken from a simulation)
Per SPARC galaxy (lib.load, Y 0.5/0.7): M200 = lib.m200_of(M*, dn) (z = 0 SHMR), c = Diemer19 c(M200, z) x 10^dc, f_b = min(M_b/M200, 0.5), initial dark mass (1 - f_b) NFW. Grid of 200 log shells from 1e-3 r_first to max(R200, 3 r_last). Baryon mass m_b(r) from SPARC v_bar (log interpolation; inside the first point m_b(r_1)(r/r_1)^2; beyond the last point constant). No baryon contraction in the primary rows (shells start where NFW puts them; contraction is R1's knob, not this lane's). The graded curve is g_b(observed) + G D(<r)/r^2, D the final dark mass. Mass ordering after a response: shells sorted by final radius (shell crossing allowed by re-sorting, stated). Control C_E: with no family effect the engine must reproduce lib's (1 - f_b) NFW curve to 1e-3 dex.

**(a)(i) DM -> dark radiation, lifetime tau.** Decayed fraction of the dark mass present at halo formation: F(t) = f_ddm [1 - exp(-(t - t_f)/tau)], t_f = cosmic age at z_f = 2 (fixed), t = age at the graded epoch; F = 0 for t < t_f. Decays before z_f only lower the collapsed mass, absorbed into the SHMR mass (stated). Slow mass loss (tau >> t_dyn), circular-orbit adiabatic invariant per shell: r_f [m_b(r_f) + D_f(shell)] = r_i [m_b(r_i) + D_i(shell)], D_f = (1 - F) D_i (damped fixed point, 40 passes). Knobs: log tau [Gyr] in [0, 3] with f_ddm = 1 (1 knob); + dn, dc in +-0.3 (3 knobs). f_ddm and tau enter only through F at z = 0, so a 2-knob row would be degenerate: reported as such, not fitted. Published-like row, 0 knobs: tau = 160 Gyr (CMB edge, from memory).
**(a)(ii) two-body decay with kick v_k (Peter-Benson type, daughter mass loss eps = v_k/c neglected).** Keplerian shell energy E = -G M(<r)/(2 r) (my stand-in, Sanchez-Salcedo 2003 / Peter 2010 shell picture from memory). Direction-averaged daughter energy E + v_k^2/2. A shell's daughters escape if v_k^2 >= G M(<r)/r (Keplerian v_c, stated as an approximation to v_esc); otherwise the shell keeps its mass and its mean energy rises by F v_k^2/2. Escaping shells lose F of their mass and expand adiabatically as in (i). New radius from |E_f| = G [m_b(r_f) + D_f]/(2 r_f) (damped fixed point). Knobs: log tau [0, 3], log v_k [km/s] in [0.5, 3] (2); + dn, dc (4). Published-edge row, 0 knobs: tau 30 Gyr, v_k 50 km/s.
Native scales: tau (a time), v_k (a speed). Accelerations they make: c/tau and v_k/tau. The clock is cosmic time t, not rho_DE.

**(b) Primordial black holes as all the DM, monochromatic mass m (fraction f_PBH = 1).** Halos CDM-like: P0 = the engine's NFW, 0 knobs. Galaxy-scale effect (the only one): two-body relaxation by PBHs. (b1) dark core: the A19/A15 SIDM isothermal-core solver (`recipe21.py` core(), reused unchanged) with the per-particle rate rho <sigma v>/m replaced by 1/t_relax, i.e. <sigma v>/m -> G^2 m ln Lambda/(0.34 sigma^3), ln Lambda = 10 fixed, age T = min(10 Gyr, age(z)) as A19; the core radius r1 is where t_relax = T.
