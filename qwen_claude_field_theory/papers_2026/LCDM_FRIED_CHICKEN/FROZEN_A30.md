## Question

## Sources (all PROVISIONAL, from memory unless stated; no web look-up made, nothing downloaded)

- k-mouflage: Babichev, Deffayet, Ziour 2009 (IJMPD 18, 2147); Brax & Valageas 2014 (PRD 90, 023507/023508); Barreira, Brax, Clesse, Li, Valageas 2015 (PRD 91, 123522, "K-mouflage gravity models that pass Solar System and cosmological constraints"). Action S = int sqrt(-g)[M_Pl^2 R/2 + M^4 K(chi)] + S_m(A^2(phi) g), chi = -(d phi)^2/(2 M^4), A = exp(beta phi/M_Pl), K = -1 + chi + K0 chi^m. The constant -1 is the cosmological-constant term, so M^4 ~ rho_DE is how the model is built (the field is the dark energy), not a derived output. Typical values from memory: beta ~ 0.1, K0 ~ 1, m = 3.
- Hu & Sawicki 2007 (PRD 76, 064004), n = 1. Thin shell: Khoury & Weltman 2004. f(R) is a chameleon with beta = 1/sqrt6 (2 beta^2 = 1/3); a body is screened when |Phi_N|/c^2 > 1.5 |f_R|. Bounds from memory: |f_R0| <~ 1e-5 clusters, <~ 1e-6 to 1e-7 dwarf distance indicators (Jain et al. 2013), ~1e-8 rotation-curve/warp analyses (Desmond & Ferreira 2020); MW screening needed for the Solar System at about f_R0 <~ 1e-6 (Hu & Sawicki).
- Symmetron: Hinterbichler & Khoury 2010 (PRL 104, 231301). A(phi) = 1 + phi^2/(2 M^2), V = -mu^2 phi^2/2 + lambda phi^4/4; symmetry restored for rho > rho_SSB = mu^2 M^2; broken vev phi0 = mu/sqrt(lambda). DE-scale choice: rho_SSB ~ rho_crit, mu^-1 ~ Mpc. Galaxy-symmetron: Burrage, Copeland, Millington 2017 (PRD 95, 064050) fit disc rotation curves with a symmetron fifth force; O'Hare & Burrage 2018 MW stellar kinematics. Not reproduced here.
- Coupled quintessence: Amendola 2000 (PRD 62, 043511); DM-DM force G(1 + 2 beta^2) within the (Hubble-scale) range; baryons uncoupled. CMB bound beta <~ 0.05 to 0.07 (Planck 2015 XIV, from memory). N-body (Baldi et al. 2010, CoDECS): halo concentrations change by of order 10 % at beta 0.1 to 0.2, sign and size from memory, so the concentration response is treated as a knob, not a prediction.
- GW170817 (c_T = c to 1e-15): all four routes are conformally coupled, so c_T = c and they survive (Ezquiaga & Zumalacarregui 2017; Creminelli & Vernizzi 2017; from memory). LLR: k-mouflage needs specific K to pass LLR perihelion/Nordtvedt bounds (Barreira et al. 2015, from memory); f(R) passes if the MW is screened; DM-only coupling does not touch baryons.

## Routes and rows

### (a) k-mouflage (primary)
Spherical static equation K'(chi) phi' = beta M(<r)/(4 pi r^2 M_Pl), fifth force y = beta phi'/M_Pl. Exact rewrite in accelerations: y [1 + lambda (y/a_K)^(2n)] = 2 beta^2 g_N, with n = m - 1, lambda = m K0, a_K = sqrt2 beta M^2/M_Pl. Define the switch a_s = a_K lambda^(-1/(2n)); total g = g_N + y. Unscreened (y << a_s): g = (1 + 2 beta^2) g_N. Screened: y -> a_s (2 beta^2 g_N/a_s)^(1/(2n+1)). The switch in Newtonian acceleration is g_* = a_s/(2 beta^2). With M^4 = rho_DE (energy density): sqrt(rho_DE)/M_Pl = sqrt(8 pi) AC, so a_K = sqrt(16 pi) beta AC and, at lambda = 1, kappa_* = g_*/AC = sqrt(4 pi)/beta. Derived: the form of the switch and the bound y <= 2 beta^2 g_N (K' >= 1). Imposed: M^4 = rho_DE (model building), beta, K0, m. The background chi_bg is neglected against the static |chi| (stated approximation).
Analytic expectation written now: g/g_N <= 1 + 2 beta^2 everywhere while the law's g/g_N grows without bound as g_N -> 0; the screened y grows as g_N^(1/(2n+1)) while the law's y -> a0 is flat. The k-mouflage transition is the inverse of the law's: linear boost at low g, sub-linear at high g. Fails E1 in every row.
- KA0 natural, no halo (scalar replaces DM): m = 3, K0 = 1, beta = 0.1, M^4 = rho_DE. 0 knobs.
- KH0 natural, LCDM + k-mouflage: same scalar on g_N = g_b + g_NFW (coupling universal, so the boost acts on the halo too). 0 knobs.
- KA1 shape fit, no halo: log10 beta in [-2, 1.5], log10 a_s/A0 in [-4, 4], n in [0.25, 10]; 3 knobs, least_squares, 4 starts; holdout.
- KH1 shape fit, LCDM + k-mouflage: KA1's 3 plus SHMR dn in [-0.54, 0.54]; 4 knobs; holdout.
- KT tie row (the swoop): no halo, M^4 = rho_DE and K0 = 1 imposed (so a_s = sqrt(16 pi) beta AC 3^(-1/4) at m = 3; for general n, lambda = n + 1), beta and n fitted: 2 knobs; holdout.
Tie gates on KT and KA0 (and reported for KA1/KH1): G1 kappa_eff = fit_a(model curve on all SPARC)/AC within 0.05 dex of 0.5; also print kappa_* = g_*/AC. G2 slope: 0.5 if M^4 = rho_DE is imposed (by construction), 0 otherwise; graded "PASS by construction" or FAIL, never as derived. G3: Lagrangian constants, ratio 1, trivial pass. GT: a(z)/a(0) = 1 (M, beta constant; the A(phi) drift neglected), residual log10 1 - T(z), PASS only if <= 0.05 at all three z. G4 per-galaxy fit_a rms over hosts with SHMR log M200 11 to 12.5 <= 0.1 dex. G5 operative: median y/g_N over SPARC points >= 0.1.
Cassini (Sun as a point mass, MW field ignored, nonlinear superposition not modelled, stated): primary gate y at Saturn (9.58 AU) < 1e-3 a0 (folder criterion, A9; Cassini ranging bounds on anomalous acceleration at Saturn are of that order, provisional) AND PPN-like |gamma - 1| ~ 2 y/(g_N + y) at 1.6 R_sun (the 2002 conjunction ray) < 2.3e-5. Both printed for KA0, KA1, KH1, KT.

### (b1) Chameleon f(R), Hu-Sawicki n = 1
Screening variable: Newtonian potential. Phi(r) from baryons (integral of g_b to the last point plus G M_b(last)/r_last) plus the (1 - f_b) NFW potential (untruncated). Thin shell: r_scr where |Phi(r_scr)|/c^2 = 1.5 |f_R0| (ambient field = cosmic f_R0; environmental screening ignored, which favours the model); y(r) = (1/3) G [M(<r) - M(<r_scr)]/r^2 for r > r_scr, 0 inside; M inside the first point as r^3. Compton length lambda_C = c sqrt(3(n+1)|f_R0|/R0), R0 = 8 pi G (rho_m + 4 rho_L)/c^2; checked much larger than galaxies (Yukawa suppression ignored).
- F0 control f_R0 -> 0 (plain NFW + baryons). F7, F6: |f_R0| = 1e-7, 1e-6 (allowed range). F5: 1e-5 (excluded by galaxies, shown). 0 knobs each.
- FK: log10 |f_R0| on a grid -8 to -4 (17) times SHMR dn -0.54 to 0.54 (11), 2 knobs, best by rms to law; holdout on the same grid.
- Screening map printed: fraction of galaxies with r_scr beyond the first point and beyond the last point at 1e-7, 1e-6; median |Phi|/c^2 at the last point.
- Derived acceleration: the only acceleration the model builds from its own scales is g_C = c^2 |f_R0|/lambda_C = c sqrt(|f_R0| R0/(3(n+1))). G1 on g_C at 1e-6 and 1e-7 (and |f_R0| needed for kappa 0.5). G2 slope of g_C over the three J cosmologies (OL, H0) = (0.70, 67.4), (0.685, 73), (0.65, 73) at fixed f_R0. G4 on FK's per-galaxy fit_a. G5: median y/g_N over SPARC at 1e-6.
- Cassini: Sun alone, F5/g_N = (1/3) min(1, 4.5 |f_R|/|Phi_sun|), Phi_sun = GM_sun/(R_sun c^2); with MW screening (|Phi_MW(8 kpc)|/c^2 > 1.5 f_R0, Phi_MW from an NFW 1e12 Msun Diemer19 halo plus a 6.5e10 Msun point), ambient f_R = f_R0 (rho_bar_R/rho_loc)^2, rho_bar_R = rho_m + 4 rho_L, rho_loc = 0.1 Msun/pc^3. ### (b2) Symmetron, adiabatic stand-in
Field tracks its local minimum: phi^2 = phi0^2 max(0, 1 - rho/rho_SSB). Fifth force on matter a5 = -c^2 d/dr[phi^2/(2 M^2)] = -c^2 Phi_s (d rho/dr)/rho_SSB where rho < rho_SSB (0 elsewhere), Phi_s = phi0^2/(2 M^2) (dimensionless, the thin-shell potential). rho(r) spherical-equivalent: baryons from d M_b/dr/(4 pi r^2) (np.gradient on measured points, clipped >= 0), plus NFW analytic where a halo is used. Adiabatic validity (Compton length << density scale height) not checked, stated. g = max(g_N + a5, 0.01 g_N).
- S0 DE-scale: rho_SSB = rho_DE (mass density), Phi_s = 1e-6, with halo. 0 knobs. Print min rho over measured points against rho_SSB.
- SH: with halo, grid log10 rho_SSB [kg/m^3] -27 to -18 (19) times log10 Phi_s -9 to -3 (25); 2 knobs. SB: baryons only (the galaxy-symmetron reading), same grid. Holdout for the better of SH, SB on the same grid.
- Derived scale: none is an acceleration; the force is c^2 Phi_s |d ln rho/dr| rho/rho_SSB, set by density and density gradients. G1 graded on fit_a/AC of the best row, as a diagnostic of the fitted level; G2: the density threshold carries rho_DE only if rho_SSB = rho_DE is imposed; G5 for S0 (median a5/g_N >= 0.1). GT: constants, ratio 1, graded as for k-mouflage.
- Cassini: solar wind rho = 1.2e-20 (r/AU)^-2 kg/m^3 (provisional): where rho < rho_SSB, a5 = c^2 Phi_s 2 rho/(r rho_SSB) at Saturn, gate < 1e-3 a0, for SH/SB best and S0.

### (c) Coupled quintessence, DM-only coupling, evolving field
Baryons feel standard gravity from the dark mass: no fifth force on baryons. DM feels G(1 + 2 beta^2) from DM. beta = 0.05 (allowed, provisional).
