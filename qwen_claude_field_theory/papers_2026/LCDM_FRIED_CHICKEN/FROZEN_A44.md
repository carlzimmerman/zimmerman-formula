Literature inputs, all FROM MEMORY unless stated (no web look-up made for this lane; PROVISIONAL):
- Halo shape with baryons: dark-only halos have minor-to-major c/a about 0.6 to 0.7 inside 0.1 R200; baryon condensation rounds them to about 0.8 to 0.95 inside, oblate and aligned with the disc (Kazantzidis et al. 2004, Abadi et al. 2010, Chua et al. 2019 Illustris, Prada et al. 2019 Auriga). Range used for the reading: q in [0.8, 0.95]; fixed literature row q = 0.85.
- Truncation: Baltz, Marshall & Oguri 2009 truncated NFW, rho_t = rho (r_t^2/(r_t^2 + r^2)), n = 1. Field halos: the density steepens near the splashback radius, about 1 to 1.5 R200 (Diemer & Kravtsov 2014); r_t below R200 needs a host's tide (satellites). Fixed rows k_t = r_t/R200 = 0.5, 1, 2.
- Substructure: bound subhalo mass fraction about 0.1 within R200 at Milky Way mass (Springel et al. 2008, Aquarius), radially anti-biased: cumulative fraction S(x) = (1 + a c) x^beta / (1 + a c x^2), x = r/R200, a = 0.244, beta = 2.75 (Gao et al. 2004 number profile, used for mass; provisional).
- Assembly bias in c: at fixed mass below M*, halos in denser large-scale environments are older and more concentrated (Wechsler et al. 2006, Gao & White 2007); amplitude taken as 0.1 dex in log c per unit standardised environment density (my reading of the literature, PROVISIONAL); SPARC is mostly field, so its mean environment is taken as -0.5 standard deviations (my judgement, no SPARC environment column exists).

## Equations as implemented (my derivations from standard results; sources named)

Halos. Plain: (1 - f_b) NFW, f_b = min(M_b/M200, 0.5), M200 from lib.py's SHMR at M* = 0.5 L, Diemer19 c(M200), 200c, no contraction (A20's halo). R11b: recipe18 gal3 at K11B (Einasto alpha 0.16, Blumenthal contraction, tanh feedback core), rebuilt with a context (rho_crit, c-grid) so that tie gates can move the cosmology; control: reproduces gal3 to < 1e-6 dex at base.

(a) Flattened halo, homoeoid formula (Binney & Tremaine 2008 eq. 2.132; my use). Density on m^2 = R^2 + z^2/q^2, minor axis along the disc axis; q < 1 oblate, q > 1 prolate along the disc axis. Midplane circular speed
 v_c^2(R) = G Int_0^R (dM/dm) dm / sqrt(R^2 - e^2 m^2), e^2 = 1 - q^2,
with dM = 4 pi q rho(m) m^2 dm the homoeoid mass. Normalisation (primary, "geo"): the homoeoid of in-plane semi-axis m holds the mass that the spherical profile holds at the geometric-mean radius q^(1/3) m, so dM/dm = q^(1/3) M_sph'(q^(1/3) m). This keeps the spherically averaged profile near the spherical fit that c(M) describes; checked by control C3 (spherically averaged mass of the flattened NFW against M_NFW, |difference| reported at r_s/10, r_s, R200 for q 0.8 and 0.5). Sensitivity row ("major"): dM/dm = M_sph'(m), the in-plane axis keeps the spherical mass, which overstates the in-plane force and is not the like-for-like comparison (disclosed, not primary). Implemented as a ratio F_q(r) = v_c,flat^2 / v_c,sph^2 of the base profile (NFW, or the uncontracted Einasto for R11b) multiplied onto the dark mass after contraction and core: an approximation (as A25's HD and SI responses), stated. Gauss-Legendre 48 nodes on m in [0, R]. Controls: C1 q = 1 gives F = 1 (< 1e-6); C2 singular isothermal sphere analytic F = q^(1/3) arcsin(e)/e (geo) and arcsin(e)/e (major) against quadrature for q 0.8 (< 1e-4).
 Knobs: q fixed 0.85 (0 knobs); q global in [0.3, 1.5] (1 knob; [0.8, 0.95] is the literature range, outside it disclosed); q tied to baryons q = q0 (Sigma_b/100 Msun pc^-2)^s_q, clipped to [0.3, 1.5], s_q in [-0.5, 0.5] (2 knobs), Sigma_b = M_b/R_d^2 as in A16 N2.

(b) Truncation (BMO n = 1): M_t(<r) = Int_0^r M_sph'(s) r_t^2/(r_t^2 + s^2) ds, r_t = k_t R200, rho_s kept (mass removed), ratio M_t/M_sph on the dark mass. Gauss-Legendre 48 nodes; control C4 k_t = 1e6 gives ratio 1 and the quadrature of M_sph' matches M_sph (< 1e-4). Knobs: k_t fixed 0.5, 1, 2 (0 knobs); log10 k_t global in [-1.5, 1] (1 knob). Reported: median r_last/R200 and the fraction of points with r > r_t/2; the change at the last point; diagnostic past the data on the plain row, at 2 and 4 r_last with g_b = G M_b,last/r^2 (no data graded there).

(c) Substructure (smooth fraction). Reading S2 (graded): c(M) describes the smooth host; total M200 kept; dark mass (1 - f_b)[(1 - f_sub) M_sph(r) + f_sub M200 S(min(r/R200, 1))], with Gao's S above (c in S is the halo concentration). Knobs: f_sub 0.1 fixed (0); f_sub global in [0, 0.5] (1).

(d) Environment-dependent concentration. d1 mean shift for a field sample: log c -> log c + Delta_env; 0-knob row Delta_env = 0.1 x (-0.5) = -0.05; fitted row Delta_env in [-0.1, 0.1] (1 knob).
