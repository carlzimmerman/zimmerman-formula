\begin{abstract}\noindent
The non-baryonic mass is real, conserved and gravitating; there is no particle in it.
What the data measure about it is one relation, and that relation is a measurement, not a hypothesis: around galaxies the enclosed
non-baryonic-to-baryonic mass ratio follows $X(x)=\tfrac12(\sqrt{1+4x^2}-1)$ with $x=r/r_M$, to a worst bin median of 0.057\,dex
canonical and 0.021\,dex alt over $x=0.18$ to 10, against a pre-registered line of 0.10 (SHP1, 2700 points, 149 galaxies).
Form B is the claim that this component is the cold, clustering sector of cosmology --- cold dark energy --- counted once, present in
galaxies as the law's halo and present in hot systems at the cosmic share.
This note states form B as six rules and then reports the result that ends it: {\bf the amount cannot balance.}
Two exact results cap what a halo can hold. T4: inside any ball whose boundary free-falls, the net polarization charge is exactly zero,
at all times, for any momentum equation, so no charge is manufactured during infall. LFS1: for a standing clock the polarization is frozen,
its charge moving only by order $|\Phi|/c^2\sim10^{-6}$, so a static halo cannot grow.
The halo is therefore whatever charge the clock carried in, and the record's own infall dynamics deliver 1.06\,$M_b$ while the law's
halo needs 41.5\,$M_b$ at the inner edge of KiDS's window and 97.8\,$M_b$ at its middle edge, against a cosmic budget of
5.41\,$M_b$ that is already spent at 38\,kpc. KiDS rejects the cosmic share on its own, at $+4.85$/$+4.93\sigma$.
The last flank --- that the missing amount is really missing baryons in $g_{\rm bar}$ --- is closed today by GAS1: the gas the
reading needs is a fixed column $\Sigma_{\rm gas}=a_0/(\pi G x_b^2)=6.16$/$7.45\,M_\odot\,{\rm pc}^{-2}$ out to 2.6\,Mpc, and putting it
into $g_{\rm bar}$ moves the outer amplitude to $0.039\pm0.006$/$0.032\pm0.006$ against the record's 0.891 gate.
The reading destroys the fit it was invoked to save.
What survives is stated last, with what to do about it.
\end{abstract}

\section{The law as a measurement}
The statistic is SHP1's. $X=M_C(<r)/M_b(<r)=g_{\rm obs}/g_{\rm bar}-1$, the enclosed non-baryonic over the enclosed
baryons, against $x=r/r_M$ with $r_M=\sqrt{GM_b/a_0}$; it is scored as $Y=\log_{10}(1+X)$, so an offset is dex in
$g_{\rm obs}$ at fixed $g_{\rm bar}$. The law's prediction is
$X(x)=\tfrac12(\sqrt{1+4x^2}-1)$: $\simeq x^2$ inside $r_M$ (density $\sim1/r$), $\simeq x$ outside it
(density $\sim1/r^2$). This is the counted-once law, certified in Lean.

\textbf{SPARC.} SPARC has 2700 points in 149 galaxies. Its reduction is reproduced exactly (175 files, 2700 points against
Lelli+2017's 2693, and 149 galaxies against 153). The law's shape is followed in every bin. The worst bin median is within
0.057\,dex canonical (0.021 alt) for $x$ from 0.18 to 10, against a line of 0.10. Independently, form B passes
SPARC rotation curves at rms 0.0563 (line 0.0660) canonical and 0.0558 alt, with no galaxy truncated inside its
last measured point (median truncation radius 186\,kpc).

\textbf{KiDS.} On B21's isolated lenses the data have the law's shape and sit above it: $1.40\pm0.03$/$1.28\pm0.03$ times
the law, roughly flat from $x\sim15$ to $\sim200$ ($p$ of order $10^{-33}$/$10^{-21}$). On the host-free subset with every
catalogued neighbour carrying the same share, form B {\bf passes} its frozen gate at the 1.6\,$R_h$ veto, for the share
$X=59.49$/$59.50\,M_b$; the measured outer amplitude there is $1.4002\pm0.2211$/$1.2740\pm0.2012$ of the law.

That is the certified accuracy: 0.057/0.021\,dex on 2700 rotation-curve points, and a pass on KiDS host-free lenses whose
error bar is $\pm0.22$/$\pm0.20$ of the law's level. Both are quoted, not rounded, and both are reproduced by
PAPER73\_audit.py from the lane files named beside them.

\section{Form B, in six rules}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Fields.} The metric, one clock field that picks a slicing, and a polarization on its slices.
  Matter couples to the metric only. The action is QX17's (A), certified (label CERTIFIED).
\item \textbf{One conserved charge.} The clock equation is a conserved current. The universe carries 5.41 units of its
  charge per unit of baryons, a fitted number.
\item \textbf{Falling clock.} Where the clock free-falls with the expansion the charge is cold dust. This is the cold
  component of cosmology.
\item \textbf{Standing clock.} Where the clock stands still around baryons its own equations give the law, with
  $\kappa=\tfrac12$ fitted. The halo is the charge itself and gravitates and lenses as mass.
\item \textbf{Counted once.} A halo is made of the charge its region brought. A hot system holds 5.41 times the baryons
  that fell into it. A body that brought no charge has no halo.
\item \textbf{The joint.} How a falling clock becomes a standing one is not derived.
\end{enumerate}
Rules~1 and~2 are certified or fitted; rules~3 to~5 are two solutions of one action; rule~6 is the missing equation,
and the hot branch of rule~5 is a rule and not a solution of the action.

\subsection{T4, the conservation cap (exact)}
Inside any ball whose boundary free-falls, the net polarization charge is exactly zero, at all times, for any exchange
rate, alignment and multipole --- and this holds {\it for any momentum equation}, variational or not, provided the Legendre
map is kept. It is Gauss's theorem, not dynamics, so no candidate is exempt from it by changing the dynamics. Its two
landed records are: \textbf{T4 GAUSS (exact; SMF1 S0b reproduced)} (\texttt{toe\_2026\_11\_10/AKS1/FROZEN\_CRITERIA\_T.md})
and \textbf{S0b (exact, Gauss)} (\texttt{closure\_2026\_11\_10/SMF1/RESULT.md}), where the identity is the statement that
in a region bounded where the frame free-falls the frame's polarization charge is zero at every time, so in net the exchange
feeds only the frame's dust part and the boundary fluxes.
The consequence is one line: {\bf a halo's polarization charge cannot be manufactured during infall.}

\subsection{LFS1, the standing halo is frozen (exact)}
LFS1 asks whether the clock's leafwise flux can supply the halo and returns {\bf ILL-POSED}. The flux is not a separate
supply; it is the normal equation's own bookkeeping, and it adds no charge. Spherically, the polarization flux through a
sphere is carried with the clock's flow, so with a standing clock it is frozen, and the enclosed charge inside a sphere
that moves with the clock changes only by a fraction of order $|\Phi|/c^2$, about $10^{-6}$. The lane states the consequence
plainly: {\bf a static halo cannot grow.} (Its other two failures, the edge and the match, do not depend on its
amendment: the static region ends where the clock falls freely, so its total polarization charge is zero, and the
match to the expanding clock outside is a wall.)

T4 and LFS1 together are the cap, and it is not a claim about a mechanism:
