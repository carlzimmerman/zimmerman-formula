\begin{abstract}\noindent
We report one night of structural lanes on a gravity framework whose galactic law is $g_N=g^2/(g+a_0)$ with $a_0=\tfrac12 c\sqrt{G\rho_{\rm DE}}$ (the $\tfrac12$ is fitted). Its extra gravitating mass is a collisionless ``cold energy'' medium, not a particle. The question was what any consistent theory of that medium must look like. Every lane froze its criteria before computing and ran a mutation control. Post-hoc amendments are disclosed, and both $a_0$ footings ($9.36\times10^{-11}$ and $1.13\times10^{-10}$\,m\,s$^{-2}$) are reported and never pooled. The companion note PAPER56 has the data-side results of the previous day.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{A covariant parent.} A fluid effective theory of comoving labels is a covariant parent of the record's static field equation. It reproduces the law to $2.2\times10^{-16}$ and has an isotropic dust background, a derived field-aligned stress, linear stability on static backgrounds and slip $\le1.54\times10^{-5}$, under five conditions (CONDITIONAL). On expanding or contracting backgrounds its constraint pair is Hadamard ill-posed, and its tensor speed is not exactly $c$ (Correction v1.1). The medium's defining identity holds exactly through shell crossing, so the record's postulate that freed cold energy stays free cannot be derived from it.
\item \textbf{Four no-go results.} (D-T) If the medium's excess is the law's phantom, the law's deep limit forces the tether to cancel the medium's own gravity: no linear self-gravity, and no gravity between tethered components. (G) No local Lagrangian in the medium's expansion and tether invariant is ghost-free, gives the law and stays off in the linear universe, unless every static region carries a vacuum offset 15--25 times the slip bound. (L-G, T-O2) A local second-order field that supplies the deep law must carry an external-field effect, so with no satellite external-field effect the law's extra mass must be the cold energy itself. (S) Any tether without membership labels makes a satellite's medium hover while its stars fall, which the satellite data exclude.
\item \textbf{Non-local parents.} No causal non-local parent has the record's cosmological engine as its exact limit. The engine's source relocation cannot be conserved, its RMIN cut switches the law off for 128 of 175 SPARC galaxies, and its phantom is a global QUMOND field with an external-field effect. On the positive side, a parent that changes the source of gravity has no hover, $a^1$ growth and a forced zero-sum.
\item \textbf{Energy.} The record's settling does not conserve energy (CFG932). No energy-conserving repair that keeps the released settling energy inside matter brings an infalling halo onto the law (CFG935: NO REPAIR under the frozen rule). Read post hoc, the only form found that holds the law needs a dynamical dark-energy partner plus a thermal reservoir.
\item \textbf{The labelled-domain construction, tested.} Only labelled domains, one tether per collapsed object, pass the satellite test. Written down (CFG950), they remove the satellite hover and the linear-growth flag, but energy at label events closes only through a non-dynamical label energy: NOT CONSISTENT AS SPECIFIED under the frozen rule, CONDITIONAL only under a post-hoc amendment. In clusters they fail (CFG951): members' labelled media source the host's law, and the predicted eRASS1 outer shear overshoots ($Z=-9.3$ canonical, $-11.9$ alternative). The engine design is SMOKE PARTIAL (CFG952): the momentum-conserving draw is infeasible, and at $1024^3$ the engine applies the law only above $2.19\times10^{11}\,M_\odot$ of retained baryons. No complete parent survives, and the two-footing shear pass of the earlier notes remains a computational result of an engine whose physical parent is not established.
\item \textbf{Data.} Faint gas-rich rotators do not reach the dwarfs' accelerations (not diagnostic). Coma ultra-diffuse galaxies sit above the framework's derived ceiling, but not at $3\sigma$ once systematics are included. Clusters modelled as untethered cold energy match the shear stack at the primary corner, but the match is fragile.
\end{enumerate}
\end{abstract}

\subsection{Theorem D-T: the tether cancels the medium's own gravity (CFG941)}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Assumptions.} A cold static medium is held by any always-on tether force $F(u)$, and its phantom is its own gravitating mass, so $g=g_N+u$.
\item \textbf{Statement.} Equilibrium gives $F(u)=g$. With the law this is $F(u)=a_0u/(a_0-u)=u+u^2/a_0+\dots$ For any law with the deep limit $g_N\to g^2/a_0$, the linear coefficient is 1, whatever the higher terms. Only $c_2=1$ gives the baryonic Tully--Fisher relation: with $F=c_2u+u^2/a_0$ the limit is 1 at $c_2=1$, infinite at $c_2=\tfrac12$ (an acceleration floor) and 0 at $c_2=2$ (rescaled Newton). The linear self-gravity coefficient is $(1-c_2)K\rho_c$, and the mutual term between two tethered components carries the same factor. So the law implies no linear self-gravity of the settling medium and no gravity between tethered components.
\item \textbf{The diagnosis.} This is not a double count of the source ($\nabla^2\Phi=K(\rho_b+\rho_c)$ appears once). It is a force cancellation: the quadratic term of $W_{\rm full}$ gives the tether a term that cancels the medium's own Newtonian pull.
\item \textbf{What it rules out.} No repair passes every test. Replacing $W_{\rm full}$ (R1), splitting free and tethered populations (R2: growth needs a tethered fraction $f_t\le0.060$, so the free mass, $\times15.6$, enters the law's source), a density gate (R3n) and a range gate (R3$\ell$: the required windows lie 4.0--6.7 decades apart) are NOT REPAIRABLE. A turnaround gate (R3$\theta$) is PARTIAL: the law, the background, $a^1$ growth and slip pass, but it has a ghost at $k_gR=0.23$--2.19.
\item \textbf{The hover lemma.} In any static cold tethered equilibrium, medium at rest feels no net force. A satellite's tethered medium therefore feels no host force while its stars do: $\Delta a/g_{\rm host}=1.02$--1.11. The stars leave their medium in 9--106\,Myr, and a static satellite would lose 0.10--0.73\,dex of phantom, an external-field effect. So the record's reading that satellites show no EFE (CFG925 R3) is not respected. This is an estimate with its assumptions listed.
\item \textbf{Shear.} The class-A engine is not the non-relativistic limit of any tested local parent, so the T1x/T2x/NC1x shear passes do not transfer. Lane class: STRUCTURAL OBSTRUCTION. Amendment A1 was pre-result (a numerical test point moved to respect the law's own $\sqrt y$ correction).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Theorem G: no local gate cures the ghost (CFG944)}
\begin{itemize}

\subsection{Lemma L-G and theorem T-O2: cold energy must be the phantom (CFG946)}
