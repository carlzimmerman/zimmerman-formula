\title{Observing Case: Does the Galactic Acceleration Scale Evolve?\\ One Deep-MOND Rotator at $z\simeq2.5$ Decides}

\begin{abstract}
Companion to the pre-registered measurement (doi:10.5281/zenodo.22563139), written for observers. Two readings of the radial-acceleration scale $a_0\simeq1.2\times10^{-10}\,{\rm m\,s^{-2}}$ predict opposite redshift behaviour: tied to the dark-energy density, $a_0=\kappa c\sqrt{G\rho_\Lambda}$, it is constant; as the emergent scale of $\Lambda$CDM haloes it tracks $H(z)$ and rises by $2.1^{1/4}=1.20$ in rotation speed at fixed baryonic mass by $z\simeq2.5$. The frozen statistic is the deep-MOND Tully--Fisher zero-point shift, $0.00$ dex (constant) versus $+0.33$ dex (rising), decidable at $\pm0.13$ dex with a single galaxy that is genuinely deep-MOND, rotation-dominated, gas-measured and lens-controlled. We give the target gate, the NIRSpec-then-ALMA funnel, the per-galaxy error budget, the current candidate ledger (no known object passes the gate today; the decisive galaxy must be found from low-mass highly magnified lensed discs), the controls, an order-of-magnitude time request, and the frozen decision rule. Either outcome is a publishable, pre-registered result.
\end{abstract}
\section{The two predictions}

$\Delta_{\rm BTFR}=\log_{10}M_b-4\log_{10}V_f-C_0$ & $0.00$ dex & $+0.33$ dex\\
flat speed of a $60\ {\rm km\,s^{-1}}$ dwarf & $60$ & $72$ km s$^{-1}$\\
required precision per decisive galaxy & \multicolumn{2}{c}{$\sigma(\Delta_{\rm BTFR})\le0.13$ dex}\\\bottomrule\end{tabular}\end{center}
$C_0$ is frozen from the local calibration on both $a_0$ footings ($9.36\times10^{-11}$, $1.13\times10^{-10}\ {\rm m\,s^{-2}}$) before any high-redshift value is examined. Estimator: galaxy median, then median across galaxies; no drift nuisance is introduced after observation. The existing archive (twelve published constraints) is exhausted and prior-dominated; it cannot decide.
\section{The target gate}
A galaxy enters the decisive sample only if all hold: (1) $g_{\rm bar}(R_{\rm out})<0.30\,a_0$ on both footings at the upper end of the mass uncertainty, from the resolved stellar and gas distribution (preferred $<0.20$); (2) $V_{\rm rot}/\sigma>1.5$ from a forward-modelled source-plane disc fit with dispersion, inclination, beam smearing, asymmetric drift, clumps and outflows fitted together (preferred $>2$), regular monotonic field; (3) $\delta\log_{10}\mu<0.08$ dex with no credible alternative reconstruction outside the budget; (4) $35^\circ<i<75^\circ$ and $\ge5$ source-plane resolution elements along the major axis; (5) $2.32<z<3.12$ so that H$\alpha$ and [O\,III] fall together in NIRSpec G235H/F170LP and CO(3--2) falls in ALMA Band~3. Failing (1) or (2) means rejection without interpretation.
\section{The funnel and the budget}
\textbf{Stage 1, NIRSpec IFU G235H/F170LP:} a two-dimensional velocity field and a forward-modelled source-plane velocity cube (never a de-lensed rotation curve); advance only if the rotation gate passes and $g_{{\rm bar},*}+g_{\rm gas,max}<0.3\,a_0$ with a conservative upper gas estimate. \textbf{Stage 2, ALMA Band 3 CO(3--2):} integrated H$_2$ mass and a resolved CO velocity field; two independent tracers are required and a material discrepancy invalidates the target. $M_b=M_\star+1.36(M_{\rm HI}+M_{\rm H_2})$ with $X_{\rm CO}$ and excitation propagated. \textbf{Budget:} $\delta V/V<5\%$ (combined tracers), $\delta\log_{10}M_b<0.10$ dex, $0.05$--$0.07$ dex for inclination, lens model and asymmetric drift, hence $\delta\log_{10}\mu\lesssim0.05$--$0.08$ dex (this alone removes MACS J0451, whose lens models disagree, 21.5 vs 49).
\section{Candidates, controls, time}
The committed ledger of 21 objects, scored on both footings, contains \textbf{no object that passes the flagship gate today}: every measured rotator is high-acceleration and every deep-MOND candidate lacks resolved rotation. Inside the joint window: Cl~0949+5153 arc ($z=2.39$, merger, rotation unknown); A1689B11 ($z=2.54$, $V/\sigma=8.7$, high-acceleration: a control); A68-HLS115 ($z=2.49$, no kinematics); Abell 68 C4 ($z=2.622$) and C20b ($z=2.689$), unscored low-mass lensed candidates with H$\alpha$, [O\,III] and CO(3--2) all reachable: the first Stage-1 targets. Outside the window, SL2S~0217 ($z=1.84$, $\mu=17$, deep-MOND score 0.92, rotation unknown, CO(4--3) in Band~4) is the best deep-MOND candidate if the window is relaxed to H$\alpha$ only. Mandatory controls: a high-acceleration rotator in the window (A1689B11) to recover the local zero-point where both predictions agree, and a cold-tracer control (zC-400569, $z=2.24$, $V/\sigma=20$). Time, order of magnitude and not an exposure-time-calculator run: NIRSpec IFU $\sim3$--$5$ h per candidate ($10$--$20$ h for 2--4), ALMA Band 3 $\sim5$--$10$ h per source ($10$--$20$ h for 1--2).
\section{Decision rule}
Near $0.00$ dex the constant law survives and the rising reading is excluded at the stated precision; near $+0.33$ dex the constancy prediction is falsified; inconsistent with both is evidence against both and is reported as such. One galaxy is enough if and only if it passes the gate. A null on the gate itself (no galaxy passes) is informative about the deep-MOND population at $z\simeq2.5$.
