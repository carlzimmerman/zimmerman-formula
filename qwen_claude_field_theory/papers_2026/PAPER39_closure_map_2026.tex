\begin{abstract}
\noindent The programme ties the MOND acceleration scale to the vacuum, $a_0=\kappa c\sqrt{G\rho_\Lambda}$, with $\kappa=\tfrac12$ fitted, never derived. Its best current effective law, candidate B, is the MOND kernel plus hierarchical ownership (only the outermost bound system carries the phantom) plus a cold component with the cosmic amount $\Omega_ch^2\approx0.12$: the mass is still required, and no dark-matter particle is added. No committed action produces B. This note is a map and a specification, not a result. (i)~Three gaps separate B from a mechanism: a legal owned switch (Gap~1), the cold fluid that \emph{is} the law's dark density (Gap~2), and the $a_0$--$\Lambda$ tie with its coefficient (Gap~3). (ii)~A Lean library of 331 theorems (standard axioms only) certifies, from stated premises, that ownership is not a function of local field data, that a continuous law with no external-field effect is linear, that a length $\propto M^{1/2}$ needs an acceleration scale, and that an AQUAL action gives the kernel in spherical symmetry; it certifies no empirical fact. (iii)~We tabulate 25 committed routes to the missing object: 22 are scoped no-gos on their frozen classes, two owner-directed hand-checks find no mechanism, and one shows realisability only. None is a mechanism. (iv)~The failures sharpen the target into a specification: an early cold fluid present before structure forms (a dust created at turnaround supplies $\Omega h^2=0.1200\times10^{-1376.8}$ at $z=1100$); ownership that remembers boundness (a causal latch keeps $n=0.791$ at one free-fall time against the required 0.99); a distribution that carries the $M^{1/2}$ scale itself (passive bound infall scales as $M^{0.33-0.34}$, and relaxation at a vacuum-set rate reaches $\Gamma\tau\le0.79$ against the $\approx4.1$--4.2 needed); a target that is field-local in the Newtonian field but needs bound-domain labels; and no created rest mass. (v)~B's ultra-faint failure (3.5--3.9$\sigma$) does not move under the 2026 binary-corrected and DEIMOS dispersions, and the bound-core ($\Lambda$CDM-comparator) reading is preferred at satellites by small margins: a lean, not a detection. (vi)~$\kappa=\tfrac12$ is fitted ($0.465\pm0.076$ from the baryonic Tully--Fisher relation), and Milgrom's $cH_0/2\pi$ footing fits SPARC as well. (vii)~Gaia DR4 (2 December 2026) is a survival test of B against the bare MOND law; B, $\Lambda$CDM and Newton all predict $\hat\gamma=1.000$.
\end{abstract}

\section{The specification}\label{sec:spec}

Each item is a necessary condition read off the failures of scored classes; none is shown sufficient, and the set is not shown jointly satisfiable. CFG230 lists twelve requirements of the same kind (R01--R12) and finds that only R01 and R02 are met by any scored row as a mechanism.
\begin{enumerate}[label=S\arabic*.]
\item \textbf{An early cold fluid, present before structure.} The fluid must exist with the cosmic amount at recombination. A conserved dust created at turnaround supplies $\Omega h^2=0.1200\times10^{-1376.8}$ at $z=1100$, and in a baryon-only universe the trigger never fires (CFG243; the baryon-only $\sigma_b$ is 0.076 against the threshold 1.276). % AUDIT: B33
\item \textbf{Ownership that remembers boundness, on that early fluid.} A causal doubled-field latch passes causality and bound-only rows but forgets a static bound system ($n(t_{\rm ff})=0.791$ against 0.99); a post-hoc energy-driven repair needs 5.0 free-fall times and an untied coupling (CFG242). A first-crossing rule gives ownership to the smallest progenitors: only 0.16--0.21 of the baryons first turn around in a system of at least 0.1 of the final mass (CFG243, post hoc). Ownership is not field-local (Lean, Section~\ref{sec:lean}).
% AUDIT: B34
\item \textbf{A distribution that carries the $M^{1/2}$ acceleration scale itself.} By the dimension theorem an acceleration scale must enter. Passive infall does not supply it ($M^{0.33}$; CFG118, CFG244), and a relaxation at a vacuum-set rate is too slow: the 10\% line needs $\Gamma\ge$ 5.37 / 5.17 $H_\Lambda$ (can/alt) at $\tau=t_0$, and partial relaxation misses the RAR by 0.15--0.28 dex against the 0.10 line (CFG245). \item \textbf{Field-local in the Newtonian field, but labelled by bound domains.} The law's local-field (QUMOND phantom) density is a function of the Newtonian field, but it is not the CFG44 target for extended baryons (ratio up to 2.33 for P2), and the local form does not give ownership (internal-to-Newtonian ratio up to 3.16 where ownership needs 1): the local target needs non-local labels, a bound domain and a supply cap (CFG245, G0).

\textbf{Satellites.} The ultra-faint test is capped near 2.5$\sigma$ by systematic floors; what would move it is multi-epoch, binary-tested dispersions for more systems [O26, MC10] and a collapse-mass floor that data can shrink. Gate H's lean would need its power raised well above 0.28 before it could be called a preference.

\section{Limitations}\label{sec:lim}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{The menu was written knowing the target.} The doors, door 11's variants and the four swings are not a blind sample of theory space; several frozen hand estimates were wrong and are kept in the lane READMEs (for example CFG243's survival ratio, wrong in direction).
\item \textbf{Referees were not blind.} The independent re-derivations saw the README numbers; CFG242--245 were re-run, not re-derived; CFG244's Gate A is not independent of CFG118.
\item \textbf{Scope.} Every failure is a scoped no-go on its frozen class. Untested across the doors: non-spherical baryons, mergers, relativistic completions, Boltzmann-code CMB runs, a growing baryon core, a non-Lorentz-invariant vacuum, and Hossenfelder's covariant Lagrangian. Several gates fail post hoc, after a stop rule, and those cells are labelled post hoc in their lanes.
\item \textbf{Declared control failures are kept,} for example CFG244's energy-drift control and CFG245's MG2 premise; they do not change the binding failures but are part of the record.
\item \textbf{Literature.} Some literature facts in the lanes are from memory and unverified, as each lane states.
\item \textbf{What is not claimed.} Nothing here is claimed closed; $\kappa=\tfrac12$ is fitted; nothing says the data favour the framework or $\Lambda$CDM; the cold mass is still required, and no dark-matter particle is introduced. A lean is not a detection.
\end{itemize}

\section{Reproducibility}\label{sec:rep}

Every number carried by an audit row of \fpath{PAPER39_audit.py} is re-read from a committed file at git HEAD: the script checks each value against its committed source (a regex with one capture group over \texttt{git show HEAD:<path>}) and checks that the value is printed in the tex block carrying its audit tag. The tex side is checked at block level, not sentence level. It has two mutation modes (an altered expected value; an altered tex literal) that must fail. Numbers quoted only in running text and the references' journal data are not checked by the script. The sources it reads are the status page, the closure-map files, the lane READMEs of CFG28, CFG29, CFG118, CFG131, CFG230, CFG231, CFG232, CFG242--CFG245, CFG251, CFG253 and CFG259, the ChainCert README and verifier output, and the two notes cited for $\kappa$. No new analysis is done here.
