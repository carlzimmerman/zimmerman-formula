\begin{abstract}
\noindent The programme ties the MOND acceleration scale to the vacuum, $a_0=\kappa c\sqrt{G\rho_\Lambda}$, with $\kappa=\tfrac12$ fitted, never derived. Its best current effective law, candidate B, is the MOND kernel plus hierarchical ownership (only the outermost bound system carries the phantom) plus a cold component with the cosmic amount $\Omega_ch^2\approx0.12$: the mass is still required, and no dark-matter particle is added. No committed action produces B. This note is a map of failed routes and a reading of what they suggest, not a result. (i)~Three gaps separate B from a mechanism: a legal owned switch (Gap~1), the cold fluid that \emph{is} the law's dark density (Gap~2), and the $a_0$--$\Lambda$ tie with its coefficient (Gap~3). (ii)~A Lean library of 331 theorems (standard axioms only) certifies, from stated premises, that the postulated ownership rule is not a function of the internal and external field at a point, that a continuous law with no external-field effect is linear, that among monomials a length $\propto M^{1/2}$ needs an acceleration scale, and that an AQUAL action gives the kernel in spherical symmetry; it certifies no empirical fact. (iii)~We tabulate 25 committed routes: 22 are scoped no-gos on their frozen classes, two owner-directed lanes find no mechanism (11D's hand-check, and CFG253's background calculation, which asks where the cold amount comes from rather than how the missing object arises; without it the count is 24), and one shows realisability only. None is a mechanism. (iv)~The failures suggest a specification. It is a reading of the scored classes, not a theorem: only the acceleration-scale item rests on a theorem (for monomials) and the cosmic amount on the inherited CMB gate; the rest rests on the failure of one to three scored classes per item. The reading asks for an early cold fluid present before structure forms (a dust created at turnaround supplies $\Omega h^2=0.1200\times10^{-1376.8}$ at $z=1100$); ownership that remembers boundness (a causal latch keeps $n=0.791$ at one free-fall time against the required 0.99); a distribution that carries the $M^{1/2}$ scale itself (passive bound infall scales as $M^{0.33-0.34}$, and relaxation at a vacuum-set rate reaches $\Gamma\tau\le0.79$ against the $\approx4.1$--4.2 needed); a target that is field-local in the Newtonian field but needs bound-domain labels; and no created rest mass. (v)~B's ultra-faint failure (3.5--3.9$\sigma$) does not move under the 2026 binary-corrected and DEIMOS dispersions, and Gate~H leans toward the bound-core ($\Lambda$CDM-comparator) reading at satellites by small margins (planted-sample power 0.28, carried entirely by the ultra-faints): a lean, not a detection. (vi)~$\kappa=\tfrac12$ is fitted ($0.465\pm0.076$ from the baryonic Tully--Fisher relation), and Milgrom's $cH_0/2\pi$ footing fits SPARC at least as well as either framework footing. (vii)~Gaia DR4 (2 December 2026) is a survival test of B against the merged (no-ownership) law: decisive against Arm~A's $\nu_{\rm RAR}$ kernel, 2.3--3.7$\sigma$ against the P2 kernel (canonical footing, $N=30{,}000$), and no separation from the derivation chain's ceiling; B, $\Lambda$CDM and Newton all predict $\hat\gamma=1.000$.
\end{abstract}

\section{The law, candidate B, and the three gaps}\label{sec:law}

\textbf{The law.} $a_0=\kappa c\sqrt{G\rho_\Lambda}$ with $\kappa=\tfrac12$ fitted; two footings are carried, canonical 9.36e-11 and alternative 1.13e-10\,m\,s$^{-2}$ (written can/alt). With the monotone kernel $\nu_{\rm mono}$ the law fits SPARC's radial acceleration relation with an rms of 0.1003\,dex, against 0.2755 for Newton (CFG4, re-run clean). The distinctive galaxy-scale content and the high-redshift side (a flat $a_0(z)$, which a decisive test would need baryon masses calibrated to 0.10\,dex to see) are reported in PAPER38 [P38] and not repeated here.

\textbf{Candidate B} is an effective law: the kernel, a hierarchical ownership rule (a top-level bound system carries the phantom; an owned subsystem is Newtonian internally), and a conserved cold fluid with $\Omega_ch^2=0.1200$ whose amount is fitted. The cold mass is still required; candidate B adds no particle species (doors 4 and 7 scored particle models as routes, and both failed). The status page's bottom line is that ``no committed action produces candidate B'' (\fpath{campaign_fresh_gravity/STANDING_2026-09-29.md}).

\textbf{Gap~1, the legal owned switch.} A gate multiplying a shift-symmetric field leaves the dynamical mass equal to the baryonic mass beyond its edge (Gauss); local gates are unstable in 44 of 48 cases; a nonlocal enclosed-mass gate is stable in 48 of 48 with no new constant, but it is bilocal (not a legal local term) and its edge sits at 0.11--0.24 $r_{\rm ta}$, below B's window (CFG48); a dynamical gate scalar is stable only with two new untied constants (CFG49).
% AUDIT: B04

\textbf{Gap~2, the cold fluid that is the law's dark density.} For a spherical baryon profile the fluid must satisfy $C(r)=\rho_c r^3 g_{\rm tot}=(a_0/4\pi)M_b(<r)$ (CFG44). No local or equation-of-state closure gives it, the tidal-tensor closure fails (CFG50), and written as a memory-kernel or light-cone exchange it reacts on the baryons at 0.065--22.5 $g_{\rm law}$ and costs 23--73$\times$ their orbital energy in one $r_{\rm ta}$ convention, 57--318$\times$ in B's committed one (CFG70, CFG72). A documentary count of 54 committed constructions finds none that derives the dispersion from an action (D = 0, P = 11, I = 5, X = 27, U = 11). In this record Gap~2 reduces to Gap~1's object, as an identification, not a theorem.

\textbf{Gap~3, the tie.} The tie can act on a conserved fluid's stress cap with no new local degree of freedom, but the cap's entry is postulated (CFG43), and a barotropic saturating cap needs a new constant $\nu^*$ and engages over a convention-dependent window of 11$\times$ up to 1e4--1e5 in mass, against the 1e4 the BTFR needs. The coefficient $\kappa$ is the subject of Section~\ref{sec:kappa}.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Ownership is non-local.} If $\nu\ne1$, a top-level and an owned system with the same internal and external fields get different internal accelerations, so no function of the internal and external field at a point reproduces the rule (\texttt{ownership\_distinguishes}, \texttt{ownership\_not\_field\_local}); given the postulated rule this is close to definitional, and the rule itself stays a postulate. B's own kernel meets the premise (\texttt{CandidateB.ownership\_nonlocal}).
\item \textbf{No external-field effect forces linearity.} A continuous map with $F(z+z_e)-F(z_e)=F(z)$ is linear (\texttt{noEFE\_continuousLinear}); separately, no pointwise law with MOND's deep limit is EFE-free, even without continuity, in scalar or vector form (\texttt{law\_not\_efeFreeRay}, \texttt{vecLaw\_not\_efeFree}). This covers pointwise (QUMOND-type) laws only, not the AQUAL or QUMOND field equations. Ownership escapes because it is non-local; anisotropic linear, linear-in-source and screened laws also escape (DOOR11\_RESULT, Addendum~2).
\item \textbf{The dimension theorem.} No monomial in $(G,M,c)$ is a length $\propto M^{1/2}$; with one extra constant $X$ such a length exists if and only if $(G,c,X)$ form an acceleration scale. What is forced is an acceleration scale, not that $X$ is an acceleration ($\hbar$ is admissible). Monomials only.
\item \textbf{The AQUAL action gives the kernel.} For an AQUAL $\mu$ with $x\mu(x)$ increasing onto $(0,\infty)$, the spherical Gauss form $\mu(g/a_0)g=g_N$ is equivalent to $g=\nu(g_N/a_0)g_N$, and P2's kernel follows from $\mu=(\sqrt{1+4x^2}-1)/(2x)$. Premises: the reduced Euler--Lagrange equation and regularity; spherical symmetry only. Ownership and the cold-mass rule stay postulates.
\item \textbf{The calibration wall (CFG240).} In the deep limit $g_{\rm obs}$ depends only on $f\cdot a_0$ ($f$ a baryon calibration); for exact P2 two distinct $g_{\rm bar}$ identify $(f,a_0)$; with $f$ free, $\sigma(\log a_0)\ge3\sigma/\sqrt N$ (conditional on local slopes in $[0,\tfrac12]$, independent equal Gaussian errors and an invertible Fisher matrix). These are the theorems behind PAPER38 [P38].
\end{itemize}

\section{A reading of the requirements}\label{sec:spec}

Each item is a reading of the failures of scored classes: a necessary condition only inside those classes' hypotheses (CFG230); none is shown sufficient, and the set is not shown jointly satisfiable. Only two parts rest on more than the failure of one to three scored classes: S3's acceleration scale (the dimension theorem, for monomials) and S1's cosmic amount at recombination (the inherited CMB gate). CFG230 lists twelve requirements of the same kind (R01--R12) and finds that only R01 and R02 are met by any scored row as a mechanism.
\begin{enumerate}[label=S\arabic*.]
\item \textbf{An early cold fluid, present before structure.} The fluid must exist with the cosmic amount at recombination. A conserved dust created at turnaround supplies $\Omega h^2=0.1200\times10^{-1376.8}$ at $z=1100$, and in a baryon-only universe the trigger never fires (CFG243; the baryon-only $\sigma_b$ is 0.076 against the threshold 1.276). On the status page's reading, a turnaround source can at most add to or relabel the early fluid. Continuous vacuum-to-fluid transfer after recombination can make only a few per cent of it (CFG253). The premise of the conservation rule [P36] is exactly what such classes cannot supply.
% AUDIT: B33
\item \textbf{Ownership that remembers boundness, on that early fluid.} A causal doubled-field latch passes causality and bound-only rows but forgets a static bound system ($n(t_{\rm ff})=0.791$ against 0.99); a post-hoc energy-driven repair needs 5.0 free-fall times and an untied coupling (CFG242). A first-crossing rule gives ownership to the smallest progenitors: only 0.16--0.21 of the baryons first turn around in a system of at least 0.1 of the final mass (CFG243, post hoc). Ownership is not field-local (Lean: a consequence of the postulated rule, Section~\ref{sec:lean}).
% AUDIT: B34
\item \textbf{A distribution that carries the $M^{1/2}$ acceleration scale itself.} By the dimension theorem (monomials) an acceleration scale must enter; that the distribution itself carries it is the ten-door reading. Passive infall does not supply it ($M^{0.33}$; CFG118, CFG244), and a relaxation at a vacuum-set rate is too slow: the 10\% line needs $\Gamma\ge$ 5.37 / 5.17 $H_\Lambda$ (can/alt) at $\tau=t_0$, and partial relaxation misses the RAR by 0.15--0.28 dex against the 0.10 line (CFG245). The galaxy and cluster windows overlap at 1.73--1.93 $H_\Lambda$ only in the generous bracket (empty in the central one; post hoc), and no vacuum candidate provides such a rate.
% AUDIT: B35
\item \textbf{Field-local in the Newtonian field, but labelled by bound domains.} The law's local-field (QUMOND phantom) density is a function of the Newtonian field, but it is not the CFG44 target for extended baryons (ratio up to 2.33 for P2), and the local form does not give ownership (internal-to-Newtonian ratio up to 3.16 where ownership needs 1): the local target needs non-local labels, a bound domain and a supply cap (CFG245, G0).
% AUDIT: B36
\item \textbf{No created rest mass.} The heat ledger is not the obstacle (at most 1.9e-4 of the vacuum energy of the ball, CFG242), but for any class that creates rest mass from the vacuum the ledger is an obstacle (post hoc): $f(30)=24.8$ in CFG48's $r_{\rm ta}$ convention and 1.7 against 1 on the Lagrangian-volume ledger; in B's own $r_{\rm ta}$ convention $f(30)=$ 0.30--1.65, and the failure stands through the $a_0$-shift line (CFG243, CFG131).
% AUDIT: B37
\item \textbf{The inherited gates.} Cold at $z\gtrsim10$ to $k=30$/Mpc ($c_s^2\le$ 4.608e-12, CFG230 R05); reaction and energy within G3 (exchange forms give 22.5 $g_{\rm law}$, CFG242); no new constant (G4); an external-field branch chosen explicitly (ownership, an EFE that DR4 can see, or one of the other escapes of the pointwise theorem: linear-in-source, anisotropic linear or screened laws); and a Solar tail handled by ownership or by screening $\ge$ 3.5e3 for any kernel with $(yq)'\ge0$ inside the G1 band (tail $\ge$ 0.28205 $a_0$, CFG230 R09).
% AUDIT: B38
\end{enumerate}
In one sentence, the reading is: an early cold fluid with the cosmic amount, owned by a memory-carrying, bound-domain assignment, whose distribution carries the $M^{1/2}$ scale itself; neither passive infall nor vacuum-rate relaxation supplies it, and no committed construction derives it.

\section{Satellites: where B already fails}\label{sec:sat}

\textbf{The failure.} Under ownership an accreted satellite obeys the isolated law of its infall baryons. For the Milky Way ultra-faints that law under-predicts the dispersions: the Kaplan--Meier median offset is $+0.3245\pm0.038$ (3.77$\sigma$, canonical) with the nine upper limits restored and a 0.077-dex systematic floor (CFG28); tides and noise do not explain it, and the published binary bounds do not either (CFG29). The $\Lambda$CDM side is weakly diagnostic here: its pass window spans 3.5--5 decades of halo mass.
% AUDIT: B39

\textbf{The 2026 dispersions (CFG259).} Re-scoring with the DEIMOS archive [G26] and the binary corrections of [AP26] moves B's median by at most 0.005 dex; the significance stays 3.5 to 3.9$\sigma$ on both footings. The corrections reach 8 of 40 systems, with a median of $-0.068$ dex (free binary fraction) or $-0.104$ dex ($f=0.7$), a fifth to a third of the offset. The one place they bite is CFG29's weak-binary subsample (a reported row, not a verdict), which drops to 2.8$\sigma$ / 2.6$\sigma$ under the $f=0.7$ correction. The Bo\"otes~I conflict between the multi-epoch value and the single-epoch correction changes none of this.
% AUDIT: B40

\textbf{Which reading the satellites lean toward (CFG244 Gate H, re-scored by CFG259).} Reading (a) is ``satellites own nothing'' (B's isolated law); reading (b) is a bound cold core, which is the record's $\Lambda$CDM comparator (a stellar-to-halo relation [M13] with NFW concentrations [D08]). On the committed values (b) leads by $\Delta\chi^2$ +13.58 / +11.32 (P2 kernel, record's error model), but the frozen class is H4 (ambiguous) because the record's own kernel falls to +8.60 in the strict view on the alternative footing. Under every 2026 variant both kernels cross the line and the class moves to H2; the margins are small (the variant DV3 sits only 0.24 above 9), the planted-sample power is 0.28, the whole lean comes from the ultra-faints (without them $\Delta\chi^2$ is $-1.3$ to $-1.8$), and 33 of the 40 ultra-faints take the clamped stellar-to-halo value $10^9\,M_\odot$, an extrapolation.
% AUDIT: B41

\textbf{What this implies.} If the lean held, an accreted satellite would keep a bound cold core of order $10^9\,M_\odot$ rather than obey the isolated law of its stars. For B, the ownership rule would have to let satellites own a bound remnant of the early fluid; equally, the satellites would then be described by the $\Lambda$CDM comparator: a bound-core reading that matches $\Lambda$CDM at satellites is $\Lambda$CDM there (CFG244), and Gate~D (post hoc) finds that the route reduces to $\Lambda$CDM plus the $a_0$--$\Lambda$ coincidence. The class cannot supply that collapse mass from its own constants, and at hosts the same class fails Gate A; H2 says that the bound-core route and B differ at satellites, ``not a lean for the framework''. It says nothing about hosts, and it is a lean, not a detection.

\section{Gap 3: the coefficient}\label{sec:kappa}

\textbf{$\kappa$ is fitted.} The measured values are $0.465\pm0.076$ from the baryonic Tully--Fisher relation and $0.547\pm0.175$ distance-free; $\tfrac12$ lies inside both, and the Unruh/de Sitter route is retired ($n=2$ gives $\kappa=1.447$, 12.9$\sigma$ off). On the footing question, Milgrom's $cH_0/2\pi$ fits SPARC at least as well as the framework ($\Delta\chi^2$ 5.34, against 7.04 alt and 63.9 canonical, relative to the free best fit), and ``beats $1/2\pi$'' holds only on the $\rho_\Lambda$ footing. Written as $\kappa c\sqrt{G\rho}$ with $\rho=3H_0^2/(8\pi G)$, the critical density, Milgrom's value is $\kappa=\sqrt{2/(3\pi)}=0.461$ (Lean, \texttt{Footing}); with $\rho=\rho_\Lambda$ it is about 0.56.
% AUDIT: B42

\textbf{The $32\pi$ puzzle.} Writing $C=\Lambda_{\rm geom}/a_0^2=32\pi$ into the canonical-clock construction forces $R=G_{\rm cosm}/G_N<0.824$, while BBN alone gives $G_{\rm BBN}/G_0=0.98\pm0.06$ at 95.4\% [A20, eq.~9]: that branch is excluded under its transfer assumptions (constant couplings through BBN, standard radiation and rates, negligible clock stress, the local Newton calibration); evolving couplings and extra radiation are untested, and $C>1198$ follows at $R\ge0.92$ (a lane result the orchestrator did not re-run). This is a conditional no-go for one route to $32\pi$, not for $32\pi$ itself; the later variable-clock results are toy-level, and the rational 4 in $G\rho_\Lambda=4a_0^2$ stays underived. Another agent's note (\fpath{gemini_pi_puzzle/SOLVING_THE_PUZZLE.md}), which says the puzzle is solved, is read on the status page as an interpretation, not a derivation. It sets $R^*\equiv c/\sqrt{G\rho_\Lambda}$ with $R^{*2}\Lambda=8\pi$ and $a_0=c^2/(2R^*)$; the algebra is correct, but the step that borrows a horizon surface gravity at a radius that is not a horizon is postulated. The status page's wording stands: a coherent physical reading of the $32\pi$, not a derivation of $\kappa=\tfrac12$.

\section{Limitations}\label{sec:lim}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{The menu was written knowing the target.} The doors, door 11's variants and the four swings are not a blind sample of theory space; several frozen hand estimates were wrong and are kept in the lane READMEs (for example CFG243's survival ratio, wrong in direction).
\item \textbf{Referees were not blind.} The independent re-derivations saw the README numbers; CFG242--245 were re-run, not re-derived; CFG244's Gate A is not independent of CFG118.
\item \textbf{Scope.} Every failure is a scoped no-go on its frozen class. Untested across the doors: non-spherical baryons, mergers, relativistic completions, Boltzmann-code CMB runs, a growing baryon core, a non-Lorentz-invariant vacuum, and Hossenfelder's covariant Lagrangian [H17] beyond the class-V reconstruction scored in door 12. Several gates fail post hoc, after a stop rule, and those cells are labelled post hoc in their lanes.
\item \textbf{Declared control failures are kept,} for example CFG244's energy-drift control and CFG245's MG2 premise; they do not change the binding failures but are part of the record.
\item \textbf{Literature.} This note compiles the programme's committed lanes; it does not review the literature on these classes, and each lane scores its own frozen version of a class, not its authors' full theory. Some literature facts in the lanes are from memory and unverified, as each lane states.
\item \textbf{What is not claimed.} Nothing here is claimed closed; $\kappa=\tfrac12$ is fitted; nothing says the data favour the framework or $\Lambda$CDM; the cold mass is still required, and no dark-matter particle is introduced. A lean is not a detection.
\end{itemize}
