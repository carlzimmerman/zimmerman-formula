\section{The action, in Legendre form}
PAPER68 prints $S=\frac1{4K}\int\!\sqrt{-g}\,[R-2\mathcal J(Y)+2\mathcal K(Q)]-\frac{\gamma_L}2\int\!\sqrt{-g}\,[\max(D\!\cdot\!P,0)]^2+S_m$.

The form every lane has actually solved puts $\mathcal J$ in Legendre form (QX17):
\begin{equation}
\frac{\mathcal L}{\sqrt{-g}}=\frac{R-2\Lambda+2\mu_K^2(Q-1)^2}{4K}+P^mA_m-W(K|P|)-\gamma_L F(\Pi)+\lambda\,n^mP_m,\qquad
W(p)=\frac{a_0^2}{K}\Big[-\frac p{a_0}-\ln\!\Big(1-\frac p{a_0}\Big)\Big],
\label{eq:A}\end{equation}
with $F(\Pi)=\tfrac12[\max(\Pi,0)]^2$, $\Pi=D_mP^m$ and $\gamma_L=KL_\star^2/2$, plus minimally coupled matter $S_m$.
Maximising over $P$ returns the printed $\mathcal J$ exactly: $\max_P[P\!\cdot\!A-W(K|P|)]=(a_0^2/K)[u-\ln(1+u)]$ with $u=|A|/a_0$, attained at $K|P|=a_0u/(1+u)$ (sympy, residual 0).
So at $\gamma_L=0$, Eq.~(\ref{eq:A}) is the printed action; with $\gamma_L>0$, $P$ is an independent field and Eq.~(\ref{eq:A}) is the theory.

\section{Four walls}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Cold fluid} (KD1, QX3, QX7). Every well captures it, discs included. The CMB and BBN need $\mu_K\ge8.9\times10^6$\,Mpc$^{-1}$; galaxies allow at most 79.
\item \textbf{Warm fixed-temperature sea} (QX12). Retention $\exp(\text{depth}/c_s^2)$ is too sharp; the temperature would have to track each host.
\item \textbf{Hot thermal fermion} (HC1, HF1, VW1). Hot enough to stay out of discs, it under-fills clusters, cannot fill dSphs or UDGs, and fails CMB lensing; cold enough for the CMB, discs capture it. No window.
\item \textbf{Shock-entropy sector} (SE1). A conserved, non-cooling sector heated by its own shocks either sits isentropic, and holds almost nothing, or needs a different entropy history for each class at the same entropy. Its conserved rest mass also caps a host near the cosmic share, against the $\sim80$ the Coma UDGs need.
\end{enumerate}
The common reason: an ingredient that tracks each host's virial state has to virialise with the host; if it is also cold early, nothing keeps it out of discs. And the CMB needs the extra gravity at $z\simeq3400$, where nothing is virialised, so any key that is on only in today's hot systems is off exactly where the CMB needs it on.
