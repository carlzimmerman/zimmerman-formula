\begin{abstract}
\noindent\emph{Version 2 corrects version 1.} Version 1 presented a ``cubic, one-scale khronon'' as a repair that passes cosmology and KiDS. It does not. At the parameters that passed cosmology, the cubic term dominates the static equation in galaxies, and MOND ends at 1--4\,kpc. The repair is withdrawn, and a general identity explains why no kinetic function can supply one.
PAPER32 named one open door: a khronon sector that is itself the dark fluid, as in the theory of Blanchet \& Skordis (JCAP 11 (2024) 040). We run that theory through the same two gates, using its own equations.
\emph{Moving source (passed).} Its phantom is a conserved fluid carried by the aether, and a galaxy moving uniformly with its phantom is an exact post-Newtonian solution. The costs: the phantom is assembled rather than generated, it follows a change of velocity only inside $r\approx2GM_b/\Delta u^2$, and the static halo is stable only for a monotone phantom law.
\emph{KiDS external field and $\sigma_8$ (failed).} The MOND function reads the gradient of the khronon's own potential. The benchmark $\mu^{-1}=22.3$\,Mpc puts 24 times the KiDS-1000 bound on it. The benchmark 223\,kpc passes, but its mass term ends MOND-like lensing at $\sim$80\,kpc. The theory's own scale-dependent sound speed gives linear $\sigma_8\approx0.21$--$0.26$.
\emph{The identity.} For any kinetic function $\mathcal K(\mathcal Q)$, the static mass term a galaxy feels and the khronon's late cosmological Jeans scale are the same scale: $\mu_{\rm eff}^2=k_J^2(1+c_{\rm ad}^2)$, exactly. KiDS's field bound needs that scale below 0.22\,Mpc, while MOND-like lensing to 1\,Mpc needs it above $\sim$10\,Mpc. The khronon-as-dark-fluid aether is therefore closed by KiDS for every $\mathcal K$.
\emph{The mass budget.} Even if galaxies accrete the dark fluid as $\Lambda$CDM halos do, the conserved phantom of a KiDS lens runs out at 0.13--0.67\,Mpc, inside the clean 0.3\,Mpc range for most lens masses.
\emph{What stands.} A one-scale MOND function $\mathcal J=\Lambda\,j(\mathcal Y/\Lambda)$ makes $a_0=\kappa c\sqrt{G\rho_\Lambda}$ an identity of the action, with $\kappa=2\sqrt{8\pi}/(3\beta)$; this is algebra, and $\kappa$ is not derived. The design lesson is that the MOND function must not read the acceleration of the cosmological dark fluid.
\end{abstract}

\section{The route}
PAPER32 [Z26d] ended with a specification any completion of $a_0=\kappa c\sqrt{G\rho_\Lambda}$ must meet. It named as an open door ``a khronon sector that itself behaves as dust on FRW, the route of Blanchet \& Skordis''. That theory [BS24] is
\begin{equation}
S=\frac{c^3}{16\pi G}\int\!\sqrt{-g}\,\big[R-2\mathcal J(\mathcal Y)+2\mathcal K(\mathcal Q)\big]+S_m,\qquad \mathcal Q=c\sqrt{-g^{\mu\nu}\partial_\mu\tau\partial_\nu\tau},\quad \mathcal Y=\frac{A_\mu A^\mu}{c^4},
\end{equation}
where $A_\mu=c^2n^\nu\nabla_\nu n_\mu$ is the acceleration of the khronon's unit normal $n_\mu$.
Their MOND function is $\mathcal J=\Lambda-\mathcal Y+\frac{2c^2}{3a_0}\mathcal Y^{3/2}+\dots$ at low acceleration (their eq.~37), with $\mathcal J\to\Lambda_\infty$ at high acceleration (eq.~40). Their kinetic function is $\mathcal K=\mu^2(\mathcal Q-1)^2+\dots$ (eq.~7), in a DBI form (eq.~87) for cosmology.
Their post-Newtonian system (eqs.~19--30) has three parts:
\begin{itemize}
\item the unitary-gauge lapse $\Xi=\phi-\dot\sigma+\tfrac12|\nabla\sigma|^2$, with $\tau=t+\sigma/c^2$;
\item a khronon mass density $4\pi G\rho_\tau=-[\nabla\!\cdot(\mathcal J_{\mathcal Y}\nabla\Xi)+\mu^2\Xi]$, which is the MOND phantom;
\item a khronon velocity $v_\tau=-\nabla\sigma$.
\end{itemize}
In the static case this reduces to AQUAL with a mass term (eq.~35):
\begin{equation}
\nabla\!\cdot[f(|\nabla\phi|/a_0)\nabla\phi]+\mu^2\phi=4\pi G\rho_m,\qquad f=1+\mathcal J_{\mathcal Y}.
\end{equation}
All numbers below come from committed scripts (lanes BSK1, BSX1, BSX2, BSX3) run on both $a_0$ footings where $a_0$ enters. Each lane has a mutation control that must fail.

\section{Gate 1: the moving source --- passed (BSK1, 8/8)}
In unitary gauge the extra sector depends only on the lapse and its spatial gradient: $\mathcal Q=1/N$ and $\mathcal Y=\gamma^{ij}\partial_i\ln N\,\partial_j\ln N$. That is the shift-free class in which L330 found a frozen phantom.
BS24's own equations show why it is not frozen. The divergence of the momentum equation, combined with the time derivative of the Poisson equation and matter continuity, gives
\[
\partial_t\rho_\tau+\nabla\!\cdot(\rho_\tau v_\tau)=0 .
\]
The phantom is therefore a conserved fluid carried by the aether's flow. About flat space its transport is second order: that is L330's identity, and BS24's dispersion $\omega=0$. About a galaxy it is first order.
A galaxy moving uniformly with its phantom is an exact solution. With $\sigma=-V\!\cdot x+V^2t/2$ the aether moves with the galaxy, $\Xi=\phi$, and eqs.~27a, 29a and 35 all hold (symbolic, in comoving coordinates). With the aether pinned (the control) the same moving phantom violates continuity.
Passing the gate has three costs:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item The phantom is conserved, so it is assembled from the cosmic khronon dust, never generated by baryons.
\item About a static halo the fluid obeys, in the WKB approximation,
\begin{equation}
\omega^2=\frac{4\pi G\rho_\tau\,(k^2f_\theta-\mu^2)}{k^2(1-f_\theta)+\mu^2},\qquad f_\theta=f+yf'(y)\cos^2\theta .
\end{equation}
Its static limit is AQUAL's linear response. For $k\gg\mu$ the frequency is $k$-independent, so the phantom follows a change of velocity $\Delta u$ only inside $r\approx2GM_b/\Delta u^2$: 1.3\,kpc for the Milky Way at 620\,km\,s$^{-1}$, 860\,kpc for a $10^{14}M_\odot$ cluster at 1000\,km\,s$^{-1}$.
\item The halo is stable only if $0<f_\theta<1$, i.e.\ only if the phantom acceleration increases with $g_N$. This is the monotone-phantom condition L340 found, reached here from the fluid side.
Any tail that recovers GR (their eq.~40) makes the phantom fall. Where it falls the fluid is unstable at rate$^2=(2+2/n)GM/r^3$, the local orbital rate, whatever the tail's amplitude. With $\nu_{\rm RAR}$ the unstable region is the inner Milky Way, with e-folding times of 1--4\,Myr at 1--3\,kpc. Placing the fall above $g\sim10^3a_0$ confines it to where the phantom is below $10^{-3}$ of the field.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Gate 2 and $\sigma_8$: the theory as built fails (BSX1, 8/8)}
The argument of the MOND function is the aether's acceleration, $A=\nabla\Upsilon$, with $\Upsilon=-4\pi Ga^2\rho_K\Delta_K/(k^2+k_J^2)$ (their eqs.~61b and 67). This is the khronon's own potential gradient, filtered above the late Jeans length. The late Jeans wavenumber is $k_J=a\mu$ exactly, as the check confirms. The field is not the web's Newtonian field; that is the field which closed the programme's C-H/K construction [Z26d].
We integrate two-fluid growth (baryons plus the khronon, with their DBI $w(a)$, $c_{\rm ad}^2(a)$ and the scale-dependent sound speed of their eq.~71) from $z=1000$.
The controls reproduce the $\Lambda$CDM $\sigma_8=0.811$, and the $\Lambda$CDM web field at $z=0.25$ comes out at 0.0134\,$a_0$ against an independent mock's 0.0150. A pure-dust khronon returns $\sigma_8=0.82$--$0.85$, so the suppression below is the sound speed alone.

\section{The identity: one scale for every kinetic function (BSX3, 5/5)}
On FRW, BS24's khronon has $8\pi G\rho=\mathcal Q\mathcal K_{\mathcal Q}-\mathcal K$ and $8\pi G P=\mathcal K$. Hence $c_{\rm ad}^2=\mathcal K_{\mathcal Q}/(\mathcal Q\mathcal K_{\mathcal{QQ}})$, and the Jeans wavenumber of their eq.~71 is $k_J^2=4\pi G\rho(1+w)/c_{\rm ad}^2=\tfrac12\mathcal Q^2\mathcal K_{\mathcal{QQ}}$.
In a static region $\mathcal Q=\bar{\mathcal Q}(1-\Xi/c^2)$ (their eq.~18a with the cosmic $\bar{\mathcal Q}$), so the kinetic part of the 00 equation linearises to $\mu_{\rm eff}^2\Xi$ with
\begin{equation}
\mu_{\rm eff}^2=\tfrac12\bar{\mathcal Q}\big(\mathcal K_{\mathcal Q}+\bar{\mathcal Q}\mathcal K_{\mathcal{QQ}}\big)\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad \frac{\mu_{\rm eff}^2}{k_J^2}=1+c_{\rm ad}^2
\end{equation}
exactly (symbolic, general $\mathcal K$). BS24's quadratic $\mathcal K$ reproduces their $\mu^2\Xi$ (eq.~25) and their $k_J=a\mu$.
The same second derivative at the cosmic background sets both the range of the galactic mass term and the late Jeans length, so no choice of $\mathcal K$ separates them.
With Sec.~3 this closes the route. KiDS's external-field bound needs $1/k_J\lesssim0.22$\,Mpc at $z=0.25$. MOND-like lensing to 1\,Mpc around a Milky-Way lens needs $r_C=(r_M/\mu_{\rm eff}^2)^{1/3}\geq1$\,Mpc, i.e.\ $1/\mu_{\rm eff}\gtrsim10$\,Mpc. The factor-$\sim$46 gap is larger than the order-of-magnitude uncertainty in their eq.~38.

\section{Version 1's repair, withdrawn}
Version 1 proposed $\mathcal K=\tfrac{2M^2}{3}(\mathcal Q-1)^3$ with no quadratic term. It passed $\sigma_8$, the forest proxy, KiDS and the early equation of state for late Jeans lengths of 0.3--3\,kpc (lane BSX2, which stays on the record).
It claimed that the cubic term drops out of galaxies because it is formally 1PN. That was a $c\to\infty$ limit at fixed $M^2$, and it does not hold at the parameters used: $M^2\sim10^{15}$--$10^{17}$\,Mpc$^{-2}$.
Evaluated there (BSX3 I2), the mass-term range equals the Jeans length (0.3--3\,kpc). MOND then ends at $r_C=0.9$--$4.4$\,kpc for the Milky Way, and the kinetic sector's density $M^2\Xi^2/(4\pi Gc^2)$ exceeds the MOND phantom by $10^8$--$10^{12}$ at 10\,kpc.
The control reproduces the slip: dropping the cosmic background from $\mathcal Q$, as a formal PN count does, makes the identity fail.

\section{The mass budget (BSX3)}
A conserved phantom must be assembled from dark fluid that fell in. The deep-MOND phantom grows as $M_{\rm ph}(<r)=(r/r_M-1)M_b$. We compare it with two budgets:
\begin{itemize}
\item a generous one, the $\Lambda$CDM halo mass $M_{200}$ of the lens's stellar mass (Moster, Naab \& White 2013 at $z=0.25$);
\item a tight one, the galaxy's own cosmic share $(\Omega_{\rm dm}/\Omega_b)M_b$.
\end{itemize}
For $\log M_*=10.0$--$10.9$ the generous budget runs out at 0.13--0.67\,Mpc; in 6 of the 8 lens-mass and footing cases that is inside KiDS's clean isolated range of 0.3\,Mpc. The tight budget runs out by 82\,kpc.
So a conserved phantom reproduces KiDS's MOND-like lensing only if galaxies accrete more dark fluid than $\Lambda$CDM halos hold at those radii. The swarm lane BSK3's KiDS failure corresponds to the tight budget; it is not reproduced here.

\section{The programme's relation, inside the action}
$\mathcal J(0)=\Lambda$ is the cosmological constant that cosmology measures, and the $\mathcal Y^{3/2}$ coefficient is $2c^2/(3a_0)$ (their eq.~37). With one scale in $\mathcal J$,
\begin{equation}
a_0=\frac{2c^2\sqrt\Lambda}{3\beta}=\kappa\,c\sqrt{G\rho_\Lambda},\qquad \kappa=\frac{2\sqrt{8\pi}}{3\beta},\qquad \rho_\Lambda=\frac{\Lambda c^2}{8\pi G}
\end{equation}
(symbolic). So $\kappa=\tfrac12$ is $\beta=4\sqrt{8\pi}/3=6.684$, and Planck's $\Lambda$ ($h=0.6736$, $\Omega_\Lambda=0.6847$) then returns $a_0=9.35\times10^{-11}$\,m\,s$^{-2}$, the canonical footing.
The relation stops being a coincidence between two constants: $a_0$ and the vacuum energy become two coefficients of one function. The programme's earlier principle that there is no independent $a_0$ [Z26b] becomes a property of the action.
The coefficient remains a shape number and is not derived, consistent with the zero-mode theorem [Z26a].

\section{What this leaves}
The khronon route closes on its own equations, for every $\mathcal K$. The lesson is structural.
\begin{itemize}
\item The MOND function must not read the acceleration of the cosmological dark fluid: its Jeans physics then fixes the galactic mass term.
\item It must not read the web's Newtonian field either, which is what closed C-H/K in PAPER32.
\end{itemize}
What survives is a completion in which the phantom is generated by the baryons (C-H/K's $\lambda$-channel does this, unlike a conserved fluid), the MOND kernel is blind to the large-scale field, and the dark matter is a separate component invisible to the kernel. That route is being built elsewhere in the programme.
The one-scale identity $a_0=\kappa c\sqrt{G\rho_\Lambda}$ of Sec.~7 is algebra and carries over to any action whose MOND function contains the cosmological constant as its zero-acceleration value.
Blanchet \& Skordis's Khronon-Tensor theory [BS25] is not examined.
