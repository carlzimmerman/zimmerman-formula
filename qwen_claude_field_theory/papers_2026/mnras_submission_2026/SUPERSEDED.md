manuscript. Three statements in this directory should not be reused: the a0(z) law written as sqrt(rho_DE(z)) ("0.82 at

and a galaxy bootstrap are included), and the checklist's statement that MNRAS carries no charge (it is fully open
access, with an article processing charge and a waiver route). See section 7 of the v2 checklist.
