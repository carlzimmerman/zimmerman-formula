Dear Editor,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript, "The MOND acceleration scale and the cosmological constant: what fixes the coefficient, and the observations that decide it", for publication in Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society as a Main Journal paper.

The numerical closeness of the radial-acceleration scale a₀ to c√(Gρ_Λ)/2 has been noticed repeatedly, and coefficients for it have been proposed since Milgrom (1999). The manuscript does three things that, to my knowledge, have not been done together:

1. It shows that no local relativistic MOND action of the aether–scalar class (the class containing the Skordis–Złośnik theory) can fix the coefficient: the additive constant of the MOND primitive is a zero mode of the field equations, degenerate with Λ on the cosmological background, and the two natural repairs of that zero (an empty Newtonian vacuum; a sequestering-type global constraint) fail by sign and by five orders of magnitude. A four-form construction that fixes the sign and makes a₀ ∝ √(Gρ_Λ) structural is shown to leave the coefficient as a coupling ratio, with a computed observational side effect.

2. It quantifies what the data can decide, including a point I have not seen stated: at fixed Ω_Λ the prediction scales as κH₀, so the two surviving coefficient candidates, ½ and the horizon form √(8π/3)/2π = 0.461, are exactly degenerate with the Planck-versus-SH0ES H₀ values. Both available SPARC estimators of κ are shown to carry an irreducible H₀-convention dependence, and the baryonic Tully–Fisher route is shown to have a 9.5 per cent mass-budget floor.

3. It pre-registers, before the data, two observations that decide the redshift dependence of the scale and the survival of the covariant candidate: the Gaia DR4 wide-binary boost, with two mutually exclusive arms and a fixed decision rule, and the deep-MOND Tully–Fisher zero point of one lensed rotator at z ≈ 2.5 (0.00 versus +0.33 dex).

The manuscript makes no claim that the relation or its coefficient is derived; its purpose is to state precisely what is and is not fixed, and by what. Every numerical result is produced by a script in a public repository with checks that can fail, and the pre-registrations are hash-stamped and append-only. Earlier versions of parts of this work are deposited on Zenodo (DOIs in the manuscript); no part of it has been published in a journal or is under consideration elsewhere.
