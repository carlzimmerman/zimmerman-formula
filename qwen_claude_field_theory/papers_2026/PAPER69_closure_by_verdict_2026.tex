\begin{abstract}\noindent
``Closure'' here means that every gate the programme set itself has a frozen verdict, not that every gate passes.
On 2026-11-06 the programme ran each remaining gate for the galaxy law $g_N=g^2/(g+a_0)$, $a_0=\kappa c\sqrt{G\rho_{\rm DE}}$,
and for its adopted, scoped action (v3s; PAPER68) and candidate successor (AC2q). Each lane froze its criteria before any number was computed and ran a mutation twin that must fail.
The full covariant field equations of both actions are now derived and checked against direct variation.
Every step from the action to the observables is tagged as Lean-proved, symbolic, numerical or postulated.
The law passes SPARC on the action's exact field on both footings, along with the Solar System, PPN, the tensor speed and galaxy lensing.
It fails clusters, CMB lensing and linear growth. These are no-go results over every term the action has, not missed fits.
Among galaxies it fails the X-ray ellipticals, two ultra-diffuse galaxies (Malin 1, AGC 114905) and, on the canonical footing only, the $\Upsilon$-tilt row.
The failures form a single step: hot, pressure-supported systems sit 0.32--0.37\,dex above cold discs at matched baryonic mass, acceleration, depth, radius and gas fraction.
Five candidate keys for that step were each frozen and graded: environment, cold gas, kinematic support, morphology and IMF. None explains it; IMF variation removes about half of it in ellipticals only.
We list the gates that wait on data (Gaia DR4, a proposal-class ALMA sample, the unscored ALMA release rows) and the decisions that wait on the owner.
\end{abstract}

\section{What ``closure'' means here}
A gate is closed when it carries one of four labels, each fixed by criteria committed before the first graded number: PASS, FAIL, a proven no-go (FAIL for a whole class), or CONDITIONAL with the condition named. NO VERDICT closes a gate only when the lane shows the test has no power, and says what data would give it power. A gate that is RUNNING is open. Two footings, canonical $a_0=9.3603\times10^{-11}$ and alt $1.1312\times10^{-10}$\,m\,s$^{-2}$, are carried throughout and never pooled. $\kappa$ is fitted; a Lean proof shows that it is not derivable from the programme's premises.

\section{What the record says, plainly}
\begin{enumerate}
\item The law, and the action in its scope, describe cold rotating discs (SPARC on the exact field, both footings) and the Solar System. The exceptions among discs and dwarfs are the $\Upsilon$-tilt row on the canonical footing and two ultra-diffuse galaxies. The action's health failures (light-cone causality, the raw switch) are structural; the first is far below any measurement.
\item Outside discs the law misses by a \emph{step}, not a trend. No measured present-day variable we tried keys it. Environment, cold gas, support and morphology are each killed or give no verdict. A heavier IMF in high-$\sigma$ spheroids (QX26) removes about half of the step in ellipticals and almost none in groups, clusters or dSphs, so the step that remains is carried by the systems dominated by hot gas.
\item The action has no term that can supply cluster mass or CMB-lensing growth. Those are proven no-gos over the action's whole term class, so a fix needs new physics, not a refit.
\item An idea checked after EB1, that early-collapse black holes are the hot systems' missing mass, fails on numbers already on the record. The per-star yield would have to differ by at least 15$\times$ between ellipticals and clusters, and a gas-proportional amount fails the Bullet (closure\_2026\_11\_06/IDEA\_EARLY\_BH\_DARK\_BARYONS\_CHECK.md).
\end{enumerate}
