\begin{abstract}
\noindent We work with the law $a_0=\kappa c\sqrt{G\rho_\Lambda}$, $\kappa=\tfrac12$ fitted, with the exponential kernel $\nu(y)=1/(1-e^{-\sqrt y})$, and a conserved cold fluid whose amount ($\Omega_c/\Omega_b=5.364$) is an input. In particle-mesh simulations the law's phantom over-builds small-scale structure: with the corrected pressure-filtered baryon source and a mass-conserving reservoir rule, the excess at $k\le1\,h$/Mpc is $+15.3\%$/$+19.3\%$ (canonical/alternative footing) at $256^3$ and grows to $+17.4\%$ at $512^3$. About 81\% of the switch-ON mass behind it lies inside host turnaround spheres. Re-fitting KiDS-1000 isolated-lens lensing with a free two-halo amplitude shows the law need only extend to $x\simeq0.3$--$0.5$ of the turnaround radius (best $x=0.48$/$0.53$). We note that this is where a host's real cold supply runs out: the phantom mass $M_{\rm ph}(<r)=M_b/(e^{s}-1)$, $s=r_t/r$, equals the supply $5.364\,M_b/f_{\rm ret}$ at $r_{\rm supply}=r_t/\ln(1+f_{\rm ret}/5.364)$, which for census retention $f_{\rm ret}=0.07$--$0.10$ sits at $0.24$--$0.54\,r_{\rm ta}$ for KiDS lenses and $\simeq0.24$--$0.33\,r_{\rm ta}$ for the groups and clusters that drive the excess. Inverting the relation gives a lensing-based cold-to-baryon ratio of $4.6$--$13.0$ (canonical; $6.0$--$16.5$ alternative), bracketing the CMB's $5.364$ on the canonical footing. We record this supply-edge hypothesis, its inputs and caveats (above all, the free two-halo term) and the pre-registered simulations that will test it.
\end{abstract}

\section{Framework and status}
The acceleration scale is $a_0=\kappa c\sqrt{G\rho_\Lambda}$ with $\kappa=\tfrac12$ \emph{fitted} (two footings: $9.3603\times10^{-11}$ and $1.1312\times10^{-10}$\,m\,s$^{-2}$, never pooled). The baryon-sourced phantom is $\rho_{\rm ph}=\nabla\!\cdot[(\nu-1)g_b]/4\pi G$. A cold fluid of fixed cosmic amount is required; no particle species is added and the amount is not derived. In the working model the phantom is a target that real cold fluid must fill, and a host can fill it only as far as its supply allows.

\section{How far must the law extend for lensing?}
An earlier fit required the law ON to $r_{\rm ta}$ because the two-halo amplitude was fixed at zero. With it free, KiDS-1000 isolated lenses prefer truncation at $x=0.48$ (canonical) and $0.53$ (alternative); $x=0.3$ passes with any single bin dropped. In the nine bins the survey trusts, all $x$ fit equally; the preference comes from outer bins where the free template carries much of the model. At $x=0.4$, 0.83 of the excess source lies beyond the truncation in the simulations (an estimate, not a simulation result).

\section{The supply edge}
For a point mass with this kernel, $M_{\rm ph}(<r)=M_b/(e^{s}-1)$ with $s=r_t/r$, $r_t=\sqrt{GM_b/a_0}$. Setting it equal to the supply $5.364\,M_b/f_{\rm ret}$ gives
\[ r_{\rm supply}=\frac{r_t}{\ln(1+f_{\rm ret}/5.364)}\;\approx\;\frac{5.364}{f_{\rm ret}}\,r_t , \]
equal to $5.850\,r_t$ for $f_{\rm ret}=1$. With census retention, KiDS lenses ($\log M_b=10.5$--$11.3$) have $x_{\rm edge}=0.24$--$0.54$ (canonical footing, small-$f_{\rm ret}$ form; about 10\% lower on the alternative footing), and the simulations' resolved groups and clusters, with group/cluster retention, $x_{\rm edge}=0.24$--$0.33$. Both overlap the window that lensing allows and growth needs. If confirmed, the truncation radius is not a new constant but the point where the host's cold supply runs out.

\section{A lensing estimate of the cold fluid's amount}
Inverting, $R_c=x\,r_{\rm ta}f_{\rm ret}/r_M$ with KiDS's preferred $x$ gives $4.6$--$13.0$ on the canonical footing (containing $5.364$) and $6.0$--$16.5$ on the alternative footing. This is a consistency test of the supply-edge picture, not a derivation of the amount.

\section{Pre-registered tests (running)}
(i) The switch confined to $0.4\,r_{\rm ta}$ around resolved peaks at $512^3$; (ii) the switch confined to each host's own supply edge with a declared retention-mass relation, at $512^3$; (iii) the same with $a_0$ tracking DESI's evolving dark energy. Pass: $|\sigma_8-1|\le5\%$ and $|P-1|\le10\%$ on both footings.

\section{Caveats}
The lensing window rests on a free two-halo amplitude with no external prior. Retention $f_{\rm ret}$ varies (7--18\% in the census). The reservoir is gravity-level bookkeeping, the simulations do not model baryon depletion, the confinement's legality is conditional, and a separate consequence test of a Tolman reading of the factor 4 ($\kappa=1/|1+3w_0|$) is not yet decisive with current DESI precision. $\kappa=\tfrac12$ remains fitted; the cold fluid's amount and $\rho_\Lambda$ are not derived.
