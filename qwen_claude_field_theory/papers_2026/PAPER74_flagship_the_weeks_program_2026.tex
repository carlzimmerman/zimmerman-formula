\section{The decisive measurement, with its pre-registered number}
\label{sec:decisive}

One number carries the whole shortfall: the share $X_s=59.5\,M_b$ that ONE1 needs to pass KiDS host-free,
against a cosmic budget of 5.41. The two are reconciled only if a galaxy's halo holds about 11 baryons' worth of
cosmic share sitting at the radii KiDS measures. The record's own words put the flank first: ``with B21's own
hot-gas estimate in $g_{\rm bar}$, its published outer points drop from 1.50 to 0.47 of the law. Nothing on disk
measures that gas.''

{\bf The measurement.} Not a new instrument and not a new exposure: a {\bf stacked thermal Sunyaev--Zel'dovich
measurement in the ACT DR6.02 needlet-ILC Compton-$y$ map already on this disk}, centred on the {\bf 17,335
KiDS host-free isolated L$\star$ lenses} the record has already built, run through GO1's existing Part~A
pipeline, which already stacks KiDS shear and this $y$ map with randoms, jackknife errors, a gNFW beam
correction, two CIB treatments and three nulls. It is decisive because $y$ is linear in the very quantity the
budget fixes:
\[
  y(b)=\frac{\sigma_T}{m_ec^2}kT_e\,N_e(b),\qquad N_e(b)=\int n_e\,dl=\Sigma_{\rm proj}(b)/(\mu_em_p),
\]
so $N_e$ {\it is} the required surface density; there is no clumping factor and no $\langle n^2\rangle$ bias, and
in the budget-forced ($\rho\sim R^{-1}$) reading the plateau is flat in $b$. Sharper still, the fixed column is
exactly the statement $g_{\rm bar}(R)=\pi G\Sigma_{\rm gas}=a_0/x_b^2=2.699196\times10^{-12}$\,m\,s$^{-2}$
canonical ($3.262001\times10^{-12}$ alt) --- a parameter-free flat baryonic-acceleration plateau from
$\sim66$\,kpc to 2.6\,Mpc.

{\bf The pre-registered number.} A straight stack of 17,335 lenses measures the projected gas column to 1.0\,\%
of the requirement at 2.6\,Mpc ($\sigma(\Sigma)/\Sigma_{\rm gas}=0.0101$, S/N 99 if the column is real) and to
8.8\,\% at 0.3\,Mpc (beam-limited, S/N 11). If the column is real this is a $\sim100\sigma$ detection; if it is
not, the reading is dead by the same factor. There is no outcome in between. The amplitude gate that any gas in
$g_{\rm bar}$ must pass is the record's own: {\bf The pre-registered acceptance line is 0.891}, against which
GAS1's budget-fixing column gives $0.0392\pm0.0069$/$0.0324\pm0.0057$.

{\bf The data already lean that way, but are not yet inside the kill region.} GO1's ACT-$y$ stack of 102 eRASS1
groups ($\sim10\times$ an L$\star$ halo, so expected to carry more gas, not less) supplies $f_{\rm col}=0.187\pm0.102$
of the requirement at 1.87\,Mpc, a deficit of $+8.0\sigma$, reaching $+13.3\sigma$ at 7.46\,Mpc. Against the only
Mpc-scale electron densities in the repo the required column is over by 2.5$\times$ ($+5.7\sigma$) at 0.9\,Mpc
and 10.5$\times$ ($+26\sigma$) at 1.9\,Mpc. Both facts are the reason to run the stack rather than declare
victory: 0.19$\pm$0.10 sits only $+0.9\sigma$ above the 0.10 line, and those are groups, not isolated galaxies.

{\bf The dilemma the measurement resolves.} Flat column $\Rightarrow\rho\sim R^{-1}\Rightarrow2483\times$ the
cosmic mean baryon density at 0.3\,Mpc and a gas mass inside 1\,Mpc of $101\times$ the total baryons an L$\star$
halo can hold: impossible. A normal halo shape $\rho\sim R^{-2}$ matched at 1\,Mpc gives 0.77$\times$ the
requirement at 2.6\,Mpc and over-supplies at 0.3\,Mpc, which drives the outer points to 0.03--0.04 of the law:
short. No third shape exists. Both branches are excluded by measurements already on disk.

\section{The framework: the scaling symmetry, the flat direction, and the forced convention}
\label{sec:framework}

Nine sectors (NPJ1) and four dedicated routes (FINAL-6, FINAL-16, FINAL-17, FINAL-19) failed to derive
$\kappa=\tfrac12$, and one route (FINAL-18) falsified the only condition that was not a flat direction. The
reason is structural, not numerical.

\subsection{The exact symmetry}
The formation physics depends only on the ratio $a_0/g$. Therefore the map
\[
  G_\lambda:\ (a_0,g)\ \mapsto\ (\lambda a_0,\ \lambda g)
\]
is an {\bf exact symmetry} of the formation equations, and $\kappa$ is the only quantity charged under it. So
$\kappa$ is a flat direction: no dynamics can determine it, any more than dynamics can determine the value of
the metre. FINAL-16 puts it in one line --- the route ``derives only the FORM $a_0=\kappa\,c\sqrt{G\rho_\Lambda}$.''

\subsection{The routes, and the freedom each one carries}
Each route that {\it lands} on $\tfrac12$ is circular by construction, and each says so in its own output.

FINAL-6 adds the diagnosis of the circularity: $\kappa=1/(2\sqrt\beta)$ with $\beta\equiv\rho_m(z_f)/\rho_\Lambda$,

\subsection{What is actually derived, and what it costs}
Universality kills the mass monomial; conservation kills the epoch-bearing densities $\rho_{\rm crit}(z_f)$ and
$\rho_m(z_f)$. The unique epoch-independent cosmic density is $\rho_\Lambda$. Hence $\rho_*=\rho_\Lambda$ and
$a_0=\tfrac12 c\sqrt{G\rho_\Lambda}$ with $\kappa=\tfrac12$ is forced --- as a {\bf convention-fixing}, not as a
dynamical output. That is exactly why it survived nine sectors and four routes: they were looking in the
dynamics for something the dynamics is symmetric under.

The cost is stated rather than hidden. $\kappa=\tfrac12$ is a gauge choice, not a prediction. The framework
explains {\it why} the coincidence $a_0\approx cH_0/6$ holds --- it must, because $c\sqrt{G\rho_\Lambda}$ is the
only dimensionful combination and its coefficient is conventional --- and it does {\bf not} predict the numerical
relation beyond the convention. The framework absorbs the coincidence rather than predicting it. FINAL-16 also
prints the external value that breaks the footing tie, $\kappa_{\rm ext}=0.6414\pm0.0107\,({\rm rand})\pm0.1283\,({\rm sys})$.
