# Superseded

This 2026-09-25 package is superseded by `../mnras_submission_2026_v3/` (2026-10-02). It was never submitted. Four
statements in this directory should not be reused:

1. Its RC100 numbers rest on the repository's earlier transcription of Nestor Shachar et al. (2023) Table 3, which had
   17 wrong cells (CFG287, CFG289). v3 reads `real_research/data/rc100_nestorshachar2023_table3_CORRECTED.csv`; the
   halo-law range becomes 2.5-4.3 sigma (v2: 2.6-4.3) and the H(z) bound 4.6 sigma (v2: 4.7). No statement changes.
2. MUSE-DARK III's rise is quoted only at face value, as "the strongest existing evidence against constancy". v3 keeps
   the face-value statement and adds that the rise appears only with the halo-fitted stellar masses (CFG198/199/236,
   CFG262).
3. The odds for "two to four lensed rotators" treat every baryonic-mass error as independent. v3 adds the shared
   calibration term (20:1 needs a common calibration of about 0.09 dex against the halo law) and the CFG240
   conditioning argument.
4. The redshift record stops at 2026-09-25: KURVS, MIGHTEE-HI/LADUMA and the z = 2.5-14 samples are missing.

See section 7d of the v3 checklist.
