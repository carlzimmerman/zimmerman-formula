Dear Editor,

I submit the manuscript "The galactic acceleration scale and the cosmological constant: the coefficient measured, its
degeneracy with H0, and a redshift test" for consideration as a Paper in Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical
Society.

The manuscript is original, has not been published in a journal and is not under consideration elsewhere. Earlier
versions of parts of the work were posted by me on the Zenodo repository, which is not peer reviewed
(DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22559892 and DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22563138). Both are cited in the manuscript, and the manuscript
states where its results supersede them.

Use of AI tools. Generative AI tools were used in preparing this work, and the use is disclosed in the
Acknowledgements. Large language models (Anthropic Claude, through the Claude Code tool, and in earlier exploratory
stages OpenAI models) assisted with derivations, with writing the analysis code and with drafting the text. No AI tool
is an author. I ran every script myself, I have reviewed the entire content, and I take full responsibility for it.
All analysis code is public, and each script contains checks that can fail.

Conflicts of interest: none. Funding: none.

Data and code are public; the Data Availability statement gives the repository and the sources of the two public
data sets used (SPARC; Nestor Shachar et al. 2023).

I have no request to exclude particular editors or referees.
