\section{Why the switch}
With an unswitched stiffness $-(\gamma_L/2)(D\!\cdot\!P)^2$, eq.~(\ref{eq:disp}) at $\rho_\tau<0$ and $L_\star>0$ gives $-\omega^2$ growing without bound in $k$: the theory is Hadamard ill-posed in every region of negative khronon charge. Such regions exist around outer globular clusters and satellites. The constraint algebra is not the cause: it closes with 3 degrees of freedom, 8 second-class constraints and rank 4 on every background tested. The ill-posedness is the scalar's own dynamics, so a cure has to change those dynamics.

The switched form does, and it adds no constant. Proved pointwise at leading WKB order (Lean):

\item \texttt{switched\_bounded\_where\_negative}: the growth rate is $k$-independent wherever $\rho_\tau<0$;
\item \texttt{switched\_stable\_where\_positive}: stable wherever $\rho_\tau\ge0$ and $M\ge1$;
\item \texttt{switched\_agrees\_where\_screened}: identical to the unswitched theory wherever $D\!\cdot\!P\ge0$, which covers every screened region;
\item \texttt{switched\_uniform\_bound}: $-\omega^2\le4\pi G|\rho_\tau||M-1|$ for all $k$.
\end{itemize}

\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Nonlinear, 1D.} The khronon pair is exactly a barotropic Euler fluid with $c^2=\gamma_L(D\!\cdot\!P+\rho_d)\Theta(D\!\cdot\!P)\ge0$ for $\rho_d\ge0$. Any smoothing of the switch gives $c^2\approx-0.058\,\epsilon\gamma_L<0$, so the switch must be exact.
