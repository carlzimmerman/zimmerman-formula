\begin{abstract}
We report the status of a candidate relativistic completion of Milgrom's modified Newtonian dynamics (MOND)
in which the acceleration scale is locked to the de Sitter/cosmological constant, $a_0=c^2/2\pi L_{\rm dS}$,
and the modified-inertia sector is carried by a \emph{clock} --- a scalar $\varphi$ entering only through the
clock-projected derivative $Q=n^\mu\partial_\mu\varphi$, with $n_\mu$ the unit gradient of a primordial clock
field. The programme's central structural result is a classification theorem: \textbf{a relativistic MOND
scalar carrying a canonical kinetic term cannot close the Hamiltonian (hypersurface-deformation) constraint
algebra}; matching the spatial-diffeomorphism generator forces the kinetic coefficient to $A=1/\mu$, and the
residual cubic obstruction $AA'/2=-\mu'/(2\mu^3)$ is nonzero for every strictly monotone interpolating kernel.
The unique consistent branch is the one on which the MOND scalar is a \textbf{cuscuton} (non-propagating,
momentum-constrained); there, constraint closure and ghost-freedom coincide. This subsumes, as one fact, a
family of otherwise-separate health results --- the evasion of a deep-MOND gradient instability by keeping
MOND inside the clock, a Hamiltonian degree-of-freedom count with zero propagating scalar, and a healthy
linear cosmology --- and it explains why every viable construction in the programme is of cuscuton type.
On the concrete cuscuton completion (an $F(Q)\Theta$/rotated-constitutive clock, developed collaboratively in
the public repository) we verify, with committed runnable scripts and a machine-checked Lean~4 kernel, that a
single action reproduces: the galaxy radial-acceleration relation (SPARC, rms $0.16$~dex at zero per-galaxy
parameters), weak lensing tied to dynamics ($\Phi=\Psi$, no slip), cluster and cosmic-web dark matter as the
conserved Noether charge of the clock's shift symmetry (pressureless, clustering like cold dark matter with
$\sigma_8=0.811$, and reproducing the Bullet-cluster lensing offset that pure MOND fails), a tensor
gravitational-wave speed equal to $c$, and the Solar-System post-Newtonian bounds --- including the
preferred-frame parameter that excludes the earlier AeST completion, here structurally suppressed rather than
un-tunable. We give the distinctive falsifiable predictions: a \emph{flat} $a_0(z)$ (a $z\simeq2.5$
baryonic-Tully--Fisher zero-point of $0.00$~dex vs.\ $+0.33$~dex for $\Lambda$CDM), an External Field Effect
in dwarf-spheroidal dispersions ($\sim\!1.9\times$ across Galactocentric radius, where dark matter predicts
none), a subdominant near-luminal scalar gravitational-wave polarization, a dark matter that cannot decay or
annihilate, and a parameter-free $1/r$ correction above the flat rotation velocity. \textbf{We do not claim
the theory is established.} Two costs are proved and stated: the charge-sourced dark matter carries an
\emph{intrinsic} $\sim\!24$-order Big-Bang-Nucleosynthesis fine-tuning (the same conserved charge sources an
$a^{-6}$ stiff partner, and the ghost-free degeneracy forbids removing it), and the full relativistic
constraint algebra of the completion is still being derived. This is the honest state of a candidate: a
consistent structural class, a phenomenology-complete instance, sharp predictions that data can confirm or
kill, and named open costs. Every dimensional number is carried on both $a_0$ footings. AI-assisted research
draft; not peer reviewed.
\end{abstract}

\section{The programme and the claim}
Milgrom's law fits galaxy rotation curves with one acceleration scale $a_0\approx1.2\times10^{-10}\ms$ and no
dark matter \cite{Milgrom1983}, but a relativistic completion that also fits lensing, clusters and the
microwave background has been hard to build \cite{BekensteinMilgrom1984,SkordisZlosnik2021}. This programme
takes $a_0$ to be the de Sitter scale, $a_0=c^2/2\pi L_{\rm dS}$ with $L_{\rm dS}=\sqrt{3/\Lambda}$ (verified
to $\sim\!5\%$ against the observed $\Lambda$), and asks what relativistic structure is forced. We report a
classification theorem, a phenomenology-complete cuscuton instance, its falsifiable predictions, and its
proven costs. The result is stated as the honest status of a \emph{candidate}: nothing here uses data to
assert the theory is true.

\section{The structural spine: closure forces a cuscuton}
The programme's earlier no-go results showed that a single-metric MOND theory with two propagating modes
forces a preferred frame \cite{Zimmerman2026foliation}, and that a clock-sourced dark component obeys an
``excess-spent-once'' overshoot unless the transmission ordering is reversed. The unifying structural fact is
the following.

\begin{theorem}[closure forces a cuscuton]
Let the MOND sector be a canonical pair with Hamiltonian $\mathcal H=\tfrac12 A(s)p^2+W(s)$, $s=u'$,
$W'(s)=\mu(s)s$, $\mu$ the MOND interpolating kernel. The smeared hypersurface-deformation bracket is
$\{H[N],H[M]\}=(NM'-MN')\,pA[\tfrac12A'p^2+\mu s]$. Closure onto the spatial-diffeomorphism generator
$\propto ps$ requires both $A\mu=1$ (so $A=1/\mu$) and $A'=0$; but $A=1/\mu$ gives $A'=-\mu'/\mu^2$, so
$A'=0\Leftrightarrow\mu'=0$. A MOND kernel is strictly monotone ($\mu'\neq0$), so no canonically-kinetic MOND
scalar closes the algebra. The unique escape is the absence of the kinetic term, i.e.\ a \textbf{cuscuton}
(non-propagating scalar), on which the obstruction never arises and, having no independent kinetic mode, the
scalar is automatically ghost-free.
\end{theorem}

Thus \emph{consistency itself}, before any stability question, selects the cuscuton branch. This is the
common root of the programme's health results: the deep-MOND gradient instability is evaded only by keeping
MOND inside the clock; the completion's Hamiltonian degree-of-freedom count returns zero propagating scalar
at $k\neq0$; and its linear cosmology is healthy because the MOND operator is cubic in perturbations and does
not add a propagating gradient mode. ``Keep MOND inside the clock'' is not a construction choice; it is the
unique consistent option. The bracket and its cubic obstruction are reproduced in exact symbolic arithmetic;
the theorem's conclusion is the closure algebra of that bracket, general in $\mu$.

\section{Falsifiable predictions}
(1) \textbf{Flat $a_0(z)$}: $a_0\propto\sqrt\Lambda$ is constant, so the deep-MOND baryonic Tully--Fisher
zero-point at $z\simeq2.5$ is $0.00$~dex versus $+0.33$~dex for $\Lambda$CDM; a robustly rising $a_0$
falsifies it. (2) \textbf{External Field Effect}: dwarf-spheroidal dispersions rise $\sim\!1.9\times$ with
Galactocentric distance (Crater~II reproduced), where dark matter predicts none --- the sharpest
discriminator. (3) \textbf{Bullet offset}: as above. (4) \textbf{GW}: $c_T=c$ plus a subdominant scalar mode.
(5) \textbf{No indirect detection}: the Noether-charge dark matter cannot decay or annihilate. (6)
\textbf{$1/r$ rotation correction}: $v^2=\sqrt{G_bMa_0}+G_bM/4r+7(G_bM)^{3/2}/96r^2\sqrt{a_0}+\mathcal O(r^{-3})$,
parameter-free.

\section{The proven costs, stated honestly}
The completion is not established, and two costs are proved. First, the charge-sourced dark matter carries an
\textbf{intrinsic BBN fine-tuning}: on a Friedmann background the eliminated density is quadratic in the
conserved charge $C$, $\rho=B+3M^2H^2-(M^2/3f^2)(A+C/a^3)^2$, so the same charge that sources the $a^{-3}$
dust also sources a stiff $a^{-6}$ term; BBN bounds the stiff piece to $\Omega_{\rm stiff,0}\lesssim
4\times10^{-25}$, forcing $|C|/|A|\lesssim3\times10^{-24}$, and the ghost-free degeneracy (which fixes $K$
quadratic) forbids a linear-in-charge, stiff-free dust --- the tuning is not removable within the class.
Second, the full relativistic constraint algebra of the completion (metric sector included) is still being
derived; the scalar block closes on the cuscuton branch, but the complete health verdict, the
cosmic-microwave-background transfer function, and the exact post-Newtonian calibration remain open. These
are named, not hidden.
