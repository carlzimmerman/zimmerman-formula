\section{Postquantum classical gravity as the origin of $a_0\propto\sqrt\Lambda$}\label{sec:cq}

It was therefore the one external claim of the relation's scaling that had not been priced (lanes L313, L314).

For the programme's $\Lambda$-triggered kicked-decay carrier over $f_d(0)=0.8$--$0.99$ and $v_k=1000$--$3000$\,km\,s$^{-1}$, no cell passes X-COP [Eck19], $S_8$ and galaxies together.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Net.}
Without a switch, C-H/K fails linear growth by an order of magnitude. With a switch made consistent with GW170817 ($\tilde x$) and Planck (leaf-average term), Gauss's law leaves at most a KiDS window at $x_c\approx2$--$3$, and the construction's own web field fails KiDS isolated lensing unless the kernel is blind to it. \emph{C-H/K is a local-gravity construction without a working cosmology.}

\section{The specification any completion must now meet}\label{sec:spec}
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item The linear web must stay Newtonian ($\sigma_8$), and the $z\approx2$--3 intergalactic medium nearly so (flux power within $\sim$10\%).
\item The Mpc-scale lensing flux around isolated galaxies must be supplied. By Gauss's law that means MOND switched on there, or real mass.
\item Whatever supplies (ii) must not itself be MOND-boosted (L345).
\item The kernel must not read a $\Lambda$CDM-level large-scale Newtonian field ($\sim\!10^{-2}a_0$; KiDS allows $e\lesssim7\times10^{-5}$), or the web's field must itself be MOND-level, as in pure MOND.
\item Any switch variable must enter the kinetic and gradient terms together ($c_T=1$).
\end{enumerate}
The doors this leaves are each a new ingredient: a kernel blind to the large-scale field, for example one sourced by the bound region's own matter (BS3; a baryons-only kernel survives only inside 0.3\,Mpc); a carrier coupled directly to $U$ (L345); a time-dependent threshold; and a khronon sector that itself behaves as dust on FRW, the route of Blanchet \& Skordis [BS24, BS25]. Running that route through the same two gates is the natural next test; the moving-source half is begun in lane BSK1.
One prediction of the local construction survives whatever the cosmology turns out to be. Stacked weak lensing split by the lens's velocity through the CMB frame should show the $1/(1-v_\parallel^2/c_s^2)$ amplification, at up to tens of per cent in deep-MOND outskirts if $c_2$ sits at the Planck caps.

\section{What this changes for the core relation}\label{sec:core}
\begin{itemize}
\item $a_0=\kappa c\sqrt{G\rho_\Lambda}$ is unaffected as a phenomenological law. Neither test uses or moves $\kappa$, which remains fitted ($0.465\pm0.076$) and, by the zero-mode theorem, free in local actions [Z26a].
\item The one published first-principles route to $a_0\propto\sqrt\Lambda$ reproduces the scaling but not the coefficient (factor 2.8--3.1), the shape, or the matter-tracking. It cannot be cited as a derivation of the relation.
\item The relation's distinctive observational prediction, $a_0$ flat in redshift (tested by deep-MOND rotators at $z\approx2.5$ [Z26c]), is independent of both results. If a switch of this kind is used, its lensing edge at $z=2.5$ lies at 0.3--0.5\,Mpc for a Milky-Way mass, far outside the rotation curves that test it.
\item The completion question has sharpened. Local gravity with a moving phantom is achievable at the orders computed, and the open problem is cosmology, in the form (i)--(v) above.
\end{itemize}
