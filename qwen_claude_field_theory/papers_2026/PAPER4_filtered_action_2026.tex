\begin{abstract}
We collect, verify and extend the results landed in the closure programme of the de Sitter--MOND repository between 4 and 5 September 2026 for the \emph{filtered} two-potential MOND action (a spatially filtered specialisation of QUMOND with the exact inverse partner of $\mu(y)=1-e^{-y}$ and one coherence length $\xi$). (i) The action's linear response about a constant external field gives a central tidal identity, $Q_2/D=2y/\{5[3\lambda(y)-y]\}$, $\lambda=1+(y-1)e^{-y}$, independent of source mass and filter amplitude; we reproduce it with independent nonlinear machinery to 1.4\% (canonical footing) and 0.6\% (alternate), with the mass and $\xi$ independence holding to $5\times10^{-5}$. (ii) Varying both source positions gives the comparable-mass force; for an isolated compact filtered pair its leading harmonic coefficient $h=\tfrac{2}{9}\sqrt{GMa_0}/\xi^2$ is reproduced to 2--5\%, converging as $\xi/r_M$ grows, and in the external-field regime the identity becomes a three-frequency relation $2y(\omega_r^2-\omega_\phi^2)=3[5\lambda-y](\omega_\phi^2-\omega_z^2)$. (iii) The exact two-body force from the stationary action reproduces Milgrom's deep-MOND two-body law to 1\%, verifies that a bounded body's phantom exerts no net force on it in a uniform external field, and shows that the total-mass one-body reduction used by external-field wide-binary pipelines errs by under 2.5\% except for an 8--10\% spike at $d=\sqrt{GM_{\rm tot}/g_e}$ (aligned geometry only). (iv) Solar-System gates: the double filter survives for $\xi\ge0.02$--$0.03$ pc. (v) A first explicit covariant clock/heat action (C-H) reduces at leading weak static order to the filtered functional with both filters; its physical mode count and causal feasibility remain open, and an intrinsic-elliptic Deffayet--Woodard variant is excluded exactly because it admits no expanding FLRW background. (vi) The local term $\xi^2|\nabla_\perp V|^2$ added to the scalar's gradient invariant $Y$ inside the MOND function, $V_\mu=q_\mu^{\ \nu}\partial_\nu\phi$, reproduces the coherent stiffening $J_Y\to J_Y(1+\xi^2k^2)$ exactly in $\alpha_1$ in both an aether host and a hypersurface-orthogonal clock host, suppresses the scalar's $\alpha_2$ drag by $3\times10^{-5}$ at $(\xi k)^2=10^4$, adds no degree of freedom, and leaves the static PPN ladder under its bounds at the Cassini floor. Nothing here is a completed relativistic theory, a derivation of $\xi$ or of $a_0$, or an empirical detection; the wide-binary pre-registration is untouched.
\end{abstract}
\section{The filtered action}
With $u$ the Newtonian potential, $\Phi$ the potential felt by matter, $S=e^{\xi^2\Delta/2}$ a Gaussian filter of standard deviation $\xi$ (or the Helmholtz filter $(1-\xi^2\Delta)^{-1}$), and $q'(s^2)=\nu(s)-1$ where $\nu$ is the exact inverse partner of $\mu(y)=1-e^{-y}$ ($s=y\mu(y)$, $\nu=y/s$),
\begin{equation}
I=\int dt\,d^3x\Big\{L_{m}-\rho\Phi-\frac{2\nabla\Phi\cdot\nabla u-|\nabla u|^2-a_0^2\,q(|\nabla Su|^2/a_0^2)}{8\pi G}\Big\}.
\end{equation}
Variation gives $\Delta u=4\pi G\rho$ and $\Delta\Phi=4\pi G\rho+S\nabla\!\cdot\![(\nu-1)\nabla Su]$: the outer (adjoint) filter is forced by the variation, not assumed. The on-shell energy is $E=-(8\pi G)^{-1}\!\int|\nabla u|^2-(a_0^2/8\pi G)\!\int Q_t(|\nabla Su|/a_0)$ with the exact primitive $Q_t(s)=y^2+2-(2y^2+2y+2)e^{-y}-s^2$, $y=s\nu(s)$. Both footings $a_0=9.3619\times10^{-11}$ and $1.1279\times10^{-10}\,{\rm m\,s^{-2}}$ are carried throughout; $\kappa=\tfrac12$ in $a_0=\kappa c\sqrt{G\rho_\Lambda}$ is fitted, not derived.
\section{The central tidal identity}
Linearising about a constant filtered Newtonian background $s_e a_0$ and adding a spherical source, the additional central Hessian $H_{ij}=\partial_i\partial_j\Phi_{\rm ph}(0)$ has trace $D=4\pi G\rho_{\rm ph}(0)$ and axial quadrupole $Q_2=H_\perp-H_\parallel$ obeying
\begin{equation}
\frac{Q_2}{D}=\frac{2y}{5[3\lambda(y)-y]},\qquad \lambda(y)=1+(y-1)e^{-y},\qquad s_e=y(1-e^{-y}),
\end{equation}
for any isotropic filter and any source amplitude (the division-free form and the anisotropic control that violates it are in the lead's report). At $y=1$ the ratio is $1/5$; as $y\to0$ with external dominance kept it tends to $2/25$. Independent check: the nonlinear axisymmetric machinery of the Solar-System gate (log-radius by Legendre grid, mode-wise output filter) gives $Q_2/D=1.0931$ against $1.1089$ at $y=2.478$ (canonical) and $0.6137$ against $0.6102$ at $y=2.057$ (alternate), constant to $5\times10^{-5}$ across $\xi=0.3$--$10$ pc and source masses $1$ and $10^{-3}\,M_\odot$. This cross-check also exposed and fixed a wrong-direction external-field conversion in the first Solar-System scan.
\section{Both sources varied}
With $v_\xi(x)=-{\rm erf}(|x|/\sqrt2\xi)/|x|$ and its Hessian $H_\xi$, independent variation of the source positions gives the phantom acceleration of body $a$,
\begin{equation}
\mathbf a_{a,{\rm ph}}=-\frac{1}{4\pi}\int H_\xi(x-x_a)\,[f(p_e+p)-f(p_e)]\,d^3x,\qquad f(p)=[\nu(|p|/a_0)-1]\,p,
\end{equation}
equivalent to $F_i=-\int\rho_{\rm ph}\,g_i^{(S)}$ with the output filter moved onto the body by self-adjointness. Results, all from committed scripts with checks that can fail:
\begin{itemize}
\item Isolated compact filtered pair ($d\ll\xi$, $\xi\gg r_M$): the relative equation is $\ddot{\mathbf r}=-GM\mathbf r/r^3-h(M)\mathbf r$ with $h=\tfrac29\sqrt{GMa_0}/\xi^2$ at leading order; direct quadrature gives $0.95$--$0.96$ of this at $\xi/r_M\approx8$ and $0.98$ at $25$, independent of $d/\xi$ between $0.1$ and $0.2$ to $0.5\%$.
\item Isolated pair at $\xi=0$: Milgrom's exact deep-MOND two-body force $Fd=\tfrac23\sqrt{Ga_0}[M^{3/2}-M_1^{3/2}-M_2^{3/2}]$ reproduced to $1.0\%$ (equal masses) and $0.8\%$ (10:1) at $d=100\,r_M$; $-\partial E/\partial d$ equals the direct force to $0.05$--$0.3\%$; third law to $0.1\%$ of the total force.
\item Theorem (QUMOND stress tensor $T_{ij}=\partial_ju\,\partial_i\Phi+\partial_j\Phi\,\partial_iu-\nu\,\partial_ju\,\partial_iu-\delta_{ij}(\nabla u\cdot\nabla\Phi-\tfrac12a_0^2\mathcal Q)$): the total force on bounded matter in a uniform external Newtonian field is $\nu_e g_e M_{\rm tot}$ exactly, so the phantom exerts no net force on the bodies. Verified to $2\times10^{-4}$ ($\xi=0$) and $10^{-5}$ ($\xi=0.03$ pc); holds for the filtered action by translation invariance.
\item External-field regime: the identity becomes $2y(\omega_r^2-\omega_\phi^2)-3[5\lambda(y)-y](\omega_\phi^2-\omega_z^2)=0$ for infinitesimal radial and vertical perturbations of a circular orbit perpendicular to the field axis (harmonic-core, linear-response limit; equal to 6 at $y=1$). It is an orbital translation of the tidal identity, not new dynamics.
\item Aligned wide binaries in the Galactic field ($g_{\rm obs}=2.32\times10^{-10}$, converted to the Newtonian gradient by the spherical relation): the total-mass one-body reduction errs by $<2.5\%$ at 4--30 kAU except an 8--10\% spike at $d=\sqrt{GM_{\rm tot}/g_e}$ (7 kAU for $2M_\odot$, 5 kAU for $1M_\odot$), the on-axis field null. At the Solar-System floors the double filter holds the aligned boost below 1\% inside 15 kAU. The perpendicular orientation is not computed; these are not $\gamma_v$ predictions.
\end{itemize}
\section{Solar-System gates}
Signed quadrupole against the Park et al.\ (2026) two-sigma ceiling $5.2\times10^{-27}\,{\rm s^{-2}}$, phantom mass inside Saturn's orbit against Pitjev--Pitjeva, and the radial anomaly at Mercury--Neptune against the repository's $\alpha=1$ sunward gate, at three external-field inputs: the double filter survives for $\xi\ge0.02$ pc (Gaussian, canonical), $0.03$ pc (Gaussian alternate; Helmholtz both). The strict exponential AQUAL law without a length fails the quadrupole by 4--5$\times$ on both footings by two independent discretisations. A single-response Helmholtz proxy for the local operator of \S6 survives for $\xi\ge0.03/0.05$ pc. These are tabulated grid points under inherited observational inputs, not continuum boundaries.
\section{Toward a covariant action}
\textbf{C-H (the lead's G03).} An explicit action with metric, a dynamical clock $\tau$ (unit normal $n_\mu=-\partial_\mu\tau/\sqrt{-(\partial\tau)^2}$), an auxiliary potential $U$, a heat coordinate $z$ realising the filter as $\partial_zW=\Delta_hW$ on the leaves, two multipliers, $\Lambda$, minimally coupled matter and temporal boundary terms; gravitational modification $2|DU-a|^2+2\alpha^2q(|DW_b|^2/\alpha^2)+\int_0^{\xi^2/2}dz\,L(\partial_zW-\Delta_hW)+\lambda_0(W_0-U)$, $\alpha=a_0/c^2$. Established: eliminating the spatial metric potential at leading weak static order gives exactly the filtered functional with both filters; $W_0=U$, $W_b=SU$ and the weighted adjoint $N^{-1}SN$ are derived; 13/13 finite and symbolic checks (nonlinear first variations to $6\times10^{-11}$, with the outer filter's removal producing $0.03$--$0.08$ errors). Open: the physical mode count of the clock/metric system, causal feasibility of the heat sector (the naive scalar-wave realisation fails), $\Phi=\Psi$ beyond leading order, the isolated $\mathbb R^3$ limit.\\
\textbf{An intrinsic-elliptic Deffayet--Woodard variant is dead:} its action forces $R_{\mu\nu}\hat u^\mu\hat u^\nu=0$ on every homogeneous solution, hence $\ddot a=0$: no de Sitter, radiation, matter or $\Lambda$CDM background.
\section{The operator that screens the Solar System}
In an aether-scalar host the preferred-frame parameter $\alpha_1$ carries an untunable scalar drag; only a \emph{coherent} stiffening of the scalar's whole quadratic form, $J_Y\to J_Y(1+\xi^2k^2)$, suppresses it, and local fourth-order operators on the scalar alone fail. The term
\begin{equation}
Y\;\to\;Y+\xi^2\,q^{\lambda\sigma}q^{\mu\nu}\nabla_\lambda V_\mu\nabla_\sigma V_\nu,\qquad V_\mu=q_\mu^{\ \nu}\partial_\nu\phi,\quad q_{\mu\nu}=g_{\mu\nu}+n_\mu n_\nu,
\end{equation}
does it from an action: $V$'s background vanishes, so at quadratic order $(\nabla_\perp V)^2=[k^2+(n\!\cdot\!k)^2]\,V_1\!\cdot\!V_1$ multiplies the whole $Y$ sector, mixings included. In the repository's boosted PPN pipeline (Will dictionary, static ladder to second order in the boost) $\alpha_1$ of the operator equals the coherent-stiffening value $-4(2-K_B)/(J_Y(1+\xi^2k^2)+1)$ plus the host's own $-4c_{14}$ exactly at every ladder point, in the aether host and in the hypersurface-orthogonal clock host (aether replaced by $-\partial\tau/|\partial\tau|$ to first order, transverse components included). With $c_2=c_4=1/10$ the scalar's $\alpha_2$ drag is suppressed from $16.5$ to $5.9\times10^{-4}$ (aether) and $5.7$ to $1.8\times10^{-4}$ (clock) at $(\xi k)^2=10^4$; in the khronometric corner $c_{13}=0$, $c_{14}=10^{-5}$, $c_2=0.1$ the clock's own $\alpha_1=-4.7\times10^{-5}$ and $\alpha_2=-5.9\times10^{-6}$, and $\gamma=1$, $\alpha_3=0$ throughout. At the Cassini floor $(\xi k)^2\ge8.6\times10^7$ at 1 AU, every scalar drag is below $10^{-7}$. The operator is purely spatial in the clock frame: one degree of freedom, positive Hamiltonian, dispersion $\omega^2=c_s^2k^2(1+\xi^2k^2)$ (the healing-length form), group velocity $2\xi k\,c_s$ at short wavelength. The last number is what the causality gate must judge; placed outside the MOND function with its own coefficient the same term makes the static operator uniformly elliptic at zero field, which is a separate, uncomputed claim.
\section{What is not claimed}
No completed relativistic theory; $\xi$ is a constraint ($0.02$--$0.05$ pc from the Solar System, 15--20 kAU knee for wide binaries, 50--140 pc wanted by three outer-halo globular clusters) not a derivation; $a_0=\tfrac12c\sqrt{G\rho_\Lambda}$ is an optional relation; the exponential and RAR kernels remain undecided on galaxy data once $a_0$ and mass-to-light are profiled; the clock corner's health with the scalar present, FLRW, the measured $G$ and the zero-field limit are open; the wide-binary registration and its hashes are unchanged.
