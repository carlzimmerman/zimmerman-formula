{\Large\bfseries Why Cold Energy Settles}\\[3pt]
{\large A tethered medium whose displacement is the gravitational polarization}\\[5pt]
{\small Working note, 2026-10-14, v1.0. AI-assisted research programme; not peer reviewed.}
\end{center}

\paragraph{The question.} Galaxy rotation curves follow a single relation between observed and baryonic acceleration. In the framework studied here the acceleration scale is set by dark energy:
$$a_0=\kappa c\sqrt{G\rho_{\rm DE}}\simeq0.173\,cH_\infty,\qquad \kappa=\tfrac12\ \text{(fitted)}.$$
Clusters, the Bullet Cluster, Local Group timing and the cosmic web also require a cold, collisionless mass component, ``cold energy'', with $\Omega_c/\Omega_b=5.364$. Why should that component arrange itself, galaxy by galaxy, into exactly the profile the acceleration relation demands? This note gives the mechanism in its simplest form.

\paragraph{1. The medium and its displacement.} Treat cold energy as a cold medium of real rest mass. Let $\mathbf P$ be its displacement field:
$$\partial_t\mathbf P=-\mathbf j\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\nabla\!\cdot\!\mathbf P=\rho-\bar\rho.$$
So the ``polarization'' of the gravitational vacuum is not an extra field: it is how far the medium has moved. This is a gravitational analogue of a dielectric, close in spirit to Blanchet's dipolar dark matter [2] and to superfluid dark matter [3].

\paragraph{2. The action.} With $K=4\pi G$ and $u=K|\mathbf P|$,
$$\mathcal L=-\frac{|\nabla\Phi-K\mathbf P|^2}{2K}-\rho_b\Phi-W_{\rm rem}(u)-\frac{\gamma}{2}|\nabla\mathbf P|^2,\qquad W_{\rm rem}=W_{\rm full}-\frac{u^2}{2K},\qquad K\frac{dW_{\rm full}}{du}=\frac{a_0u}{a_0-u}.$$
The vacuum term saturates at the wall $u\to a_0$. (The subtraction in $W_{\rm rem}$ is required; pairing the completed square with $W_{\rm full}$ double-counts the quadratic term.) The stiffness $\gamma=KL_*^2/2$ is the one extra constant, $L_*\in[5.75\,{\rm pc},\gtrsim1\,{\rm kpc}]$. It is not optional: without it the Sun's own field carries the simple function's constant $a_0$ tail, which the planetary ephemerides exclude by $\sim2600\times$. It is irrelevant on galaxy scales.

\paragraph{3. The tether.} Varying $\Phi$ gives the Newtonian flux of the baryons, $g-u=g_N$. Varying $\mathbf P$ gives the balance of the medium. Its self-gravity cancels against the quadratic part of the field energy, so each element feels only
$$\underbrace{g_N}_{\text{baryons pull in}}\quad\text{against}\quad\underbrace{R(u)=K\frac{dW_{\rm rem}}{du}=\frac{u^2}{a_0-u}}_{\text{vacuum pushes back}}.$$
Like a mass on a spring, the medium comes to rest where the two balance. The balance point is unique (the energy is convex) and gives
$$g_N=R(u)\ \Longrightarrow\ u=\frac{a_0g}{a_0+g}\ \Longrightarrow\ \boxed{\,g_N=\frac{g^2}{g+a_0}\,}$$
This is the ``simple'' interpolating function [1]: $g\to\sqrt{a_0g_N}$ for $g_N\ll a_0$, giving $V_f^4=GM_ba_0$; and $g\to g_N$ for $g_N\gg a_0$. We checked it numerically: $\nu=g/g_N=100.5$ at $g_N=10^{-4}a_0$. \textbf{Cold energy settles because the law's profile is the only state in which the medium is at rest.}

\paragraph{4. How it gets there.} Galaxies assemble over $\gtrsim10^9$\,yr, far slower than the medium's response time. The medium therefore follows the baryons adiabatically, and essentially no energy is radiated ($\le10^{-195}$ of the settling energy). A sudden start would instead relax by emitting longitudinal waves of $\mathbf P$, but that case is not realised in nature. The tether's projected capacity is $\bar\Sigma\le a_0/\pi G$ ($\approx214$\,M$_\odot$\,pc$^{-2}$). The law's own profile never reaches it.

\paragraph{5. What settles, and what does not.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Galaxies.} Nearly all baryons are condensed (stars and cold gas), and the medium tethers to them. This reproduces the radial acceleration relation: 0.100\,dex on raw SPARC.
\item \textbf{Solar System.} The stiff medium cannot bend on scales below $\sim2$\,pc, so the Sun is screened even inside the Galaxy's field. Cassini's quadrupole comes out at $\le3.5\times10^{-6}$ of the 2026 bound [4], even though the Galactic boost at the Sun is kept at 35--57\%. The only residue is a uniform sunward pull $GM_\odot/L_*^2\le4.2\times10^{-15}$\,m\,s$^{-2}$. Wide binaries come out Newtonian ($-0.8\sigma$ against Gaia DR3); cluster satellites keep their boost.
\item \textbf{Large scales.} Two rules (each halo tethers only its own supply, and only condensed baryons source the tether) make the framework's own simulations consistent with raw KiDS-1000 cosmic shear: $\Delta\chi^2=+2.03$ against a same-pipeline control.
\item \textbf{Mergers.} At collision speeds the tether cannot follow, so cold energy is collisionless. This is consistent with the Bullet Cluster.
\item \textbf{Hot-gas systems (prediction).} Groups and clusters are dominated by hot, pressure-supported gas. We predict that much of their cold energy stays \emph{untethered}: collisionless and concentrated in the core. This is a candidate for the extra core mass seen in cluster lensing ($\times1.8$ inside $\sim$1\,Mpc, $7\sigma$). A frozen test is queued.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{What is not yet derived.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Why only condensed baryons tether. The action as written responds to all baryons.
\item The energy-export channel and the frame anchor.
\item The origin of the medium's mass, possibly set by $\rho_{\rm DE}$.
\item The relativistic completion, needed so that lensing is predicted rather than assumed.
\item $\kappa$ is fitted, $L_*$ is only bounded, $\Omega_c/\Omega_b$ is measured, and massive early-type galaxies still show unexplained core mass.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Sealed predictions that can kill it.} (i) A Cavendish experiment whose source acceleration is changed $\times4$ must see $|\Delta G/G|<10^{-6}$; MOND-boosted lab models predict $\sim3\times10^{-4}$. (ii) A probe beyond 1000\,AU must see no non-thermal anomaly $\ge10^{-13}$\,m\,s$^{-2}$. (iii) A Sun--Earth saddle-point flyby sees nothing. (iv) Gaia DR4 wide binaries are Newtonian.

\paragraph{What is new here.} The interpolating function [1] and the polarization picture [2,3] are not new. New are: the scale is tied to dark energy, so $a_0$ is flat in redshift if $\rho_{\rm DE}$ is constant (testable with ALMA, Nov 2026); the medium-is-the-polarization identification, which makes the law the tether's unique rest state; EFE-blindness from free-fall basins (Gaia DR4, Dec 2026); and the condensed-only, own-supply settling that passes raw cosmic shear.
