## Title
Relativistic MOND Is a Cuscuton Theory: A Candidate de Sitter–MOND Completion, Its Structural Spine, Falsifiable Predictions, and Open Costs

## Author
Carl P. Zimmerman (Briar Creek Tech)

## Abstract (plain text — paste directly)
We report the status of a candidate relativistic completion of Milgrom's modified Newtonian dynamics (MOND) in which the acceleration scale is locked to the de Sitter/cosmological constant, a0 = c^2 / (2 pi L_dS), and the modified-inertia sector is carried by a "clock" — a scalar entering only through the clock-projected derivative Q = n·∂φ, with n the unit gradient of a primordial clock field. The central structural result is a classification theorem: a relativistic MOND scalar carrying a canonical kinetic term cannot close the Hamiltonian (hypersurface-deformation) constraint algebra — matching the spatial-diffeomorphism generator forces the kinetic coefficient to A = 1/μ, and the residual cubic obstruction A A'/2 = −μ'/(2 μ^3) is nonzero for every strictly monotone interpolating kernel. The unique consistent branch is the one on which the MOND scalar is a cuscuton (non-propagating, momentum-constrained); there, constraint closure and ghost-freedom coincide. This subsumes, as one fact, a family of otherwise-separate health results and explains why every viable construction in the programme is of cuscuton type. On the concrete cuscuton completion (an F(Q)Θ / rotated-constitutive clock, developed collaboratively in the associated public repository) we verify, with committed runnable scripts and a machine-checked Lean 4 kernel, that a single action reproduces the galaxy radial-acceleration relation (SPARC, rms 0.16 dex at zero per-galaxy parameters), weak lensing tied to dynamics (no slip), cluster and cosmic-web dark matter as the conserved Noether charge of the clock's shift symmetry (pressureless, clustering like cold dark matter with sigma8 = 0.811, reproducing the Bullet-cluster lensing offset that pure MOND fails), a tensor gravitational-wave speed equal to c, and the Solar-System post-Newtonian bounds including the preferred-frame parameter that excludes the earlier AeST completion, here structurally suppressed rather than un-tunable. We give the distinctive falsifiable predictions: a flat a0(z) (a z ≈ 2.5 baryonic-Tully–Fisher zero-point of 0.00 dex versus +0.33 dex for LambdaCDM), an External Field Effect in dwarf-spheroidal dispersions (about 1.9x across Galactocentric radius, where dark matter predicts none), a subdominant near-luminal scalar gravitational-wave polarization, a dark matter that cannot decay or annihilate, and a parameter-free 1/r correction above the flat rotation velocity. We do not claim the theory is established. Two costs are proved and stated: the charge-sourced dark matter carries an intrinsic ~24-order Big-Bang-Nucleosynthesis fine-tuning (the same conserved charge sources an a^−6 stiff partner, and the ghost-free degeneracy forbids removing it), and the full relativistic constraint algebra of the completion is still being derived. This is the honest state of a candidate: a consistent structural class, a phenomenology-complete instance, sharp predictions data can confirm or kill, and named open costs. Every dimensional number is carried on both a0 footings; no observation is used to assert the theory is true. AI-assisted research programme with a collaborative construction; not peer reviewed.
