\begin{abstract}\noindent
This note states the form on which the record closes and grades it. It does not complete the theory.
The $a_0$ law alone fails the CMB, the growth of structure, clusters and the Bullet, each for want of the same cold, clustering component of about 5.4 times the baryons.
Every way tried of keeping that component out of galaxies has a failing verdict; the last one is recorded here.
The form chosen by the owner on 2026-11-09 counts the component once: it is in galaxies too, and around condensed baryons the law describes how it sits. It is real mass, not a second force.
In the adopted action this is the static state's own reading: the law's halo is the khronon sector's energy density, carried by the one conserved charge that is dust in the expanding background.
We regrade the form with criteria frozen before any number.
On groups and clusters it is not falsified, by a gate that can fail from one side only, and the richest clusters sit at its ceiling; the Bullet passes only if the subcluster's stripped gas is counted.
On hosted dwarfs it fails: it removes most of the law's shortfall in every class, but two classes stay high and four low rows cannot be reached.
On KiDS lensing by galaxy type the test returns no verdict; its readings put early types between the law and the form's truncated halo and cannot place discs; on host-free lenses the form passes a gate that does not separate it from the law.
The form passes rotation curves. Its CMB, lensing and growth rows hold when computed, on an amended reading of that lane's checks. With the amplitude of fluctuations pinned by large-angle polarisation, CMB lensing matches the prediction to 0.3$\sigma$ and the growth rate passes. Raw KiDS weak lensing, on a gravity-only spectrum, prefers an amplitude five of its own widths lower: the absolute fit is excluded at 3$\sigma$ by the frozen line and reads tension once the reference run's realisation noise is corrected, a check made after grading. This is the tension that any cold component fitted to the CMB carries; the form states neither gas ejection nor neutrino mass, and does not relieve it.
With gravity coupled the static state is linearly stable around a point mass; an instability found earlier belonged to a planar background.

Version 1.1 adds the lanes that landed on 2026-11-10 and states the theory in six rules (Section~7).
The collapse was computed and breaks down: a clock that starts as dust folds at the centre, and the equations give no answer beyond that moment.
The static state is stable around isolated discs away from their centres; a disc in an external field has no verdict, with stability excluded there, and a disc's own centre is open.
No dust term tried, keyed on the clock's offset, on its expansion or on its acceleration, joins the falling state to the standing one; the clock's leafwise flux cannot supply the halo; and the action has no cluster state at the form's own dust constant.
A survey of media with more structure than one clock finds no class without a failing verdict.
On the data side, most of the weak-lensing gap was the survey's superseded release and our own inputs: on KiDS-Legacy, with a neutrino floor, the covariance at the model and the reference run's draw corrected, the form's amplitude sits 1.71/1.88 of the survey's widths high with an acceptable fit, while DES Y3 stays at 2.18/2.23 on a re-scanned fit. These readings rest on post hoc amendments: the lane's amended label is GAP STAYS and its frozen labels are NO VERDICT. With fixed-amplitude reference runs spliced in at low $k$, an interim reading on one full-size pair puts DES Y3 at 1.85/1.83 and both surveys inside two widths; that lane's frozen label is NO VERDICT and its amended label was pending.
The two failing dwarf classes do not survive the record's own systematics budget, and a gas-history reading does not explain them.
The form's data rows are computed without the joint between its two states, and several of them fail or have no verdict. The form is not complete.
We list what is open, the measurement that would decide each item, and our own wrong estimates.
\end{abstract}

\section{Two forms}
Cold dark energy is this record's name for a gravitating field component with $w\approx0$ at the cosmic amount; it is not a particle. Two footings, canonical $a_0=9.3603\times10^{-11}$ and alt $1.1312\times10^{-10}$\,m\,s$^{-2}$, are carried throughout and never pooled; pairs of numbers are canonical/alt unless a row says otherwise.

\textbf{Form A, the law alone,} is what PAPER70 states. It passes rotation curves and the Solar System. It fails the CMB (which needs a cold component of 5.41 times the baryons), growth, X-COP by $+0.4675$/$+0.4345$\,dex, the Bullet ($Z=-4.55$, the mass sits on the gas) and Coma's ultra-diffuse galaxies ($+1.12$\,dex with a static external field, $+1.41$ with a moving host).

\textbf{Form B, the law with cold dark energy counted once,} is the single-counting reading that lanes PD1 and PD2 graded with one fixed share per halo. As graded there it is FAIL/FAIL: it passes its population budget, rotation curves (rms 0.0563/0.0558 against a line of 0.0660) and the carried cosmology, and fails KiDS's pooled outer points (worst $-0.635$\,dex), the groups and the Bullet's amount; X-COP passes only in a reported bracket ($+0.019$/$+0.010$).

On 2026-11-09 the owner chose B as the form the record closes on, with both forms scored side by side from the record. The choice is of a form, not of a pass: no label was changed by it. It reopens the cold sector retired on 2026-11-01 for this one reading. Section~3 is the regrade that followed.

\section{Form B in the adopted action}
The action of PAPER70 has certified field equations (lane QX17). A note written after the decision, and checked independently for this paper, reads form B out of them for a static clock.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{The law's halo is energy density.} The khronon sector's energy density contains $\Pi=D\!\cdot\!P$, and the static weak-field 00 equation is Poisson's with source $\rho_b+D\!\cdot\!P$. Light and matter both see it.
\item \textbf{One conserved charge.} The khronon equation is a conserved current whose charge is, to leading order, $D\!\cdot\!P+\mu_K^2(Q-1)/K$. With $P=0$ it is cold dust in the expanding background; around a static source it is the law's halo plus the static dust term $-\mu_K^2\Phi/K$, which is zero at $\mu_K=0$. Nothing is counted twice.
\item \textbf{The static state is in force balance.} With $J(g)=(a_0^2/K)[u-\ln(1+u)]$, $u=g/a_0$, the law sector's stress satisfies $\partial_j\Pi_{ij}=(D\!\cdot\!P)\,g_i$. In the deep field $J=g^2/2K$, which is the pressure of an isothermal halo with $\sigma^2=\tfrac12\sqrt{GM_ba_0}$: the relation PD1 had to assume. The identity holds for any $J(g)$ with a curl-free field, so it shows that the action's static state balances itself; it is not evidence for this $J$.
\item \textbf{Force balance is not stability; the stability was then computed.} With the metric frozen (the decoupling limit) and in a planar background, lane TPA1 found the action's law-on phase gradient-unstable, with no ghost, wherever the clock's own dust density is below a critical value; the e-folding times on its grid are 5.0\,Myr to 0.62\,Gyr. The note on form B read the form's static state, which has almost no dust of its own, as lying in that range. Lane SCS1 then coupled gravity around a point mass and labels the state STABLE on both footings: two independent routes find no growing mode at any wavenumber, and disturbances oscillate at 0.08 to 12 times the local free-fall rate. The clock's gradient coefficient is its own charge density, $n_d+D\!\cdot\!P$. In TPA1's planar background $D\!\cdot\!P=p'$, which is negative; around a point mass $D\!\cdot\!P=p'+2p/r$ is the phantom density, which is positive. SCS1 reproduces TPA1's numbers in the planar background, and the growth returns when that background is put back into the coupled system. TPA1's other findings stand, and the note's sentence has been corrected.
\item \textbf{What that does not settle.} The result is linear, and for point masses only. For flattened baryons the law's phantom density can be negative in places, and there the same closed form gives growth; SCS1 did not analyse discs or extended spherical distributions. Version 1.1: lane SCS2 analysed discs. Isolated discs (razor-thin exponential and Kuzmin at four masses, a thick disc, a disc with a bulge) are STABLE on both footings: $D\!\cdot\!P$ is positive in every cell on both grids and no mode grows on either route. SCS2's frequency known answer fails with its frozen fit and passes under its fourth amendment, which is post hoc and followed a graded run. With a uniform external field along the axis or in the plane the label is NO VERDICT on both footings: both routes find unstable regions, and the magnitudes are not converged or rest on one grid. Lane SCS3 took the external-field case alone and also returns NO VERDICT on both footings; it says that the sign is not in doubt and that STABLE is excluded there. It also says that SCS2's label for isolated discs does not cover the centre, and that whether the static clock is stable at a disc's own centre is open. Lane DCN1 then took the centre. Every reading it froze before its outcome was seen is NO VERDICT, each on a known-answer gate; under the last of its six post hoc amendments, two of them written after the outcome was seen, it reads ARTEFACT on both footings, with the isolated centre stable. Whatever the reading, every one of its 24 resolved solves has $D\!\cdot\!P$ positive in every cell and no growing rate. Extended spherical distributions remain unanalysed. At the dust constant the CMB needs, the static state is Jeans-unstable at the local free-fall rate (e-folding times of 17\,Myr to 3.9\,Gyr): that is the cosmological setting and not the form's galaxy setting. The lane's three amendments are post hoc and disclosed; one mutation arm does not bite, being a field redefinition, as the lane said before any run.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{The theory in six rules}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Fields.} The metric, one clock field that picks a slicing, and a polarization on its slices. Matter couples to the metric only. The action is lane QX17's action (A), which is PAPER70's.
\item \textbf{One conserved charge.} The clock equation is a conserved current. The universe carries 5.41 units of its charge per unit of baryons, a fitted number. It is not derived: in the action it is an integration constant (note R541, which is not a graded lane), and four attempts to derive it are EXCLUDED on both footings: a clock-matter coupling (R541A), a Gauss-Bonnet source (R541C), a baryon-current coupling (R541D) and a coupling to $B-L$ (R541E). A fifth, an early potential on the clock (R541B), reads TRADES: it moves the number into the potential's height and does not fix it.
\item \textbf{Falling clock.} Where the clock free-falls with the expansion the charge is cold dust. This is the cold component of cosmology.
\item \textbf{Standing clock.} Where the clock stands still around baryons its own equations give the law, with $a_0=\tfrac12c\sqrt{G\rho_{\rm DE}}$ and $\kappa=\tfrac12$ fitted. The halo is the charge itself and gravitates and lenses as mass.
\item \textbf{Counted once.} A halo is made of the charge its region brought. A hot system holds 5.41 times the baryons that fell into it. A body that brought no charge has no halo.
\item \textbf{The joint.} How a falling clock becomes a standing one is not derived.
\end{enumerate}
The data rows of this note are computed from rules 3 to 5, and several of them fail or have no verdict. Rule 6 is the missing equation, and the hot branch of rule 5 is a rule and not a solution of the action.
