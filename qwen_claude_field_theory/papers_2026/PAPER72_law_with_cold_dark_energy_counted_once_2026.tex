\begin{abstract}\noindent
This note states the form on which the record closes and grades it. It does not complete the theory.
The $a_0$ law alone fails the CMB, the growth of structure, clusters and the Bullet, each for want of the same cold, clustering component of about 5.4 times the baryons.
Every way tried of keeping that component out of galaxies has a failing verdict; the last one is recorded here.
The form chosen by the owner on 2026-11-09 counts the component once: it is in galaxies too, and around condensed baryons the law describes how it sits. It is real mass, not a second force.
In the adopted action this is the static state's own reading: the law's halo is the khronon sector's energy density, carried by the one conserved charge that is dust in the expanding background.
We regrade the form with criteria frozen before any number.
On groups and clusters it is not falsified, by a gate that can fail from one side only, and the richest clusters sit at its ceiling; the Bullet passes only if the subcluster's stripped gas is counted.
On hosted dwarfs it fails: it removes most of the law's shortfall in every class, but two classes stay high and four low rows cannot be reached.
On KiDS lensing by galaxy type the test returns no verdict; its readings put early types between the law and the form's truncated halo and cannot place discs; on host-free lenses the form passes a gate that does not separate it from the law.
The form passes rotation curves. Its CMB, lensing and growth rows hold when computed, on an amended reading of that lane's checks, with the amplitude of fluctuations unsolved.
No solution has been computed that takes infalling dust to the static state; that state is unstable in the one limit analysed; no single dust term serves the CMB and galaxies; and no cluster state has been solved.
We list what is open, the measurement that would decide each item, and our own wrong estimates.
\end{abstract}

\section{Two forms}
Cold dark energy is this record's name for a gravitating field component with $w\approx0$ at the cosmic amount; it is not a particle. Two footings, canonical $a_0=9.3603\times10^{-11}$ and alt $1.1312\times10^{-10}$\,m\,s$^{-2}$, are carried throughout and never pooled; pairs of numbers are canonical/alt unless a row says otherwise.

\textbf{Form A, the law alone,} is what PAPER70 states. It passes rotation curves and the Solar System. It fails the CMB (which needs a cold component of 5.41 times the baryons), growth, X-COP by $+0.4675$/$+0.4345$\,dex, the Bullet ($Z=-4.55$, the mass sits on the gas) and Coma's ultra-diffuse galaxies ($+1.12$\,dex with a static external field, $+1.41$ with a moving host).

\textbf{Form B, the law with cold dark energy counted once,} is the single-counting reading that lanes PD1 and PD2 graded with one fixed share per halo. As graded there it is FAIL/FAIL: it passes its population budget, rotation curves (rms 0.0563/0.0558 against a line of 0.0660) and the carried cosmology, and fails KiDS's pooled outer points (worst $-0.635$\,dex), the groups and the Bullet's amount; X-COP passes only in a reported bracket ($+0.019$/$+0.010$).

On 2026-11-09 the owner chose B as the form the record closes on, with both forms scored side by side from the record. The choice is of a form, not of a pass: no label was changed by it. It reopens the cold sector retired on 2026-11-01 for this one reading. Section~3 is the regrade that followed.

\section{Form B in the adopted action}
The action of PAPER70 has certified field equations (lane QX17). A note written after the decision, and checked independently for this paper, reads form B out of them for a static clock.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{The law's halo is energy density.} The khronon sector's energy density contains $\Pi=D\!\cdot\!P$, and the static weak-field 00 equation is Poisson's with source $\rho_b+D\!\cdot\!P$. Light and matter both see it.
\item \textbf{One conserved charge.} The khronon equation is a conserved current whose charge is, to leading order, $D\!\cdot\!P+\mu_K^2(Q-1)/K$. With $P=0$ it is cold dust in the expanding background; around a static source it is the law's halo plus the static dust term $-\mu_K^2\Phi/K$, which is zero at $\mu_K=0$. Nothing is counted twice.
\item \textbf{The static state is in force balance.} With $J(g)=(a_0^2/K)[u-\ln(1+u)]$, $u=g/a_0$, the law sector's stress satisfies $\partial_j\Pi_{ij}=(D\!\cdot\!P)\,g_i$. In the deep field $J=g^2/2K$, which is the pressure of an isothermal halo with $\sigma^2=\tfrac12\sqrt{GM_ba_0}$: the relation PD1 had to assume. The identity holds for any $J(g)$ with a curl-free field, so it shows that the action's static state balances itself; it is not evidence for this $J$.
\item \textbf{Force balance is not stability.} With the metric frozen (the decoupling limit), lane TPA1 finds the action's law-on phase gradient-unstable, with no ghost, wherever the clock's own dust density is below a critical value. For a point mass that value is 0.08 to 0.9 of the law's phantom density, and the e-folding times on its grid are 5.0\,Myr to 0.62\,Gyr. TPA1 states this for its law-on phase. The note on form B reads the form's static state, which has almost no dust of its own, as lying in that range. The analysis coupled to gravity has not been done.
\end{itemize}
What the action does not give is stated in Section~5, items 1 to 4.

\subsection{Groups, clusters and the Bullet (RB1): NOT FALSIFIED (ONE-SIDED) on both footings}
In form B a hot system holds $R=5.41$ times the baryons that fell into it, and the component stays when gas is expelled or stripped. The retained fraction $f_{\rm ret}=R\,M_{b,\rm obs}/(M_{\rm dyn}-M_{b,\rm obs})$ can therefore not exceed 1 at a system's edge. This gate can fail from above only; the two-sided amount gate stays FAIL as PD1 graded it.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Groups.} The 20 hydrostatic groups read $f_{\rm ret}=0.597$ [0.556, 0.637] at $R_{500}$, none above 1. Form B reads the missing 40\,\% as gas outside $R_{500}$. Nothing on disk measures it.
\item \textbf{Clusters sit at the ceiling.} X-COP at its last radius reads 1.095 [0.885, 1.259], with 4 of 7 clusters above 1. At $R_{500}$ on the forward-fitted mass the class reads 1.057 [1.046, 1.072], above the ceiling beyond its errors; this row is a reported bracket, and graded there the gate would fail. On the NFW-fit hydrostatic mass the same class reads 0.959. The verdict for the deepest wells turns on about 10\,\% in the hydrostatic mass.
\item \textbf{Trend.} $f_{\rm ret}$ rises with depth: slope $+0.160$ [$+0.080$, $+0.225$] in $\log M_{500}$ from groups to X-COP.
\item \textbf{Radial shape.} One collisionless component with the record's own profile, anchored at each cluster's last radius, gives missing-mass fractions 0.72 / 0.63 / 0.55 / 0.47 / 0.35 per $r/R_{500}$ bin against the measured 0.71 / 0.66 / 0.58 / 0.46 / 0.32 (canonical); bin residuals $-0.007$ / $+0.033$ / $+0.006$ / $-0.030$ / $-0.041$\,dex against a line of 0.10. \item \textbf{The Bullet.} PD1's 2.6$\sigma$ deficit in the subcluster is the bookkeeping of the stripped gas. \end{itemize}

\section{What stays open}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{The collapse.} For a static clock the action's equations give the law's shape and hold it. No solution takes a free-falling clock, dust falling in with the baryons, to that state. This is the spiral wall of the 2026-11-01 ledger.
\item \textbf{Stability of the static state.} The law-on phase is gradient-unstable in the decoupling limit when the clock carries little dust of its own, which is how the note on form B reads the form's galaxies. The coupled analysis has not been done.
\item \textbf{One dust term.} With the quadratic $K(Q)$ the background has $w=w_0/(w_0+a^3)$ with $w_0=I_0/4\mu_K^2$. Our arithmetic, not a lane, with $H_0$ 70 and $\Omega_c$ 0.26: $w_0=1.063\times10^{-8}/\mu_K^2$ ($\mu_K$ per Mpc), so at $\mu_K=3.71$ per Mpc the component still has $w=\tfrac12$ at $z=1089$. Dust by recombination needs $\mu_K$ well above that; the galaxy-lensing bound is 0.1.
\item \textbf{Clusters are not a static-clock state.} Closing X-COP with the static dust term needs $\mu_K$ of 1.78/1.74 per Mpc, 17 to 18 times the galaxy bound. No other state has been solved.
\item \textbf{Groups} are untested from below: form B needs 40\,\% of their baryons to lie outside $R_{500}$.
\item \textbf{The richest clusters} sit at or above the form's ceiling, depending on the hydrostatic mass used.
\item \textbf{The Bullet} passes only with gas credited to the subcluster that no measurement fixes.
\item \textbf{Ultra-faints and M31's bright dwarfs} sit above the form.
\item \textbf{DF2 and DF4} sit below it and need a host 17 to 100 times heavier than the form gives.
\item \textbf{KiDS.} On all lenses, early types need more beyond the inner window than PD1's truncated halo and less than the law. On host-free lenses the form passes a gate that the law passes too. Discs are not tested by either.
\item \textbf{The outer cluster shear} stands near 3$\sigma$.
\item \textbf{Cosmology.} The amplitude is not solved, the forest row is carried, and the redistribution rows rest on an inherited halo list and on the cluster share being counted inside $R_{200c}$.
\end{enumerate}

\section{What would decide it}
\begin{itemize}
\item The spherical collapse of the clock around a baryon mass with the record's own equations. It is a mechanism lane and has not been started.
\item The stability of the static state with gravity coupled.
\item The baryons between $R_{500}$ and the turnaround radius around the same groups (thermal SZ, X-ray, stars) with a lensing mass to that radius. Form B needs $f_{\rm ret}=1$ at turnaround; a census that falls short falsifies it.
\item A cluster mass beyond $R_{500}$ that does not assume hydrostatic equilibrium.
\item A measured mass for NGC\,1052 inside 79 and 165\,kpc.
\item The dwarf classes regraded with a full systematics budget, frozen before the numbers.
\item Lensing by type with more area. A spectroscopic veto with the redshifts on disk has no power: the late-type error at the widest veto is 0.68/0.62 of the law in KO1 and is forecast at 0.57/0.52 with such a veto, against about 0.47 needed.
\item A CMB fit with low-multipole polarisation, to fix the amplitude that weak lensing then tests.
\item Gaia DR4 (2026-12-02), scored as pre-registered.
\end{itemize}

\section{Our own estimates}

(v) The Bullet estimate, closer than 2.6$\sigma$ once the stripped gas is counted, held; so did the group range and the Coma shift.
