\begin{abstract}
\noindent The programme ties MOND's acceleration scale to the vacuum, $a_0=\kappa c\sqrt{G\rho_\Lambda}$, with $\kappa=\tfrac12$ fitted, not derived; its footings are $9.3603\times10^{-11}$ ($\rho_\Lambda$) and $1.1312\times10^{-10}$\,m\,s$^{-2}$ ($\rho_{\rm crit}$). We apply a width chain, frozen before any width was read, to the MeerKAT MIGHTEE-HI COSMOS catalogue: $V$ comes from the rest-frame $W_{50}$ and optical inclination, the baryons are $1.33\,M_{\rm HI}+M_\star$ at half the H\,{\sc i} diameter, and a median-residual estimator with the exponential kernel returns $a_0$. Forty-seven golden discs at $0.027\le z\le0.093$ survive, all at $g_{\rm bar}/a_0\le0.084$, 37 gas-dominated. The catalogue paper's worked examples show its widths are rest-frame; version 1.0 divided them by $1+z$ again. On the catalogue's flux scale $a_0=1.31\times10^{-10}$\,m\,s$^{-2}$ (statistical $\pm0.034$\,dex, recipe $\pm0.128$\,dex), $\kappa=0.70$ or 0.58: consistent with SPARC and the alternative footing, the canonical footing just outside the recipe width. Arecibo fluxes exceed the catalogue's for brighter, nearer galaxies; applied to our discs' gas, $a_0$ falls to 1.07 (0.97--1.13)$\times10^{-10}$, inside the recipe width of both footings. Line-width corrections lower $a_0$ by up to 0.10\,dex; kernels without the exponential's next-order term raise it by 0.06--0.07\,dex. A SPARC closure tests only the baryon recipe. The result is not a footing discriminator and, with a lever of 0.009\,dex, not an $a_0(z)$ test. The cold mass ($\Omega_ch^2\approx0.12$) is still required.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

\textbf{The relation and its scale.} Milgrom's modified dynamics [M83] replaces the Newtonian acceleration below a scale $a_0$. In rotating galaxies the observed centripetal acceleration $g_{\rm obs}$ follows the baryonic one $g_{\rm bar}$ along a single radial acceleration relation: 2693 points in 153 SPARC galaxies [LMS16, MLS16, L17], with SPARC's fitted scale $g_\dagger=1.20\times10^{-10}$\,m\,s$^{-2}$ and a systematic uncertainty of $\pm0.24$ in the same units, 0.08\,dex [MLS16]. A full joint inference over the SPARC distances, inclinations, luminosities and mass-to-light ratios finds $a_0=(1.19\pm0.04\,{\rm(stat)}\pm0.09\,{\rm(sys)})\times10^{-10}$\,m\,s$^{-2}$ [D23]. From the baryonic Tully--Fisher relation (BTFR) of gas-rich galaxies, McGaugh [McG12] finds the equivalent of $a_0=1.3\pm0.3$ in the same units.
% AUDIT: B02

\textbf{MeerKAT and line widths.} The MIGHTEE-HI survey [MM26] measures H\,{\sc i} out to $z\approx0.09$ with one instrument and one pipeline. Ponomareva et al.\ [P21] built the H\,{\sc i} BTFR of 67 early-science galaxies over $0\le z\le0.081$ from the global-profile width $W_{50}$ (slope 3.66) and from resolved rotation curves, and found no evolution. Rajohnson et al.\ [R22] measured the H\,{\sc i} size--mass relation of 204 galaxies (slope 0.501, intercept $-3.252$). V\u{a}r\u{a}\c{s}teanu et al.\ measured the radial acceleration relation with resolved, SED-based mass-to-light ratios, first in 19 H\,{\sc i}-selected COSMOS galaxies to $z=0.08$ [V25], with $a_0=(1.69\pm0.13)\times10^{-10}$\,m\,s$^{-2}$ (as restated in V26) and tentative evidence for redshift evolution, then in 130 resolved MIGHTEE-HI and LADUMA galaxies [V26]. V26 fit the \emph{same} exponential function that we use and find $a_0=(1.50\pm0.05)\times10^{-10}$ (direct photometry) and $(1.48\pm0.04)\times10^{-10}$ (S\'ersic photometry); with the $\delta$-family of interpolating functions they find $(1.86\pm0.06)\times10^{-10}$. Their MIGHTEE and LADUMA galaxies alone give $a_1=(-1.60\pm2.33)\times10^{-10}$\,m\,s$^{-2}$ per unit redshift; anchored at $z\approx0$ by SPARC the same fit gives a formal $5.0\sigma$ rise of $a_0$ with $z$, which they attribute to the different selection of the anchor sample. For line widths, Lelli et al.\ [L19] give statistical relations between $W_{50}$ and the flat rotation speed $V_{\rm flat}$ for SPARC, and Papastergis et al.\ [PAH16] measured a $W_{50}$-based BTFR slope of $3.75\pm0.11$ for heavily gas-dominated, edge-on ALFALFA galaxies, with a dependence on profile shape. The standard width corrections are for turbulence [VS01] and instrumental broadening [S05]. The MIGHTEE-HI COSMOS catalogue [MM26] lists 293 sources at $0.004<z<0.093$ with H\,{\sc i} masses, $W_{50}$ widths, inclinations and stellar masses.
% AUDIT: B03

\textbf{The framework.} The programme ties the scale to the vacuum energy density, $a_0=\kappa c\sqrt{G\rho_\Lambda}$, with $\kappa=\tfrac12$ fitted, not derived. With $H_0=67.4$\,km\,s$^{-1}$\,Mpc$^{-1}$ and $\Omega_\Lambda=0.6847$ [PL20] this gives the canonical footing $a_0=9.3603\times10^{-11}$\,m\,s$^{-2}$; the same coefficient on the critical density gives the alternative footing $1.1312\times10^{-10}$. Its galaxy law is a MOND kernel $\nu$ with $g_{\rm obs}=\nu(g_{\rm bar}/a_0)\,g_{\rm bar}$. The framework's own closed form, $\nu=\sqrt{1+a_0/g_{\rm bar}}$, is the relation contained in Milgrom's vacuum-effect paper [M99] (with a different coefficient). The measurement lanes use the monotone kernel $\nu_{\rm mono}$, which below $g_{\rm bar}/a_0=2.54$ equals the exponential form $\nu=[1-\exp(-\sqrt{y})]^{-1}$ ($y=g_{\rm bar}/a_0$) to $2\times10^{-4}$; that form is the fitting function of [MLS16] and V26 and the $\alpha=\tfrac12$ member of the family of Milgrom \& Sanders [MS08]. The footings are numbers fixed by $\kappa$ and the density and carry no kernel, but any $a_0$ inferred from data does: at the accelerations of this sample the inferred value depends on the kernel (Section~\ref{sec:sys}), so every comparison with a footing below is conditional on the exponential kernel, and we give the closed form alongside. The distinctive prediction is a constant $a_0(z)$; the rival reading $a_0\propto H(z)$ rises by a factor $E(z)$. The framework adds no dark-matter particle, but the cold mass ($\Omega_ch^2\approx0.12$) is still required for cosmology.
% AUDIT: B04

\textbf{This paper.} We report a catalogue-based estimate of the local $a_0$ from MIGHTEE-HI (lane CFG301), with criteria frozen and committed before any width, velocity or mass value was read, a re-measurement of the inputs from the released cubes (CFG302), a check of the H\,{\sc i} flux scale against Arecibo single-dish fluxes (CFG304) and a test of the velocity frame of the catalogue widths (CFG309). No halo model enters; the only cosmological input is the catalogue's luminosity distance. Each lane is a folder of the public repository (Data availability). \emph{Version note.} Version 1.0 read the catalogue widths as observed-frame and divided them by $1+z$. A referee (CFG306) showed from the catalogue paper that they are rest-frame, and a frozen follow-up lane (CFG309) confirmed it. Version 1.1 makes the rest-frame reading primary, which raises the pooled $a_0$ by 0.098\,dex; it also replaces the flux-scale range, adds the kernel, width-side and selection systematics, and corrects the description of prior work. The version 1.0 numbers are kept as a disclosed variant.
% AUDIT: B05

\section{Data}\label{sec:data}

\textbf{The catalogue.} The MIGHTEE-HI COSMOS catalogue [MM26] gives, per source: $z_{\rm HI}$; the luminosity distance $D_L$ (flat $\Lambda$CDM, $H_0=70$, $\Omega_M=0.3$, no peculiar-velocity correction); $\log M_{\rm HI}$; the 3-D signal-to-noise $S\!N_{\rm 3D}$; $W_{50}$ from a busy-function fit [W14] at 50\% of the average of the two peaks, not corrected for inclination; the inclination from $g$-band isophotes with intrinsic thickness $q_0=0.2$; the stellar mass from BAGPIPES SED fits [C18]; and five quality flags (low confidence, blended, confused, bad ellipse, contaminated). The \emph{golden sample} has all five flags zero. The catalogue's distances agree with flat $\Lambda$CDM at $H_0=70$ to 0.45\% over the survivors, and its inclinations follow from its axis ratios with $q_0=0.2$ to $0.8^\circ$ (rounding; this paper's script).
% AUDIT: B06

\textbf{The frozen cut.} Applied by code, in this order, before any width was read as data: the golden sample (293 to 202 sources); $0.02\le z_{\rm HI}\le0.093$ (188; a 300\,km\,s$^{-1}$ peculiar velocity is then at most 5\% of the distance); $45^\circ\le i\le80^\circ$ (122); $\log M_{\rm HI}\ge9.0$ (78); $W_{50}\ge80$\,km\,s$^{-1}$ with $\sigma_{W_{50}}/W_{50}\le0.15$ (70); $S\!N_{\rm 3D}\ge8$ (47); finite inputs (47). The 47 survivors are split by redshift into three equal-count windows: W1 (16 galaxies, $z$ 0.0266--0.0476, median 0.042, median $D_L$ 185\,Mpc), W2 (16, $z$ 0.0506--0.0756, median 0.067, 300\,Mpc) and W3 (15, $z$ 0.0773--0.0930, median 0.081, 368\,Mpc). The median gas fraction is 0.70.

\section{Method}\label{sec:method}

\textbf{The chain.} For each galaxy,
\begin{equation*}
V=\frac{W_{50}-\delta}{2\sin i},\qquad M_b=1.33\,M_{\rm HI}+M_\star,\qquad R=\tfrac12D_{\rm HI},\quad \log_{10}\frac{D_{\rm HI}}{\rm kpc}=0.506\log_{10}\frac{M_{\rm HI}}{\rm M_\odot}-3.293,
\end{equation*}
\begin{equation*}
g_{\rm bar}=\frac{GM_b}{R^2},\qquad g_{\rm obs}=\frac{V^2}{R},\qquad D=\frac{g_{\rm obs}}{g_{\rm bar}},
\end{equation*}
with $W_{50}$ the catalogue's rest-frame width (Section~\ref{sec:sys}), $\delta=0$ (the primary; a turbulence term $\delta=11$\,km\,s$^{-1}$ is a recipe knob), the catalogue inclination, the factor 1.33 for helium, no molecular gas, and the BAGPIPES $M_\star$. CFG301 froze the chain with a division of $W_{50}$ by $1+z$; CFG309 re-ran CFG301's committed script with that one factor removed (Section~\ref{sec:sys}). The size relation has the form of Wang et al.\ [W16]; its coefficients are those adopted in the programme's earlier width lane (CFG260) and were not re-checked against the text of [W16], whose abstract gives only the scatter (0.06\,dex). All baryons are placed inside $R$ as a point mass, so $g_{\rm bar}$ uses the total $M_b$: an asymptotic approximation, not the mass enclosed at $R$. The radius that $W_{50}$ samples is H\,{\sc i}-weighted and usually smaller than $R_{\rm HI}$, so the $D_{\rm HI}$ knob below may understate the radius uncertainty.
% AUDIT: B08

\textbf{The estimator.} The implied ratio $s^*$ is the root of $\mathrm{median}_i\,\log_{10}[D_i/\nu(g_{{\rm bar},i}/(a_0s))]=0$ with $\nu=\nu_{\rm mono}$ and $a_0$ the canonical value; the measured scale is $s^*\times9.3603\times10^{-11}$\,m\,s$^{-2}$ (the alternative footing gives the same absolute $a_0$ to $3\times10^{-17}$\,dex). Intervals come from a bootstrap over galaxies ($B=4{,}000$). In the deep limit $\nu\to y^{-1/2}$ and the estimator reduces to $a_0=V^4/(GM_b)$, independent of $R$; at the next order a galaxy on the law shows $V^4/(GM_b)=a_0\,y\,\nu(y)^2>a_0$, and the estimator removes that factor. The exponential kernel behaves as $\nu\approx y^{-1/2}+\tfrac12$ at small $y$; kernels without the $+\tfrac12$ return a larger $a_0$ from the same data.
% AUDIT: B09

\textbf{The recipe half-width} is the quadrature of four knobs, each applied to every galaxy: $\delta=11$\,km\,s$^{-1}$; $M_\star\pm0.25$\,dex; $D_{\rm HI}$ (hence $R$) $\pm0.15$\,dex; and the distances moved from $H_0=70$ to 67.4 (the value behind $\rho_\Lambda$). The isotropic $\sin60^\circ$ in place of the catalogue inclinations is reported as a sensitivity. A second statistic, frozen with the first, is the direct ratio $a_0^{\rm BTFR}=V^4/(GM_b)$ per galaxy, with its median over (i) all survivors, (ii) the gas-dominated discs ($1.33\,M_{\rm HI}>M_\star$) and (iii) the deep discs ($y<0.5$).
% AUDIT: B10

\textbf{Calibration controls (frozen; each could fail).} The values below are at the rest-frame reading (CFG309); all four also pass in the version 1.0 frame.
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{CC1, local level:} the nearest window W1 must return $s^*$ within $\pm0.15$\,dex of the SPARC scale ($1.20/0.93603=1.282$) and of the programme's ALFALFA control (CFG281, $s^*=1.449$ [H18, D20]), with a recipe half-width $\le0.20$\,dex. W1 gives $s^*=1.440$: $+0.050$\,dex from 1.282, $-0.003$\,dex from 1.449, half-width 0.135. PASS. CFG281 is itself \emph{not calibrated} by its own record and uses $\delta=9$\,km\,s$^{-1}$, observed-frame ALFALFA widths and the isotropic $\sin60^\circ$, so it is not the same recipe; the $\pm0.15$\,dex tolerance is also wider than the 0.082\,dex between the footings.
\item \emph{CC2, SPARC closure of the baryon side:} the same $M_b$ (with $0.5\,L_{[3.6]}$ for the stars), size relation and point mass applied to 123 SPARC galaxies (quality $\le2$, $i\ge30^\circ$, $V_{\rm flat}>0$) with $g_{\rm obs}=V_{\rm flat}^2/R$ return $s^*=1.304$, $+0.008$\,dex from 1.282. With the measured $R_{\rm HI}$ in place of the relation the value is 1.324; the relation is unbiased on SPARC (median $-0.002$\,dex). PASS. CC2 uses $V_{\rm flat}$ and SPARC's own $M_{\rm HI}$; it tests neither $W_{50}$, the frame, $\delta$ nor the MeerKAT flux scale.
\item \emph{CC3, drift:} $|\log s^*({\rm W3})-\log s^*({\rm W1})|\le0.10$\,dex. The drift is $+0.022$\,dex. PASS, with little power: the statistical width of the drift is 0.141\,dex.
\item \emph{CC4, self-test:} in 100 worlds with widths fabricated from the law at $s=2$ on the survivors' own masses and inclinations (0.15\,dex scatter, 10\% width errors), the bias is $-0.013$\,dex (tolerance 0.023) and the 95\% interval covers the truth in 93 of 100. PASS (run once; it tests the estimator, which the frame does not enter).
\end{itemize}
% AUDIT: B11

\textbf{The width side, tested on SPARC (post hoc, CFG306).} On 22 SPARC galaxies with an ALFALFA width (quality $\le2$, $i\ge30^\circ$, ALFALFA code 1), $W_{50}/(2\sin i)$ exceeds $V_{\rm flat}$ by $+0.026$\,dex (68\% $+0.022$ to $+0.032$), which in the deep limit is $+0.10$\,dex in $a_0$. The CC2 recipe run on the same galaxies with $W_{50}/(2\sin i)$ in place of $V_{\rm flat}$ returns $s^*$ higher by $+0.184$\,dex at $\delta=0$ and by $+0.071$\,dex at $\delta=11$\,km\,s$^{-1}$. The chain does not correct for this; on these numbers a correction would \emph{lower} $a_0$, and $\delta=5$\,km\,s$^{-1}$ lowers our pooled value by 0.044\,dex (Table~\ref{tab:sys}).
% AUDIT: B12

\textbf{Reactivity and reproduction.} In the version 1.0 frame, widths multiplied by 1.1892 raise the pooled $s^*$ by $+0.322$\,dex (expected $0.30\pm0.05$; MUTATE=1); $\sin i_{\rm eff}=0.70$ against the $\sin60^\circ$ variant raises it by $+0.392$\,dex (expected $0.37\pm0.06$; MUTATE=2). The chain, fed CFG281's ALFALFA arrays, reproduces CFG281's committed $s^*$ exactly (1.332408 unmatched, 1.449173 matched; M5). All six frozen hand estimates hit. CFG309's re-run reproduces CFG301's committed values exactly when the factor is kept, and its rest-frame value agrees with CFG306's independent code to $2\times10^{-16}$\,dex.
% AUDIT: B13

\textbf{Disclosures.} Before the freeze only the catalogue's header line had been read. The frozen text asks for mass-matched windows in CC1 and CC3 but does not define the matching; the rule fixed in the script before any value was read (W1 and W2 matched to W3's $\log M_{\rm HI}$ quartile bins, at least three galaxies per bin) could not be met (W1 had 8/3/2/2 galaxies per bin), so CC1, CC3 and the drift use unmatched windows. W1's median $\log M_{\rm HI}$ (9.605) is 0.155\,dex below W3's (9.76). A reader who insists on matching should treat CC1 and CC3 as not evaluable. The BTFR kernel-factor diagnostic was added after the SPARC control and before the measurement, using baryons only. The rest-frame reading was adopted after the measurement, on CFG309's frozen rule.

\textbf{The level.} On the catalogue's flux scale the pooled scale is $a_0=1.311\times10^{-10}$\,m\,s$^{-2}$ ($s^*=1.401$), with a bootstrap 68\% interval of 1.273--1.418, a 95\% interval of 1.206--1.669 and a bootstrap standard deviation of 0.034\,dex (the 68\% interval of a median of 47 is lumpy), and a recipe half-width of 0.128\,dex (Table~\ref{tab:res}, Fig.~\ref{fig:levels}). It lies $+0.146$\,dex above the canonical footing, $+0.064$\,dex above the alternative and $+0.038$\,dex above SPARC's 1.20, inside SPARC's own 0.08\,dex systematic. Both footings lie outside the 95\% statistical interval; with the recipe half-width the alternative footing is inside and the canonical footing is outside by 0.019\,dex. The recipe knobs contribute 0.098 ($\delta$), 0.069 ($M_\star$), 0.026 ($D_{\rm HI}$) and 0.036\,dex ($H_0$); the $\sin60^\circ$ sensitivity is 0.035\,dex (0.132 with it in the quadrature). All 47 galaxies are deep: $y=g_{\rm bar}/a_0$ at $R$ has quartiles 0.028, 0.033 and 0.042 and a maximum of 0.084. In the version 1.0 frame the pooled value is $1.046\times10^{-10}$ ($-0.098$\,dex).
% AUDIT: B16

\textbf{The windows and the drift.} The window medians are 1.348, 1.305 and 1.416 (Table~\ref{tab:res}); their bootstrap intervals are lumpy, because a median of 15--16 galaxies jumps between galaxies. The slope of $\log_{10}s^*$ on $\log_{10}D_L$ is $+0.05$ per dex (68\% $-0.44$ to $+0.35$; 95\% $-1.03$ to $+0.64$): not resolved. The ratio W3/W1 is 1.051 ($\log_{10}$: $+0.022\pm0.141$). Version 1.0's remark that its drift had the sign of the programme's ALFALFA control is withdrawn: the drift changed sign. Between W1 and W3 the rival $a_0\propto H(z)$ predicts $+0.009$\,dex, far below the statistical width. A joint fit of the per-galaxy residual in $z$ and $\log M_{\rm HI}$ (post hoc, this paper) gives $-2.6$ per unit $z$ (95\% $-8.0$ to $+1.5$) and $+0.09$ per dex of $M_{\rm HI}$ (95\% $-0.23$ to $+0.49$): neither is resolved.
% AUDIT: B17

\textbf{The direct BTFR.} The median $V^4/(GM_b)$ is $1.530\times10^{-10}$\,m\,s$^{-2}$ over all 47 (68\% 1.460--1.725; $+0.213$\,dex from the canonical footing, $+0.131$\,dex from the alternative; recipe 0.111\,dex) and $1.464\times10^{-10}$ over the 37 gas-dominated discs (68\% 1.457--1.530; $+0.194$ and $+0.112$\,dex; recipe 0.120\,dex). The deep subset (iii) is the same 47 galaxies as (i). These rows sit above the $s^*$ row by design: computed from the baryons alone, the median kernel factor $y\nu_{\rm mono}(y)^2$ is 1.197 ($+0.078$\,dex) at the canonical $a_0$, 1.172 at $1.20\times10^{-10}$ and 1.183 for the gas-dominated discs. The gas-dominated median differs from the full one by $-0.019$\,dex, but the per-galaxy residual correlates with the gas fraction and with $\log M_\star$ (Section~\ref{sec:sys}), so the stellar mass is not negligible. The inverse BTFR slope of the 47 is 3.43 (68\% 3.11--3.79; post hoc), against 3.66 [P21] and a forward-equivalent 3.73 [V26]; with a slope below 4 the median $V^4/(GM_b)$ depends on the sample's mass distribution. The same recipe on SPARC gives a BTFR level of $1.526\times10^{-10}$ (CC2's diagnostic, with $V_{\rm flat}$). Fig.~\ref{fig:btfr} shows the 47 discs against the deep-MOND line on both footings.
% AUDIT: B18

\textbf{The coefficient.} Read as $\kappa=\tfrac12\,a_0/a_0^{\rm footing}$ (with $\kappa=\tfrac12$ itself fitted), the pooled scale gives $\kappa=0.700$ on the $\rho_\Lambda$ footing (bootstrap 68\% 0.680--0.758, 95\% 0.644--0.892; scaled by $10^{\pm0.128}$, 0.52--0.94) and $\kappa=0.580$ on $\rho_{\rm crit}$ (68\% 0.563--0.627, 95\% 0.533--0.738; recipe 0.43--0.78). On statistics alone $\kappa=\tfrac12$ lies below the 95\% interval on both footings; with the recipe width it is just outside on $\rho_\Lambda$ and inside on $\rho_{\rm crit}$. These are on the catalogue's $H_0=70$ distances. On the $H_0=67.4$ distances that match $\rho_\Lambda$, the scale is $1.208\times10^{-10}$ and the central values become 0.645 and 0.534 (Section~\ref{sec:sys}).
% AUDIT: B19

\textbf{Against other determinations.} The pooled scale is $+0.042$\,dex from the joint inference of [D23] (1.19) and $-0.058$\,dex from the MIGHTEE-HI/LADUMA resolved fit of [V26] (1.50), which uses the same exponential function with resolved rotation curves and resolved SED mass-to-light ratios; their sub-samples span 1.31--1.80 (CFG279), and their 19-galaxy COSMOS fit gave 1.69 [V25]. The two MIGHTEE analyses are not the same measurement: ours places all baryons at $D_{\rm HI}/2$ and reads the velocity from $W_{50}$, and at most 28 of V26's 130 galaxies are in COSMOS (V25's 19 and 9 from the new catalogue). V26's H\,{\sc i} comes from 3D Barolo fits to the MeerKAT cubes with a smoothed $3\sigma$ source mask, so both analyses carry MeerKAT's interferometric flux scale.

\textbf{The velocity frame (CFG309).} Under criteria frozen before the catalogue paper's text was read for this purpose, CFG309 found the following. (T1) The catalogue paper never states the frame in words. (T2) Its five worked example spectra print each $W_{50}$ both in km\,s$^{-1}$ and in 26.126\,kHz channels; in all five the km\,s$^{-1}$ per channel equals $c\,\Delta\nu/\nu_{\rm obs}$, the rest-frame width, to $+0.066$ to $+0.068$\% (spread $1.4\times10^{-5}$), and the observed-frame and radio conventions are excluded in all five. Four of the five printed widths equal the catalogue's values; the fifth (J100404.9+014303: 237.858\,km\,s$^{-1}$ printed, 223 catalogued) is an unexplained anomaly. (T3) The ALFALFA comparison cannot separate the frames (a lever of 0.012\,dex). (T4) Busy-function fits to the released cubes lean towards the observed frame (median offset $-0.017$\,dex for the rest-frame reading), but the test failed its injection control (rest-frame worlds recovered as rest-frame in 13 of 20) and is inadmissible. The decision therefore rests on the catalogue paper's own worked numbers; no independent data test could decide it. CFG302's raw widths (rest-frame) agree with the catalogue's to $-0.022$\,dex and would agree to $+0.002$\,dex under the observed-frame reading, which does not decide it either. Version 1.0's $(1+z)$ division came from the programme's ALFALFA lanes, whose widths are observed-frame.
% AUDIT: B24

\textbf{The H\,{\sc i} flux scale (CFG302).} CFG302 re-measured the 188 golden sources at $0.02\le z\le0.093$ from the four released MIGHTEE-HI r1p0 cubes (circular restoring beam 62.2--77.3$''$ over the valid channels, median 75.9$''$; per-sub-cube medians 74.7--77.3$''$; 8$''$ pixels), with criteria frozen before any spectrum was extracted. Of the 179 sources in its primary set, 58 reach its frozen detection threshold. \emph{The widths agree:} the median log ratio of our rest-frame $W_{50}$ to the catalogue's is $-0.022$\,dex with a robust scatter of 0.039\,dex (4 of 58 pull outliers). This confirms the catalogue's $W_{50}$ \emph{measurement}, not the conversion of $W_{50}$ into a rotation speed. \emph{The fluxes do not:} the median ratio over all 179 is 0.22, and over the 58 detections the median log ratio is $-0.30$\,dex (a ratio of 0.50, scatter 0.24\,dex; 46 of 58 pull outliers). The aperture is complete ($S(2.5\theta)/S(1.5\theta)=1.002$), and the stacked line holds $0.60\pm0.05$ of the catalogue's mean flux. One frozen control failed and is kept: the line-free signal-to-noise spread is 0.489, not about 1, because the noise is anticorrelated over long spectral lags, as if the released spectra were high-pass filtered. For exactly our 47 discs (post hoc join with CFG302's committed table), 45 are in its primary set and 20 reach its threshold; their median cube-to-catalogue flux log ratio is $-0.443$\,dex, while their widths agree ($-0.017$\,dex).
% AUDIT: B25

\textbf{An independent flux scale (ALFALFA, CFG304).} CFG304 matched the catalogue with the ALFALFA catalogue of Arecibo single-dish fluxes [H18], under criteria frozen before any per-galaxy flux was read: 23 matches, 15 of them ALFALFA code 1. The matches are real: shuffled positions give 0.275 matches on average. The catalogue and ALFALFA widths have a median log ratio of $+0.000$\,dex, which sits on exact ties (68\% $-0.025$ to $0.000$); the rest-frame reading predicts $-0.012$\,dex and the observed-frame reading zero, so this does not decide the frame. On the 15 code-1 pairs the median log ratio of catalogue to single-dish flux is $-0.195$\,dex (68\% $-0.250$ to $-0.156$), a ratio of about 0.64; for the 7 of them with a cube detection the cube ratio is $-0.308$\,dex. Without pairs that have a catalogued neighbour within 3.5$'$ the two ratios are $-0.175$ and $-0.383$. The catalogue's $\log M_{\rm HI}$ sit 0.22\,dex below ALFALFA's for the same galaxies. The frozen decision is ``neither'': the single dish sees more H\,{\sc i} than both MeerKAT scales, and the cube scale lies further from it than the catalogue's, so the cube scale is not the relevant systematic. Its cause is open; the catalogue paper's iterated $3\sigma$ moment-map mask is a plausible one, not demonstrated.
% AUDIT: B26

\textbf{How far the offset carries to our discs (post hoc, CFG306).} The matched pairs are not like our discs. The 15 code-1 pairs have median $z$ 0.028 (ours 0.065), $S\!N_{\rm 3D}$ 18.7 (ours 12.4) and H\,{\sc i} diameter 66$''$ (ours 40$''$); only 30\% of our discs lie inside the pairs' redshift range, and 7 of the 47 have a pair. Within the code-1 pairs the ratio becomes less negative at lower $S\!N_{\rm 3D}$ (Spearman $-0.62$, $p$ 0.01), and linear trends carried to our medians give $-0.115$ (in $S\!N_{\rm 3D}$) and $-0.087$ (in $z$, all 23 pairs); CFG304's $z\ge0.02$ subsets give $-0.137$ (19 pairs) and $-0.120$ (confusion-clean). The direction is robust: SPARC's own $M_{\rm HI}$ sit $+0.053$\,dex above ALFALFA's for 35 common galaxies at the same distance, so the catalogue is about 0.25\,dex below the H\,{\sc i} scale of CC2's calibrator; confusion would need about 500 times the mean cosmic density of uncatalogued H\,{\sc i} in an Arecibo beam; and MeerKAT's 29\,m shortest baseline recovers scales of about 25$'$, far larger than our discs.
% AUDIT: B27

\textbf{What the flux scale does to $a_0$.} The chain uses the catalogue's $M_{\rm HI}$. An H\,{\sc i} flux error changes the gas, not the BAGPIPES stellar masses, so the reading is the gas alone raised, with $R$ following the size relation (CFG306 P3, reproduced exactly here). With the ratio carried to our discs in $S\!N_{\rm 3D}$, $a_0=1.07\times10^{-10}$\,m\,s$^{-2}$; the 7 matched pairs that pass CFG301's own cut give 0.97, the $z$ extrapolation 1.13, the $z\ge0.02$ subsets 1.02 and 1.06, and the unadjusted 15 code-1 pairs 0.90 (all $\times10^{-10}$). We take 1.07 (0.97--1.13) as the single-dish reading for our discs. On it $\kappa$ is 0.571 ($\rho_\Lambda$) or 0.472 ($\rho_{\rm crit}$); the canonical footing is $+0.058$\,dex and the alternative $-0.025$\,dex away, both inside the recipe width. Version 1.0's reading with all baryons raised (0.60--0.75$\times10^{-10}$ in that frame) is withdrawn as a flux systematic: it scales the stellar masses with the H\,{\sc i} flux. Across the two flux scales the honest range is about 1.0 to $1.31\times10^{-10}$, and on present evidence the catalogue-scale value is the least consistent with the single dish, not the top of a range.
% AUDIT: B28

\textbf{Kernel.} At $y\approx0.03$ the inferred $a_0$ depends on the kernel. The exponential and simple forms carry the $+\tfrac12$ term and agree ($-0.001$\,dex); the standard $n=2$ form, the framework's closed form $\sqrt{1+1/y}$ and V26's best $\delta$-family member lack it and raise $a_0$ by $+0.06$ to $+0.07$\,dex, to 1.50 with the closed form (1.22 in the version 1.0 frame). This is comparable to the 0.082\,dex between the footings. Against CC2 run with the same kernel, the MIGHTEE value moves only between $+0.001$ and $+0.032$\,dex, so the comparison with SPARC is robust to the kernel while the comparison with the footings is not. V26 see the same effect on MIGHTEE data (1.50 against 1.86).
% AUDIT: B29

\textbf{Line width, inclination, stellar mass, molecular gas and size.} No turbulence or instrumental correction is applied; $\delta=11$\,km\,s$^{-1}$, the largest recipe term, lowers $a_0$ by 0.098\,dex, and the SPARC comparison above suggests a correction of that sign. The catalogue's inclinations follow from its axis ratios with $q_0=0.2$; $q_0=0.10$ or 0.30 moves the pooled $a_0$ by $+0.029$ or $-0.050$\,dex (post hoc) and the isotropic $\sin60^\circ$ by $+0.035$\,dex. The $45^\circ$ cut keeps the face-on end out; optical inclinations can differ from the H\,{\sc i} disc's, and seeing affects the axis ratios of the smallest discs. The per-galaxy residual shows no trend with inclination (Spearman $-0.08$) or axis ratio ($+0.09$). The stellar mass moves $a_0$ by $-0.076$ / $+0.063$\,dex for $\pm0.25$\,dex. Molecular gas at 0.1 or 0.3 of $M_{\rm HI}$, omitted by the chain, lowers $a_0$ by 0.025 or 0.073\,dex. In the deep limit the estimator does not depend on $R$, so the size relation enters only at next order: $D_{\rm HI}\pm0.15$\,dex moves $a_0$ by 0.026\,dex (half-width), and MIGHTEE's own relation [R22] by $-0.001$\,dex ($D_{\rm HI}$ smaller by 0.008\,dex at the median $\log M_{\rm HI}=9.73$). The point mass at $D_{\rm HI}/2$ is a recipe choice, not a resolved mass model.
% AUDIT: B30

\textbf{Selection.} The frozen $S\!N_{\rm 3D}\ge8$ cut removes 23 of 70 discs. Alone, those 23 give $2.23\times10^{-10}$, and all 70 give $1.59\times10^{-10}$ ($+0.084$\,dex; post hoc; in the version 1.0 frame 1.72 and 1.23, CFG306). Between thresholds of 6 and 12 the pooled value moves by $-0.009$ to $+0.034$\,dex. Over the 70, the catalogue's $S\!N_{\rm 3D}$ scales as $W_{50}^{-0.50}$ at fixed flux (CFG306), so the cut favours narrow lines; low-S/N fluxes or widths may also be biased. The per-galaxy residual correlates with $W_{50}$ (Spearman $+0.63$), with the gas fraction ($-0.45$, $p$ 0.002) and with $\log M_\star$ ($+0.36$, $p$ 0.01), but not with $z$ ($-0.11$) or $\log M_{\rm HI}$ ($+0.04$). The sample is H\,{\sc i}-flux-selected; V26 show that for such a sample the BTFR fitting direction moves their evolution term from $-11.4\pm1.3$ to $-8.1\pm2.4$ (both $\times10^{-10}$\,m\,s$^{-2}$ per unit $z$).
% AUDIT: B31

\textbf{Distances and $H_0$.} The catalogue's distances assume $H_0=70$ and no peculiar velocities, while $\rho_\Lambda$ uses $H_0=67.4$. Moving the distances to 67.4 raises every mass by $2\log_{10}(70/67.4)$ and lowers the pooled $a_0$ by 0.036\,dex, to $1.208\times10^{-10}$ ($s^*=1.290$); against the footings this is the like-for-like value. Peculiar velocities are random per galaxy and at most 5\% in distance above $z=0.02$.
% AUDIT: B32

\textbf{Mass matching and sample size.} The windows could not be mass-matched (Section~\ref{sec:method}), so the drift compares W1, whose median $\log M_{\rm HI}$ is 0.155\,dex lower, with W3. With 15--16 galaxies per window the window medians are lumpy, CC3 has little power and the drift slope is unresolved.
% AUDIT: B33

\section{What the result can and cannot say}\label{sec:say}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{It can say:} an independent instrument and sample, with no halo model, give a deep-regime scale of $1.311\times10^{-10}$\,m\,s$^{-2}$ on the catalogue's flux scale and about $1.07\times10^{-10}$ with the gas on the single-dish scale. The catalogue-scale value is consistent with SPARC ($+0.038$\,dex) and with the alternative footing ($+0.064$\,dex, inside the recipe half-width of 0.128\,dex); the canonical footing ($+0.146$\,dex) is just outside it on that scale and inside it on the single-dish scale.
\item \textbf{It is not a footing discriminator.} The footings differ by 0.082\,dex. The flux scale moves $a_0$ by $-0.06$ to $-0.16$\,dex, the width side by up to $-0.10$\,dex and the kernel by up to $+0.07$\,dex.
\item \textbf{It is not an $a_0(z)$ test.} At $z\le0.093$ the rival $a_0\propto H(z)$ differs from a constant by 0.009\,dex between W1 and W3, against a statistical width of 0.141\,dex. The distinctive constant-$a_0(z)$ prediction needs $z\gtrsim2$ and baryon calibrations near 0.1\,dex [P38].
\item \textbf{The calibration is partial.} CC2 closes the baryon side only; CC1 and CC3 are evaluable only on unmatched windows, and CC1's second reference is itself not calibrated. The velocity side and the flux scale are not calibrated.
\item \textbf{Not claimed:} nothing here says the data favour the framework over $\Lambda$CDM or over standard MOND; $\kappa=\tfrac12$ is fitted, not derived; the theory is not claimed closed; the cold mass is still required and no dark-matter particle is introduced.
\end{itemize}
% AUDIT: B34

\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:concl}

On 47 golden, deep-regime ($y\le0.084$) MIGHTEE-HI discs, 37 of them gas-dominated, a frozen width chain with the catalogue widths read as rest-frame gives $a_0=1.311\times10^{-10}$\,m\,s$^{-2}$ (statistical $\pm0.034$\,dex; recipe $\pm0.128$\,dex) on the catalogue's flux scale; $\kappa=0.70$ ($\rho_\Lambda$) or 0.58 ($\rho_{\rm crit}$). The frame rests on the catalogue paper's worked examples; independent data tests could not decide it. With the gas on the Arecibo single-dish scale the value is $1.07\times10^{-10}$ (0.97--1.13). Width corrections would lower it by up to 0.10\,dex and kernels without the exponential's next-order term would raise it by 0.06--0.07\,dex. The result is consistent with SPARC and the alternative footing; the canonical footing is just outside the recipe width on the catalogue scale and inside it on the single-dish scale. It neither separates the footings nor tests $a_0(z)$. Total-power fluxes and a $W_{50}$-to-$V_{\rm flat}$ calibration for these discs themselves would remove the two largest systematics.
