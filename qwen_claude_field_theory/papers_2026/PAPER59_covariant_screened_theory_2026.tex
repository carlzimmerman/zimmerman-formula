{\Large\bfseries A Covariant Screened Theory for a Dark-Energy-Tied Galaxy Law:\\ a Khronon Parent with One Stiffness Term, Healthy in Its Literal Form,\\ Clean in the Solar System, with Fitted Constants and Pending Cosmology}\\[6pt]

\begin{abstract}\noindent
The owner's programme studies a gravity framework whose galactic law is $g_N=g^2/(g+a_0)$, with $a_0=\kappa c\sqrt{G\rho_{\rm DE}}$ and $\kappa=\tfrac12$ fitted; its extra gravitating mass is a collisionless ``cold energy'' medium, not a particle. The companion note PAPER58 found that only a screened version, with a stiffness length $L_\star$, passes both Cassini and the galactic law. This note writes that screened theory covariantly and tests it. Every lane froze its criteria before computing and ran a mutation control; both $a_0$ footings ($9.36\times10^{-11}$ and $1.13\times10^{-10}$\,m\,s$^{-2}$) are reported and never pooled.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{The action.} The Blanchet--Skordis Khronon theory in its literal form (no extra curvature term, $\beta=0$; no kinetic term for the spatial covector, $\eta=0$; Blanchet--Skordis $K(Q)$ dust), with an independent spatial covector $\mathbf P$ and one stiffness term $-(\gamma_L/2)(D\cdot P)^2$. Its static limit is AQUAL with $\mu=x/(1+x)$ exactly. The free function that gives this lies outside Blanchet and Skordis's admissible class; that choice, and the stiffness term, are postulated. AeST reproduces everything except the stiffness term, which is identically zero there. The record's earlier action (DSF-21 A33) is this theory with $\eta>0$, which is ill-posed.
\item \textbf{Health and well-posedness.} The literal form has no ghost and no gradient instability on the backgrounds tested; only the two tensor modes propagate, at $c$. Its leaf equations are those of one strictly convex functional, so the instantaneous sector is well posed (weak field). It violates the criterion ``no influence faster than light'' by a real signal of about $1.2\times10^{-46}$\,m\,s$^{-2}$, and meets the criterion of a single global time.
\item \textbf{Tests.} Galaxies: the law itself. Cassini: all 24 cells pass, by $4.8\times10^{7}$ to $5.1\times10^{9}$ (the frozen rule reads UNDECIDED on one float-cancellation check). Planets: at most 0.17 of a bound. Outer globulars: the unscreened law is excluded in all 18 cells, which sets a lower bound on $L_\star$ (21.68 / 22.85\,pc with the stellar-population $M/L$) but no finite $L_\star$ beats Newton. Binary pulsars and GW170817: no constraint. Dwarf satellites: a $+0.89$\,dex gap whose supply is only conditionally met. ALMA: expected unscorable.
\item \textbf{Fitted, not derived:} $\kappa$, $L_\star$ and the dust abundance.
\item \textbf{Pending, stated plainly:} the native CMB (a patched Boltzmann code is validated but no native spectrum has been run; it can test only the dust sector and the cosmological $G$ ratio, because $a_0$ is absent at linear order) and the native cosmic-shear run (running, no verdict). Gaia DR4 is the sealed decisive test: screened $\gamma_v-1\approx8.1\times10^{-6}$ against $\gamma_v\approx1.17$ for the unscreened reading.
\end{enumerate}
\end{abstract}

\section{The action (C1, C3, C4, C5)}
The Blanchet--Skordis Khronon action is $S=(c^3/16\pi G)\int\sqrt{-g}\,[R-2J(Y)+2K(Q)]$, with a preferred foliation $\tau$, khronon acceleration $A_\mu$ and $Y=A\cdot A/c^4$ (Blanchet \& Skordis 2024, 2025; the equations were read via the arXiv HTML and re-derived symbolically in C4, not verified on disk). The screened theory is:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Literal form.} $\beta=0$ (no extra curvature term), $\eta=0$ (no kinetic term for $\mathbf P$), Blanchet--Skordis $K(Q)$ dust.
\item \textbf{The free function.} $J=J_x=\Lambda-2a_0^2[y-\ln(1+y)]$, $y=\sqrt Y/a_0$, gives $1+J'=y/(1+y)$: AQUAL with $\mu=x/(1+x)$ exactly (C4, sympy). But $J_x/\sqrt Y\to-2a_0$ at large $Y$, so it lies outside Blanchet and Skordis's admissible class, which demands $J\to$ const. POSTULATED.
\item \textbf{The addition.} $\mathbf P$ promoted to an independent spatial covector ($n\cdot P=0$) with one stiffness term $-(\gamma_L/2)(D_iP^i)^2$, $\gamma_L=KL_\star^2/2$: one new field and one new constant. The pure Khronon cannot screen the Sun: with $J_x$ the planets see the full $a_0$, $1.08\times10^{4}$--$8.07\times10^{4}$ times their bounds; with a turn-off $J$ the AQUAL $Q_2$ still fails by at least 2.44$\times$ (canonical) and 2.85$\times$ (alternative) (C4, b0ea16ecf1 / 5f4f845554).
\item \textbf{Static limit.} $\phi=\psi$ (lensing sees the dynamical potential) and $\nabla\cdot[(1+J')\nabla\psi]=4\pi G\rho$ for any mass distribution: the field equation is AQUAL, not QUMOND, matching PAPER58's E2. The curl that AQUAL needs lives in the unconstrained auxiliary $P^\ast$ ($|\nabla\times P^\ast|/|\nabla P^\ast|=0.126$ on a two-mass test), not in the foliation.
\item \textbf{Relation to AeST (C1, C3).} AeST (Skordis \& Z{\l}o{\'s}nik 2021) reproduces AQUAL with $\mu=x/(1+x)$, the clock term and the curl half of the stiffness exactly, but the longitudinal stiffness $\gamma_L(D\cdot P)^2$ is identically zero in AeST and in any first-derivative extension; it needs second derivatives of AeST's scalar (C1, d9a9a17f4e / 6502ef90a9). No term in AeST's class screens small systems (C3, SCOPED NO-GO, c16ef33ae5 / fd7196584c): AeST's mass term acts at large $r$ and in the opposite direction. So $L_\star$ is a new constant of the covariant theory.
\item \textbf{Relation to DSF-21's A33.} A33 is this theory with $\eta>0$. With the Khronon's curvature term ($\beta=0$), $\eta>0$ gives a gradient instability wherever the field is nonzero; in the Solar System at $k=1/{\rm AU}$ the rate is 83.3\,/yr (canonical) and 95.09\,/yr (alternative). So $\eta$ is dropped (C5).
\item \textbf{A closure identity (C4).} If $J(\infty)=0$ (Blanchet--Skordis 2025), the action gives $\Lambda c^4/a_0^2=2F(y_t)$, twice the record's closure, so the turn-off scale would be 33.94 (canonical), 23.70 (alternative) or 33.98 ($\kappa=\tfrac12$). This applies only to the turn-off branch, which fails Cassini anyway, and it does not select $\kappa$.
\end{itemize}

\section{Health (C5)}
Criteria f002ce909a, result f3e7d4d661. Verdict CONDITIONALLY HEALTHY, and only in the literal form.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{The literal form.} No ghost and no gradient instability on every background tested where the frozen-coefficient (WKB) expansion is valid; only the two tensor modes propagate, at $c$; $\mathbf P$ and the khronon scalar obey elliptic constraints; the $\omega=0$ mode has positive energy at every $k$; the FRW dust has $c_s^2=(\bar Q-1)/\bar Q$. Conditions: $\gamma_L>0$; $W$ convex (the record's $W$ meets this at every field strength); a positive dust charge; $\beta=0$ and $\eta=0$ exactly.
\item \textbf{What fails.} $\eta>0$ with $\beta=0$: ill-posed (above; the stability boundary is the record's $\beta>2\eta a_0^2/K$, confirmed at $\beta=1.48707\times10^{-19}$). $\beta>0$ with the $K(Q)$ dust: an infrared gradient band. AeST plus the stiffness term is second best: its $\omega=0$ mode has negative energy at every $k$ in vacuum, and the stiffness term gives an unbounded speed.
\item \textbf{Checks.} 7/10, exit 1. Three frozen checks fail and are kept: H5 (a weak-field factor fails at neutron-star compactness), H6 (all failing points lie where the background is not local; this reading is post hoc) and H9 (the $\beta>0$ dust band, physics). Strong coupling is far below every sub-Hubble scale.
\end{itemize}

\section{Well-posedness and causality (C8)}
Criteria f2399aaf5e, result aa113a5e96. WELL-POSED + UNCONSTRAINED, scoped to the weak-field leaf problem; 8/8 scored checks.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Well posed.} On each leaf the $\mathbf P$ and lapse equations are the Euler--Lagrange equations of one strictly convex functional, so the solution exists and is unique on a bounded domain for every source below the cap ($\gamma_L>0$). Because the action has no $\dot N$ or $\dot P$, the time-dependent leaf problem is exactly the static one. OPEN: the infinite-volume theorem, the coupled system with GR's tensor sector and the dust, and strong fields.
\item \textbf{Criterion A is violated; criterion B is met.} The response is a real, gauge-invariant infinite-speed influence on the khronon's leaves. It never goes backward in $\tau$, so the single-global-time criterion holds. Its size: moving 1000\,kg by 1\,m changes the force 1\,km away by $1.2\times10^{-46}$\,m\,s$^{-2}$ at $L_\star=24$\,pc.
\item \textbf{No existing data constrain it.} Binary pulsars: no radiative channel, and the conservative correction is at most $3.2\times10^{-14}$ of the double pulsar's precision. Preferred frame: an $\alpha_2$-equivalent of $1.7\times10^{-7}$ of the bound (this also corrects the record's A22 D5, which missed the leaf tilt). GW170817: $\Delta v/c\le6.2\times10^{-32}$.
\end{itemize}

\section{Tests}
\subsection{Galaxies}
The static limit is the law itself (AQUAL, $\mu=x/(1+x)$; C4, PAPER58 E2). Galaxy rotation curves therefore test the law, as in the companion notes; this theory adds nothing at galactic scales.

\subsection{Cassini and the planets (C6, D7d)}
Criteria 1b2ced55a4, result ae428dc252.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{The literal form passes Cassini.} All 24 cells ($L_\star=24$, 50, 90\,pc; both footings; four Galactic fields) pass Hees et al.\ (2014) and Park et al.\ (2026). At 24\,pc $Q_2=0.93$--$1.86\times10^{-34}$\,s$^{-2}$; at 90\,pc $1.76$--$3.52\times10^{-36}$. The margin below the Hees line is $4.8\times10^{7}$ to $5.1\times10^{9}$.
\item \textbf{Mechanism.} Inside $L_\star$ the Sun's field is a constant hedgehog $GM/L_\star^2\,\hat r$ with no quadrupole. Removing the curl stiffness (D7d used it) lowers $Q_2$ by 9--19\%; it does not open a quadrupole.
\item \textbf{The frozen rule reads UNDECIDED.} One validation check (C2) fails at $3.8\times10^{-11}$ against a $10^{-12}$ line: rounding in $(\kappa^2/2+1)-1$ at $\kappa=0.01$. The identity itself is exact in sympy. The script exits 1 (7/8).
\item \textbf{Planets.} The anomaly is the hedgehog $GM/L_\star^2$: at 24\,pc Mars is at 0.173 of its bound, Saturn 0.035 and Earth 0.028.
\item \textbf{The unscreened law, for contrast,} fails Cassini by 2.44--5.19$\times$ under AQUAL (PAPER58, E2).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Outer globular clusters (D10)}
Each cluster was solved as a Plummer sphere in the Galactic field with the literal form's own equations. The unscreened law ($L_\star=0$) is excluded in all 18 cells ($\Delta\chi^2=4.9$--22.8), and no finite $L_\star$ beats Newton. So the globulars give a lower bound only. Pal~4 and Pal~14 dominate; the bound is limited by the stellar $M/L$ of Pal~4.

\subsection{Dwarf satellites (E3, E6)}
\begin{itemize}
\item The satellites sit $+0.89\pm0.13$\,dex above the screened prediction (PAPER58, E3).
\item \textbf{The framework's dynamics cannot fix a satellite's catchment} (E6, c42925d9dc / 0bea588820): it is set by the patch's initial overdensity and infall epoch. Median $z_{\rm req}=11.28$; the required catchment is an ordinary $1.21\sigma$ fluctuation in an externally calibrated initial field.
\item \textbf{The satellites' own chemistry} (leaky box with their [Fe/H], no $\Lambda$CDM input) bounds the catchment from below at a median 38.33 times the present baryons, so the median need is 0.211 of the supply; 6 of 36 tested systems are short at central values and none at $2\sigma$. This is CONDITIONAL on $\zeta=1$ winds and no pre-enrichment, and it still needs a mechanism that packs the cold mass inside the half-light radius, which the record does not have.
\end{itemize}

\section{What this is not}
\begin{itemize}
\item Not a UV completion: an effective theory with a preferred foliation.
\item Not derived constants: $\kappa$, $L_\star$ and the dust abundance are fitted; the free function $J_x$ and the stiffness term are postulated.
\item The CMB cannot test the galactic law at linear order.
\item Criterion A (no influence faster than light) is violated, by a real signal too small for any test identified.
\item The dwarf supply is conditional (chemistry assumptions, no packing mechanism).
\item No particle species is added: the extra mass is the cold-energy sector, and the dust here is a field sector. No result here is evidence for the framework over its rivals.
\end{itemize}
