\begin{abstract}\noindent
The owner's programme studies a gravity framework whose galactic law is $g_N=g^2/(g+a_0)$, with $a_0=\kappa c\sqrt{G\rho_{\rm DE}}$ and $\kappa=\tfrac12$ fitted; its extra gravitating mass is a collisionless ``cold energy'' medium, not a particle. The companion note PAPER58 found that only a screened version, with a stiffness length $L_\star$, passes both Cassini and the galactic law. This note writes that screened theory covariantly in two forms and tests both. Every lane froze its criteria before computing and ran a mutation control; both $a_0$ footings ($9.36\times10^{-11}$ and $1.13\times10^{-10}$\,m\,s$^{-2}$) are reported and never pooled.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Clusters fail, in both forms.} Rebuilt from the raw eRASS1 and KiDS data, the cluster cores (0.3--1\,Mpc) sit 1.68--1.85 (canonical) and 1.54--1.70 (alternative) times above the law on their baryons, at $Z\approx+5$ to $+6$; any added cold profile that fixes the cores overshoots the outskirts, and AeST's own static dust has the wrong shape. This is a REAL FAILURE.
\item \textbf{v3: the Blanchet--Skordis Khronon in its literal form} (no extra curvature term, $\beta=0$; no kinetic term for the spatial covector, $\eta=0$; Blanchet--Skordis $K(Q)$ dust), with an independent spatial covector $\mathbf P$ and one stiffness term $-(\gamma_L/2)(D\cdot P)^2$. Its static limit is AQUAL with $\mu=x/(1+x)$ exactly (the free function is postulated, outside Blanchet and Skordis's admissible class). It is healthy at linear order, well posed in the weak field, and passes Cassini (all 24 cells, by $4.8\times10^{7}$ to $5.1\times10^{9}$; the frozen rule reads UNDECIDED on one float-cancellation check), the planets and the outer globulars (which set $L_\star\ge21.68$ / 22.85\,pc). \textbf{But it fails linear growth}: its free function has $J'(0)=-1$, so the Newtonian term vanishes on FRW, and the dust mass $\mu_K$ has no allowed window (a gap of 24--172$\times$).
\item \textbf{v4: AeST with the same stiffness term and C1's capped free function} keeps everything v3 passed (the exact law, Cassini, BBN) and removes the growth failure at linear order: the Newtonian term survives in the infrared for any free function. Its one flagged health defect was a mis-specified criterion (it flags ordinary Newtonian dust); the mode is ghost-free \textbf{at linear order only}, with a bounded growth rate. Open: a contact-term binding up to $2.3\times10^{4}$ times Newton between $\mu$ and $k_c$, the stiffness term's lack of a causal cone, nonlinear behaviour and strong fields. v4 is the better-surviving candidate; it is not established.
\item \textbf{Other tests.} Outer galaxy lensing: UNDECIDABLE (the frozen REAL FAILURE is not robust to the host veto). Dwarf satellites: a $+0.89$\,dex tension, supply only conditionally met. Binary pulsars and GW170817: no constraint. ALMA: expected unscorable. $\kappa$, $L_\star$ and the dust abundance are fitted, not derived.
\item \textbf{Pending, stated plainly:} the native CMB (validated code, no native spectrum run; it can test only the dust sector and the cosmological $G$ ratio, because $a_0$ is absent at linear order) and the native cosmic-shear run (running, no verdict). Gaia DR4 is the sealed decisive test of screening: screened $\gamma_v-1\approx8.1\times10^{-6}$ against $\gamma_v\approx1.17$ for the unscreened reading.
\end{enumerate}
\end{abstract}

\section{The v3 action (C1, C3, C4, C5)}
The Blanchet--Skordis Khronon action is $S=(c^3/16\pi G)\int\sqrt{-g}\,[R-2J(Y)+2K(Q)]$, with a preferred foliation $\tau$, khronon acceleration $A_\mu$ and $Y=A\cdot A/c^4$ (Blanchet \& Skordis 2024, 2025; the equations were read via the arXiv HTML and re-derived symbolically in C4, not verified on disk). The screened theory is:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Literal form.} $\beta=0$ (no extra curvature term), $\eta=0$ (no kinetic term for $\mathbf P$), Blanchet--Skordis $K(Q)$ dust.
\item \textbf{The free function.} $J=J_x=\Lambda-2a_0^2[y-\ln(1+y)]$, $y=\sqrt Y/a_0$, gives $1+J'=y/(1+y)$: AQUAL with $\mu=x/(1+x)$ exactly (C4, sympy). But $J_x/\sqrt Y\to-2a_0$ at large $Y$, so it lies outside Blanchet and Skordis's admissible class, which demands $J\to$ const. POSTULATED.
\item \textbf{The addition.} $\mathbf P$ promoted to an independent spatial covector ($n\cdot P=0$) with one stiffness term $-(\gamma_L/2)(D_iP^i)^2$, $\gamma_L=KL_\star^2/2$: one new field and one new constant. The pure Khronon cannot screen the Sun: with $J_x$ the planets see the full $a_0$, $1.08\times10^{4}$--$8.07\times10^{4}$ times their bounds; with a turn-off $J$ the AQUAL $Q_2$ still fails by at least 2.44$\times$ (canonical) and 2.85$\times$ (alternative) (C4, b0ea16ecf1 / 5f4f845554).
\item \textbf{Static limit.} $\phi=\psi$ (lensing sees the dynamical potential) and $\nabla\cdot[(1+J')\nabla\psi]=4\pi G\rho$ for any mass distribution: the field equation is AQUAL, not QUMOND, matching PAPER58's E2. The curl that AQUAL needs lives in the unconstrained auxiliary $P^\ast$ ($|\nabla\times P^\ast|/|\nabla P^\ast|=0.126$ on a two-mass test), not in the foliation.
\item \textbf{Relation to AeST (C1, C3).} AeST (Skordis \& Z{\l}o{\'s}nik 2021) reproduces AQUAL with $\mu=x/(1+x)$, the clock term and the curl half of the stiffness exactly, but the longitudinal stiffness $\gamma_L(D\cdot P)^2$ is identically zero in AeST and in any first-derivative extension; it needs second derivatives of AeST's scalar (C1, d9a9a17f4e / 6502ef90a9). No term in AeST's class screens small systems (C3, SCOPED NO-GO, c16ef33ae5 / fd7196584c): AeST's mass term acts at large $r$ and in the opposite direction. So $L_\star$ is a new constant of the covariant theory.
\item \textbf{Relation to DSF-21's A33.} A33 is this theory with $\eta>0$. With the Khronon's curvature term ($\beta=0$), $\eta>0$ gives a gradient instability wherever the field is nonzero; in the Solar System at $k=1/{\rm AU}$ the rate is 83.3\,/yr (canonical) and 95.09\,/yr (alternative). So $\eta$ is dropped (C5).
\item \textbf{A closure identity (C4).} If $J(\infty)=0$ (Blanchet--Skordis 2025), the action gives $\Lambda c^4/a_0^2=2F(y_t)$, twice the record's closure, so the turn-off scale would be 33.94 (canonical), 23.70 (alternative) or 33.98 ($\kappa=\tfrac12$). This applies only to the turn-off branch, which fails Cassini anyway, and it does not select $\kappa$.
\end{itemize}
