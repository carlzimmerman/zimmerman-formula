\begin{abstract}
\noindent The programme ties the MOND acceleration scale to the vacuum, $a_0=\kappa c\sqrt{G\rho_\Lambda}$, with $\kappa=\tfrac12$ fitted, not derived [Z26a,Z26c]. Its best current version, candidate B, is an \emph{effective law}: no committed action produces it. This note records what one action would have to supply, and what is already known about each requirement. (i)~A gate list was frozen before any assembly attempt: 63 gates in five groups. B passes 42 of 48 harness gates by recount, inferred rather than established, and is short or marginal on a further list of rows that we give with their committed sizes. (ii)~Two objects are missing between B and one action: a legal, bound-only, owned switch (Gap~1), and a conserved cold fluid that \emph{is} the law's dark density (Gap~2) carrying the $a_0$--$\Lambda$ tie (Gap~3, partly filled). The tie can be written into a conserved fluid's stress cap in one action without a new local degree of freedom, but a barotropic saturating cap then engages over at most about $11\times$ in mass against the $10^4$ the baryonic Tully--Fisher relation needs (a scoped screening result; the referee's caveat that this is not a ceiling is given). The fluid's target, $C(r)=\rho_c r^3 g_{\rm tot}=(a_0/4\pi)M_b(<r)$, is excluded as an equation of state, a local closure, a second force, a constraint action, a coupling to the phantom potential, a density slaving and a tidal-tensor coupling, each under stated hypotheses; what is left reduces to Gap~1. (iii)~The derived cold-mass rule closes the ultra-faint offset (from +0.325\,dex to $-0.06$\,dex, a weak test by the referee's account) but over-predicts the classical satellites (M31 LVD $-2.67\sigma$); no one of four pre-declared readings passes all seven gates. (iv)~Lean certifies 52 theorems: that stated conclusions follow from stated premises, no empirical fact. (v)~What decides the rest: Gaia DR4 wide binaries (2~December 2026), $a_0$ at $z\approx2.5$ (not testable with the data on disk) and multi-epoch ultra-faint data. The document is a map, not a result.
\end{abstract}

\section{The effective law B, and what an action must supply}\label{sec:law}

Candidate B is CFG4's target law (T1--T6) plus a hierarchical ownership rule (FG001). It has no action.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{T1:} $a_0=\kappa c\sqrt{G\rho_\Lambda}$, with $\kappa=\tfrac12$ fitted; the form is unique, $\kappa$ is not derived.
\item \textbf{T2:} $g=\nu_{\rm mono}(g_{\rm bar}/a_0)\,g_{\rm bar}$ inside ``on'' regions.
\item \textbf{T3:} a region is on iff (i) it is a top-level bound system that has turned around, (ii) a density edge $x_e\in[0.31,0.48]\,r_{\rm ta}$ (declared $0.4$), and (iii) $M_b\ge M_*$.
\item \textbf{T4:} a cold fluid with $\Omega_ch^2=0.12$, fitted as in $\Lambda$CDM.
\item \textbf{T5:} the dark mass is $\max\!\big(M_{\rm ph},(\Omega_c/\Omega_b)M_b\big)$; the max rule is declared.
\item \textbf{T6:} a late allowance.
\item \textbf{Ownership:} top-level systems only; accreted systems keep the isolated law of their infall baryons; systems formed embedded are Newtonian.
\end{itemize}
% AUDIT: B25

An action that produces B has to supply, at least:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item the tie of $a_0$ to $\Lambda$, with $\kappa$ entering as a datum;
\item the kernel and the galaxy law inside on regions;
\item a switch that is a legal action term: bound-only, top-level-owned, baryon-reading, frame-covariant, with a source-confined edge (Gap~1);
\item a conserved fluid whose bound-region density \emph{is} the law's dark density, and is plain CDM elsewhere, without double counting (Gap~2);
\item one action carrying both the tie and the fluid, with post-Newtonian and cosmological sectors closed (Gap~3);
\item the stability, causality and conservation requirements of group (5) of the gate list.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{What exists.} Its pieces sit in three separate places. The derivation-chain action holds the tie and a static kernel but a postulated fluid coupling, a squeezed separator and no ownership; V0 holds a region-local kernel and a baryon-reading gate but its varied gate is obstructed; the astra CA4/CA5 action holds a fluid, a heat gate and Ward/Dirac bookkeeping, but its $a_0$--vacuum relation is ``an optional input'', it has no bound-only switch, and every astra result is marked unreviewed. The committed verdicts are ``INCOMPLETE'' for each surviving row. \emph{No committed action produces candidate B.}

\section{The remaining gaps, and the reduction of Gap 2 to Gap 1}\label{sec:gaps}

\textbf{Gap 1: a legal switch.} Missing is a gate variable equal to ``inside a turned-around, top-level bound system with $M_b\ge M_*$'' that enters as a Lagrangian term. The committed gates read the baryon flow $\theta_b$, a density, the khronon curvature $\langle K\rangle$ or a field energy; none reads boundness or hierarchy. Ownership adds history (accreted systems keep their infall halo), which no instantaneous state variable carries. A gate that is varied as an action term is unstable: its second variation lands on the field it reads (DE12, DE13), with $c_{\rm gate}=1500$--3700 against gas at 37--117\,km\,s$^{-1}$, and no single smoothing length works (it needs at least 500\,kpc while the flagship case holds only to 100\,kpc; XR15). The dynamical gate field (CV6) was never run.
% AUDIT: B27

\textbf{Gap 2: the target, exactly (CFG44).} Candidate B needs a cold fluid whose density is the law's phantom,
\begin{equation}
\rho_c\,g_{\rm tot}=\frac{a_0}{3}\,\bar\rho_b(<r)=\frac{a_0\,M_b(<r)}{4\pi r^3},\label{eq:target}
\end{equation}
a charge $a_0/4\pi G=53.4\,M_\odot\,{\rm pc}^{-2}$ per unit enclosed baryonic mass, half of the 106.9\,$M_\odot\,{\rm pc}^{-2}$ column ceiling. For a point mass with $x=r/r_M$: $M_c=M(\sqrt{1+x^2}-1)$, $P=a_0M/8\pi r^2$ and $\sigma^2=V_c^2/2$. For extended baryons $\rho_c$ depends only on $M_b(<r)$, and the target is equivalent to a locally virialised collisionless fluid with anisotropy $\beta=-\tfrac32\rho_b/\bar\rho_b$. An additive law (phantom plus a cosmic-share cold fluid) overshoots by +0.43\,dex at $x=3$ and +0.68\,dex at $x=1$. With the fluid feeling the Newtonian potential of all mass, hydrostatics is an identity, so the whole content of the target is the closure. The point-mass identities are certified in Lean (24 theorems, Section~\ref{sec:lean}).

Two constructions survive, both as restatements: a temperature-slaved fluid (it postulates $\sigma_\infty^2=\tfrac12\sqrt{Ga_0M_b}$, the baryonic Tully--Fisher relation, the cusp amplitude and the nonlocal exterior term) and a locally virialised collisionless fluid with $\beta=-\tfrac32\rho_b/\bar\rho_b$ (the Jeans identity is derived, $\beta$ is postulated, and positivity of $f(E,L)$ is open). \textbf{The sharpest statement.} The single function of radius
\begin{equation}
C(r)=\rho_c\,r^3\,g_{\rm tot}=\frac{a_0}{4\pi}\,M_b(<r)\label{eq:C}
\end{equation}
has no dynamical origin in any construction tried. It is not an equation of state, a linear or Poisson coupling, a local function of the fields at $r$, a state-independent stress, a constraint under GR plus real mass, a coupling to the phantom potential, a density slaving, a tidal-tensor coupling, or adiabatically maintainable. What is left is a non-adiabatic, nonlocal exchange of energy between the baryons and the fluid that depends on the enclosed baryonic mass. The baryonic Tully--Fisher relation is the postulate.

\textbf{The reduction of Gap 2 to Gap 1.} An enclosed-mass, non-adiabatic exchange that acts only where the system is bound and is set by the system's own baryons is the same kind of object as a bound-only, owned switch: both are functionals of the baryon history of a top-level system, not of the local instantaneous state. The committed maps therefore count the remaining gaps as two, a legal bound-only owned switch and the tie, and state that Gap~2 ``still reduces to Gap~1'' after the tidal test. We flag this as an identification made in the maps from the form of the missing object; it is not a theorem, and a construction that produced (\ref{eq:C}) without a bound-only switch would falsify it.

\section{The tie: one action for $a_0(\Lambda)$ and a conserved fluid (CFG43)}\label{sec:tie}

The construction (units $c=1$, $M_P^2=1/8\pi G$, $\epsilon=\kappa^2/8\pi$, $\kappa=\tfrac12$ fitted) is
\begin{equation}
S=\int d^4x\Big\{\sqrt{-g}\big[\tfrac12 M_P^2R-M_P^2\Lambda\big]+M_P^2\Lambda\,\partial_\mu T^\mu-\sqrt{-g}\,\rho(n;\Lambda)+J^\mu\partial_\mu\theta\Big\},
\end{equation}
with $\Lambda$ a Henneaux--Teitelboim [HT89] field with multiplier $T^\mu$, a Schutz--Sorkin [SS77] irrotational fluid with one conserved current, and the cap entering as $\rho(n;\Lambda)=mn+P_{\rm cap}\,x\arctan x$, $x=mn/(\nu_*M_P^2\Lambda)$, $P_{\rm cap}(\Lambda)=\epsilon M_P^2\Lambda=a_0^2/8\pi G$. That gives $P=P_{\rm cap}x^2/(1+x^2)\le P_{\rm cap}$; with $\kappa=\tfrac12$, $a_0=\sqrt{8\pi GP_{\rm cap}}=9.3603\times10^{-11}$\,m\,s$^{-2}$, so the kernel and the fluid's cap read the same constant.
% AUDIT: B31

\textbf{Derived by the scripts, from the action} (11, 5 and 11 checks in the three main runs; all six controls fail as required): $\Lambda$ is a global integration constant; number conservation; Friedmann equals GR plus $\Lambda$ plus dust; the Dirac count on a lattice (fluid alone $2N$; adding the HT sector $2N+2$ with $2N-1$ first-class constraints and no tertiary one, so the local degrees of freedom are unchanged and there is one global pair); $a_0(z)$ flat to $6\times10^{-11}$ for $z\le10$; $\Omega_c$ is initial data; a column bound $\Sigma\le a_0/2\pi G$ (106.9/129.2\,$M_\odot\,{\rm pc}^{-2}$).
% AUDIT: B32
\textbf{Postulated:} the tie function $P_{\rm cap}=(\kappa^2/8\pi)\rho_\Lambda c^2$ (so it agrees with the earlier kernel-only tie by construction); the saturating shape and its argument; irrotational flow; a hard bound; minimal coupling. \textbf{Fitted:} $\kappa=\tfrac12$; $\Omega_c$; and a new dimensionless constant $\nu_*$. \textbf{Open:} a cap that binds across the baryonic Tully--Fisher mass range; shell crossing; the galaxy law itself; the bound-only switch; T5's max rule; PPN.

\textbf{The scoped obstruction.} Hypotheses: a single conserved current; an equation of state barotropic in $\nu=\rho/\rho_\Lambda$ with $P\le P_{\rm cap}$; linear growth close to $\Lambda$CDM; the cap reached at $r_M$. (1) At $\nu_*=1$, $c_s^2\approx6\times10^{-3}$ today and linear growth is 5--14\% of $\Lambda$CDM. (2) Growth within 5\% needs $\nu_*\ge2\times10^4$ to $1\times10^7$ at $k=0.5$--30\,Mpc$^{-1}$. (3) The density at $r_M$ in units of $\rho_\Lambda$ scales as $M_b^{-1/2}$ exactly, and the growth bound at the halo's own scale scales the same way, so $g_0=\nu_M/\nu_{\min}$ is a pure number and one $\nu_*$ engages the cap over a mass window $g_0^2$ whatever $\nu_*$ is: direct re-solves at $10^9$ and $3\times10^{11}\,M_\odot$ give $g_0=1.08$ and $1.04$; over convention choices $g_0$ ranges over 0.08--3.3, so the window is \emph{at most about} $11\times$ in mass, against the $10^4$ the baryonic Tully--Fisher relation needs. This is a screening estimate (two-fluid Newtonian growth).
% AUDIT: B33

\textbf{The referee's caveat, which limits how this is read.} An independent re-implementation with its own growth solver reproduced the obstruction ($\nu_{\min}=2.2\times10^4$, $1.8\times10^5$, $2.0\times10^6$, $1.0\times10^7$ at $k=0.5$, 2, 10, 30\,Mpc$^{-1}$; the lane's values differ by 5--12\%; the exponent 1.49--1.50; $g_0=0.36/0.355$ at the 5\% criterion, $1.06/1.04$ at 20\%). But ``at most about $11\times$'' is too strong as a ceiling: it holds only if suppression beyond 50\% is disallowed and $F\ge\tfrac12$ is kept; stacking the generous choices reaches $10^4$--$10^5$, each individually unmotivated, and $F\ge0.01$ contradicts the hydrostatic requirement. Other cap shapes were not tested. The obstruction stands as stated for the saturating barotropic equation of state; it does not touch a fluid whose cap is not a barotropic function of its own density, for example an order-parameter fluid.
% AUDIT: B34

\textbf{Status.} Gap~3 is partly filled: the tie can act on a conserved fluid in one action without adding local degrees of freedom. The cap's entry cannot be derived, and the natural saturating form needs a new constant. This is an existence result and a scoped no-go, not a mechanism. In addition the P2 point-mass pressure exceeds this cap for $r<r_M$ (its $P/P_{\rm cap}=g_N/a_0$), so the fluid of this section and the fluid of eq.~(\ref{eq:target}) are not one fluid.
