\begin{abstract}
\noindent The programme ties the MOND acceleration scale to the vacuum, $a_0=\kappa c\sqrt{G\rho_\Lambda}$, with $\kappa=\tfrac12$ fitted, not derived [Z26a,Z26c]. Its best current version, candidate B, is an \emph{effective law}: no committed action produces it. This note records what one action would have to supply, and what is already known about each requirement. (i)~A gate list was frozen before any assembly attempt: 63 gates in five groups. B passes 42 of 48 harness gates by recount, inferred rather than established, and is short or marginal on a further list of rows that we give with their committed sizes. (ii)~Two objects are missing between B and one action: a legal, bound-only, owned switch (Gap~1), and a conserved cold fluid that \emph{is} the law's dark density (Gap~2), with the $a_0$--$\Lambda$ tie (Gap~3) partly filled. Gap~1 is a scoped no-go in every construction tried: a gate on a shift-symmetric field leaves the dynamical mass equal to the baryonic mass beyond its edge (Gauss), ownership is acausal inside a reduced single-field action or a prescribed label (a causal doubled-field version returns the same reaction), and the enclosed-mass exchange reacts on the baryons. Local gates fail a second-variation stability test on 44 of 48 cases, nonlocal enclosed-mass and top-level-ball gates pass 48 of 48, and a dynamical gate scalar is stable only with two new untied constants and DE13's own cost. Gap~2's target, $C(r)=\rho_c r^3 g_{\rm tot}=(a_0/4\pi)M_b(<r)$, is excluded as an equation of state, a local closure, a second force, a constraint action, a coupling to the phantom potential, a density slaving and a tidal-tensor coupling, each under stated hypotheses. Gap~1 and Gap~2 reduce to one non-derivable object (as an identification, not a theorem), and a documentary count of 54 committed constructions (48 construction rows plus six screened-out rows) finds none that derives it (D=0, P=11, I=5, X=27, U=11; U=5 without the screened-out rows). The tie can be written into a conserved fluid's stress cap in one action without a new local degree of freedom, but a barotropic saturating cap engages over at most about $11\times$ in mass against the $10^4$ the baryonic Tully--Fisher relation needs (a scoped screening result; the referee's caveat that this is not a ceiling is given). (iii)~The derived cold-mass rule closes the ultra-faint offset (from +0.325\,dex to $-0.06$\,dex, a weak test by the referee's account) but over-predicts the classical satellites (M31 LVD $-2.67\sigma$); calibrated on the galaxies' own inner kinematics it leaves the SLUGGS early types 2.6$\sigma$ short; no one of four pre-declared readings passes all seven gates, and no single fraction of its debris reconciles ten populations at a 1$\sigma$-per-population acceptance (at 2$\sigma$ CFG59's ten populations do share $\varphi\approx0.5$--$0.7$, CFG75; with SLUGGS's dynamical masses only SLUGGS blocks the 2$\sigma$ intersection). These dwarf verdicts are kernel-robust, robust in size to the concentration relation (though marginal in significance, CFG65) and to an Einasto shape, and a Burkert core removes both the closure and the over-prediction. (iv)~Lean certifies 161 theorems: that stated conclusions follow from stated premises, no empirical fact. (v)~What decides the rest: Gaia DR4 wide binaries (2~December 2026), which can separate B only from the bare MOND law; $a_0$ at $z\approx2.5$ (not testable with the data on disk; the PHIBSS sample is empty under the pre-registered selection); and multi-epoch ultra-faint data. The document is a map, not a result.
\end{abstract}

\section{The effective law B, and what an action must supply}\label{sec:law}

Candidate B is CFG4's target law (T1--T6) plus a hierarchical ownership rule (FG001). It has no action.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{T1:} $a_0=\kappa c\sqrt{G\rho_\Lambda}$, with $\kappa=\tfrac12$ fitted; the form is unique, $\kappa$ is not derived.
\item \textbf{T2:} $g=\nu_{\rm mono}(g_{\rm bar}/a_0)\,g_{\rm bar}$ inside ``on'' regions.
\item \textbf{T3:} a region is on iff (i) it is a top-level bound system that has turned around, (ii) a density edge $x_e\in[0.31,0.48]\,r_{\rm ta}$ (declared $0.4$), and (iii) $M_b\ge M_*$.
\item \textbf{T4:} a cold fluid with $\Omega_ch^2=0.12$, fitted as in $\Lambda$CDM.
\item \textbf{T5:} the dark mass is $\max\!\big(M_{\rm ph},(\Omega_c/\Omega_b)M_b\big)$; the max rule is declared.
\item \textbf{T6:} a late allowance.
\item \textbf{Ownership:} top-level systems only; accreted systems keep the isolated law of their infall baryons; systems formed embedded are Newtonian.
\end{itemize}
% AUDIT: B25

An action that produces B has to supply, at least:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item the tie of $a_0$ to $\Lambda$, with $\kappa$ entering as a datum;
\item the kernel and the galaxy law inside on regions;
\item a switch that is a legal action term: bound-only, top-level-owned, baryon-reading, frame-covariant, with a source-confined edge (Gap~1);
\item a conserved fluid whose bound-region density \emph{is} the law's dark density, and is plain CDM elsewhere, without double counting (Gap~2);
\item one action carrying both the tie and the fluid, with post-Newtonian and cosmological sectors closed (Gap~3);
\item the stability, causality and conservation requirements of group (5) of the gate list.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{What exists.} Its pieces sit in three separate places. The derivation-chain action holds the tie and a static kernel but a postulated fluid coupling, a squeezed separator and no ownership; V0 holds a region-local kernel and a baryon-reading gate but its varied gate is obstructed; the astra CA4/CA5 action holds a fluid, a heat gate and Ward/Dirac bookkeeping, but its $a_0$--vacuum relation is ``an optional input'', it has no bound-only switch, and every astra result is marked unreviewed. Switch constructions built on the MOND flux, on a Brown--Schutz dust turnaround gate (a ghost pole on 24/24 layers) and on generated phantoms were excluded or withdrawn. The committed verdicts are ``INCOMPLETE'' for each surviving row. \emph{No committed action produces candidate B.}

\textbf{Gap 1: a legal switch.} Missing is a gate variable equal to ``inside a turned-around, top-level bound system with $M_b\ge M_*$'' that enters as a Lagrangian term. The committed gates read the baryon flow $\theta_b$, a density, the khronon curvature $\langle K\rangle$ or a field energy; none reads boundness or hierarchy. Ownership adds history (accreted systems keep their infall halo), which no instantaneous state variable carries. A gate that is varied as an action term is unstable: its second variation lands on the field it reads (DE12, DE13), with $c_{\rm gate}=1500$--3700 against gas at 37--117\,km\,s$^{-1}$, and no single smoothing length works (it needs at least 500\,kpc while the flagship case holds only to 100\,kpc; XR15). That obstruction assumes the gate variable is slaved to the baryon field; two later lanes relax that hypothesis, below.

\textbf{Gap 1 attacked directly (CFG48).} CFG48 attacks Gap~1 in six scripts, written by a lane session against action-level gates (GA--GG) that, by the lane's own record, were written before any script (the referee's file birth times are consistent with this, but everything was committed together, so git cannot order them), and re-run by the orchestrating session. A referee reproduced all 12 outputs from a clean export and re-derived 7/7 headline numbers with independent code. Four obstructions, each with its hypotheses in the lane README. (1)~\emph{Gauss.} A gate that multiplies a shift-symmetric MOND field whose only source is the baryons gives $M_{\rm dyn}=M_b$ exactly beyond the edge, while B's target is $M_b\sqrt{1+x_e^2}$ (15--33\,$M_b$); the phantom inside the edge would have to be a negative shell of $3.2\times10^{11}$, $2.2\times10^{12}$ and $1.4\times10^{13}\,M_\odot$ for $M_b=10^{10}$, $10^{11}$ and $10^{12}\,M_\odot$. The form that does keep the mass (a gate on the phantom \emph{density}, not its flux) fails the Helmholtz test: it is not the Euler--Lagrange system of any Lagrangian in those fields. (2)~\emph{History.} Ownership is not a function of the baryon state, and a history variable inside a reduced single-field action (a one-degree-of-freedom toy; doubled-field actions are not covered by this obstruction) makes the residual at time $t$ depend on the future trajectory; the only causal carrier there is a prescribed label, that is, ownership as initial data. CFG70 then writes the enclosed-mass exchange as a causal, reciprocal retarded Schwinger--Keldysh memory-kernel action: causality holds, but it returns CFG48's reaction and energy demand unchanged, a scoped no. (3)~\emph{Exchange.} CFG44's enclosed-mass energy exchange, written as a bilocal action term, reacts on the baryons outward with 0.06\,$g_{\rm law}$ at $x=r/r_M=0.3$, 0.4--0.5 at $x=1$ as CFG48 reports (0.40--0.53 in the rows of its G4 output) and 11--22 at $x=30$ (pass line 0.10) and must supply 23--50 times the baryons' orbital kinetic energy ($M_b=10^{10}$--$10^{12}\,M_\odot$, CFG48's convention; 23--73 down to $10^9\,M_\odot$ in CFG70, and 57--318 with B's committed $r_{\rm ta}$). (4)~\emph{Top-level detection is not smooth:} the maximal-ball functional finds the edge with no new constant but it jumps by a factor 2 at every merger, so a variation needs a smoothing width.

\textbf{A dynamical gate scalar (CFG49).} The one construction that DE12, DE13 and XR36 never ran is a gate with its own stiffness: a scalar $\chi$ with kinetic coefficient $\mu/c^2$ and mass term $m_2$, sourced by a baryon-only invariant and entering through the gate $W(\chi)$. The cost is acceptable only at $m_2\lesssim10^{-16}$, where 16 of 24 edges are off by more than 10\% and the force is still 0.09\,$g_{\rm MOND}$. Main runs: A 12/12, C 5/6, D pass and B 7/8, the one failure being a hypothesis (H2) that the run refuted and that is kept; every MUTATE control fails as required. \emph{Relation to CFG48.} The scalar is a local field sourced by the baryon density, the class that CFG48's G6 found unstable; CFG49 shows how far that class can be repaired: it becomes stable, but only with two untied constants and DE13's cost. \textbf{Gap 2: the target, exactly (CFG44).} Candidate B needs a cold fluid whose density is the law's phantom,
\begin{equation}
\rho_c\,g_{\rm tot}=\frac{a_0}{3}\,\bar\rho_b(<r)=\frac{a_0\,M_b(<r)}{4\pi r^3},\label{eq:target}
\end{equation}
a charge $a_0/4\pi G=53.4\,M_\odot\,{\rm pc}^{-2}$ per unit enclosed baryonic mass, half of the 106.9\,$M_\odot\,{\rm pc}^{-2}$ column ceiling. For a point mass with $x=r/r_M$: $M_c=M(\sqrt{1+x^2}-1)$, $P=a_0M/8\pi r^2$ and $\sigma^2=V_c^2/2$. For extended baryons $\rho_c$ depends only on $M_b(<r)$, and the target is equivalent to a locally virialised collisionless fluid with anisotropy $\beta=-\tfrac32\rho_b/\bar\rho_b$. An additive law (phantom plus a cosmic-share cold fluid) overshoots by +0.43\,dex at $x=3$ and +0.68\,dex at $x=1$. With the fluid feeling the Newtonian potential of all mass, hydrostatics is an identity, so the whole content of the target is the closure. The point-mass identities are certified in Lean (24 theorems, Section~\ref{sec:lean}).

\section{The tie: one action for $a_0(\Lambda)$ and a conserved fluid (CFG43)}\label{sec:tie}

The construction (units $c=1$, $M_P^2=1/8\pi G$, $\epsilon=\kappa^2/8\pi$, $\kappa=\tfrac12$ fitted) is
\begin{equation}
S=\int d^4x\Big\{\sqrt{-g}\big[\tfrac12 M_P^2R-M_P^2\Lambda\big]+M_P^2\Lambda\,\partial_\mu T^\mu-\sqrt{-g}\,\rho(n;\Lambda)+J^\mu\partial_\mu\theta\Big\},
\end{equation}
with $\Lambda$ a Henneaux--Teitelboim [HT89] field with multiplier $T^\mu$, a Schutz--Sorkin [SS77] irrotational fluid with one conserved current, and the cap entering as $\rho(n;\Lambda)=mn+P_{\rm cap}\,x\arctan x$, $x=mn/(\nu_*M_P^2\Lambda)$, $P_{\rm cap}(\Lambda)=\epsilon M_P^2\Lambda=a_0^2/8\pi G$. That gives $P=P_{\rm cap}x^2/(1+x^2)\le P_{\rm cap}$; with $\kappa=\tfrac12$, $a_0=\sqrt{8\pi GP_{\rm cap}}=9.3603\times10^{-11}$\,m\,s$^{-2}$, so the kernel and the fluid's cap read the same constant.

\textbf{Postulated:} the tie function $P_{\rm cap}=(\kappa^2/8\pi)\rho_\Lambda c^2$ (so it agrees with the earlier kernel-only tie by construction); the saturating shape and its argument; irrotational flow; a hard bound; minimal coupling. \textbf{Fitted:} $\kappa=\tfrac12$; $\Omega_c$; and a new dimensionless constant $\nu_*$. \textbf{Open:} a cap that binds across the baryonic Tully--Fisher mass range; shell crossing; the galaxy law itself; the bound-only switch; T5's max rule; PPN.

(1) At $\nu_*=1$, $c_s^2\approx6\times10^{-3}$ today and linear growth is 5--14\% of $\Lambda$CDM. (2) Growth within 5\% needs $\nu_*\ge2\times10^4$ to $1\times10^7$ at $k=0.5$--30\,Mpc$^{-1}$.
