\begin{abstract}\noindent
This note follows PAPER64 (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23771199). The programme pairs a galaxy law with $a_0=\kappa c\sqrt{G\rho_{\rm DE}}$ ($\kappa=\tfrac12$ fitted) and a gravitating $w\approx0$ component called \emph{cold dark energy}; this is not matter and not a particle. Its halos obey an empirical core rule:
\[
\rho_0r_0=\max\!\big(a_0/2\pi G,\ \eta\,\Sigma_{\rm coll}\big).
\]

We report three things. Each comes from frozen lanes: criteria committed before any number, a mutation twin, and identity checks against the committed predecessor.

\textbf{(i) The floor is not derived, and five routes to deriving it are closed.} A local tether, three gated extra pulls on the cold component, phantom-halo tracking, pressure floors and halo reshaping all fail. \textbf{(ii) The floor is empirically preferred.} Softening it in low-acceleration galaxies worsens the affected half of SPARC by 43--55\%. \textbf{(iii) A smooth-cored halo} $\rho_0/(1+x^2)^{3/2}$, with the collapse coefficient re-derived for that shape, passes every graded line on the canonical footing at the assumed stellar fraction. With measured stellar fractions alone, the heaviest-cluster outskirts fail at $Z\approx2.3$; that miss is X30's truncated gas tail. With every input measured or native (measured X-COP gas slope to turnaround, measured stellar fractions, the programme's own $\Omega_c/\Omega_b$), the smooth core passes SPARC, the groups and all 15 cluster keys on the canonical footing at $c_f=4$. The pass is fragile in the extrapolated gas slope.

\textbf{(iv) The collapse coefficient measured from our own halos} (Section~5): fitting the smooth core directly to native cold-collapse zoom halos gives $\eta$ at the edge of what the data allow at every mass, with nothing tuned. The full grade is a knife edge: canonical pass at $f_\star=0.034$, one key just past $|Z|=2$ at $f_\star=0.0615$. [PENDING A4] The cluster-mass inputs are still at the coarser grid. This is not evidence for the framework over $\Lambda$CDM.
\end{abstract}

\eta_{\rm native}\equiv\frac{\rho_0r_0}{18\pi^2\rho_m r_{\rm vir}}=\frac{4a^2}{3B_S(a)},\qquad a=r_{\rm vir}/r_0 .
