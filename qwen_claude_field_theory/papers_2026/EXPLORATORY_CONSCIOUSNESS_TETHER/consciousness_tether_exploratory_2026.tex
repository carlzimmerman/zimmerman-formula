\begin{abstract}\noindent
We ask whether consciousness could be what ties cold dark energy to dark energy, and split the question into three readings.
(1)~\emph{Causal}: minds move energy between the components or set their ratio. This fails by about 38 orders of magnitude in energy, and on timing and locality.
(2)~\emph{Selection}: observers can exist only in the epoch where the two densities are comparable. This is viable, recasts the coincidence problem as an observer-selection effect, and has a sharper form through $a_0=\kappa c\sqrt{G\rho_{\rm DE}}$; it does not explain why the CMB's cold mass is missing from rotation curves.
(3)~\emph{Bookkeeping}: the split into a cold part and a smooth part is drawn by the observer when fitting data. This is supported at the CMB and background level by the CMB3 audit, where only the assumption that CMB cold mass equals rotation-curve mass breaks; KR1, KR2 and BG1 show that it names the tether without building it.
Kill tests for each reading are listed. None has been run.
\end{abstract}

\section{The tether problem}
The two dark components look linked, and nothing in the action links them. Three facts make the link look real.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{The coincidence.} Today the cold component and dark energy have comparable densities (about 0.3 and 0.7 of critical). Their ratio changes as $(1+z)^3$, so ``comparable'' holds for only a narrow slice of cosmic history.
\item \textbf{The $a_0$ scale.} $cH_0/2\pi\approx1.04\times10^{-10}$\,m\,s$^{-2}$ against $a_0\approx1.2\times10^{-10}$\,m\,s$^{-2}$. The framework writes $a_0=\kappa c\sqrt{G\rho_{\rm DE}}$ with $\kappa=\tfrac12$ fitted, not derived.
\item \textbf{The A5 break.} The CMB3 audit (origin \texttt{41a041fddd}) found the CMB needs a cold component under every assumption tested except A5: that the CMB's cold mass is the rotation-curve mass. The cold-gas conversion test (BG1) and the running-mass lanes (KR1, KR2) then failed to make one substance do both jobs.
\end{itemize}
A \emph{tether} is any principle that explains why these line up. We ask whether consciousness could be that principle.

The readings ask less of consciousness as they go down, and more of each survives the data.

\section{Reading 1: consciousness as a causal tether}\label{sec:causal}
\textbf{Verdict: fails on energy, timing and locality.}

\textbf{Energy.} With $H_0=67.4$\,km\,s$^{-1}$\,Mpc$^{-1}$ and $\Omega_{\rm DE}=0.685$, the dark-energy density is $5.3\times10^{-10}$\,J\,m$^{-3}$. Inside one Hubble radius ($1.37\times10^{26}$\,m),
\begin{equation}
E_{\rm DE}=\rho_{\rm DE}c^2\cdot\tfrac43\pi\,(c/H_0)^3\approx5.7\times10^{69}\ {\rm J}.
\end{equation}
Eight billion human brains at 20\,W each, run for 13.8\,Gyr, give $7.0\times10^{28}$\,J. The whole biosphere ($\sim10^{14}$\,W) gives $4.4\times10^{31}$\,J. The gaps are $10^{41}$ and $10^{38}$.

\textbf{Timing.} The cold-to-dark-energy ratio was already fixed at $z\approx1100$, where the CMB reads it. Nothing conscious existed then. A cause cannot come after the effect it sets.

\textbf{Locality.} Dark energy is smooth to the precision of every survey; minds are clustered on at least one planet. A coupling to mind would make $w$ or $a_0$ vary near Earth. Solar-system tests (Cassini) and the frozen local gates show no such variation.

\textbf{Collapse models.} The strongest route from mind to gravity is Penrose's gravity-driven collapse, on which Orch-OR builds. The parameter-free Di\'osi--Penrose version was ruled out underground at Gran Sasso~\cite{donadi}. Surviving collapse models move no energy between cosmic components.

This reading also breaks two standing rules of the record: it adds a sector with no action, and it posits an observable instead of deriving it.

\section{Reading 2: consciousness as a selection tether}\label{sec:selection}
\textbf{Verdict: open. It explains the coincidence without a mechanism, and it pays a price.}

Here consciousness does nothing to the components; it marks where observers can stand. Observers need galaxies, metal-rich stars and billions of years of stable planets. These form only after the cold component has clustered and before dark energy shuts down growth, so any observer tends to find the two densities comparable. Weinberg used this logic to bound the cosmological constant~\cite{weinberg}; Lineweaver and Egan argued the coincidence is what a typical observer should see~\cite{lineweaver}.

\textbf{A sharper version.} If $a_0=\kappa c\sqrt{G\rho_{\rm DE}}$, dark energy also sets the acceleration scale that shapes disc galaxies. The observer window is then set by dark energy twice: through expansion and through $a_0$. The tether runs through galaxies; minds are only the readout.

\textbf{The price.}
\begin{itemize}
\item It predicts the coincidence but not the A5 break: selection cannot say why the CMB's cold mass is missing from rotation curves.
\item In an eternal de~Sitter future, random ``Boltzmann brain'' observers outnumber evolved ones. A selection reading must let dark energy decay or adopt a measure that suppresses them.
\item It is hard to falsify alone. Its testable form is the $a_0$ version: the $\rho_{\rm DE}$ window that allows observers should match the $a_0$ window that allows stable discs.
\end{itemize}

\section{Reading 3: consciousness as a bookkeeping tether}\label{sec:book}
\textbf{Verdict: supported at the CMB and background level; it does not by itself fix galaxies.}

Gravity responds to the total dark stress-energy, not to its parts. Kunz called this the dark degeneracy~\cite{kunz}: any split of the dark sector into a cold piece plus a smooth piece is one of many that fit the same metric,
\begin{equation}
T^{\mu\nu}_{\rm dark}=T^{\mu\nu}_{\rm cold}+T^{\mu\nu}_{\rm DE},\qquad\text{only }T^{\mu\nu}_{\rm dark}\text{ is measured.}
\end{equation}
The split is drawn by the observer when choosing a model to fit. In that sense the tether is the mind that writes the decomposition. The record's name already points this way: ``cold dark energy'' says the cold component is a face of dark energy, not a separate substance.

\textbf{Where the record supports it.} CMB3 found the cold inference holds under A1--A4 and A6. What breaks is A5. That is a statement that the decomposition drawn by the CMB observer is not the one drawn by the galaxy observer.

\textbf{Where the record limits it.} A one-field dark sector that is cold early and smooth late is the $K(Q)$ idea. KR1 and KR2 closed it at the level of its functional form: the mass is set by local density, and no window survives at X-COP. BG1 found dark-ages neutral gas clears the cold piece about 400 times too fast. So the bookkeeping reading names the tether but does not build it. It says what to look for: one dark stress tensor whose cold face fades in bound, accelerating systems (where the $a_0$ law already does the work) and persists in the linear early universe.

\section{Conclusion}
Consciousness is not a force between cold dark energy and dark energy: it is too weak by 38 orders of magnitude, too late by billions of years, and too local. What survives is quieter. Observers may be selected to live where the two are comparable, possibly through $a_0$; and the line between ``cold'' and ``dark energy'' is drawn by the observer, which the A5 break in CMB3 already shows.

The useful question left is physical, not mental: what single dark stress tensor shows a cold face to the early universe and a smooth face to galaxies? Consciousness points at that question. It does not answer it.
