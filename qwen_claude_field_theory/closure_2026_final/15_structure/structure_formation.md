# Phase XVII — Structure Formation

Status labels: `DERIVED` / `IMPOSED` / `FITTED` / `UNKNOWN`.

## 1. The MOND prediction for structure formation

MOND (and TeVeS/EMOND relativistic completions) make specific predictions for
the growth of structure:
- The **growth rate** `f(d ln D / d ln a)` of density perturbations,
- The **matter power spectrum** `P(k)`,
- The **cluster abundance**,
- The **weak-lensing** signal around clusters.

These are **testable** against CMB (Planck), galaxy surveys (DES, KiDS,
Euclid), and cluster counts.

## 2. The candidate's structure-formation prediction

**DERIVED / UNKNOWN:** The candidate's structure-formation prediction is
**not determined** in the frozen form, for the same reasons as Phase XVI:
1. The background M-term is undefined (F₋ gap).
2. The T-gap.
3. The ghost (unstable growing mode corrupts the perturbation spectrum).

Without a defined, stable linear perturbation spectrum (Phase XVI), the
**nonlinear** structure-formation prediction (which builds on the linear
spectrum) is also not determined.

**DERIVED:** The ghost instability (Phase XVI, item 3) means that even if the
linear spectrum were defined, the **nonlinear** evolution would be dominated by
the growing ghost mode, producing a structure-formation signal that is
**not** the standard MOND/ΛCDM signal. The candidate would predict a
**corrupted, ghost-dominated** structure formation, which is **not** observed.
