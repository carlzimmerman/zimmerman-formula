**Abstract**

We prove the Friedgut–Kalai sharp-threshold conjecture. For every integer *n≥* 2, every nontrivial increasing family of graphs on *n* vertices invariant under all vertex permutations, and every0 *< ε <* 1*/*2, the edge probabilities at which its probability equals *ε* and1 *−ε* differ by at most*C*log(1*/*(2*ε*))*/*(log*n*) 2, for a universal constant*C*.
