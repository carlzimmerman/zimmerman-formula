**Abstract**

We resolve De Giorgi’s conjecture positively in dimension eight: every entire *C²* solution *u* : ℝ⁸ *→* (*−*1*,*1)ofΔ*u* = *u³ −u* with an everywhere positive directional derivative is a planar heteroclinic. We prove that every stable solution *v* : ℝ⁷ *→* [*−*1*,*1]of this equation is a constant well or a planar heteroclinic, without an energy-growth assumption.
