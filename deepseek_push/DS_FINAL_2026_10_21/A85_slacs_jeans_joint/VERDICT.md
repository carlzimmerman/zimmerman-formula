# A85 — VERDICT

**The σ channel does not break the B–κ degeneracy. The lane's value is the zero-parameter
consistency test it makes possible, and the honest size of the IMF tension at the thermal a₀.**

## 1. Does the theory's own lensing mass reproduce the dynamics? Yes, at 2.3 per cent.

Route 1 fixes each lens's boost `B_i` from `R_E` alone — **no free normalisation anywhere** — and
predicts the SDSS 1.5 arcsec aperture dispersion with a real spherical Jeans solver on the same `g`
that the lens equation integrates. At a₀ = 8.625700e-11:

* mean `log10(σ_pred/σ_obs)` = **−0.0103 ± 0.0065 dex** (−2.33 per cent, 1.58 sigma)
* per-lens scatter **0.0542 dex** (13.29 per cent) against 0.0628 median fractional errors
* χ²/N = 3.89 with zero free parameters

The *mean* dynamical mass is the lensing mass to 2.3 per cent. The *per-galaxy* identity fails at
13.29 per cent, and that scatter is 2.12× the quoted errors — real astrophysical scatter, not noise.

**But**: `n_0 = 4.74` (Sérsic index) or `beta_0 = +0.223` (constant anisotropy) each zero the 2.3 per
cent offset completely, and neither is measured. The offset is therefore **not evidence for or
against the theory**; it is a 2 per cent measurement of an n–β degeneracy. The scatter is the part
that is not degenerate, and the scatter is 13 per cent.

## 2. The shared B at the thermal a₀ is 2.2 sigma above Treu.

| | B | tension with 1.000 ± 0.0361 dex |
|---|---|---|
| Route 2, β = 0, a₀ = 8.625700e-11 | **1.2269 ± 0.0043 (formal) ± 0.0106 (scaled)** | **2.27 sigma** |
| Route 2, β profiled (+0.250) | 1.1852 | **1.89 sigma** |
| Route 2, β = 0, a₀ = 9.362600e-11 | 1.2174 | 2.19 sigma |
| Route 2, β profiled, a₀ = 9.362600e-11 | 1.1758 | 1.80 sigma |

A70's 1.80 sigma was quoted at a₀ = 1.132e-10. **The thermal a₀ is 0.7620 of that**, and a lower a₀
requires more stellar mass: `B_i` goes from 1.1754 to 1.2209 (+3.87 per cent). The thermal point is
allowed at ~2 sigma, not comfortably, and the number is robust to the fit's error model because
Treu's 0.0361 dex dominates the denominator (0.0391 dex).

## 3. The degeneracy: formally broken, practically intact.

`dlnσ_ap/dlnκ = 0.0345` against `dlnσ_ap/dlnB = 0.4706` → ratio **0.0732**, versus the lensing
**0.1550**. The aperture dispersion is *more* B-dominated than lensing, so adding it buys less than
one would hope:

* angle between the two constraint directions: **3.079 deg** (sin = 0.0537)
* 2×2 Fisher: det = **1.4278e+05**, condition number **746.4**, B–κ correlation 0.9291
* σ(κ): **0.6292** from `R_E` alone → **0.2677** joint (factor **2.35**)

A κ measured to ±0.27 is 10× A71's statistical error (0.0258) and 2× its systematic (0.1179). It
neither confirms, kills nor strengthens A71's Δχ² = 6.35.

## 4. The exact profile prefers high κ — and it does not survive an honest error model.

Profiling the shared B over κ ∈ [0.20, 1.10], χ² falls monotonically to the edge (best κ = 1.1000,
a₀ = 2.059772e-10), a change of **−28.4** in Δχ² from the thermal point (Δχ² = 61.13 across the
whole scan, against A70's 0.26). But the σ channel sits at **χ²/dof = 8.13** with **17.8 per cent**
RMS residuals (`2.83`× e_sigma). Scaled to χ²/dof = 1 the same preference is **Δχ² = 2.96** —
below 3 sigma. The apparent constraint is the fit shaving the scatter of an already-bad fit.

The high-κ branch is tempting (at κ = 1.1000 the shared B falls to 1.0920, essentially Treu's 1.000,
which would dissolve the IMF tension by raising a₀ by a factor 2.3879). **It is a 1.7 sigma
preference built on a χ²/dof = 8 fit and must not be claimed.**

## 5. What would change the answer

A per-lens Sérsic index (A80) and a per-lens anisotropy would convert the 2 per cent n–β degeneracy
into a measurement, and would let the 13.29 per cent scatter be decomposed. Until then the σ channel
is a consistency check — a good one, which the theory passes at 2.3 per cent in the mean — and not a
κ measurement.
