constant rescalings `h → Ω²h`, `N → χN`, `P → λP`, and the three measured normalisations that must survive them — the
`R₃` coefficient (the static `G`), the `K_ijK^ij` coefficient (`c_T² = 1`, GW170817 at 1e−15) and the unit clock

trivial (they change the reduced action only at `O(δ²)`, ratio **4.000000** on halving ε) **[C26_SHIFT2]**; and the one
on-shell quantity that carries β, the fifth mode's pole `ω² = αp⁴/(K(2+3β)/β + ηp²)`, moves by `15/11 = 1.3636` between
β = 1 and β = 3 while being invariant under all field rescalings **[C20_RATIO, C28_SMAT]**, which is exactly what a
parameter — not a redundancy — does. β is invisible to the law, to α₁/α₂/β_PPN and to the TT sector for one structural
reason, computed here: the β-term is `−(1+β)θ²/(4K)` and `θ ≡ 0` on **every** static configuration, so the static action

**`G_cosmo/G_N = 2/(2+3β)`** **[C01, C03]**. That ratio is measurable because BBN knows `ρ_rad` from `T`, and
