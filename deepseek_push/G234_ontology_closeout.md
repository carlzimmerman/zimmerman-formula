### The test: what observable would STILL require a separate substance?

(i) **A mass that separates from its baryonic source.** The Gauss-map charge
rides on the baryons by construction (it is the surface term of the field the
baryons source). A collision system whose dark centroid is displaced from its
gas/stars — a Bullet-class offset — is impossible in (a)/(c) and trivial in (b).

(forest m ∈ [4.6, 5.2] keV confirms the triangle; m < 4 or m > 6 keV kills it,
G168 V3) — a mass that exists independent of any equilibrium.

(iii) **A particle-imprint in the structure.** Free-streaming or any cutoff:
λ_fs > 0, R(k) < 1, the SHMF slope inversion at 95% in the 1e5–1e8 decade
(G156's RELIC declaration). The equilibrium statistics knows no particle scale.

(iv) **Dynamics decoupled from the equilibrium.** A phantom with its own
degrees of freedom: an independent Jeans scale, self-interaction signatures,
non-thermodynamic relaxation, phantom-specific oscillation modes, any velocity
dispersion that does not track the baryonic thermometer.

(v) **Mass beyond the equilibrium's envelope.** The phantom share is capped:
the pie's constitution curve s_ph(M) = s_b(M)·g(M) fixes the share by the baryon
share with no free parameter (G187/G222), and the Gauss charge saturates at the
EFE cap √(a0/g_ext)·M_b (0.660 canonical). A system measuring M_ph above its
pie-line or growing past its cap would exceed what the equilibrium can produce.

(vi) **A universal a0 gap.** If the 22% gap is environment-independent
(every environment, same value), H054 C4 dies: a universal constant scale is a
fundamental residue, not an emergent one. If it correlates with environment and
formation history, emergence — and with it the dissolution direction — stands.
