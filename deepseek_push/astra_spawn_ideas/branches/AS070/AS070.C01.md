## 2. Exact new claim

**Claim C01.** Extend the parent cell by a host sector that depends on the constrained
variables, minimally through a coefficient `chi_a` in `S_host` (or a kinetic/self-energy term
for `rho_L` or `a0`), i.e. `dS_host/drho_L = u` and/or `dS_host/da0 = w` (local coefficients,
evaluated at the static configuration). Then the parent's constraint chain changes to:

```
{pi_rho, H_T} = -u + ...  =>  eta = u/(G c^2)            (shifted multiplier value; u := dS_host/drho_L,
                                                          e.g. u = -c^2 for the parent vacuum term -> eta = -1/G)
{pi_a0, H_T}  = 8 a0 eta + w     =>  consistency: w = -8 a0 eta = -8 a0 u/(G c^2)
{F, H_T}      = ...              =>  F constraint propagation acquires a host source term
```

and the Poisson matrix of `{pi_a0, pi_rho, pi_eta, F, eta}` is unchanged in rank **iff** the
host terms contribute no new momentum dependence; the first-class direction `chi` survives iff
`u` and `w` satisfy the joint consistency relation, otherwise the sector is fully second-class
and the constraint then **does** eliminate a degree of freedom — at the price of the theory
being changed (parent T6) and `eta` being fixed by host data rather than derived.

Precise target: (i) derive the exact rank/first-class condition in terms of `u, w` (and any
`rho_L`/`a0` kinetic coefficients); (ii) when `chi` survives, show the family remains a gauge
orbit and `kappa = 1/2` stays adopted; when `chi` dies, exhibit the constraint-driven DOF
removal and its stress `T^eta` on-shell; (iii) state the minimum local host term that
resurrects the parent counterexample (a genuine premise removal vs a theory change).

statement with no classification; the child supplies the classification and the exact
