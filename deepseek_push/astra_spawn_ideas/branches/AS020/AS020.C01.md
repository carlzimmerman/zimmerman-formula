## Ordered work

1. Reconstruct the parent's decisive result (Π-invariance theorem and hybrid (kpc/km)^∓1
   mismatch) and verify the audited formula's unit assignments against AS020 §2.
2. Derive per-formula n from exponent bookkeeping (kpc-kind minus km-kind length powers of
   the formula's factors at the hybrid convention).
3. Test with one hand-reduced formula (the deep law for one galaxy, M = 10^10 M_sun) through
   both SI and hybrid numberings.
4. Run the negative control *capable of failing*: a deliberately misbalanced transcription
   (G with all three powers in km, i.e. 1.32713e10 km³ M_sun⁻¹ s⁻², paired with kpc-kind ρ)
   must give n ≠ 0 and Π ≠ 0.5.
5. Return the strongest supported claim: the exact audit table, or the first misbalanced
   formula found (a counterexample to "all quoted formulas in the hybrid are unit-true").

## Success, failure and continuation

Pass: every audited formula is unit-true (all implicit factors ≥ 0 mismatch — none) or all
mismatches are quantified and repaired in screen-output (never editing another worker's
files). Failure: a formula whose quoted numbers violate Π or v⁴-balance in an *unrepairable*
way exposes a transcription error in a repository result — preserve the formula and the
parent claim's integrity; do not silently patch; register the finding. Continuation: only a
genuinely new unit-dependence class (e.g., a velocity-squared-ratio law already quoted with an
implicit kpc factor) justifies AS020.C02; otherwise stop at the audit table.
