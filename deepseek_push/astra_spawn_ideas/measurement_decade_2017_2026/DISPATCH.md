## Fair coverage and finding-driven branches

Maintain a durable ledger over all 2,500 MY seeds (and over the AS catalog if the launch scope includes it). Use dependency-aware round-robin scheduling across years and source families. Dedicate at least three of every four available seed/child assignment opportunities to unassessed ready seeds until every seed has a reviewed disposition; use the remaining opportunity for supported children. A time-critical or load-bearing prerequisite may temporarily change this ratio if recorded. Limit simultaneous work to actual runner capacity. Do not dispatch 4,500 processes at once.

A seed disposition is completed-and-reviewed, counterexample, inconclusive, blocked with an exact dependency, or covered by named accepted evidence with a substantive equivalence review. Blocked is not completed science. Queue an unblock action only when it can supply new information, and resume when that evidence arrives. Do not keep retrying unavailable data or fabricate completeness. Report all categories separately at the end of every wave.

Every task has three proposed continuations: promising extension, independent bridge, and failure repair. Dispatch only a child justified by an actual result, with a genuinely changed target and duplicate check. Child IDs use MY2026-001.C01, etc.; specifications go under branches/<parent>/<child>.md. Each child cites parent task/run/artifact hashes, its exact new claim, inputs, equations, controls, completion condition and own write scope. Use at most two live children per parent and return to the orchestrator after two levels for reconciliation; further reviewed waves may continue. If no spawn mechanism exists, save a ready specification and report it as queued, not run.

## Closure is a separate claim

Any seed may pursue a first-principles derivation and submit a full closure candidate. The candidate must use one action, physical metric, matter coupling, source/state domain, boundary prescription and overlapping parameter cell across all thirteen amended gates. Require explicit proofs or appropriately scoped empirical evidence at every edge, plus an independent falsification review. Keep primitive assumptions, measured calibrations and derived mechanisms separate. If kappa=1/2, the vacuum magnitude or a constitutive kernel remains adopted, say so. A conditional effective theory is a different claim from derivation of those inputs. No number of completed tasks alone closes gravity.
