## Source verification and limits

All 30 primary arXiv abstract pages were opened. Initial submission dates, current version histories, titles and reported observables were checked. Years refer to initial measurement reports, not later journal dates or revisions. Examples of deliberately preserved chronology are ACT DR6 lensing (2023 despite 2024 revision), SPT 2018 lensing (2023 despite 2024 revision), Union3 (2023 despite 2025 revision), DESI DR1 full shape (2024 despite 2025 corrections), and GW230529 phase tests (2024 report of a 2023 event, with later revisions).

The sources are actual measured-data reports or analyses of actual observations. No forecast, instrument proposal, or theory-only paper is used as an anchor. DES Y3 map statistics, DES supernova lensing, the joint shear papers and GW propagation are new analyses of existing observations; they are not represented as new independent sky/event datasets.

Authentication was at the primary abstract and submission-history level. Full paper derivations, exact likelihoods, masks, source rows, calibration matrices, data archives and raw-data completeness were not inspected. Availability claims are restricted to links/statements actually visible in the abstracts/comments. Observation epochs not extracted are marked unknown. Every task makes missing extraction or a missing theory-to-observable bridge a reportable dependency; no numerical result, sample size, covariance or theoretical closure is invented.
