# Measurement-decade authoring contract

Requested: 250 additional task files per calendar year 2026 back through 2017, 2,500 total. Cutoff is 2026-09-27. These are original proposed research work orders, not executed science. Preserve the original AS001–AS2000 catalog and the independently owned fresh_gravity_followups namespace.

Each year must use at least 10 distinct authenticated primary measurement/data-report sources. Prefer first public measurement report or actual data-release date. A later journal publication alone cannot move an old measurement into a newer year. Record date basis, observation dates separately if known, versions and limitations. Do not use forecasts, instrument plans, proposed future releases, theory-only papers or media summaries as measurement anchors. Read/open the primary source, not search snippets alone. A primary paper abstract can authenticate a report and reported observables; do not claim full data/covariance or exact likelihood inspection unless actually inspected. Unknown raw-data availability must be marked, never invented.

250 tasks does not mean 250 independent datasets. A source may support multiple genuinely different scientific outputs. Avoid universal checklists multiplied mechanically over datasets. Each task needs a release-specific target, equation/estimand, measurable input, exact output, independent control, framework bridge and continuation. Reusing a data release requires distinct outputs; different years using overlapping observations require an incremental/new-information or cross-release systematic objective. No repeated old-AS obligation with only the dataset name changed: state the extra information unavailable to that older task, or make the task an application with a genuinely new measurement result sought and explain dependence.

Write only assigned <year>_sources.json and <year>_tasks.json (and your own generator if needed) in this directory. Root renders files and shared indexes. IDs MY<year>-001 through MY<year>-250.

Source record required fields:
source_id (Y<year>S01 etc), year, title, authors_or_collaboration, primary_url, identifier, report_date (ISO date or ISO month with date_precision), date_precision, date_basis, version, observation_epoch (or unknown), measurement (short factual description of actual measured observables), data_access (verified link/status or explicit unknown), verification (what was opened/read, what remains unverified), source_locator (section/table/abstract/release-note locator actually observed), overlap_family (stable survey/event family to catch reused data), search_queries (array), checked_on (2026-09-27).

Task record required fields:
id, year, source_id, title, principle, math, measurement_input, deliverable, steps (4–6 ordered strings), controls (>=2 with first explicitly negative), first_principles, closure_bridge, new_information (specific new measurement/release or distinctive output versus old generic seeds), overlap_handling (same-data covariance/incremental exclusions), continuation (3 task-specific strings: promising extension, independent bridge, failed-route repair), depends_on (other MY IDs or old AS IDs when genuinely required), priority P0/P1/P2, kind derivation/inference/computation/audit.

Framework: a0=(c/2)sqrt(G_N rho_Lambda), with mass-density vacuum; canonical9.3619e-11 and alternative1.1279e-10 m/s² kept separate. G_N vsG_E/bare/cosmological G distinct. Filtered MONO and criterionB operative13gate target; Q/RAR/MU2 comparisons only unless bridge proved. No particle additions silently. Exact theory-derived observation map required before empirical claims; published GR/LambdaCDM posteriors are not theory-independent raw data. Adoption vsderivation of1/2, kernels, vacuum magnitude explicit. Classical theory does not imply a quantum completion. New findings may branch under parent protocol, never self-award closure.

Source verification facts stay short; task mathematics and future experiments are original proposals, not claims the cited source performed them. Use no invented measured central values, sample sizes, dates, public-data links or inferred full likelihoods.
