## Authentication scope

Opened the primary arXiv abstract pages for all 30 source reports, reading their report identity, measurement descriptions and submission history. The MICROSCOPE primary PRL abstract and publication metadata were also opened. Gaia DR3 v1 HTML was opened for contents and caveat context. The abstracts authenticate that these are actual measurement/data reports, not proposed instruments or forecasts. They do not authenticate full data arrays, covariance matrices, executable likelihoods, or every equation in a source. Those remain explicit extraction requirements. No central values or likelihood files were invented.

Most first-report dates are the first arXiv submission for the named detailed report, rather than the later journal issue date. A catalog paper can follow low-latency discovery alerts; the dates here classify the detailed measurement or processing report, not a claim that the sky event was first observed in that year. `search_queries` records direct primary identifier lookup where that was the actual lookup mechanism; it does not fabricate a search-query history.

## Semantic overlap and incremental information

- All O3a catalog and individual-event sources use overlap family `LVK_O3a`. GW190521 and GW190814 task sets isolate different short-signal or asymmetric-harmonic outputs and explicitly exclude those events from any combined catalog factor. GWTC-2.1 asks for correlated old/final-strain changes, selection changes, and new-candidate support. It never supplies independent repeat-strain likelihood factors.
- GWTC-3 task work uses O3b-only measurements or an explicitly conditional O3a-to-O3b contrast.
- Planck NPIPE uses the same sky as earlier Planck. Its tasks target calibration, processing, detector-set and simulation-transfer increments. No new sky campaign is claimed.
- Gaia EDR3, resolved pairs, DR3 and BH1 are all marked in `Gaia_stellar_astrometry`. New pair membership/error calibration, radial velocities/non-single-star products, and BH1 follow-up spectroscopy are distinct outputs. Inherited astrometry is one shared block.
- SH0ES and both Pantheon+ reports share `SH0ES_Pantheon_distance_ladder`. Flux comparisons, sibling controls, ladder calibration, and distance/bias inference are different obligations with explicit covariance and conditional-likelihood rules.
- The two SPT-3G releases share `SPT3G_2018_CMB`. New TT is conditioned on old TE/EE; the updated covariance is separately identifiable from added temperature observations.
- Both Cosmicflows reports share `Cosmicflows_distance_velocity`. Reconstructed fields are model-conditioned processing products and cannot be multiplied by their parent-distance likelihood as independent data.
- SMACS0723 tasks distinguish new JWST arcs from pre-JWST HST/MUSE constraints. Model-predicted source redshifts are not measurements.
- EHT polarization adds Stokes information to the same 2017 M87 campaign. Its likelihood is conditional on the inherited total intensity.
- LEGA-C full-spectrum and earlier Lick-index analyses may use the same galaxies and photons; method covariance and population/stellar-model dependence are explicit.
