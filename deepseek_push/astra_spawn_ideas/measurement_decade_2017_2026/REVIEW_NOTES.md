# Catalog review and limits

The delivered scope is 250 work orders in each of 2017–2026, 2,500 additional seeds, based on 100 distinct primary report/release URLs. Sources were authenticated through primary abstracts, submission histories, publisher metadata and/or official release pages. This authenticates dates and reported observables; it does not certify a source's conclusion or imply that its full numerical likelihood was inspected. The 2026 cutoff is 27 September. Later revisions do not move an earlier report into a newer year.

## Scientific distinction and overlap

Every seed has a distinct equation/estimand, requested output and explicit new-information statement. Shared derivation methods are reusable dependencies, not claimed new theorems. The individual task target is the particular measured response, identifiable combination, error bound, conditional update or first-principles bridge. Titles and hashes alone cannot establish substantive distinction.

The author reviews for [2020–2022](_catalog/REVIEW_2020_2022.md) and [2023–2025](_catalog/REVIEW_2023_2025.md) document chronology and overlap decisions. The 2017–2019 author checked public-announcement dates against later arXiv deposits for GW170104, J0337 and EHT; DF2 and DF4 now have different tracer-conditioning targets. The 2026 author individually specified 25 outputs per report. Source access and overlap are repeated in every card so a small worker does not need to infer them.

Specific repaired or bounded overlaps include HSC real/harmonic same-galaxy estimands; NANOGrav timing versus spatial correlations; DES photometric-classification sensitivity versus Union3; DESI DR2 conditional innovations over DR1; KiDS new-area/calibration contrasts; SPT temperature-conditioned polarization; GW individual events versus cumulative catalogs; and updated Gaia/Pantheon processing of inherited measurements. These are different requested outputs with explicit covariance requirements, not independent repeated detections.

The 2026 anchors add Lyman-alpha broadband/AP modes beyond BAO; O4b catalog information with inherited-event deduplication; DES Y6 three-probe response; eROSITA three-scan catalog selection; multifrequency tSZ pressure statistics; SPT quadratic reconstruction; new/archival 3D wide-binary follow-up; lunar satellite clock comparison; J1537 joint delays/imaging/kinematics; and SN2025wny spectroscopic phases. Their tasks target distinct release-specific obligations and cannot multiply shared information as independent evidence.

## Availability and theory boundaries

- eROSITA DR2 is catalog-only. Its work orders cannot extract nonexistent DR2 event lists, resolved temperature/pressure profiles or raw spectra. Hydrostatic claims require separately obtained follow-up; several tasks explicitly prove the catalog's identifiability limit.
- The lunar-maser source has an authenticated March 2026 journal issue and an indicated earlier January online appearance whose exact primary date was not independently authenticated. It remains a 2026 anchor; month precision and the uncertainty are explicit. The quoted violation-parameter convention must be extracted before numerical translation.
- Gaia DR3 uses an authenticated July 2022 report date, distinguished from its earlier archive release. Primary release-page access failed for the author; no later report is represented as a new independent observation.
- An existing spectrum, posterior, catalog or fitted mass is not automatically a theory-independent likelihood. Each work order requires the appropriate observation map, calibration and covariance.
- A source's claimed anomaly, H0 value, cosmological preference or inferred mass is not endorsed by catalog authoring. The task may support, reject or leave unidentified the framework implication.
- The core action-to-observable map is itself often open. Tasks must name that gap, derive a valid conditional result, or pursue the missing derivation. They cannot invent the map merely to run a fit.

## Verification boundary

The validator checks exact counts, contiguous IDs, required content, controls, continuations, dates, source references, task hashes, local links, pinned local base and dependency cycles. Exact-duplicate checks cover titles, math fields, deliverables and scientific fingerprints. Human/agent semantic review examines selected high-overlap families; it is not a mathematical proof of global novelty. Before dispatch, compare the actual target against live AS/MY/FGF evidence and merge any newly discovered substantive duplicate into one owned obligation, preserving a reuse disposition for the seed.

All files are research specifications. No empirical fit, physics simulation, proof verification, DeepSeek execution or theory-closure claim is supplied by this authoring task. The original 2,000 AS task files and their existing execution receipts are preserved.
