Some work orders intentionally reconstruct existing identities as independent checks or prerequisites. They are not all claims of novel research. Other tasks test candidate extensions, which must remain labelled until their additional assumptions and equations are reviewed. Existing source documents are evidence to examine; their prose verdicts are not adopted as axioms.

The structural validator checks exactly 2000 task files, 20 groups of 100, nonempty required fields, local source/link existence, task/source hashes and acyclic explicit prerequisites. It does not establish that every proposed construction will succeed, that every source claim is true, or that a theory is closed. Mathematical and observational acceptance require the subsequent result-and-review workflow.

The expanded catalog has exactly 2,000 separate seed files, with 100 in each of 20 research areas. Each includes a mathematical target, explicit framework base, first-principles obligation, common-theory bridge, ordered bounded steps, controls, return contract and three continuation directions. Child tasks are additional and must pass the mathematical-fingerprint duplicate check.

Three agents reviewed disjoint expansion ranges and cross-range overlap candidates; the orchestrator integrated their repairs. Existing concurrent FGF work is linked through REUSE_BEFORE_DISPATCH.md; it was not overwritten or counted as an AS execution.
