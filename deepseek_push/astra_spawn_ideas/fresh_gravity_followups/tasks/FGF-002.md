# FGF-002: Skew instrumental noise and fourth-moment bias

Status: `ready_for_dispatch`. Lane: `measurement`. Parent: accepted Astra stage 3.

Read `../FRAMEWORK_AND_EXECUTION.md` and `../RESULT_CONTRACT.json`. This is a bounded research task, not an accepted result. The framework instructions are mandatory.

## Target

Extend the moment correction to zero-mean broadening with nonzero third moment; identify when PSF mixing prevents removing the new odd-velocity term.

## Equations

- For independent additive epsilon with E epsilon=0, E(v^4|u)=u^4+6u² sigma²+4u m3_epsilon+m4_epsilon.
- The extra PSF contribution is 4P[I u m3_epsilon]; observed first moments are P[Iu].

## Domain

Two and four source cells; constant versus varying known m3_epsilon; centered two-point noise epsilon={-h,2h} with probabilities {2/3,1/3} and h=.05,.2 in dimensionless velocity units.

## Steps

1. Derive correction weights from the actual first/fourth-moment operators.
2. Integrate the two-point distribution exactly; compare Q/R velocities separately.
3. Construct a pair of opposite-sign source velocities to test when odd contributions cancel and when unequal weights break cancellation.

## Decisive controls

- Set m3_epsilon=0 and recover the earlier formula.
- Apply the old symmetric-noise correction deliberately and record the resulting scale bias rather than hiding it in an effective width.

## Acceptance scope

An explicit skew-noise correction or finite-dimensional non-identifiability example; no assertion that a real instrument has this skewness.
