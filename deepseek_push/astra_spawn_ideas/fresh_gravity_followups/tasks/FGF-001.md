# FGF-001: Masked-source estimator rank and exact support

Status: `ready_for_dispatch`. Lane: `measurement`. Parent: accepted Astra stage 3.

Read `../FRAMEWORK_AND_EXECUTION.md` and `../RESULT_CONTRACT.json`. This is a bounded research task, not an accepted result. The framework instructions are mandatory.

## Target

Determine when deleting dark source cells changes existence of an unbiased moment estimator; produce an exact rational example and a support-aware numerical algorithm.

## Equations

- alpha=P^T l4; beta=P^T l2; correction error=6 sum I_i(alpha_i s_i²-beta_i)u_i².
- Necessary and sufficient for arbitrary supported velocity squares: diag(I)(s² alpha-beta)=0. Formal full-space cancellation is a different quantifier.

## Domain

Begin P=(1,1), I=(1,0), s²=(1/25,4/25), l4=1, l2=1/25. Then examine all 15 nonempty emission masks of P=[[1,.7,.2,0],[0,.3,.8,1]], with s²=(.04,.16,.09,.25).

## Steps

1. Derive the active-column nuisance operator before computing rank.
2. Compare full-space and active-support nullspaces using exact rational arithmetic in the two-cell case and reported singular-value cutoffs in the four-cell case.
3. For each claimed estimator verify the correction for independent choices of nonnegative emitting velocities, including zero.

## Decisive controls

- Deliberately enforce the full-vector condition and show whether it incorrectly excludes an active-support solution.
- A tiny positive intensity is not exact zero: track conditioning as I_dark moves from 0 to 1e-12 to 1e-4.

## Acceptance scope

A scoped support criterion plus a reproducible counterexample/conditioning table; do not infer statistical robustness from exact cancellation.
