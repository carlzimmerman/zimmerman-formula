## Equations

- chi=eta² ell d, psi=pi d, tau=pi/eta; d=psi² ell/(chi tau²).
- For local illustrative ell=tau^alpha and chi=psi=1, d=tau^(alpha-2).

## Domain

Symbolic alpha first, then alpha=-1,0,.5,1,2,3; tau in [.5,1.5]; no claim that these alpha values represent an actual detector or plasma. Use central cluster roots only as conditional context.

## Steps

2. Combine the thermal equation with B_prime=eta d Bgas+upsilon Bstar and gH_prime=pi gH/(eta d).
3. List the bandpass/spectral response and independent measurements required to determine ell rather than choosing alpha to force agreement.
