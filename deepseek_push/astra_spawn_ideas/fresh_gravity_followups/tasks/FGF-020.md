Independently determine whether two idealized normalized volume moments
mean(x)=mu and mean(x²)=nu, with 0<nu-mu²<mu², bound a positive shell density L
when its volume fraction may be arbitrarily small. Reconstruct a positive
piecewise-constant countermodel or show where it fails. Derive the strongest
necessary shell-volume condition from nonnegative complementary variance.

between a physical feature width and an instrumental PSF.

binary64 synthetic Q/R force conversions. Both scale normalizations and the
vacuum/frozen-H distinction remain required, but no actual observed cluster
profile is fitted. Determine whether preserving the idealized emission moment
can be promoted to preserving detector counts when density changes at fixed
pressure; if it cannot, state the missing thermal/emissivity dependence.

Return raw independent derivation, result contract and source/output hashes in
the assigned unique results/FGF-020/<run_id> directory. Proof-only review is
acceptable with explicit absence of new numerical experiments. If computing,
use <=120-second bounded processes, one numerical thread, and manifests.

instrument, hydrostatic, cosmological or theory-closure claim is authorized by
