# Preserved routes that do not close the actual budget

1. **Matter momentum conservation.** False as an equation-level shortcut:
   P'=-[cs^2 rho]-int ng=-int(ng-rho g0). Full coupled momentum is
   conserved after the exact hydrostatic stress cancellation. Dropping
   pressure even spoils the stationary control. Replaced by (12)-(13).
2. **Bounded edge data as zero flux.** A bound on traces alone cannot make
   v,p,w vanish. Replaced by the explicit exterior cones and comparison
   with the stationary solution, yielding exact trace equality in (8).
3. **Small full energy implies small enough field momentum.** The complete
   energy estimate (22)-(26), including internal and interaction energies,
   yields |F|=O(N^-1), while P0=O(N^-2). Its time integral is O(N^-5),
   while the gate needs O(N^-6). This bound fails to close by a factor N.
   It is not a coupled counterexample and does not prove impossibility of
   a sharper dynamical estimate.
4. **Zero initial field momentum supplies a time gain for free.** It supplies
   F(t)=int_0^t A, but A=int(ng-rho g0) is exactly the uncontrolled
   correlated force. No time factor is available without bounding it.
5. **Mass/positivity or an L2 field estimate bounds the correlated force.**
   int n, int g and int g^2 do not themselves give the needed estimate
   on int ng. No density or gradient supremum is inferred from them.
   The isothermal entropy contribution remains in the energy and has not
   been converted into a suitable force estimate. No arbitrary-state
   example is claimed to be an actual evolution from the specified data.
6. **The accepted local edge theorem implies whole-branch existence.**
   It gives exact exterior traces and comparison only where a competing
   branch exists. The uncovered core contribution to (27) remains open;
   no full-cone gate, endpoint classification or extension is inferred.

These failures are retained as bounded route limitations, not as objections
to the physical framework or evidence of an actual PDE counterexample.
