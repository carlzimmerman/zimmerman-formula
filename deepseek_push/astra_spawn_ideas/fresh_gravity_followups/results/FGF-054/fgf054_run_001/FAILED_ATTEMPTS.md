# Rejected shortcuts and route boundary

1. Substituting v0=V/N into the old fixed-v0 proof was rejected: its sound,
   inverse-chart, coefficient and source constants then vary with N, and its
   threshold is not uniform. No failed numerical run is concealed here.
2. Keeping the old N^-7/2 protected collar at epsilon=L N^-3 was rejected:
   fixed finite acoustic/field travel over epsilon/V is order epsilon.
3. Keeping the old 15/8 moment coefficient was rejected: the new exterior
   buffers add order-epsilon background mass and capacity. Endpoints at
   +/-2epsilon yield coefficient 15/2. Section 8 optimizes this particular
   symmetric stationary-buffer method and shows its precise obstruction.
4. Bounding only the initial speed was rejected. Centered acoustic invariants
   and a strictly smaller V<Vmax give the actual propagated speed margin.
5. Deleting pressure, freezing gravity, or inferring force control from an
   unsigned energy integral was rejected. The actual exp(q) source is bounded
   after density invariants; full stress and transfer give the moment budget.
6. Treating a stationary collar as a wall was rejected. Its cone comparison
   is proved along background characteristics with the unknown-gradient
   coefficient explicitly retained, before gluing on open overlaps.
7. Calling the conditional cell physically calibrated was rejected. No
   supplied numerical coefficients establish Vmax>(15/2)max(cs,b*,d).
   Outside this cell the upper bound remains unresolved; shrinking epsilon
   alone cannot pay the travel cost, and the failure is not PDE failure.

No numerical experiment, random sampling, compilation, imported theorem,
service or installation was attempted. No physical closure is claimed.
