# Preserved failed shortcuts and exact limits

These are rejected proof attempts, not numerical failures or counterexamples.

1. **Take the maximal lifespan of one auxiliary line extension.** Its distant
   nonhydrostatic evolution could fail first. Rejected. The actual germ uses
   only the core and exact hydrostatic collars, patched on a fixed physical J.
2. **Define maximal time by stopping at the desired upper comparison time.**
   That manufactures an endpoint. Rejected. A strictly larger cap Theta0 is
   used; actual non-survival gives T_N<Theta0 before the continuation argument.
3. **Restart on a shrinking cone with no reserved exterior width.** This can
   lose the original domain. Rejected. K_t contains a fixed J1, the old branch
   equals H on collars twice the required restart width, and the restart is
   patched back to the same J.
4. **Infer C1,1 endpoint regularity from bounded C1 norms alone.** Such an
   abstract inference is false without PDE control. The coupled (10)–(12)
   give uniform derivative-Lipschitz and time-Lipschitz bounds, then a trace.
5. **Glue arbitrary primitive gradients without potential/scale compatibility.**
   Values of phi on the two sides could disagree and chi_X=k could fail.
   The exact H collars fix both potential constants; C1 limits and integral
   reconstruction preserve both constraints and the dynamic Q equation.
6. **Make fixed-N density bounds uniform in N.** Both invariants give bounds
   of order exp(O(N)); no uniform density statement follows. The low-velocity
   density cap used in source integration is a different, restricted bound.
7. **Use old unsigned energy or the FGF049 adverse-gradient gate as solved.**
   The energy bound remains insufficient and the adverse-gradient gate remains
   open. The distinct accepted FGF052 whole-path mass/source method is used.
8. **Call full-gradient limsup divergence a shock, a limit, or falsification.**
   No component selection, shock profile, weak continuation, observational
   speed window or physical completion is established.

No unresolved implication was identified inside the precise candidate theorem.
The new collar-patching equivalence and restart need independent review; that
is a promotion requirement, not a claimed mathematical counterexample or a
substitute for a proof. The separate evolving-reference problem remains open.
