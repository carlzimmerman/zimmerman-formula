but A644's M_FORW box has no globally nondecreasing realization on its entire
raw support. A hydrostatic-equivalent mass is a pressure-force proxy and is
not automatically an actual monotone enclosed source mass. The main rectangle

empty extra family must not be advertised as a substantive physical exclusion.

check monotonic feasibility on the minimum contiguous native knot range needed

from a failure at a tested shell; it does not repair the data.

As an adversarial margin diagnostic, multiply both gas error magnitudes by
lambda, leaving central masses fixed. The first rectangular lower-slope bound
on one interval reaches exactly one at

This follows by setting (M2-lambda err_low2)/(M1+lambda err_high1)=q.
It is a deterministic sensitivity scale, not a sigma value or a new allowed
error model. Check positivity of the two endpoint masses at that crossing.

four-knot profile with positive increasing masses and middle slope below one
will control the failed-certificate interpretation even though no real
