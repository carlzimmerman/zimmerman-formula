# Refinement: is asymptotic safety's "Λ→0" fatal to the framework? — No, it's a tension, not a prohibition

*C. Zimmerman, June 2026. Follow-up to the QG-program grading (`QG_PROGRAMS_TOE_GRADING.md`), which named asymptotic
safety (AS) the #1 QG-UV lead but flagged that AS drives a positive Λ to zero in the deep IR. Is that fatal? Checked.*

## The mechanism is real

In AS, a positive cosmological constant is **dynamically driven to zero in the deep infrared** by graviton
fluctuations (Wetterich, "graviton fluctuations erase the cosmological constant"; the conformally-reduced and ADM
cosmological-background FRG studies). Current (2024–2025), though — as the field itself stresses ("Critical
Reflections on Asymptotically Safe Gravity," Bonanno et al.) — **truncation-dependent and debated**, not settled.

## Why it is NOT fatal to the framework (and is even mildly aligned)

The key contrast: **string theory's de Sitter swampland *forbids* a stable Λ>0** (a hard prohibition of the
framework's basis). **AS does not forbid Λ>0 — it makes it *transient*** (a positive Λ is *allowed*, and slowly
*flows* toward zero in the far IR). Those are very different verdicts:

- For the framework, a₀ = c²√(Λ/32π), so AS's "Λ → 0 in the far IR" means **a₀ → 0 in the far future** too. That is
  *not* a contradiction — the framework *already* predicts a₀ → 0 in the far future, because under DESI's thawing
  w(a)=w₀+wₐ(1−a) the dark-energy density `ρ_DE` dilutes away as a→∞ (w→+∞), so `a₀ ∝ √ρ_DE → 0`. **Both pictures send
  the acceleration scale to zero in the far future** — by different mechanisms (AS: RG flow of Λ; framework: classical
  dilution of ρ_DE), but the *same direction*.
- Deeper (and stated as a rhyme, not a mechanism): AS treats Λ as a **dynamical, flowing quantity, not a fundamental
  constant** — which is *exactly* the framework's distinctive stance that **a₀ is not a fundamental constant but
  evolves** (a₀ ∝ √ρ_DE). The conceptual posture matches. It is a resonance, not a derivation — AS's RG flow is not
  the framework's ρ_DE evolution, and AS supplies no a₀, no MOND, no coefficient.

## Honest disposition (refines the grading)

AS stays a **genuine QG-UV lead**, with a corrected caveat: it does **not** give the framework a *fundamental* Λ>0
(it treats Λ>0 as transient/flowing), but it is **not hostile** (unlike string). Of the two QG-UV leads:

- **DSSYK is the cleaner Λ>0 home** — it *is* the de Sitter static-patch model, with no swampland tension and no
  Λ-erasure; its weakness is incompleteness (no 4D dual, the coefficient route computed to fail).
- **AS is the QFT-side companion** — a genuine interacting UV fixed point that makes real SM-coupling predictions, and
  whose "Λ is dynamical, flowing to zero" picture is *directionally consistent* with evolving a₀ — but it neither
  fixes Λ>0 as fundamental nor produces a₀.

**Net:** the AS Λ→0 result is a *tension and a caveat*, not a showstopper. It does not move the meta-conclusion (no
unified path; the SM stays disconnected), but it usefully reorders the QG-UV leads: **DSSYK first (cleanest Λ>0),
AS second (dynamical-Λ companion, not hostile)** — and it removes any worry that AS *rules out* the framework's basis.
