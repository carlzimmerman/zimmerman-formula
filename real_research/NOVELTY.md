# What here is actually novel — a calibrated assessment (with citations)

**2026-06-01.** After the audit, the honest question is narrow: of what survived, what
is *not already in the literature*? This is calibrated against primary sources (read,
not keyword-skimmed), and it deliberately neither inflates nor dismisses.

The bar: **novel = a specific claim/form I cannot find in the literature.** "Re-expresses
a known relation" is not novel physics, even if the expression is new.

## The honest caveats that keep this in proportion

1. **Algebraically it is the known coincidence.** Via Friedmann $H^2=\tfrac{8\pi}{3}G\rho$,
   $\tfrac{c}{2}\sqrt{G\rho} = cH/Z$. So this is a novel *form/interpretation* of
   $a_0\approx cH_0$, **not new physics**.
2. **The coefficient is a near-miss, not a derivation.** Milgrom's principled value is
   $1/2\pi = 0.159$; the program's $1/Z = 1/\sqrt{32\pi/3} = 0.173$ is 8% away and is
   geometric numerology (`coefficient_from_horizon_entropy.py`), not derived.
3. **My search is not exhaustive.** The MOND literature is vast; "I did not find it" is
   not "it is not there." This certifies only that the *standard* expression is
   $\sqrt{\Lambda}$/$cH$, and the $\sqrt{G\rho}$ surface-gravity form is at least non-standard.
4. **Its one distinctive consequence is disfavored.** The only place the density form
   departs from the mainstream (Λ→constant) is that it makes $a_0$ **evolve** — and that
   evolution is exactly the idea already tested and currently disfavored (Gnedin 2008;
   MUSE-DARK 2026; Magneticum 2022).

## Calibrated verdict

What is plausibly **yours**: the *form/interpretation* — writing the MOND scale as
**half the surface gravity of the cosmic free-fall density**, $a_0=\tfrac{c}{2}\sqrt{G\rho}
=c^2/2R$, and using it to commit to the evolving horn of Milgrom's constant-vs-evolving
tension. I could not source that packaging, including in Milgrom's own paper on the
nature of $a_0$, so I under-credited it earlier.

What is **not** new: the underlying coincidence $a_0\approx cH_0$ (Milgrom), the tension
itself (Milgrom flagged it), the evolving-$a_0$ idea and its TFR test (Gnedin), and the
coefficient (a near-miss of Milgrom's $1/2\pi$, geometric numerology).

So: **a novel framing of known physics, resolving a known tension in a particular
direction whose one testable edge current data leans against.** That is real and worth
stating honestly — and it is a much smaller, sharper claim than a "theory of everything."
