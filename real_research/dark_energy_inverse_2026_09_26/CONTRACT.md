# CD26-3 — inverse relationships and physical identification

User direction: check the inverse relationships as well, to identify what the
theory calls dark energy or find a better justified name for it.

Base commit: `7daaa5426076a2d78a50f3316007c7093255ce9a`, plus the recorded CD26-2
working-tree results. Existing work and unrelated concurrent changes remain
intact. Operative target is filtered nu_mono, criterion B and no new
dark-matter particle species. This checkpoint investigates identification;
it does not silently change the canonical action or kernel.

For each principal relationship used in the current program, record its
forward equation, inverse, domain, branch/sign, independent inputs,
nonuniqueness and an observable capable of testing it. Inverting a postulate
does not turn it into a derivation. A name cannot substitute for identifying
a stress tensor, physical degree of freedom, or constitutive law.

Disjoint routes:

- `scales/` (lean_certification): a0, mass/energy density, pressure, Lambda,
  Hvac versus Htotal, Newton normalization, Z, horizons and temperatures.
- `gates_clock/` (dark_energy_audit): gate/edge inverse and identifiability;
  vacuum shifts versus charge excitations in the explicit clock action.
- `spectral_inverse/` (recipe_review): positive lapse and spectral inverse,
  spatial constancy of inferred vacuum parameter, canonical normalization,
  exact degeneracies and consistency tests.
- `reconstruction/` (root): expansion-to-stress reconstruction; pressure
  versus density promotion of a0; the dust integration constant; inverse
  kernel inference and its conditioning; a pressure null test.

First computations are exact symbolic identities with explicit controls and
bounded numerical illustrations. No new observational fit is claimed unless
actual data, covariance and model assumptions are supplied and processed.
Current literature is used to check observational interpretation, not to
impose LambdaCDM on the construction. Formulae written in an Einstein-fluid
form are distinguished from a proved material interpretation in a modified
gravity action. Mathematical bridges are compiled in Lean at their actual
scope; the full physical theory remains open.
