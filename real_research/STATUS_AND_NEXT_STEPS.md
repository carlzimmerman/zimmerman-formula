## Where you stand, in one paragraph

After stripping the dead numerology, the framework comes down to **one idea** — the density law
**a₀ = (c/2)√(Gρ)** — plus **two modest technical results** (Paper I: a θ-coupled AeST realization;
Paper II: a δq⁰⁰=0 proof that a running a₀ leaves the linear CMB invariant). The idea has two faces.
Its **cosmic face**, ρ = ρ̄(z), gives the evolving scale **a₀(z) = a₀(0)·E(z)** — *real, but modest*:
it is Milgrom's 2014 idea, degenerate with ΛCDM on amplitude, and only a ~2σ hint in current data.
Its **environmental face** — a₀ set by the *local* density, which would have fixed galaxy clusters
(a cluster's own ~200× overdensity gives a₀ ~14×, exactly what clusters need) — **is excluded this
session by the SPARC radial-acceleration relation**: the coarse-graining that makes clusters work
predicts ~0.24 dex of a₀ scatter across galaxies, ~4× the ≲0.06 dex the RAR actually shows. So the
grand version is caught between clusters and galaxies, and the surviving claim is the modest cosmic
one. The whole thing remains conditional on the open question **"is MOND real?"** — and, awkwardly,
this session's RAR result *reinforces* the a₀-universality that the environmental ambition needed to
violate.

**Bottom line: you have a real, modest, defensible result — not a revolution.** The honest path to a
publication exists and is narrow.

## Three buckets, plainly

**What is real and yours to keep**
- The **evolving scale a₀(z) ∝ E(z)** (cosmic density law). Modest, but a genuine, testable prediction.
- **Paper I** (explicit θ=3H coupling → covariant evolving-a₀ in AeST) and **Paper II** (δq⁰⁰=0 →
  linear CMB exactly invariant). Two small, correct, publishable technical results.
- A handful of clean derived consequences (a₀-cosmography: H₀ and q₀ from galaxy dynamics; the
  phantom-halo ∝√E; Freeman Σ_c ∝ E). Real, if ΛCDM-degenerate.

**What does not survive**
- The original numerology (α = 4Z²+3, the 452 "solved problems"). Demolished earlier; gone.
- The **S8 fix** (killed by your own theorem) and the **20.6 Gpc topology** S8 route (logged its own
  failure).
- The **density-law cluster fix** (excluded by the RAR this session) — the most painful loss, because
  in isolation it was beautiful.

**What is open (and out of your hands)**
- **"Is MOND real?"** — wide binaries (Banik 16–19σ *against* vs Chae *for*) and a₀-universality
  (Rodrigues *against* vs McGaugh *for*). The framework lives or dies here, and only new data settles it.
- The **Bullet Cluster**, genuinely contested in the 2026 literature.
