# The framework's natural home is modified inertia — where both exposures dissolve and a₀(z) is automatic

*C. Zimmerman, 2026-06-05. The Cassini generalization (`CASSINI_QUADRUPOLE_CONSTRAINT.md`) ended on a reframing: the
framework's concept is **modified inertia**, forced into a modified-**gravity** realization (AeST) for covariance.
Pushed one door further, that reframing turns into the most consequential — and most *hopeful* — finding of the
session, and it is honest both ways.*

## The framework's concept is modified-inertia, not modified-gravity

The framework's own statement of `a₀` is **about the inertial/dynamical clock**, not the gravitational field:

> `a₀ = c²/(2R*)` = surface gravity of the free-fall horizon at the ambient density; equivalently **`a₀ ~ the
> acceleration at which a system's dynamical time `t_dyn` reaches the cosmic causal time `t_cosmic`.**

That is Milgrom's *modified-inertia* reading of MOND (and the repo's own working "Layer 0," de Sitter–Unruh modified
inertia, which fits SPARC at 0.105 dex). It is a statement about *when inertia changes*, not about a fifth force.

## Milgrom's theorem, and why it dissolves BOTH exposures

**Milgrom (1994) proved rigorously:** MOND realized as modified inertia, while preserving Galilean symmetry, **must be
time-nonlocal** — a particle's dynamics depend on its *entire trajectory*, and (the key structural consequence) the
**center-of-mass acceleration of a composite body** is the quantity that determines its MOND behavior, not the
accelerations of its parts. Two consequences fall straight out:

- **Cassini dissolves.** In a modified-*inertia* world the internal Solar-System accelerations (planets: `g ≫ a₀`,
  Newtonian) are **unmodified**; only the Solar System's *center-of-mass* galactic orbit (`g ~ a₀`) feels MOND. There
  is **no internal EFE quadrupole** of the kind Desmond+2024/2026 constrains at 3–15σ — that quadrupole is a
  *modified-gravity* artifact (the nonlinear Poisson equation coupling internal to external field). Modified inertia
  has no such coupling. **The framework's most pressing near-term exposure is an artifact of the AeST stopgap, not of
  the idea.**
- **a₀(z) becomes automatic.** Time-nonlocality means the dynamics depend on the *cosmic history / background* — so an
  `a₀` that **tracks the cosmic state (e.g. `√ρ_DE`) is the *natural* expectation, not an imposed parameterization.**
  Independently, even a *modified-gravity* relativistic MOND yields a **time-varying a₀** once the static assumption is
  relaxed (Time evolution of local gravitational parameters in RMOND, *PRD* 107 044062, 2023). The distinctive claim
  is theoretically *motivated* in this class, not bolted on.

**One realization choice fixes both of the framework's problems at once.** That is not a coincidence — both problems
were *created* by using modified gravity (AeST), and both are *native-absent* in modified inertia.

## The honest catch — why the framework still leans on AeST

Modified inertia is the framework's natural home, but it is **not a finished house**:

- **No complete CMB-safe covariant modified-inertia theory exists.** Time-nonlocal theories are unwieldy (energy and
  momentum must be non-trivially redefined; predictions depend on the whole orbit), and a fully relativistic,
  CMB-passing modified-inertia MOND has **not** been constructed. AeST remains the *only* complete CMB-safe
  realization — and it is modified gravity. So the framework currently *needs* AeST for the CMB, and *inherits* its
  Cassini exposure, precisely because the better home is unbuilt.
- **It is, however, an active program** (so the door is open, not walled): local-Lagrangian modified-inertia attempts
  (arXiv:1904.07321), Machian / Sciama-inertia constructions conceptually close to the free-fall-clock idea
  (arXiv:2410.19007), and metric-only UV-vanishing IR deformations recovering AQUAL at low acceleration
  (arXiv:2601.04290, 2026). None yet does the CMB, but the direction is alive.

## The catch, sharpened — it is a Cassini / CMB / a₀(z) *trilemma*, and the CMB is the hard corner

*Self-correction: the section heading above said modified inertia "dissolves both exposures." That was too rosy — it
dissolves the Cassini and a₀(z) exposures but **buys a CMB exposure in return**, and that one is the deep obstruction.
The honest object is a trilemma, not a free lunch.* I checked **why** no CMB-safe modified inertia exists, rather
than just asserting it:

- **The CMB-epoch perturbations straddle a₀.** At recombination the peculiar gravitational accelerations
  `g = |∇Φ|c²` (with `Φ ~ 3×10⁻⁵`) run from `g ~ a₀` at low-ℓ (Sachs–Wolfe, ℓ≲30) to `g ~ 10–40 a₀` at the acoustic
  peaks (ℓ ~ 220–1000) — *spanning the MOND transition*. A **direct** inertia modification therefore changes the
  acoustic physics by `~a₀/g ~ 2–8%` at the peaks (above Planck's <1% precision) and by order-unity at low-ℓ. (Numbers
  reproduced inline; OOM, but the straddling is robust.)
- **No `Ȳ=0` escape is available to inertia.** AeST is CMB-safe because its *scalar field* has a kinetic term that
  vanishes on FRW (`Ȳ=0`), so the MOND piece is off at the background and the dark sector behaves as **dust** — the
  field does *double duty*: it both **supplies the CMB dark-matter mimic** (Ω≈0.265, the 3rd-peak height) and keeps
  the modification clean. That trick is intrinsically **modified-gravity** (it needs a field). A pure inertia
  modification has **no such field**, so it both (a) modifies the acoustic physics across the a₀-straddling scales and
  (b) supplies **no** dark-matter-like component for the peak heights. *That* is the real reason the house is unbuilt.

**The trilemma, stated cleanly** — of {**Cassini-safe**, **a₀(z)-natural**, **CMB-safe**}, each known realization gets
a different subset:

| realization | Cassini-safe | a₀(z) natural | CMB-safe |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Modified inertia** (the framework's concept) | ✓ (COM accel.) | ✓ (time-nonlocal) | ✗ (acoustic mod + no DM-mimic) |
| **AeST** (modified gravity) | ✗ (3–15σ quadrupole) | ~ (parameterized via K(𝒬)) | ✓ (Ȳ=0 + dust mode) |

No known theory holds all three. **Fairness both ways:** the modified-inertia CMB failure is *not proven fatal* — the
~few% peak shift might be partly absorbed by re-fitting parameters, and a small *real* component (e.g. light sterile
neutrinos) could supply the peak heights while inertia does galaxies. But nobody has shown it works, and it has the
two concrete obstructions above. So: **unsolved and obstructed, not merely unbuilt.**

## Disposition — the real theoretical frontier, restated

This **supersedes** `THE_NEXT_CALCULATION_aest_quasistatic.md` as the *deeper* target. Computing AeST's quasi-static
`μ(x)` is still the right *near-term* test (it scores the realization the framework actually uses against Cassini and
high-z). But the genuine resolution of *both* exposures is not a better `β₀` inside AeST — it is:

> **A covariant, CMB-safe modified-inertia theory that realizes `a₀ = c²/(2R*)` (the free-fall clock) directly.**
> In it, Cassini is native-absent and `a₀(z) ∝ √ρ_DE` is the natural background-tracking, not a posit.

That is a hard, field-wide open problem — but it is the *correct* problem, and it is the one the framework's own
concept points at. The honest one-line standing, updated:

- **Not refuted; carries MOND's shared tensions; distinctive a₀(z) undecided-leaning-unfavorable, hostage to DESI.**
- **Its two framework-specific exposures (Cassini, realization-of-a₀(z)) are symptoms of the AeST stopgap and are
  native-absent in the framework's natural class (modified inertia) — but modified inertia trades them for a CMB
  exposure (the trilemma above).** The deepest honest statement of the program is therefore: *the idea wants to be
  modified inertia, where Cassini and a₀(z) come for free; the unsolved price is CMB-safety (no Ȳ=0 trick, no
  DM-mimic, an a₀-straddling acoustic modification); AeST pays that price but only by being modified gravity, which
  re-incurs Cassini. Getting all three — Cassini-safe, CMB-safe, a₀(z)-natural — is the genuine open problem, and the
  verdict on the distinctive claim belongs to DESI + z~3.*
