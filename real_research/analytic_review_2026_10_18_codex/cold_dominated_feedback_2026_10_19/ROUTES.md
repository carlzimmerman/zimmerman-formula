The direct sign/energy Neumann route at large mass was not revived: the actual
field matrix is indefinite and the equilibrium susceptibility is order one.
The successful Neumann estimate here instead uses high frequency. Radial
endpoint ODE uniqueness was also not asserted: eliminating the kinetic mode
leaves orbital integrals, rather than a pointwise local radial ODE.

The falsification route for exceptional prescribed DFs is open. A prescribed local circular response with
nonzero force trace is energy integrable and has unbounded receiving quotient,
but is not a self-consistent eigenmode. The next decisive test must use the
actual homogeneous integral equation. The generic route in
`GENERIC_FORCE_CANCELLATION.md` now supplies an explicit transverse variation
and a finite-nullity argument. It does not certify a particular old DF merely
because the good class is open dense.
