This is a substantive first-response result, not a spectral or nonlinear closure. Full feedback depends on the fixed equilibrium F_E and is order one in the perturbation amplitude. Initial compact action support is not an invariant response subspace. Feedback on the whole occupied set, the circular/flat boundaries, and the nonlinear label gradients remain outside the proved estimate.
