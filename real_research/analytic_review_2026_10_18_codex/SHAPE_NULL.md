# Calibration elimination for two candidate rotation laws

18 October 2026. Candidate test ZAR-D1. This is an analytic proposal before any new observational statistic was evaluated. It is not a registered empirical verdict, and it changes no existing frozen test.

## Claim and hypotheses

For one galaxy at radii i, write

\[
q_i=v_{{\rm los},i}^2/\theta_i,\quad
g_i=Qq_i,\quad Q=(D\sin^2\iota)^{-1}>0,\quad
b_i=\lambda h_i,\quad h_i>0,\quad \lambda>0.
\]

Here theta is angular radius in radians, iota is a single disk inclination, v_los is the circular line-of-sight rotation amplitude after systemic subtraction, b is Newtonian baryonic acceleration, and h is a fixed gas-acceleration shape template. Its geometry is held fixed. The relation b = lambda h is exact for gas-only baryons with a common conversion factor, or another exactly fixed-composition template. It is not exact for a merely gas-dominated galaxy with a radially varying stellar contribution.

Assume a radius-independent Q, lambda and positive a0, and an operative **algebraic radial law**. Such a rule is not automatically the disk solution of a nonlinear field action; field geometry/curl and finite-stiffness corrections need their own bounds.

Define z_i = q_i^2/h_i. Three distinct positive h values provide the nondegenerate case.

## Elimination

The simple action law is

\[
g^2-bg-a_0b=0.
\]

Substitution gives

\[
z_i=\frac\lambda Q q_i+\frac{a_0\lambda}{Q^2},
\qquad D_s=\det\begin{pmatrix}
z_1&q_1&1\\z_2&q_2&1\\z_3&q_3&1
\end{pmatrix}=0.
\]

The quadratic a0-line is

\[
g^2-b^2-a_0b=0,
\]

and instead gives

\[
z_i=\frac{\lambda^2}{Q^2}h_i+\frac{a_0\lambda}{Q^2},
\qquad D_q=\det\begin{pmatrix}
z_1&h_1&1\\z_2&h_2&1\\z_3&h_3&1
\end{pmatrix}=0.
\]

The zero conditions are unchanged by common positive rescalings of q or h. Distance, the common velocity inclination normalization, a0, and the common gas conversion do not have to be fitted to test these zero conditions. Inclination's separate effect on the gas template, or a warp changing inclination with radius, does not disappear.

A zero determinant is necessary, not sufficient by itself: both slope and intercept must be positive to represent the stated positive parameters. Given any prescribed positive a0, any positive slope and intercept admit positive Q and lambda under either law. Thus the positive-line condition is sufficient for the abstract calibration-free law, but can still fail independent distance or calibration bounds. The elimination intentionally discards that amplitude information.

## Exact separation and a hand example

For the simple law,

\[
q(h)=\frac{\lambda h+\sqrt{\lambda^2h^2+4a_0\lambda h}}{2Q}.
\]

The square-root term has strictly negative second derivative for h > 0 and a0 > 0. The quadratic law similarly gives q(h) = sqrt(lambda^2 h^2 + a0 lambda h)/Q, which is strictly concave. If both determinants vanished for three distinct h values generated by one of these laws, then its three q values would have to be affine in h, contradicting strict concavity. This proves exact separation in principle, not statistical power.

For a rational hand example take Q = lambda = a0 = 1 and simple-law q values 1, 2, 3. Then h = q^2/(q+1) has values 1/2, 4/3, 9/4 and z = 2, 3, 4. The simple determinant is zero. The quadratic determinant is

\[
(3-2)(9/4-1/2)-(4-2)(4/3-1/2)=1/12\ne0.
\]

This is exact arithmetic on manufactured values, not an observational result or a computational experiment.

## Identifiability and errors

Both laws approach g^2 = a0 b in the deep limit, so z becomes nearly constant. In the Newtonian limit both approach g = b. Practical separation then becomes poor despite strict finite-a0 curvature. Gas-dominated dwarfs may have excellent baryon calibration and insufficient transition leverage at the same time.

The existing [k01 shape estimator](../../hunt_2026/k01_shape_only_estimators.py) and [its output](../../hunt_2026/k01_shape_only_estimators.out) already identify the deep-limit loss of a0 information. [k02](../../hunt_2026/k02_rar_slope_law.py) already studies slope information. The old [a0-estimator audit](../reviews/mi_routeA_a0_estimator_invariance_2026.py) calls its quadratic construction kernel-assuming. Generic shape testing is therefore prior work. The exact nuisance-elimination presentation here was not found in the targeted search; that is not a global novelty result.

An empirical implementation must propagate correlated errors: z and q share the same velocity measurement, and gas templates and neighboring velocity bins can be correlated. Independent vertical and horizontal error bars would be wrong. Tabulated errV values alone do not supply the full radial covariance. Stellar contamination, beam smearing, noncircular motion, warps and geometry need an envelope. Select radii or weighting rules before examining law residuals, then assess whether the data have enough power before assigning a rejection threshold.

For positive nonzero spans, det[z,x,1]/[(max z-min z)(max x-min x)] is dimensionless and scale invariant, where x = q or h. It becomes ill-conditioned as either span vanishes and is not a substitute for an observational covariance or a preregistration.

## Existing data and pipeline provenance

**SPARC.** The [source catalogue](../data/SPARC_Lelli2016c.mrt) and [data correction note](../data/SPARC_table.README.md) identify the valid table and 175 rotmod files. The file named SPARC_table.txt is preserved failed-download HTML, not data; the note records that no committed result parsed it. Rotmod velocities are reduced circular-curve estimates; component velocities are Newtonian templates, with signed V|V| conventions where appropriate. These avoid an imported halo profile, but are not detector-level spectra or images. The [primary SPARC paper](https://astroweb.case.edu/ssm/papers/AJv152n157.pdf) and [CDS definitions](https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/viz-bin/ReadMe/J/AJ/152/157?format=html&tex=true) describe the reduction and assumed distances/inclinations.

**Gaia wide binaries.** The [stage-G builder](../../prep_2026/gaia_dr4_prep/catalog_builder/build_catalog.py), lines 311-323 at the pinned baseline, computes distance as the average of two inverse parallaxes, separation using the first parallax, photometric masses, and a direct proper-motion-vector difference. There is no explicit perspective correction in that estimator. The [frozen source disclosure](../../campaign_fresh_gravity/CFG816_dr4_r_statistic_scoring/FROZEN_CRITERIA.md), lines 14-16, describes Cookson's inverse-variance common distance, perspective corrections, and FLAME-or-photometric masses. The converter preserves stage G, so these are different estimators. This is an audit issue; it has not been shown here to change a scientific verdict.

The [pair-table manifest](../../campaign_fresh_gravity/CFG816_dr4_r_statistic_scoring/pairs_dr3.csv.manifest.json) and [README](../../campaign_fresh_gravity/CFG816_dr4_r_statistic_scoring/README.md) record 175 outer and 173 inner pairs, NSS-clean inputs, and a maximum separation of 29.59 kAU. The NSS branch cannot test extra screening on these inputs, and the 50-kAU cap is inactive. The registered DR3 R statistic remains unrun in the inspected status; it is a code-path test when executed, not new validation data. Counts already disclosed by the converter are not an R measurement.

**KiDS cosmic shear.** The [CFG592 loader](../../campaign_fresh_gravity/CFG592_cosmic_shear_likelihood/cfg592_native.py), lines 94-102, loads released xi-plus/minus, covariance and n(z), not raw detector images. [CFG596 criteria](../../campaign_fresh_gravity/CFG596_lean_pm_1024/FROZEN_CRITERIA.md), section 5, explicitly retain an EH initial spectrum at sigma8 = 0.811, a Planck-like flat w = -1 background, a LambdaCDM turnaround shell table, survey nuisance priors, and massless neutrinos. Removing HMcode and BAHAMAS does not remove those inputs. README line 139's broad claim of no LambdaCDM-calibrated machinery should be read with that explicit list. The stored canonical exclusion remains an exclusion of the tested prescription under those assumptions.

The useful data-first standard is to fit calibrated observable space with the candidate's own response and selection model, and disclose every calibration or physical prior. Detector data also require an instrument model; assumption-free astrophysical inference is not available.

## Outcome

The determinant identities are proved under the stated restrictions and independently rederived. No SPARC data value was used to select radii or evaluate a determinant. No significance, preferred kernel, normalization measurement, new frozen threshold, or validated observational result is claimed. The next decision is whether suitable data and covariance exist, not whether to launch another parameter scan.
