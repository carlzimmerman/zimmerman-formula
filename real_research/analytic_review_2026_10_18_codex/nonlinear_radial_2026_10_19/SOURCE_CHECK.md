# Adjacent nonlinear stability theorem

Checked 19 October 2026: Mohammed Lemou, Florian Mehats and Pierre Raphael, *A new variational approach to the stability of gravitational systems*, arXiv:0904.2443v2, dated 5 March 2010 in the PDF header. [Versioned primary source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0904.2443v2).

Theorems 1.3, 1.4 and 1.5 respectively establish a local constrained energy minimum, compactness of suitable minimizing sequences and nonlinear radial stability for the paper's gravitational Vlasov-Poisson system. Its equilibrium assumptions include compact support, negative energy cap and decreasing energy dependence. The theorem does not directly cover the present two response laws, positive point baryon mass and cubic interaction. Direct application here is therefore inapplicable.

This is an adjacent result only. Angular momentum squared translates to this checkpoint's L; the one-species Poisson field does not translate to the full coupled force matrix. The present inverse-entropy, compactness, cubic-remainder and first-exit steps are derived separately.

The primary PDF header, theorem statements and hypotheses were inspected. No full text was retained. This bounded check establishes neither global novelty nor a general transfer theorem.
