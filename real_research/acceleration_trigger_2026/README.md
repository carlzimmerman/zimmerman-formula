# acceleration_trigger_2026: a carrier that converts where the MOND kernel's own argument is large

Every carrier window on the record failed at high redshift. GP5 and L356: with the carrier still inside galaxies at z ≈ 2.5, the flagship deep-MOND Tully–Fisher zero point moves +0.82..+1.05 dex (framework 0.00, ΛCDM +0.33). L357's density trigger clears galaxies, but as a local trigger it fires in every halo down to 1e8 M☉. The vacuum gate that rescued it keeps z ≥ 2 halos intact, which is GP5's failure again.

These lanes build a trigger keyed on the kernel's own variable. The carrier converts where the bound region's baryonic acceleration y_b = |∇w|/a0 (L361) exceeds a threshold y_v. The conversion is fast, so it happens the first time an orbit enters r_v, and in steady state it empties the whole loss cone. Baryons reach y_v only where they have condensed, so the kernel's argument supplies the mass selection with no resolution scale. y_v = 1, the kernel's own Newtonian/MOND transition, adds no constant.

As on the rest of the record, this is a **construction**: the conversion rule is put in by hand. The carrier is a state of the framework's own field, not a new particle species, and its **mass is still required**.

**Standing (AT1–AT3).**

- **What it fixes.** The acceleration trigger is the first carrier on the record to keep the flat-a₀ flagship at every redshift (z = 0.5–2.5). It is mass-selective by the kernel's own variable, with no resolution scale.
- **The forest.** Its high-z clearing costs little on the forest's own flux (≤ 1.7%). L319's matter proxy, which charges every such clearing, is the wrong yardstick for it (AT2).
- **Every other gate.** With L372's uniform mode, it passes every gate on the record except cosmic shear, on the alternative set, at the program's common kernel cell (AT3). It is the first construction to do so with the high-z gates included.
- **What it does not fix.** Cosmic shear, at the common cell: the trigger clears galaxies' inner regions, not the group-scale power cosmic shear wants handed over by z ≈ 0.5.
- **The two levers pull against each other.** A faster kick buys cosmic shear and costs Harvey.
- **Open.**

  - The switch-cell scan with an intermediate kick (the next lane).
  - L361's region-kernel liabilities flagged by the parallel cross-thread review (XR4): the Local Group's zero-velocity radius (+0.13 to +0.23 dex), the 09-03 EFE samples, and gas in active filaments at z ≲ 0.5.
  - The σ = 1 edge layer's lensing, which is unscored (the chk_v0_2026 README records it).
- **It is a construction.** The trigger, its threshold and the kick are chosen, not derived. The carrier is a state of the framework's own field, and its mass is still required.
