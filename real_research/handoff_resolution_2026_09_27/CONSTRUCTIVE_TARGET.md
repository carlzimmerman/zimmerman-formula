# Reduced constructive target after the handoff swings

This is a target with derived local consistency conditions, not a completed theory. It preserves kappa=1/2 as the only accepted fit of the acceleration scale. It does not hide the remaining cold abundance, matter-mass scale, stellar calibration, scope and formation inputs by changing their labels.

## Interior and exterior must be specified together

For the CFG2 point-baryon hydrostatic postulate, the interior P2 field follows conditionally:

    M_total(r)^2 = M_b^2 + a0 M_b r^2/G.

A positive-density finite halo can stop at r_e only if its previously enclosed mass remains in the exterior Poisson field. In spherical symmetry, outside that edge:

    g(r) = G [M_b + M_dark(r_e)]/r^2.

Turning the entire extra force off outside r_e is a different construction: a compensating negative surface contribution removes the interior effective mass. These two edge definitions must not be mixed in lensing, budget and turnaround calculations.

The finite-density construction has the pressure match

    P(r) = a0 M_b/(8 pi) [r^(-2)-r_e^(-2)] + P_external.

It establishes an interior equilibrium for a supplied edge, not an equation selecting that edge. A constant pressure offset is not automatically an innocuous term in a relativistic matter action. The full stress tensor and boundary variation have to produce it. Matching the unshifted law directly to the vacuum pressure selects r_e=sqrt(G M_b/a0), which is too small for the proposed lensing edge; simply choosing a smaller external pressure reintroduces a boundary input.

## Use the cold budget once

The cosmological cold component and the halo's effective dark mass cannot both be added when they are claimed to be the same field. The completed source-gate CMASS check demonstrates the size of that bookkeeping problem. A tested conditional repair is to replace the existing carrier profile by

    M_dark,new(<r) = min[M_phantom(<r), M_available,cold].

It preserves the supplied total cold budget and the exterior mass, and gives a concrete profile to test. It remains an effective rule: a mass history, transport equation and positive phase-space/stress realization must explain the redistribution. The rule does not derive the cosmological amount or the galaxy stellar-to-host mapping. It should be checked jointly against the galaxy RAR, CMASS, isolated-galaxy lensing and cluster profiles; an improvement on one radial subset is not a likelihood pass.

## A frame prescription is an action term, not a coordinate slogan

A region-constant auxiliary vector can be varied to cancel a uniform field. Its stationary equation is a nonlinear weighted average, generally different from the material center-of-mass acceleration. A density tide remains; at large radii the isolated-field approximation fails. Region labels and boundaries are dynamical data unless supplied by the varied action. Varying a potential at a fixed, externally supplied mask omits the fluid and moving-boundary equations.

## Kinetic repair must include the metric

The smooth saturated gate's negative kinetic curvature has a precise frozen-coefficient repair by an added shear term. That partial success does not establish a viable action: the covariant local shear term also changes the tensor kinetic coefficient and hence the gravitational-wave speed. Any compensator must therefore be checked after eliminating all matter/metric constraints and with the same static and cosmological equations used in the observational tests. A term whose background value vanishes can still alter perturbations.

## Exact next common calculation

The surviving target needs one evolved dark-field/region system that outputs, rather than fits, all three of:

1. The top-level host membership, pre-turnaround seed mass and formation history.
2. The density edge and its pressure/phase-space matching, with conserved exterior mass and one global cold budget.
3. A stable metric-plus-matter kinetic operator whose tensor propagation and full tidal response satisfy the measured gates.

The new local lemmas and experiments supply constraints and component constructions for these arrows. None supplies their conjunction. A source-gated finite-budget halo with a history-dependent edge is the concrete surviving effective family. Calling its edge “splashback” or its source “free-falling” does not remove those missing equations.

The conditional Moster-mapped cap-only test now retains the SPARC fit but fails KiDS.
