# Two bounded source checks

Checked 2026-09-27, after the internal constructions were derived. These are attribution/application checks, not a novelty search and not imported bases for the fresh campaign.

## Splashback

Primary source: [Splashback in accreting dark matter halos, arXiv:1409.4482v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1409.4482), 2014-09-16. The returned PDF's first page explicitly labels v1. Section II, equations (1)–(2), and Figure 2 were inspected.

The source evolves a spherical shell in a prescribed growing halo, with M proportional to a^s. It predicts splashback as a function of accretion rate and cosmological epoch. Its absence of fitted coefficients does not make the accretion history an output of the acceleration-scale relation. The source does not derive a universal x_e in [0.31,0.48] in units of each system's turnaround radius, nor a regulated dark-field edge for this project.

Dictionary: source s is d ln M/d ln a; its first post-infall apocentre is the proposed physical edge; its enclosed overdensity is relative to mean matter density, not the critical density. The source's numerical profile prescription and the project's CFG4 identity rule are different constructions. Classification: adjacent known mechanism; project transfer remains conditional on an actual mass-accretion history and coupled evolution.

## Dark-energy scaling attribution

Primary source: [The Redshift Evolution of the Tully-Fisher Relation as a Test of Modified Gravity, arXiv:0809.2790v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/0809.2790), 2008-09-17. The primary record explicitly treats a MOND scale tied to the square root of the dark-energy density and compares its redshift evolution with a Hubble-scale alternative. Thus the scaling itself predates this campaign. This attribution supports the handoff's credit correction; it does not validate the present coefficient, DESI-era extrapolations, or any covariant action.
