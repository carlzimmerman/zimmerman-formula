built on the ONE surviving result — a₀ = (c/2)√(Gρ) = cH/Z, evolving as E(z), read as the surface

## Division I — First principles / the engine (why MOND, why this sign)

**1. 🔴 Derive the deep-MOND *sign* from a defensible entropy correction.** The central open theory
problem. Temperature-modified Clausius gives anti-MOND; the entropy/DOF route gives MOND but the
correction is posited. *Deliverable:* a non-area horizon entropy, defensible from QG, that yields the
deep-MOND sign — or a proof it requires contested input. This is the prize.

**2. 🔴 Derive the AeST cosmological function 𝒦(𝒬) from the horizon foundation.** The clean-slate field
theory forced ~80% of AeST; 𝒦(𝒬) (the CDM-like background) is the residual. *Deliverable:* a forced form
of 𝒦(𝒬) or a precise statement of the extra input it needs.

**3. 🟡 Settle *apparent vs event horizon* theoretically.** Rigorous FRW apparent-horizon thermodynamics
(Cai–Kim, Padmanabhan) to determine whether a₀ ∝ H(z) (rises) or ∝ √Λ (constant) is forced. *Deliverable:*
a principled argument for which horizon sets the IR scale — the theoretical half of the z~3 test.

**4. 🔴 The DSSYK / de Sitter-complexity sign.** Formulate a baryonic probe in the solvable static-patch
dual and compute whether the response enhances at low acceleration (the second-law-of-complexity route).
*Deliverable:* the sign of the complexity-sourced force in a model under control. Frontier, low odds, high payoff.

**5. 🟡 A complete, ghost-free action for modified inertia — or a no-go.** The Unruh route fits SPARC but
is non-local with no action (closed-orbit/Ostrogradski). *Deliverable:* a consistent action, or a theorem
that none exists (which would force the modified-gravity reading).

data under controlled systematics, (ii) stresses the foundation (is MOND real?), (iii) sharpens the one
distinctive bet (apparent vs event horizon), or (iv) attacks the one open theory problem (the deep-MOND
sign). Nothing here derives the Standard Model from a number — that road is closed, and keeping it closed
is what makes the rest credible.
