  one of the ten that targets the κ-forcing wall at its exact opening (the close was continuous-only;
  topological forcing was explicitly NOT ruled out, and the close doc names "fix N by a fixed-point BC /

  route because the rest of the coefficient (√(8π/3)) is already forced — only the clean rational ½ needs

  route on the dS horizon, not a microscopic dof count.

**Net:** chase **#2 (topological κ via an η-invariant / horizon-level term)** as the headline next theory
move, with **#1 (f(Q)→K(Q))** as the tractable parallel calculation on GAP-1. Everything else is mapped,
walled, and quarantine-held. No standing flips either way.
