## Work worth doing next

1. **D06 geometry:** The [new calculation](LYALPHA_SHAPE_RESULT.md) finds a small but concrete conditional shape mismatch between the DESI Lyα distance ratio and the Unite central CPL background. Refit with *both* experiment posteriors and their overlapping BAO/SN inputs when products are public; do not add paper significances.
2. **D10/D20 plus D12 lensing and growth:** Derive `Φ(k,z)`, `Ψ(k,z)`, matter growth and the projected shear/CMB convergence spectra from the same candidate action. Then use the released DES Y6 and SPT covariance, with survey overlap modeled. D20's supernova-excluded split is a useful calibration check. This directly probes requirements 3 and 8.
3. **D04 Bullet image positions:** Compare the newly published 135 image constraints to the already analyzed 2026 mass ledgers. A distinct image-position likelihood is potentially useful for the no-slip/lensing requirement if the object catalog and lens-model posterior are accessible.
4. **D13/D14 tensor propagation:** Derive `c_T`, damping, polarization content and `d_L^GW/d_L^EM` from the action, then run GWTC-5 event likelihoods. Existing catalog limits cannot count tensor degrees of freedom for an unwritten action.

These opportunities remain conditional on an explicit common action. They are tests that can reject candidate theories, not evidence that the 13 requirements are already met. The density-tracking `a0(z)` branch is optional in the [spec](../../qwen_claude_field_theory/closure_2026/FRIED_CHICKEN_SPEC.md); a cosmological mismatch there alone does not rule out a constant-`a0` construction.
