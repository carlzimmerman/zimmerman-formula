# Academic papers using early or restricted 2025–26 data: usable findings

**Search date:** 2026-09-27. This follow-up looks beyond release announcements to primary analysis and validation papers by teams with collaboration or data-rights access. I searched the checkout for each exact paper ID and title terms before listing it; none of the seven exact papers below had a fit or standalone analysis script in the repo before this folder. That is a scoped repository finding, not a global novelty claim. These papers use overlapping observations where noted. The [one-action target](../../qwen_claude_field_theory/closure_2026/FRIED_CHICKEN_SPEC.md) remains incomplete, so no published ΛCDM or generic modified-gravity fit is transferred to it.

| Paper and access | Specific finding that changes the research plan | What can be used now, and what remains gated |
|---|---|---|
| **SPT-3G × DES Y3 polarization-only cross-lensing**, [Ouellette et al., 2026-06-24](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.26223v2); SPT collaboration used D1 lensing maps before the main D1 lensing paper. | A CMB-lensing × galaxy-shear detection at about `14σ` over `~1300 deg²` survives the polarization-only reconstruction. Its [Table 4](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.26223v2) reports amplitude relative to Planck ΛCDM of `1.02±0.073` (Pol) versus `0.86±0.060` (raw temperature-inclusive GMV); simulations identify foreground bias in raw GMV. **Gem:** use Pol or foreground-mitigated spectra as the primary cross-lensing gate; an apparent low lensing amplitude from raw GMV is not clean evidence for the theory. [This folder's quantitative screen](SPT_POL_SCREEN.md) shows why the line-of-sight weighting matters. | Published amplitude and `S8=0.833(+0.047,-0.061)` are summary screens. The paper shows bandpowers but I did not find a public numerical cross-spectrum covariance link in it. The main [SPT D1 auto-lensing likelihood](https://pole.uchicago.edu/public/data/omori26/likelihoods/overview.html) is public; its [map documentation](https://pole.uchicago.edu/public/data/omori26/maps/overview.html) is still a placeholder. Auto and cross spectra share SPT maps, so do not combine as independent. An action-level prediction needs both metric potentials and growth. |
| **DESI DR2 Lyα three-bin BAO**, [Herrera-Alcantar et al., 2026-07-21](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.19619v2); the authors analyzed unreleased DR2 forest correlations. | Three redshift bins (`2.13, 2.40, 2.81`) give a directly measured expansion slope `H∝(1+z)^n`, `n=1.34±0.16`. **Gem:** the slope cancels both `H0` and sound-horizon scale, probing the action's high-z FLRW solution with fewer calibration assumptions. The [bounded calculation](LYALPHA_SHAPE_RESULT.md) gives endpoint secants `n=1.432` for Unite's central CPL point and `1.420` for same-Ωm ΛCDM; current slope precision does not separate them. | Paper-level distance points and fit are public; the [data availability statement](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.19619v2) says underlying data will arrive with DESI DR2. This analysis shares DR2 Lyα observations with D06, so it is a different summary of the same field, not independent evidence. A full fit requires the three-bin covariance and overlap with the one-bin AP likelihood. |
| **DESI DR2 Lyα full-shape validation**, [Herbold et al., 2026-07-29](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27411v2); validation completed before unblinding. | Hundreds of mocks and data splits support the BAO/AP inference, but show a significant bias in the inferred `fσ8`; the collaboration **excluded that growth measurement**. **Gem:** do not treat a DR2 Lyα growth-rate number from the same pipeline as a new test of the theory. The geometry is the usable observable here. | The paper provides the validation and its limitations; underlying forest data are pending DR2. Any future growth test needs a revised, mock-validated estimator. |
| **DES Year 6 galaxy–galaxy lensing**, [Giannini et al., 2026-01-21](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.15175v1); internal Y6 analysis preceded full community use. | A `4031 deg²` tangential-shear data vector has total signal/noise `173`, falling to `75` after cosmology scale cuts. The fiducial analysis **excludes lens bin 2** because of internal tension and unexplained redshift-calibration behavior, before unblinding. The paper also presents high-S/N small-scale *shear ratios*, largely geometric in selected lens/source combinations. **Gem:** lock the published bin mask and use the ratio as a geometry/calibration control before reading lensing residuals as altered gravity. | [DES Y6 products](https://des.ncsa.illinois.edu/releases/y6a2/) are reported public. The paper says the selected geometric ratios are within `2.5σ` of the fiducial calibration prediction; this is not a discovery claim. A theory fit still needs the Y6 likelihood, nuisance model and action-derived lensing kernels. It reuses D10, so no independent Y6 count. |
| **GWTC-5 tests of GR**, [LVK, 2026-07-21](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19293); O4b collaboration analysis. | Tests using a cumulative `168` confident events report no strong evidence for extra polarizations and improved parameterized inspiral constraints. **Gem:** the [released polarization and waveform-test products](https://dcc.ligo.org/LIGO-P2600410/public) can become an explicit tensor-sector likelihood, stronger than a generic “GWTC-5 exists” note. | Public test products and [event strain](https://gwosc.org/GWTC-5.0/) are available. They reuse D13/D14 events. Lack of detected extra polarizations does **not** prove the action propagates exactly two gravitational degrees of freedom; that must be derived from its constraint algebra. |
| **FRB baryon-feedback calibration**, [Sharma et al., Nature Astronomy 2026-09-08](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41550-026-02957-9), [authors' public data/code](https://github.com/krittisharma/dmz_spk_sharma2026). This is an **adjacent independent academic dataset**, not restricted early access to DES/SPT. | Version of record uses `114` FRBs to constrain diffuse baryons, gas fraction in groups/clusters and feedback-induced matter-power suppression at `k≈0.1–3 h/Mpc`. **Gem:** FRB dispersion can constrain the baryonic-feedback nuisance that otherwise mimics changes in DES/SPT lensing power. The [catalog audit](FRB_SAMPLE_AUDIT.md) found the crucial `92 yes + 22 unclear` selection split. | The authors provide an [FRB sample CSV](https://github.com/krittisharma/dmz_spk_sharma2026/blob/main/data/frb_results/frb_sample.csv) and reproduction package. The older [arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17162v1) describes `109` FRBs; use the `114`-FRB extended version of record/repo and record a data commit before calculating. This is an astrophysical control, not an action-level gravity measurement by itself. |
| **Rubin DP2 Virgo low-surface-brightness sample**, [Le et al., 2026-09-16](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19246v2); authors used data-rights DP2 before open science release. | Seven “almost-dark” Virgo-region galaxies with reported stellar masses `10^6–10^7 M☉` define a possible low-baryon-mass target list. **Gem:** identify objects for future kinematic/lensing follow-up rather than assume existing galaxy samples cover this regime. | [Rubin DP2 access](https://dp2.lsst.io/) is restricted to data-rights holders. The paper supplies photometry, not rotation curves or dynamical masses, so it cannot currently decide a low-acceleration law. |

## Access and inference checks

- [Unite v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05053v2) still promises its Hubble diagram and likelihood **upon acceptance**. I found no independent full-likelihood paper using those unreleased Unite products. Its central CPL values can be used only in a labeled conditional calculation, as in [this folder](LYALPHA_SHAPE_RESULT.md).
- The [DESI paper index](https://data.desi.lbl.gov/doc/papers/) still says DR2 object spectra/redshifts are not public. Academic DR2 preprints supply published summaries, validation and model choices; they do not grant access to raw DR2 data or make separate DR2 papers statistically independent.
- [Euclid DR1's official schedule](https://euclid.caltech.edu/page/euclid-dr1) starts after this 2026-09-27 cutoff. I did not find a verifiable pre-release DR1 cosmic-shear likelihood paper. Euclid early-release cluster papers use earlier ERO data and therefore do not establish access to the forthcoming DR1 cosmology data.
- The high-value joint mathematical target from these papers is `C_ell^(κ_CMB γ)` alongside `C_ell^(κ_CMB κ_CMB)` and DES shear, with the same action's `Φ+Ψ` and growth transfer functions. The foreground-controlled Pol cross-spectrum, Y6 bin mask, FRB feedback information and covariance from overlapping maps must be part of that comparison. A numerical ΛCDM-amplitude substitution would not test the specified one-action theory.
