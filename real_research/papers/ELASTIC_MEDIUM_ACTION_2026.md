### 1. The construction

**Kinematics (standard relativistic elasticity).** Three scalars $\varphi^a(x)$ label the medium's elements; the internal metric is $B^{ab} = g^{\mu\nu}\partial_\mu\varphi^a\partial_\nu\varphi^b$. The number density is $n = n_0\sqrt{\det B^{ab}}$ (with $n_0$ the relaxed density), the medium four-velocity $u^\mu$ is the unique timelike direction with $u^\mu\partial_\mu\varphi^a = 0$, the **volumetric strain** is $J = -\tfrac12\ln\!\left(\det B/\det \bar B\right)$ relative to the relaxed configuration $\bar B$, and the **shear invariant** is $\Sigma = \tfrac12\!\left[\mathrm{tr}\,\tilde B - 3\right]$ built from the unimodular part $\tilde B^{ab} = B^{ab}/(\det B)^{1/3}$ (for small strains, $\Sigma \to \tfrac12 e_{ij}e^{ij}$, the squared deviatoric strain — the $J_2$ of the campaign lemmas). Internal $SO(3)$ symmetry among the $\varphi^a$ enforces isotropy; the two invariants $(J,\Sigma)$ are the *only* independent scalars to this order — the two-invariant structure that decouples the monopole (bulk, $\ell=0$) from the quadrupole (shear, $\ell=2$), which the campaign proved scalar AQUAL cannot do.

**Dynamics.** $S_{\mathrm{med}} = -\int\sqrt{-g}\;\rho_\Lambda c^2\,\mathcal{E}(J,\Sigma)$ with $\mathcal{E} = 1 + [V(J) + \mu_s\Sigma]/\rho_\Lambda c^2$ and:

- **The relaxed point is the dark energy.** $V(0)=0$, $\Sigma=0$: the medium contributes $T_{\mu\nu} = -\rho_\Lambda c^2 g_{\mu\nu}$ — an equation-of-state $w_{\mathrm{eos}}=-1$ fluid. The medium does not *add* a dark sector; it *is* the dark energy, deformed.
- **The volumetric sector carries the MOND response.** For $0 < J < J_c$ the potential has the scale-free branch $V'(J) = 2K_{\mathrm{eff}}\sqrt{J\,/\,\kappa^2}$-normalized such that the static response to a baryonic source reproduces the displacement law $g_D = \sqrt{a_{0V} g_{\mathrm{bar}}/6}$, with the total deep stiffness (volumetric plus the shear share fixed by the compatibility lemma $J_2 = J_1^2/6$) equal to $K_{\mathrm{eff}} = a_0^2/16\pi G = 2.6\times10^{-12}$ Pa. This is the $\sqrt{Z/6}=0.982$ match, now a property of $V$.
- **The budget boundary.** The domain of $V$ ends at $J_c$ (equivalently the response terminates at $y_c = Z/2$): implemented as the constrained sector $J \le J_c$ with multiplier — *not* as a feature of $V$ itself, honoring the impossibility theorem that no single-valued constitutive law can carry the tail. The exact refusal $\rho_\Lambda c^2/K_{\mathrm{eff}} = 6Z^2$ caps $v_T \le 0.17c$ if the deep amplitude is to survive — the medium's shear waves are slow material sound. **Couplings.** Baryons couple to $g_{\mu\nu}$ only; photons couple to $g_{\mu\nu}$ only; the medium couples to $g_{\mu\nu}$ only. One metric.
