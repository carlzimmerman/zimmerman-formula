### 1. The construction

**Kinematics (standard relativistic elasticity).** Three scalars $\varphi^a(x)$ label the medium's elements; the internal metric is $B^{ab} = g^{\mu\nu}\partial_\mu\varphi^a\partial_\nu\varphi^b$. - **The relaxed point is the dark energy.** $V(0)=0$, $\Sigma=0$: the medium contributes $T_{\mu\nu} = -\rho_\Lambda c^2 g_{\mu\nu}$ — an equation-of-state $w_{\mathrm{eos}}=-1$ fluid. The medium does not *add* a dark sector; it *is* the dark energy, deformed.
