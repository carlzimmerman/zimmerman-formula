exceptional-Jordan-algebra fit to the six charged-fermion mass ratios. This is *active
progress on the same open problem* — but it is a **different mechanism** (J₃(𝕆_ℂ)
spectral invariants / E₈×ωE₈), not a dS-Unruh or a₀-linked one, and it does not derive

mass sector looks kernel-free). So: the *problem* is being worked, the framework's
*specific spine* (dS-Unruh → mass) is not advanced by anyone.

**On the dS↔flavor bridge — NO.** No one built a de-Sitter-gravity-to-flavor bridge.
The octonion programs are algebra→SM (no dS); Zlosnik's Spin(4) is gravity→dark-sector
(no flavor, no a₀); these lines did not converge in-window.

**On a₀'s value / the √(Λ/32π) coefficient — NO.** Nobody derived, constrained, or
even targeted the a₀ coefficient in the window.

**On the dS/Λ foundation — MOVEMENT, not for-or-against.** Lüst's DESI-portal paper
(2605.10476) keeps the constant-Λ-vs-evolving-DE question live, which is exactly the
axis the framework's a₀(z) hostage sits on — but it neither supports nor refutes the
declining-√ρ_DE reading.

**On modified-inertia theory — NO new theory** (Milgrom dormant since 2023 on MI);
only MI-*vs-data* tests advanced (Banik wide binaries — a both-ways item: titled "No
Evidence for MOND," it constrains the wide-binary regime the framework treats as
a₀-degenerate/below-floor, so it is *not* a clean kill of the framework's
modified-inertia EFE).

**Honest bottom line:** In the last 3 months, the only genuine forward motion on a
framework open problem is **Singh's Jordan-algebra mass-ratio fit (rival mechanism, not
the framework's)**. The framework's distinctive content — a₀=√(Λ/32π), the dS-Unruh-MI
spine, a dS↔flavor bridge — was **not advanced by anyone.**
