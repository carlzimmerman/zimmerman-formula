### 1. dS/CFT and the SO(4,1) conformal door — *the most TOE-flavored, and Milgrom pointed right at it*

conformal (quantum) field theory." SO(4,1) is *also* the conformal group of 3-space, and **dS/CFT** (Strominger

classical limit of a de Sitter CFT, with SO(4,1) as the bridge?** Genuinely untouched in the repo (the DSSYK work
is dS holography, but the *conformal-MOND ↔ dS-CFT* link is a different, deeper thread). Hard, contested — but it's
the single most "unification-shaped" door, and the symmetry match is a *theorem*, not a hope.

### 2. Causal-set theory and Sorkin's Λ — *the one approach that touches the keystone (the value of Λ)*
