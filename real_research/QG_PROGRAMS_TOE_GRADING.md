### 3A. CAUSAL SETS → the value of Λ (debt #3, the CC-problem door)

This is the framework's strongest connection to an *unpaid debt*, precisely because it is a **genuine, pre-registered, microphysically-grounded prediction**, not a rhyme.

This directly addresses the one debt the framework explicitly punts on (the value of Λ).
- **The sign problem (decisive caveat).** Das–Nasiri–Yazdi 2023 (arXiv:2304.03819, *Aspects of Everpresent Λ I*) state verbatim that Λ "fluctuates, in magnitude and in sign, over cosmic history" and "is likely to change sign roughly every Hubble timescale," with mean **assumed** zero (an assumption they concede "require[s] justification"). Confirmed current (2025): everpresent-Λ remains alive but goes negative every Hubble time. **The framework needs Λ > 0** (de Sitter) for a₀ = c²√(Λ/32π) to be real; a mean-zero sign-flipping Λ makes a₀ **imaginary for ~half of cosmic history** — structurally hostile to a₀~√Λ.

- **2024 phenomenology** (*Aspects II*, arXiv:2307.13743): marginally beats ΛCDM on SN Ia but "struggles to improve on ΛCDM at describing the CMB."

**Honest disposition:** worth the user's time **for the CC-problem door only** — as a genuine mechanism for the *scale/value* of Λ. It is **not** a lead toward the a₀↔Λ coefficient or the SM, and its sign behavior contradicts the bridge. The open question is narrow and real: *does a fixed-positive-mean variant exist?*
