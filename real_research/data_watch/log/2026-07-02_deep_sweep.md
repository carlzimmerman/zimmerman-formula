## B. High-τ deep-read verdict — arXiv:2606.30903 (Sullivan, de Belsunce & Ivanov, MIT-CTP/6048)

*The most consequential item this window: it targets both DESI-hostage fronts at once.*

### What the paper actually does
Extends the 2025 high-τ program (Sailer+ PRL 136.081002 [τ=0.090±0.012, 87 citations]; Jhaveri/Karwal/Hu PRD 112.043541 [τ=0.091±0.011, 69 citations]) to a **hand-set Gaussian prior τ=0.11±0.006**, replacing the Planck low-ℓ EE likelihood entirely. Crucially, τ=0.11 is **requirement-calibrated, not measured**: chosen via the Δτ≈ΔH₀/(33 km/s/Mpc) degeneracy to make Σm_ν positive and push CMB H₀ to the DESI full-shape anchor H₀=69.08±0.37 — which is Ivanov's own group's DESI DR1 reanalysis (self-anchoring caveat). Data: Planck PR3/CamSpec/WMAP9 variants + ACT DR6 + lensing + DESI DR2 BAO + DESI FS + Pantheon+/DES-Dovekie (Union3 excluded).

### Mechanism (verbatim chain, verified against full text)
With low-ℓ EE fixing τ≈0.06, A_s is pinned via A_s·e^(−2τ); explaining the observed lensing excess forces high Ω_m from the CMB, clashing with DESI's low Ω_m — w₀wₐ (and negative Σm_ν) are the pressure valves. Raising τ raises A_s instead: Ω_m falls 0.311→0.294, H₀ rises 67.66→68.89, and the CMB-predicted BAO ratios match DESI with zero fitting. Results at τ=0.11: w₀=−0.766, wₐ=−0.55 (<1σ from the cosmological constant, CMB+BAO); **Σm_ν=0.10⁺⁰·⁰⁴₋₀.₀₅ eV — first 2σ positive detection**; A_L anomaly resolved (1.180→0.95); H₀ tension 6σ→4.4σ ("substantial reduction, though not a resolution"); S8 unchanged.

### Why it is NOT in-hand (paper's own concessions + independent counterweights)
- **The prior contradicts every direct measurement.** My computation: 5.9σ vs SRoll2 (0.0627), 5.9σ vs Planck 2018 lowE (0.0544). No specific instrumental systematic is alleged. Independent counterweights published: elica all-satellite LFI+HFI+WMAP cross-spectra τ=0.0581⁺⁰·⁰⁰⁴⁸₋₀.₀₀₅₉ (arXiv:2603.22454, 6.4σ away, explicitly anti-high-τ); lowE-independent astrophysical τ=0.0552 (Kageura+ arXiv:2601.09644, 8.5σ, and finds a residual 2.4σ DESI tension "suggestive of physics beyond ΛCDM"); Lyα forest τ≈0.042 (arXiv:2510.00107). The friendliest exotic reionization model (Pop III.1 flash, arXiv:2606.19459 — the paper's own ref [6]) tops out at τ=0.087.
- **Their own free-τ fit undershoots the prior:** CMB(no lowE)+DESI-FS gives τ=0.092±0.013, "−1.5σ lower than our high-τ prior." The 0.11 is a what-if.
- **At the data-preferred τ=0.09, the dissolution does NOT happen** (paper's own words): ≈2σ w₀wₐ preference survives and there is no positive m_ν. Only the extra hand-push to 0.11 buys the full concordance.
- **The SNe leg is τ-blind:** even at τ=0.11, +Pantheon+ gives w₀=−0.893±0.051 (2.1σ from −1); +DES-Dovekie 2.4σ. High τ weakens the SNe-included DDE preference by >1σ; it does not zero it.
- **Ceiling reached:** τ=0.15 breaks DESI Ω_m agreement (>2σ); H₀ can't be pushed further.
- **Community standing:** 3 days old, 0 citations, no rebuttals yet; heavyweight draft feedback (Efstathiou, Ferraro, Hill, Kamionkowski, White) — the program is mainstream-hot, not fringe.

### Fair scoring (both-ways)
The paper legitimately proves the three tensions are **equivalent to a single ~6σ τ-anomaly** — one dial, three payoffs, plus A_L resolved. Genuinely elegant, with pre-registered falsifiers (CLASS σ_τ≈0.003 ground-based, nearest-term; LiteBIRD σ_τ≈0.002 ~2030s; Rubin/Roman SNe Ω_m). **It does not show the tensions are artifacts.** At today's data it is anomaly relocation, not resolution: τ=0.11 requires a universe ~half-ionized by z≈15–20 that no standard source population produces, sitting 5.7–8.5σ from three independent probe families.

### Per-front threat levels
- **a0(z) — MODERATE, mechanism-real, not in-hand.** Kills it: CLASS/LiteBIRD landing at τ≈0.10–0.11, or Rubin/Roman Ω_m converging to the BAO value. Saves it: τ confirmed ≈0.055–0.06 — where elica, Kageura, and Lyα already independently sit — which would re-certify the DESI evolving-DE signal as physical.
- **growing-ν — HIGHER than a0(z)'s, face this honestly.** The host anomaly is only 2–3σ and now has **TWO** published standard-physics escape hatches: (i) the τ-trade, and (ii) Wang & Shan 2606.27903's reionization-shape freedom, which relaxes Σm_ν<0.39 eV **while keeping low-ℓ EE** — a much cheaper escape than a 6σ amputation. Additionally, DESI's bispectrum full-shape (2606.23936) shifts Σm_ν to a POSITIVE peak 0.26±0.17 eV, and Ivanov's own FS reanalysis claims normal-hierarchy evidence — the FS side of the anomaly is already softer than the BAO-compressed headline. **A 2–3σ anomaly with two escape hatches is weak hostage collateral; do not treat it as load-bearing.**
