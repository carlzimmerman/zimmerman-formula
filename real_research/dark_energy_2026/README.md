(a₀ = κc√(Gρ_Λ), flat in time because the vacuum has w = −1 exactly), and — in the construction the record has
assembled — its share of the expansion, Ω_Λ(z) = 3Λc²/K² (a local scalar of the khronon's foliation), gates **where** the
MOND response acts:

**Correction (2026-09-26, late) — the cosmic-shear side of DE2/DE3/DE5 is not established.** Those lanes scored cosmic shear on L363's region kernel run on L364's 100 Mpc mock. That mock cannot grow a phantom-supported region around an isolated galaxy (MS3's U1, reproduced here on the upper-branch builder itself in DE5b), and it holds too few clusters (MS3's M1). On L363's resolution-free halo model the region kernel's lensing power is dominated by clusters and fails cosmic shear for every carrier history unless MOND regions are capped (about 1.75 Mpc at z = 0.5; MS3). So DE2's window stands on its KiDS, flagship and forest sides only; its shear floor, DE3's margins and DE5's T_max tables are mock-based numbers, kept for the record, not constraints. The halo model over-counts phantoms in crowded places, so the true bound lies between the two estimates.
