## PAPER IMPACT — the one decision that sets the wording

The AS door CLOSES, but **softly** (no formal no-go; the unproven AS-MOND-IR truncation is logically conceivable, just
unforced and circular-to-insert). Two honest framings:

- **Option A (bury it):** "κ=½ is unforceable across ~25 routes *including* the asymptotic-safety fixed point." This is
  *defensible* — AS was run, it does not output ½ non-circularly, it joins the closed ledger for an AS-specific reason
  (orthogonality + scheme-mush + opposite-IR).
- **Option B (name it — RECOMMENDED):** Keep the route count at the rigorously-closed ~24, and **EXPLICITLY NAME
  asymptotic safety as the one acknowledged theoretical avenue not foreclosed by a formal no-go** — closing only "no
  non-circular construction found, and any construction that worked would have to un-cancel G or insert a MOND operator
  absent from the framework's forced content." This is **more honest than burying it**: it concedes the soft edge openly,
  pre-empts a referee who knows AS outputs real numbers, and costs nothing (the closure reasons are strong). It also
  matches the banked ASYMPTOTIC_SAFETY_LAMBDA_NOTE posture (AS is a *non-hostile QG-UV companion*, directional Λ-dynamical
  rhyme — not a κ source, not an enemy).

**My recommendation: Option B.** The framework's credibility is built on calling the soft edges; naming AS as the
acknowledged un-foreclosed avenue is the both-ways-honest move and is harder to attack than a clean "~25 routes" claim
that a sharp referee could poke (since AS's closure is structural, not a theorem).

**Net on the empirical program: unchanged.** a₀'s value stays NOT-derived; framework remains a provably ONE-parameter EFT;
