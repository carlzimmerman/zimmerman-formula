## Exact claim and scope

State quantifiers, domains, dimensional conventions and hypothesis class. Identify what would refute this claim. Separate theory selection from conditional implication.

## Inputs and dependency bridge

List assumed, independently measured, fitted and derived quantities separately. Link the reviewed upstream artifacts actually used. Name every unproved bridge. If an input changed, state which older result becomes stale.

## First discriminator and result

Show the calculation or counterexample and explain what it decides. Preserve negative outcomes. A code error, a failed method and a false proposition are different outcomes.

## Evidence

Give commands, exit codes, source/toolchain/data hashes, raw logs, seed, tested range and resource use in manifest.json. Include Lean theorem names/full types/axioms, symbolic domains, numerical residuals and convergence, or the observational likelihood/covariance as appropriate. State what was not tested.

## Independent check

Give the other derivation or adversarial control. Name whether review is independent or self-review. Reviewer agreement does not replace the proof or data.

## Novelty and physical interpretation

Record the nearest primary sources and exact differences. Do not infer new physics from successful algebra. Identify whether the result is a theorem, bounded computation, instrument design, observed constraint or empirical finding.

## Decision and next action

Give one of: proved conditional, refuted in stated scope, finite evidence, inconclusive, or still open. State the strongest surviving claim, the first remaining uncertainty and the next cheap discriminating test. Name dependent packets affected and what must be re-run.
