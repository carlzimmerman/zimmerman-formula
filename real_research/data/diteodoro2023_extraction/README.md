Exploratory scripts (PyMuPDF; run from the arXiv source's `work/` sub-directory with `../images/atlas/<NAME>_all.pdf`) used to read the plotted HI points out of the vector atlas figures of Di Teodoro et al. 2023, and the Lelli+2016 flat-part algorithm. They are kept as the record of how `diteodoro2023_hi_rotation_curves.tsv` was made; the load-bearing validation is CFG41's control C2 (the algorithm applied to the TSV reproduces the paper's tabulated v_flat for 12 of 15 galaxies).
