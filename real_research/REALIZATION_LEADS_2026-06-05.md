# The realization frontier — the Cassini failure is escapable, and the trilemma is partly threadable

*C. Zimmerman, 2026-06-05. After the coefficient question closed (not derivable) and AeST was shown to fail Cassini, the
framework's real open problem is the **realization**: is there a covariant theory that gives MOND + a₀ and is
Cassini-safe + CMB-safe? Pursued honestly — and it's better news than the AeST result alone suggested. C.Z. corrected
one of his own earlier overclaims in the process.*

## Lead 1 — Vainshtein/k-mouflage MOND is **Cassini-safe** (corrects my "Cassini is generic" claim)

**Babichev, Deffayet & Esposito-Farèse 2011** (arXiv:1106.2538, *"Improving relativistic MOND with Galileon
k-mouflage"*): a covariant Galileon term added to relativistic MOND **Vainshtein-screens** the fifth force in the Solar
System (the Sun's Vainshtein radius ~100 pc) while preserving MOND at galaxy scales — and **the Cassini bound can be
achieved** at post-Newtonian order. So:

- **The AeST Cassini failure (~15–25σ) is *specific to AeST*** (its μ²Φ screening scale is ~Mpc, too large to reach the
  Sun) — **not generic to all covariant MOND.** I previously wrote "swapping realization doesn't help"; that was an
  overclaim, now corrected in `CASSINI_QUADRUPOLE_CONSTRAINT.md`.
- **A Galileon-k-mouflage host is a genuine Cassini escape** for the framework. The framework's a₀ scale and its
  a₀(z) claim are realization-agnostic; hosting them in a Vainshtein-screened MOND instead of AeST is Cassini-safe.
- **Honest cost:** that host builds on TeVeS and likely re-incurs the CMB-3rd-peak problem (its cosmology, arXiv:1702.00683,
  needs a separate check). So it may trade the *Cassini* corner of the trilemma for the *CMB* corner — but the Cassini
  corner, which looked fatal, is **escapable**.

## Lead 2 — Superfluid dark matter threads the trilemma most completely (but a₀ ≠ dark-energy origin)

**Berezhiani & Khoury 2015** (arXiv:1507.01019, *"Theory of Dark Matter Superfluidity"*): dark matter is an eV-scale,
strongly self-interacting, axion-like particle that **Bose-condenses into a superfluid inside galaxies**, whose
**phonons mediate a MOND force**; in clusters (higher velocity dispersion → higher temperature) it's in the *normal*
phase and acts as ordinary dark matter. So it natively threads three corners:

- **CMB-safe** — cosmologically it *is* dark matter (gives the 3rd peak).
- **MOND in galaxies** — the phonon force reproduces the RAR.
- **Clusters work** — normal-phase DM supplies the missing mass (the corner where plain MOND fails).

**But two honest caveats for *this* framework:** (i) it ties a₀ to the **superfluid's microphysics** (the axion mass and
self-coupling), **not to ρ_Λ / dark energy** — so it's a *different* origin for a₀ than the framework's; (ii) it has
real 2020s tensions (weak-lensing, the "three problems of superfluid DM" arXiv:2009.03003, and reconciling a superfluid
with collisionless-DM searches).

## Honest realization status — the trilemma is "pick a subset," but no corner is individually fatal

| corner | a solution exists? | by what |
|---|---|---|
| **Cassini-safe** | **yes** | Galileon k-mouflage (Vainshtein screening) |
| **CMB-safe (3rd peak)** | **yes** | AeST 𝒬-dust mode; or superfluid DM; or an 11 eV sterile-ν |
| **MOND galaxies** | **yes** | all of the above |
| **a₀ from *dark energy*** (the framework's distinctive tie) | **only as a posit** | none derives it; the coefficient is free |
| **all of the above in one theory** | **no (open)** | the conditional no-go: needs a second sector or covariant modified inertia |

**Net:** the framework's realization is **not doomed** — each corner has a known solution, and the Cassini corner that
looked like a kill is escapable (Vainshtein). What no single theory does is satisfy *all* corners *and* tie a₀
specifically to dark energy — that remains the open problem (the trilemma no-go), and it's a field-wide hard problem,
not a framework-specific death. **The most promising concrete realization to pursue is a Galileon-k-mouflage MOND
(Cassini-safe) combined with a dust/superfluid sector (CMB-safe)** — at the cost of the "one number" unification, which
the trilemma already showed must be paid. The distinctive a₀(z) claim rides on top of *any* of these, coefficient-free,
and is still decided at z~3.
