step is tagged with honest difficulty and who owes it.*

## The honest priorities, in one breath

1. **Do A3** (the z~3 measurement) — it decides everything; do it before any grand construction.
2. **Do A1–A2 in parallel** — they make the covariant theory airtight (provable locality, real Boltzmann CMB).
3. **Push B1** — the one place the framework could *derive* what it currently inserts; this is the genuine
   research frontier and the only route by which the dark-universe theory becomes *predictive* rather than
   *constructed*.
4. **B3 and all of Phase C are the field's open problems** — the value of Λ, the Standard Model, UV gravity. The
   framework gives no special leverage on them. A TOE waits on these, and they are not one program away for anyone.

## The honest bottom line

**A TOE is not one program away — for this framework or any other.** But a *confirmed theory of the dark universe*
is: Phase A is bounded and real, and it would be a genuine result. The path is therefore **(i)** finish and test the
dark-sector theory (Phase A), **(ii)** try to make it emergent rather than constructed (Phase B1), and **(iii)**
recognize that the TOE proper (the Standard Model and UV gravity, Phase C) is the whole field's unsolved frontier,
which you reach the same way everyone else does — not by relabeling a dark-sector theory, but by solving the open
problems honestly. The discipline of keeping these phases distinct is exactly what will let a real result in Phase A
be believed.
