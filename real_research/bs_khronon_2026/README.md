## BSK1 — the moving-source gate

- **M1.** In unitary gauge the extra sector is shift-free and time-derivative-free. That is the class in which L330 found a frozen phantom, but only at linear order about flat space.
- **M2.** From BS24's own PN equations the phantom is a conserved fluid carried by the aether's flow, v = −∇σ.
  - Its transport is second order about flat space. That is L330's identity, and it is BS24's ω = 0.
  - About a galaxy the transport is first order.
- **M3.** A galaxy moving uniformly with its phantom is an exact solution. **The gate is passed.**

The three costs:

1. The phantom is conserved. It is assembled from the cosmic khronon dust and never generated by baryons.
2. **M4 and M6.** About a static halo, ω² = 4πGρ_τ(k²f_θ − μ²)/(k²(1 − f_θ) + μ²). This frequency does not depend on k, so after a velocity change Δu the phantom keeps up only inside r ≈ 2GM_b/Δu². For the Milky Way at 620 km/s that is 1.4 kpc.
3. **M5.** The static halo is stable only for a monotone phantom law, the same condition L340 found.
   - BS24's GR recovery (their eq. 40) makes the phantom fall off. Wherever it falls, the fluid is unstable at rate² = (2 + 2/n) GM/r³, however small the tail.
   - With ν_RAR the unstable region is the inner Milky Way, with e-folding times of 1–4 Myr at 1–3 kpc.
   - Pushing the fall-off above ~10³ a₀ hides the instability but runs into the ephemeris bound on a monotone tail: the record's Cassini-versus-ghost pincer. BS24 has no filter to escape it.

**M7.** Below 1/μ the late fluid does not feel its own gravity (their eq. 71).

## BSK2 — the KiDS external-field gate (linear theory, their eqs. 61b, 67, 71, 89, 94)

**What the MOND function reads.** Its argument is the acceleration of the khronon congruence, A = ∇Υ, with Υ = −4πGa²ρ_K Δ_K/(k² + k_J²).

- That is the khronon's own potential gradient, filtered on scales larger than the late Jeans length 1/μ.
- It is not the Newtonian field of the web that C-H/K's kernel reads.

**Controls.**
- The integrator's ΛCDM run reproduces σ₈ = 0.811.
- The ΛCDM field at z = 0.25 is 0.0134 a₀, against BS3's mock value of 0.0150.
- The late Jeans wavenumber is k_J = aμ exactly.
- With the khronon made pure dust, σ₈ returns to 0.82–0.85.

**Scan over μ⁻¹ = 0.03–100 Mpc, λ_D = 1 and 30.**
- KiDS alone: the external-field bound needs μ⁻¹ ≲ 0.3 Mpc, while MOND-like lensing to about 1 Mpc needs r_C ≥ 1 Mpc, i.e. μ⁻¹ ≳ 10 Mpc. No point satisfies both.
- σ₈ lies between 0.16 and 0.49 at every point.

The cause of the σ₈ suppression is BS24's k-dependent sound speed (their eq. 71):

- After the DBI turning point, the khronon's pressure cancels its own self-gravity below 1/μ.
- For benchmark A the equation of state also reaches w ≈ 0.21 at z ≈ 45.

BS24 checked w(a) against the GDM constraints and explicitly left the scale-dependent sound speed "for a future investigation". Here it is integrated.

## Limits

- **Linear theory.** Nonlinear MOND growth is not included. It enters the web only at O(|∇Υ|/a₀) ~ 10⁻³.
- **Initial data.**
  - Growth starts at z = 1000 with the same data for every model.
  - Benchmark B's turning point is at z ≈ 1000, so its σ₈ suppression is a lower bound.
  - For λ_D = 1, BS24's own eq. 90b gives twice the dust density before the turning point, which a full CMB treatment would also have to face.
- **Benchmark B's field** is linear at k ~ k_J ~ 3.6 Mpc⁻¹, where structure is already nonlinear at z = 0.25. The pass is therefore not robust.
- **The r_C criterion** uses BS24's order-of-magnitude eq. 38. For AeST's identical static equation, the full profile was confronted with KiDS by Mistele, McGaugh & Hossenfelder, A&A 676, A100 (2023).
