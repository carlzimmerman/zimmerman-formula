## 1. HEADLINE

**The framework is NOT refuted by any current data.** No single observable delivers a clean kill, and the framework reproduces the z = 0 record that constant-a0 MOND is celebrated for.

But its standing is **honestly mixed, and it is not confirmed either**:

- It rests on **MOND's z = 0 successes** (RAR, BTFR, lensing-RAR, most dwarfs, solar system, CMB-by-construction) — *solid, but inherited, not new*.
- It **carries MOND's shared, unresolved liabilities wholesale** — clusters and the Bullet are genuine **serious tensions** (~2× too little mass at R500 on 9830 real clusters; a collisionless lensing residual on the galaxies), and its only distinctive lever (a0(z)) does **nothing** to fix them (+0.025 dex vs the +0.66 dex needed — off by ~1.5 orders of magnitude).
- It has **exactly ONE framework-specific exposure**: its distinctive **declining** a0(z) is **currently unsupported-to-mildly-disfavored**. The single direct a0(z) measurement (MUSE-DARK III, z~1) **RISES** the wrong way (+1.59 ± 0.1 ×10⁻¹⁰ slope, ~15–30σ against the declining branch at face value), and the intermediate-z BTFR z-trend is systematics-limited and undecided.

The framework's distinctiveness is **hostage to DESI**: if dynamical dark energy survives, the declining branch lives; if DESI reverts to w = −1, ρ_DE is constant, a0(z) goes flat, and the framework **loses all distinctive content**, collapsing to ordinary constant-a0 MOND (which still carries the cluster failure). Verdict belongs to the **clean z~3 deep-MOND test (does not yet exist) + DESI DR3 (~2027)**.

## 3. THE SERIOUS TENSIONS (developed straight)

### 3a. Galaxy clusters — SHARED, serious, robust *(the framework's heaviest real liability)*
On **9830 real eRASS1 clusters**, with the framework's *own* a0 = 9.36×10⁻¹¹, the MOND boost still misses **~2.1–2.4× the mass at R500** (η_median = 2.15, geomean = 2.36). This is the textbook MOND cluster problem, reproduced on real data and **robust to every choice**: interpolating function (2.15–2.40), stellar fraction f_star = 0–0.5 (1.9–2.4). Per-cluster significance ~3.3σ; against a generous 0.15–0.20 dex absolute-scale systematic floor it is ~1.7–2.2σ, but the offset is **unanimous across every mass/T bin**.

**The distinctive a0(z) claim is irrelevant to it.** To erase η needs a0 ×4.6 (≈ +0.66 dex, since M_eff ∝ √a0); a0(z)~√ρ_DE supplies only +0.025 dex (~4%) by the cluster epoch z~0.3 — **off by ~1.5 orders of magnitude**. Honest caveats both ways: (i) absolute η carries ~50% WL/scaling-relation systematic, so the *magnitude* (not existence) is uncertain; (ii) I do **not** claim η worsens with mass in *this* catalog — with eRASS1's own FGAS500 the η–M500 slope is slightly *negative* (−0.03), so "worsens with mass" is baryon-prescription-dependent here; (iii) KATRIN (<0.8 eV, now 0.45 eV) has removed the canonical ~2 eV-neutrino escape, **hardening** the shared tension.

**But — unlike phenomenological MOND — the framework's *covariant* realization carries an intrinsic candidate** (see
`AEST_MASS_SCALE_two_doors.md`). AeST is not plain QUMOND: its mass term `μ²Φ` (`1/μ ≳ 1 Mpc`, pinned by the CMB fit)
switches on **at the cluster scale**, producing an oscillatory regime where the RAR "peaks then drops as if there were
negative mass density" — and the AeST authors note **"similar features of the galaxy-cluster RAR have been reported…
the potential to address the shortcomings of MOND in clusters"** (Durakovic & Skordis 2024). So the deficit may be
supplied by a field the framework *already needs*, switched on at the right scale (and off in galaxies). **Two serious
caveats keep this a candidate, not a cure:** the oscillatory regime involves negative-energy-density condensates the
AeST literature flags as **expected-unstable** (Mistele+2023), and only isothermal toy models exist — the eRASS1
η ≈ 2 is **not yet shown closed**. Net: the heaviest liability moves from "MOND fails, needs an *ad hoc* component" to
"AeST has an *intrinsic* mechanism, caveated by a possible instability and no quantitative fit."

### 3b. The Bullet Cluster — SHARED, serious, but OPEN/contested
The lensing-convergence peaks sit on the **collisionless galaxies**, not on the dominant (~85–90%) X-ray gas. The 2026 JWST strong-lensing analysis (Famaey, arXiv:2605.10022) finds baryon-only MOND reaches only κ ≈ 0.5 where the data demand κ ≥ 1, and a **~3.4×10¹⁴ M⊙ collisionless residual on the galaxies** (factor-few-to-~10 in cores). a0(z) does **nothing** here — the Bullet is pure constant-a0 MOND at z = 0.296.
**Not cleanly fatal, because as of 2026 it is actively contested**: Hernandez (arXiv:2604.10811) argues QUMOND density-weighting (compact galaxies → sharp phantom vs diffuse gas → broad phantom) reproduces the on-galaxy peaks from baryons alone. The repo's *own* "non-equilibrium phantom" escape is correctly self-flagged **BROKEN** (the AeST field re-settles in R/c ≈ 2 Myr ≪ the 150 Myr collision, so the phantom tracks the gas). **Honest verdict:** clusters and the Bullet are where this framework — like *all* MOND — still needs a second dark component (the AeST Q-field engineered as dust, or eV-scale sterile neutrinos). Addressable in principle, unresolved in practice; a standing liability, neither a clean kill nor a win.

### 3c. ⭐ THE FRAMEWORK-SPECIFIC EXPOSURE — declining a0(z), currently leaning the WRONG way *(do not hide this)*
This is the **only** place the framework is exposed *beyond* generic MOND, and it is the one to watch.

- **Direct a0(z) measurement (MUSE-DARK III, Ciocan+2026, z~1):** RISES. Slope a1 = **+1.59 ± 0.1 ×10⁻¹⁰** and a0(z~1)/a0(0) = **2.38 ± 0.11**, vs the framework's predicted ~1.01 (flat-to-declining). Face-value tension vs the declining branch ≈ **15–30σ**; the authors note a0(z) runs **"faster than H(z),"** robust across NFW/DC14/Burkert + MOND, and they **disown** the stellar-mass escape hatch. **The honest, systematics-aware significance is ~2σ, not 15–30σ** — the entire rise rests on this single (real, literature-confirmed, *not* fabricated) point; jackknife → 1.7σ, and an empirically-revealed inter-method systematic (the z = 0 vs z = 0.05 pair differ by 0.39e-10 over an interval too short to be real evolution) → 1.4σ. Critically, the rise is **LCDM-degenerate** (Mayer+2023: assembly alone produces apparent a0 ×3 with no fundamental a0) — the same degeneracy that spares the framework from falsification also **strips the rise of its power to falsify**.

- **Intermediate-z BTFR z-trend (KMOS3D, 135 discs) — UNDECIDED, not a tension:** the systematics-immune statistic (the z-*trend*, which cancels a constant zero-point error) is d(δlogV)/dz = **−0.029 ± 0.012 dex/z**, which **leans the framework's declining way** and matches Übler+2017's published direction, disfavoring the rising-(cH) branch at 6.9σ at the BTFR level. **But it is not a clean detection:** adding asymmetric drift (σ0/Vc) as a covariate collapses it to −0.017 ± 0.012 (1.4σ, non-significant), it is degenerate with LCDM gas-fraction evolution (Übler's own interpretation), and the repo's *own* direct-RAR compilation rises (opposite). **Correction to the repo's narrative:** the old "leans rising" headline applies only to the *untestable absolute offset* (+0.13–0.16 dex, a zero-point systematic), **not** to the *evolution* the framework actually predicts.

**Net on the specific exposure:** **undecided, leaning unfavorable.** The distinctive declining-a0(z) is neither confirmed nor cleanly refuted. The one direct probe rises against it (but only at a contested ~2σ once systematics + the LCDM degeneracy are folded in); the intermediate-z kinematic trend actually leans *toward* it but is systematics-dominated; and the surviving predicted declining signal (≤0.033 dex to z = 3) is **below the 0.026 dex BTFR intrinsic scatter** — so existing high-z kinematics *tolerate* it but cannot confirm it. **This is the framework's true vulnerability, and it is real — reported straight.**

## 5. THE HONEST BOTTOM LINE

**(a) Not refuted.** No current dataset delivers a clean falsification. The framework reproduces MOND's z = 0 record (RAR/BTFR/lensing/most-dwarfs/solar-system) and is provably invariant where it must be (linear CMB, S8, H0).

**(b) It carries MOND's shared tensions wholesale.** Clusters (η ≈ 2.1–2.4 on 9830 real eRASS1 clusters) and the Bullet (a collisionless on-galaxy lensing residual) are **serious, robust, and real** — but **SHARED with all MOND**, and the framework's distinctive a0(z) lever is **~1.5 orders of magnitude too weak** to fix them. Dwarfs add a mild shared ~2σ residual on Draco/UMi. These are inherited liabilities, not new strikes against a0 = Λ. Like all MOND, the framework still needs a second dark component for clusters.

**(c) It has exactly ONE framework-specific exposure, and it currently leans the wrong way.** The distinctive **declining** a0(z) is **unsupported-to-mildly-disfavored**: the single direct measurement (MUSE-DARK III) **RISES** (~2σ against, honestly de-systematized from a naive 15–30σ; rescued from falsification only by an LCDM degeneracy that *also* strips the prediction's distinctiveness). The intermediate-z BTFR trend leans *toward* the framework but is systematics-limited (undecided). This is the framework's true vulnerability — **not hidden**, but also **not a refutation**.

**(d) The verdict belongs to the clean z~3 test + DESI DR3.** The framework's entire distinctiveness is **hostage to DESI dynamical dark energy** (currently favorable but only 2.5–3.9σ and contested): if w reverts to −1, ρ_DE is constant, a0(z) goes flat, and the framework **collapses to ordinary constant-a0 MOND** (keeping the shared cluster failure, losing all distinctive content). The decisive datum — **one clean deep-MOND (g ≪ a0) extended rotation curve at z~3 to a few percent** — **does not yet exist**. Until then the framework is best described as **a viable, internally-consistent re-derivation of MOND's z = 0 phenomenology from Λ, whose one novel prediction is presently undecided-leaning-unfavorable and awaiting its decisive test (~2027 DR3 + z~3 kinematics)** — neither vindicated nor killed.
