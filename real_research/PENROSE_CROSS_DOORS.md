## (3) THE GENUINE TESTABLE DOORS — *there are none; here are the three that came closest, and why each fails*

No idea earned an A/B/C. In the spirit of the mandate, here are the **three strongest near-misses**, developed enough to show exactly where the door is bolted shut, plus the one honest next step each implies.

**Near-miss #1 — Conformal infinity / spacelike scri⁺ (the single best lead).**
*The real physics:* For Λ>0, Penrose's future null/conformal boundary scri⁺ is **spacelike** (this is genuine Penrose physics and the premise of CCC; confirmed in the literature). scri⁺ is the t→∞ surface where matter has redshifted away and ρ_total → ρ_DE. So **if** one ties a₀'s horizon to the conformal future boundary, one **re-selects the √ρ_DE branch** of the a₀(z) evolution — the same branch the framework already favors (Limbach 2008; Milgrom 2017) and that holographic dark energy independently favors (Li 2004 rejects the Hubble IR cutoff for the future event horizon).
*Why it is not a door:* In **exact** de Sitter the future event horizon, the apparent/Hubble horizon, and the static-patch horizon **all coincide at R_dS** (verified symbolically). The conformal structure — a property *of* asymptotic dS — has nothing to choose between them; the event-vs-Hubble fork only opens once matter makes H(z) ≠ H_Λ, which is *outside* dS where the conformal-boundary argument no longer applies. So the payoff is **conceptual reinforcement of a choice the framework already made**, with the *same* prediction (a₀(z=3)/a₀(0) ≈ 0.70 if dark energy is dynamical/DESI; computed ρ_total branch E(z) = {1.00, 1.79, 3.03, 4.57} at z = 0,1,2,3 vs √ρ_DE = const), the *same* z~3 deep-MOND rotation-curve test already in `THE_EVENT_HORIZON_DOOR.md`. **Zero new content.**
*Next step (if pursued):* none specific to Penrose. The discriminating observation is the framework's existing one — high-z (z~3) halo-free rotation curves separating a₀(z)↑ (framework, ρ_DE-dynamical) from constant-√Λ from SIV↓ — and it does not need the scri⁺ argument to define it.

**Near-miss #2 — Twistor infinity twistor I_{αβ} (the best Door-A candidate).**
*The real physics (verified verbatim from primary sources):* Adamo–Mason–Skinner (arXiv:1508.02554, eq. 2.3) write I_{AB} with a block **∝ √Λ**; it is "the ingredient required to break conformal invariance on twistor space," it "can be thought of as setting a mass scale," non-degenerate **iff Λ≠0**. Cachazo–Skinner (arXiv:1207.0741): gravity amplitudes "depend monomially on the infinity twistor that **explicitly breaks conformal symmetry to Poincaré**." This is a real echo of the framework's central claim — *a₀ is THE conformal-symmetry-breaking scale, welded to Λ.*
*Why it is not a door:* The welding is only to the **√Λ scaling**, which the framework already has (a₀ ∝ √ρ_DE) and data confirm. Two hard mismatches kill any number: (i) **dimensions/dynamics** — I_{αβ} supplies only the inverse-length √Λ; twistor geometry is massless-ray kinematics with *no* dynamics, so it carries no c, no ½, no 32π. The bare guess c²√Λ = **9.39×10⁻¹⁰ m/s², exactly 10.03× too big** (Z = 1/√3 = 0.577), *excluded* like the naive Z=1 horizon guess. (ii) **The dimensionless coupling** g in I_{AB} = g·√Λ·δ_{AB} is a **free parameter twistor theory never pins** — an even sharper reason it cannot select Z. (iii) Same-label-different-group trap: twistor SO(4,2)→SO(4,1) acts on 4D spacetime; Milgrom's deep-MOND SO(4,1) acts on 3D space. The repo already flags the "two SO(4,1)s" identification as a *posit*; twistor theory does not supply the missing theorem.
*Next step:* none — a dedicated literature search for twistors↔MOND/a₀ returned **zero** connecting work.

**Near-miss #3 — de Sitter horizon entropy (WCH / Bekenstein–Hawking ¼).**
*The real physics:* a₀'s emergent reading #3 uses S_dS = A/4. The algebra is exact: **a₀ = [c²√π/(Z·ℓ_p)]·S_dS^(−½)**, i.e. a₀ ∝ 1/√S_dS, with S_dS = 3π/(Λℓ_p²) = **3.31×10¹²² k_B** (verified; R_dS/ℓ_p = 1.03×10⁶¹). Penrose's WCH is the *other* entropy bookend (Weyl/clumping ~0 at the Big Bang, growing since), and the framework even hands WCH's vague "high-entropy future" a concrete endpoint — the 10¹²² dS ceiling.
*Why it is not a door:* (i) S_dS is a **pure-Λ quantity** — C_{abcd}(t=0), the thing WCH constrains, does **not** appear in it, so fixing/changing the WCH leaves a₀ untouched. (ii) The relation carries only the **scale** (a₀ ~ c²/R_dS), never Z; the "¼" is the *universal* Bekenstein–Hawking factor present in **any** horizon argument, identical whether Z = 5.79 or 2π. (iii) The repo's own number-field theorem (`desitter_entropy_coefficient.py`) shows any area/entropy O(1) is rational×πⁿ, whereas **Z = √(32π/3) carries an irreducible square root** — the fingerprint of a density/collapse origin √(Gρ), *foreign* to area thermodynamics. (iv) The BH-temperature route gives G_eff/G = κ/√(κ²+(cH)²) → 0 at low acceleration = **anti-MOND**, the opposite of what is needed. **Caution flagged and verified:** the real "Weyl gravity → a₀" results (Mannheim–O'Brien; Varieschi; Scholz) use **conformal Weyl gravity** (action ∝ C²), a *different object* from Penrose's WCH (a boundary condition *on* the Weyl tensor). Conflating them manufactures a fake door.

## (5) DOES ANY PENROSE IDEA BEAR ON THE OPEN COEFFICIENT FRONTIER (fix Z = √(32π/3)) OR THE EVENT-vs-HUBBLE-HORIZON EVOLUTION FORK? — **NO.**

**The holy grail (Door A): no.** And the failure is *structural, not incidental*. The three Penrose ideas that look most like coefficient-fixers — CCC, conformal infinity, twistors — all rest on **conformal (scale) invariance**, which by construction **discards the metric scale** (g → Ω²g; the conformal factor is "scaled away," encoding only causal structure — Frauendiener, *Living Rev. Rel.*). Scale-blind structure cannot output a length unit, a dimensionful a₀, or a unique pure number Z. The available dS-boundary numbers are kappa→Z=1, the Gibbons–Hawking 2π→Z=6.28, and the dS/CFT Cardy–Verlinde result reproducing S = A/4 — **none equals 5.789**, and the 32π/3 = 8π(Einstein)×⅓(Friedmann)×4(free-fall) decomposition contains **no conformal-boundary number**. This is exactly the repo's principle that *a symmetry never fixes the scale that breaks it* — Penrose's conformal machinery **is** that symmetry, so it argues against Door A.

**The one program that derives both a₀ and Λ is not Penrose's, and still does not fix 32π.** Kanatchikov's **precanonical quantum gravity** (arXiv:2311.05525; 2511.15025, Nov 2025) is the one real quantum-geometry program yielding **both** a MOND a₊ **and** Λ from a single hadronic-scale κ. Verified from the full text: a₊ = 8πℏGκ (eq. 14), with structural relation **a₊ = √(Λ/λ)** (eq. 15) where **λ = Λ/(8πℏGκ)²** (eq. 7) is an O(1) operator-ordering / spacetime-dimensionality constant — **explicitly not 32π or 32π/3**. Crucially, a₊ and Λ are **siblings of κ**, not the parent-child a₀ = c²√(Λ/32π); and the authors concede the magnitude is uncertain "**by several orders of magnitude**" (a₊ ~ 10⁻²⁷ m⁻¹; they do *not* obtain 1.2×10⁻¹⁰). So it is a **competing derivation of the a₀~√Λ coincidence, not a door that fixes this framework's Z** — and, again, it is Kanatchikov's, not Penrose's. (It does usefully *correct* any blanket "discreteness is always UV" claim — it places these scales "in the cosmological domain rather than the Planckian.")

**The evolution fork (event vs Hubble horizon, √ρ_DE vs √ρ_total): no new resolution.** The only Penrose contribution is the **spacelike scri⁺** observation (Near-miss #1), which *re-motivates* the √ρ_DE branch the framework already favors — but in exact dS all horizons coincide, so it adds **zero** discriminating power. The fork is resolved (or not) by the framework's existing finite-z a₀(z) test on high-z rotation curves, with or without Penrose.
