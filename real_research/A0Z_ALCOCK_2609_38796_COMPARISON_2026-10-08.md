# a0(z) on MUSE-DARK: this repository's record vs Alcock, arXiv:2609.38796 (2026-10-08)

**Script:** `real_research/reviews/alcock_2609_38796_comparison.py` → `.out`, `_results.json`. It uses only on-disk inputs (the MUSE-DARK III stated-results CSV and the CFG262 stage-B JSON), with no downloads.
**Read of Alcock:** this note uses the arXiv abstract only. The full text has not been read, so treat any point about its method beyond the abstract as provisional.

## The paper

G. T. Alcock, *Is the acceleration scale of the radial acceleration relation tracking the cosmic expansion rate?*, arXiv:2609.38796, posted 2026-09-30, single author. The paper refits the MUSE-DARK III binned a0 values (Ciocan et al., arXiv:2604.22613, A&A 709 L16) with a0(z) = A(1+z)^γ. Its abstract claims:

- γ = 0.78 ± 0.15;
- a constant a0 (γ = 0) is excluded at 5.3σ;
- the matter-density scaling (γ = 3/2) is excluded at 4.8σ;
- a0 ∝ H(z) (γ_eff ≈ 1.10) is consistent within 2.1σ and is preferred by information criteria, with A = 1.40e-10;
- the survey's "faster than H(z)" depends on the anchor;
- the exponent is invariant under redshift-independent rescalings of a0, so it is robust to common-mode M* systematics.

## Where the numbers agree (script sections B, C, A)

- **The binned exponent is reproducible.** The printed endpoints 1.99 → 2.71 give γ = 0.67–1.07, depending on where the unprinted bin centres sit. Alcock's 0.78 is inside that range.
- **The H(z) effective exponent over 0.33–1.44** (Ωm 0.315) is 1.08 here vs Alcock's ~1.10.
- **Alcock's anchor point is right.** The survey's own linear fits, a0(0) + a1·z, imply γ_eff = 1.27 (DC14 halo), 1.25 (best ΛCDM halo) and 1.09 (MOND refit). The binned values give ~0.8. The "faster than H(z)" reading comes from the linear form's z = 0 intercept.

## Where they differ

**1. The invariance argument misses the systematic that matters (section E).**
MUSE-DARK III's own App. C says the M* shift that returns a0 = 1.2e-10 is:

- 0.2 dex in the low-z bins;
- 0.45 dex in the highest bin.

That is a **z-dependent** offset of 0.25 dex. The paper also puts unmodelled molecular gas at ~0.2 dex, and molecular fractions rise with z. Invariance under a z-independent rescaling does not touch a drift of this kind. CFG255 shows the same thing in lensing: an M* calibration drift δ between z bins moves log g_obs by δ/2, exactly like an a0 drift.

**2. The rise depends on which baryon masses are used (section D, CFG262).**
CFG262 measured the implied-a0 change from the lowest to the highest z-third (z 0.52 → 1.20). The table compares each route with three expectations:

- flat: 0;
- H(z): +0.177 dex;
- Alcock's γ = 0.78: +0.125 ± 0.024 dex.

| Baryon route | third3 − third1 (dex) | vs flat | vs H(z) | vs Alcock |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| (i) fitted DC14 masses | +0.570 ± 0.094 | +6.1σ | +4.2σ | +4.6σ |
| (ii) SED M* + H2 gas | −0.115 ± 0.489 | −0.2σ | −0.6σ | −0.5σ |
| (iii) SED M* | +0.036 ± 0.138 | +0.3σ | −1.0σ | −0.6σ |

- With the survey's fitted masses, the rise is steeper than both H(z) and Alcock's γ.
- With independent SED masses, it is consistent with flat, H(z) and Alcock alike.
- In the top third, the three routes differ by ×12.7 in level (4.53 / 0.36 / 1.71).

A 5σ exclusion of a constant a0 therefore holds only for one mass route. Nothing on hand shows which route is correct.

**3. A ΛCDM universe produces an apparent rise anyway.**
Mayer+2023 (Magneticum) shows that fitted RAR a0 rises about ×3 to z 2.3 with no fundamental a0, driven by baryon fraction and assembly history. So a fitted a0(z) trend is not, on its own, evidence for any a0(z) law (repository record of 2026-06-06).

## Bottom line

- Alcock's arithmetic on the binned points is reproducible.
- Their conclusion that a constant a0 is excluded at 5.3σ, with H(z) preferred, rests on the survey's fitted masses. With the other two routes, MUSE-DARK cannot separate flat, H(z) or γ = 0.78.
- This repository reached that non-diagnostic verdict on this data, with a0 ∝ H(z) named as the rival, in dated Zenodo deposits:
  - 21444998 (2026-07-19), the H(z) rival without MUSE-DARK;
  - 22563139 (2026-09-06), which includes MUSE-DARK III.
  
  Both predate the arXiv posting. That is a priority on the **confrontation and its verdict**, not on the a0–cosmology link.
- Neither side has a measurement that decides FLAT vs a0 ∝ H(z). The decisive object is still a deep-MOND rotator at z ~ 2.5 (PAPER7), or self-calibrated discs (CFG385).
