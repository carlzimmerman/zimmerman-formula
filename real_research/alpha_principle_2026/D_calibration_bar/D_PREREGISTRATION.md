# D -- the calibration bar for any claimed derivation of alpha (pre-registration)

Written 2026-09-28 BEFORE any script in this directory was run. This lane proposes no derivation. It builds the bar a candidate must clear
(look-elsewhere machinery), audits the known formulas by that machinery, and ships a reusable checker.

Target: 1/alpha = 137.035999177 (Thomson limit, CODATA 2022), relative uncertainty about 1.6e-10 (delta_CODATA = 1.6e-10).
Everything below is about the Thomson-limit value. alpha runs (1/alpha(M_Z) ~ 127.95); a route that does not say which scale it means has a
multiplicity of target values, handled by the checker's `n_targets` factor.

## Sources read before the pre-registration (facts used, nothing else asserted about Wyler)

* arXiv:1411.4673 (Jentschura and Nandori, "Attempts at a determination of the fine-structure constant from first principles", read as pdftotext): Wyler's
  closed form alpha = (9/(8 pi^4)) (pi^5/(2^4 5!))^(1/4) = 1/137.0360824 (their Eq. 8), built from the volumes of the bounded symmetric domain D_5, the
  characteristic boundary Q_5 and the sphere S^4 (their Eq. 7), motivated by the invariance group O(5,2) of the 5D Klein-Gordon equation; they cite
  Pease (the coefficient of the Poisson kernel used is incorrect, so the value may not be derivable from Wyler's assumptions), Robertson (an extra scaling factor
  destroys the approximate agreement; PRL 27, 1545 (1971)), and state that Eq. 8 disagrees with the latest CODATA adjustment. Wyler's originals: C. R. Acad. Sci. Paris
  269A, 743 (1969) and 271A, 186 (1971). Adler, Phys. Rev. D 5, 3021 (1972) is in their reference list.
* MathWorld "Wyler's Constant" (secondary; read through a fetch summary): same formula and value 137.0360824; quotes Robertson that the radius of the spaces is
  set to 1 without reason, and Adler's 'a number in search of a theory'. I did NOT read Robertson's or Adler's papers themselves; the criticism is reported as cited by these two sources.
* Not verified and therefore not used: any claim about Gilmore 1972.

## Definitions (fixed now)

* Value of an expression v is compared as |v/T - 1| with T = 137.035999177; the expression grammars are closed under reciprocal at the root, so an expression for alpha
  is covered by its inverse.
* Grammar atoms A(N): integers 1..N, pi, e, Z (Z = 2 sqrt(8 pi/3) = 5.7888, the framework constant, treated charitably as ONE atom). kappa = 1/2 is reachable as 1/2.
* Unary ops U = {sqrt, square, reciprocal, ln}. Binary ops B = {+, -, *, /, pow}. Only positive values are kept (an expression with negative value has an equivalent
  positive twin generated by the swapped subtraction); ln(1)=0 and non-finite or |v| outside [1e-30,1e30] are discarded.
* L1 = atoms and U(atoms). B1 = B(L1,L1). T1 = B1 and U(B1). B2 = B(L1,T1) and B(T1,L1). Distinctness = relative 1e-12.
* E1(N)  = L1 and B1 and U(B1)               (at most 1 binary op, leaves and root decorated).
* E2(N)  = E1 and B2 and U(B2)               (at most 2 binary ops).
* E3(N)  = E2 and B(L1,T2) and B(T2,L1) and B(T1,T1), T2 = B2 and U(B2), root decorated (3 binary ops). NOT enumerated: size and hit density by Monte Carlo (seeded) from the
  distinct sub-expression sets, syntactic count as an upper bound and the E2-measured duplicate ratio applied for an estimate.
* Declared N values: N = 6 and N = 12. Total grammars reported: E1(6), E1(12), E2(6), E2(12), E3(6), E3(12) = 6.
* Tolerances tested: tol in {1e-3, 1e-5, 1e-9}. Also delta_CODATA = 1.6e-10.
* Expected chance hits lambda(tol) = (number of distinct grammar values within a relative window w of T) x tol / w, with w = 1e-2 (dense window, primary) and cross-checked at
  w = 1e-3. This assumes only that the density of values is locally flat over a relative window of 1e-2; it is checked on 400 log-uniform decoy targets T*exp(u), 0.05 < |u| < 1.2.
  Probability of at least one chance hit p = 1 - exp(-lambda).
* Bits: N_bits = log2(N_distinct), match bits B(tol) = log2(1/(2 tol rho)) where rho = fraction of distinct values per unit ln v near ln T.

## Hypotheses (all stated so they can be false)

* H1: E2(12) has of order 1e7 to 1e8 distinct values; lambda(1e-3) >> 1 (hundreds to tens of thousands) for E2, so a 1e-3 match from a two-operation formula in small integers is
  expected many times by chance.
* H2: lambda(1e-9) for E2(12) lies between 1e-4 and 1e-1 (so a CODATA-precision match from a <=2-operation formula would be significant), while for E3(12) lambda(1e-9) lies between 0.1 and 100 (so
  even a 1e-9 match from a three-operation formula in small integers is NOT evidence).
* H3: alpha^-1 = 4 Z^2 + 3 is an E2 member matching at about 3.9e-5; its chance probability there is of order 1 or larger (it is not evidence).
* H4: Wyler's formula matches at about 6e-7 (not at CODATA precision; about 4000 sigma off); within a generous free-integer template family its chance probability is O(1) or larger;
  as a forced-integers formula (N = 1) it passes the look-elsewhere bar but FAILS the precision bar and is falsified by CODATA as an exact formula.
* H5: the AH5 families (a real free exponent/coefficient fitted to the single number alpha) have chance probability 1 by construction (a fitted real parameter always reaches the target);
  the 'natural constant nearby' (a = 0.49 vs kappa = 1/2, within 2%) has a chance probability well above 5%.
* H6: the AH6 handle scan (7 declared handles plus integer N) is consistent with chance; a KK handle k = R/l_P = 23.41 is reached by many E1/E2 expressions within 5e-4 (k tolerance), so
  hitting it would not have been evidence either.

## Everything that will be run (declared list; count = 13 computations)

1. d1_grammar_sizes.py: six grammars (sizes, distinct counts, lambda at 3 tolerances, decoy calibration, bits) and the self-tests S1-S3 below. MUTATE control: density mis-scaled x50.
2. d2_audit_known_formulas.py: audits A1 (4Z^2+3), A2 (Wyler, templates W0, W1, W2), A3 (alpha = x^p, p fitted; nearest simple rational exponent), A4 (1/alpha = a ln(1/x), a fitted; natural a),
   A5 (1/alpha = b ln(1/sqrt x), b fitted) and A6 (AH6 handles: k = 2/sqrt(alpha), 7 declared handles, integer N): 6 audits, of which A2 has 3 templates. MUTATE control: an audit that
   feeds 4Z^2+3 with the precision it does not have (1e-9) must be rejected.
3. alpha_bar_checker.py: the reusable checker (self-test mode plus CLI). MUTATE control: a 1e-3 hit and a fitted-parameter hit must be rejected; a positive control (a forced tiny-grammar exact match) must pass.
4. d3_rule_of_thumb.py: the table of the maximum number of free choices as a function of precision. MUTATE control: a mis-stated bit budget.

Templates for A2, declared: W1: 1/alpha = (b pi^c/a) (k pi^-d)^(1/g), a,b in 1..16, c,d,g in 1..8, k in 1..2000 (N = 16*16*8*8*8*2000 = 2.6e8 syntactic).
W2: same with k = 2^i * j!, i in 0..8, j in 1..8 (N = 16*16*8*8*8*72 = 9.4e6). W0: all integers forced by a derivation (N = 1); W0 is a claim about Wyler's group theory, not something I can verify here.
Wyler's own values are a=9, b=8, c=4, d=5, g=4, k=1920 (= 2^4 5!), a member of W1 and W2.

## Pass / fail criteria for THIS lane (declared now)

* V1 (grammar): the sizes and hit counts are reproducible (identical on a second run, fixed seeds) and lambda from the w=1e-2 window agrees with the w=1e-3 window and with the hold-out decoys
  (S3: mean of predicted/observed on 100 held-out decoys within [0.8, 1.25]).
* V2 (positive control S1): a synthetic target defined as the value of a specific E1 expression, in a grammar declared with its true size, is accepted by the checker at 1e-12; the same target
  in a mis-declared 1e15-size grammar is rejected.
* V3 (negative control S2): every hit found at 1e-3 in E2 is rejected by the checker.
* V4 (hypotheses): H1-H6 each scored true/false against the numbers; a false hypothesis is reported as false.
* The BAR itself (declared now, applied to the checker): a candidate clears it iff (i) p_lookelsewhere = 1-exp(-N_eff rho 2 delta_eff n_targets) < 1e-3, where N_eff is the declared size of the family the formula was
  drawn from (all integers, constants, operations and shapes it could have used) and delta_eff = max(achieved deviation, route's a-priori predicted precision); (ii) achieved |delta| <= 5e-10 (about 3 x delta_CODATA) unless the route itself
  predicted a larger error before looking (then delta_eff enters (i) and the claim is scored at that predicted precision); (iii) zero real parameters fitted to alpha; (iv) the scale of alpha is stated.
  These thresholds are declared conventions, not results.

## Scope / not done

* No derivation is proposed. Grammar-size estimates use a specific declared grammar; a different grammar changes N and the constants but not the scaling law (expected hits proportional to N x tol). No claim is made about
  formulas outside the grammars other than through the MDL size estimate in the checker (an upper-bound style count, tested against E2 in d2).
* The quoted history of Wyler's formula is from secondary sources as stated above.
* kappa = 1/2 stays FITTED; the SM mass sector stays walled; no dark-matter particle.
