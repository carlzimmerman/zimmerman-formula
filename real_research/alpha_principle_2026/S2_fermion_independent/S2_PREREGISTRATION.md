## What is compared (the Q1 numbers that are the targets)

  sigma_HFY(M) = (1/(3 pi^2)) [ ln M - Re psi(iM) - pi M (4 M^2 + 1) / (3 sinh 2 pi M) ],   sigma_c/H = e^2 sigma_HFY = alpha G_f(M),  G_f = 4 pi sigma_HFY.

Two NUMERICAL results of Q1 are not coefficient identities and are the targets:
* T1 (light end): the additive constant in G_f = (4/(3 pi)) (ln M + gamma_E - 1/6) + O(M^2), i.e. c_Q1 = 3 pi^2 sigma_HFY - ln M -> gamma_E - 1/6 = 0.410548... as M -> 0.

NOT counted as a target: the coefficient 1/(3 pi^2) of ln M equalling the one-loop Dirac running times |nabla F| (a coefficient identity; it is reproduced along the way but scores nothing).
Sign convention (physical, fixed independently of Q1): the current component ALONG THE FORCE on the charge, J_par; Q1's J_HFY > 0 means along the force (Q1's own V1s check at large L).

## The routes (two chosen, plus one method validation)

### Route B -- the exactly massless limit (chosen (b) of the brief). Scheme stated: "on-shell at mass m": the renormalization condition is that the flat-space vacuum polarization vanishes at q^2 = 0 for the massive field, then m -> 0.
Flat massless Dirac field on the conformally related Minkowski space with A_z(tau) = -E/tau (F_{tau z} = E a^2, force along -z), linear response (weak field, first order in E). Ingredients:
 (1) Weyl map (sympy): for g = a^2 eta the massless Dirac operator on psi = a^{-3/2} Xi is a^{-5/2} times the flat one; A_mu has weight zero. So the Bunch-Davies state is the flat vacuum.
 (2) Flat vacuum polarization, on-shell scheme: Pi_hat(Q^2) = -(2 alpha/pi) Int_0^1 x(1-x) ln(1 + x(1-x) Q^2/m^2) dx -> -(alpha/(3 pi)) [ln(Q^2/m^2) - 5/3] for Q^2 >> m^2 (Q^2 = q^2 in mostly-plus signature, = -omega^2 for a homogeneous field). Effective Lagrangian -(1/4) F (1 + Pi_hat) F; induced current J^nu = d_mu(Pi_hat F^{mu nu}).
 (3) Retarded response of the flat theory to D(tau') = d_tau F^{tau z}(tau') = 2E/tau'^3 at tau = -1: analytically through the Laplace transform of the plus-distribution ([1/u]_+ with L{[1/u]_+}(s) = -gamma_E - ln s), numerically, independently, through the exact spectral representation of the MASSIVE
     Pi_hat (Pi_hat = -(alpha/(3 pi)) Q^2 Int_{4m^2}^inf ds R(s)/(s (s + Q^2)), R = (1 + 2m^2/s) sqrt(1 - 4m^2/s), retarded Green function sin(omega u)/omega) at m = 0.1, 0.03, 0.01, 0.003, 0.001.
 (4) Weyl-anomaly term: the curved-space effective action is the flat one plus (2 alpha/(3 pi)) ln a(x) inside the F^2 term (the D-dimensional counterterm a^{D-4} F^2 times the 1/epsilon pole); coefficient checked by (a) the sympy pole algebra and (b) demanding that the physical current be independent of tau (dS invariance).
 (5) Assemble J_par and read sigma_HFY: predicted (by the ingredients, no Q1 number entering) sigma_HFY(M) = (1/(3 pi^2)) (ln M + c_B) + O(M^2), where c_B is EVALUATED by the script from the numerical ingredient constants (not typed in).
