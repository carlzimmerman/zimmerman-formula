## Question

The campaign found that every route to alpha ends at a free ultraviolet boundary value or a free charged spectrum. This lane goes into the one frame the campaign
had not touched: the ALGEBRAIC derivation of the charged spectrum (Gunaydin-Gursey, Dixon, Furey, Furey-Hughes, Todorov-Dubois-Violette, Krasnov, Boyle, Baez-Huerta,
Nesti-Percacci). For each construction: (1) does it reproduce the charges and hypercharges of one generation by explicit computer algebra; (2) what does it FIX and what
does it leave FREE; (3) does the group-theory normalisation of the gauge kinetic term (embedding index) force alpha_em / alpha_GUT or only a ratio; (4) could it supply the
charged SPECTRUM, and does an ultraviolet boundary value remain free. Target if a coupling VALUE were forced: alpha = 1/137.035999177 (Thomson limit); every claim carries its scale.

## Disclosed hand expectations (made while reading, BEFORE any computation; not blind)

* Furey's Q = N/3: the algebra allows Q = cN for every real c (the hermitian form generates u(3) = su(3) + u(1)_N, and the coefficient r0 is free); the value 1/3 is the unique
  one (mod integers) for which charge mod 1 tracks colour triality as in the SM. Expect: c FREE in the algebra, c in 1/3 + Z from an SM correlation.
* Q lies outside so(6) = su(4) (trace 4 on the 8): Q - 1/2 lies inside. Expect: the "+1/2" is not generated by the Cl(6) bivectors.
* The un-modified adjoint action of su(3) + u(1) on Cl(6) = End(V) will not by itself give the content (20) of 1910.08395; the four-block prescription (19) will.
* Furey-Hughes: the *-invariant subalgebra of the whole so(10)_A is so(7) + so(3) (dim 24, because sigma_1..sigma_7 are real, sigma_8..sigma_10 imaginary), and only its
  intersection with the chosen g_sm is su(3) + u(1)_em (dim 9). The relative weight in Y = T3R + (B-L)/2 is not fixed by anomaly cancellation (lane G: the whole
  Cartan plane span{T3R, B-L} is anomaly-free on a 16).
* F4: the SM subgroup is the intersection of Spin(9) with (SU(3)long x SU(3)short)/Z3; the 26 has hypercharge ratios (3,2):(3,1):(1,2) = 1:2:3 with a UNIQUE Cartan direction;
  sin^2 theta_W at a putative F4-symmetric point is 3/4 (NOT 3/8); alpha_3 : alpha_2 : alpha_Y = 2 : 1 : 3. E6: the commutant of SU(3) x SU(2) has rank 3, so the hypercharge
  direction is NOT unique there; 3/8 is one point of a family.
* Nothing computed here will force a coupling VALUE; the expected verdict is: charge RATIOS and integers (N_c = 3, thirds) are fixed given the algebra, the SM
  hypercharge direction and the unit of charge are matched to the SM, and the overall coupling(s) and the ultraviolet boundary remain free.

## What would count as a lead (declared)

A construction that forces (i) a dimensionless coupling VALUE (not a ratio of couplings, not an integer, not a charge ratio) at a stated scale, or (ii) alpha_G itself through an
algebraic (not fitted) normalisation of the gauge kinetic term. Any such value goes at once through lane U3's joint three-coupling scorer and lane D's bar (`alpha_bar_checker.py`)
BEFORE anything is reported. A ratio (3/8, 3/4, N_c, charge thirds) is NOT a lead: it is listed under FIXED. Declared expected outcome: no lead.
