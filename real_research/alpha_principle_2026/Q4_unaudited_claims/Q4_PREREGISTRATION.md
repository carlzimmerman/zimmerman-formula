## Per-claim protocol (fixed now; identical for all)
(a) Locate the primary document (arXiv / Zenodo / Preprints.org / journal); record how it was read: FULL TEXT (fetched as PDF/HTML to disk and read in full or the relevant sections, with pdftotext where PDF),
    SUMMARY (a WebFetch model summary), ABSTRACT, or SNIPPET (search result only). A claim read below FULL TEXT gets no structural credit for content not read (K's policy).
(b) State the claim precisely: the formula (or model), the stated value of 1/alpha, the stated scale and the stated uncertainty.
(c) Free choices: integer count F of discrete choices (integers, exponents, constants, operations, conventions), fitted reals R (numbers set from CODATA or from the target), and 'derived vs asserted' per choice.
(d) Miss vs T = |value/T - 1|, sigma = miss / 1.6e-10; where a source quotes an older CODATA value the miss is ALSO reported against that value (info only; the audit target is 2022).
(e) Checker, three ways as in K: P_MDL (checker default shape count, intmax = max(12, largest integer in the formula)); P_forced (N = 1; the steelman); P_decl (size declared in a later Amendment from the
    free choices actually documented in the source, written after reading and BEFORE running). Also c1-c4. A claim with no closed evaluable formula is scored on its stated number only (no P_decl).
(f) Strongest criticism: published if found; otherwise constructed and labelled 'constructed'.
(g) Structural verdict with K's rule: S1 mechanism, S2 zero fitted reals and independently defined dimensionless inputs, S3 a testable statement beyond alpha, S4 not closed by lanes A,B,C,E,F,G,N1-N5 and not refuted by CODATA on its own terms.
    Scored 0/1 each, evidence line per score. 'Deserves a proper pre-registered test' iff S1 = S2 = S3 = 1 (S4 may be judged separately) -- declared now; a claim with 3/4 failing only S4-by-CODATA does not qualify.
A claim is a NUMERICAL WIN only if all four bar criteria hold under P_decl AND under P_MDL. 'Passes only if every choice is forced' is reported separately (P_forced).
If a claim clears the bar: it is reported as a CANDIDATE for adversarial follow-up, then attacked in this lane as hard as I can (independent re-implementation of the value; enumeration of the family the source itself implies;
sensitivity to each choice; test against a second, structurally similar target such as the muon g-2 coefficient or the proton/electron mass ratio only as a control of the SAME grammar, never as a claim about the SM mass sector, which is walled) before reporting.
Sources that cannot be found, or that are not derivations at all (no evaluable value, purely verbal), are reported as such; no verdict beyond that.
