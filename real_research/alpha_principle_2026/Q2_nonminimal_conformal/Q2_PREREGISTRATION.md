## Amendment 3 (2026-09-28, BEFORE the first run of q3, disclosed; found by inspecting a symbolic intermediate, not from any q3 output)

While testing the building blocks of q3 in a scratch script (symbolic angular integral I(p; mu, s), one Delta value, one f_0 value; none of the q3 direct sums or checks had been run) I printed the exact angular integral and found
I(p; mu, s) = lam p^2 (3 mu^2 - 2 p^2) / (12 (p^2 + mu^2)^{7/2}), i.e. INDEPENDENT of the second-order constant s (the s-term is proportional to p_z/Omega^3, odd in the angle).
Consequences (each is a correction to what H3/H4 above declared, made before any q3 number existed):
1. H4 said Delta is an absolutely convergent integral. It is not: the leading tail of p_z [S~_a - S~_b] is -(Dm2) p_z/(4 p^3) with Dm2 = (mu_a^2 + s_a) - (mu_b^2 + s_b), a linearly divergent, angle-odd integrand, so its value depends on the SHAPE of the cutoff. The direct sums (and AH4's closed form) use a radial cutoff in k; the p-space (spherical, kinetic-momentum) integral differs from it by a surface term. Derived by the shell argument: Delta_k = Delta_p - lam Dm2/3. Hence, with Dm2 = (mu_eff^2 - 2) - (mu_sub^2 + s_sub):
   Delta = (lam/3) ln(mu_sub/mu_eff) - (lam/3)(mu_eff^2 - 2 - mu_sub^2 - s_sub),   where Delta_p = (lam/3) ln(mu_sub/mu_eff) (elementary integral; q3 S3 checks it numerically to 1e-12).
2. The subtraction must be the WKB expansion of the equation actually solved: the matched rule is s_sub = mu_eff^2 - 2 - mu_sub^2 (so that Omega_sub^2 + s_sub/tau^2 reproduces the true potential). For a massive field with mu_sub = mu this is s = 12 xi - 2 as declared. For the exactly massless conformal scalar with an IR reference mass mu_s it is s = -mu_s^2, NOT s = 0 as declared under H9/direct set C. With the matched rule the surface term vanishes and f = f_0(lam, mu_eff) + (lam/3) ln(mu_sub/mu_eff) exactly; a mismatched s adds a gauge-non-invariant term proportional to lam (mu_eff^2 - 2 - mu_sub^2 - s_sub).
3. Direct set C is therefore run as 5 matched points (s = -mu_s^2) as intended, plus the 2 pre-registered mismatched variants (s = 0; points (0.3, mu_s = 1) and (0.8, mu_s = 2)) as a test of the surface-term formula (the p-space-only Delta of H4-literal is printed next to them and is expected to FAIL there; that failure is recorded, not scored as a check on the physics). Direct points: 3 + 9 + 5 + 2 = 19 in the real run.
4. Predictions derived by hand from item 1 BEFORE running q3: (i) f_xi(lam, mu) = f_0(lam, sqrt(mu^2 + 12 xi)) + (lam/3) ln(mu/sqrt(mu^2 + 12 xi)) (the explicit log keeps the Lagrangian mass; xi enters through the mode index); (ii) at heavy mass F_xi -> (C0 - 2 xi)/mu^2, i.e. c1 = -2 exactly at leading order (test: |c1 + 2| < 5e-3 from the Richardson coefficients); (iii) at fixed modes the coefficient of ln(mu_sub) is lam/3 for every xi (H5), with the matched rule; (iv) for the massless conformal scalar f(lam; mu_s) = f_0(lam, sqrt 2) + (lam/3) ln(mu_s/sqrt 2), an exact log in the scale at which the charge is defined.
