## Part 1: consistency bounds  (script i1_consistency_bounds.py, i2_stellar_window.py)

### i1
* L1 (Landau pole above the Planck mass). One-loop QED, 1/alpha(mu) = 1/alpha_0 - (2/(3 pi)) sum_f N_c Q_f^2 ln(mu/m_f) for mu > m_f.
  (a) electron only: alpha_max = 3 pi / (2 ln(M_Pl/m_e)) in closed form (checked symbolically); (b) toy "QED with all SM charged fermions" with fixed PDG-like masses as inputs (declared list: e, mu, tau, u, d, s, c, b, t with MSbar/pole-type masses; the quark-mass ambiguity is handled by reporting the edge for two quark-mass conventions); the requirement is Landau scale >= M_Pl (non-reduced, 1.22e19 GeV) and also >= reduced M_Pl. This is a TOY: real electroweak running (W, hypercharge) differs, lane A found the fermion-only running misleading above m_Z; I report that the toy edge is an upper edge of a toy and NOT a claim about the SM. PASS (as a computation) if the edge is finite and the closed form for (a) matches the numerics; the edge is scored only as a band edge.
  Expected (by hand): (a) about 0.09, (b) about 1.5-2 alpha_0.
* L2 (atoms: Dirac Coulomb problem). E_1s = m sqrt(1 - (Z alpha)^2) is real only for Z alpha < 1 (point nucleus). Declared requirement: chemistry with elements up to Z_req = 26 (iron), edge alpha < 1/26. Also Z=1 alone: alpha < 1. Symbolic derivation from the Dirac Coulomb formula. Expected: edge 1/26 = 5.3 alpha_0.
* L3 (positivity/unitarity). (a) Euler-Heisenberg for a Dirac fermion: L = (2 alpha^2/(45 m^4))[(E^2-B^2)^2 + 7 (E.B)^2]; both positivity coefficients are positive for EVERY alpha > 0 (sympy). Expected: positivity gives NO constraint on alpha in QED-alone. (b) with gravity (Cheung-Remmen, abstract only): the graviton-exchange contribution scales like alpha/(M_Pl^2 m^2) against alpha^2/m^4; their ratio ~ m^2/(alpha M_Pl^2) gives a LOWER edge alpha >~ (m_e/M_Pl)^2 with an unknown O(1) constant. I report this scaling only (constant set to 1, flagged as convention, and the resulting 1e-45 edge is scored as a band edge with 40 orders of slack). Perturbative unitarity: alpha/pi < 1 as the loop-expansion parameter (convention, not scored beyond a coarse edge alpha < pi).
* L4 (hydrogen stability against e + p -> n + nu). Requires m_n - m_p > m_e (neutrinos massless). Decompose (recalled) m_n - m_p = D_QCD - D_EM * (alpha/alpha_0) with D_QCD = 2.05 MeV, D_EM = 0.76 MeV (both with recalled uncertainty ~0.3 MeV on D_EM), m_e held fixed. Edge: alpha/alpha_0 < (D_QCD - m_e)/D_EM. Reported with the D_EM uncertainty propagated. Recalled inputs; flagged.

## Part 2: measure and selection arguments  (script i3_vacuum_energy_alpha.py)
Question: does Lambda_eff depend on alpha in a way that gives an extremum of the Hartle-Hawking weight W = exp(S_dS), S_dS = pi/(G H^2) = 3 pi/(G Lambda) (the pi/(G Lambda) form in the task brief differs by the convention factor 3; irrelevant to extremum questions)?
* V1: d ln W/d alpha = -(3 pi/(G Lambda_eff^2)) dLambda_eff/d alpha: symbolic. An interior extremum requires d Lambda_eff/d alpha = 0; otherwise W is monotonic in alpha and the maximum sits on a boundary (Lambda_eff -> 0+ where W diverges, or the edge of an anthropic band).
* V2: derive d Lambda_eff/d alpha explicitly from the QED vacuum energy: rho_vac = rho_1loop(m) + rho_2loop(alpha, m) in dimensional regularisation, MS-bar. Two-loop Dirac + photon (Feynman gauge) "theta" diagram, reduced to A(m)^2 and the vacuum integral I(m,m,0), the latter from the closed-form position-space Bessel integral (checked numerically at fractional d). Then (V2a) check that non-local poles cancel and that the L^2 coefficient equals the RG-predicted one (this validates sign and normalisation); (V2b) extract rho_2 = alpha m^4/(16 pi^2) (a2 L^2 + a1 L + a0), L = ln(m^2/mu^2); (V2c) convert to fixed POLE mass with the recalled one-loop relation m_pole = m_MS[1 + (alpha/pi)(1 + (3/4) ln(mu^2/m^2))] and get c_alpha(L) = d rho/d alpha as a function of the scale mu at which the vacuum-energy counterterm is defined; (V2d) show that c_alpha depends on mu (the counterterm runs at two loops), hence the "alpha-dependence of Lambda_eff" is defined only after choosing which combination is held fixed; check whether c_alpha has a zero in L and how it moves with mu.
* V3: magnitudes: m_e^4 / rho_Lambda ~ 1e34; the resulting |d ln W/d alpha| and the required precision of the bare Lambda_0 for a boundary solution alpha* to land within 1e-3 of alpha_0 under a Planck-scale prior on Lambda_0 (declared prior: Lambda_0 uniform in [-M_Pl^2, M_Pl^2] in the boundary equation Lambda_0 + 8 pi G rho(alpha*) = 0).
* Declared expectation: NO interior extremum from the perturbative QED dependence (monotonic, linear in alpha at this order); the boundary solution alpha* is a function of an undetermined Lambda_0; the stated scale-dependence of c_alpha makes the entire dependence a scheme choice. The route yields no alpha.
* MUTATE for i3: flip the fermion-loop sign in the two-loop vacuum energy; the RG check of the L^2 coefficient (V2a) must FAIL. `python3 i3_vacuum_energy_alpha.py MUTATE` exits 1.
