# Fine-structure lane (AH1-AH4): what it does and does not show

*For the closure map. Written 2026-09-28. Commits: AH1 5db88bfc2 (amendments 147b1498c), AH2 5823f6bfe, AH3 Lean 86f3c7a9a, AH4 0c72bfdec.*

**What it does and does not show.** This lane tests one concrete route from the programme's inputs (c, G, Lambda, kappa = 1/2, the last still FITTED) to the fine structure constant alpha: the Schwinger/Unruh pair production and induced current of a charged scalar in de Sitter space, with the horizon's electromagnetic response tied to kappa. It shows that the route does not fix alpha. The pair-production factor depends on the charge only through lambda = eE/H^2, so alpha is a free direction (AH1; the mathematics is machine-checked in Lean, AH3: ten theorems, standard axioms only, mutation controls fail as required). The induced current does carry e^2, but the response is sigma/H = alpha * G(m/H) with G varying by orders of magnitude with the mass, so a tie on the conductivity fixes only alpha times a mass function, never alpha (AH2 in dS_2, AH4 in dS_4, each computed by a direct mode sum that reproduces the published closed form to 1e-6 and 2e-5). Heavy fields respond exponentially weakly in dS_2 but as a power, about 0.124/mu^2, in dS_4, where the ln(m/H) term is the one-loop running of e, so alpha enters as the measured low-energy input; at the electron mass over H_0 an O(1) horizon-conductivity tie would need alpha of order 1e77 (an extrapolation). The value E/H^2 = 1.651 that the tie eE = kappa H^2 would need to give 1/137.036 is a target for a missing input, not a derivation, and no natural handle declared in advance reaches it. What it does **not** show: that alpha is underivable by any mechanism (it is a no-go for observables of this class, not for the physics); anything about fermions, backreaction, other charged species or nearly massless fields, all untested; and the dS_2 results (AH1-AH3) are a two-dimensional toy in which e has mass dimension, so only the structural statement (rate depends on eE) and the tie arithmetic carry to 4D, while the dS_4 scalar result is AH4 alone. Lean certifies the mathematics of the no-go; it does not prove alpha, because there is no proof of alpha to check. The SM mass and coupling sector stays walled and this lane leaves it untouched; kappa = 1/2 stays fitted.
