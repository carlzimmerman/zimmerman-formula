## #1 — The "one scale" unification is PARTIAL (a correction to my own overclaim)

I have been saying *dark matter and dark energy are two faces of one scale — 95% of the universe from Λ.* **Worked
adversarially, that is only true at galaxy scales.** Quantified (`verified inline`):

- **Galaxies** (isolated, g < a₀): the a₀-modification reproduces rotation curves with no dark matter. Here the
  unification holds — galaxy "dark matter" = a₀ = √Λ. *Validated on 175 SPARC galaxies to ~10%.*
- **The CMB**: the acoustic peaks and P(k) require a **clustering, dust-like component of density Ω_dm ≈ 0.27.**
  a₀ is a *force* modification, not an energy density — it contributes **zero** to Ω. So the CMB's dark matter is
  supplied by AeST's **separate 𝒬-field** (engineered to behave like dust) or by sterile neutrinos — *a second dark
  component, not tied to Λ.*
- **Clusters / the bullet cluster**: even deep in MOND, clusters under-predict by ~2× (the residual-mass problem),
  and the bullet cluster's lensing-vs-gas offset needs *collisionless* mass. a₀ supplies neither.

**So the dark sector has *two* pieces, not one:** a₀ ∝ √Λ (galaxy phenomenology) **and** a separate 𝒬-field/neutrino
dust (the cosmic Ω_dm of the CMB and clusters). The honest claim is *"the framework unifies the galaxy-scale
dark-matter phenomenology with dark energy"* — **not** *"95% of the universe is one scale."* I overstated it; the
cosmic dark matter is not Λ.

## #2 — It inherits all of MOND's hard problems, and fixes none of them

The framework *is* MOND at galaxy scales, so it carries MOND's unsolved failures: the **cluster residual** (~2× missing
even in deep-MOND), the **bullet cluster** (collisionless lensing mass), and the **CMB third peak** (needs the extra
field content). The a₀ ∝ √Λ idea touches none of these — they are inherited wholesale. If any one of them is
ultimately fatal to MOND, it is fatal to the framework.

## #3 — The √ρ_DE reading is favored but not forced; if it's really √ρ_total, the framework is disfavored

The entire distinctive content rests on a₀ ∝ √ρ_DE (dark energy) rather than √ρ_total (Hubble/total density). Four
things favor √ρ_DE (the literal formula; "MOND as a vacuum effect"; the high-z TF data, Limbach/Milgrom; the B1
de-Sitter-entropy derivation), **but the apparent-horizon thermodynamic derivation (Cai–Kim) is rigorous and gives
√ρ_total.** The fork is *selected, not proven.* If √ρ_total is the correct emergent-gravity reading, the framework
predicts the steep rising a₀(z) that the data already disfavor — i.e. it is in trouble. This is a genuine, live
vulnerability, not a settled question.

## #4 — The distinctiveness is hostage to DESI's contested result

Under a *constant* Λ, the framework predicts *constant* a₀ — observationally **identical** to ordinary MOND. Its
only distinctive, falsifiable content (the declining a₀(z)) exists **only if dark energy is dynamical**, which rests
on DESI's ~3–4σ, SN-sample-dependent, actively-contested evolving-DE preference. If DESI reverts to w = −1, the
framework has *no distinctive prediction left* — it collapses to "MOND, with a₀ ≈ cH₀ by coincidence."

## #5 — The central causal claim may be untestable

"a₀ is *set by* Λ" is a causal claim. But under constant Λ it makes the *same* predictions as "a₀ ≈ cH₀ is a
coincidence." The only way to demonstrate causation (not coincidence) is the a₀(z) ∝ √ρ_DE bridge — which (per #4)
needs dynamical DE. So the framework's deepest claim is **unfalsifiable unless DESI's dynamical DE holds.** A causal
claim that is untestable in the (still quite possible) constant-Λ universe is a real philosophical weakness.

## #6 — The un-derivable coefficient is a yellow flag

32π is GR-traceable but route-forced; no mechanism (symmetry, bound, transmutation, DSSYK, heat-kernel) uniquely
fixes it, and B1 derives the *form* but not the number. That a "fundamental" relation has an un-derivable O(1) is a
mild numerology flag. *Mitigant:* the coefficient-free bridge sidesteps it for the empirical test — but it remains a
hole in the theory's claim to be derived rather than fitted.
