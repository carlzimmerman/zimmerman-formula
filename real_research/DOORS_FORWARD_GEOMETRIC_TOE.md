## 0. The one-paragraph orientation

horizon's surface gravity, a₀ = cH/Z, and the first direct data say it *evolves* rather than being a constant.**
That is a genuine geometric reference point — it ties sub-kpc dynamics to the size of the Universe. But the deep
review delivers a hard boundary: **geometry/horizon physics can reach the gravitational and dark sectors, and
*cannot* reach matter.** "Deriving the Standard Model from a₀ or a geometric number" is, on the unanimous
evidence of the literature, a **category error** — the same trap as the abandoned Z²→SM route. So the honest
target is not "geometry → everything," but **"geometry → gravity + the dark sector, from the horizon"** — which
is still a serious, publishable, partly-original program. Below: what we hold, the landscape, and the tiered
doors.

## II. The honest landscape (what the field can and cannot do, mid-2026)

**Emergent gravity (Jacobson, Padmanabhan, Verlinde).** Exactly *one* rigorous result: Jacobson (1995) — the
Einstein equations follow from δQ = T dS on local horizons *given* area-entropy. It is an **equivalence, not a
derivation from below**: it presupposes the area law and predicts neither G, nor Λ, nor a₀'s coefficient.
Padmanabhan's holographic equipartition reproduces Friedmann but **fixes its prefactor to Bekenstein–Hawking by
hand** (this is exactly why our Z cannot be claimed as "derived"). Verlinde 2017 is the weakest link — its MOND
limit is internally disputed (Dai–Stojkovic 2017 recover *Newton*) and it fails dwarfs, clusters, and the RAR
shape (Lelli+2017). **Lesson: lean on the dimensional horizon statement, never on Verlinde's elasticity.**

**De Sitter / DSSYK holography.** This is the **one place in quantum gravity where geometry provably emerges from
a quantum operator**: Blommaert–Mertens–Papalini (2024) show DSSYK = sine-dilaton gravity, with the bulk length =
chord number, quantization *discretizing* spacetime. That is the real geometric foundation. Caveats, loud: it is
**static-patch only** (global/FRW de Sitter is unsolved); the emergent bulk is generically a "fake geometry with
a defect" sweeping dS/AdS/Minkowski, not pure de Sitter; and the identification our sign-derivation needs — **de
Sitter = the spectral *center* (E=0, infinite temperature)** — is **actively contested**: Rahman (2025) derives
dS-JT at the spectral *edge* (E=E₀) instead, and Susskind himself issued a Nov-2025 entropy correction. The field
is *splitting on exactly the point we depend on*. (Independent support for the spirit of the center reading:
Chandrasekaran–Penington–Witten's rigorous Type-II₁ static-patch algebra, whose max-entropy state = empty de
Sitter has the flat spectrum the "center" wants.)

**Matter from geometry — the wall.** All three reviews converge, with zero counterexamples: in *every*
emergent-gravity framework (Jacobson, Padmanabhan, Verlinde, DSSYK, Bianconi 2025), **matter is inserted by
hand** — the score on "derive the matter content" is 0-for-everything. Connes' noncommutative geometry best
*classifies* the SM's algebra but inputs the gauge group and three generations, made one sharp prediction (170
GeV Higgs) that was *falsified*, and carries unsolved signature/fermion-doubling problems. String/F-theory is the
*only* place chiral generations *emerge* from geometry — via index theorems — but in ~10²⁷²⁰⁰⁰ vacua with **no
selection principle**. Chirality from any local/entanglement substrate is blocked by **Nielsen–Ninomiya**. And
connecting an IR horizon scale (a₀ ~ cH ~ 10⁻³³ eV) to UV group theory (the SM) is a **category error** — there
is no symmetry, anomaly, or RG argument linking them. **This is the same numerology the Z²→SM route died of.**

## III. The doors, tiered by tractability × payoff × honesty

### TIER 1 — Tractable now, high-value, defensible (months; publishable)

**1a. Bulletproof the a₀(z) discriminator: does a₀ track H(z) or √Λ? — THE door.**
This is where our validated-in-direction content lives and it settles a 40-year-old ambiguity. cH(z) (rising) and
√Λ (≈constant) diverge with z; the data can decide. The work is *not* new theory — it is making the measurement
unkillable: (i) confront Milgrom (2017) head-on by showing the MUSE RAR fit reaches the *deep-MOND* outer disk,
not the baryon-dominated interior; (ii) re-extract the MUSE slope **model-independently** (rotation-curve-only,
no DC14 halo) to test whether the rise is physical or halo-model-induced; (iii) push a clean, gas-traced a₀ to
z ≈ 2–3. *Payoff:* a genuine discovery (a₀ evolves) or a clean null. *Risk:* low (data exist/are coming).
*This is the recommended primary path.*

**1b. Pin the coefficient: is Z forced or conventional?**
No one has enumerated *why* the horizon→a₀ coefficient is 2π (Milgrom), 1 (Verlinde), or our 2√(8π/3). A careful
paper listing which de Sitter surface-gravity convention (using ρ vs H vs Λ; with/without the equipartition ½;
which horizon) yields which O(1) — and whether any *fixes* the number — is a real clarifying contribution. *Be
prepared for the honest answer "it's convention-dependent."* *Payoff:* medium (clarity, credibility). *Risk:* low.

**1c. The rate law: what density gives a₀ ∝ (1+z)^≈1.3?**
MUSE rises ~30% faster than ∝E(z) (≈√ρ_total) and slower than ∝(1+z)^1.5 (≈√ρ_matter). If the rise is real, *what
geometric quantity* interpolates? A concrete, bounded derivation target now that the *sign* is provisionally
data-pinned. *Payoff:* medium-high (could expose the right horizon variable). *Risk:* medium (may be systematics).

### TIER 2 — Frontier, higher-risk, real (the geometric foundation for *gravity*)

**2a. Adopt DSSYK sine-dilaton gravity as the geometric substrate.**
This is the concrete realization of your "geometric reference points": **bulk length = chord number**, spacetime
discretized by quantization. Engaging Blommaert–Mertens–Papalini and the chord formalism is the honest way to
build "geometry from a quantum operator" for the gravitational sector. *Payoff:* high (a real microscopic geometry).
*Risk:* high (static-patch only; hard formalism; you are a consumer of a fast-moving field).

**2b. The center-vs-edge robustness test — the crux for our microscopics.**
Our deep-MOND *sign* and coefficient assume de Sitter = the spectral **center**. Rahman (2025) says **edge**. The
sharp, decidable sub-problem: **does the sign survive the edge reading?** If it *only* works at the center, that
is not a weakness — it is a *prediction* (our MOND result becomes evidence *for* the center identification). The
sine-dilaton "metric as a function of energy" framework is the natural arbiter. *Payoff:* high (ties our result
to a live QG dispute). *Risk:* medium-high (depends on settling, or at least bounding, a contested question).

**2c. The decoherence lab bridge.**
If a₀ = cH(z)/Z means gravity is literally horizon-entropic, the 2025 entropic-force/decoherence formalism
predicts an interferometric decoherence rate. Compute it and compare to neutron/atom-interferometry bounds. A
rare bridge from a *cosmological* claim to a *lab-testable* one. *Payoff:* high if it predicts something near a
bound; *Risk:* medium (may predict an unobservably small effect).

### TIER 3 — The geometric-TOE aim, honestly scoped

- **Gravity + dark sector from the horizon: REACHABLE.** Horizon thermodynamics (Jacobson) + DSSYK emergent
  geometry (chord = length) + a₀ as the horizon scale + the dark-energy connection (a₀ ~ √Λ at order unity) form a
  coherent geometric program *for gravity and the dark sector*. This is a legitimate "geometric theory" — just not

- **Matter / the Standard Model: NOT REACHABLE from this framework.** Unanimous across the review: matter is

So the precise, honest framing of the aspiration: **build the geometric theory of gravity and the dark sector
anchored on the horizon; do not attempt to reach matter.** That is ambitious enough, and it is real.
