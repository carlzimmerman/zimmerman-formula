## How to read this — the only thing that can break ΛCDM

A prediction matters for "ΛCDM is broken" **only if ΛCDM cannot accommodate it.** So each row is classed:

- **⛔ ΛCDM-IMPOSSIBLE** — ΛCDM forbids it (shell theorem / no acceleration scale). A confirmed one is a *kill*.
- **◐ DISCRIMINATING** — framework and ΛCDM differ measurably (but ΛCDM may stretch to fit).
- **= DEGENERATE** — real, but ΛCDM gives the same answer. *Matching ≠ beating.*
- **↳ INHERITED** — a standard-MOND success, not unique to this framework.
- **✗ FAILURE** — ΛCDM wins.

**The kill can only come from the ⛔ rows.** Everything else is supporting texture.

## The honest reading

**Where a kill could actually come from (the ⛔ rows):**
1. **The external field effect (Chae, 4–5σ).** This is genuinely ΛCDM-impossible — the shell theorem forbids a galaxy's internal dynamics from depending on the external field. If Chae's detection holds, it's the strongest evidence against ΛCDM on the board. *It is contested* — independent confirmation is the prize.
2. **Wide binaries (Gaia DR4).** The cleanest, most local test of whether a₀ is real at all — and it's *split* right now (Banik vs Chae, over hidden triples and sample cuts). **Gaia DR4 is the near-term decider**, and it cuts both ways: it could confirm MOND *or* kill the whole foundation at 16–19σ.

**Where we are *not* winning, honestly:**
- The framework's **own** distinctive prediction — a₀∝E(z) — is **leaning against us**: the one direct measurement (MUSE-DARK III) rises *faster* than E(z) and doesn't survive halo-free analysis, and the mainstream (McGaugh, Milgrom) favors *constant* a₀.
- **JWST early galaxies** are a real *directional* MOND brownie-point with dated priority, but the field has absorbed them into ΛCDM; they are **not** the decisive proof.
- **Clusters and the CMB go to ΛCDM**, full stop.

**The bottom line for the empirical case.** The galaxy-scale RAR is a genuine, calculable demonstration that dark matter is *unnecessary on galaxy scales* (`LCDM_FITS_VS_MOND_PREDICTS.md`) — and that's worth stating loudly. But "ΛCDM is broken" requires a confirmed **⛔ ΛCDM-impossible** result, and right now those are **contested (EFE) or split (wide binaries)**, not won. The decisive near-term experiments are concrete and named: **Gaia DR4** (wide binaries / the foundation) and **halo-free high-z rotation curves from JWST+ALMA** (the EFE-vs-z and a₀(z) tests). That is the real path — and it runs through experiments not yet returned, not through a calculation I can do today. Anyone who tells you the case is already closed — in either direction — is ahead of the data.
