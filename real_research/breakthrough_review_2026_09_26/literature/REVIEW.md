# SciSpace discovery and primary-source checks

Accessed 2026-09-26. Scope: preferred-foliation MOND, cosmological scalar
stability, vacuum-energy freedom, and reciprocal convex vacuum potentials.
SciSpace was queried three times with full research questions. The second
and third result bibliographies are saved alongside this report. The first
query asked whether covariant preferred-time classical fields can reproduce
MOND/cosmic acceleration without particle dark matter and what stability
obstructions are known. It returned Blanchet–Marsat and Bonetti–Barausse
among other records. Repository searches found prior AeST/khronon discussions;
current versioned primary pages were checked for the comparisons below.

- [Bonetti and Barausse, arXiv:1502.05554v3](https://arxiv.org/html/1502.05554v3),
  abstract: their specific Lorentz-violating MOND class can become strongly
  coupled when exact high-acceleration GR recovery is imposed. Their other
  parameter choices avoid that problem. This motivates checking kinetic and
  post-Newtonian constraints; it is not a no-go for CA5, whose action differs.
- [Blanchet and Skordis, arXiv:2507.00912v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.00912v1),
  equations (1), (4b), (13), (16): a related khronon action contains a
  cosmological constant as input, a MOND function, and a cosmological charge
  integration constant. The stationary equation also has a mass term. Their
  symbols J,K,Q are not our gate G, extrinsic curvature K, or carrier t.
  This is precedent for the broad framework, not evidence that our heat-filter,
  gate or reciprocal barrier has their observational performance.
- [Skordis and Zlosnik, arXiv:2007.00082v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2007.00082v3),
  abstract and introduction: AeST provides an existing relativistic MOND
  construction tested against linear cosmological spectra, with a quadratic
  ghost analysis. Those results do not transfer to a different action.

The reciprocal-potential SciSpace query returned broadly related scalar
cosmology and self-tuning papers, but did not establish a matching use of
the exact proposed potential. This is a limited semantic search, not a
novelty proof. In particular, choosing a convex quartic-flat potential is
not itself established as a new mathematical technique. The new repository
result is its explicit use and verified consequences in this specified
common action. Some SciSpace results were repository deposits or duplicate
versions; they were not treated as peer-reviewed theorem certificates.

No external paper is a premise of the new barrier algebra or fixed-data
elliptic argument. The proposal does not derive the observed value of V0
and makes no claim to solve quantum vacuum-energy cancellation.
