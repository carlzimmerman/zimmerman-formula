# Bounded attribution check for the carrier construction

Checked 2026-09-20. Source question: is the fixed-Y power-law carrier with an affine equation of state new?

Primary source: Claudia Quercellini, Marco Bruni and Amedeo Balbi, *Affine equation of state from quintessence and k-essence fields*, [arXiv:0706.3667v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0706.3667v2), 5 September 2007. The version label and equations were read in the primary PDF through the browser tool; no local PDF copy is claimed.

Section III.B, equation (25), gives `P=-rho_Lambda+c chi^[(1+alpha)/(2alpha)]`, with density `rho_Lambda+(c/alpha) chi^[(1+alpha)/(2alpha)]`. Its adjacent discussion identifies constant characteristic speed squared `alpha`; equation (3) gives the corresponding density scaling.

Dictionary at a fixed Y: `alpha=1/(2m-1)`, source kinetic variable `chi=X/X0` after constant normalization, `c=rho0/(2m)` and `rho_Lambda=rho0/(2m)`. Thus the constant-Y power law and affine EOS are **known after translation of notation**. Our symbolic work checks their use and normalization directly.

The checked paper does not establish the present gradient-dependent exponent, the all-X homogeneous decoupling, coupling to the repository's clock/MOND action, or finite-gradient health. No global novelty claim is made for those extensions. Search scope was the local closure/research tree and primary-source web searches for affine EOS, purely kinetic k-essence, and constant sound speed. This is an attribution check, not an exhaustive novelty review. No external observational bounds from that paper are imported into this audit.
