# Lane 3 — The No-Dark-Matter Relativistic-MOND Landscape

Goal: survey every relativistic MOND / no-DM theory that (i) bends light correctly and
(ii) is single-metric GW170817-safe (c_GW = c), rank them, and locate the framework's
best home — the road closest to keeping **a0 = c²√(Λ/32π) = cH_Λ/Z = 9.36e-11**
(Z = √(32π/3) = 5.789), the de-Sitter–Unruh interpolation ν(y)=√(1+1/y), AND lensing
AND Cassini Q2 AND GW-safety — and what it costs.

Footings carried throughout: a0 = 9.36e-11 (canonical, ρ_DE / cH_Λ) vs 1.13e-10 (alt, ρ_tot / cH0).

## The structural gate (established, published — trilemma v2, Zenodo 21310146)

Pure modified-INERTIA CANNOT lens (trilemma theorem + anisotropic-slip loophole closed:
T_μν ~ ρ_m baryon-confined + traceless-compact → far-field slip is a 1/r³ quadrupole,
cannot lens). **Correct no-DM lensing REQUIRES touching curvature → modified GRAVITY.**
So every viable entry below is MG-like, not MI-like. This is why the framework's *own*
MI core must borrow a curvature-touching completion to lens at all.

## The a0 verdict up front (the honest core finding)

**No relativistic no-DM theory in the literature DERIVES a0 = c²√(Λ/32π).** Every one —
AeST, Khronon, nonlocal Deffayet–Woodard, f(Q), TeVeS, Verlinde — takes a0 as a **free
input** buried in a free function, a nonlocal kernel, or a coupling. Woodard's nonlocal
model *inserts* a0 ≈ 1.2e-10 by hand; AeST/Khronon fix a0 via the free function J(Q).
The framework's a0-from-Λ derivation is therefore an **add-on the whole field lacks**,
NOT a feature any external host supplies. The single place a0=cH_Λ/Z is *native* is the
framework's OWN Branch B medium (stiffness tied to Λ). This is the load-bearing price:
borrowing lensing from external MG means a0 reverts to a tunable constant.

## The honest answer: the framework's best home

There is **NO single no-DM theory that simultaneously (a) keeps a0=cH_Λ/Z, (b) lenses,
(c) passes Cassini Q2, and (d) is GW-safe — all proven.** The field forces a genuine FORK:

- **To KEEP the a0=c²√(Λ/32π) derivation** (the framework's true retracted-down position,
  the a0-reframing) → **Branch B** is the only home. It lenses, is GW-safe, and its
  stiffness ties a0 to Λ natively. **Cost:** Cassini Q2 is MARGINAL — passing needs the
  free shear Poisson ratio β stiffer than natural (fails ×1.1–1.8 at natural values). Not
  yet a clean pass.

- **For the cleanest COMPLETE lensing+GW+likely-Cassini theory** → **Nonlocal
  Deffayet–Woodard MOND** is the best external host. Single metric, lenses correctly,
  GW-safe *by construction* (MOND term ~ two Ricci factors → vanishes for radiation), and
  its **exponential** PN screening is the one structure that could sit far below the Q2
  ceiling where the vector class (AeST/Khronon) fails. **Cost:** a0 is a **free input** —
  Woodard inserts ≈1.2e-10, does NOT derive 9.36e-11.

**The cleanest synthesis (and the honest recommendation):** the two roads are
complementary, not competitors. Nonlocal MG supplies what MI structurally cannot —
lensing + GW-safety + (plausibly) Cassini. The framework supplies what nonlocal MG
lacks — a *derivation* of the a0 Woodard puts in by hand. The prize theory would be a
nonlocal metric MOND whose kernel scale is FORCED to c²√(Λ/32π) rather than fitted. That
forcing is **not demonstrated by anyone**, and is the single most valuable uncomputed
result in this program — alongside the equally-uncomputed nonlocal Cassini Q2 quadrupole.

**Cost, stated plainly:** every external road charges the a0 derivation (it becomes a
free constant); the framework's own Branch B keeps the derivation but charges Cassini
margin. Neither is free. No road currently pays *both* prices in full.
