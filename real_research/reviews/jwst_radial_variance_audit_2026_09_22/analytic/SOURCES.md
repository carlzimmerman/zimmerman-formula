Verified primary mathematical source: Zhaoyang Liu, Yong Jiao, Guoxin Liu,
*Measure-Valued Generators of General Piecewise Deterministic Markov Processes*,
arXiv:1704.00938v3, 26 April 2017.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1704.00938v3
PDF header, Theorem 5.5 (equation 5.5), Definition 5.7 and Theorem 5.8
(equations 5.7-5.10) inspected. The generator consists of the derivative along
the deterministic flow plus rate times the jump-kernel difference; the
compensated difference is a local martingale. The theorem requires pathwise
regularity and locally integrable jump increments. Here the flow is straight
flight, rate is k, jump kernel is Thomson, and the functions are smooth and
bounded on each finite time interval. Bounded rate gives integrable jump sums
on those intervals. The separate escape-tail and dominated-convergence proof
in THEOREM.md supplies passage to unbounded escape time. The source does not
state our photon variance bound; this is methodological overlap, not a
novelty certificate. Time is included as an extra deterministic coordinate.
