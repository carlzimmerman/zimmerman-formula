# Source scope for the whole-layer attempt

Checked 2026-09-22. This was an application check, not a novelty search.
The new geometric, Gaussian and counterexample arguments are derived in
`PROOF.md`; no external Yang–Mills theorem is assumed to supply their missing
nonlinear extension.

J. Dimock, *Multiscale block averaging for QED in d=3*,
[arXiv:1712.10029v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1712.10029v3), version stamped
3 March 2020, manuscript dated 4 March 2020. The primary PDF's title page
and introduction, pp. 1–2, were checked.

The model is three-dimensional Euclidean Abelian QED, with a real gauge
potential and Grassmann fermions. Its introduction separates bounded-field
analysis from control of the complementary large-field regions and discusses
propagators, minimizers and effective densities under block averaging.
This is relevant methodological context. It does not identify its covariance
estimates with our exact Hamiltonian Schur reduction, nor provide the required
non-Abelian 3+1-dimensional actual-vacuum coercivity estimate. No such
implication is credited to this source, and no audit of its full proofs is
claimed.

Discovery searches covered exact Balaban/Dimock block-averaging and
fluctuation-covariance terminology, including arXiv and publisher records.
No claimed new mass-gap solution encountered in broad search was used as
evidence. The earlier YM-C1 record of the unavailable exact Balaban theorem
is not upgraded here. No primary PDF is redistributed in this checkpoint.
