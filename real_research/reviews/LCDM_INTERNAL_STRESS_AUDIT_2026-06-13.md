## The 4 genuine cracks (real ammunition, used carefully)

1. **The CC magnitude problem** — ΛCDM treats the 10⁻¹²² vacuum-energy value as a measured input it cannot
   derive; 35+ years (SUSY, quintessence, sequestering, landscape) yields no consensus. A genuine failure of
   *explanation* at the foundation — but **not a data tension** (the value is precise and stable). **Use as:**
   "ΛCDM cannot derive Λ — and neither can my framework; but my framework at least gives Λ a *local observable
   job* (it sets a₀ via a₀=cH_Λ/Z)." **Forbidden** (your own `CKN_LAMBDA_VALUE_VERDICT` forbids it): "I solve
   the CC problem" — the seesaw is an algebraic identity that *relocates* the smallness into √(E_Hubble/E_Planck),
   explaining none of it.

2. **The Hubble tension** — a live ~5σ (worst-case ~3.9σ in the 2026 sound-horizon-free census) parameter-
   consistency crack three independent CMB instruments (Planck, ACT DR6 67.6, SPT) and 15+ years have failed to
   close, with no successful new-physics competitor. **Honest reading:** the leading resolution is now a *Cepheid
   first-rung distance-ladder systematic*, not broken expansion physics — and the framework does **not** fix it
   (a₀ is an output of H0). Cite as a real ΛCDM stress; never as a framework win.

3. **The undetected DM particle** — 40 years, billions spent, the WIMP window swept to the neutrino fog with
   zero signal; axions null so far. **But** the gravitating dark *component* is positively detected (Bullet
   lensing offset, Ω_c h²=0.8%) — so it's a *promissory-note* weakness (the particle is unconfirmed), not a
   non-detection of dark mass. And the framework still needs a dark component for clusters/CMB.

4. **DESI evolving dark energy** — DESI+CMB already disfavors constant-Λ at ~3.1σ frequentist (up to ~4.2σ with
   recalibrated SNe), all three SNe samples + three CMB pipelines pushing the same way (w₀>−1, w_a<0). The
   **single ΛCDM stress sign-aligned with the framework**: w₀>−1, w_a<0 ⇒ ρ_DE lower in the past ⇒ a₀∝√ρ_DE
   *declines* into the past, the exact shape `DESI_AND_THE_A0Z_TEST.md` commits to. **But not vindication:**
   DESI measures the *input* ρ_DE(z), not a₀(z) (a redshift survey, not kinematics) — by construction it cannot
   confirm the prediction; and the faithful a₀ shift is scatter-sized (~0.033 dex at z=3 vs ~0.026 dex BTFR
   scatter), tolerated-but-undetected. **Decided by:** DESI DR3 past 5σ **AND** a dedicated z~3 deep-MOND disc
   kinematics campaign. Honest pitch: "sign-aligned; the decisive test is high-z kinematics, not DESI, pending
   DR3" — never "DESI confirms evolving a₀."
