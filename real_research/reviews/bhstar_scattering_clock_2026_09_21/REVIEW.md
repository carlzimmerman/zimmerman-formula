# Adversarial self-review

This is an author self-review, not independent peer review.

1. **Stopping bias:** scattering number is correlated with direction and path
   length. The proof averages the instantaneous generator over ALL photons;
   it does not substitute an independent geometric distribution for N. Direct
   photons and the final uncompleted free flight belong in the accounting.
   The deliberate scattered-only selection fails, as expected.
2. **Two independent stochastic checks:** Maxwell velocities produce frequency
   increments, angular sums compute their conditional quadratic variation, and
   analytically integrated flight segments compute density-temperature exposure.
   Agreement is assessed with paired Monte Carlo errors, not a guessed percent
   threshold. The result was not used to tune a tolerance after failure.
3. **Transport scope:** unpolarized Thomson phase function, nonrelativistic
   thermal velocities, linear Doppler increments, no photon destruction or
   bulk flow. Physical recoil/Compton drift is not simulated. The mathematical
   statement is exact in this specified approximate model, not full QED.
4. **Observer selection:** geometry independence of total escaping luminosity
   does not imply geometry independence of a line of sight. Rotational symmetry
   supplies that bridge in the observational example. Reflected-only or
   occulted observations need their own transfer calculation.
5. **Density:** n_e differs from n_H in recombining gas; the relevant density is
   that along the photon paths, not necessarily that in an absorption slab.
   Empty gaps invalidate the positive-density time bound. This is the main
   missing observation, not a technical nuisance to fit away.
6. **Line width:** the theorem uses the FULL second moment. FWHM alone does not
   bound variance without a profile assumption. Unseen broad tails, host-narrow
   dilution and missing direct light are serious limitations. The numbers use a
   specified normalized Laplace distribution, not an actual fitted LRD profile.
7. **Time:** rest-frame P is a Fourier period, not an observing baseline. A
   variability non-detection does not measure suppression relative to an unknown
   input. Distributed-source light-travel and excitation delays are distinct
   from the additional scattering delay bounded here.
8. **Units:** n_cm^-3 becomes n_m^-3 with 1e6. At 1e8 cm^-3, the mean Thomson
   flight time is about 5.8 days. The code checks this separately. An early
   mental estimate was too short by 100; only the corrected dimensional result
   is used in the report. At 1e8 cm^-3 the proposed 200-day exclusion disappears.
9. **Catalogue:** median of luminosity ratios is descriptive; joint posterior
   samples are unavailable. No measurement-error likelihood, completeness
   correction or global significance is claimed. The bootstrap cannot certify
   a constant, and the 1/15 target was noticed after examining the median.
10. **Novelty:** variance accumulating with electron-scattering exposure is
    standard physics. The result is a usable missing consistency check in the
    inspected campaign, not evidence for a new force or a Kepler-grade law.

Remaining work that would change the scientific status: obtain matched,
complete line profiles and independent electron-density constraints for a
source with a justified driving light curve, then fit a transfer model that
includes line creation/destruction and directional selection. The current
public summary catalogue cannot supply those inputs.
