Primary source: Guillaume Bal and Alexandre Jollivet, *Time-dependent angularly
averaged inverse transport*, author manuscript dated 7 May 2009, 30 pages,
https://galton.uchicago.edu/~guillaumebal/PAPERS/InvTrAngAverTime.pdf .
Published in Inverse Problems 25, 075010 (2009),
https://doi.org/10.1088/0266-5611/25/7/075010 .
The separate extended preprint is arXiv:0902.3432; numbering here refers to
the inspected author manuscript, not an assumed identical preprint version.

Read Sections 2.1–2.4 and 4.1. Theorems 4.1–4.2, especially (4.3),(4.6), state
three-dimensional logarithmic single-scattering and excess-time times squared
logarithm multiple-scattering bounds for bounded kernels. Section 4 says proofs
are supplied in the extended version. These are strongly adjacent orders.

Their source and measurement points are on the boundary, their variable tau
is travel time, and their excess is tau minus boundary-point separation. Here
tau is opacity, emission is central, and D=T-x_exit dot u_exit. No exact mapping
between the measurement operators has been proved. Classification: adjacent
result; exact equivalence and priority remain unverified. The elementary proof
in ALL_ORDERS.md does not depend on this source.
