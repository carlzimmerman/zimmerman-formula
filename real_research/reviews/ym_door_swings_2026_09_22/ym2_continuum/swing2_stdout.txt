====================================================================================================
  d=2: |xi| =    -2  |A| =     0  |A dA| - |xi| =    1   |A^3| - |xi| =    2   -> subcritical
  d=3: |xi| =  -5/2  |A| =  -1/2  |A dA| - |xi| =  1/2   |A^3| - |xi| =    1   -> subcritical
  d=4: |xi| =    -3  |A| =    -1  |A dA| - |xi| =    0   |A^3| - |xi| =    0   -> CRITICAL
  d=5: |xi| =  -7/2  |A| =  -3/2  |A dA| - |xi| = -1/2   |A^3| - |xi| =   -1   -> supercritical
  [PASS] R1a d = 2 and d = 3 are subcritical (where the 2D/3D stochastic Yang-Mills constructions of Chandra-Chevyrev-Hairer-Shen live)
  [PASS] R1b d = 4 is EXACTLY critical (margin 0 for both nonlinearities): regularity structures and paracontrolled calculus do not apply
  => route R1 needs a theory of CRITICAL singular SPDEs. None exists; in the critical scalar analogue, phi^4_4 is trivial
     (Aizenman & Duminil-Copin 2021). Yang-Mills differs by asymptotic freedom, which is exactly what a critical theory would
     have to capture. Door R1: CLOSED with current mathematics (not a gap in this repo, a gap in the field).

R2  analytic continuation of the strong-coupling series into the scaling window
====================================================================================================
  SU(3) Wilson roughening beta_R = 5.8-5.9 (t_R = 0.4 +/- 0.01 in d = 4)
  scaling window (09-22 table) starts at beta = 5.7; m_G a there = 1.43
  [PASS] R2a the SU(3) roughening point lies INSIDE the scaling window: surface observables (Wilson loops, string tension) cannot be continued from strong coupling into it  (window from 5.7, roughening 5.8-5.9)
  Roughening is a SURFACE singularity: it does not obstruct bulk quantities such as the glueball mass m(beta).
  For m(beta) the continuation route needs two statements, and both are open:
   (i)  analyticity of the SU(3) Wilson theory on the whole real axis 0 < beta < inf (no bulk transition) -- not proved;
   (ii) the beta -> inf asymptotics m(beta) ~ C a(beta) Lambda with C > 0 -- this IS the continuum problem.
  [RECORD, not a check] R2b the bulk route does not bypass the problem: its requirement (ii) is the continuum statement itself

R3  an RG bridge: block-spin steps from the scaling window to a strong-coupling-like correlation length
====================================================================================================
  beta = 5.7: m a' after 2^k blocking, k = 0..3: 1.43, 2.87, 5.73, 11.46
  beta = 6.0: m a' after 2^k blocking, k = 0..3: 0.78, 1.57, 3.14, 6.27
  beta = 6.2: m a' after 2^k blocking, k = 0..3: 0.57, 1.14, 2.28, 4.56
  [PASS] R3a from beta = 6.0 two block-spin steps (factor 4) already bring the correlation length below half a block spacing  (k = 2, xi/a' = [1.27, 0.64, 0.32, 0.16])
  So the missing control spans only O(1) blocking steps in SCALE. What is missing is not distance but a CLASS of estimate:
  the blocked measure is not a Wilson action (multi-plaquette, non-local couplings, large-field regions), and no theorem puts
  it inside ANY convergent expansion or Dobrushin/Dobrushin-Shlosman regime. Balaban's multiscale analysis controls blocked
  actions only while the effective coupling is small (small-field, perturbative remainder).
  Computer-assisted Dobrushin-Shlosman checks on a finite box are the one concrete way to attack this, but they need rigorous
  integration over ~10^4 SU(3) link variables with boundary conditions. That is far beyond interval arithmetic today.
  [RECORD, not a check] R3b the bridge is short in scale, but no rigorous estimate of the needed class exists (open problem)
