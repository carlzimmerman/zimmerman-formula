the metric's balance. Does the framework's a0 sector have one with kappa = a0 and Lambda A = 32 pi^2?

  Einstein-aether/khronon black hole with Lambda; compute r_UH, kappa_UH, A_UH.

A MUTATE control (a corrupted effective-metric coefficient) must flip each branch's verdict.
