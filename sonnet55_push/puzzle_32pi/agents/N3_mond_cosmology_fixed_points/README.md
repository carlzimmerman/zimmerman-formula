# N3: MOND applied to the expansion with Lambda -- fixed points and self-consistency

## Bottom line
**Verdict: SHARP NO-GO (scoped) + one structural result.** The shell equation R'' = -nu(g_N/a0) g_N + H^2 R (H = H_Lambda, Lambda = 3H^2, c = 1) has exactly one fixed point for every mass and every monotone nu, it is ALWAYS a saddle (lambda^2/H^2 = 1 + 2 dln g/dln g_N, between 2 (deep MOND) and 3 (Newtonian); never marginal), and its radius is (GM a0)^(1/4)/H (deep MOND) or (GM/H^2)^(1/3). These contain a0, H and M as independent parameters: no fixed point, stability threshold or energy condition fixes a0/H. The only homogeneous (M tied to rho_Lambda) closure gives a radius, not Z: with the vacuum's own density and R* = c/H it gives Z = 2x* = 0.58 to 1.0 (nu(x*) = 2), about six times too small; hitting 5.789 needs an inserted density factor f = 1.705 (simple nu: f = 2Z/(Z+1), so any Z incl. decoys 4.5/7.3/8.1 is reachable). kappa = 1/2 stays FITTED. a0(z): such a closure gives a FLAT a0 only if the variable is Lambda (H_Lambda); the total-density homogeneous closure has no fixed point at all for z > 0.296, so it cannot be a law at z = 2.5.

Criteria were declared and hashed first (`CRITERIA.md`, `CRITERIA_HASH.txt`; `n32` re-checks the hash). Scripts: `n31_dynamical_system.py` 23/23, `n32_homogeneous_closure.py` 20/20, `n33_a0z.py` 8/8 (first runs of n32/n33 kept as `*_firstrun.out`: two of my own test thresholds/assertions were wrong, fixed openly; no physics claim changed).

## Closures (stated)
- **C1**: Lambda kinematic (unmodified, a cosmological-constant term), point mass M at the centre, shell R(t) of test particles. MOND enters only through g = nu(g_N/a0) g_N, g_N = GM/R^2. For a spherical point source AQUAL/QUMOND and any algebraic nu coincide (checked: mu = 1/nu monotone inversion, 5 interpolating functions incl. the RAR family), so "Newtonian-form" and "AQUAL-form" give the identical shell equation. (Relativistic completions -- AeST, khronon, BIMOND -- were NOT opened here; I state no result for their FRW backgrounds beyond this spherical static-source equivalence.)
- **C2**: Lambda as a negative-pressure source inside the MOND operator (g_N,total = GM/R^2 - H^2 R).
- **C3**: homogeneous closure M = (4 pi/3) f rho_Lambda R^3, i.e. g_N = f H^2 R/2 (Friedmann: 8 pi G rho_Lambda = 3H^2), f = 1 the vacuum's own density.
Variable held fixed throughout: **Lambda (H_Lambda)** for the static results; in n33 also H(z) and rho_m for comparison.

## Results
1. **Dynamical system (n31).** Deep MOND: R_ZF^2 = sqrt(GM a0)/H^2, i.e. R_ZF = (GM a0)^(1/4)/H (dimension checked), versus the Newtonian turnaround (GM/H^2)^(1/3). Deep-MOND validity x = g_N/a0 = sqrt(GM H^4/a0^3) < 1 iff M < M_c = a0^3/(G H^4) (= 9.4e20 Msun for a0 = 1.1e-10, H_Lambda = 55.8 km/s/Mpc; = (2/Z^3) of the vacuum mass in the Hubble sphere). Numerical fixed point unique over GM = 1e-8..1e8, five nu. Linearisation: lambda^2 = H^2 + 2 (g_N/R) dg/dg_N = H^2 (1 + 2 dln g/dln g_N): 2 H^2 deep MOND, 3 H^2 Newtonian; ODE growth rate 1.54235 vs analytic 1.54241. Mutations: a constant outward force instead of H^2 R gives lambda^2 = 0.94 (outside [2,3], so the bracket is specific to Lambda ~ R); attractive Lambda has no fixed point. C2: the fixed point is the Newtonian R^3 = GM/H^2 (g = 0 iff g_N = 0) but the force is ~ |delta|^0.5 there (slope 0.501), non-analytic, still repulsive outside and attractive inside. Illustration only (isolated, no external field): M = 2e12 Msun gives a turnaround 7.4 Mpc (MOND, scaling M^(1/4)) versus 1.4 Mpc (Newtonian); the observed local zero-velocity surface ~1 Mpc is not compared here (external-field effect and the mass uncertainty not modelled). Criteria: S1 PASS (exists, unique), S2 "marginal for some nu/M" FAIL -> always unstable, S5 (energy): the deep-MOND potential is sqrt(GM a0) ln R - H^2R^2/2, log, so the barrier energy depends on an arbitrary reference radius; no closure (not scripted beyond this analytic remark).
2. **Homogeneous self-consistency (n32).** nu(x*) = 2/f, R* H = 2x*/(f Z). Vacuum density (f=1): x* = 1/2 (simple nu), 1/sqrt12 (standard), (ln 2)^2 (RAR) => with R* = c/H, Z = 1.00, 0.577, 0.961. That is the same equation as "horizon = zero-force radius for the vacuum mass in the Hubble sphere" (nu(Z/2) = 2). The deep-MOND version of that coincidence is inconsistent: at Z = 5.789 x = Z^(3/2)/sqrt2 = 9.9 >> 1, exact R_ZF/R_H = 0.84 not 1 (deep-MOND formula (2Z)^(-1/4) = 0.54). Z as a pure number is obtained only by inserting f and the radius ratio s (Z = 2x*/(f s)): S3 FAIL. Matching freedom: f = 1.705 for 5.789, 1.677 for 3 sqrt3, 1.714 (= 12/7 exactly) for 6, 1.725 for 2 pi, 1.64/1.76/1.78 for decoys 4.5/7.3/8.1; a one-parameter interpolation family (delta = 0.47 at f = 1, s = 1) also reaches all of them. f within +-20% of 1 gives Z <= 1.5: a genuine miss, not an absence of discrimination. S6: no target is distinguished; nothing to count as a match.
3. **pi-content.** nu, x*, f -> Z is algebraic (simple nu: Z = f/(2-f), no pi); pi could only enter through the inserted f or s, or through going from Lambda to rho_Lambda (held-fixed-variable dependence: in the Lambda form nothing is pi-carrying). The puzzle's pi (Z^2 = 32 pi/3) is therefore not produced; it would have to be supplied by the coefficient.
4. **a0(z) (n33).** (i) Static point-mass closure C1: depends on H_Lambda only, so a0 flat in z is the self-consistent reading (constant Lambda), z-independent by construction. (ii) If instead a constant Z ties a0 to H(z): x3.77 at z = 2.5 (x1.79 at z = 1); to sqrt(Om)(1+z)^1.5 H0: x6.55. (iii) The homogeneous total-density closure f(z) = 1 + rho_m/rho_Lambda needs f < 2, i.e. z < 0.296; there the implied a0 (radius = c/H(z)) is 0.25 H0 at z = 0 falling >30x to 0 at z = 0.296: not a law. At z = 2.5, f = 20.7 and ANY nu (>= 1) decelerates the shell. Shell-level q = 0 would need nu = 2 OL/Om(z) = 4.35 at z = 0, 0.93 at z = 0.67, 0.10 at z = 2.5. So MOND on the expansion cannot be the origin of a0 at high z, and the a0(z) discriminator (flat vs H(z), sibling lane Z1) is not touched by this mechanism: flat comes from the choice of Lambda as the variable, H(z) from the choice of H.

## Verdict
SHARP NO-GO (scoped to the shell/boundary closure, C1-C3): fixed points and stability are a0/H-independent facts; the homogeneous closure fixes a radius and needs two inserted numbers (f, s) to give Z; with f = 1, s = 1 it gives Z ~ 1, not 5.79.
