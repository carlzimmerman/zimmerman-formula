## 2. Newtonian action and the localisation of field energy (s02)
- Metric -(1+2 e phi)dt^2 + (1-2 e psi)dx^2: the O(e) term of sqrt(-g)R is a total divergence; (1/16 pi G)(sqrt(-g)R)_2 has the same Euler-Lagrange operators as (1/8 pi G)[(grad psi)^2 - 2 grad phi . grad psi]; adding -rho phi gives psi = phi and **lap phi = 4 pi G rho** (mutations: 1/(4 pi G) gives 8 pi G rho; dropping the cross term leaves phi unconstrained). So the Poisson-normalised 1/(8 pi G) is derived: L_field = -(1/8 pi G)(grad phi)^2.
- **Sign / localisation.** Static energy E = -L: gradient part +g^2/(8 pi G), interaction rho phi, on-shell total (1/2) int rho phi = -(1/8 pi G) int g^2 (uniform ball: gradient-only +3GM^2/5R0, on shell -3GM^2/5R0). Same total M, four different local partitions: +1, -1, -1 (BY), -7. Consequence for the puzzle: u_g(a0) = a0^2/(8 pi G) is a Poisson-normalisation coefficient; "field energy density equals vacuum energy density" has no covariant meaning, only the total field energy (BY) does.
