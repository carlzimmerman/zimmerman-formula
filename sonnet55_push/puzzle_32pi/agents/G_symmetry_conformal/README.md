# Lane G: can a symmetry fix a0/sqrt(G rho_Lambda)?  (c = G = 1; Lambda = 32 pi a0^2  <=>  G rho_Lambda = 4 a0^2)

## Bottom line -- verdict: SHARP NO-GO for a symmetry-only origin (nothing derived; kappa = 1/2 stays FITTED)
1. **Exact dichotomy (Lindemann).** With kappa = a0/sqrt(G rho_Lambda) = (a0/H) sqrt(8 pi/3): if a0/H is a de Sitter / conformal representation number (algebraic), kappa is transcendental and cannot be 1/2; if kappa = 1/2, then a0/H = sqrt(3/(32 pi)) is transcendental and is no representation number. "a0/H from SO(4,1) data" and "kappa = 1/2 exactly" exclude each other. Only the pi-free statement G rho_Lambda = 4 a0^2 (coefficient 4) is symmetry-accessible in principle.
2. **Every symmetry I could attach is blind to that coefficient.** Dilatation acts on (a0, Lambda) with Lambda/a0^2 invariant; the AQUAL field equation does not contain the additive constant of its Lagrangian (which IS a vacuum energy); the conformal group of 3-space is SO(4,1) but the dS Killing fields do not contain H; the vacuum has T^mu_mu = -4 rho_Lambda while the deep-MOND field has spatial trace 0 and four-trace -u (same sign, so no cancellation, and no amplitude relation).
3. **What the dS symmetry does supply is a crossover at a = H (Z = 1)**, a factor 5.79 above the framework a0, plus the Casimir numbers {0, 5/4, 2, 9/4}: none is 1/Z^2 = 0.0298 or Z, and none could be (transcendence).
4. **Only new number, honestly a relocation:** reading the AQUAL constant as the vacuum energy gives G rho/a0^2 = c/(8 pi), c = int(1-mu)dy, a functional of the far tail of mu (1/3 ... infinity). A RAR-shaped mu gives 1.03 (vs 4); c = 32 pi needs a tuned tail exponent s* = 0.4085. Not a derivation.

## pi-counting first: what a symmetry-only route can and cannot deliver
Representation theory of SO(4,1), SO(4,2), SO(4) produces rational or algebraic numbers (Casimirs Delta(3-Delta), dimensions, degeneracies l(l+3), (l+1)(l+2)(2l+3)/6). pi appears only through (a) Einstein's 8 pi G (Gauss-law solid angle), (b) an invariant-measure volume or area (Vol S^2 = 4 pi, Vol S^4 = 8 pi^2/3), (c) a thermal 2 pi, (d) a loop factor 16 pi^2 with hbar. None is a Casimir eigenvalue.
- CAN: fix the *form* a0 proportional to H (H is the only scale of the dS structure); fix algebraic ratios between quantities normalised the same way (Lambda = 3 H^2); fix field weights and exponents (Delta_phi = 0 for deep MOND, tracelessness iff F = y^{d/2}).
- CANNOT: give the exact 32 pi/3 (transcendental) for a0^2/H^2; fix an invariant of a symmetry that acts on parameters; see the additive constant (vacuum energy) of the a0-sector; know the value of H at all.
- The two statements: `Lambda = 32 pi a0^2` (coefficient 32 pi, transcendental: not symmetry-accessible) and `G rho_Lambda = 4 a0^2` (coefficient 4, algebraic: accessible in principle). The conversion between them is Einstein's 8 pi G, so the algebraic form only helps if the a0-sector's own stress scale is a0^2/G times an algebraic number. The scale the sector supplies is the Poisson-normalised sigma0 = a0^2/(8 pi G) (field energy of a field of strength a0), and rho_Lambda/sigma0 = 32 pi. That normalisation is a coupling, and dilatation invariance does not fix couplings.

## Results
**R1 dichotomy (g01).** Symbolic reduction kappa = (a0/H) sqrt(8 pi/3) (A1-A2). No polynomial relation (degree <= 8, coefficients <= 1e5) for kappa^2 over ten rational and four algebraic-irrational choices of (a0/H)^2, nor for 3/(32 pi); the same search finds an algebraic control, and the mutation (8 pi -> 8) makes kappa algebraic, so the test sees the pi. The integer-relation search is evidence only; the theorem is Lindemann's. Consequence: an algebraic Z_alg is within reach of the data (Z = 6 is 3.6% from Z = 5.789, kappa = 0.4824, Z = 2 pi is 8%), but any of them gives kappa exactly different from 1/2. Data that resolve a0 to ~10% cannot separate them.

**R2 Casimir / spectra (g04, g05).** Scalar: Box eta^Delta = H^2 Delta(3-Delta) eta^Delta; massless minimally coupled 0, conformal 2, principal-series edge 9/4 (m^2/H^2). Spin 2: the helicity-2 mode of the covariant equation obeys the same relation (massless graviton Delta = 0,3; partially massless and Higuchi edge m^2 = 2H^2, Delta = 1,2). The Higuchi bound itself is quoted from the literature, not recomputed here. The Casimir of SO(4,1) built from the bulk Killing fields equals -Box/(6H^2). If one insisted a0 be an inverse-length mass of a light dS field, m^2/H^2 = 3/(32 pi) = 0.02984 gives Delta = 0.00998: complementary series, nearly massless, nothing special, and it is the same statement as a0/H = 1/Z. The look-elsewhere test (menu written in the file with the target known, so it characterises and does not discover): 0 of 62 candidates within 2% of Z; 50% of random targets in Z*[1/2,2] would have had a hit, so a hit would have meant nothing. Nearest: spin-2 Casimir s(s+1) = 6 (+3.6%), an inserted map, not a result.

**R3 The group of deep MOND is the dS group and cannot see H (g04).** Conf(R^3) has Killing signature (6,4): it is so(4,1) (abstractly the de Sitter isometry group). The deep-MOND static equation (the 3-Laplacian) is invariant under all 10 conformal transformations with phi of weight 0, whose Casimir is 0 (the massless minimally coupled scalar, Delta = 0); the Newtonian Laplacian needs the Kelvin weight Delta = 1/2 (Casimir value 5/4 in dS units). So both regimes are conformally symmetric with the same group and different representations, and a0 is where the representation changes; the group itself carries no scale. The ten dS Killing fields in flat slicing contain no H (verified with symbolic H): a constant rescaling of the metric leaves them unchanged, so no group-theoretic statement can fix a0 L. The identification of the MOND R^3 with the boundary of dS is formal; L drops out of it.

**R4 The dilatation acts on parameters; the puzzle number is an invariant of it (g02).** The unit group (L,T,M) on (a0, Lambda, G, c, rho_Lambda) leaves two dimensionless invariants; one is Einstein's 1/(8 pi), the other is Lambda c^4/a0^2 = 32 pi. The deep-MOND field equation is covariant under (t,r) -> q(t,r) at fixed mass (9 random cases, deviation 7e-16); a uniform vacuum density is not (the dilated solution solves the equation with rho_L -> q^-3 rho_L), so Lambda carries explicit scale weight and the dilatation maps (Lambda, a0) -> (Lambda/q^2, a0/q). A symmetry acting on couplings cannot constrain the invariant. Separately, the Euler-Lagrange equation of L = -(a0^2/8 pi G) F(y) - rho phi depends on F' only: F -> F + C changes it by nothing but shifts the energy density by a0^2 C/(8 pi G). So the vacuum energy of the a0-sector is unconstrained by any symmetry of the field equation.

**R5 Traces and the scale current (g03).** With T^{ij} = (1/4 pi G) mu phi_i phi_j - delta_ij (a0^2/8 pi G) F (force balance verified for generic F, phi): T^i_i = (a0^2/8 pi G)(2 y F' - d F) is identically zero for F = y^{d/2} (d = 2..6) and equals -g^2/(8 pi G) for Newton in d = 3. Deep MOND: u = g^3/(12 pi G a0), T^i_i = 0, four-trace -u, effective w = 0. Vacuum: T^mu_mu = -4 rho_Lambda, p = -rho. Both four-traces are negative, so dilatation restoration by cancellation is impossible with a positive-energy deep-MOND field (a cancelling component would need w > 1/3), and the Ward identity d_mu D^mu = T^mu_mu relates each sector to itself only. The scale-current flux through a large sphere around a point mass is exactly (2/3) sqrt(G a0) M^{3/2}, the exact deep-MOND virial coefficient: the dilatation charge is where M^{3/2} lives, and it contains no Lambda. Answer to the lane's question: dilatation invariance plus the vacuum equation of state does not fix a ratio of the MOND-sector stress to rho_Lambda.

**R6 The AQUAL constant read as the vacuum energy (g02 part E).** If F is normalised to F -> y at high acceleration (no extra vacuum energy in the Newtonian region), the g = 0 state carries rho = c a0^2/(8 pi G), c = int_0^inf (1 - mu) dy; the puzzle needs c = 32 pi = 100.5. My pre-run expectation ("c = O(1), 30-300x too small") was WRONG and is corrected: c depends on the far tail of mu. Sharp transition 1/3; 1 - exp(-x): 2; x/(1+x^n)^{1/n}: 1.0 (n=3), 0.60 (n=4), 0.43 (n=6); the simple and standard forms diverge (linearly and logarithmically); tail exp(-x^s): c = (2/s) Gamma(2/s), s = 1/2 gives 24. The RAR-shaped mu gives c = 25.98, i.e. G rho/a0^2 = 1.03, 3.9 times below the puzzle's 4; c = 32 pi is reached only at s* = 0.4085, a tuned exponent. The data do not constrain the tail beyond g ~ 10 a0. This relocates the puzzle into a tail exponent of mu; it does not derive it. I claim no significance for the 1.03 and note it is an accident of an empirical fit's tail.

**R7 What the dS symmetry does deliver (g06).** For static observers a = H x/sqrt(1-x^2) and, by Tolman, T_loc = sqrt(a^2 + H^2)/(2 pi) exactly. The only intrinsic scale of this function is a = H; the inertia mu_V = a/sqrt(a^2+H^2) crosses 1/2 at H/sqrt(3). The abstract of arXiv:astro-ph/9805346 (opened) states the same identification, a0 = (Lambda/3)^{1/2} = H. The symmetry-derived crossover is therefore a factor Z_fw = 5.79 above the framework a0 = H/Z_fw (kappa = 2.894 vs 1/2); T(a0)/T_dS = 1.0148 is not special; the a0 Rindler length Z L is reached at static radius x = 0.1702 (bookkeeping, as in p06). To reach the data one must insert a divisor N = 5.79 (or 6, or 2 pi): an insertion.

## Not established
- That no symmetry can do it: I tested the dilatation, the conformal group of 3-space, the dS isometry group, its Casimirs, and the scale/trace Ward identities. A larger algebra in which a0 and Lambda are two deformation parameters tied by a Jacobi identity was NOT investigated (the standard kinematical-algebra classification has two independent parameters, quoted from memory, not recomputed). SO(4,2) Casimir numbers were not computed (expected also integers; unverified).
- The Higuchi bound is quoted; the Deser-Levin form is derived here only for static observers.
- Nothing here bears on why kappa = 1/2. Nothing here excludes a *dynamical* selection (a vacuum-selection or variational principle) fixing the invariant; the finding is that symmetry alone cannot.
- R6 is a check of one reading (normalisation F -> y at high g), not a claim that the a0-sector's vacuum energy is the dark energy.

## Verdict
**SHARP NO-GO** for the symmetry-only route: (i) exact incompatibility between an algebraic symmetry-derived a0/H and kappa = 1/2 (Lindemann); (ii) the puzzle number is an invariant of the dilatation acting on (a0, Lambda), so no dilatation-type symmetry constrains it; (iii) the additive constant of the a0-sector is outside every symmetry of its field equation; (iv) the dS/conformal group and its Casimirs contain no H and only algebraic numbers, and the crossover they do supply (a = H) misses the framework a0 by 5.79. Narrowing: the coefficient 4 in G rho_Lambda = 4 a0^2 must come from a coupling normalisation or a dynamical principle, and any exact symmetry-derived ratio would falsify kappa = 1/2 as an exact value while remaining within the current data's resolution of a0.
