## (2) Can any of it be tied to a0^2/(G rho_Lambda) = 1/4? Principles P1-P5 (declared first), outcomes
- **P1 sector independence: holds (by construction).** With a0 a gravity-side scale and rho = c_vac Lambda^4/(hbar c)^3, the ratio is 1/4 for every (N, nf, g, Lambda_YM, c_vac); d/d(each) = 0 (q03 C3). This is a tautology of the premise; its content is what would have to differ.
- **P2 trace/dilatation (1/4 = 1/D): a rewriting.** eps = theta/4 is 1/D (dilaton-potential check; in D = 3 the same construction gives 1/3). In vacuum rho, |T|/4, |rho+3p|/2 are the same number, so no YM vacuum calculation can prefer "a0^2 = G|T|/16" over the others. They differ off-vacuum (dust 1/4,1/16,1/8; radiation 1/4,0,1/4) and applied to the total content they make a0^2 grow x5.3 (trace) or x11.5 (SEC) by z = 2.5, opposite to the framework's flat a0(z): the 1/D reading would need restricting to the vacuum sector by hand. kappa_trace(d) = 1/sqrt(d+1) is one more function equal to 1/2 at d = 3 (0.577, 0.5, 0.447, 0.408 at d = 2..5) and does not discriminate (lane F).
- **P5 operator link: fails universality.** Tying a0^2 = C G <O> to <(alpha_s/pi)G^2> or to n needs C = b0/128 resp. b0/16 (N-dependent), so a universal C cannot give 1/4 for all N. The only N-free numbers of a Lorentz-invariant vacuum are p/rho = -1, T = -4 rho, rho+3p = -2 rho.
- **What exactly would be needed** for the 1/2 to come from the sector: (i) a0 defined in the gravity/MOND action through a coupling to a YM composite (condensate, dilaton chi, theta field), not through the vacuum stress tensor (which gravity sees only as rho); (ii) that operator's vev over eps_vac a universal pure number, independent of N, b0, nf and scheme; (iii) the coefficient C in the action fixed, not fitted. None of the computable YM ratios (condensate/eps = -32/b0, n/eps = -4/b0, and topological susceptibility = n in the dilute gas) satisfies (ii); c_vac itself (~ -(b0/4) n/Lambda^4, ~ N^2 at large N) is a non-universal O(1) number I did not compute (no lattice data used).
- **hbar, exactly.** (i) In SI, G rho_E has the units of acceleration^2 with no hbar and no c: a0^2 = G rho_E/4 is hbar-free when rho_E is an energy density (q03 C1: Pi_A = a0^2/(G rho_E), Pi_B = rho_E hbar G^2/c^7 are the only two groups). (ii) hbar does NOT cancel when rho_E is written through the sector: a0 = (sqrt(c_vac)/2) c Lambda_YM^2/(hbar E_P) ~ hbar^{-3/2} at fixed Lambda_YM, G, i.e. a0 ~ Lambda_YM^2/M_P (2.24 meV scale at H0 = 67.4, a0 = 9.36e-11 m/s^2; 2.34 meV, 1.03e-10 at H0 = 73), Pi_2 = hbar G Lambda_cc/c^3 = 8 pi c_vac (Lambda_YM/E_P)^4 = 2.9e-122. (iii) Pi_1 = a0^2/(c^4 Lambda_cc) = 1/(32 pi) for every c_vac and Lambda_YM: the sector moves Pi_2 and cannot touch Pi_1 (consistent with lane A's theorem). The YM 32 pi^2 comes with hbar (loop counting, instanton weights); the puzzle's does not.

## (4) Classical YM density of the instanton vs rho_Lambda = 3/(8 pi G L^2) (q04)

## (3) The magnitude route (q05): a fit with two free numbers
rho_Lambda/M_P^4 = 1.1e-123 (8e-121 with the reduced Planck mass). At mu = 2e16 GeV: x = 257, b0 g^2 = 1.23; at 1 TeV: 135, 2.34.
- Correction of the brief's estimate: at fixed Lambda, x = 4 ln(mu/Lambda) changes by 4 per e-fold = **9.2 per decade** of mu (rho = mu^4 e^{-x} is RG-invariant); the implied 1/g^2 drifts 3%/decade.
- A factor 4 in rho is dx = 1.39, a 0.5% shift of g^2. The two-loop invariant scale shifts the required g^2 by -2.9% (N-independent, since 2 b1/b0^2 and b0 g^2 are) and rho at fixed g by x4.2e3; one extra heavy Dirac fundamental between M_P and 2e16 GeV changes rho by x0.21; at 1e13 GeV x0.033.

Two free numbers (a discrete b0 and a continuous g) against one fitted magnitude: not a derivation, and it says nothing about the coefficient 4.
