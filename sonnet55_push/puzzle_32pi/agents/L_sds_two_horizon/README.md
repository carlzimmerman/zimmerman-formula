## The pi-count claim, checked in both variables (answer to the audit's caveat)
- Hold Lambda fixed (H = 1). K = kappa_b/H is an algebraic function of mu (elimination polynomial P(K, m) = 0 with integer coefficients, m = mu^2, l03 B); mu algebraic => K algebraic. The puzzle needs K = sqrt(3/(32 pi)), transcendental by Lindemann (imported theorem; the findpoly/PSLQ checks in l03 are only illustrations, and my first run at 60 digits produced spurious degree-8 relations, l03 C5, which is why the rule is now 100 digits demanded for <= 57 digits of freedom). So no SdS-internal algebraic principle gives Z. PROVED for that class.
- Hold G rho_Lambda fixed (u^2 = G rho_Lambda, Lambda = 8 pi u^2). Then c_rec := kappa/u = K sqrt(8 pi/3) exactly, so at any point of the family K and c_rec are never both algebraic. The a0-point has c_rec = 1/2 (rational), hence K transcendental. In these units the puzzle is the algebraic statement kappa_b^2 = G rho_Lambda/4, and a principle of that form is NOT excluded by pi-counting (this is the audit's H3 point, confirmed). What changes: the pi moves into L and r (r_b a0 = (sqrt(1+32 pi) - 1)/(32 pi)). What does not change: at the a0-point every SdS quantity other than kappa_b/a0 itself is non-algebraic in a0 units (l03 C6), and the SdS family, being a bijection onto all kappa_b/u in (0, infinity), places no constraint on c_rec. The 8 pi of the Einstein dictionary is exactly what the family cannot fix.
- Consequence: c_rec at the natural SdS points is (algebraic) x sqrt(8 pi/3): dS horizon 2.894 (the record's forced kappa = 1 kernel), Smarr point 1.294, Nariai (BH norm) 5.013; none is 1/2, and none can be.
