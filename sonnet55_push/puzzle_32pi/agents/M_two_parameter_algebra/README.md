# Lane M: a larger algebra with a0 and Lambda as two deformation parameters (c = G = 1 in the puzzle; Lambda = 32 pi a0^2, i.e. G rho_Lambda = 4 a0^2)

## Bottom line -- verdict: SHARP NO-GO (structural, computed); kappa = 1/2 stays FITTED
1. **No algebra in the class ties a0 to H_Lambda or to G rho.** Jacobi forces only relations among *rates and curvatures* (spatial curvature = (time curvature)/c^2), and the magnitude of any ratio of two dimensionful structure constants is a unit convention (rescaling weights independent, det = -4). The variety is defined over Q, so a forced ratio would be algebraic; sqrt(3/(32 pi)) is excluded twice (transcendence of pi, and because nothing is forced at all).
2. **Newton-Hooke does NOT admit an a0-deformation.** The only structure constant with the dimension of an acceleration is c3 in [K_i,K_j] = c3 eps_ijk P_k (dimension T^2/L = 1/a0); it exists on Galilei only, is D-invariant exactly at the dilatation exponent z = 1/2 (the scaling that leaves an acceleration invariant), is parity-odd, and Jacobi forces **c3 * (1/c^2) = 0** and, for c3 != 0, **the ad_H spectrum on (K,P) to have a zero eigenvalue or the exact ratio 2:1**. NH (eigenvalues +-w, ratio -1) is excluded; the relation between c3 and the rate scale is free (16 pairs over 12 orders of magnitude, all Jacobi-exact). This is a known known kinematical algebra (eq. 64 of arXiv:1711.06111), not a new one.
3. **The puzzle relation cannot be a relation among structure constants:** a0 = 1/c3, c^2 = 1/h1, G rho -> b1 (the only T^-2 constant) gives 1/c3^2 = kappa^2 b1/h1, which has no solution with c3, h1, b1 nonzero once c3 h1 = 0. Central charges (H^2 of Galilei, Bargmann, Newton-Hooke, conformal Galilei) are the mass (and the 2+1 exotic charge) only: none carries a0, c or Lambda. The integer N = 2l of the conformal-Galilei ladder multiplies an arbitrary frequency unit; matching the puzzle would need N = 32 pi (or 32 pi/3, 16 pi), never an integer; the pi-free normalisation X = G rho gives N = 4 = the same identity as G rho_Lambda = 4 a0^2.
4. **Every algebra-forced rational (z_a0 = 1/2, the 1:2 ad_H ratio, N = 4 <-> z = 1/2) has ONE origin: a bilinear bracket [K,K] -> P makes w(P) = 2 w(K).** Mapping it to kappa = a0/(c sqrt(G rho_Lambda)) needs c, a0, G and rho at once; the class contains at most two of them and never G, rho. The map is assumed in every case, derived in none. hbar-carrying halves (spin 1/2, zero-point) cannot enter a0 (Pi = hbar G^2 rho/c^5 = 1.1e-123).

## Results
**R1 What Jacobi forces among the kinematical parameters (m01).** With parity and time reversal: [H,K] = a2 P, [H,P] = b1 K, [K,K] = c1 J, [P,P] = d1 J, [K,P] = h1 delta H; Jacobi <=> c1 = a2 h1 and d1 = -b1 h1, with (a2,b1,h1) free. Dimensions: b1 ~ T^-2 (time curvature = Newton-Hooke / Lambda), h1 ~ c1 ~ 1/c^2, d1 ~ L^-2 (spatial curvature) = -(b1)(h1) forced. Under K -> aK, P -> bP, H -> gH the three constants have weights (g a/b, g b/a, a b/g) with det W = -4: no nonconstant monomial is invariant, so only signs (nine classes) and zero/nonzero survive. Every ratio of two dimensionful structure constants is a convention: "a0/H fixed by closure" is not even a well-posed statement inside the algebra; it can only be a property of a realisation (which fixes K = tP - m x etc.). The general (non-P,T) ansatz needs 16 constants (the J-components of [H,K], [H,P] are needed for closure under K -> K + wJ, P -> P + wJ; see own errors).

**R2 Which dilatation each parameter tolerates (m02, m04 C, m05).** Derivations of the nine classes: Galilei any z (2 outer); Poincare-type z = 1 only (Weyl, forced by c1 p1 = 0); Newton-Hooke z = 0 only (forced by b1 (p1 - f1 - q2) = 0 => w(H) = 0: time cannot be dilated); every class with b1 h1 != 0 (de Sitter, anti-de Sitter): none (rigid). MOND's z = 1 therefore exists for Galilei and Poincare-type and is **incompatible with a nonzero H_Lambda** at the level of the algebra; z = 1/2 (a0 invariant) needs 1/c^2 = 1/tau^2 = 0. Cohomology confirms it at first order (the derivative of the family H_s = H + sC is a cocycle of Galilei x| D_z only for z = 0; the derivative of K_eps only for z = 1; neither at z = 1/2). At Galilei x| D_z, H^2(g,g) contains a "change the weights" direction at every z: the dynamical exponent z is a genuine continuous modulus. A mass central charge has weight z - 2: only z = 2 (Schroedinger); at z = 1/2 no mass is D-invariant.

**R3 The a0-direction (m05, m06).** Exact rotation-covariant H^2(g,g): Galilei has four first-order directions, a1 (a rate: [H,K] = a1 K), b1 (Newton-Hooke), (c1,h1) (1/c^2), and **c3** ([K,K] = c3 eps P, dimension 1/acceleration); Newton-Hooke has {a1, (c1,d1,h1)} and no c3; dS/AdS, Newton-Hooke x| D_0 and Poincare x| Weyl are rigid; Galilei x| D_{1/2} has {weight change, c3}. Full solution for c3 != 0 (gauge e3 = e4 = 0 is always reachable): (i) c3 h1 = 0 (Jacobi polynomial -3 c3 h1); (ii) for h1 = 0, a2 != 0 all other constants but one are fixed and the single residual condition is Q = -(c3/(3 a2)) det(ad_H) (2 tr^2 - 9 det) = -(c3/3) l1 l2 (2 l1 - l2)(l1 - 2 l2) = 0, with l1, l2 the eigenvalues of ad_H on span(K,P); (iii) a2 = 0: b2 = 2 a1, spectrum (a1, 2 a1). So with c3 != 0 the H-action has a zero eigenvalue or the exact 2:1 ratio; expanding or oscillating Newton-Hooke (Q = 3 c3 w^4 != 0 for l = +-w) is excluded, as is any Lorentz c. Independent check: 400 random Jacobi solutions of the un-gauged 16-constant system at c3 = 1.7: defect 4e-14; the 99 nondegenerate ones all have |l_max/l_min| = 2.000000; at c3 = 0 the same sampler finds generic spectra (117 of 390); at c3 = 1.7, h1 = 0.9 it finds none. The 2 is the weight additivity of [K,K] -> P (in the ad_H eigenbasis w(P) = 2 w(K)); it is the same arithmetic that gives z = 1/2 from 2(z - 1) = -1. The algebra is not isomorphic to Galilei (derivation algebras of dimension 11 vs 12), is P-odd and T-even (chiral), has no dilatation once a rate is on, and its constant c3 rescales as alpha^2/beta with the rate scale gamma independent: a0/(rate) = (velocity) is free. Literature cross-check (arXiv:1711.06111, PDF pages 1-3, 7-9 opened): the classification of rotation-covariant kinematical algebras contains "[H,B] = 2B, [H,P] = P, [P,P] = eps B" (eq. 64; roles of P and B exchanged relative to mine) and a one-parameter family [H,B] = gamma B, [H,P] = P (eq. 59): my 2:1 has the same structure as that entry (I compared the brackets by eye, not by an explicit isomorphism) and was obtained without using it, and the continuous modulus of the general classification is the free ratio of two ad_H eigenvalues, pinned to 2 only when a bracket [.,.] -> . links them. I did not read the rest of that paper.

**R4 Both parameters inside one algebra: the conformal-Galilei ladder (m03).** cg_l(3) (l = 1/2 Schroedinger, l = 1 GCA of dimension 15 = dim so(4,2), l = 3/2, 2) closes for 2l integer and fails (mutations) for a wrong coefficient or non-integer 2l. Newton-Hooke sits inside: for l = 1/2 the elliptic H + w^2 C and hyperbolic H - w^2 C with the (J,P,K) block give oscillating (b1 = -w^2) and expanding (b1 = +w^2) Newton-Hooke of frequency w. the MOND dilatation D and the Newton-Hooke time translation H_hyp are two hyperbolic elements of the SAME sl(2,R); D-conjugation maps every w to every other (all frequencies are one orbit; w is a unit), and the relative rapidity theta of two such elements has tr(XY) = 2 cosh 2 theta, a continuous invariant in [2, infinity) that no algebraic condition fixes. The spectrum of ad(H +- w^2 C) on the multiplet is 2 m w (i for the elliptic one), m = -l..l, so the top frequency is N w, N = 2l an integer: this is the natural algebraic source of the integer that the abstract of arXiv:1104.1502 calls a "quantized reduced negative cosmological constant lambda = -N" (I did not verify that identification) (I opened pages 1-4 and 15-20; I did NOT verify the normalisation of their reduced lambda). z = 2/N, so the a0-invariant scaling z = 1/2 is N = 4. Required N for the puzzle (m07 C): with omega = xi a0: X = Lambda: 32 pi; X = H^2 = Lambda/3: 32 pi/3; X = 4 pi G rho: 16 pi (all transcendental for algebraic xi); only X = G rho gives N = 4, i.e. the same statement as G rho_Lambda = 4 a0^2 rewritten. Also omega = a0 is dimensionally impossible in a Galilean algebra (a0/omega is a velocity, i.e. c is inserted by hand). The coincidence "N = 4 vs the 4 in G rho = 4 a0^2" therefore exists only in the pi-free normalisation and is not evidence.

**R5 Central extensions (m04).** 3+1: Galilei and both Newton-Hooke algebras have exactly one genuine central charge (the mass); Poincare, Euclid-type, so(4,1), so(3,2) none; the mass-extended (Bargmann-type) algebras none further; Galilei x| D_z has a genuine central charge only at z = 2 (weight z - 2); the GCA cg_1(3) has none, Schroedinger (l = 1/2) and cg_{3/2} have the mass, cg_2 none (half-integer l <-> mass in d = 3). 2+1: Galilei and Newton-Hooke have two (mass, exotic [K1,K2]); Galilei x| D_1 has the exotic one only (weight 2(z - 1) = 0 at z = 1); the GCA cg_1(2), cg_2(2), cg_{1/2}(2) and cg_{3/2}(2) have one genuine class each (by the literature statement of arXiv:1104.1502, p. 15, the exotic type for integer l and the mass for half-integer l; I verified the counts, and the type explicitly only for Galilei x| D_z). The full H^2 of Galilei(2+1) (dimension 3) is spanned by three explicit classes (mass, exotic, and psi(J,H) from the abelianisation). so(4,1), so(3,2), so(4,2) are rigid (H^2(g,g) = 0). None of these classes is a rate or an acceleration; the mass is continuous (no quantisation in the algebra), the integers of R4 are multiplet labels. So neither a0 nor Lambda is a central charge, and no rational 4 or 1/2 arises from a quantised central charge.

**R6 Rep-theory halves and the map (m07).** Dimensional analysis: from (c, G, rho) the acceleration is unique, a0 = nu c sqrt(G rho), all of kappa is nu; from the kinematical scales (c, a rate) it is nu c H; the only dimensionless group with hbar is Pi = hbar G^2 rho/c^5 = 1.1e-123 (observed rho_Lambda), so any hbar-carrying number (spin 1/2, zero-point hbar w/2) enters a0 only if hbar cancels. Declared candidates (all equal 1/2 at d = 3, listed before classification): inversion weight (d-2)/2 and higher-dimensional Schwarzschild surface gravity x r_s (the same function, values 0, 1/2, 1, 3/2, 2 for d = 2..6: fail a second setting), spin 1/2 and zero-point (hbar), z_a0 = 1/2, the 1:2 ad_H ratio, z = 2/N at N = 4 (the last three are hbar-free and d-independent, and one origin). The map kappa := candidate is an assumption in every case. Two dS subalgebras of so(2,4) (stabilisers of unit timelike vectors) meet in a 6-dimensional semisimple subalgebra for every generic relative angle: the relative modulus is free (a degenerate pair is detected by the Killing rank, control).

## What kind of input is missing
A kinematical algebra contains rates (H), 1/c^2 or 1/a0 (never both), and central charges; it does not contain G or rho, and the puzzle relation uses c, a0, G and rho together. A relation of the form "a0 = kappa c sqrt(G rho_Lambda)" needs (i) a field equation that couples the frame's acceleration field to a density (the Poisson/Einstein coupling: G, and Einstein's 8 pi when rho_Lambda is traded for Lambda or H), (ii) a realisation that fixes the generator normalisations (K = tP - m x, i.e. a2 = 1), and (iii) a dynamical statement about the value of the density or of the coupling. In every case above the coefficient is put in by the realisation, not forced by closure; this matches the record ("the coefficient 4 in G rho_Lambda = 4 a0^2 must come from a coupling normalisation or a dynamical principle") and does not narrow it further.

## What is NOT established
- Only the ansatz class was classified: rotation-covariant, 3+1, generators (J,K,P,H) plus one scalar D (25 constants). Larger algebras (so(4,2) contractions with several parameters, infinite-dimensional Virasoro-type extensions, algebras with extra vector or tensor generators) were not classified; the conformal-Galilei ladder and the so(2,4) pair modulus are checks, not a classification. The GCA as a contraction of so(4,2) was taken from arXiv:0902.1385 (abstract summary only), not recomputed.
- The a0-direction is a formal algebra. I did not find a physical realisation (a MOND field theory with this symmetry), did not show it is the MOND symmetry, and the deep-MOND kinematics itself (Galilei x| D_1) does NOT contain c3. c3 is the only D_{1/2}-invariant a0-type deformation of Galilei, nothing more.
- The reductive-subalgebra spectral-sequence identification (rotation-covariant H^2 = full H^2) is used as a standard theorem; my evidence is the agreement of exact and numerical counts for 16 algebras.
- H^2 computations by SVD ranks (one exact rational cross-check, Galilei(3): 45 - 35 - 9 = 1). The 2+1 and cg_2(3) results rely on the same code.
- Nothing here bears on why kappa = 1/2 beyond showing where it cannot come from. It does not exclude a dynamical selection (a variational, attractor or BPS principle) or a coupling normalisation.
- The normalisation of the "reduced cosmological constant" of arXiv:1104.1502 (Newton-Cartan) was not checked; the table of m07 C lists what each convention would need.

## Verdict
**SHARP NO-GO** for a two-parameter (or larger, within the classified class) algebra fixing a0 relative to H_Lambda or G rho_Lambda. Narrowing: (i) no relation among the structure constants of the class involves a0/H; (ii) a0 (as c3 = 1/a0) can be a deformation parameter only at c = infinity and Lambda = 0 (or a degenerate/2:1 rate), so the puzzle's relation, which needs c, a0 and rho together, is not an intrinsic algebra statement; (iii) the only rational numbers the algebra forces (1/2 as a dilatation exponent, 2:1 as a rate ratio, N = 4) share one origin and map to kappa only by assumption; (iv) the missing ingredient is a coupling/dynamics (G, rho, 8 pi), consistent with lane G's dichotomy and the wave-2 statement that the target is the coefficient 4 in G rho_Lambda = 4 a0^2 inside a model or a principle.
