# Lane T: Weyl (conformal) gravity -- does conformal invariance fix a0^2/Lambda? (c = G = 1 unless stated; puzzle: G rho_Lambda = 4 a0^2, i.e. Lambda = 32 pi a0^2)

## Bottom line -- verdict: SHARP NO-GO (nothing derived; kappa = 1/2 stays FITTED)
1. **Conformal invariance leaves a one-parameter family, it does not fix a0^2/k.** The static vacuum solution B = 1 - 3 beta gamma - beta(2 - 3 beta gamma)/r + gamma r - k r^2 (the MK solution) is the general solution of the Bach equation in the gauge g_tt g_rr = -1 (three constants, one algebraic constraint w^2 = 1 + 3uv). A real special-conformal map r' = r/(1+ar) with Weyl factor Omega = 1/(1+ar) shifts (gamma, k) -> (gamma - 2a, k + a gamma - a^2) at beta = 0 (general formulas in section 2); what it leaves invariant is only gamma^2 + 4k = 4 kappa_h^2 (beta = 0), i.e. **(a0)^2 + k = kappa_h^2 with a0 := gamma/2**. For EVERY MK solution the linear term can be removed by such a map (image = Schwarzschild-de Sitter). So a0 = c^2 gamma/2 is a conformal-gauge parameter of the vacuum solution, not a physical scale, and a0^2/k is not invariant.
2. **The scalar (compensator) sector fixes only the invariant sum.** Solving the scalar's T_mn = 0 on the MK metric (derived here, not assumed) gives k' = k + gamma^2(1 - beta gamma)/(2 - 3 beta gamma)^2 = -2 lambda phi0^2 (= Lambda/3). G_eff = -3/(4 pi phi0^2), rho_V = lambda phi0^4, G_eff rho_V = 3k'/(8 pi): the de Sitter Friedmann identity, with **no alpha_g and no relation to a0**. G_eff rho_V/a0^2 = (3/8pi)(1 + k/a0^2) is a free integration ratio; the puzzle value 4 needs k/a0^2 = 32 pi/3 - 1 = 32.51. The known CG numbers miss it by 1e5 (fitted kappa) and by factors 46-57 (Omega_k/Omega_Lambda).
3. Numbers (script t04): the CG universal acceleration gamma0 c^2/2 = 1.38e-11 m/s^2 is 7.8 times below SPARC a0 (1.08e-10); the paper's own factor '4' (a0 = 4 gamma0 c^2 = 1.10e-10) comes from a galaxy-dependent crossing argument (a0_eff = 2c^2(gamma0 + gamma* N*) spans x18 over N* = 1e9..1e12); the galactic gamma0 implies Omega_k = 4e-4 while the same authors' Hubble-plot cosmology has Omega_k = 0.63 (|K| differs x1400).
4. What the puzzle becomes inside CG: an epoch / initial-condition statement (a0/H_dS = 1/sinh u, i.e. sinh u = Z, equivalently T0 T_max/T_V^2 = 1/Z), not a coupling relation. The Weyl action on the de Sitter instanton is exactly zero for every L; the 32 pi^2 that does appear (integral of R^2/3 - Ric^2 = 32 pi^2) is L-independent.

## 2. Results
**R1 The MK solution, reproduced (t01).** With ds^2 = -B dt^2 + dr^2/B + r^2 dOmega^2 and generic B(r): Weyl^2 = (r^2B'' - 2rB' + 2B - 2)^2/(3r^4); the Bach tensor is diagonal, traceless, and b^t_t - b^r_r = -(B/6)(B'''' + 4B'''/r) = -(B/6)(rB)''''/r. Hence (rB)'''' = 0, B = w + v/r + u r - k r^2 (four constants), and on that family **every** Bach component equals -+(1 + 3uv - w^2)/(6 r^4). So the general solution has three constants, w^2 = 1 + 3uv, and w = 1 - 3 beta gamma, v = -beta(2 - 3 beta gamma), u = gamma satisfies it identically. Any other w, v, u, or an r^3 term fails (5 mutations).

**R2 The conformal map and its invariants (t02).** The map r' = r/(1+ar), g'_mn = Omega^2 g_mn, Omega = 1/(1+ar) preserves the gauge (B'(r') = (1-ar')^2 B(r'/(1-ar')); verified with the explicit Jacobian for a generic B, and the wrong map r' = r/(1+ar^2) breaks g_tt g_rr = -1). On the cubic family: w' = w - 3av, v' = v, u' = u - 2aw + 3a^2 v, k' = k - a^2 w + a^3 v + a u; group law T_a2 T_a1 = T_(a1+a2).
- **Invariants:** v; H = w^2 - 3uv (so the MK constraint is itself invariant); the discriminant of the cubic p(x) = v x^3 + w x^2 + u x - k (x = 1/r; the map is the shift x -> x + a). **Not invariant** (controls that must fail, and do): u, k, w, u^2/k, u^2 - 4k, u^2 + k, u^2 + 2k. So a0^2/k is not conformally invariant.
- **beta = 0 slice:** B = 1 + gamma r - k r^2 has vanishing Weyl tensor for every (gamma, k); (gamma, k) -> (gamma - 2a, k + a gamma - a^2); **gamma^2 + 4k = 4 kappa_h^2** (kappa_h = horizon surface gravity with t normalised by B(0) = 1; pure de Sitter gamma = 0, k = H^2 gives H^2). The metric divided by r^2 is AdS2 x S^2 with unit radii for all (gamma, k) (unequal radii is not conformally flat: control); the invariant is (f'/2)^2 - f = Delta/4 for the Killing vector d/dt of AdS2, an sl(2,R) Casimir-type quantity: this is the form of an sl(2,R) Casimir (I did not verify that the map is literally SL(2,R) conjugation), so the **two parameters (a0, k) lie on one curve a0^2 + k = const**; nothing in the algebra singles out a point of it. (This is the concrete answer to lane G's open question about a two-parameter algebra: here the two parameters are tied by a Casimir, which fixes their sum and nothing else.)
- **General beta** (k' below is the value in the Schwarzschild-de Sitter frame, i.e. the transformed k at a = a*): a* = (w-1)/(3v) = gamma/(2 - 3 beta gamma) makes u' = 0, w' = 1: every MK solution is the image of Schwarzschild-de Sitter with mass M' = beta(2 - 3 beta gamma)/2 and **k' = k + gamma^2(1 - beta gamma)/(2 - 3 beta gamma)^2**; the quadratic for u'(a) has discriminant 4(w^2 - 3uv) = 4 > 0, so the removal is possible for all beta, gamma. The cubic discriminant equals the SdS Nariai discriminant 4k'(1 - 27M'^2 k'). These reproduce the metric map of the critical re-analysis (2103.13451 eq. 14-15) independently.
- **Hubble-flow frame:** the static metric with (gamma, k) is conformal to a Robertson-Walker metric of 3-curvature **K = -(gamma^2/4 + k)** (verified by explicit substitution, mutation K = -(gamma^2/4 - k) fails). So K depends on (gamma, k) only through the same invariant. The 1996 paper's gamma0/2 = sqrt(-K) is the special case k = 0; the 2010-11 fit papers state that splitting the single cosmological scale K into (gamma0, k) is artificial and drop k on the grounds that r should run to infinity. That choice is the whole content of a0 = c^2 sqrt(-K).

**R3 Scalar sector, G_eff and the puzzle ratio (t03).** Conformal scalar: L = -(grad phi)^2/2 - R phi^2/12 - lambda phi^4 (mostly plus; potential V = lambda phi^4), T_mn with trace identity T^m_m = phi E (control: xi = 1/5 breaks it).
- On the MK metric with phi = phi0/(1 + r/a), solving T_mn = 0 (all components) gives **a = (2 - 3 beta gamma)/gamma** and **lambda phi0^2 = -k'/2**, k' as above (the critical re-analysis states this as its eqs 18-19; here it is derived). Mutations (wrong a, sign of lambda, the beta = 0 form of k' at beta != 0) fail; at beta = 0 the exponent p = -1 is unique among p = -2, -1, +1.
- Einstein frame g~ = (phi/phi0)^2 g: SdS with the same k'; constant phi0 requires -R phi0/6 - 4 lambda phi0^3 = 0, i.e. k' = -2 lambda phi0^2 again (two routes agree). At fixed lambda phi0^2 the beta = 0 solutions gamma = 2, 4, 1/3 (with k = k' - gamma^2/4) all satisfy T = 0: **a0 = gamma/2 is a free integration constant; only a0^2 + k is fixed by (lambda, phi0)**.
- G_eff = -3/(4 pi phi0^2) (negative for the standard-sign kinetic term), rho_V = lambda phi0^4, Lambda = 8 pi G_eff rho_V = -6 lambda phi0^2 = 3k'; **G_eff rho_V = 3k'/(8 pi)** exactly, alpha_g nowhere. Hence G_eff rho_V/a0^2 = (3/8pi)(1 + k/a0^2) (b -> 0): a free number; 4 iff k/a0^2 = 32 pi/3 - 1 = 32.51 (control: k = a0^2 gives 3/(4 pi)). The 3/(8 pi) is Friedmann; nothing produces a 4 or a 32 pi.
- Cosmology of the same theory (open slicing): a0_phys^2/H_dS^2 = Omega_k/Omega_Lambda-bar = 1/sinh^2 u (identity), and with the quoted (T0, T_max, T_V) variables of the CG cosmology sinh^2 u = T_V^4 (T_m^2 - T0^2)/(T0^2 (T_m^4 + T_V^4)) -> T_V^4/(T0 T_m)^2. So a0/H_dS = 1/Z is the statement **sinh u = Z** (Omega_k = 1/(1+Z^2) = 0.029, Omega_Lambda-bar = 0.971), i.e. T0 T_max = T_V^2/Z at leading order: T_max is the bounce temperature, an initial-condition constant. a0 = H_dS/sinh(H_dS t) is not constant in time in this identification (if a0 is identified with the PHYSICAL curvature scale; the papers call the curvature epoch-independent in the comoving sense, so this is an interpretive premise, not tested here).
- A massive particle whose mass is m = mu phi moves on **geodesics of the Einstein-frame metric** (its Lagrangian is identical to the geodesic Lagrangian of (phi/phi0)^2 g for any B; control: constant mass is not). So in the Einstein frame the exterior is SdS with no linear term: a0 = 0. The statement that the linear term is a gauge artefact for orbits (2103.13451) rests on exactly this identity; I verified the mathematics, I did not adjudicate the physics dispute (the authors' replies were not opened).

## 4. What is NOT established
- Whether the MK-frame linear term is physical. Verified only the mathematics (map, k', T_phi = 0 profile, variable-mass = Einstein-frame geodesic); the opposing papers' replies were not opened, and I did not fit any data. If the linear term is physical (source matching in a non-Einstein frame) then a0 is a boundary datum; if not, a0 = 0 in vacuum. Either way it is not fixed by the action.
- The 2011 formulation (dynamical fermion condensate, no fundamental Einstein term; reference [1] there) was not opened; my G_eff, rho_V and k' use the compensator-scalar formulation of the 2005 review, the cosmology paper and the critical re-analysis. In the condensate formulation the induced cosmological constant is claimed cancelled, which would make rho_V and G_eff undefined rather than 4 a0^2.
- The source-matching relations for beta and gamma (quoted 1101.2186 eq. 58, proportional to 1/alpha_g times source moments) were not evaluated; gamma* is treated as the fitted constant.
- The Hubble-plot Omega_k = 0.63 is a 2005 phenomenological value; CMB, growth and BBN in CG are open or negative in the literature I saw (the critical paper cites GRB/quasar Hubble-diagram and nucleosynthesis tensions, references not opened).
- a0_eff(N*) is my generalisation of a footnote's crossing argument, not a fit to rotation curves (no RAR test was run). No data comparison of a0(z).
- Nothing here bears on why kappa = 1/2. kappa = 1/2 stays FITTED.

## 6. Verdict: SHARP NO-GO
Conformal invariance, with or without the scalar sector, fixes only the invariant sum a0^2 + k = k' (= Lambda/3 = -2 lambda phi0^2 at beta = 0); a0 alone is an SL(2,R)-orbit coordinate (a gauge parameter in vacuum), k alone is not invariant, and G_eff rho_Lambda/a0^2 = (3/8pi)(1 + k/a0^2) is a free integration ratio with no alpha_g dependence. The puzzle's coefficient 4 would be the statement k/a0^2 = 32 pi/3 - 1, equivalently Omega_k/Omega_Lambda-bar = 3/(32 pi), sinh u = Z, T0 T_max/T_V^2 = 1/Z: an epoch or initial-condition datum of the CG cosmology, not a coupling. The CG papers' own numbers do not satisfy it (a0 too low by 7.8, or the two curvature determinations disagree by 1400, fitted kappa 2600 x Lambda/3). Narrowing for the puzzle: in any theory with exact conformal invariance the ratio can live only in a frame/boundary datum, and the sole invariant structure available is a Casimir sum, which is rational-algebraic in a0 and k and contains no pi.
