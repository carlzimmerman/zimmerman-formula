# Separate internal review of the NC checkpoint

Implementer self-review; no independent-agent referee review is implied.

1. Original scope remains formation plus preservation with all joint conditions.
   A fixed-background six-mode model cannot test the galaxy law, moving
   interface, outer F phase or global mass redistribution. Local success is
   never substituted for that target.
2. Bare cubic algebra includes both velocity and quantum-pressure terms. Their
   on-shell cancellation in the free Schrödinger limit is an essential check.
   The nonlocal W tensor vertex passes independent full-energy derivatives.
3. The full CC conservative vertex is obtained by eliminating the original
   phase/scalar square before differentiating. Its independent variable-density
   stationary-elimination derivative agrees to2.6e-14. This supplies the term
   missing in the earlier reduction; the original full CC code already had it.
   Adding it again to compression.py would double count it.
4. Near-field frequency changes small relative to Omega can be large relative
   to a nearly cancelled bare vertex or narrow triad resonance. No physical
   no-go is inferred from the incomplete bare-triad reduction.
5. Local parent amplitudes are declared weak inputs, not the infall release.
   Their initial5% normalization is not a rigorous later density supremum.
   Angular occupation, phase coherence and mean-flow work are not inferred
   from a radial rotation curve or from kinetic eigenbasis labels.
6. The local field is actually evolved from zero incident field. Its source
   and reciprocal material force use the same quadratic charge. Pump depletion,
   near-field reaction and outgoing absorption are included. Negative internal
   Hamiltonian composition steps are numerical fourth-order construction;
   absorber substeps always have positive duration and positive energy loss.
   No rejected-step or integrator energy defect is counted as radiation.
7. Wave spectra distinguish finite-radius radiation component, boundary storage
   and absorber arrival time. The daughter source/force ablation nearly removes
   the daughter-frequency line, limiting the parent-harmonic interpretation.
   It is a diagnostic control, not a proposed alternate physical coupling.
8. Baseline time and wave-grid controls agree; the predicted half-shift geometry
   also has a dedicated time-halving control. Total energy and charge ledgers
   close well below the exported local energy. This is finite numerical
   evidence, not a continuum theorem or validation of omitted material orders.
9. The phase-match angles are constant and computed before their runs from
   the same source self-energy. No external chirp or extra energy is supplied.
   Half-shift compensation centers leading detuning over a depletion interval.
   The approximate coherent-source power threshold is instantaneous and phase
   dependent; it is not a theorem of global cooling efficiency.
10. The3D continuation of the radial response is explicitly chosen. Its local
    WKB coefficients do not uniquely follow from the radial formula. Exact
    spherical data cannot generate noncollinear modes without a seed.
11. Material mesh reflection was isolated by matched-energy unitary packet
    propagation. Energy conservation alone cannot certify outgoing material
    waves. Refined force checks pass; no inherited static finite-grid claim
    is silently upgraded to full3D or continuum stability.
12. Global reference and global CC candidate are different evolutions. The
    g2=0 run's135Myr law/gap failure is not a physical counterexample to the

13. Refined initial states conserve species masses but are not newly force-
    certified static equilibria. The transferred allF seed is not a new fine
    eigenvector. Undefined rebound for a tiny seed structure is not a pass.

    Coupling homotopy is inside a root only; rejected third warm trial is
    excluded from accepted-state ledgers in run_warm_checked.

    frozen sources/contracts/results. MANIFEST_AUDIT validates provenance, not
    scientific success; completed audit processes include failed hypotheses.
16. Microscopic g2, consistent kinetic completion, mean-flow pumping, higher
