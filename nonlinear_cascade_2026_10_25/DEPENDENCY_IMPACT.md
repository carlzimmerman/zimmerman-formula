# What changes, what does not, and what was corrected

This phase does not overwrite earlier hashed evidence.

* The old NT scalar-work/far-flux ledgers remain valid discrete results. Scalar
  mesh refinement did not certify propagation of material waves: the old
  outer material mesh has a spectral band barrier, now explicitly demonstrated.
  Old absence of F cooling over30Myr cannot be promoted to a physical theorem.
* The static radial benchmark and restoring Hessian remain finite-grid claims.
  The CC kinetic extension leaves relaxed statics unchanged, but this phase
  does not certify a full3D minimum or global basin of formation.
* A high kinetic eigenbasis label can describe phase winding as well as fast
  matter fluctuations. Exact band work is retained, but temporal density and
  current spectra were added before interpreting it as a fast population.
* momentum.py removes angle subtraction algebraically but did not improve the
  stalled energy defect. Its proposed cure is falsified by the matched audit.
  It is not used to claim an advanced physical trajectory.
* Recovery probes address a real controller deadlock: an acceptable larger
  step can exist when a tiny step will not trigger ordinary step growth. They
  retain all local guards. They do not cure every unresolved fast-carrier step.
* The trigonometric carrier divided difference has an exact fixed-density
  potential identity, but does not conserve the full density-dependent inertia
  energy; its coupled birth-step defect remains too large. No long success
  trajectory is based on it.
* run_compare_runs used partial termination time with an earlier final sample,
  causing a mismatch. run_compare_checked uses equal actual sample times;
  only the latter supports convergence statements.
* The bare cubic vertex and its quantum-pressure cancellation are correct for
  the bare material Hamiltonian. run_triad omitted the CC conservative cubic
  inertia while adding CC radiation. Its weak/slow cascade is not an obstruction
  to the full CC operator. The high export case there was primarily direct
  parent radiation, not an upward cascade (run_triad_interpret).
* The full CC action already included this conservative interaction. The new
  CC_CUBIC_DERIVATION extracts it correctly for the local reduction; it is not
  an extra physical term silently added to compression.py. It is independently
  checked by stationary elimination of the original full spatial square.
* The new local mode-wave test retains that complete cubic vertex, depleted
  pumps and a reciprocal outgoing wave field. It omits higher material
  interactions, background evolution and the galaxy interface. It cannot
  establish a full formation result, even with excellent numerical ledgers.
* The pure-T angular extension W(|g0+delta g|) and local WKB assumption are
  explicit. The radial formula alone did not uniquely specify a3D theory.
  Local noncollinear triads do not arise from exact spherical initial data
  without an angular seed. A radial global test alone cannot validate the
  proposed angular cascade.
