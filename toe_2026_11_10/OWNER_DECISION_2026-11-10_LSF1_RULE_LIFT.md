# OWNER DECISION 2026-11-10: two standing rules lifted for test lane LSF1 only

**Asked** (lead, in chat): "Lift the no-particle and metric-only rules for one test lane on the k^6 superfluid?"
**Answer** (owner, in chat, 2026-11-10): "Lift both, launch".

## What is lifted, and where

For lane **LSF1** (`toe_2026_11_10/LSF1_lifshitz_fermion_superfluid/`) and nowhere else:

1. **The no-particle rule.** LSF1 may introduce one new fermion species (a hidden superfluid) as the law's microscopic
   origin. It gravitates, so it is a dark component carried by a particle, and it is named as such.
2. **The metric-only rule** (rule 1 of `closure_2026_11_10/THEORY_ASSEMBLED_2026-11-10.md`; QX17 E5). LSF1 may couple
   baryons directly to the superfluid's phase, as the Berezhiani-Khoury superfluid needs for its MOND force. The
   coupling's equivalence-principle and fifth-force costs are graded, not assumed away.

## What is not changed

- Form B, action (A), the register, STANDING.md and every other lane are unchanged. The lift is not general; it is not a
  precedent for any other lane.
- LSF1 is a TEST lane, quarantined: adopts nothing, relabels nothing, registers nothing.
- A pass in LSF1 is a rival candidate to form B, not its completion. Adopting it would need a separate owner decision.
- The TOE0 battery's labels (`toe_2026_11_10/TOE0_gate_battery/RESULT.md`) are not reopened by this file.

## Why

TOE0, ROOT and NPJ1 (2026-11-10) leave one candidate that passes shape, size and locality: the Berezhiani-Khoury
superfluid (NPJ1 a1). It fails only on a renormalizable action, because its three-body contact has dimension -4. The
lead found that a degenerate Fermi gas with dispersion k^z in 3-d has P(mu) proportional to mu^((z+3)/z), which is
mu^(3/2) at z = 6, where the contacts are super-renormalizable (two-body) and marginal (three-body); a k^6 dispersion
needs the preferred time the clock supplies.

The lead also flagged, before launch, that this match is for the uniform equation of state only. MOND needs the
superfluid's response to phase gradients, which for k^6 fermions probably scales as |grad phi|^9, not |grad phi|^3.
LSF1 tests that first.

Adopts nothing. kappa = 1/2 FITTED; not theory closed.
