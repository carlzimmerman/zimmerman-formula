## What the swing established

1. **(A) works as the effective theory of a medium with a conserved charge.** No ghost; weakly coupled wherever the
   charge is non-zero; matter loops cannot touch a0 or mu (QG1).
2. **The law cannot be a fundamental term.** alpha = 2 is forced by any deep-MOND regime; there the static sector loses
   the Newtonian potential and the leading term is non-polynomial (ROOT, A1, HZ1).
3. **No sector computed so far produces the law.** Perturbative leaf fields (10^55), acceleration-gapped sectors
   (10^5.4 on locality), a superfluid's phase gradients (LSF1).
4. **Dissipation does not close the joint.** A free-falling clock has no acceleration to key on; a rate a0/c is too slow
   by itself; a viscosity that spares the forest cannot settle massive haloes by today (AKS1).
5. **The 5.41 is still free.** A clock that drives a baryon asymmetry pays for it out of its own charge (TOE0-P).

## Named and not run

- A keyed viscosity (off on the z = 3 forest, on in z = 0 haloes): needs a new threshold constant (AKS1 Part T).
- Non-local dissipation: FA3's class.
- A coupling of 10^6.5 or more between a sector and the clock's acceleration (ROOT A1, NPJ1).
- The 5.41 from dust made during inflation, or from early breaking of the clock's shift symmetry (R541).
