Before symbolic verification of this candidate, extend the clock route with
an explicit bounded-expansion action. Let Z=Theta-Theta_star(phi), where
Theta=nabla.n and Theta_star is the chosen homogeneous branch's3H(phi).
For B>0 and T>0, consider

    Fcap(Z)=Mpl^2 B T^2 [sqrt(1-Z^2/T^2)-1], |Z|<T.

It starts as -Mpl^2 B Z^2/2. Check its auxiliary representation, signs,
effective expansion stiffness and its implication for the proper-volume
Jacobian of the clock congruence. A domain inequality that forbids a regular
fold is not a proof of global existence: identify possible failure at the cap,
at Q=0, or in metric/scalar equations. Do not hide a new singularity as a fix.

Keep the native static polarization potential. Compare native switched
stiffness with nondegenerate unswitched (D.P)^2, set mu_K=0 in this candidate,
and derive the frozen gravity/clock quadratic constraints. Test whether the
MOND acceleration cancellation makes expansion stiffness alone ineffective
at P=0. Check the alternative bare-acceleration repair against the exact
weak-field MOND limit. No numerical parameter is fitted or called predicted.

Symbolic checks include the auxiliary elimination, exact expansion stiffness,
constraint elimination, dispersion signs, degenerate native limit, static
MOND tradeoff, linear-wave canonical clock identities and a smooth spherical
canonical-clock counterexample. An expansion-sign mutation must fail the
positive-stiffness/kinetic witness. At most60seconds, one thread.
