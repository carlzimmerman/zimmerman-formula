3. DIAGNOSTIC -- how much apparent drift does each model NEED? (this IS the answer)
======================================================================================================
  Magneticum/Mayer+2023 calibrated amplitude: p_mag = 0.920  =>  3.00x apparent
  a0 rise at z=2.3 in PURE LCDM with NO fundamental a0. That is the prior's DEFENSIBLE ceiling.

  model    p_required  x-rise @z2.3  vs Magneticum  chi2_min(p)
  --------------------------------------------------------------
  DEC           1.405         5.35x          1.78x          4.9
  RISE          0.345         1.51x          0.50x         10.9
  FLAT          1.300         4.72x          1.57x          3.8

  READING (the load-bearing sensitivity, stated plainly):
   * M-RISE needs only p~0.35 (1.5x, 50% of Magneticum): most of Ciocan's rise
     is FUNDAMENTAL under M-RISE, so it sits comfortably inside the Magneticum-calibrated prior.
   * M-DEC needs p~1.41 (5.4x apparent rise at z=2.3) = 1.8x MORE drift than
     Magneticum delivers; M-FLAT needs p~1.30 (4.7x) = 1.6x more.
   * Therefore M-DEC and M-FLAT are pinned AT the drift-prior CEILING: their evidence is set by
     wherever that ceiling is drawn, NOT by the data. Move the ceiling from 1.0x to 1.5x of the
     Magneticum amplitude and B(DEC/RISE) swings from ~1e-8 to ~10. THAT SWING IS THE RESULT:
     the existing high-z data do NOT decide DEC-vs-RISE; the apparent-drift amplitude does.
   * Neither direction is manufactured here: Ciocan keeps its FULL stat error (0.105) in the
     headline run (no systematics inflation), and its face-value ~30s rise is NOT taken as a
     fundamental-a0 result (face-value chi2/dof >> 1 for ALL THREE models -- see run F).
