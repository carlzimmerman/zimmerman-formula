AMENDMENT 15 -- The frozen cut list (§1.2) is mapped onto DR4's table structure before release, so that no source
  table is chosen after the data exist.  (a) The base sample is gaiadr4.gaia_source (the curated ~2 billion
  subset); quantities for cuts 1-8, 10-11 and 14-15 come from gaia_source where DR4 carries them, otherwise from
  the all_source_* table that carries them, joined on source_id.  Cut 9 (A_V) comes from the ap_* table that
  carries it.  Cut 13's resolved-triple search runs against the full all_source_astrometry (~2.8 billion
  sources), never the curated subset alone.  (b) Cut 12 (NSS screen) reads the DR4 nss_* tables: a pair is
  rejected if either component appears in any non-single-star table carrying an astrometric orbit, an
  acceleration solution or a spectroscopic orbit, with no quality threshold.  (c) A sample-definition variant
  with all_source_* as the base runs alongside the primary and is reported, never substituted.  (d) Table and
  column names are read from the published DR4 data model on release day and recorded in the fetch manifest;
  no cut value, threshold, estimator setting or decision row changes; a quantity DR4 lacks makes that cut
  UNIMPLEMENTABLE and flagged, with no substitute chosen after the data exist.  (e) A difference between the
  primary and the variant larger than sigma_fit is reported as a systematic and never used to pick one.
  ARMS A, B AND C, THE ESTIMATOR AND EVERY FROZEN NUMBER ARE UNTOUCHED.
