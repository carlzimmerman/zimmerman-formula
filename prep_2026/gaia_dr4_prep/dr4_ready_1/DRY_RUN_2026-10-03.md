## Gaps found

1. **The Amendment 14 distances were not printed by anything.** Amendment 14(f) extends the 7(e) rule (report the
   distances to the chain ceilings 1.0725 / 1.0900) and says "No pipeline file changes", so the frozen pipeline
   never prints them (`seed_label.py` lists the ceiling as NOT_IN_CODE). New `amdt14_distances.py` reads a pipeline
   log and prints the distances in σ_fit and σ_tot, and the Amendment 14(d) interval named by its edges only. Every
   number is read from the frozen texts. Self-test 6/6; MUTATE=1 fails.
2. **Every proj-PARALLEL fit sits on the frozen grid floor 0.90** (4 of 4 fits, both builds, both footings). Those
   fits are boundary-pinned, so their σ_fit is not a measurement and the anisotropy split (+0.30 to +0.35, "+4σ") is
   inflated by the edge. The grid is frozen; `amdt14_distances.py` flags such fits as GRID-EDGE. The anisotropy
   falsifier is non-scoring (Arm-A rule only); whether a boundary-pinned split may be quoted at all is an owner
   decision to record BEFORE the data, not on release day.
