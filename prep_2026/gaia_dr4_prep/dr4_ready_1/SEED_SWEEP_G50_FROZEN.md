# SEED SWEEP — FROZEN QUESTION Q-G50: is the stage-G-only spread, measured on 51 builds, the same size as the all-stream spread? (DR3, registered fit path, descriptive)

Written 2026-09-30 and committed BEFORE the run. **DR3 numbers are code-path tests, never results (Amendment 7(e)); NON-SCORING; no verdict words; no threshold.** Not blind to the ten-build family results of `seed_sweep_streams_dr3.out` (a51d6f8f2: SD(gamma-hat)/sigma_fit canonical / alt ALL 0.320 / 0.276, G-only 0.405 / 0.223) and to the mechanism (`seed_sweep_marginal_dr3.out`, 2f2354b18). Ten builds give an SD with a relative error of about 24%, so 'each stream alone reproduces the spread' was a statement at that precision; 51 builds give about 10%.

**Design.** `seed_sweep.py --release dr3 --K 50 --mode g-only --fit --jobs 4 --tag g50`: builds k = 0..50 on the extract's own stage F (k = 0 the frozen build), only stage G's velocity-error Monte Carlo seeded SEED + k; every final CSV fitted by the pipeline's own `--catalog` run (registered seed 20261216); the fit-only control = the k = 0 CSV refitted with `--seed 20261216 + j`, j = 1..50.
**Reported, nothing else** (a small summary script reads the manifest): SD (ddof = 1) of gamma-hat over the 51 builds per footing, SD/mean sigma_fit, a 2,000-resample bootstrap percentile interval (16th-84th) of that ratio (builds resampled with replacement), the mean one-way pair flips against k = 0, the fit-only SD over the 51 fits (registered + 50) and its ratio, the kappa range; next to them the ALL-family ten-build values (0.320 / 0.276) and the G-only ten-build values (0.405 / 0.223) quoted from a51d6f8f2. **No threshold is declared and no word such as consistent, different or equal is used: the numbers and their intervals are placed side by side.**

**Declared limits.** The 51 G-only builds share the frozen E/F and therefore the same candidate pairs; the ALL comparison values are ten-build SDs; the fit-only control carries the forward-model population and the bootstrap (the pipeline's single seed); DR3 at N about 6,200 only.
