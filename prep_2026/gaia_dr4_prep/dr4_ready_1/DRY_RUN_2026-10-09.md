## Gap found and closed

Arm C's rows, and the settling model that Amendment 21 scores against the same rows, are decided by
z_C = (gamma_hat - 1.000)/sigma_tot, with sigma_tot = sqrt(sigma_fit^2 + 0.02^2) (section 1). The pipeline's [7(e)] block prints the
Newtonian distance only in sigma_fit. No tool printed z_C or named the Arm C row, so on release day it would have been hand
arithmetic. New `dr4_ready_1/arm_c_rows.py` reads a pipeline log and prints, per footing, sigma_tot, z_C, and the row chosen by the
operative z-rule. It also prints the illustrative-edge interval and the Amendment 21 reminder. Every number is read from the frozen
text (gamma_C, sigma_sys, the edges, the 1.23 guard). No verdict word is printed, and the stability requirements are not
evaluated: the operator reads them from the log. DR3 code-path values: z_C = +1.28 / +1.41 (builder), non-scoring.
