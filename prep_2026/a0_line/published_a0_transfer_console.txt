     make the published number an UNDER-estimate, not an over-estimate.
  3. THE REPO'S BIAS IS ESTIMATOR-SPECIFIC AND GETS WORSE, NOT BETTER, ON THE FULL
     RANGE: gls_origin goes from +10.3 pp (gas-dom) to +16.1 pp (full range).
     It is a property of that statistic, not of SPARC.
  4. DECOMPOSITION OF THE GAP between this analysis's full-sample value and 1.2e-10:
