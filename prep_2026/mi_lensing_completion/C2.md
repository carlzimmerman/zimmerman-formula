## Why this class closes the wedge pure MI cannot

The falsification of pure MI (`mi_lensing_final/`) is that its assembled stress tensor
sources $\rho_{\rm eff}=\rho\,K=\rho/\nu$ — **suppressed** — plus a bounded $O(K)$
tension term, giving $F=g_{\rm lens}/(\nu g_{\rm bar})<1/\nu$ (Brouwer 2021, ~27σ).
The gap is the **phantom mass** $M_{\rm ph}=(\nu-1)M_{\rm bar}$ that MI never sources
gravitationally.

The DW nonlocal term sources exactly that phantom mass **on the single metric, as a
genuine scalar energy density** (§1 of the script):

| branch | isotropic source | anisotropic stress | result |
|---|---|---|---|
| pure MI (falsified) | $\rho/\nu$ (down) | $-\rho_{\rm eff}/(2y{+}1)$ (tension) | $F<1/\nu$ |
| **DW nonlocal** | $\nu\rho$ (up, phantom added) | **0** | **$F=1$** |

The phantom density carries no anisotropic stress, so the **no-slip theorem** gives
$\Phi=\Psi$, hence $g_{\rm lens}=\Phi'=\nu g_{\rm bar}$ and the deep-MOND slope
$\nu g_{\rm bar}\to\sqrt{a_0 g_{\rm bar}}$ (both machine-checked). Lensing tracks
dynamics — the whole point of a single-metric MG realization.

## The crux — is $a_0$ derived or free? (both footings)

**FREE.** This is the honest cost that makes C2 a partial, not an MI completion.

- The MOND relation $g_{\rm obs}=\sqrt{g_{\rm bar}^2+a_0g_{\rm bar}}$ is **form-invariant**
  under $(a_0,g_{\rm bar})\to\lambda(a_0,g_{\rm bar})$ (machine-checked): $a_0$ sets **no
  preferred scale** inside $f$.
- Consequently **both footings fit equally** — $9.36\times10^{-11}$ and
  $1.13\times10^{-10}$ are just two values of the fitted coupling. $a_0$ is
  **footing-non-diagnostic**, the signature of a free parameter.
- There is **no passive frame $u$, no kernel $K(\Box_u)$, no $Z=\sqrt{32\pi/3}$.** The
  nonlocal operator acts on $R$ (matter curvature), not on the vacuum frame. The
  vacuum/horizon **derivation** $a_0=cH_\Lambda/Z$ is forfeited.

**The one partial in the partial:** $\Box^{-1}$ carries a horizon IR scale (memory over
$\sim1/H$), so $a_0\sim cH_\Lambda$ is **natural parametrically** — DW-nonlocal makes the
famous $a_0\sim cH$ coincidence structural. But *natural* $\ne$ *derived*: the coefficient
$Z=\sqrt{32\pi/3}=5.789$ and hence the exact $9.36\times10^{-11}$ are **not** sourced by
this construction. The MI-distinctive content is lost.
