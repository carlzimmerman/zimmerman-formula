## What the variation gives (Sec. 2)

For the scalar-Ricci form the field equation is the standard scalar-tensor one,
$$(1+F)\,G_{\mu\nu} + \big(g_{\mu\nu}\Box-\nabla_\mu\nabla_\nu\big)F(K) \;=\; \tfrac{8\pi G}{c^4}T_{\mu\nu},$$
so the **new** gravitating source (vs. GR) is $-(g_{\mu\nu}\Box-\nabla_\mu\nabla_\nu)F(K)$. Its weak-field,
static $00$-projection is a **Laplacian of a local scalar**, $\propto\nabla^2 F(K)$ — a *total divergence*.
By Gauss (machine-checked: $4\pi r^2\nabla^2F=\partial_r[4\pi r^2 F']$) the enclosed phantom mass is a pure
surface flux
$$\boxed{\,M_{\rm ph}(r)=\frac{c^2}{G}\,\kappa\,r^2\,F'(r)\,}\qquad(\kappa=O(1)\ \text{from the variation}).$$
This *is* the QUMOND / nonlocal-MG phantom-source mechanism (Deffayet–Woodard 2011; Skordis–Zlosnik AeST;
Milgrom) — **but here $F$ is a LOCAL function of the acceleration $|a|$**, and that is what breaks it.

## The decider: a universal local $F(K)$ cannot source $(\nu-1)M_{\rm bar}$ (Sec. 3–4)

Impose lensing closure $M_{\rm ph}(r)=(\nu-1)M_{\rm bar}(r)$ on the cleanest source, a point mass $M$
($g_{\rm bar}=GM/r^2$, $y=GM/(a_0 r^2)$). With $F=F(y)$ universal, the requirement reduces (sympy-exact) to
$$F'(y) \;=\; -\,\frac{\nu(y)-1}{2\kappa\sqrt y}\,\frac{\sqrt{G\,M\,a_0}}{c^2}\;\propto\;\sqrt{M}.$$
The required shape **carries $\sqrt{M}$**: $F'_{\rm req}(y;M_2)/F'_{\rm req}(y;M_1)=\sqrt{M_2/M_1}$,
independent of $y$. A local $F(K)$ sees only $|a|$ — it is **blind to the source mass** — so **no single
universal $F$ can source the phantom for more than one object.** (This is the textbook reason local
curvature couplings don't give the MOND phantom: the phantom needs the *nonlocal inverse-Laplacian*
of QUMOND, not a local $F(|a|)$.)

Quantified miss (both footings identical): fix $F$ on a $6\times10^{10}M_\odot$ galaxy and apply it to a
$10^{14}M_\odot$ cluster — the cluster receives a phantom fraction $\sqrt{M_1/M_2}=0.024$, i.e.
$F_{\rm lens}=0.32$ ($y{=}0.1$) → $0.12$ ($y{=}0.01$): **massive under-lensing**. Reverse the tuning and the
galaxy is **over-lensed $\sim$40×** ($F_{\rm lens}=29$–$37$). Verified closed form
$M_{\rm ph}^{\rm produced}=(\nu-1)\sqrt{M_aM_b}\ne(\nu-1)M_b$.

## Ghost / Ostrogradsky (Sec. 5)

Under **honest** metric variation $X=|a|^2/a_0^2$ is metric-dependent: the static-frame acceleration is
$a^r=dP/(1+2P)\to\Phi'=\partial_r g$ (machine-checked), so $F(K(X))=F((\Phi')^2/a_0^2)$ is a function of
**first derivatives of the metric**. Then the field-equation term $\Box F(K)$ contains
$\partial^2[(\partial g)^2]\sim(\partial g)(\partial^3 g)$ — **third metric derivatives** (verified: $\Box F$
contains $\Phi'''$). There is no $f(R)$-type degeneracy (F couples through the *connection*/$|a|$, not
through $R$), so this is a genuine **Ostrogradsky ghost**. The only ghost-free reading **freezes** $X$
(treats $|a|$ as an externally prescribed, $g$-independent profile) → $F(K)$ becomes a *prescribed* scalar
$\phi(x)$ and $\phi R$ is Brans–Dicke with $\phi$ put in by hand — i.e. one has **left MI for prescribed
nonlocal QUMOND**.

## Verdict

As posed — **local, ghost-free, $a_0$-derived** — the candidate **FAILS LENSING**: the non-universality
($\propto\sqrt M$, Sec. 3–4) and the Ostrogradsky ghost (Sec. 5) are independent, decisive obstructions,

nonlocally — sliding to QUMOND/nonlocal-MG: then it is single-metric and (in that reading) ghost-free, but
