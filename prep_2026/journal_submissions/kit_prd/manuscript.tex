\begin{abstract}
\noindent
The de Sitter--Unruh modified-inertia (MI) program reproduces galaxy dynamics through a passive vacuum-response kernel $K(\Boxu/a_0^2)$ that ties the MOND acceleration scale to the cosmological horizon, $a_0=cH_\Lambda/Z$ with $Z=\sqrt{32\pi/3}$. We present a single covariant action realizing this program --- Einstein--Hilbert gravity, a passive unit-timelike frame with zero propagating degrees of freedom, and a matter sector dressed by a bounded, causal, positive-measure (Herglotz--Nevanlinna) kernel --- and show that the coupled system is ghost-free and well posed, conditional on the passive-frame premise. The interpolating kernel $\nu=\sqrt{1+1/y}$ is Milgrom's (1999) and the $a_0\propto\sqrt{\rho_{\rm DE}}$ scaling is Limbach--Psaltis--\"Ozel's (2008); the distinctive content is the $cH_\Lambda/Z$ coefficient and the covariant passive-frame completion, with the value of $a_0$, the constant $Z$, and the sign $s=-1$ postulated, not derived. Within this theory we prove an obstruction: conditional on kernel passivity, a horizon-tied $a_0$ ($D$) and single-metric propagation with $c_\gamma=\cgw$ ($S$) forbid single-metric MOND weak lensing ($L$): $D\wedge S\Rightarrow\lnot L$, with $\{D,S,L\}$ the unique minimal unsatisfiable subset of $\{D,S,G,L\}$ and ghost-freedom $G$ entering no step. The mechanism is a passivity/amplification dichotomy: a passive kernel ($\sup K=1$) can only suppress a source to $\rho/\nu$, whereas the MOND-lensing phantom demands enhancement to $\nu\rho$. Completions reproducing lensing exist, but only as modified gravity with $a_0$ demoted to a free coupling. All results are reported on both cosmological footings ($9.36\times10^{-11}$ and $1.13\times10^{-10}\,\ms$); the obstruction is footing-independent, and every load-bearing claim traces to a committed, runnable script.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

\subsection{The framework, and the credit}

Modified inertia (MI) treats MOND phenomenology not as a modification of the gravitational field sourced by matter, but as a modification of the inertial response of matter to a field. Any honest statement of this program begins with the credit. Milgrom's original MOND is Ref.~\cite{milgrom1983}. In 1999 Milgrom observed \cite{milgrom1999} that a non-inertial observer in a de Sitter universe sees an Unruh temperature $T\propto\sqrt{a^2+c^2H_\Lambda^2}$, and that the excess of that temperature over the inertial-observer floor defines an acceleration function with exactly the MOND limits; his Eq.~(9) contains the interpolating modified-inertia kernel used here, machine-verified identical. The $a_0\propto\sqrt{\rho_{\rm DE}}$ scale coincidence likewise predates this work: it is Limbach, Psaltis \& \"Ozel \cite{lpo2008}. The framework's distinctive content is precisely (i) the coefficient --- $a_0=cH_\Lambda/Z$ with $Z=\sqrt{32\pi/3}=5.789$, a factor $2Z\approx11.58$ below Milgrom's $\hat a_0=2cH_\Lambda$ --- and (ii) the covariant modified-inertia completion built on a passive frame, which Milgrom's 1999 note explicitly left open (he declined to endorse the kernel for circular orbits absent a modified-inertia formulation).

In the de Sitter--Unruh realization, a passive cosmic rest frame $u^\mu$ --- a unit-timelike vector carrying \textbf{zero} propagating degrees of freedom, fixed by a Lagrange constraint $(u\cdot u+1)=0$ --- mediates a vacuum response whose kernel acts on the frame d'Alembertian $\Boxu$. The worldline law that emerges is
\begin{equation}
\gobs=\nu(y)\,\gbar,\qquad \nu(y)=\sqrt{1+\tfrac1y},\qquad y\equiv\frac{\gbar}{a_0},
\label{eq:law}
\end{equation}
reducing to the deep-MOND law $\gobs\to\sqrt{a_0\gbar}$ as $y\to0$ and to Newton as $y\to\infty$. This fits the SPARC rotation-curve sample \cite{lelli2016} at $0.108$ dex scatter ($\Upsilon=0.70$), competitive with regularized MOND (reproducible via the companion tool \texttt{a0kit} \cite{a0kit}).

Throughout, \emph{footing} denotes the choice of cosmological input to $a_0=cH_\Lambda/Z$: the \textbf{canonical} pure-$\Lambda$ footing ($\rho_{\rm DE}$, $cH_\Lambda/Z$) gives $a_0=9.36\times10^{-11}\,\ms$, and the \textbf{alternate} footing ($\rho_{\rm total}$, $cH_0/Z$) gives $1.13\times10^{-10}\,\ms$. Galaxy dynamics is a worldline phenomenon; weak lensing is not. Lensing probes the deflection of null geodesics, fixed by the gravitating stress tensor that the field equations actually see. This paper contains two results, presented as one story. First (Secs.~\ref{sec:action}--\ref{sec:tmunu}), the \emph{positive} construction: a single covariant action realizing the MI program, with its degree-of-freedom count, kernel rigor, stress tensor, and ghost analysis, machine-verified. \emph{Assume kernel passivity. Then $D\wedge S\Rightarrow\lnot L$: no field theory extending the MI action keeps $a_0$ tied to the passive kernel ($D$), propagates photons and gravitons on one metric ($S$), and reproduces the single-metric MOND-lensing phantom $(\nu-1)\rho$ ($L$). The physical crux is a passivity/amplification dichotomy (Sec.~\ref{sec:dichotomy}): the property that derives $a_0$'s scale (passivity, $\sup K=1$) is the very property that forbids the lensing enhancement ($\nu>1$, outside the passive cone). We frame this as neither a triumph nor a funeral: it is an exact obstruction, conditional on passivity, that shows the $a_0$ reframing is load-bearing, and it redirects the search for an MI-versus-MG discriminant away from lensing.

\subsection{The worldline law: first-moment reduction}\label{sec:firstmoment}

The bridge from the nonlocal operator to phenomenology is the first-moment identity \cite{zconcept}
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\;u_\mu\,\Boxu\,u^\mu=-|a|^2\;}
\end{equation}
re-derived three independent ways (flat general worldline; general curved metric, symbolic, arbitrary $g_{ab}$ and full connection; static Schwarzschild observer in closed form). The only inputs are unit norm $u\cdot u=-1$ and metric compatibility $\nabla g=0$: no circularity, no geodesy, no spacetime symmetry. Hence $K(\Boxu/a_0^2)\to K(X)$ with $X=|a|^2/a_0^2$ and $a^\mu=u^b\nabla_b u^\mu$ --- the acceleration enters as a first-order \emph{field} gradient, not a worldline $\ddot x$, which is how the classic modified-inertia Ostrogradsky trap is evaded.

On the rotation-curve shell the kernel argument collapses. With the on-shell condition $|a|=\gobs=\nu\gbar$ (so $X=y^2+y$), one has $1+4X=(2y+1)^2$, and the radical collapses:
\begin{equation}
K\big|_{\rm on\text{-}shell}=\frac{(2y+1)-1}{2\sqrt{y^2+y}}=\frac{1}{\nu(y)}\ \text{exactly},\qquad
\left.\frac{2K'X}{K}\right|_{\rm on\text{-}shell}=\frac{1}{2y+1}\le1,
\label{eq:onshell}
\end{equation}
giving the ring-exact law Eq.~(\ref{eq:law}) with ring residual ${\sim}10^{-13}$, both footings. The on-shell dressing is the \emph{suppression} $1/\nu\le1$, and the anisotropic slip factor $2K'X/K=1/(2y+1)$ is bounded and tension-signed. The static real-space dressing $K(X)$ is a bounded, increasing (Bernstein-type) function: $K(0^+)=0$ at the deep-MOND end and
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\ \sup_{X\ge0}K(X)=1\ }\qquad(\text{Newtonian saturation; }K\to0\text{ deep-MOND}),
\label{eq:supK}
\end{equation}
saturated as $X\to\infty$. ($K(X)$ is not completely monotone --- completely monotone functions decrease; here $K$ increases and $K'$ is completely monotone, the Bernstein/Stieltjes dual. The Herglotz--Nevanlinna, $\|K\|\le1$, and sum-rule properties attach to the frequency-domain response kernel $K(\Boxu)$; the static $K(X)$ is the corresponding real-space dressing.) The single fact the obstruction uses downstream is the uniform bound (\ref{eq:supK}): the passive kernel can only ever \emph{suppress} a source to $\rho K\le\rho$.

\begin{equation}

\frac{\Mph(r)}{r}\;\xrightarrow{\ r\to\infty\ }\;\sqrt{\frac{a_0M}{G}}\neq0,
\end{equation}
an \textbf{unbounded halo} whose enclosed mass keeps growing where $\rho=0$. Because $a_0$ enters only through $X$ inside $K$ and the prefactor is the bare $c^4/16\pi G$, this is a clean $D$-side witness. Scalar--Ricci variation gives the standard scalar-tensor form; the new gravitating source is $-(g_{\mu\nu}\Box-\nabla_\mu\nabla_\nu)F(K)$, whose static $00$ projection is a Laplacian of a local scalar. By Gauss the enclosed phantom is a pure surface flux, $\Mph(r)=(c^2/G)\,\kappa\,r^2F'(r)$ --- nonzero off-source, so a local term \emph{does} source \emph{a} phantom. But imposing lensing closure $\Mph=(\nu-1)M_{\rm bar}$ on a point mass yields (sympy-exact)
\begin{equation}
F'(y)=-\frac{\nu(y)-1}{2\kappa\sqrt y}\,\frac{\sqrt{GMa_0}}{c^2}\;\propto\;\sqrt M.
\end{equation}
The required shape \textbf{carries the source mass}: $F'_{\rm req}(y;M_2)/F'_{\rm req}(y;M_1)=\sqrt{M_2/M_1}$, independent of $y$. A local $F(K)$ sees only $|a|$ --- it is blind to the source mass --- so no single universal $F$ sources the phantom for more than one object (Lemma~1). Fixing $F$ on a $6\times10^{10}M_\odot$ galaxy and applying it to a $10^{14}M_\odot$ cluster, the cluster receives phantom fraction $\sqrt{6\times10^{10}/10^{14}}\approx0.024$, i.e.\ ${\sim}2.4\%$ of the needed phantom --- under-lensed by a factor ${\approx}41$; the reverse tuning over-lenses by the same factor. The miss is footing-independent ($a_0$ cancels in the ratio). Separately, under honest metric variation $X=|a|^2/a_0^2$ is a function of first metric derivatives, so $\Box F(K)$ contains third metric derivatives ($\Phi''')$ --- a genuine Ostrogradsky ghost, unless one freezes $X$ and prescribes the scalar by hand (no longer MI). Crucially, \textbf{C1 fails $L$ by mass-blindness regardless of its ghost}, so it is a clean $D=1$ witness for the ``$G$ is not the lever'' step. Verdict: \textbf{fails lensing, $D$ kept}.

\section{The obstruction theorem}\label{sec:theorem}

\subsection{Statement}

\textbf{Theorem (conditional on kernel passivity).} \emph{Assume the passivity premise (Sec.~\ref{sec:action}). Then $D\wedge S\Rightarrow\lnot L$: no field theory extending the MI action keeps $a_0$ as the passive-kernel argument ($D$), propagates on one metric ($S$), and reproduces the MOND-lensing phantom $(\nu-1)\rho$ ($L$). Equivalently, $\{D,S,L\}$ is the \textbf{unique minimal unsatisfiable subset} of $\{D,S,G,L\}$: each proper subset is realized ---}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{$\{D,S\}$ $=$ pure MI (the present theory; $L$ off);}
\item \emph{$\{S,L\}$ $=$ Deffayet--Esposito-Far\`ese--Woodard / AeST with $a_0$ a \textbf{free} coupling ($D$ off);}
\item \emph{$\{D,L\}$ $=$ a disformal two-cone construction $\tilde g=g+B(\Boxu)\,u_\mu u_\nu$ with $B$ in the passive kernel, which keeps $a_0$ derived but carries a second photon cone excluded observationally by GW170817 ($S$ off).}
\end{itemize}
\emph{Ghost-freedom $G$ enters no step.}

The connective is ``cannot coexist,'' $\lnot(D\wedge L)$ under $S$ --- \emph{not} exclusive-or: the pure-GR corner has neither $D$ nor $L$, which an XOR would misclassify. Earlier notes that wrote ``$D\oplus L$ / XOR'' are corrected here.

\subsection{Why $S$ is essential (and the theorem non-redundant)}\label{sec:whyS}

Because $L$ is stated metric-agnostically (Sec.~\ref{sec:desiderata}), $S$ is a genuine, independent hypothesis with a precise role: under $S$, lensing is fixed by the one metric's $T_{\mu\nu}$, so $L$ forces the phantom to be real gravitating stress-energy, and Lemma~1 then bites. Drop $S$ --- allow a disformal second cone $\tilde g=g+B\,uu$ --- and photons see a \emph{different} metric: the phantom becomes pure geometry with no new stress-energy, Lemma~1 is evaded, $a_0$ stays inside the passive kernel $B(\Boxu)$, and $L$ is achieved with $D$ kept. That $\{D,L,\lnot S\}$ construction is logically satisfiable as a field theory; it is killed only \emph{observationally}, by GW170817 \cite{abbott2017}: a disformal coupling large enough to carry the $O(\nu)$ lensing deflection splits the photon and graviton cones far above the bound $|c_\gamma/\cgw-1|\lesssim10^{-15}$ --- computed in full along the actual NGC~4993 sightline, the exclusion is ${\sim}6$--$7$ orders of magnitude, robust across both footings \cite{zftE,boran2018} --- while the GW-safe small-$B$ regime cannot source an $O(\nu)$ deflection: the same passivity/amplification wedge, now on the disformal axis. (A merely \emph{conformal} bimetric $\tilde g=A(\phi)g$ preserves the null cone but cannot bend light beyond its baryonic content at all --- the historical reason TeVeS \cite{bekenstein2004} needed a disformal/vector part.) This is exactly why $S$ belongs in the minimal unsatisfiable subset: the $\{D,L\}$ witness exists, and $S$ is what excludes it.

\section{The passivity/amplification dichotomy}\label{sec:dichotomy}

Here is the physical crux, the intended central idea of the paper.

$a_0=cH_\Lambda/Z$ is scale-derivable \emph{only because} the MI modification is a passive, causal vacuum-response kernel with a normalized positive measure. Passivity floors the response amplitude at the horizon scale --- it is the source of the scale-tie. But passivity has a mathematical consequence: $\sup K=1$. A passive kernel can only \textbf{suppress}. On the RAR shell it delivers exactly $\rho/\nu$; deep-MOND $K\to0$, the source dressed all the way down.

MOND lensing demands the opposite. The phantom $(\nu-1)\rho$ is an \textbf{enhancement} to $\nu\rho$, with $\nu>1$ --- strictly outside the passive cone whose supremum is 1. The needed $uu$ coefficient is $\nu-1/\nu=1/\sqrt{y(y+1)}$ (unbounded, deep ${\sim}1/\sqrt y$); the passive kernel supplies $1/\nu$; the shortfall $\nu^2-1=1/y$ diverges as $y\to0$. So the dichotomy is exact and structural, not a failed search:
\begin{quote}
\textbf{The very property that ties $a_0$'s scale to the vacuum --- passivity, $\sup K=1$ --- is the property that forbids the lensing phantom.} Enhancement requires an anti-dissipative (pumped) kernel, whose amplitude is a free coupling, so $a_0$ would no longer be $cH_\Lambda/Z$.
\end{quote}
The nonlocality lemma closes the loop from the other side: the mass-correct phantom of a point mass is an unbounded halo ($\Mph(r)/r\to\sqrt{a_0M/G}$), so sourcing it universally forces a nonlocal carrier with a spatial-kinetic term --- a propagating dof whose acceleration scale is a free coupling. Passivity forbids the enhancement; the halo's nonlocality forces propagation; propagation frees $a_0$. The two horns are the same wall seen from two sides.

Two honest guards on this statement. First, boundedness ($\lnot L$) uses \emph{only} $\sup K=1$ --- it does not invoke the $a_0$ value at all. So ``$a_0$ scale-tied'' and ``$\lnot L$'' are not one claim chained back on itself; they are two consequences of the \textbf{one} passivity premise (a common-premise entailment, not a biconditional --- we do not claim, and do not need, the converse ``$a_0$-derived $\Rightarrow$ passive''). Second, this is sufficiency, not necessity: passivity $\Rightarrow$ ($a_0$ scale-tied $\wedge\ \lnot L$). Whether some non-passive kernel could also tie $a_0$'s scale --- breaking the lock --- is precisely the open pump door of Sec.~\ref{sec:conditionality}, so the lock itself is conditional on staying inside the passive class.

\section{Scope, conditionality, and consequences}\label{sec:conditionality}

We are explicit, both ways, about the strength of this result.

\textbf{It is conditional.} The obstruction rests on the single premise of MI-kernel passivity: the response is bounded, causal, positive-measure (Herglotz--Nevanlinna), $\|K\|\le1$, with the sum rule $\int d\mu/|t|=1$, so its real-space dressing obeys $\sup K=1$. This premise is banked and frozen \cite{zv4,zv11} but it is a \emph{premise}, not a metatheorem. Were the kernel not passive, the argument would not run.

\textbf{Within the passive class, $D$ and $\lnot L$ share one origin.} The same passivity premise is what ties $a_0$'s scale and what caps $K$ at 1; $\lnot L$ follows from the cap alone (Sec.~\ref{sec:dichotomy}). One cannot, inside the passive class, keep the scale-tie while discarding the boundedness that forbids $L$ --- they are two faces of the one hypothesis, which is what makes the obstruction a real dichotomy rather than a coincidence of two separate assumptions.

\textbf{The one MI escape, and why it is currently shut.} The only way out that keeps $a_0$ tied to $cH_\Lambda$ with a derived amplitude is a causal \emph{enhancing} kernel ($\nu>1$) --- an anti-dissipative / pumped vacuum response. The banked sign walls \cite{zv4,zv11} find no such channel pump-free: passive, reactive, and 4-point/dS-positivity edges are all sign-blind or anti-MOND-locked, and the enhancement reopens only under a new forced pump mechanism that has not been exhibited. \textbf{We do not claim the theory is closed.} The door labeled ``forced pump'' remains open in principle; what the banked results establish is that it is not open by any pump-free route so far identified --- and, since a non-passive $a_0$-scale-tying kernel is exactly what would break the $D$/boundedness lock, that same door is the one place the lock could fail.

\textbf{Consequences.}
\emph{(i) A completion exists --- as modified gravity, at the cost of the $a_0$ scale-tie.} C2 and C3b constructively demonstrate a single-metric, GW170817-safe field theory reproducing MOND dynamics and MOND lensing with the correct deep slope --- real, honest content --- but it is modified gravity in the Deffayet--Esposito-Far\`ese--Woodard / AeST class \cite{dew2011,skordis2021}, forfeiting the vacuum-tied $a_0=cH_\Lambda/Z$, plus (C2) a separate cosmology fit and a contested would-be ghost, or (C3b) one new propagating scalar and a forfeited cosmology. The cost is precisely the program's single most distinctive claim.
\emph{(ii) Lensing becomes a shared-MG observable.} Because the only completions that reproduce the lensing phantom are MG, weak lensing can no longer discriminate MI from MG --- both predict the same $F\to1$ relation. The clean modified-gravity-impossible discriminant that remains is the non-adiabatic relational velocity-dispersion spread (${\sim}6$--$13\%$ in MI from frame-history-dependent inertia; exactly zero in MG) --- currently underpowered; it is developed separately in Ref.~\cite{zsigma} and only pointed to here. Any MG completion also inherits the AeST-class Solar-System $Q_2$-quadrupole caveat \cite{desmond2024,park2026}, a shared-MG cost, honestly stated.
\emph{(iii) The obstruction shows the $a_0$ reframing is load-bearing.} If $a_0=cH_\Lambda/Z$ were a cosmetic relabeling, one could keep it while adding any lensing structure. That it is incompatible --- within the passive class --- with single-metric MOND lensing shows it is a genuine, restrictive, physical commitment. Far from a funeral, this is evidence the reframing is doing work.

\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

We have presented a single covariant action for the de Sitter--Unruh modified-inertia program --- Einstein--Hilbert gravity, a passive unit-timelike frame with a machine-verified second-class Dirac closure keeping it at zero propagating degrees of freedom, and a matter sector dressed by a rigorously defined Herglotz--Nevanlinna kernel --- ghost-free and well posed conditional on the passive-frame premise, with the ring-exact reduction to Milgrom's interpolating law as a derivation from the first-moment identity $u\cdot\Boxu u=-|a|^2$ rather than an ansatz. Within this theory we have identified, and machine-verified on both footings, an obstruction --- conditional on kernel passivity: \textbf{a passive-kernel-tied $a_0$ and single-metric MOND weak lensing cannot coexist.} The central implication is $D\wedge S\Rightarrow\lnot L$; equivalently $\{D,S,L\}$ is the unique minimal unsatisfiable subset of $\{D,S,G,L\}$, with $\{D,S\}$ $=$ pure MI, $\{S,L\}$ $=$ Deffayet--Esposito-Far\`ese--Woodard / AeST ($a_0$ free), and $\{D,L\}$ $=$ a disformal two-cone construction ($a_0$ kept) killed observationally by GW170817. Ghost-freedom is not the lever. The mechanism is a passivity/amplification dichotomy reinforced by a nonlocality lemma; three explicit candidates witness the surviving corners. The result is conditional on kernel passivity --- but that premise is the very thing that ties $a_0$'s scale, so within the passive class the scale-tie and the obstruction are two faces of one hypothesis. We do not claim the theory is closed. The honest map: the MI program is complete for dynamics ($0.108$ dex on SPARC) and viable in cosmology, and its lensing sector completes only by crossing into modified gravity at the cost of the $a_0$ reframing. Lensing is now a shared observable; the clean discriminant is the relational $\sigma$-spread \cite{zsigma}. And the obstruction itself is the strongest evidence that the $a_0=cH_\Lambda/Z$ reframing is load-bearing physics rather than a relabeling.
