Please consider the enclosed manuscript, "A covariant, ghost-free modified-inertia completion of the horizon-tied acceleration scale a0 = cH_Lambda/Z, and a passivity obstruction to single-metric MOND lensing," for publication in Physical Review D (Gravitation and Cosmology).

The single result. The paper writes down a covariant, ghost-free field theory in which the MOND acceleration scale a0 appears only as the horizon-floored argument of a passive vacuum-response kernel -- a modified-INERTIA realization, with a passive unit-timelike frame carrying zero propagating degrees of freedom and the phenomenology living in the matter sector. Within that construction we prove an exact obstruction theorem: conditional on kernel passivity, {a0 tied to the passive kernel} and {single-metric, c_gamma = c_GW} together forbid single-metric MOND weak lensing (D AND S implies NOT L; {D,S,L} is the unique minimal unsatisfiable subset of {D,S,G,L}, with ghost-freedom G entering no step). The mechanism is a passivity/amplification dichotomy: the property that ties a0's scale to the de Sitter horizon (sup K = 1) is the same property that caps the kernel's dressing at suppression rho/nu, whereas MOND lensing requires enhancement to nu*rho.

Why PRD. This is a gravitational field-theory result -- a covariant action, its Hamiltonian degree-of-freedom count, a ghost/Ostrogradsky analysis, and an obstruction theorem relating the theory's stress tensor to a weak-lensing observable -- positioned explicitly against PRD's modified-gravity literature: AeST (Skordis & Zlosnik, PRL 2021), the Deffayet-Esposito-Farese-Woodard nonlocal completions (PRD 2011), and Bekenstein's TeVeS (PRD 2004).

Honest scope, stated up front. The value of a0, the constant Z = sqrt(32*pi/3), and the coupling sign s = -1 are postulated, not derived -- this is a one-parameter effective field theory, not a first-principles computation of a0. The interpolating kernel nu(y) = sqrt(1+1/y) is Milgrom's (1999; his coefficient was 2cH_Lambda), and the a0 ~ sqrt(rho_DE) coincidence is Limbach-Psaltis-Ozel's (2008); the distinctive content is the cH_Lambda/Z coefficient and the covariant passive-frame completion. I have publicly retracted, and do not re-assert, earlier claims of a derived a0, a Standard-Model bridge, or a "theory of everything." The obstruction cuts against the program's own most distinctive claim, and I present it that way: a clean, falsifiable negative structural result on the theory space.
