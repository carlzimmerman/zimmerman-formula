FOR J0332 — image-level test of the dark-energy acceleration-scale model
2026-11-13

OUTCOME

The specified smooth, isolated, unscreened AQUAL galaxy branch can reproduce
the Euclid VIS arcs with the released stellar-mass uncertainty included.
Conditioning on the released multiple-image association, zero external
convergence, and the fixed light geometry, it requires about 1.08–1.10e12 Msun
of stars. The released Salpeter SED estimate is 6.78e11 Msun, with its 16–84%
interval 5.33–8.27e11 Msun. The fitted mass is about 1.59–1.62 times its median.

This is moderate stellar-mass tension, approximately 2.3–2.5 upper-side
catalogue standard deviations under those conditional error summaries.
That number is NOT a calibrated physical-theory exclusion significance.
The images are compatible with this branch; neither a decisive falsification
nor a clean confirmation of the complete adopted screened theory follows.
No new-physics breakthrough has been established.

NOT a fresh joint eight-band stellar-population fit.

The field solves div[mu(|gradPhi|/a0) gradPhi]=4piG rho with
mu(x)=x/(1+x), using an axisymmetric finite-volume solver. Deflections are
computed by line-of-sight projection of the field, with equal weak-field
metric potentials. The non-spherical algebraic MOND shortcut was not used.

Stars follow an edge-on oblate, constant-M/L, Abel-deprojected Sersic profile.
The COOLEST point light shape is held fixed:
  geometric-mean Re=1.1391817887 arcsec, n=5.86871910, q=0.8133792204.
The observed light centre and angle are used. Stellar mass and two external
shear components are fitted; baseline external convergence is zero.
The adopted galaxy branch sets mu_K=0. No bound dark halo was added.
Gas and a central black hole were not independently constrained or fitted;
the mass-to-light model attributes the fitted baryonic component to stars.
Distances use flat LambdaCDM with H0=67.74km/s/Mpc and Omega_m=.3089.
No evolving-dark-energy history or DESI cosmological likelihood was jointly
fitted; this tests the specified fixed acceleration value at the lens redshift.

A continuous bilinear source grid is reconstructed through the PSF. Proper
Gaussian source, lens-light-amplitude and sky priors permit analytic
marginalization over their linear coefficients. Source regularization is
sampled in the final posterior. The stellar mass constraint is a two-half-
normal quantile approximation with median log10M=11.8310873126 and lower/
upper 68% offsets 0.1041923878 / 0.0863788677 dex. The quoted mass errors were
not mistaken for absolute bounds, and the Salpeter IMF was not switched.
Shear components have independent zero-mean Gaussian priors of width0.1.

SOURCE ASSOCIATION IS A NECESSARY CONDITION

The release supplies four candidate multiple-image positions and a maximum
source-plane separation threshold0.3 arcsec. The final conditional posterior
requires those positions to trace within that separation. A 0.5arcsec
threshold is a separately reported sensitivity check, not additional data.

Without this association condition, a wide source grid finds a branch at
the lower searched mass bound, logM=11.56, partly reconstructing the observed
arc as intrinsic source structure. Its position separation is1.518arcsec;
the released SIE has0.19155arcsec. This is the known demagnified-source
failure mode of flexible lens inversions. It must not be silently discarded,
nor interpreted as a robust measurement of low stellar mass. All branches
are preserved. The final mass is explicitly conditional on the strong-lens
source association; it is not a source-prior-independent mass measurement.

SOURCE, NOISE AND NUMERICAL CHECKS

With the0.3arcsec association gate, wide/compact source grids yield
logM=12.033283 / 12.041171: a1.83% mass shift. The0.5arcsec checks retain a
similar high-mass branch. Several starts were tested; inferior local optima
are recorded and no global optimization proof is claimed.

Before association conditioning, other source-grid/centre/subpixel choices
shifted the high-mass branch by0.48%. A separable correlated-noise sensitivity
with nearest-neighbour correlation0.23 gives logM=12.04056, close to the
diagonal-RMS result. This approximate covariance model is not a measured
full covariance. A separate global RMS rescaling is labelled only a stress
case and is not substituted for the actual noise model.

A source-only holdout of49 faint-counterimage pixels gives chi2=30.25 for
AQUAL and48.08 for the fixed SIE control. The mass/shear used these pixels
in their earlier fit and PSF coupling remains, so this is a consistency
check, not an independent validation dataset.

Nonnegative-source MAP checks at fixed pre-gate nonlinear parameters still
fit: chi2 rises by66.98 for AQUAL and87.72 for SIE. Neither unconstrained
source contains a pixel negative by more than3 conditional standard
deviations. These checks do not turn a Gaussian-source marginal likelihood
into a positivity-constrained Bayesian evidence.

Independent field checks cover spherical Abel projection, analytic elliptical
Newtonian deflections, density normalization, AQUAL mesh/outer-domain/LOS
refinement and map interpolation. At the tested arc positions, AQUAL mesh
refinement changes deflection by at most0.0623%, outer-domain doubling by
0.00309%, LOS refinement by0.00119%; map interpolation was checked to about
0.026%. These are finite tests with explicit ranges, not universal bounds.

An initial source-window implementation was discontinuous. Its old fits
were withdrawn, preserved, and rerun with a continuous zero-padded basis.
Even the corrected bilinear basis has derivative kinks; inverse-Hessian
uncertainties were therefore rejected. The first two sampler runs did not
meet their registered convergence checks and are not the final posterior.

MATERIAL PHYSICAL LIMITS

The ECLIPSE-D methods explicitly define Re=sqrt(ab), a circularized scale.
All non-VIS shapes are fixed to VIS; their fluxes and centres can change.
The photometric extraction also freezes the initial lens mass model. Thus
the SED mass constraint is conditional and data-dependent, not an independent
joint posterior for stellar mass, mass profile and lens geometry. Joint
shape covariances and IMF/SPS/M/L-gradient uncertainties are not supplied
by the marginal table. Earlier optical/NIR radii from Cabanac's de Vaucouleurs
fits are considerably smaller; this discrepancy has not been fully resolved.

The SED mass uses photometric z=0.98. Pure luminosity-distance rescaling to
the spectroscopic redshift would shift logM by+0.00653dex, mildly reducing
the quoted tension, but is not a replacement for a stellar-population refit.
The independent spherical calculation propagates reported Re,n,thetaE,z
marginals and obtains a similar conditional tension,2.475sigma canonical
and2.241sigma for the alternative a0. It is a diagnostic, not this image fit.

Changing only a0 to1.1312e-10 at the same geometry gives about logM12.01999
on the pre-gate high-mass branch, roughly5% lower. This is a sensitivity
calculation, not a complete alternative-cosmology fit or an a0 measurement.

Allowing a hypothetical external convergence can change the mass requirement.
At the SED median mass a fitted kappa_ext=0.30879 gives chi2=3169.53 and an
association separation0.21042arcsec. That convergence has NOT been measured;
the fit is worse than the baseline under the stated source prior. A free-
convergence search also runs to its negative search boundary, demonstrating
prior sensitivity rather than measuring an acceptable physical environment.
A lens-plane shear/convergence is not a self-consistent AQUAL external-field
or full multiplane solution. The environment cannot be silently used to
declare a successful theory prediction.

The adopted screened theory does not uniquely fix Lstar;21.7pc is a lower
bound. An actual smooth spherical solve of its leading weak screened-P
equation with the adopted charge blend gives negligible deflection changes
for the named floor/100pc/1kpc fixtures. This supports the smooth nominal
galaxy-limit approximation locally. It is not an oblate full-action solve,
an arbitrary-Lstar bound, a treatment of stellar granularity, or a covariant
accuracy certificate. See theory_audit.md and screened_diagnostic/README.md.

LEAN AND DESI

Nine algebraic identities compile in Lean, with independent review and a
deliberately false mutation that fails. They prove positive AQUAL flux/
residual scaling under explicit linear geometry assumptions, linear
projection scaling, mass-sheet/source-brightness identities, and density
scaling under the postulated square-root relation. They establish a precise
conditional identifiability limitation. They do not prove the observations,
the numerical PDE implementation, the screened action's validity, novelty,
or that the theory passes this lens.

The official DESI DR1 strong-lens VAC has no FOR J0332 counterpart. This is
not an exhaustive search of all spectroscopy. Its five1arcsec matches to
the ECLIPSE A/B catalogue identify L015780 as a possible future joint test,
with observed dispersion289.09+/-45.78km/s and lens z=.4282704. Its source
redshift remains unverified. An actual recovered DESI spectrum was searched
conservatively for background multiplets; no candidate passed. Cabanac's
302+/-12km/s for FOR is lens-inferred, not independent dynamical data.
