# CS28: can the collective vacuum select its couplings?

Continue MV28 without fitting a ratio. Test three routes: renormalization-group
fixed points/rays, exact superconformal constraints, and a larger multiplet
normalization. A negative result must identify its hypotheses and cannot be
promoted to a no-go for all microscopic theories.

Inputs: the canonical SU(5) two-adjoint plus singlet model and its one-loop
normalizations in `../microscopic_vacuum_2026_10_28/INVERTED_HIERARCHY.md`.
Starting repository revision: c6783290b3a6701d62c0dce1e3cd657ba26cad5d.
Other researchers' working files are outside this work's scope.

Success means either an independently selected numerical stiffness ratio,
or a verified discriminator that rules out a proposed selection mechanism
and specifies what a replacement model must change. It does not mean the
gravity matching, complete thresholds, or halo dynamics are closed.
