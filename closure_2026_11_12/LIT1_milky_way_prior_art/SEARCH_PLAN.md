# LIT1 SEARCH PLAN: Milky Way prior art under MOND / modified gravity

Written 2026-11-12, before any search was run. A literature sweep, not a graded lane. Everything
fetched from the web is PROVISIONAL and is cited by URL. Search queries contain only generic
physics terms and public arXiv identifiers.

## Questions

Q1. Since DESI DR2 Results III (arXiv:2611.05518, 3 Nov 2026), has anyone posted an analysis of the
Milky Way mass profile or its supporting papers (2611.05519, 2611.05520, 2611.05521) under MOND or
any modified gravity, or criticised its calibration on LambdaCDM mocks?

Q2. Published MOND / modified-gravity fits to the Milky Way rotation curve since Gaia DR3, including
responses to the claimed Keplerian decline (Jiao et al. 2023, Ou et al. 2024, replies): required
baryonic / stellar mass, interpolating function, external field. Is a stellar mass near 7e10 Msun
already required in the literature?

Q3. External field at the Milky Way in the MOND literature: strength (a0 units or m/s^2), direction,
origin (M31, Virgo, large-scale structure). Compare with 0.0127 a0.

Q4. MOND treatments of the outer Milky Way: escape speed, satellite orbits, Sagittarius or other
distant streams, external-field truncation of the phantom halo. Does anyone include hot
circumgalactic gas in the baryon budget, and with what mass?

Q5. For each of NMB1, PLT1, MWJ1, MWG1, MWI1: duplicated, contradicted, or supported by published
work, with the source.

## What counts as an answer

- Q1: a paper (arXiv ID, date on or after 2026-11-03) that analyses the DESI DR2 MW mass profile
  under MOND/modified gravity or criticises the mock calibration; or a citation list for 2611.05518
  showing none. If no citation index is reachable, "not found" with the searches listed.
- Q2: per paper, the baryonic or stellar mass used/required (Msun), the interpolating function, the
  external field adopted, and URL. "Near 7e10 stars" = a published MOND fit whose stellar mass is
  in 6.5e10 to 7.5e10 or whose total baryonic mass implies it.
- Q3: per paper, the field value (a0 units or m/s^2), direction if given, source of the field, URL.
- Q4: per paper, the outer-galaxy quantity treated, the result, whether hot gas is included and its
  mass, URL.
- Q5: one line per lane naming the source(s) and the classification. "Supported" requires a
  published number on the same quantity; otherwise "no published comparator found".
