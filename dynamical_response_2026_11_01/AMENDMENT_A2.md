vary less than 1% between the final two counts. Angular-momentum quadrature,

spatial basis; spatial truncation is the identified remaining error.

phases to resolve the richer spatial basis. This tests the named numerical

baseline if needed. Retain any unresolved final difference.

even when integrated work has converged. It therefore cannot support a claim

20→40 pulse-duration control tests finite-pulse energy, subject to independent
Krylov comparison; it does not repair or assume the late-time interpretation.
