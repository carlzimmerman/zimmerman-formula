κ = ½ FITTED; not theory closed

Pre-result. Record: ls SPARC and AZ directories, git log SPARC/resolvent, rg resolvent/finite-volume in KT19 and AZ. KT19 is Gaussian; AZ10 squared resolvent has no SPARC result. No new external data. This is a fixed-rule test, not a mass-to-light or length scan.

Inputs: real_research/data/sparc_data/*_rotmod.dat and real_research/data/SPARC_Lelli2016c.mrt, exactly the KT19 selection: >=3 usable rows; R>0, Vobs>0, eV>0 and eV/Vobs<0.1; positive total baryonic V². Stellar V²=.5 Vdisc|Vdisc|+.7 Vbulge|Vbulge|; gas V²=Vgas|Vgas|. These mass-to-light values are FITTED/held conventions, not derived. Total inventory for xi is Mb=.5 L+1.33 MHI (same KT19 convention; helium factor an assumed inventory coefficient). xi=.5 sqrt(G Mb/a0); kappa=.5 FITTED. This remains a system-mass benchmark, not a field-local rule.

Construct spherical enclosed M(R)=max_accumulate(max(Vbar² R/G,0)), as KT19. Inside first point M~r³; beyond last point add max(Mb-Mlast,0)*[1-(1+x/Rd)exp(-x/Rd)] with Rd=max(tabulated Rd,.1 kpc). No change to the observed input field gb at data points. The spherical surrogate and outer continuation are assumptions, NOT a disc field solve. Apply (I-xi² Laplacian)^-2 to its density with finite-volume radial fluxes. Domain 0 to Rlast+30xi+20Rd, log shells from min(Rfirst,.01)*.001; zero flux at centre/outer edge. Integrate smoothed shell masses to form ratio Msm(R)/Minput(R). Then gh=gb*ratio and gmodel=gb+[nu(gh/a0)-1]gh, simple nu. Two a0 footings unchanged.

Frozen gates from KT19: galaxy-equal rms residual log10(gobs/gmodel) minus plain-law rms <=0.005 dex AND |pooled median residual|<=0.05 dex, both footings separately. These are carried LINE-ONLY comparison gates, not newly inferred measurement errors. No full A1/A2/SPARC claim from this different fixed-U statistic. Report per-galaxy changes and raw margins. No parameter search.

Known controls: delta-like source in first shell with xi=1, outer30, N=2048: enclosed fractions at r=1,2,4 must match gammainc(3,r) to absolute .002. Conservative solve preserves total mass to relative 1e-9; positivity >=-1e-12 times peak density. Zero width gives ratio1 (control applied algebraically), reproducing plain law to 1e-12. Real refinement N=1024 then2048: rms and median change <=.001 dex and no gate-label change, else NO VERDICT. MUTATE nu=1: model must equal gb and must fail at least one frozen SPARC line on both footings. Mutation evaluated against the real unmutated plain-law reference, not allowed to mutate its own comparator. Expected same usable population as KT28 (155 galaxies,2788 points), else disclose and NO VERDICT pending reconciliation.
