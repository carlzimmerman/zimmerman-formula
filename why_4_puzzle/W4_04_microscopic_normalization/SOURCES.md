were downloaded or retained, so there are no retained source-file hashes.
Source formulas are inputs; the local algebra, proposed dictionaries and

## Local supersymmetry

Bergshoeff, Freedman, Kallosh and Van Proeyen, *Pure de Sitter Supergravity*,
[1507.08264v6](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.08264v6), 10 October 2016,
equations 1.2-1.3 and 2.1: vacuum V=f^2-3Mp^2m32^2 and a genuinely local
supersymmetry including the vierbein. Authenticated/extracted by the route
agent and checked by the reviewer. The fixed-V deformation and absence of
a MOND force in the Einstein+V bosonic truncation are local applications.

Cribiori, Farakos and Tournoy, *Supersymmetric Born-Infeld actions and new
Fayet-Iliopoulos terms*, [1811.08424v3](https://arxiv.org/html/1811.08424v3),
10 April 2019, equations 3.2 and 4.12. Coordinator, route agent and reviewer
inspected the primary action. The canonical dictionary is T=beta m_BI,
F_c=sqrt(beta/m_BI)F, V=2T-3Mp^2m32^2. The local derivation computes
the electric quartic coefficient and exact fixed-embedding electric field.
The equality c4 V=1/4 assumes m32=0 and concerns that classical model's
electric susceptibility. It is not xi, an all-orders theorem, or a MOND
force prediction. A proposed heavy charged matter extension is not an
audited component-level completion and is not needed for the exponent test.

## Finite microscopic induction and thermal escape

Frolov, Fursaev and Zelnikov, *Statistical Origin of Black Hole Entropy in
Induced Gravity*, [hep-th/9607104v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/9607104),
12 July 1996, section 2, equations 2.1-2.14. Primary constraints and induced
Einstein coefficient were inspected by coordinator, agent and reviewer.
The first five spectral cancellations imply finite vacuum/Einstein terms;
the sixth separately sets vacuum zero in the source. The local example
omits that sixth condition, explicitly assumes no bare terms, and constructs
a positive finite spectrum. Its proposed source spurion and spectral
deformations are calculations here, not source theorems. The example is not
a self-consistent low-curvature cosmological solution.

Vassilevich, *Heat kernel expansion: user's manual*,
[hep-th/0306138v3](https://arxiv.org/html/hep-th/0306138v3), equations
1.18-1.22 and 4.26-4.28. The agent checked the determinant/heat-kernel
dictionary and the scope of its local expansion. Our analytic/gapless-power
argument concerns free constant backgrounds with the stated local
renormalization and does not exclude interacting nonlocal IR mechanisms.

Arnold and Espinosa, *The Effective Potential and First-Order Phase
Transitions: Beyond Leading-Order*,
[hep-ph/9212235v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-ph/9212235v2), equations
2.4-2.7 and 2.12. Agent and reviewer authenticated the primary thermal
expansion, negative bosonic cubic, fermionic absence of a zero-mode cubic,
and thermal-mass screening replacement. The ratios 16/15 and 16/5 and
relative-slope deformation are independently derived here. The thermal state
is radiation; its small-X quadratic coefficient is not a high-X force
coefficient. Those failed bridges prevent an operational xi prediction.

## String and compactification

Myers, *Dielectric-Branes*,
[hep-th/9910053v2](https://arxiv.org/html/hep-th/9910053v2), equations
1,3,21-23 and section 2 before equation 6. The route agent extracted D3
tension, endpoint-normalized Abelian DBI and closed/open coupling
conventions. The E reviewer independently checked the tension and canonical
normalization. Our formulas use e^2=2pi g_s for that Abelian endpoint,
not a nonabelian generator convention. The brane's electric force scale
and an endpoint's acceleration are distinct observables.

Callan and Maldacena, *Brane Dynamics From the Born-Infeld Action*,
[hep-th/9708147v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/9708147v2), section 2,
equations 2.3-2.9, section 3, equations 3.2-3.7. The route agent checked
the transverse-scalar/BPS embedding alternative: the full BPS configuration
is not a rigid electric source and its total force must not be inferred
from the rigid electric threshold alone.

Giddings, Kachru and Polchinski, *Hierarchies from Fluxes in String
Compactifications*, [hep-th/0105097v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/0105097v2),
equations 2.24,2.30-2.32,3.17,3.19 and Appendix A. The agent verified the
specific tree-level Minkowski/no-scale example and D3 probe cancellation.
This does not imply all BPS compactifications have zero vacuum energy.

Kachru, Kallosh, Linde and Trivedi, *de Sitter Vacua in String Theory*,
[hep-th/0301240v2](https://arxiv.org/html/hep-th/0301240v2), equations
11-16 and section III.2 final displayed potential. Agent and isolated E
reviewer checked the source. D is defined here as the actual coefficient
of sigma^-3 to avoid the source's factor-eight notation change. The
Minkowski crossing and nearby uplift derivative are derived locally in
the single-real-modulus leading EFT. Exact realization of each continuous
choice by discrete flux data is not established.
