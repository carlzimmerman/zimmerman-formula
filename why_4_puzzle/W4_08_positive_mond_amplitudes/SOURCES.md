Text/PDF inspected through bounded web reads, 2026-10-20; no source files
downloaded or retained. Versions below identify the inspected sources;
there is no claimed byte-hash cache. All displayed band integrals, R(d),
stress identities, raw fluctuation expansions and constrained construction
were derived here and independently reconstructed in the scoped review.

## Critical fermions and supersymmetry

- J. L. Davis, P. Kraus, A. Shah, *Gravity Dual of a Quantum Hall Plateau
  Transition*, JHEP 11 (2008) 020, Appendix/section 2 homogeneous
  Gross-Neveu formulas, Eqs. 2.14-2.17; [published PDF](https://inspirehep.net/files/0168a297cdf680eeef86c0c8f11a6848).
  Supports the critical homogeneous auxiliary-field potential and its
  nonanalytic cubic. It does not establish the gradient identification,
  a MOND force, our finite paired-band energy, or the bubble formula.
  PROOF C's bubble was computed directly by Feynman parameterization.
- I. Antoniadis and D. M. Ghilencea, *Low-scale SUSY breaking and the
  (s)goldstino physics*, [arXiv:1210.8336v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.8336),
  2013-01-24, Eqs. 5-6, 9-13. Supports Kähler-curvature control of a
  scalar/goldstino splitting. A local Polonyi pattern
  `K=|Z|^2-|Z|^4/M_K`, `W=fZ` has V=f^2 and scalar gap squared
  `4f^2/M_K`; the curvature scale remains free. This is a diagnostic
  example, not a verified supersymmetric embedding of the gradient band.
  Adding `m(h)Z^2/2` induces a tadpole at the proposed origin, so that
  shortcut does not establish the desired pairing mechanism.

## Two-field MOND superfluid

- T. Mistele, *Two-field superfluid dark matter*,
  [arXiv:2009.03003v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2009.03003v3),
  2021-01-15, Eqs. 15-17 (charge), 27, 29 and 37 (model), Appendix B
  Eqs. 60-64 (amplitude parent). These define the action and charge
  convention used in PROOF D. The full radial quadratic Hamiltonian,
  finite-cutoff derivative, susceptibility and P(1) calculation are our
  direct expansions of that action; the source is not cited as proving
  the requested normalization or our stronger causal qualification.
- L. Berezhiani and J. Khoury, *Theory of dark matter superfluidity*,
  [arXiv:1507.01019v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1507.01019v2),
  2016-03-23, section 4.1 Eqs. 50-62 and section 4.2. The original
  one-field spacelike MOND branch has the familiar instability; finite
  temperature is an additional proposal. It is not the positive
  two-field Hamiltonian derived here.
- D. T. Son and M. Wingate, *General coordinate invariance and conformal
  invariance in nonrelativistic physics: Unitary Fermi gas*,
  [cond-mat/0509786v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0509786), Eqs. 2-3.
  The unitary gas gives the nonrelativistic pressure exponent 5/2 in
  three space dimensions, not the required static gradient cube.

  [arXiv:1909.02571v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.02571), 2019-12-21:
  timelike large-charge O(2N) sectors in 2+1 dimensions do not by
  themselves provide the spacelike MOND source/vacuum bridge.

  [arXiv:2403.18898v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.18898), 2024-07-18:
  Schrödinger symmetry fixes a dimension-dependent pressure power;
  it does not establish this four-dimensional MOND normalization.
- The [AFP Pi Transcendental development](https://isa-afp.org/browser_info/current/AFP/Pi_Transcendental/Pi_Transcendental.html),
  theorem `transcendental_pi`, supplies the standard mathematical fact
  used only for the algebraic-gap exclusion in PROOF B.

The constrained action in PROOF E is original. No cited supersymmetry,

singular second-class constraint as a healthy quantum microscopic theory.
