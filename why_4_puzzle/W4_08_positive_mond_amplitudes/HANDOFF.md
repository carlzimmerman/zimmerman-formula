## Conditions for useful continuation

The most direct remaining constructive route is a genuinely protected
critical band or interacting fixed point with an analytic local gradient
coupling. It must fix a dimensionless splitting/crossover parameter and
the gravitational vacuum stress from the same action. A split multiplet
or scalar/goldstino mass relation alone does not do this: Kähler curvature
can vary the gap at fixed supersymmetry-breaking energy. A superpotential
mass added to a Polonyi origin produces a tadpole and needs a new vacuum
analysis.

For a critical Gross-Neveu order parameter, do not replace a dynamical
sigma by yh. Minimizing `c|sigma|^3-J sigma` gives a negative |J|^(3/2)
source contribution, not the assumed positive |grad phi|^3. Derive the
actual gradient coupling and stable branch first. A strong interacting
amplitude may contain pi or transcendental data; the algebraic-gap
exclusion in this lane does not rule it out.

For any proposed completion, necessary new evidence is: protected zero
quadratic stiffness; a controlled spatial/frequency kernel and halo
source solution; stable microscopic fluctuations; a covariant stress or
specified medium/gravity completion; an independently fixed gap/vacuum
normalization; and a second physical prediction. Choosing the explicit
target-matching gap or adding the needed vacuum constant is not progress
on selection.
