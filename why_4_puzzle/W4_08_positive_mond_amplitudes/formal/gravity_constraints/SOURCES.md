# Authenticated source cards: gravitational constraints

Checked online 2026-10-20. No source files were downloaded. Version-qualified
arXiv HTML and its explicit version header were read. Equations in this
report beyond the source dictionaries are applications or new calculations,
not conclusions attributed to the papers. No measured gamma, a0, H0 or
cosmological density is an upstream input.

## G1. Einstein-aether Newtonian, PPN and cosmological dictionaries

Brendan Z. Foster and Ted Jacobson, *Post-Newtonian parameters and constraints
on Einstein-aether theory*, arXiv:gr-qc/0509083v2, 8 February 2006;
[exact version](https://arxiv.org/html/gr-qc/0509083v2),
[journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.73.064015).

Pins: Eqs.1-3 specify the action/conventions and G_N=G/(1-c14/2).
Eqs.8-11 separate gamma=beta=1 from preferred-frame parameters.
Eqs.13-14 give G_cos=G/[1+(c13+3c2)/2]. Appendix A.2, Eqs.52-54,
derives the temporal potential; A.3, Eqs.55-57, derives the spatial
potential. Eq.15 (spin-2 line) gives the squared speed 1/(1-c13).
The paper uses +---; RESULT.md consistently uses -+++.

Translation: bare G=1/(8pi M_Pl^2), c1=M/M_Pl^2, c2=c3=c4=0.
Therefore Gt=1/[8pi(M_Pl^2-M/2)], Gcos=1/[8pi(M_Pl^2+M/2)].
The source assumes universal metric matter. Our conformal matter
S_m[exp(2q phi)g] and its gamma_UV formula are separately derived;
the pure-aether PPN result is not quoted as a theorem about the hybrid.

## G2. Real global super-aether and its gravitational limitation

Oriol Pujolas and Sergey Sibiryakov, *Supersymmetric Aether*,
arXiv:1109.4495v1, 21 September 2011;
[exact version](https://arxiv.org/html/1109.4495v1).
Eqs.19-24 define chiral U^m, U.U=-1 and its superspace action.
Eqs.29,33 identify the real flat bosonic derivative sector:
c1=2f'(-1), c2+c3=c4=0. Eq.38 gives the real-vacuum tilt kinetic term.
Positive f'(-1) supplies positive real tilt kinetic, not a restoring mass.

Flat space fixes only c2+c3, not the curvature-sensitive difference.
Thus our c1-only covariant representative is an additional model choice.
The paper's Sec.6 discusses supergravity obstacles; it does not derive our
nonlinear rational chiral portal coupled to gravity. Its chiral-khronon
discussion also must not be treated as a universal clock no-go.

## G3. A later linearized supergravity construction

Arthur Marakulin and Sergey Sibiryakov, *Linearized supergravity with a
dynamical preferred frame*, arXiv:1610.07805v3, 25 October 2021;
[exact version](https://arxiv.org/html/1610.07805v3),
[journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP10(2021)214).
Sections 2-5 construct a linearized nonminimal/on-shell supergravity
coupling; Sec.6 and Appendix F discuss its spectrum. This prevents
presenting the older global-super-aether limitation as a blanket absence
of supergravity constructions. The source is linearized around its
Lorentz-breaking vacuum, not a nonlinear positive-vacuum completion of
the rational chiral model or a proof of its finite-gradient stability.
No source spectrum is imported into our uncomputed full constraint matrix.

## G5. Original Blanchet-Marsat action and stationary source

Luc Blanchet and Sylvain Marsat, *Modified gravity approach based on a
preferred time foliation*, arXiv:1107.5264v1, 26 July 2011;
[exact version](https://arxiv.org/html/1107.5264v1).
Sec.III, HTML Eq.13, gives the adapted ADM action; Eqs.14-15 define Kij
and a_i. Eqs.20-21 give lapse and shift equations. Sec.IV, Eq.36,
gives f=f0-a^2+2a^3/(3a0)+..., and Eqs.40-41 give the stationary
MOND action and source equation. HTML equation numbers are flattened;
the original PDF uses sectional numbering. The arXiv version header,
not the HTML title-page regenerated date, pins the source date.

Eqs.37-39 introduce an interpolation/Planckian analogy without selecting
it from microscopic dynamics. Those equations are not a derived vacuum/MOND
normalization. Our de Sitter constraint calculation and f_delta test are
new applications of the displayed action, not claims made by this source.

## G6. Exact scope of stationary khronon stability

Eanna E. Flanagan, *Khronometric theories of modified Newtonian dynamics*,
arXiv:2302.14846v3, 24 September 2023;
[exact version](https://arxiv.org/html/2302.14846v3).
Sec.II, Eqs.3-4, specifies the BM action/expansion; Sec.III, Eq.20,
gives the slow-motion action. Sec.IV, Eqs.40-54, studies stationary
background perturbations. Eq.43 states f'<=0,f''<=0; Eq.48 requires
nonnegative background khronon density. The conventional MOND background
is assumed stable. Spherical/cylindrical/planar symmetry establishes the
needed density sign in the stated low-acceleration regime; broader
background stability remains open. We do not transfer this result to the
rational chiral/aether model, arbitrary deformations or quantum health.

## G7. Optional KT condensate term and flat zero-frequency mode

Luc Blanchet and Constantinos Skordis, *Khronon-Tensor theory reproducing
MOND and the cosmological model*, arXiv:2507.00912v1, 1 July 2025;
[exact version](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.00912v1).
Eq.1 gives R-2J(Y)+2K(Q), Eq.5 gives K=mu^2(Q-1)^2.
In unitary coordinates Q=1/N. At Q=1 the term has zero background
energy and contributes the quadratic bracket +2mu^2 n^2.
This short summary does not prove the de Sitter reduction in RESULT.md.

Their full paper, *Relativistic Khronon Theory in agreement with Modified
Newtonian Dynamics and Large-Scale Cosmology*, arXiv:2404.06584v2,
27 November 2024,
[exact version](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.06584v2),
Secs.5-6, retains a zero-frequency scalar on its Minkowski background.
Eq.118 supplies the deconstrained Hamiltonian, including its sign domain.
It is not a general positive-Hamiltonian, all-wavelength or nonlinear
stability theorem. We do not independently import a nonlinear AeST result
through the source's high-wave-number discussion.

## G8. Cassini comparison, downstream only

Bruno Bertotti, Luciano Iess and Paolo Tortora, *A test of general relativity
using radio links with the Cassini spacecraft*, Nature 425, 374-376,
25 September 2003; [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature01997),
[authors' original abstract at PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14508481/),
PMID 14508481. The bibliographic record and original abstract were read.
The abstract explicitly reports gamma=1+(2.1+/-2.3)10^(-5).
This is an experimental result, not a microscopic normalization theorem.
RESULT.md uses it only to compare the already-derived conditional
gamma_UV<1/2 prediction, assuming the model's unscreened local UV metric
applies to the experiment. No reported value is an upstream action input,
and no confidence/significance conversion is made here.
