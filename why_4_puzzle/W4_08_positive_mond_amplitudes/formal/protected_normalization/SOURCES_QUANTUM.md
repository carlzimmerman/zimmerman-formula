## Q1. Exact sphere spectrum and standard versus constrained potential

Lucas Vicente García-Consuegra and Arttu Rajantie, *Scalar effective
potentials in de Sitter spacetime*, arXiv:2511.23076v2, 15 May 2026;
[exact HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.23076v2),
[version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.23076v2).
The record identifies Phys. Rev. D 113, 125016 (2026),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/tkw7-dkq6).

Pins: Eqs.27-34 give the one-loop determinant, discrete eigenvalues and
degeneracies on S^d, constant lowest mode and sphere volume.
Eq.31 translates to A_l=H^2 l(l+D-1)+m0^2 and
d_l=(2l+D-1)(l+D-2)!/[l!(D-1)!] in our D-dimensional notation.
Secs.III.2.2 and IV.2.2 distinguish heavy/light expansions.
Eqs.61-63 define the constrained partition function and probability;
Eq.71 removes the l=0 determinant factor. Eqs.89-90 identify the light
zero-mode perturbative problem and the 4H^2 first nonzero gap.
Sec.V.2 explicitly qualifies the relation to Lorentzian stochastic variables.
These are verified extractions, not a theorem that this potential equals
our inhomogeneous gravitational scalar's force functional.

## Q2. The interacting zero mode must be retained nonperturbatively

M. Beneke and P. Moch, *On "dynamical mass" generation in Euclidean
de Sitter space*, arXiv:1212.3058v1, 13 December 2012;
[exact HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/1212.3058v1),
[version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1212.3058v1),
[journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.87.064018).

Pins: Eqs.3-5 give the sphere Laplacian and volume. Eqs.10-15 isolate
the singular free zero mode and define its interacting dynamical mass.
Eqs.18-22 retain the ordinary quartic integral and gamma-function moment;
Eqs.36-40 include an arbitrary mass source. Their quartic convention is
lambda_p chi^4/4!, whereas ours is u4 chi^4/4, hence lambda_p=6u4.
The exact isolated integral is the leading nonperturbative zero-mode
sector; nonzero modes give corrections. Eq.24 explicitly notes a UV
subtraction dependence of the first correction. Our z-variance and
analyticity statements concern that explicit integral, not a complete
all-order Lorentzian effective action or a fixed gravitational vacuum energy.

## Q3. De Sitter dimensional reduction is zero-dimensional in this scope

Maxime Guilleux and Julien Serreau, *Quantum scalar fields in de Sitter
space from the nonperturbative renormalization group*,
arXiv:1506.06183v2, 29 September 2015;
[exact HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/1506.06183v2),
[version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.06183v2),
[journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.92.084010).

Pins: Sec.III.2, Eqs.23-25, derives the infrared effective ordinary integral
in the local potential approximation. Sec.III.4, Eqs.31-38, gives sphere
harmonics, lambda_L=L(L+D-1), and decoupling below the first nonzero mode.
Secs.III.3-4 relate that scope to stochastic equilibrium and the Euclidean
zero mode; Secs.IV-V discuss mass generation and finite-N restoration.
This is not a claim that every interacting de Sitter theory is described
by our quartic integral, nor that a local-potential approximation computes
the spatially varying source response needed for MOND.

tensor in de Sitter space*, Phys. Rev. D 13, 3224-3232, 15 June 1976;
[primary publisher record and abstract](https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevD.13.3224),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.13.3224).

separately computes the effective Lagrangian and renormalized stress for
a scalar with curvature coupling, using dimensional/zeta regularization.

epsilon=W-H partial_H W/4 in our report is independently derived by
uniform metric variation of the complete renormalized invariant family.
