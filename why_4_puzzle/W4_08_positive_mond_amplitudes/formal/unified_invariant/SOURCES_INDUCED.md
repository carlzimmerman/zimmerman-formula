## I2. What the minimal dynamical frame actually retains

Ted Jacobson and David Mattingly, *Gravity with a dynamical preferred
frame*, arXiv:gr-qc/0007031v4, 2 June 2001;
Phys. Rev. D64,024028.
[Exact version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0007031v4),
[primary HTML with authenticated v4 header](https://arxiv.org/html/gr-qc/0007031),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.64.024028).

Pins: equations 3--5 define general two-derivative frame invariants and
the restricted Maxwell-frame action; equations 9--18 include the
constraint stress and charged-dust relation. Section IV's static and
singular-congruence discussion and equations 28--35 retain additional
longitudinal/initial-data modes. These prevent treating a fixed rest
frame as a proved globally healthy branch. Section VI explicitly
requires matter's frame variation in addition to metric variation.

The packet's anomaly-free current portal, conditional static Newton
coefficient, chemical-pressure stress cancellation and phase/frame
determinant are separately derived. No general claim that all preferred
frame theories are unstable is taken from this restricted action.
