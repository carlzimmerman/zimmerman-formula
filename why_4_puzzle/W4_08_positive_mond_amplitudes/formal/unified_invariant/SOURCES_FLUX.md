## F1. Actual Einstein--Maxwell flux sphere and radion

Min-Seok Seo, *Kaluza-Klein tower and bubble nucleation in six dimensional
Einstein-Maxwell theory*, arXiv2312.03184v2,2May2024;
EPJC84,449(2024), published2May2024.
[Exact primary HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.03184v2),
[version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.03184),
[publisher](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1140/epjc/s10052-024-12833-3).
The HTML header/version and publisher metadata were authenticated.

Pins: section2 Eqs1--5 gives the six-dimensional action, flux, Einstein
frame, Planck scale and canonical radion. Eqs6--9 gives the three-term
potential/minimum; Eqs16--17 gives the radion/KK mass comparison. The
source's m_KK means the inverse sphere radius, not the first physical
spin-two mass sqrt(2)/R. Our physical-source and hierarchy calculations
do not import any observed scales discussed elsewhere in the source.
The swampland conjectures, bubble transition and flux-selection proposals
are not assumed as a normalization theorem here.

Our gauge connection convention has kinetic coefficient 1/g_e6^2 and
flux integral2pi N. The source canonically normalizes its Maxwell field
and calls its dimension-one magnetic coupling g6. The dictionary is
F_source=F/g_e6 and g6,source=2pi/g_e6 for these charge conventions.
This translates the flux energy before comparing potentials. M4 is fixed
by the chosen vacuum/reference radius, not varied independently during
a frame normalization change. The potential is rederived directly from
the displayed metric/action, avoiding ambiguity in rescaled variables.

## F2. Full classical bosonic harmonic spectrum

Adam R. Brown and Alex Dahlen, *Spectrum and Stability of Compactifications
on Product Manifolds*, arXiv1310.6360v2,23December2014;
Phys.Rev.D90,044047(2014). The arXiv version record gives the slightly
different title order *Stability and Spectrum of Compactifications on
Product Manifolds*; the checked full-text title is retained here.
[Exact HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/1310.6360v2),
[exact PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1310.6360v2),
[version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.6360).
Both full-text headers explicitly identify v2 and its date.

The HTML flattens equation numbering differently from the PDF. Pins below
identify the section and both numbering systems where load bearing:

- Action/background: HTML Eqs1,10--11; PDF Eqs1,10--11.
- Radion: section5.1.1, HTML and PDF Eqs102--103. PDF Eqs97--99
  instead describe the massless graviton and the general zero-mode system.
- Coupled scalars: section5.2.1 displayed2x2 block, HTML Eqs142--146
  for special modes/stability; PDF Eqs121--127. The q=2 higher-mode
  positivity is also stated in section1.1 and summarized after that block.
- Vectors: section5.4, the matrix immediately before HTML Eq226
  (PDF Eq161), followed by printed roots HTML Eq228/PDF Eq164.
- Tensors: section5.5, HTML Eqs240--243; PDF Eqs176--179, giving physical
  masses -lambda and their Higuchi comparison.

Its field convention is S=int sqrt(-G)[R-F_source^2/4-2Lambda]. Relative
to our action, c_F^2=2Q^2/(g_e6^2 M6^4R^4), and Lambda=Lambda6/M6^4.
The checked spectrum is the unwarped fixed-flux vacuum of that ordinary
Einstein/form theory. It is classical linear stability, not a quantum
microscopic completion or a nonlinear sourced-halo result.

### Preserved vector-root discrepancy

The checked HTML rootEq228 and PDF rootEq164 contain +3H^2, whereas
the immediately preceding matrix has trace2k_l^2-6H^2. Its literal
eigenvalues therefore contain -3H^2. The source's following claim of
a massless Killing vector agrees with that matrix diagonalization, not
the printed plus sign. FLUX_GEOMETRY.md explicitly uses the matrix,
derives its roots/trace/determinant, and tests the Killing mode. It does
not silently quote the inconsistent root or infer other source errors.
The scalar roots and all hierarchy/force conclusions do not depend on
that disputed printed expression.
