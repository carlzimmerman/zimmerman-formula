## C1. An actual interacting dimension-4/3 Coulomb operator

Anindya Banerjee and Matthew Buican, *Non-Perturbative Explorations of
Chiral Rings in 4d N=2 SCFTs*, arXiv:2306.12521v2, 19 July 2023.
[Version metadata](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.12521v2),
[versioned full HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.12521v2).
Published as *Nonperturbative explorations of chiral rings in 4D N=2
SCFTs*, Phys. Rev. D 108, 105010 (17 November 2023),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.108.105010).
The published PDF was also checked at the
[SCOAP3 primary publication mirror](https://scoap3-prod-backend.s3.cern.ch/media/files/81790/10.1103/PhysRevD.108.105010.pdf).

Exact locations used: introduction's moduli interpretation; section 4.3
in v2 HTML, section IV.C in publication; dimension statement immediately
before (4.4), and the freely generated Coulomb powers after (4.9).
The protected operator is the H1=(A1,A3) Coulomb primary, not an arbitrary
postulated scalar. The source does not prove the bottom-source
hyperscaling energy, its stability, a MOND source map, or a positive
gravitating vacuum. The full-branch variational runaway is a new
conditional application of the stated moduli and rigid SUSY vacuum
properties, not an attributed theorem from this paper.

## C2. Supersymmetric descendants versus bottom-component sources

Clay Córdova, Thomas T. Dumitrescu and Kenneth Intriligator,
*Deformations of Superconformal Theories*, arXiv:1602.01217v1,
3 February 2016.
[Version metadata](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.01217v1),
[full versioned HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/1602.01217v1),
[publication DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP11(2016)135).
This source has v1 only in the authenticated arXiv history; no v2 is used.

Exact locations: Eq. (1.10) for a half-BPS generalized F-term and the
N1 chiral example; sections 3.2.1--3.2.2, Tables 20 and 22 for N1 Q^2
and N2 Q^4 deformations. Each Q has dimension 1/2. Applied to the C1
operator, this yields the source dimensions in the packet. Conditional
one-scale energy powers are dimensional deductions, not source-proven
vacuum energies or a claim that all supersymmetric deformations have a
stable vacuum.

## C3. Four-dimensional primary unitarity bounds

Benjamin Grinstein, Kenneth Intriligator and Ira Z. Rothstein,
*Comments on Unparticles*, arXiv:0801.1140v2, 25 January 2008,
Phys. Lett. B 662 (2008) 367--374.
[Version metadata](https://arxiv.org/abs/0801.1140v2),
[authenticated PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0801.1140),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physletb.2008.03.020).
The PDF header explicitly identifies v2 and its date; the direct
version-suffixed PDF and HTML endpoints did not load and are not claimed
as checked texts.

Eq. (1.3), its surrounding discussion and footnotes 3--4 give the
gauge-invariant-primary restriction, scalar bound 1 and vector bound 3;
the same Lorentz-representation formula gives 4 for a symmetric
traceless spin-two primary. Descendants and gauge potentials require
separate treatment. The packet's one-source scaling classification is
an application with extra hypotheses, not a theorem quoted from this
source about all portals or MOND.

## C4. Protected anomaly coefficient and independent invariant terms

Zohar Komargodski and Adam Schwimmer, *On Renormalization Group Flows in
Four Dimensions*, arXiv:1107.3987v2, 22 August 2011,
JHEP 12 (2011) 099.
[Version metadata](https://arxiv.org/abs/1107.3987v2),
[authenticated versioned PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1107.3987v2),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP12(2011)099).
The PDF header/version were checked. The v2 HTML endpoint returned 404,
so only PDF is claimed as the checked full text.

Exact locations: Eqs. (2.2)--(2.5), (2.9), (2.13)--(2.14), (3.1).
They separate invariant terms and anomaly terms and specify when an
equation-of-motion replacement is an on-shell scattering statement.
The packet retains the off-shell expression for its diagnostic. No
protected cubic-gradient coefficient, physical matter charge or positive
vacuum-energy normalization is supplied by these equations.

## C5. Interacting large-charge states and the scalar/gauge dual

Simeon Hellerman, Domenico Orlando, Susanne Reffert and Masataka Watanabe,
*On the CFT Operator Spectrum at Large Global Charge*,
arXiv:1505.01537v4, 3 December 2019; JHEP 12 (2015) 071.
[Version metadata](https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.01537v4),
[authenticated versioned HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/1505.01537v4),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP12(2015)071).

Exact locations: introduction around Eq. (1.5); sections 3.3, 5.1,
Eqs. (5.1)--(5.7); section 6.2, Eqs. (6.3)--(6.6). These are actual
interacting critical O(2) and supersymmetric W=Phi^3 examples. Section
3.3 states the finite-density domain of the Goldstone expansion. The
packet independently derives its thermodynamic and Legendre formulae,
defines its flux convention explicitly and does not transfer the
paper's rescaled dual-field factors to a gravitational source charge.
No four-dimensional spacelike MOND energy or positive vacuum stress is
claimed from this finite-charge construction.

## Application and overlap limits

No claim of exhaustive literature novelty or a universal CFT obstruction
is made. This packet does not rerun the old free sphere determinant,
gapped analyticity theorem, five-dimensional Chern-Simons prepotential,
pure-electric D3/BI or FI radial calculation. The general concavity
principle is explicitly inherited; its present use is the interacting
critical portal and the common-domain Hermitian Hamiltonian formulation.
All force/stress and dimensional bridges are displayed in the packet.
