## E1. Electric and magnetic FI with partial breaking

I. Antoniadis, H. Partouche and T. R. Taylor, *Spontaneous Breaking of
N=2 Global Supersymmetry*, hep-th/9512006v1, 3 December 1995;
Phys. Lett. B372 (1996) 83--87.
[Version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9512006),
[primary PDF with authenticated v1 header](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/9512006),
[publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/0370-2693(96)00028-7).
Only one arXiv version is listed. The direct versioned PDF/HTML fetches
were unavailable, so the unversioned PDF's explicit v1 header supplies
the exact-version check; no different copy was silently substituted.

Pins: Eqs.1--9 give K, gauge kinetic function, FI normalization,
W=e a+m F_a and the scalar potential. Eqs.12--17 give the N2 auxiliary
triplets, electric/magnetic parameters and the additional constant 2mp.
Eq.21 and the following spectrum discussion give the regular minimum
and preserved N1. The paragraph after Eq.24 explicitly retains p as a
constant and discusses its significance upon coupling gravity. The
xi_FI=0,m=0 paragraphs and Eqs.25--26 require retaining massless charged
states at the relevant singularities. The final discussion leaves a
dynamical microscopic origin of the FI parameters open.

The source is an Abelian low-energy action with a holomorphic prepotential,
not a theorem that every prepotential is realized by a UV-complete theory.
Its scalar potential is used exactly, including p. Our canonical masses
are derived from the explicitly stated kinetic normalization rather than
copying a mass formula across different field coordinates.

## E2. Exact domain of Higgs-branch nonrenormalization

P. C. Argyres, M. R. Plesser and N. Seiberg, *The Moduli Space of Vacua
of N=2 SUSY QCD and Duality in N=1 SUSY QCD*, hep-th/9603042v1,
7 March 1996; Nucl. Phys. B471 (1996) 159--194.
[Exact HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/hep-th/9603042v1),
[primary PDF, authenticated v1 header](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/9603042),
[version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9603042).

Pin: PDF section3, “Quantum Higgs Branches and the Non-Renormalization
Theorem” (HTML renderer labels that heading2). The argument treats
massless neutral hypers and Abelian vectors on rigid N2 branches, derives
local metric factorization, and promotes coupling/strong-scale and masses
to background vector multiplets. Branch attachment loci may change.
The checked implication protects the intrinsic two-derivative Higgs metric;
it does not cover a positive supersymmetry-breaking vacuum, arbitrary
gravitational higher-derivative invariants or a local SUGRA completion.

## E3. Moment-map quotient and renormalizable gauge parents

I. Antoniadis and B. Pioline, *Higgs Branch, HyperKähler quotient and
duality in SUSY N=2 Yang--Mills theories*, hep-th/9607058v1,
8 July 1996; Int. J. Mod. Phys. A12 (1997) 4907--4932.
[Exact HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/hep-th/9607058v1),
[primary PDF, authenticated v1 header](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/9607058),
[version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9607058).

Pins: section2.2 Eqs.2--5 defines triholomorphic isometries/moment maps;
section2.3 Eqs.6--9 displays nonnegative potential and zero-energy N2
vacuum equations. The accompanying text leaves central-U1 FI levels
free. Sections2.4--2.5 give quotient/flat hypermultiplet parent and
Eqs.14--17 moment maps/superpotential. Section3.1 Eqs.18--19 specializes
the Abelian quotient constraints. These verify the construction and its
vacuum hypotheses, not a positive-vacuum normalization theorem.
The SQED component potential in EXTENDED_SUSY.md uses its explicitly
declared sqrt(2) superpotential/gauge convention; no raw FI number is
identified across differing source normalizations.

## E4. Independent higher-derivative invariants

P. C. Argyres, A. M. Awad, G. A. Braun and F. P. Esposito,
*Higher-Derivative Terms in N=2 Supersymmetric Effective Actions*,
hep-th/0306118v2, 8 July 2003; JHEP07 (2003) 060.
[Primary HTML, authenticated v2 header](https://arxiv.org/html/hep-th/0306118),
[version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0306118).
The direct versioned HTML fetch failed; the primary HTML explicitly
identifies v2. No source-file retention is claimed.

Pins: introduction footnote1 excludes FI terms in its massless-branch
counting. Section2 Eqs.9--11 lists holomorphic and full-superspace
nonholomorphic higher-derivative functions; the following paragraphs
separate the protected holomorphic Higgs term from nonholomorphic terms
which may receive corrections. The no-three-derivative Higgs statement
has this derivative-expansion/domain contract and the stated assumption
about superspace CS-like terms. It is not used as a no-go for a broken,
critical or nonanalytic MOND response outside that contract.
