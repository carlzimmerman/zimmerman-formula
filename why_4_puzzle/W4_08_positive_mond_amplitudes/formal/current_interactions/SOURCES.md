# Exact source check for the gauge continuation

Checked 2026-10-20. No source file downloaded or cached. Other current
and variational results in RESULT.md are internal analytic derivations.

Paola Arias, Edwin Ireson, Fidel A. Schaposnik and Gianni Tallarita,
*Chern-Simons-Higgs Theory with Visible and Hidden Sectors and its N=2
SUSY Extension*, arXiv:1505.06705v3, 17 August 2015.
[Versioned HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/1505.06705v3),
[PDF internally identifying v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1505.06705).
The versioned PDF URL was unavailable on the first root fetch; the
unversioned PDF's first-page identifier authenticates the read version.

Authentication and extraction: Eq.4 defines the bosonic CS normalization;
Eqs.6-9 give its current and Gauss law; Eqs.24-25 eliminate A0 and give
the positive static energy. Eqs.32 and34 give a SUSY component action
and sextic potential. In a=eA conventions, Eq.4 uses K=kappa/e^2 in
our K epsilon a f/4 action, while Eq.32 uses K=2kappa/e^2. With the
latter dictionary, Eq.34 is V=Q(Q-v^2)^2/K^2. The square completion in
RESULT.md was reconstructed independently; Eq.11 was not imported as
its numerical coupling.

Application verdict: verified for the stated Gauss/energy dictionary;
conditional/incomplete for a microscopic magnetic equation of state
because the covariant spatial term remains. Inapplicable as a derivation
of three-dimensional attractive MOND or positive vacuum normalization.
No claim of novelty or comprehensive literature coverage is made.
