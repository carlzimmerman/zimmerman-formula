## Chiral saturated operator

Nitta--Sasaki, arXiv:1406.7647v3, 4 November 2014,
[exact version](https://arxiv.org/html/1406.7647v3), Eqs.2.2-2.3 and2.7.
The earlier authenticated component dictionary is preserved in
../protected_auxiliaries/SOURCES.md. Its derivative-dependent coefficient
freedom permits constructing the displayed local superspace portal on
a timelike clock patch. It supplies neither the selected discriminant,
coefficient 5, dynamical clock health nor gravitating vacuum selection.

## A dynamical supersymmetric unit-vector clock

Pujolas--Sibiryakov, *Supersymmetric Aether*, arXiv:1109.4495v1,
21 September 2011, [exact version](https://arxiv.org/html/1109.4495v1).
Eqs.19-24 define the chiral Lorentz-vector U and holomorphic constraint
U.U=-1. Eqs.29,33 give the real bosonic derivative action with
c1=2 f'(-1), c2+c3=c4=0. Eq.38 at the real timelike vacuum gives positive
tilt kinetic for f'(-1)>0. Homogeneous real tilts have no restoring
potential in this derivative sector. The paper studies global SUSY;
Sec.6 leaves its supergravity embedding unresolved and notes a minimal
supergravity obstruction. Our portal changes its dynamics: the new tilt
tachyon is our component calculation, not a claim in that paper. A
complete coupled fermion or gravitational analysis is not established.

## Top-form boundary and vacuum stress

Nitta--Yokokura, *Higher derivative three-form gauge theories and their
supersymmetric extension*, arXiv:1809.03957v2, 26 October 2018,
[exact version](https://arxiv.org/html/1809.03957v2).
Eqs.2.7-2.10 set the canonical bulk/boundary sign. Eqs.2.24-2.38 treat
a pure function G(f), its boundary term and on-shell action G-fG'.
Eq.2.29 gives T^{mn}=eta^{mn}(G-fG'), hence epsilon=fG'-G in our
signature. The pure-function absence of additional local top-form modes
does not prove global Legendre invertibility, branch selection or health
of the new scalar-gradient portal. Its correct fixed-q first-order
action is derived explicitly in RESULT.md. Compact-coordinate momentum
quantization and membrane jumps there are conditional canonical
calculations, not assumptions that quantize a portal amplitude.
