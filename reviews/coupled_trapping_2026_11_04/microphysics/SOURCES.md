1. [De Simone, Giudice, Strumia, *Benchmarks for Dark Matter Searches at the LHC*,
   arXiv:1402.6287v3, 30 April 2014](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1402.6287).
   Eqs.(2.6)--(2.12), including the footnote below Eq.(2.12), were checked.
   Their cosmological coefficient averages particles and antiparticles; it
   is half the physical pair coefficient used here. The scalar/Dirac color
   normalization agrees after this conversion. Early search results exposed
   an older factor-two convention; only v3 is used. Application verdict:
   verified for this normalization comparison, not a model collision rate.

2. [von Harling, Petraki, *Bound-state formation for thermal relic dark matter
   and unitarity*, arXiv:1407.7874v2, 13 January 2015](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1407.7874).
   Eqs.(2.2)--(2.7) were checked: Coulomb s-wave Sommerfeld, ground-state
   Abelian capture and the excited-level sum. Application: the Sommerfeld
   formula applies in each fixed Coulomb representation channel; the capture
   formula alone does not establish a product-group bound.

3. [Harz, Petraki, *Radiative bound-state formation in unbroken perturbative
   non-Abelian theories and implications for dark matter*, arXiv:1805.01200v2,
   17 July 2018](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1805.01200).
   Eqs.(2.40)--(2.42), (3.10)--(3.14) were inspected. Capture includes
   representation transitions and nonabelian emission terms. Application:
   conditional until the actual (3,2) product-group transition algebra and
   scalar-exchange modifications are supplied. The source is not used as an
   evaluated capture rate here.
