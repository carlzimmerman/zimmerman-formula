Pursue its smallest unresolved implication: locate its first bracketed relaxed-compressibility zero in [1.01,1.05] and its radial-D zero in [1.5,1.8], for e=.03 and.1 with the same frozen occupations and exact dispersion. No density, phase-space level or coupling is adjusted. Report required density, pF/m, vacuum-subtracted pressure and energy, and c_s^2 at the boundary. Direct differentiation of the occupied branch must agree with dn/ds=-D/epsilon_ns on an interior state (epsilon_ns=m m_s/E_F). Keep this conditional on the collisionless filled-sphere state and on the same fixed one-loop U.

Thus reducing the common coupling does not automatically change dimensionless coldness or the branch signs at fixed z in this frozen model.
