# TP02 independent implementation audit

Reviewer: `/root/independent_math_transport`, 1 November 2026. The coefficient
and CS reconstruction in DERIVATION.md, including the completed 49-field
contraction, preceded reading the coordinator's polynomial audit and the
RG implementation. The implementation was then inspected directly; this is
a separate cross-review, not a blind or physical-validation certificate.

**Verdict: conditional pass for the implemented RG-resummed full-one-loop
potential and its stated reduced-CS log completion.** It is not a complete
finite two-loop threshold potential or kinetic matching calculation.

## Interface and retained terms

- `gammas` and `beta` in `rg_threshold/improved.py` match all independently
  derived one-/two-loop coefficients. The independently contracted full
  49×49 gamma matrices also match; the gauge Yukawa trace is included.
- `flow` runs h,λ,g,f and both field integrals. It starts on the comparator
  relation only at Q0 and does not reset it. The saved interface record has
  nonzero critical-ratio drift, as required by the beta functions.
- Original X transports with −γ_X. The re-minimized canonical T, reconstructed
  as √Δ X/λ, agrees with its separate −γ_eff integral. Neither is identified
  with a constant-canonical logarithmic field. Re-minimizing A remains a
  different operation from transporting the original full heavy background.
- The general-f threshold dictionary
  f²CW_f=1(X/√f)−ln(Q/√f)C1 is correct. In particular C1 already includes
  general f² and equals STr M⁴/(32π²). This avoids a missing factor two in
  the explicit Q logarithm and retains dimensional f running.
- `C2` equals β2·∂U0. The reduced `D1_threshold` holds Q_m fixed during
  coupling/field differentiation, as the CS construction requires.
- The correction −δ(D1 U1bar+C2)+δ²D1C1/2 has the correct signs. Computing
  those coefficients with endpoint-running couplings rather than central
  couplings changes terms beyond the retained two-loop order. Derivatives
  of the chosen field-dependent matching scale are included when forming
  the final reference-X profile.
- `rg2log` defines only the scale-log difference around its central Q_m.
  It does not calculate or fit the unknown central finite U2bar. Its
  correction vanishes at η=1 by construction; equality with the `rg2`
  central result is not independent evidence for finite matching.
- The potential does not add the old common-threshold leading-log radial
  curvature as a separate term. Such an addition would double count logs
  already generated by the characteristic/full-threshold construction.
- F=T² is explicitly its RG angular metric contribution. Unknown finite
  kinetic matching is not claimed to be included; the comparator's pressure
  uses the reference logarithmic field, and a microscopic matter source is
  a separate problem.

## Numerical coverage and limits

`check_interface.py` checks RK4 step refinement, the independently integrated
T dictionary, an independent logarithmic derivative stencil, finite scale
variation and non-reset critical drift. Its successful record at ε=.03
supports the declared interface; the decrease of scale spread is not an
estimate of the missing finite matching function.

`scan_pressure.py` uses explicit compact brackets around the inherited branch
and records missing/partial roots instead of claiming an exhaustive search.
Its roots are obtained with a numerical stencil and checked against direct
high-precision derivatives. The original comparator portal is stated in its
non-claims. All four completed ε=.01,.03,.05,.1 records find their seven
declared scheme roots, with positive-source, radially stable pressure
boundaries and negative-compressibility witnesses. This is 28 successful
bounded scheme cases, not an exhaustive branch search. The review did not
execute an all-missing-root fixture; handling every possible failure branch
is not certified by these successful records. Exact coverage and hashes
are in VERIFICATION.json.

## Finite normal matching subset

The later `relaxation.py` matrix differentiation correctly includes F and
D Hessians, Weyl/gaugino mixing and vector derivatives on the D-flat singlet
slice. Its first Fréchet trace derivative and h'(0)=0 treatment are valid;
the independent polynomial matrix differences and CP-odd tadpole tests
support the finite implementation. The fixed-X classical Hessian and
geometric δF formulas independently agree with the action, as derived in
DERIVATION.md. A general higher off-shell derivative at Goldstone zero
is not inferred from that first-derivative check.

`scan_relaxed_pressure.py` adds the central finite vacuum relaxation once
to the declared central `rg2` comparator and keeps the baseline in high
precision. Five-point finite field derivatives are compared across steps
and against direct high-precision baseline plus a separately differentiated
finite contribution. All four weak cases complete. Their pressure ratios
are approximately 1.177112,1.176779,1.177392,1.185303; each boundary has
positive source/radial curvature and each 1.2 witness has negative old-
waterbag compressibility. This is a finite subset sensitivity result.

At ε=.03 this known finite term shifts the pressure ratio by about
6.14×10⁻⁴, whereas the central/η=.5,2 CS-completed pressure-ratio spread
is about 1.11×10⁻⁹. Comparing the same root-ratio quantity demonstrates
why a small residual scale spread does not bound finite matching. The
remaining genuine finite two-loop graphs may reinforce or oppose this
subset; no bound on their sign or size is supplied.

The subset's off-shell normal gradients and isolated energy correction
are gauge/scheme dependent. Their physical cancellation requires the
uncomputed genuine two-loop and kinetic matching terms. The same source
elimination in an occupied phase must keep its full square and quartic
frequency term; that separate calculation is reviewed in OCCUPIED_REVIEW.md.

Scale variation η=.5,1,2 and one-/two-loop running are useful alternative
partial approximations. Agreement among them does not bound an arbitrary
uncomputed finite two-loop derivative. The action fixes that finite term in
principle, but neither the running nor the CS differential equation has
computed it. The C² continuity margin in DERIVATION.md states precisely
what additional bound would be sufficient for the comparator pressure sign.

The universal one-loop shape and the associated critical-pressure ratios
are not presumed invariant under the running: the critical relation drifts,
finite thresholds remain, and reference-field matching derivatives matter.
The exact tree transverse positivity proved in TP01 helps define a local
heavy branch at finite field, but it does not establish quantum transverse
positivity or integrate out the massless gauge, Goldstino and phase sectors.

## Remaining implication

At the order claimed, the implementation correctly distinguishes complete
one-loop finite thresholds, two-loop RG coefficients, CS-required logarithms
and one calculated finite heavy-relaxation subset of the still incomplete
reduced two-loop matching function. The next
physical implication requires that finite function, kinetic matching and
the actual occupied-state interaction, with light/IR control. None is
supplied by a small residual matching-scale spread alone.
