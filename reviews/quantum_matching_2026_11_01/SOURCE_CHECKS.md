# TP02 primary-source conventions and overlap

Checked 1 November 2026. Exact primary versions were inspected through the
browser; no full external text was retained. These checks authenticate source
leaves, not the model-specific contractions or a physical completion.

## General supersymmetric RG functions

Martin and Vaughn, *Two-Loop Renormalization Group Equations for Soft
Supersymmetry-Breaking Couplings*, hep-ph/9311340v4, 24 July 2008:
<https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-ph/9311340v4>.

Section II, equations (2.1)–(2.3), pp. 6–7, gives gauge running with total
Dynkin index, the Casimir-weighted Dynkin sum and the Yukawa contraction
divided by the gauge-algebra dimension. Equations (2.7)–(2.11), pp. 7–8,
give superpotential and linear-coefficient running through anomalous
dimensions. The one-loop anomalous dimension uses a Yukawa Gram matrix
with factor 1/2 and gauge contribution -2g²C. The two-loop expression has
a quartic Yukawa contraction, mixed gauge/Yukawa Casimirs and a pure-gauge
term. These are the supersymmetric dimensional-reduction conventions;
one must not import nonsupersymmetric MS finite threshold constants.

Verdict: these general formulas apply to the declared rigid renormalizable
model. Its SU(5) contractions, field transport, valley matching and pressure
remain separate internal derivations. This source does not supply the
finite two-loop threshold potential for this model.

## Rotating complex field: an established adjacent construction

Boyle, Caldwell and Kamionkowski, *Spintessence! New Models for Dark Matter
and Dark Energy*, astro-ph/0105318v2, 17 September 2002:
<https://arxiv.org/pdf/astro-ph/0105318v2>.

The decomposition and circular-motion discussion on pp. 1–2 and equations
(1)–(3) derive the charge-supported homogeneous branch of a complex scalar.
Equations (5)–(6) retain coupled amplitude/phase fluctuations; equation (10)
discusses their gravitational instability scale. Their radial convention
has kinetic coefficient 1/2, so its R equals sqrt(2)|T| in TP02.

Overlap: the conserved-phase construction and its amplitude/phase stability
mechanism are known after normalization translation. TP02 applies that
construction to the explicitly calculated threshold model; it does not
claim a new class of charged scalar field. An exact symmetry, selected
charge state, gravity and microscopic matching are not supplied by the
source for TP02's anomalous R symmetry.
