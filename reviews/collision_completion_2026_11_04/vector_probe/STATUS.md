# Broken-vector pair probe

The four massive singlets have an actual nonzero on-shell VV channel in
the warm region. Its average width is **1.63–1.98%** of the included SSS+FFS
width, and a coherent bright state sees a relative width of **7.31–8.95%**.
At the corrected new-history midpoint the corresponding figures are
**1.9309%** and **8.7077%**. The channel is closed at the cold pole.

The result uses actual K=dMV²/dS and all twelve real massive vectors,
equivalently six charged complex pairs. Parent identity and basis are rebuilt
at each x. Canonical finite derivatives, Goldstone Ward normalization,
explicit transverse/longitudinal polarization sums, the identical-vector
special formula and parent/vector basis covariance pass independently.
The same-basis SSS+FFS comparison matches the existing grouped operator.

The corrected target-reached warm endpoint's actual eight-phase population
has **0.22347%** of parents and **0.54597%** of particle energy passing the
existing conservative omitted-vector threshold screen; its maximum phase
energy fraction is **0.55196%**. These are kinematic eligibility fractions,
**not omitted-rate bounds**. V+S/F widths, vector populations/inverses,
polarization transport, force and charged returns remain uncomputed here.
The VV channel is a real percent-level inclusive-width omission, but this
probe does not establish that it dominates the whole warm flow or certify
the rest of the vector sector negligible.
