# Independent normalization source check

Checked 4 November 2026: A. Djouadi, *The Anatomy of Electro-Weak Symmetry
Breaking, Tome I*, arXiv:hep-ph/0503172v2, 3 May 2005,
[exact author text](https://arxiv.org/html/hep-ph/0503172v2), equations
(1.218)-(1.219), (2.27)-(2.28). These give the identical-Z half, the W/Z
state-count ratio and the massive-vector polarization polynomial. Inserting
the canonical SM c=2mV²/v into our independently derived identical formula
reproduces the stated Higgs result. This is a normalization benchmark, not
an assertion of a visible-sector portal.

were derived/tested independently here. The CERN CDS mirrors returned a bot
