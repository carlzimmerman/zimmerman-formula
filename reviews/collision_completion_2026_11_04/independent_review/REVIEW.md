## Decisive findings

1. **The frozen field and event energies are not literally identical for the three corrected gaugino groups.** `VertexBackground` uses a cubic spline of mass squared for `mass2`; its `at()` separately interpolates `current_source` gaugino masses linearly. `full_events()` then overwrites those group masses from that source, whereas `GenericOrbit.gas()` uses the cubic-spline masses. Thus phase-local `Σ E_event C=0` does not imply exact `Σ E_gas C=0` in the frozen interface. This is expected to be a small loop-mass interpolation discrepancy, but this review did not measure its size. The root was notified and reports a new wrapper under construction; that wrapper is outside this review and is not treated as already verified.

2. **The warm orbit closure is not certified by the reported success flags.** In `coupled_averaged_v2.py`, neither the change of an individual fermionic occupation during one orbit nor the action-covariance error controls acceptance. The accepted-step condition only limits total number change. The provisional warm diagnostics supplied to this reviewer, approximately 0.277 occupation change per orbit and 0.22 covariance error, cannot support a slow-orbit physical-history claim. The completed short controls reach only `N=1e-5`; at 8 versus 16 phases and a halved N step, their final charged fractions are 0.99740610 and 0.99739461. This is useful finite evidence near the initial state, not validation of the later warm window. At first inspection `run_warm001` had a result checkpoint and no manifest; it is not counted among completed evidence here. The root is continuing this work separately.

3. **Two ledger/check names require narrow interpretation.** The integration `energy_work_ledger` is identically telescoping: the update adds `E_old−E_new` to the stored work. A zero value checks arithmetic/storage, not an independently evaluated field-energy equation. Separately, `run_stage001` imports memory v3, whose `work_bound` uses the positive-exit phase-energy-assignment bound. That is not a bound on general signed scalar action work. V4 correctly distinguishes these quantities; the old stage flag does not independently validate field feedback.

4. **The full common pipeline remains absent in the inspected files.** The 25-group quantum gas/field model, four-state coherent memory response, cascade terminal cohorts, and new loop exits are separate computations. The late memory stage evolves a changing charged scalar distribution on an old recorded orbit and omits occupied inverse/medium feedback. The neutral gauge exits are not fed back into that stage. The newest signed-work stage is a first correction on that prescribed orbit. Its small correction is useful conditional evidence, not a jointly evolved solution.

5. **Soft scalar–gaugino–gaugino insertions must remain soft.** The triplet mixed vertex changes by 15.297% between ξ=1 and ξ=2; the corresponding reported soft widths are 5.82665e-27 and 4.18036e-27 in μ units. The flag called `mixed_gauge_covariance` tests representation covariance of the width matrix, not ξ independence. Neither step convergence nor the small heavy-mass expansion parameter removes this gauge dependence. A finite-momentum, physical on-shell three-point matching remains necessary before assigning an exact photino branching fraction.

For the quantum projection let `s` be the signed fractional stoichiometry, `w df/dτ = S J`, and `J_r=k_r(F_r−R_r)`. The code gives `log(F/R)=−Sᵀ log[f/(1+ηf)]`.
