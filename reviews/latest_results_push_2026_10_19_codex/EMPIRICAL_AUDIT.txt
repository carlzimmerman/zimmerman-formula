Independent forward fit and score checks
Objective is equal-object mean squared residual in log10 sigma. Models have one parameter each: additive sigma_pred^2=sigma_law^2+A, A>=0; multiplicative sigma_pred^2=Q*sigma_law^2, Q>=1. Circular extra-speed equivalent of A is sqrt(3A), not sqrt(A). For Q, analytic minimization gives max[1, exp(mean(log sigma_obs^2-log sigma_law^2))], matching the code.
For A, the audit used an independent derivative-root implementation: scan a 4,097-point log1p grid on [0,max(0,max(sigma_obs^2-sigma_law^2))], refine every sign-changing derivative root by 75 bisections, and compare roots and boundaries. Beyond that upper bound every derivative summand is nonnegative. Each actual fit showed only one root on the grid. This is a finite numeric cross-check, not a universal proof that arbitrary datasets have unimodal loss. It is independent of the packet's golden search. The packet's exact synthetic recovery and 10,001-point objective-grid checks are valid sanity checks, with the latter correctly described as finite rather than optimization proofs.
