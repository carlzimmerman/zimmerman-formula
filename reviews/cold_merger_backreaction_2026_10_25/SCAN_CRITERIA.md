# Adaptive follow-up after the initial six-speed run

Do not infer absence of resonance from six speeds. Scan 161 log-spaced T values
.01–2 using the unperturbed orbit and scalar monodromy over its density period
(half the orbital period). Check determinant and endpoint closure. Select the
largest linear multiplier as an explicitly optimized mathematical control, not
a physical prediction. Evolve its full backreacting system for 100 unperturbed
orbital periods, with the same B and seed as the original test, and repeat at
tighter tolerance. Check energy conservation and the exact finite reservoir
bound. This selected T must not be reported as derived from cluster data.
