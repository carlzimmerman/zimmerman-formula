# Dilute gravitational clumps and abundance

Base 5d3367ed36. Do not insert scalar density into the external baryonic d.
Use the actual broken-vacuum potential (d=0) for isolated dilute clumps, with
vacuum energy retained gravitationally. Derive the cubic-corrected quartic
normal-form coefficient and check against full asymmetric oscillator averages
at E/D=1e-4,3e-5,1e-5. Relative coefficient agreement target .01.

Use an explicitly Gaussian, nonrelativistic, Newtonian envelope variational
model with approximate conserved mass, not an exact real-scalar star. Derive
stationary radii, radial Hessian and attractive critical mass without fitting a
self-coupling. Include the positive-vacuum tidal term and derive the condition
for a local radial minimum. Compare size with the cosmological turnaround and
horizon scales; do not call an inconsistent large-radius extrapolation a star.
Compute hypothetical required object counts using prior 70 inverse mass
requirements. Counts are budget illustrations, not abundances or observed counts.

Do not exclude dense relativistic branches, claim full stability or calculate
formation rates from unspecified initial conditions. Mutation deletes the cubic
normal-form correction and must fail the full-potential oscillator comparison.
