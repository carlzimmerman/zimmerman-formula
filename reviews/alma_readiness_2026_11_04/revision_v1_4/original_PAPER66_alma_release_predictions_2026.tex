\begin{abstract}\noindent
Observing unit uid://A001/X3788/Xbfd2 of ALMA programme 2024.1.00100.L (CONDOR) is listed to become public at 02:42:31 UTC on 2026-11-06. We state this programme's predictions for it before any of its data can be read.

The unit's science target is U4\_28473, at $z=2.52$, not U4\_27928, the galaxy this programme sealed for this date. U4\_27928 lies 7.36$''$ off the phase centre, so its reading is serendipitous. At its empirical gas mass the frozen aperture gives S/N 0.16, so we expect that test to score nothing.

U4\_28473 has no earlier seal, so we add one here. Its published CO(3--2) flux gives $\log M_{\rm gas}=11.33$ ($\alpha_{\rm CO}=4.36$) or 10.69 ($\alpha_{\rm CO}=1$). At 3\,kpc the baryons alone, under Newtonian gravity, reach 431--846\,km\,s$^{-1}$ across the input branches. The galaxy therefore tests its mass budget, not $a_0(z)$: where the CO reaches, $g/a_0$ is large and the readings differ by far less than the stellar-mass and $\alpha_{\rm CO}$ ranges. Real noise measured in a public cube of the same programme is 2.54 times below the archive's listed figure; with that noise and beam, mock discs of the published size are detected in 6 of 6 seeds, so a detection and a usable $V_c$ are the expected outcome, though the unit's own noise is unknown until release. One disc excludes no model.
\end{abstract}

\section{The framework, as it stands}
The programme studies a galaxy law $g_N=g^2/(g+a_0)$ with $a_0=\kappa c\sqrt{G\rho_{\rm DE}}$, on two footings never pooled ($a_0(0)=9.3603\times10^{-11}$ and $1.1312\times10^{-10}$\,m\,s$^{-2}$), and a gravitating $w\simeq0$ field component called cold dark energy, which is neither matter nor a particle. \textbf{Since v1.2 the cold sector's halo rule (route N, a core $S_{\rm cold}=f_c\,\eta(M_t)\,\Sigma_{\rm coll}(M_t)$ with no $a_0$ in it) has been retired} after failing on measured cluster gas; the programme's current statement is the law alone (PAPER67, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23883741). Route N's rows below were computed and sealed before that and are kept for the record; none of them is a live prediction.

The record's current position is unfavourable and is stated here so the predictions are read in context. On measured gas no tested rule fits galaxies, clusters and lensing together with what has been derived. The law remains the best-performing galaxy description, with misses of its own (Milky Way $K_z$, IC~1613), and has no cluster fit. Rule N failed dwarfs, had a hand-chosen core, and is now retired. Nothing in this note changes that, and nothing here says the data favour the framework.

\subsection*{Statements fixed before the data}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Prediction B0.} CO(3--2) is detected and a $V_c$ is scored, if the noise and beam ratios of the similar unit hold. If the noise is as listed and the gas smooth, an upper limit is likely instead. Neither outcome bears on the law.
\item \textbf{Prediction B1 (every reading with $\nu\ge1$, and route N).} $V_c(R)$ at or above the Newtonian value of the true baryons. At 3\,kpc the lowest branch gives 431\,km\,s$^{-1}$ (lg $M_\star$ 11.11, S2, $\alpha_{\rm CO}=1$). A lower $V_c$ requires lower $M_\star$, a more extended stellar disc, $\alpha_{\rm CO}<1$, or missing pressure support, and it is not a statement about $a_0$.
\item \textbf{Prediction B2.} If $V_c(3\,{\rm kpc})$ is near the 430\,km\,s$^{-1}$ of our low-S/N H$\alpha$ fit, $\alpha_{\rm CO,req}$ is 0.88 for lg $M_\star$ 11.11, S2, 0.07 for 11.31, S2, and FLOOR for every S1 branch ($n=1$, flat, canonical). $\alpha_{\rm CO}=4.36$ with published masses is then out for every reading. The H$\alpha$ fit is weak: flagged low-S/N, edge-limited, dispersion at the prior's bound.
\item \textbf{Prediction B3.} No pair among flat, F-DESI and the band will be separated. At the branch of Table~\ref{t:read}, flat and F-DESI differ by 2\,km\,s$^{-1}$ at 3\,kpc.
\item \textbf{Prediction B4.} R-H sits 22\,km\,s$^{-1}$ above flat at 3\,kpc on the same branch, while the mass branches span hundreds. No separation from R-H is expected either, unless the masses are fixed independently.
\item \textbf{Prediction B5 (record only; route N retired).} The law exceeds route N by 9\,km\,s$^{-1}$ at 3\,kpc and by 22 at 8\,kpc on this branch. The forecast does not reach 8\,kpc, so this release does not separate them.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Scoring}\label{s:score}
Frozen in commit 57d3ac6b64, alone, before any script. \textbf{Detection} was amended before the data by addendum G1 (0113d554d3, owner-approved, chosen on mock cubes only). The frozen moment-0 gate is printed but no longer decides. The cube is smoothed to 0.15$''$ and 100\,km\,s$^{-1}$; connected voxels $\ge2.5\sigma$ with a peak $\ge4\sigma$ touching a 0.5$''$ cylinder at the phase centre are summed, and the S/N against the same procedure at 200 off-source seeds must reach 5, with $z$ within 0.004. A fit is scored only if no parameter sits at a bound, three starts agree within 15\,\%, and $\sigma(V_c)/V_c\le0.25$. The $V_c$ error is the larger of the quoted one and the mock scatter. $V_c$ from a thin-disc forward model, with inclination and pressure branches never pooled. The primary statistic is $\alpha_{\rm CO,req}$ at the measured $V_c$, with its interval from $V_c\pm1\sigma$ and $M_\star\pm0.2$\,dex. A reading is \emph{consistent} if that interval includes $\alpha_{\rm CO}$ in [0.5, 6], \emph{in tension} if it lies wholly outside, and FLOOR if stars alone exceed the requirement even at $M_\star-0.2$\,dex. Two $a_0$ readings are separated only if their requirements differ by more than the $M_\star$ interval on both. Pooling, branch choice after the data, exclusion statements and law-form statements from one disc are forbidden. U4\_27928 stays under its own plan (CFG806).

\section{Limits}
\begin{itemize}
\item One disc is a demonstration. No model is excluded.
\item The detection gate was changed after mock tests and before the data. The first written expectation, that the moment-0 gate would pass, was wrong on mocks. Both are on record.
\item The scoring code has run on one real pipeline product, at a much larger beam, and otherwise on mock cubes; it has not run on this unit. Its injection test starts at the true values and flatters recovery. Mock $V_c$ errors understate the scatter in places.
\item Stellar masses and fluxes keep the authors' reductions, including their cosmology for distances. Rivals and CFG571's angular size are as sealed.
\item The rotation at these radii may not trace circular speed; pressure branches stay separate. An unmodelled bulge or AGN is not included.
\item The E, E-CCBH and BH-$k_3$ $a_0$ branches are not recomputed at this redshift.
\item Two rows were added after the criteria as information: route N on U4\_27928 and $\alpha_{\rm CO,req}$ at 430\,km\,s$^{-1}$. One script check failed first on a threshold not in the criteria; 82 cells matched exactly, and the correction is disclosed in the lane README.
\end{itemize}
