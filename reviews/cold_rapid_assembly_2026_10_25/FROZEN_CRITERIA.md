# Rapid assembly: selection tests fixed before execution

Authorized continuation of the cold-sector mechanism search, not a new fitted model.
Use the selective CCT equations and imposed eta, not the literal v3s equations.

Test separately: (1) crossing times from the previous positive-shell records;
(2) analytic sudden-potential capture versus independent equal-volume quadrature;
(3) equal-mass/equal-energy/zero-net-spin orbit ensembles with differing radial profiles;
(4) the necessary dry-merger gravitational-radius budget;
(5) required enclosed cold mass from existing cluster inversions.

The impulsive capture control is a classical tracer limit: uniform initial sphere
of radius 4b, common speed q sqrt(GM/b), external potential switched suddenly
from zero to -GM/sqrt(r²+b²). q = .25,.5,.75,1,1.25,1.5,2 are sensitivity cases,
not parameters proposed for the framework. Compute capture by negative final
energy; no claim of settled density or a self-consistent cluster collapse.

Orbit controls are phase-mixed Kepler tracer ensembles at common semimajor axis,
e=.2,.6,.9, isotropically oriented, with counter-rotation and zero vector spin.
Compare analytic enclosed mass at .5a with time-weighted eccentric-anomaly
quadrature. These are admissible different equilibria, not predicted collapse outcomes.

Dry equal-progenitor merger: no escaping mass/energy, no external work, virial
endpoints. Test orbital energies -1,0,+1 in units GM²/(2 rg), but do not claim
all cases actually merge. This is an energy constraint, not a merger simulator.

Numerical bounds: 200000 quadrature points/case; 60 s, one numerical thread.
Capture error <= 1/N; orbit error <= 2/N. Mutation drops factor 2 in capture
threshold and must fail independent quadrature. No random samples or parameter fit.

Success would require a derived supply, retained fraction, profile AND galaxy
control without adjusted initial conditions. Mere fast times, a bound state,
or energy conservation do not count. An analytic obstruction to unrestricted
uniqueness does not exclude a statistical attractor for a specified cosmological
ensemble. Explicitly preserve that distinction and all previous covariance caveats.
