S5. What a non-circular condition would have to look like (stated, not fitted)
==============================================================================================
  To fix a0 the axiom set needs ONE relation of the form
      F( K(z*) ) = G( H_Lambda )     with z* a FIXED PHYSICAL frequency,
  e.g. 'the kernel's spectral weight below omega = H_Lambda equals X'.
  Then a0 appears on the left via z* = -(H_Lambda c/a0)^2 and NOT on the right,

  z* = -32pi/3 on the kernel's dimensionless axis'. That is a RESTATEMENT of
