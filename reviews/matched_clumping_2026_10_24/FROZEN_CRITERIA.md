# Frozen matched-acceleration empirical comparison

Question: in the actual common baryonic-acceleration range, do pressure-supported dwarf galaxies and hot-gas-supported clusters follow the measured SPARC rotation relation, or require an additional system-dependent gravitational response? This is descriptive data comparison, not a microscopic kappa derivation or causal identification of dark matter. No MOND kernel, fitted a0, per-object cold mass or clump-velocity parameter enters the primary calculation. Kappa=1/2 remains FITTED; no change to the campaign's vacuum selection status.

Not blind: CFG828, CFG432, CFG966 and CFG880 conclusions were read before this protocol. New work differs by using direct exact-acceleration interpolation of individual SPARC galaxy curves with equal galaxy weights, no theoretical law subtraction, and a cluster sample with measured stellar profiles. Do not label the comparison an independent observational replication; local data overlap earlier analyses.

Inputs: existing SPARC_Lelli2016c.mrt and all sparc_data/*_rotmod.dat; LVD MW/M31/local-field CSVs; X-COP hydro_mass/fgas_profile/mstar FITS. No downloads or edits to inputs. Verify schema/units; retain file hashes. GC parameter table contains dynamical-fit masses, so it is excluded rather than using those masses as independent baryon measurements. No matched supernova-history dataset or low-mass group sample is included; report these gaps.

SPARC: Q<=2, inclination>=30 degrees, positive radius and speed, fractional speed error<0.10, positive computed gbar. gbar=(Vgas*abs(Vgas)+0.5 Vdisk*abs(Vdisk)+0.7 Vbul*abs(Vbul))*1e6/r_SI; gobs=Vobs^2*1e6/r_SI. Keep original radial ordering and segment adjacency (do not bridge a rejected row). Interpolate log gobs at the target log gbar within observed adjacent bracketing points whose separation in log gbar is <=0.30 dex. Never extrapolate. Median across multiple crossings within each galaxy, then median across galaxies. Require at least eight distinct reference galaxies. Record reference count and spread for every target; interpolation interval is analysis resolution, not a physical parameter. Sensitivities max interval 0.15/0.50 dex and SPARC stellar coefficients times 0.8/1.2.

Dwarfs: measured positive dispersion, circularized projected half-light radius, M_V; confirmed_real=confirmed_galaxy=1; M_V<=-6; mean quoted dispersion error/dispersion<=0.30; no detected HI mass; exclude known rotation-sensitive M32, NGC147/185/205. Both error sides must be positive. Use r_3D=4 r_projected/3, Mstar=2*10^[0.4(4.83-M_V)] Msun, gbar=G Mstar/(2r_3D^2), gobs=3 sigma_los^2/r_3D. Upsilon_V=1 and 3 as sensitivities, not fitted. Missing gas detection does not prove negligible unseen gas. Keep rejected/missing-overlap names and reasons. This selection reduces but does not remove tides, binaries, anisotropy or non-equilibrium. Host labels MW/M31/field reported descriptively, not treated as matched causal covariates.

Clusters: primary sample only systems with measured stellar mass profiles (seven anticipated). Log-interpolate forward hydrostatic mass, gas mass and stellar mass at fractions [0.1,0.2,0.3,0.5,0.7,1] of each file's R500, no radius extrapolation. gbar=G(Mgas+Mstar)/r^2; gobs=G M_FORW/r^2. Primary no hydrostatic correction. Sensitivities Mtrue=M_HSE/(1-b_HSE), b_HSE=0.2/0.3, and measured Mstar times 0.5/2. Separate report for three previously designated relaxed systems A1795/A2029/A2142. For a descriptive mass comparison split primary systems at median M_HSE(R500); do not call this a low-mass-group test or independent mass trend.

Statistic Delta=log10(gobs_target)-median_reference_galaxy(log10 gobs at exactly same gbar). One weight per dwarf and per cluster (cluster value median across matched radii). Report median, multiplicative factor and 16th/84th target spread. Paired hierarchical bootstrap: resample reference galaxies globally and targets by system, maintaining correlation across a cluster's radii; 1000 draws, seed 20261024. Intervals are conditional sampling uncertainty, not complete measurement/systematic error or discovery significance. No p-value-based clump detection.

Secondary shape: effective residual mass relative to disk benchmark Mres=[gobs-gref]r^2/G, divided by Mgas. Fit per-cluster logarithmic slope vs r for at least four positive matched radii; also repeat with a fixed common reference-galaxy intersection across radii when at least eight exist. This is a gravitational mass-equivalent diagnostic, not measured physical cold density or a fitted halo profile. Do not convert log-median residual to mean mass.

Controls: analytic interpolation fixture with known power-law; no extrapolation; signed gas contribution; unit checks and direct mass/acceleration round trips; synthetic target gobs=gref returns Delta=0; doubling target gobs shifts Delta by log10(2). Separate deliberate mutation doubles target accelerations but compares their physical-unit reconstruction with unmutated expectations, must fail and exit nonzero. No fitted force-law target. Use bounded single-thread run and manifests. A plot and report must label sample limitations, matched support and systematics. Original cold-sector microphysics remains unresolved whatever the outcome.
