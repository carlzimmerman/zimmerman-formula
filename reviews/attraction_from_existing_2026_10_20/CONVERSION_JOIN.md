# Attraction does not supply a missing conversion channel

This is a coordinator derivation from the equations used in the three
routes. It is a scoped conservation argument, not an exclusion of every
possible physical memory mechanism.

## 1. Separately transported canonical populations

The reciprocal extension in
`reviews/final_closure_push_2026_10_20/reciprocity/SOURCE_RESPONSE.md`
specifies separately conserved baryonic, tethered and free populations.
Let their regular phase-space distributions satisfy the conservative
equations

\[
 \partial_t f_s+\{f_s,h_s[f]\}=0,\qquad s=t,f.
\]

With periodic boundaries or sufficient decay and no boundary flux,
integration over phase space gives

\[
 {d\over dt}\int f_t\,d\Gamma=0,
 \qquad {d\over dt}\int f_f\,d\Gamma=0.                 \tag{1}
\]

The Poisson bracket is a phase-space divergence. Its integral vanishes
even when the one-body Hamiltonians depend on both populations and a
responsive host. Adding a reciprocal attractive density interaction
changes those Hamiltonians, but not this identity. The argument also
holds for number-preserving elastic collisions in each population.

Thus the relaxed energies for different *total* tethered masses are
different conserved sectors of those equations. A favorable difference
of their energies cannot itself generate a trajectory between them.
One must derive the reaction term, physical boundary exchange, or an
alternative spatial meaning of tethered/free from the same underlying
model. If the intended occupation is instead a subregion's mass, its
flux can be nonzero; its density, energy, recrossing and retention must
then be calculated as spatial capture. Equation (1) does not forbid
ordinary spatial capture or history stored in orbital distributions.

## 2. Static density baths

For the retained occupation-bath Hamiltonian

\[
 H=E_{\rm loc}(n_1,n_2)+\sum E_\nu b_\nu^\dagger b_\nu
 +\sum[(n_1g_{1\nu}+n_2g_{2\nu})b_\nu^\dagger+\mathrm{h.c.}],
\]

both `n_i` commute with `H`. Exact square completion or a symmetry-
preserving elimination changes their sector energies and cannot produce
a previously absent occupation-changing operator. If the original
kinetic Hamiltonian has mode exchange, that exchange must be retained
and dressed by the bath; the static energy formula alone does not give
its amplitude or rate. An unspecified attempt frequency is not derived
by knowing an attractive sector curvature.

## 3. The actual scalar source reaction is a different inventory

The existing scalar vertex `b^dagger a^2 + h.c.` changes the source count
but preserves `Q=n_a+2 n_b`. It supplies a real coherent conversion
operator in its stated projection. The canonical tethered/free label
is not identified with these charge sectors in the source equations.
At fixed `Q=4` the literal source-count diagonal energies are

\[
 d=(12\kappa,\ 2\kappa+\Delta_h,\ 2\Delta_h),
 \qquad d_2-2d_1+d_0=8\kappa>0.                    \tag{2}
\]

Charge-addition binding, measured instead by `E(Q=4)-2E(Q=2)`, is a
different observable. It can become negative in a sufficiently deep
host in the one-mode projection. It does not replace (2) or identify
two free/tether memory states. The actual matrix elements and the
ground-state population test are derived in `scalar/RESULT.md`.

## Consequence

The existing attraction can be derived and meaningfully tested without
fitting a new coupling. Completing the physical memory join still
requires one consistent definition of the occupation and its physical
transition/receiver. The current absence of that join is an exact
limitation of these specified equations and dictionaries, not evidence
that attraction has the wrong sign in every allowed host.
