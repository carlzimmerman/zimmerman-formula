## Fixed decision

Use the already counted finite memory control: complete sector energies
`E0,E1,E2`, one conserved inventory and environment, and multiplicities
`(1,4,2)`. Define `U_pair=E2-2E1+E0`. A linear binding bias cannot change
this invariant. With a freely available endpoint bias, both endpoints can
be strict occupancy maxima iff `U_pair < -3 theta log(2)`, `theta>0`.
This is a necessary static hurdle in that control, not sufficient evidence
for a physical writer, retention lifetime, release mechanism, or S4 memory.
Different inventories or degeneracies require their own counting.

No new phenomenological `J`, occupation-selective portal, host spring,
chosen noise law, or fitted release scale may be inserted. Existing
couplings can be projected or integrated out only in their admitted regime
and only once. Host masses, angular momentum, bath density, orbitals,
geometry and temperature are physical state inputs: an expression in them
has no new coupling but is not a parameter-free prediction unless the
existing model/data specifies them. Optimizing those inputs to satisfy the
inequality is an existence construction, not empirical closure.

## Success and partial results

Full closure requires identifiable physical conversion states from one
existing action, complete counted energies meeting the threshold in an
inherited supported state, stable coupled mechanics, and a derived physical
transition/receiver and retention/release mechanism. An attractive term,
an uninstantiated host construction, or a negative energy in the wrong
charge sector is partial progress. A scoped exclusion rejects only its
stated model and regime. Local novelty means a new implication relative to
the nearest pinned repository results; no global priority claim is made.

All available analytic continuations are preferred over new large runs.
