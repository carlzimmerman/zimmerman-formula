# Adaptive finite-amplitude oscillator check

The quartic surrogate is outside its small-amplitude regime for most required
densities. Do not extrapolate it. At each of the same 303 (d,E) pairs, solve the
full potential for the turning amplitude and integrate a complete symmetric
oscillation. Check the virial identity <kinetic>=<q V'>/2 to relative 1e-7.
Calculate w_eff=(2<kinetic>-E)/E and the positive matter-energy correction
<d(A-1)>/E. This is fixed-background averaging, not a coupled gravitational
fluid closure. Compare w_eff with existing vc²/c² only as a pressure scale.
