## Target and interpretation

Determine whether the specified weakly coupled SU(5) model admits a controlled
gravity vacuum, a sufficiently surviving cold photino population and an
explicit production history. Test both the raw-source DE identification and
the separately tuned net-vacuum identification. A successful numerical point
with fitted W0 or initial abundance is conditional existence, not a derivation
of DE, cosmological initial conditions, kappa or the V3 khronon/P action.

Success requires the stated K,W,gauge kinetic data, admitted stationary point,
physical scalar/fermion stability, relevant decay currents and thresholds,
derived population from stated initial data, and numerical/source validation
at the accuracy actually used. A source-link obstruction or a failed producer
may settle a narrower obligation; it does not exclude all other embeddings.
No claim of solving the cosmological-constant problem is inferred from tuning.
