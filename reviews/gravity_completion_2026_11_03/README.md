# TP06: photino, gravity and production

The combined goal remains open. Finite gravity vacua and partial survival
bounds exist; no complete cold photino producer or predictive dark-energy
mechanism has been established. Full results and review conditions are in
[CLOSEOUT.md](CLOSEOUT.md); resume with [HANDOFF.md](HANDOFF.md).

The direct raw-vacuum DE identification fails on the specified weak critical
gauge branch. The [unequal-coupling reduction](COUPLING_HIERARCHY.md),
[normalization clarification](HIERARCHY_CLARIFICATION.md) and
[independent review](hierarchy_review/REVIEW.md) show that a Yukawa hierarchy
does not repair it. A separately tuned net residual remains an input.

The canonical finite scalar point has a25.8667keV photino; the protected point
has a leading.974358MeV photino. [Canonical status](canonical/STATUS.md) and
[protected status](protected/STATUS.md) state their finite-action limits.
Read the [protected pole/current correction](protected_review/REVIEW.md):
the81eV extra-fermion mass is not established, while a fixed-leading-current
partial lifetime bound survives its unknown mass.

The tested radial sources are warm or lack enough initial energy. See
[production status](production/STATUS.md), its
[current derivation](production/DERIVATION_CURRENT.md) and the
[independent production audit](review/PRODUCTION_AUDIT.md). The1kpc gate and
radiation allowances are benchmarks, not an observational likelihood.

The [thermal tests](thermal/STATUS.md) exclude the specified equilibrium
low-temperature charged-parent decay source. A
[frozen-distribution kinetic bridge](thermal_kinetic/STATUS.md) is possible,
but the [stable charged-carrier budget](CHARGED_RELIC.md) is severe. The
[independent energy audit](thermal_review/REVIEW.md) found no release-energy
catastrophe; that speculative obstruction was not supported.

The next decisive step is coupled field, charged-carrier and neutral-cascade
kinetics. Preserving enough particles to trap the field and then removing
the heavy charged relics are simultaneous requirements. A frozen gas and an
equilibrium bath give different partial limits; neither supplies the full
history. Full SUGRA1PI currents are a separate open obligation.
