# Primary-source checks,3 November2026

Authenticated directly through web tool reads; no full external paper is
copied here. Formula applications below are our derivations, with their
own model restrictions.

* [Cheung,D'Eramo,Thaler1104.2598](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1104.2598), displayed
  v2,12 May2011. Eq. 36 identifies the eaten Gi chi^i direction; Eq. 37 and
  Appendix F give m32(nabla_i G_j+G_i G_j) before unitary projection. Our
  D_iW kernel projection and explicit K normalization implement that
  source, rather than interpreting the eigenvalues of an off-shell tree
  matrix as loop poles. The scalar Ward relation is independently derived
  by differentiating the specified potential and checked against it.
* [Cheung,D'Eramo,Thaler1104.2600](https://arxiv.org/html/1104.2600), displayed
  v3,30 August2011. Section IV and Eqs. 37–39 show how an almost-no-scale
  modulino can remain massless under the paper's assumptions. Those
  assumptions are not transferred wholesale to n=3-epsilon with a nonzero
  collective matter vev. Our81 eV result instead comes from its own metric,
  stationarity and Ward calculation.
* [D'Eramo,Thaler,Thomas1202.1280](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1202.1280), displayed
  v3,12 June2012. Eqs. 3.11–3.15 keep super-Weyl,Kähler and sigma-model
  anomalies together and distinguish gaugino mass from its eaten-Goldstino
  coupling. We substitute the actual small-field logarithmic matter
  metric in all terms. This does not constitute finite Higgs-threshold
  matching.
* [Brignole,Feruglio,Zwirnerhep-ph/9703286](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-ph/9703286),
  displayed v2,5 February1999. Eqs. 2.26–2.27 give the physical photino/photon
  Goldstino dipole and massless-final-fermion width. The application here
  first rotates the actual matter current into the uneaten fermion. Its
  small nonzero mass makes a negligible correction; no longitudinal
  gravitino equivalence approximation at E<m32 is used for that mode.
