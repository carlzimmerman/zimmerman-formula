# Fermion and current matching: independent source and algebra check

Checked 3 November2026 by the coordinator. These records authenticate formulas
and their domain; they do not certify a new loop-corrected SU5 spectrum.
Primary PDFs were read with the web tool. No full external work was retained.

## Chiral mass matrix

Cheung, D'Eramo, Thaler, *Supergravity Computations without Gravity
Complications*, arXiv1104.2598, displayed v2,12May2011,
<https://arxiv.org/pdf/1104.2598>, printedp6 Eqs35--37 and AppendixF.
This is a tree-level near-Minkowski matter spectrum, retaining m3/2 effects.
G=K+ln|W|² in their Mp=1 convention. Eq36 identifies eta=Gi chi^i/sqrt3;
Eq37 supplies the pre-unitary-gauge matrix m32(nabla_i Gj+GiGj).

For canonical K in project dimensionless p=Mp/mu units, this is

    M0_ij=exp(K/2p²)[Wij+(zi*Wj+zj*Wi)/p²+zi*zj*W/p⁴].

Let N be a canonically normalized frame with (DiW)N=0. Every rank-one
correction c(DiW)(DjW) vanishes in N^T M N. In particular the familiar
near-Minkowski unitary-gauge subtraction
exp(K/2p²)(2/3)(DiW)(DjW)/W yields the same restricted matrix. This elementary
identity is independent of the numerical coefficient, but requires the proper
kinetic inner product and complex transpose/conjugation convention.

A loop-stationary point is generally off shell for the tree action. The tree
mass times the tree Goldstino need not vanish there. Projecting it out does
not establish the loop Ward identity, physical pole or a fully matched
super-Higgs spectrum. Tree background/gradient residuals and loop 1PI current
and self-energy corrections must be assessed before claiming those results.

## Added no-scale neutral modes

Same authors, *The Spectrum of Goldstini and Modulini*, arXiv1104.2600,
displayed v3,30August2011, <https://arxiv.org/pdf/1104.2600>.
The paper treats distinct assumptions for F/D breaking and almost-no-scale
breaking; the latter can leave a tree-massless modulino. Thus the generic
2m32 mass statement cannot be imported into the proposed logarithmic model
without checking its hypotheses, stationarity and actual kinetic geometry.
Application to the TP06 protected proposal remains conditional on its solve.

## Gauge mass versus longitudinal-gravitino current

D'Eramo, Thaler, Thomas, *The Two Faces of Anomaly Mediation*,
arXiv1202.1280v3,12June2012,
<https://arxiv.org/pdf/1202.1280v3>, printedp11 Eqs3.11--3.15 and p14 Eq4.22.
The high-energy Goldstino gauge current depends on

    c_lambda=m_lambda-m_AdS,
    m_AdS= -g²(3T_G-T_R)m32/(16pi²),

with the appropriate light degrees of freedom, matching and threshold
contributions. Matter metric derivatives and K_i F^i also enter the full
anomaly mass/current. Replacing c_lambda by the total mass without checking
these terms can fail in a protected/modulus embedding. In the canonical
small-field branch they can be estimated against its strong hierarchy.
Constant classical gauge kinetic data remove the direct tree soft mass;
they do not by themselves remove anomaly or finite threshold contributions.
