# Authenticated external formula sources

Read through browser PDF extraction on 2026-11-03/04 UTC, with visible title,
author/version and equation text. These are formula sources, not adopted
observational bounds.

1. Peter Arnold and Olivier Espinosa, *The Effective Potential and First-Order
   Phase Transitions: Beyond Leading-Order*, hep-ph/9212235v2, 10 December 1992,
   [primary research PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-ph/9212235).
   Equation (2.4) is the ideal boson thermal-gas integral; (2.13) sums bosonic
   and fermionic species with their polarization factors. Section 5.1 specifies
   the Dirac trace and fermionic thermal contribution. Sections 2–3 explain
   ring improvement and thermal power counting. This supports the gas formula
   and the need for screening; it does not supply this model's self-energies.

2. Mariano Quiros, *Finite Temperature Field Theory and Phase Transitions*,
   hep-ph/9901312v1, 14 January 1999,
   [derivation/review PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-ph/9901312).
   Equations (172)–(174) define J_B and its massless/high-temperature limits;
   (198)–(200) define J_F with the Weyl factor two; (211) gives massive-vector
   factor three. Section 6 describes the infrared/ring limitation near critical
   mass cancellation. Used as an independent normalization check.

No thermal decay formula is assumed in the static runs. Any subsequent decay
or inverse-decay kinetics requires its own source/normalization check.

3. Lawrence Hall, Karsten Jedamzik, John March-Russell, Stephen West,
   *Freeze-In Production of FIMP Dark Matter*, arXiv:0911.1120v2,
   8 December 2009, [primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0911.1120v2).
   Equations (6.6)–(6.8) explicitly give the equilibrium Maxwell-Boltzmann
   parent decay source g M^2 T Gamma K1(M/T)/(2pi^2) and its yield integral.
   This source assumes equilibrium parents and negligible inverse production;
   the recorded low-reheat calculation retains those hypotheses and changes
   variables to separate hidden T from visible entropy/expansion.
