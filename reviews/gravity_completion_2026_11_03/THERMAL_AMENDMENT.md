# TP06 continuation: existing-heavy-sector thermal production

After completing the canonical/protected finite scalar calculations and the
radial/direct-gravitational producer tests, the user-authorized objective
remains open. The next distinct producer uses existing heavy messengers in a
specified reheated hidden bath. The first uncertain implication is that such
a bath permits the same shallow vacuum and then yields cold photinos.

`thermal/`, thermal_production owns finite-temperature free energy, actual
stationary tracking and equilibration/production discriminators. `thermal_review/`,
thermal_energy_audit owns the independent energy/entropy/transition check.
Sibling TP06 records and all TP01--05 evidence are immutable inputs.

The initial hidden/visible temperature ratio xi=.02 is explicitly a trial
initial condition, motivated by the earlier conditional cold relic but not
derived from reheating. No added portal is included. Start from h=lambda=.003
and actual finite canonical/protected examples. A hot equilibrium state is an
assumption to test against interaction rates, not a consequence of naming a bath.

Compute full tree-spectrum thermal free energy with all field-dependent
degrees of freedom, including charged zero fermions at X=0. State ideal-gas,
thermal-mass/ring and finite-order accuracy. Local finite two-loop interpolation
fits cannot be extrapolated outside their domain. A leading global CW/tilt
thermal profile must retain that label.

Discriminate thermal trapping, local disappearance and any false-vacuum energy
relative to the visible bath. If necessary, test early bubble escape before
the release-energy budget fails. Do not infer that a disappearing zero-T
minimum excludes every thermal history: supercooling, phase transition,
equilibration and the eventual population require their own checks.

Contracts precede bounded v2 runs, with fresh directories, full inputs/hashes,
one numerical thread, <=240s per run and measured RSS. Read-only Git provenance
is allowed; Git mutations, installations and queue changes are outside this
continuation. An explicit conditional or negative result is preserved without
claiming the overall cold-matter/DE goal has been achieved.
