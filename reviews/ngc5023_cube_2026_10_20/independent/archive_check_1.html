The Database

This page shows the different levels of the WHISP database. The status of the WHISP Database gives the development of the observations and reduction so far. The WHISP Overview Catalog and The WHISP Database of Galaxy Properties are the main part of the database and describe the results of the reduction and the (preliminary) analysis. The published results are given in publications .
