# Primary-source equation check

Source: C. G. Böhmer and T. Harko, Dynamical instability of fluid spheres in the
presence of a cosmological constant, arXiv:gr-qc/0504075v3 (2007 version),
https://arxiv.org/pdf/gr-qc/0504075 . Read equations (13)–(14), PDF page 8
(zero-based page 7), through the web PDF text. The numerator includes -Lambda r³/3
and denominator includes -Lambda r²/3. M integrates material energy without the
explicit vacuum. Under ell=c/sqrt(4pi G D_mass), Lambda ell²=2 v_vac, giving the
report's equation. This source supports only the equilibrium convention used.
No quoted stability theorem is used to certify the candidate EOS, and the paper's
example stars are not treated as data for this framework. The dimensionless
uniform-density solution in verify.py is an independent integration check.
