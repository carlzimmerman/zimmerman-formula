## One exact bounded question and its stopping rule

**Recommended question:** In a finite spherical patch with a declared material
site stock and one finite energy reservoir, can an LR-c-like baryon down/up
history leave an additional positive tethered occupancy at the same final
baryon and total-cold profile while obeying the canonical full-R force
equations and the full reaction affinity, or does that branch require extra
source work/stress/inventory beyond the proposed memory mechanism?

Make this question reviewable before solving it:

1. Pin finite radial support, nonnegative `T,F`, `N=T+F`, a uniform
   `GT/r^2<a0` margin, actual sites/material capacity and initial reservoir
   energy/entropy. A current target `C(B)` is not a historical material stock.
2. Use the counted full-R energy. Reconstruct both mechanical variations and
   the conversion derivative from it. Include the critical positive self-energy
   as well as the remainder. The full force law is the equation being retained;
   an arbitrary instantaneous total-law profile is not assumed dynamically.
3. Specify the internal binding/cooperative energy and its reciprocal stress.
   Define the reaction affinity from the *complete* functional derivatives,
   with finite reverse flux or explicitly counted driving. A fitted alpha
   mobility is not the binding-state derivation.
4. Compare a load/unload loop and a no-loop control at identical final `B,N`.
   Count net retained `T`, nonlinear field-energy change, internal/bath energy,
   source work and any mass/heat flux once. Returning baryons to their initial
   profile need not return the source's energy. Close the ledger on the enlarged
   state, not just on visible density.
5. Report exactly one of: a conditional admissible memory witness with all
   stocks positive and affordable work; an exclusion for the specified finite
   reservoir/coupling branch; or underdetermination because a material/binding
   input is absent, accompanied by the actual required bound. Do not choose an
   unlimited bath or tune a binding offset after seeing the deficit.

Every outcome teaches something: a witness identifies a counted interaction
that could replace a passive tag; exclusion removes that specific physical
branch; underdetermination identifies the missing measurable/derivable input
and the necessary energy bound. None yields a dwarf/cluster fit, rates,
cosmological production, or a global physical closure by itself.

**Veto criterion:** if the delivered result stops after `mu_switch>0`, the
exterior artanh integral, a supplied double well, an infinite-bath entropy
inequality, or a chosen hysteretic tag, it has not reached the newly proposed
implication. Record it as an explicit extension or model definition and stop;
do not market it as the missing O1/O4 puzzle piece.
