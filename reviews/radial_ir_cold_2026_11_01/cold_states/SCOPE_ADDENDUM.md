# Residual photon and cosmological scope

The residual SU5 U1 photon has not been identified with observed
electromagnetism by this action or by the cold-state calculation. Calling
its sector hidden/dark is a conditional interpretation, not a derived
identification. If its decay photon is visible, age survival alone is
insufficient: observational photon-line and energy-injection constraints
have not been tested. A conditional hidden-radiation FD example is not a
proof that the actual U1 is hidden, nor a production mechanism.

The independently chosen particle scale, a compatible vacuum-energy
offset, gravity embedding, abundance, initial phase-space distribution
and visible-sector portal remain open inputs. A future inclusive Wilson
bound would establish only the stated leading perturbative rigid-model
lifetime, not complete phenomenological viability. The frozen REPORT and
DERIVATION retain their original bytes; this addendum qualifies their
occasional generic use of the term hidden sector.
