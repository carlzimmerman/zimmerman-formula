# Microscopic decay and state audit

## Conventions and tree calculation

All quantities with hats use f=1, with physical masses and rates equal to
mu times the hatted values, mu=sqrt(f). The background is the original-X
classical valley at X=z0/sqrt(31e), z0=7.367143721278508, unless a sibling
finite-matched radial input explicitly supplies another vacuum. Canonical
flat T=sqrt(31)X has v=z0/sqrt(e), Ftot=f/sqrt(31). The scalar chart is
z=z0+(q+ip)/sqrt(2). Fermion Takagi columns satisfy psi_old=U psi_mass.
Use L=-y_ijk S_k psi_i psi_j/2+h.c.; the full frozen builder includes the
gauge/gaugino vertices as well as superpotential vertices.

The normalized Goldstino is (W_i*,0_gaugino)/||W|| in the old Weyl basis.
Its mass-basis coefficient is cG=U†G. The 13-dimensional tree null space
contains this one Goldstino and 12 unbroken gauginos. Summing squared
vertices over that entire space, then subtracting |y.cG|², separates the
gaugino final states without choosing an arbitrary basis in a degenerate
space. The two physical zero-scalar tangents are the real and imaginary
parts of d z/dT=(1,0_A,D_B)/sqrt(31). The twelve gauge Goldstones are
excluded from scalar final states and from a physical charged mass gap.

For a massive initial Majorana fermion of mass M, a real scalar of mass
ms and a distinct massless Weyl/Majorana eigenstate, the spin-averaged
matrix element is |y|²(M²-ms²); two-body phase space gives

    Gamma = |y|² M/(16 pi) (1-ms²/M²)².

For a real scalar decaying into null Weyl eigenstates, summing ordered
indices gives Gamma=mS sum_ij|y_ij|²/(16 pi). Ordered off-diagonal entries
count both distinct final labels; diagonal entries already have the
identical-particle factor. Cubic decay to two real massless scalars gives
sum_ij lambda_ij²/(32 pi mS), likewise using ordered indices.

The light SU3 fermion is not stabilized by its representation or an
assigned occupation: a lighter scalar adjoint and an unbroken gaugino are
present and have an actual nonzero tree coupling. The finite one-loop
gaugino masses are negligible in this tree phase space (mass ratio below
1e-6). The light scalar's tree couplings to two null fermions and two flat
scalars vanish. Gauge invariance also makes its tree coupling to two
unbroken vectors vanish: each unbroken generator annihilates the vacuum.
Its tree gauge-plus-flat-scalar vertex vanishes for the same reason. These
are tree statements, not stability theorems. A SU3 adjoint scalar operator
Tr(phi F F), with the appropriate invariant tensor, is symmetry-allowed;
its finite one-loop coefficient has not been computed here. A color-adjoint
occupation is not an elementary gauge-invariant asymptotic gas after
confinement. Bound states and nonperturbative widths require another IR
calculation. Neither adjoint candidate is established as cold matter.

## Perturbative transpose involution

The unitary transformation is

    X -> X, A -> A^T, B -> B^T,
    V -> -V^T, lambda_gauge -> -lambda_gauge^T.

The trace of A²B is invariant by transpose and cyclicity. Canonical K and
the gauge kinetic term are invariant; the reversed commutator compensates
the minus sign on the vector. The diagonal D vacuum is fixed. In the
explicit fundamental basis, ca=Tr(Ta Ta^T)=+/-1; matter signs are
Cm=diag(1,ca,ca), fermion signs Cf=diag(Cm,-ca), real-scalar signs
Cr=diag(Cm,Cm). The executable tests W_ijk, R, MF, H, Cm² and the vacuum
directly, with no mass-basis parity assumption. This is a perturbative
selection rule of the stated microscopic action. We have not authenticated
the nonperturbative discrete-anomaly classification or possible
gravity/UV violation, and do not claim a completed-theory exact symmetry.

The residual U1 photino and photon are odd; the Goldstino, radial scalar
and phase are even. Thus chi1 -> G+phase/radial and chi1 -> 3G are
forbidden. Gauge singletness forbids a single color-adjoint final particle.
The gauge-invariant dipole chi1 Tr(lambda3 F3) is odd and forbidden; the
same holds for SU2 and its chirality-preserving derivative variants.
The scalar singlets cannot repair that selection rule.

Four-fermion dimension-six transitions deserve a separate threshold
check: a possible three-gluino final state has threshold
3 m_lambda3/m_chi1=1.24484636>1; all combinations with heavier SU2/U1
gauginos are also closed. A color-singlet gaugino pair plus Goldstino is
C-even and forbidden as the final state of an odd photino.

Allowed higher-body transitions include chi1 -> lambda3+two gluons,
and mixed gluon/U1-photon variants where the group trace permits them.
The first local two-field-strength operator has dimension seven,
chi1 lambda3 F F/M³. This operator is **not** assigned zero coefficient.
It is generated by the heavy charged sector at one loop in general.
The corresponding finite-F box/seagull matching has not been executed.
The physical charged gap is a useful hierarchy diagnostic, not a proof
that its Wilson coefficient is bounded by one. Charged Landau-gauge
Goldstones cannot be used as a positive physical threshold; the first
diagnostic mistakenly included them and is corrected in run_002.

## Radial modes and scalar source

For T=(v+sigma/sqrt2) exp[i pi/(sqrt2 v)], canonical kinetic energy has
g sigma(derivative pi)² with g=1/(sqrt2 v). Its identical-phase width is
Gamma_phase=g²m³/(32pi)=m³/(64pi v²). Finite matching replaces g by
F_s/[2F sqrt(2A)] in the original-log chart, where radial kinetic energy
is A(s)(derivative s)². Sibling radial/run_dynamics supplies that actual
metric and curvature. The phase partial width is an unavoidable decay
in the perturbative R-symmetric vacuum, hence an upper bound on the
radial particle lifetime, not a proof that other channels vanish.
Coherent radial oscillations additionally require the sibling Floquet
analysis; a dilute gas of radial quanta is a different state.

The actual finite-F photino threshold depends on s=ln(X/X0). The
canonical radial Yukawa is y_sigma=(partial_s mchi)/sqrt(2A). This is a
threshold derivative, not an imposed exponential matter portal. For
unpolarized nonrelativistic Majorana occupation, the leading rest-energy
source relaxes the radial scalar:

    E(n)=n mchi+Ekin(n)-n² y_sigma²/(2 mrad²).

For an ideal distribution with dPkin/dn=mchi vdisp², the relative
attractive contribution to compressibility is
n y_sigma²/(mrad² mchi vdisp²). The executable evaluates that actual
rest source at the parent's conditional mchi=1MeV,e=.003,
rho=.3GeV/cm³,vdisp=.001. A leading weak A and mrad is sufficient for
the >50-order separation; it is explicitly not relabeled a finite-.003
radial matching. The state has no net axial R charge, and is not the
homogeneous circular-phase waterbag whose pressure failed in TP01--03.
Abundance and initial cold phase-space population remain external inputs.
