The necessary physical choices cannot be silently exchanged:

- The MeV particle point needs an independent vacuum normalization; its raw
  V0/rho_DE is about 1.396e74. If raw V0 itself equals DE, mu is fixed near
  5.286e-12 GeV. That variant fails the photino density and small-harmonic-
  radial energy tests. Large bosonic/nonperturbative states are not excluded.
- The residual photon has no specified observational identity. A hidden-bath
  example and a visible-photon model require different phenomenology.
- A relic FD distribution, its temperature and any entropy heating are
  assumptions. No production, abundance or thermal history was derived.
- Rigid SUSY has a massless Goldstino. A chosen supergravity embedding must
  check its gravitino, vacuum cancellation and extra soft terms. The minimal
  conditional gravitino estimate is about 14 keV at the MeV example, but
  that estimate does not construct the embedding.
