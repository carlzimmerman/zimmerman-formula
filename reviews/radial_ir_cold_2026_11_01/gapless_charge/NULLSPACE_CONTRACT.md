# Neutral nullspace check, frozen before execution

At z0 and e=.01,.03, extend the twelve unbroken raw gaugino external legs by
the canonically normalized chiral Goldstino F*/sqrt(V0). Evaluate the same
full finite one-loop chirality flip; require mixed gaugino/Goldstino entries
below1e−7 relative to the U1 photino mass. This checks that neutral degeneracy
does not change the reported leading photino pole. Output the Goldstino
diagonal separately: it depends on loop stationarity; no exact vanishing is
assumed or imposed. At r1.21 the zero-frequency gaugino entry is a background
threshold, not a vacuum pole or a rotating-state quasiparticle mass.
