# Degenerate-basis and exact scaling controls

Frozen before execution. The verified model.py is immutable. At e=.03,
f=1 and inherited zminimum, rotate real scalar, Takagi fermion and vector
bases by deterministic real Givens rotations within each exact mass group.
Rebuild the quartic_iijj contraction from the transformed YR and D tensors,
not from its old diagonal alone. Compare every grouped nonvector graph
weight and all ten finite graph values before and after rotation, with
source prefactors unchanged. No random sampling is used.

At e=.01 with identical dimensionless z and Q²/e, require each graph to
scale as e^4. Separately evaluate actual four-class SU5 f=0,g=0 supersymmetric
graphs, whose sum must vanish (unlike the f=0,g!=0 case, this test needs no
vector classes). The coordinator independently already tested three fully
gauged SUSY points and complete reduced CS; this run is an orthogonal basis
and scalar/fermion control, not an identical rerun of those points.

Float64/complex128; relative grouped-weight and graph tolerances1e-8,
SUSY cancellation relative to sum of absolute contributions1e-9. These are
finite numerical controls, not interval error certificates. One thread,
180wall/170CPU seconds, measured RSS below1GB, fresh v2 provenance.
