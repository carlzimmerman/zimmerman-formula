# A constructive attempt: curvature-triggered reservoir production

**This is a concrete candidate action, followed through its gravitational backreaction. It is not the requested completed theory.** The mechanism would use high curvature to hold a field at a symmetric vacuum, then release potential energy when falling curvature destabilizes it. Unlike a prescribed production pulse, the trigger and energy reside in one action. The test finds a sharp obstruction to its intended late-transition, heavy-cold-field limit, and exposes which relations still lack symmetry protection.

The new result is a bound on the *physical*, canonically normalized scalar mass:

    m_E/H_E <= 2(1-s)/(s sqrt(r)),
    r=V0/DeltaV,  s=xi v²/M².

It includes mixing with metric gravity. For equal potential scales r=1, matching the existing imposed eta at the illustrative vacuum Hubble scale requires s<=1.85e-12. The transition curvature then satisfies R_trigger/R_false>=2.70e11, far from a transition set by the late vacuum curvature. Setting a bare scalar mass by eta without this kinetic correction would give a false success.

## 1. The actual mechanism being attempted

In units hbar=c=1, take metric gravity with Levi-Civita connection and

    S = integral sqrt(-g) [F(phi) R/2 - (partial phi)²/2 - U(phi)] + S_b[g,b],
    F(phi) = M² - xi phi²,
    U(phi) = V0 + lambda (phi²-v²)²/4,
    DeltaV = lambda v^4/4.

All of V0, lambda, xi and v² are positive. S_b denotes minimally Jordan-coupled baryons. The Z2 symmetry phi -> -phi forbids odd powers, and high positive R gives a positive contribution xi R to the mass at phi=0:

    m_eff,0² = -lambda v² + xi R,
    R_trigger = lambda v²/xi = 4 DeltaV/(s M²).

If curvature falls through this threshold, fluctuations can grow and the field can roll toward a broken state. Oscillations and fragmentation could in principle provide a reservoir; their abundance and momentum spectrum have not yet been computed. The exact homogeneous solution phi=0 does not spontaneously move without a seed. A quantum state and its fluctuations must be specified before claiming production is predicted.

This is a new covariant prerequisite model, not the previously postulated CCT selective action. It has no derived MOND response or protected vanished terms. We do not append a fitted MOND force and count that as completion. Curvature-driven scalar production is not a novelty claim: [Laulumaa, Markkanen & Nurmi](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.04061) study a distinct sign/potential regime with curvature-induced breaking during inflation; that abstract establishes related prior work, not this model's validity.

## 2. Solve the vacuum and gravity together

For constant phi and a de Sitter endpoint, the field and metric equations are

    U'(phi) + xi R phi = 0,
    F R = 4 U.

Define y=phi²/v², r=V0/DeltaV and s=xi v²/M². On a nonzero branch,

    y = [1-s(1+r)]/(1-s),
    f = F/M² = 1-s y,
    u = U/DeltaV = r+(1-y)².

The healthy broken branch continuously reached from F>0 at the origin has

    0 < s < 1/(1+r),  0<y<1,  f>0.

It has positive potential and, as verified below, a positive canonical scalar mass. At the threshold the nonzero branch merges into the origin. The s>=1 branch does not restore an admissible endpoint: for r>0 its formal f is negative (s=1 has no nonzero solution).

At phi=0 the false-vacuum curvature is

    R_false = 4(V0+DeltaV)/M²,
    R_trigger/R_false = 1/[s(1+r)].

The same inequality s<1/(1+r) is necessary for the origin to become unstable even after the non-vacuum curvature source has diluted. If the vacuum floor remains above the trigger, simply waiting longer does not cause the proposed transition. This is why inserting R=12 H_Lambda² by hand while ignoring U(phi=0) is inconsistent.

This calculation characterizes constant endpoints and the linear instability, not a full trajectory connecting them. Radiation, matter, gradients and finite-rate effects have not been evolved. In particular, Ricci curvature is not generically 12H² during an early radiation/matter epoch.

## 3. Physical mass: the metric-induced kinetic term is decisive

Transform to Einstein metric g_E=(F/M²)g. The potential and kinetic factor are

    W(phi)=M^4 U/F²,
    (d chi/d phi)² = K = M²/F + 3 M² F'²/(2F²).

The second term is generated by the curvature coupling. It cannot be omitted in metric gravity. At a stationary point the canonical mass is m_E²=W''/K. Substituting the exact broken branch gives

    m_E² = 8 DeltaV y(1-s) / [ f (v² f + 6 s² M² y) ],
    H_E² = DeltaV u/(3 M² f²).

The bare flat-space curvature of U at phi=v is m0²=2lambda v². It is not the physical mass at the self-consistent curved endpoint.

Algebra reduces the mass bound to an exact positive-gap identity:

    [4(1-s)²/(r s²)] / [m_E²/H_E²]
       = 1 + v² f/(6 s² M² y) > 1.

Thus the bound holds throughout the healthy branch, not only for the finite tested grid. In the strong-mixing limit v² f << 6s²M²y it is approached from below. Late triggering means s(1+r) is not very small. When r is of order unity, that puts the physical mass near the Hubble scale rather than near the imposed cold-wave mass. For example, at r=1 the upper limits m_E/H_E for s=.1,.25,.49 are 18,6,2.08.

A field with a Hubble-scale canonical mass is not the existing rapidly oscillating cold-wave degree of freedom. This is a matching obstruction, not proof that it cannot play some other cosmological role.

## 4. Match to the existing cold scale without confusing frames

The current candidate takes eta=1500 pc km/s. Under the conventional scalar dictionary the corresponding mass frequency is

    omega_c = mc²/hbar = c²/eta.

At H_reference=1.8e-18 s^-1, omega_c/H_reference=1.07877e12. This is an external benchmark, not a value derived from this new action. At a constant endpoint the mass/Hubble ratio is invariant under the constant Jordan/Einstein conformal rescaling, so using the canonical Einstein ratio does not hide this mismatch in a unit change. Matching measured Newton's constant and local screening remains a separate obligation.

For r=1, even the most generous upper bound requires

    s <= 2/[omega_c/H_reference + 2] = 1.85397e-12,
    R_trigger/R_false >= 2.69691e11.

These are necessary conditions, not a viable fitted parameter point. The exact mass is smaller than its bound unless mixing dominates. This says that a transition close to this model's vacuum-curvature scale cannot yield the inherited cold mass when released and residual potential scales are comparable. It does not say that every earlier transition fails. Nor does it exclude taking r extremely small, but that introduces a new large hierarchy between released and residual vacuum energy rather than deriving it.

Even identifying V0 with observed residual vacuum energy is premature: the actual endpoint has U>V0 and W=M^4U/F². A potential offset is not automatically the measured cosmological constant.

## 5. Baryon coupling and the galaxy law cannot be ignored

Minimal Jordan coupling becomes a conformal matter coupling A(phi)=M/sqrt(F) in the Einstein frame. Define alpha=M d ln A/d chi. On the broken branch,

    alpha² = s² M² y / (v² f + 6s² M² y) < 1/6.

In the mixing-dominated regime it approaches 1/6. For weak, unscreened sources at separations shorter than the scalar Compton wavelength, scalar exchange then contributes a force fraction 2alpha² approaching 1/3 relative to the Einstein spin-2 force. That is an extra effect, not automatically the existing galaxy law. Density-restoration screening is conceivable in this class of models but has not been established for galaxies, clusters or local tests here; extended sources require their scalar charges and field profiles.

This also shows that “neutral under the extra baryon force” in CCT is not obtained merely by adding this scalar to a covariant metric theory. The full matter coupling must be reconciled rather than counted twice.

## 6. Why the symmetry still does not select the answer

Z2 permits independent invariant operators 1, phi², phi^4 and R phi². It does not require DeltaV=V0, choose xi, or relate them to the MOND response amplitude. A small independent change of V0 preserves Z2 and a healthy branch away from its boundary while changing r, the endpoint and the mass bound. Thus this explicit symmetry fails the requested protection/selection test. Equal potential scales were a diagnostic assumption, never a derivation.

The action provides an actual potential energy source and curvature trigger. It does not yet provide a parameter-free reservoir. Moreover, oscillation energy is not automatically all cold matter: metric/baryon work, gradients, defects, remaining potential and any outgoing radiation must be tracked. A real broken Z2 field can generate domain structure. Neither an occupation spectrum nor a mass fraction can be assigned after the transition and called a prediction.

The mass bound is specific to metric scalar-tensor gravity with this canonical scalar and potential. Independent-connection (Palatini) formulations or additional kinetic/field operators can change the canonical mixing and evade this particular bound, but then their field equations, baryon couplings and microscopic coefficient selection must be supplied anew. They are untested extensions, not excluded theories or demonstrated rescues.

Symbolic differentiation verifies the endpoint, canonical mass, exact positive-gap bound and alpha expression. A separate bracketed root solver checks stationary points across 27 combinations of r=.1,1,10; s=(.1,.5,.9)/(1+r); v/M=1e-6,.1,1. All satisfy the trace equation, F>0 and the derived bounds. A mutation deliberately removes the metric-induced kinetic term and fails comparison with the independent canonical derivative. The initial prose miscount of the 3x3x3 grid as 81 was corrected to 27; the grid was unchanged and evidence regenerated after correction.

`run/manifest.json` and `mutation/manifest.json` record bounded execution and hashes. `check.py`, `contract.json` and `FROZEN_CRITERIA.md` specify reproduction. This is self-reviewed analytic and finite computational evidence, not an independent referee report, production simulation or observational fit.

The useful advance is that a promising-looking curvature trigger cannot be evaluated separately from the physical mass and baryon coupling it generates. Any successor must explicitly evade the derived mixing bound AND supply a symmetry or microscopic calculation selecting the energy ratio and production spectrum. Adding an arbitrary time history is no longer an acceptable shortcut. The reservoir, MOND normalization and protected response objective remains OPEN; this candidate does not solve it.
