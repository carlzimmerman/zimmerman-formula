## Targets and decisive checks

1. **Spin and contraction.** Determine what the nine existing LC1 paired
   simulations establish about the joint dependence of measured contraction
   on pre-disc spin and concentration. Forward-test any justified measured
   ranking against HS1 and rotation-curve normalization without fitting an
   independent scatter or response strength. Small-sample associations or a
   one-mass extrapolation are partial evidence, not a universal response law.
2. **Microscopic pressure.** Derive the complete spectrum of the specified
   collective model as far as its actual action permits; distinguish a full
   threshold result from a leading-log radial approximation. Test radiative
   shape protection and the matter-response condition U_ss > 3 U_s for the
   stated portal, with gauge modes, multiplicities, light modes and matching
   assumptions explicit. A missing portal or gravity completion must remain
   an explicit unresolved dependency, not an invented input.
3. **Law and certificates.** Reconstruct the simple-mu and squared-excess
   dictionaries and correct current references to their equations, limits,
   landmarks and external-field claims. Preserve each original numerical fit
   and Lean theorem under its actual hypotheses. Correct current summaries;
   use dated errata for frozen historical verdicts. Do not edit sealed
   predictions, empirical inputs, published artifacts or formal theorems to
   obtain agreement.
