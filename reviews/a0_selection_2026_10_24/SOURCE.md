Equations (1), (37), (39), with massless limit, no sextic contribution, and zero anomalous dimension, give the one-loop scalar beta functions used. Dictionary: their quartic lambda4=6*lambda here; their Euclidean curvature coefficient xi_E=-xi in the Lorentzian tracker F=M^2+xi*phi^2 convention. This yields beta_lambda=18lambda^2/(16pi^2), beta_xi=6lambda(xi+1/6)/(16pi^2). The conformal sign also follows directly from the canonical Lorentzian conformal-scalar action.

Implication: verified for this massless matter-loop truncation. Not the full gravitational or massive broken-vacuum flow. Section 3 describes mass-dependent decoupling; extension of the massless flow to arbitrarily low scale is only formal. No novelty or complete model-viability claim. The paper's heat-kernel coefficient named a0 is unrelated to the galactic acceleration a0.
