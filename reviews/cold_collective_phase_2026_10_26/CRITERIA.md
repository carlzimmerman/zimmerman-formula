Use the inherited canonical potential and 303 positive cluster requirements.
No fitting of action constants, population abundance or galaxy tolerance.
1. For all 303 restored-background homogeneous oscillators, integrate the exact
   real-field variational equation over one full period for k/m=0 plus 64 points
   from .025 to8. Check background return, energy and unit Wronskian.
2. Refine numerical tolerance and k sampling for representative weak, middle
   and strong nonlinearity E/d states, plus the largest detected growth rate.
   Finite sampled stability is not absence of narrow unsampled resonance bands.

   periodic cubic box, with explicitly supplied small three-dimensional modes.
   Check energy conservation, spatial/time refinement and pressure identity.
   The box supplies confinement, not a bound object or production mechanism.

   P=<|grad q|²>/3 + <q U'/2-U> >=0. Audit hypotheses and distinguish fixed-source
   effective stress from a coupled matter/metric stress tensor.
Resource cap: each bounded run <=300s, one numerical thread. Floquet unit
Wronskian error <1e-5, relative background energy error <1e-7, return <1e-5.
Nonlinear run energy drift <.005. Use double precision; all initial conditions
and wave numbers are sensitivity choices, not predicted primordial data.
No claimed cosmological abundance, full GR, nonuniform matter or galaxy success.
