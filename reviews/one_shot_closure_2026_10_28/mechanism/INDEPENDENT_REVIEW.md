## Claim card and hypotheses

The objects are nonrelativistic streams with positive mass weights, separately conserved masses, finite moments, a common ordinary kinetic metric, and interaction

\[
L=\sum_b\rho_b(|v_b|^2/2-\Phi)+L_0(n,\mathrm{fields})
  +j\cdot\mathbf A(n,\mathrm{fields}),
\quad n=\sum_b\rho_b,\quad j=\sum_b\rho_bv_b.
\]

The interaction has no independent relative-velocity dependence and no independent current dependence in \(\mathbf A\). Smoothness and moment integrations are required locally; the proof is used away from the inherited distribution's derivative cusps. The pressure coefficient, denoted \(A\) below, is distinct from the vector \(\mathbf A\).

Under these hypotheses:

1. The common interaction force contributes zero direct work to local random kinetic energy.
2. For an existing compact isotropic equilibrium with the stated exact inner kinetic pressure, a small canonical radial velocity boost is admissible and immediately violates a fixed-parameter material log closure.
3. Separately, a bare Newtonian halo restricted to the canonical homology family has energy \(I_0\dot a^2/2+K_0/a^2+W_0/a\). With the specified independent outgoing boundary-wave coupling, the reduced ODE converges to \(a=1\) for every initial mechanical energy below zero.
4. That homology family is not an exact nontrivial Vlasov–Poisson evolution for the nonuniform compact log halo. Thus item 3 is not a formation theorem.

The existence and global compactness of the inherited MC27 equilibrium are hypotheses here. This review checks its quoted inner pressure algebra and its use in the new argument, not the complete upstream global Poisson existence construction.

## Independent derivation

**Common force and energy exchange.** Put \(B_0=\partial_nL_0+j\cdot\partial_n\mathbf A\). Conserved-stream displacement variation gives momentum \(p_b=v_b+\mathbf A\) and the density derivative stated in the report. In index notation, after cancelling \(v_j\partial_i v_j\) and the velocity-gradient terms multiplying \(A_j\), the remaining vector-potential term is

\[
v_j(\partial_i A_j-\partial_j A_i)
  =[v\times(\nabla\times\mathbf A)]_i.
\]

Therefore the reported sign of the Lorentz-type term is correct. \(B_0\) can depend self-consistently on total current, but its gradient is still common to all streams at a given point. With \(w_b=v_b-u\),

\[
\sum_b\rho_bw_b\cdot E=0,\qquad
\sum_b\rho_bw_b\cdot(u\times B)=0,\qquad
w_b\cdot(w_b\times B)=0.
\]

This establishes direct trace cancellation without assuming isotropy. It does not forbid indirect transfer through strain, transport or mixing. Taking the central second-moment trace gives exactly

\[
D(3p/2)+(3p/2)\theta+\Pi:\nabla u+\nabla\cdot q=0.
\]

**Admissibility and log-closure failure.** The shift \((r,v)\mapsto(r,v+Hr)\) is generated by a quadratic scalar, preserves phase-space volume and Casimirs, and leaves density unchanged. For the scalar extension explicitly chosen in the revised report, spatially constant \(\chi\) makes the canonical vector shift vanish; its scalar canonical-momentum offsets depend on density and tracker position, both unchanged. There is no electromagnetic Gauss constraint, and the Newtonian Poisson constraint is unchanged. Thus the witness is an allowed initial state of the stated model. It is not established as allowed data of an unspecified relativistic cold-field completion, nor is that required by the restricted claim.

The initial centered distribution is the old isotropic one, so \(q=0\), \(\Pi=p_0 I\), and \(\theta=3H\). The exact derivatives are

\[
D\rho=-3H\rho,\qquad Dp=-5Hp_0.
\]

The proposed material law requires \(Dp=-3HA\). The inherited finite completion gives
\(p_0/A=-\ln x+2/3\geq2/3>3/5\) throughout its inner interval. The derivatives therefore disagree at every such point for nonzero \(H\). This is a valid tangent-to-the-closure-manifold counterexample, even if a finite tracker prevents the unboosted bare halo from being an equilibrium of the complete extended model: invariance would still have to hold at the admitted initial log-closure state.

With an additional random-energy source \(Q\), the necessary relation is correctly signed:

\[
\nabla\cdot q-Q=(3A-5p)\theta/2.
\]

For zero flux, compression requires \(Q<0\). A current-only common force does not supply this direct source. This does not exclude an approximate late-time closure after a transient, a nonlocal heat flux, different stream couplings, or a collision operator exchanging energy with other fields.

**Restricted energy and outgoing ledger.** For \(r=aX\), \(v=\dot aX+V/a\), the block map has symplectic form preserved; this statement is used in the bare Newtonian phase space. Local zero mean of the reference distribution removes the \(X\cdot V\) energy cross term. The random kinetic and gravitational energies scale as \(a^{-2}\) and \(a^{-1}\), respectively. The zero-pressure exterior removes the virial surface term, yielding \(W_0=-2K_0\). Hence

\[
V(a)+K_0=K_0(1/a-1)^2.
\]

This is the energy of the bare restricted halo, not MP01's finite-tracker Hamiltonian. The revision explicitly states that distinction. Its outgoing half-line solution has \(\psi_x(0,t)=-g\dot a/c_\chi\), so the reciprocal body force is \(-Tg^2\dot a/c_\chi\); the positive outgoing flux is \(Tg^2\dot a^2/c_\chi\). Both signs and the factor of wave speed are correct.

Constant wave prehistory and nonzero initial body velocity are not classical C1-compatible corner data. The corrected report permits the finite-energy weak outgoing wave with a derivative front, or a smooth nonconstant history with its initial field energy counted. This resolves the issue without changing the ODE or its positive-time ledger.

For \(E_0<0\), the sublevel set \(V(a)\leq E_0\) is compact inside \(a>0\); energy is bounded below and speed is bounded. The report's finite-dissipation/uniform-continuity argument gives \(\dot a\to0\), and any limit point must satisfy \(V'=0\). Its only critical point is \(a=1\). The all-negative-energy ODE conclusion follows. It is conditional on the imposed collective boundary coupling; the spatial finite-body current source has not been projected to this nonlinear homology equation.

**Full kinetic residual.** Along bare self-gravitating characteristics, the transformed variables satisfy

\[
\dot X=V/a^2,\qquad
\dot V=-a\ddot aX-a^{-1}\nabla_X\Phi_0.
\]

Substituting these into \(f_0(X,V)\) and using its stationary Vlasov equation yields precisely

\[
R=[(a^{-2}-a^{-1})\nabla_X\Phi_0-a\ddot aX]\cdot\nabla_Vf_0.
\]

For an isotropic \(F(E_0)\) with nonzero derivative on open energy ranges, the available velocity directions force the bracket to vanish pointwise. A nontrivial breathing interval then requires \(\nabla\Phi_0\propto X\) throughout the occupied region, incompatible with the nonuniform MC27 density. The cusps do not matter because the contradiction occurs in smooth open subsets. A finite-dimensional symplectic family is dynamically accessible but need not be an invariant PDE manifold; the report correctly distinguishes these notions.
